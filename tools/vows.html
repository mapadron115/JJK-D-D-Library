Multi-party vows (reference)

Master and Servant

Two or more sorcerers. The servant takes a restraint decided by the master — CE maximum reduction, range reduction (minimum 5 ft), damage dice reduction (minimum 1 die), or no simple domains/barriers — then unleashes for a number of rounds equal to the master's proficiency bonus: CE maximum doubles and lost CE is regained, or range doubles, or damage dice double. Once per long rest.

Taming

Sorcerer and cursed spirit. The spirit's CR must be no greater than the sorcerer's proficiency bonus. Training takes 8 hours per CR, worked in 8-hour periods; each period requires an Animal Handling check of DC 10 + 2× the spirit's CR, repeated on failure. The spirit is commanded as a bonus action. Attacking the spirit breaks the vow.

Breaking a binding vow

After breaking a multi binding vow, lose all benefits and either gain 5 levels of exhaustion for 10 days, or roll d6 misfortune:

d6Misfortune
1Death
2Lose a limb
3Lose a sense (d4: 1 Touch, 2 Taste, 3 Smell, 4 Sight)
4Stats reduced by 2
5Cannot use CE for 8d4 days
6Innate technique confiscated for 6d4 days

Avoidable if both parties agree to end the vow.

V6 anti-abuse patterns (reference)
  1. Togglable vows — a vow that can be switched on and off at will is not a binding vow.
  2. Not actually giving up anything — if the sacrifice costs you nothing in practice, the GM denies it.
  3. More CE cost for DC — paying extra CE for a higher save DC is not a vow trade.
  4. Reaction negation — vows that negate a reaction for free are auto-rejected.
  5. Mimicry — copying another vow's effect to stack benefits is denied.
  6. Death-saving vows — vows that only trigger to avoid death are not binding trades.
  7. Rule-breaking vows — a vow cannot be used to break or bypass core rules.
  8. Refinement-increasing vows — no vow can grant domain refinement points, never allowed.
  9. Metagaming vows — vows trading on out-of-character knowledge are denied.
