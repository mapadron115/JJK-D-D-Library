Binding Vow Forge

A guided forge for binding vows: state the price, name the gain, set the penalty — Heaven judges the scales, and the forge enforces the iron law before a card is struck. Forged vows save to your character and appear on the Binding Vow Cards page.

Step 1 — The Price

The iron law: no vow grants anything for nothing. The price must be real — lost options, lost resources, a genuine vulnerability. A vow whose "restriction" never actually restricts you grants nothing. Heaven can tell.

Restriction for power

Limit yourself — what you cannot do, must do, or give up — and gain something in return. (Revealing One's Hand, Overtime.)

Power for consequence

Declare something now and name the price you pay if you break it. The consequence fires automatically, without appeal.

The price, stated plainly

Step 2 — The Gain

What the vow returns: more output, a new option, a sharper edge — sized to the price. The GM adjudicates whether a price is genuine. Err on the side of a steeper price.
