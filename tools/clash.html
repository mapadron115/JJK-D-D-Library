Clash Resolver

Two kinds of clash, one page. Domain clashes are decided by refinement points — deterministic, no countdown. The weapon-clash reaction answers a single attack with a melee weapon damage roll. Pick the clash you're resolving.

Combatants

Clash notes

  • Open Domains in a clash: an open Domain makes a barrier attack at initiative 20 during the clash — 4d10 + CE modifier force damage to the closed barrier. If both Domains are open, skip this.
  • Open Domains collapse too: an open Domain trailing by 100 or more points collapses even if its anchor survives.
  • Barrierless Domains: can only be destroyed by taking 1/3 of max HP in a single instance — the caster then makes a CON save (DC 15 + half the damage taken). In a clash, a barrierless Domain's effects can target the other Domain's outside barrier directly.
  • The collapse itself deals no damage. The defeated creator suffers Burnout: 10 rounds below 500 refinement points, 5 rounds at 500–999, 3 rounds at 1,000.
  • The winner's sure-hit resumes at the next initiative count 20 — not the round the rival Domain broke.
