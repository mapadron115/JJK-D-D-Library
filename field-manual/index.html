What this is

Ritual Archive is a fan-made Jujutsu Kaisen adaptation for Dungeons & Dragons 5th Edition. It assumes you already know how to play 5e — ability scores, advantage, spell slots — and layers the jujutsu system on top: Cursed Energy with its own reserve economy, innate techniques with level-by-level progressions, binding vows with real prices, Domain Expansions with initiative-20 sure-hits, Reverse Cursed Technique, Black Flash, and deterministic clash rules.

Start with the Field Guide to learn the system, build your sorcerer, browse the Technique Codex for the engine you'll run on, and open the Story So Far when you're ready to arrive at the school.
