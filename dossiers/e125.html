Core law

What the technique actually does.

Core Engine, Warrior's Favor. The currency of witnessed courage. Favor maxes at her proficiency, earned by parrying, protecting, winning grapples, or standing against the stronger, and spent on divine counters and weapon techniques. Gain Favor (max PB) when you parry, protect an ally, win a grapple, or succeed a save against a stronger foe. Spend it on divine counters and weapon techniques.

Field notes. Subject: a ledger that only pays the brave. Three truths govern her. First: her Favor answers witnessed courage alone, parry, protect, outlast a stronger foe, and the cursed energy pays interest; hide, and it pays nothing. Second: the kit is the doctrine made metal, bracelets that answer missiles, a lasso that forbids the lie, a leap that shortens every distance, a divine weapon that is an oath rather than an object. Third: the escalation is moral before it is martial, Godkiller Training pays Favor against the larger and the higher-graded, and the capstone binds truth and war into one title. Mark this: she is not strong because she is armed. She is armed because she has never once been found hiding.

Full progression

Level 1 through 20.

Passive · Divine weapon of your choice (sword, spear, or shield; 1d10). Reaction · 1 Favor: reduce ranged damage you take by 1d10 + your level; if reduced to 0, redirect a mundane projectile to its sender.

3Lasso of Truth

Action · Make a 30-ft cursed tool attack; on a hit the target is grappled (escape: Strength (Athletics) or Dexterity (Acrobatics) vs your tool save DC); a Restrained target cannot knowingly speak a lie but may stay silent; supernatural deception contests your tool DC.

Thirty feet of golden argument: on a hit the target is grappled, and while Restrained it cannot knowingly lie, though it may keep its silence. Supernatural deception must beat her tool DC. Make a 30-ft cursed tool attack; on a hit the target is grappled. The target may use its action to attempt escape: Strength (Athletics) or Dexterity (Acrobatics) vs your tool save DC. A Restrained target cannot knowingly speak a lie while bound, but it may remain silent; supernatural deception contests your tool DC.

Passive · Jump distance ×4; reduce falling damage by 5 × your level; once per turn after moving 20 ft, your weapon hits deal +2d8 damage.

The leap quadruples and falling damage shrinks by five per level, and after twenty feet of movement, her weapon hits deal two extra dice once per turn. Distance is a down payment. Your jump distance is multiplied by 4 and you reduce falling damage by 5 × your level. Once per turn, after moving 20 ft, your weapon hits deal +2d8 damage.

9Divine Guard

Free · 1 Favor: advantage on a Strength, Dexterity, or Wisdom save. Reaction (shield wielded) · Interpose your shield: an ally gains +2 AC against the triggering attack, or you take the damage in its place.

Spend 1 Favor to gain advantage on a Strength, Dexterity, or Wisdom save. If you wield a shield, you may use your reaction to interpose your shield: the ally gains +2 AC against the triggering attack, or you take the damage in its place (your choice).

Fifty feet of her own, and the divine weapon learns manners: mundane resistance ignored, a radiant die once per turn. The sky admits the brave. You gain a 50-ft fly speed. Your divine weapon attacks ignore mundane resistance and deal +1d8 radiant damage once per turn.

Against creatures larger than you or of higher grade, your critical range becomes 19–20, and the first time you hit each round you gain 1 Favor.

20Truth & War

If reduced to 0, emit 60-ft line back toward source for prevented damage capped at 12d12 force/radiant.

Feats

Build-defining options.

Bullets & Bracelets

The negated projectile is not wasted: one Favor as a reaction ricochets it back at sixty feet for its original damage. The enemy's aim becomes her ammunition. when your Bracelets reduce a ranged attack's damage to 0, you may spend 1 Favor as a reaction to ricochet it: make a ranged technique attack against a creature within 60 ft, dealing the projectile's original damage on a hit.

Amazon Grappler

The lasso'd cannot use reactions, and a bonus action drags them fifteen feet. Truth is also a leash. a creature grappled by your Lasso cannot use reactions, and you may use your bonus action to drag it up to 15 ft.

Princess of Peace

She is an emissary, not only a blade: one full minute of formal de-escalation earns her expertise, the Lasso's spirit of truth presiding over the table. You are an emissary of Themyscira, not only its blade. If you spend one full minute formally de-escalating, speaking terms, offering quarter, letting the Lasso's spirit of truth hang over the table, you gain expertise in Charisma (Persuasion) for the duration of the negotiation, and any creature that accepts your terms must succeed on a Wisdom save to break its word to you within the next 24 hours. Rushed words gain nothing; peace takes a minute.

Warrior Born

The weapon is an oath, not an object: ten minutes of weapon-rites in a rest rebinds sword to spear to shield. The arsenal is a single vow in different clothes. Your divine weapon is an oath, not an object. During a short or long rest, you may spend 10 minutes in Themysciran weapon-rites to rebind your armament: your sword becomes a spear, your spear a shield, your shield a sword, carrying the same divine enchantment, damage die, and Favor interactions. You may also reshape its appearance freely each rest; the gods recognize the wielder, not the shape.

Rules integration

Edge cases and shared systems.

Codex rulings: Direct rolled technique/weapon attacks can use the campaign's Black Flash procedure when otherwise eligible. Passive damage, hazards, summons acting independently, and initiative-20 Domain events cannot. Generic Maximum Output applies only to ordinary features, never named Maxima or Domains.
