Amazon Champion

Divine physique / bracelets / lasso / warrior mastery

Power tierTier V · Special Grade+
Core law

What the technique actually does.

Core Engine — Warrior's Favor. Gain Favor (max PB) when you parry, protect an ally, win a grapple, or succeed a save against a stronger foe. Spend it on divine counters and weapon techniques.

Full progression

Level 1 through 20.

1Amazon Arms

Choose sword/spear/shield. Divine weapon 1d10. Bracelets: reaction spend 1 Favor, reduce ranged damage 1d10+level; if 0, redirect mundane projectile.

3Lasso of Truth

30-ft cursed tool attack; on hit grapple. Restrained target cannot knowingly speak a lie while bound, but may remain silent; supernatural deception contests tool DC.

6Amazon Leap

Jump ×4 and reduce falling damage. Once/turn after moving 20 ft, weapon hit +2d8.

9Divine Guard

Spend 1 Favor to gain advantage on Str/Dex/Wis save. Shield user may protect adjacent ally with reaction.

11Flight

50-ft fly. Divine weapon attacks ignore mundane resistance and add 1d8 radiant once/turn.

13Godkiller Training

Against creatures larger/higher grade, crit 19–20 and gain 1 Favor first time you hit each round.

15Bracelet Shockwave

3 Favor + 4 CE: clash bracelets, 30-ft cone Con save 7d10 thunder/force and push 30.

18Champion of Themyscira

6 CE, 1 minute: resistance B/P/S and radiant/necrotic, fly 80, weapon +2d10, Favor cannot fall below 1.

20Truth & War

Favor max 10. First bracelet/lasso Favor cost each round 0. Lasso can bind Huge creatures and suppress teleportation while target remains grappled.

Pinnacle expression

Maximum, Domain, or equivalent.

Maximum — Godwave Counter (10 CE + all Favor): reaction to a colossal attack; reduce damage to you/allies in 20 ft by 10d12 + level. If reduced to 0, emit 60-ft line back toward source for prevented damage capped at 12d12 force/radiant.

Rules integration

Edge cases and shared systems.

Codex rulings: Direct rolled technique/weapon attacks can use the campaign's Black Flash procedure when otherwise eligible. Passive damage, hazards, summons acting independently, and initiative-20 Domain events cannot. Generic Maximum Output applies only to ordinary features, never named Maxima or Domains.
