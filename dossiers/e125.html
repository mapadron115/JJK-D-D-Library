Amazon Champion

Divine physique / bracelets / lasso / warrior mastery

Power tierTier V · Special Grade+
Core law

What the technique actually does.

Core Engine — Warrior's Favor. Gain Favor (max PB) when you parry, protect an ally, win a grapple, or succeed a save against a stronger foe. Spend it on divine counters and weapon techniques.

The fiction. Registry notes describe the Amazon Champion's Favor as cursed energy that only answers witnessed courage: parry, protect, outlast a stronger foe, and the CE pays interest; hide, and it pays nothing. The bracelets, the lasso, the leap are the kit of someone whose awakening came in the middle of a battle they refused to lose. It is not divine heritage in any clan's records — it is a warrior's resolve, so total the energy began keeping score.

Full progression

Level 1 through 20.

1Amazon Arms

Choose a sword, spear, or shield as your divine weapon (1d10). Your Bracelets: as a reaction, spend 1 Favor to reduce ranged damage you take by 1d10 + your level; if the damage is reduced to 0, you may redirect a mundane projectile.

3Lasso of Truth

Make a 30-ft cursed tool attack; on a hit the target is grappled. The target may use its action to attempt escape: Strength (Athletics) or Dexterity (Acrobatics) vs your tool save DC. A Restrained target cannot knowingly speak a lie while bound, but it may remain silent; supernatural deception contests your tool DC.

6Amazon Leap

Your jump distance is multiplied by 4 and you reduce falling damage by 5 × your level. Once per turn, after moving 20 ft, your weapon hits deal +2d8 damage.

9Divine Guard

Spend 1 Favor to gain advantage on a Strength, Dexterity, or Wisdom save. If you wield a shield, you may use your reaction to interpose your shield: the ally gains +2 AC against the triggering attack, or you take the damage in its place (your choice).

11Flight

You gain a 50-ft fly speed. Your divine weapon attacks ignore mundane resistance and deal +1d8 radiant damage once per turn.

13Godkiller Training

Against creatures larger than you or of higher grade, your critical range becomes 19–20, and the first time you hit each round you gain 1 Favor.

15Bracelet Shockwave

Spend 3 Favor and 4 CE to clash your bracelets: creatures in a 30-ft cone make a Constitution save, taking 7d10 thunder/force damage on a failure and being pushed 30 ft.

18Champion of Themyscira

For 6 CE, for 1 minute: you gain resistance to bludgeoning, piercing, slashing, radiant, and necrotic damage, a fly speed of 80, +2d10 weapon damage, and your Favor cannot fall below 1.

20Truth & War

Your Favor maximum becomes 10. The first Favor cost for your bracelets or lasso each round is 0. Your lasso can bind Huge creatures and suppresses teleportation while the target remains grappled.

Pinnacle expression

Maximum, Domain, or equivalent.

Maximum — Godwave Counter (10 CE + all Favor): reaction to a colossal attack (an attack or effect dealing 40+ damage to a single target, or any attack from a Gargantuan or larger source); reduce damage to you/allies in 20 ft by 10d12 + level. If reduced to 0, emit 60-ft line back toward source for prevented damage capped at 12d12 force/radiant.

Feats

Build-defining options.

Bullets & Bracelets

when your Bracelets reduce a ranged attack's damage to 0, you may spend 1 Favor as a reaction to ricochet it: make a ranged technique attack against a creature within 60 ft, dealing the projectile's original damage on a hit.

Amazon Grappler

a creature grappled by your Lasso cannot use reactions, and you may use your bonus action to drag it up to 15 ft.

Princess of Peace

You are an emissary of Themyscira, not only its blade. If you spend one full minute formally de-escalating — speaking terms, offering quarter, letting the Lasso's spirit of truth hang over the table — you gain expertise in Charisma (Persuasion) for the duration of the negotiation, and any creature that accepts your terms must succeed on a Wisdom save to break its word to you within the next 24 hours. Rushed words gain nothing; peace takes a minute.

Warrior Born

Your divine weapon is an oath, not an object. During a short or long rest, you may spend 10 minutes in Themysciran weapon-rites to rebind your armament: your sword becomes a spear, your spear a shield, your shield a sword — carrying the same divine enchantment, damage die, and Favor interactions. You may also reshape its appearance freely each rest; the gods recognize the wielder, not the shape.

Rules integration

Edge cases and shared systems.

Codex rulings: Direct rolled technique/weapon attacks can use the campaign's Black Flash procedure when otherwise eligible. Passive damage, hazards, summons acting independently, and initiative-20 Domain events cannot. Generic Maximum Output applies only to ordinary features, never named Maxima or Domains.
