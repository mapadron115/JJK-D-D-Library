One Too Many

Make a group lose certainty about its own membership. Headcounts become cursed facts, and every contradiction gives you Uncertainty to sabotage teamwork.

InspirationGoatman / campfire impostor folklore
RoleInfiltration / Coordination Disruption
TierTier III · Apex
Core statCHA or WIS
Scaling dieDoubt Die

Character fantasy

Make a group lose certainty about its own membership. Headcounts become cursed facts, and every contradiction gives you Uncertainty to sabotage teamwork.

Combat loop

  1. Establish a Headcount on a group.
  2. Create contradictions while appearing to belong.
  3. Gain Uncertainty when members waste effort verifying or coordinating around false assumptions.
  4. Spend Uncertainty to break Help, reactions, formation bonuses, and confident targeting.

Headcount & Uncertainty

  • Headcount: action, 2 CE, choose up to PB creatures you can see within 60 feet that regard themselves as one group. For 10 minutes you know whenever one enters/leaves 60 feet and the technique records the original count.
  • False Member: while Headcount is active you can make your appearance, voice, scent, and superficial CE signature read as “one member of the group” without copying a specific individual. A creature inspecting you may make Insight against your CE save DC.
  • Uncertainty cap: PB + 2. Once per creature per round, gain 1 Uncertainty when a Headcount member spends an action/bonus action to verify identity, uses Help on the wrong creature/decoy, misses an opportunity because it mistook you for an ally, or publicly contradicts another member about the group’s count or position.

Count Again

When a creature fails an Insight check to identify you or a decoy, gain 1 Uncertainty.

Campfire Voice

You can perfectly reproduce any voice heard for at least 1 minute, though this alone never grants a Headcount benefit.

Wrong Footprints

As an action and 1 CE, create false tracks, scent, and mundane evidence for one Headcount creature across a 60-foot trail.

Seventh Shadow

Prerequisite: There Were Six of Us. Your decoy may take the Help action once on your turn; doing so destroys it after the helped roll resolves.

Building the character

Strategist and Support Specialist fit best. It is intentionally much stronger against organized squads than lone monsters, and its combat power comes from ruining teamwork rather than controlling enemy turns wholesale.

Technique die: Doubt Die follows the shared d6 → d8 → d10 → d12 progression.

Counterplay

Split the party deliberately, use predetermined passwords, rely on area effects instead of coordination, and spend actions to identify the impostor early.

Adaptation note. This is original JJK 5e homebrew inspired by Goatman / campfire impostor folklore. The myth/folklore provides imagery and thematic rules; the combat engine, progression, CE economy, feats, and Domain/Maximum procedures are game design for this codex.
