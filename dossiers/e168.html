One Too Many

Make a group lose certainty about its own membership. Headcounts become cursed facts, and every contradiction gives you Uncertainty to sabotage teamwork.

Combat roleInfiltration / Coordination Disruption
TierTier III · Apex
Core law

Character fantasy

Make a group lose certainty about its own membership. Headcounts become cursed facts, and every contradiction gives you Uncertainty to sabotage teamwork.

Combat loop

  1. Establish a Headcount on a group.
  2. Create contradictions while appearing to belong.
  3. Gain Uncertainty when members waste effort verifying or coordinating around false assumptions.
  4. Spend Uncertainty to break Help, reactions, formation bonuses, and confident targeting.

Core law

Headcount & Uncertainty

Headcount:

action, 2 CE, choose up to PB creatures you can see within 60 feet that regard themselves as one group. For 10 minutes you know whenever one enters/leaves 60 feet and the technique records the original count.

False Member:

while Headcount is active you can make your appearance, voice, scent, and superficial CE signature read as “one member of the group” without copying a specific individual. A creature inspecting you may make Insight against your CE save DC.

Uncertainty cap:

PB + 2. Once per creature per round, gain 1 Uncertainty when a Headcount member spends an action/bonus action to verify identity, uses Help on the wrong creature/decoy, misses an opportunity because it mistook you for an ally, or publicly contradicts another member about the group’s count or position.

Full progression

Level progression

1False Member

Gain Headcount, False Member, and Uncertainty. Spend 1 Uncertainty as a reaction when a Headcount creature within 60 feet uses Help, grants a reaction attack, or provides a formation/flanking bonus to another creature; the assisting benefit is suppressed for that event.

3There Were Six of Us

Bonus action, 2 CE: create a False Member decoy in an unoccupied space within 30 feet for 1 minute. It has your AC, 1 HP, no attacks, and can move 30 feet on your turn without an action. A creature wasting an attack or reaction on it grants you 1 Uncertainty. Maintain one decoy.

6Wrong Voice in the Dark

Action, 2 CE: choose one Headcount creature within 60 feet. Until start of your next turn, sounds you make may originate from its space and your decoy may look specifically like it. The first attack made against the decoy before then grants 2 Uncertainty instead of 1.

11Someone Is Missing

Headcount can affect PB + CE modifier creatures. Spend 2 Uncertainty when a Headcount creature targets one of its allies with a beneficial single-target action or reaction; it must succeed on an Insight check against your CE save DC or the action is delayed until the end of its turn, when it may retarget legally. Resources are not spent on a failed check unless it still resolves.

18Domain Expansion: Campfire of Seven Clash bookkeeping: 500 clash points (Tier III). A 100+ point advantage collapses the weaker Domain; collapse deals no damage. Barrier AC 12 + PB; barrier HP = 10 × max(1, your CE modifier) + 500. Technique Burnout: 10 rounds.

Full-turn action, 20 CE, 90-foot radius, 1 minute. Everyone appears as a dim silhouette around an endless forest camp. The Domain’s law is Verify Before You Trust: at initiative 20 each chosen hostile gains one Doubt token. Before that creature can use Help, a beneficial reaction on another creature, a single-target buff/heal on another creature, or an opportunity attack, it must either spend its Doubt token or make an Insight save against your CE DC. On failure the attempted supportive/reaction effect is lost with no target; on success it resolves and clears Doubt. Anti-domain protection grants advantage on this save. Ordinary attacks remain unaffected. Your False Member decoys cost 0 CE and you may maintain PB of them during the Domain.

Feats

Build-defining options.

Count Again

When a creature fails an Insight check to identify you or a decoy, gain 1 Uncertainty.

Campfire Voice

You can perfectly reproduce any voice heard for at least 1 minute, though this alone never grants a Headcount benefit.

Wrong Footprints

As an action and 1 CE, create false tracks, scent, and mundane evidence for one Headcount creature across a 60-foot trail.

Seventh Shadow

Prerequisite: There Were Six of Us. Your decoy may take the Help action once on your turn; doing so destroys it after the helped roll resolves.

Rules integration

Building the character

Strategist and Support Specialist fit best. It is intentionally much stronger against organized squads than lone monsters, and its combat power comes from ruining teamwork rather than controlling enemy turns wholesale.
Technique die: Doubt Die follows the shared d6 → d8 → d10 → d12 progression.
Rules integration

Counterplay

Split the party deliberately, use predetermined passwords, rely on area effects instead of coordination, and spend actions to identify the impostor early.
