Field entry

Journal Entry field report, converted to a table-ready stat block

Field notes. Subject: the skirmisher. Four legs, fifty feet of speed, and the oldest cavalry doctrine on the field — it circles, probes, and tests your reactions before it commits, and attempting to outrun it is a mistake. Mark its economy: two mantis slashes (1d8+4) to carve through wood and light barriers and bleed your options, then the barbed tail at ten feet’s reach — 1d10+4 bludgeoning with a discharge of concentrated cursed energy (1d6 force) on impact. It disengages as a bonus action without provoking; it will not stay where you pin it. So do not chase it — pin it. Hold the ground it wants and let it come to you. Speed that cannot flee into open space is just exhaustion in advance.

Classification & Risk

An Imaginary Cursed Spirit, Grade 3 (CR 1–3 band). Aggressive in form but not reckless: relies on mobility and pressure rather than raw strength. Attempting to outrun it is a mistake.

Actions

Multiattack. Two Mantis Slash attacks and one Tail Whip. Mantis Slash. Melee Attack: +6 to hit, reach 5 ft. Hit: 1d8 + 4 slashing damage — fast cuts that carve through wood and light barriers. Tail Whip. Melee Attack: +6 to hit, reach 10 ft. Hit: 1d10 + 4 bludgeoning damage plus 1d6 force damage as concentrated cursed energy discharges on impact.

Risk

Special

It tests reactions before committing — expect it to circle and probe for an opening rather than charge blindly. As an imaginary curse it lacks a clear origin, making its behavior erratic but not mindless. Pin it down; do not race it.

Campaign canon notes

Field rule — low-grade perception: Per the C&C field rules, Grade 3 and lower curses are invisible to creatures without cursed energy. A creature in a life-or-death situation at less than half its hit point maximum may temporarily or permanently gain the ability to see them. This spirit carries the Invisibility trait below.
