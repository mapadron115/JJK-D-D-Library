Mycelial Testament

Grow a network through the battlefield, cast support effects from remote Nodes, and convert deaths or spare cursed energy into Nutrients for stronger fungal blooms.

Core Gameplay Loop

Scaling

Main resource

Nodes & Nutrients

Mycelial Nodes

Bonus Action • 1 CE • 30 feet. Plant a Node on a surface. You can maintain Nodes equal to your proficiency bonus. Nodes are Tiny, AC 13, HP equal to 5 + twice your sorcerer level. Nodes within 30 feet of another Node or you are Connected.

Remote Fruiting

Your Mycelial Testament abilities may originate from you or any Connected Node. If an ability measures an aura or range, measure from the chosen origin.

Symbiotic Pulse

Bonus Action • 1 CE. One willing creature within 10 feet of a Connected Node regains HP equal to one Spore Die + your CE modifier. A creature can benefit from this healing only once per round.

Nutrients

You can hold Nutrients equal to your proficiency bonus. When a Small or larger creature drops to 0 HP within 10 feet of a Node, gain 1 Nutrient (once per creature). Once per turn you may also spend 1 CE to gain 1 Nutrient. Nutrients vanish after a long rest.

Full progression

Level Progression

1Nodes, Remote Fruiting, Symbiotic Pulse, Nutrients

3Grasping Mycelium —

Action, 2 CE or 1 Nutrient. A 10-foot radius around one Node becomes difficult terrain for 1 minute. A hostile entering/starting there makes a Strength save or is restrained until the start of its next turn.

6Fruiting Bodies —

spend 2 Nutrients as a bonus action to convert one Node for 1 minute into one Bloom: Bulwark grants half cover to adjacent allies; Lantern reveals invisible creatures within 15 feet that fail a Dexterity save; Puffball uses your reaction when a hostile enters 10 feet to deal 2 Spore Dice poison/necrotic damage (Constitution save half). Only one Bloom per Node.

11Distributed Organism —

once per round when a willing creature within 10 feet of a Node takes damage, reaction and 2 CE: teleport it to an unoccupied space within 10 feet of another Connected Node within 60 feet, reducing the triggering damage by one Spore Die + CE modifier.

18Maximum Technique: Worldroot Fruiting —

Action, 10 CE, 1 minute. Ground within 60 feet counts as Connected network terrain. At the start of each turn gain 1 Nutrient. Symbiotic Pulse may target two creatures, and hostile creatures that start within your network make a Constitution save or take 2 Spore Dice necrotic damage and have speed reduced by 10 feet until their next turn.

Innate Technique Feats

Technique Feats

Old Growth

After a long rest, choose one of your Nodes to become old growth: it becomes Large with double HP and counts as two Connected Nodes for adjacency and teleportation, but it can never be converted into a Bloom.

Medicinal Culture

You cultivate a healing strain: as an action for 1 Nutrient, graft a poultice onto a willing creature within 10 feet of a Node. The next time it takes damage within 1 minute, the poultice bursts and heals it for 2 Spore Dice.

Cordyceps Reflex

When a hostile creature within 10 feet of a Node falls prone, grappled, or restrained, gain 1 Nutrient. Once per round.

Rules integration

Table Rulings

Nodes are manifestations, not independent creatures; they do not take turns.
A corpse can grant Nutrient only once, regardless of being revived and reduced again.
Worldroot Fruiting does not pass through floors/walls into disconnected rooms unless there is a physical route for the network.
