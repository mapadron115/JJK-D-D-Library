Mycelial Testament

Grow a network through the battlefield, cast support effects from remote Nodes, and convert deaths or spare cursed energy into Nutrients for stronger fungal blooms.

Power tierTier III · Apex

Field notes. Subject: the healer who lets nothing rot unused. Three truths govern her. First: the network is the body, Nodes planted across the field stand Connected within thirty feet of each other, and every pulse, fruiting, and relocation may originate from any of them. Second: death is compost, Nutrients harvested from the fallen (each corpse pays once) feed Blooms, grasping terrain, and the great relocation that negates a wound entirely. Third: she is everywhere the network reaches, Distributed Organism sinks a wounded ally into the mycelium and surfaces him whole, and the Maximum makes the whole ground her organism. Assess her correctly: kill her allies and you only water her garden. Maxim: what falls feeds what stands.

Spore Die

Passive · The Spore Die grows with the network: d6 → d8 (6th) → d10 (11th) → d12 (18th).

The organism's measure, growing as the network deepens.

Main resource

Passive · Nodes and Nutrients are the network’s body and its fuel.

The body of the network, and the food that feeds it.

Mycelial Nodes

Bonus action · 1 CE, 30 ft · Plant a Node on a surface; maintain up to PB Nodes · Nodes are Tiny, AC 13, HP 5 + twice your sorcerer level; Nodes within 30 ft of another Node or you are Connected.

She plants the network's organs across the field: tiny, fragile, and each within reach of another.

Remote Fruiting

Passive · Your Mycelial Testament abilities may originate from you or any Connected Node; measure auras/ranges from the chosen origin.

Mark this: the network has no single center. Every ability may bloom from her or from any Connected Node.

Symbiotic Pulse

Bonus action · 1 CE · One willing creature within 10 ft of a Connected Node regains one Spore Die + CE modifier HP · A creature benefits once per round.

The pulse is the mycelium's heartbeat: a willing creature knits whole.

Nutrients

Passive · Hold up to PB Nutrients · When a Small or larger creature drops to 0 HP within 10 ft of a Node, gain 1 Nutrient (once per creature) · Once per turn, spend 1 CE to gain 1 Nutrient · Nutrients vanish after a long rest · Spend 1 Nutrient when you use Symbiotic Pulse to add one extra Spore Die to the healing.

The dead pay the living's debts: each fallen creature feeds the network once, and only once.

3Grasping Mycelium —

Action · 2 CE or 1 Nutrient · A 10-ft radius around one Node becomes difficult terrain for 1 minute · A hostile entering or starting there makes a Str save or is restrained until start of its next turn.

The network shows its teeth: a Node's radius becomes difficult ground, and the entering hostile may be held fast.

6Fruiting Bodies —

Bonus action · Spend 2 Nutrients · Convert one Node for 1 minute into one Bloom (one per Node): Bulwark grants half cover to adjacent allies; Lantern reveals invisible creatures within 15 ft that fail a Dex save; Puffball uses your reaction when a hostile enters 10 ft to deal 2 Spore Dice poison/necrotic (Con save half).

The network learns to fight back: bulwark, lantern, or puffball blooming from a Node.

11Distributed Organism —

Reaction · 2 CE, once per round · When a willing creature within 10 ft of a Connected Node would take damage: it sinks into the mycelium and re-emerges in an unoccupied space within 10 ft of another Connected Node within 60 ft · The triggering damage is negated entirely; no die is rolled.

The organism's masterpiece: a wounded ally sinks into the mycelium and surfaces elsewhere, the blow falling on shed husks instead.

18Maximum Technique: Worldroot Fruiting —

Maximum Technique · Action · 10 CE, 1 minute · Ground within 60 ft counts as Connected network terrain · Start of each turn: gain 1 Nutrient · Symbiotic Pulse may target two creatures; hostile creatures starting within your network make a Con save or take 2 Spore Dice necrotic damage and have speed −10 ft until their next turn.

The maximum: the ground itself becomes her organism, and hostiles are bled and slowed by the network.

Innate Technique Feats

Technique Feats

Old Growth

She keeps one ancient Node: doubled in body, doubled in connection, but old growth fruits no new blooms. After a long rest, choose one of your Nodes to become old growth: it becomes Large with double HP and counts as two Connected Nodes for adjacency and teleportation, but it can never be converted into a Bloom.

Medicinal Culture

She cultivates a healing strain apart: a poultice grafted before the wound, waiting to burst into repair. You cultivate a healing strain: as an action for 1 Nutrient, graft a poultice onto a willing creature within 10 feet of a Node. The poultice persists until the bearer takes damage. If Distributed Organism negates the triggering damage, the poultice is not expended. When that happens, the poultice bursts and heals it for 2 Spore Dice.

Cordyceps Reflex

The network rewards her predators: every fallen or bound hostile feeds it one more Nutrient. When a hostile creature within 10 feet of a Node falls prone, grappled, or restrained, gain 1 Nutrient. Once per round.

Rules integration

Table Rulings

Nodes are manifestations, not independent creatures; they do not take turns.
A corpse can grant Nutrient only once, regardless of being revived and reduced again.
Worldroot Fruiting does not pass through floors/walls into disconnected rooms unless there is a physical route for the network.
