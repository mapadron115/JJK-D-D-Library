Feature map

The kit, piece by piece. Memorize it. Burn after reading.

L1

Pickpocket

Bonus action · 5 ft, one creature.

THE LIFT. As a bonus action, steal one held, worn, or small carried object from a creature within 5 ft: make a Sleight of Hand check contested by the creature's passive Perception. On a success, the object is on your person and the target notices nothing — unless you want it noticed. You cannot lift worn armor or anything larger than 1 ft in any dimension. Works in combat. Works best in combat.

L1

Crack the Safe

Action · touch · magical barrier, ward, or glyph.

THE SAFE. Wards are just padlocks with better lawyers. Touch a magical barrier, ward, glyph, or alarm and make a DEX or INT check with your thieves' tools (add your proficiency bonus if you are proficient) against a DC set by the DM — typically 10 + the effect's spell level, or the trap's save DC. On a success, choose one: suppress the effect for 1 minute, or open a 5-ft passage through it for 1 minute that only you and creatures you designate can use. On a failure, it triggers or holds — and you know you failed. No second guesses on a job.

L3

Misdirection

Passive · the crew's distraction.

THE DISTRACTION. When an ally within 30 ft of you takes the Help action to aid you, you gain advantage on the aided ability check or attack roll as normal — and you add 1d6 to the roll. The crew's noise buys you the extra inch. The bonus becomes 2d6 at 11th level.

L6

"Good"

Passive · Good points (maximum 3).

THE CHOICE. The job nobody planned. Whenever you take the Help action to aid an ally, spend your action protecting an ally (interposing, shoving them clear, taking the hit — the DM adjudicates), or deliberately spare a creature you could have killed or captured, you gain 1 Good point (maximum 3). You may spend 1 Good point to add 1d8 to any d20 roll you make — declare after seeing the roll, before the outcome is determined. Unspent points fade when you finish a long rest. Being good pays. Literally, now.

L11

The Heist

1 minute of planning · allies · 10 minutes.

THE PLAN. One minute, the whole crew, no interruptions. Declare a heist: name a mark (a creature, object, or location) and an objective (steal, infiltrate, extract, deceive). For the next 10 minutes, every ally who planned with you has advantage on ability checks made directly toward the objective, and enemies have disadvantage on Perception checks to notice the crew's preparations. Only one heist may be active at a time. The plan survives contact — that is what the planning is for.

L14

Every Lock Has a Key

Passive · Crack the Safe upgrade.

THE UPGRADE. Fingers learn the shape of magic. Crack the Safe now works on any barrier, ward, glyph, or magical trap you can see within 30 ft — no touch required. You learn the nature of anything you attempt to crack (its trigger, its rough strength, the school of magic that shaped it), whether you succeed or fail. Suppression now lasts 10 minutes. There is no lock. There are only doors that haven't met you.

L18

The Long Con

1/long rest · Sleight of Hand.

THE LONG CON. The heist was over before it began. Once per long rest, choose one object you can see — unattended, or carried by a creature — and declare that you stole it up to 10 minutes ago. Make a Sleight of Hand check with advantage, contested by the holder's passive Perception (unattended objects are simply yours). On a success, the object is in your possession: your past self lifted it, stashed it, and nobody noticed until now. On a failure, it stays put — but nobody knows you tried.

Maximum Technique

The Perfect Crime

Action · 10 CE · 60 ft, one creature.

THE PERFECT CRIME. You don't steal the thing. You steal the moment. Choose one creature within 60 ft and make a Sleight of Hand check contested by its passive Perception. On a success, choose one theft: its reaction (it cannot take reactions for 1 minute); its bonus action (it cannot take bonus actions for 1 minute); or one use of a limited feature or prepared spell (the DM adjudicates — the use is gone, as if spent, until the creature finishes a short rest). On a failure, nothing is stolen — and the attempt leaves no trace. The target never knows. A creature can only be robbed by one Perfect Crime at a time.

Level 20 · Capstone

The Good Wolf

Passive · 1/long rest rescue.

THE REFORMED THIEF'S PINNACLE. Your Good maximum becomes 5. When you spend a Good point on your own d20 roll, you may grant one ally within 30 ft the same 1d8 on a d20 roll they make before the start of your next turn — goodness is contagious. Once per long rest, when an ally within 60 ft drops to 0 hit points, you may use your reaction to cross the room impossibly fast: teleport up to 60 ft to an unoccupied space adjacent to them (no opportunity attacks), and they drop to 1 hit point instead. Good wolves show up.

Innate Technique Feats

Build-defining options.

These are optional character-building feats. You must meet each listed prerequisite before selecting it.

Prerequisite: Pickpocket

Lift the Spell

You steal things that were never objects. As an action, choose one creature within 5 ft concentrating on a spell or effect: make a Sleight of Hand check contested by its passive Perception. On a success, its concentration breaks — you lifted the thread holding the spell together, and it felt like nothing at all. The creature does not learn what broke its focus.

Prerequisite: Crack the Safe

Leave It Re-Keyed

You don't just open the safe — you change the combination. When you successfully suppress a ward, glyph, barrier, or alarm with Crack the Safe, you may re-key it instead of merely silencing it: for the duration of the suppression, the effect ignores you and your allies but triggers normally against your enemies. The owners never know the lock changed hands.

Prerequisite: "Good"

Share the Take

Goodness, fenced and distributed. As a bonus action, you may give any number of your Good points to willing allies within 30 ft — a creature holding your Good point may spend it to add 1d8 to one d20 roll it makes, as if it were yours. Points you give away count against your maximum until spent. The crew eats first.

Prerequisite: The Heist

Plan B

Every good plan has one. Once per declared heist, when an ally fails an ability check made directly toward the heist's objective, you may let them reroll it and take the higher result — you accounted for this. If the reroll succeeds, you gain 1 Good point. The crew trusts the planner.
