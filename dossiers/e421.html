Floating Armor

A Grade 3 cursed armor, light, medium, or heavy (requires attunement).

FormArmor (light, medium, or heavy)
GradeGrade 3
AttunementRequired

Field notes. Subject: the curse of a fear of heights, turned against the sky itself. Three truths govern the armor. First, the terror is the teacher: imbued with curses born of a fear of heights, the armor grants what the fear denied, flight, on terms. Second, the menu has two prices: as a bonus action you may spend 2 cursed energy to cast Jump, or 4 to cast Levitate, targeting only yourself, a great leap for the impatient, true levitation for the patient, concentration and all. Third, the gift is worn, not carried: light, medium, or heavy, but attunement is required, the hour-long ritual, one of your slots. Mark the inversion I never tire of: the ground-dweller's dread, bound into steel, teaching its wearer the sky.

Features & procedures

Properties & techniques

Exact player-facing procedures

Bonus action

Curse-Powered Lift

While you wear this armor, you can use a bonus action to expend 2 or more cursed energy to cast one of the following spells from the armor, targeting only yourself: Jump (2 CE) or Levitate (4 CE). The spells otherwise work as normal (Jump: 1 minute; Levitate: up to 10 minutes, concentration as usual).

Rules integration

Edge cases and shared systems.

Campaign canon notes

Cursed-tool attunement: you have PB attunement slots (2–6). This tool occupies one slot and requires a one-hour ritual. Attunement ends voluntarily over a short rest, on death, or when you replace it while over capacity. Distance does not break attunement. Cursed-tool attunement is separate from magic-item attunement.
Table function: the source says the spells are cast "from the armor" with the CE costs above; they otherwise use the normal spell rules for duration, concentration, and effect. The armor is found as one of light, medium, or heavy armor.
