White Flag

1d6 piercing spear. Plant as action to create 15-ft Truce Zone for 1 minute, 2 CE, Focus.

FormSpear-banner
RoleParley / battlefield support
EngineTruce
Attunement1 hour*
Properties & techniquesExact player-facing procedures

Feature

Creatures entering know the rule: the first creature each round to deal damage inside loses reactions and speed is halved until end next turn.

Feature

Aid, healing, movement, grappling without damage, and talking remain legal.

Break Parley

wielder may end zone as bonus action; if they were first to deal damage that round they suffer the penalty too.

Feature

No effect prevents violence outright; it makes betrayal costly.

Balance & counterplay. Rule is symmetric and visible, so it creates negotiations without mind control.
