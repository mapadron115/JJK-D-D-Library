White Flag

Features & procedures

Properties & techniques

Exact player-facing procedures

Feature

Creatures entering know the rule: the first creature each round to deal damage inside loses reactions and speed is halved until end next turn.

Feature

Aid, healing, movement, grappling without damage, and talking remain legal.

Break Parley

wielder may end zone as bonus action; if they were first to deal damage that round they suffer the penalty too.

Feature

No effect prevents violence outright; it makes betrayal costly.

Rules integration

Cursed-tool attunement

Campaign canon

Cursed-tool attunement: you have PB attunement slots (2–6). This Grade 2 tool functions as a mundane tool while unattuned, or with its full properties for one slot and a one-hour ritual at GM discretion. Attunement ends voluntarily over a short rest, on death, or when you replace it while over capacity. Distance does not break attunement. Cursed-tool attunement is separate from magic-item attunement.
Balance & counterplay. Rule is symmetric and visible, so it creates negotiations without mind control.
