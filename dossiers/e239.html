Features & procedures

Properties & techniques

Exact player-facing procedures

Morph

bonus action choose Blade (1d10 slashing), Shield (+2 AC but no two-handed weapons), or Tendril (1d6, 15-ft reach).

Reactive Carapace

reaction spend Instinct to reduce damage by 1 Tool Die each, max 2.

Predator Memory

2 CE after being hit: until end next turn first attack against attacker adds 2 Tool Dice.

Release: Full Carapace

7 CE, 1 minute: maintain two Morphs simultaneously and gain temp HP = level + 2 Tool Dice.

Rules integration

Cursed-tool attunement

Campaign canon

Cursed-tool attunement: you have PB attunement slots (2–6). This tool occupies one slot and requires a one-hour ritual. Attunement ends voluntarily over a short rest, on death, or when you replace it while over capacity. Distance does not break attunement. Cursed-tool attunement is separate from magic-item attunement.
Balance & counterplay. Adaptation is reactive and finite; it does not permanently learn enemy powers.
