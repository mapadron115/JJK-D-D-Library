Release: Full Carapace

Bonus action · 7 CE · 1 minute: maintain two Morphs simultaneously; gain temp HP equal to level + 2 Tool Dice.

7 CE, 1 minute, bonus action to activate (invented): maintain two Morphs simultaneously and gain temp HP = level + 2 Tool Dice.

Field notes. Subject: a bargain wearing your arm. Three truths govern the Carapace. First, pain is the currency: you gain Instinct only when a creature within 30 ft wounds you, to a maximum of PB, the symbiote learns the threat by studying the bruise. Second, the lesson is repaid in kind: a bonus-action Morph chooses blade, shield, or tendril, and Reactive Carapace spends Instinct on a reaction to blunt incoming damage. Third, the memory is old: Predator Memory spends 2 CE after a hit to crown your first retaliation against that attacker with 2 Tool Dice, and the Release, Full Carapace, holds two Morphs at once with temporary HP of level plus 2 Tool Dice for a minute. Assess it correctly: the armor is alive, and it eats first.

Rules integration

Cursed-tool attunement

Campaign canon

Cursed-tool attunement: you have PB attunement slots (2–6). This tool occupies one slot and requires a one-hour ritual. Attunement ends voluntarily over a short rest, on death, or when you replace it while over capacity. Distance does not break attunement. Cursed-tool attunement is separate from magic-item attunement.
Balance & counterplay. Adaptation is reactive and finite; it does not permanently learn enemy powers.
