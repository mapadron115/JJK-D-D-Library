Dragonheart Incarnation
Generic Dragon Communion Technique

Choose a draconic lineage, manifest claws and jaws before growing wings and scales, then abandon the human outline entirely at the pinnacle.

Power tierTier IV · Special Grade · Tier V true-form ceiling
Combat roleTransformation bruiser / elemental artillery
Core law

What the technique actually does.

Rules are written to plug into the finalized campaign glossary: normal actions stay normal, direct attacks roll, and only named Domain events bypass the initial roll.

Choose at level 1

Draconic Lineage

Choose Flame, Ancient Lightning, Glintstone Frost, Magma, or Scarlet. This sets your breath damage and resistance. The choice normally changes only on level-up or major story transformation.

Natural weapon

Dragonclaw

Manifest one claw for 1 CE as part of an unarmed attack. It deals 1 Dragon Die + attack modifier slashing plus one Dragon Die lineage damage and counts as a cursed natural weapon.

Bonus action · 2 CE · Focus · 1 minute

Gravel Scales

Gain +1 AC and resistance to your lineage damage.

Full progression

Level 1 through 20.

Milestones use the campaign’s familiar 1 / 3 / 6 / 11 / 18 structure and add an explicit level-20 mastery feature.

1First Communion

Dragonclaw

Choose your Draconic Lineage (Flame, Ancient Lightning, Glintstone Frost, Magma, or Scarlet; this sets your breath damage and resistance). Gain Dragonclaw (manifest one claw for 1 CE as part of an unarmed attack: 1 Dragon Die + attack modifier slashing plus one Dragon Die lineage damage) and Gravel Scales (bonus action, 2 CE, Focus, 1 minute: +1 AC and resistance to your lineage damage).

Draconic Senses

Darkvision 60 feet or +60 if already; advantage on Perception checks using smell while transformed.

Scaling

Dragon Die d6; range touch.

3Breath of the Heart

Dragon Breath

Action · 3 CE: 30-foot cone, Dex or Con save chosen by lineage, 4 Dragon Dice lineage damage, half on success. Scarlet instead deals poison/necrotic and adds one Rot-like mark; Magma leaves difficult terrain until next turn.

Dragon Roar

Action · 2 CE: hostiles within 15 feet make Wisdom saves or frightened until end of next turn; a success grants immunity to your Roar for 24 hours.

Communion Bite

Action · 3 CE: manifest Dragonmaw and make melee CE attack, 5 Dragon Dice bludgeoning/piercing and prone on failed Strength save.

Scaling

Dragon Die remains d6; range extends to a 30-foot cone.

6Half-Dragon

Wings

Bonus action · 3 CE · 1 minute: gain flying speed equal walking speed. If Gravel Scales active, wings share its Focus rather than requiring a second slot.

Tail / Horn Choice

Choose one adaptation after long rest: Tail gives reaction shove on adjacent hit; Horn gives charge attack after 20-foot straight movement for +2 Dragon Dice.

Greater Breath

Breath becomes 60-foot cone or 90-by-5-foot line, chosen each use, and 5 Dragon Dice.

Scaling

Dragon Die becomes d8; range extends to 60 feet.

11Ancient Blood

Greater Scales

Gravel Scales becomes +2 AC. Nonmagical/unenhanced weapon damage is reduced by PB while active.

Lineage Ascension

Gain a second lineage resistance and one secondary breath rider from another lineage, but your primary damage type remains the original.

Dragonstorm

Action · 6 CE: 30-foot radius centered on you. Ancient: red lightning; Flame: firestorm; Frost: moonfrost shards; Magma: erupting ground; Scarlet: rotburst. Creatures make saves for 7 Dragon Dice and a lineage rider.

Scaling

Dragon Die becomes d10; range extends to 90 feet.

18True Dragon Incarnation

Gravel Scales

Gravel Scales improves to +3 AC.

Maximum: True Dragon

Full-turn action · 12 CE · Focus · up to 10 minutes: become Huge. Reach +10 feet, flight 80 feet, Gravel Scales +3 AC, Dragonclaw uses 2 Dragon Dice base, breath is 8 Dragon Dice and recharges at start of turn on d6 roll 4–6 instead of CE cost. You cannot wield normal weapons or fit human spaces.

Dragon Fear

Hostiles entering within 30 feet of True Dragon first time make Wisdom saves or frightened for 1 minute, repeating at end of turns.

No Domain

The technique’s apex is incarnation, not a barrier. True Dragon is a named Maximum and cannot receive generic Maximum Output.

Scaling

Dragon Die becomes d12; range extends to 120 feet.

20Dragonlord Ascendant

Beyond Time

Once per True Dragon transformation, vanish into a thundercloud/elemental rift at end of your turn and reappear at start of your next turn within 300 feet, making one Dragonclaw attack with advantage.

Primeval Breath

Once per turn when Breath recharges naturally, you may spend 5 CE to turn it into a 120-foot cone for 12 Dragon Dice; you take 2 Dragon Dice unavoidable damage from overpressure.

Elder Heart

You no longer suffer age-related physical decline and gain immunity to your primary lineage damage while transformed. This is longevity, not immortality.

Scaling

Dragon Die remains d12; range extends to 180 feet.

Pinnacle expression

Domain, Maximum, or equivalent.

Not every technique needs a Domain. When one exists, its sure-hit is a unique autonomous initiative-count-20 event unless the Domain is explicitly rule-based.

No natural Domain Dragonheart Incarnation follows the Elden Ring idea that communion changes the body. Its capstone is a Huge true-dragon Maximum with breath recharge, fear aura, and Dragonlord movement.

Innate Technique Feats

Four build-defining options.

These broaden the law instead of merely inflating the same number.

Prerequisite: Dragonheart Incarnation

Dragon Communion Savant

Learn one additional lineage’s breath rider; you may apply it to your primary Breath by paying +1 CE, without gaining its resistance.

Prerequisite: Gravel Scales

Scale Shelter

While Gravel Scales is active, as a bonus action you may extend stony plates over one willing creature within 5 feet; it gains +1 AC until the start of your next turn. Your own scales are not diminished by the sharing.

Prerequisite: Wings

Winged Predator

After descending at least 20 feet before a melee attack, add 2 Dragon Dice once per turn and land without falling damage.

Prerequisite: level 11

Dragonlord’s Echo

The storm answers at a distance. You may originate Dragonstorm at a point you can see within 90 feet instead of centered on yourself, shaping the same 30-foot-radius storm around that point.

Rules integration

Edge cases and shared systems.

These rulings are designed around the revised Player Guide rather than the raw PDF where the two differ.

True Dragon changes size and reach exactly as printed; ordinary partial transformations do not.
Breath is a saving-throw feature and cannot Black Flash.
A secondary lineage does not grant all of its features unless stated.
Dragon Communion body changes are technique manifestations, not permanent ancestry changes unless a story vow makes them so.
Technique DC: 8 + proficiency bonus + your chosen Cursed Energy ability modifier (Wisdom or Charisma unless your class says otherwise).
Direct criticals: rolled weapon, unarmed, and CE attacks can critical normally. After a qualifying critical, roll the campaign's unmodified Black Flash confirmation d20; a natural 20 triples the completed critical damage. Autonomous Domain events, damage-over-time, hazards, and unrolled pulses cannot Black Flash.
Maximum Output: unless a feature says otherwise, double that feature's base CE cost and enhance one supported axis—damage, range, duration, or accuracy/DC—using the campaign's Maximum Output rules. Named Maximum Techniques and Domains do not receive generic Maximum Output.
Technique Focus: effects marked Focus occupy the shared Technique Focus slot. Opening a Domain ends other Focus effects unless the Domain expressly preserves them.
Two-technique characters: these guides assume each technique progresses from character/class level as agreed by the campaign. Multiclassing itself does not grant another Innate Technique.
