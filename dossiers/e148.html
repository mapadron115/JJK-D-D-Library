Territorial Influence

Turn the battlefield into a Go board. Stones create overlapping zones of influence, and opponents who allow themselves to be surrounded lose movement and tactical options.

Power tierExceptional

Field notes. Subject: she does not fight on the board; she makes it. Three truths govern her. First: influence is real before it is violent — two overlapping Stones declare Atari, and the enemy's speed and Dash are taxed without a single blow. Second: the verdict compounds — three overlapping Stones Capture, and Capture is the board speaking its ruling: speed zero, no reactions. Third: position is supply — six Stones become eight, then ten, their Influence growing, and the Domain summons nine spectral stones that she may relocate at initiative count 20 as the sure-hit. Assess her correctly: every move she makes narrows your future moves. Maxim: surround, and the enemy's strength becomes the map of his cage.

Main resource

Cursed Stones

Six stones at first, more with mastery: the entire technique is what she can place on the ground.

Cursed Stones

You carry or manifestThe pieces are humble — tiny, breakable, replaceable after rest — because the power was never in the stone; it is in the placement. a set of cursed black/white stones. You have 6 Stones at 1st level. A deployed Stone is Tiny, AC 15, HP equal to 5 + your sorcerer level, and immune to poison/psychic damage. Destroyed Stones return after a short or long rest.

Place Stone

One bonus action, one coin of CE: a stone laid, ten feet of law radiating from it. Bonus Action • 1 CE • 30 feet. Place one Stone on an unoccupied surface. You may retrieve one within reach for free on your turn. Each Stone projects 10 feet of Influence.

Atari

The game's first verdict: where two influences overlap, the enemy's speed and Dash are taxed without a blow struck. A hostile creature inside the overlapping Influence of at least 2 Stones is in Atari: its speed is reduced by 10 feet and it cannot take the Dash action.

Capture

The board speaks its ruling: where three influences overlap at the start of his turn, he is Captured — speed zero, no reactions. When a hostile creature starts its turn inside the overlapping Influence of at least 3 Stones, it makes a Strength or Dexterity save (its choice). On failure it is Captured until the start of its next turn: speed 0 and no reactions. A creature can suffer Capture only once per round.

3Sente —

Initiative is the game's soul: when the enemy enters her influence, she answers with a stone — the right of first reply. reaction when a hostile creature enters one of your Influence zones: move one deployed Stone up to 10 feet across a surface. This movement cannot itself trigger another Sente this round.

6Cut and Connect —

She draws her own lines between stones: the first hostile to cross each turn pays in force damage and stops at the border. you have 8 Stones. As an action for 2 CE, draw a cursed line between two Stones within 40 feet until the start of your next turn. The first hostile creature each turn to cross the line makes a Dexterity save, taking 2 Influence Dice force damage and stopping at the line on failure; half damage on success.

11Living Board —

The board matures: ten stones, wider influence — and now a stone may be placed in the rhythm of the strike itself. you have 10 Stones and their Influence radius becomes 15 feet. Once per turn when you hit a creature, you may place one Stone in an unoccupied space within 5 feet of it without spending an action (still costs 1 CE).

18Domain Expansion: Nineteen-by-Nineteen Funeral Board —

The full board at last: nine spectral stones that do not count against her set, relocated at initiative count twenty — the sure-hit is the funeral procession of the enemy's movement. 10 CE. Nine spectral Stones appear throughout the Domain and do not count against your set. At initiative count 20, relocate up to 3 spectral Stones anywhere on the ground. Every hostile creature influenced by 3+ Stones becomes Captured until the next initiative count 20 and takes 3 Influence Dice force damage. This Capture/damage is the sure-hit.

Innate Technique Feats

Technique Feats

Joseki Study

She knows the classical shapes: a stone placed in good company lets her reposition — the old sequences still win. You know the classical shapes: when you place a Stone adjacent to at least two of your other Stones, you may immediately move one deployed Stone up to 10 feet across a surface, as if using Sente.

Sacrifice Play

A stone may be given to take: when one would be destroyed, she detonates it — the sacrifice was always the plan. Reaction when one of your Stones would be destroyed: detonate it. Creatures within 5 feet make a Dexterity save or take 2 Influence Dice force damage.

Reading Ahead

The master reads before the game begins: one stone placed free when initiative is called. At the start of combat, after initiative is rolled, place one Stone within 10 feet of yourself for free.

Rules integration

Table Rulings

Influence ignores creatures and low cover but cannot pass through a solid wall or sealed barrier.
A creature can be in Atari/Captured regardless of which side of the Stones it visually stands on; overlapping Influence is the mechanical test.
Moving a Stone can immediately end Atari/Capture if the required overlap no longer exists.
