Territorial Influence

Turn the battlefield into a Go board. Stones create overlapping zones of influence, and opponents who allow themselves to be surrounded lose movement and tactical options.

Power tierTier IV · Special Grade

Field notes. Subject: she does not fight on the board; she makes it. Three truths govern her. First: influence is real before it is violent, two overlapping Stones declare Atari, and the enemy's speed and Dash are taxed without a single blow. Second: the verdict compounds, three overlapping Stones Capture, and Capture is the board speaking its ruling: speed zero, no reactions. Third: position is supply, six Stones become eight, then ten, their Influence growing, and the Domain summons nine spectral stones that she may relocate at initiative count 20 as the sure-hit. Assess her correctly: every move she makes narrows your future moves. Maxim: surround, and the enemy's strength becomes the map of his cage.

Full progression

Level Progression

1Cursed Stones, Influence, Atari, Capture

Influence Die

Passive · The Influence Die grows with mastery: d4 → d6 (6th) → d8 (11th) → d10 (18th).

The weight of each stone on the board.

Main resource

Passive · Cursed Stones are the technique’s whole resource: six at first, more with mastery.

The entire technique is what she can place on the ground.

Cursed Stones

Passive · You carry or manifest 6 Stones (1st level) · A deployed Stone is Tiny, AC 15, HP 5 + your sorcerer level, immune to poison/psychic · Destroyed Stones return after a short or long rest.

The pieces are humble: tiny, breakable, replaceable after rest, because the power was never in the stone; it is in the placement.

Place Stone

Bonus action · 1 CE, 30 ft · Place one Stone on an unoccupied surface (retrieve one within reach for free on your turn) · Each Stone projects 10 ft of Influence.

One bonus action, one coin of CE: a stone laid, ten feet of law radiating from it.

Atari

Passive · A hostile inside the overlapping Influence of 2+ Stones is in Atari: speed −10 ft and cannot take the Dash action.

The game's first verdict: where two influences overlap, the enemy's speed and Dash are taxed without a blow struck.

Capture

Passive · A hostile starting its turn in the overlapping Influence of 3+ Stones makes a Str or Dex save (its choice); on failure it is Captured until start of its next turn: speed 0, no reactions · A creature suffers Capture only once per round.

The board speaks its ruling: where three influences overlap at the start of his turn, he is Captured — speed zero, no reactions.

3Sente —

Reaction · 1 CE · When a hostile enters one of your Influence zones: move one deployed Stone up to 10 ft across a surface · This movement can’t trigger another Sente this round.

Initiative is the game's soul: when the enemy enters her influence, she answers with a stone, the right of first reply.

6Cut and Connect —

Action · 2 CE · Draw a cursed line between two Stones within 40 ft until start of your next turn (you now have 8 Stones) · The first hostile each turn to cross it makes a Dex save: 2 Influence Dice force damage and stops at the line on failure, half damage on success.

She draws her own lines between stones, and the crossing becomes a border the enemy pays to pass.

11Living Board —

Passive · You have 10 Stones; Influence radius becomes 15 ft · Once per turn when you hit a creature, place one Stone in an unoccupied space within 5 ft of it without spending an action (still costs 1 CE).

The board matures, and now a stone may be placed in the rhythm of the strike itself.

18Domain Expansion: Nineteen-by-Nineteen Funeral Board —

Domain Expansion · 10 CE · Nine spectral Stones appear throughout the Domain (not against your set); at initiative 20 relocate up to 3 anywhere on the ground · Every hostile influenced by 3+ Stones is Captured until next initiative 20 and takes 3 Influence Dice force damage — this Capture/damage is the sure-hit.

The world becomes a board, nineteen lines by nineteen, stretching past the barrier's edge into fog. The stones are headstones, black and white, and each one hums faintly with the name of someone buried. The fog at the board's edge moves like it is deciding.

The full board at last: nine spectral stones beyond her set, and the sure-hit is the funeral procession of the enemy's movement.

Innate Technique Feats

Technique Feats

Joseki Study

She knows the classical shapes: a stone placed in good company lets her reposition, the old sequences still win. You know the classical shapes: when you place a Stone adjacent to at least two of your other Stones, you may immediately move one deployed Stone up to 10 feet across a surface, as if using Sente.

Sacrifice Play

A stone may be given to take: when one would be destroyed, she detonates it, the sacrifice was always the plan. Reaction for 1 CE when one of your Stones would be destroyed: detonate it. Creatures within 5 feet make a Dexterity save or take 2 Influence Dice force damage.

Reading Ahead

The master reads before the game begins: one stone placed free when initiative is called. At the start of combat, after initiative is rolled, place one Stone within 10 feet of yourself for free.

Rules integration

Table Rulings

Influence ignores creatures and low cover but cannot pass through a solid wall or sealed barrier.
A creature can be in Atari/Captured regardless of which side of the Stones it visually stands on; overlapping Influence is the mechanical test.
Moving a Stone can immediately end Atari/Capture if the required overlap no longer exists.
