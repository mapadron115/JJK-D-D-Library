Territorial Influence

Turn the battlefield into a Go board. Stones create overlapping zones of influence, and opponents who allow themselves to be surrounded lose movement and tactical options.

Power tierExceptional

The fiction. The prevailing theory holds that Territorial Influence is strategy that refused to stay abstract: the user's cursed energy learned the shape of surrounding rather than striking, and each stone is a commitment the battlefield cannot unmake. Jujutsu scholars describe Atari and Capture not as attacks but as the board's verdict — the moment the energy's patience becomes the enemy's prison.

Main resource

Cursed Stones

Cursed Stones

You carry or manifest a set of cursed black/white stones. You have 6 Stones at 1st level. A deployed Stone is Tiny, AC 15, HP equal to 5 + your sorcerer level, and immune to poison/psychic damage. Destroyed Stones return after a short or long rest.

Place Stone

Bonus Action • 1 CE • 30 feet. Place one Stone on an unoccupied surface. You may retrieve one within reach for free on your turn. Each Stone projects 10 feet of Influence.

Atari

A hostile creature inside the overlapping Influence of at least 2 Stones is in Atari: its speed is reduced by 10 feet and it cannot take the Dash action.

Capture

When a hostile creature starts its turn inside the overlapping Influence of at least 3 Stones, it makes a Strength or Dexterity save (its choice). On failure it is Captured until the start of its next turn: speed 0 and no reactions. A creature can suffer Capture only once per round.

L3Sente —

reaction when a hostile creature enters one of your Influence zones: move one deployed Stone up to 10 feet across a surface. This movement cannot itself trigger another Sente this round.

L6Cut and Connect —

you have 8 Stones. As an action for 2 CE, draw a cursed line between two Stones within 40 feet until the start of your next turn. The first hostile creature each turn to cross the line makes a Dexterity save, taking 2 Influence Dice force damage and stopping at the line on failure; half damage on success.

L11Living Board —

you have 10 Stones and their Influence radius becomes 15 feet. Once per turn when you hit a creature, you may place one Stone in an unoccupied space within 5 feet of it without spending an action (still costs 1 CE).

L18Domain Expansion: Nineteen-by-Nineteen Funeral Board —

10 CE. Nine spectral Stones appear throughout the Domain and do not count against your set. At initiative count 20, relocate up to 3 spectral Stones anywhere on the ground. Every hostile creature influenced by 3+ Stones becomes Captured until the next initiative count 20 and takes 3 Influence Dice force damage. This Capture/damage is the sure-hit.

Innate Technique Feats

Technique Feats

Joseki Study

You know the classical shapes: when you place a Stone adjacent to at least two of your other Stones, you may immediately move one deployed Stone up to 10 feet across a surface, as if using Sente.

Sacrifice Play

Reaction when one of your Stones would be destroyed: detonate it. Creatures within 5 feet make a Dexterity save or take 2 Influence Dice force damage.

Reading Ahead

At the start of combat, after initiative is rolled, place one Stone within 10 feet of yourself for free.

Rules integration

Table Rulings

Influence ignores creatures and low cover but cannot pass through a solid wall or sealed barrier.
A creature can be in Atari/Captured regardless of which side of the Stones it visually stands on; overlapping Influence is the mechanical test.
Moving a Stone can immediately end Atari/Capture if the required overlap no longer exists.
