Core law

What the technique actually does.

Passive · This entry is a modular cursed-technique framework with no native combat loop · Defeating a major supernatural foe can crystallize a Remembrance · Each equipped Art must cite its reviewed source and reduced-scale numbers at crystallization.

Core Engine, Remembrance Slots. Read this as a primer, not a technique. There is no combat loop here, only a framework: slots to fill, Arts to cite, numbers kept honest at reduced scale. The fighting comes from what has been buried. This entry is a subsystem primer, not an ordinary innate technique: it has no native combat loop of its own. It is a modular cursed-technique framework. Defeating a major supernatural foe can crystallize a Remembrance. Each equipped Remembrance Art must cite its reviewed source and reduced-scale numbers at crystallization; no importing Arts from unreviewed guides. You may know many, but you can only equip a few at a time, your slot count grows as you level (see the level progression below). Each provides one active Art and sometimes one passive Echo.

Field notes. Subject: grief with a catalogue. Three truths govern the Bearer. First: nothing slain is simply gone, the fallen crystallize into Remembrances because mourning was never an option, and each equipped memory is a kept debt that still fights. Second: this is no innate technique but a framework, no native combat loop, only slots to fill and Arts to cite; the Bearer is a librarian whose books were all written by the dying. Third: the slots are the measure of mastery, two at the start, six by the summit, and every equipped Art must cite its reviewed source and reduced-scale numbers. Mark the Pinnacle: at eighteen the Bearer chooses an ending the way other sorcerers choose a Domain. The archive does not lend. It inheres.

Features & procedures

Slot procedures by level.

1Grace & Runes

Passive · You may equip 2 Remembrances at a time.

The first shelf: two slots, no more. The collection begins in modesty, as all archives do. You may equip 2 Remembrances at a time.

Long rest · At a declared Grace: restore your CE and swap your equipped Remembrances · Once per long rest, when reduced to 0 HP you automatically stabilize and leave a Rune Echo at that location.

The archive opens at a declared hearth. Grace is the checkpoint: long rests there restore the Bearer and re-shelve the Remembrances, and one death per day is politely declined, with an Echo left where it happened. Long rests taken at a Grace you have declared restore your CE and let you swap your equipped Remembrances. Once per long rest, when you are reduced to 0 HP, you automatically stabilize and leave a Rune Echo at that location.

3Ash of War

Downtime · Bind one weapon art (an Ash of War) to a weapon for 1–3 CE — e.g. Bloodhound Step, Lion’s Claw, Storm Blade, Carian Grandeur.

The first borrowed technique: one weapon art, bound for one to three CE. The Bearer does not invent, the Bearer checks out. You may bind one weapon art (an Ash of War) to a weapon for 1–3 CE. Examples: Bloodhound Step, Lion’s Claw, Storm Blade, Carian Grandeur.

6Third Remembrance

Passive · Unlock a third Remembrance slot (equip 3) · During downtime, once per major arc, you may duplicate one Remembrance to choose its alternate reward or Art.

A third shelf. And once per arc, in the quiet between campaigns, one Remembrance may be duplicated to take its other ending, the archive permits a single revision. You unlock your third Remembrance slot (you may now equip 3). During downtime, once per major arc, you may duplicate one Remembrance to choose its alternate reward or Art.

9Great Rune

Rest · After a rest, equip one Great Rune: Godrick (+1 to two abilities, max 22), Radahn (+10% HP and CE), Morgott (+15% HP), Malenia (heal HP = your PB once per turn after damaging the creature that last hurt you), or a GM-approved equivalent.

The great seals of the fallen lords: Godrick's twin talents, Radahn's bounty, Morgott's vigor, Malenia's answer to pain, each a passive law worn after rest, each GM-approved. After a rest, you may equip one Great Rune: Godrick (+1 to two abilities, maximum 22), Radahn (+10% HP and CE), Morgott (+15% HP), Malenia (heal hit points equal to your proficiency bonus once per turn after damaging the creature that last hurt you), or a GM-approved equivalent.

11Legendary Armament

Passive · Bind one legendary cursed tool and use its Art for 1 less CE once per turn · Your Remembrance slots increase to 4.

One legendary tool bound, its Art one CE cheaper once per turn. Four slots now; the collection outgrows the case. You may bind one legendary cursed tool and use its Art for 1 less CE once per turn. Your Remembrance slots increase to 4.

13Spirit Ash

Action · 5 CE, once per long rest · Summon one approved Spirit Ash companion for 1 minute · A Mimic Tear copies only your mundane stats and equipment (no Domain, Maximum, consumables, or Remembrance resources).

For five CE the dead walk beside the Bearer for a minute. The Mimic Tear copies only the mundane self, no Domain, no Maximum, no consumables; the copy is honest about its limits. For 5 CE, once per long rest, you may summon one approved Spirit Ash companion for 1 minute. A Mimic Tear copies your mundane stats and equipment but gains no Domain, Maximum, consumables, or independent Remembrance resources. Sample ashes: Lone Wolf Pack, three wolves (AC 13, HP 11 each, speed 40 ft; bite at your CE attack bonus, 2d6 + CE modifier); they act on your turn without a separate action. Banished Knight Oleg, (AC 16, HP 45, speed 30 ft; greatsword at your CE attack bonus, 2d8 + CE modifier); acts after you, one action per round. Approval checklist: CR no higher than half your level; no Domains, Maxima, or legendary actions; one stat block, 1-minute duration; the GM approves the submission before play.

15Boss Art Mastery

Free · Once per turn, after you use a Remembrance Art: make one weapon attack or move up to half your speed as a bonus action.

Fluency: after a Remembrance Art, one weapon strike or half a move as a bonus action. The archive reader has learned to turn pages fast. Once per turn, after you use a Remembrance Art, you may make one weapon attack or move up to half your speed as a bonus action.

16Fifth Remembrance

Passive · Your Remembrance slots increase to 5.

A fifth slot. The catalogue swells; the librarian does not. Your Remembrance slots increase to 5.

18Elden Lord Candidate

Special · Choose one ending-aligned Pinnacle (Order, Stars, Frenzy, Death, Dusk, or Curse): it grants one 8-CE, once-per-long-rest, world-shaping combat feature agreed with your GM · Not a Domain.

The Pinnacle: choose an ending, Order, Stars, Frenzy, Death, Dusk, or Curse, and agree its one world-shaping feature with the GM. Not a Domain. The world's last page. Choose one ending-aligned Pinnacle: Order, Stars, Frenzy, Death, Dusk, or Curse. It grants one 8-CE, once-per-long-rest, world-shaping combat feature, agreed with your GM.

20Lord of Remembrance

Passive · Your Remembrance slots increase to 6 · Once per short rest, swap one equipped Remembrance as an action · The first Remembrance Art you use each combat costs 0 CE (base cost 3 CE or less).

Six slots, one swapped memory per short rest as an action, and the first cheap Art of every combat costs nothing. The archive is complete, and it fights for free. Your Remembrance slots increase to 6. Once per short rest, you may swap one equipped Remembrance as an action. The first Remembrance Art you use each combat costs 0 CE, provided its base cost is 3 CE or less.

Pinnacle expression

Maximum, Domain, or equivalent.

Example Remembrance Arts: Starscourge, Gravity Draw / Meteor Crash (see e046 Gravity Sovereignty); Black Blade, Destined Death max-HP cut (see e047 Destined Death); Rot Goddess, Waterfowl / Scarlet Aeonia (see e052 Scarlet Bloom); Full Moon, Moon sorcery and magic debuff (see e048 Dark Moon Covenant); Dragonlord, lightning claw / ruin breath (see e054 Dragonheart Incarnation); Blood Lord, bloodflame ritual; Lion, storm assault. Feats

Build-defining options.

Let Me Solo

This boss has been read before. One minute of study, or an action and a hard Insight, and the familiar foe keeps one fewer secret. You know this boss: when facing a boss-tier creature you have fought before, you may spend 1 minute studying its patterns before combat (or an action and a DC 15 Insight check during it) to learn one of its resistances, immunities, or legendary actions.

Potent Ash

One weapon, two arts. One extra CE to bind the second, and the blade changes its mind as a bonus action. You layer arts on a single weapon: when you bind an Ash of War, you may bind a second art to the same weapon for 1 extra CE, switching between them as a bonus action.

Rune Arc

The seal is lent away: suppress the Great Rune's own benefit for a minute so allies within thirty feet may wear it. The archive shares what it cannot keep twice. You project the rune outward: you may suppress your Great Rune's passive benefit for 1 minute to instead grant it to allies within 30 ft, they gain the benefit while you lose it.

Thy Strength Befits a Crown

Victories crystallize faster now. Slay a boss-tier foe personally and the Remembrance is taken at once, no waiting for the post-combat quiet. Your victories crystallize faster now: when you personally reduce a boss-tier creature to 0 HP, you may immediately attempt to crystallize a Remembrance from it, spending the usual costs, without waiting for post-combat downtime.

Rules integration

Edge cases and shared systems.

Shared rulings: Technique DC = 8 + PB + CE ability modifier. Direct rolled attacks may crit and use the campaign Black Flash procedure; autonomous hazards and Domain events cannot. Maximum Output may modify ordinary supported features, never named Maxima/Domains. Two-technique characters use the campaign's agreed character-level progression.
