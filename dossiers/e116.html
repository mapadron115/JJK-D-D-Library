Core law

What the technique actually does.

Core Engine — Remembrance Slots. This entry is a subsystem primer, not an ordinary innate technique: it has no native combat loop of its own. It is a modular cursed-technique framework. Defeating a major supernatural foe can crystallize a Remembrance. Each equipped Remembrance Art must cite its reviewed source and reduced-scale numbers at crystallization; no importing Arts from unreviewed guides. You may know many, but you can only equip a few at a time — your slot count grows as you level (see the level progression below). Each provides one active Art and sometimes one passive Echo.

The fiction. Scholars describe the Remembrance Bearer framework as grief with an archive attached: the user's cursed energy refuses to let anything it has killed be simply gone, and crystallizes the fallen into Remembrances because mourning them was never an option. Each equipped Remembrance is a kept debt — the technique's own citation requirement, demanding every scar prove its source, is the CE's way of remembering honestly rather than romantically. The slot limit is the sorcerer's whole capacity for the dead made visible: they can carry many ghosts, but they can only look at a few of them at once.

Features & procedures

Slot procedures by level.

1Grace & Runes

You may equip 2 Remembrances at a time.

Long rests taken at a Grace you have declared restore your CE and let you swap your equipped Remembrances. Once per long rest, when you are reduced to 0 HP, you automatically stabilize and leave a Rune Echo at that location.

3Ash of War

You may bind one weapon art (an Ash of War) to a weapon for 1–3 CE. Examples: Bloodhound Step, Lion’s Claw, Storm Blade, Carian Grandeur.

6Third Remembrance

You unlock your third Remembrance slot (you may now equip 3). During downtime, once per major arc, you may duplicate one Remembrance to choose its alternate reward or Art.

9Great Rune

After a rest, you may equip one Great Rune: Godrick (+1 to two abilities, maximum 22), Radahn (+10% HP and CE), Morgott (+15% HP), Malenia (heal hit points equal to your proficiency bonus once per turn after damaging the creature that last hurt you), or a GM-approved equivalent.

11Legendary Armament

You may bind one legendary cursed tool and use its Art for 1 less CE once per turn. Your Remembrance slots increase to 4.

13Spirit Ash

For 5 CE, once per long rest, you may summon one approved Spirit Ash companion for 1 minute. A Mimic Tear copies your mundane stats and equipment but gains no Domain, Maximum, consumables, or independent Remembrance resources.

15Boss Art Mastery

Once per turn, after you use a Remembrance Art, you may make one weapon attack or move up to half your speed as a bonus action.

16Fifth Remembrance

Your Remembrance slots increase to 5.

18Elden Lord Candidate

Choose one ending-aligned Pinnacle: Order, Stars, Frenzy, Death, Dusk, or Curse. It grants one 8-CE, once-per-long-rest, world-shaping combat feature, agreed with your GM.

20Lord of Remembrance

Your Remembrance slots increase to 6. Once per short rest, you may swap one equipped Remembrance as an action. The first Remembrance Art you use each combat costs 0 CE, provided its base cost is 3 CE or less.

Pinnacle expression

Maximum, Domain, or equivalent.

Example Remembrance Arts: Starscourge — Gravity Draw / Meteor Crash; Black Blade — Destined Death max-HP cut; Rot Goddess — Waterfowl / Scarlet Aeonia; Full Moon — Moon sorcery and magic debuff; Dragonlord — lightning claw / ruin breath; Blood Lord — bloodflame ritual; Lion — storm assault. Each Art uses the standalone technique's established numbers where that guide already exists, usually at a reduced single-feature scale.

Feats

Build-defining options.

Let Me Solo

You know this boss: when facing a boss-tier creature you have fought before, you may spend 1 minute studying its patterns before combat (or an action and a DC 15 Insight check during it) to learn one of its resistances, immunities, or legendary actions.

Potent Ash

You layer arts on a single weapon: when you bind an Ash of War, you may bind a second art to the same weapon for 1 extra CE, switching between them as a bonus action.

Rune Arc

You project the rune outward: you may suppress your Great Rune's passive benefit for 1 minute to instead grant it to allies within 30 ft — they gain the benefit while you lose it.

Thy Strength Befits a Crown

Your victories crystallize faster now: when you personally reduce a boss-tier creature to 0 HP, you may immediately attempt to crystallize a Remembrance from it, spending the usual costs, without waiting for post-combat downtime.

Rules integration

Edge cases and shared systems.

Shared rulings: Technique DC = 8 + PB + CE ability modifier. Direct rolled attacks may crit and use the campaign Black Flash procedure; autonomous hazards and Domain events cannot. Maximum Output may modify ordinary supported features, never named Maxima/Domains. Two-technique characters use the campaign's agreed character-level progression.
