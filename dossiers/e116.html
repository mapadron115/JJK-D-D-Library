Core law

What the technique actually does.

Core Engine — Remembrance Slots. This entry is a subsystem primer, not an ordinary innate technique: it has no native combat loop of its own. It is a modular cursed-technique framework. Defeating a major supernatural foe can crystallize a Remembrance. Each equipped Remembrance Art must cite its reviewed source and reduced-scale numbers at crystallization; no importing Arts from unreviewed guides. You may know many, but equip only 2 at level 1, 3 at 6, 4 at 11, 5 at 16, and 6 at 20. Each provides one active Art and sometimes one passive Echo.

Features & procedures

Slot procedures by level.

1Grace & Runes

Long rests at a declared Grace restore CE and let you swap Remembrances. Once/long rest when reduced to 0, stabilize automatically and leave a Rune Echo at that location.

3Ash of War

Bind one weapon art to a weapon; 1–3 CE. Examples: Bloodhound Step, Lion's Claw, Storm Blade, Carian Grandeur.

6Third Remembrance

Unlock third slot. Duplicate one Remembrance during downtime once per major arc to choose its alternate reward/Art.

9Great Rune

Equip one Great Rune after rest: Godrick (+1 to two abilities, max 22), Radahn (+10% HP/CE), Morgott (+15% HP), Malenia (heal PB HP once/turn after damaging the creature that last hurt you), or GM-approved equivalent.

11Legendary Armament

Bind one legendary cursed tool and use its Art for 1 less CE once/turn. Fourth Remembrance slot.

13Spirit Ash

5 CE, once/long rest: summon one approved Spirit Ash companion for 1 minute. Mimic Tear copies your mundane stats/equipment but no Domain, Maximum, consumables, or independent Remembrance resources.

15Boss Art Mastery

Once/turn after using a Remembrance Art, make one weapon attack or move half speed as bonus action.

18Elden Lord Candidate

Choose one ending-aligned Pinnacle: Order, Stars, Frenzy, Death, Dusk, or Curse. It grants one 8-CE once/long-rest world-shaping combat feature agreed with GM.

20Lord of Remembrance

Six slots; swap one equipped Remembrance as an action once/short rest. First Remembrance Art each combat costs 0 CE up to 3 CE base cost.

Pinnacle expression

Maximum, Domain, or equivalent.

Example Remembrance Arts: Starscourge — Gravity Draw / Meteor Crash; Black Blade — Destined Death max-HP cut; Rot Goddess — Waterfowl / Scarlet Aeonia; Full Moon — Moon sorcery and magic debuff; Dragonlord — lightning claw / ruin breath; Blood Lord — bloodflame ritual; Lion — storm assault. Each Art uses the standalone technique's established numbers where that guide already exists, usually at a reduced single-feature scale.

Rules integration

Edge cases and shared systems.

Shared rulings: Technique DC = 8 + PB + CE ability modifier. Direct rolled attacks may crit and use the campaign Black Flash procedure; autonomous hazards and Domain events cannot. Maximum Output may modify ordinary supported features, never named Maxima/Domains. Two-technique characters use the campaign's agreed character-level progression.
