Remembrance Bearer

Tier V · ModularRemembrances / runes / armaments / boss inheritance

Core Engine — Remembrance Slots. This is a modular cursed-technique framework. Defeating a major supernatural foe can crystallize a Remembrance. You may know many, but equip only 2 at level 1, 3 at 6, 4 at 11, 5 at 16, and 6 at 20. Each provides one active Art and sometimes one passive Echo.

Lv.FeatureRules
1Grace & RunesLong rests at a declared Grace restore CE and let you swap Remembrances. Once/long rest when reduced to 0, stabilize automatically and leave a Rune Echo at that location.
3Ash of WarBind one weapon art to a weapon; 1–3 CE. Examples: Bloodhound Step, Lion's Claw, Storm Blade, Carian Grandeur.
6Third RemembranceUnlock third slot. Duplicate one Remembrance during downtime once per major arc to choose its alternate reward/Art.
9Great RuneEquip one Great Rune after rest: Godrick (+1 to two abilities, max 22), Radahn (+10% HP/CE), Morgott (+15% HP), Malenia (heal PB HP once/turn after damaging the creature that last hurt you), or GM-approved equivalent.
11Legendary ArmamentBind one legendary cursed tool and use its Art for 1 less CE once/turn. Fourth Remembrance slot.
13Spirit Ash5 CE, once/long rest: summon one approved Spirit Ash companion for 1 minute. Mimic Tear copies your mundane stats/equipment but no Domain, Maximum, consumables, or independent Remembrance resources.
15Boss Art MasteryOnce/turn after using a Remembrance Art, make one weapon attack or move half speed as bonus action.
18Elden Lord CandidateChoose one ending-aligned Pinnacle: Order, Stars, Frenzy, Death, Dusk, or Curse. It grants one 8-CE once/long-rest world-shaping combat feature agreed with GM.
20Lord of RemembranceSix slots; swap one equipped Remembrance as an action once/short rest. First Remembrance Art each combat costs 0 CE up to 3 CE base cost.
Example Remembrance Arts: Starscourge — Gravity Draw / Meteor Crash; Black Blade — Destined Death max-HP cut; Rot Goddess — Waterfowl / Scarlet Aeonia; Full Moon — Moon sorcery and magic debuff; Dragonlord — lightning claw / ruin breath; Blood Lord — bloodflame ritual; Lion — storm assault. Each Art uses the standalone technique's established numbers where that guide already exists, usually at a reduced single-feature scale.

Feats

Let Me Solo: advantage on first save against a boss fought before.Potent Ash: Ash of War DC +1.Rune Arc: once/long rest empower Great Rune for 1 minute.Thy Strength Befits a Crown: when you defeat a boss, regain PB CE and Inspiration.
Shared rulings: Technique DC = 8 + PB + CE ability modifier. Direct rolled attacks may crit and use the campaign Black Flash procedure; autonomous hazards and Domain events cannot. Maximum Output may modify ordinary supported features, never named Maxima/Domains. Two-technique characters use the campaign's agreed character-level progression.
