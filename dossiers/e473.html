Cursed Tool

Kamutoke

A Special Grade cursed club, a storm bound into a weapon.

FormWeapon (Club)
GradeSpecial Grade
AttunementNot required

Field notes. Subject: a storm kept in the quartermaster’s ledger. Three truths govern the club. First, the thunder is metered: every coin of cursed energy spent buys exactly 2d12 lightning damage, up to ten coins an action, against up to five chosen creatures at a hundred and twenty feet, the sky strikes only where it is pointed. Second, the blast is shared: one damage roll for all targets, the same thunder falling on each alike. Third, a clean strike stops what it burns: any creature rolling ten or less on its save stands paralyzed until the end of its next turn. Consider: the storm does not only wound. It holds. And what the storm holds, the wielder finishes.

Features & procedures

Properties & techniques

Exact player-facing procedures

Cursed edge

The hilt of the storm: +4 to attack and damage rolls. Before the thunder is called, the club is already a serious argument. You have a +4 bonus to attack rolls and damage rolls made with this weapon.

Action

Lightning blast

The sky, portioned out. As an action, spend up to ten cursed energy, each coin buys 2d12 lightning for up to five chosen creatures within 120 feet, DC 22 Constitution for half. And the storm holds what it burns: a save of ten or less leaves the creature paralyzed until the end of its next turn. As an action, you can spend up to 10 cursed energy to send out a blast of lightning against up to 5 creatures within 120 ft. Each creature must make a DC 22 Constitution saving throw, taking 2d12 lightning damage per cursed energy spent on a failed save, or half as much on a successful one. A creature that gets a 10 or less on the save is paralyzed until the end of its next turn.

Rules integration

Edge cases and shared systems.

Campaign canon notes

Procedure: declare the cursed energy spent before any target rolls its save; one shared damage roll applies to all targets. Paralysis triggers on a save total of 10 or less, even if that total still beats DC 22 (it cannot, but the threshold is absolute). You choose the targets, the blast does not strike indiscriminately.
