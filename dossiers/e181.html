Starvation Colossus

Character fantasy

Violence feeds Hunger. Spend it to assemble giant skeletal arms, ribs, jaws, armor, and finally a towering cursed exoskeleton around yourself.

Combat loop

  1. Gain Hunger as nearby violence escalates.
  2. Spend it on skeletal body parts that solve immediate problems.
  3. Keep enough Hunger in reserve to grow larger forms.
  4. Avoid overfeeding: excessive unspent Hunger begins harming the user.

Hunger & Colossus Parts

  • Hunger cap: 2 × PB. Begin at 0. Once per round gain 1 Hunger when a creature within 60 feet takes damage equal to at least your level, falls below half HP, or drops to 0. A death within 60 feet grants 2 instead, still once per round.
  • Starvation: if you end your turn at Hunger cap, take one Hunger Die necrotic damage that ignores resistance. Spending at least 1 Hunger before the end of your next turn prevents further starvation damage.
  • Parts: manifested skeleton is part of your body, uses your AC/saves, and vanishes when its duration ends.

Innate Technique FeatsOptional character-building feats

Bone Chime

You can sense creatures below half HP within 30 feet if they have bones or a comparable internal structure.

Graveyard Architecture

Torso of a Nation may anchor to terrain and counts as a structure for allies using cover or climbing.

Starved Longer

Starvation damage begins only after ending two consecutive turns at Hunger cap.

Jaw of Famine

A creature grappled by Jaw cannot regain HP from food, potions, or mundane medicine until grapple ends; RCT/magic healing functions normally.

Building the character

Juggernaut or Warrior. Unlike a normal transformation, this one escalates with the battle’s violence, so the player constantly decides whether to cash Hunger now or hold it for a larger body.

Technique die: Hunger Die follows the shared d6 → d8 → d10 → d12 progression.

Counterplay

Fight at range, deny the user sustained nearby damage events, force Hunger cap when they cannot spend, and target Focus to end large skeletal forms.

Adaptation note. This is original JJK 5e homebrew inspired by Gashadokuro. The myth/folklore provides imagery and thematic rules; the combat engine, progression, CE economy, feats, and Domain/Maximum procedures are game design for this codex.
