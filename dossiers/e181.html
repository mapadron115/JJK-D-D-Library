Core law

Character fantasy

Violence feeds Hunger. Spend it to assemble giant skeletal arms, ribs, jaws, armor, and finally a towering cursed exoskeleton around yourself.

Combat loop

  1. Gain Hunger as nearby violence escalates.
  2. Spend it on skeletal body parts that solve immediate problems.
  3. Keep enough Hunger in reserve to grow larger forms.
  4. Avoid overfeeding: excessive unspent Hunger begins harming the user.

The fiction. Jujutsu scholars describe the Colossus as hunger given a skeleton — the prevailing theory holds that the user's cursed energy, awakened in the shadow of famine or want, learned that the only safe shape is a bigger mouth. Violence feeds it because violence is the only language starvation understands; the bones are not armor so much as a stomach's architecture. The starvation at full Hunger is the technique's honesty: it was never built to protect the sorcerer. It was built to feed.

Core law

Hunger & Colossus Parts

Hunger cap:

2 × PB (3 × PB at 11th level). Begin at 0. Once per round gain 1 Hunger when a creature within 60 feet takes damage equal to at least your level, falls below half HP, or drops to 0. A death within 60 feet grants 2 instead, still once per round.

Starvation:

if you end your turn at Hunger cap, take 5 necrotic damage that ignores resistance. Spending at least 1 Hunger before the end of your next turn prevents further starvation damage.

Parts:

manifested skeleton is part of your body, uses your AC/saves, and vanishes when its duration ends.

Full progression

Level progression

1Bones of the Hungry

Spend Hunger as a bonus action: Arm (1) reach +10 feet until end of turn and one melee hit deals +4 damage; Ribs (1) gain temporary HP equal to 4 + your CE modifier; Jaw (2) melee CE attack with 10-foot reach, dealing 9 piercing damage and grappling on a failed Strength save until the end of your next turn.

3Rattle of the Giant

Action, 2 CE: creatures of choice within 20 feet make Wisdom save; failure frightened until end of your next turn. For each failed creature, gain 1 Hunger, maximum PB Hunger from this activation. Once per short rest.

6Skeletal Armor

Spend 3 Hunger as a bonus action for 1 minute Focus: AC becomes at least 16 + floor(PB/2), size Large if you choose, and unarmed reach +5 feet. While active, Arm costs 0 Hunger once per turn but still adds only +4.

11Torso of a Nation

Hunger cap becomes 3 × PB. Spend 4 Hunger as action to manifest a 20-foot-tall rib-and-spine construct centered on you for 1 minute: allies inside gain half cover from outside attacks; enemies entering make Strength save or are pushed 10 feet. You may move it with you at half speed or leave it stationary.

18Maximum: Gashadokuro — Starvation Colossus

Action, 10 CE and at least 6 Hunger, consume all Hunger, Focus, 1 minute. Become Huge with reach 20, gain temporary HP equal to 10 per Hunger consumed, and your unarmed attacks deal 9 + STR/CE modifier. Once per turn after a melee hit, make a free Jaw grapple without spending Hunger. Colossus Hunger: at the end of each of your turns, if a creature within 60 feet took 20+ damage that round, regain 1 consumed Hunger as Colossus Hunger; spend 2 to make one extra Arm attack as a bonus action, or spend 3 to restore 12 temporary HP. When the Maximum ends, all remaining temporary HP and Hunger vanish and you take 5 unavoidable damage per 2 Colossus Hunger left.

Feats

Build-defining options.

Bone Chime

You can sense creatures below half HP within 30 feet if they have bones or a comparable internal structure.

Graveyard Architecture

Torso of a Nation may anchor to terrain and counts as a structure for allies using cover or climbing.

Starving Maw

When you would take starvation damage for ending your turn at Hunger cap, you may instead force one creature grappled by your Jaw to take that damage as necrotic, your manifested skeleton gnawing through its own hunger. The grapple continues.

Jaw of Famine

As a bonus action while a creature is grappled by your Jaw, you may end the grapple to feed: gain 1 Hunger and temporary HP equal to 5.

Rules integration

Building the character

Juggernaut or Warrior. Unlike a normal transformation, this one escalates with the battle’s violence, so the player constantly decides whether to cash Hunger now or hold it for a larger body.
Rules integration

Counterplay

Fight at range, deny the user sustained nearby damage events, force Hunger cap when they cannot spend, and target Focus to end large skeletal forms.
