Vengeful Spirit Manipulation

A bound spirit supplies escalating partial strikes, a dangerous full manifestation, copied base techniques, and mutual love.

The fiction. Jujutsu scholars classify this as love that refused to end — a bond so absolute the user's cursed energy never accepted the beloved's death and keeps building her a body out of itself. The spirit's copied techniques are not theft, the registry notes, but devotion: Rika's love extends to everyone the user loves, and her CE wears their shapes out of grief, not greed. Nothing here is inherited or bargained for; it is mutual, and that is what makes it Special Grade.

Full progression

Feature map

1Partial Manifestation.

Partial Manifestation. Once per turn after you make at least one attack, pay 1 CE. The spirit makes one melee CE attack at level 1, two at level 3, three at level 6, and four at level 10, each against a creature within 10 feet of you and dealing 1d12 slashing on hit. It is not targetable during this flash of manifestation.

The binding weapon uses the source’s cursed-energy-enhanced weapon benefit. If the bound object is destroyed, Partial and Full Manifestation are unavailable until a replacement is bound during a long rest.

3Involuntary Full Manifestation.

Involuntary Full Manifestation. Partial Manifestation now grants two spirit attacks.

After one damage instance exceeds your level + CE modifier, make a DC 20 Wisdom save. On failure the appendix spirit manifests in an open space within 10 feet and acts after you. Before level 6 it is berserk, attacking the last creature to damage you or the closest other creature. Once per turn with no action, attempt the printed Persuasion check to dismiss it.

6Controlled window / Mimicry.

Partial Manifestation now grants three spirit attacks.

Controlled window. From level 6, on a failed danger trigger the spirit obeys you for CE modifier rounds (minimum one) and then dismisses.

Mimicry. While fully manifested, use a bonus action to command a claw against a creature. On hit, the spirit takes a DNA trace. Make a CE ability check with PB against 10 + half the copied feature’s base CE cost, rounded up; DC 10 if it has no cost. Success stores that Innate Technique’s base, non-passive feature. Domains, Maximum Techniques, entire feature progressions, CE pools, and chassis traits cannot be copied.

Store up to CE modifier copied features (minimum one), replacing one when over capacity. Use them only during full manifestation, with your action, CE, attack, DC, Output Rank, and the copied feature’s base numbers. A previously successful DNA pattern succeeds automatically if acquired again.

10Voluntary Manifestation.

Partial Manifestation now grants four spirit attacks.

Voluntary Manifestation. From level 10, triggers no longer force a save; instead, once per turn with no action you may manifest the spirit for 1 minute. Uses equal half CE modifier rounded down (minimum one) per long rest, and one use returns on a short rest. A controlled spirit follows one simple spoken order without costing your action; without an order it Dodges and protects you.

11Stronger Bond.

Stronger Bond. Choose two spirit ability scores to increase by 2, including maxima. While manifested, your CE ability and maximum rise by 2; current CE rises by 2 on entry and falls by up to 2 when it leaves, so remanifesting cannot farm CE. The spirit gains temporary HP equal to half your current HP on arrival.

Pinnacle expression

Maximum, Domain, or equivalent.

Domain — True and Mutual Love: 18 — full-turn action, 20 CE; 60-foot-radius enclosed Domain centered on you; 1 minute; Burnout 10 rounds. Field of Swords (continuous law). When the Domain opens, every technique in your Mimicry library manifests as a sword planted in the ground — one sword per stored technique. The swords are indistinguishable: you do not know which sword holds which technique until you pick it up (which sword you grab is random among the techniques currently manifested, or chosen by the GM). Once per turn as a free object interaction, you may take up a sword; when you do, you learn which stored technique it holds and may use that technique once, paying no CE, following its normal action cost, attack or save mechanics, and riders. The sword dissipates once its technique is used. At the start of your next turn the technique re-emerges in a new sword elsewhere in the Domain. No two swords ever hold the same technique at once. Sure-Hit Event (initiative 20). When the Domain opens, choose one stored copied technique as the sure-hit — its sword burns with visible light, and you always know which it is. At initiative count 20 each round, that technique strikes every hostile creature inside the Domain: an unprotected hostile suffers its full printed effect with no attack roll and no initial saving throw. A creature with an anti-domain defense instead faces the technique’s own saving throw; if the technique allows none, it makes a Dexterity saving throw against your CE save DC (invented), taking half damage and suffering no rider on a success.

Innate Technique Feats

Build-defining options.

These are optional character-building feats. You must meet each listed prerequisite before selecting it.

Prerequisite: Partial Manifestation

Partial Manifestation Versatility

Once per turn when Partial Manifestation would add damage, forgo all of that added damage to attempt one grapple or shove against the hit target as part of the attack, using your CE ability in place of Strength if desired.

Prerequisite: Mimicry

Technique Storage

A technique your spirit has mimicked can be wielded through you: while the spirit is manifested, partially or fully, you may use your action to activate one stored mimicry as if you knew it, paying its CE cost and using your attack bonus and save DC. The spirit cannot activate that same mimicry on the same turn.

Prerequisite: Stronger Bond

Directed Frenzy

While the appendix spirit is berserk, use a bonus action to name one creature you can see. Until the start of your next turn the spirit treats that creature as the last creature to damage you when choosing targets, and its attacks against the named creature have advantage. This does not end the berserk state.

Rules integration

Edge cases and shared systems.

Rules priority: the reviewed rulings, explicit codex clarifications, and the level progression always control when original wording conflicts. Legacy Domain auto-hit language, action terminology, event timing, missing saves, and older scaling never override revised player rules.
Why Tier IV: even a modest copy library adds a second durable body and access to other techniques’ defining base moves. The tier reflects that expanding rules surface, not an assumption that the player copies the perfect list.

The Vengeful Cursed Spirit technique involves the use of the spirit
of one you care about. As well as your malleable Cursed Energy.
Cursed Energy
Starting at 1st level, you can harness your negative feelings into a
usable source of energy, called cursed energy. These feelings can be
harnessed into attacks or abilities. The number of cursed energy you
possess is based on your class, some classes use a modifier that
being charisma or wisdom (Player’s choice)
Your cursed energy DC is 8 + user cursed energy ability modifier +
proficiency bonus.
Partial Manifestation
Beginning at 1st level, at some point in your lineage or family,
someone you cared about has died. Unable to accept this person's
death, you have unconsciously cursed them, and it became a vengeful
spirit of great power. Once per turn, you can call this spirit to
your aid in combat by spending 1 Cursed Energy, you can make an
additional attack roll. This additional claw attack will deal an
additional 1d12 slashing damage.
In addition, you have learned that to control your spirit better you
must focus its cursed energy on an object or weapon. You will gain
the cursed energy-enhanced weapon cursed energy enhancement.
At 3rd level, the number of attacks increases to 2, to 3 At 6th
level, and to 4 at 10th level.
Vengeful Spirit Manifestation
At 3rd level, your life is very stressful, being a sorcerer is
filled with danger and trouble. When these times of danger come, your
Vengeful Spirit may manifest into the physical world. Whenever you
take damage above your level + your Cursed Energy modifier, you must
make a DC 20 Wisdom saving throw. On a success, you can handle
yourself in the fight and don't let your spirit get out. On a
failure, however, your Vengeful Spirit will manifest itself into the
world to protect you while being berserk. It acts on the same turn as
you do, it is charmed by you and cannot go more than 200 feet away
from you. As a free action, you may make
a DC 20 Persuasion check against it, on
a success it will stop attacking and go
back to its dormant state, on a failure
it will keep attacking until there are
no more creatures but you in the radius.
They always focus on either the last
thing to damage you, or the closest creature to you.
At 6th level, the DC for the Persuasion check is reduced to 15, and
your Vengeful Spirit will stay in the physical world for a number of
rounds equal to your Cursed Energy modifier, being under your control
during it.
At 10th level, you have gained more control over your cursed spirit
and don't have to do the Wisdom saving throw anymore. You can now
manifest your spirit as a free action for 1 minute. It will now obey
your orders on who to attack, and who not to attack. You may summon
the spirit a number of times equal to half your Cursed Energy
modifier equal to your Cursed Energy modifier before needing to take
a long rest. On a short rest, you will regain one use of it. The
melee unarmed attack is equal to 1d8 or your own unarmed attack,
whichever is higher.
Mimicry
When you reach 6th level, you begin to learn about your innate
technique. As a bonus action, while your Vengeful Spirit is in the
world, you can command it to attack a creature. If the attack hits,
it can extract the creature's DNA with a claw attack. After that, you
can attempt to replicate it on a Charisma saving throw, on a success,
you can copy the Cursed Technique. On a failure save, you need to
command your Vengeful Spirit to hit the creature again:
-Cursed Technique = 10 + half the Cursed Energy required to perform

You cannot copy maximum techniques or Domain Expansions with
Mimicry.
When you copy a cursed technique, you must spend as much Cursed
Energy as the technique requires. No matter what you copy, you'll
only receive the technique in its base form. For example, a maximum
output hollow purple would only come as a hollow purple.
You can have up to your Cursed Energy Modifier of these techniques
prepared. You can only use it while your vengeful spirit is out.
Whenever you learn more than your limit, you must remove another to
make space for the technique.
In addition, you'll automatically pass the Charisma check if you
have already copied the cursed technique before.
