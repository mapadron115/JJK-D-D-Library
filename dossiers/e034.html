Vengeful Spirit Manipulation

A bound spirit supplies escalating partial strikes, a dangerous full manifestation, copied base techniques, and mutual love.

Field notes. Subject: a boy loved past death. His beloved refused the grave, and his cursed energy, which never learned to grieve properly, keeps building her a body out of itself. Assess this correctly: she is not his weapon. She is his army, his treasury, and his home garrison, all devoted past reason. The first of the five factors is the Moral Law: the people in complete accord with their ruler, following him regardless of their lives, undismayed by any danger. I have read that verse for decades. I had never seen it walking around until now. Three things make them Special Grade. First, devotion cannot be disarmed, there is no technique to steal, only a bond to witness. Second, her love is generous: it extends to all he loves, and learns the shapes of their power the way a devoted student learns her teacher's hand. Third, she is already dead, and therefore cannot be threatened with dying. An army with nothing left to lose is the most expensive thing on any battlefield.

Feature map

1Partial Manifestation.

Once per turn · After you make at least one attack: pay 1 CE · The spirit makes melee CE attacks vs creatures within 10 ft of you — 1 at level 1, 2 at level 3, 3 at level 6, 4 at level 10 · 1d12 slashing each on hit · Spirit untargetable during the flash.

Partial Manifestation. After his own strike, hers follows, one blow, then two, then a flurry as the bond deepens. A flash of her, there and gone, untouchable as a memory. The skilful commander does not spend his whole army on every skirmish; he sends a detachment. She is his detachment: devotion in passing. Once per turn after you make at least one attack, pay 1 CE. The spirit makes one melee CE attack at level 1, two at level 3, three at level 6, and four at level 10, each against a creature within 10 feet of you and dealing 1d12 slashing on hit. It is not targetable during this flash of manifestation.

Passive · The bound object grants the cursed-energy-enhanced weapon benefit · If destroyed: Partial and Full Manifestation unavailable until a replacement is bound (long rest).

The vow needs an anchor, a ring, a weapon, something the living can hold. Through it she reaches the world. If the anchor breaks, the channel closes until a new one is bound. Every army needs its standard; capture the standard and the army cannot march.

The binding weapon uses the source’s cursed-energy-enhanced weapon benefit. If the bound object is destroyed, Partial and Full Manifestation are unavailable until a replacement is bound during a long rest.

3Involuntary Full Manifestation.

Trigger · After one damage instance exceeds your level + CE modifier: DC 20 Wisdom save · Failure: the spirit manifests in an open space within 10 ft and acts after you · Before level 6 it is berserk (attacks the last creature to damage you or the closest other creature) · Once per turn (no action): Persuasion check to dismiss.

Involuntary Full Manifestation. Wound him past bearing and she comes unbidden, no summons, no permission, grief arriving like weather. Before the bond is trained she is berserk: love with no orders is a flood, and floods do not choose what they drown. He may plead with her to stand down. Sometimes pleading works. This is the dangerous season of every devotion: strength without discipline. Partial Manifestation now grants two spirit attacks.

After one damage instance exceeds your level + CE modifier, make a DC 20 Wisdom save. On failure the appendix spirit manifests in an open space within 10 feet and acts after you. Before level 6 it is berserk, attacking the last creature to damage you or the closest other creature. Once per turn with no action, attempt the printed Persuasion check to dismiss it.

6Controlled window / Mimicry.

From level 6: on a failed trigger the spirit obeys you for CE modifier rounds (min 1), then dismisses · Mimicry (while fully manifested): bonus action, command a claw attack vs a creature · On hit: DNA trace; CE ability check vs 10 + half the feature's base CE cost, rounded up (DC 10 if no cost) · Success: store that Innate Technique's base non-passive feature (max CE modifier stored, min 1; replace when over capacity) · Copied features usable only during full manifestation.

Partial Manifestation now grants three spirit attacks.

Controlled window. The flood learns banks. When grief takes her now, she obeys, for a counted span his orders hold, and then she rests. Discipline does not diminish love; it gives love its orders. An army that charges on command is worth ten that charge on feeling. From level 6, on a failed danger trigger the spirit obeys you for CE modifier rounds (minimum one) and then dismisses.

Mimicry. While she stands fully in the world, he may bid her taste the enemy, a claw, a trace of blood, a pattern learned. What she devours, she keeps: the shapes of other techniques, stored like scrolls in a library of appetite. Mark the doctrine: she copies only the base arts, never the pinnacles, even hunger respects what it cannot digest. And note the deeper art: the enemy's strength, converted, becomes his own. There is an old verse about using the conquered foe to augment one's strength. She is that verse, with teeth. While fully manifested, use a bonus action to command a claw against a creature. On hit, the spirit takes a DNA trace. Make a CE ability check with PB against 10 + half the copied feature’s base CE cost, rounded up; DC 10 if it has no cost. Success stores that Innate Technique’s base, non-passive feature. Domains, Maximum Techniques, entire feature progressions, CE pools, and chassis traits cannot be copied.

Store up to CE modifier copied features (minimum one), replacing one when over capacity. Use them only during full manifestation, with your action, CE, attack, DC, Output Rank, and the copied feature’s base numbers. A previously successful DNA pattern succeeds automatically if acquired again.

10Voluntary Manifestation.

Once per turn (no action) · Manifest the spirit for 1 minute (danger triggers no longer force a save) · Uses = half CE modifier rounded down (min 1) per long rest; one use returns on a short rest · Controlled spirit follows one simple spoken order; without orders it Dodges and protects you.

Partial Manifestation now grants four spirit attacks.

Voluntary Manifestation. No longer must he be broken for her to come. He calls, and she answers, for a minute, a counted number of times, resting between wars. She takes one simple order and executes it; without orders she guards him and waits. This is the mature form of every alliance: devotion that can be summoned, commanded, and, hardest of all, dismissed. From level 10, triggers no longer force a save; instead, once per turn with no action you may manifest the spirit for 1 minute. Uses equal half CE modifier rounded down (minimum one) per long rest, and one use returns on a short rest. A controlled spirit follows one simple spoken order without costing your action; without an order it Dodges and protects you.

11Stronger Bond.

Passive · Choose two spirit ability scores +2 (including maxima) · While manifested: your CE ability and maximum +2; current CE +2 on entry, −2 when it leaves (no farming) · Spirit gains temporary HP = half your current HP on arrival.

Stronger Bond. The bond deepens, and both grow mighty with it, her frame strengthened, his energy swollen by her presence. When she arrives she lends him power; when she leaves she takes back only what was lent, never more. True mutuality: neither is diminished by the other's greatness. Armies held together by love do not count the cost of reinforcing each other. Choose two spirit ability scores to increase by 2, including maxima. While manifested, your CE ability and maximum rise by 2; current CE rises by 2 on entry and falls by up to 2 when it leaves, so remanifesting cannot farm CE. The spirit gains temporary HP equal to half your current HP on arrival.

Pinnacle expression

Maximum, Domain, or equivalent.

True and Mutual Love, opened: sixty feet of enclosed devotion. Domain, True and Mutual Love: 18: full-turn action, 20 CE; 60-foot-radius enclosed Domain centered on you; 1 minute; Burnout 10 rounds. A garden of swords, planted by devotion. Field of Swords (continuous law). When the Domain opens, every technique in your Mimicry library manifests as a sword planted in the ground, one sword per stored technique. The swords are indistinguishable: you do not know which sword holds which technique until you pick it up (which sword you grab is random among the techniques currently manifested, or chosen by the GM). Once per turn as a free object interaction, you may take up a sword; when you do, you learn which stored technique it holds and may use that technique once, paying no CE, following its normal action cost, attack or save mechanics, and riders. The sword dissipates once its technique is used. At the start of your next turn the technique re-emerges in a new sword elsewhere in the Domain. No two swords ever hold the same technique at once. Sure-Hit Event (initiative 20). When the Domain opens, choose one stored copied technique as the sure-hit, its sword burns with visible light, and you always know which it is. At initiative count 20 each round, that technique strikes every hostile creature inside the Domain: an unprotected hostile suffers its full printed effect with no attack roll and no initial saving throw. A creature with an anti-domain defense instead faces the technique’s own saving throw; if the technique allows none, it makes a Dexterity saving throw against your CE save DC (invented), taking half damage and suffering no rider on a success.

Innate Technique Feats

Build-defining options.

These are optional character-building feats. You must meet each listed prerequisite before selecting it.

Prerequisite: Partial Manifestation

Partial Manifestation Versatility

Her partial hand need not only wound, it can seize, shove, unbalance. The commander who uses his detachment only for killing wastes half its worth. A hand that can grip is worth a hand that can cut. Once per turn when Partial Manifestation would add damage, forgo all of that added damage to attempt one grapple or shove against the hit target as part of the attack, using your CE ability in place of Strength if desired.

Prerequisite: Mimicry

Technique Storage

What she has learned, he may wield through his own hands, her library opened to him while she stands near, the techniques flowing through his action instead of hers. Two warriors, one arsenal. The deepest trust in war: to let another's learning become your own muscle. A technique your spirit has mimicked can be wielded through you: while the spirit is manifested, partially or fully, you may use your action to activate one stored mimicry as if you knew it, paying its CE cost and using your attack bonus and save DC. The spirit cannot activate that same mimicry on the same turn.

Prerequisite: Stronger Bond

Directed Frenzy

Even in berserk grief, she can be pointed. Name the enemy, and the flood aims itself, her fury given a single direction, her blows falling with advantage on the named. The storm does not calm. It is merely told where to rain. There is no taming love like this; there is only aiming it. While the appendix spirit is berserk, use a bonus action to name one creature you can see. Until the start of your next turn the spirit treats that creature as the last creature to damage you when choosing targets, and its attacks against the named creature have advantage. This does not end the berserk state.

Rules integration

Edge cases and shared systems.

Rules priority: the reviewed rulings, explicit codex clarifications, and the level progression always control when original wording conflicts. Legacy Domain auto-hit language, action terminology, event timing, missing saves, and older scaling never override revised player rules.
Why Tier IV: even a modest copy library adds a second durable body and access to other techniques’ defining base moves. The tier reflects that expanding rules surface, not an assumption that the player copies the perfect list.

Partial Manifestation
Beginning at 1st level, at some point in your lineage or family,
someone you cared about has died. Unable to accept this person's
death, you have unconsciously cursed them, and it became a vengeful
spirit of great power. Once per turn, you can call this spirit to
your aid in combat by spending 1 Cursed Energy, you can make an

must focus its cursed energy on an object or weapon. You will gain
the cursed energy-enhanced weapon cursed energy enhancement.

Vengeful Spirit Manifestation
At 3rd level, your life is very stressful, being a sorcerer is
filled with danger and trouble. When these times of danger come, your
Vengeful Spirit may manifest into the physical world. Whenever you
take damage above your level + your Cursed Energy modifier, you must
make a DC 20 Wisdom saving throw. On a success, you can handle
yourself in the fight and don't let your spirit get out. On a
failure, however, your Vengeful Spirit will manifest itself into the
world to protect you while being berserk. It acts on the same turn as
you do, it is charmed by you and cannot go more than 200 feet away
from you. As a free action, you may make
a DC 20 Persuasion check against it, on
a success it will stop attacking and go
back to its dormant state, on a failure
it will keep attacking until there are
no more creatures but you in the radius.
They always focus on either the last
thing to damage you, or the closest creature to you.
At 6th level, the DC for the Persuasion check is reduced to 15, and
your Vengeful Spirit will stay in the physical world for a number of
rounds equal to your Cursed Energy modifier, being under your control
during it.
At 10th level, you have gained more control over your cursed spirit
and don't have to do the Wisdom saving throw anymore. You can now
manifest your spirit as a free action for 1 minute. It will now obey
your orders on who to attack, and who not to attack. You may summon

modifier equal to your Cursed Energy modifier before needing to take

melee unarmed attack is equal to 1d8 or your own unarmed attack,
whichever is higher.
Mimicry
When you reach 6th level, you begin to learn about your innate
technique. As a bonus action, while your Vengeful Spirit is in the
world, you can command it to attack a creature. If the attack hits,
it can extract the creature's DNA with a claw attack. After that, you
can attempt to replicate it on a Charisma saving throw, on a success,
you can copy the Cursed Technique. On a failure save, you need to
command your Vengeful Spirit to hit the creature again:

When you copy a cursed technique, you must spend as much Cursed

You can have up to your Cursed Energy Modifier of these techniques

In addition, you'll automatically pass the Charisma check if you
