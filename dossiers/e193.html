Serpent Against the Dawn

Core law

Character engine

Track Night Depth 0–3. Extinguishing meaningful light, imposing blindness, or landing necrotic pressure deepens Night; radiant effects that overcome you reduce it.

Technique save DC: 8 + PB + chosen CE ability modifier. Technique attack: PB + chosen CE ability modifier.

The fiction. Scholars classify the Serpent as the oldest kind of opposition: cursed energy that exists only in argument with light. The prevailing theory holds the user's CE awakened on a night that would not end — a darkness that learned to deepen itself rather than wait for dawn — and the Night Depth track is the original night, still falling. Registry notes add the warning that such techniques are never about darkness itself; they are about refusing the morning.

Full progression

Level progression

L1Swallow the Light

As an action for 1 CE, you create a 15-foot radius of supernatural dimness centered within 60 feet for 1 round; mundane flames inside it are extinguished. At Night Depth 2 or higher, the area becomes darkness instead.

L3Coil of Night

Make a technique melee attack (2 CE) with 20-foot reach: on a hit, it deals 2 dice of necrotic damage plus your CE modifier, and the target is grappled. A grappled creature's emitted light radius is halved.

L6Solar Struggle

When you are at Night Depth 3, at initiative 20 you may spend 2 CE to unleash a 30-foot Eclipse Pulse: each creature in the pulse makes a Constitution save, taking 2 dice of necrotic damage on a failure and becoming unable to regain hit points until your next turn. Your Night Depth then resets to 1.

L11Uncreated Darkness

At Night Depth 3 you gain resistance to radiant damage, but each radiant hit still reduces your Night Depth by 1. When a radiant hit knocks you from Depth 3 to Depth 2, you may use your reaction to move up to half your speed.

L18Domain Expansion: Twelve Gates Before Sunrise

Full-turn action, 20 CE, enclosed 90-foot radius centered on you, up to 1 minute, Technique Focus. The Domain begins at Gate 1. At initiative 20 advance Gate 1 → Gate 2 → Gate 3 → Gate 1 and increase Night Depth by 1, maximum 3. At Gate 3, Eclipse Pulse resolves as the Domain’s sure-hit: each chosen hostile in the Domain automatically takes 2 Night Dice necrotic damage and cannot regain HP until the next initiative 20, then Night Depth becomes 1. A protected creature makes a Constitution save, taking half damage and avoiding the healing lock on success. Three Solar Braziers appear when the Domain opens. A creature adjacent to one may use an action and a meaningful radiant/CE effect to light it; each lit Brazier reduces the next Eclipse Pulse by 1 Night Die, and all three lit Braziers suppress the healing lock. All Braziers extinguish after the Pulse. Clash bookkeeping: 1,000 clash points (Tier V). A 100+ point advantage collapses the weaker Domain; collapse deals no damage. Barrier AC 12 + PB; barrier HP = 10 × max(1, your CE modifier) + 2,000. Technique Burnout: 3 rounds.

Feats

Build-defining options.

Light-Eater

When Swallow the Light extinguishes a light source, you may use your bonus action to weaponize the eaten light: one creature within 30 feet that you can see must succeed on a Constitution save against your CE DC or be blinded until the end of your next turn.

Endless Coil

At Depth 3, your Coil of Night can strike through darkness itself: you may originate the attack from any dimness or darkness you created within 60 feet instead of from yourself.

Enemy of Sunrise

When a radiant effect reduces your Night Depth, you may use your reaction to lash back at the light-bringer: it must succeed on a Constitution save against your CE DC or take 2 Night Dice necrotic damage.

Chaos Before Creation (11+)

The first time each long rest your Night Depth would fall to 0, you may unleash its dregs instead: every light source within 30 feet is extinguished, and each creature of your choice within 15 feet must succeed on a Constitution save against your CE DC or be blinded until the end of your next turn. Your Depth becomes 0 afterward.

Rules integration

Domain procedure

This entry uses the repaired campaign Domain rules. The level-18 feature above is complete: its printed action, CE cost, radius, duration, initiative-20 law, fallback protection, and counterplay replace any legacy blanket auto-hit wording.
Rules integration

Counterplay

Radiant effects and protected light actively reduce Night Depth. In the Domain, Solar Braziers let enemies weaken the guaranteed cycle.
