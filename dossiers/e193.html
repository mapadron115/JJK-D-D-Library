Serpent Against the Dawn

Apep / Apophis inspiration. Track Night Depth 0–3. Extinguishing meaningful light, imposing blindness, or landing necrotic pressure deepens Night; radiant effects that overcome you reduce it.

Combat roleDarkness controller / cyclical boss
Power tierTier V · Calamity
Core law

Character engine

Track Night Depth 0–3. Extinguishing meaningful light, imposing blindness, or landing necrotic pressure deepens Night: gain 1 Night Depth the first time each round you extinguish a meaningful light source, impose the blinded condition, or deal necrotic damage to a hostile (invented); maximum 3. Radiant effects that overcome you reduce it.

Technique save DC: 8 + PB + chosen CE ability modifier. Technique attack: PB + chosen CE ability modifier.

Field notes. Subject: cursed energy that exists only in argument with light. Three truths govern the Serpent. First: the darkness is not the point. Extinguishing light, imposing blindness, landing necrotic pressure, each deepens Night Depth, but the technique is never about darkness itself; it is about refusing the morning. Second: the night is a cycle, not a condition. At Depth 3 the Eclipse Pulse spends the accumulated dark in one convulsion of wounds-that-do-not-heal, and the night falls back to Depth 1 to begin again, the serpent's siege is fought in tides. Third: light is the enemy's supply line. Radiant that overcomes the bearer reduces the Depth; at Depth 3 the serpent even wears resistance to it, retreating half a step to strike again. The Domain stages the myth: three Gates before sunrise, braziers the enemy must light, or the Pulse falls as sure-hit. I have watched dawn-breakers learn what the myth always knew: the night does not fight the sun. It outlasts the argument.

Full progression

Level progression

1Swallow the Light

The first refusal. One CE buys a fifteen-foot radius of supernatural dimness, mundane flames inside it die, and at Depth 2 the dimness becomes true darkness. Mark this: the Serpent does not begin by striking the enemy. It begins by striking the enemy's visibility. As an action for 1 CE, you create a 15-foot radius of supernatural dimness centered within 60 feet for 1 round; mundane flames inside it are extinguished. At Night Depth 2 or higher, the area becomes darkness instead.

3Coil of Night

The dark reaches out and takes hold. Twenty feet of reach, necrotic damage, the grapple, and the grappled creature's own light radius halves, as if the night were drinking it. This is the technique's close argument: come into the dark, and the dark keeps you. Make a technique melee attack (2 CE) with 20-foot reach: on a hit, it deals 2d8 of necrotic damage (invented: a Night Die is a d8) plus your CE modifier, and the target is grappled. The target may use its action to escape, DC = your CE save DC (invented). A grappled creature's emitted light radius is halved.

6Solar Struggle

The night spends itself all at once. At Depth 3, at initiative 20, the Eclipse Pulse: necrotic convulsion and wounds that refuse to heal until the next turn, then the Depth collapses to 1 and the cycle begins again. Consider: the Serpent's greatest blow is also its reset. Every war has this season. When you are at Night Depth 3, at initiative 20 you may spend 2 CE to unleash a 30-foot Eclipse Pulse: each creature in the pulse makes a Constitution save, taking 2 dice of necrotic damage on a failure and becoming unable to regain hit points until your next turn. Your Night Depth then resets to 1.

11Uncreated Darkness

At full Depth the night hardens against its opposite: resistance to radiant, but each radiant hit still costs one Depth, and the knock from 3 to 2 buys a reaction's half-move retreat. The Serpent knows it cannot stop the light forever. It merely refuses to stop first. At Night Depth 3 you gain resistance to radiant damage, but each radiant hit still reduces your Night Depth by 1. When a radiant hit knocks you from Depth 3 to Depth 2, you may use your reaction to move up to half your speed.

18Domain Expansion: Twelve Gates Before Sunrise

Twelve gates ring the field in a dwindling spiral, each taller and darker than the last, their lintels carved with a serpent swallowing its own length. The sky above is the color of a held breath, dawn arrested at the exact moment before breaking, and a thin ring of eclipse-fire burns where the sun should be. Between the gates the ground is ash-white, and every footprint fills slowly with shadow.

The myth, staged. Gates advance at initiative 20, each advancing the Depth; at Gate 3 the Eclipse Pulse falls as sure-hit on the chosen, healing locked until the next event. Three Solar Braziers offer the enemy its counter-ritual: light them, weaken the Pulse, or light all three and suppress the dawn-denial entirely. The Domain is a wager, and the night is betting the enemy cannot keep three candles lit. Full-turn action, 20 CE, enclosed 90-foot radius centered on you, up to 1 minute, Technique Focus. The Domain begins at Gate 1. At initiative 20 advance Gate 1 → Gate 2 → Gate 3 → Gate 1 and increase Night Depth by 1, maximum 3. At Gate 3, Eclipse Pulse resolves as the Domain’s sure-hit: each chosen hostile in the Domain automatically takes 2d8 necrotic damage (invented) and cannot regain HP until the next initiative 20, then Night Depth becomes 1. A protected creature makes a Constitution save, taking half damage and avoiding the healing lock on success. Three Solar Braziers appear when the Domain opens. A creature adjacent to one may use an action and a meaningful radiant/CE effect to light it; each lit Brazier reduces the next Eclipse Pulse by 1 Night Die, and all three lit Braziers suppress the healing lock. All Braziers extinguish after the Pulse. Clash bookkeeping: 1,000 clash points (Tier V). A 100+ point advantage collapses the weaker Domain; collapse deals no damage. Barrier AC 12 + PB; barrier HP = 10 × max(1, your CE modifier) + 2,000. Technique Burnout: 3 rounds.

Feats

Build-defining options.

Light-Eater

The eaten light is weaponized. When Swallow the Light kills a source, the bonus action turns the corpse of it against one creature within thirty feet, blinded until the end of its next turn. The Serpent wastes nothing; even the dark's leavings are spent. When Swallow the Light extinguishes a light source, you may use your bonus action to weaponize the eaten light: one creature within 30 feet that you can see must succeed on a Constitution save against your CE DC or be blinded until the end of your next turn.

Endless Coil

At Depth 3 the coil strikes from anywhere the dark has already won. Originate the attack from any dimness or darkness you created within sixty feet, the serpent's body is wherever the night is. Distance, to this technique, is a rumor. At Depth 3, your Coil of Night can strike through darkness itself: you may originate the attack from any dimness or darkness you created within 60 feet instead of from yourself.

Enemy of Sunrise

The light-bringer is answered. When radiant reduces your Depth, the reaction lashes back, two Night Dice of necrotic on a failed Constitution save. Mark this: the Serpent does not merely endure the sun's allies. It bills them. When a radiant effect reduces your Night Depth, you may use your reaction to lash back at the light-bringer: it must succeed on a Constitution save against your CE DC or take 2 Night Dice necrotic damage.

Chaos Before Creation (11+)

The night's dregs, spent instead of surrendered. The first time each long rest your Depth would fall to 0, extinguish every light within thirty feet and blind the chosen within fifteen. This is the oldest lesson the myth carries: even the defeated night gets one last refusal. The first time each long rest your Night Depth would fall to 0, you may unleash its dregs instead: every light source within 30 feet is extinguished, and each creature of your choice within 15 feet must succeed on a Constitution save against your CE DC or be blinded until the end of your next turn. Your Depth becomes 0 afterward.
