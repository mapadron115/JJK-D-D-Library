Swallow the LightAction, 1 CE: 15-ft radius within 60 ft becomes supernatural dimness for a round; mundane flames extinguish; at Depth 2+ it becomes darkness.
Coil of NightTechnique melee attack, 2 CE, 20-ft reach: 2 dice necrotic + CE mod and grapple; grappled target's emitted light radius is halved.
Solar StruggleInitiative 20 at Depth 3: spend 2 CE for 30-ft Eclipse Pulse, Con save, 2 dice necrotic + healing lock until next turn; Depth resets to 1.
Uncreated DarknessAt Depth 3 gain radiant resistance, but each radiant hit still reduces Depth by 1; when knocked 3→2, reaction move half speed.
Domain Expansion: Twelve Gates Before SunriseFull-turn action, 20 CE, enclosed 90-foot radius centered on you, up to 1 minute, Technique Focus. The Domain begins at Gate 1. At initiative 20 advance Gate 1 → Gate 2 → Gate 3 → Gate 1 and increase Night Depth by 1, maximum 3. At Gate 3, Eclipse Pulse resolves as the Domain’s sure-hit: each chosen hostile in the Domain automatically takes 2 Night Dice necrotic damage and cannot regain HP until the next initiative 20, then Night Depth becomes 1. A protected creature makes a Constitution save, taking half damage and avoiding the healing lock on success. Three Solar Braziers appear when the Domain opens. A creature adjacent to one may use an action and a meaningful radiant/CE effect to light it; each lit Brazier reduces the next Eclipse Pulse by 1 Night Die, and all three lit Braziers suppress the healing lock. All Braziers extinguish after the Pulse.

Domain procedure

This entry uses the repaired campaign Domain rules. The level-18 feature above is complete: its printed action, CE cost, radius, duration, initiative-20 law, fallback protection, and counterplay replace any legacy blanket auto-hit wording.

Character engine

Track Night Depth 0–3. Extinguishing meaningful light, imposing blindness, or landing necrotic pressure deepens Night; radiant effects that overcome you reduce it.

Technique save DC: 8 + PB + chosen CE ability modifier. Technique attack: PB + chosen CE ability modifier.

Counterplay

Radiant effects and protected light actively reduce Night Depth. In the Domain, Solar Braziers let enemies weaken the guaranteed cycle.
