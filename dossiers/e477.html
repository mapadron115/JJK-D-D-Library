Rumelian Blade

Features & procedures

Properties & techniques

Exact player-facing procedures

Cursed edge

You gain a +1 bonus to attack and damage rolls with this weapon.

Reaction

Catch the blade

As a reaction to a melee weapon attack made against you, you can make an attack roll with the Rumelian Blade. If your roll is equal to or higher than the attacker's attack roll, you catch the weapon in the sword's channel and block the attack entirely — it misses, dealing no damage and applying no on-hit effects.
