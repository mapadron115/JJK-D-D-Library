Cursed Tool

Rumelian Blade

A Grade 3 cursed shortsword of the Rumelian people, its channel was made to catch blades.

FormWeapon (Shortsword)
GradeGrade 3
AttunementNot required

Field notes. Subject: a blade shaped to receive another. Three truths govern it. First, the fuller is a channel: the sword was made to catch an incoming blade, defense built into the steel rather than bolted onto the arm. Second, the contest is honest: as a reaction to a melee weapon attack, meet the attacker’s roll with your own, equal or higher, and the attack is blocked entirely, dealing no damage and no on-hit effects. Third, the edge is modest: +1 to strike and wound, the catch is the whole philosophy; the edge is merely the price of admission. Mark this: the perfect parry is silence. No clang, no trade, only a blade that went somewhere and came back with nothing.

Exact player-facing procedures

Cursed edge

Passive · No CE · +1 bonus to attack and damage rolls made with this weapon.

A modest edge: +1 to attack and damage rolls. The blade’s philosophy is not in its sharpness. You gain a +1 bonus to attack and damage rolls with this weapon.

Reaction

Catch the blade

Reaction · No CE · Against a melee weapon attack: make an attack roll; if equal to or higher than the attacker's roll, the attack is blocked entirely (no damage, no on-hit effects).

The fuller was made for this moment. As a reaction to a melee weapon attack against you, meet the attacker’s roll with your own, equal or higher, and the weapon is caught in the channel, the attack blocked entirely: no damage, no on-hit effects. As a reaction to a melee weapon attack made against you, you can make an attack roll with the Rumelian Blade. If your roll is equal to or higher than the attacker's attack roll, you catch the weapon in the sword's channel and block the attack entirely, it misses, dealing no damage and applying no on-hit effects.
