Red Thread of the Unmet

A 30-ft red cord visible to cursed sight. Bonus action attach one end to a willing creature or unattended object within reach; second end stays on you.

FormCursed cord Thirty feet of red cord, visible to cursed sight — a bond that outlived its maker.
RoleMobility / pairing It grants not strength, but a second place to be — one partner, one cord, one standing agreement.
EngineThread The cord between two endpoints: shortened by will, severed only by force — and force is answered.
Attunement1 hour* An hour's ritual — the thread must choose you back.
Features & procedures

Properties & techniques

Exact player-facing procedures

Feature

While both ends are near, the cord keeps its promise of company: each endpoint always knows the other's direction and distance. No one tied to this thread is ever truly out of reach. While endpoints are within 30 ft, each knows the other's direction and distance.

Tug

A gentle pull — one CE, a bonus action: either willing endpoint moves ten feet toward the other, unhindered. The thread shortens the world between you. bonus, 1 CE: either willing endpoint moves 10 ft toward the other without opportunity attacks.

Severance Pain

Break the cord by force and it answers: the breaker suffers a die of psychic pain. The thread reforms after a short rest — bonds this stubborn do not stay severed. if hostile force breaks the cord, breaker takes 1 Tool Die psychic; cord reforms after short rest.

Crossed Fate

The rescue itself: when your partner fails a Dexterity save, spend two CE as a reaction and trade places — then let them roll again. You take the blast's seat so they need not. reaction, 2 CE when partner fails Dex save: swap positions if both fit, then partner rerolls save.

Field notes. Subject: a refusal to stand alone. Three truths govern it. First, the Thread grants nothing but company: no damage, no armor — only a second place to be, and the certainty of the other's direction. Second, it is woven from a failure: cursed energy that refused to dissipate when its technique could not save someone, knotting itself into a cord that still reaches for a partner. Third, the rescue is literal and costly: trade places into the blast, and your partner rolls the save again — the thread's whole fiction being that no one should have to stand alone where someone once did. Consider: a tool that has never once protected anyone, only placed someone else beside them. That is protection enough.

Rules integration

Cursed-tool attunement

Campaign canon

Cursed-tool attunement: you have PB attunement slots (2–6). This Grade 2 tool functions as a mundane tool while unattuned, or with its full properties for one slot and a one-hour ritual at GM discretion. Attunement ends voluntarily over a short rest, on death, or when you replace it while over capacity. Distance does not break attunement. Cursed-tool attunement is separate from magic-item attunement.
Balance & counterplay. Only one partner; range and physical severing keep the rescue utility honest.
