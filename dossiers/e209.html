Features & procedures

Properties & techniques

Exact player-facing procedures

Feature

While endpoints are within 30 ft, each knows the other's direction and distance.

Tug

bonus, 1 CE: either willing endpoint moves 10 ft toward the other without opportunity attacks.

Severance Pain

if hostile force breaks the cord, breaker takes 1 Tool Die psychic; cord reforms after short rest.

Crossed Fate

reaction, 2 CE when partner fails Dex save: swap positions if both fit, then partner rerolls save.

The fiction. Registry notes describe the Red Thread as a bond that outlived its maker — woven from cursed energy that refused to dissipate after the technique it served failed to save someone, knotting itself into a cord that still reaches for a partner. It does not grant its bearer strength; it grants them a second place to be, because the thread's whole fiction is that no one should have to stand alone where someone once did.

Rules integration

Cursed-tool attunement

Campaign canon

Cursed-tool attunement: you have PB attunement slots (2–6). This Grade 2 tool functions as a mundane tool while unattuned, or with its full properties for one slot and a one-hour ritual at GM discretion. Attunement ends voluntarily over a short rest, on death, or when you replace it while over capacity. Distance does not break attunement. Cursed-tool attunement is separate from magic-item attunement.
Balance & counterplay. Only one partner; range and physical severing keep the rescue utility honest.
