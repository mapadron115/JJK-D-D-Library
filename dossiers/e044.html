Original Codex

War God's Household

Give a willing wandering soul a name and it joins a covenant—not as inventory, but as family. Each Regalia can become a weapon, garment, guide, shield, scale, or sacred beast. One sorcerer carries an armory; a worthy commander eventually marches with every name at once. Complete Tier IV War God's Household innate technique guide for JJK 5e, inspired by Bishamon's regalia: named spirit companions, eight vessel forms, Blessed Vessels, Heaven's Grand Muster, four technique feats, and a unique Domain sure-hit event.

Core law

Core covenant A person first. A weapon second.

Regalia are named dead souls who voluntarily accept the sorcerer's protection and become divine cursed vessels. They remember personality and agency, but their combat contribution is expressed through one vessel form so the technique never creates a dozen extra turns.

Tier IV comes from breadth, not compulsory optimization.

An unlimited narrative household, up to ten simultaneous vessel forms, repeatable formation attacks, and a Blessed Vessel are Special Grade tools. The technique is not Tier V because every result still uses ordinary attacks, saves, CE costs, breakable companions, and finite action economy. A character with modest statistics remains fully valid; the inheritance measures potential, not a demanded build.

Level 1 · 10-minute rite · 3 CE

Bestow a Name

You may offer a covenant to a willing, unclaimed soul you can perceive. The soul understands the permanent nature of the offer before choosing. On acceptance, choose its name, Vow, and one vessel form. It becomes one of your Regalia and can communicate telepathically with you while on the same plane.

You begin play with four Regalia created with the GM. There is no maximum number of named Regalia in your household. Story access to willing souls—not a level gate—determines how quickly it grows.

Consent and campaign safety

Naming is not soul theft

The rite fails against an unwilling soul, a living creature, a soul already bound by another effect, or a character whose player has not agreed. Naming never overrides resurrection, possession, or soul-destruction rules. A Regalia may be released during a 10-minute rite, after which it becomes an ordinary wandering soul and keeps its memories.

Bonus action · 1 CE

Battle Muster

Call, dismiss, or exchange any number of your Regalia, ending with no more vessel forms active than your Active Limit. A called form appears worn, held, or in an adjacent legal space. Forms remain active until dismissed, Broken, you fall unconscious, or a specific nullification effect ends them; Battle Muster does not require Technique Focus.

If the vessel is already active before initiative, you do not pay again merely because combat began.

Regalia weapons

Divine Instrument

You are proficient with every Regalia weapon. For its attacks and damage, use Strength or Dexterity normally, or use your CE ability. A Regalia weapon counts as a cursed weapon and deals one Household Die plus the chosen ability modifier unless its form says otherwise.

CE attack = PB + CE modifier
CE save DC = 8 + PB + CE modifier

Once on each of your turns · 1 CE

Rend

Immediately after a Regalia weapon attack hits, add PB Household Dice of the weapon's damage type. These dice belong to that attack, double on a critical hit, and can be part of a Black Flash. At level 18, you may use Rend twice on each of your turns, never twice on the same hit.

One body, many names

No extra initiative

A Regalia in vessel form has no turn, actions, reactions, or separate position unless its form expressly creates one. In human form it can speak and roleplay, but it supplies no combat action economy and normally withdraws from danger.

Same-name effects

Duplicates do not multiply passives

Your household may contain two sidearms, shields, or other repeated forms. A passive benefit with the same name applies only once. Separate weapons remain separate objects, but no creature gains duplicate AC, Focus advantage, or another repeated passive.

Choose a Vow:

each Regalia begins with a plain-language purpose such as Guard, Guide, Hunt, Shelter, Witness, or Mercy. A Vow creates characterization and tells the table what the spirit will not casually do; it is not a hidden optimization tax or a daily resource.

Full progression

The household learns to march.

The number of souls you know is narrative and uncapped. Active Limit controls simultaneous mechanical benefits; level—not ability-score optimization—sets that limit and the Household Die.

1The first four names

Build the family, select three active forms, and let a weapon hit carry Rend.

Starting household

Create four Regalia with names, appearances, Vows, personalities, and vessel forms. They may have known one another in life or meet through you. None must copy a canon character.

Portable armory

Battle Muster allows three active passives, weapons, or mounts. You still have only your own actions and hands; being able to call three weapons does not let you wield all three at once without a feature that says so.

3Concordance & Borderline

Different Regalia begin finishing one another's techniques.

Once per turn · +1 CE

Concordance

Immediately after you hit with a Regalia weapon or resolve a Regalia Art, choose a different active Regalia and apply its Concord effect. Concord belongs to the triggering activation, requires no extra action, and cannot trigger another Concord. You may use only one copy of a named Concord on the same activation.

Action · 2 CE

Borderline

Draw a 30-foot line between two points you can see within 60 feet. It lasts until the start of your next turn. The first hostile creature to touch or cross it makes a Strength save. On failure it takes 2 × PB Household Dice force damage, returns to the last legal space on its original side, and its speed becomes 0 for the turn. On success it takes half and crosses normally.

At level 11, the line may be 60 feet and can last up to 1 minute with Technique Focus, triggering against the first hostile creature on each turn.

6The Guidepost

One trusted name coordinates the household, and the lion takes to the sky.

Long-rest appointment

Guidepost

Choose one willing Regalia as your Guidepost. While it is active, add your CE modifier (minimum +1) to initiative and you cannot be surprised while conscious. As a bonus action for 1 CE, designate a creature you can see as your quarry for 1 hour with Technique Focus; while on the same plane, you know its direction and distance, and your Regalia attacks against it ignore half and three-quarters cover.

Coordination

First one is free

The first Concordance you use in each round costs 0 CE. Any later Concordance that round keeps its printed cost and once-per-turn limit.

Quick Muster

One name at a thought

Once on each of your turns, call, dismiss, or exchange one Regalia without an action or CE. Changing any additional forms still uses Battle Muster.

Sacred flight

Sky Lion ascendant

An active Sky Lion gains a 60-foot flying speed. It still uses your movement and carries only the creatures its form permits.

11Blessed Vessel

Devotion transforms one companion; the armory can now attack as a formation.

Story-earned evolution

Hafuri Vessel

One Regalia that has freely risked its name or existence to protect the household becomes Blessed. This requires a breakthrough event agreed with the GM, not merely reaching level 11; once the event occurs, the feature activates permanently. Its Integrity doubles, it gains its form's Blessed benefit, and it may supply its Art and its Concord in the same activation.

Reaction · once per long rest

Sacrificial Bloom

When you would be reduced to 0 HP while your Blessed Vessel is active, it may become Broken to leave you at 1 HP instead. You gain PB Household Dice temporary HP. The intercepted damage is still considered dealt for effects triggered by taking damage, but excess damage does not carry through.

Action · 5 CE

Sevenfold Muster

Choose up to PB + 1 different active Regalia and one or more targets within 120 feet. Make one CE attack for each chosen Regalia; on a hit, that strike deals one Household Die of a type appropriate to its form. Add your CE modifier to the damage of only one hit. Each attack is separate and can critically hit or Black Flash. At level 18 this becomes Tenfold Muster and may use up to ten different active Regalia.

18Sovereign Household

Ten forms answer instantly, a second name may be Blessed, and the citadel opens.

Ten active forms

Your Active Limit becomes 10. At the start of each of your turns, you may set your entire active loadout without action or CE; every called form must appear in a legal place.

Second Blessed Vessel

A second Regalia may become Blessed after its own breakthrough event. Each companion retains its own form benefit and Sacrificial Bloom use.

Two Rends

You may use Rend twice on each of your turns, provided the uses enhance different hits. Every use still costs 1 CE.

Domain Expansion

Unlock Golden Citadel of Ten Thousand Names. The Domain must use the campaign's complete opening procedure and creates one autonomous initiative-count-20 sure-hit event.

Features & procedures

Every name has a shape.

Custom appearances

Form is function, not a costume lock

A Greatblade may be a spear, axe, odachi, or impossible ritual weapon; Twin Sidearms may be bows, hand cannons, or thrown talismans. Keep the listed reach, range, hands, damage, and Art. Cosmetic changes never cost a feat.

Changing a form

A covenant can grow

When you gain a level, a willing Regalia may permanently change its form after a conversation or story event. A Blessed Vessel changes through its own rules. The GM may also approve a change when the old form no longer fits the companion.

Pinnacle expression

Level 11 · Heaven's Grand Muster.

Every unbroken name answers once. Weapons, garments, claws, scales, and borders overlap into a single procession before settling into the loadout you choose.

Action · 8 CE · 120 feet Maximum: Heaven's Grand Muster

Choose a number of your unbroken Regalia up to your Active Limit, whether active or stored, and assign each to a creature or object within 120 feet. Make one CE attack for each chosen Regalia. On a hit, it deals two Household Dice of an appropriate slashing, piercing, bludgeoning, force, or radiant type. Add your CE modifier to one hit in the entire activation.

Resolve every attack separately. A single target may receive any number of the attacks. After the final attack, choose which participating Regalia remain active, up to your Active Limit; all others return to storage. This activation does not use their individual Arts or Concord effects.

Critical sequence Each name gets its own roll

Each Grand Muster strike is a Regalia weapon attack and may critically hit. A qualifying critical receives its own separate unmodified Black Flash roll. One Black Flash does not multiply the other strikes.

No false drawback The family does not disappear afterward

Grand Muster does not Break, dismiss, exhaust, or lock the household after use. Its 8 CE cost and action are the complete price. It may be used again if you still possess the action and CE.

Pinnacle expression

Level 18 · Golden Citadel of Ten Thousand Names.

A vast violet-and-gold fortress closes around the battlefield. Every hall bears a household name; every wall remembers whom that name swore to protect.

Opening The household takes the walls

Use the campaign's full-turn Domain opening, clash, barrier, CE, duration, and Technique Focus rules. When the barrier closes, every stored, unbroken Regalia appears as a spectral witness throughout the citadel. This does not grant those spirits independent turns.

Domain environment Every form is on call

Active Limit is ignored while the Domain lasts. You gain every distinct vessel passive once and may use any Regalia Art, but still need the action, reaction, hands, CE, and other requirements printed for it. Your ordinary attacks, Arts, Muster attacks, and Maximum continue to roll normally.

Family refuge The citadel recognizes its guests

At the end of each chosen ally's turn, if it is inside the barrier and within 60 feet of you, it gains one Household Die temporary HP and may move 10 feet toward a door, wall, or nonhostile creature without provoking. This is terrain support, not the sure-hit.

Witness record The fortress remembers harm

A hostile creature becomes Accused until the next initiative count 20 if it damages you, an ally, or a Regalia after the barrier closes. Accused is a Domain tag, not a condition; it has no effect outside Testimony of Every Name.

Initiative 20 · Testimony of Every Name · sure-hit The household passes judgment by itself

At initiative count 20 after clash checks, the spectral regalia speak as one — Testimony of Every Name. Each chosen hostile hears its true name spoken by the household, and the name itself weighs on them: each automatically takes PB Household Dice + CE modifier radiant or force damage. An Accused creature instead automatically takes 2 × PB Household Dice + CE modifier, is pulled up to 30 feet toward the center of the citadel, and is Restrained by spectral instruments until initiative count 20 of the next round.

A creature protected from the sure-hit makes a Charisma save. On failure, resolve the applicable full result. On success, it takes half damage, is not moved, and is not Restrained. Clear Accused from every creature after the event resolves.

One autonomous event The player does not aim the certainty

Testimony is the Domain's sole sure-hit and occurs once at initiative 20. It uses one damage roll for all creatures receiving the same version of the event, cannot critically hit or Black Flash, and does not count as a weapon attack, Art, Rend, or Muster.

Anti-domain protection Suppress judgment, not the fortress

Simple Domain, Hollow Wicker Basket, Falling Blossom Emotion, or a successful clash supplies the Charisma save; it does not dismiss Regalia, remove the citadel's refuge, erase Accused, or cause the user's ordinary rolled attacks to miss automatically.

Innate Technique Feats

Choose how the household grows.

These are optional feats, not automatic level features. Four is the complete list and hard maximum for this technique; each occupies a normal feat choice and may be taken only once.

Prerequisite: War God's Household

Many Names, One Banner

Your names answer from afar. When you use Battle Muster, you may call one Regalia into vessel form at a point you can see within 60 feet instead of beside you — a Sky Lion where your allies need it, a shield already in a friend's hands. It still counts against your Active Limit.

Prerequisite: Borderline

Line That Casts Out Evil

Your Borderline may be up to 120 feet long, may bend once at a point you can see, and ignores chosen nonhostile creatures. A hostile creature that fails its save is also Restrained until the end of its turn. If it succeeds, it crosses and cannot be affected by that Borderline again that turn.

Prerequisite: Blessed Vessel, level 12

Blessed Beyond Death

A blessed vessel's devotion extends to its kin. When a non-Blessed Regalia would become Broken, your Blessed Vessel may use its reaction to suffer the breaking in its place: the Blessed Vessel becomes Broken instead, and the other Regalia remains at 1 Integrity.

Prerequisite: Sky Lion vessel, level 6

Cavalry of Heaven

Every Sky Lion can carry you plus PB willing Medium or smaller creatures. Once on each of your turns after the Lion moves at least 30 feet, one rider may use its reaction to make one weapon attack or take the Disengage action. A creature can benefit from Cavalry of Heaven only once per round.

Rules integration

The name settles the argument.

Use these rules for breaking vessels, discord, Black Flash, equipment, targeting, Maximum Output, voice loss, and multiclass action economy.

Integrity Breakable, not disposable

Regalia AC = 12 + PB + CE modifier
Integrity = 10 + (5 × character level)

A manifested Regalia uses your saving throws. Unless a rule can target a worn or carried object, it targets you—not the Regalia. Area damage does not separately damage a vessel unless it expressly damages objects.

Broken The owner feels the name vanish

At 0 Integrity the Regalia becomes Broken, is dismissed, and you take PB Household Dice psychic damage. It returns at full Integrity after a long rest, or after a 10-minute repair costing 3 CE. Only an effect expressly able to destroy a soul can make this loss permanent.

Healing Repair is not ordinary HP healing

During a short rest, a Regalia may spend your Hit Dice as if it were you to restore Integrity. RCT restores Integrity only if its feature can heal a soul, cursed object, shikigami, or construct; ordinary medicine does not.

Discord Stain is one state, not a meter

A Regalia can refuse a direct order that unmistakably violates its Vow. You may overrule that refusal by paying 2 CE; the ordered Art or form functions, then that Regalia becomes Stained. A Stained Regalia cannot use its Art, Concord, Blessed benefit, or Sacrificial Bloom. At the start of each of your turns, each active Stained Regalia deals one Household Die psychic damage to you.

Ablution Repair the relationship

A Stain caused by household discord clears after a short rest spent in genuine reconciliation; no ability check dictates the roleplay. A Stain imposed by a hostile curse instead requires Remove Curse, suitable RCT, or a 10-minute Ablution by you and two willing unstained Regalia: spend 3 CE and make a CE ability check against the hostile effect's save DC.

Criticals and Black Flash Every rolled instrument can spark

Ordinary Regalia weapon attacks, Pounce, Crossfire, Sevenfold/Tenfold Muster, and Grand Muster may critically hit. After each qualifying critical, roll a separate unmodified d20; a natural 20 produces Black Flash and triples the entire completed critical damage of that attack. There is no frequency cap.

Automatic and save effects No attack roll means no critical

Rending Arc, Borderline, passive Crossfire Concord damage, collision, damage reduction, and Testimony of Every Name cannot critically hit or trigger Black Flash. Rend doubles with the critical because it is added to the qualifying weapon hit before damage is completed.

Hands and equipment Manifesting is not wielding

Calling a weapon does not create another hand. Greatblade needs two hands, Bulwark needs one, Twin Sidearms need two to fire both through Crossfire, and a Guide Charm or Battle Garb occupies no hand. The Garb and Bulwark AC bonuses stack because they are differently named sources.

Voice A name is normally spoken

Battle Muster normally includes an audible vessel name. If you cannot speak, you may call through the established telepathic covenant by paying 1 additional CE. Dismissing a form and using an already active form never require speech.

Movement The Lion is part of your turn

Sky Lion movement replaces your movement rather than creating a second pool. Mounting or dismounting uses half your speed unless a feature moves a rider. If it is Broken in the air, every unsupported rider immediately falls.

Maximum Output Enhance one printed activation

Maximum Output may enhance Rend, a Regalia Art, Borderline, Sevenfold/Tenfold Muster, or Battle Muster by the campaign's universal modes and doubled total cost. For a multiattack activation, Accuracy affects every attack; Damage adds half the activation's original dice, rounded up, distributed among hits. Heaven's Grand Muster and Domain Expansion are ineligible.

Multiclassing Extra Attack remains useful

Regalia weapons work with Attack actions, Extra Attack, reactions, feats, and class features that accept their listed weapon properties. Rending Arc, Crossfire, and Pounce replace exactly one attack. Sevenfold Muster, Grand Muster, and Domain opening are separate actions and do not inherit extra attacks.

No Reversal Rare means rare

War God's Household has no Cursed Technique Reversal. RCT may heal the user or repair a compatible Regalia, but it does not automatically name living people, resurrect the dead, invert a Vow, or create malicious anti-Regalia.

Storage Stored names are not active buffs

A stored Regalia can communicate and roleplay but grants no passive, weapon, Art, Concord, target, reaction, scouting body, or extra carrying capacity. It must be called into a legal vessel form before supplying mechanics.

Concord timing Finish the first effect first

Resolve the triggering hit or Art, including its saves and movement, then pay and apply one Concord. A Concord cannot make an illegal original target legal retroactively and cannot trigger itself, Rend, another Concord, or a new Art unless it expressly calls for an attack.

Source inspiration

The family is the source of the power.

  • The adaptation keeps the recognizable pillars—dead souls becoming varied instruments, a vast household, guide and lion roles, Blessed evolution, and dangerous blight—while translating them into one playable JJK 5e technique.
  • Official publisher Noragami: Stray God

    Adachitoka's completed manga is published in English by Kodansha. Browse the official series catalog.

  • Lore reference Many forms, one household

    Bishamon's recorded Shinki include a tracking earring, lion, battle attire, handguns, knife, great sword, whip, scales, and other instruments. See the Bishamonten reference.

  • Lore reference Vessels and Blessed Vessels

    Shinki are dead human spirits granted names and vessel forms; a rare Blessed Vessel transforms after risking its name or life for its god. See the Shinki reference.

  • Adaptation boundary No canon character is required

    The technique grants a household covenant, not divine immortality, worshippers, fortune-god status, reincarnation, Bishamon's personality, Kazuma's identity, or the exact canon clan. Every Regalia should be an original campaign character.
