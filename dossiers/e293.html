The Thing

Sedimented cursed energy / living stone / momentum / protection

Power tierTier IV · Special Grade
Core law

What the technique actually does.

Core Engine — Bedrock. Your cursed energy does not flow — it settles. Where other sorcerers circulate CE through living flesh, yours sediments, layer upon layer, until flesh gives way to living cursed stone: a reinforcement the body performed on itself and never undid. Reduce all bludgeoning, piercing, and slashing damage you take by your proficiency bonus (minimum 1); the rock chips, it never cracks. You have advantage on Strength saving throws and ability checks made to resist being moved, shoved, or knocked prone, and any effect that would move you against your will fails outright if its source is smaller than Huge.

The fiction. Jujutsu scholars classify Bedrock as a reinforcement that overshot — the user's CE was too dense to circulate, so in a moment of absolute resolve ('I won't move'), it answered by rebuilding the body in stone. The rocky hide is not armor worn over flesh; it is what the sorcerer became. Nothing here is inherited or bestowed — it is one person's cursed energy, refusing to stay liquid.

Full progression

Level 1 through 20.

1Bedrock Body

Bedrock Body. The Core Engine above is always on — no activation, no meter: your cursed energy has already finished settling. Your unarmed strikes deal 1d8 bludgeoning damage, and you count as Large for carrying, lifting, pushing, dragging, and breaking objects.

3Ripped-Up Arsenal

As an action, spend 2 CE: channel a pulse of dense CE through your grip and tear a lamppost, girder, or paving slab from the terrain as an improvised weapon — 10-ft reach, 2d8 + Strength modifier bludgeoning damage, lasting 1 minute. Alternatively, hurl a chunk of rubble as a ranged attack (60 ft), dealing 2d8 + Strength modifier bludgeoning damage on a hit.

6Bull Rush

When you take the Dash action, your sedimented CE carries your momentum — you may move through hostile creatures' spaces. Each creature whose space you enter makes a Strength saving throw: on a failure it takes 2d8 + your Strength modifier bludgeoning damage and falls prone; on a success it takes half damage and is not knocked prone.

9Stonewall

When an ally within 10 ft of you is hit by an attack, you may use your reaction to interpose your stone body: you become the attack's target instead, moving up to 10 ft as part of the reaction if needed. Your Bedrock reduction applies to the damage.

11Groundbreak Stomp

As an action, spend 4 CE: discharge a pulse of dense cursed energy into the ground — a 20-ft radius centered on you becomes difficult terrain (rubble) for 1 minute. Creatures in the radius make a Dexterity saving throw or fall prone. This deals no damage: the immovable object makes the world move instead.

13Shake It Off

As a bonus action, slough the affliction off like loose sediment: end one of the following conditions on yourself — grappled, restrained, or poisoned.

15Unbreakable Line

As a bonus action, spend 3 CE: plant yourself and loose a CE-thickened roar — each hostile within 30 ft that can hear you makes a Wisdom saving throw; on a failure it has disadvantage on attack rolls against creatures other than you until the end of your next turn. Your allies within 30 ft gain temporary hit points equal to twice your proficiency bonus.

18Living Avalanche

As an action, spend 8 CE: drive your cursed energy output to its peak for 1 minute — your Bedrock reduction doubles, you are immune to forced movement, and any creature that hits you with a melee attack takes bludgeoning damage equal to your proficiency bonus as the stone answers.

20Foundation Stone

Your sedimentation is complete: your Bedrock reduction now applies to all damage except psychic. You count as Gargantuan for carrying, lifting, and breaking objects. Allies within 20 ft of you share your advantage on saving throws and ability checks against forced movement — the bedrock steadies everyone standing on it.

Pinnacle expression

Maximum, Domain, or equivalent.

Maximum — Clobberin' Time (10 CE): For 1 minute, your cursed energy surges through the stone — your Bedrock reduction doubles (this does not stack with Living Avalanche); hostiles within 30 ft have disadvantage on attack rolls against creatures other than you; and when a creature misses you with a melee attack, you may use your reaction to slam it — 4d8 + your Strength modifier bludgeoning damage, and it is knocked prone.

Feats

Build-defining options.

Prerequisite: Ripped-Up Arsenal

Fastball Special

As an action, hurl one willing creature one size smaller than you up to 60 ft in a straight line. If it lands adjacent to a hostile creature, it may immediately make one melee attack against that creature with advantage, adding your Strength modifier to the damage roll. Otherwise it lands on its feet in an unoccupied space. The thrown creature must be willing, and you cannot throw anything you could not lift.

Prerequisite: none

Dead Stick Landing

The test pilot's last trick — and proof the stone was always the point: when you fall, you take no falling damage. Each creature within 10 ft of your landing point makes a Dexterity saving throw, taking 2d8 bludgeoning damage and falling prone on a failure, as your dense CE discharges into the ground. The ground where you land becomes difficult terrain until cleared.

Prerequisite: Ripped-Up Arsenal

Yancy Street Rules

The street fights back. When you hit a creature with an improvised weapon, it must succeed on a Strength saving throw or fall prone; once per turn, you may instead force it to drop one object it is holding as the blow jars it loose.

Prerequisite: none

Seismic Sense

Your sedimented body conducts tremors the way a medium conducts CE: while standing on stone or earth, you gain tremorsense out to 30 ft, and you cannot be surprised by creatures in contact with the same ground.

Rules integration

Edge cases and shared systems.

Codex rulings: Direct rolled technique/weapon attacks can use the campaign's Black Flash procedure when otherwise eligible. Passive damage, hazards, summons acting independently, and initiative-20 Domain events cannot. Generic Maximum Output applies only to ordinary features, never named Maxima or Domains. Bedrock is flat damage reduction, not a die: it never adds dice to attacks and is not modified by effects that alter dice. Bedrock's fiction is sedimented cursed energy, not biology: effects that suppress or steal cursed energy interact with it as a technique.
