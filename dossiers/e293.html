The Thing

Sedimented cursed energy / living stone / momentum / protection

Power tierTier IV · Special Grade
Core law

What the technique actually does.

The engine is geology, not machinery, cursed energy that settled instead of flowing, and the file’s reductions and immovability follow from that one fact. Core Engine, Bedrock. Your cursed energy does not flow, it settles. Where other sorcerers circulate CE through living flesh, yours sediments, layer upon layer, until flesh gives way to living cursed stone: a reinforcement the body performed on itself and never undid. Reduce all bludgeoning, piercing, and slashing damage you take by your proficiency bonus (minimum 1); the rock chips, it never cracks. You have advantage on Strength saving throws and ability checks made to resist being moved, shoved, or knocked prone, and any effect that would move you against your will fails outright if its source is smaller than Huge.

Field notes. Subject: what the battlefield stands on. Three truths govern it. First: the Bedrock has no switch. No activation, no meter, no concentration, the cursed energy finished settling long ago, and the reduction to bludgeoning, piercing, and slashing simply is, the way a mountain simply is. You cannot disarm geology. Second: the rock is cursed energy, not biology. The file says so plainly, effects that suppress or steal CE interact with Bedrock as a technique, because the stone is sedimented resolve, layer upon layer of a sorcerer who decided, once, not to move. Third: the kit is the immovable object making the world move instead, the stomp that renders the ground difficult, the dash that carries momentum through hostile spaces, the body that interposes itself between an ally and a blade. This one does not escalate. It endures, and it makes the enemy escalate against it.

Full progression

Level 1 through 20.

1Bedrock Body

The first level confirms the settlement is finished, the reduction is always on, unarmed strikes deal a d8, and the user counts as Large for every feat of lifting and breaking. Bedrock Body. The Core Engine above is always on, no activation, no meter: your cursed energy has already finished settling. Your unarmed strikes deal 1d8 bludgeoning damage, and you count as Large for carrying, lifting, pushing, dragging, and breaking objects.

3Ripped-Up Arsenal

At the third level the terrain becomes the weapon, two coins tear a girder or paving slab free for a minute of ten-foot reach, or hurl the rubble sixty feet. As an action, spend 2 CE: channel a pulse of dense CE through your grip and tear a lamppost, girder, or paving slab from the terrain as an improvised weapon, 10-ft reach, 2d8 + Strength modifier bludgeoning damage, lasting 1 minute. Alternatively, hurl a chunk of rubble as a ranged attack (60 ft), dealing 2d8 + Strength modifier bludgeoning damage on a hit.

6Bull Rush

The sixth level weaponizes the Dash, the sedimented momentum carries the user through hostile spaces, and everything entered saves or takes the blow prone. When you take the Dash action, your sedimented CE carries your momentum, you may move through hostile creatures' spaces. Each creature whose space you enter makes a Strength saving throw: on a failure it takes 2d8 + your Strength modifier bludgeoning damage and falls prone; on a success it takes half damage and is not knocked prone.

9Stonewall

The ninth level is the body as a wall, a reaction interposes the stone between an ally and an attack, the Bedrock reduction applying to the damage. When an ally within 10 ft of you is hit by an attack, you may use your reaction to interpose your stone body: you become the attack's target instead, moving up to 10 ft as part of the reaction if needed. Your Bedrock reduction applies to the damage.

11Groundbreak Stomp

The eleventh level makes the immovable object move the world, four coins turn a twenty-foot radius into rubble and put everything in it on the ground. As an action, spend 4 CE: discharge a pulse of dense cursed energy into the ground, a 20-ft radius centered on you becomes difficult terrain (rubble) for 1 minute. Creatures in the radius make a Dexterity saving throw or fall prone. This deals no damage: the immovable object makes the world move instead.

13Shake It Off

At the thirteenth level afflictions slough off like loose sediment, a bonus action ends grappled, restrained, or poisoned. As a bonus action, slough the affliction off like loose sediment: end one of the following conditions on yourself, grappled, restrained, or poisoned.

15Unbreakable Line

The fifteenth level plants the flag, three coins and a roar give hostiles disadvantage against anyone but the user, and allies gain temporary hit points. As a bonus action, spend 3 CE: plant yourself and loose a CE-thickened roar, each hostile within 30 ft that can hear you makes a Wisdom saving throw; on a failure it has disadvantage on attack rolls against creatures other than you until the end of your next turn. Your allies within 30 ft gain temporary hit points equal to twice your proficiency bonus.

18Living Avalanche

The eighteenth level peaks the output for a minute, the reduction doubles, forced movement fails outright, and melee attackers are answered by the stone. As an action, spend 8 CE: drive your cursed energy output to its peak for 1 minute, your Bedrock reduction doubles, you are immune to forced movement, and any creature that hits you with a melee attack takes bludgeoning damage equal to your proficiency bonus as the stone answers.

20Foundation Stone

The twentieth level completes the sedimentation, the reduction covers all damage but psychic, the user counts as Gargantuan for breaking, and nearby allies share the steadiness. Your sedimentation is complete: your Bedrock reduction now applies to all damage except psychic. You count as Gargantuan for carrying, lifting, and breaking objects. Allies within 20 ft of you share your advantage on saving throws and ability checks against forced movement, the bedrock steadies everyone standing on it.

Pinnacle expression

Maximum, Domain, or equivalent.

The Maximum surges the stone for a minute, the reduction doubles, hostiles are taunted off the user’s allies, and a missed melee swing earns a 4d8 slam and a prone attacker. Maximum, Clobberin' Time (10 CE): For 1 minute, your cursed energy surges through the stone, your Bedrock reduction doubles (this does not stack with Living Avalanche); hostiles within 30 ft have disadvantage on attack rolls against creatures other than you; and when a creature misses you with a melee attack, you may use your reaction to slam it, 4d8 + your Strength modifier bludgeoning damage, and it is knocked prone.

Feats

Build-defining options.

Prerequisite: Ripped-Up Arsenal

Fastball Special

The classic, filed as written, hurl a willing smaller creature sixty feet, and let it land swinging with advantage and your Strength behind the blow. As an action, hurl one willing creature one size smaller than you up to 60 ft in a straight line. If it lands adjacent to a hostile creature, it may immediately make one melee attack against that creature with advantage, adding your Strength modifier to the damage roll. Otherwise it lands on its feet in an unoccupied space. The thrown creature must be willing, and you cannot throw anything you could not lift.

Prerequisite: none

Dead Stick Landing

The test pilot’s last trick, falls deal the user nothing, and the landing discharges 2d8 into everything within ten feet and leaves the ground difficult. The test pilot's last trick, and proof the stone was always the point: when you fall, you take no falling damage. Each creature within 10 ft of your landing point makes a Dexterity saving throw, taking 2d8 bludgeoning damage and falling prone on a failure, or half as much damage and not falling prone on a success, as your dense CE discharges into the ground. The ground where you land becomes difficult terrain until cleared.

Prerequisite: Ripped-Up Arsenal

Yancy Street Rules

The street fights back, improvised-weapon hits knock targets prone, or once per turn jar an object from their grip. The street fights back. When you hit a creature with an improvised weapon, it must succeed on a Strength saving throw or fall prone; once per turn, you may instead force it to drop one object it is holding as the blow jars it loose.

Prerequisite: none

Seismic Sense

The sedimented body reads tremors, standing on stone or earth grants thirty feet of tremorsense, and the grounded cannot be surprised. Your sedimented body conducts tremors the way a medium conducts CE: while standing on stone or earth, you gain tremorsense out to 30 ft, and you cannot be surprised by creatures in contact with the same ground.

Rules integration

Edge cases and shared systems.

Codex rulings: Direct rolled technique/weapon attacks can use the campaign's Black Flash procedure when otherwise eligible. Passive damage, hazards, summons acting independently, and initiative-20 Domain events cannot. Generic Maximum Output applies only to ordinary features, never named Maxima or Domains. Bedrock is flat damage reduction, not a die: it never adds dice to attacks and is not modified by effects that alter dice. Bedrock's fiction is sedimented cursed energy, not biology: effects that suppress or steal cursed energy interact with it as a technique.
