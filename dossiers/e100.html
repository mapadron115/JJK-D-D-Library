Dominion Hierarchy

Core law

What the technique actually does.

Hierarchy Marks

  • You can maintain a number of fully Dominated creatures equal to your CE ability modifier (minimum 1). Lesser animals do not count.
  • A creature becomes Eligible for Dominion when one is true: it is Charmed/Frightened by you, it willingly acknowledges your authority, it is at or below 25% HP, you defeated it personally within 24 hours, or it is restrained/incapacitated and has failed a prior Dominion save.
  • Dominion uses Charisma saves. A creature immune to charm has advantage rather than blanket immunity unless it is mindless.
  • Controlled creatures keep personalities but obey direct commands. The GM still plays them unless a player companion sheet is easier.

Dominion Die

Levels 1–4 d6
Levels 5–10 d8
Levels 11–16 d10

The original expansion repeatedly references a Dominion Die without printing its value. This reviewed edition completes that omission using the same four-step progression explicitly established by Vector Authority in the same book. This is a codex clarification, not a claimed quotation from the source.

Full progression

Level 1–20 Progression

1Leash

Action, 2 CE: one Eligible creature within 30 ft makes Cha save. Failure is Charmed and Compelled for 1 minute; while Compelled it obeys simple commands that do not obviously guarantee immediate self-destruction. Repeat save after taking damage from you.

3Public Safety Contract

During a long rest, make a voluntary Binding Contract with a willing creature. Write one benefit and one real price. You may maintain PB active written contracts. Contracts cannot grant features neither party possesses.

6Borrowed Authority

A creature that fails Dominion by 5+ becomes Dominated for 24 hours instead of 1 minute. While within 120 ft, action + normal CE cost lets you use one non-Domain, non-Maximum technique feature it possesses, using your CE DC. That creature cannot use the borrowed feature until your next turn.

9Eyes and Ears

You can perceive through controlled Tiny beasts within 1 mile as an action. Rat Road: action, 3 CE; if controlled vermin exist at both points, teleport between two spaces within 1 mile.

11Chain Invocation

Bonus action, 3 CE: manifest spectral chains to one Dominated creature within 120 ft for 1 minute. Once/turn use one eligible feature through the chain without preventing the subject from using its other features. Damage dealt through Borrowed Authority cannot exceed 6 Dominion Dice before other modifiers.

13Sacrificial Contract

During downtime, create one major contract with a willing polity, organization, or group represented by a specific NPC. The GM defines the reciprocal price. A fatal hit against you may be redirected once per long rest to the contract's stated payment if the agreement explicitly supports that consequence.

15Invisible Force

Action, 4 CE: choose creature within 90 ft; Con save, 6 Dominion Dice force. Failure also prone and cannot take reactions. If target is Dominated, it automatically fails the prone/reaction portion but still saves for damage.

18Rule Domain — Cabinet of Necessary Evil

Level-18 nonlethal/rule Domain, 8 CE. No generic sure-hit. On entry each creature states or conceals one allegiance. At initiative 20, the Domain evaluates hierarchy: each hostile not currently protected by anti-domain tech makes Cha save. Failure gains one Rank Below token. At 2 tokens it is Charmed until next initiative 20; at 3 it becomes Dominated until the Domain ends. Dealing damage to you removes one token from the attacker.

20Conquest Devil

Your Dominion range becomes 120 ft and creatures you personally reduce to 0 HP may be stabilized and offered a final Cha save; failure makes them Dominated for 7 days. You may maintain twice your CE modifier Dominated creatures, but only CE modifier can be active combatants at once.

Pinnacle expression

Pinnacle / Maximum

Maximum Technique — National Chain (10 CE): choose up to PB Dominated/contracted creatures anywhere on the same plane that you can name. For 1 minute you may borrow one eligible feature from each once; every use still costs CE. Afterward all borrowed access from this Maximum locks until long rest.

Prerequisite: Public Safety Contract

Contract Lawyer

You may maintain PB + CE modifier written contracts and have advantage on checks to identify loopholes or coercive language in supernatural bargains.

Prerequisite: level 6

Bonus action, 2 CE: one creature you damaged this turn makes Wis save; failure is Frightened of you until end next turn and therefore becomes Dominion-eligible.

Prerequisite: Eyes and Ears

Remote Handler

You may issue simple commands telepathically to Dominated creatures within 1 mile.

Rules integration

Binding Rulings

Dominion is a charm/control system, not permission to remove every boss from combat with one failed save. Legendary Resistance works normally.
Borrowed powers never include Domains, innate resource pools, passive immortality, or another creature's summons unless specifically approved.
Contracts require genuine reciprocal prices; they are not free feat construction.
The rule Domain resolves its own initiative-20 hierarchy event and does not make ordinary attacks auto-hit.
