Dominion Hierarchy

Control is strongest when hierarchy is established in play. The technique never asks the GM to accept 'I think I'm superior' as enough; superiority must be mechanically or narratively demonstrated.

Power tierTier IV · Special Grade
Combat roleControl / contracts / borrowed powers / surveillance
Core law

What the technique actually does.

Hierarchy Marks

Passive · Maintain fully Dominated creatures equal to your CE ability modifier (minimum 1; lesser animals do not count) · A creature becomes Eligible for Dominion if: Charmed/Frightened by you, willingly acknowledges your authority, at or below 25% HP, personally defeated by you within 24 hours, or restrained/incapacitated after a prior failed Dominion save · Dominion uses Charisma saves; charm-immune creatures get advantage instead (unless mindless).

The ledger of ownership: how many Dominated she may maintain, and the five ways a creature becomes Eligible, charmed, frightened, acknowledging, broken, or bound. Every conquest below is an entry in this book. Controlled creatures keep personalities but obey direct commands; the GM still plays them unless a player companion sheet is easier.

Field notes. Subject: obedience made structural. Three truths govern her. First, she does not overpower minds, she audits them: every surrender, defeat, and acknowledgment a creature has already lived is read, filed, and collected on. Second, her CE only recognizes masters who have done the proving; power must be demonstrated, not claimed. Third, the chain is maintained by debt, contracts with real prices, tokens that accrue, borrowed features that cost their normal price. Hence the maxim: she never takes what was not already offered. She merely enforces the terms.

Full progression

Level 1–20 Progression

1Leash

Action · 2 CE · One Eligible creature within 30 ft: Cha save; failure: Charmed and Compelled for 1 minute (obeys simple commands, not obvious self-destruction) and +1 Dominion Die psychic damage. Repeat save after taking damage from you.

The first instrument. Note the mercy written in: obedience cannot order obvious self-destruction. Even she respects the limit of what a leash can ask.

Passive · Dominion Die scales with character level: d6 (1–4) → d8 (5–10) → d10 (11–16) → d12 (17–20).

Dominion Die progression: the original expansion repeatedly references a Dominion Die without printing its value. This reviewed edition completes that omission using the same four-step progression explicitly established by Vector Authority in the same book — a codex clarification, not a claimed quotation from the source.

3Public Safety Contract

Long rest · Voluntary Binding Contract with a willing creature: one benefit, one real price. Maintain PB active written contracts. Contracts cannot grant features neither party possesses.

Between battles she does paperwork. A chain of command is, after all, only as strong as its signatures.

6Borrowed Authority

Passive · Fail Dominion by 5+: Dominated for 24 hours instead of 1 minute. While within 120 ft: action + normal CE cost to use one of its non-Domain, non-Maximum features with your CE DC; it can't use that feature until your next turn.

Borrowing, not theft — she leaves the receipt.

9Eyes and Ears

Action · Perceive through controlled Tiny beasts within 1 mile. Rat Road: action · 3 CE · teleport between two spaces within 1 mile if controlled vermin stand at both ends.

Her dominion acquires senses. An empire of small obedient eyes.

11Chain Invocation

Bonus action · 3 CE · Spectral chains to one Dominated creature within 120 ft for 1 minute: once/turn use one eligible feature through the chain. Damage through Borrowed Authority capped at 6 Dominion Dice.

The chain is a conduit, not a muzzle.

13Sacrificial Contract

Downtime · One major contract with a willing polity/organization (GM sets the price). Once per long rest: redirect a fatal hit to the contract's stated payment if the agreement supports it.

Downtime becomes diplomacy. She insures herself against death with other people’s signatures.

15Invisible Force

Action · 4 CE · Creature within 90 ft: Con save, 6 Dominion Dice force; failure also prone and cannot take reactions. Dominated targets auto-fail the prone/reaction portion.

Her first honest blow. Even her fists are an argument for hierarchy.

18Rule Domain, Cabinet of Necessary Evil

Domain · Level 18 · 8 CE · No sure-hit; a rule instead. On entry each creature declares or conceals an allegiance; at initiative 20 hostiles make a Cha save: failure gains a Rank Below token (2 = Charmed, 3 = Dominated until the Domain ends). Damaging you removes one token from the attacker.

The Domain states no sure-hit; it states a rule. She wins not by striking but by seating. Each hostile not currently protected by anti-domain tech makes the Cha save; at 2 tokens it is Charmed until the next initiative 20, at 3 it becomes Dominated until the Domain ends.

20Conquest Devil

Passive · Dominion range 120 ft. Creatures you reduce to 0 HP may be stabilized and offered a final Cha save; failure: Dominated for 7 days. Maintain 2× CE modifier Dominated (only CE modifier as active combatants).

The empire has a standing army now.

Pinnacle expression

Pinnacle / Maximum

The Maximum is her whole empire at once. This is not a technique. It is a requisition. Maximum Technique, National Chain (10 CE; action): choose up to PB Dominated/contracted creatures anywhere on the same plane that you can name. For 1 minute you may borrow one eligible feature from each once; every use still costs CE. Afterward all borrowed access from this Maximum locks until long rest.

Feats

Technique Feats

Prerequisite: Dominion

Good Dog

Your Compelled creatures throw themselves in harm's way: once per round, when you would be hit by an attack, a Compelled creature within 15 ft may use its reaction to interpose itself, becoming the target instead. Loyalty, operationalized.

Prerequisite: Public Safety Contract

Contract Lawyer

You enforce the fine print: when a creature breaks the stated price of a written contract you hold or witnessed, you may spend a reaction and 2 CE to call in the debt, it makes a Cha save, becoming Dominion-eligible until the end of its next turn on a failure. Default is a kind of confession.

Prerequisite: level 6

Lower Yourself

Bonus action, 2 CE: one creature you damaged this turn makes Wis save; failure is Frightened of you until end next turn and therefore becomes Dominion-eligible. She manufactures the very conditions her leash requires.

Prerequisite: Eyes and Ears

Remote Handler

You may issue simple commands telepathically to Dominated creatures within 1 mile. The chain of command, at the speed of thought.

Rules integration

Binding Rulings

Dominion is a charm/control system, not permission to remove every boss from combat with one failed save. Legendary Resistance works normally.
Borrowed powers never include Domains, innate resource pools, passive immortality, or another creature's summons unless specifically approved.
Contracts require genuine reciprocal prices; they are not free feat construction.
The rule Domain resolves its own initiative-20 hierarchy event and does not make ordinary attacks auto-hit.
