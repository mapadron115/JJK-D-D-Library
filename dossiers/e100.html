Dominion Hierarchy

Control is strongest when hierarchy is established in play. The technique never asks the GM to accept 'I think I'm superior' as enough; superiority must be mechanically or narratively demonstrated.

Power tierTier IV · Special Grade
Combat roleControl / contracts / borrowed powers / surveillance
Core law

What the technique actually does.

Hierarchy Marks

The ledger of ownership: how many Dominated she may maintain, and the five ways a creature becomes Eligible, charmed, frightened, acknowledging, broken, or bound. Every conquest below is an entry in this book.

  • You can maintain a number of fully Dominated creatures equal to your CE ability modifier (minimum 1). Lesser animals do not count.
  • A creature becomes Eligible for Dominion when one is true: it is Charmed/Frightened by you, it willingly acknowledges your authority, it is at or below 25% HP, you defeated it personally within 24 hours, or it is restrained/incapacitated and has failed a prior Dominion save.
  • Dominion uses Charisma saves. A creature immune to charm has advantage rather than blanket immunity unless it is mindless.
  • Controlled creatures keep personalities but obey direct commands. The GM still plays them unless a player companion sheet is easier.

Field notes. Subject: obedience made structural. Three truths govern her. First, she does not overpower minds, she audits them: every surrender, defeat, and acknowledgment a creature has already lived is read, filed, and collected on. Second, her CE only recognizes masters who have done the proving; power must be demonstrated, not claimed. Third, the chain is maintained by debt, contracts with real prices, tokens that accrue, borrowed features that cost their normal price. Hence the maxim: she never takes what was not already offered. She merely enforces the terms.

Full progression

Level 1–20 Progression

1Leash

The first instrument: 2 CE, an action, a Cha save, and for one minute the target is Charmed and Compelled to simple commands. Note the mercy written in: obedience cannot order obvious self-destruction. Even she respects the limit of what a leash can ask. Action, 2 CE: one Eligible creature within 30 ft makes Cha save. Failure is Charmed and Compelled for 1 minute; while Compelled it obeys simple commands that do not obviously guarantee immediate self-destruction. Repeat save after taking damage from you.

Dominion Die progression: d6 at levels 1–4, d8 at levels 5–10, d10 at levels 11–16, d12 at levels 17–20. The original expansion repeatedly references a Dominion Die without printing its value. This reviewed edition completes that omission using the same four-step progression explicitly established by Vector Authority in the same book. This is a codex clarification, not a claimed quotation from the source.

3Public Safety Contract

Between battles she does paperwork: during a long rest, a voluntary Binding Contract with a willing creature, one benefit, one real price. She maintains PB such contracts. A chain of command is, after all, only as strong as its signatures. During a long rest, make a voluntary Binding Contract with a willing creature. Write one benefit and one real price. You may maintain PB active written contracts. Contracts cannot grant features neither party possesses.

6Borrowed Authority

Failure by 5 or more buys her 24 hours instead of a minute, and now she may wear her subject’s power: an action at normal CE cost to use one of its non-Domain, non-Maximum features with her own DC. The subject cannot use the borrowed feature until her next turn. Borrowing, not theft, she leaves the receipt. A creature that fails Dominion by 5+ becomes Dominated for 24 hours instead of 1 minute. While within 120 ft, action + normal CE cost lets you use one non-Domain, non-Maximum technique feature it possesses, using your CE DC. That creature cannot use the borrowed feature until your next turn.

9Eyes and Ears

Her dominion acquires senses: perception through controlled Tiny beasts within a mile, and the Rat Road, 3 CE to teleport between two spaces where her vermin stand at both ends. An empire of small obedient eyes. You can perceive through controlled Tiny beasts within 1 mile as an action. Rat Road: action, 3 CE; if controlled vermin exist at both points, teleport between two spaces within 1 mile.

11Chain Invocation

Spectral chains to one Dominated creature within 120 ft, for a minute: once per turn she may use an eligible feature through the chain without silencing the subject’s others. Damage through borrowed features is capped at 6 Dominion Dice. The chain is a conduit, not a muzzle. Bonus action, 3 CE: manifest spectral chains to one Dominated creature within 120 ft for 1 minute. Once/turn use one eligible feature through the chain without preventing the subject from using its other features. Damage dealt through Borrowed Authority cannot exceed 6 Dominion Dice before other modifiers.

13Sacrificial Contract

Downtime becomes diplomacy: one major contract with a willing polity or organization, price set by the GM, and once per long rest a fatal hit may be redirected to the contract’s stated payment, if the agreement supports it. She insures herself against death with other people’s signatures. During downtime, create one major contract with a willing polity, organization, or group represented by a specific NPC. The GM defines the reciprocal price. A fatal hit against you may be redirected once per long rest to the contract's stated payment if the agreement explicitly supports that consequence.

15Invisible Force

Her first honest blow: 4 CE, an action, a creature within 90 ft, Con save, 6 Dominion Dice force, prone and reactionless on failure. A Dominated target fails the prone portion automatically. Even her fists are an argument for hierarchy. Action, 4 CE: choose creature within 90 ft; Con save, 6 Dominion Dice force. Failure also prone and cannot take reactions. If target is Dominated, it automatically fails the prone/reaction portion but still saves for damage.

18Rule Domain, Cabinet of Necessary Evil

The Domain states no sure-hit; it states a rule. Every creature declares or conceals an allegiance, and at initiative 20 the Domain audits the hierarchy: failures accrue Rank Below tokens, Charmed at two, Dominated at three. Dealing damage to her removes a token. She wins not by striking but by seating. Level-18 nonlethal/rule Domain, 8 CE. No generic sure-hit. On entry each creature states or conceals one allegiance. At initiative 20, the Domain evaluates hierarchy: each hostile not currently protected by anti-domain tech makes Cha save. Failure gains one Rank Below token. At 2 tokens it is Charmed until next initiative 20; at 3 it becomes Dominated until the Domain ends. Dealing damage to you removes one token from the attacker.

20Conquest Devil

The leash lengthens to 120 ft; those she reduces to 0 HP may be stabilized and offered one final Cha save, failure is seven days of Domination. She keeps twice her CE modifier Dominated, though only her modifier may serve as active combatants. The empire has a standing army now. Your Dominion range becomes 120 ft and creatures you personally reduce to 0 HP may be stabilized and offered a final Cha save; failure makes them Dominated for 7 days. You may maintain twice your CE modifier Dominated creatures, but only CE modifier can be active combatants at once.

Pinnacle expression

Pinnacle / Maximum

The Maximum is her whole empire at once: for one minute she may borrow one eligible feature from each of up to PB Dominated or contracted creatures anywhere on the same plane that she can name, paying CE each time. Afterward the access locks until a long rest. This is not a technique. It is a requisition. Maximum Technique, National Chain (10 CE; action): choose up to PB Dominated/contracted creatures anywhere on the same plane that you can name. For 1 minute you may borrow one eligible feature from each once; every use still costs CE. Afterward all borrowed access from this Maximum locks until long rest.

Feats

Technique Feats

Prerequisite: Dominion

Good Dog

Her Compelled throw themselves into harm’s way: once per round, when she would be hit, one within 15 ft may interpose itself with its reaction. Loyalty, operationalized. Your Compelled creatures throw themselves in harm's way: once per round, when you would be hit by an attack, a Compelled creature within 15 ft may use its reaction to interpose itself, becoming the target instead.

Prerequisite: Public Safety Contract

Contract Lawyer

She enforces the fine print: when a creature breaks the price of a written contract she holds, a reaction and 2 CE calls in the debt, a failed Cha save makes it Dominion-eligible. Default is a kind of confession. You enforce the fine print: when a creature breaks the stated price of a written contract you hold or witnessed, you may spend a reaction and 2 CE to call in the debt, it makes a Cha save, becoming Dominion-eligible until the end of its next turn on a failure.

Prerequisite: level 6

Lower Yourself

Bonus action, 2 CE: a creature she damaged this turn saves or is Frightened of her, and fear is eligibility. She manufactures the very conditions her leash requires. Bonus action, 2 CE: one creature you damaged this turn makes Wis save; failure is Frightened of you until end next turn and therefore becomes Dominion-eligible.

Prerequisite: Eyes and Ears

Remote Handler

Her commands no longer need her voice: she may issue simple orders telepathically to Dominated creatures within a mile. The chain of command, at the speed of thought. You may issue simple commands telepathically to Dominated creatures within 1 mile.

Rules integration

Binding Rulings

Dominion is a charm/control system, not permission to remove every boss from combat with one failed save. Legendary Resistance works normally.
Borrowed powers never include Domains, innate resource pools, passive immortality, or another creature's summons unless specifically approved.
Contracts require genuine reciprocal prices; they are not free feat construction.
The rule Domain resolves its own initiative-20 hierarchy event and does not make ordinary attacks auto-hit.
