Core law

What the technique actually does.

Core Engine — Dominion. The ledger of concession. Dominion maxes at ten, banked when hostiles fail saves against him, kneel, or fall as major foes — spent to bend beams, impose commands, or empower the Avatar. An avatar-scale conversion, the file notes; not the true form. Gain Dominion (max 10) when a hostile fails a save against you, kneels/surrenders, or you defeat a major foe. Spend Dominion to bend Omega Beams, impose commands, or empower an Avatar. This is an avatar-scale player conversion, not unrestricted true-form Darkseid.

Field notes. Subject: authority, distilled and drunk. Three truths govern him. First: his cursed energy feeds on concession itself — every failed save against him, every kneeling enemy, every major foe defeated is Dominion banked, max ten; surrender is his favorite food group. Second: the ledger is spent on the beams — bending them around cover, retargeting the miss, turning corners in pursuit — and on the Avatar that makes him Large and armored; authority, converted to ballistics. Third: the escalation is judicial — Sanction banishes the condemned to a hostile pocket dimension, the Anti-Life Fragment compels obedience, and the Domain offers the only two verdicts: TYRANNY or OMEGA. Mark the final title: at twenty, the dossier stops calling him a sorcerer. Darkseid Is.

Full progression

Level 1 through 20.

1Omega Beam

The opening argument: two CE, ninety feet, three dice of force and necrotic — and one Dominion bends the beam around cover or re-aims the miss. The evidence always arrives. For 2 CE, make a ranged technique attack (90 ft) dealing 3d10 force/necrotic damage. Spend 1 Dominion to bend the beam around cover or retarget after a miss, once.

3God of Tyranny

Advantage against being Frightened or Charmed — and one CE as an action to Frighten a failed Wisdom save until his next turn. The court is in session. You have advantage on saves against the Frightened and Charmed conditions. As an action, spend 1 CE: one creature makes a Wisdom save or is Frightened until your next turn.

6Omega Pursuit

The beam learns patience: it turns corners and chases to one hundred twenty feet — and two Dominion buy advantage on the attack. There is no outrunning the verdict. Your Omega Beam can turn corners and chase a target up to 120 ft; the target still defends normally. Spend 2 Dominion to gain advantage on the attack.

9Boom Tube

Three CE for a portal between two visible spaces within one hundred twenty feet, crossed by the willing until his next turn. The tyrant's commute. For 3 CE, open a portal between two visible spaces within 120 ft; willing creatures can cross through it until the start of your next turn.

11Avatar

Five CE for a minute of the throne made flesh: Large, +2 AC, resistance to the physical, 1d12 plus a force die on the fists. The authority takes a body. For 5 CE, for 1 minute: you become Large, gain +2 AC, resistance to bludgeoning, piercing, and slashing, and your unarmed strikes deal 1d12 + 1d10 force.

13Omega Sanction

Six CE and three Dominion: a failed Constitution save takes seven dice and banishment to a hostile pocket dimension until his next turn ends — then the return, prone. The sentence, served elsewhere. For 6 CE + 3 Dominion, target a creature; it makes a Constitution save. On a failure it takes 7d10 force/necrotic damage and is banished until the end of its next turn into a hostile pocket dimension, then returns prone.

15Anti-Life Fragment

Five CE for a glimpse of the Equation: a failed Charisma save and the target is Charmed and Compelled until his next turn ends — though Legendary Resistance holds, and suicide and permanent betrayal are beyond even this. The fragment has limits. He notes them. For 5 CE, target one creature; it makes a Charisma save. On a failure it is Charmed/Compelled until the end of its next turn. Legendary Resistance works against this; you cannot force suicide or permanent betrayal.

18Domain — Apokolips Is

The Domain offers two verdicts at initiative twenty: TYRANNY kneels the hostile — prone, speed zero, Charisma to refuse — and OMEGA fires one autonomous beam per hostile of his proficiency for six dice. Tier X, a thousand clash points; his own attacks still roll honestly. At initiative 20, choose TYRANNY or OMEGA. TYRANNY: hostiles make a Charisma save or kneel (prone, speed 0) until the next initiative 20. OMEGA: one autonomous beam targets each of up to PB hostiles (Dexterity save, 6d10 force). Your ordinary attacks do not auto-hit. Clash bookkeeping: 1,000 clash points (Tier X). A 100+ point advantage collapses the weaker Domain; collapse deals no damage. Barrier AC 12 + PB; barrier HP = 10 × max(1, your CE modifier) + 2,000. Technique Burnout: 3 rounds.

20Darkseid Is

Dominion caps at fifteen; the first Avatar each long rest costs nothing; the beam's base becomes four dice — and once per round, a miss may be spent into a hit for three Dominion, never a critical. The dossier's last line is a name, not a title. Your Dominion maximum becomes 15. The first time you use your Avatar each long rest, it costs 0 CE. Your Omega Beam’s base damage becomes 4d10, and once per round, when you miss with a beam, you may spend 3 Dominion to turn it into a hit — but never a critical hit.

Pinnacle expression

Maximum, Domain, or equivalent.

Maximum — Omega Erasure (12 CE + 10 Dominion; action): one creature within 120 ft Con save. Failure takes 14d10 force/necrotic; if reduced to 0 it is banished rather than killed and requires a major supernatural return method. Success half damage and no banishment. This is not Pochita-style conceptual erasure.

Feats

Build-defining options.

Parademon General

Apokolips provides: three CE for a minute's hunting pack — three 1-HP parademons, one bonus action commanding all. The tyrant travels with an entourage. Apokolips provides. As an action for 3 CE, you may crack a brief Boom Tube and call a parademon hunting pack: three 1-HP parademons (AC 13, fly 40, bite 1d8+2) that last 1 minute and share one command as a bonus action. They are cowardly — if the whole pack dies, you cannot call another until you finish a short rest. Their minds are too broken to carry messages or leave the battle.

Stone God

While the Avatar holds, the beam may originate from any point he can see within fifteen feet. The throne has many windows. while your Avatar is active, your Omega Beam may originate from any point within 15 ft of you that you can see.

Equation Fragment

Advantage on Intimidation against the Frightened — and successful intimidation of such leaves its mark. The fragment shows in the voice. You have glimpsed a fragment of the Anti-Life Equation, and it shows in your voice. You have advantage on Charisma (Intimidation) checks against creatures Frightened of you, and when you successfully intimidate such a creature it also cannot willingly move away from you until the end of its next turn — despair roots it in place. This is a fragment, not the Equation; it compels dread, never action.

There Is No Escape

After a missed beam, the next turn's bonus action redirects the still-burning shaft at the same target, no CE re-spent. The evidence refuses to be dismissed. after a missed Omega Beam, you may use your bonus action on your next turn to redirect the still-burning beam at the same target with a new attack roll, without spending CE again.

Kneel

When a hostile kneels, surrenders, or falls to his hand, a reaction demands fealty of one witness within thirty feet — Wisdom or Frightened until his next turn ends. The audience participates. when a hostile kneels, surrenders, or drops to 0 HP from your actions, you may use your reaction to demand fealty: one other hostile within 30 ft that sees it makes a Wisdom save or is Frightened of you until the end of your next turn.

Final Crisis

Once per long rest, the Sanction opens as a doorway instead of a prison: he or a willing ally steps through as a bonus action, emerging within sixty feet of the condemned when it returns. The sentence commutes the jailer. once per long rest, when you use Omega Sanction you may open it as a doorway instead of a prison: you or one willing creature within 30 ft may step through as a bonus action, emerging within 60 ft of the target when it returns.

Rules integration

Edge cases and shared systems.

Codex rulings: Direct rolled technique/weapon attacks can use the campaign's Black Flash procedure when otherwise eligible. Passive damage, hazards, summons acting independently, and initiative-20 Domain events cannot. Generic Maximum Output applies only to ordinary features, never named Maxima or Domains.
