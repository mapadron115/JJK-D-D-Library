Omega Effect

Omega beams / tyranny / avatars / Anti-Life

Power tierTier X · Calamity
Core law

What the technique actually does.

Core Engine, Dominion. The ledger of concession. Gain Dominion (maximum 10) when a hostile fails a save against you, kneels or surrenders, or you defeat a major foe — each condition grants 1 Dominion, and multiple conditions can pay out in the same turn. Gain 1 Dominion when your Omega Beam scores a critical hit or reduces a creature to 0 HP. Spend Dominion to bend Omega Beams, impose commands, or empower an Avatar. This is an avatar-scale player conversion, not unrestricted true-form Darkseid.

Field notes. Subject: the tyrant's arithmetic. I have studied conquerors. I have been one. The Omega Effect is not a conqueror's technique, it is the part of conquest that remains after the conqueror has left the room. Two truths govern it. First: the beam does not miss the verdict, it turns corners, chases to one hundred twenty feet, bends around cover and re-aims the miss; the evidence always arrives. Second: the fragment is load-bearing, a splinter of the Anti-Life Equation, which compels dread but never action, which is why the will behind it still matters and why I study the will most carefully. Dominion is the currency, the Avatar the throne made flesh, the Domain the court in session. I do not fear the beam. I respect the patience that fires it.

Full progression

Level 1 through 20.

1Omega Beam

Action · 2 CE · Ranged technique attack, 90 ft: 3d10 force/necrotic damage. Special · 1 Dominion: bend the beam around cover or retarget after a miss (once).

The evidence always arrives. I have built defenses against many things. I have not built a defense against arrival itself.

3God of Tyranny

Passive · Advantage on saves against the Frightened and Charmed conditions. Action · 1 CE: one creature makes a Wisdom save or is Frightened until your next turn.

The court is in session. He cannot be made afraid. I note this the way one notes that a mountain cannot be made to hurry.

6Omega Pursuit

Passive · Omega Beam turns corners and chases a target up to 120 ft (target defends normally). Special · 2 Dominion: advantage on the attack.

There is no outrunning the verdict. I have outrun many things. I do not propose to outrun geometry that has been instructed not to permit it.

9Boom Tube

Action · 3 CE · Open a portal between two visible spaces within 120 ft; willing creatures can cross until the start of your next turn.

The tyrant's commute. Distance, to him, is an administrative detail. I have started treating my own positioning as a suggestion.

11Avatar

Action · 5 CE · For 1 minute: you become Large, +2 AC, resistance to bludgeoning, piercing, and slashing; your unarmed strikes deal 1d12 + 1d10 force. Special · 3 Dominion: while your Avatar lasts, its unarmed strikes deal +2d10 force.

The authority takes a body. I have seen authority take many bodies. This one, I note, is the authority's own.

13Omega Sanction

Action · 6 CE + 3 Dominion · Target makes a Constitution save; on a failure it takes 7d10 force/necrotic and is banished to a hostile pocket dimension until the end of its next turn, then returns prone.

The sentence, served elsewhere. Exile, as a combat action. I have been exiled. It took longer.

15Anti-Life Fragment

Action · 5 CE · Target makes a Charisma save; on a failure it is Charmed/Compelled until the end of its next turn (Legendary Resistance works; no suicide or permanent betrayal).

The fragment has limits. He notes them. So do I. I note them carefully, and I note that he knows I am noting them.

18Domain, Apokolips Is

Domain · At initiative 20 choose TYRANNY (hostiles make a Charisma save or kneel — prone, speed 0 — until the next initiative 20) or OMEGA (one autonomous beam per hostile, up to PB: Dexterity save, 6d10 force). Your ordinary attacks still roll. Clash 1,000; burnout 3 rounds.

A thousand points, and honesty. I respect both, and I fear the combination. Clash bookkeeping: 1,000 clash points (Tier X). A 100+ point advantage collapses the weaker Domain; collapse deals no damage. Barrier AC 12 + PB; barrier HP = 10 × max(1, your CE modifier) + 2,000. Technique Burnout: 3 rounds.

20Darkseid Is

Passive · Dominion maximum 15; first Avatar each long rest costs 0; Omega Beam base damage 4d10. Special · Once per round: spend 3 Dominion to turn a beam miss into a hit (never a critical).

The dossier's last line is a name, not a title.

Pinnacle expression

Maximum, Domain, or equivalent.

Maximum, Omega Erasure (12 CE + 10 Dominion; action): one creature within 120 ft Con save. Failure takes 14d10 force/necrotic; if reduced to 0 it is banished rather than killed and requires a major supernatural return method. Success half damage and no banishment. This is not Pochita-style conceptual erasure.

Feats

Build-defining options.

Parademon General

The tyrant travels with an entourage. Apokolips provides. As an action for 3 CE, you may crack a brief Boom Tube and call a parademon hunting pack: three 1-HP parademons (AC 13, fly 40, bite 1d8+2) that last 1 minute and share one command as a bonus action. They are cowardly, if the whole pack dies, you cannot call another until you finish a short rest. Their minds are too broken to carry messages or leave the battle.

Stone God

The throne has many windows. I have started assuming that every window is a muzzle. While your Avatar is active, your Omega Beam may originate from any point within 15 ft of you that you can see.

Equation Fragment

The fragment shows in the voice. I have heard that voice. It does not raise itself. It does not need to. I have noted the exact pitch at which my own resolve begins to annotate itself. You have glimpsed a fragment of the Anti-Life Equation, and it shows in your voice. You have advantage on Charisma (Intimidation) checks against creatures Frightened of you, and when you successfully intimidate such a creature it also cannot willingly move away from you until the end of its next turn, despair roots it in place. This is a fragment, not the Equation; it compels dread, never action.

There Is No Escape

The evidence refuses to be dismissed. After a missed Omega Beam, you may use your bonus action on your next turn to redirect the still-burning beam at the same target with a new attack roll, without spending CE again.

Contagious Defeat

The audience participates. Defeat, in his presence, is contagious. I have begun to counsel my own forces against watching. When a hostile kneels, surrenders, or drops to 0 HP from your actions, you may use your reaction to demand fealty: one other hostile within 30 ft that sees it makes a Wisdom save or is Frightened of you until the end of your next turn.

Kneel

2 Dominion: issue a one-word command to a creature that can hear you, as the command spell (no concentration). The word is not a request. He does not repeat himself.

Final Crisis

The sentence commutes the jailer. Once per long rest, when you use Omega Sanction you may open it as a doorway instead of a prison: you or one willing creature within 30 ft may step through as a bonus action, emerging within 60 ft of the target when it returns.

Rules integration

Edge cases and shared systems.

Codex rulings: Direct rolled technique/weapon attacks can use the campaign's Black Flash procedure when otherwise eligible. Passive damage, hazards, summons acting independently, and initiative-20 Domain events cannot. Generic Maximum Output applies only to ordinary features, never named Maxima or Domains.
