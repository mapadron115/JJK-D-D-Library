Omega Effect

Omega beams / tyranny / avatars / Anti-Life

Power tierTier X · Calamity
Core law

What the technique actually does.

Core Engine — Dominion. Gain Dominion (max 10) when a hostile fails a save against you, kneels/surrenders, or you defeat a major foe. Spend Dominion to bend Omega Beams, impose commands, or empower an Avatar. This is an avatar-scale player conversion, not unrestricted true-form Darkseid.

The fiction. Jujutsu scholars classify the Omega Effect as authority made fuel: the user's cursed energy feeds on concession itself, growing denser every time the world kneels, surrenders, or admits the sorcerer was right — which is why Dominion is earned, never conjured from nothing. The prevailing theory holds that the beams bend because submission bends: energy shaped by tyranny does not travel in straight lines, it pursues. Registry notes add, dryly, that no wielder of this technique has ever been recorded negotiating.

Full progression

Level 1 through 20.

L1Omega Beam

For 2 CE, make a ranged technique attack (90 ft) dealing 3d10 force/necrotic damage. Spend 1 Dominion to bend the beam around cover or retarget after a miss, once.

L3God of Tyranny

You have advantage on saves against the Frightened and Charmed conditions. As an action, spend 1 CE: one creature makes a Wisdom save or is Frightened until your next turn.

L6Omega Pursuit

Your Omega Beam can turn corners and chase a target up to 120 ft; the target still defends normally. Spend 2 Dominion to gain advantage on the attack.

L9Boom Tube

For 3 CE, open a portal between two visible spaces within 120 ft; willing creatures can cross through it until the start of your next turn.

L11Avatar

For 5 CE, for 1 minute: you become Large, gain +2 AC, resistance to bludgeoning, piercing, and slashing, and your unarmed strikes deal 1d12 + 1d10 force.

L13Omega Sanction

For 6 CE + 3 Dominion, target a creature; it makes a Constitution save. On a failure it takes 7d10 force/necrotic damage and is banished until the end of its next turn into a hostile pocket dimension, then returns prone.

L15Anti-Life Fragment

For 5 CE, target one creature; it makes a Charisma save. On a failure it is Charmed/Compelled until the end of its next turn. Legendary Resistance works against this; you cannot force suicide or permanent betrayal.

L18Domain — Apokolips Is

At initiative 20, choose TYRANNY or OMEGA. TYRANNY: hostiles make a Charisma save or kneel (prone, speed 0) until the next initiative 20. OMEGA: one autonomous beam targets each of up to PB hostiles (Dexterity save, 6d10 force). Your ordinary attacks do not auto-hit. Clash bookkeeping: 1,000 clash points (Tier X). A 100+ point advantage collapses the weaker Domain; collapse deals no damage. Barrier AC 12 + PB; barrier HP = 10 × max(1, your CE modifier) + 2,000. Technique Burnout: 3 rounds.

L20Darkseid Is

Your Dominion maximum becomes 15. The first time you use your Avatar each long rest, it costs 0 CE. Your Omega Beam’s base damage becomes 4d10, and once per round, when you miss with a beam, you may spend 3 Dominion to turn it into a hit — but never a critical hit.

Pinnacle expression

Maximum, Domain, or equivalent.

Maximum — Omega Erasure (12 CE + 10 Dominion): one creature within 120 ft Con save. Failure takes 14d10 force/necrotic; if reduced to 0 it is banished rather than killed and requires a major supernatural return method. Success half damage and no banishment. This is not Pochita-style conceptual erasure.

Feats

Build-defining options.

Parademon General

Apokolips provides. As an action for 3 CE, you may crack a brief Boom Tube and call a parademon hunting pack: three 1-HP parademons (AC 13, fly 40, bite 1d8+2) that last 1 minute and share one command as a bonus action. They are cowardly — if the whole pack dies, you cannot call another until you finish a short rest. Their minds are too broken to carry messages or leave the battle.

Stone God

while your Avatar is active, your Omega Beam may originate from any point within 15 ft of you that you can see.

Equation Fragment

You have glimpsed a fragment of the Anti-Life Equation, and it shows in your voice. You have advantage on Charisma (Intimidation) checks against creatures Frightened of you, and when you successfully intimidate such a creature it also cannot willingly move away from you until the end of its next turn — despair roots it in place. This is a fragment, not the Equation; it compels dread, never action.

There Is No Escape

after a missed Omega Beam, you may use your bonus action on your next turn to redirect the still-burning beam at the same target with a new attack roll, without spending CE again.

Kneel

when a hostile kneels, surrenders, or drops to 0 HP from your actions, you may use your reaction to demand fealty: one other hostile within 30 ft that sees it makes a Wisdom save or is Frightened of you until the end of your next turn.

Final Crisis

once per long rest, when you use Omega Sanction you may open it as a doorway instead of a prison: you or one willing creature within 30 ft may step through as a bonus action, emerging within 60 ft of the target when it returns.

Rules integration

Edge cases and shared systems.

Codex rulings: Direct rolled technique/weapon attacks can use the campaign's Black Flash procedure when otherwise eligible. Passive damage, hazards, summons acting independently, and initiative-20 Domain events cannot. Generic Maximum Output applies only to ordinary features, never named Maxima or Domains.
