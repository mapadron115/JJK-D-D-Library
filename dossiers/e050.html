BLACK FLAME APOSTASY
Core law

What the technique actually does.

Rules are written to plug into the finalized campaign glossary: normal actions stay normal, direct attacks roll, and only named Domain events bypass the initial roll.

Bonus action · 1 CE

Godskin Stance

Choose Apostle or Noble for 1 minute. Apostle grants +10 speed, +5-foot reach on melee attacks, and Dex-based mobility. Noble grants +1 AC, advantage on checks/saves against forced movement, and counts one size larger for grapples.

On hit · 1 CE

Black Flame

After a direct weapon or CE attack hits, add one Blackflame Die fire/necrotic. At the start of the target’s next turn it takes additional necrotic damage equal to 2% of its maximum HP (minimum PB, maximum 5×level). A creature can suffer this delayed burn once per round.

Manifest

Godskin Armament

As part of entering stance, form a Twinblade in Apostle stance or Stitcher in Noble stance. Use any proficient equivalent weapon profile; the armament counts as cursed.

11The Duo RiteSkinbound Partner and 2 more features

Skinbound Partner

Action · 8 CE · Focus · 1 minute: manifest the opposite stance as a Medium shikigami-like partner. AC 14+PB, HP 8×level, speed 40. It acts after you and can move plus make one Godskin Armament attack for 2 Blackflame Dice + PB. It cannot use your Domain/Maximum.

Shared Health Rite

When partner takes damage, you may use reaction to take any amount of that damage instead. When you take damage, partner may absorb up to PB Blackflame Dice worth once per round.

Black Flame Tornado

Action · 7 CE: creatures within 15 feet make Dex saves, 7 Blackflame Dice fire/necrotic and delayed burn on failure, half and no burn on success.

18Apostasy Refuses DeathResurrection Chant and 2 more features

Resurrection Chant

If Skinbound Partner reaches 0 HP while you remain conscious, it reforms at full HP after 2 rounds if you spend 5 CE at the end of each of those turns. Damage to you can interrupt via Technique Focus checks.

Maximum: Godskin Sabbath

Action · 10 CE: both you and partner (or a phantom opposite form if partner absent) perform a synchronized 60-foot path attack. Creatures crossed make Dex saves for 10 Blackflame Dice split slash/fire and delayed burn; half/no burn on success.

No Domain

This technique does not naturally form a Domain. Its binding identity is two bodies sharing one rite, not an imposed barrier law.

20Godhunt RestoredTrue Black Flame and 2 more features

True Black Flame

Delayed Black Flame burn becomes 3% of maximum HP (same minimum/maximum damage caps doubled to 10×level). It ignores fire resistance but not fire immunity; necrotic portion remains.

Duo Without Focus

Skinbound Partner no longer requires Technique Focus, though only one partner may exist.

Gloam Hunt

Once per turn when both you and your partner hit the same creature before your next turn, the second hit gains +PB Blackflame Dice and the target cannot regain HP until start of your next turn.

Pinnacle expression

Domain, Maximum, or equivalent.

Not every technique needs a Domain. When one exists, its sure-hit is a unique autonomous initiative-count-20 event unless the Domain is explicitly rule-based.

No natural Domain

Black Flame Apostasy reaches its pinnacle through the Twin Rite, resurrection, and restored god-hunting flame. A character may still learn generic barrier techniques or Simple Domain through class features, but the innate technique itself does not automatically grant a Domain.

Rules integration

Edge cases and shared systems.

These rulings are designed around the revised Player Guide rather than the raw PDF where the two differ.

Delayed percentage burn is not part of the attack and does not double on criticals.
Partner is a technique summon; if technique suppression affects the user, partner becomes inert.
Stance changing does not change ability scores or creature size.
True Black Flame uses the printed percentage caps to keep boss HP from becoming an automatic deletion.
Technique DC: 8 + proficiency bonus + your chosen Cursed Energy ability modifier (Wisdom or Charisma unless your class says otherwise).
Direct criticals: rolled weapon, unarmed, and CE attacks can critical normally. After a qualifying critical, roll the campaign's unmodified Black Flash confirmation d20; a natural 20 triples the completed critical damage. Autonomous Domain events, damage-over-time, hazards, and unrolled pulses cannot Black Flash.
Maximum Output: unless a feature says otherwise, double that feature's base CE cost and enhance one supported axis—damage, range, duration, or accuracy/DC—using the campaign's Maximum Output rules. Named Maximum Techniques and Domains do not receive generic Maximum Output.
Technique Focus: effects marked Focus occupy the shared Technique Focus slot. Opening a Domain ends other Focus effects unless the Domain expressly preserves them.
Two-technique characters: these guides assume each technique progresses from character/class level as agreed by the campaign. Multiclassing itself does not grant another Innate Technique.
