Black Flame Apostasy
Godskin Twin Rite

Alternate between elastic Apostle reach and crushing Noble mass while god-slaying black flame eats a percentage of whatever survives the hit.

Power tierTier IV · Special Grade
Combat roleStance fighter / attrition summoner
Domain / pinnacleNone — pinnacle is the Twin Rite
Core law

What the technique actually does.

Rules are written to plug into the finalized campaign glossary: normal actions stay normal, direct attacks roll, and only named Domain events bypass the initial roll.

Bonus action · 1 CE

Godskin Stance

Choose Apostle or Noble for 1 minute. Apostle grants +10 speed, +5-foot reach on melee attacks, and Dex-based mobility. Noble grants +1 AC, advantage on checks/saves against forced movement, and counts one size larger for grapples.

On hit · no action · 1 CE

Kindling

After a direct weapon or CE attack hits, spend 1 CE to leave the target Kindled until the start of its next turn. A Kindled creature cannot regain HP, and at the start of its turn, if it is still Kindled, it takes necrotic damage equal to 2% of its maximum HP (minimum PB, maximum 5 × your level). A creature can suffer this delayed burn once per round.

Manifest

Godskin Armament

As part of entering stance, form a Twinblade in Apostle stance or Stitcher in Noble stance. Use any proficient equivalent weapon profile; the armament counts as cursed.

The fiction. Registry scholars describe the flame as heresy with an appetite: it is the cursed energy of something that once killed gods, and it learned hunger before it learned obedience. The prevailing theory holds that the god-skin the technique burns is not fuel but memory — the user's CE reproduces the moment a mortal's flame proved stronger than a god, and the percent-burn is the flame still convinced every body contains something divine worth eating.

Full progression

Level 1 through 20.

Milestones use the campaign’s familiar 1 / 3 / 6 / 11 / 18 structure and add an explicit level-20 mastery feature.

1Vestments of the Godhunt

Godskin Stance

Gain Godskin Stance (bonus action, 1 CE: choose Apostle or Noble for 1 minute — Apostle: +10 speed, +5-foot reach on melee attacks, Dex-based mobility; Noble: +1 AC, advantage on checks/saves against forced movement, counts one size larger for grapples), Godskin Armament (as part of entering stance, form a Twinblade in Apostle stance or Stitcher in Noble stance using any proficient equivalent weapon profile; the armament counts as cursed), and Kindling (on hit, no action, 1 CE: after a direct weapon or CE attack hits, the target is Kindled until the start of its next turn; a Kindled creature cannot regain HP, and at the start of its turn, if still Kindled, it takes necrotic damage equal to 2% of its maximum HP, minimum PB and maximum 5 × level; a creature suffers this delayed burn once per round).

Swaddling Recovery

Once per turn when your direct melee attack deals damage, gain temporary HP equal PB. If you already have temporary HP, instead heal 1 HP per PB; you cannot be healed above half your maximum HP by this feature.

Scaling

Range 30 feet.

3Black Flame Scripture

Black Flame Orb

Action · 2 CE: ranged CE attack 90 feet; on a hit the target is Kindled.

Scouring Black Flame

Action · 3 CE: 30-foot cone; creatures in the area make a Dexterity save. On a failure the target is Kindled and blinded until the start of your next turn; on a success, no effect.

Twin Detonation

Once per turn · no action · 1 CE: when you hit a Kindled creature while in the opposite stance from the one that Kindled it, consume the Kindling — the delayed burn triggers immediately, and the eruption forces a Strength save; on a failure the target is knocked prone.

Ritual Circle

Action · 3 CE: create 15-foot-radius black-flame ring for 1 minute, Focus. The first time each turn a creature crosses its edge, it is Kindled.

Scaling

Range extends to 60 feet.

6Second Skin

Apostle Extension

Apostle melee reach becomes 15 feet. Once per turn after a hit, move 10 feet without provoking from that target.

Noble Roll

Bonus action · 2 CE: move up to your speed in a line through hostile spaces. Each creature whose space you enter makes a Strength save; on a failure it is knocked prone and dragged 10 feet along your path; on a success, no effect.

Stance Cancel

Once per turn after resolving a stance-specific feature, switch stance without an action and without CE.

Scaling

Range extends to 90 feet.

11The Duo Rite

Skinbound Partner

Action · 8 CE · Focus · 1 minute: manifest the opposite stance as a Medium shikigami-like partner. AC 14+PB, HP 8×level, speed 40. It acts after you and can move plus make one Godskin Armament attack; its armament attack applies Kindling for 0 CE on a hit. It cannot use your Domain/Maximum.

Shared Health Rite

When your partner takes damage, you may use your reaction to take any amount of that damage instead. When you take damage, your partner may absorb up to 5 × PB of that damage from one instance per round.

Black Flame Tornado

Action · 7 CE: creatures within 15 feet make Dexterity saves. On a failure the target is Kindled and pulled 10 feet toward you; on a success, no effect.

Scaling

Range extends to 120 feet.

18Apostasy Refuses Death

Resurrection Chant

If Skinbound Partner reaches 0 HP while you remain conscious, it reforms at full HP after 2 rounds if you spend 5 CE at the end of each of those turns. Damage to you can interrupt via Technique Focus checks.

Maximum: Godskin Sabbath

Action · 10 CE: you and your partner (or a phantom opposite form if your partner is absent) sweep a 60-foot path. Creatures crossed make Dexterity saves; on a failure they are knocked prone and Twin-Kindled — at the start of their turn the delayed burn triggers twice; on a success they are Kindled only.

No Domain

This technique does not naturally form a Domain. Its binding identity is two bodies sharing one rite, not an imposed barrier law.

Scaling

Range extends to 180 feet.

20Godhunt Restored

True Black Flame

Delayed Black Flame burn becomes 3% of maximum HP (minimum PB, maximum 10 × your level). It ignores fire resistance but not fire immunity; the necrotic portion remains.

Duo Without Focus

Skinbound Partner no longer requires Technique Focus, though only one partner may exist.

Gloam Hunt

Once per turn, when both you and your partner hit the same creature before your next turn, the second hit consumes any Kindling for the immediate double-burn — the burn triggers immediately, twice — and the target cannot regain HP until the start of your next turn.

Scaling

Range extends to 300 feet.

Pinnacle expression

Domain, Maximum, or equivalent.

Not every technique needs a Domain. When one exists, its sure-hit is a unique autonomous initiative-count-20 event unless the Domain is explicitly rule-based.

No natural Domain Black Flame Apostasy reaches its pinnacle through the Twin Rite, resurrection, and restored god-hunting flame. A character may still learn generic barrier techniques or Simple Domain through class features, but the innate technique itself does not automatically grant a Domain.

Innate Technique Feats

Four build-defining options.

These broaden the law instead of merely inflating the same number.

Prerequisite: Black Flame Apostasy

Apostle’s Reach

In Apostle stance, your opportunity attacks may apply Kindling, paying its normal CE cost — the long limb punishes retreat with godfire.

Prerequisite: Black Flame Apostasy

Shared Vestments

As a bonus action for 1 CE, extend your Godskin Stance to one willing creature within 15 feet until the start of your next turn. It gains the stance’s passive benefits — Apostle’s speed and reach, or Noble’s armor and stability — but not your armament, Black Flame, or stance actions.

Prerequisite: Swaddling Recovery

Swaddling Cloth

When you would gain temporary HP from Swaddling Recovery, you may instead grant those temporary HP to one willing creature within 15 feet, wrapping it in spectral swaddling cloth.

Prerequisite: Skinbound Partner

Choir of Two

Partner gains proficiency in one skill you have; while within 30 feet, you may perceive through its senses without an action.

Rules integration

Edge cases and shared systems.

These rulings are designed around the revised Player Guide rather than the raw PDF where the two differ.

Delayed percentage burn is not part of the attack and does not double on criticals.
Partner is a technique summon; if technique suppression affects the user, partner becomes inert.
Stance changing does not change ability scores or creature size.
True Black Flame uses the printed percentage caps to keep boss HP from becoming an automatic deletion.
Technique DC: 8 + proficiency bonus + your chosen Cursed Energy ability modifier (Wisdom or Charisma unless your class says otherwise).
Direct criticals: rolled weapon, unarmed, and CE attacks can critical normally. After a qualifying critical, roll the campaign's unmodified Black Flash confirmation d20; a natural 20 triples the completed critical damage. Autonomous Domain events, damage-over-time, hazards, and unrolled pulses cannot Black Flash.
Maximum Output: unless a feature says otherwise, double that feature's base CE cost and enhance one supported axis—damage, range, duration, or accuracy/DC—using the campaign's Maximum Output rules. Named Maximum Techniques and Domains do not receive generic Maximum Output.
Technique Focus: effects marked Focus occupy the shared Technique Focus slot. Opening a Domain ends other Focus effects unless the Domain expressly preserves them.
Two-technique characters: these guides assume each technique progresses from character/class level as agreed by the campaign. Multiclassing itself does not grant another Innate Technique.
