Projection Sorcery

A declared 24-frame route converts strict movement into accelerating speed, impact dice, and frame-stun vulnerability.

The fiction. Registry notes describe Projection Sorcery as discipline that pre-rendered the world: the user's CE was so ritual-bound that it began declaring movement before the body performed it, and the declaration became the law. The twenty-four frames are the sorcerer rendering themselves like film — each second storyboarded, each route unbreakable. The frame-stun is the technique's cruelest lesson: it forces the victim's body to obey the 24th frame too.

Full progression

Feature map

1Projection.

Activate Projection as a bonus action for 1 CE and pay 1 CE at the start of each turn. While active, your walking speed increases by 10 feet and the Attack action contains one additional attack.

When activated and at the end of every turn thereafter, declare a legal route for your next turn as a sequence of spaces up to your current speed. On that turn you may stop early but cannot leave the route, reverse, add a new turn, or pass through an unknown obstruction. Forced movement is not part of the route.

If you willingly break the route, hit an unexpected obstruction, or cannot enter the next declared space, movement ends and Projection ends after the current action. Teleportation does not count as walking the route. The additional attack is part of the Attack action and cannot stack with another extra-attack source beyond the core limit.

3Acceleration.

Acceleration. After each consecutive turn in which you move at least half your speed on-route, add 10 feet to speed, up to 10 × PB additional feet. Once per turn after moving at least 10 feet in one straight, uninterrupted segment, a melee hit can add 1d6 per 10 feet of that segment.

624 Frame Rule.

24 Frame Rule. After an unarmed hit, deal no damage and pay 3 CE. On a failed Intelligence save the target is stunned until the start of your next turn. The first attack that hits before then is automatically critical and applies one penetration step, then the stun ends. On a successful save, no CE is refunded.

11Fastest Sorcerer Alive.

Fastest Sorcerer Alive. Activation immediately adds 30 feet. At the start of each paid turn add 20 feet, capped at 20 × PB additional feet from this feature; losing Projection clears both acceleration pools. Dexterity +2 and maximum +2 apply only while active.

Pinnacle expression

Maximum, Domain, or equivalent.

Domain Expansion — Lunar Palace Womb of Time: 18 — full-turn action, 20 CE; 60-foot-radius enclosed Domain centered on you; 1 minute; Burnout 10 rounds. Sure-Hit Event — Lunar Palace Womb of Time: at initiative count 20 each round, every chosen creature that moved or took any action since the last event takes 8d10 necrotic damage as its cells rebel against the 24fps rule, with no attack roll and no initial save. A creature protected by an anti-domain defense makes a Constitution saving throw vs your CE save DC (invented); on a success it takes half damage.

Innate Technique Feats

Build-defining options.

These are optional character-building feats. You must meet each listed prerequisite before selecting it.

Prerequisite: 24 Frame Rule

White Space

Reaction when targeted by an attack, 2 CE: step between frames. Move up to 15 feet to an unoccupied space you can see without provoking opportunity attacks; the triggering attack resolves against your new position and misses automatically if you left its range or reach. You may use this only while Projection is active.

Prerequisite: 24 Frame Rule

Air Shatter

When you complete a straight, uninterrupted segment of at least 20 feet on your declared route, use a bonus action and 3 CE to shatter the displaced air. Creatures in a 30-foot line along the segment you just traveled make a Dexterity save, taking CE modifier d10 force on failure or half on success. Roll damage once.

Prerequisite: Fastest Sorcerer Alive and CE score 20

Maximum: Sakuga

Action, 10 CE: declare a complete route of up to twelve 5-foot frames. Until the end of your next turn you execute it at blinding speed - you may take the Attack action at any point along the route, splitting movement before and after, opportunity attacks against you have disadvantage, and you may pass through hostile spaces. Deviating from the declared route ends the effect.

Rules integration

Edge cases and shared systems.

Rules priority: the reviewed rulings, explicit codex clarifications, and the level progression always control when original wording conflicts. Legacy Domain auto-hit language, action terminology, event timing, missing saves, and older scaling never override revised player rules.
Why Tier III: even without an optimized Dexterity score, sustained Projection adds attacks, extreme speed, burst damage, and a critical-hit setup that shapes every encounter map.
