Completed rules

  • Activate as a bonus action for 1 CE and pay 1 CE at the start of each turn. While active, walking speed increases by 10 feet and the Attack action contains one additional attack. When activated and at the end of every turn thereafter, declare a legal route for your next turn as a sequence of spaces up to current speed. On that turn you may stop early but cannot leave the route, reverse, add a new turn, or pass through an unknown obstruction. Forced movement is not part of the route.
  • If you willingly break the route, hit an unexpected obstruction, or cannot enter the next declared space, movement ends and Projection ends after the current action. Teleportation does not count as walking the route. The additional attack is part of the Attack action and cannot stack with another extra-attack source beyond the core limit.
  • Acceleration: after each consecutive turn in which you move at least half your speed on-route, add 10 feet to speed, up to 10 × PB additional feet. Once per turn after moving at least 10 feet in one straight, uninterrupted segment, a melee hit can add 1d6 per 10 feet of that segment.
  • 24 Frame Rule: after an unarmed hit, deal no damage and pay 3 CE. On failed Intelligence save, stunned until the start of your next turn. The first attack that hits before then is automatically critical and applies one penetration step, then the stun ends. On a successful save, no CE is refunded.
  • Fastest Sorcerer: activation immediately adds 30 feet. At the start of each paid turn add 20 feet, capped at 20 × PB additional feet from this feature; losing Projection clears both acceleration pools. Dexterity +2 and maximum +2 apply only while active.
  • Domain — Frame Sentence: at initiative 20, each chosen hostile automatically receives a twenty-four-frame script until the next initiative 20. The first time before then it moves at least 5 feet or uses an action, bonus action, or reaction, it takes 8d10 necrotic damage and its speed becomes 0 until the end of that turn. A protected creature makes an Intelligence save at the pulse, avoiding the script on a success. Ordinary Projection attacks roll normally.

Power identity

Why Tier III: even without an optimized Dexterity score, sustained Projection adds attacks, extreme speed, burst damage, and a critical-hit setup that shapes every encounter map.

Pinnacle

Prerequisite: Fastest Sorcerer Alive and CE score 20

Bonus action, 1 CE; for up to 1 minute with Technique Focus pay 1 CE at each turn’s start. Speed doubles, Dexterity saves have advantage, and you gain one restricted extra action for one weapon attack, Dash, Disengage, Hide, or Use an Object. It stacks with distinct extra-action sources unless Sakuga itself is duplicated.

24 Frame Rule
Once you reach 6th level, you have learned how to affect creatures
with the 24 FPS rule, making them become stuck in one frame of
animation. While your cursed technique is active, when you hit an
unarmed strike on a creature you may choose to deal no damage by
spending 3 Cursed Energy, forcing the creature that received the hit
to make an Intelligence saving throw. On a failure they will be stuck
in one frame of animation, making them stunned until the beginning of
your next turn. On a success, nothing happens. The creature loses its
stunned condition early if it is hit by an attack roll which will
automatically count as a critical and will treat immunity as
resistance and resistance as normal.
Fastest Sorcerer Alive
At 11th level, due to your advanced studies, you can now reach a
higher speed level than any sorcerer ever could. While your cursed
technique is active, you will gain the following benefits:
● You will gain +30 feet to your walking speed.
● You will gain +20 feet to your walking speed at the beginning
of your turns.
● You will gain advantage on Dexterity saving throws.
● Your Dexterity score increases by +2 and so does your maximum.
Domain Expansion: Lunar Palace Womb of Time
Finally, at 18th level, you reach the pinnacle of Projection
Sorcery. All creatures in your domain take 8d10 Necrotic damage when
they move or take any type of action, their cells don't move at the
24fps rule.
