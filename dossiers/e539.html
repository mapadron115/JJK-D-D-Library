Core law

What the technique actually does.

Core Engine, Reversed Incantation. Magic that runs on stagecraft and nerve: each spell is a command word spoken backwards, aloud, at the table, with cursed energy paying the effect's real cost. Refuse the performance or fumble the word and the spell costs 2 extra CE — the stumble tax. Speak the command forwards and it rebounds onto you instead; the universe heard you fine, you just aimed it wrong.

Field notes. Subject: a showman with a loaded deck. Three truths govern her. First: the backwards speech is not flavor, it is the targeting system — the reversal tells reality which way is out, and CE pays for the exit. Second: the tax is honest; magic done without conviction costs more, because the universe charges interest on hesitation. Third: the rebound is not a punishment, it is physics — a forwards word is still a word, and words go somewhere. Mark the economy: she is the cheapest reality-warper alive exactly as long as she commits to the bit.

Full progression

Level 1 through 20.

1Backwards Magic

You know three verbs. Speak each backwards aloud to cast at its cost: "EVOM EHT TCEJBO" — action · 1 CE · telekinesis: move an object or a Large-or-smaller creature 30 feet (creature may make a Strength save to resist). "ERIF" — action · 1 CE · ranged CE attack, 2d10 fire damage on a hit. "DLEIHS" — reaction · 1 CE · +3 AC against one attack, or resistance to one damage instance.

Three words, said wrong on purpose: move it, burn it, stop it. The stage lights are on and the universe is a volunteer from the audience.

3Stagecraft

Action · 2 CE · "NOISULLI": weave sight and sound into a 20-foot-cube illusion for 10 minutes (investigation vs your CE save DC to disbelieve). Bonus action · 1 CE · "ESAPAHC": vanish — invisible until the end of your next turn or until you attack or cast.

Misdirection is a spell with better lighting: the eye goes where she points, and where she was standing is suddenly a matter of opinion.

6Bigger Words

Three new verbs: "LAEH" — action · 2 CE · restore 1d8 + WIS HP to a touched creature. "TROPELET" — bonus action · 2 CE · teleport 60 feet to a space you can see. "EERF" — action · 2 CE · a creature makes a Constitution save or is restrained in ice for 1 minute (save ends; the ice breaks early if it takes 10+ fire damage at once).

The vocabulary grows teeth: mend, relocate, and hold still. Every word still backwards, every effect still billed in full.

11Countercharm

Reaction · 3 CE · "LLEPS EHT OTUAH": when a technique, spell, or magical effect manifests within 60 feet, contest it — make a CE ability check against the originator's CE save DC. On a success the effect unravels entirely.

Every spell has an undo button and she knows where it is: say the magic backwards-er, and watch someone else's miracle file for a refund.

18The Headliner

Passive · Your stumble tax drops to +1 CE, and your verbs' damage increases by 1d8. Once per long rest, you may cast two verbs with one action by paying both costs + 1 CE — speak both backwards, back to back, no pause.

Twenty years of stage time, compounded: the words come faster, hit harder, and occasionally double-book. The audience has never seen the trick; the universe has never stopped falling for it.

Pinnacle expression

Maximum, Domain, or equivalent.

Maximum, The Backwards Word (10 CE): speak one full sentence backwards, aloud, without stopping: rewrite up to three elements of the scene for 1 minute — terrain, weather, creatures' forms, the position of every object and creature in a 120-foot radius (GM adjudicates scope; cannot undo death or affect deities). Stumbling no longer ends the effect early — instead, each stumble costs 2 CE to push through. Additionally, once during the minute, speak a single creature's true name backwards as a bonus action: it makes a WIS save or is banished to a harmless demiplane until the Maximum ends (legendary resistances apply normally). For one minute, the stage directions are hers.

Feats

Build-defining options.

Escape Artist

When you are grappled or restrained, use a bonus action · 1 CE to automatically escape — no check. It was a trick all along; the ropes were never tied.

The Prestige

Once per long rest, when damage would drop you to 0 HP, you may immediately cast TROPELET as a free action to teleport 60 feet before falling unconscious. The audience gasps. You were never there.

Stage Fright

When you cast a verb, one creature of your choice within 30 feet that can see you makes a WIS save or is frightened of you until the end of your next turn. Showmanship has a body count of nerves.

Rabbit Out of the Hat

"TAH EHT FO TUO": action · 1 CE · conjure one nonmagical object (worth 25 gp or less, 10 lb or less) into your hand for 1 hour. It was in the hat the whole time. It was always in the hat.

Rules integration

Edge cases and shared systems.

Codex rulings: Direct rolled technique/weapon attacks can use the campaign's Black Flash procedure when otherwise eligible. Passive damage, hazards, summons acting independently, and initiative-20 Domain events cannot.
Generic Maximum Output applies only to ordinary features, never named Maxima or Domains.
The stumble tax and rebound apply per casting; a silenced caster cannot speak verbs at all and must pay the tax or forgo the spell.
