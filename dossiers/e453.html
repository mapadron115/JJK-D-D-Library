Cursed Tanto

A short blade for the last argument, it defends your honor, or ends it on your own terms.

Field notes. Subject: the last argument. Three truths govern it. First, the turning: as a reaction to an attack against you, roll 1d6 and raise your AC against that blow, and if the blade turns it aside, the tanto strikes back in the same breath, should the attacker be within reach. Second, the final clause, written in the oldest ink: as an action, the honorable death, disembowel yourself, die with no saving throws, and five rounds later (ten minutes, out of combat) return as a vengeful cursed spirit under indefinite madness. The duel's last argument is to refuse the ending. Third, honor must be bonded to be borrowed: attunement is required. Hence the maxim: the blade that answers every strike need never win the duel, it need only outlast it.

Features & procedures

Properties & techniques

Exact player-facing procedures

Honored Edge

Passive · Melee weapon attack (dagger): deals 1d6 damage on a hit.

The honest part: a duelist's dagger, nothing more, until it is needed for more. This weapon deals 1d6 damage on a hit.

Turning the Blade

Reaction · No CE · When attacked: roll 1d6 and add it to your AC against the triggering attack; if this turns the attack into a miss, make an attack with the tanto as part of the same reaction if the attacker is within range.

The blade that answers: turn the blow aside and the tanto strikes back in the same breath, if the attacker is within reach. As a reaction to an attack made against you, you can roll 1d6 and add the result to your AC against the triggering attack, potentially causing it to miss. If this causes the attack to miss, you can make an attack with the Tanto as part of the reaction if the creature is within range.

Honorable Death

Action · No CE · Commit seppuku with the tanto: you die with no saving throws; after 5 rounds (10 minutes out of combat) you return as a Vengeful Cursed Spirit under Indefinite Madness.

The final clause, written in the oldest ink. Read it twice before you sign. As an action while holding the tanto, you can commit Seppuku and disembowel yourself with the weapon. You die without any saving throws, and after 5 rounds (10 minutes if out of combat) have passed, you return as a Vengeful Cursed Spirit under the effects of Indefinite Madness, as stated in Cursed Upon Death.

Rules integration

Edge cases and shared systems.

Campaign canon notes

Cursed-tool attunement: you have PB attunement slots (2–6). This tool occupies one slot and requires a one-hour ritual. Attunement ends voluntarily over a short rest, on death, or when you replace it while over capacity. Distance does not break attunement. Cursed-tool attunement is separate from magic-item attunement.
Note: Turning the Blade's riposte is part of the same reaction, made only if the AC bonus turns the triggering attack into a miss and the attacker is within the tanto's range. Honorable Death uses the campaign's Cursed Upon Death and Indefinite Madness rules; your return is governed by them.
