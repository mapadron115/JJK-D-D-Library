Core law

Completed rules

Attack substitutions:

Stingers and drills are attack substitutions, not new actions — one cursed energy trades a swing for a barb. Scorpion and Drill replace attacks of the Attack action for 1 CE—they are attack substitutions, not full actions for every stinger. Scorpion’s printed bonus is its grade-related attack/damage progression, not a second bonus added again from the tool chapter.

Hair Spray bonus action:

The spray is a bonus action, once per turn: advantage on an attack or disadvantage on a save, each with its own surcharge. Hair Spray is a bonus action, correcting the contradictory “special free action” phrase. One Spray use per turn: choose either attack mode with advantage/base cost +2 CE or saving-throw mode with disadvantage/base cost +4 CE. A single attack with an attached explosion uses the chosen attack mode; it does not gain both benefits for one surcharge. Hair Spray does not grant another bonus action alongside helicopter Dash.

Explosive Drill misses:

A missed drill still lands somewhere — declare the payment first, then place the scatter honestly. declare explosive Drill payment before the attack. On a miss, no hit-target impact is invented: choose a legal surface impact or scatter point determined before resolving victims, with no direct piercing damage. Source omits miss placement; this remains a flagged fill.

Helicopter reach:

The rotor's reach is five feet, ten with Spray — the paragraph's silence filled by campaign measure. helicopter base melee reach 5 ft, 10 ft with Hair Spray, because its paragraph omits reach.

Jet Dash speed:

The jet's Dash is flying speed added to the turn's own movement — and each creature is crashed through once per line. Jet’s Dash grant is flying speed, in addition to unused ordinary movement; do not multiply the whole turn’s movement again for each Dash. A crash can affect a creature once per straight-line Dash, not every occupied square. Collision distance counts travel before hitting that target.

Silken Strand Execution Ground law:

The Domain's law counts real distance, not spent movement — teleportation crosses nothing, forced marches count all. a harmful movement-triggered Domain law, not a generic initiative-20 blast. Count actual distance traveled within the Domain, including forced movement; teleportation crosses no intervening squares. Carry distance remainder per creature across moves in the same turn, clear remainder at turn end; each full 5 ft triggers once. Difficult terrain affects movement expenditure, not distance traveled, so 5 actual feet costs 10 movement but triggers only one packet. Anti-domain protection prevents the law’s damage while protected. Jet’s failed 30-ft push therefore can trigger 12d10 slashing, in addition to normal crash and any real collision. Resolve movement packets in order: death or a genuinely blocking obstacle can end movement; damage alone does not automatically halt it. No triggers for announcing movement, remaining still, being prone in place or counting both creature footprint squares. The Domain does not damage its caster.

Field notes. Subject: the weaver whose loom is her own body. Three truths govern her. First, the energy refused to leave: where other techniques project outward, hers colonized the deadest part of the self — and so stingers, drills, and rotors all grow from the scalp, every strand a stored decision. Second, the forms escalate like an armory: the scorpion's barb for the patient strike, the drill for the distant one, and at 6th level the aircraft — helicopter blades and a crashing jet, the hair become vehicle. Third, the Domain is the honest capstone: the user's hair has become the terrain itself, and everything that moves through it is trespassing — 2d10 slashing for every five feet, the whole field made difficult ground. Mark this: she does not wield a weapon. She is the ground the weapon grows from, and the Domain is her scalp laid over the world.

Full progression

Feature map

1Scorpion Hair.

The first form: a barbed stinger traded for an attack, reaching ten feet, with a barb that only sharpens with level. Attack action: 1 CE replaces an attack with a barbed stinger—melee CE attack, 10 ft, 1d10 + CE modifier piercing; +1/+2/+3/+4 attack and damage at 3rd/6th/11th/18th.

3Drill Hair.

The second form: a ranged drill for the distant fight, with an optional explosion bought two energy at a time. Attack action: 1 CE replaces an attack with a ranged drill—30 ft, 1d6 + CE modifier piercing (60 ft at 6th). Optional +2 CE: 5-ft explosion, DEX save, 3d6 fire (5d6 at 6th, 8d6 at 11th, 10d6 at 18th), half on success.

6Aircraft Hair.

The complex forms: a helicopter of whirling blades or a jet that crashes through the line — the hair become aircraft. As an action for 3 CE, you form your hair into either a Jet or Helicopter for 1 minute and can spend 1 CE to keep the form active for 1 minute. Switching requires another Aircraft activation.

Helicopter Hair. You gain a flying speed equal to your walking speed and can take the Dash action as a bonus action. When you take the attack action, you can spend 2 cursed energy to replace one attack by extending your blades and attempting to slash an enemy. Make a melee cursed energy attack, on a hit you deal 6d8 + your CE modifier slashing damage. This damage increases to 8d8 at 11th level, and 10d8 at 18th level. A creature that hits you with an Opportunity attack takes slashing damage equal to your CE modifier. This becomes twice your CE modifier at 11th level.

Jet Hair. You gain a flying speed equal to your walking speed. When you take the dash action while flying, you not only do not provoke opportunity attacks, but can also choose to move in a straight line and crash through creatures. All creatures in your path must make a Dexterity saving throw. On a failed save, they take Xd8 bludgeoning damage and are pushed back 30 ft away from you and knocked prone. If they impact a solid surface, they take 1d4 bludgeoning for every 10 ft you traveled in a straight line before crashing into them. On a successful save they take half as much damage and are not pushed or knocked prone. (X = CE modifier) At 11th level, taking the Dash action while flying you instead move up to twice your flying speed. This increases to three times your flying speed at 18th level.

11Hair Spray.

The hair hardens to a graded tool and lengthens the reach — and once per turn, an attack or a save may be bought as a bonus action. melee reach of your techniques +5 ft; hair counts as a Grade 1 Cursed Tool (Special Grade at 18th). Once per turn, activate an attack-roll feature as a bonus action (+2 CE, advantage) or a save-forcing feature as a bonus action (+4 CE, disadvantage).

18Silken Strand Execution Ground.

The Domain is the scalp laid over the world: every five feet of movement taxed in slashing, and the ground itself resisting. Domain Expansion. Creatures other than you take 2d10 slashing per 5 ft moved within the Domain; the Domain is difficult terrain for them. Jet’s failed 30-ft push can trigger 12d10 slashing inside.

Pinnacle expression

Maximum, Domain, or equivalent.

Silken Strand Execution Ground — the movement-punishing law, the difficult terrain, the trespass priced in strands. Domain Expansion — Silken Strand Execution Ground: 18 — creatures other than you take 2d10 slashing damage for every 5 ft they move within your domain as infinitesimally small and sharp hairs cut into them with their every movement. Your domain counts as difficult terrain for creatures other than you. Count actual distance traveled including forced movement; teleportation crosses no intervening squares; carry remainders per creature across moves in the same turn, cleared at turn end.

Innate Technique Feats

Build-defining options.

These are optional character-building feats. You must meet each listed prerequisite before selecting it.

Prerequisite: Must have Scorpion Hair

Scorpion Tactics

The barb's three maneuvers: a riposte against the attacker, a grapple in place of damage, a barb left to work deeper. You have trained with your hair and unlocked the following maneuvers. Barbed Defense: as a reaction to being targeted by an attack, you can make a Scorpion Hair attack against the attacker if they are within range. Hair Wrapping: on a hit with your Scorpion Hair, you can choose to deal no damage and instead grapple the creature; you can use your Cursed Energy modifier to make grapple checks with your hair. Embedded Barb: once per turn when you hit with your Scorpion Hair, you can spend 1 CE to leave a barb embedded in the wound — at the start of the creature's next turn it takes 1d6 piercing damage as the barb works deeper, then dissolves.

Prerequisite: Must have Drill Hair

Mega-Drill

One drill, made enormous: an eighty-foot line of piercing ruin that ignores cover and deals its maximum to structures. You can now use more cursed energy to imbue one Mega-drill. As an action for 4 cursed energy, you can shoot this Mega-drill in a 5 ft wide, 80 ft long line. Creatures in this line must make a Dexterity saving throw. On a failure, it deals 6d10 piercing damage. On a success, they take half the damage. This technique deals maximum damage to objects and structures and ignores half and three quarters cover. The damage of this technique increases to 8d10 at 6th level and becomes 10d10 at 11th level.

Prerequisite: Must have Aircraft Hair

Air Bomber

The aircraft's destructive rites: the helicopter's frenzy, and the jet's crash angled into a cone of shockwave. You have learned the following destructive ways to amplify your Aircraft Hair forms. Copter Frenzy: as an action for 5 CE, you force all creatures within 15 ft to make a Dexterity saving throw. On a failed save they take your Helicopter Hair damage. On a successful save, they take half as much. Jet Crash: when you crash your Jet Hair into a creature or surface, you can spend 2 CE to angle the impact — creatures in a 15 ft cone behind the point of impact must make a Dexterity saving throw, taking half the crash damage on a failed save as the shockwave carries through, or no damage on a success.

Rules integration

Edge cases and shared systems.

Rules priority: the reviewed rulings, explicit codex clarifications, and the level progression always control when original wording conflicts. Legacy Domain auto-hit language, action terminology, event timing, missing saves, and older scaling never override revised player rules.
Why Tier III: a full blaster kit—stingers, explosive drills, 6–10d8 helicopter blades, crashing jets, and a movement-punishing Domain—but no sure-hit blast or fate-level control; the printed high dice are preserved rather than cut to generic weapon damage.
Structures: structures can be hit hard; no arbitrary once-per-round restriction replaces the source once-per-Dash proposal. Mega-Drill deals maximum damage to objects and structures.

Additional material.

Hair Manipulation
Your technique allows you to form your hair into a variety of shapes.

Cursed Energy
 Starting at 1st level, you can harness your negative feelings into a
usable source of energy, called cursed energy. These feelings can be
harnessed into attacks or abilities. The number of cursed energy you
possess is based on your class, some classes use a modifier that
being charisma or wisdom (Player’s choice)
Your cursed energy DC is 8 + user cursed energy ability modifier +
proficiency bonus.

Scorpion Hair
 Beginning at 1st level, you have learned to move your hair into
different forms using cursed energy. When you take the Attack action,
you can spend 1 CE to replace an attack by shaping your hair into a
barbed stinger. Make a melee cursed energy attack against a creature
within 10 ft of you. On a hit you deal 1d10 + your CE modifier
piercing damage. You gain a +1 bonus to attack and damage rolls with
this attack at 3rd level. This increases to a +2 bonus at 6th level,
+3 bonus at 11th level and +4 bonus at 18th level.

Drill Hair
 At 3rd level, you have learned to form your hair into drill bits and
shoot them off as projectiles. When you take the Attack action, you
can spend 1 cursed energy to replace an attack with a ranged cursed
energy attack roll with a range of 30ft. This attack deals 1d6 + your
CE modifier piercing damage. The range of this technique increases to
60 ft at 6th level.
 When attacking with a drill, you can spend an additional 2 cursed
energy to make the drill explode in a 5 ft radius upon the point of
impact causing creatures in range to make a Dexterity saving throw. On
a failure, they take an additional 3d6 fire damage. On a success, they
take half as much damage. The fire damage increases to 5d6 at 6th
level, 8d6 at 11th level and 10d6 at 18th level.

Aircraft Hair
 At 6th level you have learned to make more complex constructs from

and can take the Dash action as a bonus action. When you take the
attack action, you can spend 2 cursed energy to replace one attack by
extending your blades and attempting to slash an enemy. Make a melee
cursed energy attack, on a hit you deal 6d8 + your CE modifier
slashing damage. This damage increases to 8d8 at 11th level, and 10d8
at 18th level. A creature that hits you with an Opportunity attack
takes slashing damage equal to your CE modifier. This becomes twice
your CE modifier at 11th level.

Jet Hair. You gain a flying speed equal to your walking speed. When
you take the dash action while flying, you not only do not provoke
opportunity attacks, but can also choose to move in a straight line and
crash through creatures. All creatures in your path must make a
Dexterity saving throw. On a failed save, they take Xd8 bludgeoning

they impact a solid surface, they take 1d4 bludgeoning for every 10 ft
you traveled in a straight line before crashing into them. On a
successful save they take half as much damage and are not pushed or
knocked prone. (X = CE modifier)
 At 11th level, taking the Dash action while flying you instead move

Hair Spray
 At 11th level, your hair has become almost indestructible, allowing
you to grow it to longer lengths and activate your techniques more
freely. The melee reach of your techniques increases by 5 ft and your
hair counts as a Grade 1 Cursed Tool. At 18th level, your hair counts
as a Special Grade Cursed Tool.
 Once per turn you can spend 2 additional CE to activate one of your
technique features requiring an attack roll as a bonus action. An
attack roll made using this special free action is made at advantage.
Alternatively, once per turn you can spend 4 CE to activate one of
your technique features requiring a saving throw as a bonus action. A
saving throw forced by this special free action is made at
disadvantage.
