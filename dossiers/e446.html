Cursed Seal Stamp

A small stamp bearing your clan's seal, a signature that Heaven itself can read.

Field notes. Subject: a signature Heaven can read. Three truths govern it. First, distance dissolves: for one coin of cursed energy the stamp signs a binding vow into a contract without the parties ever meeting, long vows and distant vows travel by seal. Second, the ink is honest: alter one word of the contract and the stamp dies, to be reapplied, tamper-evident as doctrine, trust kept in wax. Third, it signs but never writes: the stamp does not create the vow's terms; the binding vow still follows the campaign's law. The tool witnesses; it never authors. Hence the maxim: the strongest vow is written so clearly it needs no witness, and this stamp is the witness anyway.

Seal of Agreement

Spend 1 CE · Stamp a contract to agree to a binding vow within its contents · Used for long or complex vows, or to make a vow without meeting in person.

One coin of cursed energy, and the clan's seal lands where the hand cannot reach. Long vows, distant vows, the stamp carries them both. By spending 1 CE, you can stamp a contract with your seal to agree to a binding vow within the contents of a contract. These are usually used for long and complicated binding vows, or to make a binding vow without meeting in person.

Tamper-Evident

Passive · If the contract or its contents are changed or altered in any way, the stamp is invalidated and must be reapplied.

Mark the honesty of the ink: change one word of the contract and the seal dies. It must be reapplied, trust, but in wax. If the contract or the contents of the contract is changed or altered in any way, the stamp is invalidated and must be reapplied.
