Nine Knots

Bind motion, distance, wounds, and relationships with cursed cord. The technique is simple to learn and frighteningly flexible once several knots overlap.

Core law

Core Gameplay Loop

Cursed Cord

You manifest cursed cord from your hands. It has 30-foot reach for technique purposes, cannot be used as mundane rope by another creature, and vanishes when detached from all your Knots.

Knot Capacity

You can maintain active Knots equal to your proficiency bonus + CE modifier. A creature may bear no more than 3 of your Knots before 11th level. Each Knot normally costs 1 CE to tie as a bonus action after you touch the target or hit it with a weapon/cursed attack this turn.

Anchor Knot

A creature’s speed is reduced by 10 feet. It adds your Knot Die to checks/saves made to resist forced movement, but it cannot willingly teleport more than 30 feet at once without first succeeding on a CE save.

Hitch Knot

Tie two creatures/objects within 30 feet of one another. Until the knot ends, they cannot willingly move more than 30 feet apart. A creature attempting to exceed the tether makes a Strength save; on a failure its movement ends at the boundary.

Stitch Knot

Tie two willing creatures. Reaction: when one takes damage, you may transfer damage up to your Knot Die + CE modifier to the other creature. This does not change damage type and cannot reduce the original damage below 1.

3Snare & Slip —

Snare: if a hostile creature bears 2 of your Knots, spend 2 CE as a bonus action; it makes a Strength save or its speed becomes 0 until the end of its next turn. Slip: reaction when a knotted creature moves; spend 1 CE to shift it 5 feet to an unoccupied space it could normally occupy (Dexterity save negates if hostile).

6Compound Knot —

a creature can bear 4 Knots. When two different Knot types affect the same creature, your first damaging hit against it each turn deals one Knot Die extra force damage. You may also tie or remove one Knot as part of the Attack action once per turn.

11Gordian Binding —

a creature may bear 5 Knots. As an action, spend 4 CE and consume 3 Knots on one creature. It makes a Strength save; failure restrains it for 1 minute. It repeats the save at the end of each turn, ending the effect on success. On a success against the initial save, its speed is merely halved until the end of its next turn.

18Domain Expansion: Temple of the Nine Ties —

10 CE. At initiative count 20, choose one ephemeral Knot effect for every hostile creature in the Domain: Anchor, Hitch (to a fixed point), or Snare. This is the Domain’s sure-hit and lasts until the next initiative count 20. Your ordinary cord, attacks, and Gordian Binding still resolve normally.

Feats

Technique Feats

Sailor’s Hands

You can splice your cords through existing Knots: when you tie a Knot, you may route it through another creature or object already bearing one of your Knots, extending your tying range to 60 feet for that Knot and ignoring cover between the relay and the target.

Quick Release

When one of your Knots ends, move up to 5 feet without provoking opportunity attacks. Once per turn.

Executioner’s Knot

As an action for 3 CE, you can tie a hangman's noose around a creature bearing 3+ of your Knots within 30 feet: it makes a Constitution save or is restrained until the end of your next turn, unable to speak or use verbal components while the noose holds.

Rules integration

Table Rulings

Knots are technique effects; cutting visible cord does not automatically end them unless an ability specifically suppresses cursed techniques.
A creature can be affected by only one instance of each named Knot from you at a time.
Forced movement created by Slip cannot place a creature inside solid matter or into a space it cannot physically occupy.
