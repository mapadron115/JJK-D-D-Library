Feature map

Level-by-level features, as the figure refined them.

L1

Empty Hands

Passive.

Tier IV · Controller · suggested CE ability WIS · Primary verb: Release. What you hold, holds you. What you release, releases you. Renunciation treats accumulation — buffs, temp HP, hoarded power — as a structural weakness: the tower, built up, is already falling.

His unarmed strikes deal 1d8 + WIS modifier bludgeoning damage. While he holds nothing in his hands, has no temporary HP, and is not concentrating on a spell or effect: his technique save DC increases by 1, and he has advantage on saving throws against being charmed or frightened. (The vow, in his body.)

L1

Song of the Question

Action · 2 CE.

One creature within 60 ft that can hear him must succeed on a Wisdom saving throw against his technique DC. On a failure, one beneficial magical effect of Milarepa's choice on the target ends. (The song asks what it was for. Heaven agrees it was nothing.)

L3

The Tower Falls

Action · 3 CE · usable PB times per long rest.

Each creature in a 30-ft radius sphere centered on a point he can see within 90 ft that has temporary HP must make a Constitution saving throw against his technique DC. On a failure, it loses all its temporary HP and takes psychic damage equal to half the amount lost (rounded down). On a success, it keeps its temporary HP. (What is built up, falls.)

L3

The Laugh Delays

Reaction · 2 CE · once per round.

When a creature he can see within 60 ft would gain temporary HP, he laughs: the gain is delayed until the start of that creature's next turn instead of occurring now. (The mountain is in no hurry.)

L6

The Mountain Sits

Passive.

He cannot be moved against his will, and cannot be knocked prone while conscious. (A mountain does not relocate for weather.)

L6

The Cave Seals

Action · 4 CE · 1 minute (concentration).

A 20-ft radius sphere centered on a point he can see within 90 ft becomes a sealed cave. Hostile creatures inside cannot gain temporary HP and cannot be targeted by new beneficial magical effects. Willing creatures inside can take the Hide action as a bonus action. (The cave keeps what it keeps.)

L11

Nothing to Trade

Passive.

Any magical or social effect structured as an offer — a benefit with a price — fails against him outright unless he willingly accepts it. If he refuses such an offer, the offerer takes 2d10 psychic damage as Heaven notes the attempted purchase. Additionally, Song of the Question may now target two creatures. (There is nothing to buy. There never was.)

Maximum Technique

Maximum: One Lifetime

Action · 10 CE · once per long rest.

Each hostile creature he can see within 120 ft must make a Wisdom saving throw against his technique DC. On a failure, all beneficial magical effects on it end and it loses all temporary HP. On a success, it keeps its temporary HP and one beneficial effect of its choice (all others end). (Enlightenment, all at once, for everyone present.)

Domain Expansion

The Hundred Thousand Songs: Drakar Taso

Full-turn action · 20 CE · 800 clash points · burnout 5 rounds · duration 2 minutes · 50-ft radius · disclosed.

The white-rock cave at the moment of realization. Inside, the sure-hit is The Song Unravels — at initiative 20, the song peaks: each hostile creature inside must succeed on a Wisdom saving throw against his technique DC or lose all temporary HP and have one beneficial magical effect of its choice end. A creature may instead willingly take 4d10 psychic damage to keep everything until the next initiative 20 — a willing-decline valve Heaven honors. While the domain stands, his Empty Hands DC bonus becomes +2. (The less he holds, the more certain the song.)

Level 20 · Capstone

The Last Laugh

Passive · once per long rest.

When he would be reduced to 0 hit points, he instead drops to 1 hit point and laughs: each hostile creature within 60 ft that can hear him must succeed on a Wisdom saving throw against his technique DC or lose all temporary HP, and one beneficial magical effect of Milarepa's choice on it ends. Additionally, his body cannot be animated, bound, or raised as undead by any means — Heaven claimed it nine centuries ago. (He has been laughing at death for nine hundred years. Death has noticed.)
