Completed rules

  • Ice restraining a creature is an object occupying its space with AC 15, HP equal to 5 × PB, immunity to cold and poison, and vulnerability to fire. The creature repeats a Strength save at the end of each turn; any creature may also attack the ice. Destroying it ends restrained.
  • Free-standing technique ice persists until dismissed, destroyed, or you become poisoned, paralyzed, stunned, petrified, or unconscious. It is difficult terrain. Restraining ice cannot stack on one creature.
  • Extreme Cold: a creature restrained by your ice suffers one additional exhaustion level. This level cannot stack with itself and vanishes immediately when the restraint ends. A creature without cold resistance or immunity has halved speed while within 5 feet of any persistent technique ice.
  • Frost Calm: use the same ice-object profile for each restrained target. The 30-foot-radius area becomes difficult terrain until all its ice is destroyed or dismissed.
  • Ice Fall: you fall prone until the start of your next turn. Choose any number of creatures within 30 feet. Make one CE attack and compare it with each target’s AC for the 1d10 cold and restraint; regardless of that result, each target separately makes the printed Dexterity save against falling spikes. Roll the spike damage once.
  • Domain — Ice Age: at initiative 20 each chosen hostile automatically takes 8d10 cold damage and is restrained in Domain ice until the next initiative 20. The ice uses the normal AC 15 and 5 × PB HP profile. A protected creature makes a Constitution save, taking half damage with no restraint on a success. Ordinary ice attacks and saves resolve normally.

Power identity

Why Tier II: it reliably controls space and actions, but the ice is tangible, destructible, and governed by repeated saves.

Recovered character-build options

Innate Technique Feats 3

These are optional character-building feats. You must meet each listed prerequisite before selecting it.

Iceshape

Standard

Prerequisite: Ice Creation

Action, 1 CE: form one simple or martial melee weapon from ice in your reach. You are proficient with it, it counts as cursed, and it lasts 1 hour. It has AC 13 and HP equal to 5 + level, melts harmlessly when destroyed, and grants no copied item property.

Frostbite

High impact

Prerequisite: Frost Calm

Whenever a creature becomes restrained by your ice, it gains one exhaustion level. The same uninterrupted restraint applies this only once, but a later new restraint can add another level; there is no added Frostbite ceiling.

Maximum: Shatter

Pinnacle

Prerequisite: Ice Fall

Action: choose a creature restrained by your ice; it makes a Constitution save. Failure breaks one chosen limb and ends the restraint. It severs instead only if the restraint or same activation meets the revised Sever threshold, or the target is at or below 25% maximum HP. Success ends the restraint without injury.

Extreme Cold
At 3rd level, being stuck on your ice feels
like staying in a cold mountain while you
wait for death. A creature inside your ice is
under one additional level of Exhaustion. Any
creatures within 5ft. of your ice that don't
have cold resistance or immunity have their movement speed halved.
Frost Calm
When you reach 6th level, you have learned how to unleash thick
layers of ice through the mist. As an action for 4 cursed energy, you
can send a cloud of freezing mist to a spot within 60 feet of you.
Every creature in a 30-foot radius circle in that spot must make a
Dexterity saving throw. On a failure, they take 7d8 cold damage and
are restrained by the ice layer formed in the square. On a success,
they take half as much damage and are not restrained.
The creature may make a Strength saving throw at the beginning of
its turns to break the ice and end the restrained condition.

makes sure your enemies won't escape. As an action for 8 cursed

to create ice on the ground. You telepathically manipulate the ice
created, launching one for every creature within 30ft. of you of your
choice. Make a cursed energy attack roll, on a hit they take 1d10
cold damage and become restrained.
The creature may make a Strength saving throw at the beginning of
its turns to break the ice and end the restrained condition.
Hitting or missing, giant spikes of ice will fall from the sky on
top of the targeted creatures, forcing them
to make a Dexterity saving throw. On a
failure, they take 6d8 cold damage and 6d8
piercing damage. On a success, they take
half as much damage.
Domain Expansion: Ice age
If you achieve 18th level, you hone your
technique to a fine edge, allowing you to
use your Domain Expansion. While inside it,
every creature except you must attempt a
