Core law

What the technique actually does.

Rules are written to plug into the finalized campaign glossary: normal actions stay normal, direct attacks roll, and only named Domain events bypass the initial roll.

After a direct hit · 1 CE

Deathbrand

After you deal weapon, unarmed, or CE-attack damage, add one Fate Scar and 1 Death Die necrotic damage. A target can hold scars up to the current cap. Scars last 1 minute.

Persistent wound

Fate Scar

Each Fate Scar reduces the target’s maximum HP by 5% of its original maximum, rounded up. This reduction cannot lower max HP below 25% from scars alone. When a scar expires, maximum HP returns but missing HP does not.

Passive

Healing Interference

While a creature has a Fate Scar, healing it receives is reduced by 10% per scar, maximum 50%. RCT can remove one Fate Scar instead of restoring HP if the healer spends at least 3 CE on that activation.

Weapon freedom

Black Blade Conductor

Any melee weapon can carry the technique. As a bonus action, form a cursed black blade with the statistics of any proficient melee weapon; it vanishes when released for 1 minute.

Once per long rest or once per major scene, when you hit a creature with 5 Fate Scars, spend 10 CE to force a Constitution save. Pinnacle expression

Domain, Maximum, or equivalent.

Not every technique needs a Domain. When one exists, its sure-hit is a unique autonomous initiative-count-20 event unless the Domain is explicitly rule-based.

Rune Unbound — Night of Destined Death

Level 18 · Full-turn action · 20 CE · 60-foot radius · 1 minute. A dead red sky hangs over a ruined sanctum. Ordinary Black Blade attacks still roll.

Initiative 20 — Mortality Remembered: each chosen hostile automatically takes 6d12 necrotic and gains one Fate Scar. If already at scar cap, it instead takes +2d12 necrotic. A protected creature makes a Constitution save; success halves damage and prevents the scar.

Fate Scars created by the Domain expire when the Domain ends unless refreshed by an ordinary Deathbrand.

Rules integration

Edge cases and shared systems.

These rulings are designed around the revised Player Guide rather than the raw PDF where the two differ.

Maximum-HP reduction is not damage and is not doubled by critical hits.
Fate Scar damage riders on a critical are part of the direct attack and double normally; later damage-over-time or Domain pulses do not.
Technique suppression pauses scar effects but does not erase remaining duration.
A target whose maximum HP rises again does not heal automatically.
Technique DC: 8 + proficiency bonus + your chosen Cursed Energy ability modifier (Wisdom or Charisma unless your class says otherwise).
Direct criticals: rolled weapon, unarmed, and CE attacks can critical normally. After a qualifying critical, roll the campaign's unmodified Black Flash confirmation d20; a natural 20 triples the completed critical damage. Autonomous Domain events, damage-over-time, hazards, and unrolled pulses cannot Black Flash.
Maximum Output: unless a feature says otherwise, double that feature's base CE cost and enhance one supported axis—damage, range, duration, or accuracy/DC—using the campaign's Maximum Output rules. Named Maximum Techniques and Domains do not receive generic Maximum Output.
Technique Focus: effects marked Focus occupy the shared Technique Focus slot. Opening a Domain ends other Focus effects unless the Domain expressly preserves them.
Two-technique characters: these guides assume each technique progresses from character/class level as agreed by the campaign. Multiclassing itself does not grant another Innate Technique.
