The Black Blade Technique

Core law

What the technique actually does.

Rules are written to plug into the finalized campaign glossary: normal actions stay normal, direct attacks roll, and only named Domain events bypass the initial roll.

DESTINED DEATH

After a direct hit · 1 CE

Deathbrand

After you deal weapon, unarmed, or CE-attack damage, add one Fate Scar and 1 Death Die necrotic damage. A target can hold scars up to the current cap. Scars last 1 minute.

Persistent wound

Fate Scar

Each Fate Scar reduces the target’s maximum HP by 5% of its original maximum, rounded up. This reduction cannot lower max HP below 25% from scars alone. When a scar expires, maximum HP returns but missing HP does not.

Passive

Healing Interference

While a creature has a Fate Scar, healing it receives is reduced by 10% per scar, maximum 50%. RCT can remove one Fate Scar instead of restoring HP if the healer spends at least 3 CE on that activation.

Weapon freedom

Black Blade Conductor

Any melee weapon can carry the technique. As a bonus action, form a cursed black blade with the statistics of any proficient melee weapon; it vanishes when released for 1 minute.

Full progression

Level 1 through 20.

Milestones use the campaign’s familiar 1 / 3 / 6 / 11 / 18 structure and add an explicit level-20 mastery feature.

1A Fragment of the Rune

Deathbrand and 1 more feature

Deathbrand

Gain Deathbrand (after a direct hit, 1 CE: add one Fate Scar and 1 Death Die necrotic damage; scars last 1 minute), Fate Scar (each scar reduces the target’s maximum HP by 5% of its original maximum, rounded up, never below 25% from scars alone; when a scar expires maximum HP returns but missing HP does not), Healing Interference (a scarred creature’s healing received is reduced 10% per scar, maximum 50%; RCT can remove one scar instead of restoring HP by spending at least 3 CE), and Black Blade Conductor (any melee weapon can carry the technique; bonus action: form a cursed black blade with any proficient melee weapon’s statistics).

Beast Step

Once per turn after you hit a scarred creature, move 10 feet without provoking from that creature.

3Beast Clergyman

Bestial Sling and 2 more features

Bestial Sling

Action · 2 CE: 30-foot cone; Dex save, 3 Death Dice bludgeoning/necrotic, half on success. A creature that fails by 5+ gains one Fate Scar.

Black Blade Wave

Replace one weapon attack · 2 CE: make a ranged CE attack to 60 feet. Hit deals weapon base die + 2 Death Dice necrotic and can Deathbrand normally.

Stone of Gurranq

Action · 3 CE: hurl a cursed boulder to 90 feet; 4 Death Dice bludgeoning, and prone on failed Strength save.

6The Blade is Unsealed

Death in Motion and 2 more features

Death in Motion

Your jump distance doubles; once per turn, after moving at least 20 feet, add one Death Die to a melee hit.

Mortal Wound

At 3 Fate Scars, ordinary healing cannot increase the target above its reduced maximum and RCT must first remove a scar before it can restore HP on the same activation.

Black Blade Spiral

Action · 5 CE: move up to half speed through spaces adjacent to enemies and make one melee attack against up to PB different creatures. Each hit can gain Deathbrand by paying separately.

11Destined Death Released

Greater Fate Scar and 2 more features

Greater Fate Scar

Each scar now reduces original max HP by 7.5%, still respecting the current scar cap. Healing reduction remains 10% per scar.

Maximum: Thousand Black Blades

Action · 10 CE: stab the ground or a surface. Creatures of your choice in a 30-foot radius make Dex saves, taking 8 Death Dice necrotic on failure or half on success. Failure also adds two Fate Scars; success adds one. Named Maximum.

Death Denies Death

When a scarred creature would be revived from 0 HP, it returns with one Fate Scar still active unless the revival explicitly repairs soul/fate damage.

18Rune Unbound

Domain Expansion and 2 more features

Domain Expansion

Unlock Rune Unbound — Night of Destined Death.

Black Blade Eclipse

Bonus action · 8 CE · Focus · 1 minute: every direct weapon hit deals +2 Death Dice and adds a Fate Scar without additional CE, but you take one Death Die unavoidable necrotic at the end of each turn.

Fourth Scar

Fate Scar cap becomes 4.

20Death Restored to the World

True Destined Death and 2 more features

True Destined Death

Each Fate Scar now reduces original max HP by 10%. The scar floor becomes 10% instead of 25%; healing reduction can reach 50%.

Fifth Scar

Scar cap becomes 5.

Final Black Blade

Once per long rest or once per major scene, when you hit a creature with 5 Fate Scars, spend 10 CE to force a Constitution save. On failure it is reduced to 0 HP; on success it takes 10 Death Dice necrotic. Creatures immune to instant defeat automatically use the success result. This is not a Domain sure-hit and cannot be Maximum Output.

Pinnacle expression

Domain, Maximum, or equivalent.

Not every technique needs a Domain. When one exists, its sure-hit is a unique autonomous initiative-count-20 event unless the Domain is explicitly rule-based.

Rune Unbound — Night of Destined Death

Level 18 · Full-turn action · 20 CE · 60-foot radius · 1 minute. A dead red sky hangs over a ruined sanctum. Ordinary Black Blade attacks still roll.

Initiative 20 — Mortality Remembered: each chosen hostile automatically takes 6d12 necrotic and gains one Fate Scar. If already at scar cap, it instead takes +2d12 necrotic. A protected creature makes a Constitution save; success halves damage and prevents the scar.

Fate Scars created by the Domain expire when the Domain ends unless refreshed by an ordinary Deathbrand.

Clash bookkeeping: 1,000 clash points (Tier V). A 100+ point advantage collapses the weaker Domain; collapse deals no damage. Barrier AC 12 + PB; barrier HP = 10 × max(1, your CE modifier) + 2,000. Technique Burnout: 3 rounds.

Innate Technique Feats

Four build-defining options.

These broaden the law instead of merely inflating the same number.

Prerequisite: Destined Death

Beast’s Agility

After using Beast Step, you may move along walls or ceilings for that movement without falling until it ends, the beast’s stride ignoring the floor’s claim on it.

Prerequisite: Deathbrand

Rune-Eater

When a creature with Fate Scars spends 3+ CE on one activation, once per round you may use your reaction to extend all its Fate Scars by 1 round.

Prerequisite: Black Blade Wave

Black Knife Geometry

Black Blade Wave ignores half and three-quarters cover and can originate from the edge of your manifested blade even while you are airborne.

Prerequisite: level 11

The Beast Collects

You harvest the dooms you have marked. When you would drop to 0 HP, use your reaction and spend 3 CE to consume all Fate Scars on one creature you can see within 60 feet, regaining 1 Death Die per scar consumed. If this healing leaves you above 0 HP, you do not fall unconscious.

Rules integration

Edge cases and shared systems.

These rulings are designed around the revised Player Guide rather than the raw PDF where the two differ.

Maximum-HP reduction is not damage and is not doubled by critical hits.
Fate Scar damage riders on a critical are part of the direct attack and double normally; later damage-over-time or Domain pulses do not.
Technique suppression pauses scar effects but does not erase remaining duration.
A target whose maximum HP rises again does not heal automatically.
Technique DC: 8 + proficiency bonus + your chosen Cursed Energy ability modifier (Wisdom or Charisma unless your class says otherwise).
Direct criticals: rolled weapon, unarmed, and CE attacks can critical normally. After a qualifying critical, roll the campaign's unmodified Black Flash confirmation d20; a natural 20 triples the completed critical damage. Autonomous Domain events, damage-over-time, hazards, and unrolled pulses cannot Black Flash.
Maximum Output: unless a feature says otherwise, double that feature's base CE cost and enhance one supported axis—damage, range, duration, or accuracy/DC—using the campaign's Maximum Output rules. Named Maximum Techniques and Domains do not receive generic Maximum Output.
Technique Focus: effects marked Focus occupy the shared Technique Focus slot. Opening a Domain ends other Focus effects unless the Domain expressly preserves them.
Two-technique characters: these guides assume each technique progresses from character/class level as agreed by the campaign. Multiclassing itself does not grant another Innate Technique.
