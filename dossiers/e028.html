Sky Manipulation

Space bends into reactive armor, reflected attacks, flight, redirected areas, and a Thin Ice Breaker.

Field notes. Subject: the sorcerer who wears the sky like still water. Three truths govern her. First: her defense is not armor but refusal, the space around her bends like water around a stone; a reaction and a coin buy four points of armor against a single attack, and what misses is returned: a ranged strike reflected with its own dice, a melee limb folded useless until its owner's next turn. Second: she answers areas the same way, a cone or line that includes her can be caught for six coins and thrown back at its source, which saves against its own damage; a redirected effect cannot be redirected again. Third: the Domain is a falling sky, at initiative twenty, chosen hostiles without flight are considered falling, and when the Domain ends the fall is priced at the maximum, one hundred twenty bludgeoning. Assess her correctly: she does not block attacks. She declines to be where they land.

Rules integration

Edge cases and shared systems.

Rules priority: the reviewed rulings, explicit codex clarifications, and the level progression always control when original wording conflicts. Legacy Domain auto-hit language, action terminology, event timing, missing saves, and older scaling never override revised player rules.
Why Tier II: it has a powerful answer to almost any projectile or line, but it lives in reactions, positioning, and one bounded damage technique rather than permanent resources.
