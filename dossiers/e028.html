Completed rules

  • Sky Shape: use the reaction after an attack roll targeting you or an ally within reach is seen but before its result. Pay 1 CE and add +4 AC against that attack only. You still have only one reaction even when Dodge lets you protect an ally.
  • If a ranged attack misses because of this bonus, make one CE attack against its source if within the original attack’s range. On hit, reroll the original attack’s base damage dice and add its ordinary damage modifier, using the original damage type; do not copy a critical, limited-use rider, poison, technique, or condition. If a melee weapon attack misses because of the bonus, the attacking limb is unusable until the start of that creature’s next turn; this is not lasting limb damage.
  • Sky Armor: as a bonus action for 1 CE, for 1 minute your AC equals 11 + CE modifier + PB and you gain flight equal to walking speed. This is an alternative AC calculation and does not add armor or shield bonuses; you may use a higher legal calculation instead.
  • Area redirection: when included in a cone or line, use a reaction, pay 6 CE, and make its save. On failure, resolve normally. On success, you and every other original target are unaffected; redirect the same shape from your space toward its source. The source makes its own save against the original DC and damage. A redirected effect cannot be redirected again.
  • Thin Ice Breaker: use a 30-foot-long, 5-foot-wide line. Flying grants disadvantage only on the initial Dexterity save. Failure takes CE modifier d10 force and falls prone; success takes half and remains standing.
  • Heaven’s Gate: at initiative 20 each chosen hostile is automatically marked as falling and its speed becomes 0 until the next initiative 20, even if it can fly. A protected creature makes a Wisdom save and avoids the mark on a success. Marks do not stack. When the Domain ends, every creature still marked takes maximum falling damage (120, equivalent to 20d6 maximized) and falls prone.

Power identity

Why Tier II: it has a powerful answer to almost any projectile or line, but it lives in reactions, positioning, and one bounded damage technique rather than permanent resources.

Recovered character-build options

Innate Technique Feats 3

These are optional character-building feats. You must meet each listed prerequisite before selecting it.

Cloak of Invisibility

Standard

Prerequisite: Sky Shape

Action, 3 CE: cast Invisibility on yourself using Technique Focus, without needing spellcasting. If you have Sky Armor, its AC and flight remain active while invisible. Attacking or activating a hostile technique ends invisibility as the spell states.

Spatial Tear

High impact

Prerequisite: Thin Ice Breaker

Corrected feature name: Thin Ice Breaker. When activating it, spend 2 additional CE to replace the line and save with one melee CE attack. On hit, deal force damage equal to a number of d10s equal to your CE modifier and knock the target prone; on miss, no damage. It remains one activation.

Maximum: Hurricane

Pinnacle

Prerequisite: Thin Ice Breaker and CE score 20

Action, 10 CE: creatures of your choice within 30 feet make Strength saves. Failure launches a creature 1,000 feet upward; success leaves it in place. Flying, reactions, ceilings, and normal falling damage apply; a ceiling stops ascent at the available distance.

Sky Armor
Once you reach 3rd level, you have become better at manipulating the
space around you. As a bonus action for 1 Cursed Energy, you may
cover part of your body with a part of the sky, making it seem like
the parts covered are invisible for 1 minute. While you have this
armor equipped, your AC is 11 + Your Cursed Energy modifier + your
proficiency bonus. You will also gain a flying speed equal to your
movement speed while under the effects of Sky Armor.
Sky Shape Improvement
When you reach 6th level, your manipulation has become so refined
even techniques cannot pass through you. You may now use your
reaction to magic attack rolls, and you may use this reaction for 6
Cursed Energy to redirect a cone or line saving throw, returning the
attack to sender if you pass the saving throw, making it not affect
you or the creatures in the area.
Thin Ice Breaker
At 11th level, you have created an extension technique to your sky
manipulation, literally breaking the sky as if it were ice. As an
action for 8 Cursed Energy, you can force every creature in a 30 feet
line to make a Dexterity saving throw. On a failure, they will take a
number of d10 equal to your Cursed Energy modifier in force damage
and will be knocked prone, on a success they will take half damage
and will not be prone. If you are flying, the creatures have
disadvantage in the saving throw.
