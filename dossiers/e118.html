Carnage

Symbiote weapons / biomass / bloodlust / autonomous tendrils

Power tierTier V · Calamity
Core law

What the technique actually does.

Core Engine — Mass (fixed arsenal). Your symbiote maintains a fixed weapon loadout, not a pool. Mass = PB + 2 allocation points (doubled at level 20). After each long rest, distribute your Mass among the weapon configurations below; the total committed cannot exceed your Mass. As a bonus action, switch between your committed configurations. Mass never increases or decreases during play — bloodshed does not feed it; it IS the weapon.

Arsenal (configuration — Mass cost — stats):

Reaper Blade — 1 — 1d8, choose slashing/piercing when manifested

Cleaving Axe — 2 — 1d12 slashing

Harpoon Spear — 2 — 1d10 piercing, reach 10 ft, thrown 60 ft (reforms instantly)

Maw Tendrils — 2 — 1d8; on a hit you may grapple the target (escape DC = technique DC) instead of dealing damage

Shard Volley — 2 — 60-ft ranged attack, 2d8 piercing (detached projectile)

Twin Fangs — 3 — maintain two configurations manifested at once

Full progression

Level 1 through 20.

1Red Weapon

Gain Mass allocation (PB + 2); commit among configurations; bonus-action switch. (Base: Reaper Blade 1d8.)

3Shard Volley

Once per turn, add 1d8 to a Red Weapon hit for 1 CE. (Configuration added to the arsenal.)

6Autonomous Tendrils

2 CE, 1 minute: reaction once/round — tendril attack vs a creature entering 15 ft or attacking you.

9Red Feast

When you deal slashing/piercing damage to a living creature with a Red Weapon, regain HP equal to PB (once per turn). Fire or sonic damage you take suppresses this until your next turn. (Automatic; no Mass spend.)

11Twin Manifestation

Your Mass allocation increases by 2, and you may keep two configurations manifested at once.

13Biomass Spawn

4 CE: commit 3 Mass for 1 minute to detach a symbiote spawn (acts after you, one attack 3d8, 1 HP, AC = yours − 2). While committed, your allocation is reduced by 3.

15Carnage Rules

Critical hits on 19–20 with Red Weapons. When you reduce a living creature to 0 HP with a Red Weapon, you may switch configurations without spending your bonus action. When Frightened or Charmed, you may use your bonus action to manifest a configuration and end the condition — the bloodlust steadies the host.

18Maximum Carnage

8 CE, 1 minute: every committed configuration manifests at once — you become Large, tendril reach 30 ft, two reactions/round (tendrils only), Red Feast healing maximized once/turn. (No Mass spend.)

20Red Event

Mass allocation doubles. Once per turn when you hit with a Red Weapon, the wound erupts: creatures within 10 ft of the target (excluding you) take 4d10 slashing, Dexterity save for half.

Pinnacle expression

Maximum, Domain, or equivalent.

Maximum — Carnage Storm (10 CE): 40-ft radius erupts with blades and tendrils; Dexterity save 12d10 slashing, half on success; failures are Restrained until the end of your next turn.

Rules integration

Edge cases and shared systems.

Codex rulings: Direct rolled technique/weapon attacks can use the campaign's Black Flash procedure when otherwise eligible. Passive damage, hazards, summons acting independently, and initiative-20 Domain events cannot. Generic Maximum Output applies only to ordinary features, never named Maxima or Domains.
