Carnage

Symbiote weapons / biomass / bloodlust / autonomous tendrils

Power tierTier V · Calamity
Core law

What the technique actually does.

Core Engine — Mass (fixed arsenal). Your symbiote maintains a fixed weapon loadout, not a pool. Mass = PB + 2 allocation points. After each long rest, distribute your Mass among the weapon configurations below; the total committed cannot exceed your Mass. As a bonus action, switch between your committed configurations. Mass never increases or decreases during play — bloodshed does not feed it; it IS the weapon.

Arsenal (configuration — Mass cost — stats):

Reaper Blade — 1 — 1d8, choose slashing/piercing when manifested

Cleaving Axe — 2 — 1d12 slashing

Harpoon Spear — 2 — 1d10 piercing, reach 10 ft, thrown 60 ft (reforms instantly)

Maw Tendrils — 2 — 1d8; on a hit you may grapple the target (escape DC = technique DC) instead of dealing damage

Shard Volley — 2 — 60-ft ranged attack, 2d8 piercing (detached projectile)

Twin Fangs — 3 — maintain two configurations manifested at once

Core Engine — Kill Chain. Your Chain runs 0–10 and starts at 0. When you hit with a Red Weapon attack, +1 Chain (+2 instead if the target has not yet taken a turn in combat). When you miss with a Red Weapon attack, or you end your turn without having hit anything, Chain resets to 0. Chain 3+: your Red Weapon attacks score critical hits on 19–20. Chain 6+: your speed increases by 10 ft and opportunity attacks against you have disadvantage — the blood keeps up with you.

The fiction. The prevailing theory holds that Carnage is not a technique at all but a cursed wound that learned to walk — the user's cursed energy fused with something that ate violence and began wearing flesh the way other sorcerers wear reinforcement. Jujutsu scholars describe the fixed arsenal as the entity's honesty: it was born with exactly these teeth, and it does not grow new ones because its hunger never learned to want anything it hasn't already killed with. The Mass allocation is a kindness the host tells themselves; the red weapons were always coming out. Carnage does not fight — it performs vivisection at speed. Each cut is a question; the next cut is the answer.

Full progression

Level 1 through 20.

L1Red Weapon

You gain your Mass allocation (proficiency bonus + 2): after each long rest, distribute it among the arsenal configurations in the Core law above (the total committed cannot exceed your Mass). As a bonus action, switch between your committed configurations. Your base configuration is the Reaper Blade (1d8).

L3Kill Chain

You gain the Kill Chain engine in the Core law above: consecutive Red Weapon hits build Chain toward critical range (3+) and predator speed (6+), and a miss breaks it. Striking a creature before it has acted in combat builds +2 Chain. This also adds the Shard Volley configuration to your arsenal (60-ft ranged attack, 2d8 piercing, 2 Mass) — the chain starts at range.

L6Autonomous Tendrils

Red Cascade: when your Chain reaches 4 or higher, a tendril lashes out on its own — immediately make one free Red Weapon attack against a creature within 15 ft of you. This can trigger once per round, and the free attack builds Chain as normal. For 2 CE, for 1 minute: once per round, as a reaction, you may also make a tendril attack against a creature that enters within 15 ft of you or attacks you.

L9Red Feast

When you deal slashing or piercing damage to a living creature with a Red Weapon, you regain hit points equal to your proficiency bonus (once per turn). Fire or sonic damage you take suppresses this until your next turn. This happens automatically and costs no Mass.

L11Twin Manifestation

Your Mass allocation increases by 2, and you may keep two configurations manifested at once.

L13Red Finale

Cash out: when you hit with a Red Weapon while your Chain is 6 or higher, you may reset your Chain to 0 to make every wound erupt at once — add +1d10 slashing damage per Chain point consumed. Once per turn. The chain is not the weapon; it is the countdown.

L15Carnage Rules

Your Chain maximum becomes 12. When you reduce a living creature to 0 HP with a Red Weapon, your Chain does not reset — it carries to your next target — and you may switch configurations without spending your bonus action. When you are Frightened or Charmed, you may use your bonus action to manifest a configuration and end the condition — the bloodlust steadies the host.

L18Maximum Carnage

For 8 CE, for 1 minute: every committed configuration manifests at once — you become Large, your tendril reach becomes 30 ft, you gain two reactions per round (usable for tendrils only), your Red Feast healing is maximized once per turn, and missing no longer resets your Chain (only a hitless turn does). This costs no Mass.

L20Red Event

Your Mass allocation doubles. Death by a Thousand Cuts: while your Chain is 6 or higher, you may make one additional Red Weapon attack as a bonus action on each of your turns. Once per turn, when you hit with a Red Weapon, the wound erupts: creatures within 10 ft of the target (excluding you) take 4d10 slashing damage, with a Dexterity save for half.

Pinnacle expression

Maximum, Domain, or equivalent.

Maximum — Carnage Storm (10 CE): you blur through a 40-ft radius, and it erupts with blades and tendrils; Dexterity save 12d10 slashing, half on success; failures are Restrained until the end of your next turn. For each creature that fails the save, gain +2 Chain.

Feats

Build-defining options.

Blood Trail

When you damage a living creature with a Red Weapon, you taste its blood: for the next hour you unerringly know its direction while it is within 1 mile of you, and you have advantage on Wisdom (Perception) checks to detect it while it is within 60 ft. The trail ends early if you wound another creature — you can only savor one hunt at a time.

Wall of Teeth

You catch what strikes you: when a creature hits you with a melee attack, you may spend your reaction to snare the limb in the symbiote's maw — it makes a Str save or is grappled by you (escape DC = technique DC).

Red Rain

Instead of adding Shard Volley's die to a weapon hit, you may spend 1 CE as an action to hurl a spray of symbiote shards at up to two creatures within 30 ft: make a ranged technique attack against each, dealing 1d8 piercing damage on a hit. This counts as your Shard Volley use for the turn.

Born Killer

You mark the dying: once per turn, when you hit a creature below half HP with a Red Weapon, you may forgo the damage to leave a bleeding wound instead — the next attack against it before the end of your next turn has advantage.

Rules integration

Edge cases and shared systems.

Codex rulings: Direct rolled technique/weapon attacks can use the campaign's Black Flash procedure when otherwise eligible. Passive damage, hazards, summons acting independently, and initiative-20 Domain events cannot. Generic Maximum Output applies only to ordinary features, never named Maxima or Domains.
