Carnage

Tier V · CalamitySymbiote weapons / biomass / bloodlust / autonomous tendrils

Core Engine — Mass. Mass max = level + CE modifier. Gain 1 when you deal slashing/piercing damage to a living target, max once/turn; gain 2 when reducing one to 0. Spend Mass on weapons, regeneration, and detached attacks.

6Autonomous Tendrils2 CE + 2 Mass, 1 minute: reaction once/round make tendril attack against creature entering 15 ft or attacking you.
9Red RegenerationBonus action spend up to PB Mass, heal 1d8 per Mass. Fire/sonic damage since last turn makes each die d4.
11Living ArsenalMaintain two weapon forms; when taking Attack, one attack may originate from any point within 20 ft.

15Carnage RulesCriticals on 19–20 with Red Weapons; critical grants 2 Mass. When Frightened/Charmed, may spend 3 Mass to end condition.
18Maximum Carnage8 CE + 8 Mass, 1 minute: Large, 30-ft tendril reach, two reactions/round usable only for tendrils, regeneration dice maximized once/turn.
20Red EventMass max doubles. Start combat at PB Mass. Once/turn weapon hit can spend 3 Mass to erupt 4d10 extra slashing in 10-ft burst around target.
Maximum — Carnage Storm (10 CE + 10 Mass): 40-ft radius erupts with blades and tendrils. Dex save 12d10 slashing, half success; failures are Restrained until end next turn.
