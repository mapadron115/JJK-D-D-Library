Carnage

Symbiote weapons / biomass / bloodlust / autonomous tendrils

Power tierTier V · Calamity
Core law

What the technique actually does.

Core Engine — Mass (fixed arsenal). The arsenal is fixed — no growing, no borrowing, no improvisation. Mass is allotted each dawn among the configurations: blade, axe, spear, maw, volley, fangs. This is the entity's honesty: it was born with exactly these teeth, and its hunger never learned to want anything it has not already killed with. The allocation is a kindness the host tells himself. The red weapons were always coming out. Your symbiote maintains a fixed weapon loadout, not a pool. Mass = PB + 2 allocation points. After each long rest, distribute your Mass among the weapon configurations below; the total committed cannot exceed your Mass. As a bonus action, switch between your committed configurations. Mass never increases or decreases during play — bloodshed does not feed it; it IS the weapon.

Arsenal (configuration — Mass cost — stats):

Reaper Blade — 1 — 1d8, choose slashing/piercing when manifested

Cleaving Axe — 2 — 1d12 slashing

Harpoon Spear — 2 — 1d10 piercing, reach 10 ft, thrown 60 ft (reforms instantly)

Maw Tendrils — 2 — 1d8; on a hit you may grapple the target (escape DC = technique DC) instead of dealing damage

Shard Volley — 2 — 60-ft ranged attack, 2d8 piercing (detached projectile)

Twin Fangs — 3 — maintain two configurations manifested at once

Core Engine — Kill Chain. The Chain: appetite given arithmetic. Each landed blow feeds it; each miss starves it to zero. At three, the blows turn crueler; at six, the blood itself hurries to keep up. Mark the doctrine: momentum is a weapon, and it must be fed without pause. The onrush of a conquering force is like pent-up waters bursting — but waters that stop, stop. Your Chain runs 0–10 and starts at 0. When you hit with a Red Weapon attack, +1 Chain (+2 instead if the target has not yet taken a turn in combat). When you miss with a Red Weapon attack, or you end your turn without having hit anything, Chain resets to 0. Chain 3+: your Red Weapon attacks score critical hits on 19–20. Chain 6+: your speed increases by 10 ft and opportunity attacks against you have disadvantage — the blood keeps up with you.

Field notes. Subject: a host, and the red thing wearing him. The prevailing theory is the correct one: this is not a technique but a cursed wound that learned to walk — the user's energy fused with something that ate violence, and now wears flesh the way other sorcerers wear reinforcement. Do not assess the host. Assess the appetite, which has its own doctrine. Three truths. First, the arsenal never grows: it kills with what it was born with, the way a wolf needs no new teeth. Second, the hunger is honest, it does not scheme, does not feint, does not retreat; it only continues. Third, and most instructive: the Chain. Every army knows that victory loves momentum and defeat loves interruption. This one has made momentum its entire art of war.

Full progression

Level 1 through 20.

1Red Weapon
He wakes each dawn and allots his Mass — the day's teeth, chosen among blade, axe, spear, maw, and volley. The base is the Reaper Blade. Switching is a breath's work. A soldier's loadout, decided before the march: the wise host chooses for the ground he expects to bleed on.

You gain your Mass allocation (proficiency bonus + 2): after each long rest, distribute it among the arsenal configurations in the Core law above (the total committed cannot exceed your Mass). As a bonus action, switch between your committed configurations. Your base configuration is the Reaper Blade (1d8).

3Kill Chain
Now the arithmetic begins: consecutive hits build the Chain — crueler blows at three, predator's speed at six — and a single miss breaks it. Strike before the enemy has moved and the Chain leaps by two: the first blood is always the sweetest. And the arsenal grows a new tooth — the Shard Volley, so the chain may begin at range. Even hunger learns to throw.

You gain the Kill Chain engine in the Core law above: consecutive Red Weapon hits build Chain toward critical range (3+) and predator speed (6+), and a miss breaks it. Striking a creature before it has acted in combat builds +2 Chain. This also adds the Shard Volley configuration to your arsenal (60-ft ranged attack, 2d8 piercing, 2 Mass) — the chain starts at range.

6Autonomous Tendrils

Red Cascade: At Chain four, the tendrils hunt on their own — a free strike, unbidden, once per round, feeding the count like a tributary feeding a river. And for a little energy, they may also answer intruders and attackers for a minute. The appetite develops reflexes. The pack grows a second jaw that thinks for itself. when your Chain reaches 4 or higher, a tendril lashes out on its own — immediately make one free Red Weapon attack against a creature within 15 ft of you. This can trigger once per round, and the free attack builds Chain as normal. For 2 CE, for 1 minute: once per round, as a reaction, you may also make a tendril attack against a creature that enters within 15 ft of you or attacks you.

9Red Feast
What the red weapons wound, they drink — a little healing with every cut, once per exchange, costing nothing. The battlefield feeds the fighter. But fire and screaming sound spoil the meal: burned or deafened, the feast pauses. Even hunger has its allergies. Mark it: the wise enemy brings torches.

When you deal slashing or piercing damage to a living creature with a Red Weapon, you regain hit points equal to your proficiency bonus (once per turn). Fire or sonic damage you take suppresses this until your next turn. This happens automatically and costs no Mass.

11Twin Manifestation
His Mass swells, and he may wear two configurations at once — blade and maw, spear and volley. Two teeth in one mouth. The appetite learns to bite and hold simultaneously.

Your Mass allocation increases by 2, and you may keep two configurations manifested at once.

13Red Finale

Cash out: The Chain is not the weapon; it is the countdown. At six or higher, he may spend the whole count at once — every wound erupting together, a die of ruin per link. The harvest: all the momentum of the fight, reaped in a single cut. The skilful predator does not hoard his hunger. He spends it, all at once, on the killing blow. when you hit with a Red Weapon while your Chain is 6 or higher, you may reset your Chain to 0 to make every wound erupt at once — add +1d10 slashing damage per Chain point consumed. Once per turn. The chain is not the weapon; it is the countdown.

15Carnage Rules
The Chain's ceiling rises to twelve. And now the perfect economy: a kill no longer breaks the count — it carries, red-handed, to the next victim — and configurations change freely in the moment of slaughter. Fear and charm, which unman lesser hosts, are shrugged off with bared teeth: the bloodlust steadies the mind it rides. Consider: an appetite that cannot be frightened, cannot be charmed, and never drops its momentum between kills.

Your Chain maximum becomes 12. When you reduce a living creature to 0 HP with a Red Weapon, your Chain does not reset — it carries to your next target — and you may switch configurations without spending your bonus action. When you are Frightened or Charmed, you may use your bonus action to manifest a configuration and end the condition — the bloodlust steadies the host.

18Maximum Carnage
For eight coins and a minute: everything, all at once. Every configuration manifested, the host grown Large, tendrils reaching thirty feet, two reactions, the feast maximized, and the Chain unbroken by missing — only a truly idle turn stills it. This is not a technique. This is the appetite with its mask off. Pray the minute ends before you do.

For 8 CE, for 1 minute: every committed configuration manifests at once — you become Large, your tendril reach becomes 30 ft, you gain two reactions per round (usable for tendrils only), your Red Feast healing is maximized once per turn, and missing no longer resets your Chain (only a hitless turn does). This costs no Mass.

20Red Event
The Mass doubles — the mouth grows more teeth. And Death by a Thousand Cuts: at Chain six, an extra strike each turn, and every landed blow erupts outward, wounding all who stand near the victim. The final doctrine: he does not merely kill his enemy. He makes the enemy's neighbors regret standing close.

Your Mass allocation doubles. Death by a Thousand Cuts: while your Chain is 6 or higher, you may make one additional Red Weapon attack as a bonus action on each of your turns. Once per turn, when you hit with a Red Weapon, the wound erupts: creatures within 10 ft of the target (excluding you) take 4d10 slashing damage, with a Dexterity save for half.

Pinnacle expression

Maximum, Domain, or equivalent.

The Maximum: ten coins, one action — he blurs through forty feet and the radius erupts in blades and tendrils. Twelve dice of ruin, halved for the quick; the slow are held fast. And every failure feeds the Chain by two. The storm does not merely wound. It dines, and grows stronger with each course. Maximum — Carnage Storm (10 CE; action): you blur through a 40-ft radius, and it erupts with blades and tendrils; Dexterity save 12d10 slashing, half on success; failures are Restrained until the end of your next turn. For each creature that fails the save, gain +2 Chain.

Feats

Build-defining options.

Blood Trail

Wound the living and he tastes the blood — for an hour, he knows its direction to the mile, and his senses sharpen near it. One hunt at a time: wound another, and the first trail fades. The predator's courtesy: he savors his prey singly. When you damage a living creature with a Red Weapon, you taste its blood: for the next hour you unerringly know its direction while it is within 1 mile of you, and you have advantage on Wisdom (Perception) checks to detect it while it is within 60 ft. The trail ends early if you wound another creature — you can only savor one hunt at a time.

Wall of Teeth

Strike him in melee and the symbiote answers — the limb caught in a red maw, held fast unless torn free. The counter that bites back: every attack on him risks becoming his grip. You catch what strikes you: when a creature hits you with a melee attack, you may spend your reaction to snare the limb in the symbiote's maw — it makes a Str save or is grappled by you (escape DC = technique DC).

Red Rain

A spray of shards for a coin — two targets, thirty feet, the volley's smaller cousin. The appetite learns to share its attention. As an action, spend 1 CE to hurl a spray of symbiote shards at up to two creatures within 30 ft: make a ranged technique attack against each, dealing 1d8 piercing damage on a hit. This counts as your Shard Volley use for the turn.

Born Killer

He marks the dying: forgo the wound's damage, and leave instead a bleeding promise — the next blow against the marked falls with advantage. The patient hunter: sometimes the kindest cut is the one that only points. You mark the dying: once per turn, when you hit a creature below half HP with a Red Weapon, you may forgo the damage to leave a bleeding wound instead — the next attack against it before the end of your next turn has advantage.

Rules integration

Edge cases and shared systems.

Codex rulings: Direct rolled technique/weapon attacks can use the campaign's Black Flash procedure when otherwise eligible. Passive damage, hazards, summons acting independently, and initiative-20 Domain events cannot. Generic Maximum Output applies only to ordinary features, never named Maxima or Domains.
