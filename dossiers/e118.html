Carnage

Symbiote weapons / biomass / bloodlust / autonomous tendrils

Power tierTier V · Calamity
Core law

What the technique actually does.

Core Engine — Mass. Mass max = level + CE modifier. Gain 1 when you deal slashing/piercing damage to a living target, max once/turn; gain 2 when reducing one to 0. Spend Mass on weapons, regeneration, and detached attacks.

Full progression

Level 1 through 20.

1Red Weapon

Manifest axe, spear, blade, claw, or tendril; 1d8 and choose slashing/piercing. Once/turn 1 CE adds 1d8.

3Projectile Flesh

Spend 1 Mass: 60-ft ranged attack, 2d8 piercing; projectile dissolves after scene.

6Autonomous Tendrils

2 CE + 2 Mass, 1 minute: reaction once/round make tendril attack against creature entering 15 ft or attacking you.

9Red Regeneration

Bonus action spend up to PB Mass, heal 1d8 per Mass. Fire/sonic damage since last turn makes each die d4.

11Living Arsenal

Maintain two weapon forms; when taking Attack, one attack may originate from any point within 20 ft.

13Biomass Spawn

4 CE + 5 Mass: create one symbiote spawn for 1 minute; acts after you, one attack for 3d8, 1 HP, AC yours -2.

15Carnage Rules

Criticals on 19–20 with Red Weapons; critical grants 2 Mass. When Frightened/Charmed, may spend 3 Mass to end condition.

18Maximum Carnage

8 CE + 8 Mass, 1 minute: Large, 30-ft tendril reach, two reactions/round usable only for tendrils, regeneration dice maximized once/turn.

20Red Event

Mass max doubles. Start combat at PB Mass. Once/turn weapon hit can spend 3 Mass to erupt 4d10 extra slashing in 10-ft burst around target.

Pinnacle expression

Maximum, Domain, or equivalent.

Maximum — Carnage Storm (10 CE + 10 Mass): 40-ft radius erupts with blades and tendrils. Dex save 12d10 slashing, half success; failures are Restrained until end next turn.

Feats

Build-defining options.

Blood Trail:

know direction of creature you wounded within 1 mile for 1 hour.

Wall of Teeth:

reaction 1 Mass reduce melee damage 1d10+PB.

Red Rain:

Projectile Flesh can split between two targets.

Born Killer:

once/turn +1d8 against target below half HP.

Rules integration

Edge cases and shared systems.

Codex rulings: Direct rolled technique/weapon attacks can use the campaign's Black Flash procedure when otherwise eligible. Passive damage, hazards, summons acting independently, and initiative-20 Domain events cannot. Generic Maximum Output applies only to ordinary features, never named Maxima or Domains.
