Core law

At-the-table character reference

Recovered printed values & campaign clarifications

Maximum: Sacrifice:

at 11th level, when you have at least 3 Fangs of Orochi summoned, you can command them as an action for 6 cursed energy to move up to 40 ft, encircle a creature, and embed themselves into the ground in an unoccupied area. A barrier forms, connecting the Fangs like a cylinder resembling a trigram and trapping any within. The barrier has a height of 20 ft and can have a maximum diameter of 15 ft. It has an AC of 20 and its hit points equals hit points of all Fangs of Orochi used to create it. A trapped creature must make a cursed energy save at the start of their turns, taking 10d4 necrotic damage on a failed save or half as much on a successful save. If this damage reduces a humanoid to 0 hit points, the creature dies and you regain one life. Once you regain a life in this way, you cannot regain a life in this way for 1 week.

Core law

Completed rules

Eight Lives:

starting at 1st level, you have the ability to come back to life after you die, up to 8 times. When you are reduced to 0 hit points or die outright, you can spend 1 CE to come back to life at half your hitpoint maximum and harmful conditions other than exhaustion are removed. You have 8 lives and can use a life this way three times per long rest. This increases to four at 3rd level and eight at 6th level. You regain one expended life after eight days.

Collective Will:

at 3rd level, when you make an Intelligence, Wisdom, or Charisma ability check or saving throw, you can spend 1 cursed energy to reroll and must use the new roll. You can use this feature up to 8 times per long rest.

Fangs of Orochi:

at 6th level, as an action you can spend up to 8 cursed energy to summon an equal number of fangs from the jaws of Yamata no Orochi as shikigami. They appear in unoccupied spaces of your choice within 10 feet of you. You can command any Fangs you have summoned as a bonus action. These Fangs disappear if you become unconscious. The Fangs hit points increase to 20 at 11th level.

Innate Technique Feats

Build-defining options.

These are optional character-building feats. You must meet each listed prerequisite before selecting it.

Prerequisites: Must have Eight Lives

Snake Scales

Like Orochi, you have learned to shed your skin. As a reaction to taking damage for 2 CE, you slough off your outer scales: you end one of the following conditions affecting you — grappled, restrained, or poisoned — and can move up to 10 ft without provoking opportunity attacks, leaving the shed skin behind.

Prerequisites: Must have Collective Will

Blessing of the Hydra

When you use Eight Lives to return to life, you can gain resistance to one damage type that reduced you to 0 hit points or immunity to one harmful condition you had. This benefit lasts for 1 hour.

Prerequisites: Must have Fangs of Orochi

Fang Versatility

You have trained your technique to be more versatile in combat. You can wield Fangs of Orochi as a Dagger or Shortsword, and you can throw a Fang as a thrown weapon (range 20/60 ft) — a thrown Fang slithers back to your hand at the start of your next turn. When one of your Fang shikigami hits a creature, you can forgo the damage to have the Fang coil around the target's limb instead: the target has disadvantage on attack rolls until the end of your next turn.

Rules integration

Edge cases and shared systems.

Eight Lives: the persistent 8-life bank and the per-long-rest use allowance are separate — a long rest does not refill banked lives. Recover one banked life per 8 elapsed calendar days, capped at 8; no eight individual timers generating eight lives at once. Returning restores half your hit point maximum (rounded up) and removes conditions, but not already-spent CE or feat resources; pay the 1 CE from your actual remaining CE. The source explicitly handles outright death — keep that strength unless an effect expressly forbids revival or destroys the soul.
Collective Will: one reroll per check or save, and you must accept the new result; each reroll costs 1 CE and one of the 8 uses.
Fangs of Orochi: at most 8 active at once, one CE each, appearing within 10 ft; they disappear if you become unconscious. Base fang statistics beyond hit points (20 at 11th level) remain an image hold — do not substitute a generic stat block and call it verified.
Sacrifice: the barrier's hit points equal the sum of the committed fangs' current hit points; each committed fang cannot also attack elsewhere. Proposed: 1-minute barrier duration, and destroying the barrier releases the trapped creature — no automatic teleport capturing beyond actual encirclement. Restored life cannot exceed the bank of 8, and the weekly gate is one period per sorcerer, not per sacrifice zone.
Domain: on the first initiative 20 the apparition forms, then roll the d8 and bite up to that many distinct targets; subsequent initiative-20 events repeat the bites. No additional independent eight-head multiattack or legendary turns. The bite's damage and other traits remain an explicit hold.
Blessing of the Hydra: on revival choose resistance to the damage type that killed you OR immunity to one harmful condition you held — not both, and not all types. Lasts 1 hour.
Incarnation: the legacy Incarnation feat (collecting eight Heads of Orochi to incarnate the Special Grade Yamata no Orochi as a shikigami) is not offered and no current feat or feature grants Heads of Orochi — head acquisition is unavailable. The ritual's summon cost, duration, command rules, and combat block remain an explicit hold: the full ritual does not grant an unreviewed combat-ready Special Grade from the name alone. The Domain's false incarnation (initiative-20 bites, no independent actions) is the sole reviewed Orochi manifestation.

Snake Scales
Prerequisites: Must have Eight Lives

 As an action for 4 cursed energy, you summon the scales of Yamata no
Orochi to protect your flesh. Your AC cannot be lower than 14 + your
proficiency bonus and you become resistant to poison and slashing
damage for 1 hour.

Blessing of the Hydra
Prerequisites: Must have Collective Will

 When you use Eight Lives to return to life, you can gain resistance
to one damage type that reduced you to 0 hit points or immunity to
one harmful condition you had. This benefit lasts for 1 hour.

Fang Versatility
Prerequisites: Must have Fangs of Orochi

 You have trained your technique to be more versatile in combat. You
can wield Fangs or Orochi as a Dagger or Shortsword. On a hit with
this weapon, a creature must make a Constitution saving throw. On a
failure, the creature takes an additional 2d6 poison damage and is
poisoned until the end of their next turn. Your Fang Shikigami gain
the following improvements:
   ● The AC of your Fangs becomes 15 + your proficiency bonus.
   ● Your Fangs deal an additional 2d4 poison damage on a hit.

Incarnation
Prerequisites: Must have Maximum: Sacrifice

 When you use Sacrifice and kill a humanoid, instead of gaining a
life, you can summon a Head of Orochi as a shikigami under your
control. You can control or unsummon the head as a bonus action or
summon it as an action for 3 CE later, being stored somewhere on your
body as a tattoo of the head of a snake. This replaces one of your
Fangs of Orochi shikigami you can have active at once.

 Once you have used Sacrifice to gain all eight heads, the tattoos
come together to form a knot on your chest. As a Full-Turn Action
(Action + Bonus Action + Movement), you can complete the ritual and
incarnate the Special Grade Cursed Spirit: Yamata no Orochi as a
shikigami in an unoccupied space within 10 ft of you.
