Features & procedures

Properties & techniques

Exact player-facing procedures

Reconnaissance

action, 1 CE: throw to point within 90 ft; until next turn perceive from Sharur's position.

Counsel

once/round add one Tool Die to Investigation/Perception after rolling, 1 CE.

Crushing Report

if Sharur observed a target unseen by you since last turn, first hit against it adds 2 Tool Dice.

Release: Envoy of the Storm

7 CE, 1 minute, action to activate (invented): Sharur can hover 60 ft away and you may originate one tool attack/turn from its space.

Field notes. Subject: a counselor who can strike but never march. Three truths govern this mace. First, counsel is its whole engine: thrown to a point within 90 ft, it lends you its eyes until your next turn, and once per round it steadies a rolled Investigation or Perception check with one Tool Die — intelligence gathered, never combat fought. Second, it remembers: the Crushing Report rewards what Sharur observed but you did not, striking through your enemy's blindness with two extra Tool Dice. Third, it cannot be spent: it never takes a combat turn of its own, and the Release — Envoy of the Storm — merely lets its counsel arrive from sixty feet up for one minute. Assess it correctly: not a servant, not a soldier, but a voice with a reach. Attend to what it reports, and it will not be mistaken for quiet again.

Rules integration

Cursed-tool attunement

Campaign canon

Cursed-tool attunement: you have PB attunement slots (2–6). This tool occupies one slot and requires a one-hour ritual. Attunement ends voluntarily over a short rest, on death, or when you replace it while over capacity. Distance does not break attunement. Cursed-tool attunement is separate from magic-item attunement.
Balance & counterplay. Information is excellent, but the mace never becomes a free autonomous extra character.
