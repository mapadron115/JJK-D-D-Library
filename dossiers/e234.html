Special Grade cursed tool

Sharur

1d8 bludgeoning, thrown 30/90, returns. The tool can speak, perceive 30 ft, and has a distinct GM-controlled personality but cannot independently take combat turns.

FormTalking mace
RoleScout / returning weapon
EngineCounsel
Attunement1 hour*
Properties & techniquesExact player-facing procedures

Reconnaissance

action, 1 CE: throw to point within 90 ft; until next turn perceive from Sharur's position.

Counsel

once/round add one Tool Die to Investigation/Perception after rolling, 1 CE.

Crushing Report

if Sharur observed a target unseen by you since last turn, first hit against it adds 2 Tool Dice.

Release: Envoy of the Storm

7 CE, 1 minute: Sharur can hover 60 ft away and you may originate one tool attack/turn from its space.

Balance & counterplay. Information is excellent, but the mace never becomes a free autonomous extra character.
