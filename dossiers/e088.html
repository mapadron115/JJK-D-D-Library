Field notes. Subject: the Djinn of the loyal green world. The registry's phrasing stays with me: Zagan's cursed energy refuses to let anything under its care stay inert, wood fights, flesh regrows, flora answers. Three truths govern him. First, his loyalty is armed: Zaug Mobarezo fills a 180-ft line with wooden dragons, 7 Djinn Dice, grappling on failure, a spearhead that gardens. Second, his loyalty is a government: Life Dominion charms and commands beasts, plants, monstrosities, and fungi within 180 ft for a minute, or grows you a plant limb for eight hours. Third, the Garden of the Loyal King: a 250-ft ecosystem, enemies take 16 Djinn Dice and are Restrained on failure, allies heal 2 Djinn Dice at initiative count 20 for three rounds, and it remains as living forest afterward. Mark it: most techniques end when the fight ends. This one stays to tend the field.
