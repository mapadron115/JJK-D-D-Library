Features & procedures

Exact player-facing procedures.

Weapon Equip • Level 5

Living Spear.

Weapon Equip becomes Zagan’s double-ended spear and can create living projectiles, plants, and temporary prosthetics.

Action • 3 Reserve

Zaug Mobarezo

Wooden dragon projectiles fill a 180-ft line, 30 ft wide. Dexterity save; 7 Djinn Dice piercing/acid, half on success. Failed creatures are Grappled by living wood until the end of your next turn.

Action • 4 Reserve

Life Dominion

Choose a beast, plant, monstrosity, fungus colony, or Zagan-created lifeform within 180 ft. Wisdom save if hostile; on failure Charmed/controlled for up to 1 minute, repeating when harmed. Commanding a controlled creature is a bonus action; you can issue one command per round, the creature acts on your initiative directly after you, and you cannot order it to harm itself. Utility: create a functional plant limb/tool for 8 hours.

Action • 5 Reserve

Zaug Al-Adhra

Fire a swarm of living monsters into a 120-ft-radius point within 300 ft. Dexterity save; 10 Djinn Dice acid/piercing. Failed targets are Restrained by spawned life until the end of your next turn.

Full Djinn Equip • Level 9

Fly 90 ft; climb 90 ft; life-sight 120 ft. You can control plants/fungi within 300 ft and create harmless flora freely. Once per turn after an attack, spawn a Small acidic familiar in an unoccupied space within 5 ft of you; it threatens opportunity attacks until your next turn. AC 12 + PB, HP 5 × your level, speed 30 ft; its opportunity attack is +PB + CE modifier, dealing 1d8 + CE modifier acid.

14 Reserve • Codex Extreme

Garden of the Loyal King

A 250-ft-radius ecosystem erupts within 500 ft. Enemies make Dexterity saves; 16 Djinn Dice acid/piercing, half on success, and Restrained on failure until next turn. Allies gain half cover and heal 2 Djinn Dice at initiative count 20 for 3 rounds while inside. The terrain remains a living forest after the fight unless dismissed.

Anime-scale: Strategic scale: turn a city block into a predatory forest or raise an army of living constructs from the environment.

The fiction. Registry notes describe Zagan's vessel as loyalty imposed on the living world: the user's cursed energy refuses to let anything under its care stay inert, so wood fights, flesh regrows, and flora answers like subjects. The prevailing theory holds that the Garden of the Loyal King is not a battlefield reshaped but a kingdom remembered — life, once commanded by this sorcerer, simply never learned how to stop obeying.

Rules integration

Djinn Die, Equip & Extreme Magic

Independent Djinn Reserve: 18 (Rank S). Refreshes on a long rest. Every Djinn cost below — Weapon Equip, Full Equip, abilities, reactions, Extreme Magic — spends this Djinn’s Reserve, never your CE. If this Djinn’s Reserve is empty it simply cannot fuel more until you rest; your other Djinn keep their own separate Reserves.
Weapon Equip · level 5: bonus action, 2 Reserve, lasts 10 minutes.
Full Djinn Equip · level 9: bonus action, 4 Reserve, lasts 1 minute. Only one Equip can be active.
Deep Synchronization · level 11: once per short rest, one Equip activation costs 1 Reserve less.
Extreme Magic · level 13: action; requires that Djinn’s Full Equip; pay the listed Reserve cost from this Djinn’s Reserve; usable once per long rest per Djinn. Full Equip ends afterward, and you cannot Equip that Djinn again until you finish a short rest.
King Vessel · level 17: switch directly from one Full Equip to another as a bonus action, paying the new Equip cost. At level 20, once per long rest, immediately transition from one Djinn’s Extreme into another Djinn’s Full Equip.
Attack / save: use your CE attack modifier and CE save DC unless this Djinn explicitly says otherwise.
