DJINN 61 • Life / Plants / Beasts

Zagan — Loyalty & Purity

Rank S
Canon master: Hakuryuu RenBasis: Canon core • Zaug Mobarezo, Zaug Al-Adhra, life/plant/fungus control

Weapon Equip • Level 5

Living Spear. Weapon Equip becomes Zagan’s double-ended spear and can create living projectiles, plants, and temporary prosthetics.

Zaug Mobarezo

Action • 3 CE

Wooden dragon projectiles fill a 180-ft line, 30 ft wide. Dexterity save; 7 Djinn Dice piercing/acid, half on success. Failed creatures are Grappled by living wood until the end of your next turn.

Life Dominion

Action • 4 CE

Choose a beast, plant, monstrosity, fungus colony, or Zagan-created lifeform within 180 ft. Wisdom save if hostile; on failure Charmed/controlled for up to 1 minute, repeating when harmed. Utility: create a functional plant limb/tool for 8 hours.

Zaug Al-Adhra

Action • 5 CE

Fire a swarm of living monsters into a 120-ft-radius point within 300 ft. Dexterity save; 10 Djinn Dice acid/piercing. Failed targets are Restrained by spawned life until the end of your next turn.

Full Djinn Equip • Level 9

Fly 90 ft; climb 90 ft; life-sight 120 ft. You can control plants/fungi within 300 ft and create harmless flora freely. Once per turn after an attack, spawn a Small acidic familiar that occupies a nearby space and threatens opportunity attacks until your next turn.

EXTREME MAGIC • LEVEL 13+

Garden of the Loyal King

14 CE • Codex Extreme

A 250-ft-radius ecosystem erupts within 500 ft. Enemies make Dexterity saves; 16 Djinn Dice acid/piercing, half on success, and Restrained on failure until next turn. Allies gain half cover and heal 2 Djinn Dice at initiative count 20 for 3 rounds while inside. The terrain remains a living forest after the fight unless dismissed.

Anime-scale: Strategic scale: turn a city block into a predatory forest or raise an army of living constructs from the environment.
