Features & procedures

Exact player-facing procedures.

Weapon Equip • Level 5

Decomposition Spear.

The vessel becomes a long spear with one doctrine: ordinary metal, touched, disintegrates into particles. The first lesson of contempt, do not bother defeating what you can simply dissolve.

Weapon Equip becomes the long spear and can decompose ordinary metal on contact.

Action • 3 Reserve

Metal Decomposition

Nonmagical metal within thirty feet comes apart; magical or cursed metal must defend itself or be suppressed and battered. The audit, delivered at range: the arrogant man's gaze, weaponized.

Nonmagical metal within 30 ft disintegrates into particles. A magical/cursed metal object makes an item/technique save; on failure it is suppressed until the end of your next turn and takes 5 Djinn Dice force damage.

Bonus Action • 3 Reserve

Polarity Dominion

He takes hold of the battlefield's steel itself, up to his full measure of objects or armored creatures, dragged forty feet, disarmed on a failed save. The proud man's prerogative: the enemy's armor is his property; it merely hasn't been repossessed yet.

Manipulate up to PB metal objects or armored creatures within 120 ft. Hostile creatures make Strength saves or are pulled/pushed 40 ft and may be disarmed.

Action • 5 Reserve

Hadit Barad Iltehaq

Liquid metal becomes a storm of armor-piercing rounds, a hundred-and-eighty-foot cone that treats half and three-quarters cover as no cover. Cover is a negotiation, and this technique does not negotiate. The storm answers the question of shelter with contempt.

Liquid metal forms a storm of armor-piercing rounds in a 180-ft cone. Dexterity save; 9 Djinn Dice piercing/lightning, half on success. Treat half and three-quarters cover as no cover.

Full Djinn Equip • Level 9

He takes the sky on subjugated steel: flight, resistance to lightning and to blades of metal, and command over unattended metal within one hundred and eighty feet, his melee weapon now decomposing on hit for less. The principle, inverted: metal that has been properly humbled is permitted to serve.

Fly 90 ft; resistance to lightning and attacks made primarily of metal. Control unattended metal within 180 ft. Your melee weapon can attempt Metal Decomposition on hit for 1 Reserve less.

14 Reserve • Codex Extreme

Slaughtering Iron Heaven

The slaughter field forms, two hundred feet of magnetic judgment. The failed in metal armor are restrained; mundane steel disintegrates; magical metal must save or be suppressed for a full minute. Fortresses are stripped, formations annihilated, battlefields turned to controlled storms. This is not a battle. It is a recall of every blade on the field.

A 200-ft-radius magnetic slaughter field forms within 300 ft. Dexterity save; 16 Djinn Dice slashing/lightning, half on success. Failed creatures in metal armor are Restrained until next turn; mundane metal in the area disintegrates and magical metal must save or be suppressed for 1 minute, repeating each turn.

Anime-scale: Strategic scale: strip a fortress of metal, annihilate weapon formations, or turn a battlefield’s steel into a controlled storm.

Field notes. Subject: contempt, given a magnetic field. Three truths govern this arrogance. First: the energy does not manipulate metal so much as refuse to tolerate it, ordinary steel comes apart on contact because it was never bent, merely unmade. Second: the arrogance named in the title is not a flaw but the operating principle: dominion expressed as decomposition, an energy too proud to share the world with anything lesser. Third: the same principle, inverted, permits flight, metal properly subjugated is allowed to carry its master. Mark the maxim: he does not fight steel. He audits it, and finds it wanting.

Rules integration

Djinn Die, Equip & Extreme Magic

Damage-type chooser: when a Djinn ability lists two damage types, the Djinn King chooses which applies when the damage is dealt.
Independent Djinn Reserve: 16 (Rank A). Refreshes on a long rest. Every Djinn cost below, Weapon Equip, Full Equip, abilities, reactions, Extreme Magic, spends this Djinn’s Reserve, never your CE. If this Djinn’s Reserve is empty it simply cannot fuel more until you rest; your other Djinn keep their own separate Reserves.
Weapon Equip · level 5: bonus action, 2 Reserve, lasts 10 minutes.
Full Djinn Equip · level 9: bonus action, 4 Reserve, lasts 1 minute. Only one Equip can be active.
Deep Synchronization · level 11: once per short rest, one Equip activation costs 1 Reserve less.
Extreme Magic · level 13: action; requires that Djinn’s Full Equip; pay the listed Reserve cost from this Djinn’s Reserve; usable once per long rest per Djinn. Full Equip ends afterward, and you cannot Equip that Djinn again until you finish a short rest.
King Vessel · level 17: switch directly from one Full Equip to another as a bonus action, paying the new Equip cost. At level 20, once per long rest, immediately transition from one Djinn’s Extreme into another Djinn’s Full Equip.
Attack / save: use your CE attack modifier and CE save DC unless this Djinn explicitly says otherwise.
