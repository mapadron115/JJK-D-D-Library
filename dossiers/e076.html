DJINN 25 • Lightning / Magnetism / Metal

Glasya-Labolas — Slaughter & Arrogance

Rank A
Canon master: BarbarossaBasis: Canon core • metal manipulation, decomposition, Hadit Barad Iltehaq

Weapon Equip • Level 5

Decomposition Spear. Weapon Equip becomes the long spear and can decompose ordinary metal on contact.

Metal Decomposition

Action • 3 CE

Nonmagical metal within 30 ft disintegrates into particles. A magical/cursed metal object makes an item/technique save; on failure it is suppressed until the end of your next turn and takes 5 Djinn Dice force damage.

Polarity Dominion

Bonus Action • 3 CE

Manipulate up to PB metal objects or armored creatures within 120 ft. Hostile creatures make Strength saves or are pulled/pushed 40 ft and may be disarmed.

Hadit Barad Iltehaq

Action • 5 CE

Liquid metal forms a storm of armor-piercing rounds in a 180-ft cone. Dexterity save; 9 Djinn Dice piercing/lightning, half on success. Treat half and three-quarters cover as no cover.

Full Djinn Equip • Level 9

Fly 90 ft; resistance to lightning and attacks made primarily of metal. Control unattended metal within 180 ft. Your melee weapon can attempt Metal Decomposition on hit for 1 CE less.

EXTREME MAGIC • LEVEL 13+

Slaughtering Iron Heaven

14 CE • Codex Extreme

A 200-ft-radius magnetic slaughter field forms within 300 ft. Dexterity save; 16 Djinn Dice slashing/lightning, half on success. Failed creatures in metal armor are Restrained until next turn; mundane metal in the area disintegrates and magical metal must save or be suppressed for 1 minute, repeating each turn.

Anime-scale: Strategic scale: strip a fortress of metal, annihilate weapon formations, or turn a battlefield’s steel into a controlled storm.
