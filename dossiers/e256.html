Core law

At-the-table character reference

Recovered printed values & campaign clarifications

Shroud rider damage:

The printed +1d10 on a hit with a melee attack omits a damage type; it is read as necrotic, once per hit, because a single attack from a shrouding centipede cannot trigger the rider repeatedly. Two shrouding centipedes do not grant a second copy of AC + PB.

Damage transfer order:

Damage transfer is a choice on one resolved event before HP deduction: the new recipient takes that same already-mitigated amount without applying another set of resistances, cannot redirect it back, and centipede overflow reaches you. Two centipedes may retain two HP buffers even though duplicate AC bonuses do not stack.

Vermin of Disaster dice:

Vermin of Disaster deals 8d10 per projectile, not divided among the three; pay 8 cursed energy for the whole activation (Maximum Output surcharge is 4 CE before other legal adjustments). Repeated targets are allowed because the source does not require distinct targets. With two shrouds, the two bonus-action melee attacks replace the one-attack option.

Earthquake counterplay:

Earthquake is centered on you unless a verified block expressly changes it. It affects allies as written, and airborne creatures avoid it. If your cursed centipede is summoned, you can instead use it as a bonus action.

Storage capacity:

The printed score-based storage is kept: capacity equals twice your Cursed Energy score times your proficiency bonus in pounds (160 pounds at CE 20/PB 4). The centipede must be summoned to store or withdraw; stored objects emerge on your death.

Domain framing:

Oomukade Banzai is a hybrid Domain: ordinary summon attacks roll; at initiative 20 all other creatures in the effective area are restrained, escaping at the start of each turn with a Strength (Athletics) check against your CE save DC. "Restrained" is used consistently; no extra saving throw is added to the initial restraint. Anti-domain protection blocks this law.

The fiction. Scholars describe Centipede Shroud as hunger that learned to wear a person: the user's cursed energy refuses the boundary between devouring and protecting, so the same hundred-legged mouth that stores an ally's armaments eats an enemy whole. The prevailing theory holds that the shikigami is the sorcerer's own appetite given independence — it fights beside them, carries their burdens, and consumes their foes because those were always the same act to it. Registry notes add the grim rider: a centipede does not distinguish what it keeps from what it kills. It only knows what it has.

Full progression

Feature map

1Cursed Centipede.

Bonus action summon; bonus action command. As a shroud: +1d10 damage on melee hits, AC + PB, a bonus-action melee attack, and damage transfer — when you or the centipede take damage, you can choose to have the other take the damage instead. If this damage would reduce the centipede to 0 hit points, you take the remaining damage. One free summon per long rest, then 3 CE each; one centipede at a time.

3Insect Storage.

Store items in the centipede's stomach (no larger than a shield or polearm); capacity equals twice your Cursed Energy score times your proficiency bonus; store or withdraw as an object interaction while it is summoned.

6Earthquake.

Action for 3 CE: creatures within 30 feet on the ground make a Strength saving throw, 6d6 bludgeoning and prone on failure, half on success (10d6 at 11th, 14d6 at 18th). Bonus action if your centipede is summoned. Gain 5 stat points for the centipede's ability scores (max 22) and add your PB to its saves and checks.

11Vermin of Disaster.

Action for 8 cursed energy: launch up to 3 giant centipedes at targets within 120 feet, making a ranged cursed energy attack roll for each. On a hit, they deal 8d10 necrotic damage and the target must make a Strength saving throw. On a failure, the target is pushed or pulled 30ft closer or further (your choice) and falls prone. Additionally, you can now have two cursed centipedes summoned at a time; while both act as your shroud, you can make 2 melee attacks as a bonus action. You gain 5 additional stat points you can spend to increase your centipede's ability scores (max of 22).

18Domain Expansion: Oomukade Banzai.

At 18th level, you have mastered your technique allowing you to open your domain. Within your domain, your cursed centipedes's attacks are guaranteed hits. At initiative 20, all creatures are restrained by cursed centipedes and can make a Strength (Athletics) check at the beginning of their turns to escape. The cursed centipedes disappear once the creature is no longer grappled.

Innate Technique Feats

Build-defining options.

These are optional character-building feats. You must meet each listed prerequisite before selecting it.

Prerequisite: Must have Cursed Centipede

Dowsing Vibrations

While your centipede shikigami is summoned, you gain tremorsense out to 100 feet while both of your feet are on the ground. You cannot be surprised by creatures touching the ground within your tremorsense, and you know the exact location of invisible creatures touching the ground within it. The range increases to 150 feet at 6th level, 200 feet at 11th, and 300 feet at 18th level.

Prerequisite: Must have Insect Storage

Wu Gong

You have learned to concentrate the venom of your cursed centipede and add it to your attacks. When you or your centipede hit with a melee attack, you can spend 1 CE to force the creature to make a Constitution saving throw. On a failure, the creature takes your proficiency in d6s of poison damage and they are stunned until the beginning of their next turn.

Prerequisite: Must have Insect Storage

Living Gullet

Your centipede’s stomach can carry living cargo. It may swallow one willing or incapacitated creature of Medium size or smaller; the creature can breathe inside but is blinded and restrained while swallowed. Disgorging the creature takes an action. The centipede cannot store items while carrying a creature.

Rules integration

Edge cases and shared systems.

Rules priority: the reviewed rulings, explicit codex clarifications, and the level progression always control when original wording conflicts. Legacy Domain auto-hit language, action terminology, event timing, missing saves, and older scaling never override revised player rules.
Why Tier III: a shrouded shikigami that adds damage, AC, attacks, and a shared HP buffer, with burst necrotic projectiles and a restraining Domain — a premier skirmishing striker.
Hold — base creature block: the base centipede's AC, hit points, attacks, and duration remain a stat-block hold; the technique is not field-ready until the creature block is recovered. The rider damage type (necrotic) and transfer order are campaign proposals.
Wu Gong: no once-per-turn limit is printed — multiple legal hits can each pay the 1 CE. Poison-damage immunity does not itself imply stun immunity.
Centipede Festival: one corpse reward total, paid to one centipede; temporary hit points never stack.
Appendix

Additional material.

Centipede Shroud
Your technique summons a centipede shikigami to fight with you.

Cursed Energy
Starting at 1st level, you can harness your negative feelings into a
usable source of energy, called cursed energy. These feelings can be
harnessed into attacks or abilities. The number of cursed energy you
possess is based on your class, some classes use a modifier that
being charisma or wisdom (Player's choice)
Your cursed energy DC is 8 + user cursed energy ability modifier +
proficiency bonus.

Cursed Centipede
Starting at 1st level, you can summon your shikigami as a bonus
action. You can command your shikigami on your turn as a bonus
action. Your centipede can enter your space, floating around you as a
shroud providing the following benefits:
- On a hit with a melee attack, you deal an additional 1d10 damage
as your Centipede fights with you.
- You gain a bonus to AC equal to your proficiency bonus.
- You can make an additional melee attack as a bonus action.
- When you or the centipede take damage, you can choose to have the
other take the damage instead. If this damage would reduce the
centipede to 0 hit points, you take the remaining damage.
You can summon your shikigami for free once per long rest and again
for 3 CE. You can only have one Cursed Centipede summoned at a time.

Insect Storage
Starting at 3rd level, you can store items in the stomach of your
centipede. You must have your centipede summoned to take items in or
out of it and items can be no larger than a shield or polearm. Your
centipede can store a number of pounds equal to twice your Cursed
Energy score times your proficiency bonus and you can store or
withdraw an item as an object interaction. If you die, the items you
had stored in your centipede explode from your stomach.

Earthquake
Starting at 6th level, as an action you can spend 3 CE to force all
creatures within 30 feet to make a Strength saving throw if they are
on the ground. On a failure, they take 6d6 bludgeoning damage and
fall prone. On a success, they take half as much damage. If your
cursed centipede is summoned, you can instead do this as a bonus
action. This damage increases to 10d6 at 11th and 14d6 at 18th level.

Additionally, you gain 5 stat points you can spend to increase your
centipede's ability scores (max of 22). You can now add your
proficiency bonus to any saving throw or skill check it makes.
You gain 5 additional stat points at 11th level.

Vermin of Disaster
At 11th level, your technique increases by leaps and bounds. As an
action for 8 cursed energy, you can launch up to 3 giant centipedes
at targets within 120 feet, making a ranged cursed energy attack roll
for each. On a hit, they deal 8d10 necrotic damage and the target
must make a Strength saving throw. On a failure, the target is pushed
or pulled 30ft closer or further (your choice) and falls prone.
Additionally, you can now have two cursed centipedes summoned at a
time. While you have two cursed centipedes acting as your shroud, you
can make 2 melee attacks as a bonus action.

Domain Expansion: Oomukade Banzai
At 18th level, you have mastered your technique allowing you to open
your domain. Within your domain, your cursed centipedes's attacks are
guaranteed hits. At initiative 20, all creatures are restrained by
cursed centipedes and can make a Strength (Athletics) check at the
beginning of their turns to escape. The cursed centipedes disappear
once the creature is no longer grappled.
