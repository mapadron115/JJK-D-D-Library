Calamitous Strikes

When attacking with the Claws of Calamity you gain a +3 to attack and damage rolls, and deal 1d12 bludgeoning, slashing, or piercing damage.

Ungranted Mercy

The damage required for Limb Removal for arms increases by 20.

Campaign canon notes

Damage type [INVENTED]: the source lists three damage types without saying who chooses. Choose bludgeoning, slashing, or piercing when you make each attack.
Limb Removal threshold [INVENTED]: the source adds "20" to "the damage required for Limb Removal for arms" without a base threshold. Read as +20 to the arm-severing damage threshold under the campaign's Limb Removal rules — the increase applies when the wearer is the attacker, not when they are attacked.
