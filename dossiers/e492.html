Field entry

Journal Entry field report, converted to a table-ready stat block

Field notes. Subject: a haunting made of neglect. This one died slowly, illness, age, long unattended suffering, and what lingers is more lost than hostile: listless, slow to rise (disadvantage on initiative while its ward lies undisturbed), its touch a mere withering, 1d6+2 necrotic and a brief poisoning of the vitality. Consider the paradox of its vengeful classification: the violence is conditional, not native. Disturb its rest and the grudge wakes, +1 to its attacks against the disturber. Mark this for the junior sorcerers: the mission here is not to fight it but to find its haunt and end it gently, before anyone disturbs the ward. Some exorcisms are mercy work.

A Vengeful Cursed Spirit, Grade 4 (CR 1/8–1/2 band). Haunts hospitals, nursing homes, abandoned medical wards. Rarely attacks unless disturbed.

The effect is unpleasant but rarely fatal. Despite the vengeful classification, most Forgotten Patients appear more lost than malicious — exorcism is straightforward once the haunt is found; disturbing the ward is what provokes them.
