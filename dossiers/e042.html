100-Type Guanyin Bodhisattva

A prayer completed in less time than violence takes to begin calls forth a colossal golden Guanyin — the Hundred-Type Guanyin Bodhisattva of Isaac Netero, rebuilt here in the Archive's own language. Ten approach vectors and ten intentions form one hundred practiced hands: strikes, guards, grips, sweeps, and merciful repositioning. Every prayer resolves faster than sound, answering violence before it finishes — and when prayer is not enough, there is the Zero Hand: everything the user is, converted into light, and then the dark.

Feature map

Level-by-level features

L1

Prayer Palm

The hundredfold matrix: ten approach vectors (Zenith, Nadir, Forward, Return, Left, Right, four diagonals) × ten intentions (Open, Focused, Driving, Guarding, Pressing, Grasping, Piercing, Sweeping, Crossing, Merciful) = one hundred practiced hands. When you take the Attack action, you may replace any attack with a Palm (1 CE each): make a ranged attack using your CE ability against a target within Palm Reach (60 ft). On a hit, deal your Prayer Die (d6) + CE modifier force or radiant damage, plus the intention's effect — Driving hurls the target 30 feet along the vector, Grasping grapples it, Focused simply hits harder.

The motion is the condition. Each Palm requires one free hand and a deliberate prayer gesture. Being unconscious, paralyzed, or deprived of every usable hand prevents the gesture; silence does not.

First Hand: Heaven's Descent (2 CE, replaces one attack): the Guanyin manifests above a creature within Palm Reach — ranged CE attack; on a hit, 2 Prayer Dice + CE modifier force or radiant damage, and the target is driven 20 feet straight down. A grounded target falls prone and cannot take reactions until the start of its next turn.

L3

Prayer Before Violence

When a creature you perceive within Palm Reach declares an attack, begins voluntary movement, or begins activating a technique, you may use your reaction (1 CE plus the Palm's normal cost) to answer with a single Palm before it resolves — faster than sound. If the Palm incapacitates the creature, removes its legal targets, or moves it beyond the activation's range, the declared activation and the action used to begin it are expended; costs already paid stay spent. A durable opponent may simply endure the strike and finish what it began.

Third Hand: Converging Mercy (action, 3 CE): two converging ranged CE attacks against one creature within Palm Reach. Each hit deals Prayer Dice force or radiant damage and moves the target 10 feet toward a point you choose between the hands. If both hit, the target is restrained between persistent hands until the start of your next turn (escape: an action, Athletics, Acrobatics, or a CE-ability check against your CE save DC).

L6

Faster Than Sound

Your Prayer Die becomes d8 and Palm Reach becomes 120 feet. After completing an Attack action containing at least one Palm, you may use a bonus action (1 CE) to perform one additional Palm.

The statue answers twice in an action — and the interval keeps shrinking.

L11

Prayer of the Heart

Your Prayer Die becomes d10 and Palm Reach becomes 300 feet. No free hand is required for the prayer gesture, and your vector origins ignore half and three-quarters cover.

Ninety-Ninth Hand: Endless Litany (action, CE equal to your PB): make 2 × PB ranged CE attacks distributed among creatures within Palm Reach. Each hit deals 2 Prayer Dice force or radiant damage, and you may move each hit target up to 5 feet along any vector. It is called ninety-ninth because no one has needed the hundredth.

L18

Hundredfold Response

You may take a number of reactions each round equal to your proficiency bonus; the first is unrestricted, and every later one must be spent on Guanyin features (Prayer Before Violence or Guarding Palm). No more than one Guanyin reaction may occur during the same creature's turn.

Guarding Palm, perfected. When you or a creature within Palm Reach takes damage, use your reaction and 1 CE: reduce that instance by your Prayer Dice + CE modifier (minimum 0), then move a willing protected creature up to 10 feet along a vector of your choice. Gratitude guards as readily as it strikes.

L20

Prayer Outside Time

Your Prayer Die becomes d12 and Palm Reach becomes 600 feet. The prayer completes itself: at the start of each of your turns, perform one ordinary Palm without spending an action or CE.

The interval between gratitude and violence is now zero — and it belongs to you.

Pinnacle expression

Maximum and Domain.

Capstone material

Maximum: Zero Hand: Final Gratitude

As a full-turn action, spend all of your current CE (minimum 15): the Guanyin closes both hands around one creature you perceive within 600 feet and releases your entire reserve through its mouth as light. The target makes a Dexterity saving throw against your CE save DC: (CE spent + 2 × PB)d12 + CE modifier force or radiant damage on a failure, half on a success. Zero Hand ignores resistance and immunity. Unattended objects, structures, and barriers in a 20-foot sphere around the target take maximum rolled damage.

Afterwards your CE is 0 and you gain 2 levels of exhaustion. Zero Hand cannot critically hit or Black Flash, and it is a Maximum Technique — not a Reversal; mercy was always an intention, never an inversion. Netero's final argument, made of dawn.

Domain Expansion: Temple of Ten Thousand Gratitudes

18th level. Sure-hit domain. Activation: full turn, 20 CE (D008 baseline for a normal Domain). Clash: 1000 points; burnout 3 rounds; duration 3 min (Tier V mapping); radius 120 ft. Palm Reach covers the whole domain; vector origins need no physical route back to you and ignore cover; you need no hand or audible prayer.

Hundredfold Benediction (sure-hit). At initiative count 20, the Guanyin closes one massive golden hand around each hostile creature you choose inside — no attack roll: each takes 2 × PB Prayer Dice + CE modifier force or radiant damage and is grasped (restrained until the next initiative count 20). Each grasping hand has AC equal to your CE save DC and HP equal to 5 × PB (immune to poison and psychic); destroying a hand ends that creature's restraint. A creature protected from the sure-hit instead makes a Strength save: half damage, no grasp. Additionally, once during each of your turns, perform one ordinary Palm with no action and no CE. The temple has no walls. It has hands.

Feats

Five feats, numbered.

Each feat is optional, and you must meet its listed prerequisite before taking it.

Prerequisite: Prayer Palm

Unbroken Mudra

Your interrupted gestures still complete themselves. When a Palm attack misses, the mudra flows through unbroken: make a second Palm attack against a different creature within 15 feet of the original target as part of the same Palm, paying 1 CE for the echo. If the echo misses, the gesture ends.

Prerequisite: Prayer Palm

Great Compassion

Your mercy widens to cover two. When Merciful Palm moves a willing creature, you may spend 1 CE to move a second willing creature within 15 feet of the first the same distance along the same vector, resolved with the same roll. When Guarding Palm shields a willing creature, you may spend 1 CE to extend its damage reduction to a second willing creature adjacent to the first.

Prerequisite: Prayer Before Violence

Opening Salutation

Immediately after initiative is rolled, once per combat, you may perform one Merciful Palm on yourself or a willing creature within Palm Reach without spending an action, reaction, or CE. Resolve this movement before the first turn is taken; it cannot target an unwilling creature.

Prerequisite: Third Hand: Converging Mercy

Hundred-and-First Hand: Lotus Prison

As an action for 4 CE, choose one creature within Palm Reach and make three ranged CE attacks against it. Each hit deals 2 Prayer Dice of force or radiant damage; add your CE modifier to one hit only. If at least two attacks hit, spectral petals restrain the target until the end of your next turn. The prison has AC equal to your CE save DC and HP equal to 10 × PB; destroying it ends the restraint.

Prerequisite: Ninety-Ninth Hand: Endless Litany

The Hundredth Name

Invent your own hand: combine two intentions into a single Palm, paying 1 CE per intention and resolving both effects — a Grasping-Merciful hand that restrains a willing ally to drag them from danger without harming them, a Guarding-Driving hand that shoves an ally clear while shielding them. Once named, the hand is yours permanently; describe its prayer.

Rules integration

Edge cases and shared systems.

Campaign canon notes

Cursed Energy baseline: CE pool is based on class; CE ability is Charisma or Wisdom (player's choice). CE save DC = 8 + CE ability modifier + proficiency bonus. Regain all spent CE on a long rest.
Technique math: CE attack = proficiency bonus + CE modifier; CE save DC = 8 + proficiency bonus + CE modifier. Choose Wisdom or Charisma when gaining the technique; the choice governs every Guanyin attack, save, escape DC, and persistent hand.
The hundredfold matrix: ten approach vectors — Zenith, Nadir, Forward, Return, Left, Right, four diagonals — × ten intentions: Open (damage + 10-ft move), Focused (heavier damage, no move), Driving (30-ft move), Guarding (reaction: reduce damage), Pressing (prone + speed 0 against a wall), Grasping (grapple), Piercing (maximized dice vs objects and structures), Sweeping (cone or line, Dexterity save), Crossing (two targets), Merciful (move a willing creature, no damage). Name each combination however your tradition records it.
Prayer Die and Palm Reach by level: L1 — d6 / 60 ft; L6 — d8 / 120 ft; L11 — d10 / 300 ft; L20 — d12 / 600 ft.
No Reversal: the technique has no Cursed Technique Reversal. Merciful Palm is controlled forward application — RCT does not produce a wrathful deity, a healing Guanyin, or an absorption statue.
Criticals and Black Flash: each rolled Palm attack (Open, Focused, Driving, Pressing, Grasping, Piercing, Crossing, First, Third, Ninety-Ninth, Prayer Before Violence) may critically hit — its technique dice double — and may attempt Black Flash. Save-based effects, Guarding Palm, Zero Hand, and Hundredfold Benediction cannot critically hit or Black Flash.
Forced movement: follow the chosen vector in a straight legal path; it causes no opportunity attacks and ends at the nearest legal space if blocked. For each 10 feet of movement prevented by solid terrain, the moved creature takes 1d6 bludgeoning damage, maximum PBd6.
Domain activation defaults: rendered as full turn + 20 CE per the D008 normal-Domain baseline, with Tier V clash stats (1000 / 3-round burnout / 3 min) and a 120-ft radius.
Maximum vs. Reversal: the Guanyin's pinnacle is written as a Maximum Technique (Zero Hand: Final Gratitude), not a Reversal; it occupies the technique's pinnacle slot.
