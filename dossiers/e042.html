100-Type Guanyin Bodhisattva

A prayer completed in less time than violence takes to begin calls forth a colossal golden Guanyin. Ten approach vectors and ten intentions form one hundred practiced hands: strikes, guards, grips, sweeps, merciful repositioning, and the final gratitude that empties the user completely.

Tier IV measures the inheritance, not its most famous user.

The technique earns Special Grade through long-range control, action interception, repeated reactions, and an all-reserve ultimate. It remains below Tier V because it does not passively rewrite reality or make its user untouchable. It supplies the prayer-speed attack engine—not Netero’s age, martial-arts mastery, physical statistics, durability, political authority, or implanted weapon.

Core prayer

Gesture. Manifest. Strike.

The Bodhisattva is an instantaneous technique manifestation. It has no initiative, independent actions, creature statistics, or permanent position. Each completed prayer creates exactly the hand needed for that activation.

Level 1 · replaces one attack · 1 CE

Prayer Palm

When you take the Attack action, replace one attack with a Palm. Choose one of the ten vectors and one of the ten intentions in the Hundredfold Matrix, then resolve that pattern. An attacking Palm uses a ranged CE attack against a target within Palm Reach and suffers no disadvantage because an enemy is adjacent to you.

A Palm is a cursed technique attack even when its intention uses a saving throw. You may replace as many attacks as you possess, paying for each. The matrix does not impose a universal once-per-turn limit.

Action · sequence

Prayer Sequence

As an action, perform a number of Palms up to your Hands per Sequence, paying 1 CE for each Palm actually performed. Choose every target, vector, and intention separately and resolve the hands in order. You may stop the sequence early. Prayer Sequence is not the Attack action.

At level 1 it contains one Hand, becoming two at level 6, three at level 11, and four at level 18. Extra actions and attacks from other features remain distinct and can coexist normally.

Prayer gesture

The motion is the condition

At levels 1–10, each Palm requires one free hand and the ability to make a deliberate prayer gesture. A hand supporting a two-handed weapon may be released and returned as part of the Palm. Being unconscious, paralyzed, or deprived of every usable hand prevents the gesture. Silence does not.

At level 11, Prayer of the Heart removes the physical-hand requirement. A user may still perform the gesture because its meaning and visual identity remain.

Manifestation

No summon stat block

The Guanyin appears adjacent to you or through the vector origin, completes the chosen motion, and vanishes. It cannot normally be attacked, grappled, banished, commanded, ridden, or used as independent cover. Persistent Grasping and Pressing Hands use the specific construct rules printed in the matrix.

Suppression prevents new Hands and ends persistent ones. An anti-summon effect does nothing unless it also suppresses cursed techniques or manifestations.

Forced movement

The vector is chosen before the roll

When an intention moves a target, follow the chosen vector in a straight legal path. This is forced movement and causes no opportunity attacks. Movement ends at the nearest legal space if another creature or solid obstacle blocks the path.

For each 10 feet of movement prevented by a solid collision, the moved creature takes 1d6 bludgeoning damage, maximum PBd6. A struck creature or destructible object takes the same collision damage after a Dexterity save for half.

Vertical hands

Up and down remain physical

An upward vector can leave a target airborne; it falls normally after the Palm unless another effect supports it. A downward vector against a grounded creature converts otherwise impossible downward movement into prone. Pressing Hand supplies its additional pin.

Forced movement cannot pass through total cover, a sealed barrier, or an occupied space too small for the target. Palm Reach measures the target, not the final square of forced movement.

Ordinary example: a level-6 user chooses the right-side vector and Driving intention. On a hit, the target takes 2d8 + CE modifier force damage and moves up to 30 feet to the user’s right. If a wall stops 20 feet of that movement, add 2d6 collision damage.

Hundredfold Matrix

Ten vectors × ten intentions.

The one hundred types are combinations, not one hundred unrelated subsystems. Choose an approach vector, then an intention. Players may number, name, or describe individual combinations however their character’s tradition records them.

Vector origin procedure

The Hand approaches from the declared angle

Choose an unoccupied origin no more than 10 feet from the target in the vector’s direction. The origin must lie within Palm Reach and have a physical route to you that does not cross total cover or a sealed barrier. Resolve attack cover and forced movement from that origin. At level 11, Prayer of the Heart expands the origin distance to 30 feet and lets the Hand ignore half and three-quarters cover, though total cover remains total.

Persistent Hands

Grasp and press can be broken

A persistent Grasping or Pressing Hand has AC equal to your CE save DC and HP equal to 5 × PB. It is immune to poison and psychic damage and resistant to attacks that are neither magical nor CE-infused. Destroying or suppressing it ends its condition.

Damage choice

Force or radiant

Choose force or radiant damage separately for each Guanyin activation unless an intention specifies piercing. The visual appearance need not change. Collision and falling damage retain their normal types.

Same pattern

Repeat what works

You may use the same vector and intention repeatedly. The hundred-pattern library rewards options but never requires forced rotation, random rolls, a combo meter, or pretending a mastered Hand becomes unavailable.

Why the matrix matters: Focused Palm from Zenith is a different learned prayer from Focused Palm through Return, even when neither moves the target. Its origin changes cover, interception angle, presentation, and where the Guanyin can legally manifest.

Canonical named prayers

Four Hands stand outside the matrix.

These named patterns have bespoke costs and effects. They do not consume or lock ordinary Hands, and the user may repeat them whenever their timing and CE permit.

Level 1 · replaces one attack · 2 CE

First Hand: Heaven’s Descent

Choose a creature within Palm Reach. The Guanyin manifests above it; make a ranged CE attack. On a hit, the creature takes 2 × PB Prayer Dice + CE modifier force or radiant damage and is driven up to 20 feet straight downward. A grounded target instead falls prone and cannot take reactions until the start of its next turn.

First Hand is one direct attack. Its Prayer Dice double on a critical hit and belong to the completed critical damage if it becomes a Black Flash.

Level 3 · action · 3 CE

Third Hand: Converging Mercy

Choose one creature within Palm Reach and make two ranged CE attacks against it, representing the two converging palms. Each hit deals PB Prayer Dice force or radiant damage; add your CE modifier to one hit of the activation. Each successful palm moves the creature up to 10 feet toward a point you choose between the hands.

If both attacks hit, the target is restrained between persistent Hands until the start of your next turn. It may use an action for Strength (Athletics), Dexterity (Acrobatics), or a CE ability check against your CE save DC to escape early. Each attack may critically hit and attempt Black Flash independently.

Level 11 · action · PB CE

Ninety-Ninth Hand: Endless Litany

Make 2 × PB ranged CE attacks and distribute them among creatures within Palm Reach. Each hit deals 2 Prayer Dice of force or radiant damage; add CE modifier to one hit of the entire activation. After each hit, move that target up to 5 feet in any matrix vector. Resolve attacks and movement one at a time.

Each attack can critically hit and attempt Black Flash independently. If the sequence drives a creature into solid terrain, calculate collision damage once after its final hit from the total movement that terrain prevented, maximum PBd6. Endless Litany is not the Attack action and does not inherit ordinary matrix intentions.

Level 18 · full-turn action · all current CE, minimum 15

Zero Hand: Final Gratitude

Choose one creature you perceive within 600 feet and spend all current CE. The Guanyin manifests behind it, closes both hands around it for the activation, and releases the user’s entire reserve through its mouth. The target makes a Dexterity save, taking (CE spent + 2 × PB)d12 + CE modifier force or radiant damage on failure or half on success. Zero Hand ignores resistance and immunity.

Unattended objects, structures, barriers, and technique constructs in a 20-foot-radius sphere around the target take maximum rolled damage. After resolution, your current CE is 0 and you gain 2 exhaustion levels. Zero Hand has no per-rest cap: acquiring enough CE and accepting further exhaustion are the limits.

Zero Hand classification: it is a named ultimate release, not a Cursed Technique Reversal or generic Maximum Technique. It cannot critically hit, Black Flash, receive Maximum Output, or combine with Chanting. Its all-current-CE cost is paid after every other cost or reduction affecting the user at that moment.

Reaction tempo

The prayer resolves before the declared act.

Netero’s defining advantage is not simply damage. The ritual motion occupies an impossibly short interval and lets the Guanyin answer an opponent before their chosen violence finishes.

Level 3 · reaction · normal Palm cost

Prayer Before Violence

When a creature you perceive within Palm Reach declares an attack, begins voluntary movement, or begins activating a spell, Innate Technique, cursed tool, Domain, or other perceivable feature, use your reaction before it resolves. Perform one Open, Focused, Driving, Pressing, Grasping, Piercing, or Merciful Palm against that creature, paying the normal 1 CE.

After the Palm resolves, the triggering activation continues if it remains possible. If the creature is incapacitated, loses every legal target, is moved outside the feature’s range or line of effect, or otherwise can no longer perform the declared activation, that activation and the action used to begin it are expended; costs that had already been paid remain spent. Domain Expansion cannot be stopped merely by moving its center once its full-turn activation has legally begun unless the user is incapacitated or its own rule says otherwise.

Level 11

Faster Than Thought

You gain one additional reaction each round. It may be used only for Prayer Before Violence or Guarding Palm. You cannot respond to one trigger more than once, and you cannot take more than one reaction during the same creature’s turn unless another feature explicitly permits it.

Level 18

Hundredfold Response

You may take up to PB reactions each round. Your first reaction is unrestricted; every later reaction is restricted to Prayer Before Violence or Guarding Palm. No more than one Guanyin reaction may occur during the same creature’s turn. Unused reactions do not carry into the next round.

Simultaneous declarations

Resolve the visible commitment

If several creatures trigger the feature simultaneously, choose one with each available reaction and resolve those Palms in any order. A creature whose activation becomes impossible loses only its own activation; other declared actions continue.

Other reaction features

Distinct sources coexist

An extra reaction granted by a class, feat, item, Domain, or another feature remains distinct and can coexist. Each reaction retains its printed restrictions. One triggering event cannot be answered twice unless a feature expressly permits multiple responses to that event.

Important limit: Prayer Before Violence does not automatically counter techniques. The Palm must hit and change the fiction enough to make the declared activation impossible. A durable opponent may simply endure the strike and finish what they began.

Level 18 · Domain Expansion

Temple of Ten Thousand Gratitudes.

An endless mountaintop temple encloses the battlefield. Every bell toll is one completed prayer, and a ring of golden Guanyin figures surrounds those selected for judgment.

Expansion

The temple closes

As a full-turn action, spend 20 CE to form an enclosed 60-foot-radius Domain centered on you for 1 minute. Choose creatures to bring inside using the campaign’s complete Domain rules. Barrier defenses, clashes, refinement, anti-domain techniques, and Technique Burnout function normally.

Every vector present

The target stands at the center of the mandala

Palm Reach covers the Domain. Vector origins can appear in any unoccupied space inside, ignore cover, and require no physical route back to you. You need no hand or audible prayer. Persistent Hands created inside gain twice their normal HP.

Sole sure-hit event

Benediction—not every Palm—is inevitable

Hundredfold Benediction at initiative count 20 is the Domain’s only guaranteed hostile effect. Ordinary Palms, named Hands, Prayer Before Violence, and Zero Hand use their normal attack rolls or saving throws. The temple perfects origins, tempo, and reach without replacing the user’s rolls.

Ceaseless gratitude

One free Hand between moments

Once during each of your turns, perform one ordinary matrix Palm without an action or CE. It remains subject to its intention’s timing; Guarding Palm still requires a reaction and therefore is not eligible for this free Hand. This Palm may be used before, between, or after other attacks.

Reaction ring

Every hostile movement is heard

Hundredfold Response can perceive every chosen hostile creature inside. Total cover does not prevent its trigger or manifestation. Anti-domain protection removes the sure-hit but does not make a visible creature imperceptible.

Zero inside the temple

Final Gratitude remains final

Zero Hand retains its Dexterity save even inside the Domain. It still spends all current CE, grants 2 exhaustion, and receives no free damage, reduced cost, critical hit, or refund from the temple. Only Hundredfold Benediction possesses the sure-hit.

Initiative 20 · Hundredfold Benediction · sure-hit

All hands descend together

Each chosen hostile creature automatically takes 2 × PB Prayer Dice + CE modifier force or radiant damage, moves up to 40 feet along a vector you choose, and falls prone. A creature protected from the sure-hit instead makes a Strength save, taking the complete result on a failure or half damage with no movement or prone on a success. This event costs no CE and is one damage instance per target.

Domain ending: free Hands and Domain vector origins vanish with the barrier. Ordinary persistent Hands created before or during the Domain survive only if their own duration remains and their post-Domain origin and target positions still form a legal effect.

Rules reference

No empty interval between rulings.

Use these procedures whenever a Hand interacts with critical hits, Black Flash, Maximum Output, cover, forced movement, sequences, Domains, or multiclass action economy.

Technique math

Normal CE statistics

CE attack = PB + CE modifier
CE save DC = 8 + PB + CE modifier

Choose Wisdom or Charisma when gaining the technique. The choice governs every Guanyin attack, save, escape DC, and persistent Hand.

Prayer Dice

Level changes the die

The Prayer Die is d6 at levels 1–5, d8 at 6–10, d10 at 11–17, and d12 at 18–20. PB, intention, and named Hand determine the number rolled.

No Reversal

Mercy is an intention, not inversion

The technique has no Cursed Technique Reversal. Merciful Palm is controlled forward application. RCT does not automatically produce a wrathful deity, healing Guanyin, absorption, or another reverse statue.

Criticals and Black Flash

Each direct Palm is its own chance

Open, Focused, Driving, Pressing, Grasping, Piercing, Crossing, First, Third, Ninety-Ninth, and Prayer Before Violence attacks may critically hit. Their technique dice double on the critical. After a qualifying critical, roll the second unmodified d20; a natural 20 makes Black Flash and triples the entire completed critical damage. There is no frequency cap.

Save and automatic effects

No roll means no critical

Guarding Palm, Sweeping Palm, Merciful movement, Zero Hand, Hundredfold Benediction, collision damage, and Domain automatic hits cannot critically hit or trigger Black Flash. Crossing, Third, and Ninety-Ninth resolve each rolled attack independently.

Maximum Output: ordinary Palm

Double one Hand’s complete cost

Pay 2 CE instead of 1 and choose one current Maximum Output mode. Damage adds half the intention’s original Prayer Dice, rounded up. Accuracy adds half the original CE attack bonus, rounded up, or raises a save DC by half PB, rounded up. Range increases Palm Reach or one area measurement by half. A persistent Hand’s duration instead increases by half.

Maximum Output: sequences

One declaration covers the activation

For Prayer Sequence, Third Hand, or Ninety-Ninth Hand, double the full activation cost. Accuracy enhances every attack in that activation. Damage adds half the activation’s total original Prayer Dice, rounded up, distributed among successful attacks as you choose. Range increases Palm Reach by half. Zero Hand and the Domain are ineligible.

Flat modifiers

Apply exactly where printed

CE modifier applies once to an ordinary single-target Palm. Crossing, Third, and Ninety-Ninth explicitly apply it to only one hit of their activation. Critical hits double dice, never a flat modifier.

Collision

One movement, one impact pool

A creature takes collision damage once for each distinct Palm that moves it into an obstruction. Ninety-Ninth combines its entire barrage into one collision pool after the last attack. Fall damage remains a separate environmental instance.

Conditions

Same-name restraint does not stack

Several Grasping or Pressing Hands can coexist, but Grappled, Prone, speed 0, and Restrained do not intensify through same-name stacking. Destroying one Hand removes only the effect it created.

Cover

Origin changes the attack line

Before level 11, a vector may attack around partial cover only if a legal origin and route exist. At level 11 it ignores half and three-quarters cover. Total cover, sealed barriers, and effects that forbid manifestation still block ordinary Hands.

Objects

Piercing is the demolition prayer

Other Palms damage objects normally when targeted or through collision. Piercing Palm maximizes its rolled Prayer Dice against unattended objects, structures, barriers, and constructs; it does not automatically erase artifacts or plot-defined indestructible matter.

Actions and multiclassing

Attack replacement is not a global cap

Every attack in an Attack action may be replaced with a Palm if its CE is paid. Prayer Sequence and Ninety-Ninth are separate actions. Faster Than Sound and Prayer Outside Time use their printed timing. Distinct extra actions, attacks, and reactions from other sources can stack unless expressly forbidden.

Attack interruptions

Resolve the Palm, then reassess legality

Prayer Before Violence does not rewind a completed effect. It occurs after declaration and payment but before resolution. If the triggering creature remains conscious and has a legal way to finish, it does. Otherwise the committed action and paid cost are lost.

Fast sequence procedure

Resolve a Prayer Sequence in four lines

  1. State how many Hands you will attempt; pay 1 CE immediately before each Hand.
  2. Name that Hand’s target, vector, and intention.
  3. Resolve its attack or saving throw, movement, collision, and persistent effect completely.
  4. Continue to the next Hand or stop; unused planned Hands cost no CE.
Four Innate Technique feats

Devotion becomes personal style.

These optional feats specialize prayer form, protection, opening tempo, or one additional named Hand. Each occupies a normal feat choice, may be taken only once, and this four-feat list is complete.

Prerequisite: Prayer Palm

Unbroken Mudra

You may complete the prayer gesture with any usable limb or a deliberately moved held weapon, so lacking a free hand does not prevent a Palm; unconsciousness, paralysis, or losing every means of deliberate movement still does. Once on each of your turns, a Prayer Sequence may contain one additional ordinary Palm beyond your Hands per Sequence if you pay that Palm’s normal CE.

Prerequisite: Hundredfold Matrix

Great Compassion

Guarding Palm rolls one additional Prayer Die and may move its willing protected creature up to 20 feet instead of 10. When Merciful Palm moves a willing creature, choose a second willing creature within 15 feet of the first; move it up to the same distance along the same vector without another roll or CE cost.

Prerequisite: Prayer Before Violence

Opening Salutation

Add your CE modifier, minimum +1, to initiative. Immediately after initiative is rolled, once per combat, perform one Merciful Palm on yourself or a willing creature within Palm Reach without an action, reaction, or CE. Resolve this movement before the first turn; it cannot target an unwilling creature.

Prerequisite: Third Hand, level 8

Hundred-and-First Hand: Lotus Prison

As an action for 4 CE, choose one creature within Palm Reach and make three ranged CE attacks against it. Each hit deals 2 Prayer Dice of force or radiant damage; add your CE modifier to one hit only. If at least two attacks hit, spectral petals restrain the target until the end of your next turn. The prison has AC equal to your CE save DC and HP equal to 10 × PB; destroying it ends the restraint.

Character guide

The inheritance is broad; the discipline is personal.

The technique supports martial artists, armored guardians, ranged tacticians, contemplative sorcerers, or frightening prodigies. It does not demand maximum CE statistics or one moral interpretation of gratitude.

Striker

Focused and First

Use Focused Palm for efficient direct damage and First Hand when extra CE, prone, and reaction denial justify the heavier prayer.

Controller

Drive, press, and grasp

Choose vector before rolling. Walls, ledges, hazards, ally reach, and the target’s next legal action matter more than repeating the largest dice pool.

Guardian

Guard and mercy

Guarding Palm reduces one damage instance; Merciful Palm extracts allies without damaging or dropping them. Neither requires a specific class or weapon.

Duelist

Make their first move second

Prayer Before Violence becomes strongest when you understand the opponent’s reach, line of effect, and activation requirements. Movement only interrupts what it actually makes illegal.

CE economy

One Palm is cheap; tempo is not

A level-18 Sequence, free-timing Palm, and several reactions can spend 8 or more CE in one round before a named Hand. The technique’s ceiling is allowed because every completed prayer is paid.

Ability priorities

Build the sorcerer beneath the statue

Wisdom or Charisma governs the technique. Dexterity supports initiative and armor; Constitution protects HP, Focus, Domain, and survival after Zero. Strength is optional unless the character’s class needs it.

Complete combat loop: choose a vector before the roll → use a light Palm to test movement → intercept the opponent’s committed answer → sequence different intentions through the new position → reserve Ninety-Ninth or Zero for the moment ordinary gratitude is no longer enough.

Adaptation choice

What “one hundred” means here

Only a handful of numbered Hands receive explicit individual demonstrations in the source. This guide therefore preserves First, Third, Ninety-Ninth, and Zero as bespoke features while representing the unrevealed library through ten origins multiplied by ten intentions. The result is complete without inventing ninety-six supposedly canonical attacks.

Adaptation boundary: the miniature rose, Netero’s martial arts, superhuman physical speed outside the prayer, raw durability, age, memories, social position, and personal philosophy are not granted by the Innate Technique.
