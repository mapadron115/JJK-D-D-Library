100-Type Guanyin Bodhisattva

A prayer completed in less time than violence takes to begin calls forth a colossal golden Guanyin, the Hundred-Type Guanyin Bodhisattva of Isaac Netero, rebuilt here in the Archive's own language. Ten approach vectors and ten intentions form one hundred practiced hands: strikes, guards, grips, sweeps, and merciful repositioning. Every prayer resolves faster than sound, answering violence before it finishes, and when prayer is not enough, there is the Zero Hand: everything the user is, converted into light, and then the dark.

Feature map

Level-by-level features

Field notes. Subject: the sorcerer who prays faster than violence. Three truths govern him. First, gratitude is the condition: every Palm begins with a deliberate prayer gesture, the motion is the technique, and the statue answers only the praying. Second, the hundredfold matrix is complete: ten approach vectors crossed with ten intentions, one hundred practiced hands, strikes, guards, grips, sweeps, and merciful repositioning, each resolving faster than sound, answering violence before it finishes. Third, the prayer has a final argument: Zero Hand converts everything he is into light, and the Domain’s temple has no walls, it has hands. Mark the Chairman’s arithmetic: first, gratitude. Then, the strike. You will not hear the space between.

L1

Prayer Palm

Attack action (replace one attack) · 1 CE each · Ranged CE attack within Palm Reach (60 ft); on hit: Prayer Die (d6) + CE mod force/radiant + intention effect (Driving: hurl 30 ft; Grasping: grapple; Focused: harder hit). Requires one free hand and a prayer gesture.

The hundredfold matrix: ten approach vectors crossed with ten intentions, one hundred practiced hands.

The hundredfold matrix: ten approach vectors (Zenith, Nadir, Forward, Return, Left, Right, four diagonals) × ten intentions (Open, Focused, Driving, Guarding, Pressing, Grasping, Piercing, Sweeping, Crossing, Merciful) = one hundred practiced hands.

The motion is the condition. Each Palm requires one free hand a deliberate prayer gesture. Being unconscious, paralyzed, or deprived of every usable hand prevents the gesture; silence does not.

First Hand: Heaven's Descent (2 CE, replaces one attack): the Guanyin manifests above a creature within Palm Reach, ranged CE attack; on a hit, 2 Prayer Dice + CE modifier force or radiant damage, and the target is driven 20 feet straight down. A grounded target falls prone and cannot take reactions until the start of its next turn.

L3

Prayer Before Violence

Reaction · 1 CE + Palm cost · When a perceived creature within Palm Reach declares an attack, movement, or technique: answer with a Palm before it resolves; if it removes the activation's conditions, the declared action is expended. Third Hand: Converging Mercy: action · 3 CE · two ranged CE attacks vs one creature in Reach; each hit: Prayer Dice force/radiant + move 10 ft; both hit: restrained until start of your next turn (escape: action, Athletics/Acrobatics/CE-ability check vs CE save DC).

The statue answers first. Faster than sound.

A durable opponent may simply endure the strike and finish what it began.

L6

Faster Than Sound

Passive · Prayer Die d8, Palm Reach 120 ft. Bonus action · 1 CE · After an Attack action containing a Palm: one additional Palm.

The interval shrinks. The statue answers twice in an action.

The statue answers twice in an action, and the interval keeps shrinking.

L11

Prayer of the Heart

Passive · Prayer Die d10, Palm Reach 300 ft; no free hand needed; vector origins ignore half/three-quarters cover. Ninety-Ninth Hand: action · CE = PB · 2×PB ranged CE attacks within Reach; each hit: 2 Prayer Dice force/radiant and move the target 5 ft along any vector.

The prayer internalizes. Called ninety-ninth because no one has needed the hundredth.

Ninety-Ninth Hand: Endless Litany — the attacks may be distributed among creatures within Palm Reach.

L18

Hundredfold Response

Passive · Reactions per round = PB (first unrestricted; rest on Guanyin features only; max one Guanyin reaction per creature's turn). Guarding Palm: reaction · 1 CE · when you or a creature in Reach takes damage: reduce by Prayer Dice + CE mod, then move a willing protected creature 10 ft along a vector.

The statue multiplies its attention. Gratitude guards as readily as it strikes.

Guarding Palm, perfected (reduction minimum 0).

L20

Prayer Outside Time

Passive · Prayer Die d12, Palm Reach 600 ft. Start of each turn: one ordinary Palm, no action or CE.

The prayer completes itself. The interval between gratitude and violence is now zero, and it belongs to him.

Capstone material

Maximum: Zero Hand: Final Gratitude

Full-turn action · Spend all current CE (min 15) · One perceived creature within 600 ft: Dex save vs CE save DC: (CE spent + 2×PB)d12 + CE mod force/radiant, half on success; ignores resistance/immunity; 20-ft sphere of unattended objects/structures/barriers takes max damage. Afterwards: CE 0, 2 exhaustion. Can't crit or Black Flash.

The final argument, made of dawn: the Guanyin closes both hands around one perceived creature and releases his entire reserve as light. And mercy was always an intention, never an inversion.

Zero Hand is a Maximum Technique, not a Reversal. Netero's final argument, made of dawn.

Domain Expansion: Temple of Ten Thousand Gratitudes

Domain · Full turn · 20 CE · 120 ft, 3 min, 1000 clash, 3-round burnout. Sure-hit at initiative 20: each chosen hostile, no roll: 2×PB Prayer Dice + CE mod force/radiant, grasped (restrained until next initiative 20). Grasping hands: AC = CE save DC, HP = 5×PB, immune to poison/psychic.

A temple with no walls, only pillars of golden light receding into brightness. Above, the Bodhisattva unfolds, vast and serene, her hundred arms spreading like dawn breaking in every direction at once. The air is thick with the scent of incense, and every breath feels like a prayer being answered by force.

The temple closes. The temple has no walls. It has hands.

Palm Reach covers the whole domain; vector origins need no physical route back to you and ignore cover; you need no hand or audible prayer.

Hundredfold Benediction (sure-hit). Destroying a grasping hand ends that creature's restraint. A creature protected from the sure-hit instead makes a Strength save: half damage, no grasp. Additionally, once during each of your turns, perform one ordinary Palm with no action and no CE.

Feats

Five feats, numbered.

Each feat is optional, and you must meet its listed prerequisite before taking it.

Prerequisite: Prayer Palm

Unbroken Mudra

The interrupted gesture completes itself.

Your interrupted gestures still complete themselves. When a Palm attack misses, the mudra flows through unbroken: make a second Palm attack against a different creature within 15 feet of the original target as part of the same Palm, paying 1 CE for the echo. If the echo misses, the gesture ends.

Prerequisite: Prayer Palm

Great Compassion

His mercy widens to cover two. Compassion, budgeted.

Your mercy widens to cover two. When Merciful Palm moves a willing creature, you may spend 1 CE to move a second willing creature within 15 feet of the first the same distance along the same vector, resolved with the same roll. When Guarding Palm shields a willing creature, you may spend 1 CE to extend its damage reduction to a second willing creature adjacent to the first.

Prerequisite: Prayer Before Violence

Opening Salutation

The first prayer, before the battle. The statue greets before it strikes.

Immediately after initiative is rolled, once per combat, you may perform one Merciful Palm on yourself or a willing creature within Palm Reach without spending an action, reaction, or CE. Resolve this movement before the first turn is taken; it cannot target an unwilling creature.

Prerequisite: Third Hand: Converging Mercy

Hundred-and-First Hand: Lotus Prison

The hundred-and-first hand.

As an action for 4 CE, choose one creature within Palm Reach and make three ranged CE attacks against it. Each hit deals 2 Prayer Dice of force or radiant damage; add your CE modifier to one hit only. If at least two attacks hit, spectral petals restrain the target until the end of your next turn. The prison has AC equal to your CE save DC and HP equal to 10 × PB; destroying it ends the restraint.

Prerequisite: Ninety-Ninth Hand: Endless Litany

The Hundredth Name

The open hand. A Grasping-Merciful hand to drag an ally from danger unharmed; a Guarding-Driving hand to shove one clear. The hundredth was never numbered; it is named by its user.

Invent your own hand: combine two intentions into a single Palm, paying 1 CE per intention and resolving both effects, a Grasping-Merciful hand that restrains a willing ally to drag them from danger without harming them, a Guarding-Driving hand that shoves an ally clear while shielding them. Once named, the hand is yours permanently; describe its prayer.

Rules integration

Edge cases and shared systems.

Campaign canon notes

Cursed Energy baseline: CE pool is based on class; CE ability is Charisma or Wisdom (player's choice). CE save DC = 8 + CE ability modifier + proficiency bonus. Regain all spent CE on a long rest.
Technique math: CE attack = proficiency bonus + CE modifier; CE save DC = 8 + proficiency bonus + CE modifier. Choose Wisdom or Charisma when gaining the technique; the choice governs every Guanyin attack, save, escape DC, and persistent hand.
The hundredfold matrix: ten approach vectors, Zenith, Nadir, Forward, Return, Left, Right, four diagonals, × ten intentions: Open (damage + 10-ft move), Focused (heavier damage, no move), Driving (30-ft move), Guarding (reaction: reduce damage), Pressing (prone + speed 0 against a wall), Grasping (grapple), Piercing (maximized dice vs objects and structures), Sweeping (cone or line, Dexterity save), Crossing (two targets), Merciful (move a willing creature, no damage). Name each combination however your tradition records it.
Prayer Die and Palm Reach by level: L1: d6 / 60 ft; L6: d8 / 120 ft; L11: d10 / 300 ft; L20: d12 / 600 ft.
No Reversal: the technique has no Cursed Technique Reversal. Merciful Palm is controlled forward application, RCT does not produce a wrathful deity, a healing Guanyin, or an absorption statue.
Criticals and Black Flash: each rolled Palm attack (Open, Focused, Driving, Pressing, Grasping, Piercing, Crossing, First, Third, Ninety-Ninth, Prayer Before Violence) may critically hit, its technique dice double, and may attempt Black Flash. Save-based effects, Guarding Palm, Zero Hand, and Hundredfold Benediction cannot critically hit or Black Flash.
Forced movement: follow the chosen vector in a straight legal path; it causes no opportunity attacks and ends at the nearest legal space if blocked. For each 10 feet of movement prevented by solid terrain, the moved creature takes 1d6 bludgeoning damage, maximum PBd6.
Domain activation defaults: rendered as full turn + 20 CE per the D008 normal-Domain baseline, with Tier V clash stats (1000 / 3-round burnout / 3 min) and a 120-ft radius.
Maximum vs. Reversal: the Guanyin's pinnacle is written as a Maximum Technique (Zero Hand: Final Gratitude), not a Reversal; it occupies the technique's pinnacle slot.
