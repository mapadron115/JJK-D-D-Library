Chimeric Night Cry

Core law

Character engine

Choose one Aspect each turn—Monkey, Tiger, Serpent, Tanuki—and cannot choose the same one three turns consecutively. Later features briefly overlap bodies without giving permanent all-mode superiority.

Technique save DC: 8 + PB + chosen CE ability modifier. Technique attack: PB + chosen CE ability modifier.

The fiction. The prevailing theory holds that Chimeric Night Cry is not inherited but witnessed — the sorcerer's CE imprinted on a thing seen once in the dark, and has been refusing to settle on a single shape ever since. Registry scholars classify the four Aspects as one person's energy admitting it could be many bodies at once: monkey's precision, tiger's fury, serpent's reach, tanuki's elusiveness, swapped turn by turn because the night-cry that made the technique refuses to be only one animal. The rule against choosing the same Aspect three turns in a row is, they note, the technique's honesty — a chimera that could stay still would have no reason to exist.

Full progression

Level progression

1Fourfold Body

You gain the four Aspects of the technique. Each turn you choose one active Aspect — Monkey, Tiger, Serpent, or Tanuki — and you cannot choose the same Aspect three turns consecutively. Monkey: skill and object precision. Tiger: +1d8 on your first melee hit (invented: the die is a d8). Serpent: one attack gains +10 feet of reach and a CE-based grapple. On a hit you may grapple the target using your CE ability modifier in place of Strength for the grapple check; the escape DC = your CE save DC (invented). Tanuki: +10 speed, and opportunity attacks against you have disadvantage.

3Night Cry

As an action for 2 CE, you loose the Night Cry in a 30-foot cone. Each creature in the cone makes a Wisdom save: on a failure it takes 2d8 of psychic damage (invented) and is frightened of you until the end of your next turn. If you changed Aspect since your last turn, the wail deals +1d8 of psychic damage (invented).

6Crossbred Transition

Whenever you change Aspect, you may spend 1 CE to retain your previous Aspect through the current turn as well, for a maximum of two Aspects active at once.

11Unclassifiable Beast

When you are targeted by an attack or effect, you may use your reaction to change your Aspect immediately. If the new body meaningfully counters the attack — the GM decides, using the guideline that the new Aspect’s listed benefit directly opposes the attack’s vector (invented) — you gain +2 AC or +2 to the relevant saving throw. You can use this reaction once per round.

18Maximum: Chimera Beneath the Black Cloud

As an action (invented), you spend 6 CE to become the Chimera Beneath the Black Cloud for 1 minute: you maintain two Aspects automatically, and you may add a third Aspect for 1 CE per turn. Night Cry becomes a 60-foot cone, and a creature that fails its save by 5 or more is stunned until the start of your next turn.

Feats

Build-defining options.

Monkey's Cunning

While the Monkey aspect is active, you gain proficiency in Sleight of Hand, or expertise if you are already proficient. You may perform one fine-manipulation object interaction — picking a lock, palming a component, cutting a purse — as a bonus action instead of an action, and creatures have disadvantage on Wisdom (Perception) checks to notice it while you remain in Monkey aspect. The aspect's precision is in the hands, not just the eyes.

Tiger's Pounce

While the Tiger aspect is active, when you hit a creature with a melee attack after moving at least 15 feet straight toward it, you may force it to make a Strength saving throw against your technique DC. On a failure it is knocked prone; on a success it is pushed 5 feet away from you. You may use this once per turn, and it has no effect on a creature two or more sizes larger than you.

Serpent's Patience

While the Serpent aspect is active, when you have a creature grappled you may use your action to make a contested grapple check; on a success the grapple tightens into a restraint as your coils lock, lasting until the grapple ends or you change Aspect. A restrained creature takes 1d8 of bludgeoning damage (invented) at the start of each of its turns while the restraint holds. Changing Aspect ends the restraint but not the underlying grapple.

Tanuki Mist

While the Tanuki aspect is active, you may attempt to Hide even when only lightly obscured by natural phenomena — mist, foliage, falling snow, or dim light. If you end your turn hidden this way without having attacked, you may move up to 10 feet without breaking hiding, your Tanuki swiftness carrying you between cover. Creatures have disadvantage on checks to track you by scent or sound while you remain hidden under this feat.

Rules integration

Counterplay

The active body plan is visible and the same Aspect cannot be spammed forever. Force choices between offense, mobility, reach and utility.
