Core law

Character engine

Choose one Aspect each turn—Monkey, Tiger, Serpent, Tanuki—and cannot choose the same one three turns consecutively. Later features briefly overlap bodies without giving permanent all-mode superiority.

Technique save DC: 8 + PB + chosen CE ability modifier. Technique attack: PB + chosen CE ability modifier.

Full progression

Level progression

1Fourfold Body

Monkey: skill/object precision. Tiger: first melee hit +1 die. Serpent: one attack +10 reach and CE-based grapple. Tanuki: +10 speed and OAs against you have disadvantage.

3Night Cry

Action, 2 CE, 30-ft cone Wis save; 2 dice psychic + frightened until end next turn; +1 die if you changed Aspect since last turn.

6Crossbred Transition

On Aspect change, spend 1 CE to retain previous Aspect through current turn; max two Aspects.

11Unclassifiable Beast

Reaction when targeted: change Aspect immediately; if new body meaningfully counters attack, +2 AC or relevant save. Once/round.

18Maximum: Chimera Beneath the Black Cloud

6 CE, 1 minute: maintain two Aspects automatically, optional third for 1 CE/turn; Night Cry 60 ft and failure by 5+ stuns until start next turn.

Feats

Build-defining options.

proficiency/expertise in Sleight of Hand while Monkey is active.

Tiger's Pounce

Serpent's Patience

sustained grapple can become restrained after contested action.

Tanuki Mist

Rules integration

Counterplay

The active body plan is visible and the same Aspect cannot be spammed forever. Force choices between offense, mobility, reach and utility.
