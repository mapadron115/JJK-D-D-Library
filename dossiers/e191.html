Core law

Character engine

Choose one Aspect each turn—Monkey, Tiger, Serpent, Tanuki—and cannot choose the same one three turns consecutively. Later features briefly overlap bodies without giving permanent all-mode superiority.

Technique save DC: 8 + PB + chosen CE ability modifier. Technique attack: PB + chosen CE ability modifier.

Full progression

Level progression

1Fourfold Body

Monkey: skill/object precision. Tiger: first melee hit +1 die. Serpent: one attack +10 reach and CE-based grapple. Tanuki: +10 speed and OAs against you have disadvantage.

3Night Cry

Action, 2 CE, 30-ft cone Wis save; 2 dice psychic + frightened until end next turn; +1 die if you changed Aspect since last turn.

6Crossbred Transition

On Aspect change, spend 1 CE to retain previous Aspect through current turn; max two Aspects.

11Unclassifiable Beast

Reaction when targeted: change Aspect immediately; if new body meaningfully counters attack, +2 AC or relevant save. Once/round.

18Maximum: Chimera Beneath the Black Cloud

6 CE, 1 minute: maintain two Aspects automatically, optional third for 1 CE/turn; Night Cry 60 ft and failure by 5+ stuns until start next turn.

Feats

Build-defining options.

Monkey's Cunning

While the Monkey aspect is active, you gain proficiency in Sleight of Hand, or expertise if you are already proficient. You may perform one fine-manipulation object interaction — picking a lock, palming a component, cutting a purse — as a bonus action instead of an action, and creatures have disadvantage on Wisdom (Perception) checks to notice it while you remain in Monkey aspect. The aspect's precision is in the hands, not just the eyes.

Tiger's Pounce

While the Tiger aspect is active, when you hit a creature with a melee attack after moving at least 15 feet straight toward it, you may force it to make a Strength saving throw against your technique DC. On a failure it is knocked prone; on a success it is pushed 5 feet away from you. You may use this once per turn, and it has no effect on a creature two or more sizes larger than you.

Serpent's Patience

While the Serpent aspect is active, when you have a creature grappled you may use your action to make a contested grapple check; on a success the grapple tightens into a restraint as your coils lock, lasting until the grapple ends or you change Aspect. A restrained creature takes 1 die of bludgeoning damage at the start of each of its turns while the restraint holds. Changing Aspect ends the restraint but not the underlying grapple.

Tanuki Mist

While the Tanuki aspect is active, you may attempt to Hide even when only lightly obscured by natural phenomena — mist, foliage, falling snow, or dim light. If you end your turn hidden this way without having attacked, you may move up to 10 feet without breaking hiding, your Tanuki swiftness carrying you between cover. Creatures have disadvantage on checks to track you by scent or sound while you remain hidden under this feat.

Rules integration

Counterplay

The active body plan is visible and the same Aspect cannot be spammed forever. Force choices between offense, mobility, reach and utility.
