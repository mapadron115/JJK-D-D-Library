Field entry

Journal Entry field report, converted to a table-ready stat block

Field notes. Subject: noise weaponized. One Fly Head is a joke the Registry barely records; a hundred of them, clenched into a single buzzing cube, become an argument against thinking itself. Mark the doctrine: the swarm does not wound so much as unmake. Its Buzzing Mass forces a Constitution save on any creature starting its turn inside, and failure means a minute of dulled senses and frayed concentration, disadvantage on Perception, and on saves made to hold a spell’s thread. It resists bludgeoning, piercing, and slashing, so it shrugs off the obvious answers. This is the censor as a weapon: disperse it, or end it on sight, before your sorcerers spend their turns inside its noise.

Buzzing Mass. The combined noise and movement overwhelm the senses: each creature that starts its turn inside the swarm must succeed on a DC 11 Constitution saving throw or have disadvantage on Wisdom (Perception) checks and on Constitution saving throws made to maintain concentration for 1 minute. Field guidance: disperse the swarm or exorcise it on sight — the chance of harm is low, but the distraction is real.
