Trick Mirror

A Grade 4 cursed item, a small handheld mirror.

FormCursed Item
GradeGrade 4
AttunementNot required

Field notes. Subject: the sidelong glance, made glass. Curses cast no reflection and appear in no photograph, the registry’s oldest frustration, and the reason this small mirror keeps its one useful lie. Two truths govern it. First, it shows only what the glass could truly show: study the reflection as an action, and any curse in its reflected field appears, presence and position, revealed, but only where line of sight to the reflected image honestly reaches. Second, it is detection, not dominion: the mirror names the enemy’s place; it does not touch him. Mark this: some truths you can only meet sideways. Look directly at the curse and see nothing; look at the glass, and there he stands.

Action

Reflection of Curses

An action spent in deliberate study, not a glance, but a looking. What the mirror’s field could honestly reflect, it refuses to hide: the curse’s presence, and where it stands. As an action, you can study the mirror's reflection to look for curses in its field of view. Any curse within the mirror's reflected area that would be invisible to you appears in the reflection, letting you detect its presence and position. A curse is revealed only if it would actually be visible in the reflection from where you hold the mirror, line of sight to the reflected image; the GM adjudicates distance (invented).

Campaign canon notes

Table function: the source gives no action cost for using the mirror. Scanning the reflection deliberately costs an action; the mirror only reveals what is in its reflected field of view.
