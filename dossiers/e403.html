Properties & techniques

Exact player-facing procedures

Action

Reflection of Curses

As an action, you can study the mirror's reflection to look for curses in its field of view. Any curse within the mirror's reflected area that would be invisible to you appears in the reflection, letting you detect its presence and position. The mirror does not grant sight beyond what is reflected in it.

Rules integration

Edge cases and shared systems.

Campaign canon notes

Table function: the source gives no action cost for using the mirror. Scanning the reflection deliberately costs an action; the mirror only reveals what is in its reflected field of view.
