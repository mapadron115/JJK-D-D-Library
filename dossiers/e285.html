Starlight Symphony

You can create and mold a shining cursed fog.

Power tierTier III · Apex
Combat roleController
Core engineCursed fog and fox shikigami
DomainHeaven and Earth (L18)
Full progression

Feature map

1Cursed Fog.

Starting at 1st level, you can spend Cursed Energy up to your Cursed Energy modifier as an action. You create 10 feet of fog around yourself for each cursed energy. The fog lasts for 1 minute, spreads around corners, heavily obscures the area and you choose if the fog follows you or not as part of the action to create it. Creatures within your fog have disadvantage on Perception and Investigation checks and cannot detect cursed energy.

At 6th level, you can use your reaction to give advantage or disadvantage to a roll made by a creature within your cursed fog and can spend up to twice your Cursed Energy modifier when creating your cursed fog. You can spend up to thrice your Cursed Energy modifier at 11th level.

3Kitsune Creation.

At 3rd level, you can summon a fox shikigami as an action for 2 cursed energy. The shikigami lasts for 24 hours or until dismissed as a free action. Your fox shikigami act on your turn and you can command all fox shikigami as a bonus action. You can have a number of shikigami summoned equal to your proficiency bonus and always know if your shikigami has died.

6Living Fog.

At 6th level, you have learned to become the fog itself. As an action for 3 Cursed Energy you transform yourself or one of your fox shikigami into a misty cloud for up to 1 hour while concentrating. While in this form, your only method of moving is a fly speed of 30 feet. You can enter and occupy the space of another creature. While occupying the space of another creature, you can force them to make a Constitution saving throw, taking Xd8 equal to your Cursed Energy modifier in cold damage on a failed save as they choke on your fog.

You have resistance to nonmagical damage and advantage on Strength, Dexterity, and Constitution saving throws. You can pass through small holes, narrow openings, and even mere cracks, though you treat liquids as though they were solid surfaces. You can't fall and remain hovering in the air even when stunned or otherwise incapacitated. While in the form of a misty cloud, you can't manipulate objects, and any objects you were carrying or holding can't be dropped, used, or otherwise interacted with. You can spend an additional 3 cursed energy to also become invisible for the duration.

11Inari.

At 11th level, as a full turn action for 8 cursed energy you can summon Inari in an unoccupied space within 10 feet. Inari disappears after 1 minute and uses your proficiency bonus. You can command Inari and your fox shikigami as an action on your turn. Inari is considered one of your fox shikigami for any of your features other than Living Fog.

Maximum: Fog of Exorcism.

Prerequisite: Inari. As an action for 10 cursed energy, you can blast a 60 ft cone with an erupting wave of destructive exploding fog. All creatures within range must make a Constitution saving throw, taking twice your CE modifier XD10 + 6d10 cold damage on a failed save or half as much damage on a success. If a creature is a cursed spirit, the damage ignores any resistance to cold damage and it makes the save at disadvantage. If used at Maximum Output, Cursed spirits automatically fail their saving throw.

18Domain Expansion: Heaven and Earth.

At 18th level, you have completed your technique, allowing you to expand your domain. Your domain is an expansive field of fog with two towering spirits at the north and south. All creatures other than you are completely blinded by the cursed fog within your domain and suffer the effects of your Cursed Fog. At initiative 20, all creatures take 5d10 cold damage as they struggle to breathe in the cursed fog.

Innate Technique Feats

Build-defining options.

These are optional character-building feats. You must meet each listed prerequisite before selecting it.

Prerequisite: Must have Kitsune Creation

Fox in the Mist

You can fold a fox shikigami into a mote of your cursed fog and carry it with you. As an action for 1 cursed energy, you dismiss one of your fox shikigami into a mote you carry: it does not count against your active shikigami limit while carried. As a bonus action, you can release it into an unoccupied space within 30 feet of you.

Prerequisite: Must have Living Fog

Fox Fire

You can create incredibly dense fog that can be triggered to erupt in a vapor explosion. For an additional 3 CE when you create an area of Cursed Fog, you make the fog incredibly thick. As an action, you can cause the fog to detonate in a vapor explosion. All creatures within the fog other than yourself must make a Constitution saving throw, taking twice your Cursed Energy modifier in d8s of cold damage or half as much damage on a success as a blue explosion rips through, igniting the fog.
