Feature map

Level-by-level features, as the figure refined them.

L1

Elastic Center

Bonus Action · 1 CE.

Two ground points ≤30 ft apart define a Center Line (1 min). Once per round, when a hostile crosses toward your side, a willing ally within 5 ft of the line may use their Reaction to step 5 ft back (no opportunity attack); the line shifts with them.

L3

Left & Right Wings

Passive (requires Center Line).

10-ft Wing zones open at each endpoint. A hostile across the line while both Wings hold conscious allies is Committed. Once per round, when a Committed creature ends movement, one ally per Wing may shift 5 ft toward it, no action required. No separate action or CE — the rider runs off the established line.

L6

Close the Pocket

Passive (requires Center Line).

A Committed creature within the Center ally's reach and at least one creature from each Wing becomes Enveloped. Disengage protects from only one direction per turn. No separate action or CE — the rider runs off the established line.

L11

Cavalry Reserve

Bonus Action · 0 CE · once per Center Line.

Designate one willing ally outside the formation as the reserve. Once per round, when the geometry is breached, the reserve ally may use their Reaction to move half speed and repair it. No damage buff — discipline, not fury.

L18

Cross the Impossible Ground

Passive (requires Center Line).

The line extends to 60 ft, the Wings to 15 ft; the formation ignores nonmagical difficult terrain, and you may bend one endpoint 10 ft once per round. No separate action or CE — the rider runs off the established line.

Capstone material.

Maximum Technique

Cannae: The Double Envelopment

Action · 8 CE · Focus, 1 min.

The line extends to 60 ft with both Wings established. Enveloped creatures treat retreat as difficult terrain and cannot Disengage against more than one role. The working breaks if a role is incapacitated or displaced — a cage made of people and positioning, not an invisible wall.

Level 20 · Capstone

Every Field Is Cannae

Once per long rest.

When you establish your Center Line, you may declare it self-commanding: the formation persists for 1 minute after you are incapacitated or leave the field, anchored on the Center ally — roles, Committed, and Enveloped states continue in your name. The doctrine outlives the commander.
