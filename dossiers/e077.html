Exact player-facing procedures.

Undying Ember

Add 3 Djinn Dice fire and ignite the target. At the start of its turns it takes 2 Djinn Dice fire for up to 1 minute; an action and successful Dexterity/technique check can suppress it for that creature.

Action • 4 Reserve

White Hellfire

Create a 70-ft-radius blast within 180 ft. Dexterity save; 7 Djinn Dice fire. The area remains burning for 1 minute, dealing 2 Djinn Dice to creatures entering it.

Action • 5 Reserve

Purgatory Dragon

A white-flame dragon tears through a 300-ft line, 30 ft wide. Dexterity save; 10 Djinn Dice fire, half on success. The burning trail persists until dismissed or magically drained.

Full Djinn Equip • Level 9

Fly 110 ft; fire resistance; your fire ignores resistance, and immunity becomes resistance. Creatures burning from Astaroth cannot regain hit points until the start of your next turn.

14 Reserve • Canon Extreme

Astor Inqerad

Summon the Flying White Flash Dragon from Purgatory through a 600-ft-long, 100-ft-wide path. Dexterity save; 18 Djinn Dice fire/radiant, half on success. Failed creatures ignite for 3 Djinn Dice per turn. The white flames remain on terrain until you dismiss them or an external absorption/negation effect removes them.

Anime-scale: Strategic scale: incinerate an army corridor or leave a fortress route burning in enduring white hellfire.

The fiction. Djinn scholars describe Astaroth's white hellfire as endurance made flame: fire that refuses to go out because Terror and Meditation are both refusals — one of fear, one of stillness. The vessel's theory holds that the Djinn's power answers only sorcerers who can hold a contradiction — to burn a fortress while sitting perfectly still — and the undying ember is the price of that answer.

Rules integration

Djinn Die, Equip & Extreme Magic

Damage-type chooser: when a Djinn ability lists two damage types, the Djinn King chooses which applies when the damage is dealt.
Independent Djinn Reserve: 18 (Rank S). Refreshes on a long rest. Every Djinn cost below — Weapon Equip, Full Equip, abilities, reactions, Extreme Magic — spends this Djinn’s Reserve, never your CE. If this Djinn’s Reserve is empty it simply cannot fuel more until you rest; your other Djinn keep their own separate Reserves.
Weapon Equip · level 5: bonus action, 2 Reserve, lasts 10 minutes.
Full Djinn Equip · level 9: bonus action, 4 Reserve, lasts 1 minute. Only one Equip can be active.
Deep Synchronization · level 11: once per short rest, one Equip activation costs 1 Reserve less.
Extreme Magic · level 13: action; requires that Djinn’s Full Equip; pay the listed Reserve cost from this Djinn’s Reserve; usable once per long rest per Djinn. Full Equip ends afterward, and you cannot Equip that Djinn again until you finish a short rest.
King Vessel · level 17: switch directly from one Full Equip to another as a bonus action, paying the new Equip cost. At level 20, once per long rest, immediately transition from one Djinn’s Extreme into another Djinn’s Full Equip.
Attack / save: use your CE attack modifier and CE save DC unless this Djinn explicitly says otherwise.
