DJINN 29 • Heat / Eternal Hellfire

Astaroth — Terror & Meditation

Rank S
Canon master: Kouen RenBasis: Canon core • white flames and Astor Inqerad

Weapon Equip • Level 5

Purgatory Sword. Weapon Equip channels white hellfire that persists unnaturally and can only be dismissed, drained, or overcome by comparable magic.

Undying Ember

On hit • 2 CE

Add 3 Djinn Dice fire and ignite the target. At the start of its turns it takes 2 Djinn Dice fire for up to 1 minute; an action and successful Dexterity/technique check can suppress it for that creature.

White Hellfire

Action • 4 CE

Create a 70-ft-radius blast within 180 ft. Dexterity save; 7 Djinn Dice fire. The area remains burning for 1 minute, dealing 2 Djinn Dice to creatures entering it.

Purgatory Dragon

Action • 5 CE

A white-flame dragon tears through a 300-ft line, 30 ft wide. Dexterity save; 10 Djinn Dice fire, half on success. The burning trail persists until dismissed or magically drained.

Full Djinn Equip • Level 9

Fly 110 ft; fire resistance; your fire ignores resistance, and immunity becomes resistance. Creatures burning from Astaroth cannot regain hit points until the start of your next turn.

EXTREME MAGIC • LEVEL 13+

Astor Inqerad

14 CE • Canon Extreme

Summon the Flying White Flash Dragon from Purgatory through a 600-ft-long, 100-ft-wide path. Dexterity save; 18 Djinn Dice fire/radiant, half on success. Failed creatures ignite for 3 Djinn Dice per turn. The white flames remain on terrain until you dismiss them or an external absorption/negation effect removes them.

Anime-scale: Strategic scale: incinerate an army corridor or leave a fortress route burning in enduring white hellfire.
