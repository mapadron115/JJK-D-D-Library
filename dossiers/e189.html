Hundred-Demon Revel

Oni inspiration. Switch visible Oni Masks: Red for force, Blue for endurance, Black for terror. Revel earned through takedowns and successful saves lets you switch more aggressively and power physical dominance.

Combat roleStance-switch bruiser
Power tierTier IV · Special Grade
Core law

Character engine

Switch visible Oni Masks: Red for force, Blue for endurance, Black for terror. Revel earned through takedowns and successful saves lets you switch more aggressively and power physical dominance.

Technique save DC: 8 + PB + chosen CE ability modifier. Technique attack: PB + chosen CE ability modifier.

Field notes. Subject: a sorcerer who wears his intent in the open and dares the enemy to read it. Three truths govern the Revel. First: the mask is the telegraph. Red for force, Blue for endurance, Black for terror, the wearer declares his stance and forfeits surprise; victory must be won by declaration, not by deception. Second: belief is fuel. Revel is earned only through takedowns and techniques weathered, the story grows truer each time it is tested, and the tested story hits harder. Third: mastery is the change, not the mask. Anyone can wear one face; the bruiser who swaps stances mid-exchange fights three opponents inside one body. Maximum: all three masks at once, the Revel so certain it no longer asks the wearer's permission. Mark the paradox: the more completely he is believed, the less he needs to be understood.

Full progression

Level progression

1Three Oni Masks

Three faces of the same performance, and the performer chooses the scene. I have watched sorcerers win battles in the moment they switched, not because the new mask was stronger, but because the enemy had finished studying the old one. Red for force, Blue for endurance, Black for terror: the mask is both the armor and the announcement. Three Oni Masks (bonus action, 1 CE, 1 minute): assume one mask. Red: your first melee hit after closing distance (you moved at least 10 feet toward the target before the hit, invented) deals +1d8 (invented: the die is a d8). Blue: reduce the first physical damage you take each round by your PB. Black: on a hit, you can prevent the target from moving closer until the end of your next turn on a failed Wisdom save (invented: duration).

3Kanabō Principle

Mark this: the mask promises force, and the Kanabō settles the promise, every weapon in his hand agrees to become the iron club, because the role of the oni was never subtlety. When the critic demands the ending, he obliges. Any melee weapon you wield becomes a magical iron club with a minimum versatile d10 damage die. Your critical hits push the target 10 feet or knock it prone.

6Demon's Feast

Revel is the audience's applause converted to fuel. Consider the economy: each takedown, each technique withstood, buys one freer switch, the story keeps itself believable by outrunning the enemy's read on it. Strength here is not summoned; it is invoiced, and paid in full. When you score a takedown or succeed on a save against a technique, you gain 1 Revel (maximum PB). You may spend Revel for a free Mask switch once per turn, or to add one d8 (invented) to a Strength check or save.

11Night Parade

The performer multiplies himself, and the crowd follows. Three spectral oni hold his reach like ushers holding doors, he strikes from any of them, because a story believed by four bodies is believed four times as fast. Destroy them, and he regains his applause simply by switching masks again. Night Parade (action, 3 CE): summon three spectral oni silhouettes for 1 minute. They appear in unoccupied spaces within 30 feet of you and remain stationary (invented). Once per turn you may originate your melee reach from one silhouette. They have AC 15 and 1 HP; switching your Mask and gaining 1 Revel restores them.

18Maximum: Ō-Oni

The performance's final form wears every face at once. One mask rules the other two, its promise doubled, its terror made inescapable. This is what the Revel was always building toward: not a sorcerer wearing a mask, but the mask wearing the battle. Maximum: Ō-Oni (7 CE, 1 minute, once per long rest): activating it costs an action (invented); you become Large, gain all three base Mask benefits, +10 feet of reach, and regain 1 Revel per turn. One Mask remains Dominant: its numeric benefit is doubled, Red +2d8, Blue reduces the first physical damage by 2 × PB, Black forces the Wisdom save at disadvantage (invented).

Feats

Build-defining options.

Iron Skin

The Blue Mask's mercy: the wall steps in front of its own people. I have watched the bruiser take a companion's wound and stand under it, the mask's endurance is not selfishness but stewardship. The mountain does not move; it lets the storm break on itself. While wearing the Blue Mask, when an ally within 10 feet takes physical damage you may use your reaction to interpose yourself: you take the damage instead, reduced by your Blue Mask's damage reduction.

Red Horn

The Red Mask's charge, given a crown. Mark this: fifteen feet of straight-line intent before the hit, the technique rewards the sorcerer who commits without flinching. Force announced, force delivered; the prone enemy is the period at the end of the sentence. While wearing the Red Mask, if you move at least 15 feet straight toward a creature before hitting it with a melee attack, the target must succeed on a Strength save against your CE DC or be knocked prone.

Dread Laugh

The Black Mask laughs, and the battlefield leans away. This is terror as distance: one creature, thirty feet, hears the laugh and forgets how to approach. No damage dealt, no blood drawn, the fight is won in the half-turn of a heel. While wearing the Black Mask, you may use your bonus action to laugh: one creature within 30 feet that can hear you must succeed on a Wisdom save against your CE DC or be unable to move closer to you until the end of your next turn.

Parade Master (11+)

The dancer enters his own chorus line. By merging with one of his spectral oni, he wears two masks at once, two negotiations with his own myth, settled simultaneously. The price is the silhouette itself; the technique understands that a second face is worth a sacrificed body. As a bonus action, you may merge with one of your spectral oni silhouettes within 30 feet: until the start of your next turn you gain the benefits of a second Mask of your choice in addition to the one you wear; the silhouette is consumed.

Rules integration

Counterplay

The visible Mask telegraphs the active strength. Force switches and attack the weakness of the current stance.
