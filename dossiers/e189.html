Character engine

Switch visible Oni Masks: Red for force, Blue for endurance, Black for terror. Revel earned through takedowns and successful saves lets you switch more aggressively and power physical dominance.

Technique save DC: 8 + PB + chosen CE ability modifier. Technique attack: PB + chosen CE ability modifier.

Counterplay

The visible Mask telegraphs the active strength. Force switches and attack the weakness of the current stance.

Adaptation note. This is original JJK 5e homebrew inspired by Oni and the named tradition. The folklore/myth supplies the motif; the resource engine, CE economy, progression, feats, Maximum and Domain procedures are game mechanics created for this codex.
