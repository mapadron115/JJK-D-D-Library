Hundred-Demon Revel

Oni inspiration. Switch visible Oni Masks: Red for force, Blue for endurance, Black for terror. Revel earned through takedowns and successful saves lets you switch more aggressively and power physical dominance.

Combat roleStance-switch bruiser
Power tierTier IV · Special Grade
Core law

Character engine

Switch visible Oni Masks: Red for force, Blue for endurance, Black for terror. Revel earned through takedowns and successful saves lets you switch more aggressively and power physical dominance.

Technique save DC: 8 + PB + chosen CE ability modifier. Technique attack: PB + chosen CE ability modifier.

Full progression

Level progression

1Three Oni Masks

Three Oni Masks (bonus action, 1 CE, 1 minute): assume one mask. Red: your first melee hit after closing distance deals +1 die. Blue: reduce the first physical damage you take each round by your PB. Black: on a hit, you can prevent the target from moving closer on a failed Wisdom save.

3Kanabō Principle

Any melee weapon you wield becomes a magical iron club with a minimum versatile d10 damage die. Your critical hits push the target 10 feet or knock it prone.

6Demon's Feast

When you score a takedown or succeed on a save against a technique, you gain 1 Revel (maximum PB). You may spend Revel for a free Mask switch once per turn, or to add one die to a Strength check or save.

11Night Parade

Night Parade (action, 3 CE): summon three spectral oni silhouettes for 1 minute. Once per turn you may originate your melee reach from one silhouette. They have AC 15 and 1 HP; switching your Mask and gaining 1 Revel restores them.

18Maximum: Ō-Oni

Maximum: Ō-Oni (7 CE, 1 minute, once per long rest): you become Large, gain all three base Mask benefits, +10 feet of reach, and regain 1 Revel per turn. One Mask remains Dominant for upgrades.

Feats

Build-defining options.

Iron Skin

While wearing the Blue Mask, when an ally within 10 feet takes physical damage you may use your reaction to interpose yourself: you take the damage instead, reduced by your Blue Mask's damage reduction.

Red Horn

While wearing the Red Mask, if you move at least 15 feet straight toward a creature before hitting it with a melee attack, the target must succeed on a Strength save against your CE DC or be knocked prone.

Dread Laugh

While wearing the Black Mask, you may use your bonus action to laugh: one creature within 30 feet that can hear you must succeed on a Wisdom save against your CE DC or be unable to move closer to you until the end of your next turn.

Parade Master (11+)

As a bonus action, you may merge with one of your spectral oni silhouettes within 30 feet: until the start of your next turn you gain the benefits of a second Mask of your choice in addition to the one you wear; the silhouette is consumed.

Rules integration

Counterplay

The visible Mask telegraphs the active strength. Force switches and attack the weakness of the current stance.
