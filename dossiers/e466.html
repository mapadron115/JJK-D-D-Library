Properties & techniques

Exact player-facing procedures

Five-foot seam

Action · No CE · Attach to a nonmagical object or structure: create a 5-ft hole; lasts until the zipper is detached; one hole at a time.

The world is fabric to this tool, as an action, attach it to a nonmagical object or structure and unzip a five-foot hole that lasts until the zipper is detached. One seam at a time; choose your doorway. As an action, you can attach this zipper aglet to a nonmagical object or structure and zip or unzip it to create a 5 ft hole. The hole lasts until the zipper is detached. Only one hole per zipper at a time.

Field surgery

No action stated · No CE · An attuned bearer can temporarily reattach a severed limb; it functions until the zipper is removed or 24 hours pass, then severs again.

And it sews flesh as readily as walls: an attuned bearer can zip a severed limb back on, and it functions until the zipper is removed or twenty-four hours pass, then it severs again. A day's grace, bought with a tooth of brass. A creature attuned to this item can temporarily reattach a severed limb with the zipper. The reattached limb functions until the zipper is removed or 24 hours pass, whichever comes first, then it severs again.

Field notes. Subject: a zipper aglet that treats the world as cloth. Two truths. First: it opens doors where none were sewn, attached as an action to any nonmagical object or structure, it zips a five-foot hole that lasts until the zipper is detached, one seam at a time. Walls become curtains. Second: it is a field surgeon in miniature, an attuned bearer can zip a severed limb back onto its owner, and the limb works until the zipper comes off or twenty-four hours pass, at which point it severs again. Mark both limits honestly: no seam in magical barriers, and no permanent mending, this is borrowed time with teeth. Assess it as the tailor's philosophy made cursed: nothing is broken that cannot be re-zipped, at least for a day.

Rules integration

Edge cases and shared systems.

Campaign canon notes

Cursed-tool attunement: you have PB attunement slots (2–6). This tool occupies one slot and requires a one-hour ritual. Attunement ends voluntarily over a short rest, on death, or when you replace it while over capacity. Distance does not break attunement. Cursed-tool attunement is separate from magic-item attunement.
Note: the seam works on nonmagical targets only, barriers of cursed energy, wards, and enchanted objects resist the zipper.
