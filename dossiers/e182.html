Feathers of Remembered Wisdom

Combat roleHigh-End Support / Rescue
TierTier IV · Special Grade
Core law

Character fantasy

Record exceptional moments as Memory Feathers, then spend those memories later to help allies repeat the shape of past success without copying entire class features.

Combat loop

  1. Watch allies accomplish something exceptional.
  2. Record the category of that success in a Feather.
  3. Spend Feathers later for the same kind of challenge.
  4. Use cleansing and rescue tools to turn remembered success into party-wide resilience.

The fiction. Jujutsu scholars describe this as grief with a filing system: the user's cursed energy learned the shape of every exceptional moment it ever witnessed — the strike, the save, the rescue — and molted into feathers so none of them would be lost. The prevailing theory holds the technique is memory refusing to stay passive; each Feather is a moment that mattered too much to forget, lent back to whoever needs to repeat it. The Simurgh remembers all things, and so, in miniature, does the sorcerer.

Core law

Memory Feathers

Feather cap:

PB. Once per round when a willing creature within 60 feet rolls a natural 20, succeeds on a save by 5+, succeeds on a skill check by 5+, restores HP to another creature, or moves 30+ feet to rescue/reach an ally, use reaction +1 CE to create one Feather of the matching type: Strike, Resolve, Skill, Mercy, or Journey.

Spend Feather:

reaction when a willing creature within 60 feet makes a matching roll/event. Consume the Feather to add your proficiency bonus to the roll after seeing the d20 but before the outcome; Mercy instead adds twice your proficiency bonus to the healing, and Journey adds 10 feet of movement without provoking. One Feather per event.

Feathers record a category, not the original ability:

they never duplicate spells, Innate Techniques, extra actions, critical effects, or class features.

Full progression

Level progression

1Remembered Wisdom

Gain Memory Feathers. When a creature benefits from a Feather and the modified roll succeeds, that creature also gains temporary HP equal to your proficiency bonus.

3Healing Plumage

Action, 2 CE and consume any Feather: one creature within 30 feet heals 8 + your CE modifier. This is technique-limited healing, not Reverse Cursed Technique: it cannot restore HP that RCT restrictions forbid. If consuming Mercy Feather, also end frightened or poisoned.

6Wing of Rescue

Manifest radiant wings for 2 CE, Focus, 10 minutes, fly 40 feet. When you spend Journey Feather on another creature, you may fly up to 20 feet toward it without reaction. Healing Plumage becomes 12 + your CE modifier.

11Ancient Counsel

Feather cap becomes PB + CE modifier. Once per short rest, spend two Feathers of different types to let a willing creature reroll one failed attack, save, or check and add your proficiency bonus to the reroll. You may maintain one Great Feather created from a natural 20; when spent, it adds twice your proficiency bonus.

18Domain Expansion: Nest at the End of Time

Full-turn action, 20 CE, 90-foot radius, 1 minute. A vast tree-nest filled with luminous feathers surrounds the party. When the Domain opens, each ally you choose creates one Feather based on the last category it successfully performed this encounter, if any; these temporary Feathers vanish when the Domain ends. At initiative 20 choose one Feather type. Until the next initiative 20, the first time each ally performs that category it gains your proficiency bonus to the roll/healing/movement without consuming a Feather. This is a beneficial guaranteed Domain law, not hostile damage. Once per round, Healing Plumage costs 0 CE but still consumes a Feather.

Feats

Build-defining options.

Zal’s Feather

Once per long rest, when a creature within 30 feet would fall to 0 HP, consume any Feather to leave it at 1 HP instead. This cannot prevent instant death that does not reduce HP.

Scholar of Ages

Gain proficiency or expertise in History or Medicine. A Skill Feather may be spent on either of those skills regardless of how it was created.

Carry the Young

While flying, you may carry one willing creature of your size or smaller without reducing your speed.

Memory of Fire

When a willing creature within 60 feet fails a save against fire, fear, or death effects, you may use your reaction and spend 1 CE to record the moment of survival: create one Resolve Feather immediately (once per round, still subject to your cap).

Rules integration

Building the character

Support Specialist is ideal, but any party-oriented character can use it. The technique deliberately rewards observing allies rather than monopolizing spotlight.
Rules integration

Counterplay

Separate the support from allies, deny reaction use, force many small checks rather than dramatic successes, or exhaust the finite Feather store before the decisive exchange.
