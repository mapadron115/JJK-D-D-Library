Feathers of Remembered Wisdom

Record exceptional moments as Memory Feathers, then spend those memories later to help allies repeat the shape of past success without copying entire class features.

InspirationSimurgh
RoleHigh-End Support / Rescue
TierTier IV · Special Grade
Core statWIS or CHA
Scaling dieMemory Die

Character fantasy

Record exceptional moments as Memory Feathers, then spend those memories later to help allies repeat the shape of past success without copying entire class features.

Combat loop

  1. Watch allies accomplish something exceptional.
  2. Record the category of that success in a Feather.
  3. Spend Feathers later for the same kind of challenge.
  4. Use cleansing and rescue tools to turn remembered success into party-wide resilience.

Memory Feathers

  • Feather cap: PB. Once per round when a willing creature within 60 feet rolls a natural 20, succeeds on a save by 5+, succeeds on a skill check by 5+, restores at least one Memory Die of HP to another creature, or moves 30+ feet to rescue/reach an ally, use reaction +1 CE to create one Feather of the matching type: Strike, Resolve, Skill, Mercy, or Journey.
  • Spend Feather: reaction when a willing creature within 60 feet makes a matching roll/event. Consume a Feather to add one Memory Die after seeing the d20 but before outcome; Mercy instead adds the die to healing, Journey adds 10 feet movement without provoking. One Feather per event.
  • Feathers record a category, not the original ability: they never duplicate spells, Innate Techniques, extra actions, critical effects, or class features.

Innate Technique FeatsOptional character-building feats

Zal’s Feather

Once per long rest, when a creature within 30 feet would fall to 0 HP, consume any Feather to leave it at 1 HP instead. This cannot prevent instant death that does not reduce HP.

Scholar of Ages

Gain proficiency or expertise in History or Medicine. A Skill Feather may be spent on either of those skills regardless of how it was created.

Carry the Young

While flying, you may carry one willing creature of your size or smaller without reducing your speed.

Memory of Fire

When you spend Resolve Feather on a save against fire, fear, or death effects, the beneficiary gains resistance to the triggering damage until end of that event.

Building the character

Support Specialist is ideal, but any party-oriented character can use it. The technique deliberately rewards observing allies rather than monopolizing spotlight.

Technique die: Memory Die follows the shared d6 → d8 → d10 → d12 progression.

Counterplay

Separate the support from allies, deny reaction use, force many small checks rather than dramatic successes, or exhaust the finite Feather store before the decisive exchange.

Adaptation note. This is original JJK 5e homebrew inspired by Simurgh. The myth/folklore provides imagery and thematic rules; the combat engine, progression, CE economy, feats, and Domain/Maximum procedures are game design for this codex.
