Field notes. Subject: the fixed price. A copper amulet of tiny interlocking gears, ticking softly on unknown cursed energy — and once a day, it lets the wearer trade the gamble of the d20 for a guaranteed ten. Two truths govern it. First, the bargain is declared before the dice are cast: forgo the roll, take the ten, add modifiers as normal — certainty purchased in advance, never after the fact. Second, the gears keep one strict ledger: a single use, and no second bargain until the next dawn. Mark this: the gambler prays for twenty; the general settles for ten. There is a whole philosophy in choosing the number you can count on.

Features & procedures

Properties & techniques

Exact player-facing procedures

1/day

Fortune Gears

When you attack wearing the amulet, declare before the roll: no d20, just ten, with modifiers as normal. The gears tick once per dawn — spend the certainty where the gamble would hurt most. When you make an attack roll while wearing the amulet, you can forgo rolling the d20 to get a 10 on the die instead. Declare this before you roll; modifiers apply to the 10 as normal. Once used, this property can't be used again until the next dawn.
