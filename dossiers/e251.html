Disaster Storms

A storm-sorcerer package: cutting winds from your hands, lightning that arcs to a second target, a localized storm that follows you into battle, and the Divine Storm Sovereign crowning it — capped by a Domain where every technique strikes everyone inside.

Power tierTier III · Apex
Combat roleBlaster / controller
Core engineStorms of Disaster
Domain · pinnacleHeavenly Throne (L18)

Field notes. Subject: the sovereign of the sky. Three truths govern him. First, jurisdiction: the storm is not weather but law — wherever his radius reaches, the ground becomes difficult, the unready fall prone, arrows fly blind, and his own bolts strike harder. Second, escalation: he begins with the cutting breath of his hands — a fifteen-foot cone — and a single executioner’s stroke that arcs to a second victim, and each feature widens the radius of his rule: ten feet of personal storm, a hundred-foot sovereign sphere, a sixty-foot throne. Third, the Throne’s decree: inside his Domain, every technique strikes every creature — the first rule of tyranny is that no subject is excepted. Maxim: a storm asks no one’s permission. It only asks where its throne stands.

Full progression

Feature map

1Storms of Disaster.

The basics: a bonus-action exhalation, a fifteen-foot cone of cutting wind — slashing or thunder, the sorcerer’s choice. Note it early: a storm has two edges. Starting at 1st level, you have learned the basics of storm generation and can launch a wave of cutting winds from your hands as well as the ability to cast Gust. As a bonus action for 1 Cursed Energy, you unleash a 15-foot cone which forces targets in range to make a Dexterity save. On a failure, they take 2d8 slashing or thunder (your choice) damage or half as much damage on a success. Your damage dice increases to 3d8 at 5th level, 4d8 at 11th level, and 5d8 at 17th level.

3Lightning.

Then the executioner’s stroke: two CE, an action, a ranged bolt — and on a hit, a second victim within ten feet takes half. The lightning does not finish its business on one man. At 3rd level, as an action for 2 cursed energy you can make a ranged cursed energy attack roll against a creature you can see within 60 ft. On a hit, you deal 3d6 lightning damage. If another creature is within 10 ft of the target, you can make another attack against them, dealing half as much damage on a hit. This damage increases to 4d6 at 6th level, 6d6 at 11th level, and 8d6 at 18th level.

6Storm Creation.

The court moves with the king. Ten feet of his own weather for one minute — difficult terrain, prone checks, arrows blunted, his own bolts landing harder. The radius is purchasable: five feet per coin, up to his proficiency bonus. At 6th level, you can spend 3 cursed energy as an action to create a localized storm centered on you in a 10 ft radius for 1 minute. You can spend additional cursed energy up to your proficiency bonus to increase the radius by 5 ft per cursed energy. This area is difficult terrain and creatures that start their turn within range must make a Strength saving throw. On a failed save, they are knocked prone. Ranged attacks targeting a creature within this storm are made at disadvantage. When you deal lightning or thunder damage to a creature within this storm, you add your Cursed Energy modifier to the damage roll.

11Maximum: Divine Storm Sovereign.

The Maximum: a hundred-foot sphere within three hundred feet, heavily obscured to all but him, bleeding 3d10 at every start-turn — and for two CE an attack, he may strike from anywhere inside it, unseen. The kingdom, declared. At 11th level, you have learned to greatly amplify your storms. As an action for 10 cursed energy, you fill a 100-foot-radius sphere (invented) within 300 ft that you can see with a thick storm. The sphere is considered difficult terrain and heavily obscured to all creatures other than you. In addition to the effects of Storm Creation, all creatures other than you that start their turn within range take 3d10 lightning damage. When you take the Attack action, you can spend 2 cursed energy to replace one of your attacks with a use of Lightning, targeting a creature within the storm sphere with a ranged cursed energy attack even if you can't see them.

18Domain Expansion: Heavenly Throne of the Raging Tempest.

The pinnacle: Heavenly Throne of the Raging Tempest. Full-turn, twenty CE, sixty feet, five hundred clash points — and its law is the simplest and most terrible: every technique used inside strikes everyone inside. At initiative 20, the sky itself renders judgment — 6d12 to all creatures. The first rule of tyranny: no subject is excepted. Deploying the Domain costs a full-turn action and 20 CE (invented): 60-foot radius, up to 1 minute, 500 clash points (Tier III) (invented). Finally at 18th level, you have learned to expand your domain. At initiative 20, all creatures take 6d12 lightning damage. Any Disaster Storms technique you use within your domain targets all creatures within your domain.

Innate Technique Feats

Build-defining options.

These are optional character-building feats. You must meet each listed prerequisite before selecting it.

Prerequisite: Must have Storms of Disaster

Aerokinesis

New applications of old storms: Suffocation — the air denied for two CE and a Constitution save — and Storm Sense — feeling each body displace the air inside his weather. The same hands that make the storm learn to strangle with it. Through training and understanding, you have found new applications for your storms. Choose one; you may select this feat multiple times, choosing a different application each time. Suffocation. As an action for 2 cursed energy, you target a creature within 30 feet that you can see. It must make a Constitution saving throw, taking your proficiency bonus in d6s of necrotic damage and beginning to suffocate on a failed save. While suffocating, its movement is halved and its attack rolls have disadvantage. If you move more than 30 feet away or lose concentration, the suffocation ends. Storm Sense. While a storm of your creation is active, you can sense the location of any creature within it, feeling the air each one displaces.

Prerequisite: Must have Storm Creation

Flying Nimbus

The storm carries its maker: flight at walking speed, hovering, while the Creation endures — and a ten-foot drift on each Storms of Disaster exhalation. The sovereign does not walk where he can ride. While using Storm Creation, you have a flying speed equal to your walking speed and can hover. When you use Storms of Disaster while flying, you can immediately move up to 10 ft without spending movement or provoking opportunity attacks.

Prerequisite: Must have Maximum: Divine Storm Sovereign

Squall

The final intimacy: within his own storm, he steps anywhere as a bonus action, and his techniques may originate from any point inside. The storm is no longer weather — it is an extension of his will. While you are within a storm of your creation, you may teleport to a different point within the storm as a bonus action, and you may originate your Storms of Disaster techniques from any point within the storm.

Rules integration

Edge cases and shared systems.

Lightning arc: one additional target roll, not recursive chain. Proposed: require primary hit before jump, matching "on a hit" sequence.
Storm Creation: storm center follows user as proposal. The caster is included in the difficult terrain, start-turn Strength prone check, and ranged-attack disadvantage unless exempted elsewhere. When you deal lightning or thunder damage to a creature within this storm, you add your Cursed Energy modifier to the damage roll.
Divine Storm Sovereign: 100-ft RADIUS sphere within 300 as explicit geometry choice; 1 minute proposed. Heavily obscured except the user; 3d10 start-turn lightning to all creatures other than you; Storm Creation effects apply. Attack-action Lightning replacement costs 2 cursed energy per attack, no unprinted once/turn cap; you can select targets in the storm without seeing them but need accurate location. It does not grant auto-hit.
Domain: the all-target rule is strong and retained for Lightning, but free chain arcs cannot double-hit each recipient unless a separate normal feature legally does. Proposed: caster exemption from the 6d12 (NEW choice, needs review).
Aerokinesis: action, 2 cursed energy, 30 ft, Constitution save or your proficiency bonus in d6s necrotic and Focus-bound breath denial. Proposed: 1 minute maximum; ends when out of range or focus breaks; normal breath-holding rules rather than instant death.
Flying Nimbus: fly at walking speed and hover while Storm Creation is active. Storms of Disaster while flying allows one 10-ft free move with no opportunity attacks per activation.
Squall: +20 speed and bonus-action teleport within your own storm; once per long rest, survive 0 hit points at 1 hit point elsewhere in that storm. Squall does not negate instant death without 0 hit points or soul destruction.
Imagery note: no canon Disaster Storms user exists; the dossier image is thematic lightning imagery (Hajime Kashimo).
