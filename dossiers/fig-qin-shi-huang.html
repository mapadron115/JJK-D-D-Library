Full progression

Feature map

Level-by-level features, as the figure refined them.

L1

Crux Star Perception

Bonus action · 1 CE · 1 minute.

Your gaze extends 60 feet: you perceive the Crux Stars — where power converges in creatures, objects, and effects. The Gaze reveals, never the hidden rules. Price — Mirror-Touch Synesthesia: whenever a creature you can see takes 10 or more damage, you take 1 Imperial Die of psychic damage. Your Imperial Die is a d6 (d8 at L6, d12 at L18).

L1

Flow Break

Applied through the Fivefold Chi You forms.

Your signature debility. A creature under Flow Break suffers: −1 Imperial Die to attack rolls; −1 die + your modifier to one damage roll; −1 to save DCs. You maintain Flow Break on 1 target (2 at L6, PB at L18).

L1

Tribute

Passive resource.

Your Tribute cap is PB (PB + CE modifier at L18). Gain 1 Tribute per round (it persists 1 minute) when you: meaningfully reduce damage with Heavenly Phoenix, cause a failed attack through Flow Break, or redirect force with a Chi You form.

L1

Drifting Tortoise

Replace one attack · 1 CE.

A ranged Crux strike that applies Flow Break and deals no damage. The tortoise drifts; the blow lands where the power converges.

L1

Heavenly Phoenix

Reaction · 2 CE.

Reduce incoming damage by 2 Imperial Dice + your modifier; consume the attacker's Flow Break to roll 3 dice instead. If you reduce 10 or more, gain 1 Tribute. A melee attacker is pushed 5 feet.

L3

Mount Tai Dragon Claw

Replace one attack · 2 CE.

2 Imperial Dice + modifier piercing damage. Spend Tribute to slow the target.

L3

White Tiger Crescent Moon

Action (or replace one attack) · 2 CE.

2 Imperial Dice + modifier bludgeoning damage; push the target 15 feet or knock it prone.

L6

Sovereign Sight

Passive.

Your Imperial Die becomes d8. You perceive Crux Stars in cursed tools, barriers, and shikigami — not just creatures. You may maintain Flow Break on 2 targets.

L6

Celestial Reversal

Reaction · 1 Tribute (trigger: reduce damage to 0 with Heavenly Phoenix).

When Heavenly Phoenix reduces damage to 0, spend 1 Tribute to make a free unarmed strike in the same reaction: +1 Imperial Die force damage, against an attacker within 15 feet.

L6

Imperial Disruption

Passive.

Your Flow Break may target a specific Technique Star: −1 die to its attack rolls, −2 dice to its damage, −2 to its save DC.

L11

Goujian

Bonus action · 3 CE · 1 minute.

You forge a CE sword (1d8). Imperial Power Embrace: force you reduce is stored in the blade; your next Swallow Slash gains the stored dice plus Tribute-generated dice.

L11

Swallow Slash

2 CE (sword attack).

Weapon damage + 2 Imperial Dice; spend up to 3 Tribute for +1 die each.

L11

No King Stands Above Me

Reaction · 2 CE · once per round (trigger: you succeed on a save vs. a hostile technique while your Gaze is active).

Apply Flow Break to the technique's creator.

L18

The Mandate Perfected

Passive.

Your Imperial Die becomes d12. Your Tribute cap becomes PB + CE modifier. You may maintain Flow Break on PB targets. Consuming Flow Break grants 1 Tribute, ignoring the 1/round limit.

Pinnacle expression

Maximum, Domain, or equivalent.

Capstone material.

Maximum Technique

King of All Beginnings

Action · 10 CE · requires maximum Tribute.

Until the end of your next turn, every hostile creature and effect you can see has Flow Break. Consume any number of Flow Breaks once each; your first Heavenly Phoenix is free; your next Swallow Slash gains +1 die per Flow Break consumed (cap PB).

Domain Expansion

All Under Heaven — Throne of the First Emperor

Full-turn action · 20 CE · 1,000 clash points · burnout 3 rounds · duration 3 minutes · 60-ft enclosed.

At initiative 20, the sure-hit Imperial Meridian automatically Flow-Breaks one hostile. Tribute of Heaven uncaps your Tribute gain. Five Forms One Emperor: chain 3 different Chi You forms, paying their CE costs without spending actions or reactions.
