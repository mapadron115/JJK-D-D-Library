Core law

What the technique actually does.

Core law, the threaded needle. When you take the Attack action, you may replace one attack with Needlework: 1 CE — make a melee or ranged (60 ft) CE attack; on a hit, deal 1d8 piercing damage and the target becomes Threaded for 1 minute. Threaded is a condition, not a pool — a filament of your cursed energy runs from your needle to the target, and a creature either wears it or does not. The thread breaks if the creature moves more than 120 feet from you, uses its action to succeed on a Strength check against your CE save DC, or you Unravel it. Thread verbs (L1): Pull (bonus action · 1 CE) — a Threaded creature within 60 feet makes a Strength save or is yanked 15 feet in a straight line of your choice. Mend (action · 2 CE) — a willing Threaded creature regains 1d8 + WIS HP as you stitch their wounds shut.

Field notes. Three truths from the workroom. First: every garment is a promise — a seam holds or it doesn't, and the difference was never the thread, it was the patience of the hand that laid it; the woman who taught me this had sewn twelve hours a day for thirty years, and her hands shook only when she stopped. Second: the needle does not distinguish between cloth and flesh — that distinction was always yours, and the technique simply declines to honor it; the first time you stitch a wound shut with the same motion you used on a hem, you will understand that mending was always the violent half of sewing. Third: willing or forced, the years at the needle are the technique — nobody manifests this who has not sat with the work until it sat with them. Mark the economy: the thread is cheap. What it connects is not.

Full progression

Level 1 through 20.

1Needlework

When you take the Attack action, you may replace one attack with Needlework: 1 CE · melee or ranged (60 ft) CE attack, 1d8 piercing on a hit; the target becomes Threaded for 1 minute. Bonus action · 1 CE · Pull: a Threaded creature within 60 feet makes a Strength save or is yanked 15 feet in a line of your choice. Action · 2 CE · Mend: a willing Threaded creature regains 1d8 + WIS HP.

The bread and butter: thread them, then decide what the thread is for. A yank into the barbarian's reach, a stitch across a bleeding wound — the needle doesn't care which. It only ever asks what we are making today, and it asks it every round, folded into the attacks you were making anyway.

3Hemming

Action · 2 CE · Sew a creature's shadow to the floor: one creature within 60 feet makes a Wisdom save or has its speed reduced to 0 for 1 minute (it may repeat the save as an action on its turn). You never touched them — only their shadow.

The new verb: L1 threads bodies, L3 threads shadows. Control without contact — the enemy's own silhouette becomes the shackle, and there is no armor against the floor. Ask what the technique was blind to: it could reach the flesh but not the stillness. Now it can.

6The Workroom

Passive · Needlework now deals 2d8 piercing, and your thread range extends to 120 feet. Action · 2 CE · Stitch: a Threaded creature makes a Strength save or is restrained until the end of your next turn (it may repeat the save as an action). Reversal — Unravel: bonus action · 3 CE · Rip the thread through a Threaded creature: 3d8 slashing damage, and the thread is consumed.

The system deepens and the foundation sharpens with it: harder needles, longer thread, and the vocabulary completes itself. Note the reversal honestly — Mend and Unravel are one technique read forward and backward, the stitch and its undoing, and the dossier refuses to pretend they were ever separate. What mends can also tear; the workroom has always known this.

11The Full Wardrobe

Action · 8 CE · Once per short rest · Every Threaded creature within 60 feet: choose one per target — Pull 30 feet (no save), Stitch (restrained until the end of your next turn, no initial save), Mend (regain 4d8 + WIS HP), or Unravel (4d8 slashing, thread consumed).

High power, not a finisher: the limiter is the setup, not the CE — every target must already be wearing your thread, and the wardrobe opens once per short rest. It doesn't end the fight. It re-tailors it, all at once, on your terms.

18Master Tailor

Passive · Needlework costs 0 CE. Threaded no longer expires on its own — threads persist until you release them, the creature dies, or you thread more creatures than your PB (the oldest threads slip free first).

Enablement, not a nuke: the technique stops charging you for its own existence. Everyone you have threaded is still wearing it, and the workroom has no closing hour. You are very difficult to handle now — not because the needle hits harder, but because the whole battlefield was already your garment before the first blow landed.

Pinnacle expression

Maximum, Domain, or equivalent.

The barrier does not rise — it is draped. The world becomes an atelier: measuring tapes for horizons, pin cushions for hills, and every creature inside dressed in a half-finished garment of red thread that was never there a moment ago. You stand at the center with the shears. Everyone else is here for a fitting they did not book.

Domain Expansion, The Final Fitting (action, 20 CE, 1 minute, 90-foot radius): every creature inside becomes Threaded at the start of its turn — no attack needed; the garments dress themselves (this is the sure-hit). Your thread verbs cost 1 less CE (minimum 1 CE). Once per round as a bonus action, Take In or Let Out: Take In — one creature's speed is halved and it has disadvantage on Dexterity saves until the start of your next turn (the garment tightens); Let Out — one willing creature gains 15 feet of speed and advantage on Dexterity saves until the start of your next turn (the garment loosens).

Feats

Build-defining options.

Quilted Armor

Bonus action · 2 CE · Stitch cursed energy into a garment: you or a willing creature within 30 feet gains +2 AC for 1 minute, the ward quilted invisibly into the cloth. Tailoring, but as armor.

Reattached

Action · 10 CE · Stitch a severed limb or torn organ back into a living creature: the part is reattached and functional, though it bears your stitching forever. For 30 CE, you may instead reattach the killing wound of a creature reduced to 0 HP by dismemberment within 1 minute — they stabilize and return to 1 HP. You can also do it cruelly — stitch someone else’s severed part onto a living host; it works, and it horrifies. Nothing about this is casual. Tailoring, but as surgery.

Marionette

Bonus action · 3 CE · A Threaded creature makes a Wisdom save or must use its reaction to move up to its speed in a direction you choose. The strings were always there — you merely decided to pull them all at once. Tailoring, but as puppetry.

Unpick

Reaction · 3 CE · When a Threaded creature within 60 feet activates a technique or spell, pull the thread through the working: they make a Wisdom save or the effect unravels, countered as though by Counterspell. Every working has seams. You know where they are. Stitching, but as countermagic.

Rules integration

Edge cases and shared systems.

Codex rulings: Direct rolled technique attacks (Needlework) can use the campaign's Black Flash procedure when otherwise eligible. Save-effect damage (Unravel, The Full Wardrobe's Unravel option) cannot critically hit or Black Flash.
Generic Maximum Output applies only to ordinary features, never to The Final Fitting.
Threaded ends early if the creature moves more than 120 feet from you, uses its action to succeed on a Strength check against your CE save DC, or you Unravel it. Forced movement from Pull does not provoke opportunity attacks.
Mend's healing is technique healing and functions against cursed wounds normally. Unpick follows Counterspell timing — it must be used as a reaction to the technique's activation, and it cannot unpick a Domain Expansion.
