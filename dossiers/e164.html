Braxton Veil

Flood the battlefield with a nauseating, electrically wrong atmosphere whose modes reshape sight, weight, cursed sensing, and stamina.

Character fantasy

Flood the battlefield with a nauseating, electrically wrong atmosphere whose modes reshape sight, weight, cursed sensing, and stamina.

Combat loop

  1. Create your Veil and choose its environmental mode.
  2. Keep enemies inside long enough to build Exposure.
  3. Switch modes to attack the weakness their current Exposure creates.
  4. Float through your own atmosphere while enemies struggle to perceive or approach you.

Veil Modes & Exposure

  • Veil: bonus action, 2 CE, Focus, 1 minute. A 15-foot aura centered on you moves with you. At level 6 it becomes 20 feet; level 11, 30 feet.
  • Exposure: the first time each turn a hostile creature enters the Veil or starts its turn there, it makes a Constitution save. Failure adds 1 Exposure, maximum 4. Leaving the Veil for a full round removes 1 Exposure; a short rest removes all.
  • Exposure thresholds: 1 = speed −10 feet inside the Veil; 2 = subtract 1d4 from Perception and Technique Focus/Concentration checks; 3 = cannot take reactions while inside; 4 = poisoned while inside and cannot benefit from advantage on attacks against you.
  • Mode: choose Sulfur, Crimson, Gravitic, or Static when activated and switch as a bonus action for 1 CE.

Innate Technique FeatsOptional character-building feats

Lantern Eyes

Gain darkvision 120 feet. While your Veil is active you ignore its obscurity and may see through mundane fog, smoke, and gas inside it.

Sickening Trail

Spaces you leave during your turn remain Veiled until the start of your next turn. A creature can gain Exposure from only one of your Veil areas per turn.

Static Autopsy

After observing a creature with Exposure 3+ for one round, learn whether its highest saving throw is Strength, Dexterity, Constitution, or mental (Int/Wis/Cha).

Red Star Descent

Prerequisite: Floating Apparition. When you descend at least 20 feet before using Atmospheric Pulse, add one Veil Die to its damage and push failed targets 10 feet away from the center.

Building the character

Excellent for Strategists and Support Specialists who want a moving hazard rather than spell-like burst damage. Constitution is useful because you want to remain near enemies, but your CE stat drives the important saves.

Technique die: Veil Die follows the shared d6 → d8 → d10 → d12 progression.

Counterplay

Leave the aura, force the user away from the fight, or use long-range attacks. Exposure is dangerous only if the user can keep pressure on the same targets.
