Core law

Character fantasy

Flood the battlefield with a nauseating, electrically wrong atmosphere whose modes reshape sight, weight, cursed sensing, and stamina.

Combat loop

  1. Create your Veil and choose its environmental mode.
  2. Keep enemies inside long enough to build Exposure.
  3. Switch modes to attack the weakness their current Exposure creates.
  4. Float through your own atmosphere while enemies struggle to perceive or approach you.

The fiction. The prevailing theory holds that the Braxton Veil belongs to the witness, not the cryptid: the user's cursed energy imprinted on the moment of being seen by something unknowable, and now reproduces the atmosphere of that encounter — the nausea, the wrongness in the air — as a weapon. The Flatwoods Monster never had a technique; the people who survived it gave it one, and this sorcerer's CE kept the draft. Scholars note the cruelty is precise: the Veil does not attack the body first, it attacks the certainty that the world is behaving.

Full progression

Level progression

L1Unnatural Atmosphere

Veil

Bonus action, 2 CE, Focus, 1 minute. A 15-foot aura centered on you moves with you.

Exposure

The first time each turn a hostile creature enters the Veil or starts its turn there, it makes a Constitution save. Failure adds 1 Exposure, maximum 4. Leaving the Veil for a full round removes 1 Exposure; a short rest removes all.

Exposure thresholds

1 = speed −10 feet inside the Veil; 2 = subtract 1d4 from Perception and Technique Focus/Concentration checks; 3 = cannot take reactions while inside; 4 = poisoned while inside and cannot benefit from advantage on attacks against you.

Mode

Choose Sulfur, Crimson, Gravitic, or Static when activated and switch as a bonus action for 1 CE. Sulfur: creatures with Exposure 2+ regain only half as many HP while inside (this includes healing from Reverse Cursed Technique). Crimson: the Veil is lightly obscured; creatures with Exposure 2+ treat you as heavily obscured beyond 10 feet. Gravitic: hostile movement inside costs +5 feet per 5 feet when moving toward you. Static: creatures with Exposure 2+ cannot sense cursed energy beyond 15 feet and subtract 1d4 from reaction attacks.

L3Atmospheric Pulse

Action, 3 CE. Each creature of your choice in the Veil makes a Constitution save vs your Technique DC. Failure: it gains 1 Exposure and is poisoned until the end of its next turn; success: no Exposure, unaffected.

L6Floating Apparition

The Veil’s radius increases to 20 feet. Gain a 30-foot hover speed while the Veil is active and may maintain two Veil Modes at once. Forced movement against you is reduced by 10 feet while hovering.

L11Wrong Physics

The Veil’s radius increases to 30 feet. Exposure cap becomes 5. At Exposure 5, a creature is disoriented: at the start of its turn it chooses movement or reactions; it cannot use both before that turn ends. Your Veil may originate from a point within 30 feet and remain stationary instead of following you. Atmospheric Pulse gains a rider: a creature that fails its Pulse save while at Exposure 4+ gains 2 Exposure instead of 1 (to the cap of 5).

L18The Hill Beneath the Red Star

Domain Expansion, full-turn action, 20 CE, 90-foot radius, 1 minute. The sky becomes black-red and the entire barrier counts as your Veil with all four Modes active. At initiative 20 each chosen hostile automatically gains 1 Exposure and is poisoned until the next initiative 20. A creature protected from the sure-hit makes a Constitution save: success — no Exposure, not poisoned. Exposure cannot exceed 5 and still falls away normally after the Domain ends.

Feats

Build-defining options.

Eddy Reading

While your Veil is active, you read the atmosphere eddying around bodies: you know the direction and distance of every creature inside the Veil that has at least 1 Exposure, even if you cannot see them.

Sickening Trail

Spaces you leave during your turn remain Veiled until the start of your next turn. A creature can gain Exposure from only one of your Veil areas per turn.

Static Autopsy

After observing a creature with Exposure 3+ for one round, learn whether its highest saving throw is Strength, Dexterity, Constitution, or mental (Int/Wis/Cha).

Red Star Descent

Prerequisite: Floating Apparition. When you descend at least 20 feet immediately before using Atmospheric Pulse, you may drive the Veil down with you: the Pulse affects only creatures within 10 feet of you, but creatures that fail the save gain 2 Exposure instead of 1.

Rules integration

Building the character

Excellent for Strategists and Support Specialists who want a moving hazard rather than spell-like burst damage. Constitution is useful because you want to remain near enemies, but your CE stat drives the important saves.
Rules integration

Counterplay

Leave the aura, force the user away from the fight, or use long-range attacks. Exposure is dangerous only if the user can keep pressure on the same targets.
