Braxton Veil

Combat roleArea Controller / Debuffer
TierTier III · Apex
Core law

Character fantasy

Flood the battlefield with a nauseating, electrically wrong atmosphere whose modes reshape sight, weight, cursed sensing, and stamina.

Combat loop

  1. Create your Veil and choose its environmental mode.
  2. Keep enemies inside long enough to build Exposure.
  3. Switch modes to attack the weakness their current Exposure creates.
  4. Float through your own atmosphere while enemies struggle to perceive or approach you.

Core law

Veil Modes & Exposure

Veil:

bonus action, 2 CE, Focus, 1 minute. A 15-foot aura centered on you moves with you. At level 6 it becomes 20 feet; level 11, 30 feet.

Exposure:

the first time each turn a hostile creature enters the Veil or starts its turn there, it makes a Constitution save. Failure adds 1 Exposure, maximum 4. Leaving the Veil for a full round removes 1 Exposure; a short rest removes all.

Exposure thresholds:

1 = speed −10 feet inside the Veil; 2 = subtract 1d4 from Perception and Technique Focus/Concentration checks; 3 = cannot take reactions while inside; 4 = poisoned while inside and cannot benefit from advantage on attacks against you.

Mode:

choose Sulfur, Crimson, Gravitic, or Static when activated and switch as a bonus action for 1 CE.

Full progression

Level progression

1Unnatural Atmosphere

Gain Veil and Exposure. Sulfur: creatures with Exposure 2+ regain only half as many HP while inside (this includes healing from Reverse Cursed Technique). Crimson: the Veil is lightly obscured; creatures with Exposure 2+ treat you as heavily obscured beyond 10 feet. Gravitic: hostile movement inside costs +5 feet per 5 feet when moving toward you. Static: creatures with Exposure 2+ cannot sense cursed energy beyond 15 feet and subtract 1d4 from reaction attacks.

3Atmospheric Pulse

Action, 3 CE. Creatures of your choice in the Veil make Constitution saves. Failure gains 1 Exposure and takes 2 Veil Dice poison, psychic, or lightning damage chosen on activation; success takes half and gains no Exposure. A creature already at Exposure 4 instead takes one extra Veil Die.

6Floating Apparition

Gain a 30-foot hover speed while the Veil is active and may maintain two Veil Modes at once. Atmospheric Pulse becomes 3 Veil Dice. Forced movement against you is reduced by 10 feet while hovering.

11Wrong Physics

Exposure cap becomes 5. At Exposure 5, a creature is disoriented: at the start of its turn it chooses movement or reactions; it cannot use both before that turn ends. Your Veil may originate from a point within 30 feet and remain stationary instead of following you. Atmospheric Pulse becomes 4 Veil Dice.

18The Hill Beneath the Red Star

Domain Expansion, full-turn action, 20 CE, 90-foot radius, 1 minute. The sky becomes black-red and the entire barrier counts as your Veil with all four Modes active. At initiative 20 each chosen hostile automatically gains 1 Exposure and takes 3 Veil Dice psychic damage. A creature protected from the sure-hit makes a Constitution save, taking half damage and gaining no Exposure on success. Exposure cannot exceed 5 and still falls away normally after the Domain ends.

Feats

Build-defining options.

Lantern Eyes

Gain darkvision 120 feet. While your Veil is active you ignore its obscurity and may see through mundane fog, smoke, and gas inside it.

Sickening Trail

Spaces you leave during your turn remain Veiled until the start of your next turn. A creature can gain Exposure from only one of your Veil areas per turn.

Static Autopsy

After observing a creature with Exposure 3+ for one round, learn whether its highest saving throw is Strength, Dexterity, Constitution, or mental (Int/Wis/Cha).

Red Star Descent

Prerequisite: Floating Apparition. When you descend at least 20 feet before using Atmospheric Pulse, add one Veil Die to its damage and push failed targets 10 feet away from the center.

Rules integration

Building the character

Excellent for Strategists and Support Specialists who want a moving hazard rather than spell-like burst damage. Constitution is useful because you want to remain near enemies, but your CE stat drives the important saves.
Technique die: Veil Die follows the shared d6 → d8 → d10 → d12 progression.
Rules integration

Counterplay

Leave the aura, force the user away from the fight, or use long-range attacks. Exposure is dangerous only if the user can keep pressure on the same targets.
