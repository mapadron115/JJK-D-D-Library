Core law

Character fantasy

Flood the battlefield with a nauseating, electrically wrong atmosphere whose modes reshape sight, weight, cursed sensing, and stamina.

Combat loop

Veil, expose, switch, float, the witness's fourfold procedure.

  1. Create your Veil and choose its environmental mode.
  2. Keep enemies inside long enough to build Exposure.
  3. Switch modes to attack the weakness their current Exposure creates.
  4. Float through your own atmosphere while enemies struggle to perceive or approach you.

Field notes. Subject: the witness's nightmare, weaponized. I have stood inside the Veil's edge and felt the air go electrically wrong before anything touched me. Three truths govern it. First: it attacks certainty before it attacks the body, sight, weight, stamina, cursed sensing; the nausea is the vanguard. Second: Exposure is testimony, four thresholds, each failure a line entered into the record, until the poisoned and the disoriented cannot trust their own senses. Third: the atmosphere has four flavors of wrongness, Sulfur, Crimson, Gravitic, Static, and the floating witness may wear two at once while the grounded struggle to perceive her at all. Mark the cruelty precisely placed: this technique never lies to you. It simply makes the world unrecognizable while you watch.

Full progression

Level progression

1Unnatural Atmosphere

Veil

Fifteen feet of nausea, centered on you, following you. Bonus action, 2 CE, Focus, 1 minute. A 15-foot aura centered on you moves with you.

Exposure

Four is the maximum; each failure is a line of testimony. The first time each turn a hostile creature enters the Veil or starts its turn there, it makes a Constitution save. Failure adds 1 Exposure, maximum 4. Leaving the Veil for a full round removes 1 Exposure; a short rest removes all.

Exposure thresholds

Speed, sight, reactions, then poison, the testimony compounds. 1 = speed −10 feet inside the Veil; 2 = subtract 1d4 from Perception and Technique Focus/Concentration checks; 3 = cannot take reactions while inside; 4 = poisoned while inside and cannot benefit from advantage on attacks against you.

Mode

Sulfur, Crimson, Gravitic, Static, four flavors of wrongness. Choose Sulfur, Crimson, Gravitic, or Static when activated and switch as a bonus action for 1 CE. Sulfur: creatures with Exposure 2+ regain only half as many HP while inside (this includes healing from Reverse Cursed Technique). Crimson: the Veil is lightly obscured; creatures with Exposure 2+ treat you as heavily obscured beyond 10 feet. Gravitic: hostile movement inside costs +5 feet per 5 feet when moving toward you. Static: creatures with Exposure 2+ cannot sense cursed energy beyond 15 feet and subtract 1d4 from reaction attacks.

3Atmospheric Pulse

Action, 3 CE. Each creature of your choice in the Veil The Veil exhales: Exposure and poison in one pulse. makes a Constitution save vs your Technique DC. Failure: it gains 1 Exposure and is poisoned until the end of its next turn; success: no Exposure, unaffected.

18The Hill Beneath the Red Star

Domain Expansion · Full-turn action · 20 CE · 90-ft radius, 1 minute · The whole barrier becomes the Veil with all four Modes active · Sure-hit: at initiative 20, each chosen hostile automatically gains 1 Exposure and is poisoned until the next initiative 20 (a creature protected from the sure-hit makes a Constitution save: success means no Exposure, not poisoned) · Exposure cannot exceed 5.

The sky is black-red, the color of a wound that will not close, and a red star hangs too low over a dark hill. The air shimmers with the Veil made visible, four layers of it, like heat haze over summer asphalt. Everything underneath looks already poisoned.

Feats

Build-defining options.

Eddy Reading

The atmosphere eddies around bodies, and the witness reads the eddies. While your Veil is active, you read the atmosphere eddying around bodies: you know the direction and distance of every creature inside the Veil that has at least 1 Exposure, even if you cannot see them.

Sickening Trail

The Veil lingers where you walked, but each creature testifies only once per turn. Spaces you leave during your turn remain Veiled until the start of your next turn. A creature can gain Exposure from only one of your Veil areas per turn.

Static Autopsy

Watch the Exposed for one round and learn where they are strongest. After observing a creature with Exposure 3+ for one round, learn whether its highest saving throw is Strength, Dexterity, Constitution, or mental (Int/Wis/Cha).

Red Star Descent

Descend twenty feet and drive the Veil down with you. Prerequisite: Floating Apparition. When you descend at least 20 feet immediately before using Atmospheric Pulse, you may drive the Veil down with you: the Pulse affects only creatures within 10 feet of you, but creatures that fail the save gain 2 Exposure instead of 1.

Rules integration

Building the character

Excellent for Strategists and Support Specialists who want a moving hazard rather than spell-like burst damage. Constitution is useful because you want to remain near enemies, but your CE stat drives the important saves.
Rules integration

Counterplay

Leave the aura, force the user away from the fight, or use long-range attacks. Exposure is dangerous only if the user can keep pressure on the same targets.
