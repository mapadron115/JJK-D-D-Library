Completed rules

  • Critical Focus widens the natural critical range by one and adds one extra base weapon/unarmed die on a critical. If another feature widens the range, combine them to a maximum natural range of 18–20.
  • The critical bonus dice at later levels replace, rather than add to, earlier amounts: two extra dice from level 3, three from level 6, four from level 11, and five inside Golden Ratio. “Damage dice” means the attack’s base weapon or unarmed die, not every rider die.
  • Ratio: after an attack hits but before rolling damage, pay 1 CE and roll a d10. A 3 or 7 makes that hit critical. At level 11, roll two d10 by default and the hit is critical if any die is 3 or 7. Additional CE up to CE modifier adds one d10 each. Pay before rolling the Ratio dice; multiple qualifying dice do not create multiple criticals.
  • Collapse: the 30-foot circle is centered on a ground point you touch, not necessarily on your body. It becomes difficult terrain until repaired. Roll unarmed damage once plus 3d10; failed Dexterity save takes full, success half. This is a save effect and cannot become critical through Ratio.
  • Golden Ratio: every attack that hits is critical without rolling Ratio or spending its CE. This does not turn a miss into a hit and does not make a save-based feature critical. A qualifying critical may interact with Black Flash under the current Black Flash rules.

Power identity

Why Tier I: its numbers become spectacular, but the technique remains one coherent task—convert successful attacks into decisive critical hits.

Recovered character-build options

Innate Technique Feats 3

These are optional character-building feats. You must meet each listed prerequisite before selecting it.

Focused Attacks

High impact

Prerequisite: Critical Focus

Your Ratio critical threshold improves by 1 again. This stacks with Critical Focus, producing its intended expanded range, but not with unrelated effects that merely replace rather than increase critical range.

Weapon Breaker

Pinnacle

Prerequisite: Collapse

Reaction when targeted by a weapon attack, 3 CE: wielder makes Dexterity save. On failure a mundane weapon breaks before damage; a cursed tool breaks only if your striking tool is equal or higher grade, otherwise the attack is deflected and misses. Artifacts and unique Special Grades break only when their own story permits.

Fatal Ratio

Pinnacle

Prerequisite: Improved Ratio

Corrected prerequisite: Improved Ratio. After a Ratio critical, spend 2 CE to make it a limb-tagged hit without targeted-strike disadvantage. Resolve damage and the revised injury threshold/save normally; the feat does not automatically sever a limb.
