Disaster Verdure

Roots, walls, flying seed platforms, pacifying flowers, CE-draining buds, and a hostile garden.

The fiction. Registry notes describe Disaster Verdure as a cultivation that went feral: the user's cursed energy learned the grammar of roots and petals instead of steel, and in a life spent growing things that were always taken, it answered by building a garden that keeps what it catches. Nothing here is summoned — the flowers that pacify and the buds that drink CE are the same impulse, a tenderness that stopped asking permission.

Full progression

Feature map

L1Forests of Disaster

Forests of Disaster

You manipulate plants at will and know the Druidcraft cantrip. You gain the following three techniques.

Binding Plants

As an action for 1 CE, choose one target within 30 feet. On a failed Strength save it is restrained by plants for up to 1 minute; it repeats a Strength save at the end of each turn. The plants have AC 13 and HP equal to twice your level.

Piercing Roots

As an action for 1 CE, raise roots in a 50-foot-long, 5-foot-wide line. Each creature there makes a Dexterity save: 1d12 piercing damage on failure, half on success. Damage increases to 2d12 at 5th level, 3d12 at 11th, and 4d12 at 17th.

Wall of Roots

As a reaction for 1 CE when you or an ally within 30 feet is targeted by an attack, place a 10-foot-wide, 10-foot-tall, 5-foot-thick wall on the ground between attacker and target. Recalculate cover before resolving the triggering attack. It has AC 13, HP equal to your CE modifier × PB (minimum 1), and lasts until the start of your next turn or until destroyed.

L3Root Bundle

Root Bundle

As an action for 2 CE, create exactly two wooden balls (no more than two can exist). Each floats within 5 feet of you for 10 minutes, with AC 10 + CE modifier and HP equal to your CE modifier. As a bonus action, launch any number at targets within 30 feet, making one CE attack per ball; each deals 2d10 piercing damage on a hit and vanishes after its attack. You may ride one, gaining flight while it remains within 30 feet of you; launching or destroying it makes the rider fall.

L6Flower Field

Flower Field

As an action for 10 CE, create a 30-foot-radius circle of petals at a point within 100 feet. Each creature there makes a Wisdom save; on failure it is charmed, has speed 0, and cannot take an action, bonus action, or reaction intended to advance combat, for 1 minute. It repeats the Wisdom save at the end of each turn. Being targeted by a hostile attack or save ends the condition immediately after that attack or effect resolves. Uses Technique Focus.

L11Cursed Buds

Launch buds

As an action for 8 CE, launch cursed buds equal to your CE modifier toward one or more targets within 30 feet, making a CE attack for each. Each hit attaches one bud. At the start of the host’s turn, each bud deals necrotic damage equal to your CE modifier (minimum 1). As an action, the host or an adjacent creature makes Strength (Athletics) against your CE DC, removing one bud on success.

Bud tree

Alternatively, as an action for 10 CE, create a Large tree (AC 15, HP 10 × PB, 1-minute duration, uses Technique Focus). At the start of each of your turns it fires one bud at every hostile creature within 30 feet of it.

Pinnacle expression

Maximum, Domain, or equivalent.

Domain: 18 — Ceremonial Sea of Light. at initiative 20 each chosen hostile automatically takes 2d4 necrotic damage and suffers Flower Field until the next initiative 20. A protected creature makes a Wisdom save, taking half damage and avoiding Flower Field on a success. CE regained is capped at PB per round, cannot exceed maximum CE, and does not increase for damage prevented or duplicated.

Innate Technique Feats

Build-defining options.

These are optional character-building feats. You must meet each listed prerequisite before selecting it.

Prerequisite: Forests of Disaster

Chlorokinesis

As a bonus action, transform one active root wall into a different wall option without refreshing its HP or duration.

Prerequisite: Flower Field

Bouquet

Action, 3 CE: one creature within 60 feet makes the normal Flower Field save and suffers its effects for the normal duration. It does not create an area and cannot affect additional creatures through movement.

Prerequisite: Cursed Bud

Stranglehold

As a bonus action, choose one creature hosting at least one of your buds. The buds constrict: it must make a Strength save against your CE DC. On a failure, it is restrained until the end of its next turn. On a success, it tears one bud off, destroying it.

Prerequisites: Must have Cursed Bud

Sapping Roots

Your power has increased and been honed. As an action, for 5 Cursed Energy you give yourself the ability to drain Cursed Energy for 1 minute. On a hit with a melee attack or if you damage a creature with your Innate Technique, they lose 1 cursed energy which you gain.

Rules integration

Edge cases and shared systems.

Rules priority: the reviewed rulings, explicit codex clarifications, and the level progression always control when original wording conflicts. Legacy Domain auto-hit language, action terminology, event timing, missing saves, and older scaling never override revised player rules.
Why Tier II: it answers many tactical problems and can lock down a scene, but each asset is local, destructible, and governed by a clear save or action.
