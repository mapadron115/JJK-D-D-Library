Completed rules

  • Binding Plants: on failure, restrained for up to 1 minute; repeat a Strength save at the end of each turn. The plants have AC 13 and HP equal to twice your level. Piercing Roots uses a 50-foot-long, 5-foot-wide line.
  • Wall of Roots: when an attack targets you or an ally within 30 feet, place a 10-foot-wide, 10-foot-tall, 5-foot-thick wall on the ground between attacker and target. It has AC 13, printed HP (minimum 1), and lasts until the start of your next turn or until destroyed. Recalculate cover before resolving the triggering attack.
  • Root Bundle: the action creates exactly two balls; no more than two can exist. Each floats within 5 feet of you for 10 minutes. A bonus action can launch any number, making one CE attack per ball; each vanishes after its attack. Riding one grants flight while it remains within 30 feet of you; launching or destroying it makes the rider fall.
  • Flower Field: lasts 1 minute and uses Technique Focus. An affected creature is charmed, has speed 0, and cannot take an action, bonus action, or reaction intended to advance combat. It repeats the Wisdom save at the end of each turn. Being targeted by a hostile attack or save ends the condition immediately after that attack or effect resolves.
  • Cursed Bud: each hit attaches one bud. At the start of the host’s turn, each bud deals necrotic damage equal to CE modifier (minimum 1). As an action, the host or an adjacent creature makes Strength (Athletics) against CE DC, removing one bud on success. The tree has AC 15, HP equal to 10 × PB, a 1-minute duration, and uses Technique Focus; at the start of your turn it fires once at every hostile creature within 30 feet.
  • Domain: at initiative 20 each chosen hostile automatically takes 2d4 necrotic damage and suffers Flower Field until the next initiative 20. A protected creature makes a Wisdom save, taking half damage and avoiding Flower Field on a success. CE regained is capped at PB per round, cannot exceed maximum CE, and does not increase for damage prevented or duplicated.

Power identity

Why Tier II: it answers many tactical problems and can lock down a scene, but each asset is local, destructible, and governed by a clear save or action.

Recovered character-build options

Innate Technique Feats 3

These are optional character-building feats. You must meet each listed prerequisite before selecting it.

Chlorokinesis

Standard

Prerequisite: Forests of Disaster

Repeatable; choose a different option: bonus action transforms one active root wall into another wall option without refreshing HP/duration, or learn two level 1–4 nature spells, cast with CE ability for CE equal to spell level. No spell may be selected twice.

Bouquet

Standard

Prerequisite: Flower Field

Action, 3 CE: one creature within 60 feet makes the normal Flower Field save and suffers its effects for the normal duration. It does not create an area and cannot affect additional creatures through movement.

Sapping Roots

High impact

Prerequisite: Cursed Bud

Action, 5 CE, Technique Focus for 1 minute. Once per turn after a melee or innate-technique attack deals damage, regain 1d4 CE. The activation can restore at most PB CE total and never above maximum; then it ends. This closes the self-funding attack loop without removing the drain fantasy.

Disaster Verdure
You are born with an amazing power, with many possibilities, plant
manipulation, allowing you to protect those you care about.
Cursed Energy

usable source of energy, called cursed energy. These feelings can be
harnessed into attacks or abilities. The number of cursed energy you
possess is based on your class, some classes use a modifier that
being charisma or wisdom (Player’s choice)

proficiency bonus.

Root Bundle
At 3rd level, you have learned how to create small wooden balls to
launch and pierce your opponents. As an action, you can create a
number of two wooden balls, at the price of two cursed energy. The
wooden balls will have a number of hit points equal to your Cursed
Energy modifier, an AC of 10 + your CE modifier, and will keep
floating around you. As a bonus action, you can send one or more of
your balls towards a target within 30 feet of you. Make a cursed
energy attack roll, on a hit the balls create large branches that
pierce through its target, dealing 2d10 piercing damage. Right after
they hit or miss the balls will disappear. You may also spend your
bonus action to get above one of the balls, gaining flying speed
equal to your walking speed while above them.
Flower Field
At 6th level, you can create a circle of flower petals that
surrounds your opponents and removes their will to fight. As an
action for 10 Cursed Energy, you create a 30-foot radius circle in a
point within 100 feet of yourself, all creatures within that circle
must make a Wisdom saving throw. On a failure, their will to fight is
completely drained as their mind enters a state of tranquility and
happiness, they are unable to do any kind of actions that would help
in the battle for 1 minute. On a success, nothing happens. The state
may be removed early if the target succeeds in a Wisdom saving throw
at the beginning. of its turn or is targeted by an attack or saving
throw. Any attacks against the creature in that state will be made
with advantage, and any saving throws the creature makes will be with
disadvantage.
Cursed Buds
At 11th level, you have studied the true nature of your innate
technique, creating a new type of plant that feeds on cursed energy.
As an action for 8 Cursed Energy, you can create and launch a number
of cursed buds equal to your CE modifier toward one or more targets
within 30 feet of you. Make a cursed energy attack roll for each, on
a hit they will attach to the creature. At the beginning of the
creature's turns, it will lose an amount of Hit Points equal to your
CE modifier, which. The creature can make a Strength check to remove
the curse bud, removing it on a success. You may instead spend 10
Cursed Energy to create a large-sized tree that will launch one
cursed bud for every hostile creature within 30 feet of it. The tree
lasts for 1 minute, launching more cursed buds at the beginning of
your turns.
