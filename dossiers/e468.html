Properties & techniques

Exact player-facing procedures

Haunted edge

Passive · No CE · +3 to attack and damage with this weapon.

The spirits are diligent tenants, +3 to attack and damage on every swing, asked or unasked. The blade carries its dead like ballast. You gain a +3 bonus to attack and damage rolls with this weapon.

Tormented favor

Free · No CE · When you make an attack roll or saving throw: gain advantage (declare before the roll); usable a number of times equal to your PB per long rest; cannot aid death saves.

And when the bearer calls on them, they answer: advantage on an attack roll or saving throw, a number of times per long rest equal to proficiency bonus. Declare before the roll; the dead decline to intervene at death's door. When you make an attack roll or saving throw, you can gain advantage on the roll as the tormented spirits within the blade grant you favor. You can use this a number of times equal to your proficiency bonus each long rest.

Field notes. Subject: a katana haunted by tormented spirits who grant their favor. Two truths. First: the edge is constant, +3 to strike and wound on every swing, the dead lending their strength without being asked. Second: the favor is rationed, a number of times per long rest equal to proficiency bonus, the bearer may take advantage on an attack roll or saving throw, the spirits leaning on the scales at the moment of decision. Mark the protocol: advantage must be declared before the roll, and the dead do not attend death saving throws. Assess the haunting as a ferry of the slain, each tormented spirit aboard the blade owes its passage to the living hand, and pays in small mercies. A crowded sword is a lucky one, within its limits.
