Slaughter Demon

A cursed dagger with a +1 edge and necrotic bite on every hit.

Field notes. Subject: a knife with an appetite. Three truths govern it. First, simplicity is the strategy: no activation, no attunement, no command word, the +1 edge and the necrotic rider are simply always on, and a weapon that needs no orders never disobeys them. Second, the rider is the point: an extra 1d4 of necrotic on every hit, small wounds that do not close cleanly, the blade taxes each strike, and the tax compounds across a long fight. Third, mark the grade against the hunger: Grade 3, the humblest steel in the compendium, yet its appetite is endless. The cheapest tool carries the most honest price. Hence the maxim: the cleanest blades keep the dirtiest ledgers.
