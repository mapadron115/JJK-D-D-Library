Core Mechanic — The Mark & the Triggers

Tier IV · Debuffer / Caster · Suggested CE ability: INT · Primary verb: Betray. Technique DC = 8 + proficiency bonus + INT modifier. A mark is invisible except to Medea (and to detect-magic-grade senses as a gold thread).

  • Mark of the Oathbreaker (Action, 2 CE): one creature within 60 ft makes a WIS save vs your DC. On failure, it is marked until your next long rest (or until a trigger fires — each mark fires once, then is consumed). You may also apply a mark as part of hitting a creature with any attack (no save — the blow witnessed the oath). - Marked capacity: PB marks active at once (PB × 2 at L11). - When a marked creature takes a triggering action — specific, observable, chosen by you from the list below at the time of marking — the curse fires automatically: 2d8 psychic damage (Heaven-enforced: not an attack, cannot be counterspelled, ignores resistances gained after marking), and the triggering action suffers: disadvantage on its roll, or +2 CE cost if it has no roll. The oath was marked. The breakage is collected. - Trigger list (choose any at marking; declare aloud — she names the terms): (a) casts a spell or activates a technique feature; (b) willingly moves farther from Medea; (c) attacks a creature other than Medea.

Domain Expansion

Domain Expansion: The Wedding at Corinth

  • Domain Expansion (Full-turn Action, 20 CE): "Domain Expansion: The Wedding at Corinth." A disclosed rule-Domain, 60-ft radius — the wedding hall where everyone present was complicit. Sure-hit — "All Present Are Sworn": every hostile inside is automatically marked (no save; the Domain is the oath-chamber, and attendance is consent). Inside the Domain, the trigger list expands to its final form: any hostile action other than directly engaging Medea triggers the curse — casting, fleeing, aiding another, hiding, anything but facing her. - Trigger damage inside the Domain: 6d8 psychic, Heaven-enforced. - Clash points: 800 · Burnout: 5 rounds · Duration: 2 minutes · Barrier per standard formula. At the wedding, everyone watched and no one intervened. Inside her domain, watching is complicity, and complicity is marked.

Level 20 · Capstone

"I Kept Every Oath" (Capstone)

  • The Ledger Opens (Action, 12 CE, once per long rest): Medea speaks her own ledger aloud — three thousand years of kept oaths, recited as evidence. For 1 minute, whenever any creature within 120 ft of her breaks a promise, breaks a vow, or goes back on spoken terms within her hearing, she may use her reaction to trigger her curse on them without a mark: 6d8 psychic damage (WIS save vs her DC halves). - The GM adjudicates "breaks a promise" — it must be observable and specific: a sworn oath recanted, agreed terms violated, a given word taken back. The world is full of oathbreakers. For sixty seconds, she collects from all of them. She kept every oath she ever swore. She is simply balancing the books.
