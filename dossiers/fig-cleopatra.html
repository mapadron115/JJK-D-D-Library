Feature map

Level-by-level features, as the figure refined them.

L1

The Unrolled Carpet

First Impression — Action · 2 CE · Opening Terms — Bonus action · 1 CE.

  • First Impression (Action, 2 CE): one creature within 60 ft that can see and hear her makes a WIS save vs her DC. On failure, it is charmed by her for 1 minute (concentration). While charmed, it regards her as a trusted negotiator: it cannot willingly move farther from her, and it has disadvantage on saves against her other technique features. It knows it was charmed when the effect ends. - Opening Terms (Bonus Action, 1 CE): speak Terms as above. The offered benefit may be: 1d8 + CHA temporary hit points, or advantage on the creature's next attack roll, save, or check. The condition must be specific, observable, and completable within the hour.
L3

Venom in the Wine

Second Cup — Reaction · 2 CE · Passive.

  • The Poison Rider's damage increases to 3d8, and a creature that breaks Terms is also frightened of Cleopatra for 1 minute (WIS save vs her DC negates the fear, not the damage/poison). - Second Cup (Reaction, 2 CE): when a creature charmed by her takes damage from any source, she may have her asp bite the attacker — the attacker takes 2d8 poison damage (CON save halves). Hurt what's hers, and the serpent answers.
L6

The Ptolemaic Court

Court of Asps — Bonus action · 4 CE · Terms of Surrender — Action · 5 CE.

  • Court of Asps (Bonus Action, 4 CE, 1 minute): a 30-ft aura. Enemies in the aura have disadvantage on saves against being charmed or frightened. Allies in the aura gain advantage on CHA ability checks. She can offer Terms to any number of creatures in the aura with one bonus action. - Terms of Surrender (Action, 5 CE): speak Terms to up to PB creatures at once. The offered benefit may now also be: end one condition affecting the creature (her choice of poisoned, frightened, or charmed — she decides what mercy looks like).
L11

The Last Gambit

Alexandria's Price — Reaction · 8 CE.

  • Alexandria's Price (Reaction, 8 CE): when Cleopatra is reduced to 0 hit points by a creature's attack or effect, she may offer Final Terms to that creature before falling unconscious: "Spare me, and be bound." The creature chooses freely — - Accept: she stabilizes at 1 HP, and the creature is bound for 1 hour: it cannot attack her or cause her harm, Heaven-enforced (breaking deals the Poison Rider with no save on the damage). - Decline: she falls unconscious as normal, but the asp strikes from beyond — the decliner takes 8d10 poison damage (CON save vs her DC halves). - The Poison Rider's damage increases to 4d8.
L18

The Serpent Wears the Crown

Ultimate Terms — Action · 10 CE · Passive.

  • Ultimate Terms (Action, 10 CE): one creature within 60 ft. She speaks terms whose condition may be any non-self-destructive command ("guard this door," "tell me what you know," "fight beside me"). Accept (always free to decline): the creature is bound for 24 hours — it must obey the stated condition to the best of its ability. Each time it willfully violates the condition, the Poison Rider fires (6d8 + poisoned, CON save halves damage only). - She may maintain Court of Asps and one charm simultaneously without concentration (the court still costs its activation). - Every Throne Is a Bargain (passive): creatures have disadvantage on Insight checks against her — no one has ever heard the terms she was actually offering.

Core Mechanic — Terms & the Poison Rider

Terms — Bonus action · 1 CE.

Tier III · Face / Controller (Enchanter) · Suggested CE ability: CHA · Primary verb: Negotiate. Technique DC = 8 + proficiency bonus + CHA modifier. All ranges assume she can be heard and understood (magical translation counts). A creature that cannot hear her is immune to all bargain features.

As a bonus action (1 CE), Cleopatra may speak Terms to any number of creatures that can hear her: a stated benefit she offers (e.g., temporary hit points, advantage on their next roll, healing) in exchange for a stated condition (e.g., "do not attack me," "answer my next question truthfully," "leave this place"). Each creature individually accepts or declines — declining is always free, always safe, no roll required. An accepted bargain lasts 1 hour (or until its condition is fulfilled). Breaking accepted Terms triggers the Poison Rider automatically: the breaker takes poison damage and suffers the poisoned condition for 1 minute (CON save vs her DC halves the damage and negates the condition). This is Heaven-enforced — it is not an attack, cannot be counterspelled, and ignores resistance granted after the bargain was struck. You said yes. Heaven heard you. Rider damage scales: 2d8 (L1–L2), 3d8 (L3–L10), 4d8 (L11–L17), 6d8 (L18+).

Level 20 · Capstone

Queen of Kings (Capstone)

Action · 12 CE · once per long rest.
  • The Donations of Alexandria (Action, 12 CE, once per long rest): the ceremony at which she once divided the east among her children, re-enacted as technique. She speaks Alexandria's Terms to any number of creatures that can hear her: accept, and be bound for 24 hours — take no hostile action against Cleopatra or her allies. Breaking the vow triggers the Poison Rider at 8d8 with no save on the damage (the condition is negated on a successful CON save as normal). The vow was accepted willingly. Heaven does not negotiate. - Additionally, her Court of Asps becomes permanent while she has any CE remaining — she no longer needs to activate it. Wherever the Serpent holds court, the air tastes of venom and opportunity.
