Feature map

Level-by-level features, as the figure refined them.

L1

The Ariki's Word

Bonus action · 2 CE.

Tier IV · Controller · Suggested CE ability: WIS · Primary verb: Anchor. Technique DC = 8 + proficiency bonus + WIS modifier. Spoken authority, given weight. The ariki's word stills what it names — but the binding bites trespassers only; his own people sail free. The Word. Hotu Matu'a's mooring effects never affect willing creatures — his people, his allies-by-choice, pass through his word like wind through a sail. The binding is for the uninvited.

Hotu Matu'a speaks to one creature or vessel he can see within 120 ft: WIS save against the technique DC or its speed becomes 0 until the end of his next turn. Willing creatures are unaffected — no save needed; the word passes over them.

L1

Swell-Reading

passive

He reads the water the way others read faces: Hotu Matu'a always knows the direction (not distance, not identity) of vessels and Huge-or-larger creatures moving in water within 1 mile. He has advantage on checks to navigate, predict, or intercept movement at sea. (The swells bend around islands. He reads the bending.)

L3

The Fleet Is Moored

Action · 4 CE · 1 minute.

For the duration, hostile creatures within 60 ft have their speed halved and cannot take the Dash action — WIS save against the technique DC at the start of each of their turns negates the effect for that turn. Willing creatures move freely. (The word goes out over the water, and the water keeps it.)

L3

Star-Path

Reaction · 2 CE · once per round.

When a willing creature within 120 ft would be moved against its will, Hotu Matu'a names a star: the movement is negated. The willing are not moved but by their own word. (His people sail free. That was always the verse.)

L6

The Moai Witness

Action · 5 CE · 1 minute.

The fifteen attend: Hotu Matu'a's mooring save DC increases by 2, hostile creatures have disadvantage on saves against his mooring effects, and he may perceive through the Moai's Eyes (Latent Asset 18) — seeing arrivals and departures within 10 miles of the ahu as if present. (A word witnessed by the ancestors is a word Heaven cannot mishear.)

L11

The Anchorage

Action · 8 CE · 1 minute (concentration).

A 120-ft radius centered on a point within 150 ft becomes anchored water. Hostile creatures and vessels that enter or start their turns inside must succeed on a WIS save or have speed 0 until the start of their next turn. Willing creatures sail free.

Maximum Technique

Maximum: THE SHORE HOLDS

Action · 10 CE · once per long rest · 1 minute.

For the duration, no hostile creature or vessel within 300 ft can move without his spoken leave (granting leave is a free action on his turn — invented): each must succeed on a WIS save at the start of its turn or have speed 0 for that turn. His willing allies are unaffected. The sea holds its breath. He decides when it exhales.

Domain Expansion

THE FLEET AT ANCHOR

Action · 20 CE · 60-ft radius · 800 clash points · 5-round burnout · 2-minute duration.

The sea goes glass-still; the stars come out at noon. Sure-hit (enemies): hostile creatures cannot take the Dash action, their speed is halved, and Hotu Matu'a knows the current heading, speed, and declared course of every vessel inside — what the water shows, not what the master intends. Sure-hit (allies): willing creatures' movement cannot be reduced or denied by any effect while inside. (The stillness, perfected: nothing hostile moves but by his leave.)

Level 20 · Capstone

THE FIRST ARIKI

Passive.

Once per long rest, Hotu Matu'a speaks the great mooring: every hostile vessel within 1 mile is becalmed — speed 0 for 10 minutes. Willing-decline: the master of a vessel may instead turn about — abandoning its course — and sail freely away from him. Turn back, and go free. (The first ariki apportioned the sea. The apportioning stands.)
