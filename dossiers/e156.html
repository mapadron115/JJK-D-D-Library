Pestilence Doctrine

Diagnose a target, accumulate Symptoms, cash those Symptoms into a brutal “Cure,” then turn the dead into crude Patients that extend your action economy without multiplying attacks.

Combat roleDebuffer / executioner / corpse-summoner

Research log. Subject: the physician whose patients do not survive the charting. The subject was admitted voluntarily, it walked in, asked for a ward, and began diagnosing the staff. Two truths govern this file. First: the examination is the infection, a glance reads the body's ledger, a touch writes Symptoms into healthy tissue, and the Cure that follows is only the harvest of what was planted. Second: the ward is the organism, corpses become Patients, Patients become staff, and the Domain below is an operating theater in which everyone present is triaged without consent. The subject is unfailingly polite. It apologizes to each Patient before the Cure. We have logged a formal objection to its definition of gentleness.

Full progression

Progression

1Diagnosis / Symptoms

Diagnosis Die

The prognosis is measurable: a die that grows as the practice deepens. We have stopped calling it a die and started calling it a dosage. d6 → d8 → d10 → d12.

Clinical Examination.

Examination event: costs nothing, reveals everything, above or below half health, poisoned or diseased, energy above or below half. The body's ledger, read in a glance. Our own intake examinations take forty minutes and miss things. The subject's take a glance and miss nothing. Bonus action, no CE: choose one creature you can perceive within 30 feet. Learn whether it is above or below half HP, whether it is poisoned or diseased, and whether its current CE is above or below half its maximum. You have advantage on Medicine checks concerning that creature for 10 minutes.

Clinical Touch.

Annotation event: one unit of energy after a hit writes a Symptom where there was none. The body, annotated. The subject is not discovering illness. It is authoring it, in the margins of healthy tissue. Once per turn after you hit a creature with a melee weapon, unarmed strike, or CE attack, spend 1 CE to give it 1 Symptom. A creature may hold up to your PB Symptoms; they last 1 minute after the most recent application.

Symptomatic Decline.

Compound effect: every two Symptoms decay the body's healing and weaken its Constitution against the practice. The treatment degrades the patient's ability to receive treatment. This is, technically, iatrogenic. The subject does not seem to mind the term. For every 2 Symptoms a creature has, reduce healing it receives by one Diagnosis Die and subtract 1 from Constitution saves against this technique. Healing reduction cannot reduce healing below 0.

3The Cure

The Cure.

The Cure: every Symptom consumed becomes necrotic damage, and at maximum dosage on the dying, the body is simply told to stop. No trauma is visible post-termination. The subject considers this gentleness. We have logged a formal disagreement. Action, 2 CE, melee CE attack. On hit, consume every Symptom on the target and deal (2 + Symptoms consumed) Diagnosis Dice + CE modifier necrotic damage. At 3+ consumed Symptoms the target cannot regain HP until the start of your next turn. If you consume the target’s maximum possible Symptoms while it is at or below one-quarter of its maximum HP, it makes a Constitution save after the damage; on failure it drops to 0 HP.

6Crude Surgery

Patient creation: ten minutes and five energy over a corpse produces one Patient, maintained one at a time. The ward's first permanent resident. It does not speak. It follows instructions. We are not comforted by its obedience. Spend 5 CE and 10 minutes operating on a corpse to create one Patient. You may maintain one Patient at a time.

11Systemic Pestilence

Practice expansion: the Symptom cap rises, the Cure itself may raise a Patient, and the ward now holds a full count of them, one attack a round among them. The subject is building a staff. We did not approve the hiring. Symptom cap becomes PB + 2. A corpse reduced to 0 HP by The Cure may be converted into a Patient as an action for 4 CE. Maximum Patients becomes PB, though the shared command rule still permits only one Patient attack per round.

18Ward of the Final Physician

The Touch now applies twice per turn, and the subject has manifested the Ward below. Throughput has doubled. Our containment staffing has not. Clinical Touch may apply a Symptom at most twice per turn to different targets, and you gain the Domain below.

Pinnacle expression

Domain Expansion — Ward of the Final Physician

ExpansionDomain event, designated the Ward: for one minute, the barrier is an operating theater, at each twenty-count, every hostile is diagnosed at once, and the Staff may assist uncommanded. Everyone inside is a patient now, including us. Triage is performed by the subject. Consent was not requested. Full-turn action, 20 CE, enclosed 60-foot-radius hospital/operating-theater Domain for 1 minute.

Perfect Diagnosis, sole sure-hit At initiative count 20, each chosen hostile creature automatically gains 2 Symptoms, up to its cap. A protected creature makes a Constitution save, gaining no Symptoms on success.

Sterile Field Clinical Examination works at any range inside the barrier. Once on each of your turns, you may apply one Symptom to a creature you can see as a bonus action for 1 CE; this is a normal technique activation, not a sure-hit.

Operating Staff Patients in the Domain gain +10 feet speed and may Help without being commanded. Their attacks still obey the one-Patient-attack-per-round limit.

Clash bookkeeping 800 clash points (Tier IV). In a Domain clash, a 100+ point advantage collapses the weaker Domain; collapse deals no damage. Barrier: AC 12 + PB; HP = 10 × max(1, your CE modifier) + 1,600. Burnout: 5 rounds.

Innate Technique Feats

Technique Feats

Pestilence Doctrine

House Call

Reach extension: fifteen feet through a spectral gloved hand, the examination need not touch. The subject can now diagnose at a distance. Social distancing, as a concept, has been retired for this ward. Clinical Touch and The Cure gain 15-foot reach through a spectral gloved hand. This does not let you pass through total cover.

Clinical Examination

Second Opinion

Deeper rendering: examinations now report exact hit points, or name one resistance or vulnerability. The subject's charting is more thorough than ours. This is embarrassing, professionally. Your examinations render deeper diagnoses: when you use Clinical Examination on a creature, choose one, learn its current hit points as a number, or learn one of its damage resistances or vulnerabilities.

Crude Surgery

Field Surgeon

Field adaptation noted. It works faster under pressure. So do we, now. Outside combat, Crude Surgery takes 1 minute instead of 10. In combat, a corpse you personally reduced to 0 HP may be made a Patient as a full-turn action for 4 CE even before level 11.

Pestilence Doctrine, level 11

Humoral Imbalance

Systemic stage: at four Symptoms the humors rebel, speed sapped, reactions lost on failed resistance. The body begins to agree with the diagnosis. That is the most frightening sentence in this file. A creature with 4+ Symptoms also has its speed reduced by 10 feet and cannot take reactions after it fails a Constitution save against your technique until the start of its next turn.

Rules integration

Table Rulings

Symptoms are a technique mark, not a mundane disease. Poison immunity does not prevent them unless an effect specifically blocks cursed-technique marks.
The Cure dropping a creature to 0 HP is not instant death. Normal death saves, stabilization, and execution rules still apply unless the creature is an NPC that the GM resolves immediately.
Patients retain no class features, techniques, spellcasting, memories, or equipment attunement from the corpse.
Only one Patient can make an attack per round unless a feature explicitly overrides the shared command rule.
