Pestilence Doctrine

Diagnose a target, accumulate Symptoms, cash those Symptoms into a brutal “Cure,” then turn the dead into crude Patients that extend your action economy without multiplying attacks.

Combat roleDebuffer / executioner / corpse-summoner
Core law

Core Engine — Symptoms

Diagnosis Die:

d6 → d8 → d10 → d12.

Clinical Examination.

Bonus action, no CE: choose one creature you can perceive within 30 feet. Learn whether it is above or below half HP, whether it is poisoned or diseased, and whether its current CE is above or below half its maximum. You have advantage on Medicine checks concerning that creature for 10 minutes.

Clinical Touch.

Once per turn after you hit a creature with a melee weapon, unarmed strike, or CE attack, spend 1 CE to give it 1 Symptom. A creature may hold up to your PB Symptoms; they last 1 minute after the most recent application.

Symptomatic Decline.

For every 2 Symptoms a creature has, reduce healing it receives by one Diagnosis Die and subtract 1 from Constitution saves against this technique. Healing reduction cannot reduce healing below 0.

The Cure.

Starting at level 3, action, 2 CE, melee CE attack. On hit, consume every Symptom on the target and deal (2 + Symptoms consumed) Diagnosis Dice + CE modifier necrotic damage. At 3+ consumed Symptoms the target cannot regain HP until the start of your next turn. If you consume the target’s maximum possible Symptoms while it is at or below one-quarter of its maximum HP, it makes a Constitution save after the damage; on failure it drops to 0 HP.

Full progression

Progression

1Diagnosis / Symptoms

Clinical Examination and Clinical Touch build the disease engine. Clinical Touch itself deals your normal attack’s damage; the technique rider is the Symptom.

3The Cure

Gain the Symptom cashout and execution threshold described above.

6Crude Surgery

Spend 5 CE and 10 minutes operating on a corpse to create one Patient. You may maintain one Patient at a time.

11Systemic Pestilence

Symptom cap becomes PB + 2. A corpse reduced to 0 HP by The Cure may be converted into a Patient as an action for 4 CE. Maximum Patients becomes PB, though the shared command rule still permits only one Patient attack per round.

18Ward of the Final Physician

Clinical Touch may apply a Symptom at most twice per turn to different targets, and you gain the Domain below.

Pinnacle expression

Domain Expansion — Ward of the Final Physician

Expansion Full-turn action, 20 CE, enclosed 60-foot-radius hospital/operating-theater Domain for 1 minute.

Perfect Diagnosis — sole sure-hit At initiative count 20, each chosen hostile creature automatically gains 2 Symptoms, up to its cap. A protected creature makes a Constitution save, gaining no Symptoms on success.

Sterile Field Clinical Examination works at any range inside the barrier. Once on each of your turns, you may apply one Symptom to a creature you can see as a bonus action for 1 CE; this is a normal technique activation, not a sure-hit.

Operating Staff Patients in the Domain gain +10 feet speed and may Help without being commanded. Their attacks still obey the one-Patient-attack-per-round limit.

Clash bookkeeping 800 clash points (Tier IV). In a Domain clash, a 100+ point advantage collapses the weaker Domain; collapse deals no damage. Barrier: AC 12 + PB; HP = 10 × max(1, your CE modifier) + 1,600. Burnout: 5 rounds.

Innate Technique Feats

Technique Feats

Pestilence Doctrine

House Call

Clinical Touch and The Cure gain 15-foot reach through a spectral gloved hand. This does not let you pass through total cover.

Clinical Examination

Second Opinion

After examining a creature, your next successful Clinical Touch against it before the end of your next turn applies 2 Symptoms instead of 1. Once per creature per short rest.

Crude Surgery

Field Surgeon

Outside combat, Crude Surgery takes 1 minute instead of 10. In combat, a corpse you personally reduced to 0 HP may be made a Patient as a full-turn action for 4 CE even before level 11.

Pestilence Doctrine, level 11

Humoral Imbalance

A creature with 4+ Symptoms also has its speed reduced by 10 feet and cannot take reactions after it fails a Constitution save against your technique until the start of its next turn.

Rules integration

Table Rulings

Symptoms are a technique mark, not a mundane disease. Poison immunity does not prevent them unless an effect specifically blocks cursed-technique marks.
The Cure dropping a creature to 0 HP is not instant death. Normal death saves, stabilization, and execution rules still apply unless the creature is an NPC that the GM resolves immediately.
Patients retain no class features, techniques, spellcasting, memories, or equipment attunement from the corpse.
Only one Patient can make an attack per round unless a feature explicitly overrides the shared command rule.
