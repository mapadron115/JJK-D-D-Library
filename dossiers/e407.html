Dread Helm

A Grade 4 cursed item, a fearsome steel helm.

FormCursed Item
GradeGrade 4
AttunementNot required

Field notes. Subject: the unface. A fearsome steel helm that drowns the wearer’s face in shadow and sets the eyes burning red, and while it is worn, no check of the face can name him. Two truths govern it. First, the disguise is absolute, not graded: identity by face is not harder, it is impossible, the helm does not obscure the features, it removes them from the contest. Second, the terror is the point as much as the concealment: red eyes in a drowned shadow announce the wearer before he speaks, and some who see him will remember the helm long after they forget the mission. Mark this: to be no one is the oldest armor. The face is a liability the wise discard.

Passive

Faceless Terror

Passive · While worn: eyes glow red, face hidden in shadow · Checks to determine your identity from your face are impossible.

Wear it, and the face ceases to be evidence: red eyes, drowned shadow, and any attempt to read identity from the features simply fails. He does not hide his face. He resigns it. While you are wearing the helm, your eyes glow red and the rest of your face is hidden in shadow. Any checks to determine your identity from your face are impossible.
