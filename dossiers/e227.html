Properties & techniques

Exact player-facing procedures

Feature

First hit/turn against Bane +1 Tool Die.

Hoard Breaker

2 CE on hit vs creature carrying CE-charged object: suppress one passive bonus from that object until start next turn, save if unwilling.

Fafnir's Lesson

when Bane damages you, gain advantage on next attack against it before end next turn.

Release: Dragon's Ruin

7 CE, 1 minute: Bane bonus +2 Tool Dice and resistance to one damage type the Bane dealt to you last round.

Rules integration

Cursed-tool attunement

Campaign canon

Cursed-tool attunement: you have PB attunement slots (2–6). This tool occupies one slot and requires a one-hour ritual. Attunement ends voluntarily over a short rest, on death, or when you replace it while over capacity. Distance does not break attunement. Cursed-tool attunement is separate from magic-item attunement.
Balance & counterplay. Requires declaring a prey category and adapting to it; not universal artifact suppression.
