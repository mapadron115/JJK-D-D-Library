Features & procedures

Properties & techniques

Exact player-facing procedures

Feature

First hit/turn against BaneThe appetizer is automatic: the first hit of each turn against the named prey carries an extra die. The blade's attention, once declared, does not wander. +1 Tool Die.

Hoard Breaker

Two CE on a hit and the hoard-breaker's trade lands: one passive bonus of the victim's CE-charged object is suppressed until the next turn — an unwilling bearer may contest it with Constitution against the Tool DC. Take the treasure apart from the inside. 2 CE on hit vs creature carrying CE-charged object: suppress one passive bonus from that object until start next turn; an unwilling creature makes a Constitution save vs Tool DC to resist (invented).

Fafnir's Lesson

The wounded hunter's tuition: when the named prey draws your blood, your next attack against it is advantaged. The dragon taught its slayer by biting him first. when Bane damages you, gain advantage on next attack against it before end next turn.

Release: Dragon's Ruin

Seven CE for one minute of the ruin's whole name: the Bane bonus grows to two dice, and the bearer gains resistance to whatever the prey dealt him last round. The dragon-slayer's last lesson: become the thing that cannot be burned. 7 CE, 1 minute, bonus action to activate (invented): Bane bonus +2 Tool Dice and resistance to one damage type the Bane dealt to you last round.

Field notes. Subject: a story that outlived its teller and began choosing its own prey. Three truths govern him. First, the daily Bane is appetite, not enchantment: each dawn the blade names one thing it intends to eat — dragon and serpent, cursed spirit, or cursed-tool wielder — and the first hit of every turn against it carries an extra die. Second, it eats the way a hoard-breaker eats: against a bearer of a CE-charged object, the struck object's passive bonus is suppressed until the next turn begins — the dragon's treasure, unmade piece by piece. Third, it learns from being bitten: when the Bane damages the bearer, the next attack against it is made with advantage before the next turn ends — Fafnir's lesson, the wounded hunter's tuition. Mark the discipline: the blade must declare its prey and adapt to it. It is a ledger of appetites, not an open account.

Rules integration

Cursed-tool attunement

Campaign canon

Cursed-tool attunement: you have PB attunement slots (2–6). This tool occupies one slot and requires a one-hour ritual. Attunement ends voluntarily over a short rest, on death, or when you replace it while over capacity. Distance does not break attunement. Cursed-tool attunement is separate from magic-item attunement.
Balance & counterplay. Requires declaring a prey category and adapting to it; not universal artifact suppression.
