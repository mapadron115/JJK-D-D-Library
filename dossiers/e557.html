Core law

What the technique actually does.

Core law, the spider and the thread. Two systems, one technique. The spider gives you webs, venom, physique, sense, and emergence — the body of the animal, the tools of the hunt. Fate gives you threads — every creature connected to its own future by a line you can see, pull, and cut. The spider is the skin. Fate is the skeleton. The web holds them still; the thread tells you where they will go. Everything else is application.

Field notes. Three truths from the file. First: the web is not the weapon — the restraint is. A held target is a solved target; everything else in the technique assumes the target cannot move. Second: the sense is free, the dodge is paid. You perceive every incoming attack at no cost; you spend only when you choose to not be hit. The technique never gates you; your CE does. Third: the thread is not metaphor. You see fate as a physical line. You can pull it, redirect it, glimpse along it, sever it. The Domain is what happens when you stop pulling single threads and see the whole web at once.

Full progression

Level 1 through 20.

1Webshot · Spider Physique · Manifested Weapon

Webshot. Replaces an attack · 1 CE · Ranged CE attack, 60 ft · When you take the Attack action, you may replace one attack with Webshot. On a hit, the target is Restrained by webbing. It repeats the save at the end of each of its turns, or breaks free by using an action to make a Strength check against your CE save DC, or by dealing damage to the webbing (AC 12, HP 10 + your level). You may also shoot a web at a surface to lay a 10-foot square of web terrain. The square is difficult terrain.

Observation. Primary restraint mechanism. Webbing is deployed from the wrists. Restraint is reliable. Terrain webbing is difficult ground, not a restraint. The first lesson of the web: hold them still. Everything else follows.

Spider Physique. Passive · Your Strength and Dexterity scores each increase by 2 · You count as one size larger for carrying, lifting, and dragging · Your jump height is tripled · You take no fall damage.

Observation. Proportional spider strength. Jump height measured at three times baseline. Falls from any observed height produced no injury. The spider does not respect the scale of the body it lives in.

Manifested Weapon — Stinger and Mandibles. Counts as a class weapon · Two attack modes, chosen per attack: Stinger: melee, 1d8 piercing, finesse · Mandibles: melee, 1d6 piercing, on hit the target is Grappled (escape DC = your CE save DC).

Observation. The weapon was not manufactured. It grew. Stinger and mandibles are distinct attack modes.

3Spider-Sense · Wall-Crawling · The Spider Emerges

Spider-Sense. When you see an attack roll that would hit you, you may spend CE to raise your AC against that attack. This is not a reaction — you may do it as many times per round as you can afford. L3: +5 AC · 1 CE · L6: +7 AC · 2 CE · L11: +10 AC · 3 CE · You cannot be surprised.

Observation. You perceive incoming attacks before they land. The perception is free. The evasion is paid. You choose which attacks are worth the expenditure. Seeing the roll is the tingle of the sense. Choosing to dodge is the paid activation.

Wall-Crawling. Passive · Your climb speed equals your walking speed · You can climb on ceilings · Your hands remain free.

Observation. Walls are floors that have not been introduced to you yet.

The Spider Emerges. Bonus action · 4 CE · 1 minute · Limbs burst from your body · When you take the Attack action, you make 3 additional attacks · Advantage on unarmed strikes and mandible strikes · Manifested weapon damage rolls deal an additional 2d6 · You cannot use the Spider-Sense dodge while transformed · The spider feeds: at the start of each of your turns while transformed, you take 1d6 necrotic damage.

Observation. Transformation event. Additional limbs manifest from the torso. Aggression increases markedly. Defensive precision decreases — you cannot execute the Spider-Sense dodge while transformed. The spider feeds on you during transformation. The human side drops. The predator comes out. The spider is always hungry, and it eats from the hand that feeds it — yours.

6Thread Sense

Thread Sense. Passive perception (free): you perceive the fate-threads of all creatures within 60 feet, even through walls and cover; you know their locations · Active (2 CE): as an action, pull a thread — Drag: target a creature within 60 feet. It makes a Strength saving throw. On a failure, it is dragged up to 30 feet in a direction you choose · Redirect: impose disadvantage on an attack roll made by a creature within 60 feet.

Observation. You perceive connections between creatures and their fates as physical threads. Perception is passive and constant. Manipulation requires expenditure. Every creature is a knot. Pull the right thread and the whole shape moves.

Spider-Sense improves: +7 AC · 2 CE.

11Web Dominion

Web Dominion. Action · 6 CE · Concentration, up to 1 minute · Choose a point you can see within 60 feet. A 30-foot-radius sphere of webbing fills the area · The zone is difficult terrain · Creatures that enter the zone or start their turn there take 5d10 poison damage (Constitution save for half) · You sense every creature in the zone · The zone does not restrain.

Observation. You establish a zone of webbing. The zone does not restrain — restraint is Webshot’s function, not the zone’s. The zone is territory, not a cage. The zone sets the table. It does not serve the meal.

Spider-Sense improves: +10 AC · 3 CE.

18Web of Destiny

You learn your Domain Expansion (see Pinnacle expression).

The Domain is not the capstone. It is the thesis — the whole web, seen at once. Everything before was pulling single threads. The Domain is the web itself.

Pinnacle expression

Maximum, Domain, or equivalent.

An infinite web of luminous threads stretches in every direction, each one a lifeline. Touch a thread and an entire life unfolds — every path taken, every path not taken, every ending, all at once. You sit at the center, patient as a spider at the heart of a web the size of a universe. You see all of it. You always have.

Domain Expansion, Web of Destiny. Full-turn action · 20 CE · 60-foot-radius enclosed Domain centered on you · 1 minute · Burnout 10 rounds.

Fate Vision. At the beginning of each creature’s turn in the Domain, it makes a Wisdom saving throw against your CE save DC + 5. You are immune. Failure: 10d8 psychic damage. Stunned until the end of its next turn — overwhelmed by infinite paths. Success: half damage. Not Stunned. Advantage on its next attack roll, saving throw, or ability check — it glimpsed a favorable path.

Omniscience. While the Domain stands, you know the location and condition of every creature inside it. Creatures cannot hide from you. You see threads, not bodies.

Sever Thread. As an action, spend all remaining CE (minimum 10) to cut a creature’s thread. The creature dies. No save. The Domain immediately ends, and you gain one level of exhaustion.

The save exists. The gift exists. But almost nobody makes it. Seeing every path of your life at once should break you. The capability is real. The cost is real. The cut costs so much it is a genuine last resort, not a button.

Feats

Build-defining options.

Prerequisite: Thread Sense

Swarm Body

Action · 3 CE · 1 minute · Dissolve into a swarm of spiders. While in swarm form: you can move through gaps as narrow as 1 inch; you take half damage from bludgeoning, piercing, and slashing damage; single-target attacks against you have disadvantage; you sense everything within 30 feet — each spider is an eye; creatures that start their turn in your space take 2d6 piercing damage (a thousand bites); you cannot attack or use other technique features while dispersed. You may reform as a bonus action at any point within 60 feet of your swarm. When you reform, your next attack has advantage. Hypothesis: the swarm is not a transformation. It is a distribution. You are not one thing pretending to be many. You are many things pretending to be one.

Prerequisite: Webshot

Venom

Passive · Your webs and manifested weapons carry venom. When you hit with Webshot or a manifested weapon, the target takes an additional 2d6 poison damage and must make a Constitution saving throw or be Poisoned until the end of its next turn. The bite was never the weapon. The bite was the delivery.

Prerequisite: Thread Sense

Fate’s Glimpse

Bonus action · 3 CE · Touch a creature’s thread and see its immediate future. Target a creature within 60 feet whose thread you can see. Until the end of your next turn, any attack that creature makes against you automatically fails. You already saw it coming. Learning the next action, made mechanical: you know exactly what they will do, so it cannot land.

Prerequisite: Thread Sense

Fate Sever

Action · 3 CE · Cut a creature’s thread to fate. Target a creature within 60 feet whose thread you can see. Until the end of its next turn, it has disadvantage on all attack rolls, saving throws, and ability checks. Fate is no longer on its side. Not a pull. Not a kill. A severing. The one thing Thread Sense does not do.

Rules integration

Edge cases and shared systems.

Codex rulings: Direct rolled technique attacks can use the campaign’s Black Flash procedure when otherwise eligible. Sever Thread’s instant kill is not damage and cannot critically hit or Black Flash.
Webshot’s restraint: the target repeats the save at the end of each of its turns. Breaking free costs an action (Strength check vs your CE save DC) or damage to the webbing (AC 12, HP 10 + your level). The webbing is an object; area effects that damage it apply.
Spider-Sense is not a reaction. You may activate it any number of times per round, limited only by CE. It cannot be used while The Spider Emerges is active.
Web Dominion’s zone does not restrain. Creatures restrained by Webshot inside the zone still take the zone’s poison damage on their turns.
Sever Thread (Domain): spending all remaining CE (minimum 10) ends the Domain immediately and gives you one level of exhaustion. If you have fewer than 10 CE remaining, you cannot use it.
Thread Sense perception passes through walls and cover. It does not reveal a creature’s identity or intentions — only its location and its thread.
