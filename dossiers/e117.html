Symbiote

Living armor / tendrils / interception / host bond

Power tierTier V · Calamity
Core law

What the technique actually does.

Core Engine — Bond. Bond ranges 0–6. Gain 1 when host and symbiote protect one another at meaningful cost, consume suitable biomass, or overcome a mental conflict together. Lose 1 when forcibly separated or when the host betrays the bond. At Bond 3/6, features evolve.

The fiction. Registry scholars classify the symbiote not as a technique but as a negotiation — a shikigami born of hunger that chose a host instead of a victim, and rewrote its own predation as protection. Bond is the fiction made mechanical: every point is a moment the two kept each other alive when appetite said otherwise. The prevailing theory holds the suit is simply what mutual survival looks like from the outside. Where Carnage is the wound that learned to walk, the symbiote is the hunger that learned to guard — its violence is pointed outward so its host never has to bleed.

Full progression

Level 1 through 20.

1Living Suit

As a bonus action, manifest the symbiote as a living suit: +1 AC, a climb speed equal to your walking speed, and 10-ft tendril reach. Once per turn, when you hit with a melee attack, you may spend 1 CE to deal an extra 1d8 bludgeoning or slashing damage. Ablative Weave: once per round, when you take damage from an attack, the suit hardens around the impact — reduce the damage by your proficiency bonus.

3Web & Tendril

For 1 CE, make a ranged grapple against a Large-or-smaller creature at 30 ft using your CE attack; you may pull either creature 15 ft. At Bond 3, the range becomes 60 ft.

6Predatory Morphology

As a bonus action, choose one morphology: claws (1d12 slashing), blade tendrils (15-ft reach, 1d10 slashing), rampart (the suit thickens into black armor: +2 AC, and you may use your reaction to interpose yourself between an attacker and an ally within 15 ft of you — you become the target instead), or bulk (advantage on Athletics checks, Powerful Build).

9Adaptive Camouflage

For 2 CE, you become invisible while motionless. For 3 CE, you gain active camouflage for 1 minute (requires Focus); attacking ends it until the start of your next turn.

11Symbiotic Recovery

As a bonus action, spend 3 CE to heal 4d10 + your CE modifier. Once per short rest, you may repair one Break injury. Ablative Tithe: when you take damage, you may use your reaction and spend 1 CE to reduce it by 2d8 — the symbiote pulls the wound into itself. Fire or sonic damage you have taken since your previous turn halves the healing from this feature.

13Host Override

When you would become Stunned or Paralyzed, or be knocked unconscious, you may use your reaction and spend 3 CE: the symbiote moves your body until the end of your next turn, suppressing one such condition. Once per short rest.

15Perfect Bond

At Bond 6, you gain +10 speed, +1 AC, and +10 ft tendril reach, and once per turn you may swap morphology without spending a bonus action. Your Ablative Weave now reduces damage by twice your proficiency bonus, and your rampart interception reaches 30 ft.

18King in Black Echo

For 6 CE, for 1 minute: you manifest wing-like tendrils, gain a fly speed of 60, resistance to bludgeoning, piercing, and slashing, and command up to PB detached symbiote fragments as one swarm dealing 4d10 damage per round.

20Lethal Protector

Your Bond cannot fall below 3 except through true separation. Your first morphology change and your first tendril grapple each round cost 0. Once per long rest, when you are reduced to 0 HP, you survive at 1 HP and immediately heal 5d10. The suit chooses: once per long rest, when an ally within 30 ft takes damage from an attack, the symbiote lashes out on its own — you take the damage instead (reduced by your Ablative Weave).

Pinnacle expression

Maximum, Domain, or equivalent.

Maximum — We Are Venom (10 CE): for 3 rounds use all four Predatory Morphologies simultaneously, become Large, reach +10 ft, gain one extra tendril attack when you take the Attack action, and your Ablative Weave reduces damage by twice your proficiency bonus; once per round you may intercept for an ally without spending your reaction. Afterwards gain 1 exhaustion.

Feats

Build-defining options.

Spider-Sense Echo

The symbiote watches your blind spots: attackers gain no advantage against you from flanking or pack tactics, and when a hidden creature attacks you, you learn its direction immediately after the attack resolves.

Living Parachute

When you begin to fall, you may use your reaction to have the symbiote snap out web-wings: you take no falling damage and glide instead, moving up to 60 ft horizontally for every 10 ft you descend, and you land on your feet. You may steer around obstacles while gliding but cannot gain altitude.

Eat Your Brains

You taste memories with the flesh: when you damage a grappled creature with a bite, you may spend 1 CE to learn one fact it knows (GM's discretion) or gain advantage on your next Insight check against it.

Anti-Sonic Conditioning

You separate on your own terms: once per long rest, when sonic damage would forcibly separate you, you may instead partially detach on purpose — one tendril swarm (half the King in Black Echo swarm's dice) acts independently for 1 minute while you remain bonded.

Rules integration

Edge cases and shared systems.

Codex rulings: Direct rolled technique/weapon attacks can use the campaign's Black Flash procedure when otherwise eligible. Passive damage, hazards, summons acting independently, and initiative-20 Domain events cannot. Generic Maximum Output applies only to ordinary features, never named Maxima or Domains.
