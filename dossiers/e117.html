Symbiote

Tier IV · Special GradeLiving suit / tendrils / adaptation / host bond

Core Engine — Bond. Bond ranges 0–6. Gain 1 when host and symbiote protect one another at meaningful cost, consume suitable biomass, or overcome a mental conflict together. Lose 1 when forcibly separated or when the host betrays the bond. At Bond 3/6, features evolve.
