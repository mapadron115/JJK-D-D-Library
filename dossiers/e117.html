Symbiote

Living suit / tendrils / adaptation / host bond

Power tierTier IV · Special Grade
Core law

What the technique actually does.

Core Engine — Bond. Bond ranges 0–6. Gain 1 when host and symbiote protect one another at meaningful cost, consume suitable biomass, or overcome a mental conflict together. Lose 1 when forcibly separated or when the host betrays the bond. At Bond 3/6, features evolve.

Full progression

Level 1 through 20.

1Living Suit

Bonus action manifest. +1 AC, climb speed equal speed, 10-ft tendril reach. Once/turn on melee hit spend 1 CE for +1d8 bludgeoning/slashing.

3Web & Tendril

1 CE: ranged grapple at 30 ft using CE attack; Large or smaller. You may pull either creature 15 ft. Bond 3: range 60 ft.

6Predatory Morphology

Bonus action choose claws (1d10), blade tendrils (15-ft reach 1d8), shield (+2 AC vs one attacker), or bulk (advantage Athletics, Powerful Build).

9Adaptive Camouflage

2 CE: invisible while motionless; 3 CE grants active camouflage for 1 minute, Focus, ending after attacking until start next turn.

11Symbiotic Recovery

Bonus action 3 CE: heal 4d10 + CE modifier. Once/short rest repair one Break injury. Fire/sonic damage taken since previous turn halves healing.

13Host Override

When host would be Stunned, Paralyzed, or knocked unconscious, reaction 3 CE; symbiote moves the body until end next turn, suppressing one such condition. Once/short rest.

15Perfect Bond

At Bond 6, +10 speed, +1 AC, tendril reach +10 ft, and once/turn swap morphology without bonus action.

18King in Black Echo

6 CE, 1 minute: manifest wing-like tendrils, fly 60, resistance B/P/S, command up to PB detached symbiote fragments as one swarm dealing 4d10/round.

20Lethal Protector

Bond cannot fall below 3 except true separation. First morphology change and tendril grapple each round cost 0. Once/long rest survive 0 HP at 1 and immediately heal 5d10.

Pinnacle expression

Maximum, Domain, or equivalent.

Maximum — We Are Venom (10 CE): for 3 rounds use all four Predatory Morphologies simultaneously, become Large, reach +10 ft, and gain one extra tendril attack when taking Attack. Afterwards gain 1 exhaustion.

Feats

Build-defining options.

Spider-Sense Echo

The symbiote watches your blind spots: attackers gain no advantage against you from flanking or pack tactics, and when a hidden creature attacks you, you learn its direction immediately after the attack resolves.

Living Parachute

When you begin to fall, you may use your reaction to have the symbiote snap out web-wings: you take no falling damage and glide instead, moving up to 60 ft horizontally for every 10 ft you descend, and you land on your feet. You may steer around obstacles while gliding but cannot gain altitude.

Eat Your Brains

You taste memories with the flesh: when you damage a grappled creature with a bite, you may spend 1 CE to learn one fact it knows (GM's discretion) or gain advantage on your next Insight check against it.

Anti-Sonic Conditioning

You separate on your own terms: once per long rest, when sonic damage would forcibly separate you, you may instead partially detach on purpose — one tendril swarm (half the King in Black Echo swarm's dice) acts independently for 1 minute while you remain bonded.

Rules integration

Edge cases and shared systems.

Codex rulings: Direct rolled technique/weapon attacks can use the campaign's Black Flash procedure when otherwise eligible. Passive damage, hazards, summons acting independently, and initiative-20 Domain events cannot. Generic Maximum Output applies only to ordinary features, never named Maxima or Domains.
