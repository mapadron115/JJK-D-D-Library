Symbiote

Living armor / interception / tendrils / host bond

Power tierTier V · Calamity
Core law

What the technique actually does.

Passive · Bond ranges 0–6 · Gain 1 when host and symbiote protect one another at meaningful cost, consume suitable biomass, or overcome a mental conflict together · Lose 1 when forcibly separated or the host betrays the bond · Features evolve at Bond 3 and 6.

Core Engine, Bond. The ledger of a partnership. Bond rises when each saves the other at cost, feeding it, or surviving a disagreement together, and falls when they are torn apart or betrayed. At 3 and 6, the terms are renegotiated. Bond ranges 0–6. Gain 1 when host and symbiote protect one another at meaningful cost, consume suitable biomass, or overcome a mental conflict together. Lose 1 when forcibly separated or when the host betrays the bond. At Bond 3/6, features evolve.

Field notes. Subject: a negotiation that never ended. Three truths govern them, for there are always two. First: the symbiote is not a technique but a tenant, a hunger that chose a host over a victim and rewrote its predation as protection; Bond is the ledger of every time the two kept each other alive at cost. Second: the suit is appetite made armor, Ablative Weave hardens where the wound lands, and later pulls the wound into itself entirely; fire and sonics are the landlord's only true threats. Third: the bond matures in milestones, at 3 and 6 the features evolve, and at the summit the suit begins to choose for itself, intercepting death meant for others. Classify this correctly: he is not the wearer. He is the junior partner.

Full progression

Level 1 through 20.

1Living Suit

Bonus action · Manifest the symbiote as a living suit: +1 AC, climb speed = walking speed, 10-ft tendril reach · Once per turn, on a melee hit you may spend 1 CE to deal +1d8 bludgeoning or slashing · Ablative Weave: once per round, when you take damage from an attack, reduce it by your PB + CE modifier.

The partnership signs its first contract: a bonus action to wear the other half of himself, armor, climbing, ten feet of tendril's reach, and a hardening weave that refuses the first wound each round. As a bonus action, manifest the symbiote as a living suit: +1 AC, a climb speed equal to your walking speed, and 10-ft tendril reach. Once per turn, when you hit with a melee attack, you may spend 1 CE to deal an extra 1d8 bludgeoning or slashing damage. Ablative Weave: once per round, when you take damage from an attack, the suit hardens around the impact, reduce the damage by your proficiency bonus + your CE modifier.

3Web & Tendril

Attack · 1 CE · Ranged grapple vs a Large-or-smaller creature at 30 ft using your CE attack; pull either creature 15 ft · At Bond 3 the range becomes 60 ft.

Appetite learns to fetch. One CE for a thirty-foot grapple that can pull either party, at Bond 3, twice the distance. The leash runs both ways. For 1 CE, make a ranged grapple against a Large-or-smaller creature at 30 ft using your CE attack; you may pull either creature 15 ft. At Bond 3, the range becomes 60 ft.

6Predatory Morphology

Bonus action · Choose one morphology: claws (1d12 slashing); blade tendrils (15-ft reach, 1d10 slashing); rampart (+2 AC, and use your reaction to interpose between an attacker and an ally within 15 ft — you become the target, and your Ablative Weave applies even if used this round); or bulk (advantage on Athletics, Powerful Build).

Three drafts of hunger, swapped on a bonus action: the twelve-sided claws, the fifteen-foot blades, or the rampart that lets him stand in front of someone else and mean it. As a bonus action, choose one morphology: claws (1d12 slashing), blade tendrils (15-ft reach, 1d10 slashing), rampart (the suit thickens into black armor: +2 AC, and you may use your reaction to interpose yourself between an attacker and an ally within 15 ft of you, you become the target instead; damage you take from an intercepted attack is reduced by your Ablative Weave even if you already used it this round), or bulk (advantage on Athletics checks, Powerful Build).

9Adaptive Camouflage

Action · 2 CE: become invisible while motionless · 3 CE (requires Focus): active camouflage for 1 minute; attacking ends it until the start of your next turn.

The predator learns the oldest trick of all: stillness. Two CE to vanish standing motionless, three for a minute of active hiding, though striking still gives him away until his next turn. For 2 CE, you become invisible while motionless. For 3 CE, you gain active camouflage for 1 minute (requires Focus); attacking ends it until the start of your next turn.

11Symbiotic Recovery

Bonus action · 3 CE: heal 4d10 + your CE modifier · Once per short rest, repair one Break injury · Ablative Tithe: when you take damage, use your reaction and spend 1 CE to reduce it by 3d8 · Fire or sonic damage taken since your previous turn halves this feature’s healing.

The wound goes into the suit, not the man. Three CE for four dice and the modifier, and fire or sonics taken since last turn halve the harvest. Every symbiosis has its conditions. As a bonus action, spend 3 CE to heal 4d10 + your CE modifier. Once per short rest, you may repair one Break injury. Ablative Tithe: when you take damage, you may use your reaction and spend 1 CE to reduce it by 3d8: the symbiote pulls the wound into itself. Fire or sonic damage you have taken since your previous turn halves the healing from this feature.

13Host Override

Reaction · 3 CE · When you would become Stunned or Paralyzed, or be knocked unconscious: the symbiote moves your body until the end of your next turn, suppressing one such condition · Once per short rest.

When the body refuses, the tenant drives. Three CE as a reaction to suppress stun, paralysis, or unconsciousness for a round, the passenger taking the wheel because the driver cannot. When you would become Stunned or Paralyzed, or be knocked unconscious, you may use your reaction and spend 3 CE: the symbiote moves your body until the end of your next turn, suppressing one such condition. Once per short rest.

15Perfect Bond

Passive · At Bond 6: +10 speed, +1 AC, +10 ft tendril reach, and once per turn swap morphology without a bonus action · Ablative Weave reduces damage by twice your PB + your CE modifier; rampart interception reaches 30 ft · Allies within 10 ft gain +1 AC.

At Bond 6 the negotiation ends: speed, armor, reach, free morphology swaps, allies sheltered under his feelers. They no longer argue. They anticipate. At Bond 6, you gain +10 speed, +1 AC, and +10 ft tendril reach, and once per turn you may swap morphology without spending a bonus action. Your Ablative Weave now reduces damage by twice your proficiency bonus + your CE modifier, and your rampart interception reaches 30 ft. Allies within 10 ft of you gain +1 AC as the symbiote extends watchful feelers over them.

18King in Black Echo

Action · 6 CE · 1 minute: wing-like tendrils, fly speed 60, resistance to bludgeoning, piercing, and slashing · Command up to PB detached symbiote fragments as one swarm: as a bonus action the swarm attacks one creature within 30 ft (CE attack, 4d10 slashing on hit).

Six CE for a minute of the deep memory, wings, flight, hide like stone, and a swarm of detached selves answering to one bonus action. The hunger remembers being a kingdom. For 6 CE, for 1 minute: you manifest wing-like tendrils, gain a fly speed of 60, resistance to bludgeoning, piercing, and slashing, and command up to PB detached symbiote fragments as one swarm: as a bonus action, the swarm attacks one creature within 30 ft, CE attack, 4d10 slashing on hit.

20Lethal Protector

Passive · Bond cannot fall below 3 except through true separation · Your first morphology change and first tendril grapple each round are free · Once per long rest, survive at 1 HP when reduced to 0 HP and heal 5d10 · Once per long rest, when an ally within 30 ft takes damage from an attack, the symbiote intercepts on its own: you take the damage instead.

The final clause: the suit now chooses for itself. Bond cannot fall below 3; the first grab and the first shape of each round are free; once per day, death is refused at 1 HP with five dice of healing, and once, an ally's wound is taken by the suit on its own authority. Protection, no longer negotiated. Your Bond cannot fall below 3 except through true separation. Your first morphology change and your first tendril grapple each round cost 0. Once per long rest, when you are reduced to 0 HP, you survive at 1 HP and immediately heal 5d10. The suit chooses: once per long rest, when an ally within 30 ft takes damage from an attack, the symbiote lashes out on its own, you take the damage instead (reduced by your Ablative Weave).

Pinnacle expression

Maximum, Domain, or equivalent.

Maximum, We Are Venom (10 CE; bonus action): for 3 rounds use all four Predatory Morphologies simultaneously, become Large, reach +10 ft, gain one extra tendril attack when you take the Attack action, and your Ablative Weave reduces damage by twice your proficiency bonus + your CE modifier; once per round you may intercept for an ally without spending your reaction. Afterwards gain 1 exhaustion.

Feats

Build-defining options.

Spider-Sense Echo

The blind spots are someone else's job now. No advantage from flanking or pack tactics, and a hidden striker's direction is reported after the first blow. The symbiote watches your blind spots: attackers gain no advantage against you from flanking or pack tactics, and when a hidden creature attacks you, you learn its direction immediately after the attack resolves.

Living Parachute

The fall is outsourced. A reaction, and web-wings catch what gravity dropped, sixty feet of steering for every ten descended, landed on his feet. When you begin to fall, you may use your reaction to have the symbiote snap out web-wings: you take no falling damage and glide instead, moving up to 60 ft horizontally for every 10 ft you descend, and you land on your feet. You may steer around obstacles while gliding but cannot gain altitude.

Eat Your Brains

He tastes the mind with the flesh. One CE on a grappled bite, and the enemy's knowledge becomes his, or at least his next question does. You taste memories with the flesh: when you damage a grappled creature with a bite, you may spend 1 CE to learn one fact it knows (GM's discretion) or gain advantage on your next Insight check against it.

Anti-Sonic Conditioning

He separates on his own terms. Once per long rest, when sonics would tear them apart, he detaches one tendril-swarm by choice, half the Echo's dice, acting alone for a minute. A retreat filed as a maneuver. You separate on your own terms: once per long rest, when sonic damage would forcibly separate you, you may instead partially detach on purpose, one tendril swarm (half the King in Black Echo swarm's dice) acts independently for 1 minute while you remain bonded.

Rules integration

Edge cases and shared systems.

Codex rulings: Direct rolled technique/weapon attacks can use the campaign's Black Flash procedure when otherwise eligible. Passive damage, hazards, summons acting independently, and initiative-20 Domain events cannot. Generic Maximum Output applies only to ordinary features, never named Maxima or Domains.
