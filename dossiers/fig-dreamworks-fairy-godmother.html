Feature map

Level-by-level features, as the figure refined them.

L1

The Favor Ledger

Passive · Favor pool (maximum 2 × PB).

You keep a ledger of Favors (maximum 2 × PB; it begins at 0). You gain 1 Favor, at most once per round, when a creature you can see within 60 ft thanks you sincerely, does you a courtesy, or acts on your spoken suggestion — the godmother never gives, she invests. You may spend Favors on this technique's features; unspent Favors fade when combat ends, because gratitude has a short shelf life.

L3

Bibbidi-Bobbidi-Boo

Action · 2 CE.

Point your wand and transform one willing creature you can see within 30 ft, choosing one guise that lasts 1 minute. Royal Guise — the target has advantage on CHA checks and saving throws and gains +2 AC, ballroom finery hardened by magic. Swift Steed — the target's speed increases by 20 ft and it ignores difficult terrain; the pumpkin carriage runs on time. A creature can wear only one guise at a time.

L6

Happily Ever After, Bottled

Action · 3 CE · brew PB + 1 vials per long rest.

You produce a vial of potion from your supplies; you can brew a number of vials equal to your PB + 1 per long rest, and unused vials lose potency after 24 hours. A creature that drinks a vial (an action) chooses its blessing. Dashing — it gains 3d8 + your CHA modifier temporary hit points and advantage on its next CHA check within 1 hour. Handsome — it regains 2d8 + your CHA modifier hit points and the poisoned condition ends on it. Side effects may include smugness.

L11

You Are So Rude

Reaction · 2 CE.

When a creature within 60 ft of you damages you or an ally, or openly insults you — she knows rudeness when she hears it — you hex it. It makes a WIS save against your CE save DC: 3d8 psychic damage and disadvantage on attack rolls until the end of your next turn on a failure; half damage and no hex on a success. Manners cost nothing. Rudeness costs extra.

L15

Called Due

Bonus action · 2 Favors.

The ledger is a leash. Choose a creature within 60 ft that you have granted a boon with this technique (a guise, a potion, or any Favor-spent aid). It makes a WIS save against your CE save DC; on a failure it must obey one command you speak, as if by suggestion — no concentration required, lasting 1 minute or until completed. Commands that would cause it direct harm fail automatically; even the godmother honors the letter of the contract. A creature that succeeds is immune for 24 hours.

L18

The Full Spread

Passive.

Your Favor maximum becomes 3 × PB. When you use Bibbidi-Bobbidi-Boo or hand out a potion, you may affect one additional creature — the godmother's generosity scales with the size of the room. Every kindness is an entry. Every entry is a claim.

Pinnacle expression

Maximum, Domain, or equivalent.

Capstone material.

Maximum Technique

Happily Ever After, Enforced

Action · 10 CE · one creature within 120 ft.

You unleash the wand's full charge. The target makes a CHA save against your CE save DC at disadvantage: 8d10 radiant damage, and it is transformed for 1 minute into something harmless — a frog, a pumpkin, a garden gnome (incapacitated, speed 0, cannot take actions or speak) — on a failure; half damage and no transformation on a success. A creature reduced to 0 hit points by this Maximum does not die: it is permanently transformed into a garden ornament, alive but still, a warning to the rude. Death is so gauche. Humiliation is forever.

Level 20 · Capstone

The Godmother's Ball

Action · 1/long rest · 1-minute aura.

You host the happiest evening of everyone's life: a 60-ft aura centered on you. Allies that enter gain 20 temporary hit points (once each) and have advantage on CHA checks and saving throws while inside. Enemies in the aura that damage an ally must make a WIS save or suffer your You Are So Rude hex, at no CE cost. Whenever an ally in the aura succeeds on a CHA check, you gain 1 Favor, ignoring the once-per-round limit. The ball ends early if you are incapacitated. The books must balance — and tonight, they balance in her favor.

Innate Technique Feats

Build-defining options.

These are optional character-building feats. You must meet each listed prerequisite before selecting it.

Prerequisite: The Favor Ledger

The Books Are Open

As an action, spend 1 Favor to audit a creature you can see within 30 ft: you learn one debt, obligation, or secret promise it currently owes to any creature — a favor owed, a vow sworn, a price unpaid. The world's ledger opens to the godmother. The books must balance.

Prerequisite: Bibbidi-Bobbidi-Boo

Bibbidi-Bobbidi-Boo, Reversed

Your transformations cut both ways. When you use Bibbidi-Bobbidi-Boo, you may target a hostile creature instead: it makes a CHA save against your CE save DC; on a failure its weapon becomes a pumpkin (attacks with it deal 1 damage) or its armor becomes rags (−2 AC), your choice, for 1 minute — it repeats the save at the end of each of its turns. Rudeness, redecorated.

Prerequisite: Happily Ever After, Bottled

Spiked Punch Bowl

As an action, you may hurl a vial up to 60 ft, splashing every creature in a 10-ft radius. Each ally in the splash gains the Dashing blessing; each enemy of your choice in the splash must make a WIS save against your CE save DC or be stunned until the end of its next turn, caught admiring its own reflection in the sheen. Vanity is a vintage potion.

Prerequisite: You Are So Rude

Manners Cost Nothing

Your hex reaches further into the soul. A creature hexed by You Are So Rude cannot speak above a whisper for the duration — it cannot cast spells with verbal components or issue orders. You may also trigger You Are So Rude when any ally within 60 ft takes damage, insult or no insult — the godmother defends her own.
