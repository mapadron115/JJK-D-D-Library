Field notes. Subject: the net for what slips through stone. Low-grade curses phase through walls, doors, and bars as though the physical world were a rumor, and so the registry’s answer is not a stronger wall but a humbler one: cord soaked in cursed-spirit blood, reeking and foul, that the low grades cannot pass. Three truths govern it. First, the cord is real to them: it snags, holds, and bars passage where stone would fail. Second, binding is a craft, not a miracle, an action, a willing or helpless captive, and a steady hand’s Sleight of Hand against DC 13; the bound thing is restrained, and escapes only by strength or agility against the same measure. Third, the cord knows its station: Grade 4 and Grade 3, and no higher. Mark this: the knot is older than any technique.

Features & procedures

Properties & techniques

Exact player-facing procedures

Passive

Phase-Proof Cord

The passive miracle: to the low grades, this cord is a wall where walls are rumors. It snags, holds, and bars, the humble answer to the enemy that walks through stone. Low-grade curses (Grade 4 and Grade 3) cannot phase through the cord the way they can through most physical objects. The cord is real to them: it snags, holds, and bars passage.

Action

Bind and Restrain

The active craft: an action, a captive that cannot refuse, willing, incapacitated, or restrained, and a DC 13 hand. Success ties the thing; it is restrained, unphaseable, and escapes only by beating the same DC with strength or agility. As an action, you can use the cord to bind a low-grade curse (Grade 4 or Grade 3) that is willing, incapacitated, or restrained. Make a DC 13 Dexterity (Sleight of Hand) check; on a success the curse is tied up. A curse bound in this way is restrained and cannot phase through the cord, though it can attempt to escape with a successful Strength (Athletics) or Dexterity (Acrobatics) check against the same DC.

Campaign canon notes

Table function: the source does not define "low grade" and gives no procedure for binding. At the table, low-grade means Grade 4 and Grade 3 curses, and binding uses the action + DC 13 Sleight of Hand procedure above. The pungent smell of cursed-spirit blood is descriptive only.
