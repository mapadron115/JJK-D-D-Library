Properties & techniques

Exact player-facing procedures

Passive

Phase-Proof Cord

Low-grade curses (Grade 4 and Grade 3) cannot phase through the cord the way they can through most physical objects. The cord is real to them: it snags, holds, and bars passage.

Action

Bind and Restrain

As an action, you can use the cord to bind a low-grade curse (Grade 4 or Grade 3) that is willing, incapacitated, or restrained. Make a DC 13 Dexterity (Sleight of Hand) check; on a success the curse is tied up. A curse bound in this way is restrained and cannot phase through the cord, though it can attempt to escape with a successful Strength (Athletics) or Dexterity (Acrobatics) check against the same DC.

Edge cases and shared systems.

Campaign canon notes

Table function: the source does not define "low grade" and gives no procedure for binding. At the table, low-grade means Grade 4 and Grade 3 curses, and binding uses the action + DC 13 Sleight of Hand procedure above. The pungent smell of cursed-spirit blood is descriptive only.
