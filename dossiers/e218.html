Features & procedures

Properties & techniques

Exact player-facing procedures

Feature

Each Barb reduces healing received by one Tool Die, minimum 0.

Barbed Withdrawal

action by target, Con check vs Tool DC removes one Barb but deals 1 Tool Die piercing.

Crimson Thrust

3 CE on hit: if target has 2+ Barbs, Con save or add 3 Tool Dice and prevent reactions until end next turn.

Release: Spear of the Fatal Course

level 13+, 7 CE, once/long rest: for 1 minute first hit/turn adds a Barb for free and your attacks ignore half cover; it does not reverse causality or auto-hit.

Rules integration

Cursed-tool attunement

Campaign canon

Cursed-tool attunement: you have PB attunement slots (2–6). This tool occupies one slot and requires a one-hour ritual. Attunement ends voluntarily over a short rest, on death, or when you replace it while over capacity. Distance does not break attunement. Cursed-tool attunement is separate from magic-item attunement.
Balance & counterplay. Preserves the fatal-spear identity without importing unavoidable causality.
