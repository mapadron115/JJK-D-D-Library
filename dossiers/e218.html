Features & procedures

Properties & techniques

Exact player-facing procedures

Feature

The spear's first conviction: each embedded Barb reduces healing received by one Tool Die. The weapon remembers the victim bleeding, and insists on continuity, no wound should be allowed to close. Each Barb reduces healing received by one Tool Die, minimum 0.

Barbed Withdrawal

The hook's tax: removing a Barb costs the target an action and a Constitution check, and a die of piercing either way. Mark this: the spear makes even mercy expensive. action by target, Con check vs Tool DC removes one Barb but deals 1 Tool Die piercing.

Crimson Thrust

Three CE on a hit against the twice-hooked: a Constitution save, or take three extra dice and lose reactions. The wound, deepened, becomes a command, stay still, and bleed. 3 CE on hit: if target has 2+ Barbs, Con save or add 3 Tool Dice and prevent reactions until end next turn.

Release: Spear of the Fatal Course

The release, thirteenth level and beyond: seven CE, once a day, one minute, every first hit each turn plants a Barb unasked, and half cover ceases to hide. Mark this: it does not reverse causality or strike unerringly. The fatal course is not fate; it is persistence. level 13+, 7 CE, once/long rest, bonus action to activate (invented): for 1 minute first hit/turn adds a Barb for free and your attacks ignore half cover; it does not reverse causality or auto-hit.

Field notes. Subject: a refusal. Three truths govern it. First, the spear was quenched in the cursed energy of its own kills until it absorbed one conviction: no wound should be allowed to close, each Barb is accumulated spite, a hook that taxes healing. Second, removal is punished: the barbs come out only by action and Constitution, and cost blood either way; even the release, once a day, plants its hooks unasked. Third, the registry's honesty: it does not reverse causality or strike unerringly, the fatal course is not fate but persistence, the insistence that what was opened stays open. Consider the paradox. A spear named for an unavoidable death, and its truest power is patience. The wound outlasts the warrior; the spear merely made the introduction.

Rules integration

Cursed-tool attunement

Campaign canon

Cursed-tool attunement: you have PB attunement slots (2–6). This tool occupies one slot and requires a one-hour ritual. Attunement ends voluntarily over a short rest, on death, or when you replace it while over capacity. Distance does not break attunement. Cursed-tool attunement is separate from magic-item attunement.
Balance & counterplay. Preserves the fatal-spear identity without importing unavoidable causality.
