Cursed Glasses

A Grade 4 cursed item, lenses embedded with cursed energy.

FormCursed Item
GradeGrade 4
AttunementNot required

Field notes. Subject: borrowed eyes. The curse stands in the room and the novice sees only the room, this is the oldest humiliation of the trade, and these lenses were made to end it. Three truths govern them. First, they grant nothing of their own: cursed energy embedded in glass, worn over the eyes, letting the wearer see curses as though they were visible, including those he otherwise could not perceive. Second, sight is power: what the lenses reveal can be targeted, struck, and named, effects that require seeing the target now have their witness. Third, they ask no pact and no attunement; they are an instrument, not a master. Mark this: I have written these notes through borrowed lenses of my own. To study the enemy, first concede you could not see him.

Passive

Curse Sight

Passive · While worn: see curses as if visible, including those you could not otherwise perceive; effects requiring sight can target them.

The lenses do not sharpen the eye; they lend it what it lacks. Wear them, and the invisible stands revealed, and what is revealed may be aimed at, as surely as if the eye had always seen it. While you wear the glasses, you can see curses as though they were visible to you, including curses you otherwise would not be able to perceive. Effects and attacks that require you to see your target can target curses you perceive through the lenses.

Campaign canon notes

Table function: the source only says the glasses let you see curses you could not otherwise see. At the table this is treated as granting effective sight of the curse, a curse revealed through the lenses can be targeted as if visible.
