Core law

Every strand has two honest endpoints.

Bungee Gum does not create a separate point pool. Creating a strand spends CE once. Its position, resting length, anchors, and remaining duration are the complete ledger.

Level 1 · 1 CE

Cast a Strand

Each strand has two endpoints. One endpoint must begin on you, an object you hold, or a creature, object, or surface you touch. Place the other on the same contact, on an attack’s target, or on a target within Gum Range using the ranged procedure below. The strand’s resting length begins at the current distance between its endpoints.

A strand lasts for the duration in the progression table, requires no Technique Focus, and may be dismissed at any time during your turn without an action. There is no active-strand cap: CE, time, range, and physical anchors are the limits.

Contact or attack delivery

Every hit can leave a string

After you hit with any weapon, unarmed strike, or technique attack, spend 1 CE to attach one strand between the target and you, the striking weapon, or the projectile. Attaching occurs after the hit resolves, adds no damage, and requires no additional action. You may also attach once as part of any action, bonus action, or reaction in which you deliberately touch a willing creature or unattended object.

Alternatively, as a bonus action, spend 1 CE and make a ranged CE attack against an unwilling creature or attended object within Gum Range. On a hit, attach it to you or an object you hold. A willing or unattended target needs no roll.

Bonus action · contraction

Elastic Recall

Choose one perceived strand and shorten its resting length by up to the listed Contraction, minimum 0 feet. Choose one movable endpoint. A willing creature or unattended object moves toward the other endpoint; an unwilling creature makes a Strength save and moves on a failure. If the chosen endpoint cannot move, the other endpoint moves when possible. Forced movement from Recall does not provoke opportunity attacks.

On a successful save, that contraction does not shorten the strand. On your turn, you may instead increase a strand’s resting length up to its current separation without an action, relaxing it without moving either endpoint.

Elastic collision

Distance becomes impact, not a counter

If Recall drives a creature into a solid obstruction or another creature, it takes one Gum Die of bludgeoning damage per 10 feet it actually moved, maximum PB dice. A struck creature makes a Dexterity save, taking the same damage on failure or half on success. One Recall command damages each creature at most once, regardless of how many strands contributed.

Stopping short is allowed. A strand can be used entirely for positioning, disarming, restraint, or movement without dealing damage.

Equipment handling

Return what you throw

If a thrown weapon, piece of ammunition, or Tiny object remains joined to you by a strand after its attack, return it to an empty hand at the end of that attack without an action. If a strand is attached to an object another creature holds, use Elastic Recall against the object; its carrier makes the Strength save, dropping the object on a failure. A worn object pulls its wearer instead.

Endpoint handling

Pass the gum without rebuilding it

Once during each of your turns, move one endpoint you touch between your body and a willing creature, object, or surface you also touch without an action. This does not change its resting length. You may release any number of your endpoints without an action during your turn. Releasing either endpoint ends that strand unless you transfer it to another touched anchor as part of the same handling.

This is how a line first attached to your hand becomes a wall-to-wall trap, a weapon leash, a rescue harness, or a concealed trip line. Destroying an anchor likewise ends that endpoint; the strand ends unless its user can immediately transfer it through a feature.

Core example: a strand joins an enemy 25 feet away to the user. Its resting length is 25 feet. Elastic Recall shortens it by 15 feet; on a failed Strength save, the enemy is pulled 15 feet. If the enemy strikes a stone pillar after moving 10 feet, the remaining movement ends and the collision deals 1 Gum Die.

Perception

Ordinary gum is readable

A creature with CE perception can see an unconcealed strand as glossy aura. A creature without it cannot see the aura itself but can discover it by contact, displaced dust, moving anchors, or its physical effects.

Suppression

Limp is not gone

While the technique is suppressed, strands become slack, cannot contract, impose no tether or condition, and cannot deal damage. Their durations continue. Technique Extermination or an effect that removes a technique ends them if that effect says it removes ongoing manifestations.

Line of effect

The strand traces a real path

A strand cannot pass through total cover when created. Closing a door on one does not automatically cut it; the strand bends around an edge if its endpoints still have a physical route and the resulting length remains legal.

No abstract tension tokens: a player never gains or spends “stretch points.” Use actual distance moved, actual endpoints, and the contraction value printed for the character’s level.

Combat applications

The line is setup and payoff.

These features reward weapon users, unarmed fighters, ranged attackers, rescuers, and battlefield schemers equally. Bungee Gum supplies geometry; the character supplies style.

Level 3 · once per turn

Elastic Footwork

When you use Elastic Recall with yourself as the moving endpoint, you may perform that one Recall without an action. Move up to Contraction toward the other endpoint, including vertically, and do not provoke opportunity attacks during that movement. You still need a legal strand and anchor; this does not create free flight or let you pass through obstacles.

A strand attached to your feet can also support your weight on a wall or ceiling, giving you a climbing speed equal to your walking speed while that endpoint remains attached.

Level 3 · reaction

Reel-In Counter

When your Elastic Recall moves a hostile creature into the reach of a weapon you wield or your unarmed strike, make one attack with that weapon or strike. On a hit, add one Gum Die per 10 feet that creature moved during this Recall, maximum PB dice. The momentum dice are part of the attack and are declared before damage.

This attack may critically hit and qualify for Black Flash. The reaction occurs after the movement but before any collision damage; if the target is reduced to 0 HP, unresolved physical movement and collisions still finish.

Level 3 · reaction · 2 CE

Elastic Guard

When an attack hits you or you take damage from a source you can perceive, interpose a sheet of gum and reduce that instance by PB Gum Dice + CE modifier, minimum 0. If the damage becomes 0 and its physical weapon or projectile reaches you, catch it with an endpoint at no additional CE cost.

An energy beam, mental effect, poison already inside you, suffocation, or other source with nothing physical to catch can still be reduced when appropriate, but it leaves no object to anchor.

Level 3 · +1 CE

Hidden Gum

When you create a strand, spend 1 additional CE to conceal it. Passive CE perception does not notice that strand. A creature can reveal it with an action and a Wisdom (Perception) or CE ability check against your CE save DC; touching it reveals it to that creature automatically. Once the strand visibly moves a creature or deals damage, it is revealed until the end of the current turn.

Level 6 · bonus action

Many-Threaded Hand

When you use Elastic Recall as a bonus action, choose up to PB perceived strands instead of one and set a contraction for each. Resolve them in an order you choose. One creature affected by several chosen strands makes one Strength save against the entire command; success resists every chosen contraction targeting it, and failure allows each to resolve.

Calculate collision damage from the creature’s total movement during that command, once, maximum PB Gum Dice. Separate commands on later turns remain separate damage instances.

Level 6 · passive setup

Layered Adhesion

A creature held by two taut strands leading to different immovable environmental anchors is grappled. Three such strands restrain it instead. Your body, a creature, an object someone is carrying, and several points on the same loose object do not count as different immovable anchors.

The conditions end immediately when too few qualifying strands remain taut. More than three strands do not worsen Restrained. The normal peel, attack, suppression, teleportation, and endpoint procedures remain available.

Level 6 · bonus action · 2 CE

Gum Suture

Touch yourself or a willing creature. End one nonmagical bleeding effect, stabilize the target if it has 0 HP, or support one injured limb for 1 minute. A supported limb’s injury severity counts as one step lower for penalties, minimum minor, but its HP, maximum HP, and actual injury do not change.

This is not healing or RCT. It cannot reattach a fully severed limb, repair an organ, remove exhaustion, or revive the dead.

Level 11 · Elastic Guard rider

Return to Sender

When Elastic Guard reduces a physical ranged attack’s damage to 0 and catches its projectile, immediately make a ranged CE attack against a creature within Gum Range as part of the same reaction. On a hit, it takes PB Gum Dice + CE modifier bludgeoning or force damage, your choice.

The returned attack is yours, not the original attacker’s. It can critically hit and qualify for Black Flash, but it does not copy the original attack’s damage, conditions, spell level, ammunition enchantment, or target riders.

Level 11 · action · 5 CE

Gum Prosthesis

Create a functional gum replacement for one missing or destroyed exterior body part you can reach: a hand, arm, foot, leg, ear, nose, jaw section, or similar feature. It lasts 1 hour, removes the ordinary mechanical penalties of that missing part, and can hold equipment, walk, climb, and make attacks as the original part could.

It does not restore HP, maximum HP, sensation, a destroyed organ, proficiency, an extra action, or biological function beyond the replaced exterior part. Damage to it is damage to you. Suppression makes it limp; if removal would make you fall or drop something, resolve that immediately.

Level 18 · once per turn

All Strings at Once

Once during each of your turns, use Elastic Recall on any number of your perceived strands without an action. Each affected creature still makes only one Strength save against that command, and each takes collision damage at most once from it. You may then use your bonus action for another legal feature, including Many-Threaded Hand.

These are distinct commands and may both move the same creature if their saves fail. Same-name Grappled and Restrained conditions still do not stack.

Weapon freedom: Bungee Gum grants no playing cards and requires none. Swords, polearms, firearms, bows, thrown weapons, shields, gauntlets, improvised objects, unarmed strikes, and technique attacks can all deliver a strand when they hit.

Utility branch

Texture Surprise is a surface-level lie.

It is deliberately inexpensive. The power changes what a surface appears and superficially feels like; it never receives the structural properties of the depicted substance.

Level 1 · action

Apply Texture Surprise

Touch a thin, smooth, or flat surface and manifest one static appearance you can clearly imagine. Up to 1 square foot costs no CE. A larger image costs 1 CE for each complete or partial 5-foot-by-5-foot panel. It lasts until you dismiss it, peel it away, rewrite it, or the supporting surface is destroyed. It uses no Technique Focus and has no active-image cap.

You may reproduce colors, text, pictures, tattoos, skin, cloth, wood, stone, metal, blood, wounds, credentials, maps, or another nonmagical surface. Fine detail is allowed; false information remains false.

What it changes

Appearance and superficial feel

The layer may look and feel like the depicted surface at a light touch. It does not change shape, weight, temperature, hardness, flexibility, scent, sound, durability, chemical composition, CE signature, or function. Paper disguised as steel still bends and tears like paper; gum disguised as skin still stretches like gum.

The layer cannot animate, emit light, create depth beyond the supporting form, speak, supply a password it does not know, produce a working seal, counterfeit an active cursed effect, heal a wound, or restore a body part.

Discovery

A good lie survives casual sight

A creature with no reason to doubt the surface accepts the image. A creature that takes an action to inspect or physically manipulates it makes Intelligence (Investigation) or a CE ability check against your CE save DC. Give advantage when the supporting material behaves inconsistently or the inspector knows what is false.

Forgery

Knowledge still matters

You can reproduce only details you know or invent. Texture Surprise can copy a document you studied, alter visible writing, or hide a mark; it cannot guess a serial number, encode unknown information, create legal authority, or stop another system from validating the underlying object.

Bodies

Disguise is not medicine

A patch of cloth can appear to be skin and hide an injury. It does not close the wound. At level 11, Gum Canvas supplies a three-dimensional supporting form for missing features and broader disguises.

Level 6 · bonus action

Quick Change

Once during each of your turns, apply, remove, or rewrite one Texture Surprise layer no larger than 1 square foot as a bonus action. Use this to alter a card, label, wound patch, insignia, face detail, small section of clothing, or written message in combat. Larger panels still require the normal action and CE.

Level 11 · action · 2 CE

Gum Canvas

For 1 hour, Bungee Gum counts as a valid surface and can be sculpted beneath Texture Surprise into a creature-sized curved form. Use one or more existing strands, or pay their normal creation cost in addition to this feature. You can disguise one willing creature, complete a Gum Prosthesis, reshape clothing, add apparent hair or facial structure, or create a false object no larger than that creature and anchored by at least one strand.

The gum supplies shape and movement but not true anatomy or material properties. An observer contests the disguise using the normal discovery rule. This is not a general illusion and cannot create a separate moving creature.

Level 18

Perfect Surface

Your Texture Surprise reproduces minute tactile surface detail and does not automatically reveal itself through ordinary touch or passive CE perception. Physical behavior can still betray the substrate, and an active Investigation or CE ability check uses the normal DC. You may apply Quick Change without an action once per turn instead of as a bonus action.

Perfect Surface does not upgrade the lie into transmutation, healing, invisibility, mind control, or reality alteration. Its power is that a mechanically simple false surface becomes extremely convincing in the hands of a prepared player.

Common uses: alter a letter, fake a tattoo, conceal a severed or prosthetic feature, repaint a trap, hide a strand’s anchor beneath matching wallpaper, disguise a weapon as a harmless object of the same shape, or make the real exit resemble the wall.

Level 11 · named Maximum

Maximum: Grand Cat’s Cradle.

A battlefield-sized lattice knots itself between temporary anchor points, turning a wide area into one elastic compression engine.

Action · 10 CE · 120 feet · Technique Focus

Build the lattice

Choose a point within 120 feet. For up to 1 minute, a 40-foot-radius, 40-foot-high cylinder centered there fills with Bungee Gum. The Maximum creates its own anchors along the boundary and does not require convenient terrain. When it forms, choose any creatures to be unaffected; you are always unaffected. Those creatures ignore the lattice’s difficult terrain and restraint.

Every other creature in the area makes a Dexterity save. On a failure, three taut Maximum strands attach it to separate boundary anchors and it is restrained. On a success, one strand attaches and the area is difficult terrain for it. An unprotected creature entering the area for the first time on a turn makes the same save.

Bonus action · compression

Close the cradle

While maintaining the Maximum, use a bonus action to choose any number of hostile creatures attached to its lattice. Each makes a Strength save, taking (PB + 2) Gum Dice + CE modifier bludgeoning damage and moving up to 30 feet toward the center on failure, or half damage without movement on success. A creature reduced to 0 HP is suspended rather than automatically killed.

Lattice integrity

Cut the whole knot

The lattice has AC equal to your CE save DC and HP equal to 20 × PB. It uses the normal strand immunities and resistance. Area damage dealt inside damages the shared lattice once per activation, not once per line. At 0 HP the Maximum ends. A creature can also use the normal peel action to remove its own Maximum strands; they reform at the start of its next turn while it remains inside. All Maximum strands on a creature end when it leaves the cylinder.

Named Maximum: Grand Cat’s Cradle cannot critically hit, trigger Black Flash, receive generic Maximum Output, or combine with Chanting. Compression is one saving-throw damage instance each time it is commanded, regardless of the illustrated number of lines.

Level 18 · Domain Expansion

The Magician’s False Stage.

A lightless theater opens around an impossible performance space. Painted doors lead into walls, false floors hide open air, and every chosen opponent discovers that the scenery has already stuck to them.

Expansion

Raise the curtain

As a full-turn action, spend 20 CE to form an enclosed 60-foot-radius Domain centered on you for 1 minute. Choose creatures to bring inside using the campaign’s complete Domain rules. Barrier defenses, clashes, refinement, anti-domain techniques, and Technique Burnout function normally.

Infinite anchors

The set is part of the technique

Every surface, prop, and point along the barrier is a valid immovable anchor. Your Gum Range covers the Domain, you perceive all your strands inside it, and creating or contracting a strand does not require line of effect through the false scenery.

Sole sure-hit event

Violent Applause owns initiative 20

The Domain guarantees only its Violent Applause event at initiative count 20. Loaded fists, ordinary strands, Reel-In Counter, Return to Sender, and every other attack or saving throw resolve normally. The stage improves positioning and access; it does not turn the user’s whole character sheet into automatic success.

Prepared set

The scenery begins wired

When the Domain forms, create up to three times PB strands between Domain anchors without an action or CE. They cannot initially attach to creatures, do not count against strand capacity, and vanish with the barrier. They are ordinary strands afterward and may be contracted, cut, hidden, or used for movement normally.

False scenery

Every surface can lie

Once during each of your turns without an action, apply or rewrite Texture Surprise across one surface no larger than a 20-foot cube inside the Domain. It can disguise passages, hazards, strands, creatures against scenery, or the barrier’s apparent location, but it still does not change physical properties.

Stage movement

Allies know the wires

Once on each of their turns, you and each chosen ally may move up to 60 feet along a path between Domain anchors without spending movement or provoking opportunity attacks. This is gum-assisted movement, not teleportation, and cannot pass through solid barriers.

Initiative 20 · Violent Applause · sure-hit

The theater snaps shut

Each chosen hostile creature automatically takes 2 × PB Gum Dice + CE modifier bludgeoning or force damage. Three Domain strands then attach it to distinct anchors, move it up to 60 feet along a path you choose, knock it prone, and restrain it through Layered Adhesion. The three new strands replace any strands created for that creature by the previous Violent Applause, do not count against capacity, and otherwise use normal strand statistics.

A creature protected from the sure-hit makes a Strength save. On a failure it suffers the complete result; on a success it takes half damage and receives no strands, movement, prone, or restraint. This event costs no CE and does not consume ordinary attached strands.

Domain restraint is not paralysis: restrained creatures can attack, use actions, damage strands, expand a Domain, or activate an anti-domain feature. Between initiative-20 events, every strand and technique attack follows its ordinary rolls and counterplay.

Level 18 · postmortem application

Postmortem Command: Encore.

The user gives the gum one last instruction while alive: if the heart and lungs stop, contract. It is a prepared physical resuscitation, not immortality or soul restoration.

Full-turn action · 15 CE · 1 hour

Set the command

Wrap dormant Bungee Gum around your heart and lungs and name death as its trigger. You may have only one Encore command. It requires no Technique Focus and remains after you become unconscious or die. Recasting replaces the prior command without refunding its CE.

Trigger

The body moves one turn later

When you die, the command triggers. At the end of the turn you would take next, if your brain, heart, and enough torso remain physically present within 5 feet of one another, the gum restarts circulation and respiration. You return to life with PB Gum Dice + CE modifier HP, minimum 1, and Technique Burnout until the end of your following turn.

The command ends whether it succeeds or fails. It has no per-rest limit; using it again requires surviving long enough to take another full-turn action and paying 15 CE again.

Failure boundaries

There must be someone to restart

Encore fails if the body is disintegrated, the brain or heart is destroyed or absent, the soul is destroyed or captured, death is enforced by an effect that expressly prevents revival, or Bungee Gum is removed before the trigger resolves. Suppression delays resolution until the end of the next turn after suppression ends, provided the one-hour duration has not expired.

Severed exterior limbs do not prevent revival. After returning, Gum Prosthesis and Texture Surprise may replace and disguise what remains missing.

What Encore is not: it does not heal the corpse to full, restore maximum HP, regenerate organs, remove injuries, refund CE, end other conditions, defeat soul damage, or guarantee that enemies leave the body intact during the response window.

Rules reference

Resolve the trick without argument.

These rulings connect strands, attacks, saves, collisions, Maximum Output, Black Flash, movement, and multiclass action economy to the finalized core guide.

Technique math

Normal CE statistics

CE attack = PB + CE modifier
CE save DC = 8 + PB + CE modifier

A weapon or unarmed attack retains its normal statistic. Attaching after that hit does not replace its attack roll.

Gum Dice

Level changes the die

The Gum Die is d6 at levels 1–5, d8 at 6–10, d10 at 11–17, and d12 at 18–20. Distance, PB, or the feature determines how many dice are rolled.

No Reversal

Simple does not mean incomplete

Bungee Gum has no Cursed Technique Reversal. Texture Surprise is a companion application of the forward technique, not positive-energy inversion. RCT does not automatically create solvent, anti-adhesion, or rigid crystal gum.

Criticals and Black Flash

The strike carries its momentum

Reel-In Counter’s momentum dice and Return to Sender’s direct attack can critically hit. When part of a critical, their dice double with the attack. After a qualifying critical, roll the second unmodified d20; a natural 20 makes Black Flash and triples the entire completed critical damage. There is no frequency cap.

What cannot Black Flash

Movement is not a direct strike

Elastic collision damage, Elastic Guard reduction, Grand Cat’s Cradle, Violent Applause, attachment attacks that deal no damage, peeling, and other saving-throw effects cannot critically hit or trigger Black Flash. An ordinary weapon hit can qualify normally even if it also attaches a strand.

Maximum Output: strand

Tag one creation

Declare Maximum Output when creating a 1-CE strand and pay 2 CE instead. Range increases both its creation range and maximum length by half. Duration increases its normal duration by half. Accuracy adds half the original CE attack bonus, rounded up, to its attachment attack; if no attack was required, it instead increases the DC of the next contraction save it causes by half PB, rounded up, then ends. Damage adds half the original Gum Dice, rounded up, to the next collision or Reel-In momentum pool directly caused by that strand.

Output boundaries

One strand, one chosen output

If several Maximum Output strands contribute to one command, only one same-name Damage increase applies. Touch remains touch. Texture Surprise’s free layer has no meaningful cost to double and receives no output benefit. Maximum Output cannot enhance Grand Cat’s Cradle, the Domain, Encore, RCT, or Black Flash itself.

Strand overlaps

Control stacks; collision does not multiply

Several strands may attach to the same target and can establish Layered Adhesion. One command rolls collision damage once per creature from its total movement, not once per strand. A later distinct command may damage it again.

Forced movement

No opportunity attacks

Recall, Maximum compression, Violent Applause, and gum-assisted movement are forced unless the moving creature spends its own movement. Forced movement causes no opportunity attacks but can still encounter hazards and solid collisions.

Teleportation

A pathless exit leaves gum behind

Teleportation does not stretch a strand through intervening space. Unless the teleport feature carries attached effects, that endpoint peels free at the departure space. A Domain or feature that forbids teleportation wins.

Objects and creatures

Adhesion is not body control

A strand moves an endpoint and whatever physically carries it. It cannot puppet speech, thoughts, internal organs, fine hand movements, or another technique. Pulling armor moves its wearer; pulling a weapon can disarm.

Gum and cover

Flexible is not incorporeal

Strands bend around legal edges but do not pass through total cover, sealed barriers, or creatures without a feature saying so. A hidden strand is still physical and can be found by contact.

Actions and multiclassing

Printed timing remains real

Attachment after a hit requires no additional action but still costs 1 CE. Elastic Footwork and All Strings at Once are their own once-per-turn exceptions. Distinct additional actions, attacks, reactions, and damage riders from classes or feats may stack unless one expressly forbids it.

Grapples and restraint

Layered Adhesion is its own source

Its Grappled or Restrained condition does not combine into a stronger condition with an ordinary grapple, though both sources can coexist. Removing one source does not remove the other. Freedom of Movement and similar features apply according to their text.

Fast table sequence

Resolve a contraction in five lines

  1. Name every strand being commanded and how much each resting length shortens.
  2. Choose which endpoint each strand attempts to move.
  3. Roll one Strength save for each unwilling creature affected by that command.
  4. Move failed creatures and willing or unattended endpoints in the chosen order.
  5. Apply Layered Adhesion, then roll one collision pool per creature from total distance actually moved.
Four Innate Technique feats

A simple property becomes a signature trick.

These optional feats specialize strand durability, pursuit, disguise, or projectile defense. Each occupies a normal feat choice, may be taken only once, and this is the complete four-feat list.

Prerequisite: Cast a Strand

Unbreakable Adhesive

Your strands have AC equal to 14 + PB and HP equal to 10 × PB. When a creature succeeds on the action used to peel your gum, it removes a maximum number of endpoints equal to half your PB, rounded up, rather than PB. Technique removal and suppression still work normally.

Prerequisite: Reel-In Counter

Snapback Counter

When a hostile creature attached to one of your strands completes at least 10 feet of voluntary movement, use your reaction and spend 1 CE to immediately use Elastic Recall on that strand. If the creature fails its save and ends within your reach, make one weapon or unarmed attack against it as part of the same reaction. On hit, add one Gum Die per 10 feet the Recall moved it, maximum PB dice.

Prerequisite: Texture Surprise

Living Texture

As an action for 1 CE, apply Texture Surprise across yourself or one willing creature you touch for 1 hour. The disguise moves with the body and can alter visible skin, hair, clothing, markings, and apparent injuries within the same general silhouette and size. It does not change voice, scent, weight, anatomy, CE signature, equipment function, or statistics and uses the normal active-investigation rule.

Prerequisite: Return to Sender, level 11

Catch the Impossible

Elastic Guard can catch a ranged spell attack, CE attack, or visible energy projectile as well as a physical ranged attack. If your reduction makes its damage 0, Return to Sender may launch a gum facsimile at a creature within Gum Range: make a ranged CE attack that deals PB Gum Dice + CE modifier force damage. It never copies the original effect, damage pool, conditions, area, spell level, or other riders.

Character guide

Skill changes the ceiling more than statistics.

The technique functions with imperfect ability scores and ordinary equipment. Strong statistics improve attacks and saves; good anchors and timing create the memorable victories.

Melee controller

Hit, attach, then change the angle

Use weapon or unarmed hits to establish lines without sacrificing attacks. Pull a target into reach on the following turn and reserve your reaction for Reel-In Counter.

Ranged trickster

Every projectile can come home

Tether knives, cards, arrows, bullets, stones, or cursed tools. A hit attaches to the target; a miss can leave a line on the environment if the projectile physically reaches it.

Rescue specialist

Movement does not need damage

Catch falling allies, draw them out of hazards, support injured limbs, build harnesses, cross gaps, and use Texture Surprise for concealment or signaling.

Deceiver

Tell one physical lie well

Texture Surprise cannot replace roleplay, forgery knowledge, or a disguise’s structure. It gives those plans a powerful surface and a clear DC when scrutiny begins.

CE economy

Preparation is prepaid

Ten hidden strands already cost 20 CE and ten legal creation opportunities. They can all matter later because the technique does not erase careful preparation with an arbitrary active cap.

Ability priorities

Choose the fighter under the technique

Wisdom or Charisma improves remote attachment, Recall saves, and deception DCs. Strength or Dexterity supports weapons and escape; Constitution protects Focus during the Maximum and Domain.

Complete combat loop: land ordinary attacks → leave endpoints behind → move or transfer your own anchors → command several contractions → let the terrain create restraint → reel one target into a decisive critical strike.

Design record

The canon rule, translated rather than expanded at random.

Licensed and official material establishes aura with both elastic and adhesive properties, close-combat attachment, projectile and movement tricks, false surfaces, reconstructed anatomy, and the postmortem command. This guide supplies JJK 5e timing and counterplay.
