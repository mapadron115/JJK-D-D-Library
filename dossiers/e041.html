Bungee Gum & Texture Surprise

A Transmuter's aura given the properties of rubber and gum, it sticks to anything, stretches impossibly far, and snaps back exactly when you tell it to. It slingshots its user across battlefields, snares the unwary mid-stride, and lies cheerfully about what it is. And when its user dies, the gum refuses to accept it: it squeezes his heart until it beats again.

Feature map

Level-by-level features

Field notes. Subject: the transmuter who weaponized a joke. Three truths govern him. First, the gum has the properties of both rubber and gum, it sticks to anything, stretches impossibly far, and snaps back exactly when he tells it to: adhesion, extension, and obedience, all in pink. Second, the trick is the technique’s honesty: Texture Surprise is a separate little lie, and the strands are a sense organ, a slingshot, a tripwire, a gag, one material, endless cruelties. Third, the gum loves its master more than death does: prosthetics knit the dying body, and if he dies, the strands squeeze his heart until it beats again. Mark the joker’s economy: he pays 1 CE a strand, and the whole battlefield becomes his playground.

L1

Bungee Gum

The founding strand: one CE, one attack roll, and the gum sticks, strands of pink aura that stretch sixty feet and obey without an action.

As an action, spend 1 CE and make a CE-ability attack roll against a creature, object, or surface you can see within 60 feet. On a hit, a strand of gum-aura sticks. You can maintain up to 3 strands. Strands stretch up to 60 feet, contract or extend at your will (no action), and detach at your will (no action). As a bonus action, contract a strand anchored to something heavier than you: pull yourself up to 30 feet toward the anchor (slingshot movement; doesn't provoke opportunity attacks), or pull a stuck creature up to 15 feet toward the anchor, an unwilling creature negates the pull with a successful Strength saving throw against your CE save DC. Sticky. Stretchy. Entirely under your control.

L3

Texture Surprise

The second trick, a separate little lie: the gum learns to lie about what it is. Stone, floor, wallpaper, it only has to fool the eyes, and it does.

The second trick, a separate little lie. As an action, spend 1 CE and touch a surface or object: change its apparent texture. Gum becomes stone, a pit mouth becomes solid floor, your skin becomes wallpaper. A creature that inspects it can see through the deception with a successful Investigation check against your CE save DC; touch alone doesn't betray it unless they're looking for it. Lasts until you dismiss it or finish a long rest. It only has to fool the eyes.

L6

Slingshot

The gum learns to fly: his slingshot movement reaches ninety feet, and willing creatures or objects may be flung sixty along a strand. Anchor overhead and gravity becomes a suggestion, no falling damage while the strand holds.

You contract harder and fly further. Your slingshot movement reaches 90 feet, and you can fling a willing creature or an object up to 60 feet along a strand, using the same bonus action as contracting the strand. A flung object becomes an improvised ranged attack: on a hit it deals 2d8 bludgeoning damage. Anchor a strand above you and contract to slow a fall, you take no falling damage while a strand is anchored overhead. Gravity is a suggestion.

L11

Web of Strings

The web becomes a sense organ: six strands, and every vibration within 120 feet is felt, size, number, direction. Nothing touching his strings can surprise him, and one bonus action contracts them all.

You maintain up to 6 strands, and the web becomes a sense organ: strands transmit vibration, so you feel anything touching any strand within 120 feet, size, number, direction. Creatures touching your web can't surprise you. You can contract any number of strands with a single bonus action. The whole battlefield, on a string.

L18

Prosthetic Gum

The gum replaces what the body lost: reaction and 3 CE when damage would drop him, and he falls to 1 instead, or 1 CE to grow a working hand, foot, or eye of pink aura for a day.

Gum replaces what the body lost. When damage would reduce you to 0 hit points, use your reaction and spend 3 CE: the gum knits you together mid-blow and you drop to 1 hit point instead. You can also spend 1 CE to grow a gum prosthetic, a working hand, foot, or eye of pink aura, restoring lost function for 24 hours. He walked out of that fight on feet made of gum.

L20

Post-Mortem Heartbeat

The gum’s final devotion: once per long rest, if he dies, the strands refuse, tightening around heart and lungs until, a minute later, he returns at half his maximum.

Once per long rest: if you die, the gum doesn't accept it. Strands tighten around your heart and lungs and squeeze them back to work, one minute later you return to life at half your hit point maximum, unless your body was destroyed. Anyone watching sees the corpse twitch as pink strands draw taut. Death is just another thing to stick to.

Pinnacle expression

Maximum and Domain.

Capstone material

Maximum: Gum Storm

The pinnacle: action, 5 CE, and for one minute gum erupts from every surface within sixty feet. Hostiles save or are restrained, repeating at their turns’ ends, and his bonus action yanks any restrained creature twenty feet. Difficult terrain for everyone but him.

As an action, spend 5 CE: for 1 minute, gum erupts from every surface within 60 feet of you. Hostile creatures in the area must succeed on a Strength saving throw against your CE save DC or be restrained by strands (they repeat the save at the end of each of their turns). As a bonus action, contract strands on any restrained creature and yank it 20 feet in any direction. The area is difficult terrain for everyone but you. Everything is gum now.

Domain Expansion: Playground of Strings

The playground closes: full turn, 20 CE, 120 feet for two minutes, and the sure-hit is the sticking itself. Every hostile inside is automatically stuck, no roll, no save, and the strands cannot be detached while the Domain stands.

18th level. Sure-hit domain, the sure-hit is the sticking. Activation: full turn, 20 CE (D008 baseline for a normal Domain). Clash: 800 points; burnout 5 rounds; duration 2 min (Tier III/IV mapping); radius 120 ft.

Nobody leaves the playground. Every hostile creature inside is automatically stuck by a strand, no attack roll, no save, and strands can't be detached while the domain stands. As a bonus action you may contract any strand: pull the stuck creature 20 feet in any direction (Strength save vs. your CE save DC negates). Your slingshot movement costs nothing extra inside your own domain.

Feats

Five feats, stuck on you.

Bungee Gum teaches five applications beyond the strands themselves. Each is optional, and you must meet its listed prerequisite before taking it.

Prerequisite: Bungee Gum

Tripwire

The patient strand: stretched across a doorway or trail and left, the instant anything crosses, he knows its size and number, and it cannot surprise him. No concentration; the strand simply holds.

Stretch a strand across a doorway, corridor, or trail and leave it: the instant anything crosses it you know its size and number, and it can't surprise you. The strand holds until detached, no concentration required.

Prerequisite: Bungee Gum

Gum Gag

The silencing strand: contracted across a stuck creature’s mouth in the same bonus action as the pull, no speech, no verbal components, until it breaks free.

Contract a strand across a stuck creature's mouth as part of the same bonus action you use to pull it: it can't speak or supply verbal components until it breaks free (an action, Strength check vs. your CE save DC). Quiet is a kind of winning.

Prerequisite: Texture Surprise

False Floor

The lie, weaponized: Texture Surprise disguises a pit, snare, or doorway as solid ground, and the first creature across must see through it or fall in.

Texture Surprise can disguise a pit, snare, or doorway as solid ground or blank wall. The first creature to cross it must succeed on an Investigation check against your CE save DC or fall in, or walk straight through a door it never saw.

Prerequisite: Bungee Gum

Card Return

The gambler’s old habit: a strand left on a thrown weapon recalls it to his hand as a bonus action from 120 feet, arcing back along the strand.

A strand left on a thrown weapon or object lets you recall it to your hand as a bonus action from up to 120 feet away, arcing back along the strand. Hisoka's cards always come back.

Prerequisite: Slingshot

Bungee Parachute

The falling clause: anchor to anything above, ceiling, branch, the shimmer in the air, and descend any distance safely; as a reaction, arrest another falling creature within sixty feet the same way.

Anchor a strand to anything above you, a ceiling, a branch, the shimmer in the air is enough, and descend any distance safely. As a reaction, you can arrest another falling creature within 60 feet the same way.

Rules integration

Edge cases and shared systems.

Campaign canon notes

Cursed Energy baseline: CE pool is based on class; CE ability is Charisma or Wisdom (player's choice). CE save DC = 8 + CE ability modifier + proficiency bonus. Regain all spent CE on a long rest.
Strands are aura: visible to anyone who can perceive cursed energy, invisible otherwise. Texture Surprise fools sight, not touch, a hand run across a disguised surface feels the truth.
Strand limits: 3 strands at L1, 6 at L11 (Web of Strings). A strand persists until you detach it, it is cut (AC 12, 10 HP, only by someone who can perceive it), or you finish a long rest.
Pulls vs. the unwilling: pulling a stuck creature always allows a Strength save vs. your CE save DC; the strand stays stuck whether the pull succeeds or not.
Prosthetic Gum: gum prosthetics restore function, not hit points; they read as aura constructs to anyone who can perceive cursed energy (no disguising them without Texture Surprise).
Post-Mortem Heartbeat: once per long rest; fails if the body is destroyed or disintegrated. The minute of "death" is real, death-triggered effects resolve before the heartbeat lands.
Domain activation defaults: rendered as full turn + 20 CE per the D008 normal-Domain baseline, with Tier III/IV clash stats (800 / 5-round burnout / 2 min) and a 120-ft radius.
Maximum vs. Reversal: Bungee Gum's pinnacle is written as a Maximum Technique (Gum Storm), not a Reversal; it occupies the technique's pinnacle slot.
