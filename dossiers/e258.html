At-the-table character reference

Recovered printed values & campaign clarifications

One pattern per action:

One pattern per action; a pattern cannot trigger on the same event twice. Invisible until activated means hidden to the untrained eye but still revealable.

Shockwave Blast save:

Shockwave Blast's knockback save is a Strength saving throw, not Dexterity, keep the source's strength-vs-strength flavor.

Stasis Hold condition:

Stasis Hold uses Stunned, not Paralyzed; speed is already 0, and the effect ends on the target's successful Strength save or at the start of the damage-taker's turn.

Linked-chain limits:

One activation consumes all linked patterns; no re-trigger. Maximum one relay hop per linked chain.

Advanced-circuit costs:

Advanced-circuit costs are additive: +2 CE atop the base 2 CE.

Field notes. Subject: the patient engineer. Three truths govern him. First, the charge is laid beforehand: two CE, an action, a touched surface, and the pattern waits, invisible, until a creature steps on it or he speaks the bonus action. Second, the circuit compounds: Advanced Circuits at the third, two more CE for effects beyond the basic; Linked Circuits, one CE to join patterns thirty feet apart, sixty at the eleventh, one-twenty at the eighteenth, so that activating one may activate another. Third, the pinnacle is the machine completing itself: Deus ex Machina, full-turn, twenty CE, sixty feet, one minute, keeps him always under his own Circuit, and at initiative count 20 drains each chosen creature’s current cursed energy as necrotic damage, no roll and no first save. Maxim: the trap is not where the enemy steps. The trap is the whole floor, patiently wired.

Full progression

Feature map

1Cursed Circuitry.

Action · 2 CE · Touch a surface within 5 ft to lay an invisible Circuit Pattern (AC 10, 2 HP; lasts until triggered or dispelled) · Trigger: bonus action when stepped on/touched, or action when a creature is within 5 ft · Choose one: Combustion (10-ft radius, Dex save, 3d6 fire); Lightning Surge (15-ft cone, Dex save, 2d8 lightning + stun on fail); Stasis Hold (10-ft radius, Str save or restrained to start of next turn); Burst Leap (up to two creatures within 10 ft propelled).

The foundation: two CE, an action, a touched surface, and the pattern waits, invisible, until stepped on or spoken awake with a bonus action. He does not fight the enemy. He wires the room and lets the enemy fight the room. To create a Circuit Pattern, you must touch a surface within 5 feet of you as an action, spending 2 cursed energy. The Circuit Pattern is invisible until activated. Circuit Patterns can be activated as a bonus action when a creature steps on or touches the circuit pattern, or as an action when any creature is within 5 feet of the circuit pattern. Once a Circuit Pattern is created, it remains until activated or dispelled. The patterns you create have 2 hit points and an AC of 10. Whenever you create a circuit, choose one of the following effects:

Combustion. When activated, the circuit pattern explodes in a 10-foot radius. All creatures within the radius must make a Dexterity saving throw, taking 3d6 fire damage on a failed save, or half as much on a successful save.

Lightning Surge. When activated, the circuit pattern releases a surge of lightning in a 15-foot cone. All creatures within the cone must make a Dexterity saving throw, taking 2d8 lightning damage on a failed save, or half as much on a successful save. A creature that fails the saving throw is also stunned until the start of their next turn.

Stasis Hold. When activated, the circuit pattern releases a pulse of cursed energy that immobilizes creatures. All creatures within a 10-foot radius must make a Strength saving throw. On a failed save, a creature is restrained until the start of its next turn.

Burst Leap. When activated, the circuit pattern releases a burst of cursed energy that propels creatures. You can choose up to two creatures within 10 feet of the circuit pattern. Those creatures are launched 15 feet in a direction of your choice.

Shockwave Blast. When activated, the circuit pattern releases a shockwave in a 20-foot line. All creatures within the line must make a Strength saving throw, taking 2d6 force damage on a failed save and being knocked back 10 feet, or half as much damage on a successful save with no knockback.

3Advanced Circuits.

Upgrade · +2 CE when creating a pattern: choose an advanced effect instead · Binding Chains (15-ft radius, Str save or restrained 1 min, repeat save at end of each turn); Energy Discharge (20-ft radius, Con save, 4d6 force, allies unaffected); Energy Drain (20-ft radius, Wis save or lose 2d6 CE, you regain half); Cursed Detonation (30-ft radius, Dex save, 6d10 force; once per long rest).

At the third, the charge diversifies: two more CE buys an advanced effect in place of the basic. The held breath learns new ways to exhale. Starting at 3rd level, whenever you create a circuit pattern, you can spend an additional 2 cursed energy to choose one of the following effects instead of the basic patterns. Like basic patterns, these can be activated as a bonus action when a creature steps on or touches the circuit pattern, or as an action when any creature is within 5 feet of the circuit pattern:

Binding Chains. When activated, spectral chains erupt from the circuit pattern, attempting to bind creatures. All creatures within a 15-foot radius must make a Strength saving throw. On a failed save, a creature is restrained for 1 minute. A restrained creature can repeat the saving throw at the end of each of its turns, ending the effect on a success.

Energy Discharge. When activated, the circuit pattern releases a burst of cursed energy. All creatures within a 20-foot radius must make a Constitution saving throw, taking 4d6 force damage on a failed save, or half as much on a successful save. Allies within the radius are unaffected.

Energy Drain. When activated, the circuit pattern drains cursed energy from creatures. All creatures within a 20-foot radius must make a Wisdom saving throw. On a failed save, a creature loses 2d6 cursed energy, and you regain cursed energy equal to half the amount drained (rounded down).

Cursed Detonation. When activated, the circuit pattern detonates in a massive explosion of cursed energy. All creatures within a 30-foot radius must make a Dexterity saving throw, taking 6d10 force damage on a failed save, or half as much on a successful save. This effect can only be used once per long rest.

6Linked Circuits.

Upgrade · 1 CE: link a new pattern to one within 30 ft (60 ft at 11th, 120 ft at 18th); activating one can activate the other.

Linked Circuits: one CE joins a new pattern to one within thirty feet, sixty at the eleventh, one-twenty at the eighteenth, so activating one may activate the other. The floor becomes a nervous system. Spend 1 cursed energy to link a new pattern to one within 30 feet; activating one can activate the other. Link range rises to 60 feet at 11th, 120 feet at 18th.

11Circuit Mastery.

Upgrades · Rapid Deployment: create patterns as a bonus action · Enhanced Circuitry: patterns deal +2d6 of the same type · Remote Activation (bonus action, 30 ft): activate a pattern without a trigger · Cursed Overclock (bonus action): spend up to 5 CE, +1d6 damage per CE for 1 min.

At the eleventh, mastery in full: overclocking, new effects, the link range grown to sixty feet. The engineer’s patience pays compound interest. At 11th level your mastery over cursed circuitry has grown. You gain the following benefits:

Rapid Deployment. You can now create circuit patterns as a bonus action instead of an action.

Enhanced Circuitry. Your circuit patterns deal an additional 2d6 damage of the same type when activated.

Remote Activation. You can activate circuit patterns within 30 feet of you as a bonus action, without needing a creature to step on or touch them.

Cursed Overclock. As a bonus action, you can spend up to 5 cursed energy to overclock a circuit pattern. For each cursed energy spent, the circuit pattern deals an additional 1d6 damage of the same type when activated. This effect lasts for 1 minute.

Recovered printed values: creating a pattern under Circuit Mastery costs 2 CE (the "15 cursed energy" line is a transcription error); Cursed Overclock's damage conversion applies once per pattern.

Pinnacle expression

Maximum, Domain, or equivalent.

Domain Expansion, Deus ex Machina: The pinnacle: Deus ex Machina. Eighteenth, full-turn, twenty CE, sixty feet, one minute, he is always under his own Circuit while it stands, and at initiative count 20 each round, creatures of his choice take necrotic damage equal to their current cursed energy, drained into the circuit, no roll and no first save. The machine completes itself. 18: full-turn action, 20 CE; 60-foot-radius enclosed Domain centered on you; 1 minute; Burnout 10 rounds. While the Domain stands, you are always under the effect of your Circuit. Sure-Hit Event, Deus ex Machina: at initiative count 20 each round, creatures of your choice inside take necrotic damage equal to their current cursed energy as the Domain saps it into the circuit, with no attack roll and no initial save. A creature protected by an anti-domain defense makes a Constitution saving throw vs your CE save DC (invented); on a success it takes half damage.

Innate Technique Feats

Build-defining options.

These are optional character-building feats. You must meet each listed prerequisite before selecting it.

Prerequisite: Must have Cursed Circuitry

Dead-Man's Circuit

He may key a pattern to his own vitals, if he falls unconscious or dies, the circuit fires regardless of its condition. The engineer’s final contingency: even his corpse keeps an appointment. You may key a circuit pattern to your own vital signs. When you set the pattern, you may choose for it to trigger if you fall unconscious or die, even if its normal activation condition is never met.

Prerequisite: Must have Advanced Circuits

Circuit Chain Reaction

Two CE on activation: a pattern within twenty feet fires too, regardless of its condition. One held breath wakes the next; the room exhales all at once. When a circuit pattern is activated, you can spend 2 cursed energy to cause a chain reaction. Choose a circuit pattern within 20 feet of the activated pattern. That pattern is also activated, even if its activation condition is not met. You can use this feature a number of times equal to your proficiency bonus per long rest.

Prerequisite: Must have Linked Circuits

Living Circuitry

Ten CE, an action, one minute: his own body infused, unarmed strikes carrying d6s of lightning and more besides. When the floor is not enough, he becomes the circuit. You have learned to infuse your own body with cursed circuitry. As an action, you can spend 10 cursed energy to gain the following benefits for 1 minute: your unarmed strikes deal an additional 1d6 lightning damage; you gain resistance to lightning damage; you can use Burst Leap on yourself as a bonus action without needing a circuit pattern. At 11th level, you can spend 15 cursed energy instead to also gain immunity to being stunned.

Prerequisite: Must have Circuit

Closed Circuit

Efficiency, internalized: he leaves no cursed-energy residuals, and detection checks against him are made at disadvantage. The most patient trap is the one nobody knows is laid. Your technique specializing in energy efficiency has begun to develop your body. You no longer leave residuals of your cursed energy and creatures have disadvantage on checks to directly detect you through your cursed energy. As an action, you can concentrate for up to 1 minute to hide your cursed energy within your circuit, giving anyone a -5 to their skill checks to directly sense your cursed energy.

Rules integration

Edge cases and shared systems.

Rules priority: the reviewed rulings, explicit codex clarifications, and the level progression always control when original wording conflicts. Legacy Domain auto-hit language, action terminology, event timing, missing saves, and older scaling never override revised player rules.
Why Tier II: a trap-layer controller with additive pattern options, chain activations, and a no-cost Domain field, strong area denial without calamity output.
Hold, base pattern duration: the base Circuit Pattern's duration before level 6 is not supplied; patterns persist "until activated or dispelled," so a base duration hold does not block play.
Hold, Overclock reserve profile: the audit excerpt supplies Literal Apex and Converted scaling profiles for Domain/Overclock reserves without selecting one. No profile is chosen here; the flag is preserved for the campaign.
Circuit Trap Expert: the source names Stasis Hold "restrained," and the audit maps the feat to Stunned, the clarification controls; the feat grants disadvantage on saves against the Stasis Hold pattern.
Circuit Chain Reaction: "amplify" reads as +2 damage, not doubling.
Living Circuitry: the 10 CE and 15 CE options are alternatives, not stackable; no double-dipping. It is personal-only, so Circuit Mastery does not apply to it.

Domain Expansion: Full Circuit Integration

Light etches itself across the floor in lines too straight for nature, a mandala of copper and blue fire that hums at the edge of hearing. The patterns branch and rejoin like veins in a leaf, or like the streets of a city seen from a great height, and sparks walk the lines without being called. The world has been wired, and everything in it is current.

At 18th level, you have mastered your technique, allowing you to
expand your domain. When you activate your domain, you can choose to
place any number of circuit patterns within the domain area. These
circuit patterns are created instantly and do not require cursed
energy to create. At initiative 20, all circuit patterns within your
domain are activated.
