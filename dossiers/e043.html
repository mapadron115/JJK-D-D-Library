Malicious Radiance

A calamity-grade dual engraving: shape light into roads, fortresses, and impossible barrages, then give every photon the tangible weight of a deliberate death. At its limit, the user crosses a battlefield faster than their own body can survive.

One player option; two real techniques.

Malicious Radiance is offered as one Tier V choice, but Light and Tangible Killing Intent remain distinct engravings for copying, reversal, and adaptation. The ceiling is deliberately comparable to Ten Shadows, Shrine, and Limitless: it is not balanced by pretending the calamity cannot happen.

Core law

Light supplies form. Intent supplies death.

The technique uses ordinary CE and one designated victim. There are no Malice points, photon counters, charges, or escalating stance tracks.

Murderous Designation

When initiative is rolled, you may designate one hostile creature you can perceive without an action. At any other time, use a bonus action to designate one creature you can perceive within 300 feet. The designation lasts 1 minute, requires no Technique Focus, and ends if you dismiss it, designate another creature, or the creature leaves your plane.

Once on each of your turns when the designated creature is reduced to 0 HP or dies, you may designate another creature you can perceive without an action. Designation itself imposes no condition and permits no save.

Tangible Intent · On hit · 1 CE

Killing Edge

Once per turn after you hit your designated creature with an attack carrying your cursed energy, but before damage is rolled, spend 1 CE. Add a number of Intent Dice equal to your PB as force damage. This is part of the triggering attack, doubles on a critical hit, and belongs to the completed critical damage if the attack becomes a Black Flash.

A cursed tool, CE attack, reinforced weapon, unarmed strike, or natural weapon can carry your CE. A wholly mundane, unreinforced attack cannot.

Light · Replaces one attack · 1+ CE

Light Lance

When you take the Attack action, replace one attack with a Light Lance. Spend from 1 CE up to PB CE and make a ranged CE attack against a target within 150 feet, with long range 600 feet. On a hit, deal (1 + CE spent) Light Dice + CE modifier radiant damage.

At level 11, the range becomes 300/1,200 feet and the spending ceiling becomes twice PB. Light Lance is an attack and may critically hit or become a Black Flash.

Light · Action · 1–PB CE

Luminous Architecture

Create one hard-light segment per CE spent in unoccupied spaces you can see within 60 feet. Each segment is no larger than 10 feet long, 5 feet wide, and 1 foot thick; it may float, take any orientation, connect to another segment, and support creatures and objects. Creation cannot overlap or immediately move, crush, or damage a creature.

A segment lasts 1 minute without Technique Focus, has AC 15 and HP equal to five times your level, and is immune to poison, psychic, and radiant damage. It is transparent but solid and supplies cover according to its shape. Maintain up to PB segments; creating excess segments replaces existing ones of your choice.

Technique mathematics

Use the campaign’s CE statistics

CE attack = PB + CE modifier
CE save DC = 8 + PB + CE modifier

Choose Wisdom or Charisma as the technique’s CE ability when you gain Malicious Radiance.

Harmless expression

Light without violence

Without an action or CE, you may emit, dim, color, or extinguish harmless light from yourself in a radius up to 30 feet. It cannot blind, damage, reveal invisibility, dispel darkness, or reproduce sunlight unless a feature says otherwise.

Intent is specific

No hatred requirement

The technique needs a declared lethal intention, not anger, cruelty, alignment, or a promise that the character must ultimately kill. A calm protector can designate a mortal threat and may later choose mercy. The declaration is sufficient; the table does not interrogate the player’s real emotions or hidden psychology.

The combined attack: designate a victim → replace an attack with Light Lance → hit → declare Killing Edge before damage → roll radiant and force together. It exposes a target to both engravings and makes both damage pools part of the same critical hit.

Predator’s Certainty

You know the direction and distance to your designated creature while it is within 1 mile on the same plane. Invisibility and ordinary light or darkness do not prevent you from perceiving it for this technique. Total cover still blocks paths and attacks.

Impaling Intent

After Killing Edge deals damage, the target makes a Strength save. On failure, choose to knock it prone, push it up to 30 feet, or restrain it with a tangible spike until the end of its next turn. It may use an action to repeat the save, ending restraint on success.

All-Angle Emission

A Light Lance may originate from you or one active hard-light segment. Pillar Constellation beams may also originate from unoccupied points you perceive within 30 feet of your designated creature. Determine range, line, and cover from each origin.

Refracted Body

When you are targeted by an attack or included in an area you perceive, before its roll or saving throw, use your reaction and spend 2 CE to use Luminous Passage for up to 30 feet. Recalculate the attack’s range, reach, line, and cover from your new space. If you leave the area, you are not affected; otherwise the effect resolves normally.

Advanced applications

The sky becomes an execution frame.

These procedures complete the barrage, construct, movement, and Maximum interactions without introducing another currency.

Pillar Constellation · Attack sequence

One action, several real attacks

Choose every target and origin as each beam is resolved. A beam lost to an illegal target, destroyed origin, or blocked line does not cancel later beams. The activation is not the Attack action, so features granting attacks specifically when taking the Attack action do not add attacks.

Each beam makes its own attack and critical roll. Add your CE modifier to one successful beam only. Killing Edge can join one beam normally, or several beams after Unbounded Intent.

Luminous Passage · Movement

Travel, not teleportation

The passage is visible directed movement. It ignores difficult terrain and does not use your speed, but cannot pass through total cover or a solid object. Effects can respond only if their trigger and timing permit; opportunity attacks are expressly prevented.

A grappled or restrained creature can use Luminous Passage unless an effect prevents technique activation. The movement ends a grapple when it carries you beyond the grappler’s reach.

Hard-light statistics

Transparent does not mean permeable

A wall can provide half, three-quarters, or total cover based on how much of a creature it physically blocks, even though creatures can see through it. A segment cannot be worn as armor or created around a creature. Several connected segments are still destroyed and replaced individually.

Impaling Intent · Control

One save after the damage

Impaling Intent is a rider of Killing Edge, not a second hit. Legendary Resistance can change its failed save. Forced movement stops at the last legal space and causes no collision damage unless another feature supplies it.

Level 11 · Action · 10 CE · CE score 20

Maximum: Malicious Aurora

Choose a point you perceive within 600 feet. An aurora of intersecting, murderously weighted light fills a 60-foot-radius, 120-foot-high cylinder centered there. Choose any number of creatures in the area. Each makes a Dexterity save against your CE save DC.

On a failure, a creature takes 10 Light Dice radiant + PB Intent Dice force damage and is restrained by luminous pillars until the end of its next turn. On success, it takes half damage and is not restrained. Unattended objects, structures, and barriers take maximum damage. The aurora is instantaneous, needs no Technique Focus, and is a named Maximum Technique: generic Maximum Output and Chanting cannot enhance it.

Localized aurora: the enormous colors are a byproduct of innumerable lances interacting with the atmosphere. They do not persist as weather or create a continuing damage zone after the Maximum resolves.

Rare Cursed Technique Reversal

Darkness: Black Sun

Light emits energy and carries the user outward. Its reversal removes radiance and motion into one collapsing point. This is included because Dabura canonically discovers it—not because every technique receives a reversal.

Requirements

Positive energy and a breakthrough

You must possess Reverse Cursed Technique and gain Cursed Technique Reversal through its feat, a class feature, or a GM-approved breakthrough. Darkness is never granted automatically by reaching a Malicious Radiance level. It reverses Light only; Tangible Killing Intent has no written reversal.

Action · 8 CE

Form the Black Sun

Choose a point you perceive within 300 feet. A 30-foot-radius sphere of absolute darkness forms there until the start of your next turn. You can see through it; other creatures treat it as magical darkness. It extinguishes illumination but does not automatically dispel or suppress the underlying effects that created that light.

Collapse

Emission becomes absorption

Each creature other than you in the sphere when it forms makes a Strength save. On a failure, it takes 12 Light Dice + CE modifier force damage, is pulled to the nearest open space at the center, and is restrained until the sphere vanishes. On success, it takes half damage and is neither moved nor restrained. A creature too large to fit at the center is moved as close as possible.

Malicious Black Sun

Combine both engravings

When Black Sun forms, you may spend 1 additional CE to apply tangible killing intent to your designated creature inside it. Add PB Intent Dice to that creature’s force damage, full on a failed save and half on success. This counts as one Killing Edge use that turn but is not an attack and cannot critically hit or Black Flash.

Maximum Output

Black Sun is an ordinary reversal activation

Maximum Output doubles its full activation cost, including the optional 1 CE for Malicious Black Sun, and may improve range, damage, save DC, or duration normally. Extending duration prolongs darkness and restraint but does not repeat Collapse damage.

Adaptation

Darkness is a new phenomenon

For an adaptation feature, Black Sun is the reversed Darkness phenomenon—not another exposure to Light. A creature adapted to Light is not automatically adapted to Darkness, matching why a reversal can reopen a battle after ordinary light has been solved.

Canon boundary: the source material reveals that positive energy reverses Light into a mass of darkness, but the attack is interrupted before its finished effect is demonstrated. Black Sun’s pull, damage, and restraint are this campaign’s explicit mechanical interpretation.

Level 18 · Full-turn action · 15 CE

Sub-Light Cataclysm

True light speed remains beyond mortal structure. The technique approaches it anyway, turning the air into a wall, the city into shrapnel, and the user’s limbs into the first casualties.

Declare the course

One mile of unobstructed intent

Choose a destination you perceive within 1 mile and draw a straight, 20-foot-wide path from your space to it. You cross the path and appear in an unoccupied space at the destination without provoking opportunity attacks. No creature can use a reaction in response to this activation or movement unless its feature expressly says it can respond before unperceivable or instantaneous movement resolves.

Atmospheric cataclysm

The path detonates

Every other creature in the path makes a Dexterity save, taking 20d12 force damage and falling prone on failure, or taking half damage without falling prone on success. Unattended objects, structures, and barriers take maximum damage. Resolve obstructions from nearest to farthest; if one survives, you stop in the last legal space before it and the remaining path is lost.

One warrior’s strike

Attack inside the flash

At one point during the course, make one melee weapon or unarmed attack with advantage against a creature whose space the path reaches. On a hit, add 20d12 force damage. You may apply Killing Edge to this attack without CE and without counting against its per-turn limit. The target also resolves Atmospheric Cataclysm normally; these are distinct damage sources.

This is a rolled attack. It can critically hit and attempt Black Flash. The line’s saving-throw damage cannot.

Endpoint

Reality catches up

After every target and obstruction is resolved, place yourself at the destination or the stopping point. The movement does not grant flight afterward. If unsupported, you fall normally.

Unavoidable recoil

The body was never light

After the technique resolves, take 12d12 force damage. This damage ignores resistance, immunity, temporary HP, and damage reduction. One leg of your choice is broken. If you made the warrior’s strike, the limb used for that attack is also broken. An already broken limb is severed instead; using the same leg for movement and the strike therefore severs it. These are explicit technique injuries and occur regardless of the recoil total.

RCT can repair the damage and limbs normally after the Cataclysm resolves if you remain conscious and can act. Recoil can reduce you to 0 HP or kill you through the ordinary damage and injury rules.

No artificial cooldown: Sub-Light Cataclysm is not once per rest. A character with enough CE, RCT Reserve, limbs, and courage may use it repeatedly. That capacity is part of the Tier V promise.

Level 18 · Complete Domain

Otherworld Between Darkness and Light: Reverse Transcendence

Abstract stone monoliths hang in a horizon that is simultaneously overexposed and lightless. A segmented black sun watches from behind the user while every point in space acquires homicidal direction.

Expansion

The Otherworld

Full-turn action, 20 CE, 60-foot-radius enclosed barrier centered on you, lasting 1 minute. Choose creatures to bring inside using the comprehensive Domain rules. Barrier statistics, clashes, anti-domain techniques, refinement, and Burnout function normally.

Universal Designation

Everyone inside has been chosen

Every chosen hostile creature in the Domain simultaneously counts as your designated creature. This does not end the designation you held outside. Killing Edge and every feature referring to the designated creature can apply to any of them, subject to their normal per-turn limits.

Everywhere is an origin

No blind angle exists

You may originate Light attacks from any unoccupied point in the Domain. You and creatures you permit can stand and move upon visible rays as though they were solid terrain. These rays are environmental Domain terrain, not separately damageable Architecture segments.

Sole sure-hit event

Reverse Transcendence chooses the horizon

The initiative-count-20 Reverse Transcendence event is the Domain’s only guaranteed hostile effect. Ordinary Light, Tangible Killing Intent, and learned Darkness attacks and saving throws resolve normally even though they may originate from anywhere inside.

Anti-domain defense

Suppress the horizon, not the world

Simple Domain, Hollow Wicker Basket, Falling Blossom Emotion, or a Domain clash restores the attack or save printed in the selected Reverse Transcendence mode. The rays, monoliths, ordinary rolled techniques, and Universal Designation remain.

Initiative 20 · Reverse Transcendence

Choose a horizon for every enemy

At initiative count 20, choose one of the following autonomous Domain modes separately for each chosen hostile creature. Each pulse costs no CE and is not an activation of Light Lance, Killing Edge, Collapse, or any other ordinary feature.

Malicious Light

Impaling pillars manifest through the target. It automatically takes (2 × PB + 1) Light Dice + CE modifier radiant plus PB Intent Dice force damage, then is restrained until the next initiative count 20. A protected creature makes a Dexterity save, taking half of the combined damage and avoiding restraint on a success.

Black Sun · Reversal required

A private event horizon forms within the target. It automatically takes 12 Light Dice + CE modifier + PB Intent Dice force damage, moves to any open point you choose within 120 feet, and is restrained until the next initiative count 20. A protected creature makes a Strength save, taking half damage with no movement or restraint on a success. You cannot choose this mode unless you know Darkness.

Criticals

Sure-hit is not an automatic critical

Malicious Light pulses are hits but not critical hits and cannot attempt Black Flash without an effect expressly making them critical. Rolled attacks made against a protected target may critically hit normally.

Ending

The light burns out

When the Domain ends, apply the comprehensive Technique Burnout: technique lock and 2 exhaustion levels for 1 minute, both ending immediately if you land a Black Flash unless refinement changes the result.

Homebrew sure-hit disclosure: the Domain’s canonical name, appearance, and user are established, but its effect is never shown because the duel is interrupted. Universal Designation and the initiative-20 modes are intentionally our campaign’s interpretation, not a claim about unseen canon.

Rules reference

Two engravings, no missing procedures.

Use these rulings for adaptation, copying, Maximum Output, Black Flash, Attack-action features, and interaction with the campaign’s Ten Shadows.

Character creation

One choice

Selecting Malicious Radiance grants both Tangible Killing Intent and Light as one Tier V player option. It does not consume two subclass choices, two feat slots, or two progression tracks. Burnout or an effect that disables all of your Innate Techniques disables both.

Copying

Two engravings

An effect that copies one Innate Technique copies either Light or Tangible Killing Intent, chosen when copied. It obtains both only if it explicitly copies a complete dual package or successfully copies each engraving separately. Darkness is available only if the copier can also perform RCT and Cursed Technique Reversal.

Mahoraga and adaptation

Track distinct phenomena

Radiant damage, Light Lance, Pillar Constellation, and hard-light obstruction are Light phenomena. Killing Edge’s force damage and Impaling Intent are Tangible Killing Intent phenomena. Darkness is a third reversed phenomenon. A combined Malicious Light hit exposes Mahoraga to both Light and Tangible Killing Intent once when that hit resolves; advance each applicable adaptation track according to the Ten Shadows rules.

Suppression

Specific or comprehensive

If an effect suppresses one named engraving, it can suppress Light or Tangible Killing Intent separately. A rule preventing the creature from activating an Innate Technique at all suppresses both. Existing Architecture segments follow the campaign rule for manifestations during Burnout or suppression.

Black Flash

Use the restored Project procedure

After a qualifying critical attack, roll the separate unmodified d20. On the required result, multiply the entire completed critical damage—including declared Light Lance and Killing Edge damage—by 3. Pillar Constellation attacks check independently. Saves, Black Sun Collapse, Malicious Aurora, and Atmospheric Cataclysm cannot Black Flash.

Attack-action stacking

Replacement means replacement

Each Light Lance replaces one attack inside the Attack action and can be used more than once if you have attacks and CE remaining. Pillar Constellation is its own action and receives no attacks granted specifically to the Attack action. The warrior’s strike is one attack inside a full-turn feature, not the Attack action.

Maximum Output

Choose the activation being overcharged

Maximum Output on Light Lance doubles the selected CE spend and may enhance its range, damage dice, or attack bonus. On Killing Edge it costs 2 CE, and Damage adds half PB Intent Dice, rounded up. If both join one hit, one Maximum Output declaration enhances only the named activation unless another feature supplies a second declaration.

Maximum Output: sequences

One barrage, one choice

Maximum Output on Pillar Constellation doubles its PB CE cost. Accuracy applies to every beam; Damage adds half the activation’s original total dice, rounded up, distributed among successful beams as you choose. Maximum: Malicious Aurora and Sub-Light Cataclysm are ineligible because they are named pinnacle features.

Damage identity

Radiance and mass

Light deals radiant damage. Tangible Killing Intent and relativistic impact deal force damage. These are cursed-technique damage even when visually resembling sunlight or ordinary collision.

Designation

Not a condition

Designation cannot be removed by condition cleansing and does not reveal the target to allies. It ends through its written duration and limits or through technique suppression.

Reversal

Darkness belongs to Light

Black Sun is the specific written reversal for Light and replaces the generic need to improvise an inverse. Tangible Killing Intent has no reversal in this guide. Learning RCT alone never grants Black Sun.

Counterplay

Survive the strongest—not a rules loophole

  • Destroy hard-light origins and platforms; force the user to spend actions and CE rebuilding them.
  • Total cover blocks ordinary paths even after Predator’s Certainty reveals the victim’s location.
  • Drain reactions before committing to a decisive attack so Refracted Body cannot escape it.
  • Spread beyond Malicious Aurora’s area and punish its instantaneous 10 CE expenditure.
  • Force sub-light movement through durable barriers, threaten the unconscious user after recoil, or exploit depleted RCT Reserve.
  • Adaptation tracks Light, Tangible Killing Intent, and Darkness separately, but a combined hit can begin more than one track.
  • Domains, suppression, technique extinguishment, and calamity-grade durability remain valid answers; no mundane opponent is promised a fair duel.
Four Innate Technique feats

Light and intent diverge by choice.

These optional feats specialize designation, weaponized architecture, pursuit, or the rare learned Reversal. Each occupies a normal feat choice, may be taken only once, and this is the complete feat list for the dual inheritance.

Prerequisite: Murderous Designation

Two Deaths Foretold

You may maintain two designated creatures at once and may designate both without an action when initiative is rolled. Designating another while at this limit ends the oldest designation. Killing Edge and every per-turn limit remain shared across both targets unless a later feature expressly grants more uses.

Prerequisite: Luminous Architecture

Living Architecture

When creating hard-light segments, choose any number of them up to PB and pay 1 additional CE for each chosen segment. Each chosen segment immediately launches at a creature within 60 feet of its space; make a separate ranged CE attack that deals 2 Light Dice radiant damage on a hit. A launched segment is destroyed after its attack. Unlaunched segments remain normally.

Prerequisite: Luminous Passage, level 6

Predator in the Light

When one of your designated creatures you can perceive moves or teleports at least 15 feet, use your reaction and spend 2 CE to use Luminous Passage for up to 60 feet, ending closer to it if possible. You may then spend the normal CE for one Light Lance and make that attack as part of the reaction. This is not the Attack action.

Prerequisite: Black Sun

Black Sun Dominion

When forming Black Sun, choose up to PB creatures to see through it and be unaffected by its Collapse. You may spend 4 additional CE to maintain the sphere for up to 1 minute with Technique Focus and move it up to 30 feet as a bonus action. Collapse occurs only when the sphere first forms; moving it or entering it does not repeat the damage or pull.

Character guide

Decide what deserves your light.

The package supports a dedicated technique artillery user, a weapon combatant, or an impossible hybrid. Maximum statistics are useful, never assumed.

Martial chassis

Replace attacks, keep the weapon

Extra Attack lets you mix Light Lances with weapon or unarmed strikes. A physical attack also gives Sub-Light Cataclysm its lore-accurate finishing blow and preserves access to weapon riders.

Technique chassis

Build the firing geometry

A CE-focused character gains stronger attack rolls, saves, damage modifiers, and Maximum Output. Architecture and all-angle origins reward players who think in elevation, cover, and lines.

RCT

Rare, optional, transformative

The ordinary progression works without RCT. Learning it unlocks the possibility of Darkness and makes repeat Sub-Light Cataclysms survivable; it does not erase their CE, Healing Reserve, action, or injury costs.

Temperament

Killing intent can be quiet

A gentle character may carry terrifying certainty only when protection requires it. A proud warrior may refuse to designate anyone unworthy. A frightened prodigy may experience every designation as an unwanted confession.

Origin

No required canon identity

The character need not be Dabura, Simurian, or Deskunte. The dual engraving may be extraterrestrial, inherited from an isolated clan, produced by twins, or awakened when two incomplete techniques fused in one soul.

Party play

Build roads as often as weapons

Hard-light bridges, walls, elevated firing positions, and safe platforms can solve encounters without damage. Allies can use the structures even though only you can emit attacks from them.

Ordinary loop: designate → build an angle → replace an attack with Light Lance → apply Killing Edge → impale or reposition the victim → use the free afterimage movement to prepare the next origin.

Calamity loop: open Reverse Transcendence or cross the map with Sub-Light Cataclysm → accept that the encounter and the user’s body may both be fundamentally different afterward.

Lore ledger

What is canon, and what we completed.

The mechanical guide stays honest where the source is deliberately silent.

Established

Dual techniques and hard light

Dabura is stated to possess Light and an unpronounceable Deskunte concept described as murderous intent made tangible. His light forms beams and supporting structures capable of impaling Mahoraga. See Modulo Chapter 15.

Established

The body cannot keep up

Dabura approaches light speed only by accepting catastrophic physical consequences; the sub-light passage and resulting destruction are shown across Chapter 18 and Chapter 19.

Established

RCT, Darkness, and the Domain

He learns RCT through observation, channels positive energy through Light to produce Darkness, and then constructs Otherworld Between Darkness and Light: Reverse Transcendence. See the character synopsis and Domain reference.

Completed for play

The intentionally unwritten effects

Canon never demonstrates the completed Darkness attack or the Domain’s sure-hit. Black Sun’s absorption mechanics, the Domain pulse, exact CE costs, damage dice, action timing, construct statistics, and progression are this guide’s lore-informed homebrew.
