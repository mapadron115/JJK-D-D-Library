DJINN 2 • Strength / Gravity

Agares — Crushing Earth

Rank A
Canon master: Kouen RenBasis: Canon affinity • Codex-expanded techniques

Weapon Equip • Level 5

Gravitic Royal Arm. Weapon Equip makes every strike carry adjustable weight; you may make a weapon feel weightless in motion and impossibly heavy on impact.

Weight Crown

Action • 2 CE

One creature or Huge object within 120 ft makes a Strength save. On failure its speed becomes 0 and it cannot fly until the start of your next turn; willingly affected targets can instead become nearly weightless and gain 30-ft fly until then.

Gravitic Blow

On hit • 3 CE

Add 4 Djinn Dice force. Target makes a Strength save or is knocked prone and pushed or pulled up to 40 ft.

King’s Gravity

Action • 5 CE

Create a 60-ft-radius crushing field at a point within 180 ft until the start of your next turn. Creatures entering/starting there save Strength; 7 Djinn Dice force and Restrained on failure, half damage on success.

Full Djinn Equip • Level 9

Fly 80 ft through gravity control; count as Gargantuan for carrying, grappling, shoving, and resisting forced movement. Enemies within 15 ft treat the area as difficult terrain.

EXTREME MAGIC • LEVEL 13+

Manticore’s Throne

12 CE • Codex Extreme

Collapse gravity across a 180-ft-radius sphere within 300 ft. Strength save; 16 Djinn Dice force, half on success. Failure pulls the target to the center, knocks it prone, and Restrains it until the end of your next turn. Ground and structures buckle into a deep basin.

Anime-scale: Strategic scale: crush a fortress district, collapse a mountain face, or pin a mass army beneath amplified gravity.
