Features & procedures

Exact player-facing procedures.

Weapon Equip • Level 5

Gravitic Royal Arm.

Weapon Equip makes every strike carry adjustable weight; you may make a weapon feel weightless in motion and impossibly heavy on impact.

Action • 2 Reserve

Weight Crown

One creature or Huge object within 120 ft makes a Strength save. On failure its speed becomes 0 and it cannot fly until the start of your next turn; willingly affected targets can instead become nearly weightless and gain 30-ft fly until then.

On hit • 3 Reserve

Gravitic Blow

Add 4 Djinn Dice force. Target makes a Strength save or is knocked prone and pushed or pulled up to 40 ft.

Action • 5 Reserve

King’s Gravity

Create a 60-ft-radius crushing field at a point within 180 ft until the start of your next turn. Creatures entering/starting there save Strength; 7 Djinn Dice force and Restrained on failure, half damage on success.

Full Djinn Equip • Level 9

Fly 80 ft through gravity control; count as Gargantuan for carrying, grappling, shoving, and resisting forced movement. Enemies within 15 ft treat the area as difficult terrain.

12 Reserve • Codex Extreme

Manticore’s Throne

Collapse gravity across a 180-ft-radius sphere within 300 ft. Strength save; 16 Djinn Dice force, half on success. Failure pulls the target to the center, knocks it prone, and Restrains it until the end of your next turn. Ground and structures buckle into a deep basin.

Anime-scale: Strategic scale: crush a fortress district, collapse a mountain face, or pin a mass army beneath amplified gravity.

Rules integration

Djinn Die, Equip & Extreme Magic

Independent Djinn Reserve: 16 (Rank A). Refreshes on a long rest. Every Djinn cost below — Weapon Equip, Full Equip, abilities, reactions, Extreme Magic — spends this Djinn’s Reserve, never your CE. If this Djinn’s Reserve is empty it simply cannot fuel more until you rest; your other Djinn keep their own separate Reserves.
Weapon Equip · level 5: bonus action, 2 Reserve, lasts 10 minutes.
Full Djinn Equip · level 9: bonus action, 4 Reserve, lasts 1 minute. Only one Equip can be active.
Deep Synchronization · level 11: once per short rest, one Equip activation costs 1 Reserve less.
Extreme Magic · level 13: action; requires that Djinn’s Full Equip; pay the listed Reserve cost from this Djinn’s Reserve; usable once per long rest per Djinn. Full Equip ends afterward, and you cannot Equip that Djinn again until you finish a short rest.
King Vessel · level 17: switch directly from one Full Equip to another as a bonus action, paying the new Equip cost. At level 20, once per long rest, immediately transition from one Djinn’s Extreme into another Djinn’s Full Equip.
Attack / save: use your CE attack modifier and CE save DC unless this Djinn explicitly says otherwise.
