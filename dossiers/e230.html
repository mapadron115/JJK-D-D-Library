Features & procedures

Properties & techniques

Exact player-facing procedures

Buidhe

once/turn hit reduces target max HP by one Tool Die until long rest; total reduction capped at PB Tool Dice per creature. This max-HP reduction is subordinate to the Soul Split Katana’s apex bypass; the cap is a dice-total cap, not per-hit. Wielding both spears follows the standard two-weapon fighting rules.

Dearg

2 CE on hit: Tool check vs active buff/construct effect; success suppresses one effect until start next turn, never Innate Technique itself.

Twin Hunt

after hitting same target with both spears in one turn, gain +2 AC against it until next turn.

Release: Yellow Wound, Red Severance

7 CE, 1 minute, bonus action to activate (invented): Buidhe cap doubles; Dearg suppression lasts through end next turn on success.

The fiction. Jujutsu scholars classify the pair as curses of finality: Buidhe's CE insists that some wounds were never meant to close, and the max-HP erosion is the flesh believing it. Dearg, the elder of the two, carries the opposite conviction — that no enchantment, however well-built, deserves to last. The prevailing theory holds the Twin Hunt was never a technique but a pact: the spears only sing together when both are answered, because neither curse hunts alone.

Rules integration

Cursed-tool attunement

Campaign canon

Cursed-tool attunement: you have PB attunement slots (2–6). This tool occupies one slot and requires a one-hour ritual. Attunement ends voluntarily over a short rest, on death, or when you replace it while over capacity. Distance does not break attunement. Cursed-tool attunement is separate from magic-item attunement.
Balance & counterplay. Powerful paired identity requires landing both weapons and respects technique permanence.
