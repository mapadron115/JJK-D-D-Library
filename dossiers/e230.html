Gáe Buidhe & Gáe Dearg

Two finesse spears, 1d6 each. Buidhe wounds healing; Dearg disrupts active cursed effects briefly.

FormPaired spears
RoleDual-wield curse / anti-heal
EngineTwin
Attunement1 hour*
Properties & techniquesExact player-facing procedures

Buidhe

once/turn hit reduces target max HP by one Tool Die until long rest; total reduction capped at PB Tool Dice per creature.

Dearg

2 CE on hit: Tool check vs active buff/construct effect; success suppresses one effect until start next turn, never Innate Technique itself.

Twin Hunt

after hitting same target with both spears in one turn, gain +2 AC against it until next turn.

Release: Yellow Wound, Red Severance

7 CE, 1 minute: Buidhe cap doubles; Dearg suppression lasts through end next turn on success.

Balance & counterplay. Powerful paired identity requires landing both weapons and respects technique permanence.
