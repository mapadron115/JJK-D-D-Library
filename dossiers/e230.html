Properties & techniques

Exact player-facing procedures

Buidhe

On hit · Once per turn: reduce the target's max HP by one Tool Die until your next long rest; total reduction per creature capped at your PB in Tool Dice.

The yellow wound's slow arithmetic: once per turn, a hit takes a die off the victim's maximum, capped per creature, reset by the long rest, and subordinate to the Soul Split Katana's apex where the two doctrines meet. The wound that the body cannot unlearn. once/turn hit reduces target max HP by one Tool Die until long rest; total reduction capped at PB Tool Dice per creature. This max-HP reduction is subordinate to the Soul Split Katana’s apex bypass; the cap is a dice-total cap, not per-hit. Wielding both spears follows the standard two-weapon fighting rules.

Dearg

On hit · 2 CE: make a Tool check against one active buff or construct effect; on success it is suppressed until the start of your next turn (never an Innate Technique).

Two CE on a hit, and the elder spear makes its case: a Tool check against one active buff or construct, suppressed until the next turn on success. The technique itself is never touched, Dearg unmakes the scaffolding, not the soul. 2 CE on hit: Tool check vs active buff/construct effect; success suppresses one effect until start next turn, never Innate Technique itself.

Twin Hunt

Passive · After hitting the same target with both spears in one turn, gain +2 AC against it until your next turn.

The pact's reward: both spears answered by one target in a single turn, and the bearer gains two AC against it. The spears only sing together, and the song is a ward. after hitting same target with both spears in one turn, gain +2 AC against it until next turn.

Release: Yellow Wound, Red Severance

Bonus action · 7 CE · 1 minute: Buidhe's max-HP cap doubles; Dearg's suppression lasts through the end of your next turn.

Seven CE for one minute of the pair's whole argument: Buidhe's cap doubles and Dearg's suppression lingers a turn longer. For this minute, finality is the doctrine of the whole field. 7 CE, 1 minute, bonus action to activate (invented): Buidhe cap doubles; Dearg suppression lasts through end next turn on success.

Field notes. Subject: two curses that refuse to hunt alone. Three truths govern them. First, Buidhe is the curse that some wounds were never meant to close: once per turn, a hit erodes the victim's maximum hit points by a die, the flesh believing the wound is permanent, until the long rest or the PB-dice cap calls the account. Second, Dearg is the elder and the opposite conviction: that no enchantment, however well-built, deserves to last. Two CE on a hit and a Tool check can suppress one active buff or construct until the next turn begins, never the Innate Technique itself, for even this spear respects what is written in the soul. Third, the Twin Hunt was never a technique but a pact: land both spears on one target in a turn and the bearer gains two AC against it, because neither curse sings unless both are answered. Assess them correctly: finality, wielded as a pair.

Rules integration

Cursed-tool attunement

Campaign canon

Cursed-tool attunement: you have PB attunement slots (2–6). This tool occupies one slot and requires a one-hour ritual. Attunement ends voluntarily over a short rest, on death, or when you replace it while over capacity. Distance does not break attunement. Cursed-tool attunement is separate from magic-item attunement.
Balance & counterplay. Powerful paired identity requires landing both weapons and respects technique permanence.
