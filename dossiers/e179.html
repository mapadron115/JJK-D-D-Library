Core law

Character fantasy

Wrap allies and enemies in living cursed coils. Friendly coils ward, heal, and shed conditions; hostile coils constrict, poison, and drag.

Combat loop

  1. Place Coils on creatures that matter.
  2. Decide whether each Coil protects or constricts.
  3. Shed friendly Coils to cleanse danger; tighten hostile Coils for control.

Field notes. Subject: a serpent that cannot tell embrace from binding. Three truths govern her. First: Ward and Constrict are the same gesture aimed in different directions. She never decides between love and violence, she only asks who the coils are around. Second: protection here is a spending decision. A Ward Coil is one saved blow, shed to cleanse poison, fear, or grapple; a Constrict Coil is a tightening problem the enemy must spend an action to solve. Third: the coils multiply by judgment. Layer several and the whole team becomes a living ward network, but her cap is finite, and every coil wrapped on a friend is one not choking a foe. Mark this: the serpent guards what it loves and strangles what it hates with the same motion. Watch who she coils, and you know her heart.

Core law

Sacred Coils

Place Coil:

One CE, sixty feet, a bonus action. The willing receive a Ward Coil; the unwilling test their Strength against a Constrict Coil. PB active at once. bonus action, 1 CE, 60 feet. Willing creature gains a Ward Coil automatically. Unwilling creature makes Strength save; failure gains a Constrict Coil. Coils last 1 minute. Maximum active Coils = PB.

Ward Coil:

One saved blow: +1 AC against a single attack each round, then the coil is spent. bearer gains +1 AC against one attack each round; after the attack resolves, the Coil is spent.

Constrict Coil:

Speed cut by ten, and the coil must be torn off with an action and a Strength check against her CE DC. speed −10 feet. Bearer may use an action for Strength check vs CE DC to remove one Coil.

Full progression

Level progression

1Sacred Coil

The two gestures. Tighten spends a Constrict Coil for damage and possible restraint; Interpose spends a Ward Coil to take an ally's blow whole, and catches the striking limb in the coil. Gain Place Coil. One creature may hold at most 2 Coils. Tighten: as a bonus action, spend one Constrict Coil on a creature within 60 feet; it takes 1 Sacred Die bludgeoning damage and makes a Strength save, becoming restrained until the end of its next turn on a failure. Interpose: as a reaction when an ally within 60 feet is hit by an attack, spend one Ward Coil. The coil wraps the ally and takes the blow: the ally takes no damage from the attack. The striking limb is caught in the coil, the attacker must make a Strength save vs your CE save DC or be grappled (escape DC = your CE save DC) until the end of its next turn.

3Shed Skin

The shed coil cleanses: poisoned, grappled, frightened ended on a willing bearer, once per short rest, by their bonus action or her reaction. A willing bearer may spend one Ward Coil as a bonus action to end poisoned, grappled, or frightened on itself. You may instead spend your reaction to do this for it. A creature can benefit once per short rest.

6River Venom

Tighten may carry venom for one CE, and the serpent's element grants her: forty feet of swim, and breath beneath the water while any coil is active. When Tighten damages a creature, spend 1 CE to force Constitution save; failure is poisoned until end of your next turn. You gain swim speed 40 feet and can breathe underwater while at least one Coil is active.

11Sevenfold Warding

The ward network. Cap grows, three coils per creature, Ward Coils at +2: and when both coil types share a bearer, she decides which her Tighten consumes. Active Coil cap becomes PB + CE modifier; one creature can hold 3. Ward Coil may grant +2 AC against the triggering attack instead of +1, still consuming itself. If a creature has both Ward and Constrict Coils from you, you decide which type your Tighten/Interpose consumes. At high level, layer several Coils and convert the whole team into a living ward network.

18Domain Expansion: Palace Beneath the Sacred River

A palace of gold and lapis beneath green river water, light filtering down in slow columns. Jeweled serpents coil around the pillars, and the current moves like something breathing. Everything is quiet, the way the deep places are quiet, and the water holds you up like hands.

A submerged jeweled palace, ninety feet. At initiative twenty, allies she chooses gain Ward Coils and hostiles gain Constrict Coils automatically, and once per round, Shed Skin may end a deeper condition: charmed, restrained, or blinded. Full-turn action, 20 CE, 90-foot radius, 1 minute. A submerged jeweled palace appears without imposing drowning. At initiative 20 each ally you choose gains one Ward Coil and each hostile automatically gains one Constrict Coil. A protected hostile makes Strength save and gains none on success. Your active Coil cap is ignored inside the Domain, but all Domain-created Coils vanish when it ends. Once per round, Shed Skin can end one additional condition: charmed, restrained, or blinded. Clash bookkeeping: 500 clash points (Tier III). A 100+ point advantage collapses the weaker Domain; collapse deals no damage. Barrier AC 12 + PB; barrier HP = 10 × max(1, your CE modifier) + 500. Technique Burnout: 10 rounds.

Feats

Build-defining options.

Rain-Bringer

The naga's weather: light rain for ten minutes, quenching flame and finding water. As an action and 1 CE, create light rain in a 30-foot radius for 10 minutes; it extinguishes mundane flame and gives advantage on Survival to find water.

Jeweled Hood

She senses around every coiled creature, ten-foot blindsight centered on each bearer, and no surprise from within. While at least one of your Coils is active, you sense the space around every Coil-bearing creature: you have blindsight to 10 feet centered on each creature bearing one of your Coils, and you cannot be surprised by a creature within that radius.

Constriction Expert

A Constrict Coil spent to anchor: forced movement negated entirely, by her reaction. When a creature bearing one of your Constrict Coils would be forcibly moved, you can use your reaction to spend that Coil and anchor it in place, negating the movement entirely.

Shared Skin

Ward Coils passed hand to hand, a willing bearer may give the coil to another willing creature within thirty feet. As a bonus action, a willing creature bearing one of your Ward Coils can pass it to another willing creature within 30 feet; the Coil keeps its remaining duration.

Rules integration

Building the character

Support Specialist or Strategist, with a viable melee-controller Warrior. The technique is deliberately symmetrical: the same resource can protect allies or bind enemies.
Technique die: Sacred Die follows the shared d6 → d8 → d10 → d12 progression.
Rules integration

Counterplay

Spend actions breaking Coils, attack the user to exhaust reactions, and force them to spread a limited Coil cap across too many targets.
