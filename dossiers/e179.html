Core law

Character fantasy

Wrap allies and enemies in living cursed coils. Friendly coils ward, heal, and shed conditions; hostile coils constrict, poison, and drag.

Combat loop

  1. Place Coils on creatures that matter.
  2. Decide whether each Coil protects or constricts.
  3. Shed friendly Coils to cleanse danger; tighten hostile Coils for control.
  4. At high level, layer several Coils and convert the whole team into a living ward network.

Core law

Sacred Coils

Place Coil:

bonus action, 1 CE, 60 feet. Willing creature gains a Ward Coil automatically. Unwilling creature makes Strength save; failure gains a Constrict Coil. Coils last 1 minute. Maximum active Coils = PB.

Ward Coil:

bearer gains +1 AC against one attack each round; after the attack resolves, the Coil is spent.

Constrict Coil:

speed −10 feet. Bearer may use an action for Strength check vs CE DC to remove one Coil.

Full progression

Level progression

1Sacred Coil

Gain Place Coil. One creature may hold at most 2 Coils. Tighten: as a bonus action, spend one Constrict Coil on a creature within 60 feet; it takes 1 Sacred Die bludgeoning damage and makes a Strength save, becoming restrained until the end of its next turn on a failure. Interpose: as a reaction, spend one Ward Coil on an ally to reduce incoming damage by 1 Sacred Die + your CE modifier.

3Shed Skin

A willing bearer may spend one Ward Coil as a bonus action to end poisoned, grappled, or frightened on itself. You may instead spend your reaction to do this for it. A creature can benefit once per short rest.

6River Venom

When Tighten damages a creature, spend 1 CE to force Constitution save; failure is poisoned until end of your next turn. You gain swim speed 40 feet and can breathe underwater while at least one Coil is active.

11Sevenfold Warding

Active Coil cap becomes PB + CE modifier; one creature can hold 3. Ward Coil may grant +2 AC against the triggering attack instead of +1, still consuming itself. If a creature has both Ward and Constrict Coils from you, you decide which type your Tighten/Interpose consumes.

18Domain Expansion: Palace Beneath the Sacred River

Full-turn action, 20 CE, 90-foot radius, 1 minute. A submerged jeweled palace appears without imposing drowning. At initiative 20 each ally you choose gains one Ward Coil and each hostile automatically gains one Constrict Coil. A protected hostile makes Strength save and gains none on success. Your active Coil cap is ignored inside the Domain, but all Domain-created Coils vanish when it ends. Once per round, Shed Skin can end one additional condition: charmed, restrained, or blinded. Clash bookkeeping: 500 clash points (Tier III). A 100+ point advantage collapses the weaker Domain; collapse deals no damage. Barrier AC 12 + PB; barrier HP = 10 × max(1, your CE modifier) + 500. Technique Burnout: 10 rounds.

Feats

Build-defining options.

Rain-Bringer

As an action and 1 CE, create light rain in a 30-foot radius for 10 minutes; it extinguishes mundane flame and gives advantage on Survival to find water.

Jeweled Hood

While at least one of your Coils is active, you sense the space around every Coil-bearing creature: you have blindsight to 10 feet centered on each creature bearing one of your Coils, and you cannot be surprised by a creature within that radius.

Constriction Expert

When a creature bearing one of your Constrict Coils would be forcibly moved, you can use your reaction to spend that Coil and anchor it in place, negating the movement entirely.

Shared Skin

As a bonus action, a willing creature bearing one of your Ward Coils can pass it to another willing creature within 30 feet; the Coil keeps its remaining duration.

Rules integration

Building the character

Support Specialist or Strategist, with a viable melee-controller Warrior. The technique is deliberately symmetrical: the same resource can protect allies or bind enemies.
Technique die: Sacred Die follows the shared d6 → d8 → d10 → d12 progression.
Rules integration

Counterplay

Spend actions breaking Coils, attack the user to exhaust reactions, and force them to spread a limited Coil cap across too many targets.
