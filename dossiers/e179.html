Seven Sacred Coils

Wrap allies and enemies in living cursed coils. Friendly coils ward, heal, and shed conditions; hostile coils constrict, poison, and drag.

RoleSupport / Restraint Controller
TierTier III · Apex
Core statWIS or CHA
Scaling dieCoil Die

Character fantasy

Wrap allies and enemies in living cursed coils. Friendly coils ward, heal, and shed conditions; hostile coils constrict, poison, and drag.

Combat loop

  1. Place Coils on creatures that matter.
  2. Decide whether each Coil protects or constricts.
  3. Shed friendly Coils to cleanse danger; tighten hostile Coils for control.
  4. At high level, layer several Coils and convert the whole team into a living ward network.

Sacred Coils

  • Place Coil: bonus action, 1 CE, 60 feet. Willing creature gains a Ward Coil automatically. Unwilling creature makes Strength save; failure gains a Constrict Coil. Coils last 1 minute. Maximum active Coils = PB; one creature may hold up to 2 at level 1, 3 at level 11.
  • Ward Coil: bearer gains +1 AC against one attack each round; after the attack resolves, the Coil is spent.
  • Constrict Coil: speed −10 feet. Bearer may use an action for Strength check vs CE DC to remove one Coil.

Innate Technique FeatsOptional character-building feats

Rain-Bringer

As an action and 1 CE, create light rain in a 30-foot radius for 10 minutes; it extinguishes mundane flame and gives advantage on Survival to find water.

Jeweled Hood

While you have a Ward Coil, gain advantage on saves against frightened.

Constriction Expert

A creature restrained by Tighten cannot be forcibly moved away from you unless the mover succeeds on a Strength check against your CE DC.

Shared Skin

When Shed Skin removes a condition, the bearer gains one Coil Die temporary HP.

Building the character

Support Specialist or Strategist, with a viable melee-controller Warrior. The technique is deliberately symmetrical: the same resource can protect allies or bind enemies.

Technique die: Coil Die follows the shared d6 → d8 → d10 → d12 progression.

Counterplay

Spend actions breaking Coils, attack the user to exhaust reactions, and force them to spread a limited Coil cap across too many targets.
