The Holy Lance

Features & procedures

Properties & techniques

Exact player-facing procedures

Absolute Piercing

The lance does not simply bypass armor. It attacks the claim that something cannot be pierced. Barriers, invulnerability, immortality, reincarnation anchors, soul-body separation, and “cannot be harmed” clauses are all treated as valid targets.

Ignore granted resistance

Attacks ignore resistance granted specifically by barriers, reinforcement, or invulnerability effects.

One grade lower

When striking a barrier, Domain wall, or metaphysical seal, the Lance treats the target as one grade lower.

Named defense suppression

Once per long rest, the wielder may declare a named defense as the target of the strike. On a hit, that defense is suppressed until the end of the wielder’s next turn.

Archive Warning. Do not describe a defense as “absolute” in the presence of the Lance.

Rules integration

Cursed-tool attunement

Campaign canon

Cursed-tool attunement: you have PB attunement slots (2–6). This tool occupies one slot and requires a one-hour ritual. Attunement ends voluntarily over a short rest, on death, or when you replace it while over capacity. Distance does not break attunement. Cursed-tool attunement is separate from magic-item attunement.
Balance & counterplay. Its power is aimed entirely at defenses — it adds little against a target that relies on speed, numbers, or simply not being hit. The named-defense suppression is once per long rest and still requires a hit.
