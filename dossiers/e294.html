The Holy Lance

The spear that attacks the claim of invulnerability itself. Attacks ignore resistance or immunity granted by barriers, Cursed Techniques, CE reinforcement, or effects that state the target cannot be damaged; Pierce the Absolute (3 CE) names a defense and suppresses it on a hit, and Godwound (8 CE, once per 7 days) pins a slain creature's body and soul together for 24 hours.

Features & procedures

Properties & techniques

Exact player-facing procedures

Field notes. Subject: a verdict with a haft. Three truths govern it. First: the Lance does not defeat defenses — it declines to recognize them. Absolute Piercing ignores resistance or immunity granted by barriers, techniques, or reinforcement, but it deactivates nothing; the distinction is the whole weapon. Longinus pierces a defense; it does not repeal cursed techniques. Second: the authority is specific, never general. Pierce the Absolute works only if the wielder names the protection before the attack roll — the 3 CE is lost even on a miss, because the spear’s jurisdiction comes from naming the thing it challenges, never from piercing an abstraction. Third: Godwound is the spear’s final argument, usable once in seven days — a slain creature pinned, body and soul together, for twenty-four hours against healing, regeneration, reincarnation, and revival. Mark the thesis: nothing may declare itself absolutely beyond harm, and nothing may declare itself absolutely beyond death either.

Relic profile

1d8 piercing (versatile 1d10) · +4 to attack and damage rolls · Relic DC

Jujutsu origin

The Gospel tradition gave the weapon a wound; later centuries gave it a name, a relic cult, and the reputation of a spear that touched the divine. In jujutsu terms, the accumulated belief did not make the spear holy so much as it taught the cursed energy inside the relic one impossible proposition: even the untouchable can be pierced.

Absolute Piercing

Attacks with the Lance ignore resistance or immunity granted specifically by a barrier, Cursed Technique, cursed-energy reinforcement, or an effect that states the target cannot be damaged. This does not deactivate the feature; it only allows the Lance to pass through it.

Pierce the Absolute — 3 CE

Before making a melee attack, name one active defensive feature on the target. On a hit, the attack deals an additional 6d10 force damage and that named feature is suppressed against the Lance until the end of your next turn. Against a barrier or Domain wall, this instead deals 12d10 force damage to the barrier.

Godwound — 8 CE, once per 7 days

When the Lance reduces a creature to 0 HP, you may pin its body and soul together. For 24 hours, it cannot regain HP, regenerate, reincarnate, revive, or transfer its soul to another vessel. A Special Grade effect may contest this with an Arcana check against your Relic DC.

Binding Vow — Declared Target

Pierce the Absolute only works if the wielder names the protection being challenged before the attack roll. If the attack misses, the 3 CE is still lost. The Lance receives its absurd authority because it never pierces an unnamed abstraction.

Designer’s note. Designed to sit beside, not replace, the Inverted Spear of Heaven: Longinus pierces a defense; it does not globally deactivate cursed techniques.

Rules integration

Cursed-tool attunement

Campaign canon

Cursed-tool attunement: you have PB attunement slots (2–6). This tool occupies one slot and requires a one-hour ritual. Attunement ends voluntarily over a short rest, on death, or when you replace it while over capacity. Distance does not break attunement. Cursed-tool attunement is separate from magic-item attunement.
