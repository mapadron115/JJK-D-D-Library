Prayer Song

A cumulative ten-beat dance that elevates the user while steadily dismantling one opponent’s tempo.

Field notes. Subject: the duel fought as a duet. Three truths govern her. First: time is the weapon, every turn the Prayer survives compounds, the dancer rising on the beat while her opponent falls out of step with the world; victory here is scheduled, not seized, and the tenth beat is the whole argument. Second: the tempo is fragile, it demands one visible opponent within sixty feet and unbroken Technique Focus; lose the sightline, lose the song, and the count starts again at one. Third: the crescendo is arithmetic, not inspiration, each beat a ledger entry, armor here, damage there, until the opponent's defenses read five points thinner and their turn is reduced to a single choice: action, bonus action, or movement, never all three. Consider the Caesura, her one true interruption: a forced reroll is a skipped beat in the enemy's music. She does not outfight her opponent. She conducts them.

Pinnacle expression

Maximum, Domain, or equivalent.

Domain Expansion, Dance Dance Revolution:

The ground becomes a dance floor, panels of light pulsing in rhythm, and the air itself seems to move to a beat that bypasses the ears and goes straight to the spine. Colors strobe in time, and ghostly footprints appear and fade on the panels, dancing a choreography no living feet could follow. The music does not ask you to dance. It informs you that you already are.

Her Domain is a law, not an event, no initiative-twenty pulse. While it stands, she counts as if ten turns of Prayer had already passed. The tenth beat plays from the first bar. 18: full-turn action, 20 CE; 60-foot-radius enclosed Domain centered on you; 1 minute; Burnout 10 rounds. Heian philosophy: the Domain is a law, not an event, no initiative-20 pulse. While it stands, you always count as if ten turns had passed with Prayer activated.

Rules integration

Edge cases and shared systems.

Rules priority: the reviewed rulings, explicit codex clarifications, and the level progression always control when original wording conflicts. Legacy Domain auto-hit language, action terminology, event timing, missing saves, and older scaling never override revised player rules.
Why Tier II: the tenth beat is oppressive, but reaching it asks the user to maintain one visible duel and Technique Focus through a full combat minute.
