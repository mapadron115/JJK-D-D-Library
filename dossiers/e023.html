Feature map

1Prayer:

As a bonus action for 1 CE, choose one opponent you can perceive within 60 feet and begin Prayer for up to 1 minute using Technique Focus. At the start of each of your turns, increase turns active by one before acting. Effects are cumulative. Prayer ends if the target remains beyond 60 feet or imperceptible at the end of your turn.

“Your enemy” and “your opponent” mean that chosen target. AC reductions apply against all attacks; saving-throw penalties apply only against your effects. Your bonuses apply against every source. Starting Prayer again replaces the target and resets the count to 1.

Marcado. Once per turn after an attack hits, pay 2 CE and add one of that attack’s base damage dice. This is one damage amplifier.

6Caesura.

Caesura. As a reaction when a creature you can perceive within 60 feet rolls an attack, save, or damage, force the entire roll to be rerolled and use the new result.

11Crescendo:

Crescendo replaces the five-beat track with the complete ten-beat progression: turn 1 +3 AC/Dex saves; 2 target AC −3; 3 add CE modifier to unarmed/natural damage; 4 add CE modifier to all attack damage; 5 add PB damage; 6 reduce target’s damage by PB; 7 one additional attack during Attack; 8 target totals −5 AC and −5 on saves against you; 9 critical range +1; 10 restriction below.

Turn 10: at the start of each target turn it makes a Wisdom save. On failure, during that turn it may choose only one: take an action, take a bonus action, or move up to its speed; it also cannot react. On success it ignores this restriction for that turn.

Pinnacle expression

Maximum, Domain, or equivalent.

Domain — Final Cadence: 18 — Dance Floor. your shared self-buffs are treated as ten turns active throughout the Domain, and the opponent-specific ten-turn penalties apply to every chosen hostile. At initiative 20, each chosen hostile automatically suffers the Turn 10 action restriction until the next initiative 20. A protected creature makes the normal Wisdom save and ignores that pulse on a success. All ordinary attacks and saves still roll.

Innate Technique Feats

Build-defining options.

These are optional character-building feats. You must meet each listed prerequisite before selecting it.

Prerequisite: Marcado

Fortissimo

When you begin Prayer, choose one willing creature you can perceive within 60 feet as your soloist. Your cumulative personal benefits from the Prayer track - bonuses to AC, saves, damage, attacks, and critical range - apply to the soloist instead of you; penalties to the target are unchanged. If the soloist leaves 60 feet or becomes imperceptible, the benefits return to you.

Prerequisite: Caesura

Cadence

Reaction, 4 CE, after a creature affected by your Prayer rolls but before outcome: make its attack miss or turn its successful save into a failure. This cannot override Legendary Resistance, immunity, or a natural 20 attack.

Prerequisite: Crescendo and CE score 20

Maximum: Grand Chorus

Action, 10 CE: for 1 minute using Technique Focus, your Prayer needs no single target - at the start of each of your turns, every hostile creature you can perceive within 60 feet suffers the Prayer penalties for your current beat count, and you gain the personal benefits once. Starting Prayer anew resets the count as normal.

Rules integration

Edge cases and shared systems.

Rules priority: the reviewed rulings, explicit codex clarifications, and the level progression always control when original wording conflicts. Legacy Domain auto-hit language, action terminology, event timing, missing saves, and older scaling never override revised player rules.
Why Tier II: the tenth beat is oppressive, but reaching it asks the user to maintain one visible duel and Technique Focus through a full combat minute.
