Completed rules

  • As a bonus action for 1 CE, choose one opponent you can perceive within 60 feet and begin Prayer for up to 1 minute using Technique Focus. At the start of each of your turns, increase turns active by one before acting. Effects are cumulative. Prayer ends if the target remains beyond 60 feet or imperceptible at the end of your turn.
  • “Your enemy” and “your opponent” mean that chosen target. AC reductions apply against all attacks; saving-throw penalties apply only against your effects. Your bonuses apply against every source. Starting Prayer again replaces the target and resets the count to 1.
  • At levels 1–10, use the complete five-turn Prayer table in the character reference above. At level 11, Crescendo replaces it: turn 1 +3 AC/Dex saves; 2 target AC −3; 3 add CE modifier to unarmed/natural damage; 4 add CE modifier to all attack damage; 5 add PB damage; 6 reduce target’s damage by PB; 7 one additional attack during Attack; 8 target totals −5 AC and −5 on saves against you; 9 critical range +1; 10 restriction below.
  • Turn 10: at the start of each target turn it makes a Wisdom save. On failure, during that turn it may choose only one: take an action, take a bonus action, or move up to its speed; it also cannot react. On success it ignores this restriction for that turn.
  • Marcado: once per turn after an attack hits, pay 2 CE and add one of that attack’s base damage dice. This is one damage amplifier. Caesura: as a reaction when a creature you can perceive within 60 feet rolls an attack, save, or damage, force the entire roll to be rerolled and use the new result.
  • Domain — Final Cadence: your shared self-buffs are treated as ten turns active throughout the Domain, and the opponent-specific ten-turn penalties apply to every chosen hostile. At initiative 20, each chosen hostile automatically suffers the Turn 10 action restriction until the next initiative 20. A protected creature makes the normal Wisdom save and ignores that pulse on a success. All ordinary attacks and saves still roll.

Power identity

Why Tier II: the tenth beat is oppressive, but reaching it asks the user to maintain one visible duel and Technique Focus through a full combat minute.

Recovered character-build options

Innate Technique Feats 3

These are optional character-building feats. You must meet each listed prerequisite before selecting it.

Fortissimo

Pinnacle

Prerequisite: Marcado

After a critical hit qualifies—or after Black Flash is confirmed—spend 3 CE to maximize the entire completed attack damage. It may combine with distinct effects; two maximization effects do not improve an already maximized roll.

Cadence

Pinnacle

Prerequisite: Caesura

Reaction, 4 CE, after a creature affected by your Prayer rolls but before outcome: make its attack miss or turn its successful save into a failure. This cannot override Legendary Resistance, immunity, or a natural 20 attack.

Maximum: Double Time

Pinnacle

Prerequisite: Crescendo and CE score 20

Action, 10 CE: for 1 minute with Technique Focus, your Prayer track advances by two beats instead of one at the start of each turn. Other creatures do not gain extra turns and durations do not expire twice as fast.
