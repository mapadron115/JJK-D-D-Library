Divergent Fist

Delayed impact and a disciplined martial package that eventually guarantees Black Flash under strict conditions.

The fiction. Registry scholars describe Divergent Flow not as a trick but as a desynchronization: the user's fists learned discipline before their cursed energy learned obedience, so every strike lands twice — once by the body, once by the energy arriving late. The prevailing theory holds that the eventual guaranteed Black Flash is what happens when that late energy, under absolute strictness, finally learns to arrive on time.

Feature map

1Divergent Flow.

Divergent Flow

After you hit with an attack, pay 1 CE and record the total damage that attack would deal after resistance. Deal none of it now. At the start of the target’s next turn, it takes the recorded amount as force damage. The delayed damage cannot critically hit, cannot trigger Black Flash, and is not changed again by resistance or vulnerability.

3Martial Prowess:

One option per turn

Only one Martial Prowess option may be used per turn.

Manji Kick

Declare as part of an unarmed strike. On a hit, roll normal damage; a failed Constitution save doubles that final damage and stuns the target until the end of its next turn, while success deals normal damage and no stun.

One-Two

Use a bonus action to make two unarmed attacks. The first omits the ability modifier from damage; if it hits, the second has advantage and adds three times the chosen Strength or Dexterity modifier instead of once.

Weave

Use a reaction to gain the Dodge action’s benefits until the start of your next turn.

6Controlled Divergent Flow.

Controlled Divergent Flow

Your Divergent Flow improves: you may now apply it to two different attacks per turn. Pay 1 CE after each hit; each target takes its recorded force damage at the start of its own next turn.

11Stance of the Sparks.

Stance of the Sparks

As an action, enter a stance that lasts until ended with no action, you are incapacitated, or you make more than one attack in a turn. While in it you can make only one attack total on your turn, regardless of source; if that attack hits, it is eligible for the current Black Flash roll even without a critical hit.

18Chosen by the Sparks.

Chosen by the Sparks

A critical hit or a qualifying stance hit becomes a Black Flash automatically. All once-per-turn and amplification rules for Black Flash still apply.

Innate Technique Feats

Build-defining options.

These are optional character-building feats. You must meet each listed prerequisite before selecting it.

Prerequisite: Martial Prowess

Black Belt

Repeatable; choose each maneuver once. Takedown uses a bonus-action grapple after a melee hit. Judo Throw triggers on a hit against your grappled Huge-or-smaller target: Strength save, prone on failure and stunned to end of next turn if failed by 5+. Flying Drop Kick uses an action and your jump movement for one unarmed attack, pushing 30 feet on hit.

Prerequisite: Controlled Divergent Flow

Opportunistic Blow

Once per turn after the delayed Divergent Fist damage lands, the target makes an Intelligence save. On failure, attacks against it have advantage until the start of your next turn.

Prerequisite: Chosen by the Sparks and CE score 20

Maximum: Soul Punch

Before an unarmed attack, spend 5 CE; all your unarmed hits this turn deal soul damage that ordinary RCT cannot heal. Each hit gives one Output Suppression, maximum CE modifier: reduce optional CE spending ceiling by 1 per stack and disable Maximum Output while any stack remains. All stacks end after a short or long rest.

Rules integration

Edge cases and shared systems.

Rules priority: the reviewed rulings, explicit codex clarifications, and the level progression always control when original wording conflicts. Legacy Domain auto-hit language, action terminology, event timing, missing saves, and older scaling never override revised player rules.
Why Tier I: it can become terrifying in melee, but every feature reinforces one readable plan: land one excellent physical hit at the right tempo.
Appendix

Additional material.

DivergentFist
A learnt technique using cursed energy manipulation created
accidentally due to slow cursed energy, turns out to be effective.
Cursed Energy
Starting at 1st level, you can harness your negative feelings into a
usable source of energy, called cursed energy. These feelings can be
harnessed into attacks or abilities. The number of cursed energy you
possess is based on your class, some classes use a modifier that
being charisma or wisdom (Player’s choice)
Your cursed energy DC is 8 + user cursed energy ability modifier +
proficiency bonus.
Divergent Flow
At 1st level, once per turn, you can choose the damage of one of
your attacks. For 1 cursed energy, at the start of the target’s turn,
they take the attack’s damage in force damage. These strikes can’t be
black flashes.
Martial Prowess
At 3rd level, your lack of an innate technique has caused you to
make up for it with pure martial prowess. Once per turn you can use
one of the following abilities:
● Manji Kick: Once per turn as part of an unarmed strike, drop
your knees and rotate your body down to give a massive kick in
your opponent's face. On a hit, the opponent must make a
Constitution saving throw (DC 8 + your proficiency bonus + your
Strength or Dexterity modifier). On a failure, the opponent
will be stunned until the end of their turn and take twice your
unarmed strike damage. On a success they aren’t stunned and
take half as much damage.
● One-Two: As a bonus action, you land a massive One-Two combo.
Make two unarmed attacks, the first one doesn’t add your damage
bonus, but if it hits the second gains advantage. The second
one adds three times your Strength or Dexterity modifier to the
damage roll.
● Weave: As a reaction, you enter a defensive stance. You take the
dodge action using a reaction instead.
Controlled Divergent Flow
Once you reach 6th level, you have gained better manipulation over
your divergent fist attack. You can now apply your divergent fist to
two separate attacks.
Stance of the Sparks
Starting at 11th level, as an action, crouch, placing your fist over
your palm. While in this stance, you may only make one attack on your
following turns. This attack allows you to roll for a black flash,
even if the attack is not a critical hit.
Chosen by the Sparks
Finally, at 18th level, you no longer need to roll for a black flash
after landing a critical hit or using Stance of Sparks, you always
hit a black flash.
