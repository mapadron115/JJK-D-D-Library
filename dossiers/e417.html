Lesser Exorcism Talisman

A Grade 3 cursed item, a paper talisman, expended on use.

FormCursed Item
GradeGrade 3
AttunementNot required

Field notes. Subject: a single dart of judgment, delivered by hand. Three truths govern the exorcism talisman. First, the weapon is the delivery: as an action you slap the charm onto the curse itself, a melee attack, because paper that stays in the pocket exorcises nothing. Second, it is a specialist's instrument: it functions only against cursed spirits and does nothing to any other target; a blade honed for one throat cuts deeper than a blade honed for every throat. Third, the strike is radiant and final: 3d8 radiant damage on a hit, and the talisman is expended in the detonation, no second use, no attunement, only the one judgment. Get close. The charm will not come to the curse; you must bring the charm to the curse.

Action

Exorcising Slap

Action · Melee attack with the talisman against a cursed spirit · On hit: 3d8 radiant damage · Talisman is expended · Cursed spirits only.

As an action, you can attempt to slap this paper charm onto a cursed spirit. Make a melee attack; on a hit, deal 3d8 radiant damage and the talisman is expended. This talisman only functions against cursed spirits, it does nothing to any other target.
