Field entry

Journal Entry field report, converted to a table-ready stat block

Field notes. Subject: famine’s sense of humor. A massive grasshopper born of humanity’s oldest fear — locusts, failed harvests, empty granaries — and convinced of its own genius: it speaks in complete sentences, though the complicated words collapse into stutters it never notices. It is talkative, and this is bait — do not mistake talk for parley. Its inflated intellect grants advantage against being frightened (mockery of “short-sighted human minds” only deepens the certainty), and it can leap thirty feet without a running start. When the conversation ends, the efficiency is brutal: a bite that crushes stone and bone (2d10+5) and two piston punches (1d8+5 each), all at +8. Arrogance in the jaw, artillery in the limbs. Exorcise on sight — do not debate the superior thinker.

An Imaginary Cursed Spirit, Grade 2 (CR 4–7 band). Notably intelligent by cursed-spirit standards: forms complete sentences, though complex terminology collapses into stutters it never notices. Extremely aggressive to humans.
