DJINN 9 • Wind

Paimon — Maniacal Love & Chaos

Rank B
Canon master: Hakuei RenBasis: Canon core • Magul Al-Hazard and Pa’ír Al-Hazard

Weapon Equip • Level 5

Flabellum of the Wind God. Weapon Equip turns the fan into a trident-like wind vessel and can form tornadoes around the wielder or at range.

Wind Mantle

Reaction • 2 CE

Gain +4 AC against a ranged attack or line effect you can see. If it misses/fails, redirect a mundane projectile or move the line’s origin 20 ft to either side.

Cyclone Ride

Bonus Action • 2 CE

Fly up to 120 ft without provoking. One willing creature within 10 ft may ride the current with you.

Magul Al-Hazard

Action • 5 CE

Create a 100-ft-radius tornado at a point within 240 ft. Strength save; 8 Djinn Dice slashing/thunder and lifted 60 ft on failure, half and no lift on success. The tornado lasts until the start of your next turn.

Full Djinn Equip • Level 9

Fly 140 ft; wind resistance (reduce thunder and nonmagical projectile damage by one Djinn Die); ranged attacks from more than 30 ft have disadvantage against you. You can freely shape harmless winds in 300 ft.

EXTREME MAGIC • LEVEL 13+

Pa’ír Al-Hazard

12 CE • Canon Extreme

Summon a 200-ft-radius, 600-ft-high thunder-roar tornado. Strength save; 17 Djinn Dice slashing/thunder, half on success. Failure lifts the target 150 ft, Restrains it in the vortex until the end of your next turn, then hurls it to a point you choose in the area.

Anime-scale: Strategic scale: pulverize an army formation or devastate a city district with a true wind-god cyclone.
