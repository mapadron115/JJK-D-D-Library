Features & procedures

Exact player-facing procedures.

Weapon Equip • Level 5

Flabellum of the Wind God.

Weapon Equip turns the fan into a trident-like wind vessel and can form tornadoes around the wielder or at range.

Reaction • 2 Reserve

Wind Mantle

Gain +4 AC against a ranged attack or line effect you can see. If it misses/fails, redirect a mundane projectile or move the line’s origin 20 ft to either side.

Bonus Action • 2 Reserve

Cyclone Ride

Fly up to 120 ft without provoking. One willing creature within 10 ft may ride the current with you.

Action • 5 Reserve

Magul Al-Hazard

Create a 100-ft-radius tornado at a point within 240 ft. Strength save; 8 Djinn Dice slashing/thunder and lifted 60 ft on failure, half and no lift on success. The tornado lasts until the start of your next turn.

Full Djinn Equip • Level 9

Fly 140 ft; wind resistance (reduce thunder and nonmagical projectile damage by one Djinn Die); ranged attacks from more than 30 ft have disadvantage against you. You can freely shape harmless winds in 300 ft.

12 Reserve • Canon Extreme

Pa’ír Al-Hazard

Summon a 200-ft-radius, 600-ft-high thunder-roar tornado. Strength save; 17 Djinn Dice slashing/thunder, half on success. Failure lifts the target 150 ft, Restrains it in the vortex until the end of your next turn, then hurls it to a point you choose in the area.

Anime-scale: Strategic scale: pulverize an army formation or devastate a city district with a true wind-god cyclone.

The fiction. Jujutsu scholars classify the vessel as a weather report that became a contract: the metal houses a wind spirit whose CE answers only those willing to be carried. The prevailing theory holds that every tornado is the spirit remembering it once moved freely — the wielder merely points it.

Rules integration

Djinn Die, Equip & Extreme Magic

Independent Djinn Reserve: 14 (Rank B). Refreshes on a long rest. Every Djinn cost below — Weapon Equip, Full Equip, abilities, reactions, Extreme Magic — spends this Djinn’s Reserve, never your CE. If this Djinn’s Reserve is empty it simply cannot fuel more until you rest; your other Djinn keep their own separate Reserves.
Weapon Equip · level 5: bonus action, 2 Reserve, lasts 10 minutes.
Full Djinn Equip · level 9: bonus action, 4 Reserve, lasts 1 minute. Only one Equip can be active.
Deep Synchronization · level 11: once per short rest, one Equip activation costs 1 Reserve less.
Extreme Magic · level 13: action; requires that Djinn’s Full Equip; pay the listed Reserve cost from this Djinn’s Reserve; usable once per long rest per Djinn. Full Equip ends afterward, and you cannot Equip that Djinn again until you finish a short rest.
King Vessel · level 17: switch directly from one Full Equip to another as a bonus action, paying the new Equip cost. At level 20, once per long rest, immediately transition from one Djinn’s Extreme into another Djinn’s Full Equip.
Attack / save: use your CE attack modifier and CE save DC unless this Djinn explicitly says otherwise.
