The wind takes offense on her behalf. A ranged attack or line effect meets a wall of her own weather, and if it fails, she may steal it outright: a mundane arrow turned around, a line's origin shoved twenty feet sideways. The storm does not block blows. It returns them with interest.

She becomes the weather. Flight, resistance to the storm's own anger, ranged blows arriving already exhausted, and harmless winds she shapes at will across three hundred feet. The wielder no longer points at the storm. For one minute, she is the sky's local mood.

12 Reserve • Canon Extreme

Pa’ír Al-Hazard

Canon Extreme · 12 Reserve · Summon a 200-ft-radius, 600-ft-high thunder-roar tornado · Strength save or 17 Djinn Dice slashing and thunder (half on success); failure lifts the target 150 ft, Restrains it in the vortex until the end of your next turn, then hurls it to a point you choose in the area.

The true cyclone: two hundred feet wide, six hundred high, a thunder-roar that lifts, restrains, and hurls its victims to chosen points. Armies do not fight this. They are weathered by it. The final truth of Maniacal Love, the storm's devotion, expressed at the scale of districts.

Summon a 200-ft-radius, 600-ft-high thunder-roar tornado. Strength save; 17 Djinn Dice slashing/thunder, half on success. Failure lifts the target 150 ft, Restrains it in the vortex until the end of your next turn, then hurls it to a point you choose in the area.

Anime-scale: Strategic scale: pulverize an army formation or devastate a city district with a true wind-god cyclone.

Field notes. Subject: the wind that keeps its own counsel. Three truths govern this weather. First: every tornado is the spirit remembering it once moved freely, the wielder merely points, and the pointing is called Maniacal Love because the wind answers devotion with violence. Second: the same wind that carries you can scatter your enemies; rescue and ruin are the same current, merely aimed differently. Third: chaos is not the absence of order but its refusal, the wind's moods are a courtship, and the wielder has learned to dance with them rather than tame them. Mark the maxim: she does not command the storm. She is loved by it, and loved badly, and the difference is measured in city blocks.

Rules integration

Djinn Die, Equip & Extreme Magic

Damage-type chooser: when a Djinn ability lists two damage types, the Djinn King chooses which applies when the damage is dealt.
Independent Djinn Reserve: 14 (Rank B). Refreshes on a long rest. Every Djinn cost below, Weapon Equip, Full Equip, abilities, reactions, Extreme Magic, spends this Djinn’s Reserve, never your CE. If this Djinn’s Reserve is empty it simply cannot fuel more until you rest; your other Djinn keep their own separate Reserves.
Weapon Equip · level 5: bonus action, 2 Reserve, lasts 10 minutes.
Full Djinn Equip · level 9: bonus action, 4 Reserve, lasts 1 minute. Only one Equip can be active.
Deep Synchronization · level 11: once per short rest, one Equip activation costs 1 Reserve less.
Extreme Magic · level 13: action; requires that Djinn’s Full Equip; pay the listed Reserve cost from this Djinn’s Reserve; usable once per long rest per Djinn. Full Equip ends afterward, and you cannot Equip that Djinn again until you finish a short rest.
King Vessel · level 17: switch directly from one Full Equip to another as a bonus action, paying the new Equip cost. At level 20, once per long rest, immediately transition from one Djinn’s Extreme into another Djinn’s Full Equip.
Attack / save: use your CE attack modifier and CE save DC unless this Djinn explicitly says otherwise.
