Shurikens of Ice

The Golden Weapon of Ice. Thrown, they always return; where they strike, winter follows. A single throw can send an enemy into an ice age — frozen mid-scream, mid-step, mid-thought. The cold, like the other weapons, chooses who it serves.

Features & procedures

Properties & techniques

Exact player-facing procedures

Field notes. Subject: winter, thrown. Three truths govern it. First: the Shurikens do not deal cold damage the way a torch deals fire damage — they impose the condition of winter on the target, and the damage is winter doing what winter does. This is why Ice Age paralyzes rather than merely wounds: the weapon is not attacking the enemy, it is ending the season the enemy lives in. Second: the return is absolute. Thrown shurikens come back — not to the hand, to the thrower, which means around corners, through smoke, past intervening enemies; the only thing that stops the return is the weapon deciding you are done throwing. Third: the freeze is honest. A creature encased by Ice Age is not restrained by ice — it is ice-adjacent, its biology paused, which is why the thaw requires force rather than warmth. Zane understood this better than anyone: he was built cold, and the Shurikens recognized the kinship.

Relic profile

1d6 piercing (thrown 60/120 ft, returns instantly after the attack) · +4 to attack and damage rolls · Relic DC

Jujutsu origin

Forged by the First Spinjitzu Master from the gold of the Golden Peaks, one of four weapons that created Ninjago itself. The jujutsu reading: this is entropy with an edge. Where the Sword of Fire accelerates molecules, the Shurikens arrest them — not cold as a temperature, but stillness as a verdict. In cursed-energy terms, the weapon teaches CE to stop moving, and anything the CE touches stops with it.

Ice Age — Action · 5 CE

Throw at one creature within range. On a hit, the target makes a CON save vs your Relic DC: on a failure it is encased in ice — paralyzed — and takes 4d10 cold damage at the start of each of its turns. It may repeat the save at the end of each turn, ending the effect on a success; an ally may also free it with a STR check vs your Relic DC as an action. A creature reduced to 0 HP while encased shatters.

Blizzard Fan — Action · 4 CE

A 30-ft cone of killing frost. Each creature makes a DEX save vs your Relic DC: 5d10 cold damage on a failure, half on a success. Nonmagical flames are extinguished; water and wet surfaces freeze into difficult terrain for 1 minute.

Deep Freeze

Creatures killed by the Shurikens freeze solid at the moment of death. They cannot regenerate, reincarnate, or be revived except by first thawing the body (1 hour of work or any fire damage exceeding 20 in a single instance). Undead destroyed this way cannot reform.

Ever-Returning

The Shurikens return to your hand instantly after every attack, no action required. You may make opportunity attacks with them at their full thrown range, and the return path ignores cover — the weapon comes back to the thrower, not to a point in space.

Binding Vow — The Cold Chooses

The Shurikens grant their full power only to a wielder winter recognizes — the GM judges affinity (ice techniques, cold sorcery, a heart that runs cold). All others are refused: Ice Age and Deep Freeze are unavailable, Blizzard Fan deals half damage, and the shurikens return sluggishly — opportunity attacks with them are made at disadvantage. Winter does not hurry for strangers.

Rules integration

Cursed-tool attunement

Campaign canon

Cursed-tool attunement: you have PB attunement slots (2–6). This tool occupies one slot and requires a one-hour ritual. Attunement ends voluntarily over a short rest, on death, or when you replace it while over capacity. Distance does not break attunement. Cursed-tool attunement is separate from magic-item attunement.
