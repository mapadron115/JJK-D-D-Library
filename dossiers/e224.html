Kusanagi

1d8 finesse slashing. Weapon swings can store Gale when they cut through difficult terrain, vegetation, smoke, or a wind effect, max PB.

FormTachi
RoleWind / grass-cutting control
EngineGale
Attunement1 hour*
Properties & techniquesExact player-facing procedures

Feature

Spend Gale on hit to move target or self 5 ft each.

Grass-Cutting Arc

action, 2 CE: 30-ft cone Dex save, 2 Tool Dice slashing; clears mundane vegetation/smoke and weak environmental hazards.

Wind Turn

reaction, 2 CE: spend Gale to reduce ranged projectile damage by one Tool Die each and redirect mundane projectile harmlessly.

Release: Field Beneath the Storm

7 CE, 1 minute: Gale cap +2 and Arc 60-ft cone.

Balance & counterplay. Terrain interaction is the engine; it is not generic free wind magic.
