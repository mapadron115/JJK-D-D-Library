Features & procedures

Properties & techniques

Exact player-facing procedures

Feature

On hit · Spend Gale: move the target or yourself 5 ft per Gale spent.

Spend Gale on hitThe stored wind is not for wounding; it is for repositioning. Each Gale spent on a hit moves the target or the bearer five feet, the battlefield quietly rearranged by weather. to move target or self 5 ft each.

Grass-Cutting Arc

Action · 2 CE · 30-ft cone, Dex save: 2 Tool Dice slashing damage; clears mundane vegetation, smoke, and weak environmental hazards.

Two CE and an action, and the field is swept: a thirty-foot cone that wounds and clears alike, vegetation, smoke, small hazards, all denied to the enemy. action, 2 CE: 30-ft cone Dex save, 2 Tool Dice slashing; clears mundane vegetation/smoke and weak environmental hazards.

Wind Turn

Reaction · 2 CE · Spend Gale: reduce the ranged projectile's damage by one Tool Die per Gale spent; mundane projectiles are redirected harmlessly.

The defensive face of the harvest: a reaction, two CE, and the Gale answers the projectile, shaving a die off its damage and sending the mundane ones somewhere harmless. The wind refuses the arrow's appointment. reaction, 2 CE: spend Gale to reduce ranged projectile damage by one Tool Die each and redirect mundane projectile harmlessly.

Release: Field Beneath the Storm

7 CE · 1 minute: Gale storage cap +2; Grass-Cutting Arc doubles to a 60-ft cone.

Seven CE for one minute of the tool's whole doctrine: the Gale cap grows by two and the Arc doubles to sixty feet. For this minute, the field beneath the storm belongs to the bearer. 7 CE, 1 minute: Gale cap +2 and Arc 60-ft cone.

Field notes. Subject: a blade that learned to breathe. Three truths govern him. First, the tachi is not enchanted but educated: each swing through difficult terrain, vegetation, smoke, or wind teaches the blade to keep what it passes through, banking Gale, the field's own weather, stored like grain. Second, the stored weather is spent as movement, not damage: on a hit, Gale may move the struck or the striker five feet each, and Wind Turn spends it to turn a projectile aside, the storm's logistics, not its violence. Third, the Grass-Cutting Arc is the harvest and the clearing at once: a thirty-foot cone that cuts the field clean of vegetation, smoke, and lesser hazards, and under the Release it doubles to sixty with the Gale cap rising by two. Consider the registry's paradox: the sorcerer merely gives the blade something to cut, and the blade gives him back the wind of the whole field.

Rules integration

Cursed-tool attunement

Campaign canon

Cursed-tool attunement: you have PB attunement slots (2–6). This tool occupies one slot and requires a one-hour ritual. Attunement ends voluntarily over a short rest, on death, or when you replace it while over capacity. Distance does not break attunement. Cursed-tool attunement is separate from magic-item attunement.
Balance & counterplay. Terrain interaction is the engine; it is not generic free wind magic.
