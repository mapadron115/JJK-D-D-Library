Skill Hunter

Field notes. Subject: the politest robbery ever committed. Three truths govern Bandit's Secret. First: theft has manners. Within one hour, witness the ability, ask about it and be answered, any answer counts, even a refusal; silence does not, and press the book's palm to living flesh. Rite, not grab. Second: the library charges rent. Stolen pages work only while the book lies open in the dominant hand, a hand that can hold nothing else, though the bookmark keeps one page ready beside another; and if the owner dies, the page goes blank, unless their grudge is stronger than death. Third: the victim loses what you take. Theft, not borrowing. Mark the pinnacles: the Maximum waives the manners, sight alone inscribes, and the Domain deems the rite fulfilled against every creature inside, tearing one page free from each. Scholarship, executed perfectly, is indistinguishable from theft.

L1

Bandit's Secret

The founding rite. A black book, a bloody palm print, no limit of pages, and four conditions that must all be met within the hour. The victim loses the stolen feature while the page holds it: theft, not borrowing. As a bonus action, conjure a black book bearing a bloody palm print, it persists until dismissed and weighs nothing. The book grows a new page for every theft, canon sets no limit on how many stolen abilities it can hold. Theft is a rite with four conditions, all within 1 hour: (1) you witness the victim use one Innate Technique feature with your own eyes; (2) you ask the victim a question about that feature aloud, and the victim answers aloud, any answer counts, even a refusal; silence does not; (3) you press the book's palm print against the victim's flesh (an action; against an unwilling creature this requires a melee attack using your CE ability); (4) all three within the same hour. Complete the rite and the feature is inscribed: you may use it as though it were your own, spending your CE, using your CE save DC, provided your character level is at least the feature's level. The victim loses the stolen feature while your page holds it: theft, not borrowing.

Hand economy. A stolen ability can be invoked only while the book is held open in your dominant hand, that hand can hold nothing else and cannot perform somatic components for other techniques. Dismissing the book does not erase its pages. The finest library has the strictest hours.

Politeness, with teeth. The rite's question now compels a truthful, complete answer on a failed Wisdom save, the book leans in to listen. Manners, enforced. Your politeness has grown teeth. A victim that succeeds answers or refuses as it pleases, and a creature that succeeds is immune to this compulsion for 24 hours. Manners, enforced.

Domain Expansion: The Black Chapter

A library with no walls, shelves receding into dark in every direction, and every book on them is bound in a different skin of light. The books whisper when touched by a gaze, and the air is heavy with the particular silence of a place where everything has already been read. It is a theft made architecture.

A library with no walls, and every book in it is yours. The rite is deemed fulfilled against every creature inside; one feature torn free from each, unusable by its victim while the Domain stands. 18th level. Sure-hit domain, the sure-hit is the completed rite. Activation: full turn, 20 CE (D008 baseline for a normal Domain). Clash: 1000 points; burnout 3 rounds; duration 3 min (Tier V mapping); radius 120 ft.

Rules integration

Edge cases and shared systems.

Campaign canon notes

Cursed Energy baseline: CE pool is based on class; CE ability is Charisma or Wisdom (player's choice). CE save DC = 8 + CE ability modifier + proficiency bonus. Regain all spent CE on a long rest.
The four conditions: (1) witness the feature used with your own eyes; (2) ask about it aloud and receive any verbal answer, even a refusal; silence, magical or otherwise, does not satisfy it; (3) press the book's palm print to the victim's flesh; (4) all within one hour. Gagging or magical silence blocks condition 2.
Stolen means stolen: the victim loses the inscribed feature while your page holds it. Erasing the page, or the page fading, returns the feature to a living owner.
Level gate: you can only inscribe a feature whose level does not exceed your character level. Stolen features use your CE pool, your CE ability, and your CE save DC.
Hand economy: invoking requires the book open in your dominant hand (that hand holds nothing else); a bookmarked ability can be invoked while the book is open to another page (L3), and alongside an open-page ability from L6; at L20 the book hovers and every page counts as bookmarked.
Capacity: none, the book grows a new page for every theft. Canon (manga + databooks) sets no maximum; the old proficiency-bonus cap was an invented balance limit and is removed.
Death of the owner: the page fades within 24 hours of the original owner's death, the Neon precedent, unless post-mortem Nen preserves it, which is the GM's call and should be rare and terrible. (Sun and Moon are the famous exception: both creators died, and the pages stayed warm.)
Anti-loop: the book rejects copies of copies, you cannot steal a feature from another Bandit's Secret's page, nor inscribe a feature from your own book. A stolen page cannot be stolen again.
Domain activation defaults: rendered as full turn + 20 CE per the D008 normal-Domain baseline, with Tier V clash stats (1000 / 3-round burnout / 3 min) and a 120-ft radius.
Maximum vs. Reversal: Skill Hunter's pinnacle is written as a Maximum Technique (Imprimatur), not a Reversal; it occupies the technique's pinnacle slot.
