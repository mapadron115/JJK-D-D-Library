Skill Hunter

A conjured black book with a bloody palm print on its cover — Bandit's Secret. Chrollo Lucilfer's Specialist Hatsu steals other people's miracles, but theft has manners: within a single hour you must see the ability with your own eyes, ask its owner about it and be answered, and press the book's palm to their flesh. The stolen page works only while the book lies open in your dominant hand — though the bookmark keeps one page ready with the cover closed. And if the owner dies, the page goes blank. Unless their grudge is stronger than death.

Feature map

Level-by-level features

L1

Bandit's Secret

As a bonus action, conjure a black book bearing a bloody palm print — it persists until dismissed and weighs nothing. The book can hold a number of stolen abilities equal to your proficiency bonus. Theft is a rite with four conditions, all within 1 hour: (1) you witness the victim use one Innate Technique feature with your own eyes; (2) you ask the victim a question about that feature aloud, and the victim answers aloud — any answer counts, even a refusal; silence does not; (3) you press the book's palm print against the victim's flesh (an action; against an unwilling creature this requires a melee attack using your CE ability); (4) all three within the same hour. Complete the rite and the feature is inscribed: you may use it as though it were your own — spending your CE, using your CE save DC — provided your character level is at least the feature's level. The victim loses the stolen feature while your page holds it: theft, not borrowing.

Hand economy. A stolen ability can be invoked only while the book is held open in your dominant hand — that hand can hold nothing else and cannot perform somatic components for other techniques. Dismissing the book does not erase its pages. The finest library has the strictest hours.

L3

The Bookmark and the Catalogue

A blood-red ribbon now marks one page: your bookmarked ability can be invoked with the book closed (you must still hold it). As a bonus action, you may move the bookmark to a different page.

The Catalogue. As an action, run your hand down the book's spine: you learn the exact rules-text of every stolen ability, and you sense, page by page, whether each original owner still lives — a warm page breathes; a cold one is a grave. Every thief keeps accounts.

L6

Two Pages Open

In the Hisoka fight, the troupe leader turned two pages at once — and so can you. While the book is open in your dominant hand, you may invoke your bookmarked ability in the same round as an open-book ability (paying each cost normally, using each action type normally). The book drinks deep: each round you invoke two stolen abilities, you spend 1 additional CE per ability. Why read one miracle when you can read two?

L11

Due Diligence

A thief studies the mark before the mark knows he's studied. As an action, observe a creature for 1 minute (it need not be aware): you learn the names of every Innate Technique feature it possesses. This does not satisfy the rite's witnessing — you must still see a feature used — but you will know exactly what to watch for. The question is sharper when you know the answer's shape.

L15

The Question Compels

Your politeness has grown teeth. When you ask the rite's question, the victim must succeed on a Wisdom saving throw against your CE save DC or be compelled to answer truthfully and completely — the book leans in to listen. A victim that succeeds answers or refuses as it pleases, and a creature that succeeds is immune to this compulsion for 24 hours.

Additionally, the book's capacity grows: it now holds a number of stolen abilities equal to your proficiency bonus + 2. Manners, enforced.

L20

The Open Shelf

The book no longer needs your hands at all — it hovers open at your shoulder, turning its own pages, and every stolen ability is treated as bookmarked: invoke any of them with the book merely present. Your dominant hand is finally free.

The Lending Shelf. As an action, you may lend one stolen feature to a willing ally for 10 minutes: they invoke it spending their own CE and using your CE save DC. The page remains yours. The library lends — but never gives.

Pinnacle expression

Maximum and Domain.

Capstone material

Maximum: Imprimatur

For 1 minute, theft needs no manners. As an action, spend 10 CE: until the Maximum ends, any Innate Technique feature you witness being used is immediately inscribed in the book — no question, no palm-press, no hour. Capacity still applies; if the book is full, choose which page to overwrite. Stolen features follow all the normal rules of Bandit's Secret, including the victim losing the feature while the page holds it. The only signature required is sight.

Domain Expansion: The Black Chapter

18th level. Sure-hit domain — the sure-hit is the completed rite. Activation: full turn, 20 CE (D008 baseline for a normal Domain). Clash: 1000 points; burnout 3 rounds; duration 3 min (Tier V mapping); radius 120 ft.

Every Page Turns at Once. The domain is a library with no walls, and every book in it is yours. When the domain forms, the rite's four conditions are deemed fulfilled against every creature you can see inside: you may immediately inscribe one Innate Technique feature from each of them (capacity permitting; if the number of creatures exceeds your free capacity, choose which creatures' features to inscribe, and you may overwrite existing pages to make room (invented)), and each victim feels the page tear free — they cannot use the inscribed feature while the domain stands. Theft, executed perfectly, is indistinguishable from scholarship.

Feats

Five feats, shelved.

Each feat is optional, and you must meet its listed prerequisite before taking it.

Prerequisite: Bandit's Secret

Cold Reading

When you ask the rite's question, you also learn whether the victim's technique holds features you have not yet seen — and how many. You will know the size of what you haven't stolen.

Prerequisite: The Bookmark and the Catalogue

The Placed Ribbon

You may set a trigger on your bookmarked ability — a condition you name, such as "when I fall" or "when the bell rings." When it occurs, the ability invokes itself once using your reaction, then the ribbon goes slack until you reset it as a bonus action.

Prerequisite: Bandit's Secret

Palimpsest

As an action, erase one of your pages voluntarily. The impression remains: you learn one true secret of the former victim — their greatest fear, their hidden vow, the thing they never say aloud (GM's choice, always true). A living owner's feature returns to them.

Prerequisite: Two Pages Open

Footnote

When you invoke a stolen ability, you may speak its original owner's name aloud. They feel the tug instantly — and know, with cold certainty, that their ability was just used and roughly where, within 1 mile. Some thieves sign their work.

Prerequisite: The Question Compels

Witness by Proxy

Witnessing through your own scrying sensor, a familiar's eyes, or a true recorded image counts as witnessing for the rite's first condition. The book accepts secondhand sight — but the palm-press must still be flesh to flesh.

Rules integration

Edge cases and shared systems.

Campaign canon notes

Cursed Energy baseline: CE pool is based on class; CE ability is Charisma or Wisdom (player's choice). CE save DC = 8 + CE ability modifier + proficiency bonus. Regain all spent CE on a long rest.
The four conditions: (1) witness the feature used with your own eyes; (2) ask about it aloud and receive any verbal answer — even a refusal; silence, magical or otherwise, does not satisfy it; (3) press the book's palm print to the victim's flesh; (4) all within one hour. Gagging or magical silence blocks condition 2.
Stolen means stolen: the victim loses the inscribed feature while your page holds it. Erasing the page — or the page fading — returns the feature to a living owner.
Level gate: you can only inscribe a feature whose level does not exceed your character level. Stolen features use your CE pool, your CE ability, and your CE save DC.
Hand economy: invoking requires the book open in your dominant hand (that hand holds nothing else); a bookmarked ability needs only the held, closed book; at L20 the book hovers and every page counts as bookmarked.
Capacity: the book holds a number of stolen abilities equal to your proficiency bonus (proficiency bonus + 2 from L15).
Death of the owner: the page fades within 24 hours of the original owner's death — the Neon precedent — unless post-mortem Nen preserves it, which is the GM's call and should be rare and terrible.
Anti-loop: the book rejects copies of copies — you cannot steal a feature from another Bandit's Secret's page, nor inscribe a feature from your own book. A stolen page cannot be stolen again.
Domain activation defaults: rendered as full turn + 20 CE per the D008 normal-Domain baseline, with Tier V clash stats (1000 / 3-round burnout / 3 min) and a 120-ft radius.
Maximum vs. Reversal: Skill Hunter's pinnacle is written as a Maximum Technique (Imprimatur), not a Reversal; it occupies the technique's pinnacle slot.
