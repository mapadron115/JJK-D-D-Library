Feature map

Level-by-level features, as the figure refined them.

L1

Sign the Articles

During a rest.

Up to PB willing creatures become Crew during a rest. They can always leave at the next rest; there is no punishment for refusing renewal. Consent is the hull — a Crew member who turns hostile or acts under compulsion loses Crew status immediately, and while fewer than two willing Crew remain, the Fund locks.

L1

General Fund

Passive (Fund).

Each Crew member may deposit up to PB CE (Fund cap 2 × your level + PB); deposited CE leaves the personal pool. Once per turn, a Crew member within 60 ft may draw 1 CE to pay part of one legal CE cost. No CE is created — the Articles gather, they do not conjure.

L3

Article of Mutual Defense

Reaction · 2 Fund CE · once per round.

When a Crew member within 60 ft is about to roll a save or would take damage, another Crew member may spend 2 Fund CE to add PB to the roll or reduce the damage by 2 × PB before it is applied. The assister decides.

L6

Articles May Be Amended

After a short rest.

After a short rest, the Crew may unanimously add one table-written Article (GM-approved). Any signer rejecting means no amendment.

L11

Amnesty

Action.

Offer explicit surrender terms to one hostile that understands them. If accepted and witnessed, both sides are bound for 24 hours; a knowing violator takes 5 × PB psychic damage and loses PB CE. Terms cannot require suicide, slavery, soul-surrender, or unrelated future obedience.

L18

Pension & Exit

At objective's end.

At the end of an objective, any Crew member may leave permanently and withdraw up to their contribution. Amnesty-honorers cannot be hunted by the Crew for the term.

Pinnacle expression

Maximum, Domain, or equivalent.

Capstone material.

Maximum Technique

Red Flag Confederation

Action · 10 CE · 1 min.

The Fund's range becomes 120 ft; a Crew member may draw any amount for one activation, and other willing Crew may use their Reaction to contribute personal CE (up to PB each) toward one activation. May pay Domain Expansion costs (per zack's ruling).

Level 20 · Capstone

The Fleet Stands Down

Once per long rest.

Your Amnesty is no longer offered to one hostile — it may be offered to every hostile that can hear and understand the terms within 120 ft, all bound by the same witnessed 24-hour terms if they accept. She priced surrender until surrender was the winning move; eighty thousand pirates stood down, and so can this field.
