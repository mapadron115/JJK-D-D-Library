Core law

At-the-table character reference

Recovered printed values & campaign clarifications

Cube statistics are complete:

The cube's printed object statistics are complete as printed; use them instead of the generic barrier rules while the cube persists. AC equals your Cursed Energy save DC + 5; hit points equal your proficiency bonus times twice your Cursed Energy modifier; immunity to psychic and poison damage.

Reinforcement spending:

Reinforcement spending is part of the creation cost unless a feature says otherwise.

Immovable is literal:

"Immovable" is kept literally: the cube cannot be pushed or repositioned by forced movement, but it can still be destroyed.

Domain framing:

Primordial Gap resolves at initiative 20. Domain clashes use the deterministic 1–1,000 point system; barrier per D007; burnout per D010.

The fiction. Jujutsu scholars classify the Black Box as a denial that learned geometry: the user's cursed energy answered a moment when nothing could be allowed through, and has refused to unlearn it. The cube is not a barrier raised — it is a *no* given sides, which is why "immovable" is kept literal; a refusal this absolute does not negotiate with forced movement. At its peak the sorcerer wears the refusal as armor — the technique's final lesson being that the self, too, is something to keep intact.

Full progression

Feature map

L1Dark Cube

As an action for 2 cursed energy, you can create an immovable 10 feet opaque black cube within 30 feet of you for 1 minute or until you end it as a free action. If a Large or smaller creature is within range, they must make a Dexterity saving throw or be trapped within the cube. A creature inside your cube has full cover against attacks outside the cube and creatures other than you are blinded if there isn't a source of light inside the cube.

The cube has an AC equal to your Cursed Energy save DC + 5, hit points equal to your proficiency bonus times twice your Cursed Energy modifier and immunity to psychic and poison damage.

L3Black Box Reinforcement

Starting at 3rd level you can reinforce your barriers with cursed energy. You can spend additional cursed energy up to your Cursed Energy modifier to give your Dark Cube a reinforcement for every cursed energy spent:

Persistence. Your Cube gains 10 additional hit points for every 1 reinforcement, +1 bonus to AC for every 2 reinforcements and lasts an additional minute for every 3 reinforcements.

Toughness. Your Cube reduces all damage taken by 1 for each reinforcement.

Size. For every 2 reinforcements, you can make your Dark Cube 5 feet larger, allowing you to fit more creatures. (10 ft < 15 ft < 20ft)

At 11th level, you can spend up to twice your CE modifier on reinforcements.

L6Perfect Cube

At 6th level you have learned to create a nearly indestructible Dark Cube. For an additional 5 CE, you can create a Perfect Cube (this cursed energy counts towards the Cube's Reinforcements). Your Perfect Cube has resistance to all damage and ignores features or abilities that deal higher damage against objects and structures or would destroy, see into, or invade your barrier.

L11Maximum: Midnight Armor

At 11th level, you have learned to manifest your barrier around yourself. You can spend 6 cursed energy as an action to manifest parts of your cube around your body like armor for 1 minute or until the armor is destroyed: you gain the hit points of your barrier as temporary hit points; your AC equals your Dark Cube's AC; your unarmed strikes can use your CE modifier for attack rolls and deal 3d8 + CE modifier necrotic damage on a hit; you can spend additional cursed energy up to twice your cursed energy modifier to also gain the benefits of any reinforcement other than Size.

L18Domain Expansion: Primordial Gap

At 18th level you have mastered your technique, allowing you to expand your domain. In your domain all of your Dark Cubes automatically count as Perfect Cubes. At initiative 20, you create 3 Perfect Cubes, potentially trapping 3 creatures within your domain inside Perfect Cubes. They are treated as having Reinforcements up to your CE modifier. These cubes disappear when your domain ends.

Innate Technique Feats

Build-defining options.

These are optional character-building feats. You must meet each listed prerequisite before selecting it.

Prerequisite: Must have Dark Cube

Instant Manifestation

When you are hit by an attack, you may use your reaction to manifest a Dark Cube around yourself; the cube takes the damage in your stead, applying the damage to the cube’s statistics. If you took the Dodge action on your turn, you may extend the cube to also enclose one creature within 5 feet of you.

Prerequisite: Must have Black Box Reinforcement

Offensive Creations

You have focused on using your technique to devastating effects and have learned offensive applications of your cursed technique: Crushing Cube. As an action for 4 CE, you target a 30 feet tall, 10 feet wide square cylinder within 30 feet. All creatures in range must make a Strength saving throw. On a failure they take 5d10 necrotic damage and fall prone as they are crushed under a massive Dark Cube. On a success, they take half as much damage and are not prone. Block Strike. As a bonus action for 2 CE, you cover your fist or other part of your body with a Dark Cube for 1 minute. You can make a melee cursed energy attack with your unarmed strikes. On a hit you deal your proficiency bonus in d4s + your CE modifier in necrotic damage. Dark Press. As an action for 5 CE, you can create a Dark Cube in front of you that explodes in length on one side. All creatures in a 10 feet wide 60 feet long line must make a Dexterity saving throw. On a failure, they take 6d8 necrotic damage and are pushed back to the edge of the line. If they impact a solid object or structure, they take the remaining distance as fall damage.

Prerequisite: Must have Maximum: Midnight Armor

Sealing Cube

When you trap a creature in a Perfect Cube, you can choose to seal the output of the creature. They can only spend the minimum cursed energy on a cursed technique while trapped within your Perfect Cube and cannot use cursed techniques that cost more than 5 cursed energy.

Rules integration

Edge cases and shared systems.

Rules priority: the reviewed rulings, explicit codex clarifications, and the level progression always control when original wording conflicts. Legacy Domain auto-hit language, action terminology, event timing, missing saves, and older scaling never override revised player rules.
Why Tier III: an immovable, near-indestructible containment cube with complete printed object statistics, a full armor Maximum, and three offensive applications makes Black Box a premier battlefield tank without calamity-scale output.
Hold — Sealing Cube's minimum spend: the source does not define what the minimum cursed energy spend of a trapped creature's technique is. The "cannot use cursed techniques that cost more than 5 cursed energy" half is playable now; the minimum-spend half needs an agreed definition before play. Do not invent one.
