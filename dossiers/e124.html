Kryptonian

Solar charge / strength / flight / heat vision / senses

Power tierTier V · Calamity
Core law

What the technique actually does.

Core Engine — Solar Charge. Solar Charge max 10. Begin day at 5 after normal sunlight. Gain 1 per 10 minutes in strong yellow sunlight, or by dedicated solar absorption; red-sun effects drain 1/round. Kryptonite suppresses Charge spending while exposed.

Full progression

Level 1 through 20.

1Solar Body

Spend 1 Charge for 1 minute: resistance B/P/S, unarmed 1d10, jump ×4.

3Heat Vision

1 Charge + 1 CE: 90-ft line/target, 3d10 fire/radiant.

6Flight

Spend 1 Charge for 10 minutes, fly 60. Super Breath: 2 CE 30-ft cone, Str save push 30 or cold 3d8.

9Super Senses

See/hear extraordinary distances, advantage Perception; x-ray vision blocked by dense special materials and supernatural concealment.

11Man of Steel

Solar Body resistance includes elemental damage except psychic, necrotic, magic-specific weaknesses. Reaction spend 1 Charge to halve one damaging hit.

13Solar Flare

Spend all Charge minimum 5 + 5 CE: 30-ft radius 2d10 radiant per Charge, Con half. Charge becomes 0 and Solar features dormant until recharged.

15World of Cardboard

You may safely modulate strength. When you stop holding back, spend 2 Charge: for 1 minute unarmed attacks +2d10 force and lifting becomes colossal.

18Sun-Dipped

8 CE + 5 Charge, 1 minute: fly 120, resistance all but psychic/kryptonite/red-sun effects, attacks +3d10 radiant, regenerate PB HP/turn.

20Last Son

Charge max 15; start day at 10. First Charge spent each round is free while in direct yellow sunlight. Once/long rest survive lethal physical damage at 1 HP.

Pinnacle expression

Maximum, Domain, or equivalent.

Maximum — Infinite Mass Impact (10 CE + 5 Charge): fly at least 60 ft straight, then melee attack. On hit +12d12 force; target Str save or hurled 120 ft. Miss still creates 20-ft 6d12 shockwave. Name is stylistic—the move is not literally infinite mass.

Feats

Build-defining options.

Faster Than a Bullet

When an ally within 20 feet is targeted by an attack, you may use your reaction to move up to 20 feet to a space adjacent to them — you may fly for this movement without spending Charge. If you end the movement between the attacker and your ally, the attack targets you instead. Damage you take this way is halved while your Solar Body is active; the Man of Steel was built for exactly this.

Freeze Breath

When you use Super Breath, you may flash-freeze instead of pushing: targets make a Constitution save instead of Strength. On a failure they take the breath's cold damage and are Restrained until the end of your next turn, ice sheathing their limbs; on a success they take half damage and are not restrained. The ice melts at the end of your next turn, or early if the target takes 10 or more fire damage at once.

Hope

as an action for 1 Solar Charge, each ally within 30 ft who can see you may immediately attempt a new save against one Frightened effect, with advantage.

Sun-Drinker

when you take fire or radiant damage, you may use your reaction to absorb it: halve the damage and gain 1 Solar Charge. Once per round.

Rules integration

Edge cases and shared systems.

Codex rulings: Direct rolled technique/weapon attacks can use the campaign's Black Flash procedure when otherwise eligible. Passive damage, hazards, summons acting independently, and initiative-20 Domain events cannot.
Generic Maximum Output applies only to ordinary features, never named Maxima or Domains.
