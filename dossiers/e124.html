Power tierTier V · Calamity
Core law

What the technique actually does.

Core Engine — Solar Charge. Solar Charge max 10. Begin day at 5 after normal sunlight. Gain 1 per 10 minutes in strong yellow sunlight, or by dedicated solar absorption; red-sun effects drain 1/round. Kryptonite suppresses Charge spending while exposed.

The fiction. Jujutsu scholars call the Kryptonian engine photosynthetic reinforcement: the user's cursed energy cannot sustain itself from the body's own reserves, so it learned to feed on sunlight instead — drinking the day's light and spending it as the night's strength. The body is not armored; it is overcharged, every cell holding more energy than flesh was meant to carry. Red sun and kryptonite are not weaknesses in the poetic sense — they are simply the frequencies this metabolism cannot digest, and without fuel the engine is only a body.

Full progression

Level 1 through 20.

1Solar Body

Spend 1 Charge: for 1 minute you gain resistance to bludgeoning, piercing, and slashing, your unarmed strikes deal 1d10, and your jump distance is multiplied by 4.

3Heat Vision

Spend 1 Charge and 1 CE: affect a 90-ft line or one target, dealing 3d10 fire/radiant damage.

6Flight

Spend 1 Charge to fly at 60 ft for 10 minutes. You also gain Super Breath: for 2 CE, exhale a 30-ft cone; targets make a Strength save, and on a failure they are pushed 30 ft or take 3d8 cold damage.

9Super Senses

You can see and hear at extraordinary distances and have advantage on Perception checks; your x-ray vision is blocked by dense special materials and supernatural concealment.

11Man of Steel

Your Solar Body resistance now includes elemental damage except psychic, necrotic, and magic-specific weaknesses. As a reaction, you may spend 1 Charge to halve one damaging hit against you.

13Solar Flare

Spend all your Charge (minimum 5) plus 5 CE: creatures in a 30-ft radius take 2d10 radiant damage per Charge spent, with a Constitution save for half. Your Charge becomes 0 and your Solar features go dormant until you recharge.

15World of Cardboard

You may safely modulate your strength. When you stop holding back, spend 2 Charge: for 1 minute your unarmed attacks deal +2d10 force damage and your lifting capacity becomes colossal.

18Sun-Dipped

For 8 CE + 5 Charge, for 1 minute: you fly at 120 ft, gain resistance to all damage except psychic, kryptonite, and red-sun effects, your attacks deal +3d10 radiant damage, and you regenerate hit points equal to your proficiency bonus each turn.

20Last Son

Your Charge maximum becomes 15 and you begin each day at 10. The first Charge you spend each round is free while you are in direct yellow sunlight. Once per long rest, you may survive lethal physical damage at 1 HP.

Pinnacle expression

Maximum, Domain, or equivalent.

Maximum — Infinite Mass Impact (10 CE + 5 Charge): fly at least 60 ft straight, then melee attack. On hit +12d12 force; target Str save or hurled 120 ft. Miss still creates 20-ft 6d12 shockwave. Name is stylistic—the move is not literally infinite mass.

Feats

Build-defining options.

Faster Than a Bullet

When an ally within 20 feet is targeted by an attack, you may use your reaction to move up to 20 feet to a space adjacent to them — you may fly for this movement without spending Charge. If you end the movement between the attacker and your ally, the attack targets you instead. Damage you take this way is halved while your Solar Body is active; the Man of Steel was built for exactly this.

Freeze Breath

When you use Super Breath, you may flash-freeze instead of pushing: targets make a Constitution save instead of Strength. On a failure they take the breath's cold damage and are Restrained until the end of your next turn, ice sheathing their limbs; on a success they take half damage and are not restrained. The ice melts at the end of your next turn, or early if the target takes 10 or more fire damage at once.

Hope

as an action for 1 Solar Charge, each ally within 30 ft who can see you may immediately attempt a new save against one Frightened effect, with advantage.

Sun-Drinker

when you take fire or radiant damage, you may use your reaction to absorb it: halve the damage and gain 1 Solar Charge. Once per round.

Rules integration

Edge cases and shared systems.

Codex rulings: Direct rolled technique/weapon attacks can use the campaign's Black Flash procedure when otherwise eligible. Passive damage, hazards, summons acting independently, and initiative-20 Domain events cannot.
Generic Maximum Output applies only to ordinary features, never named Maxima or Domains.
