Core law

What the technique actually does.

Core Engine, Charge. The coin purse. Max ten, opens each day at five after ordinary sunlight; ten minutes in strong yellow light earns one, dedicated absorption earns more, and red-sun effects drain it while kryptonite locks the spending. Charge max 10. Begin day at 5 after normal sunlight. Gain 1 per 10 minutes in strong yellow sunlight, or by dedicated solar absorption (1 minute of concentration in direct yellow sunlight → 1 Charge); red-sun effects drain 1/round. Kryptonite suppresses Charge spending while exposed.

Field notes. Subject: a god on an allowance. Three truths govern him. First: his cursed energy cannot sustain itself, it drinks the day's light and spends it as the night's miracles; Charge is the coin purse, and the sun is the only mint. Second: the purse has rules, it maxes at ten, opens each day at five after ordinary sunlight, refills ten minutes at a time in strong yellow light, and red-sun effects drain it round by round. Third: the spending escalates from the body outward, resistance, vision, flight, until the Solar Flare empties the entire treasury at once and everything goes dormant. Mark the economy: he is invincible only while the account is full, and the account is full only while the sky cooperates.

Full progression

Level 1 through 20.

1Solar Body

Action · 1 Charge · For 1 minute: resistance to bludgeoning, piercing, and slashing; your unarmed strikes deal 1d10; your jump distance is multiplied by 4.

One Charge for a minute of the sun's opinion of him: resistance to the physical, 1d10 fists, and a fourfold leap. The body remembers being heavier than weather.

3Heat Vision

Action · 1 Charge + 1 CE · Affect a 90-ft line: creatures in it make a Dexterity save, taking 3d10 fire/radiant on a failure, half on a success; or strike one target with a ranged CE attack for 3d10 fire/radiant on a hit.

One Charge and one CE: a ninety-foot line of fire and radiance for three dice, or a single target struck at range. The stare, filed as artillery.

6Flight

Action · 1 Charge · Fly at 60 ft for 10 minutes. You also gain Super Breath: 2 CE to exhale a 30-ft cone; targets make a Strength save, and on a failure they are pushed 30 ft or take 3d8 cold damage.

One Charge for ten minutes at sixty feet, and Super Breath, two CE for a thirty-foot cone: strength to push or cold to punish. The lungs are also weather.

9Super Senses

Passive · See and hear up to 1 mile with a clear line; advantage on Perception checks; x-ray vision is blocked by dense special materials and supernatural concealment.

The dividends of the allowance: sight and hearing to a mile, advantage on Perception, though dense materials and the supernatural still keep their secrets.

11Man of Steel

Passive · Solar Body resistance now includes acid, cold, fire, lightning, and thunder (still not psychic, necrotic, or named weaknesses). Reaction · 1 Charge: halve one damaging hit against you.

The resistance broadens to the elements, acid, cold, fire, lightning, thunder, everything but the psychic, the necrotic, and his named weaknesses, and one Charge as a reaction halves a single blow.

13Solar Flare

Action · Spend all your Charge (minimum 5) + 5 CE · Creatures in a 30-ft radius take 2d10 radiant damage per Charge spent (Constitution save for half); your Charge becomes 0 and Solar features go dormant until you recharge.

The treasury emptied at once: all Charge, minimum five, plus five CE, two dice per Charge in thirty feet, Constitution for half, and then dormancy until he recharges. The sun's whole opinion, spent in a gesture.

15World of Cardboard

Action · 2 Charge · For 1 minute: your unarmed attacks deal +2d10 force damage and your lifting capacity becomes colossal.

He stops holding back on purpose: two Charge for a minute of unmodulated strength, two extra force dice per unarmed strike and a colossal lift. Restraint, filed as a choice.

18Sun-Dipped

Action · 8 CE + 5 Charge · For 1 minute: fly 120 ft; resistance to all damage except psychic, kryptonite, and red-sun effects; your attacks deal +3d10 radiant damage; regenerate hit points equal to your PB each turn.

Eight CE and five Charge for a minute of overcharge: flight at one-twenty, resistance to all but the named weaknesses, three radiant dice per attack, and his proficiency in healing each turn. He has been somewhere the sun is closer.

20Last Son

Passive · Charge maximum 15, begin each day at 10; the first Charge you spend each round is free in direct yellow sunlight. Special · Once per long rest: survive lethal physical damage at 1 HP.

The purse grows to fifteen and opens each day at ten; in direct yellow sunlight the first Charge each round is free; and once per long rest, lethal physical damage is survived at 1 HP. The allowance becomes a trust fund.

Pinnacle expression

Maximum, Domain, or equivalent.

Maximum, World of Cardboard (10 CE + any amount of Charge): one single strike, or a 30-ft shockwave: +2d12 force per Charge spent. Until the end of your next turn, your unarmed strikes deal double dice. He has spent his whole life living in a world of cardboard — holding back, always holding back, lest the doorknob shatter in his hand. The Maximum is the day he stops.

Feats

Build-defining options.

Faster Than a Bullet

When an ally within twenty feet is targeted, a reaction carries him up to twenty feet to their side, flying, without spending, and he may interpose. The body arrives before the bullet's paperwork. If you end the movement between the attacker and your ally, the attack targets you instead. Damage you take this way is halved while your Solar Body is active; the Man of Steel was built for exactly this.

Freeze Breath

The breath learns winter: flash-freeze instead of push, Constitution instead of Strength, and failure leaves the target Restrained until his next turn ends. When you use Super Breath, you may flash-freeze instead of pushing: targets make a Constitution save instead of Strength. On a failure they take the breath's cold damage and are Restrained until the end of your next turn, ice sheathing their limbs; on a success they take half damage and are not restrained. The ice melts at the end of your next turn, or early if the target takes 10 or more fire damage at once.

Hope

One Charge and an action: every ally within thirty feet who can see him may reroll one Frightened save with advantage. The allowance pays out in courage.

Sun-Drinker

Fire and radiant damage become income: a reaction halves it and banks one Charge, once per round. He is taxed by the sun and keeps the change.

Rules integration

Edge cases and shared systems.

Codex rulings: Direct rolled technique/weapon attacks can use the campaign's Black Flash procedure when otherwise eligible. Passive damage, hazards, summons acting independently, and initiative-20 Domain events cannot.
Generic Maximum Output applies only to ordinary features, never named Maxima or Domains.
