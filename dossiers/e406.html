Perfume of Bewitching

A Grade 4 cursed item, a single-use vial of cursed perfume.

FormCursed Item
GradeGrade 4
AttunementNot required

Field notes. Subject: the gentlest siege. One vial, one wearing, one hour, and for that hour, anyone close enough to smell you finds you very persuasive. Two truths govern it. First, the weapon is proximity: advantage on Deception and Persuasion, but only against creatures within five feet, the radius of a held hand, a leaned-in whisper, a shared doorway. It does not command; it inclines. Second, the price is total: the vial is expended in a single use, and the hour cannot be banked or divided. Mark this: every fortress in this archive was built against force. None was built against being liked.

Features & procedures

Properties & techniques

Exact player-facing procedures

Action

Apply the Perfume

Action · Apply to yourself · Vial is expended after a single use; effect lasts 1 hour.

An action to wear it, and the vial is spent, one wearing, one hour, no remainder. The softest weapons are also the most perishable. As an action, you can apply the perfume to yourself. The vial is expended after a single use, and the effect lasts 1 hour.

Passive

Bewitching Presence

Passive · For the hour: Advantage on Charisma (Deception and Persuasion) checks to influence a creature within 5 feet of you.

For the hour, every lie and every plea lands easier, advantage on Deception and Persuasion, but only within five feet. The perfume does not reach across rooms. It reaches across tables. For the duration, you have Advantage on all Charisma (Deception and Persuasion) checks made to influence a creature within 5 feet of yourself.
