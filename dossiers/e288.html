Teleportation

You can teleport yourself and others to marked locations while not being viewed.

Power tierTier III · Apex
Combat roleSupport
Core engineCursed-energy mark network
DomainCrossroads of Instance (L18)
Core law

Completed rules

Teleport-spell conversion:

"casts Teleport" references the 5e Teleport spell with the technique's constraints (marks only, always exact) overriding the spell's familiarity/mishap table — the "always go to the exact mark" replaces the mishap roll. The spell's other text (e.g. associated objects) does not apply. The unwilling-teleport CE save stands; the CE is spent on a successful save. Marks on living creatures end if the creature dies (AT236).

The fiction. Registry notes describe the technique as a refusal to fully arrive: the user's cursed energy never commits entirely to where the body stands, and every mark is a place the sorcerer has, in some sense, already declined to leave. The prevailing theory holds that the unviewed constraint is the technique's confession — the energy can slip away only when no one is insisting on its location, because observation is an anchor and no eye is watching every mark at once.

Full progression

Feature map

L1Teleport

Beginning at 1st level, your technique allows you to teleport to destinations you have marked. As a bonus action for 1 cursed energy, you can mark a person, location, or object which you can touch. You can have up to twice your CE modifier in marks at once and your marks remain for up to 12 hours. You can remove any of your marks at will.

As an action for 2 cursed energy, while not being viewed, you and any willing creatures within 5ft of you that are also not being viewed teleport up to 500 feet to a mark of your choice.

“While not being viewed” means no creature has line of sight to you — invisibility, heavy obscurement, fog, and closed or blocked sight all qualify.

L3Improved Teleport

At 3rd level, you have improved your technique. The range of your teleport now increases to (your CE modifier + your proficiency bonus + your level) x 100 feet, and you can now teleport as a reaction to an attack or saving throw as long as you have a way to cause yourself to disappear from view (such as covering yourself with a sheet).

L6Mastered Teleport

At 6th level, with great training, you have reached the pinnacle of your teleport. As an action for 8 cursed energy you cast Teleport. You can only go to marks and you always go to the exact mark you want to go to, regardless of how far away from you they are. You are now able to teleport others while you do not leave. You can now choose to only teleport other creatures when using any teleport feature. Additionally, you can attempt to teleport an unwilling creature within 5 ft. They must make a Cursed Energy saving throw. If they fail, they are teleported to one of your marks.

Additionally, your markings now last for a week instead of 12 hours.

L11Improved Marking

At 11th level, you have learned to mark things more efficiently. You can now mark as a free action instead of a bonus action and your amount of markings is increased to 3 times your CE modifier.

L18Domain Expansion: Crossroads of Instance

At 18th level, you have mastered your technique allowing you to expand your domain. All space within your domain is treated as being marked by you. At initiative 20, you can change the locations of a number of creatures up to twice your CE modifier, teleporting them anywhere within your domain, regardless of whether they can be seen. Additionally, you can teleport a creature as a reaction.

Maximum: Soul Swap.

Prerequisite: Crossroads of Instance. With some creative thinking you've figured out an entirely brand new way to use your technique, swapping the souls of creatures. As an action with two willing creatures within 15 feet of you, you can swap the souls of the creatures. For 1 hour or until you dismiss it at will (no action required), all of the creatures' game statistics are swapped except for their mental stats (Intelligence, Wisdom, and Charisma) and any non-Physical feats if they had one. However, they lose access to their innate technique features and gain access to the other's technique. They also gain the other's cursed energy, hit points, racial features, and weapon proficiencies. When the swap ends both creatures are returned to their original bodies, reverting the changes to game statistics, but each creature can ignore one prerequisite of any feats the other creature used while in their body, except for prerequisites that require you to be or not be a certain race, prerequisites that require you to have a certain cursed technique, prerequisites that require other feats, prerequisites that require a breakthrough, or prerequisites that require you to be a certain class. A creature can only be swapped twice in a one month timeframe, after which you must wait one month before swapping that creature again. If a creature dies while swapped, the other dies once the duration ends.

Innate Technique Feats

Build-defining options.

These are optional character-building feats. You must meet each listed prerequisite before selecting it.

Prerequisite: Must have Mastered Teleport

Crossed Marks

Your marks are doors, and doors open both ways. As an action for 4 cursed energy, you can transpose two creatures or objects that both bear your marks, instantly trading their positions no matter the distance between them. An unwilling creature can make a Cursed Energy saving throw to resist being moved.

Prerequisites: Must have Improved Marking

Warded Marks

Your marks can watch as well as carry. When you place a mark, you can spend 1 additional cursed energy to ward it: you are mentally alerted when a creature you did not designate comes within 10 feet of the warded mark, and you learn the creature's size category. You can also set a warded mark to flare when triggered, shedding bright light in a 20-foot radius as a silent alarm.

Rules integration

Edge cases and shared systems.

Rules priority: the reviewed rulings, explicit codex clarifications, and the level progression always control when original wording conflicts. The audit dispositions for AT235/AT236/AT237 are adopted as a standalone technique per zack's promotion decision, with the exact changes noted above.
Soul Swap consent: Soul Swap works only on willing participants. A creature under charm, compulsion, or coercion does not count as willing (zack's ruling, AT237). CE farming across bodies is allowed — statistics AND current CE totals swap; this is skill expression, not an exploit. The death clause stands as printed for willing participants: if a creature dies while swapped, the other dies when the duration ends, bypassing death saves and finite RCT.
Domain conversion: Crossroads of Instance is adopted with campaign conversion: the initiative-20 relocation stands as the sure-hit event; clash via the point system; the reaction teleport is a domain-granted reaction (one per round, costs the user's reaction) (AT237).
Migration from the V.3 feat: characters who already hold the V.3 Teleportation feat (Limitless users, prereq Blue + CE 20) are grandfathered as a legacy feat, "Teleportation (Legacy)": action, teleport 10 ft per CE spent, no marks required, Limitless-holders only; it does not grant the technique's mark system and cannot be taken by new characters. zack may flip this to conversion-to-1st-level-Teleport on request (AT235).
