Completed rules

Teleport-spell conversion:

The conversion is filed first because the technique borrows a spell’s name and keeps none of its habits. "casts Teleport" references the 5e Teleport spell with the technique's constraints (marks only, always exact) overriding the spell's familiarity/mishap table, the "always go to the exact mark" replaces the mishap roll. The spell's other text (e.g. associated objects) does not apply. The unwilling-teleport CE save stands; the CE is spent on a successful save. Marks on living creatures end if the creature dies (AT236).

Field notes. Subject: a timetable with no fixed station. Three truths govern it. First: this is not travel, it is relocation, the sorcerer never crosses the distance; the marks are destinations the technique has already agreed to, and the user simply insists on being there. The mark is laid by touch and kept for hours; the teleport spends two coins. The whole economy runs on preparation, not power. Second: the unviewed constraint is the confession. Observation is an anchor, no eye can watch every mark at once, so the energy slips where it is not being watched, and even the reaction-dodge requires a way to vanish first. Third: the Maximum applies the same principle to identity. Soul Swap treats the body as just another location: two willing creatures trade statistics, techniques, and cursed energy, and the death clause stands as written. Nothing about it ever arrives. Everything simply stops being elsewhere.

Full progression

Feature map

1Teleport.

Bonus action · 1 CE · Mark a person, location, or object you can touch · Max 2× CE modifier marks (3× at 11th); marks last 12 hrs (1 week at 6th); remove at will.

The foundation is a ledger of places, a bonus action and a coin lay a mark by touch, kept for twelve hours, and an action and two coins relocate the unviewed to any mark within five hundred feet. Beginning at 1st level, your technique allows you to teleport to destinations you have marked. As a bonus action for 1 cursed energy, you can mark a person, location, or object which you can touch. You can have up to twice your CE modifier in marks at once and your marks remain for up to 12 hours. You can remove any of your marks at will.

Action · 2 CE · While not being viewed (no line of sight to you): you + willing creatures within 5 ft (also unviewed) teleport up to 500 ft to a mark of your choice.

As an action for 2 cursed energy, while not being viewed, you and any willing creatures within 5ft of you that are also not being viewed teleport up to 500 feet to a mark of your choice.

“While not being viewed” means no creature has line of sight to you, invisibility, heavy obscurement, fog, and closed or blocked sight all qualify.

3Improved Teleport.

Upgrades · Teleport range: (CE mod + PB + level) × 100 ft · Reaction (to an attack or saving throw): teleport to dodge — needs a way to vanish from view; resolves before the triggering attack's hit is determined.

At the third level the range becomes a formula and the teleport becomes a dodge, vanish from view as a reaction and the triggering attack resolves against empty air. At 3rd level, you have improved your technique. The range of your teleport now increases to (your CE modifier + your proficiency bonus + your level) x 100 feet, and you can now teleport as a reaction to an attack or saving throw as long as you have a way to cause yourself to disappear from view (such as covering yourself with a sheet). The teleport resolves before the triggering attack's hit is determined, so it can dodge the attack.

6Mastered Teleport.

Action · 8 CE · Cast Teleport to any mark, exact arrival, any distance · Can teleport others without going yourself · Unwilling creature within 5 ft: CE save or teleported to one of your marks.

The sixth level is the full timetable: cast Teleport to any mark exactly, no mishaps; send others without going; drag the unwilling along on a failed cursed-energy save; and the marks now keep for a week. At 6th level, with great training, you have reached the pinnacle of your teleport. As an action for 8 cursed energy you cast Teleport. You can only go to marks and you always go to the exact mark you want to go to, regardless of how far away from you they are. You are now able to teleport others while you do not leave. You can now choose to only teleport other creatures when using any teleport feature. Additionally, you can attempt to teleport an unwilling creature within 5 ft. They must make a Cursed Energy saving throw. If they fail, they are teleported to one of your marks.

Additionally, your markings now last for a week instead of 12 hours.

11Improved Marking.

Upgrade · Mark as a free action; max marks = 3× CE modifier.

At the eleventh level the ledger keeps itself, marks laid as a free action, three times the modifier in standing destinations. At 11th level, you have learned to mark things more efficiently. You can now mark as a free action instead of a bonus action and your amount of markings is increased to 3 times your CE modifier.

18Domain Expansion: Crossroads of Instance.

Domain (18th level) · All space inside counts as your mark · Initiative 20: teleport up to 2× CE modifier creatures anywhere in the domain (seen or not) · Reaction: teleport a creature.

The barrier's interior fractures into a lattice of glowing lines, every intersection a crossroads, every crossroads a door. The ground becomes a vast diagram, and each creature inside stands at the center of their own small constellation of marks, lit from beneath. Space here is not continuous, it is indexed.

The Domain declares the whole battlefield a mark, at initiative twenty the user rearranges up to twice the modifier in creatures anywhere inside, seen or not, and keeps a reaction teleport in hand. At 18th level, you have mastered your technique allowing you to expand your domain. All space within your domain is treated as being marked by you. At initiative 20, you can change the locations of a number of creatures up to twice your CE modifier, teleporting them anywhere within your domain, regardless of whether they can be seen. Additionally, you can teleport a creature as a reaction.

Maximum: Soul Swap.

Action · Two willing creatures within 15 ft swap souls for 1 hr (dismiss at will, no action): all game statistics swapped except mental stats and non-physical feats; they lose their innate technique and gain the other's, plus the other's CE, HP, racial features, weapon proficiencies · Feat prerequisites from borrowed feats: ignore one each (except race, technique, feat, breakthrough, class requirements) · A creature can be swapped twice per month · If one dies while swapped, the other dies when the duration ends.

Prerequisite: Crossroads of Instance. The Maximum treats the body as just another location, two willing souls trade every statistic but the mental, every technique, every coin of cursed energy, for an hour, with the death clause standing as written. With some creative thinking you've figured out an entirely brand new way to use your technique, swapping the souls of creatures. As an action with two willing creatures within 15 feet of you, you can swap the souls of the creatures. For 1 hour or until you dismiss it at will (no action required), all of the creatures' game statistics are swapped except for their mental stats (Intelligence, Wisdom, and Charisma) and any non-Physical feats if they had one. However, they lose access to their innate technique features and gain access to the other's technique. They also gain the other's cursed energy, hit points, racial features, and weapon proficiencies. When the swap ends both creatures are returned to their original bodies, reverting the changes to game statistics, but each creature can ignore one prerequisite of any feats the other creature used while in their body, except for prerequisites that require you to be or not be a certain race, prerequisites that require you to have a certain cursed technique, prerequisites that require other feats, prerequisites that require a breakthrough, or prerequisites that require you to be a certain class. A creature can only be swapped twice in a one month timeframe, after which you must wait one month before swapping that creature again. If a creature dies while swapped, the other dies once the duration ends.

Innate Technique Feats

Build-defining options.

These are optional character-building feats. You must meet each listed prerequisite before selecting it.

Prerequisite: Must have Mastered Teleport

Crossed Marks

The marks become doors that open both ways, four coins transpose any two marked creatures or objects across any distance, the unwilling saving to resist. Your marks are doors, and doors open both ways. As an action for 4 cursed energy, you can transpose two creatures or objects that both bear your marks, instantly trading their positions no matter the distance between them. An unwilling creature can make a Cursed Energy saving throw to resist being moved.

Prerequisites: Must have Improved Marking

Warded Marks

The marks learn to watch as well as carry, one more coin wards a mark, and the user is alerted when the uninvited come within ten feet. Your marks can watch as well as carry. When you place a mark, you can spend 1 additional cursed energy to ward it: you are mentally alerted when a creature you did not designate comes within 10 feet of the warded mark, and you learn the creature's size category. You can also set a warded mark to flare when triggered, shedding bright light in a 20-foot radius as a silent alarm.

Rules integration

Edge cases and shared systems.

Rules priority: the reviewed rulings, explicit codex clarifications, and the level progression always control when original wording conflicts. The audit dispositions for AT235/AT236/AT237 are adopted as a standalone technique per zack's promotion decision, with the exact changes noted above.
Soul Swap consent: Soul Swap works only on willing participants. A creature under charm, compulsion, or coercion does not count as willing (zack's ruling, AT237). CE farming across bodies is allowed, statistics AND current CE totals swap; this is skill expression, not an exploit. The death clause stands as printed for willing participants: if a creature dies while swapped, the other dies when the duration ends, bypassing death saves and finite RCT.
Domain conversion: Crossroads of Instance is adopted with campaign conversion: the initiative-20 relocation stands as the sure-hit event; clash via the point system; the reaction teleport is a domain-granted reaction (one per round, costs the user's reaction) (AT237).
Migration from the V.3 feat: characters who already hold the V.3 Teleportation feat (Limitless users, prereq Blue + CE 20) are grandfathered as a legacy feat, "Teleportation (Legacy)": action, teleport 10 ft per CE spent, no marks required, Limitless-holders only; it does not grant the technique's mark system and cannot be taken by new characters. zack may flip this to conversion-to-1st-level-Teleport on request (AT235).
Feat availability: the reviewed Innate Technique Feats list (Crossed Marks, Warded Marks) is exhaustive; Perfected Teleport and Unlimited Marking Works, which appear only in the appendix, are not offered.
