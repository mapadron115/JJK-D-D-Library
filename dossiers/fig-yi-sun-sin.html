Feature map

Level-by-level features, as the figure refined them.

L1

Draw the Strait

Action · 2 CE · Focus, up to 1 min.

Two anchors within 60 ft of you (≤30 ft apart); the line between them is a Current Line with a chosen direction. Crossing carries creatures 10 ft parallel — willing creatures may accept; unwilling make a STR save to resist.

L1

Reversing Tide

At initiative 20 · automatic (1 CE to prevent one reversal).

The line reverses direction automatically at initiative 20 (you may spend 1 CE to prevent one reversal). The direction is always visible — a timing puzzle, not a trap.

L3

Anchor Ships

Passive.

Up to PB willing creatures ignore the lines' forced movement and may ride 10 ft along the current for 5 ft of movement, once per turn each.

L6

Crane Wing

Bonus Action · 2 CE.

Open a second Current Line sharing one anchor — a bent channel. An unwilling creature carried across the second line makes a DEX save: fail = carried again and knocked prone; success = stops, stays standing.

L11

Twelve Against Hundreds

Passive.

While conscious hostiles within 60 ft outnumber allies at least 2:1, hostile saves against the lines are at disadvantage and forced movement becomes 15 ft. Ends the instant the condition fails.

L18

Do Not Announce My Death

Reaction · at 0 HP while a Current effect is active.

When you drop to 0 HP while a Current effect is active, your Reaction makes the technique self-sustaining for 1 minute — no Focus required, reversals continue, anchors persist. You take no actions; the lines remain destructible.

Pinnacle expression

Maximum, Domain, or equivalent.

Capstone material.

Maximum Technique

Myeongnyang: The Roaring Strait

Action · 10 CE · Focus, 1 min.

Anchors to 60 ft apart, with a 30-ft-wide current zone; the zone reverses each initiative 20. Hostiles entering the zone or starting their turn inside make a STR save — fail = moved 20 ft with the current. Anchored allies ride free and never collide.

Level 20 · Capstone

Noryang: The Battle Continued

Once per long rest.

When Do Not Announce My Death triggers, the self-sustaining current lasts until the encounter ends (not 1 minute), and once while it runs you may take one full turn — action, bonus action, movement — issued through the current itself, before going still. The fleet never knew; the battle continued.
