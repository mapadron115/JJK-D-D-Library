Long Step Procession

Turn distance itself into a resource: long, uncanny strides create Steps that can evade attacks, bridge terrain, or leave walking echoes behind.

InspirationFresno Nightcrawlers
RoleEvasion / Positioning Controller
TierTier II · Versatile
Core statDEX or CE stat
Scaling dieStep Die

Character fantasy

Turn distance itself into a resource: long, uncanny strides create Steps that can evade attacks, bridge terrain, or leave walking echoes behind.

Combat loop

  1. Keep moving to accumulate Steps.
  2. Spend Steps to ignore terrain, dodge through attack windows, or reposition allies.
  3. Leave Procession paths and movement Echoes behind.
  4. Use prior positions as a second battlefield only you fully understand.

Long Steps

  • Step cap: PB. The first time each turn you move 10 feet without being forcibly moved, gain 1 Step for every full 10 feet moved, up to 2 Steps per turn. Steps vanish if you end two consecutive turns without moving at least 10 feet.
  • Long-Legged Body: your reach for unarmed strikes is 10 feet while you have at least 1 Step. Your appearance may become a pale torso balanced on absurdly long legs.

11Processional EchoesAt the end of your turn, if you moved 30+ feet, create up to PB Echoes in spaces you occupied. They last until start of your next turn. Attacks against you from more than 10 feet away have disadvantage while at least one Echo is visible. As a bonus action, spend 2 Steps to swap positions with one Echo within 60 feet.
18Domain Expansion: Footage That Should Not ExistFull-turn action, 20 CE, 90-foot radius, 1 minute. Every creature leaves a translucent frame at the position where it ended its previous turn. At initiative 20 choose each hostile creature and one of its own frames within 60 feet. The target is snapped to that frame and cannot react until the movement ends. A creature protected from the sure-hit makes a Dexterity save, preventing the snap on success. You may swap with one of your own frames at initiative 20 without spending Steps. The Domain does not rewind HP, CE, conditions, or actions—only recorded position.
Innate Technique FeatsOptional character-building feats

Tall Enough

Your standing jump distance and height double, and you may step over Large or smaller creatures without treating their spaces as difficult terrain.

Walk Behind the Camera

If you end your turn in dim light or darkness after moving 30+ feet, you may Hide without an action once per round.

Friendly Procession

Allies using Procession Path may travel 15 feet instead of 10.

Dropped Frame

Prerequisite: Processional Echoes. When an attack misses you due to Echo disadvantage, gain 1 Step.

Building the character

Scout is the obvious shell, but any character who wants mobility support can use it. Dexterity matters if you fight through Stilt Kick; a pure controller can invest almost entirely in CE stat.

Technique die: Step Die follows the shared d6 → d8 → d10 → d12 progression.

Counterplay

Restrict movement, flood the area with persistent hazards, destroy lines of sight to Echoes, or force the user to hold a fixed objective.

Adaptation note. This is original JJK 5e homebrew inspired by Fresno Nightcrawlers. The myth/folklore provides imagery and thematic rules; the combat engine, progression, CE economy, feats, and Domain/Maximum procedures are game design for this codex.
