Core law

Character fantasy

Turn distance itself into a resource: long, uncanny strides create Steps that can evade attacks, bridge terrain, or leave walking echoes behind.

Combat loop

Stride, bank, spend, echo, the procession's fourfold march.

  1. Keep moving to accumulate Steps.
  2. Spend Steps to ignore terrain, dodge through attack windows, or reposition allies.
  3. Leave Procession paths and movement Echoes behind.
  4. Use prior positions as a second battlefield only you fully understand.

Field notes. Subject: motion with a filing system. I have measured the stride and found the impossible legs are the least interesting part, the interesting part is the ledger. Three truths govern it. First: distance is converted, never wasted, every ten feet becomes Steps, banked against a cap, spent to ignore terrain, gaps, and occupied spaces. Second: the stride defends itself, two Steps buy a ten-foot reposition when targeted, and if the attack cannot follow, it simply misses. Third: the march leaves ghosts, Procession Paths that carry allies and hinder enemies, Echoes that draw distant attacks, until the Domain snaps every creature backward into its own prior frame. Mark the doctrine: the ground remembers your stride, and the technique has taught it to take sides.

Feats

Build-defining options.

Tall Enough

Jumps doubled, Large creatures stepped over, the legs keep their own counsel. Your standing jump distance and height double, and you may step over Large or smaller creatures without treating their spaces as difficult terrain.

Walk Behind the Camera

Thirty feet through dim light, and the procession vanishes without an action. If you end your turn in dim light or darkness after moving 30+ feet, you may Hide without an action once per round.

Friendly Procession

An ally walks the Path; fall in step behind them for fifteen feet. When an ally moves along your Procession Path, you may use your reaction to move up to 15 feet along the same path without provoking opportunity attacks, falling in step behind them.

Dropped Frame

An attack misses through Echo disadvantage, the dropped frame becomes a Step. Prerequisite: Processional Echoes. When an attack misses you due to Echo disadvantage, gain 1 Step.

Rules integration

Building the character

Scout is the obvious shell, but any character who wants mobility support can use it. Dexterity matters if you fight through Stilt Kick; a pure controller can invest almost entirely in CE stat.
Rules integration

Counterplay

Restrict movement, flood the area with persistent hazards, destroy lines of sight to Echoes, or force the user to hold a fixed objective.
