Field notes. Subject: the abbot's discipline made wearable — beads that refuse panic and sharpen resolve. Three truths govern them. First, fear is the first enemy: while worn, the beads grant advantage on saving throws against being frightened or charmed — the mind that will not flinch commands the technique that will not miss. Second, conviction has a price, and it is payable once: when you use a cursed technique you may spend up to 3 cursed energy to raise the technique's save DC by 1 per point — but only once per long rest, because a sharpened blade cannot live forever on the whetstone. Third, the discipline is a vow: the beads require attunement, the hour-long ritual, one of your scarce slots. A steady mind strikes deeper than a frantic one; the beads exist so the hand remembers what the heart already knows.

Features & procedures

Properties & techniques

Exact player-facing procedures

Passive

Steadied Mind

While wearing these prayer beads, you have advantage on saving throws against being frightened or charmed.

1/long rest

Amplify Technique

When you use a cursed technique, you can spend up to 3 cursed energy to increase the technique's save DC by 1 for each CE spent. Once used, you cannot use this feature again until you finish a long rest.

Rules integration

Edge cases and shared systems.

Campaign canon notes

Cursed-tool attunement: you have PB attunement slots (2–6). This tool occupies one slot and requires a one-hour ritual. Attunement ends voluntarily over a short rest, on death, or when you replace it while over capacity. Distance does not break attunement. Cursed-tool attunement is separate from magic-item attunement.
