Feature map

Level-by-level features, as the figure refined them.

L1

Postpone

Reaction · 2 CE · once per round.

When you or a willing creature within 5 ft would take damage, say the two syllables: not yet. After all normal mitigation, defer up to 5 × your proficiency bonus of the final damage — record the exact amount and source as a Debt; the creature takes the remainder. The Debt comes due at the end of the holder's next turn: settlement is unavoidable damage of the recorded type — it cannot be reduced by resistance, further effects, or temporary HP; no new save; it triggers no on-damage reactions. One creature holds one damage Debt at a time. Debts outstanding when combat ends come due immediately — no bookkeeping limbo.

L3

Intercession

Reaction · 1 CE.

When a willing creature within 30 ft has a Debt due before the end of its current turn, take ownership of the Debt onto your own shoulders — its due time becomes the end of your next turn. A Debt transfers once unless a feature says otherwise. You take the wound, you hold it, and you return it when you decide.

L6

Deferred Affliction

Action · 2 CE · touch a willing creature.

Suspend a non-incapacitating condition with a duration of 1 minute or less — eligible conditions are blinded, charmed, deafened, frightened, poisoned — and carry it in your own ruined frame as an Affliction Debt. At the end of your next turn it affects you for its remaining duration (minimum 1 round). Stunned, Paralyzed, Unconscious, death, and Domain sure-hits cannot be moved — some things even he cannot reschedule.

L11

Refusal to Die

Reaction · priced in your own maximum HP.

When a Debt comes due and would reduce you to 0 HP, split it after seeing the result: half now, half as a new Debt due at the end of your next turn. Each use reduces your maximum HP by 2 × your proficiency bonus until you finish a long rest, and you cannot use this if your maximum HP would hit 0. This is the sole exception to the no-second-postponement rule — it divides what is due rather than postponing it, and the division is priced in your own ending.

L18

The Impossible Man

Passive · the arithmetic runs on.

The first Postpone each round costs 0 CE. Intercession once per round requires no reaction. You may hold up to your proficiency bonus in simultaneous Debts, each with its own source and due time. He does not die. He reschedules — thinner, paler, more unfinished each time.

Maximum Technique

Tomorrow, Then

Action · 8 CE.

Until the start of your next turn, the first damaging effect or eligible affliction on each willing creature within 30 ft is fully deferred to you — no reaction required. At the start of your next turn, you may redistribute each resulting Debt once among willing creatures within 30 ft; whatever is left stays yours. Redistributed Debts come due at the end of each new holder's next turn. The bill always arrives. He is always on time.

Level 20 · Capstone

The Nursery at Scale

Once per long rest · action.

The nursery, at scale — the starets' compulsion, grown to metaphysics. Every willing creature within 120 ft may immediately Postpone its next damaging effect: no reaction required, no CE spent, each Debt held by you with a due time of the end of your next turn. You may hold any number of these Debts simultaneously. Come to him wounded. Come to him dying. He will say the two syllables — and tomorrow, ah, tomorrow you will talk about tomorrow.
