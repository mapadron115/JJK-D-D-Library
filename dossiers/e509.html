Shattered Mirror

A cursed technique born from embedded insecurity — the dread of being seen and found wanting, given form as a storm of broken mirror shards. The glass doesn't just return what was thrown at you; it returns the thrower to themselves, the worst part first. It catches attacks and throws them back crueler; it drinks what you won't return; it keeps every wound you've ever taken in a ledger of hurts. But a blow struck hand-to-hand shatters the mirror instead — you flinch, like always. Until you don't.

Shattered Mirror

Field notes. Subject: the wound that learned to aim. Three truths govern the mirror. First: it answers cruelty with cruelty. Ranged attacks and cursed-energy effects are caught and thrown back — the attacker suffers its own effect, and sees, in the glass, the worst part of itself; the frightened see it twice. Second: it has one direction it will not face. Hand-to-hand blows, falls, the purely physical — these shatter the shards, and the user flinches, like always. Every mirror has a blind side; hers is her own body. Third: pain compounds. Every hurt is noted in a ledger, and the ledger can be opened all at once — years of slights delivered in a single attack, the victim shown everything it ever did, simultaneously. And mark the end of the flinch: once per long rest, for one minute, even melee does not shatter her. Unflinching, at last.

Feature map

Level-by-level features

L1

Face the Shards

The technique's founding posture. She does not dodge, does not harden, does not endure — she angles the pieces of every reflection she couldn't stand to see, and lets the attacker meet itself. Shards of a broken mirror hang in the air around you — the pieces of every reflection you couldn't stand to see. When you can see a ranged attack or a damaging cursed-energy effect targeting you, you may spend your reaction and 1 CE: the shards catch it and throw it back. You take no damage; the attacker suffers its own effect — the same attack roll is made against the attacker's AC, or the attacker makes its own saving throw against its own save DC. In the instant of return, the attacker sees the worst part of themselves in the glass.

The Flinch. A blow struck hand-to-hand — melee weapon attacks, unarmed strikes, falls, any purely physical direct blow — doesn't reflect. It shatters the shards instead, and you flinch, like always. Every mirror has one direction it won't face.

The Worst of You. Whenever one of your reflections turns a creature's own attack back upon it, that creature must succeed on a Wisdom saving throw against your CE save DC or become frightened of you until the end of your next turn — it saw the worst part of itself. A creature that succeeds on this save is immune to this fear for 24 hours.

L3

Look Away

Not every blow deserves an answer. Some things she survives by not looking — the effect falls between the shards and vanishes, harming no one, rebounding nowhere. Restraint, aimed. When you could reflect, you may instead avert the mirror: spend 1 CE as part of the same reaction and the effect falls into the cracks between the shards and vanishes — harming no one, rebounding nowhere. Some things you survive by not looking.

L6

Crueler Reflection

What comes back comes back crueler. The mirror shows the attacker the worst part of itself, magnified — and the wound follows the sight. The glass never flatters; doubled dice never lie. What comes back comes back crueler — the mirror shows the attacker the worst part of themselves, magnified, and the wound follows the sight. When you reflect an effect, you may spend 1 additional CE: the reflected damage dice are doubled, as though the attack had critically hit. If the reflected effect has no damage dice, the additional CE has no effect (invented). The glass never flatters.

L11

Someone Worth Shielding

Angling the shards for someone else is easier than angling them for herself — the discovery of a lifetime. An ally within thirty feet inherits her reflection, and area effects rebound to their point of origin. The mirror widens — and you learn something: angling the shards for someone else is easier than angling them for yourself. When an ally within 30 feet is targeted by a reflectable effect, you may spend your reaction and 1 CE to reflect it as though it had targeted you. You can also turn back area and line effects: the effect rebounds onto its point of origin, and the originator makes its own save — or catches its own blast.

L18

Kept Wounds

Every cut, every slight, every laugh behind her back — kept. The ledger of hurts is her true inventory: pain, saved up and spent all at once, shown back to the creditor in a single opening. Every cut, every slight, every laugh behind your back — kept. When you take damage from any source (even a physical blow that shattered your mirror), you may note the amount in a ledger of hurts (no action required). The ledger holds at most 5 × your character level; unreleased hurts fade when you finish a long rest.

As an action, you open the ledger at a creature within 60 feet: make an attack roll using your CE ability against its AC. On a hit, the shards show it everything it ever did to you, all at once — the stored amount as psychic damage. The ledger empties to 0. Hurt, saved up and spent all at once.

L20

Unflinching

Once per long rest, one minute: the Flinch is suspended. She may reflect even melee physical strikes, and reflection costs no CE — only the reaction. Look at her now. Once per long rest, as a bonus action: for 1 minute, you stop flinching. The Flinch is suspended — you may reflect melee physical strikes too — and reflection costs no CE (it still costs your reaction). Look at me now.

Reversal and Domain.

Capstone material

Reversal: Internalized

The inversion of reflection. Instead of returning the blow, she drinks it — the mirror turned inward becomes a well, and half the would-be damage returns as her own flesh restored. The inversion of reflection. You've spent a lifetime absorbing every judgment — now it's a weapon. When you could reflect, you may spend your reaction and 2 CE to drink the effect instead of returning it: you take no damage, and you regain hit points equal to half the damage the effect would have dealt (rounded down). The mirror, turned inward, becomes a well.

Domain Expansion: Hall of Broken Mirrors

A room where every surface is cracked glass and every shard shows each viewer their own flaws. No creature inside but her can land a blow — every attack rebounds automatically, no reaction, no cost. The sure-hit is the reflection itself. 18th level. Sure-hit domain — the sure-hit is the reflection. Activation: full turn, 20 CE (D008 baseline for a normal Domain). Clash: 1000 points; burnout 3 rounds; duration 3 min (Tier V mapping); radius 120 ft.

Look at Yourself. Floor, walls, ceiling: every surface is cracked glass, and every shard shows each viewer their own flaws — the attacker's face, mid-swing, twisted. While the domain stands, no creature inside except you can land a blow on you: every attack or damaging effect that targets you rebounds onto its source automatically — no reaction, no CE cost. The same attack roll is made against the attacker's AC, or the attacker makes its own save against its own DC. Every rebound carries The Worst of You (see Face the Shards). Reflected effects cannot be reflected again.

Fears

Five fears, aimed.

Shattered Mirror doesn't teach feats. It teaches fears — five shapes your insecurity takes once you learn to aim it. Each is optional, and you must meet its listed prerequisite before taking it.

Prerequisite: Face the Shards

Angle the Shard

The mirror points where she points it — a reflected attack may be redirected to another creature near the attacker. The thrower need not be the one who catches. When you reflect an effect, you may choose a different creature within 30 feet of the attacker as the new target instead of the attacker itself. The mirror points where you point it.

Prerequisite: Face the Shards

Show Them Themselves

Her mirror catches more than damage. Hostile conditions, forced movement, charms and frighten attempts — the glass returns those too. Nothing aimed at her stays aimed at her. Your mirror catches more than damage. Non-damaging hostile effects that target only you — conditions, forced movement, charm or frighten attempts from techniques — can be reflected back onto their source just like an attack.

Prerequisite: Look Away

Averted Gaze

Look Away, perfected: a swallowed area or line effect spares everyone inside it, and nothing rebounds anywhere. Mercy, aimed. Look Away can swallow an entire area or line effect, not just what targets you: everyone inside it is spared, and nothing rebounds anywhere.

Prerequisite: Kept Wounds

Nursed Grudges

Some hurts she saves for later. Opening the ledger need not empty it — half may be kept, rounded down, for the next reckoning. When you open your ledger of hurts, you may keep half of it (rounded down) instead of emptying it. Some hurts you save for later.

Prerequisite: Face the Shards

See Yourself

Hold the mirror steady as the blow returns: the attacker, unable to stop staring, is disadvantaged against anyone but her. She becomes the only thing in the room worth hitting. When you reflect an attack, you may hold the mirror steady as part of the same reaction: the attacker must succeed on a Wisdom saving throw against your CE save DC or have disadvantage on attacks against anyone other than you until the end of its next turn — it can't stop staring.

Rules integration

Edge cases and shared systems.

Campaign canon notes

Cursed Energy baseline: CE pool is based on class; CE ability is Charisma or Wisdom (player's choice). CE save DC = 8 + CE ability modifier + proficiency bonus. Regain all spent CE on a long rest.
What can be reflected: ranged attacks (even mundane ones — the shards read their trajectory as indirect) and damaging effects from cursed techniques, innate techniques, cursed strikes, and other magic. The Flinch excludes melee weapon attacks, unarmed strikes, falls, and other purely physical direct blows.
You must see it coming: reflection requires you to perceive the attack or effect. Attacks from unseen attackers and effects you are unaware of cannot be reflected.
No reflection loops: a reflected effect cannot itself be reflected — by you, by the attacker, or by the domain. The rebound resolves once and ends (the same anti-loop rule as Inverse, e272).
Ledger of hurts: note damage as you take it (no action); maximum 5 × character level; open it as an action (CE-ability attack roll vs. AC, 60 ft., psychic damage); unreleased hurts fade on a long rest.
Domain activation defaults: rendered as full turn + 20 CE per the D008 normal-Domain baseline, with Tier V clash stats (1000 / 3-round burnout / 3 min) and a 120-ft radius.
The Worst of You: the frightened condition from a reflection lasts until the end of your next turn; a creature that succeeds on the save is immune to this fear for 24 hours (the standard Frightful Presence pattern — no save-spam).
Hall of Broken Mirrors is a sure-hit domain: the sure-hit is automatic reflection of anything targeting you — there is no separate damage rider. Your own attacks are unaffected (they don't target you).
Reversal vs. Maximum: Shattered Mirror's pinnacle is written as a Reversal (Internalized), not a Maximum Technique; it occupies the technique's pinnacle slot.
