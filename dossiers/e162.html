Infinite Retail

Manifest useful store fixtures in ordinary play, then drag the battlefield into an impossible retail interior whose real weapon is its ecology: scavenge while the lights are on, build somewhere defensible before closing, and survive the Staff when night begins.

Level 1 — Flat-Pack Catalog

  • Store Die: d6 at levels 1–5, d8 at 6–10, d10 at 11–17, and d12 at 18+.
  • Flat-Pack Manifestation. Action, 1 CE: create one Merchandise object in an unoccupied space within 30 feet. Maintain up to PB Merchandise at once for 10 minutes; creating beyond the cap dismisses one of your choice.
  • Shelf Wall. Large 10-by-5-foot object, AC 13, HP = 5 × level. It provides cover normally.
  • Shopping Cart. Medium object, AC 13, HP = 3 × level. After pushing it at least 15 feet in a straight line, replace one attack with a CE attack: 1 Store Die + CE modifier bludgeoning and a Strength save or prone.
  • Display Fixture. Occupies a 10-foot square. Enemies treat the square as difficult terrain.
  • Floor Lamp / Sign. Medium object, AC 12, HP = 2 × level, bright light 30 feet and dim light 30 more.

Level 3 — Domain Expansion: Infinite Retail — Closing Shift

Early Rule-Domain Binding Vow. Full-turn action, 6 CE, enclosed 60-foot-radius Domain for up to 1 minute. The Domain's guaranteed laws never deal damage themselves. Ordinary attacks, techniques, hazards, and Staff still cause damage normally.

Everyone Is Stranded. You and every other creature inside are Stranded and subject to the same store cycle. You always know the current Store Phase, Store Nodes and Settlements.

Impossible Interior. Ordinary doors, emergency exits, and attempts to simply walk to the barrier's edge turn into additional aisles and showrooms. Barrier clashes and anti-domain techniques still use shared Domain rules.

Store Nodes. Place three indestructible 10-foot Store Nodes at least 15 feet apart: Restaurant, Warehouse, and Showroom. They are resources, not checkpoints.

Open-Hours Scavenge. Once during each Open Hours, a Stranded creature adjacent to a Store Node may use an action to Scavenge: Restaurant heals 1 Store Die + PB HP; Warehouse creates a 10-by-5-foot Barricade (AC 13, HP = 3 × caster level); Showroom repairs one unattended object for 2 Store Dice + CE modifier HP.

Found a Settlement. A creature adjacent to at least two Barricades, Merchandise objects, or substantial cover may use an action to establish a 15-foot-radius Settlement. Creatures inside gain half cover against Staff and advantage against Staff grapples.

The Store Cycle — Guaranteed Rule Engine

The Domain begins in Open Hours. At initiative count 20, advance Open Hours → Closing Warning → Closed Hours → Open Hours, then repeat. Open Hours enables Scavenge/Search and Staff are passive unless attacked. Closing Warning gives one full warning round. Closed Hours extinguishes the lights and makes every Staff hostile. The phase transition is the Domain's guaranteed rule effect; it does not make an attack roll or deal automatic damage.

Shifting Exit

During Open Hours, a Stranded creature may use an action to search for the anomalous exit with Intelligence (Investigation) or Wisdom (Survival) against your Technique DC. Failure grants a cumulative +2 to the next attempt, maximum +6. Success manifests an Exit within 10 feet until the end of its turn; moving through costs 5 feet and removes that creature from the Domain. The caster cannot use this to abandon their own Domain.

3008-2 Staff

  • PB Staff appear when the Domain opens, passive until Closed Hours unless attacked.
  • AC 12 + PB; HP = 4 × caster level; Speed 30 ft.
  • Staff Strike: CE attack, reach 5 ft.; 2 Store Dice + CE modifier bludgeoning.
  • Customer Removal: Athletics using CE attack bonus vs Athletics/Acrobatics; success grapples and Staff may move target up to 15 feet.
  • Autonomous Staff Actions: level 3: 1 each Closed-Hours round; level 11: 2; level 18: 3.
  • Priority: creatures near Staff Remains, then exposed creatures outside Settlements, then nearest accessible living creature.

Staff Remains

When Staff reaches 0 HP, leave a Staff Remains marker. At the start of Closed Hours, every two Remains manifest one additional Staff, maximum PB additional Staff. During Open Hours, creatures can haul Remains; Warehouse Scavenge can dispose of one instead of its normal benefit. Killing every Staff can therefore make the next night worse.

Progression

  • 1 — Flat-Pack Catalog: cover, carts, fixtures, signs.
  • 3 — Closing Shift: rule Domain, Store Cycle, Scavenging, Settlements, Exit, PB Staff, Remains, 1 autonomous Staff Action.
  • 6 — Settlement Founder: Flat-Pack and Settlement become bonus actions; Settlement Merchandise gains +1 AC and +2 × level HP; Closing Warning can let willing creatures move toward a Settlement as reactions.
  • 11 — Restocking Cycle: Domain 5 CE, 90 ft, 2 Staff Actions; each Open Hours restore one destroyed Merchandise/Barricade or remove one Remains.
  • 18 — Infinite Floorplan: Domain 4 CE, 120 ft, 3 Staff Actions, Flat-Pack capacity PB + CE mod; move Store Nodes and Merchandise; designate an Exchange Settlement with resistance to Staff damage and Open-Hours healing.

Technique Feats

Flat-Pack Engineer: +1 Merchandise capacity and once/short rest free Merchandise. Landmark Cartographer: you and designated allies add PB to Search for Exit and know shortest routes during Closing Warning. Night Watch: at Closed Hours, PB creatures in one Settlement gain +2 AC vs Staff and opportunity attacks against Staff crossing the Settlement. Exchange & Returns: at Open Hours, trade Restocking Cycle to let willing creatures in one surviving Settlement spend one Hit Die.

Rule-Domain Rulings

  • This is not a movement-sequence or jackpot Domain. Store Nodes can be ignored or visited in any order.
  • Guaranteed laws are Impossible Interior and Store Cycle; they never automatically damage.
  • Staff are autonomous Domain entities; their attacks/grapples roll normally and are not the sure-hit.
  • The caster can be attacked by Staff during Closed Hours.
  • Settlements do not make occupants invulnerable.
  • Barrier clashes, Simple Domain, Hollow Wicker Basket, Domain Amplification, barrier destruction, and Technique Burnout follow shared rules.
