Infinite Retail

Manifest useful store fixtures in ordinary play, then drag the battlefield into an impossible retail interior whose real weapon is its ecology: scavenge while the lights are on, build somewhere defensible before closing, and survive the Staff when night begins.

Combat roleRule-Domain controller / fortification support
Core law

Shifting Exit

Searching Instead of Following a Route

During Open Hours, a Stranded creature may use an action to search for the anomalous exit with Intelligence (Investigation) or Wisdom (Survival) against your Technique DC. It must be able to move and perceive its surroundings.

The Store Learns Your Search

On a failure, that creature gains a cumulative +2 bonus to its next Search for an Exit check during this Domain, maximum +6. On a success, an Exit manifests in an unoccupied space within 10 feet of it until the end of its turn. Moving through it costs 5 feet of movement and removes that creature from the Domain.

No Free Caster Escape

You cannot use Search for an Exit to abandon your own Domain; you may always dismiss the Domain normally. A creature that leaves through an Exit does not end the Domain for anyone still inside.

Core law

Staff Remains — The Bad Decision That Snowballs

Remains

When a Staff is reduced to 0 HP, leave a Staff Remains marker in its space. Remains are unattended objects, AC 10, 10 HP, but destroying them only scatters them and does not remove the marker.

Attraction

At the start of each Closed Hours, for every two Staff Remains currently in the Domain, one additional Staff manifests, to a maximum of PB additional Staff. If Remains are inside or within 20 feet of a Settlement, those new Staff appear as close to that Settlement as the map legally allows.

Disposal

During Open Hours, a creature adjacent to Remains can use an action to haul one marker up to half its speed. A creature adjacent to the Warehouse may use its once-per-Open-Hours Scavenge action to dispose of one Remains marker instead of taking the normal Warehouse benefit.

Why This Matters

Killing every Staff is sometimes the worst possible strategy. A party that wins one night carelessly can create a much harder next night, while clever enemies can drag Remains toward someone else's Settlement before closing.

Shopping Cart.

Medium object, AC 13, HP = 3 × level. A creature may push it while moving. After pushing it at least 15 feet in a straight line, replace one attack with a CE attack against a creature ahead of it: 1 Store Die + CE modifier bludgeoning and a Strength save or prone.

Display Fixture.

Occupies a 10-foot square. Enemies treat the square as difficult terrain; allies you designate can climb or move across it normally.

Floor Lamp / Sign.

Medium object, AC 12, HP = 2 × level, bright light 30 feet and dim light 30 more. Creatures within 10 feet have advantage on Perception checks to locate hidden creatures that are not invisible.

3Infinite Retail — Closing Shift

Closing Shift — early rule Domain

Full-turn action, 6 CE, enclosed 60-foot-radius Domain for up to 1 minute. Binding vow: the Domain’s guaranteed laws never deal damage themselves — that non-lethal limitation is what permits level-3 access at the reduced CE cost. Ordinary attacks, techniques, hazards, and Staff can still cause damage normally.

Everyone Is Stranded

There is no Employee/Customer hierarchy. You and every other creature inside are Stranded and subject to the same store cycle; Staff can attack you at night. Your advantage is knowledge: you always know the current Store Phase, the locations of Store Nodes and Settlements, and which objects are your Merchandise. Impossible Interior: ordinary doors, emergency exits, and attempts to walk to the barrier’s edge turn into additional aisles — they do not leave the Domain.

Store Cycle & Nodes

The Domain begins in Open Hours. At initiative count 20, advance Open Hours → Closing Warning → Closed Hours → Open Hours. When the Domain forms, place three indestructible 10-foot Store Nodes at least 15 feet apart: Restaurant, Warehouse, and Showroom. Once during each Open Hours, a Stranded creature adjacent to any Node may use an action to Scavenge: Restaurant heals 1 Store Die + PB HP; Warehouse creates one 10-by-5-foot Barricade within 5 feet (AC 13, HP = 3 × caster level); Showroom repairs one unattended object or Merchandise within 10 feet for 2 Store Dice + CE modifier HP.

Settlements & the shifting Exit

A creature adjacent to at least two Barricades, Merchandise objects, or substantial pieces of cover may use an action to establish a 15-foot-radius Settlement. Creatures inside gain half cover against Staff and advantage on checks or saves against Staff grapples; Staff outside must attack an obstructing founding structure before targeting a sheltered creature. During Open Hours, a Stranded creature may use an action to Search for an Exit (Intelligence (Investigation) or Wisdom (Survival) vs your Technique DC). On failure it gains a cumulative +2 to its next Search, maximum +6; on success an Exit manifests within 10 feet until the end of its turn, and moving through it (5 feet of movement) removes that creature from the Domain. You cannot use Search for an Exit to abandon your own Domain.

Staff & Staff Remains

PB Staff manifest when the Domain opens, passive during Open Hours and Closing Warning unless attacked, hostile to living Stranded creatures during Closed Hours. You get 1 autonomous Staff Action each round during Closed Hours: one Staff moves up to its speed and then Strikes (CE attack bonus, 2 Store Dice + CE modifier bludgeoning) or attempts Customer Removal (grapple and drag 15 feet). You do not choose their targets; the GM follows Staff priority. When a Staff is reduced to 0 HP, leave a Staff Remains marker; at the start of each Closed Hours, for every two Remains in the Domain, one additional Staff manifests (maximum PB additional) — Remains near a Settlement draw the new Staff toward it. Killing every Staff is sometimes the worst strategy.

6Settlement Founder

Flat-Pack Manifestation becomes a bonus action. You may establish a Settlement as a bonus action instead of an action. Merchandise you created that currently forms part of a Settlement gains +1 AC and additional HP equal to 2 × your level. When Closing Warning begins, willing creatures of your choice within 30 feet may use their reactions to move up to half their speed toward a Settlement they can perceive.

11Restocking Cycle

Domain cost becomes 5 CE, radius becomes 90 feet, and Staff Actions become 2 per Closed-Hours round. Each time Open Hours begins, restore one destroyed Merchandise or Barricade you created to full HP in an unoccupied space within 30 feet of where it was destroyed, or remove one Staff Remains marker anywhere in the Domain.

18Infinite Floorplan

Domain cost becomes 4 CE, radius becomes 120 feet, Staff Actions become 3, and Flat-Pack capacity becomes PB + CE modifier. Each time Open Hours begins, you may move one Store Node up to 30 feet to a legal unoccupied space and teleport one of your Merchandise objects to another unoccupied space you can see within 60 feet. Your chosen Settlement may be declared Exchange: its founding structures gain resistance to damage from Staff, and creatures inside Exchange restore 1 Store Die + PB HP when Open Hours begins, once per creature per cycle.

That non-lethal limitation is the Binding Vow that permits level-3 access and the reduced CE cost. Ordinary attacks, techniques, hazards, and Staff can still cause damage normally.

Your advantage is that your technique understands the building: you always know the current Store Phase, the locations of Store Nodes and Settlements, and which objects are your Merchandise.

Impossible Interior — continuous Domain law Ordinary doors, emergency exits, and attempts to simply walk to the barrier's edge turn into additional aisles and showrooms. They do not leave the Domain. Barrier clashes, anti-domain techniques, teleportation that explicitly crosses Domains, and destruction of the barrier still use the shared Domain rules; Infinite Retail does not make itself unbreakable.

Store Nodes When the Domain forms, place three indestructible 10-foot Store Nodes at least 15 feet apart: Restaurant, Warehouse, and Showroom. They are resources, not checkpoints. No creature is required to visit them in any order.

Open-Hours Scavenge Once during each Open Hours, a Stranded creature adjacent to any Store Node may use an action to Scavenge. At the Restaurant, heal 1 Store Die + PB HP. At the Warehouse, create one 10-by-5-foot Barricade within 5 feet (AC 13, HP = 3 × caster level). At the Showroom, repair one unattended object or Merchandise within 10 feet for 2 Store Dice + CE modifier HP. A creature can benefit from only one Scavenge action per Open Hours.

Found a Settlement A creature adjacent to at least two Barricades, Merchandise objects, or substantial pieces of cover may use an action to establish a 15-foot-radius Settlement centered on itself. A Settlement remains while at least one founding structure survives. Stranded creatures inside gain half cover against Staff and advantage on checks or saves against Staff grapples. Staff outside a Settlement must attack an obstructing founding structure before targeting a creature fully sheltered behind it.

Infinite Retail

Flat-Pack Trap

When you use Flat-Pack Manifestation, you may target a prone creature within 30 feet instead of an unoccupied space, assembling the Merchandise around it. The target makes a Strength saving throw against your technique DC; on a failure it is restrained inside the furniture until it uses an action to break free against your technique DC. The Merchandise has its normal statistics and can be attacked.

Closing Shift

Fire Exit Plan

When a creature you can perceive manifests an Exit by succeeding on Search for an Exit, you may use your reaction to choose where that Exit appears: any unoccupied space within 30 feet of that creature, inside the Domain, instead of within 10 feet of it.

Settlement Founder, level 6

Night Watch

When Closed Hours begins, you may use your reaction to rouse one Settlement you can perceive. Until the next Store Phase, up to PB creatures inside it may make opportunity attacks against Staff that enter or leave the Settlement, and your Display Fixtures’ difficult terrain does not impede them.

Restocking Cycle, level 11

Exchange & Returns

When Open Hours begins, instead of restoring Merchandise or removing Remains with Restocking Cycle, choose one Settlement that survived the previous Closed Hours. Every willing creature inside it may immediately spend one Hit Die and regain that much HP without using an action.

Rules integration

Rule-Domain Rulings

The Domain begins in Open Hours. At initiative count 20, advance Open Hours → Closing Warning → Closed Hours → Open Hours, then repeat while the Domain remains.
Returning to Open Hours immediately makes surviving Staff passive unless a creature attacks one again. Store Nodes restock and every creature regains its once-per-Open-Hours Scavenge opportunity.
The phase transition itself is the Domain's guaranteed rule effect. It does not make an attack roll, deal automatic damage, or force a creature through a prescribed route.
This is not a movement-sequence Domain. Store Nodes may be visited in any order or ignored entirely. They are resources, not mandatory checkpoints.
This is not a jackpot Domain. Store Phases are deterministic and public. The strategy comes from resource use, fortification, night survival, and manipulating Staff behavior.
The Domain's guaranteed laws are Impossible Interior and the Store Cycle. They change topology, availability of resources, and Staff state; they never automatically damage a creature.
Staff are autonomous Domain entities. Their attacks and grapples roll normally, can miss, and are not the Domain's sure-hit.
The caster can be attacked by Staff during Closed Hours and can worsen their own situation by leaving Staff Remains near their Settlement.
Attacking a passive Staff during Open Hours or Closing Warning makes only that Staff hostile to the attacker until the next phase transition.
Settlements do not make occupants invulnerable. Staff can destroy the structures, enter through gaps, be teleported inside, or attack anyone who is no longer sheltered behind a structure.
Barrier clashes, Simple Domain, Hollow Wicker Basket, Domain Amplification, barrier destruction, and Technique Burnout follow the campaign's shared rules. Search for an Exit is an additional SCP-3008-flavored escape option, not a replacement for those systems.
