Festering Life Sword

A Special Grade cursed shortsword — every wound it deals festers with necrotic rot.

FormWeapon (Shortsword)
GradeSpecial Grade
AttunementNot required
Features & procedures

Properties & techniques

Exact player-facing procedures

Cursed edge

You have a +4 bonus to attack and damage rolls made with this weapon.

Festering wound

On a hit with this weapon, the target must make a Constitution saving throw (DC equal to your cursed energy save DC). On a failure, it takes 6d10 necrotic damage. On a success, it takes no additional damage from this property.

Rules integration

Edge cases and shared systems.

Campaign canon notes

Save DC: the source gives no DC for the festering wound; it uses your cursed energy save DC. The necrotic damage applies per hit, after the weapon damage resolves.
