Properties & techniques

Exact player-facing procedures

Cursed edge

The steel asks no questions, +4 to attack and damage on every swing, Special Grade and plain about it. You have a +4 bonus to attack and damage rolls made with this weapon.

Festering wound

But every hit opens a second wound: a Constitution save against your CE save DC, failure costing 6d10 necrotic. The save is per hit, the blade repeats its question as often as it lands. On a hit with this weapon, the target must make a Constitution saving throw (DC equal to your cursed energy save DC). On a failure, it takes 6d10 necrotic damage. On a success, it takes no additional damage from this property.

Field notes. Subject: a shortsword whose wounds rot from within. Two truths. First: the steel is Special Grade honest, +4 to strike and wound, no conditions, no ceremony. Second: every landed hit is a verdict, the target makes a Constitution save against your cursed energy DC, and on failure suffers 6d10 necrotic as the wound festers; on success, nothing further. Mark the cruelty of the schedule: the save triggers per hit, not per round, the blade that lands twice asks the question twice. Assess it as plague made portable: the sword does not need to kill you with steel, it needs only to introduce you to the rot. The wound is the doorway; the festering is the house.

Edge cases and shared systems.

Campaign canon notes

Save DC: the source gives no DC for the festering wound; it uses your cursed energy save DC. The necrotic damage applies per hit, after the weapon damage resolves.
