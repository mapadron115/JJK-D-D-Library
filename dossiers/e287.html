Core law

Completed rules

Distinct from e101 Concept Devourer:

Voracity acquires statblock abilities by devouring corpses. The existing dossier e101 Concept Devourer denies and erases concepts. They are different engines; Voracity carries none of e101's mechanics.

Full progression

Feature map

1Hunger of the Accursed.

Starting at 1st level, you are constantly filled with the constant hunger impulses that come with your technique. When you reduce a cursed spirit, shikigami, or cursed corpse to 0 hit points, you can use your reaction to spend 3 cursed energy to devour its corpse. For 1 hour you gain a feature, action, movement, resistance, immunity, sense, AC, Ability Score or other statistic from the dead creature's statblock such as Cursed Energy Affinity (Cannot take legendary actions, Cursed Energy or Domains). You can spend an additional 3 cursed energy to steal an additional ability and can steal a number of abilities from a single corpse up to your Cursed Energy modifier.

When you steal an ability that does not suit your body (such as fly speed), your body changes to fit the ability (eg. growing wings). For the duration, you count as the creature type your stolen abilities come from. If you have stolen abilities from multiple creatures, you count as the one you have the most abilities from. You can have a number of stolen abilities equal to your Cursed Energy modifier at once.

3Regurgitation.

At 3rd level, you have learned to access previous abilities you have eaten. As a bonus action you can spend 4 cursed energy to gain an ability you have stolen in the past and can use it for 1 hour. This does not increase at 6th level.

6Digestion.

At 6th level, your stomach calls for satiation and gives you the means to feed it. The amount of cursed energy required for Hunger of the Accursed and Regurgitation decreases by 1 (minimum cost of 1) and again at 11th level. Additionally, you have learned to use cursed energy to enhance the abilities of the devoured. You can use one of the following benefits once per turn when you use a stolen ability: Efficiency. When you use an ability with a base cursed energy cost, the cost of the feature decreases by 1 (minimum cost of 1). Output. When you use an ability that lets you spend up to a certain amount of cursed energy, you can increase that amount by your cursed energy modifier. Speed. When using stolen movement, you can spend up to your cursed energy modifier to increase it by 5 ft per cursed energy spent. Reinforcement. When using stolen AC and are hit by an attack roll, you can spend 1 CE to add your proficiency bonus to your AC against that attack roll, potentially causing it to miss. Instinct. When using a feature, you can spend 1 cursed energy to decrease its action cost by one (Full-Turn Action > Action > Bonus Action > Reaction).

At 6th level, you can have stolen abilities from a number of creatures equal to your Cursed Energy modifier active at once and they last for 24 hours. You cannot use different innate techniques at once.

11Maximum: Body of the Devourer.

At 11th level you have learned to draw the power of the devoured from your stomach and into your flesh. As an action you can transform into one of the creatures you have devoured, spending cursed energy equal to twice the Devoured's CR (min of 1) or Level. Your statistics are replaced by the statblock of the chosen Devoured, but you retain your alignment, personality, mental scores, Cursed Energy and Hit Points.

You gain temporary hit points equal to the Hit Points of the Devoured. These Temporary Hit Points vanish if any remain when the technique ends. The technique ends early if you have no Temporary Hit Points from this technique left. You are limited in the actions it can perform by the anatomy of your new form and can only use your cursed techniques if your new form is capable of forming hand signs and chanting. This form lasts for a number of minutes equal to your level + your cursed energy modifier.

Pinnacle expression

Maximum, Domain, or equivalent.

Maximum: Body of the Devourer: 11 — the full transformation procedure above. The audit reads "twice the Devoured's CR (min of 1) or Level" as 2xCR for monsters, 2x character level for humanoids (AT249).

Domain Expansion: Tomb of the Devoured: 18 — At 18th level you have mastered your technique allowing you to expand your domain. At initiative 20, you can regurgitate a creature whose corpse you devoured into an unoccupied space within 5 ft of you. The creature is friendly to you and your allies and acts immediately after your turn, attacking all enemies within your domain whether you command it to or not. If the creature was sentient in life, it is barely sentient within your domain as a mere husk of its former self. All regurgitated creatures disappear once your domain ends. The regurgitated creature is a domain summon, not a second character — it takes its turn after yours with the devoured statblock.

Innate Technique Feats

Build-defining options.

These are optional character-building feats. You must meet each listed prerequisite before selecting it.

Prerequisite: Must have Hunger of the Accursed

Cannibalism

Your hunger has overwhelmed your humanity and led you to resort to this inhumane act. You can now devour the corpses of human races and use them for your innate technique features. You can take a Class Feature or Innate Technique feature using the rules of Hunger of the Accursed from a dead humanoid, changing parts of your appearance to resemble them when used. You cannot take Domain Expansions using this feature. When you steal a cursed technique or innate technique feature from a dead humanoid, you can only take it if you are of the correct level and fulfill any prerequisites for using it. Additionally, you can only spend the base cost the technique requires. No matter what you copy, you can only use the technique in its base form. For example, a maximum output hollow purple would only come as a hollow purple. This changes if used with the Output option of Digestion at 6th level. Table consent: devouring a sapient humanoid corpse requires the table's consent, and for a PC corpse, that player's out-of-character consent — without it the corpse is inedible.

Prerequisite: Must have Regurgitation

Necrophage

You have learned to read a corpse before committing your hunger. As an action, you can take a small taste of a corpse within 5 feet of you: you learn one feature, action, or trait from its statblock that you could steal with Hunger of the Accursed (your GM chooses and reveals it). Tasting costs no cursed energy and does not consume the corpse, which can still be devoured normally afterward.

Prerequisite: Must have Body of the Devourer

Gourmand

You have learned to appreciate the good food you have eaten. When you use Regurgitation, you can call upon one additional ability you stole from the same creature as part of the same bonus action, paying its cursed energy cost separately as normal. You can savor a second helping in this way once per short rest.

Rules integration

Edge cases and shared systems.

Rules priority: the reviewed rulings, explicit codex clarifications, and the level progression always control when original wording conflicts. The audit dispositions for AT248/AT249 are adopted as a V.6 addition with the exact changes noted above.
Cursed Spirit Manipulation overlap: Cursed Spirit Manipulation also consumes cursed-spirit corpses — first claimant takes the corpse; a corpse devoured by Voracity cannot also be captured/consumed by CSM and vice versa (AT248).
Statblock conversion (audit HOLD): "gain a feature… from the dead creature's statblock" has no conversion procedure; every theft is a GM adjudication (action cost, DC, damage scale). Proposed interim: each stolen ability needs GM sign-off when devoured; pre-approved abilities persist (AT248).
