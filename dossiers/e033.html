Triple Core

Three biological cores support extra HP, two chosen combat forms, and a week-long sacrificial return from 0 HP.

Field notes. Subject: the body with two spare hearts. Three truths govern him. First: the body keeps backups — the first core is simply more life, one hit point per level, recalculated as he grows; the second and third are chosen combat hearts — unblockable blows, energy blast, defensive ward — one active at a time, switchable with a bonus action. Second: a spent heart takes a week to forgive — Insurance answers the fall to zero with a reaction, sealing one chosen form for seven full days, returning him at half maximum; seal both and no rescue remains. Third: the Domain is the whole organism at once — a law, not an event, with no initiative-twenty pulse; while it stands, all three cores burn together. Mark the economy: the unblockable coin is one amplifier, never stacked with another vulnerability. Not three bodies — three ways of refusing to lose the one.

Rules integration

Edge cases and shared systems.

Rules priority: the reviewed rulings, explicit codex clarifications, and the level progression always control when original wording conflicts. Legacy Domain auto-hit language, action terminology, event timing, missing saves, and older scaling never override revised player rules.
Why Tier I: one extreme offensive option and a dramatic survival trigger remain contained by two permanent choices, ordinary CE, and a clear one-form loop.
