Triple Core

Three biological cores support extra HP, two chosen combat forms, and a week-long sacrificial return from 0 HP.

Core law

Completed rules

First Core grants +1 maximum HP per character level, including levels already gained. This maximum is recalculated when total level changes.

At level 3 choose one of Unblockable Blows, Energy Blast, or Defensive Form; at level 6 choose a different one. These choices represent the second and third core and normally change only when gaining a level. As a bonus action activate one selected form; it lasts until you switch, become unconscious, or seal that core.

Unblockable Blows:

after an attack hits, spend 1 CE. For that damage, immunity becomes resistance, resistance is ignored, or a creature with neither becomes vulnerable. It is one damage amplifier and cannot combine with another source of vulnerability.

Energy Blast:

action, spend X CE from 1 to PB, make one ranged CE attack at 120 feet, and deal Xd6 force on hit. Defensive Form: when activated and as a bonus action while active, spend X CE from 1 to PB and gain 5 × X temporary HP.

Insurance:

when reduced to 0 HP but not killed outright, use a reaction and choose one unsealed selected form. That core seals for seven full days and you return to half maximum HP. A sealed form cannot activate, provide Insurance again, or benefit from the Domain. If both technique cores are sealed, Insurance is unavailable.

Pinnacle expression

Maximum, Domain, or equivalent.

Rule of Threes: 18 — Rule of Threes. while the Domain is active, every unsealed selected form is active simultaneously along with First Core. The unchosen third form is not granted; “all cores” means the actual three-core character, not all menu options. At initiative 20 each chosen hostile automatically takes 3d10 force damage per unsealed core, maximum 9d10, and is pushed 10 feet per such core. A protected creature makes a Constitution save for half damage and no push.

Innate Technique Feats

Build-defining options.

These are optional character-building feats. You must meet each listed prerequisite before selecting it.

Prerequisite: Unblockable Blows and CE score 20

Maximum: Crippling Strikes

Unblockable Blows normally spends 1 CE to ignore resistance or impose vulnerability when none exists. With this feat, spend 2 CE total against immunity to turn that immunity into resistance for the hit. It does not both reduce immunity and ignore the resulting resistance.

Prerequisite: Energy Blast and CE score 20

Maximum: Blast Away

When Energy Blast spends at least 5 CE, its dice become d8s. On a hit, the target makes a Strength save or is pushed 10 feet. Spend the amount allowed by Energy Blast; there is no universal Output Rank or optional CE ceiling.

Prerequisite: Defensive Form and CE score 20

Maximum: Fortress

Action, 3 CE while you have Defensive Form temporary HP: gain resistance to bludgeoning, piercing, and slashing until those temporary HP are gone, you leave the form, or 1 minute passes.

Rules integration

Edge cases and shared systems.

Rules priority: the reviewed rulings, explicit codex clarifications, and character reference above always control when original wording conflicts. Legacy Domain auto-hit language, action terminology, event timing, missing saves, and older scaling never override revised player rules.
Why Tier I: one extreme offensive option and a dramatic survival trigger remain contained by two permanent choices, ordinary CE, and a clear one-form loop.
Appendix

Additional material.

Triple Core
3 cores, 1 body, due to this strange biology you have three different
forms with separate powers.
Cursed Energy
Beginning at 1st level, you can harness your negative feelings into
a usable source of energy, called cursed energy. These feelings can
be harnessed into attacks or abilities. The number of cursed energy
you possess is based on your class, some classes use a modifier that
being charisma or wisdom (Player’s choice)
Your cursed energy DC is 8 + user cursed energy ability modifier +
proficiency bonus.
First Core
Starting at 1st level, your cursed energy is gathered into a single
core. You gain an extra hit point per level.
Second Core
At 3rd level, you manifest your cursed energy into a second core. As
a bonus action, you can choose to activate a secondary form goving
you access to a specific technique. This technique is one of the
following:
● Unblockable Blows: At the cost of one CE per attack, you can go
through resistances. If the creature doesn't have resistances,
they count as vulnerable.
● Energy Blast: As an action, you can spend as many CE as you
wish. Then, you make an attack, this deal xd6 damage. X is
equal to CE spent.
● Defensive Form. After activating this form, you are better
defensively. You gain 5*CE Temporary Hit Points.
Third Core
Beginning at 6th level, you gain another Core option.
Insurance
Starting at 11th level, as a reaction after falling to 0 hit points,
you can choose to lose access to your form for 1 week. You also
return at half your maximum HP.
Domain Expansion: Rule of Threes
At 18th level, you gain true understanding over your cores. When
inside your domain, you count as under the effect of all your cores.
