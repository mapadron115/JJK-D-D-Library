Grade 1 cursed tool

Second Opinion

Two finesse knives, 1d6 slashing each. The first knife is Diagnosis; the second is Revision.

FormPaired surgical knives
RoleDebuff / wound editor
EngineDiagnosis
Attunement1 hour*
Properties & techniquesExact player-facing procedures

Diagnosis

first hit/turn marks the wound until end of next turn.

Revision

hit a Diagnosed target to choose: move the wound's damage type between slashing/piercing, reduce its speed 10 ft, or prevent reactions until start next turn.

Contradict the Chart

2 CE on Revision hit: Con save; on failure add 2 Tool Dice and move the target 5 ft as its body 'agrees' with a different injury location.

Malpractice Limit

the same creature can suffer only one Revision rider/round.

Balance & counterplay. Requires landing both halves and never rewrites lethal damage or injuries retroactively.
