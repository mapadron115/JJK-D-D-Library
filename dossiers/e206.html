Features & procedures

Properties & techniques

Exact player-facing procedures

Diagnosis

The first cut is the question. One hit a turn marks the wound until your next turn ends — the chart written on the body, ready for revision. first hit/turn marks the wound until end of next turn.

Revision

The second cut is the answer. Strike a Diagnosed wound and revise it: change the damage type, halve its speed, or steal its reactions. The body agrees with the chart; the knives are teaching it to lie. hit a Diagnosed target to choose: the Revision hit itself deals the other damage type (invented); the target's speed is reduced by 10 ft until the start of its next turn (invented); or the target cannot take reactions until the start of its next turn (invented).

Contradict the Chart

Spend two CE on the Revision strike and the body agrees further — more damage, and the wound ‘moves’ five feet as the target's own injury relocates under contradiction. 2 CE on Revision hit: Con save; on failure add 2 Tool Dice and move the target 5 ft as its body 'agrees' with a different injury location.

Malpractice Limit

One rider per round per creature — the revision is a discipline, not a lottery. The registry insists: charts can be contradicted, but never rewritten wholesale. the same creature can suffer only one Revision rider/round.

Field notes. Subject: a surgeon's habit made steel. Three truths govern it. First, it never trusted the first diagnosis: every wound it cuts becomes a chart, and charts can be contradicted — the engine is not the blade but the mark it leaves behind. Second, the revision is bounded: a changed damage type, a slowed stride, stolen reactions — never rewritten lethality, never anything retroactive. I have watched surgeons do worse with permission. Third, the limit is the honesty of it: one rider per round per creature, lest the tool promise more medicine than any body could survive. Mark this: it asks the body to reconsider where and how it was hurt — and the body, being polite, agrees.

Rules integration

Cursed-tool attunement

Campaign canon

Cursed-tool attunement: you have PB attunement slots (2–6). This tool occupies one slot and requires a one-hour ritual. Attunement ends voluntarily over a short rest, on death, or when you replace it while over capacity. Distance does not break attunement. Cursed-tool attunement is separate from magic-item attunement.
Balance & counterplay. Requires landing both halves and never rewrites lethal damage or injuries retroactively.
