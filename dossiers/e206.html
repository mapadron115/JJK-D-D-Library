Features & procedures

Properties & techniques

Exact player-facing procedures

Diagnosis

first hit/turn marks the wound until end of next turn.

Revision

hit a Diagnosed target to choose: the Revision hit itself deals the other damage type (invented); the target's speed is reduced by 10 ft until the start of its next turn (invented); or the target cannot take reactions until the start of its next turn (invented).

Contradict the Chart

2 CE on Revision hit: Con save; on failure add 2 Tool Dice and move the target 5 ft as its body 'agrees' with a different injury location.

Malpractice Limit

the same creature can suffer only one Revision rider/round.

The fiction. Registry scholars describe Second Opinion as a surgeon's habit made steel — a tool imprinted with the cursed energy of one who never trusted a first diagnosis, so its cuts keep asking the body to reconsider where and how it was hurt. Every wound is a chart, and charts can be contradicted.

Rules integration

Cursed-tool attunement

Campaign canon

Cursed-tool attunement: you have PB attunement slots (2–6). This tool occupies one slot and requires a one-hour ritual. Attunement ends voluntarily over a short rest, on death, or when you replace it while over capacity. Distance does not break attunement. Cursed-tool attunement is separate from magic-item attunement.
Balance & counterplay. Requires landing both halves and never rewrites lethal damage or injuries retroactively.
