Field entry

Journal Entry field report, converted to a table-ready stat block

Field notes. Subject: the loyal monster as luggage. Graded Grade 3 not for its fists, it has none; no offensive technique, no instinct for battle, and it flees when attacked, but for the extradimensional stomach alone. As an action it swallows any nonliving object up to five cubic feet within reach, stores any number of them, and produces them on demand. (Living creatures cannot be stored. Do not test this.) I have watched veteran sorcerers treat it as a pet, and I advise against the comfort: affection is not loyalty, and its obedience dissolves the moment cursed energy shifts. Classify correctly, not a weapon, not a friend, but a warehouse that follows you. Useful beyond measure; trusted not at all.

Record

Classification & Risk

An Imaginary Cursed Spirit, Grade 3 (CR 1–3 band) — graded solely for its unique spatial storage ability. No noticeable offensive technique, no capacity for direct harm, almost no instinct to fight.

Stats

Ability scores & traits

STR 6 (−2) · DEX 12 (+1) · CON 14 (+2) · INT 4 (−4) · WIS 10 (+0) · CHA 10 (+0). HP 32 (5d10 + 5), AC 13, speed 25 ft., climb 25 ft. Invisibility. Docile: never initiates combat; if attacked it attempts to flee.

Actions

Actions

Action · No CE · Spatial Storage: swallow one nonliving object (up to 5 cubic feet) within reach into extradimensional storage, or produce one stored object into an adjacent free space. Stores unlimited objects; living creatures can't be stored.

Spatial Storage. As an action, the curse swallows one nonliving object of up to 5 cubic feet that it can reach, storing it in an extradimensional space, or produces one stored object into an adjacent free space. It can store any number of objects; living creatures cannot be stored. No attack actions.

Risk

Special

Affection is not the same as loyalty, and obedience can dissolve the moment cursed energy shifts — handle as unpredictable despite its cooperative nature. In combat terms it is barely stronger than a nuisance spirit; its value is entirely utility.

Field rule — low-grade perception: Per the C&C field rules, Grade 3 and lower curses are invisible to creatures without cursed energy. A creature in a life-or-death situation at less than half its hit point maximum may temporarily or permanently gain the ability to see them. This spirit carries the Invisibility trait below.
