Curse Detector

A Grade 3 cursed item, a small device resembling a geiger counter with a simple screen.

FormCursed Item
GradeGrade 3
AttunementNot required

Field notes. Subject: a bloodhound of copper and paper tags, that reads the battlefield the way a scout reads a map. Three truths govern the detector. First, foreknowledge is the cheapest victory: it grants advantage on the Wisdom or Cursed Energy check to find and follow cursed-energy trails, because a trail found is half the fight won. Second, every trail has a signature, and strong curses write in a hand the weak cannot read: the DC equals the tracked creature's own cursed energy save DC, and the detector names the owner only if the bearer has felt them before, strangeness has no name. Third, the instrument drinks the same fuel it hunts: a number of uses equal to your proficiency bonus, and 1 cursed energy recharges it. Never fight on ground you have not first read.

Exact player-facing procedures

PB uses

Trace Residuals

Use item (PB uses per recharge) · Gain advantage on a Wisdom or Cursed Energy (Perception) check to locate cursed-energy residuals · DC = tracked creature's CE save DC · On success: follow the trail and recognize familiar CE signatures.

You can use this item to gain advantage on a Wisdom or Cursed Energy (Perception) check to locate cursed-energy residuals. The DC equals the cursed energy save DC of the creature being tracked. On a success, you can track where the cursed energy trail leads, and you are aware of whose cursed energy it is if you are familiar with them.

Recharge

Recharge the Device

Recharge · PB uses before a recharge is needed · Spend 1 CE to recharge.

You can use the detector a number of times equal to your proficiency bonus before it needs to be recharged. You can recharge it by spending 1 cursed energy.
