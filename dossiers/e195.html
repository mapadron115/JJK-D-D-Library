Headless Road

Dullahan inspiration. Manifest a black steed and a detachable spectral Head. Mark a Road's End; moving 20+ ft closer generates Gallop that fuels pursuit strikes and control.

Combat roleMounted pursuit / doom controller
Power tierTier IV · Special Grade
Core law

Character engine

Manifest a black steed and a detachable spectral Head. Mark a Road's End; moving 20+ ft closer generates Gallop that fuels pursuit strikes and control.

Technique save DC: 8 + PB + chosen CE ability modifier. Technique attack: PB + chosen CE ability modifier.

Full progression

Level progression

1Black Steed

Bonus, 1 CE, 10 minutes: speed 50. Spend 1 Gallop after a 20-ft straight approach to add 1 die necrotic to melee hit.

3Head Held High

Bonus detach spectral Head within 30 ft; sight/hearing originate there. AC 15, HP 2×level; if destroyed it returns and deals 1 die psychic to you.

6Name on the Road

Action, 2 CE; creature seen by Head Wis save or becomes Road's End for 1 minute; know its direction within 1 mile and it cannot hide from Head except total cover/anti-divination.

11Whip of the Spine

Technique melee, 2 CE, 15-ft reach: 3 dice necrotic; Con fail removes reactions and -15 speed; Road's End may instead be pulled 15 ft.

18Domain Expansion: The Road With No Turning

Full-turn action, 20 CE, enclosed 90-foot radius centered on you, up to 1 minute, Technique Focus. A moonless road loops impossibly through the barrier. At initiative 20 each chosen hostile must choose one disclosed route-law: Advance, moving 15 feet in a legal direction of its choice without provoking; or Hear the Hooves, remaining in place and gaining 1 Doom. At 3 Doom it becomes your Road’s End until the Domain ends and its Doom resets to 0. This is a rule-based Domain: the choice itself is the law, not automatic damage. A creature protected by an anti-domain defense may choose to ignore the Doom gain for that event, but doing so still forfeits Advance. Ordinary attacks still roll.

Rules integration

Domain procedure

This entry uses the repaired campaign Domain rules. The level-18 feature above is complete: its printed action, CE cost, radius, duration, initiative-20 law, fallback protection, and counterplay replace any legacy blanket auto-hit wording.
Rules integration

Counterplay

Break line of sight to or attack the detached Head, force movement away from Road's End, and keep moving in the Domain rather than accumulating Doom.
