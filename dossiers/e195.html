Whip of the SpineTechnique melee, 2 CE, 15-ft reach: 3 dice necrotic; Con fail removes reactions and -15 speed; Road's End may instead be pulled 15 ft.
Domain Expansion: The Road With No TurningFull-turn action, 20 CE, enclosed 90-foot radius centered on you, up to 1 minute, Technique Focus. A moonless road loops impossibly through the barrier. At initiative 20 each chosen hostile must choose one disclosed route-law: Advance, moving 15 feet in a legal direction of its choice without provoking; or Hear the Hooves, remaining in place and gaining 1 Doom. At 3 Doom it becomes your Road’s End until the Domain ends and its Doom resets to 0. This is a rule-based Domain: the choice itself is the law, not automatic damage. A creature protected by an anti-domain defense may choose to ignore the Doom gain for that event, but doing so still forfeits Advance. Ordinary attacks still roll.

Domain procedure

This entry uses the repaired campaign Domain rules. The level-18 feature above is complete: its printed action, CE cost, radius, duration, initiative-20 law, fallback protection, and counterplay replace any legacy blanket auto-hit wording.

Character engine

Manifest a black steed and a detachable spectral Head. Mark a Road's End; moving 20+ ft closer generates Gallop that fuels pursuit strikes and control.

Technique save DC: 8 + PB + chosen CE ability modifier. Technique attack: PB + chosen CE ability modifier.

Counterplay

Break line of sight to or attack the detached Head, force movement away from Road's End, and keep moving in the Domain rather than accumulating Doom.

Adaptation note. This is original JJK 5e homebrew inspired by Dullahan and the named tradition. The folklore/myth supplies the motif; the resource engine, CE economy, progression, feats, Maximum and Domain procedures are game mechanics created for this codex.
