Headless Road

Dullahan inspiration. Manifest a black steed and a detachable spectral Head. Mark a Road's End; moving 20+ ft closer generates Gallop that fuels pursuit strikes and control.

Combat roleMounted pursuit / doom controller
Power tierTier IV · Special Grade
Core law

Character engine

Manifest a black steed and a detachable spectral Head. Mark a Road's End; moving 20+ ft closer generates Gallop that fuels pursuit strikes and control.

Technique save DC: 8 + PB + chosen CE ability modifier. Technique attack: PB + chosen CE ability modifier.

Full progression

Level progression

1Black Steed

As a bonus action for 1 CE, you manifest a black steed for 10 minutes, gaining a speed of 50. After a 20-foot straight approach, you may spend 1 Gallop to add 1 die of necrotic damage to a melee hit.

3Head Held High

As a bonus action you detach your spectral Head, placing it within 30 feet; your sight and hearing originate from the Head. The Head has AC 15 and hit points equal to twice your level; if it is destroyed, it returns to you and you take 1 die of psychic damage.

6Name on the Road

As an action for 2 CE, choose a creature your Head can see: it makes a Wisdom save, and on failure becomes your Road's End for 1 minute. You know its direction within 1 mile, and it cannot hide from your Head except behind total cover or anti-divination effects.

11Whip of the Spine

Make a technique melee attack (2 CE) with 15-foot reach: on a hit, it deals 3 dice of necrotic damage. A creature that fails its Constitution save loses its reactions and has its speed reduced by 15. If the target is your Road's End, you may instead pull it 15 feet toward you.

18Domain Expansion: The Road With No Turning

Full-turn action, 20 CE, enclosed 90-foot radius centered on you, up to 1 minute, Technique Focus. A moonless road loops impossibly through the barrier. At initiative 20 each chosen hostile must choose one disclosed route-law: Advance, moving 15 feet in a legal direction of its choice without provoking; or Hear the Hooves, remaining in place and gaining 1 Doom. At 3 Doom it becomes your Road’s End until the Domain ends and its Doom resets to 0. This is a rule-based Domain: the choice itself is the law, not automatic damage. A creature protected by an anti-domain defense may choose to ignore the Doom gain for that event, but doing so still forfeits Advance. Ordinary attacks still roll.

Feats

Build-defining options.

Lantern Eyes

Your detached spectral Head gains darkvision out to 120 feet and can see invisible creatures and objects within 10 feet of it, its lantern-light burning through concealment. While your sight and hearing originate from the Head, you have advantage on Wisdom (Perception) checks that rely on sight. If the Head is destroyed while you are seeing through it, you are blinded until the end of your next turn as your senses snap violently back to your body.

Unbroken Gallop

When you would be forcibly moved, you may spend 1 Gallop as a reaction to refuse it: the forced movement becomes 0 as your steed's momentum carries straight through.

Death Coach

While your Black Steed is manifested, it may carry one willing ally in addition to you, and the ally shares your movement — including Gallop-fueled charges and Unbroken Gallop's refusal of forced movement — without costing either of you actions. An ally riding with you gains half cover against attacks coming from the steed's flanks. If the steed is dismissed or you are knocked prone while mounted together, the ally lands safely in an unoccupied adjacent space of its choice.

Last Name Spoken (11+)

As a bonus action, you can call your Road's End down the road: it must succeed on a Wisdom save against your CE DC or use its reaction to move up to its speed toward you by the most direct route.

Rules integration

Domain procedure

This entry uses the repaired campaign Domain rules. The level-18 feature above is complete: its printed action, CE cost, radius, duration, initiative-20 law, fallback protection, and counterplay replace any legacy blanket auto-hit wording.
Rules integration

Counterplay

Break line of sight to or attack the detached Head, force movement away from Road's End, and keep moving in the Domain rather than accumulating Doom.
