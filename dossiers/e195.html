Headless Road

Dullahan inspiration. Manifest a black steed and a detachable spectral Head. Mark a Road's End; moving 20+ ft closer generates Gallop that fuels pursuit strikes and control.

Combat roleMounted pursuit / doom controller
Power tierTier IV · Special Grade
Core law

Character engine

Manifest a black steed and a detachable spectral Head. Mark a Road's End; moving 20+ ft closer generates Gallop that fuels pursuit strikes and control.

Technique save DC: 8 + PB + chosen CE ability modifier. Technique attack: PB + chosen CE ability modifier.

Field notes. Subject: doom given an itinerary. Three truths govern the Headless Road. First: the rider is a herald, not an executioner. The technique treats the journey as mandatory and the arrival as already decided, the detached Head sees the marked target's name on the road, and the dullahan merely announces who is already gone. Second: inevitability compounds. Gallop accumulates when the steed closes distance in a straight line, twenty feet at a time, and the fuel of pursuit is the pursuit itself; the faster the arrival approaches, the harder it hits. Third: the mark is the sentence. Name on the Road binds the target's direction to the rider's knowledge, no hiding except behind total cover or anti-divination, and the Domain offers only two route-laws: Advance, or Hear the Hooves and gather Doom. I have watched marked prey flee and understood, watching, that the fleeing was the fuel. Consider: you cannot outrun an arrival that was scheduled before you started running.

Full progression

Level progression

1Black Steed

The journey begins with the mount. One CE, ten minutes, fifty speed, and Gallop accumulates at the end of every straight twenty-foot approach, spendable on necrotic strikes after the charge. Mark this: the steed is not transport. It is the first witness that the trip is happening. As a bonus action for 1 CE, you manifest a black steed for 10 minutes, gaining a speed of 50. Gain 1 Gallop at the end of any turn in which you moved at least 20 feet in a straight line while your steed is manifested, maximum PB (invented). After a 20-foot straight approach, you may spend 1 Gallop to add 1d8 of necrotic damage (invented: the die is a d8) to a melee hit.

3Head Held High

The sight leaves the body. Detach the spectral Head within thirty feet and see and hear from it, AC 15, twice your level in hit points, a die of psychic pain if it is destroyed. The dullahan's oldest trick: the rider keeps his eyes where the road goes, not where his body is. As a bonus action you detach your spectral Head, placing it within 30 feet; your sight and hearing originate from the Head. The Head has AC 15 and hit points equal to twice your level; if it is destroyed, it returns to you and you take 1d8 of psychic damage (invented).

6Name on the Road

The mark is the sentence. Two CE, a Wisdom save against the Head's sight, and the target becomes your Road's End for a minute, direction known within a mile, unhideable except behind total cover or anti-divination. This is not tracking. This is the itinerary being read aloud. As an action for 2 CE, choose a creature your Head can see: it makes a Wisdom save, and on failure becomes your Road's End for 1 minute. You know its direction within 1 mile, and it cannot hide from your Head except behind total cover or anti-divination effects.

11Whip of the Spine

The spine becomes the lash. Fifteen-foot reach, 3d8 necrotic, reactions stolen and speed cut on a failed Constitution, or, against the Road's End, a fifteen-foot pull toward the rider. The whip does not punish the marked. It shortens the distance the mark was always going to travel. Make a technique melee attack (2 CE) with 15-foot reach: on a hit, it deals 3d8 of necrotic damage (invented). A creature that fails its Constitution save loses its reactions and has its speed reduced by 15 until the end of your next turn (invented). If the target is your Road's End, you may instead pull it 15 feet toward you.

18Domain Expansion: The Road With No Turning

The journey becomes the law. A moonless road loops through the barrier; at initiative 20 each chosen hostile picks its route-law, Advance fifteen feet without provoking, or Hear the Hooves, stay, and gather 1 Doom. At 3 Doom the target becomes the Road's End until the Domain ends. The choice itself is the sure-hit: every option moves the rider's schedule forward. There is no turning. That was always the point. Full-turn action, 20 CE, enclosed 90-foot radius centered on you, up to 1 minute, Technique Focus. A moonless road loops impossibly through the barrier. At initiative 20 each chosen hostile must choose one disclosed route-law: Advance, moving 15 feet in a legal direction of its choice without provoking; or Hear the Hooves, remaining in place and gaining 1 Doom. At 3 Doom it becomes your Road’s End until the Domain ends and its Doom resets to 0. This is a rule-based Domain: the choice itself is the law, not automatic damage. A creature protected by an anti-domain defense may choose to ignore the Doom gain for that event, but doing so still forfeits Advance. Ordinary attacks still roll.

Feats

Build-defining options.

Lantern Eyes

The Head's lantern burns through concealment. Darkvision to one hundred twenty feet, invisible things seen within ten, and advantage on sight-based Perception while the senses ride the Head. The dullahan's passengers are never lost; the lantern has their names. Your detached spectral Head gains darkvision out to 120 feet and can see invisible creatures and objects within 10 feet of it, its lantern-light burning through concealment. While your sight and hearing originate from the Head, you have advantage on Wisdom (Perception) checks that rely on sight. If the Head is destroyed while you are seeing through it, you are blinded until the end of your next turn as your senses snap violently back to your body.

Unbroken Gallop

Momentum refuses interruption. When forcibly moved, spend 1 Gallop as a reaction and the movement becomes 0: the steed's straight line carries through. I have watched the rider hold his course through effects that would uproot a siege engine. Inevitability does not sidestep. When you would be forcibly moved, you may spend 1 Gallop as a reaction to refuse it: the forced movement becomes 0 as your steed's momentum carries straight through.

Death Coach

The journey takes a passenger. One willing ally rides with the steed, sharing its movement, Gallop-fueled charges, Unbroken Gallop's refusals, and gaining half cover against attack. This is the technique's one courtesy: the herald's road has room for two. While your Black Steed is manifested, it may carry one willing ally in addition to you, and the ally shares your movement, including Gallop-fueled charges and Unbroken Gallop's refusal of forced movement, without costing either of you actions. An ally riding with you gains half cover against attacks coming from the steed's flanks. If the steed is dismissed or you are knocked prone while mounted together, the ally lands safely in an unoccupied adjacent space of its choice.

Last Name Spoken (11+)

The mark is called down the road. One bonus action: the Road's End must save or spend its reaction moving its speed toward the rider by the most direct route. The name is spoken; the feet answer. The rider does not fetch. He summons, and the itinerary does the rest. As a bonus action, you can call your Road's End down the road: it must succeed on a Wisdom save against your CE DC or use its reaction to move up to its speed toward you by the most direct route.

Rules integration

Domain procedure

This entry uses the repaired campaign Domain rules. The level-18 feature above is complete: its printed action, CE cost, radius, duration, initiative-20 law, fallback protection, and counterplay replace any legacy blanket auto-hit wording.
Rules integration

Counterplay

Break line of sight to or attack the detached Head, force movement away from Road's End, and keep moving in the Domain rather than accumulating Doom.
