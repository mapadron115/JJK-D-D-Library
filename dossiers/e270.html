Core law

Completed rules

Purchased turns:

Turns are bought before the pause, floor of input over three, no fractional turns, no new spending inside. Majestic buys floor(input/3) turns; minimum useful input 3, and leftover CE gives no fraction of a turn. Pay before time stops. The larger campaign reserve buys more uses, not more input than the printed 2 PB cap. During stopped turns no new CE expenditure, including reinforcement, RCT, Brain Renewal, summons or another time stop. Existing paid weapons/passives continue unless their own text needs an upkeep payment then. Each granted turn has normal actions, bonus action and movement, but does not advance initiative, another creature’s turn, the outside round clock or an initiative-20 event. Duration outside stopped time does not expire merely because the caster took several internal turns.

Ranged attacks in stopped time:

Suspended arrows are recorded, not resolved, no death is declared while the projectile still hangs. record already-paid effects and resolve suspended ranged attacks in firing order when time resumes; then apply normal reactions as printed. Do not count damage as inflicted or declare a target dead while the projectile is still suspended.

Golden Week:

The alternative bargain: no stop at all, but a minute of doubled speed, harder armor, and a spare action with a short list. its extra action is limited to one weapon attack, Dash, Disengage, Hide or Use an Object; it cannot pay a Full Turn feature. Time stop remains unavailable while Week is active; dismissing Week at the start of your own normal turn costs no action.

The World timing:

The Domain's stop grants two turns at initiative 20: and anti-domain protection excludes the protected from the pause. The World grants TWO stopped turns at initiative 20 under the combined printed clauses. Majestic and Quick Pose inside gain one extra turn per activation, not one per purchased turn. No nested initiative 20, refresh of another reaction, or bypass of the CE-use prohibition. Anti-domain protection excludes a protected creature from the Domain-generated stop, allowing it its ordinary reactions in response to the caster; it does not award extra turns to that creature. Domain end cancels only its unspent granted turns.

Samsara Rewind ledger:

The rewind's accounting: snapshot at the turn's start, restore to the snapshot, and every cost paid after it stays paid. snapshot at each normal initiative turn start: HP/temp HP, CE, Healing Reserve, spent charges/uses, conditions and remaining durations, injuries, living/dead status, summoned/created objects and positions. Rewind restores affected entities to that snapshot, including any resources spent obtaining restored healing, so it cannot retain healing while refunding its reserve. All Samsara Rewind costs spent after that snapshot remain deducted after restoration; other creatures’ rewinds are also nonrefundable. Rewind cannot activate inside stopped time. Its target snapshot is the previous normal initiative turn, not an extra stopped-time turn. Select entities presently in the radius; restore position only if their former position is also in it, otherwise retain current legal position. Outside entities remain unchanged even if the restored target undoes damage they caused. Do not duplicate objects held across the boundary: each object has one identity and independently follows eligibility. Newly created eligible objects disappear if absent in the snapshot. Reversal of a death within the window is allowed. Memory and revealed information remain.

Quick Pose:

The pose quickened to a reaction, a dodge through stopped time, with the lethargy still waiting after. free-action activation of Golden Hour once per own turn under a redundancy rule; buying the same end-of-turn state repeatedly grants no extra turns. Reaction activation (5 CE) is declared at a discrete observed event before its unresolved effects; it can reposition but does not undo an already resolved hit. After time resumes, movement is 0 ft and you cannot take actions until the end of your next turn.

Prime Time:

Majestic sharpened: quadrupled speed and +5 to strikes inside the pause, for three more cursed energy on top. pays the selected Majestic input plus 3 CE, retaining the purchased turns while replacing its speed bonus with quadrupled normal speed and +5 attack within stopped time. The extra 3 CE is not additional turn-purchasing input. Both Prime Time costs are true and stack: the 3 CE upgrade grants quadrupled speed and +5 to attack rolls during stopped time, and the Prime Time feat additionally lets you spend 2 CE to carry one willing creature during stopped time. Once time resumes you are unable to move or take actions until the end of your next turn.

Maximum: Clock Hand:

The technique's weapon: a clock-hand blade with an Hour face for stopped foes and a Minute face that stops the unstopped. as a bonus action, spend 5 CE to summon the Clock Hand to your hands for 10 minutes. The Clock Hand counts as a Simple Melee weapon that you are proficient with and has the finesse and reach property (reach defaults to 10 ft). It deals 1d10 piercing damage on a hit. Hour Hand: if used against a creature in stopped time, the Clock Hand deals an additional 3d8 necrotic damage on a hit. Minute Hand: if used against a creature not in stopped time, on a hit the creature must make a Cursed Energy save or be frozen in time and suffer the penalties of stopped time until the end of their next turn. Clock Hand must be summoned before stopping time. Its passive riders need no additional CE. Minute Hand applies only the printed stopped-time penalties, not an invented stunned/paralyzed condition or automatic loss of actions.

Maximum Output:

Maximum Output counts the full activation cost, duration may stretch, but the turn-count is never multiplied. full activation cost is counted for Maximum Output; eligibility is checked per feature, not per technique name. MO duration can extend a measured duration, but does not multiply Majestic’s purchased turn count without an explicit new rule.

Field notes. Subject: the debtor of the clock, who bargains with stopped time and pays in full. Three truths govern her. First, nothing stops for free: every purchased turn is bought before the pause, Majestic Pose buys one turn per three cursed energy, and inside stopped time no new energy may be spent at all. Second, the pause has its own laws: ranged attacks hang suspended until time resumes, and the lethargy after is real, speed zero, no actions, until the next turn ends. Third, the technique keeps a deeper trick than pausing: Samsara Rewind takes the last six seconds back entirely, undoing damage and spent energy within a hundred feet, but memory and revealed information remain, and what was spent to rewind is never refunded. Mark this: the World at initiative 20 is the debt coming due all at once. Time remembers it was paused, and it collects in the order it was owed.

Rules integration

Edge cases and shared systems.

Rules priority: the reviewed rulings, explicit codex clarifications, and the level progression always control when original wording conflicts. Legacy Domain auto-hit language, action terminology, event timing, missing saves, and older scaling never override revised player rules.
Why Tier IV: stopped time with extra full turns and a one-round rewind is campaign-warping utility and offense; the printed costs (full-turn actions, lethargy, no CE during stops) keep it at Special Grade rather than calamity.
Time-theme guidance: Golden Hour shares a “time” theme with existing dossiers e103 and e133. Standing ruling: one per campaign—this is guidance, not a merge; mechanics are unchanged. Do not run two time-stop techniques in the same campaign without the table’s agreement.
Actions during stopped time: actions during stopped time can still damage the caster through valid ongoing hazards; check Stability normally.

Cursed Energy
 Starting at 1st level, you can harness your negative feelings into a
usable source of energy, called cursed energy. These feelings can be
harnessed into attacks or abilities. The number of cursed energy you
possess is based on your class, some classes use a modifier that
being charisma or wisdom (Player’s choice)
Your cursed energy DC is 8 + user cursed energy ability modifier +
proficiency bonus.

Golden Hour
 At 1st level, you can strike a pose and move so quickly it seems as
if time has stopped. As a bonus action for 1 cursed energy you can
stop time until the end of your turn. During stopped time, you cannot
use cursed energy, and are affected by the following:
   - You have advantage on all attack rolls against creatures in stopped time.
   - Ranged attacks you make become affected by stopped time and are suspended in mid-air, but finish when time resumes.
   - Other creatures in stopped time automatically fail Strength and Dexterity saves and cannot use reactions. (A creature can use their reaction against a ranged attack you have made once time has resumed.)

Majestic Pose
 At 3rd level, you can instead spend up to twice your proficiency bonus
in cursed energy as a Full Turn Action (Action + Bonus Action +
Movement) to strike a majestic pose and immediately take one turn per
three CE spent in stopped time. Doing so places extreme stress on your
body. Once time resumes your movement speed is reduced to 0 until the
end of your next turn.
 When you stop time, your speed increases by 10 ft until time resumes.
This increases by 5 ft at 6th, 11th, and 18th level.

Golden Week
 At 6th level, you have begun to master your technique. You have
learned to use your technique for longer. As an action for 4 cursed
energy instead of stopping time you can empower yourself for longer,
gaining the following benefits for 1 minute:
   - Your speed is doubled.
   - You gain a +3 bonus to AC.
   - You have advantage on Dexterity saving throws.
   - You gain an additional action on each of its turns. That action can be used only to take the Attack (one weapon attack only), Dash, Disengage, Hide, or Use an Object action.
You cannot stop time until this feature ends.

Samsara Rewind
 At 11th level, you have gained the limited ability to reverse time.
As a Full Turn Action (Action + Bonus Action + Movement) for 8 cursed
energy you can strike a supreme pose and rewind time by 6 seconds,
taking all creatures and objects within 100 ft back in time 1 round to
the start of your previous turn. Any damage taken or cursed energy
spent (other than this technique) during that round is undone and all
creatures are taken back to where they were 6 seconds ago as long as
it is within the 100 ft radius of the rewind.

Domain Expansion: The World

Light the color of late afternoon pours in from no visible sun, gilding every surface, every mote of dust, every drawn breath. The world holds its pose, caught mid-gesture like a dancer photographed at the apex of a leap, and the gold deepens by slow degrees toward something richer and more final. Time has not stopped. It has been gilded, and it gleams.

 Finally at 18th level, you have created a domain imbued with your
perfected technique. At initiative 20, you can stop time and
immediately take one turn as your domain empowers you. Whenever you
stop time in your domain using your technique, you stop time for an
additional turn.
