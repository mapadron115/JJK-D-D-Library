Ame-no-Habakiri

Features & procedures

Properties & techniques

Exact player-facing procedures

Feature

Spend Scale after hit for +1 Tool Die, max 2.

Eightfold Cut

3 CE: make one attack against up to three adjacent segments/targets of same Huge+ creature; resolve one attack roll and one damage instance, +1 Tool Die per Scale spent.

Blood on the Blade

when Large+ target drops to 0, regain 2 CE once/short rest.

Release: Orochi-Sundering Edge

7 CE, 1 minute: reach +5, Scale generation twice/round, ignore resistance (not immunity) to slashing. Release does not bypass immunities and does not replicate the Soul Split Katana’s apex bypass.

The fiction. Jujutsu scholars classify Ame-no-Habakiri as a legend that learned to cut — a blade whose cursed energy imprinted on the oldest story of the serpent slain, and which now recognizes that shape wherever it finds it. Each Scale is the tool's memory of the hunt, the prevailing theory holds: the sword does not strike serpents because it is told to, but because that is the only thing it was ever made to remember.

Rules integration

Cursed-tool attunement

Campaign canon

Cursed-tool attunement: you have PB attunement slots (2–6). This tool occupies one slot and requires a one-hour ritual. Attunement ends voluntarily over a short rest, on death, or when you replace it while over capacity. Distance does not break attunement. Cursed-tool attunement is separate from magic-item attunement.
Balance & counterplay. Boss-hunter specialization is intentionally narrower than a general-purpose Special Grade sword.
