Release: Orochi-Sundering Edge

7 CE · 1 minute: +5 ft reach, bank Scale twice per round, and ignore resistance (not immunity) to slashing; does not bypass immunities or replicate the Soul Split Katana's apex bypass.

Seven CE for one minute of the full legend: five more feet of reach, memory banked twice as fast, and slashing that ignores resistance, with the dossier's own boundary stated twice, so the bearer remembers it: immunities stand, and the apex bypass belongs to another blade. 7 CE, 1 minute: reach +5, Scale generation twice/round, ignore resistance (not immunity) to slashing. Release does not bypass immunities and does not replicate the Soul Split Katana’s apex bypass.

Field notes. Subject: a legend that learned to cut. Three truths govern him. First, each Scale is the tool's memory of the hunt: striking Large creatures or serpent-and-dragon shapes banks the memory, and the sword spends it as extra dice, it does not strike serpents because it is told to, but because that is the only thing it was ever made to remember. Second, it hunts the way the old story hunts: Eightfold Cut takes one attack roll across up to three segments of the same Huge thing, spending Scales for more dice, the serpent was always one body, and the sword treats it as one wound. Third, the hunt feeds itself: when a Large foe falls to the blade, the bearer regains CE once before the next short rest, blood on the blade, strength in the arm. Mark the Release's honesty: reach, doubled memory, and slashing that ignores resistance, but never immunity, and never the Soul Split Katana's apex. Even legends have jurisdictions.

Rules integration

Cursed-tool attunement

Campaign canon

Cursed-tool attunement: you have PB attunement slots (2–6). This tool occupies one slot and requires a one-hour ritual. Attunement ends voluntarily over a short rest, on death, or when you replace it while over capacity. Distance does not break attunement. Cursed-tool attunement is separate from magic-item attunement.
Balance & counterplay. Boss-hunter specialization is intentionally narrower than a general-purpose Special Grade sword.
