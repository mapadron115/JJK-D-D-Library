The fiction. Registry scholars note that a cursed tool has no origin of its own — it has a previous owner whose will was too stubborn to dissipate. The prevailing theory holds the Ruyi Jingu Bang carries the resolve of a wielder who learned exactly one lesson: no distance too far, no burden too heavy, no hiding place the staff cannot find you from. Its cursed energy is borrowed obedience, and attunement is the tool deciding the new hand is worthy of the old lesson.

Rules integration

Cursed-tool attunement

Campaign canon

Cursed-tool attunement: you have PB attunement slots (2–6). This tool occupies one slot and requires a one-hour ritual. Attunement ends voluntarily over a short rest, on death, or when you replace it while over capacity. Distance does not break attunement. Cursed-tool attunement is separate from magic-item attunement.
Balance & counterplay. Extreme reach is map-dependent and still costs actions/CE for battlefield-scale extension.
