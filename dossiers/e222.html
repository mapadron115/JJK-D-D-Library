Features & procedures

Properties & techniques

Exact player-facing procedures

Pillar

Action · 2 CE · Extend to a 60-ft line: Str save or 3 Tool Dice bludgeoning and pushed to the line’s end.

When discretion fails, the staff offers sixty feet of its oldest answer: a line that strikes and then relocates the enemy to the far end of it. Push first; ask later. action, 2 CE: extend to 60 ft in a line; Str save, 3 Tool Dice bludgeoning and push to line's end on failure.

Needle

Shrink to hairpin · Advantage to conceal it; draw as no action, once per turn.

The other extreme of the negotiation: a staff that becomes a hairpin, drawn for no action at all. The greatest reach in the catalogue, disguised as the least. shrink to hairpin; advantage to conceal it, draw as no action once/turn.

Sea-Pinning Weight

On hit · 3 CE · Str save or the target’s speed becomes 0 until end of your next turn.

Three CE buys the staff's heaviest opinion: whatever it touches, stops. A pinned enemy is a siege fought on the bearer's terms. 3 CE on hit: Str save or target speed 0 until end next turn.

Release: Heaven-Measuring Staff

Bonus action · 7 CE · 1 minute: reach 30 ft, Pillar extends 120 ft, count as Huge for shove/grapple leverage through the staff.

Seven CE for one minute of unmeasured ambition: thirty feet of reach, a hundred and twenty of Pillar, and the leverage of a giant wherever the staff touches. For this minute, the bearer measures the heaven of the battlefield and finds it short. 7 CE, 1 minute, bonus action to activate (invented): reach 30 ft, Pillar 120 ft, count as Huge for shove/grapple leverage through staff.

Field notes. Subject: the staff that lends its own length. Three truths govern him. First, reach is not a property but a negotiation: a bonus action buys anywhere from a hand's length to twenty feet, and only cramped rooms, the staff's one honest complaint, impose their price. Second, the staff answers its true size at both extremes: a Needle hidden as a hairpin for the discreet, a Pillar of sixty feet for the forthright, and between them every length a battlefield has ever wanted. Third, weight is the last argument: Sea-Pinning Weight pins the struck foe where he stands, and the Release teaches the bearer to stand Huge through the staff alone. Consider: the sorcerer does not carry a weapon. He carries the entire range of every fight he might ever join.

Rules integration

Cursed-tool attunement

Campaign canon

Cursed-tool attunement: you have PB attunement slots (2–6). This tool occupies one slot and requires a one-hour ritual. Attunement ends voluntarily over a short rest, on death, or when you replace it while over capacity. Distance does not break attunement. Cursed-tool attunement is separate from magic-item attunement.
Balance & counterplay. Extreme reach is map-dependent and still costs actions/CE for battlefield-scale extension.
