Blind Spot

Tax enemy reactions to maintain observation, then explode through any lapse in line of sight with impossible movement and a punishing follow-up strike.

Combat roleAssassin / movement controller
Full progression

Progression

1Observation Protocol

Observe.

At the start of its turn, a hostile creature within 60 feet that can see you may spend its reaction to Observe you until the start of its next turn. Observation ends early if it loses line of sight, becomes blinded or incapacitated, or an effect suppresses its senses. A creature cannot Observe if it has no reaction available.

Source Rule.

While at least one Observer has direct line of sight to you, you cannot use Blind Spot Step, Blink Reflex, or another feature in this technique that says it requires an unobserved interval. Your ordinary movement and class features still work; this is the playable translation of SCP-173 being immobile under direct observation.

Blind Spot Step.

Bonus action, 1 CE. If no hostile Observer can currently see you, teleport up to 20 feet to an unoccupied space you can perceive. This movement provokes no opportunity attacks.

Once per round · no action

Blink Advance

When a creature Observing you spends or loses its reaction for any reason, you may move up to 15 feet without provoking. This works while observed.

Snap Strike.

Once per turn, the first melee weapon or unarmed hit you land after a Blind Spot Step deals no bonus damage. The target makes a Constitution save: on a success it cannot take reactions until the end of its next turn; on a failure it is knocked prone and cannot take reactions until the end of its next turn.

3Blink Reflex

Once per round when the final Observer currently watching you stops Observing, you may use your reaction to teleport up to half your Blind Spot Step range without spending CE.

6Peripheral Failure

Action, 2 CE: up to PB Observers within 60 feet make Constitution saves. On a failure their Observation ends and they cannot Observe you again until the end of their next turn. Snap Strike upgrade: a failed save also blinds the target until the end of its next turn.

11Impossible Interval

Blind Spot Step range becomes 60 feet. Once on your turn you may pay its CE cost and use it immediately before one attack in your Attack action instead of using a bonus action. Snap Strike upgrade: the blind lasts until the end of your next turn, and you may move 10 feet without provoking after the Step.

18Containment Chamber: DON’T BLINK

Snap Strike upgrade: a failed save denies the target reactions until the end of your next turn and blinds it until the end of your next turn. You gain the Domain below.

Pinnacle expression

Domain Expansion — Containment Chamber: DON’T BLINK

Expansion Full-turn action, 20 CE, enclosed 60-foot-radius Domain for 1 minute. Every hostile creature knows exactly what Observation means when the barrier forms.

Biological Blink — sole sure-hit At initiative count 20, every chosen hostile creature’s Observation immediately ends and it cannot Observe you until the start of its next turn. A protected creature instead makes a Constitution save; on a success it may continue Observing if it already was.

Blink Windows Gain one Blink Window for each hostile affected by Biological Blink, maximum PB. Before the next initiative count 20, spend one Window instead of CE when using Blind Spot Step. You may spend no more than one Window per turn or reaction.

Accuracy remains yours Neck attacks, unarmed strikes, weapon attacks, and Snap Strike still require their normal attack rolls. The Domain guarantees the lapse in observation, not the kill.

Clash bookkeeping 500 clash points (Tier III). In a Domain clash, a 100+ point advantage collapses the weaker Domain; collapse deals no damage. Barrier: AC 12 + PB; HP = 10 × max(1, your CE modifier) + 500. Burnout: 10 rounds.

Rules integration

Table Rulings

Observation is a reaction commitment, not passive 360-degree D&D vision. This is what makes the SCP rule tactical instead of shutting the user off whenever an enemy exists.
Invisible creatures can Observe only if they can actually perceive you. Blindsight, truesight, cameras, familiars, and similar senses count if they provide direct perception.
Blind Spot Step is teleportation for rules purposes. It does not pass through anti-teleportation effects.
The technique never grants a literal instant neck snap against a healthy combatant; high-level lockdown is represented by Snap Strike riders and the Domain.
Snap Strike deals no bonus damage.
