Blind Spot

Tax enemy reactions to maintain observation, then explode through any lapse in line of sight with impossible movement and a punishing follow-up strike.

RoleAssassin / movement controllerSourceSCP Wiki

Core Engine — Observation Protocol

  • Blink Die: d6 → d8 → d10 → d12 using the shared scaling table.
  • Observe. At the start of its turn, a hostile creature within 60 feet that can see you may spend its reaction to Observe you until the start of its next turn. Observation ends early if it loses line of sight, becomes blinded or incapacitated, or an effect suppresses its senses. A creature cannot Observe if it has no reaction available.
  • Source Rule. While at least one Observer has direct line of sight to you, you cannot use Blind Spot Step, Blink Reflex, or another feature in this technique that says it requires an unobserved interval. Your ordinary movement and class features still work; this is the playable translation of SCP-173 being immobile under direct observation.
  • Blind Spot Step. Bonus action, 1 CE. If no hostile Observer can currently see you, teleport up to 20 feet to an unoccupied space you can perceive. This movement provokes no opportunity attacks.
  • Snap Strike. Once per turn, the first melee weapon or unarmed hit you land after a Blind Spot Step deals +1 Blink Die force damage. The target makes a Constitution save; on a failure it cannot take reactions until the end of its next turn.

Domain Expansion — Containment Chamber: DON’T BLINK

Expansion

Full-turn action, 20 CE, enclosed 175-foot-radius Domain for 1 minute. Every hostile creature knows exactly what Observation means when the barrier forms.

Biological Blink — sole sure-hit

At initiative count 20, every chosen hostile creature’s Observation immediately ends and it cannot Observe you until the start of its next turn. A protected creature instead makes a Constitution save; on a success it may continue Observing if it already was.

Blink Windows

Gain one Blink Window for each hostile affected by Biological Blink, maximum PB. Before the next initiative count 20, spend one Window instead of CE when using Blind Spot Step. You may spend no more than one Window per turn or reaction.

Accuracy remains yours

Neck attacks, unarmed strikes, weapon attacks, and Snap Strike still require their normal attack rolls. The Domain guarantees the lapse in observation, not the kill.

Technique Feats

Blind Spot, level 3

If you willingly move 0 feet on your turn, you may use a reaction before your next turn to reduce one damage instance by PB Blink Dice + CE modifier.

Blind Spot, level 6

No Peripheral Vision

If exactly one Observer can see you, you may attempt Blind Spot Step by paying +2 CE. That Observer makes a Wisdom save; on failure it experiences a micro-blink and ceases Observing before the Step resolves.

Blind Spot, level 11

Neck-Line Anatomy

Once per short rest when Snap Strike hits and the target fails its Constitution save by 5 or more, it is stunned until the end of your next turn instead of merely losing reactions.

Table Rulings

  • Observation is a reaction commitment, not passive 360-degree D&D vision. This is what makes the SCP rule tactical instead of shutting the user off whenever an enemy exists.
  • Invisible creatures can Observe only if they can actually perceive you. Blindsight, truesight, cameras, familiars, and similar senses count if they provide direct perception.
  • Blind Spot Step is teleportation for rules purposes. It does not pass through anti-teleportation effects.
  • The technique never grants a literal instant neck snap against a healthy combatant; high-level lockdown is represented by Snap Strike riders and the Domain.
