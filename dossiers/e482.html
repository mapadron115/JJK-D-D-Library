Feature map

Every feature the source provides

Base

Stone-Cut Body

Your unarmed strikes deal 1d8 slashing damage or bludgeoning damage (your choice per strike). Additionally, you automatically deal twice as much damage against objects and structures with your unarmed strikes or melee weapon attacks. Any cursed techniques you use against such targets deal maximum damage.

Form

Stone Cutting Strike

When you attack with an unarmed strike or melee weapon, you can spend 2 cursed energy to deal an additional 10 damage of the attack's base damage type on a hit. At 6th level this later increases to 15.

Form

Swift Fury

As a bonus action for 2 cursed energy, you can make two unarmed strikes or melee weapon attacks.

Build-defining options.

Feats for this technique

Keystone Break — Prerequisite: Stone Cutting Strike

[Licensed fill] When you reduce an object or structure to 0 hit points with an attack, the break keeps going: each creature within 10 feet of it makes a Dexterity saving throw against your technique save DC, taking bludgeoning damage equal to your proficiency bonus plus the strike's bonus damage on a failure, or half on a success.

Mason's Eye — Prerequisite: none

[Licensed fill] You read worked stone the way others read faces: you have advantage on Investigation checks concerning structures and constructions, and by touching a structure you learn its current hit points.

Living Quarry — Prerequisite: level 6

[Licensed fill] Constructs, earth elementals, and petrified creatures count as structures for your Stone-Cut Body: your unarmed strikes and melee weapon attacks deal twice as much damage to them, and your cursed techniques deal maximum damage to them.

Campaign canon notes

Note: The source says Stone Cutting Strike "later increases to 15" without naming a level; this dossier sets the improvement at 6th level, the standard minor-feature bump in this canon.
Note: The 2 CE for Stone Cutting Strike is spent when you declare the attack; the extra damage applies only "on a hit", so a missed strike wastes the CE.
Note: "Objects and structures" means non-creature targets — doors, walls, vehicles, pillars. Constructs, animated objects, and other creature-typed targets are creatures, not objects, and take normal damage.
Note: Maximum damage against objects means every variable die of the cursed technique counts as its maximum result; you still roll the attack and spend CE normally.
Note: The 1d8 unarmed die is a set value, not a bonus. If another feature sets your unarmed damage die, use the higher of the two.
