Major Cursed Technique

Stone Cutting Style

Your hands are the chisel; the world is the stone.

ClassificationMajor Cursed Technique
TierTier II · Versatile
CE abilitySTR
RoleStriker

Field notes. Subject: the mason’s answer to war. Three truths govern the style. First, the body is the tool: hands honed to chisels, striking with the weight of cut stone. Second, the world is the quarry: against objects and structures the art reserves its full force for what cannot resist it. Third, economy of blows: Stone Cutting Strike concentrates ruin into a single point; Swift Fury doubles the labor. Mark the maxim: the mason does not argue with the stone. He outlasts it, and what he outlasts, he breaks.

Feature map

Every feature the source provides

Base

Stone-Cut Body

Passive · No CE · Unarmed strikes deal 1d8 slashing or bludgeoning (chosen per strike) · Double damage vs. objects and structures; cursed techniques deal maximum damage to them.

The mason's first lesson: the hand is already a chisel, if you stop flinching. Stone learns to fear the unarmed man.

Form

Stone Cutting Strike

When you attack with an unarmed strike or melee weapon · 2 CE · On a hit, +10 damage of the attack's base damage type (15 at 6th).

One blow, all of it. The strike concentrates the whole quarry's patience into a single point of ruin.

Form

Swift Fury

Bonus action · 2 CE · Make two unarmed strikes or melee weapon attacks.

The mason does not rest between swings. Two strikes where one would do — the stone gets no time to remember its shape.

Feats for this technique

Keystone Break — Prerequisite: Stone Cutting Strike

When you reduce an object or structure to 0 HP · No CE · Creatures within 10 ft make a Dex save vs. your technique save DC, taking bludgeoning equal to PB + the strike's bonus damage (half on success).

Break the keystone and the arch remembers it was always falling. The rubble does the rest.

Mason's Eye — Prerequisite: none

Passive · No CE · Advantage on Investigation checks concerning structures and constructions; learn a touched structure's current hit points.

He reads worked stone the way others read faces. Every structure confesses its weak points to a patient eye.

Living Quarry — Prerequisite: level 6

Passive · No CE · Constructs, earth elementals, and petrified creatures count as structures for Stone-Cut Body.

In the master's hands, the definition of stone widens. If it stands like a structure, it breaks like one.

Campaign canon notes

Note: The source says Stone Cutting Strike "later increases to 15" without naming a level; this dossier sets the improvement at 6th level, the standard minor-feature bump in this canon.
Note: The 2 CE for Stone Cutting Strike is spent when you declare the attack; the extra damage applies only "on a hit", so a missed strike wastes the CE.
Note: "Objects and structures" means non-creature targets, doors, walls, vehicles, pillars. Constructs, animated objects, and other creature-typed targets are creatures, not objects, and take normal damage.
Note: Maximum damage against objects means every variable die of the cursed technique counts as its maximum result; you still roll the attack and spend CE normally.
Note: The 1d8 unarmed die is a set value, not a bonus. If another feature sets your unarmed damage die, use the higher of the two.
