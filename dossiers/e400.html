Basic Cursed Weapon

A Grade 4 cursed weapon — any weapon of any kind, imbued with a curse.

FormWeapon (Any)
GradeGrade 4
AttunementNot required

Field notes. Subject: the first lesson, made steel. Before a sorcerer can fight a curse, he must be able to touch it — and mundane steel passes through the enemy like wind through a net. This is the minimum rite: any weapon of any kind, imbued with a curse, now counted among the things that are real to curses and shikigami. Two truths govern it. First, it asks nothing of its bearer — no attunement, no coin of energy, no pact — because the threshold should be low enough for every student to cross. Second, it promises nothing beyond the threshold: an ordinary weapon, otherwise, that merely reaches where ordinary weapons cannot. Mark this: every great arsenal in the registry begins here, with a blade that can touch what it fights.

Curse-Imbued Strike

The whole doctrine in one rite: steel that counts as cursed, and therefore real to what ordinary steel cannot touch. No attunement — the first weapon of every student, asking nothing but a hand to hold it. The weapon functions as an ordinary weapon of its type, but it is considered a cursed weapon: its attacks can damage curses, shikigami, and any creature that resists or ignores damage from non-cursed physical weapons. No attunement is required.
