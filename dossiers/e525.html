The Unwritten

Become unmemorable. Witnesses forget you the moment you’re gone, records fade to blank, and even a learned enemy can be made to forget your tricks.

The Unwritten

The file on this subject is the thinnest we hold, and that is the point. Everyone who has met it remembers meeting someone. No one remembers meeting it. The researchers assigned to this file rotate frequently, because after a month they stop believing they were ever assigned.

Combat roleUtility
Core law

Antimemetic Presence

Unwritten.

Bonus action, 2 CE, 1 minute. Creatures that see you must succeed on a Wisdom save vs your CE DC or forget you the moment you leave their sight — they recall ‘someone,’ never who, never what you did.

The Damage Exception.

A creature you damage remembers you for 1 hour. Violence is memorable. The file underlines this twice.

Static.

Cameras, scrying sensors, and recorded footage show static where you stood. Divination about you fails or returns static. The interval does not respect recording devices.

Fading Ink.

Written records describing you fade blank within 24 hours. Ink runs from the page like it changed its mind.

Subject file

Containment & observation record.

Containment Procedures

No written records are kept on site. All notes are dictated and transcribed off-site daily, and the transcriptions are read once, then sealed.

Staff are rotated so no one works this file twice. After a month, they stop believing they were ever assigned — the rotation is not precautionary, it is scheduled.

Briefings are verbal, in pairs, with no names used. The subject is referred to as ‘the file.’

Research Log

Research log. 05/2024 — Acquisition: recovered from a records office that had no record of it. The office had a desk, a chair, and a nameplate with no name. The nameplate is the closest thing to a description on file.

Research log. 11/2024 — The forgetting is mapped: Wisdom save, sight-based, ‘someone’ never who. Damage breaks it for one hour. The file notes that every containment breach in the subject’s history began with someone deciding the forgetting was convenient.

Research log. 07/2025 — Fading Ink confirmed: three separate interview transcripts blanked within 24 hours. The interviewers remember being interviewed. They do not remember by whom, or about what, and two of them deny the transcripts are theirs. The transcripts are in their handwriting.

Research log. 03/2026 — True Unwritten observed in the field: a hostile cursed spirit that had adapted to the subject’s techniques was observed ‘re-learning’ them mid-engagement, its adaptations dissolving like writing in rain. The file has flagged this as the subject’s most significant property.

Incident Report — The Report.

A researcher submitted a full report on the subject — twelve pages, detailed, cross-referenced. The following morning she denied writing it. The report exists. It is in her handwriting. It describes events she does not remember witnessing, in a facility she does not remember visiting, concerning a subject she does not remember studying. She has been reassigned. She does not remember being reassigned. The report has been sealed, unread, because everyone who reads it forgets reading it, and the file cannot afford a twelfth page.
