Love Rendezvous

Five Southern Cross marks impose a route through space and violently attract matching stars after a violation.

The fiction. Jujutsu scholars describe Love Rendezvous as cartography made vindictive: the user's cursed energy maps the battlefield as a fixed constellation and treats any body that defies its charted route the way the sea treats a broken promise — dragged back along the star-line. The five marks are not cast, the prevailing theory holds; they are *claimed*, each one a navigational star the sorcerer's CE refused to stop sailing by.

Feature map

1Love Rendezvous:

Marking

Marking costs 1 CE, requires touch or a hit with an unarmed strike, and uses no action once per turn. One target bears one star; all objects and summons carrying that target’s CE share its mark but do not increase mark capacity. The sequence is Imai → Acrux → Mimosa → Ginan → Gacrux. Marks last 10 minutes. A mark’s light is visible; DC 15 Perception finds its name.

Capacity

You may maintain the same star on up to CE modifier targets (minimum one). If applying a mark would exceed the limit, choose an old mark of that star to end.

Route

A marked mover begins immediately after its own star in the sequence. It may always move away from marks. To willingly reduce distance to a differently marked creature or object, the destination must bear its current required star. Touching that star advances the requirement to the next. After touching Gacrux, its route is complete until its mark is reapplied. If the required star does not exist or no legal path reaches it without approaching a later star first, the route is dormant and movement is unrestricted until a legal route exists; check before each movement.

Violation

Illegal approach ends the movement; the mover is restrained until the start of its next turn. Every creature or object bearing the mover’s same star is pulled in a straight line toward the bearer of that star with the greatest current CE (objects have 0; ties are your choice), stopping adjacent or at the last legal space. This forced movement does not recursively trigger the technique. After witnessing a violation, a creature may use an action for Insight against your CE DC to understand the route, with disadvantage unless familiar with CE tracking or the Southern Cross.

Collision

For each pulled target that travels at least 5 feet and collides, roll one size die (Tiny d4, Small d6, Medium d8, Large d10, Huge d12, Gargantuan 2d12) for every 5 feet actually traveled — roll only for distance actually traveled before the collision. The anchor takes the full roll and the moving target takes half. Resolve each target separately; see the collision-die reference in the Reference section below. Immunity remains immunity.

3Effective Marking.

Effective Marking

You gain one additional reaction per round, usable only to mark a target you touch or hit during the triggering event. This produces the global maximum of two reactions, replacing the source’s undefined CE-modifier reaction pool.

Pinnacle expression

Maximum, Domain, or equivalent.

Domain Expansion — Star Crossed Lovers: 18 — full-turn action, 20 CE; 60-foot-radius enclosed Domain centered on you; 1 minute; Burnout 10 rounds. Sure-Hit Event — Star Crossed Lovers: at initiative count 20 each round, mark any creatures of your choice inside the Domain with any of your available stars. On your turn, as an action, you may slam a marked creature up to 30 feet toward another marked target; it takes collision damage per the technique’s size chart for the distance actually traveled, counting as the Domain’s guaranteed hit — an unprotected creature takes the full effect with no attack roll and no initial save. A creature protected by an anti-domain defense makes a Dexterity saving throw vs your CE save DC (invented); on a success it takes half the collision damage and is not moved. Marks created by the Domain disappear when the Domain ends.

Innate Technique Feats

Build-defining options.

These are optional character-building feats. You must meet each listed prerequisite before selecting it.

Prerequisite: Effective Marking

Lodestar

When you use Effective Marking's additional reaction, you can mark yourself instead of the triggering creature. You bear that star normally, counting toward your per-star limit. While you bear it, Gravity's violation pull can draw pulled creatures toward you whenever you hold the greatest current CE among that star's bearers.

Prerequisite: Gravity

Repelling Forces

Bonus action, spend 3–PB CE: every marked creature is forced directly away from the marked body with the greatest current CE, 10 feet per CE spent. This is simultaneous forced movement, causes no opportunity attacks, and stops at solid obstacles.

Prerequisite: Shining Star and CE score 20

Collapsing Stars

Action, 10 CE: choose a creature within 120 feet carrying all five marks. The marks collapse into it; Constitution save, taking 25d4 force on failure or half on success. All five marks are consumed. This damage cannot be multiplied by critical hits or other effects.

Prerequisites: Must have Effective Marking

Comet Reactions

Your reaction speed has further increased. At the cost of one CE, you gain the ability to use the additional marking reaction from Effective Marking for other purposes: the Dodge benefit (attacks against you have disadvantage until the start of your next turn) or the Disengage benefit (your movement doesn't provoke opportunity attacks until the end of your turn).

Rules integration

Edge cases and shared systems.

Rules priority: the reviewed rulings, explicit codex clarifications, and the level progression always control when original wording conflicts. Legacy Domain auto-hit language, action terminology, event timing, missing saves, and older scaling never override revised player rules.
Why Tier II: it can humiliate a much stronger opponent through spatial setup, but its power comes from visible preparation, route logic, and the user’s execution—not passive raw statistics.

Love Rendezvous
Love Rendezvous is a technique based on the Southern Cross. It marks
creatures or objects with cursed energy and makes the lowest star
have to pass through the other ones before they can move.
Cursed Energy

harnessed into attacks or abilities. The number of cursed energy you
possess is based on your class, some classes use a modifier that
being charisma or wisdom (Player’s choice)

Love Rendezvous
Starting at 1st level, you have a very complex technique, and you
have learned the basics of how to use it. As a free action when you
touch a creature or object(this can be done with yourself) with
cursed energy or as part of hitting an unarmed strike, you can spend

with the same star up to your Cursed Energy modifier. If you mark a

well, like Shikigami, cursed tools etc.
