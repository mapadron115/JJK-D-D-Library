Atlantean Kingship

Water shaping / marine command / trident / the ocean obeys

Power tierTier IV · Special Grade
Core law

What the technique actually does.

Core law, king's waters. The ocean keeps no account — it simply charges its king less. While you are in water or heavy rain, Atlantean features cost 1 less CE (minimum 1 CE). No pool, no tracker. The trident, once manifested, is the focus everything flows through.

Field notes. Subject: a king, not a swimmer. Three truths govern him. First: the water is not a weapon he carries, it is a subject that obeys — shaping it costs cursed energy because command is never free, even for royalty. Second: he does not fight alone; the telepathy is a draft notice, and anything with gills within a mile can be conscripted. Third: the trident is the crown made sharp — thrown, recalled, and the difference between a wave and a verdict. Mark the economy: the ocean discounts its king, and he never fights at a disadvantage near water.

Full progression

Level 1 through 20.

1Atlantean Blood

Passive · Your swim speed equals your walk speed; you breathe underwater; you are immune to deep-sea pressure and have resistance to cold damage; you can speak with beasts that live in water.

The blood remembers the trench: pressure is weather, cold is a rumor, and everything with gills speaks a language you were born understanding.

Action · 1 CE · Shape or move up to a 10-foot cube of water within 60 feet. When you take the Attack action, you may replace one attack with a lash of water: ranged CE attack against a creature within 60 feet, dealing 1d8 bludgeoning damage on a hit.

Water, asked politely and firmly: a wall, a lash, a hand. The ocean's first lesson is that it was always listening.

3Trident of the King

Bonus action · 2 CE · Manifest the trident in your hand: a martial melee weapon dealing 1d8 + WIS piercing damage, reach 10 ft, thrown (60 ft) — it flies back to your hand after a thrown attack. While you wield it, Water Shaping affects a 15-foot cube.

The crown, made sharp: thrown and returning, longer than it looks, and the water shapes itself larger around it. Kings don't carry scepters; they carry arguments.

6Marine Telepathy

Action · 2 CE · Compel sea-dwelling beasts within 1 mile: one swarm or up to PB individual beasts. They act on your turn and obey simple commands for 1 minute. Commanding sapient aquatic creatures allows a WIS save to resist.

The draft notice, signed by the throne: sharks, eels, and things with too many teeth arrive on your turn and take orders. Sapience gets a vote; instinct does not.

11King of the Seven Seas

Action · 4 CE · Once per short rest · Choose one: Tidal Wave — a 60-foot cone; creatures make a Strength save, taking 2d10 bludgeoning damage and falling prone on a failure (half, no prone on success), and are pushed 30 feet. Whirlpool — a 20-foot-radius vortex for 1 minute; creatures starting their turn inside make a Strength save or are restrained and pulled 15 feet toward the center (escape as an action).

The throne room, relocated: a wave that files paperwork as a siege engine, or a whirlpool that holds the sea's grudges in one place. The ocean does not do subtle when asked properly.

18The Trench Answers

Action · 6 CE · Call the deep: a leviathan breaches beneath a point within 120 feet. Creatures in a 60-foot radius make a Dexterity save, taking 4d10 bludgeoning plus 4d10 cold damage on a failure (half on a success). The area floods ankle-to-waist for 1 minute: difficult terrain for hostiles, free swimming for you.

Something down there heard its name: the breach, the cold that lives below light, and a battlefield that is suddenly, briefly, Atlantis. The king asked; the trench answered.

Pinnacle expression

Maximum, Domain, or equivalent.

Maximum, Leviathan's Wake (10 CE): the ocean stands up. A 120-foot wave breaks across a 120-foot line: creatures make a Strength save, taking 8d10 bludgeoning damage and being swept 60 feet on a failure (half, no sweep on a success). The area floods for 1 minute: difficult terrain for hostiles, and your Atlantean features cost 2 less CE (minimum 1 CE) while you stand in the flood. Commanded sea creatures within 1 mile converge on the area and fight for you for 1 minute without further CE. He does not summon the sea. He reminds it who it belongs to.

Feats

Build-defining options.

Pressure King

While submerged, your grapples carry the trench with them: a creature you grapple underwater takes 1d8 cold damage at the start of each of its turns and has disadvantage on checks to escape. The deep is not neutral ground; it is your palace, and the walls help hold people down.

Tidecaller's Step

Bonus action · 2 CE · Step into one body of water and emerge from another within 60 feet. Both must be at least a 5-foot cube. The ocean is one body; distance is a rumor it tells land.

Stormcaller's Wake

The trident remembers the sky too: action · 2 CE · call a lightning strike on a point within 120 feet; creatures in a 10-foot radius make a Dexterity save, taking 2d10 lightning damage on a failure (half on a success). Requires the trident manifested. Weather is just water with ambition.

Abyssal Gaze

Action · 2 CE · One creature within 30 feet that can see you makes a WIS save or is frightened of you for 1 minute (save ends). They looked into the water and the water looked back.

Rules integration

Edge cases and shared systems.

Codex rulings: Direct rolled technique/weapon attacks can use the campaign's Black Flash procedure when otherwise eligible. Passive damage, hazards, summons acting independently, and initiative-20 Domain events cannot.
Generic Maximum Output applies only to ordinary features, never named Maxima or Domains.
Commanded sea creatures use their own stat blocks and act on your turn; they are not shikigami and do not use summoning rules.
