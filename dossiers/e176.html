Gleipnir Breaker

Begin fights under self-imposed chains. Obeying them grants stability; snapping them at meaningful moments releases Ragnarök and turns enemy restraint into fuel.

Combat roleBinding-Vow Berserker / Anti-Control
TierTier IV · Special Grade
Core law

Character fantasy

Begin fights under self-imposed chains. Obeying them grants stability; snapping them at meaningful moments releases Ragnarök and turns enemy restraint into fuel.

Combat loop

  1. Choose restrictive Bonds before danger begins.
  2. Fight while genuinely obeying them.
  3. Break Bonds only when their trigger is earned.
  4. Spend Ragnarök on explosive movement, attacks, and anti-control until nothing remains bound.

Core law

Bonds & Ragnarök

Prepare Bonds:

after a long rest choose 2 Bonds from the list; level 6 = 3, level 11 = 5. Each Bond must meaningfully restrict you and cannot be ignored by alternate wording.

Breaking:

each Bond lists a trigger. When trigger occurs, you may declare it broken with no action. Gain 1 Ragnarök and its restriction ends until long rest. You cannot voluntarily break a Bond before its trigger.

Ragnarök cap:

number of prepared Bonds. Unspent Ragnarök lasts until long rest.

Bonds:

Laeding no weapons (trigger: take damage from a weapon); Dromi no reactions (trigger: ally within 30 feet is hit); Gleipnir speed capped 20 feet (trigger: hostile ends turn 30+ feet away); Tyr’s Hand cannot receive healing from others (trigger: fall below half HP); Moon Chain cannot spend more than 2 CE on one activation (trigger: hostile spends 5+ CE or opens Domain); Silent Fang cannot speak above a whisper/cast voice features (trigger: frightened/charmed/silenced or ally calls your name while endangered).

Full progression

Level progression

1Bound Wolf

Gain Bonds and Ragnarök. Spend 1 Ragnarök once per turn to add one Ragnarök Die to a melee hit, move 15 feet without provoking, or add one Ragnarök Die to a Strength/Constitution save.

3The Chain Remembers

When an enemy grapples, restrains, paralyzes, slows, or forcibly moves you, gain 1 temporary Chain token, maximum 1. Spend it like Ragnarök before end of your next turn. If you end the condition on yourself, gain PB temporary HP.

6Wolf at the Rope

Prepare 3 Bonds. When you break a Bond, creatures of your choice within 10 feet make Wisdom save or become frightened until end of your next turn. Spend 2 Ragnarök on a melee hit to add 3 Ragnarök Dice instead of 2 and knock Large or smaller target prone on failed Strength save.

11Five Impossible Bindings

Prepare 5 Bonds. Once per turn, if you are currently obeying at least 3 unbroken Bonds, reduce the CE cost of one Gleipnir Breaker feature by 1 (minimum 0). When you break your third Bond in an encounter, immediately end grappled/restrained on yourself and move 20 feet.

18Maximum: Ragnarök Unbound

Prerequisite: at least 3 broken Bonds. Bonus action, 8 CE, consume all Ragnarök, Focus, 1 minute. Gain +10 feet speed per consumed point, temporary HP = 2 Ragnarök Dice per point, and once per turn add one Ragnarök Die per consumed point (max 6 dice) to one melee hit. You are immune to frightened and cannot be restrained by non-Domain effects. The first time each round a creature tries to reduce your speed, grapple, restrain, or forcibly move you, you may make one melee attack against it if in reach. When Maximum ends, every prepared Bond counts as broken until long rest and you gain 1 exhaustion level.

Feats

Build-defining options.

Iron Appetite

While obeying Tyr’s Hand, healing you grant yourself through RCT is not prohibited, but costs +1 CE.

Moon-Eater

When Moon Chain breaks because an enemy opens a Domain, gain 2 Ragnarök instead of 1.

No Leash Holds Twice

After you succeed on a save against one creature’s grapple/restraint effect, you have advantage against that exact effect from that creature for 1 minute.

Týr’s Respect

If you complete an encounter without breaking one prepared Bond, regain 1 spent CE per unbroken Bond, maximum PB, after combat ends.

Rules integration

Building the character

Warrior or Juggernaut. The technique is powerful because its restrictions are real. It works best for players who enjoy planning around self-imposed limitations rather than trying to loophole them away.
Technique die: Ragnarök Die follows the shared d6 → d8 → d10 → d12 progression.
Rules integration

Counterplay

Do not trigger the Bonds on the user’s preferred schedule. Kite without satisfying Gleipnir, use damage types/attacks that avoid chosen triggers, and force them to choose between staying bound and losing tempo.
