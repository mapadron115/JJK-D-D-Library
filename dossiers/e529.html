Do Not Speak

Make a word about you unspeakable. Those who speak it draw the hazard — and you always know the moment they do.

Cursed TechniqueTier III · Grade 1
SCP-2521 inspired · Tier III · Grade 1

Do Not Speak

There is a word in this file that the file will not print. The researchers refer to it as ‘the designation.’ They are careful. They have read what happens to the careless, and the file — unlike its subject — can be read.

Combat roleController
Core law

The Unspeakable

Unspeakable.

Bonus action · 2 CE · Name one word · 1 hour · Any creature that speaks or writes the word within 1 mile: you instantly learn its direction and distance; the speaker makes a Wisdom save vs your CE DC or is frightened of you for 1 minute

One word — usually your name — becomes a tripwire the file will not print. The file does not need to print it: anyone who speaks or writes it has already told you exactly where they are, and the hazard is already listening.

The Hazard.

Passive · The hazard is never a statted entity and is never placed on a battlemap · Its arrival is resolved between the speaker and the GM

There is no stat block in this file, and there will never be one. What arrives does not answer to numbers, and the file has stopped asking it to.

The Mile.

Passive · Designation range: 1 mile · The hazard does not hurry

The range grows as the file thickens. The hazard has never once been observed hurrying — the timestamps have been checked — and it does not need to.

The Brush.

Trigger · When a creature speaks or writes the designation: it takes 2d8 psychic damage as the hazard brushes past

Contact leaves a mark now — not the hazard itself, just its passing. The file has stopped looking for a pattern in the damage. Some things are not patterns. Some things are warnings.

11Ten Miles

Ten Miles.

Passive · The designation's range becomes 10 miles

The tripwire stretches ten miles. The file has updated its maps. The maps look worse.

18It Comes

It Comes.

Reaction · When a creature speaks the designation: teleport to an unoccupied space within 30 ft of it · Barriers, restraints, and distance within range do not impede the arrival

The hazard does not hurry, but you do now. The file recorded one arrival under conditions it declines to describe as controlled. Once was sufficient.

Manifestation.

1/long rest · When a creature speaks the designation, you may release the hazard at it: 8d10 psychic damage and stunned 1 round · Wisdom save vs your CE DC for half damage; a success also avoids the stun

Once — and only once — the file recommends letting it all the way in. The aftermath was total silence: from the speaker, and from the recording equipment. The file's recommendation is a single sentence: do not complete the file's sentences.

Subject file

Containment & observation record.

Containment Procedures

The designation is never written in the file. Briefings are verbal, in pairs, spoken once. New staff are told the designation verbally, once, and then never again.

All documentation refers to ‘the designation’ or ‘the word.’ Euphemisms are mandatory. The file’s euphemisms are the most carefully maintained prose the Office produces.

Anyone who speaks or writes the designation is logged, interviewed, and reassigned. The log is verbal. The log is not written down. This sentence is the closest the file comes to breaking its own rule.

Research Log

Research log. 07/2024 — Protocol run 2521-A: attempted to create an unutterable designation for classified material. The test word was spoken once, aloud, in the lab. The notes were blank by the time they reached the Office. The linguist is on permanent leave. The leave was the linguist’s idea.

Research log. 01/2025 — The effect is mapped: one word, one hour, one mile — direction, distance, fear. The hazard itself has never been observed, only its consequences, which the file finds philosophically adequate.

Research log. 09/2025 — The Brush confirmed: 2d8 psychic to speakers. The file notes that the damage scales with neither the word nor the speaker, and has stopped looking for a pattern. Some things are not patterns. Some things are warnings.

Research log. 06/2026 — It Comes observed once, at range, under controlled conditions that the file declines to describe as controlled. 8d10 psychic, one round of stun, total silence afterward — from the speaker and, notably, from the recording equipment. The file’s recommendation is a single sentence: do not complete the file’s sentences.

Incident Report — The Field Report.

A researcher wrote the designation in a field report — fully, carefully, in context, the way you would write any other word. At 14:03 the report was blank. At 14:04 the researcher was on leave. The file does not record what happened at 14:03, because everyone who knows will not write it, and everyone who would write it does not know. The field report form now carries a warning in red ink. The warning does not name the word. The warning does not need to.
