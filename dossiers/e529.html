Do Not Speak

Make a word about you unspeakable. Those who speak it draw the hazard — and you always know the moment they do.

Do Not Speak

There is a word in this file that the file will not print. The researchers refer to it as ‘the designation.’ They are careful. They have read what happens to the careless, and the file — unlike its subject — can be read.

Core law

The Unspeakable

Unspeakable.

Bonus action, 2 CE: name one word (usually your name). For 1 hour, any creature that speaks or writes it within 1 mile: you instantly know the direction and distance, and the speaker must succeed on a Wisdom save vs your CE DC or be frightened of you for 1 minute.

The Hazard.

The effect is never a statted entity. It is described, never placed on a battlemap. What arrives is between the speaker and the GM — that is the entire point of the designation.

The Mile.

Range and duration scale with thresholds. The hazard does not hurry. It does not need to.

Subject file

Containment & observation record.

Containment Procedures

The designation is never written in the file. Briefings are verbal, in pairs, spoken once. New staff are told the designation verbally, once, and then never again.

All documentation refers to ‘the designation’ or ‘the word.’ Euphemisms are mandatory. The file’s euphemisms are the most carefully maintained prose the Office produces.

Anyone who speaks or writes the designation is logged, interviewed, and reassigned. The log is verbal. The log is not written down. This sentence is the closest the file comes to breaking its own rule.

Research Log

Research log. 07/2024 — Acquisition: recovered after a linguist’s field notes came back with a word in them that the linguist would not say aloud. The notes were blank by the time they reached the Office. The linguist is on permanent leave. The leave was the linguist’s idea.

Research log. 01/2025 — The effect is mapped: one word, one hour, one mile — direction, distance, fear. The hazard itself has never been observed, only its consequences, which the file finds philosophically adequate.

Research log. 09/2025 — The Brush confirmed: 2d8 psychic to speakers. The file notes that the damage scales with neither the word nor the speaker, and has stopped looking for a pattern. Some things are not patterns. Some things are warnings.

Research log. 06/2026 — It Comes observed once, at range, under controlled conditions that the file declines to describe as controlled. 8d10 psychic, one round of stun, total silence afterward — from the speaker and, notably, from the recording equipment. The file’s recommendation is a single sentence: do not complete the file’s sentences.

Incident Report — The Field Report.

A researcher wrote the designation in a field report — fully, carefully, in context, the way you would write any other word. At 14:03 the report was blank. At 14:04 the researcher was on leave. The file does not record what happened at 14:03, because everyone who knows will not write it, and everyone who would write it does not know. The field report form now carries a warning in red ink. The warning does not name the word. The warning does not need to.
