DJINN 37 • Life / Healing

Phenex — Kindness & Mediation

Rank S
Canon master: Kouen RenBasis: Canon core • healing, organ/limb transfer, Ring of Admonition

Weapon Equip • Level 5

Phoenix Hilt Ornament. Weapon Equip wraps the vessel in healing flame and allows precise Life Magic without conventional RCT.

Mending Phoenix

Action • 2 CE

A creature within 60 ft regains 4 Djinn Dice + your CE ability modifier. A creature can benefit from this specific power once per short rest.

Ring of Admonition

Action • 4 CE

A creature within 120 ft makes a Wisdom save. For 1 minute, the first time each round it willingly attempts lethal harm, the ring seals its murderous impulse: it loses reactions and must repeat the save or its action is wasted. Repeat save at end of turn.

Life Transplant

Action • 5 CE

Restore a destroyed limb/organ using compatible living material from a willing donor or a prepared biological substitute; alternatively end one major injury penalty, poison, blindness, deafness, or paralysis. No resurrection.

Full Djinn Equip • Level 9

Fly 120 ft; resistance to necrotic; life-sight 120 ft reveals living creatures and critical injuries. Healing abilities double range and add PB to each healing roll. Once per turn, stabilize a creature you can see within 120 ft without an action.

EXTREME MAGIC • LEVEL 13+

Phoenix Mediation

14 CE • Codex Extreme

Chosen living creatures in a 200-ft radius regain 10 Djinn Dice + twice your CE ability modifier and are restored to at least half maximum HP if that total would be lower. Restore one missing limb/organ and end one major condition on each. Hostile creatures acting with murderous intent make Wisdom saves or are Pacified (Incapacitated but conscious) until the end of your next turn. No resurrection.

Anime-scale: Strategic scale: mass-casualty restoration after battlefield devastation; this is an army-hospital Extreme rather than a damage nuke.
