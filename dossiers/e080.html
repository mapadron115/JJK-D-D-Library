Action • 4 Reserve

Ring of Admonition

Action · 4 Reserve · One creature within 120 ft · Wisdom save · For 1 minute, the first time each round it willingly attempts lethal harm, it loses reactions and repeats the save or its action is wasted; repeat save at end of its turns.

A creature within one hundred and twenty feet is marked by the ring, and for a minute, every attempt at lethal harm is sealed at the moment of commitment: reactions lost, the action wasted on a failed save. The admonition is not punishment. It is the flame remembering what murder felt like, and arriving first.

A creature within 120 ft makes a Wisdom save. For 1 minute, the first time each round it willingly attempts lethal harm, the ring seals its murderous impulse: it loses reactions and must repeat the save or its action is wasted. Repeat save at end of turn.

14 Reserve • Codex Extreme

Phoenix Mediation

Extreme Magic (Level 13) · Action · 14 Reserve · Requires Full Equip; once per long rest per Djinn · Chosen living creatures in a 200-ft radius regain 10 Djinn Dice + twice your CE ability modifier (raised to at least half max HP if that total is lower); restore one missing limb/organ and end one major condition on each · Hostile creatures acting with murderous intent: Wisdom save or Pacified (Incapacitated but conscious) until the end of your next turn · No resurrection.

The mediation, delivered at the scale of a battlefield: chosen living creatures in a two-hundred-foot radius restored, ten Djinn Dice and more, none left below half, limbs returned, conditions ended, while the murderous among them are pacified, incapacitated but conscious. An army hospital, not a damage nuke. The phoenix's final negotiation: everyone lives, and the war pauses to watch.

Chosen living creatures in a 200-ft radius regain 10 Djinn Dice + twice your CE ability modifier and are restored to at least half maximum HP if that total would be lower. Restore one missing limb/organ and end one major condition on each. Hostile creatures acting with murderous intent make Wisdom saves or are Pacified (Incapacitated but conscious) until the end of your next turn. No resurrection.

Anime-scale: Strategic scale: mass-casualty restoration after battlefield devastation; this is an army-hospital Extreme rather than a damage nuke.

Field notes. Subject: the phoenix that mediates. Three truths govern this kindness. First: the healing is grief given procedure, energy that learned, from bodies broken before its eyes, the exact shape of a wound, and answers it with a refusal to let death keep anything. Second: the Ring of Admonition is the same instinct inverted, flame that memorized what murder feels like at the moment of its commitment, and smothers it like a struck match. Third: the Extreme is an army hospital, not a nuke, mass restoration, limbs returned, conditions ended, and the murderous pacified. Mark the maxim: the phoenix does not fight the wound. It negotiates its surrender.

Rules integration

Djinn Die, Equip & Extreme Magic

Independent Djinn Reserve: 18 (Rank S). Refreshes on a long rest. Every Djinn cost below, Weapon Equip, Full Equip, abilities, reactions, Extreme Magic, spends this Djinn’s Reserve, never your CE. If this Djinn’s Reserve is empty it simply cannot fuel more until you rest; your other Djinn keep their own separate Reserves.
Weapon Equip · level 5: bonus action, 2 Reserve, lasts 10 minutes.
Full Djinn Equip · level 9: bonus action, 4 Reserve, lasts 1 minute. Only one Equip can be active.
Deep Synchronization · level 11: once per short rest, one Equip activation costs 1 Reserve less.
Extreme Magic · level 13: action; requires that Djinn’s Full Equip; pay the listed Reserve cost from this Djinn’s Reserve; usable once per long rest per Djinn. Full Equip ends afterward, and you cannot Equip that Djinn again until you finish a short rest.
King Vessel · level 17: switch directly from one Full Equip to another as a bonus action, paying the new Equip cost. At level 20, once per long rest, immediately transition from one Djinn’s Extreme into another Djinn’s Full Equip.
Attack / save: use your CE attack modifier and CE save DC unless this Djinn explicitly says otherwise.
