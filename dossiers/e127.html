Core law

What the technique actually does.

Core Engine — Density. Choose Solid, Light, or Phased as bonus action. Solid enhances strength; Light enables flight/mobility; Phased enables intangibility but limits offense. Fire exposure can force a Fear check and disrupt switching.

The fiction. The prevailing theory holds that the Core Engine is survival learned too well — density that can be anything, anywhere, because the sorcerer's cursed energy remembers being something that was hunted. Solid is what it became to fight, Light is what it became to flee, and Phased is what it became to disappear. Registry notes observe that only one thing breaks the engine's perfect composure: fire, which the technique treats not as damage but as a memory it never agreed to carry.

Full progression

Level 1 through 20.

L1Shapeshift

Alter your humanoid appearance freely; in Solid form your unarmed strikes deal 1d8 and you have Powerful Build. You gain telepathy (60 ft) with willing creatures.

L3Light Density

In Light density you gain a fly speed of 40, can squeeze through tiny gaps, and gain +10 speed.

L6Phase

For 2 CE, until the start of your next turn: you may move through creatures and objects, you gain resistance to bludgeoning, piercing, and slashing, and you cannot make physical attacks while Phased. If you end your turn inside an object, you are shunted out and take damage.

L9Mind Link

For 3 CE, target a creature; it makes a Wisdom save. On a failure you read its surface thoughts for 1 minute (requires Focus); willing allies can form a telepathic network of up to PB members.

L11Martian Strength

In Solid form your unarmed strikes deal 1d12, you count as two sizes larger, and once per turn you deal +1d8 force damage.

L13Deep Telepathy

For 5 CE, target a creature; it makes a Wisdom save. On a failure it is Stunned by psychic intrusion until the end of its next turn, or you alter one of its recent sensory memories (never its identity or personality).

L15Selective Phasing

You may phase only portions of your body; as a reaction, spend 2 CE to impose disadvantage on one physical attack against you without losing your ability to attack on your next turn.

L18True Martian

For 6 CE, for 1 minute: you become Large, gain a fly speed of 80, telepathy out to 300 ft, +2d10 psychic/force damage on your unarmed strikes, and you may switch Density freely once per turn.

L20Last Martian

Your fire weakness becomes manageable: after your first exposure, you gain advantage on the fear/disruption save. Phasing during the first round of each combat costs 0; your telepathic network’s range becomes 1 mile.

Pinnacle expression

Maximum, Domain, or equivalent.

Maximum — Psychic Communion (10 CE): all chosen creatures in 60 ft Wis save. Failures are linked for one round: you know their intended actions, they cannot hide from you, and take 10d10 psychic if they directly attack a protected ally; success halves damage and prevents link.

Feats

Build-defining options.

Detective Mind

Reading a mind is half of detection; understanding the reading is the other half. While you hold a creature's surface thoughts through Mind Link, you have advantage on Wisdom (Insight) checks against it, and you immediately sense when its words contradict the thoughts you are reading. Once per short rest, you may spend 1 CE as a bonus action to cross-reference: ask one yes-or-no question about the creature's current intentions, answered truthfully from what it is thinking right now.

Ghost

while Phased, you may partially solidify one hand as a bonus action to make a single unarmed strike or manipulate an object without ending Phase; attacks against you lose the B/P/S resistance until the start of your next turn.

Many Faces

Your shapeshifting is only skin-deep until you learn someone properly. If you observe a creature for at least 10 minutes — or read its surface thoughts — your Shapeshift can also mimic its voice, scent, and biometric details: retinal pattern, fingerprints, heartbeat cadence. Mundane scrutiny, guard dogs, and voice-print locks cannot tell you apart. Magical or supernatural detection still contests your technique DC, and you must have seen and heard the target to attempt this.

Fear of Flame

when you succeed on a save against fire exposure or fear, you may use your reaction to scatter your density: until the end of your next turn you count as Light density for movement (squeeze through tiny gaps, +10 speed) without spending the bonus action to switch.

Rules integration

Edge cases and shared systems.

Codex rulings: Direct rolled technique/weapon attacks can use the campaign's Black Flash procedure when otherwise eligible. Passive damage, hazards, summons acting independently, and initiative-20 Domain events cannot. Generic Maximum Output applies only to ordinary features, never named Maxima or Domains.
