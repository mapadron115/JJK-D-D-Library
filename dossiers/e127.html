Core law

What the technique actually does.

Core Engine — Density. Choose Solid, Light, or Phased as bonus action. Solid enhances strength; Light enables flight/mobility; Phased enables intangibility but limits offense. Fire exposure can force a Fear check and disrupt switching.

Full progression

Level 1 through 20.

1Shapeshift

Alter humanoid appearance freely; Solid form unarmed 1d8 and Powerful Build. Telepathy 60 ft with willing creatures.

3Light Density

Fly 40, squeeze through tiny gaps, +10 speed.

6Phase

2 CE until start next turn: move through creatures/objects, resistance B/P/S, cannot make physical attacks while Phased. Ending inside object shunts/damages.

9Mind Link

3 CE, Wis save: read surface thoughts for 1 minute Focus; willing allies can form PB-member telepathic network.

11Martian Strength

Solid unarmed 1d12, count two sizes larger, +1d8 force once/turn.

13Deep Telepathy

5 CE, Wis save: Stunned by psychic intrusion until end next turn or alter one recent sensory memory (not identity/personality).

15Selective Phasing

Phase only body portions; reaction 2 CE impose disadvantage on one physical attack without losing ability to attack next turn.

18True Martian

6 CE, 1 minute: Large, fly 80, telepathy 300, unarmed +2d10 psychic/force, switch Density freely once/turn.

20Last Martian

Fire weakness becomes manageable: advantage on fear/disruption save after first exposure. Phase first round each combat costs 0; telepathic network range 1 mile.

Pinnacle expression

Maximum, Domain, or equivalent.

Maximum — Psychic Communion (10 CE): all chosen creatures in 60 ft Wis save. Failures are linked for one round: you know their intended actions, they cannot hide from you, and take 10d10 psychic if they directly attack a protected ally; success halves damage and prevents link.

Feats

Build-defining options.

Detective Mind

Reading a mind is half of detection; understanding the reading is the other half. While you hold a creature's surface thoughts through Mind Link, you have advantage on Wisdom (Insight) checks against it, and you immediately sense when its words contradict the thoughts you are reading. Once per short rest, you may spend 1 CE as a bonus action to cross-reference: ask one yes-or-no question about the creature's current intentions, answered truthfully from what it is thinking right now.

Ghost

while Phased, you may partially solidify one hand as a bonus action to make a single unarmed strike or manipulate an object without ending Phase; attacks against you lose the B/P/S resistance until the start of your next turn.

Many Faces

Your shapeshifting is only skin-deep until you learn someone properly. If you observe a creature for at least 10 minutes — or read its surface thoughts — your Shapeshift can also mimic its voice, scent, and biometric details: retinal pattern, fingerprints, heartbeat cadence. Mundane scrutiny, guard dogs, and voice-print locks cannot tell you apart. Magical or supernatural detection still contests your technique DC, and you must have seen and heard the target to attempt this.

Fear of Flame

when you succeed on a save against fire exposure or fear, you may use your reaction to scatter your density: until the end of your next turn you count as Light density for movement (squeeze through tiny gaps, +10 speed) without spending the bonus action to switch.

Rules integration

Edge cases and shared systems.

Codex rulings: Direct rolled technique/weapon attacks can use the campaign's Black Flash procedure when otherwise eligible. Passive damage, hazards, summons acting independently, and initiative-20 Domain events cannot. Generic Maximum Output applies only to ordinary features, never named Maxima or Domains.
