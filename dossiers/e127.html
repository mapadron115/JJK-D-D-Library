Core law

What the technique actually does.

Core Engine, Density. Three skins on one wardrobe door, changed as a bonus action: Solid for strength, Light for flight, Phased for intangibility at the cost of offense. Fire is the lock on the door, fail the Wisdom save and fear takes the choice away. Choose Solid, Light, or Phased as bonus action. Solid enhances strength; Light enables flight/mobility; Phased enables intangibility but limits offense. When you take fire damage, make a Wisdom save (DC 10 or half the damage taken, whichever is higher); on failure you are frightened of the fire source until the end of your next turn and cannot switch density until then.

Field notes. Subject: a survivor's wardrobe of bodies. Three truths govern him. First: his cursed energy remembers being hunted, so it learned density as a decision: Solid to fight, Light to flee, Phased to be elsewhere entirely; the bonus action is the wardrobe door. Second: the forms are a complete doctrine, Solid's 1d12 and Powerful Build, Light's forty-foot flight and tiny gaps, Phase's intangibility with its honest price: no physical attacks, and the shunt punishes a careless exit inside stone. Third: the mind is the fourth form, telepathy at sixty feet, deep intrusion at thirteen, a network of allies at nine, and fire is the one wardrobe he cannot close: the fear save, the frightened rounds, the locked density. Classify him correctly: he is not strong in one body. He is unkillable in three.

Pinnacle expression

Maximum, Domain, or equivalent.

Maximum, Psychic Communion (10 CE; action): all chosen creatures in 60 ft Wis save. Failures are linked for one round: you know their intended actions, they cannot hide from you, and take 10d10 psychic if they directly attack a protected ally (when you activate this Maximum, designate up to PB allies you can see; they are protected for the duration); success halves damage and prevents link.

Feats

Build-defining options.

Detective Mind

Reading is half the craft: while holding surface thoughts he has advantage on Insight, and the reading arrives with understanding attached. The detective's density. Reading a mind is half of detection; understanding the reading is the other half. While you hold a creature's surface thoughts through Mind Link, you have advantage on Wisdom (Insight) checks against it, and you immediately sense when its words contradict the thoughts you are reading. Once per short rest, you may spend 1 CE as a bonus action to cross-reference: ask one yes-or-no question about the creature's current intentions, answered truthfully from what it is thinking right now.

Ghost

While Phased, one hand may solidify as a bonus action, a single strike, a turned key, though the resistance lapses until his next turn. The elsewhere keeps one foot in the world. while Phased, you may partially solidify one hand as a bonus action to make a single unarmed strike or manipulate an object without ending Phase; attacks against you lose the B/P/S resistance until the start of your next turn.

Many Faces

Ten minutes of observation, or a read of surface thoughts, and the Shapeshift learns voice, scent, biometrics. The skin becomes the person. Your shapeshifting is only skin-deep until you learn someone properly. If you observe a creature for at least 10 minutes, or read its surface thoughts, your Shapeshift can also mimic its voice, scent, and biometric details: retinal pattern, fingerprints, heartbeat cadence. Mundane scrutiny, guard dogs, and voice-print locks cannot tell you apart. Magical or supernatural detection still contests your technique DC, and you must have seen and heard the target to attempt this.

Fear of Flame

When he passes a fire or fear save, a reaction scatters his density: Light for movement until his next turn ends, gaps, speed, and gone. Even the fear is filed as an exit. when you succeed on a save against fire exposure or fear, you may use your reaction to scatter your density: until the end of your next turn you count as Light density for movement (squeeze through tiny gaps, +10 speed) without spending the bonus action to switch.

Rules integration

Edge cases and shared systems.

Codex rulings: Direct rolled technique/weapon attacks can use the campaign's Black Flash procedure when otherwise eligible. Passive damage, hazards, summons acting independently, and initiative-20 Domain events cannot. Generic Maximum Output applies only to ordinary features, never named Maxima or Domains.
