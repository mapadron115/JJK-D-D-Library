Cursed Tool

Honoyagi

A Grade 1 cursed katana, a blade that banks cursed energy for the disciplined arts.

FormWeapon (Katana)
GradeGrade 1
AttunementNot required

Field notes. Subject: a granary in steel. Three truths govern the katana. First, it banks energy: up to ten coins of your own cursed energy may be channeled into the blade with a bonus action, a reserve held against need. Second, the reserve is dedicated: stored energy may be spent only on Simple Domain or Maculation, the blade was made for the disciplined arts, and it refuses all other masters. Third, saved energy is not hoarded energy: what sleeps in the blade expires on a long rest, unrefunded. Mark the maxim: the wise sorcerer enters the night’s work with a full purse. The fool enters with empty hands and good intentions.

Features & procedures

Properties & techniques

Exact player-facing procedures

Cursed edge

The granary’s guard: +1 to attack and damage rolls. The blade fights adequately; its true work is elsewhere. You gain a +1 bonus to attack and damage rolls with this weapon.

Bonus action

Energy vessel

A purse in steel: the blade can hold up to ten of your cursed energy, banked with a bonus action while held. But the purse has one purpose, stored energy may be spent only on Simple Domain or Maculation, the disciplined arts. What is not spent by the long rest expires, unrefunded. This cursed tool can hold up to 10 cursed energy. While holding Honoyagi, you can use a bonus action to channel your own cursed energy into the blade, storing it there. Stored energy can only be spent on Simple Domain or Maculation, nothing else.

Rules integration

Edge cases and shared systems.

Campaign canon notes

Storage limits: the source does not say how energy enters the blade; charging uses a bonus action while held. Stored energy expires on a long rest and is not refunded. The blade stores energy only for itself, it cannot lend its reserve to another creature or tool.
