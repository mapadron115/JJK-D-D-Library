Feature map

Level-by-level features, as the figure refined them.

L1

Malicious Blessing

Bonus action · 1 CE · touch (via the Gentleman's Gloves).

Touch one unattended, handheld, throwable mundane object. For 1 minute it becomes a Blessed Weapon: it counts as a cursed tool, its base damage is a minimum of 1 Murder Die, you are proficient with it, and it uses your Dexterity or CE ability. Property slots: 1 (2 at L6, 3 at L11, 4 at L18). Murder Die: d6 (d8 at L6, d10 at L11, d12 at L18). Anti-stacking law: only one damage rider applies per hit, no matter how many properties the weapon carries. Choose 1 Murder Property when you bless the object: Razor (the surgical cut — the object's strikes open deep, clean cuts; +1 Murder Die on a hit (invented)); Needle (the precise puncture — the object's strikes find vitals; the weapon's critical range becomes 19–20 (invented)); Anchor (the weight that holds you down — the object's strikes drag and pin; a creature struck must make a Strength save vs your technique DC or have its speed halved until the end of your next turn (invented)); Thread (the ligature — the object's strikes bind and strangle; a creature struck is grappled, escape DC equals your technique DC (invented)); Return (the throw that comes back — a thrown blessed object returns to your hand); False Innocence (the weapon appears mundane to passive CE perception; active inspection contests your technique DC).

L3

Prepared Murder

Action · 1 CE per trap.

Bless objects and leave them. You may prepare up to PB trigger traps (2×PB at L18); each trap needs its legal target and its cursed energy, and each one is honest about what sets it off. Each trap triggers when a creature enters a 10-foot radius of it (invented). A trap is spotted with a Perception check against your technique DC and disabled with a successful thieves' tools check against your technique DC (invented). A triggered trap makes one blessed-weapon attack roll against the triggering creature, carrying the trap's assigned Murder Property (invented). Run the room as a dungeon the enemy walks into — the blessing was there before they arrived.

L3

London Bridge

Free (no action).

You get a 5-foot free move that costs no action, and once per turn you may reroll a ricochet — each free once per turn. Ricochet: when a blessed thrown weapon misses, it may bounce to a second target within 15 feet of the first; roll the attack again against the second target (invented). You slide through the fight the way you slide away from a scene.

L6

Grand Blessing

Action · 2 CE (Large) / 4 CE (Huge) / 6 CE (Structure).

Bless the environment itself: Large features for 2 CE, Huge for 4 CE, a whole Structure for 6 CE. The staircase is blessed. The chandelier is blessed. The bridge is blessed. Baseline damage: 2 dice (Large), 4 dice (Huge), or 6 dice (Structure) + your CE modifier. Your property slots become 2, your Murder Die becomes d8, and you learn two new Murder Properties: Execution (the finishing cut — the object's strikes punish the already-wounded) and Guard (reaction: reduce incoming damage by 2 Murder Dice + your modifier).

L11

Wider Repertoire

Passive.

Your property slots become 3, your Murder Die becomes d10, and you learn three new Murder Properties: Guillotine (the falling blade — the object's strikes fall heaviest on the helpless); Bloodhound (the blessed object unerringly seeks a marked target); Betrayal (bless a hostile's worn or carried object after a melee CE hit, then use a bonus action to activate it against its owner — hostile offense, not body horror: the blessing lands on the object, and the object is worn).

L18

The Blessing Lingers

Passive.

Your property slots become 4 and your Murder Die becomes d12. Handheld Blessings now last 1 hour, and once per turn you may bless an object with no action.

Maximum Technique

Dear God

Action · 10 CE.

For 1 minute, blood-coated objects and your unarmored strikes carry every known Murder Property. Blood-coated means an object that has dealt damage this encounter (invented). Final Execution: as an action during the Maximum (invented), make one blessed-weapon attack; on a hit it deals 8 Murder Dice + your CE modifier damage, ignoring resistance (invented). Usable once per Maximum (invented). When you stop experimenting and commit, the gloves come off.

Domain Expansion

London Bridge Is Falling Down

Full-turn action · 20 CE · 800 clash points · burnout 5 rounds · duration 2 minutes · 60-ft enclosed.

At initiative 20, the sure-hit auto-assigns one temporary Murder Property plus one autonomous hostile action to your enemies (save halves). The autonomous hostile action is one blessed-weapon attack — +PB + DEX to hit, 1 Murder Die + DEX damage — made against the nearest creature (invented). The save is a Wisdom save vs your technique DC (invented): on a success, the enemy takes half the autonomous action's damage, and the assigned Murder Property lasts 1 round instead of the Domain's duration (invented). Inside the Domain, the nursery rhyme is not a song. It is a procedure.

Level 20 · Capstone

The World Will Not Name Him

Once per long rest · reaction.

When you would be detected, identified, or remembered — scrying, truesight, testimony, any record — the attempt fails. Heaven declines to fix you, as your vow provides. As part of the same reaction, you may teleport up to 60 feet to an unoccupied space you can see. He will never be caught. He will never be known, either.
