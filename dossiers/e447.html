Features & procedures

Properties & techniques

Exact player-facing procedures

Spirit Sealing

As an action, you can force a cursed spirit within 5 feet to make a DC 15 Charisma saving throw. On a failure, they are sealed within the jar.

Curse Resistance

A cursed spirit with a CR lower than 1 automatically fails the save. A cursed spirit with a CR higher than 2 automatically succeeds. Spirits of CR 1–2 roll the save normally.

Capacity & Fragility

The jar can hold a number of cursed spirits with a combined CR equal to 5. If the jar is destroyed, all contained cursed spirits are released.

Rules integration

Edge cases and shared systems.

Campaign canon notes

Note: Sealing only targets cursed spirits — shikigami, sorcerers, and other creatures are unaffected. Releasing on destruction is immediate and applies to every contained spirit.
