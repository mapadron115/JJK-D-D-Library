Dead Man's Switch

Attunement binds the tool to one bearer. It stores up to PB Failsafe charges, gaining 1 when the bearer falls below half HP or fails a death save, once/round.

FormIron prayer beads / harness Beads worn through in a hundred last stands, until the tool itself believed survival is a duty.
RoleLast-stand defense Strongest while losing; nearly silent while winning — a guard that prices itself by desperation.
EngineFailsafe Stored last chances, earned at the edge of death; spent by refusing to die.
Attunement1 hour* An hour's ritual binding the beads to one bearer — they will serve no other.
Features & procedures

Properties & techniques

Exact player-facing procedures

Emergency Motion

The smallest refusal. Incapacitated but conscious, spend one charge and the beads permit what the body cannot: ten feet of crawl, one object drawn or stowed. Last chances are spent in small motions, not grand gestures. while incapacitated but conscious, spend 1 charge to crawl 10 ft or draw/stow one object at end of turn.

Refuse Flatline

The central vow. Brought to zero, spend three charges and remain standing at one hit point — once a day. Mark this: it is not healing but refusal, the beads declining the paperwork of your death. when reduced to 0 HP, spend 3 charges to remain at 1 HP. Once/long rest.

Posthumous Guard

The tool chooses survival on its own: unconscious at initiative twenty, it spends a charge unprompted and armors you in resistance. Note the doctrine — it defends the body, never swings the arm. if unconscious at initiative 20, spend 1 charge automatically to gain resistance to damage until next initiative 20; the tool chooses survival, not offense.

Final Instruction

The last will, read aloud. As you fall — no action required — spend everything and raise a fifteen-foot ward for one round: half cover and steadied death saves for your allies. The beads were forged for last stands; this is what they were saving for. 4 CE and all charges when you hit 0: it requires no action and resolves immediately when you hit 0 HP (invented); create 15-ft ward for 1 round; allies inside gain half cover and advantage on death saves.

Field notes. Subject: a duty, not a gift. Three truths govern it. First, survival here is procedural: the charges are not stored energy but stored last chances, earned at the edge of death and spent by refusal. Second, the doctrine is defensive to the last: even acting on its own, the tool buys resistance, never offense — no one ever forged it for anything else. Third, it is deliberately strongest while losing: the healthy wielder carries an ornament; the dying one carries a guard. Consider the final instruction, spent all at once at zero — a ward for allies, cover and steadied dying — and classify this correctly: the tool's last thought is not for its bearer, but for the line beside them.

Rules integration

Cursed-tool attunement

Campaign canon

Cursed-tool attunement: you have PB attunement slots (2–6). This tool occupies one slot and requires a one-hour ritual. Attunement ends voluntarily over a short rest, on death, or when you replace it while over capacity. Distance does not break attunement. Cursed-tool attunement is separate from magic-item attunement.
Balance & counterplay. It is deliberately strongest while losing and gives almost no offensive benefit to a healthy wielder.
