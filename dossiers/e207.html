Special Grade cursed tool

Dead Man's Switch

Attunement binds the tool to one bearer. It stores up to PB Failsafe charges, gaining 1 when the bearer falls below half HP or fails a death save, once/round.

FormIron prayer beads / harness
RoleLast-stand defense
EngineFailsafe
Attunement1 hour*
Properties & techniquesExact player-facing procedures

Emergency Motion

while incapacitated but conscious, spend 1 charge to crawl 10 ft or draw/stow one object at end of turn.

Refuse Flatline

when reduced to 0 HP, spend 3 charges to remain at 1 HP. Once/long rest.

Posthumous Guard

if unconscious at initiative 20, spend 1 charge automatically to gain resistance to damage until next initiative 20; the tool chooses survival, not offense.

Final Instruction

4 CE and all charges when you hit 0: create 15-ft ward for 1 round; allies inside gain half cover and advantage on death saves.

Balance & counterplay. It is deliberately strongest while losing and gives almost no offensive benefit to a healthy wielder.
