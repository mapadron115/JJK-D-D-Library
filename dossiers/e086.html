King’s Redoubt

Raise a 150-ft-radius mobile fortress aura for 1 minute. Allies inside gain three-quarters cover, 8 Djinn Dice temporary HP on creation, and reduce each instance of damage by 2 Djinn Dice. The barrier can absorb one battlefield-scale blast; when it does, it collapses after resolving that damage.

Anime-scale: Strategic scale: shelter a regiment or city square from siege-class magic rather than dealing damage.

The fiction. Jujutsu scholars classify the Bastion Judge as protection that answers like a verdict — CE shaped by a wielder whose every instinct was to stand between harm and others, so the energy learned to render that instinct as law: plates of force that arrive before the blow does. The Citadel Step is the technique's signature mercy, the prevailing theory holds — a defense that refused to stay where its wielder was, and learned to cross distance to find the ally worth judging worthy of saving.

Rules integration

Djinn Die, Equip & Extreme Magic

Independent Djinn Reserve: 12 (Rank C). Refreshes on a long rest. Every Djinn cost below — Weapon Equip, Full Equip, abilities, reactions, Extreme Magic — spends this Djinn’s Reserve, never your CE. If this Djinn’s Reserve is empty it simply cannot fuel more until you rest; your other Djinn keep their own separate Reserves.
Weapon Equip · level 5: bonus action, 2 Reserve, lasts 10 minutes.
Full Djinn Equip · level 9: bonus action, 4 Reserve, lasts 1 minute. Only one Equip can be active.
Deep Synchronization · level 11: once per short rest, one Equip activation costs 1 Reserve less.
Extreme Magic · level 13: action; requires that Djinn’s Full Equip; pay the listed Reserve cost from this Djinn’s Reserve; usable once per long rest per Djinn. Full Equip ends afterward, and you cannot Equip that Djinn again until you finish a short rest.
King Vessel · level 17: switch directly from one Full Equip to another as a bonus action, paying the new Equip cost. At level 20, once per long rest, immediately transition from one Djinn’s Extreme into another Djinn’s Full Equip.
Attack / save: use your CE attack modifier and CE save DC unless this Djinn explicitly says otherwise.
