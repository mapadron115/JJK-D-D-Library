Regalia

Your very soul has become a cursed tool that has been sealed within. You have the ability to manifest it using cursed energy.

Power tierTier III · Apex
Combat roleStriker
Core engineSoul weapon + damage-redirection pool
DomainCursed Soul Armory (18)
Full progression

Feature map

1Regalia.

Starting at 1st level, you can manifest your Regalia as an action for 2 CE for a number of hours equal to your Cursed Energy modifier. Your Regalia takes the form of a simple or martial melee weapon of your choice, such as Dagger or a Shortsword. You gain a +1 bonus to attack and damage rolls using your Regalia. Once per turn on a hit you can spend 1 CE to deal an additional 1d8 necrotic damage. This becomes 2d8 at 3rd level, 3d8 at 6th level, 4d8 at 11th level and 6d8 at 18th level. Your Regalia disappears if it is more than 30 feet away from you and cannot be wielded by other creatures.

3Cursed Wave.

At 3rd level, you have learned greater control over your Regalia. For 1 CE, you can make one of your attacks with your Regalia into a cutting wave of cursed energy. This creates a 5 ft wide 30 ft long line. Creatures in range must make a Dexterity save, taking the damage of your regalia in necrotic damage on a failed save.

6Divine Protection.

At 6th level, the seal further loosens, granting you more abilities. Your Regalia now has a +2 bonus to attack and damage rolls. This increases to a +3 bonus at 11th level and +4 at 18th level. Your Regalia now has a pool of hit points equal to 5 times your level. When you take damage, you can spend 1 cursed energy as a reaction to damage to cause that damage to instead target the regalia's pool. If this hit point pool is reduced to 0 hit points, you take any remaining damage and your regalia breaks and must be resummoned. When broken, your regalia can be resummoned a number of times equal to your Cursed Energy modifier. Otherwise, you cannot summon your regalia until you complete a long rest.

11Soul Bound Treasure.

At 11th level, your control over your Soul Regalia has loosened nearly all of the seal. Your regalia is now a special grade cursed tool. Work with your GM to decide what its technique is. As an action, you can spend 10 cursed energy to cause your regalia to hover for 1 minute. Your regalia has an AC equal to 12 + your cursed energy modifier + your proficiency bonus and hit points equal to its pool while hovering. The regalia is destroyed if reduced to 0 hit points. You gain the following actions: ● As an action you can command the weapon to move up to 100 feet and can make any attacks on your turn originate from the Regalia as if you were wielding it. You can use your cursed energy modifier for attack and damage rolls for these attacks. ● As an action, you can cause your regalia to move in a 100 foot line attack, appearing at the end of the line if it is a melee weapon. All creatures within range must make a Dexterity saving throw, taking your regalia damage on a hit. After the minute is up, the Soul Regalia moves 100 ft towards you, attempting to return. If unable to, it falls to the ground.

18Domain Expansion: Cursed Soul Armory.

At 18th level, the seal is completely removed as you have perfected a domain expansion that you put your soul into. The domain is filled with numerous types of Lesser Regalia. At initiative 20, the Lesser Regalia will attack any enemies within the domain, dealing 8d10 necrotic damage. Additionally, you can use Soul Bound Treasure as an action without spending any cursed energy. In your domain it has immunity to all damage and you can use one of its actions once per turn as a free action.

Innate Technique Feats

Build-defining options.

These are optional character-building feats. You must meet each listed prerequisite before selecting it.

Prerequisite: Must have Regalia

Regalia Versatility

You have learned to alter your regalia and can change it to a different type of weapon as a bonus action, including ranged weapons and shields. While your Regalia is a ranged weapon, it creates its own ammunition. While it is a shield, you gain a bonus to AC while wielding equal to the bonus to attack and damage rolls your regalia has. Your GM may also choose to allow more unique weapons.

Prerequisite: Must have Cursed Wave

Celestial Raiment

While you have your Regalia manifested, you can spend 3 CE as a bonus action to summon a hagoromo scarf around your body that lasts until your Regalia disappears. As a reaction, you can add your proficiency bonus to your AC until the beginning of your next turn, or to a saving throw you make as the silken scarf protects you from harm.

Prerequisite: Must have Soul Bound Treasure

Technique Release

You have learned a method that allows you to use your maximum output. On a hit, you can spend 2 CE to deal critical damage and the target must make a Strength saving throw or be knocked prone. After the attack, your regalia breaks and must be resummoned. You can also use this on a Cursed Wave attack for 4 CE.

Rules integration

Edge cases and shared systems.

Rules priority: the reviewed rulings, explicit codex clarifications, and the level progression always control when original wording conflicts. Legacy Domain auto-hit language, action terminology, event timing, missing saves, and older scaling never override revised player rules.
Missing: Cursed Wave success effect (audit HOLD). Cursed Wave states only the failed-save result; the source gives no success effect (half damage? no damage?). Do not invent one; a ruling is needed before play. The same gap recurs in Soul Bound Treasure's 100-ft line attack ("taking your regalia damage on a hit" with no stated success effect). If the Cursed Wave success-effect HOLD is ruled as half damage, carry the ruling across to Technique Release's Cursed Wave use.
Missing: Lesser Regalia stat block (audit HOLD). "Lesser Regalia" has no stat block anywhere in the supplied text (no attack bonus, AC, or HP); the Domain's 8d10 necrotic is playable as the sure-hit effect, but whether an attack roll was intended is undefined.
Domain regalia immunity: the regalia's "immunity to all damage" inside the Domain is explicit source text — kept as printed, flagged as unusually strong.
Special-grade technique: the regalia's special-grade technique ("work with your GM to decide what its technique is") is a GM-design step at adoption, not a values gap.
Domain conversion: initiative-20 timing already matches; clashes use the deterministic 1–1,000 point system; barrier per D007; burnout per D010. The initiative-20 guaranteed effect is the 8d10 necrotic damage to enemies; the costless Soul Bound Treasure activation and the once-per-turn free-action regalia action are kept as printed.
