Regalia

Your very soul has become a cursed tool that has been sealed within. You have the ability to manifest it using cursed energy.

Power tierTier III · Apex
Combat roleStriker
Core engineSoul weapon + damage-redirection pool
DomainCursed Soul Armory (18)

Field notes. Subject: the soul that agreed to be a sword. Three truths govern this technique. First, the sealing is absolute: the soul is remade as a weapon, no other hand may wield it, and past thirty feet it ceases, for a soul does not travel far from its body. Second, the weapon-body would rather be wounded than let the wielder fall: at 6th level it gains a pool of hit points, and one energy as a reaction redirects any incoming damage into the steel, but when the pool empties the regalia breaks, and resummoning is limited before the long rest. Third, the seal loosens with mastery: the bonus climbs to +4, the regalia becomes a special-grade tool, and at 11th level it hovers and fights a hundred feet away. Mark this: the Cursed Soul Armory is the technique's confession, a Domain of lesser regalia, where the soul keeps the only thing it has ever truly trusted. Itself.

Full progression

Feature map

1Regalia.

Action · 2 CE · Manifest a soul-weapon (any simple/martial melee weapon) for CE-modifier hours · +1 attack/damage · Once per turn on a hit: spend 1 CE for +1d8 necrotic (2d8/3d8/4d8/6d8 at 3rd/6th/11th/18th) · Vanishes beyond 30 ft; only you can wield it.

The manifestation: two energy as an action, and the soul takes the shape of a blade, modest at first, loyal absolutely, unusable by any other hand. Starting at 1st level, you can manifest your Regalia as an action for 2 CE for a number of hours equal to your Cursed Energy modifier. Your Regalia takes the form of a simple or martial melee weapon of your choice, such as Dagger or a Shortsword. You gain a +1 bonus to attack and damage rolls using your Regalia. Once per turn on a hit you can spend 1 CE to deal an additional 1d8 necrotic damage. This becomes 2d8 at 3rd level, 3d8 at 6th level, 4d8 at 11th level and 6d8 at 18th level. Your Regalia disappears if it is more than 30 feet away from you and cannot be wielded by other creatures.

3Cursed Wave.

No action · 1 CE · Turn one Regalia attack into a 5-ft wide, 30-ft long cutting wave: creatures in the line Dex save, take your regalia damage as necrotic, half on success.

The blade's first reach beyond itself: one energy to turn a strike into a thirty-foot wave of necrotic cutting. At 3rd level, you have learned greater control over your Regalia. For 1 CE, you can make one of your attacks with your Regalia into a cutting wave of cursed energy. This creates a 5 ft wide 30 ft long line. Creatures in range must make a Dexterity save, taking the damage of your regalia in necrotic damage on a failed save, or half as much on a successful save.

6Divine Protection.

Upgrade · Regalia attack/damage bonus: +2 (+3 at 11th, +4 at 18th) · Regalia gains HP = 5× your level · Reaction (when you take damage) · 1 CE: redirect the damage to the regalia's pool · At 0 pool: you take the remainder, regalia breaks (resummon CE-mod times, then only after a long rest).

The seal loosens: the bonus sharpens, and the regalia gains its pool of hit points, the soul learning to take the wound. At 6th level, the seal further loosens, granting you more abilities. Your Regalia now has a +2 bonus to attack and damage rolls. This increases to a +3 bonus at 11th level and +4 at 18th level. Your Regalia now has a pool of hit points equal to 5 times your level. When you take damage, you can spend 1 cursed energy as a reaction to damage to cause that damage to instead target the regalia's pool. If this hit point pool is reduced to 0 hit points, you take any remaining damage and your regalia breaks and must be resummoned. When broken, your regalia can be resummoned a number of times equal to your Cursed Energy modifier. Otherwise, you cannot summon your regalia until you complete a long rest.

11Soul Bound Treasure.

Passive · Regalia is now a special-grade cursed tool (work with your GM for its technique) · Action · 10 CE · Hover for 1 min (AC = 12 + CE mod + PB, HP = its pool; destroyed at 0) · Action: move it up to 100 ft and originate your attacks from it (CE mod for attack/damage) · Action: 100-ft line attack — Dex save or regalia damage, half on success; appears at the end of the line · After 1 min: flies 100 ft back toward you or falls.

The treasure unbound: a special-grade tool with its own technique, and for ten energy it hovers, fighting a hundred feet from the hand. At 11th level, your control over your Soul Regalia has loosened nearly all of the seal. Your regalia is now a special grade cursed tool. Work with your GM to decide what its technique is. As an action, you can spend 10 cursed energy to cause your regalia to hover for 1 minute. Your regalia has an AC equal to 12 + your cursed energy modifier + your proficiency bonus and hit points equal to its pool while hovering. The regalia is destroyed if reduced to 0 hit points. You gain the following actions: ● As an action you can command the weapon to move up to 100 feet and can make any attacks on your turn originate from the Regalia as if you were wielding it. You can use your cursed energy modifier for attack and damage rolls for these attacks. ● As an action, you can cause your regalia to move in a 100 foot line attack, appearing at the end of the line if it is a melee weapon. All creatures within the 100-ft line must make a Dexterity saving throw, taking your regalia damage on a hit, or half as much on a successful save. After the minute is up, the Soul Regalia moves 100 ft towards you, attempting to return. If unable to, it falls to the ground.

18Domain Expansion: Cursed Soul Armory.

Domain (18th level) · Filled with Lesser Regalia: initiative 20, enemies suffer a sure-hit attack for 8d10 necrotic · Soul Bound Treasure usable as an action for no CE; immune to all damage inside; one of its actions usable once per turn as a free action.

The dark fills with steel. Blades, spears, and stranger instruments hang in the air in ordered ranks, ranked like soldiers or like books on shelves, each one humming faintly with its own stored malice. Braziers of cold blue flame light the racks from below, and the floor is a map of old battlefields etched in brass. The armory does not boast, it inventories.

The armory: lesser regalia filling the Domain, the sure-hit storm at initiative 20, and the treasure wielded without cost. At 18th level, the seal is completely removed as you have perfected a domain expansion that you put your soul into. The domain is filled with numerous types of Lesser Regalia. At initiative 20, the Lesser Regalia will attack any enemies within the domain as an automatic sure-hit, dealing 8d10 necrotic damage with no attack roll; Lesser Regalia are not fielded as separate attackable creatures. Additionally, you can use Soul Bound Treasure as an action without spending any cursed energy. In your domain it has immunity to all damage and you can use one of its actions once per turn as a free action.

Innate Technique Feats

Build-defining options.

These are optional character-building feats. You must meet each listed prerequisite before selecting it.

Prerequisite: Must have Regalia

Regalia Versatility

The soul reshaped at will: a bonus action to change the weapon's form, blade, bow, or shield, as the hour demands. You have learned to alter your regalia and can change it to a different type of weapon as a bonus action, including ranged weapons and shields. While your Regalia is a ranged weapon, it creates its own ammunition. While it is a shield, you gain a bonus to AC while wielding equal to the bonus to attack and damage rolls your regalia has. Your GM may also choose to allow more unique weapons.

Prerequisite: Must have Cursed Wave

Celestial Raiment

The scarf of the heavens: three energy for a silken ward, lending proficiency to armor or to the saving throw. While you have your Regalia manifested, you can spend 3 CE as a bonus action to summon a hagoromo scarf around your body that lasts until your Regalia disappears. As a reaction, you can add your proficiency bonus to your AC until the beginning of your next turn, or to a saving throw you make as the silken scarf protects you from harm.

Prerequisite: Must have Soul Bound Treasure

Technique Release

The maximum output: two energy for the critical strike and the prone, and the regalia breaks in the spending. You have learned a method that allows you to use your maximum output. On a hit, you can spend 2 CE to deal critical damage, the attack is a critical hit: roll all of the attack's damage dice twice and add them together, then add modifiers as normal, and the target must make a Strength saving throw or be knocked prone. After the attack, your regalia breaks and must be resummoned. You can also use this on a Cursed Wave attack for 4 CE.

Rules integration

Edge cases and shared systems.

Rules priority: the reviewed rulings, explicit codex clarifications, and the level progression always control when original wording conflicts. Legacy Domain auto-hit language, action terminology, event timing, missing saves, and older scaling never override revised player rules.
Missing: Cursed Wave success effect (audit HOLD). Cursed Wave states only the failed-save result; the source gives no success effect (half damage? no damage?). Do not invent one; a ruling is needed before play. The same gap recurs in Soul Bound Treasure's 100-ft line attack ("taking your regalia damage on a hit" with no stated success effect). If the Cursed Wave success-effect HOLD is ruled as half damage, carry the ruling across to Technique Release's Cursed Wave use.
Missing: Lesser Regalia stat block (audit HOLD). "Lesser Regalia" has no stat block anywhere in the supplied text (no attack bonus, AC, or HP); the Domain's 8d10 necrotic is playable as the sure-hit effect, but whether an attack roll was intended is undefined.
Domain regalia immunity: the regalia's "immunity to all damage" inside the Domain is explicit source text, kept as printed, flagged as unusually strong.
Special-grade technique: the regalia's special-grade technique ("work with your GM to decide what its technique is") is a GM-design step at adoption, not a values gap.
Domain conversion: initiative-20 timing already matches; clashes use the deterministic 1–1,000 point system; barrier per D007; burnout per D010. The initiative-20 guaranteed effect is the 8d10 necrotic damage to enemies; the costless Soul Bound Treasure activation and the once-per-turn free-action regalia action are kept as printed.
