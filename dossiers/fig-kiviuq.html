Feature map

Level-by-level features, as the figure refined them.

L1

Not Where You Look

Passive.

Tier IV · Mythic striker · Suggested CE ability: DEX · Primary verb: Wander. He is never where you look. The Unwritten Trail treats position as a narrative convenience: Kiviuq is where the story needs him, which is never where anyone was watching.

Kiviuq cannot be tracked by nonmagical means — Survival checks to follow him automatically fail; his trail stops at the sea ice. Divination magic that would reveal his location or observe him remotely fails (the water shows the caster a story about themselves instead). This is not resistance or immunity; the attempt simply looks in the wrong place. (The story answers where he needs to be.)

L1

The Story Provides

Bonus action · 2 CE.

Kiviuq teleports up to 30 ft to an unoccupied space he can see, arriving from an unwatched angle: he has advantage on the next attack roll he makes before the end of his turn. (A tale introduces him mid-scene.)

L3

Wrong Footing

Reaction · 2 CE.

When Kiviuq is targeted by an attack he can see, the attacker has disadvantage on the roll — they strike where the story said he'd be. If the attack misses, he may move up to 10 ft without provoking opportunity attacks. Once per round. (Where he was, one telling ago.)

L3

The Tale Turns

Reaction · 3 CE.

When Kiviuq takes damage, he may halve it — the blow lands on a seal-skin, a snow-shape, a decoy-tale — and teleport up to 15 ft to an unoccupied space he can see. Once per round. (He mourns the decoy, briefly, the way you'd mourn a good lie well told.)

L6

Where the Story Needs Him

Action · 4 CE.

Kiviuq names a place whose story he has heard (described to him, told around a fire, read in a report): he teleports up to 1 mile to an unoccupied space near it, over ice no map admits. In combat, he may instead teleport up to 60 ft and make one weapon attack with advantage as part of the same action. (The trail runs where the telling ripens.)

L11

The Unmappable

Passive.

Opportunity attacks against Kiviuq have disadvantage; he ignores difficult terrain; creatures have disadvantage on Wisdom (Perception) checks to notice him. He is always, slightly, between the lines of the story. (Blades swung at his passing find only weather.)

Pinnacle expression

Maximum, Domain, or equivalent.

Capstone material.

Maximum Technique

Maximum: EVERYWHERE IN THE TELLING

Action · 8 CE · 1 minute (concentration).

Kiviuq flickers between tellings: at the start of each of his turns he may teleport up to 30 ft (no action); attacks against him have disadvantage; once per round, when he hits with a weapon attack, he may deal an extra 3d8 force damage — the blow comes from a direction the target never faced. (Here, there, mid-step between two places at once.)

Domain Expansion

The Trail With No End

Full-turn action · 20 CE · 800 clash points · burnout 5 rounds · duration 2 minutes · 60-ft radius · disclosed.

Ice to the horizon, the kayak's wake closing behind him. Inside this domain, the sure-hit is No Fixed Point — no creature inside can be tracked, scried, or magically located, and hostile creatures inside cannot take reactions (no one ever knows where he is). Additionally, Kiviuq may teleport up to 15 ft as a free action once per round. (Inside the Trail, there are no fixed points. Only the story, still being told.)

Level 20 · Capstone

The Story Goes On

Once per long rest.

When Kiviuq is reduced to 0 hit points, he does not fall — the tale refuses the ending: he drops to 1 hit point instead, teleports up to 60 ft to an unoccupied space he can see, and becomes invisible until the end of his next turn. Each hostile creature that saw him fall must succeed on a Wisdom save against his technique DC or be frightened for 1 minute (frightened of where he went). (Wars end. Stories don't.)
