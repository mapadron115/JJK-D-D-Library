Ugo — Furnace Sage

Rank S
Canon master: Solomon / AladdinBasis: Codex expansion • Ugo is treated as a special Djinn-like contract

Weapon Equip • Level 5

Furnace Counsel. Codex Weapon Equip: the vessel becomes a heat-wreathed gauntlet/staff that can physically interact with barriers and magical constructs.

Rukh Calculus

Reaction • 2 CE

After seeing a barrier, technique, spell, or supernatural structure activate, add one Djinn Die to the first check/save made to understand or resist it; on success learn one mechanical property or weakness.

Furnace Palm

Action • 3 CE

120-ft line, Dexterity save; 5 Djinn Dice fire/force, half on success. Failed creatures are pushed 20 ft.

Dimensional Hand

Action • 5 CE

Manifest a colossal spectral hand at a point within 120 ft. Creatures in a 30-ft cube make Strength saves; 7 Djinn Dice force and Restrained until the end of your next turn on failure.

Full Djinn Equip • Level 9

Fly 60 ft; reach +10 ft; resistance to fire and force. Once per round, after you succeed on a check against a barrier or supernatural effect, one ally who can hear you gains advantage on its next roll against that same effect.

EXTREME MAGIC • LEVEL 13+

Solomon’s Furnace

14 CE • Codex Pinnacle

Choose a supernatural structure, construct, barrier, or 120-ft-radius zone within 300 ft. Make a technique contest against its creator; on success suppress its defensive properties until the end of your next turn and deal 15 Djinn Dice radiant/force to constructs and barriers. Strategic scale: may dismantle a fortress-class ward, ritual engine, or Dungeon mechanism rather than deal creature damage.

Anime-scale: Analysis/dismantling rather than raw annihilation; the pinnacle is meant to make impossible magical architecture answerable.
