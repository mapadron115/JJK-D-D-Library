Anime-scale: Analysis/dismantling rather than raw annihilation; the pinnacle is meant to make impossible magical architecture answerable.

Field notes. Subject: wisdom that burned. The registry entry frames the contract as counsel given form: Ugo, treated as a special Djinn-like contract of Heat and Wisdom once carried by Solomon and Aladdin, does not lend fire so much as the conviction that no wall was built to keep this vessel out. Three truths govern the furnace. First, it analyzes before it burns — Rukh Calculus studies the barrier, then the Furnace Palm answers. Second, it treats every supernatural structure as a problem heat can solve: Dimensional Hand reaches through space itself, and the pinnacle, Solomon’s Furnace, dismantles fortress-class wards, ritual engines, and Dungeon mechanisms rather than merely damaging creatures. Third, it counsels as it conquers — the Full Equip turns one bearer’s successful reading of a barrier into advantage for every ally who hears. Hence the maxim: the sage does not break walls; it convinces them they were never walls.

Rules integration

Djinn Die, Equip & Extreme Magic

Damage-type chooser: when a Djinn ability lists two damage types, the Djinn King chooses which applies when the damage is dealt.
Independent Djinn Reserve: 18 (Rank S). Refreshes on a long rest. Every Djinn cost below — Weapon Equip, Full Equip, abilities, reactions, Extreme Magic — spends this Djinn’s Reserve, never your CE. If this Djinn’s Reserve is empty it simply cannot fuel more until you rest; your other Djinn keep their own separate Reserves.
Weapon Equip · level 5: bonus action, 2 Reserve, lasts 10 minutes.
Full Djinn Equip · level 9: bonus action, 4 Reserve, lasts 1 minute. Only one Equip can be active.
Deep Synchronization · level 11: once per short rest, one Equip activation costs 1 Reserve less.
Extreme Magic · level 13: action; requires that Djinn’s Full Equip; pay the listed Reserve cost from this Djinn’s Reserve; usable once per long rest per Djinn. Full Equip ends afterward, and you cannot Equip that Djinn again until you finish a short rest.
King Vessel · level 17: switch directly from one Full Equip to another as a bonus action, paying the new Equip cost. At level 20, once per long rest, immediately transition from one Djinn’s Extreme into another Djinn’s Full Equip.
Attack / save: use your CE attack modifier and CE save DC unless this Djinn explicitly says otherwise.
