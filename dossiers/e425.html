Cursed Battery

A runestone reservoir for cursed energy, stored overnight and drawn on demand.

FormCursed Item
GradeGrade 3
AttunementRequired
Features & procedures

Properties & techniques

Exact player-facing procedures

Identification

Runic stone

A smooth, oval stone etched with faintly glowing magical symbols. A creature can make a DC 15 Intelligence (History) check to recognize the runes as reminiscent of the Norse shamans.

Property

Store cursed energy

While attuned, at the start of a long rest you can store cursed energy within the battery up to your proficiency bonus + your Cursed Energy modifier. Once stored, the battery is considered full.

Action

Withdraw cursed energy

While holding the battery, you can take an action to touch the battery and immediately regain the stored cursed energy.

Pinnacle expression

Maximum, Domain, or equivalent.

Capstone material

This is a cursed tool, not an innate technique — it has no Maximum or Domain.

Rules integration

Edge cases and shared systems.

Campaign canon notes

Cursed-tool attunement: you have PB attunement slots (2–6). This tool occupies one slot and requires a one-hour ritual. Attunement ends voluntarily over a short rest, on death, or when you replace it while over capacity. Distance does not break attunement. Cursed-tool attunement is separate from magic-item attunement.
One refill per rest cycle: the stored cursed energy cannot be regained until you finish a long rest — once you withdraw it, the battery stays empty until the start of your next long rest.
