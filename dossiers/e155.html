Shamebound Pursuit

Make seeing your face a terrible commitment: Witnesses become valid quarry, distance builds Fury, and every obstacle between you and the quarry becomes part of the attack.

Combat roleSingle-target berserker / pursuit striker

Field notes. Subject: the wound that hunts its witnesses. Three truths govern him. First: sight is a contract — the unveiled face marks Witnesses with no save, and the mark keeps for twenty-four hours, beyond sight or closed eyes. Second: distance is fuel — every turn spent closing the gap becomes Fury, and Fury becomes Pursuit Dice the moment he arrives. Third: nothing interposed survives — obstacles are maximum-dice firewood, imprints extend the gaze to any surface, and the Domain's sure-hit makes every hostile a Witness whether or not they avert their eyes. Assess him correctly: you cannot hide from him by looking away, because the catalog was completed the moment you saw. Maxim: to look is to be hunted; to look away forever is to be ruled.

Full progression

Progression

1Witness / Pursuit

Pursuit Die

The measure of his approach — a die that grows as the hunt lengthens. d6 → d8 → d10 → d12.

Covered Face.

The seal is the strategy: avert your gaze and you are safe from the catalog — but blind to him in every way that matters. You normally keep your face covered. A creature may declare that it averts its gaze from you; while doing so it cannot become a Witness, but has disadvantage on sight-based attacks and Perception checks involving you and cannot use a sight-dependent reaction against you.

Witness.

The cataloging itself: no save, no defense — to see the face is to be entered, and the entry keeps for twenty-four hours. As a bonus action you may reveal your face. Any creature within 60 feet with direct sight that is not averting its gaze becomes a Witness for 24 hours. There is no save; seeing the face is the trigger. A Witness remains marked even if it later closes its eyes or leaves your sight.

Pursuit.

One Witness chosen as Quarry: direction and distance known across the plane, speed multiplied, terrain and obstacles dismissed as paperwork. Bonus action, 1 CE: choose one Witness as your Quarry. Until Pursuit ends, you know the Quarry’s direction and approximate distance while on the same plane, gain +10 feet × PB speed, ignore nonmagical difficult terrain while moving toward it, and have advantage on Strength checks to break obstacles between you and it.

Fury.

Distance is the fuel: every turn spent closing the gap becomes Fury, and Fury becomes Pursuit Dice on the arriving blow — but to retreat willingly is to burn the ledger. At the end of your turn, if the Quarry remains more than 10 feet away and you spent at least half your movement moving toward it, gain 1 Fury, maximum PB. If you willingly move away from the Quarry without being forced, lose all Fury. On a melee hit against the Quarry, spend any amount of Fury to add that many Pursuit Dice to the damage.

3Nothing Stops the Pursuit

The obstacles between him and the Quarry are no longer obstacles: maximum weapon dice, doubled damage against them — and the climb speed to go over what cannot be broken. Your attacks against unattended objects and structures between you and the Quarry deal maximum weapon dice and double damage. During Pursuit you gain a climb speed equal to your speed.

6Four Pixels

The gaze extends to any surface he touches: an imprinted face, maintained a day, and the first clear looker is cataloged. Action, 2 CE: imprint a cursed image of your face onto one Tiny or larger surface or object you touch. The first creature that clearly sees it and is not averting its gaze becomes a Witness. Maintain up to PB imprints for 24 hours.

11No Known Material

He cannot be turned aside: advantage against shove, grapple, restraint, and prone while pursuing — and Fury itself may be spent to blunt the damage. You have advantage on saves against being shoved, grappled, restrained, or knocked prone while moving toward the Quarry. As a reaction when you take damage during Pursuit, spend up to PB Fury; reduce the damage by that many Pursuit Dice.

18Face in Every Frame

The speed doubles, and he gains the Domain below — the face, at last, is everywhere. Pursuit speed bonus doubles, and you gain the Domain below.

Pinnacle expression

Domain Expansion — Face in Every Frame

ExpansionThe frame, perfected: for one minute, every surface carries a fragment of the face — at initiative count twenty, every hostile is a Witness whether or not they avert their eyes, and the Quarry may be chosen without action. Full-turn action, 20 CE, enclosed 60-foot-radius Domain for 1 minute. Every reflective surface, sign, screen, and wall carries an impossible fragment of your face.

Compulsory Recognition — sole sure-hit At initiative count 20, every chosen hostile creature becomes a Witness whether or not it is averting its gaze. A protected creature makes a Wisdom save and avoids becoming a Witness on success.

Unbroken Hunt At initiative count 20 you may also choose one Witness as Quarry without an action and set your Fury to at least half PB, rounded up. If that Quarry drops to 0 HP, you may immediately choose another Witness without ending Pursuit.

No automatic execution The Domain guarantees recognition and pursuit state. Your attacks still roll normally; the source’s inevitability is represented by tracking, speed, Fury, and obstacle destruction.

Clash bookkeeping 800 clash points (Tier IV). In a Domain clash, a 100+ point advantage collapses the weaker Domain; collapse deals no damage. Barrier: AC 12 + PB; HP = 10 × max(1, your CE modifier) + 1,600. Burnout: 5 rounds.

Innate Technique Feats

Technique Feats

Shamebound Pursuit

Grief-Fueled Momentum

The wound deepens the hunt: when the Quarry's blow lands, it buys him one more unit of Fury. Once per round when your Quarry damages you, gain 1 Fury after that damage resolves.

Four Pixels

Unseen Photograph

The imprint may shrink to four pixels: perception, not possession, is the trigger — merely owning the image is not enough. Your cursed face imprints may be as small as a four-pixel detail. A creature must actually perceive the detail to become a Witness; merely possessing the image is not enough.

Shamebound Pursuit, level 6

No Rest Until Silence

The Fury outlives the Quarry: half of it retained into the next turn, transferable to the next Witness chosen. When your Quarry drops to 0 HP, retain up to half your current Fury until the end of your next turn. If you choose another Witness as Quarry before then, transfer that Fury.

Shamebound Pursuit, level 11

Inhuman Acceleration

Once per short rest, the sixty-foot gap is closed in a single turn — across water, up walls, without an action spent. Once per short rest, when you start your turn at least 60 feet from the Quarry, you may Dash without an action and your movement that turn may cross liquid surfaces and vertical walls.

Rules integration

Table Rulings

Witness is informationally persistent but not a death sentence. The user still spends actions, movement, CE, and attack rolls to reach the target.
Averting gaze is real counterplay. A creature that averts cannot use effects requiring a clear look at the user.
Artistic drawings do not trigger Witness unless produced by Four Pixels; ordinary photos, recordings, mirrors, and live video do if the face is actually visible.
Pursuit bonuses apply only against the chosen Quarry. This prevents the technique from becoming generic permanent super-speed.
