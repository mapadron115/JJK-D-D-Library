Shamebound Pursuit

Make seeing your face a terrible commitment: Witnesses become valid quarry, distance builds Fury, and every obstacle between you and the quarry becomes part of the attack.

Combat roleSingle-target berserker / pursuit striker

The fiction. The prevailing theory holds that Shamebound Pursuit is inverted reinforcement — a trauma so deep the sorcerer armored themselves in being unseen, and their CE took the job personally. Registry notes describe the face as a seal: every gaze that breaks it is 'remembered' by the energy, and the Witness is not cursed but catalogued — distance, obstacles, and time are merely the filing system of a Fury that was never allowed to be looked at. It does not chase out of hatred. It chases because being seen is the one wound it cannot close.

Covered Face.

You normally keep your face covered. A creature may declare that it averts its gaze from you; while doing so it cannot become a Witness, but has disadvantage on sight-based attacks and Perception checks involving you and cannot use a sight-dependent reaction against you.

Witness.

As a bonus action you may reveal your face. Any creature within 60 feet with direct sight that is not averting its gaze becomes a Witness for 24 hours. There is no save; seeing the face is the trigger. A Witness remains marked even if it later closes its eyes or leaves your sight.

Pursuit.

Bonus action, 1 CE: choose one Witness as your Quarry. Until Pursuit ends, you know the Quarry’s direction and approximate distance while on the same plane, gain +10 feet × PB speed, ignore nonmagical difficult terrain while moving toward it, and have advantage on Strength checks to break obstacles between you and it.

Fury.

At the end of your turn, if the Quarry remains more than 10 feet away and you spent at least half your movement moving toward it, gain 1 Fury, maximum PB. If you willingly move away from the Quarry without being forced, lose all Fury. On a melee hit against the Quarry, spend any amount of Fury to add that many Pursuit Dice to the damage.

L3Nothing Stops the Pursuit

Your attacks against unattended objects and structures between you and the Quarry deal maximum weapon dice and double damage. During Pursuit you gain a climb speed equal to your speed.

L6Four Pixels

Action, 2 CE: imprint a cursed image of your face onto one Tiny or larger surface or object you touch. The first creature that clearly sees it and is not averting its gaze becomes a Witness. Maintain up to PB imprints for 24 hours.

L11No Known Material

You have advantage on saves against being shoved, grappled, restrained, or knocked prone while moving toward the Quarry. As a reaction when you take damage during Pursuit, spend up to PB Fury; reduce the damage by that many Pursuit Dice.

L18Face in Every Frame

Pursuit speed bonus doubles, and you gain the Domain below.

Pinnacle expression

Domain Expansion — Face in Every Frame

Expansion Full-turn action, 20 CE, enclosed 60-foot-radius Domain for 1 minute. Every reflective surface, sign, screen, and wall carries an impossible fragment of your face.

Compulsory Recognition — sole sure-hit At initiative count 20, every chosen hostile creature becomes a Witness whether or not it is averting its gaze. A protected creature makes a Wisdom save and avoids becoming a Witness on success.

Unbroken Hunt At initiative count 20 you may also choose one Witness as Quarry without an action and set your Fury to at least half PB, rounded up. If that Quarry drops to 0 HP, you may immediately choose another Witness without ending Pursuit.

No automatic execution The Domain guarantees recognition and pursuit state. Your attacks still roll normally; the source’s inevitability is represented by tracking, speed, Fury, and obstacle destruction.

Clash bookkeeping 800 clash points (Tier IV). In a Domain clash, a 100+ point advantage collapses the weaker Domain; collapse deals no damage. Barrier: AC 12 + PB; HP = 10 × max(1, your CE modifier) + 1,600. Burnout: 5 rounds.

Rules integration

Table Rulings

Witness is informationally persistent but not a death sentence. The user still spends actions, movement, CE, and attack rolls to reach the target.
Averting gaze is real counterplay. A creature that averts cannot use effects requiring a clear look at the user.
Artistic drawings do not trigger Witness unless produced by Four Pixels; ordinary photos, recordings, mirrors, and live video do if the face is actually visible.
Pursuit bonuses apply only against the chosen Quarry. This prevents the technique from becoming generic permanent super-speed.
