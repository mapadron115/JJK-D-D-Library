Shamebound Pursuit

Make seeing your face a terrible commitment: Witnesses become valid quarry, distance builds Fury, and every obstacle between you and the quarry becomes part of the attack.

Combat roleSingle-target berserker / pursuit striker

Research log. Subject: the pursuit that files you first. The subject arrived with its face covered and has never once been seen to hurry — it does not need to. Two truths govern this file. First: the face is a catalog, not a weapon — one clear look writes the observer into its records for twenty-four hours, no defense permitted; the only protection is to look away, which is also the only way to go blind. Second: the hunt is an economy — every turn spent closing the gap becomes Fury, and Fury becomes the arriving blow; to retreat is to burn the ledger, so nothing in this file ever retreats. The subject has never raised its voice in our presence. It has never needed to. The file recommends against becoming interesting to it.

Full progression

Progression

1Witness / Pursuit

Pursuit Die

The approach is measurable: a die that grows as the hunt lengthens. We plot it like a fever chart. d6 → d8 → d10 → d12.

Covered Face.

Containment note: averting the gaze prevents cataloging — but leaves the observer blind to the subject in every way that matters. The defense and the vulnerability are the same posture. We have no better recommendation. You normally keep your face covered. A creature may declare that it averts its gaze from you; while doing so it cannot become a Witness, but has disadvantage on sight-based attacks and Perception checks involving you and cannot use a sight-dependent reaction against you.

Witness.

Cataloging event: no defense is permitted — to see the face is to be entered, and the entry persists twenty-four hours. Hypothesis: the face is not an image but an address. Once it has yours, distance is bookkeeping. As a bonus action you may reveal your face. Any creature within 60 feet with direct sight that is not averting its gaze becomes a Witness for 24 hours. There is no save; seeing the face is the trigger. A Witness remains marked even if it later closes its eyes or leaves your sight.

Pursuit.

Quarry designation: one Witness is selected — direction and distance known across the plane, speed multiplied, terrain and obstacles processed as paperwork. The subject does not navigate. It is drawn. Bonus action, 1 CE: choose one Witness as your Quarry. Until Pursuit ends, you know the Quarry’s direction and approximate distance while on the same plane, gain +10 feet × PB speed, ignore nonmagical difficult terrain while moving toward it, and have advantage on Strength checks to break obstacles between you and it.

Fury.

Fury mechanics: every turn spent closing the gap accrues Fury, discharged as Pursuit Dice on the arriving blow. Note: voluntary retreat burns the ledger. The subject's economy punishes only hesitation, never commitment. At the end of your turn, if the Quarry remains more than 10 feet away and you spent at least half your movement moving toward it, gain 1 Fury, maximum PB. If you willingly move away from the Quarry without being forced, lose all Fury. On a melee hit against the Quarry, spend any amount of Fury to add that many Pursuit Dice to the damage.

3Nothing Stops the Pursuit

Obstacle response: barriers between subject and Quarry are destroyed at maximum effect, damage doubled — and the subject climbs what it cannot break. In trial 12 it went through a reinforced wall rather than around it. The wall was load-bearing. We have relocated the trials. Your attacks against unattended objects and structures between you and the Quarry deal maximum weapon dice and double damage. During Pursuit you gain a climb speed equal to your speed.

6Four Pixels

Imprint event: the gaze extends to any surface the subject touches — an imprinted face, maintained one day, catalogs its first clear viewer. The face is now a place. We are mapping every surface in the wing. Action, 2 CE: imprint a cursed image of your face onto one Tiny or larger surface or object you touch. The first creature that clearly sees it and is not averting its gaze becomes a Witness. Maintain up to PB imprints for 24 hours.

11No Known Material

Pursuit-state resilience: the subject resists shove, grapple, restraint, and prone while pursuing — and may spend Fury to blunt incoming damage. Conclusion: do not attempt to interrupt the pursuit. Survive it, or be the Quarry somewhere else. You have advantage on saves against being shoved, grappled, restrained, or knocked prone while moving toward the Quarry. As a reaction when you take damage during Pursuit, spend up to PB Fury; reduce the damage by that many Pursuit Dice.

18Face in Every Frame

Escalation: pursuit speed doubles, and the subject has manifested the Domain below. The face, at last, is everywhere. Staff are reminded that 'everywhere' includes reflective surfaces. Pursuit speed bonus doubles, and you gain the Domain below.

Pinnacle expression

Domain Expansion — Face in Every Frame

ExpansionDomain event: for one minute, every surface carries a fragment of the face — at each twenty-count, every hostile is cataloged as Witness regardless of averted gaze, and Quarry designation requires no action. There is no longer a correct direction to look. Recommendation: close your eyes and file the report from memory. Full-turn action, 20 CE, enclosed 60-foot-radius Domain for 1 minute. Every reflective surface, sign, screen, and wall carries an impossible fragment of your face.

Compulsory Recognition — sole sure-hit At initiative count 20, every chosen hostile creature becomes a Witness whether or not it is averting its gaze. A protected creature makes a Wisdom save and avoids becoming a Witness on success.

Unbroken Hunt At initiative count 20 you may also choose one Witness as Quarry without an action and set your Fury to at least half PB, rounded up. If that Quarry drops to 0 HP, you may immediately choose another Witness without ending Pursuit.

No automatic execution The Domain guarantees recognition and pursuit state. Your attacks still roll normally; the source’s inevitability is represented by tracking, speed, Fury, and obstacle destruction.

Clash bookkeeping 800 clash points (Tier IV). In a Domain clash, a 100+ point advantage collapses the weaker Domain; collapse deals no damage. Barrier: AC 12 + PB; HP = 10 × max(1, your CE modifier) + 1,600. Burnout: 5 rounds.

Innate Technique Feats

Technique Feats

Shamebound Pursuit

Grief-Fueled Momentum

Feedback loop confirmed: when the Quarry's blow lands, the subject gains one Fury. Being struck enrages rather than deters. Standard deterrence models do not apply; do not apply them. Once per round when your Quarry damages you, gain 1 Fury after that damage resolves.

Four Pixels

Unseen Photograph

Resolution finding: the imprint may shrink to four pixels — perception, not possession, is the trigger. Merely owning the image is insufficient; the image must be seen. Our monitors are now set to thumbnail. This feels like losing. Your cursed face imprints may be as small as a four-pixel detail. A creature must actually perceive the detail to become a Witness; merely possessing the image is not enough.

Shamebound Pursuit, level 6

No Rest Until Silence

Fury persistence: half is retained when the Quarry falls, transferable to the next Witness designated. The hunt does not end with the Quarry. It pauses, politely, and continues. When your Quarry drops to 0 HP, retain up to half your current Fury until the end of your next turn. If you choose another Witness as Quarry before then, transfer that Fury.

Shamebound Pursuit, level 11

Inhuman Acceleration

Closure event, verified: once per short rest, a sixty-foot gap is closed in a single turn — across water, up walls, without an action spent. Distance is the one variable the subject has fully solved. Once per short rest, when you start your turn at least 60 feet from the Quarry, you may Dash without an action and your movement that turn may cross liquid surfaces and vertical walls.

Rules integration

Table Rulings

Witness is informationally persistent but not a death sentence. The user still spends actions, movement, CE, and attack rolls to reach the target.
Averting gaze is real counterplay. A creature that averts cannot use effects requiring a clear look at the user.
Artistic drawings do not trigger Witness unless produced by Four Pixels; ordinary photos, recordings, mirrors, and live video do if the face is actually visible.
Pursuit bonuses apply only against the chosen Quarry. This prevents the technique from becoming generic permanent super-speed.
