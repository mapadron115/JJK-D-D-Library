What the technique actually does.

Character engine

Create Blight patches that accumulate Wither and suppress healing. Fresh running water is a hard Binding-Vow weakness that cuts safe lanes through your territory.

Technique save DC: 8 + PB + chosen CE ability modifier. Technique attack: PB + chosen CE ability modifier.

Field notes. Subject: a plague remembered by cursed energy. Three truths govern the Blight. First: the logic is reproduction, not destruction. Blighted Breath seeds patches; the patches accumulate Wither; Wither reduces every point of healing by PB, and at 3 Wither even magical healing must save or halve again, spread, wither, deny healing, wherever it is loosed. Second: the territory is the sentence. Patches merge, bloom, and poison; the mounted Horse-and-Rider Flesh carries the plague at speed. Third: the memory has one terror, water. Fresh running water removes all Wither immediately and, by binding vow, the transformed form cannot willingly cross more than five feet of it; the current breaks the recollection, because the blight it remembers was still, stagnant rot. In exchange, the vow buys resistance to non-radiant technique damage while the attacker stands in Blight. The Domain is the Shore Where Nothing Grows: streams placed across the field that even the bearer cannot remove. Mark this: every plague has its river. The wise bearer builds the battlefield on the far bank.

Full progression

Level progression

1Blighted Breath

The seed, exhaled. Fifteen-foot cone, Constitution against it: failure plants 1 Wither and grows a five-foot patch beneath the victim for a minute. The patch is the technique's real argument, hostiles starting their turns inside it test again or wither further. One breath, and the ground has joined the plague. As an action for 1 CE, you exhale a 15-foot cone. Each creature in the cone makes a Constitution save vs your technique DC; on a failure it gains 1 Wither and a 5-foot Blight patch grows beneath it for 1 minute; on a success, nothing happens. Blight patches are 5-foot, 1-minute terrain: a hostile that starts its turn standing in Blight makes a Constitution save, failure: it gains 1 Wither. Wither (formal definition): each Wither reduces all HP the creature regains by your PB. At 3 Wither, magical healing on the creature requires a Constitution save, failure halves it again after the Wither reduction. A creature loses 1 Wither at the end of each turn it spends entirely outside Blight. Contact with fresh running water removes all Wither immediately.

3Horse-and-Rider Flesh

The plague takes a body. Two CE, one minute, Large: +20 speed, advantage against being knocked prone, and the bulk that cannot squeeze through ten-foot gaps. Mark this: the Blight is faster on the horse. Plagues have always traveled. As a bonus action for 2 CE, you assume a Large mounted silhouette for 1 minute: your speed increases by 20, you have advantage on ability checks and saving throws against being knocked prone (invented), and you cannot squeeze through spaces narrower than 10 feet without ending the form.

6Withering Crop

The harvest. Adjacent patches merge into larger ones, and Bloom of Rot, bonus action, 1 CE, detonates a patch within sixty feet: Wither and poisoned on failed saves, the patch withering away after. The technique understands husbandry: plant, tend, reap, and the field is the weapon. Adjacent Blight patches merge into larger ones. Bloom of Rot: bonus action, 1 CE, choose one Blight patch within 60 feet; each hostile standing in it makes a Constitution save; failure: it gains 1 Wither and is poisoned until the end of your next turn; success: no effect. The patch withers away after blooming.

11Aversion to Fresh Water

The vow, sworn. The transformed form cannot willingly cross more than five feet of fresh running water without ending, and in exchange, resistance to non-radiant technique damage while the attacker stands in Blight. This is the blight's honesty: it names its terror in the contract, and charges the enemy full price for the rest. A Binding Vow: your transformed form cannot willingly cross more than 5 feet of fresh running water without ending the transformation. In exchange, you gain resistance to non-radiant technique damage while the attacker stands in your Blight.

18Domain Expansion: Shore Where Nothing Grows

A black shore under a green-grey sky, the water thick and slow as old blood, the sand patterned with tidemarks of ash. Nothing grows: the rocks are bare, the driftwood is white as picked bone, and three streams of clear water cut across the rot like sutures, the only clean thing in the world. The smell is sweet and wrong, decay wearing the mask of ripeness.

The plague's country, drawn in full. Three fresh-running-water streams cross the field, neutral, unremovable even by the bearer, and Blight cannot cross them; Bloom of Rot costs no action inside, once per round; at initiative 20 every chosen hostile standing in Blight automatically withers. The Domain does not remove the weakness. It maps it, and dares the enemy to reach it. Full-turn action, 20 CE, enclosed 90-foot radius centered on you, up to 1 minute, Technique Focus. When it opens, place three fresh-running-water streams across the battlefield; the streams are neutral terrain and cannot be removed by you. You may place and merge Blight normally, but Blight cannot cross a stream. Bloom of Rot costs no action inside the Domain, once per round. At initiative 20 each chosen hostile standing in Blight automatically gains 1 Wither; a protected creature instead makes a Constitution save and avoids the Wither on success. Any creature touching a stream at initiative 20 removes 1 Wither instead and cannot gain Wither from that event. The water remains your Binding-Vow weakness inside your own Domain.

Innate Technique Feats

Innate Technique Feats

4 optional build choices

Long Arms

The reach plants. While transformed, a bonus action plants Blight by touch within reach, a five-foot patch, one minute. The horse's long arms mean the plague no longer waits for the breath; the field grows wherever the rider passes. While transformed, you may use your bonus action to plant Blight with your long arms: touch a point within your reach to create a 5-foot Blight patch there for 1 minute.

Murrain Wind

The plague rides the Dash. Exhale behind while dashing transformed, and a five-foot trail of Blight follows until the next turn. I have watched the rider cross a field and leave it seeded. Speed was always the plague's favorite vector. When you take the Dash action while transformed, you may exhale your breath behind you, leaving a 5-foot-wide trail of Blight along your path until the start of your next turn.

Salt-Air Rot

The taste of the withering. Bonus action, thirty feet, a creature with 2+ Wither: learn its current HP and whether it holds Focus. The technique reads its victims the way a physician reads a chart, and prescribes accordingly. As a bonus action, you can taste the Wither on a creature within 30 feet that has at least 2 Wither: learn its current HP total and whether it is maintaining Focus on anything.

Black Tide (11+)

The field, rearranged. Once per short rest, move one Blight patch ten feet, never across fresh water. The vow holds even here. Mark this: the plague's one law is older than its hunger, and the hunger obeys. once/short rest move one Blight patch 10 ft, never across fresh water.
