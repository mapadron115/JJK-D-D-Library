Core law

What the technique actually does.

Character engine

Create Blight patches that accumulate Wither and suppress healing. Fresh running water is a hard Binding-Vow weakness that cuts safe lanes through your territory.

Technique save DC: 8 + PB + chosen CE ability modifier. Technique attack: PB + chosen CE ability modifier.

Full progression

Level progression

1Blighted Breath

Action, 1 CE, 15-ft cone Con save: 2 dice poison/necrotic; failure creates 5-ft Blight patch beneath target for 1 minute.

3Horse-and-Rider Flesh

Bonus, 2 CE, 1 minute: Large mounted silhouette, +20 speed, hard to knock prone; cannot squeeze through <10-ft spaces without ending form.

6Withering Crop

Adjacent Blight merges. At 3 Wither, magical healing requires Con save or is halved again after normal Wither reduction.

11Aversion to Fresh Water

Binding Vow: transformed user cannot willingly cross >5-ft fresh running water without ending form; in exchange gain resistance to non-radiant technique damage while attacker stands in Blight.

18Domain Expansion: Shore Where Nothing Grows

Full-turn action, 20 CE, enclosed 90-foot radius centered on you, up to 1 minute, Technique Focus. When it opens, place three fresh-running-water streams across the battlefield; the streams are neutral terrain and cannot be removed by you. You may place and merge Blight normally, but Blight cannot cross a stream. At initiative 20 each chosen hostile standing in Blight automatically gains 1 Wither; a protected creature instead makes a Constitution save and avoids the Wither on success. Any creature touching a stream at initiative 20 removes 1 Wither instead and cannot gain Wither from that event. The water remains your Binding-Vow weakness inside your own Domain.

Innate Technique Feats

Innate Technique Feats

4 optional build choices

Long Arms

While transformed, you may use your bonus action to plant Blight with your long arms: touch a point within your reach to create a 5-foot Blight patch there for 1 minute.

Murrain Wind

When you take the Dash action while transformed, you may exhale your breath behind you, leaving a 5-foot-wide trail of Blight along your path until the start of your next turn.

Salt-Air Rot

As a bonus action, you can taste the Wither on a creature within 30 feet that has at least 2 Wither: learn its current HP total and whether it is maintaining Focus on anything.

Black Tide (11+)

once/short rest move one Blight patch 10 ft, never across fresh water.
