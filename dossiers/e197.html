Withering CropAdjacent Blight merges. At 3 Wither, magical healing requires Con save or is halved again after normal Wither reduction.
Aversion to Fresh WaterBinding Vow: transformed user cannot willingly cross >5-ft fresh running water without ending form; in exchange gain resistance to non-radiant technique damage while attacker stands in Blight.
Domain Expansion: Shore Where Nothing GrowsFull-turn action, 20 CE, enclosed 90-foot radius centered on you, up to 1 minute, Technique Focus. When it opens, place three fresh-running-water streams across the battlefield; the streams are neutral terrain and cannot be removed by you. You may place and merge Blight normally, but Blight cannot cross a stream. At initiative 20 each chosen hostile standing in Blight automatically gains 1 Wither; a protected creature instead makes a Constitution save and avoids the Wither on success. Any creature touching a stream at initiative 20 removes 1 Wither instead and cannot gain Wither from that event. The water remains your Binding-Vow weakness inside your own Domain.

Character engine

Create Blight patches that accumulate Wither and suppress healing. Fresh running water is a hard Binding-Vow weakness that cuts safe lanes through your territory.

Technique save DC: 8 + PB + chosen CE ability modifier. Technique attack: PB + chosen CE ability modifier.

Counterplay

Fresh running water is a hard weakness. Use it to create safe lanes and force the transformed user to end their form or reroute.
