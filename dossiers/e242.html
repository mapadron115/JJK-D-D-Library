Features & procedures

Properties & techniques

Exact player-facing procedures

Quick Draw

once/turn draw/store a slotted tool without object interaction.

Loadout Memory

after long rest designate two stored tools; switching between them does not break their attunement if both are already attuned.

Emergency Eject

reaction, 2 CE when disarmed: summon one stored tool to hand.

Catastrophic Break

if Black Box is destroyed by artifact-breaking effect, contents eject harmlessly into adjacent spaces; they are not deleted.

Rules integration

Cursed-tool attunement

Campaign canon

Cursed-tool attunement: you have PB attunement slots (2–6). This tool occupies one slot and requires a one-hour ritual. Attunement ends voluntarily over a short rest, on death, or when you replace it while over capacity. Distance does not break attunement. Cursed-tool attunement is separate from magic-item attunement.
Balance & counterplay. Expands weapon-master flexibility but does not grant extra attunement slots or bypass CE/action costs.
