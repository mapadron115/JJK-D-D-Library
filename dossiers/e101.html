Concept Devourer

At ordinary levels this is a savage close-range chainsaw technique. Its calamity ceiling appears when a defeated supernatural concept can be partially or permanently removed from the world.

Power tierTier V · Calamity
Combat roleChainsaws / regeneration / fear / conceptual erasure
Core law

What the technique actually does.

Fear Names

The taxonomy of the technique: Fear Names are conceptual tags on major curses, shikigami, techniques, and supernatural entities — never ordinary humans, never mundane objects. Erasure is a ritual with a ledger, and the GM and player record every one.

  • Your chainsaw attacks are melee CE attacks that can Black Flash.
  • Fear Names are conceptual tags attached to major curses, shikigami, techniques, or supernatural entities. Ordinary humans and mundane objects do not have a devourable Fear Name.
  • Erasure is never triggered by ordinary damage. It requires a defeated eligible target, a Devour action, and increasingly severe conditions.
  • GM and player should record every erased Name because it can alter future encounters.

Field notes. Subject: a mouth that remembers being hungry. Three truths govern him. First, the chainsaws are not the technique — they are the teeth it grew; the hunger was there first. Second, erasure is never an accident of damage: it requires a defeated eligible target, a Devour action, and increasingly severe conditions — the ledger must balance. Third, he runs on the blood he sheds: Blood tokens earned from slashing damage, spent to heal or to extend his reach, because a predator that cannot feed itself starves mid-hunt. Hence the maxim: he does not destroy things. He refuses them.

Full progression

Level 1–20 Progression

1Rip Cord

The ignition: bonus action, 1 CE, chainsaws on arms and head for a minute — unarmed strikes become 1d8 slashing with an extra Devour Die once per turn. The sound of the cord is the sound of the technique announcing its terms. Bonus action, 1 CE: manifest arm/head chainsaws for 1 minute. Unarmed strikes become 1d8 slashing and once/turn add one Devour Die slashing.

Devour Die progression: d6 at levels 1–4, d8 at levels 5–10, d10 at levels 11–16, d12 at levels 17–20. The original expansion repeatedly references a Devour Die without printing its value. This reviewed edition completes that omission using the same four-step progression explicitly established by Vector Authority in the same book. This is a codex clarification, not a claimed quotation from the source.

3Blood-Fed Engine

The economy of the predator: slashing damage earns Blood tokens, capped at PB, and a bonus action spends them to regain Devour Dice in HP — technique healing, not RCT. He eats to heal the way a fire eats to grow. When you deal slashing damage, gain 1 Blood token, max PB. Bonus action spend any tokens to regain one Devour Die HP per token; this is technique healing, not RCT.

6Hero's Rush

While the saws run, he runs: +10 speed, and each creature he drops to 0 HP buys a reaction — half his speed of movement and one more chainsaw attack. Momentum is the second hunger. While Rip Cord is active, +10 speed and when you reduce a creature to 0 HP you may move half speed and make one chainsaw attack as a reaction.

9Devil Butcher

His saws learn what they are cutting: chainsaw attacks ignore resistance to slashing and necrotic, and against summons, curses, or shikigami, criticals suppress regeneration until his next turn. The butcher does not ask what it is made of. He cuts anyway. Chainsaw attacks ignore resistance to slashing/necrotic. Against summons, curses, or shikigami, criticals also suppress regeneration until start next turn.

11Partial Devour

The first erasure: over an eligible supernatural creature at 0 HP, an action and 4 CE, a CE check against its PB — success removes one named feature, immunity, or summon form from its future manifestations for 24 hours. Bosses may spend Legendary Resistance. A nibble, not a meal — but the world learns to flinch. Action over an eligible supernatural creature at 0 HP, 4 CE: make a CE ability check vs DC 10 + the target’s PB; for CR-based creatures use a proxy PB of 2 + (CR ÷ 4, rounded down), minimum 2. Success removes one named technique feature, damage immunity, or summon form from that creature's future manifestations for 24 hours. Bosses can resist with Legendary Resistance.

13Hero of Hell

The transformation: bonus action, 6 CE, a minute of Large size, +20 speed, climbing, advantage on Strength, 10-ft chainsaw reach, two extra Devour Dice. Once per long rest, and he cannot speak intelligibly while transformed. The hero of hell has no speeches. Bonus action, 6 CE, 1 minute, once/long rest: Large size, +20 speed, climbing, advantage Str saves/checks, chainsaw reach 10 ft, and attacks add two Devour Dice. You cannot speak intelligibly while transformed.

15Fear Resonance

His reputation becomes a weapon: while a creature within 60 ft is Frightened of him, his first attack each round has advantage. But survival is its own argument — if a creature publicly survives or defeats him, the fear drains away until he reestablishes it. Terror is a resource he must keep earning. When a creature within 60 ft is Frightened of you, your first attack against it each round has advantage. When a creature publicly survives or defeats you, the GM may reduce that social fear and deny this benefit against witnesses until you reestablish terror.

18True Devour

The technique’s true function: after personally reducing an eligible unique supernatural entity to 0 HP, 10 CE and one uninterrupted minute consuming its manifested core — a failed Cha save erases one core concept from the campaign until a Maximum-level reversal; success still applies Partial Devour for 7 days. The player and GM must agree beforehand on what the erasure means. Nothing in this dossier should be attempted casually. After personally reducing an eligible unique supernatural entity to 0 HP, spend 10 CE and 1 uninterrupted minute consuming its manifested core/name. It makes Cha save. Failure erases one core concept from the campaign until restored by a specific quest/Maximum-level reversal; success instead applies Partial Devour for 7 days. A player and GM must explicitly agree on what the erased concept means before resolving it.

20Chainsaw Man

The end of limits: Hero of Hell loses its daily cap, and once per long rest, death itself is refused — with 3 Blood tokens or a willing ally’s blood, he returns at 1 HP after one round, Rip Cord already screaming. The chainsaw man’s contract with mortality is simple: he declines. Hero of Hell no longer has a daily limit. Once per long rest when killed, if you have at least 3 Blood tokens or an ally willingly feeds you blood, return at 1 HP after 1 round and immediately activate Rip Cord for free.

Pinnacle expression

Pinnacle / Maximum

The Maximum is True Devour at full commitment: usable only on an eligible entity already at 0 HP after True Devour is unlocked — all remaining CE surrendered, 2 exhaustion after. The target saves with disadvantage: failure performs True Devour, success Partial. This is not for abstract world concepts without a manifested bearer. Some names should cost everything to eat. Maximum Technique — Eat the Name (12 CE; action): usable only on an eligible entity already at 0 HP and after True Devour is unlocked. Give up all remaining CE and gain 2 exhaustion after the attempt. The target makes Cha save with disadvantage; failure performs True Devour and success performs Partial Devour. This is not usable on abstract world concepts without a manifested supernatural bearer.

Feats

Technique Feats

Prerequisite: Rip Cord

Vrrrm Vrrrm

He revs the saws as they manifest: spending 2 Blood tokens turns the activation itself into an attack — each creature within 5 ft saves or takes a Devour Die. The entrance is the attack. You rev the chainsaws as you manifest them: when you activate Rip Cord, you may spend 2 Blood tokens to make the manifestation itself an attack — each creature within 5 ft makes a Dex save, taking one Devour Die slashing on a failure.

Prerequisite: Blood-Fed Engine

Blood Lash

Bonus action, 3 Blood tokens: pressurized blood whips extend his chainsaws to 10-ft reach until end of turn. Hunger has range. As a bonus action, spend 3 Blood tokens to extend your chainsaws with whips of pressurized blood: your chainsaw attacks gain 10-ft reach until the end of your turn.

Prerequisite: level 9

Door to Hell

When he reduces a cursed spirit to 0 HP, he learns whether it has manifested elsewhere or can reincarnate under campaign lore. The devourer keeps a map of what he has eaten. When you reduce a cursed spirit to 0 HP, learn whether it has previously manifested elsewhere or can reincarnate under campaign lore.

Prerequisite: Hero of Hell

Save Me, Chainsaw Man

Reaction when an ally within 60 ft drops to 0 HP: he moves up to his speed toward them and makes one chainsaw attack against an adjacent creature. The hero arrives the way heroes do — loudly, and through the enemy. Reaction when an ally within 60 ft drops to 0 HP: move up to speed toward them and make one chainsaw attack against a creature adjacent to them.

Rules integration

Binding Rulings

Concept erasure is campaign-changing and therefore deliberately gated behind defeated targets, saves, high CE, and explicit definition of consequences.
Erasing a technique concept does not retroactively delete player sheets unless everyone at the table wants that story.
Chainsaw criticals can Black Flash; Devour checks and erasure cannot.
Concept Devourer is Tier V, not X Tier: it changes fiction through a specific defeated-target procedure rather than freely editing game rules.
