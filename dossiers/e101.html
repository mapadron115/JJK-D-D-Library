Concept Devourer

Tier V · CalamityChainsaws / regeneration / fear / conceptual erasure

At ordinary levels this is a savage close-range chainsaw technique. Its calamity ceiling appears when a defeated supernatural concept can be partially or permanently removed from the world.

Fear Names

  • Your chainsaw attacks are melee CE attacks that can Black Flash.
  • Fear Names are conceptual tags attached to major curses, shikigami, techniques, or supernatural entities. Ordinary humans and mundane objects do not have a devourable Fear Name.
  • Erasure is never triggered by ordinary damage. It requires a defeated eligible target, a Devour action, and increasingly severe conditions.
  • GM and player should record every erased Name because it can alter future encounters.

Pinnacle / Maximum

Maximum Technique — Eat the Name (12 CE): usable only on an eligible entity already at 0 HP and after True Devour is unlocked. Give up all remaining CE and gain 2 exhaustion after the attempt. The target makes Cha save with disadvantage; failure performs True Devour and success performs Partial Devour. This is not usable on abstract world concepts without a manifested supernatural bearer.

Binding Rulings

  • Concept erasure is campaign-changing and therefore deliberately gated behind defeated targets, saves, high CE, and explicit definition of consequences.
  • Erasing a technique concept does not retroactively delete player sheets unless everyone at the table wants that story.
  • Chainsaw criticals can Black Flash; Devour checks and erasure cannot.
  • Concept Devourer is Tier V, not X Tier: it changes fiction through a specific defeated-target procedure rather than freely editing game rules.

Source Notes

The fictional ability is the inspiration; all JJK 5e numbers and progression procedures are homebrew.
