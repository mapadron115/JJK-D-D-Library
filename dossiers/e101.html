Concept Devourer

At ordinary levels this is a savage close-range chainsaw technique. Its calamity ceiling appears when a defeated supernatural concept can be partially or permanently removed from the world.

Power tierTier V · Calamity
Combat roleChainsaws / regeneration / fear / conceptual erasure
Core law

What the technique actually does.

Fear Names

  • Your chainsaw attacks are melee CE attacks that can Black Flash.
  • Fear Names are conceptual tags attached to major curses, shikigami, techniques, or supernatural entities. Ordinary humans and mundane objects do not have a devourable Fear Name.
  • Erasure is never triggered by ordinary damage. It requires a defeated eligible target, a Devour action, and increasingly severe conditions.
  • GM and player should record every erased Name because it can alter future encounters.

Full progression

Level 1–20 Progression

1Rip Cord

Bonus action, 1 CE: manifest arm/head chainsaws for 1 minute. Unarmed strikes become 1d8 slashing and once/turn add one Devour Die slashing.

Devour Die progression: d6 at levels 1–4, d8 at levels 5–10, d10 at levels 11–16, d12 at levels 17–20. The original expansion repeatedly references a Devour Die without printing its value. This reviewed edition completes that omission using the same four-step progression explicitly established by Vector Authority in the same book. This is a codex clarification, not a claimed quotation from the source.

3Blood-Fed Engine

When you deal slashing damage, gain 1 Blood token, max PB. Bonus action spend any tokens to regain one Devour Die HP per token; this is technique healing, not RCT.

6Hero's Rush

While Rip Cord is active, +10 speed and when you reduce a creature to 0 HP you may move half speed and make one chainsaw attack as a reaction.

9Devil Butcher

Chainsaw attacks ignore resistance to slashing/necrotic. Against summons, curses, or shikigami, criticals also suppress regeneration until start next turn.

11Partial Devour

Action over an eligible supernatural creature at 0 HP, 4 CE: make CE ability check vs DC 10 + target PB/CR proxy. Success removes one named technique feature, damage immunity, or summon form from that creature's future manifestations for 24 hours. Bosses can resist with Legendary Resistance.

13Hero of Hell

Bonus action, 6 CE, 1 minute, once/long rest: Large size, +20 speed, climbing, advantage Str saves/checks, chainsaw reach 10 ft, and attacks add two Devour Dice. You cannot speak intelligibly while transformed.

15Fear Resonance

When a creature within 60 ft is Frightened of you, your first attack against it each round has advantage. When a creature publicly survives or defeats you, the GM may reduce that social fear and deny this benefit against witnesses until you reestablish terror.

18True Devour

After personally reducing an eligible unique supernatural entity to 0 HP, spend 10 CE and 1 uninterrupted minute consuming its manifested core/name. It makes Cha save. Failure erases one core concept from the campaign until restored by a specific quest/Maximum-level reversal; success instead applies Partial Devour for 7 days. A player and GM must explicitly agree on what the erased concept means before resolving it.

20Chainsaw Man

Hero of Hell no longer has a daily limit. Once per long rest when killed, if you have at least 3 Blood tokens or an ally willingly feeds you blood, return at 1 HP after 1 round and immediately activate Rip Cord for free.

Pinnacle expression

Pinnacle / Maximum

Maximum Technique — Eat the Name (12 CE): usable only on an eligible entity already at 0 HP and after True Devour is unlocked. Give up all remaining CE and gain 2 exhaustion after the attempt. The target makes Cha save with disadvantage; failure performs True Devour and success performs Partial Devour. This is not usable on abstract world concepts without a manifested supernatural bearer.

Feats

Technique Feats

Prerequisite: Rip Cord

Vrrrm Vrrrm

You rev the chainsaws as you manifest them: when you activate Rip Cord, you may spend 2 Blood tokens to make the manifestation itself an attack — each creature within 5 ft makes a Dex save, taking one Devour Die slashing on a failure.

Prerequisite: Blood-Fed Engine

Blood Lash

As a bonus action, spend 3 Blood tokens to extend your chainsaws with whips of pressurized blood: your chainsaw attacks gain 10-ft reach until the end of your turn.

Prerequisite: level 9

Door to Hell

When you reduce a cursed spirit to 0 HP, learn whether it has previously manifested elsewhere or can reincarnate under campaign lore.

Prerequisite: Hero of Hell

Save Me, Chainsaw Man

Reaction when an ally within 60 ft drops to 0 HP: move up to speed toward them and make one chainsaw attack against a creature adjacent to them.

Rules integration

Binding Rulings

Concept erasure is campaign-changing and therefore deliberately gated behind defeated targets, saves, high CE, and explicit definition of consequences.
Erasing a technique concept does not retroactively delete player sheets unless everyone at the table wants that story.
Chainsaw criticals can Black Flash; Devour checks and erasure cannot.
Concept Devourer is Tier V, not X Tier: it changes fiction through a specific defeated-target procedure rather than freely editing game rules.
