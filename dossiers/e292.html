Mister Fantastic

Elasticity / grappling / kinetic soak / contortion / genius

Power tierTier IV · Special Grade
Core law

What the technique actually does.

The meter is the technique: wound by grapples and absorbed blows, unwound by idleness, and everything downstream keys off where it stands. Core Engine — Stretch. Stretch 0–10. Gain 1 when you successfully grapple a creature with this technique or take 10+ bludgeoning damage in a single turn (the impact winds you up). Lose 1 at the end of a round in which you did neither. At Stretch 5, your elastic reach becomes 20 ft (15 ft from Rubber Body plus 5 ft longer) and creatures have disadvantage on checks to escape your holds. At Stretch 10, creatures you have grappled or restrained take bludgeoning damage equal to your proficiency bonus at the start of each of their turns — but you are so extended that attacks against you have advantage until Stretch drops. You may release 2 Stretch as a bonus action (the coil snaps harmlessly back).

Field notes. Subject: a flinch that never finished. Three truths govern it. First: the Stretch meter is a wound spring — it climbs on every successful grapple and every ten points of bludgeoning absorbed, and it unwinds at the end of any round where neither happened. Rest is the tax here; the technique punishes idleness the way a bowstring punishes being unstrung. Second: the recoil is the weapon. Kinetic Soak shaves the blow, and Soak and Rebound gives it back with interest — the body does not absorb impact, it stores it and returns it on the next strike. Third: the apex is a paradox, and the file prints it honestly — at Stretch 10 the holds crush, and the sorcerer is so extended that every attack against him has advantage. Maximum tension is maximum exposure. Maximum tension is maximum exposure, and the best users ride that edge deliberately.

Full progression

Level 1 through 20.

1Rubber Body

The body is living rubber from the first level — resistance to bludgeoning, fifteen-foot reach, and the cursed-energy modifier standing in for Strength on every grapple. Your body is living rubber: you gain resistance to bludgeoning damage. Your limbs stretch — your melee reach becomes 15 ft for unarmed strikes and grapple attempts, and you may use your CE modifier in place of Strength on grapple checks.

3Wrap & Hold

At the third level the grapple becomes a restraint — two coins, one action, the escape DC set by the technique, and the Stretch meter climbs. As an action for 2 CE, make a grapple attempt against a creature within your reach. On a success the target is restrained instead of merely grappled (escape DC equals your technique DC), and you gain 1 Stretch.

6Kinetic Soak

The sixth level teaches the body to keep the change — bludgeoning reduced by the proficiency bonus after resistance, and every impact that lands still winds the spring. When you take bludgeoning damage, reduce it by your proficiency bonus after resistance applies. Impacts that slip through still wind you up — this is how Stretch is fed.

9Contortionist

The ninth level is the escape artist’s clause — one-inch openings, half-inch flat, and a bonus action to slip any grapple or mundane restraint not of the user’s choosing. You can squeeze through openings as narrow as 1 inch, flatten yourself to half an inch thick, and escape a grapple or mundane restraints as a bonus action. You have advantage on checks made to slip bonds, manacles, or a grapple you did not choose.

11Malleable Arsenal

The eleventh level shapes the limbs into tools — mallet fists with twenty-foot reach, whip-limbs that deny reactions, or a parachute that turns every fall into a glide. As a bonus action for 1 CE, shape your limbs for 1 minute (choose one): mallet fists — your unarmed strikes deal 2d8 bludgeoning damage with 20-ft reach; whip-limbs — 30-ft reach, and creatures you hit cannot take reactions until the start of your next turn; parachute — you take no falling damage and may glide 20 ft horizontally for every 5 ft you descend.

13Living Shield

At the thirteenth level the rubber becomes someone else’s armor — a reaction and two coins envelop an ally within fifteen feet, granting resistance and +2 AC. As a reaction for 2 CE, when an ally within 15 ft is hit by an attack, you envelop them: they gain resistance to the triggering damage and +2 AC until the start of your next turn.

15Soak & Rebound

The fifteenth level closes the circuit — once per turn, the bludgeoning just taken returns on the next unarmed strike, capped at the user’s level. Once per turn, after you take bludgeoning damage, your next unarmed strike before the end of your next turn deals extra bludgeoning damage equal to the damage you actually took after resistance and flat reduction (maximum your level). The body gives the blow back.

18Colossus Coil

The eighteenth level is the crowd-control thesis — four coins grapple up to four creatures at once, each restrained and taking 3d8 at the start of its turns. As an action for 4 CE, you grapple up to four creatures within your reach at once; each makes a Strength save against your technique DC or is restrained. A restrained creature takes 3d8 bludgeoning damage at the start of each of its turns while held.

20Apex Plasticity

The twentieth level finishes the sediment — thirty-foot reach, immunity to bludgeoning, six creatures in the coil, and the Stretch meter never falling below three. Your elastic reach becomes 30 ft (35 ft at Stretch 5). You gain immunity to bludgeoning damage. Colossus Coil can hold up to six creatures, and your Stretch cannot fall below 3.

Pinnacle expression

Maximum, Domain, or equivalent.

The Maximum unfurls the body into geography — a sixty-foot web of elastic bands for a minute, restraining what enters and grinding 4d8 into the held, while the user fights on unimpeded. Maximum — Infinite Coil (10 CE): your body unfurls into a 60-ft-radius web of elastic bands centered on a point within 60 ft, lasting 1 minute (Focus). Hostile creatures that enter the web or start their turn there make a Strength save against your technique DC or are restrained; a restrained creature takes 4d8 bludgeoning damage at the start of each of its turns. You act normally while the web persists — the coils work on their own. It is containment, not an explosion: the battlefield itself is held.

Feats

Build-defining options.

Reed's Calculus

The genius is the second power — analyze a creature as a bonus action, learn its resistances and immunities, and grant every ally’s first attack advantage. The genius is the second power. As a bonus action, analyze a creature you can see: you learn its damage resistances, vulnerabilities, and immunities (if any), and the first attack each of your allies makes against it before the start of your next turn has advantage. You may use this feature a number of times equal to your proficiency bonus per long rest.

Living Bridge

The body becomes infrastructure — a twenty-foot bridge, raft, or ramp for two thousand pounds, or a trampoline that turns falling allies into bouncing ones. You may shape your body into a 20-ft bridge, raft, or ramp that supports up to 2,000 lb — allies cross gaps, pits, and water on your back. Alternatively, you may become a trampoline: allies who fall onto you take no falling damage and may bounce up to 20 ft as part of their movement without provoking opportunity attacks.

Snapback

An escaped hold is not a lost one — a reaction snaps the limb back for 2d8 and an immediate fresh grapple attempt. When a creature escapes your grapple or restraint, you may use your reaction: the limb snaps back — the escapee takes 2d8 bludgeoning damage, and you may immediately make a grapple attempt against it as part of the same reaction.

Containment Wrap

Two coins wrap the hazardous instead of the hostile — flames smothered, traps untriggered, swarms contained, all suppressed for a minute. As an action for 2 CE, envelop a Medium-or-smaller unattended object or a 10-ft cube of hazardous terrain (fire, caltrops, a swarm, a triggered trap): its effects are suppressed for 1 minute (Focus) while wrapped — flames smother, traps cannot trigger, swarms cannot leave.

Elastic Rebound

The membrane learns to catch — a reaction and two coins reduce a ranged attack by proficiency plus modifier, and a fully caught projectile goes home. When a ranged attack targets you, you may use your reaction and spend 2 CE to stretch a catching membrane: reduce the attack's damage by your proficiency bonus + your CE modifier as the rubber gives. If this reduces the damage to 0, you catch the projectile — fling it back as part of the same reaction by making a ranged technique attack against a creature within 60 ft; on a hit it takes the caught attack's original damage.

Rules integration

Edge cases and shared systems.

Codex rulings: Direct rolled technique/weapon attacks can use the campaign's Black Flash procedure when otherwise eligible. Passive damage, hazards, summons acting independently, and initiative-20 Domain events cannot. Generic Maximum Output applies only to ordinary features, never named Maxima or Domains. Stretch is a technique meter, not a damage die: it never adds dice to attacks and is not modified by effects that alter dice.
