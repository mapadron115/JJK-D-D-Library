Mister Fantastic

Elasticity / grappling / kinetic soak / contortion / genius

Power tierTier IV · Special Grade
Core law

What the technique actually does.

Core Engine — Stretch. Stretch 0–10. Gain 1 when you successfully grapple a creature with this technique or take 10+ bludgeoning damage in a single turn (the impact winds you up). Lose 1 at the end of a round in which you did neither. At Stretch 5, your elastic reach is 5 ft longer and creatures have disadvantage on checks to escape your holds. At Stretch 10, creatures you have grappled or restrained take bludgeoning damage equal to your proficiency bonus at the start of each of their turns — but you are so extended that attacks against you have advantage until Stretch drops. You may release 2 Stretch as a bonus action (the coil snaps harmlessly back).

The fiction. The prevailing theory holds that Stretch is not a body plan but a flinch: the user's CE imprinted on the instant of an impact that should have broken them, and the body went elastic instead of shattering. Every grapple and absorbed blow winds it further — registry scholars describe the sorcerer as permanently mid-recoil, one endless collision the technique keeps choosing not to end. Elasticity is just what a refusal to break looks like when it goes on for too long.

Full progression

Level 1 through 20.

L1Rubber Body

Your body is living rubber: you gain resistance to bludgeoning damage. Your limbs stretch — your melee reach becomes 15 ft for unarmed strikes and grapple attempts, and you may use your CE modifier in place of Strength on grapple checks.

L3Wrap & Hold

As an action for 2 CE, make a grapple attempt against a creature within your reach. On a success the target is restrained instead of merely grappled (escape DC equals your technique DC), and you gain 1 Stretch.

L6Kinetic Soak

When you take bludgeoning damage, reduce it by your proficiency bonus after resistance applies. Impacts that slip through still wind you up — this is how Stretch is fed.

L9Contortionist

You can squeeze through openings as narrow as 1 inch, flatten yourself to half an inch thick, and escape a grapple or mundane restraints as a bonus action. You have advantage on checks made to slip bonds, manacles, or a grapple you did not choose.

L11Malleable Arsenal

As a bonus action for 1 CE, shape your limbs for 1 minute (choose one): mallet fists — your unarmed strikes deal 2d8 bludgeoning damage with 20-ft reach; whip-limbs — 30-ft reach, and creatures you hit cannot take reactions until the start of your next turn; parachute — you take no falling damage and may glide 20 ft horizontally for every 5 ft you descend.

L13Living Shield

As a reaction for 2 CE, when an ally within 15 ft is hit by an attack, you envelop them: they gain resistance to the triggering damage and +2 AC until the start of your next turn.

L15Soak & Rebound

Once per turn, after you take bludgeoning damage, your next unarmed strike before the end of your next turn deals extra bludgeoning damage equal to the damage you took (maximum your level). The body gives the blow back.

L18Colossus Coil

As an action for 4 CE, you grapple up to four creatures within your reach at once; each makes a Strength save against your technique DC or is restrained. A restrained creature takes 3d8 bludgeoning damage at the start of each of its turns while held.

L20Apex Plasticity

Your elastic reach becomes 30 ft (35 ft at Stretch 5). You gain immunity to bludgeoning damage. Colossus Coil can hold up to six creatures, and your Stretch cannot fall below 3.

Pinnacle expression

Maximum, Domain, or equivalent.

Maximum — Infinite Coil (10 CE): your body unfurls into a 60-ft-radius web of elastic bands centered on a point within 60 ft, lasting 1 minute (Focus). Hostile creatures that enter the web or start their turn there make a Strength save against your technique DC or are restrained; a restrained creature takes 4d8 bludgeoning damage at the start of each of its turns. You act normally while the web persists — the coils work on their own. It is containment, not an explosion: the battlefield itself is held.

Feats

Build-defining options.

Reed's Calculus

The genius is the second power. As a bonus action, analyze a creature you can see: you learn its damage resistances, vulnerabilities, and immunities (if any), and the first attack each of your allies makes against it before the start of your next turn has advantage. You may use this feature a number of times equal to your proficiency bonus per long rest.

Living Bridge

You may shape your body into a 20-ft bridge, raft, or ramp that supports up to 2,000 lb — allies cross gaps, pits, and water on your back. Alternatively, you may become a trampoline: allies who fall onto you take no falling damage and may bounce up to 20 ft as part of their movement without provoking opportunity attacks.

Snapback

When a creature escapes your grapple or restraint, you may use your reaction: the limb snaps back — the escapee takes 2d8 bludgeoning damage, and you may immediately make a grapple attempt against it as part of the same reaction.

Containment Wrap

As an action for 2 CE, envelop a Medium-or-smaller unattended object or a 10-ft cube of hazardous terrain (fire, caltrops, a swarm, a triggered trap): its effects are suppressed for 1 minute (Focus) while wrapped — flames smother, traps cannot trigger, swarms cannot leave.

Elastic Rebound

When a ranged attack targets you, you may use your reaction and spend 2 CE to stretch a catching membrane: reduce the attack's damage by your proficiency bonus + your CE modifier as the rubber gives. If this reduces the damage to 0, you catch the projectile — fling it back as part of the same reaction by making a ranged technique attack against a creature within 60 ft; on a hit it takes the caught attack's original damage.

Rules integration

Edge cases and shared systems.

Codex rulings: Direct rolled technique/weapon attacks can use the campaign's Black Flash procedure when otherwise eligible. Passive damage, hazards, summons acting independently, and initiative-20 Domain events cannot. Generic Maximum Output applies only to ordinary features, never named Maxima or Domains. Stretch is a technique meter, not a damage die: it never adds dice to attacks and is not modified by effects that alter dice.
