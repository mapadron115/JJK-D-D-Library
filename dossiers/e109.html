Judgement Cut

Yamato / Concentration / Doppelganger / Devil Trigger

Power tierTier V · Calamity
Core law

What the technique actually does.

Core Engine — Concentration. Begin at 0 Concentration (max 10). Gain 1 when you hit without taking damage since your last turn, perfectly evade/parry, or spend a full turn moving no more than half speed. Lose 2 when hit; lose all if Frightened or Incapacitated. Many abilities scale at 5/10 Concentration.

The fiction. Jujutsu scholars classify the Concentration engine as the technique's true body — a cursed blade that answers only to an unbroken mind, and goes quiet the instant the hand holding it shakes. The Yamato's spatial slashes are not swung; they are decided, the user's resolve folding distance itself into a cut that was always going to land. The prevailing theory holds that the Devil Trigger is not a transformation but a confession: underneath the composure was always something that wanted to end things quickly.

Full progression

Level 1 through 20.

1Yamato

Manifest a cursed katana. Once/turn spend 1 CE on hit for +1d8 force and a 10-ft spatial extension.

3Trick

Bonus action, 1 CE: teleport 20 ft to a visible space or marked target. At 5 Concentration, 30 ft.

6Judgement Cut

Action, 3 CE: target point within 60 ft; creatures in 10-ft sphere Dex save, 4d10 force/slashing. At 10 Concentration, cast as replacement for one Attack once/turn.

9Mirage Blades

Bonus action, 2 CE: create PB blades. Spend them before next long rest to add 1d8 force to attacks, pin movement by 10 ft, or make a 60-ft ranged technique attack.

11Doppelganger

4 CE, Focus, 1 minute. Spectral double repeats one weapon attack or Judgement Cut each round at end of your turn for half dice, no extra riders.

13Devil Trigger

5 CE, 1 minute: fly 40 ft, +10 speed, resistance B/P/S and necrotic, heal PB HP at start of turn, Yamato adds +1d8.

15Judgement Cut End

8 CE, action, requires 10 Concentration: 40-ft radius, Dex save, 10d10 force/slashing; success half. Concentration becomes 0.

18Sin Devil Trigger

8 CE, 1 minute, once/long rest: fly 80, resistance to all but psychic/radiant, Yamato +2d8, Trick free once/turn, regain 1 Concentration at start of turn.

20Motivated

Concentration max 12; being hit removes only 1. At 10+, first Judgement Cut each round costs 0 CE. Once/long rest enter Sin Devil Trigger at 10 Concentration for 0 CE.

Pinnacle expression

Maximum, Domain, or equivalent.

Maximum — Dimensional Judgement (10 CE): 180-ft line, Dex save, 12d10 force/slashing; barriers take maximum dice and make a stability check. It cuts space, not concepts, souls, or game rules.

Feats

Build-defining options.

Perfect Sheathe

When you reduce a hostile creature to 0 HP, you may spend 2 Concentration as a reaction to sheathe Yamato in one perfect motion: each hostile within 10 ft that witnessed it makes a Wis save, becoming Frightened of you until the end of your next turn on a failure.

Rapid Slash

After using Trick, your first Yamato attack this turn may cleave into a second creature within 10 ft of the first: split the attack's damage dice evenly between them, rolling one attack roll against each.

Summoned Sword Master

You may spend 2 unspent Mirage Blades as a bonus action to weave them into a spinning guard: until the start of your next turn, ranged attacks against you have disadvantage and you gain +2 AC against them.

Dark Slayer

While you are at 10 Concentration, when you are targeted by an attack you may use your reaction to spend 1 CE and Trick up to 30 ft before the attack resolves; the attack misses if it can no longer reach you. If you reappear within 10 ft of the attacker, you may make one Yamato attack against them as part of the same reaction.

Rules integration

Edge cases and shared systems.

Shared rulings: Technique DC = 8 + PB + CE ability modifier. Direct rolled attacks may crit and use the campaign Black Flash procedure; autonomous hazards and Domain events cannot. Maximum Output may modify ordinary supported features, never named Maxima/Domains. Two-technique characters use the campaign's agreed character-level progression.
