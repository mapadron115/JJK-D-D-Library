Core law

What the technique actually does.

Core Engine, Concentration. The engine in full: Concentration from 0 to 10: gained by hitting cleanly, by perfect evasion or a parry that reduces melee damage to 0, or by a full turn of slow deliberate movement; lost 2 per hit, all of it to fear or incapacitation. Many abilities scale at 5 and 10. Guard the mind, and the blade follows. Begin at 0 Concentration (max 10). Gain 1 when you hit without taking damage since your last turn; when you perfectly evade (an attack misses you or you succeed on a Dexterity save against an effect) or parry (you use your reaction to reduce melee damage to 0); or when you spend a full turn moving no more than half speed while taking no action or bonus action. Lose 2 when hit; lose all if Frightened or Incapacitated. Many abilities scale at 5/10 Concentration.

Field notes. Subject: a cursed blade that answers only to an unbroken mind. Three truths govern him. First, Concentration is the technique’s true body, it begins at 0 (max 10), rises when he hits without being hit, when he perfectly evades or parries, or when he spends a full turn moving slowly and striking nothing; it falls by 2 when he is hit, and vanishes entirely if he is Frightened or Incapacitated. Second, the Yamato’s spatial slashes are not swung, they are decided: ranged cuts, teleports, and mirage blades all scale at 5 and 10 Concentration. Third, his power punishes hesitation and rewards the untouched: being hit costs him more than missing ever could. Hence the maxim: the blade goes quiet the instant the hand holding it shakes.

Pinnacle expression

Maximum, Domain, or equivalent.

The Maximum is the cut aimed at distance itself: 10 CE, action, a 180-ft line, Dex save, 12d10 force or slashing; barriers take maximum dice and make a stability check. Note the honest boundary, printed in the margin: it cuts space, not concepts, souls, or game rules. Even the Yamato has a jurisdiction.
