Recovered printed values & campaign clarifications

At-the-table character reference

Extinguishment: action, 1 CE; one visible creature within 60 feet makes a Charisma save. It takes 2d12 radiant damage at levels 1–5 or 5d12 radiant damage at level 6 and beyond. Failure also suppresses spellcasting, CE expenditure, Innate Technique activation, and Technique Focus for up to 1 minute; repeat the save as described in the revised ruling. Success means half damage and no suppression.

Maximum: Jacob’s Ladder: action, 10 CE; point within 200 feet, 10-foot-radius area; Charisma save for 10d12 radiant damage and the revised nullification effect, or half damage and no nullification on success.

Completed rules

  • Extinguishment: action, 1 CE, one perceived creature within 60 feet. Charisma save; failure takes 2d12 radiant damage at levels 1–5 or 5d12 radiant damage from level 6 onward and cannot cast spells, spend CE, activate an Innate Technique, or maintain Technique Focus for up to 1 minute. Success takes half and no suppression. Repeat the save at the end of each turn. You may suppress any number of targets, but same-name effects do not stack.
  • At level 6, worn and carried magic/cursed items are also suppressed while the creature remains suppressed. Passive numerical bonuses, spells, charges, and attunement benefits switch off; the items remain intact and attuned. Artifacts are suppressed for only one round at a time unless their lore says otherwise.
  • Angel’s Blessing: bonus action, 1 CE, 1 minute. Flight is half walking speed. At the end, pay 1 CE with no action to extend another minute. Passing through a barrier costs 5 feet of movement; this works on curtains and spell barriers whose level is no greater than PB. A barrier explicitly keyed against Technique Extermination calls for a CE ability check against its DC.
  • Jacob’s Ladder: choose a point within 200 feet; 10-foot radius, every creature except you, Charisma save. Failure takes 10d12 radiant and Extinguishment for up to 2 minutes; success half and no suppression. Repeat at end of each turn. Undead, Cursed Spirits, and creatures the campaign explicitly treats as malevolent make the initial save with disadvantage.
  • Barrier/object mode: action, 6 CE, choose one visible magic/cursed item, tool, curse anchor, or barrier within 200 feet. Make a CE attack against its creator’s save DC, the item’s DC, or 15 if none. On hit, ordinary targets are destroyed and their dependent curse ends. Artifacts and Special Grade targets are unsealed or suppressed for 1 minute and are destroyed only if their own lore permits; this technique counts as a valid lore-level counter, not an automatic “plot item is immune.”
  • Maximum Output increases Jacob’s radius to 30 feet and prevents Evasion-like zero-damage results, as printed. Domain — Choir of Extinction: at initiative 20 every chosen hostile automatically takes 8d10 radiant damage and has its Innate Technique and CE-granted features suppressed until the next initiative 20. A protected creature makes a Charisma save, taking half damage and avoiding suppression on a success. Choir of Extinction is an autonomous purge, not Jacob’s Ladder; ordinary Ladder activations retain their saves and durations.

Power identity

Why Tier IV: expected play attacks other characters’ defining systems and bypasses barrier-based adventures. Its counters are saves, range, and action economy—not pretending nullification is ordinary damage.

Recovered character-build options

Innate Technique Feats 3

These are optional character-building feats. You must meet each listed prerequisite before selecting it.

Blessed Blow

High impact

Prerequisite: Extinguishment

Once per turn after an attack hits, spend 2–PB CE. Add 2d12 radiant for the first 2 CE and 1d12 for each further CE. The rider is not doubled by the critical hit.

Archangel’s Present

Pinnacle

Prerequisite: Angel’s Blessing and Breakthrough

Choose any Blessing from the campaign’s Dungeon Master reference when the breakthrough occurs and record its complete rules. A custom Blessing of comparable scope is also valid with the GM.

Sword of Michael

Pinnacle

Prerequisite: Maximum: Jacob’s Ladder and CE score 20

Action: pay Jacob’s Ladder’s normal cost +5 CE and make one melee CE attack. On hit the creature alone suffers 10d12 radiant damage and the revised Jacob’s Ladder technique-nullification effect; on miss all CE is spent. It does not create the original area.

Technique Extermination
This Innate Technique bestows its user with the gift of angelic
power, capable of smiting those they considered as ‘villains’ and
hampering the user of a particular target’s cursed techniques.
Cursed Energy
Starting at 1st level, you can harness your
negative feelings into a usable source of energy,
called cursed energy. These feelings can be
harnessed into attacks or abilities. The number of
cursed energy you possess is based on your class,
some classes use a modifier that being charisma or
wisdom (Player’s choice)
Your cursed energy DC is 8 + user cursed energy
ability modifier + proficiency bonus.
Extinguishment
Beginning at 1st level, you have gained use of the
essentials of your technique. As an action for one
cursed energy, you may target a creature as after a
trumpet of light appears, a beam of light falls
from the sky. The target must succeed a Charisma
save, if the creature fails it takes 2d12, and
loses the ability to use spell casting or Cursed
Energy abilities. On a success, they take half as
much damage and don't have their cursed energy
features or Spellcasting abilities negated. They
may remake the saving throw at the beginning of
their turns, ending the effect early on a success.
Angel’s Blessing
At 3rd level, your technique makes you feel like a divine being
made to help others, you are a true angel. As a bonus action for 1
cursed energy, you can gain flying speed equal to half your walking
speed for 1 minute, as wings of light emerge from your back and a
halo stands on top of your head. While you have the wings active, you
can freely move between barriers such as curtains without breaking
them. You may also do that with magic walls and barriers that were
cast with a spell slot equal to or lower than your proficiency bonus.
When said minute ends, you can spend another 1 more cursed energy to
keep your fly speed for another minute.
In addition, due to your holy nature you gain resistance to radiant
damage.
Improved Extinguishment
Once you reach 6th level, the damage dice increase to 5d12. Magic
items held or worn by the creature also lose their abilities.
Maximum Technique: Jacob’s Ladder
When you reach the 11th level, you have created an extension to your
technique, one so powerful that you can burn your opponents as a
radiant light falls upon them. As an action for 10 cursed energy, you
summon a trumpet of light in your hands, blowing into it as you
choose a point within 200 feet of you. A beam of light strikes down
in a 10-foot radius circle centered on the point you choose, every
creature in said area other than you has to make a Charisma saving
throw. On a failure, they take 10d12 radiant damage and suffer the
same effects as Technique Extinguishment for 2 minutes. On a success,
they take half as much damage and do not receive the side effects.
Undead and creatures of evil alignment make this saving throw with
disadvantage.
You may also use this technique as a way to destroy barriers and
cursed objects. As an action for 6 cursed energy, you can summon a
trumpet of light and blow into it, making a beam of light targeting a
cursed object, cursed item, cursed tool, magic item, or any kind of
barrier created by cursed energy or magic. Make a cursed energy
attack roll against the cursed energy or spell save DC, on a hit you
will destroy your target, removing any curses that were affecting
creatures because of it or removing creatures imprisoned inside.
At 13th level, if you use this technique with Maximum Output, its
beam of light range increases
to a 30-foot circle. Also, it
will ignore features such as
evasion that reduce damage.
Domain Expansion: Garden of Eden
Finally, 18th level, your
technique is honed to the
finest of edges. At Initiative
20 all creatures suffer all the
effects of Jacob’s Ladder.
