Technique Extermination

Radiant extinguishment suppresses spellcasting, CE, items, barriers, curses, and eventually entire areas through Jacob’s Ladder.

Field notes. Subject: the choir that sings things out of existence. Three truths govern her. First: her light does not illuminate, it refuses. Extinguishment is an action and a coin: a Charisma save, and on failure the victim cannot cast, cannot spend cursed energy, cannot raise their innate technique, cannot hold focus, for up to a minute. Second: the refusal deepens, at the sixth rank the victim's worn and carried items go dark as well, artifacts only briefly; at the eleventh the trumpet sounds over an area, Jacob's Ladder, and with Maximum Output its radius triples while Evasion-like escapes are denied. Third: the Domain is an autonomous purge, not the Ladder but the Choir of Extinction: at initiative twenty, chosen hostiles take 8d10 radiant and lose their innate technique and CE-granted features until the next pulse. Assess her correctly: she does not out-damage sorcerers. She unmakes the conditions under which they are sorcerers.

Pinnacle expression

Maximum, Domain, or equivalent.

Jacob’s Ladder: The trumpet: an action and ten coins summon a trumpet of light in your hands; choose a point within two hundred feet. A ten-foot radius where every creature but you makes a Charisma save: failure is 10d12 radiant and Extinguishment for up to two minutes, success half and no refusal. The save repeats at the end of each turn. The undead, Cursed Spirits, and creatures the campaign treats as malevolent make the initial save at disadvantage.

Barrier/object mode: The trumpet, aimed at things: an action and six coins against one visible item, tool, anchor, or barrier within two hundred feet — a cursed-energy attack against its DC (15 if it has none). On a hit the ordinary is destroyed and its dependent curse ends. Artifacts and Special Grades are unsealed or suppressed for a minute, destroyed only where their lore permits; this technique counts as a valid lore-level counter, not an automatic “plot item is immune.”

Rules integration

Edge cases and shared systems.

Rules priority: the reviewed rulings, explicit codex clarifications, and the level progression always control when original wording conflicts. Legacy Domain auto-hit language, action terminology, event timing, missing saves, and older scaling never override revised player rules.
Why Tier IV: expected play attacks other characters’ defining systems and bypasses barrier-based adventures. Its counters are saves, range, and action economy—not pretending nullification is ordinary damage.
Appendix

Additional material.

Technique Extermination
This Innate Technique bestows its user with the gift of angelic
power, capable of smiting those they considered as ‘villains’ and
hampering the user of a particular target’s cursed techniques.
Cursed Energy
Starting at 1st level, you can harness your
negative feelings into a usable source of energy,
called cursed energy. These feelings can be
harnessed into attacks or abilities. The number of
cursed energy you possess is based on your class,
some classes use a modifier that being charisma or
wisdom (Player’s choice)
Your cursed energy DC is 8 + user cursed energy
ability modifier + proficiency bonus.
Extinguishment
Beginning at 1st level, you have gained use of the
essentials of your technique. As an action for one
cursed energy, you may target a creature as after a
trumpet of light appears, a beam of light falls
from the sky. The target must succeed a Charisma
save, if the creature fails it takes 2d12, and
loses the ability to use spell casting or Cursed
Energy abilities. On a success, they take half as
much damage and don't have their cursed energy
features or Spellcasting abilities negated. They
may remake the saving throw at the beginning of
their turns, ending the effect early on a success.
Angel’s Blessing
At 3rd level, your technique makes you feel like a divine being
made to help others, you are a true angel. As a bonus action for 1
cursed energy, you can gain flying speed equal to half your walking
speed for 1 minute, as wings of light emerge from your back and a
halo stands on top of your head. While you have the wings active, you
can freely move between barriers such as curtains without breaking
them. You may also do that with magic walls and barriers that were
cast with a spell slot equal to or lower than your proficiency bonus.
When said minute ends, you can spend another 1 more cursed energy to
keep your fly speed for another minute.
In addition, due to your holy nature you gain resistance to radiant
damage.
Improved Extinguishment
Once you reach 6th level, the damage dice increase to 5d12. Magic
items held or worn by the creature also lose their abilities.
Domain Expansion: Garden of Eden

Trumpets sound from no visible throat. The barrier becomes a dawn that never fully arrives, white-gold light pouring down in columns between drifting feathers. The ground is white stone, and every shadow cast upon it looks like something being unmade.

Finally, 18th level, your
technique is honed to the
finest of edges. At Initiative
20 all creatures suffer all the
effects of Jacob’s Ladder.
