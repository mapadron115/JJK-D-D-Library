Core law

What the technique actually does.

Rules are written to plug into the finalized campaign glossary: normal actions stay normal, direct attacks roll, and only named Domain events bypass the initial roll.

Passive

Blade Lifesteal

Once on each direct melee hit that deals HP damage, regain HP equal to one Scarlet Die, maximum healing per turn equal to PB × Scarlet Die maximum. You cannot heal above half maximum HP from this feature until level 11.

On technique hit

Rot Mark

Certain features add Rot Marks, maximum 3. At 3 marks the target contracts Scarlet Rot for 1 minute and marks reset to 0.

Condition-like technique effect

Scarlet Rot

At start of each turn, take 1 Scarlet Die + CE modifier necrotic/poison damage and healing received is halved. At end of each turn make Constitution save; two total successes end it. Fire damage grants advantage on the next save. A creature can suffer only one Scarlet Rot instance from you.

Blade Lifesteal

Gain Lifesteal, Rot Marks, and Scarlet Rot.

Flowing Sword

When you make at least one melee attack on your turn, gain +10 feet movement and may move through a hostile creature’s threatened space without extra cost; you still provoke normally unless another feature prevents it.

Scarlet Thrust

Replace one attack · 1 CE: on hit add one Scarlet Die piercing and one Rot Mark.

Kick of the Severed

Bonus action after melee hit: Strength save or push 10 feet; spend 1 CE to knock prone too.

Aeonian Phantom

Reaction · 2 CE when targeted: impose disadvantage on one attack and move 10 feet after it resolves, hit or miss.

6Waterfowl DanceWaterfowl Dance and 2 more features

Waterfowl Dance

Action · 5 CE: make up to PB melee attacks while moving up to your speed, no more than two against the same creature. You do not provoke during this movement. Each hit after the first deals one fewer weapon base die minimum one, but all can critical and Lifesteal.

Rotting Edge

Once per turn, a melee critical automatically adds one Rot Mark without CE.

Unalloyed Pride

Advantage on saves against your own Scarlet Rot backlash and effects that would charm or dominate you.

11First Scarlet AeoniaScarlet Aeonia and 2 more features

Scarlet Aeonia

Action · 8 CE: leap to a point within 60 feet and bloom in a 20-foot radius. Creatures make Dex saves for 6 Scarlet Dice + CE modifier; failure also gains 2 Rot Marks. The flower remains until end of next turn; entering/starting there adds 1 Rot Mark on failed Con save.

Lifesteal Unbound

Blade Lifesteal may heal you to full maximum HP.

Scarlet Wings

Bonus action · 4 CE · Focus · 1 minute: gain 40-foot flying speed and once per turn a melee hit adds +1 Scarlet Die.

18Second Bloom — Goddess of RotGoddess Form and 2 more features

Goddess Form

When reduced to 0 HP, once per long rest you may bloom instead of falling unconscious: regain half max HP, activate Scarlet Wings without cost, and create Scarlet Aeonia centered on yourself. Gain one permanent Bloom count.

Domain Expansion

Unlock Haligtree of the Third Bloom if you have at least one Bloom count.

Scarlet Phantoms

Action · 6 CE: create PB phantom slashes. Make PB melee CE attacks against creatures within 90 feet; each hit deals 2 Scarlet Dice and one Rot Mark, but cannot Lifesteal.

20Third BloomTrue Goddess and 2 more features

True Goddess

When your third lifetime Bloom occurs (or a campaign milestone approved by the GM), your technique permanently reaches True Goddess state. Scarlet Die remains d12; you become immune to poison/disease and resistant to necrotic.

Aeonia Without Collapse

Scarlet Aeonia costs 6 CE and no longer requires you to land in the bloom; you may reappear in any open space at its edge.

Rot Is Rebirth

Once per turn when Scarlet Rot damages a hostile creature, gain temporary HP equal PB. This is not healing and does not trigger Lifesteal caps.

Pinnacle expression

Domain, Maximum, or equivalent.

Not every technique needs a Domain. When one exists, its sure-hit is a unique autonomous initiative-count-20 event unless the Domain is explicitly rule-based.

Haligtree of the Third Bloom

Level 18 · Full-turn action · 20 CE · 60-foot radius · 1 minute. The barrier becomes pale roots, red water, and endless butterfly wings.

Initiative 20 — Aeonian Bloom: a scarlet flower opens beneath each chosen hostile. Unprotected targets automatically take 5d12 necrotic + 3d12 poison and gain two Rot Marks. A protected target makes a Constitution save; success halves damage and gains no marks.

At initiative 20, you also heal one Scarlet Die per hostile damaged, maximum PB dice. This Domain healing is separate from Blade Lifesteal.

Rules integration

Edge cases and shared systems.

These rulings are designed around the revised Player Guide rather than the raw PDF where the two differ.

Lifesteal requires actual HP damage; hitting barriers, temp HP only, or immune targets does not heal unless HP is lost.
Scarlet Rot damage is ongoing and cannot Black Flash.
Goddess Form is not ordinary RCT and can function even if RCT is unavailable, unless the effect specifically prevents revival/transformation.
Bloom count is narrative-permanent and should not be farmed by staged self-knockouts.
Technique DC: 8 + proficiency bonus + your chosen Cursed Energy ability modifier (Wisdom or Charisma unless your class says otherwise).
Direct criticals: rolled weapon, unarmed, and CE attacks can critical normally. After a qualifying critical, roll the campaign's unmodified Black Flash confirmation d20; a natural 20 triples the completed critical damage. Autonomous Domain events, damage-over-time, hazards, and unrolled pulses cannot Black Flash.
Maximum Output: unless a feature says otherwise, double that feature's base CE cost and enhance one supported axis—damage, range, duration, or accuracy/DC—using the campaign's Maximum Output rules. Named Maximum Techniques and Domains do not receive generic Maximum Output.
Technique Focus: effects marked Focus occupy the shared Technique Focus slot. Opening a Domain ends other Focus effects unless the Domain expressly preserves them.
Two-technique characters: these guides assume each technique progresses from character/class level as agreed by the campaign. Multiclassing itself does not grant another Innate Technique.
