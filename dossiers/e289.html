Core law

At-the-table character reference

Recovered printed values & campaign clarifications

One Man Army · level 1:

as an action for 3 cursed energy, you can create a clone within 5 feet of yourself. The clone lasts 8 hours and is completely indistinguishable from you in every way. A clone has 1/4 of your hit point maximum (rounded up), your AC, and access to all your class features and cursed techniques — but not your innate technique features. Any cursed energy a clone spends is treated as if you spent it. Clones act on your turn but can make only one attack on their turn, even if you have Extra Attack or bonus-action attacks; you can replace any of your attacks with an attack from a clone's space. Clones do not have any of your items.

Twice the Fun · level 3:

you can now create multiple clones as an action by spending 3 CE for each clone. When you make a melee attack against a creature while a clone is within 5 feet of the target, you gain advantage on the attack roll.

Clone cap:

you can have a number of clones equal to your proficiency bonus at once. When a clone dies, the number of clones you can have is reduced by 1 for 1 minute.

Core law

Completed rules

Three is a Crowd · level 6:

your clones can now make as many attacks as you can, and have 1/2 of your total hit points. When one of your clones is reduced to 0 hit points, you regain 1 cursed energy.

Four is a Party · level 11:

you have mastered the ability to choose which of your clones is your actual body. Your clones have hit points equal to yours, and you can swap bodies with one of your clones as a bonus action for 1 CE — trading hit points and causing that clone to become your new body. When you are reduced to 0 hit points or killed outright while you have a clone active, you can instead transfer yourself into one of your clones, gaining the clone's current hit points.

Clone-spawned clones:

your clones can now create one clone each by spending your cursed energy, with each clone counting against your limit.

Full progression

Feature map

1One Man Army.

Starting at 1st level, you can spend 3 CE as an action to create a clone within 5 feet of yourself for 8 hours. Clones are completely indistinguishable from you in every way. A clone has 1/4 of your hit point maximum (rounded up), your AC, and access to all your class features and cursed techniques, but not innate technique features. Any cursed energy a clone spends is treated as if you spent it.

Your clones act on your turn, but can only make one attack on their turn even if you have Extra Attack or bonus action attacks. You can replace any of your attacks on your turn with an attack from the clone's space. Your clones do not have any of your items. You can have a number of clones equal to your proficiency bonus at once, and when a clone dies, the number of clones you can have is reduced by 1 for 1 minute.

3Twice the Fun.

At 3rd level, you can now create multiple clones as an action by spending 3 CE for each clone. When you make a melee attack against a creature while a clone is within 5 feet of the target, you gain advantage on the attack roll.

6Three is a Crowd.

At 6th level you have become more skillful and efficient with the creation of your clones. Your clones can now make as many attacks as you and have 1/2 of your total hit points. When one of your clones is reduced to 0 hit points, you regain 1 cursed energy.

11Four is a Party.

At 11th level, you have mastered the ability to choose which of your clones is your actual body. Your clones have hit points equal to yours and you can swap bodies with one of your clones as a bonus action for 1 CE, trading hit points and causing that clone to become your new body. When you are reduced to 0 hit points or killed outright while you have a clone active, you can instead transfer yourself into one of your clones, gaining the clone's current hit points.

Your clones can now create one clone by spending your cursed energy, with each clone counting against your limit.

Pinnacle expression

Maximum, Domain, or equivalent.

Domain Expansion: Divide and Conquer: 18 — At 18th level you have perfected your technique, allowing you to expand your domain. You can now create one clone as a bonus action without spending cursed energy once per turn (your clones do not gain this benefit). Within your domain, you can have an unlimited number of clones, and your clones' attacks are guaranteed hits within your domain. At initiative 20, an additional clone appears. All clones created with your domain disappear when the domain ends.

Innate Technique Feats

Build-defining options.

These are optional character-building feats. You must meet each listed prerequisite before selecting it. V.6 prints no feats for Clone; the four below are codex-authored fills grounded in the printed kit (see appendix).

Prerequisite: Must have One Man Army

Many Hands

Your clones can deliver your techniques as well as your strikes. When you use a cursed technique with a range of touch, you can originate it from the space of one of your clones within 60 feet of you instead of your own space.

Prerequisite: Must have Twice the Fun

Shell Game

Your enemies can never be sure which body is you. As a bonus action, you can swap positions with one of your clones within 60 feet of you.

Prerequisite: Must have Four is a Party

Shared Burden

You have learned to trade more than bodies and hit points. As a bonus action, you can transfer one condition affecting you — blinded, charmed, deafened, frightened, poisoned, or restrained — to one of your clones within 30 feet of you; the clone suffers the condition instead.

Prerequisite: Must have One Man Army

Long Leash

Your duplication is no longer confined to arm's reach. You can create clones within 30 feet of yourself instead of 5 feet.

Rules integration

Edge cases and shared systems.

Not Body Clone: this is the standalone V.6 Clone technique — a full 1–18 progression. It is not the "Body Clone" feature of V.3 Body Manipulation. The two are different sources with different mechanics and do not stack or convert; a character selects one or the other.
Shared cursed energy: clones draw from your cursed energy pool — any CE a clone spends is treated as if you spent it. Clone-spawned clones likewise spend your CE and count against your proficiency-bonus cap.
Clone cap: you can maintain a number of clones equal to your proficiency bonus at once; when a clone dies, your cap drops by 1 for 1 minute. The domain's unlimited clones are a domain-granted exception and vanish when the domain ends.
Domain conversion: Divide and Conquer is adopted with campaign conversion: the initiative-20 clone arrival stands as the sure-hit event; clones' guaranteed hits apply to attacks made inside the domain; clash via the point system; the bonus-action free clone costs the user's bonus action once per turn.
Rules priority: the printed V.6 values and the clarifications above always control when interpretations conflict. The four Innate Technique Feats are codex-authored fills, marked as such, and may be removed or replaced by the GM without affecting the printed kit.
