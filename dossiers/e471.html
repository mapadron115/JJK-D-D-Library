Exact player-facing procedures

Cursed edge

The staff's arithmetic starts Special Grade, +4 to attack and damage on every swing. You have a +4 bonus to attack and damage rolls made with this cursed tool.

Cloud guard

And it defends while it offends: +1 to AC while wielded. Three sections spinning are as much curtain as cudgel. While wielding this weapon, you gain a +1 bonus to AC.

Triple-weighted strike

The signature clause: on a hit, add three times your total attack bonus, ability modifier, proficiency bonus, and the staff's +4: to the damage. The better your aim, the heavier the landing. On a hit, you gain a bonus to the damage roll equal to 3 times your attack roll bonus with this weapon (your ability modifier + your proficiency bonus + this weapon's +4 enhancement bonus).

Field notes. Subject: a three-section staff that pays interest on commitment. Three truths. First: the baseline is Special Grade, +4 to strike and wound on every swing. Second: it guards as it strikes, +1 to armor class while wielded, the spinning sections a curtain as much as a weapon. Third, the signature arithmetic: on a hit, the damage gains a bonus equal to three times your total attack bonus, ability modifier, proficiency, and the staff's own +4, all tripled. Consider what this prices: the stronger the wielder's accuracy, the heavier the Cloud hits, precision is leverage here, not just consistency. The staff is a gambler's cudgel: it doubles down on the hand you brought. Bring a good attack bonus, and it will make it three times as good.

Campaign canon notes

Attack roll bonus: the source's "3 times your attack roll bonus" is read as the total bonus added to the attack roll, ability modifier, proficiency bonus, and the weapon's +4. The triple bonus applies to each hit's damage, including the +4 already added.
