Core law

Completed rules

First hit and reads:

the first hit gives a mark; the 1 CE read is still required for active foresight, avoiding an unprinted free permanent buff. Read one creature per activation; each read expires at the beginning of the caster’s next turn. Marks do not cost additional CE per hit and the printed low damage is preserved.

Six-second disclosure:

at 6 seconds disclose immediately when the reading starts at that boundary; renewing later does not travel backward to obtain an earlier decision. The GM states intended actions, targets and route; an intervening death, blocked route or changed battlefield permits a revised legal plan, with the active reader informed when that change becomes apparent. Intent disclosure is useful information, not an instruction forcing the enemy to execute an impossible plan.

Highlighted Panels scope:

applies to all damaging saves from the read creature, not only Dexterity. Require an active read for the 2/3-second benefits.

Forced criticals:

forced criticals keep the explicit Black Flash exclusion; naturally scored qualifying criticals follow normal confirmation. Precognition’s attack range and GG’s +1 stack as distinct listed bonuses.

Greater Foresight:

replaces two dice with three, never adds a fourth die to another advantage source.

GG True Warstaff:

adds one native weapon die and replaces the normal one-mark gain with two on a critical, subject to the mark cap.

Omniscient Reader:

the replacement affects one d20 test, declared after the roll before consequences, uses the recorded face plus the target’s normal modifiers, and expires at next caster turn start. Reader has no CE cost printed; retain zero CE. It neither replaces a multi-die damage roll with a d20 nor produces a naturally rolled confirmation.

Storyboard Dominion:

virtual six-second marks override the normal PB mark ceiling while inside. Initiative 20 grants the full cumulative read against eligible creatures without another 1 CE per target, lasting to the next initiative 20 or Domain end. Outside the Domain retain only the creature’s actual hit-earned marks, never six free permanent marks. Protected creatures are excluded from the Domain’s virtual marks and reading while anti-domain protection holds; previously earned marks remain available to the ordinary technique. No generic damage sure-hit is added to an information Domain; ordinary Warstaff attacks still roll with the applicable advantages.

The fiction. The prevailing theory holds that the G Warstaff is not a weapon that predicts the future but a pen that inks it — the user's cursed energy treats time as a page, and the marks are panels the enemy is already drawn into. Six seconds of foresight is not the sorcerer seeing ahead; it is the sorcerer's perception outrunning their body, sketching the next moments so densely in CE that the world reads them as already true. Registry scholars classify it as the only technique in the archive whose Domain is a storyboard — because for its user, the fight was always a comic, and they were always the one holding the pen.

Full progression

Feature map

L1Warstaff

As a bonus action for 2 cursed energy, you create an oversized pen-like cursed tool. This cursed tool has the reach and two-handed properties and you can use your CE modifier for its attack and damage rolls. On a hit it deals 1d4 + your CE modifier piercing damage and counts as a grade 4 cursed tool and can be used with martial arts die. You can dismiss the Warstaff at will and can only have one created at a time.

When you hit a creature with G Warstaff, you fulfill the condition for ink and attach a manga panel to the target, being able to view 1 second into the target’s future. Hitting a target more than once adds another 1 second to the total, making you gain the benefits of the earlier seconds and the current. As a reaction or bonus action for 1 cursed energy, you can view that creature’s future again and gain the following foresights until the beginning of your next turn depending on the creature’s marks. You cannot use this while blinded.

1 Second — the creature’s attack rolls against you are made at disadvantage. 2 Seconds — you have advantage on saving throws from that creature. 3 Seconds — you have advantage on attack rolls against that creature. 4 Seconds — you know the next actions of the creature (the DM tells you at the start of their turn). 5 Seconds — you know the next actions of the creature (the DM tells you at the end of your turn). 6 Seconds — you know the next actions of the creature (the DM tells you at the beginning of your turn).

You can only see into a number of seconds into a creature’s future equal to your proficiency bonus. The number of creatures that can be affected by this technique is equal to your CE modifier, and you can end the technique at will or it ends when the combat ends.

L3Highlighted Panels

seeing 2 seconds into a creature’s future: half damage on a failed save, no damage on a success, against saving throws caused by that creature.

L6Predictable Strikes

once per turn, turn a Warstaff hit into a critical against a creature you can see 3 seconds into the future. No Black Flash roll from this feature.

L11Precognition

advantage from your technique expands your critical range by half your CE modifier (rounded down); disadvantage your technique imposes expands the enemy’s critical fail range by the same amount.

L18Storyboard Dominion

Domain Expansion. All creatures in your domain count as having 6 seconds marked, and at initiative 20 you can see 6 seconds into the future of all creatures. Virtual six-second marks override the normal PB mark ceiling while inside; the initiative-20 read costs no extra CE and lasts to the next initiative 20 or Domain end.

Innate Technique Feats

Build-defining options.

These are optional character-building feats. You must meet each listed prerequisite before selecting it.

Prerequisite: Must have Predictable Strikes

Greater Foresight

Your foresight spills over to those you fight beside. As a reaction for 1 CE when a creature you can see within 30 ft makes an attack roll, ability check, or saving throw, you can call out what you have already seen and grant them advantage on the roll. You can use this a number of times equal to your CE modifier per long rest.

Prerequisite: Must have Precognition

True Warstaff: Full Spread

You have learned to unfold your Warstaff into a full manga spread. As an action for 4 CE, you can drive the Warstaff into the ground at a point within 30 ft, projecting a standing panel that fills a 15 ft cube for 1 minute. Creatures inside the panel count as marked by your technique, and you can read their futures without spending the usual cursed energy.

Prerequisite: Must have Storyboard Dominion

Maximum: Omniscient Reader’s Viewpoint

You have gained the ability to not only see, but also manipulate the contents of your manga panel. At the beginning of your turn, you can choose a creature that you can see that is marked by 6 seconds and roll a d20. Record the roll. As a reaction, you can replace one roll that creature makes with the result of that roll. At the beginning of your turn, you lose any unused rolls.

Prerequisite: Must have Precognition

GG True Warstaff

You have created a more powerful version of your G Warstaff, being a true weapon of destruction. As a bonus action while you have G Warstaff active, you can spend 4 cursed energy to evolve it into its true form, GG True Warstaff. It gains the following benefits for 1 minute: ●​ It deals one additional damage dice ●​ Its reach is 15ft instead of 10ft ●​ When you score a critical hit against a creature, you gain 2 seconds instead of 1 ●​ It gains a +1 to critical range

Rules integration

Edge cases and shared systems.

Rules priority: the reviewed rulings, explicit codex clarifications, and the level progression always control when original wording conflicts. Legacy Domain auto-hit language, action terminology, event timing, missing saves, and older scaling never override revised player rules.
Why Tier III: the Warstaff is a weapon-master striker whose power is information and crit-fishing, not raw damage; the printed low damage is preserved and the Domain is an information Domain with no generic damage sure-hit.
Minimum-one targets: a target cap at nonpositive CE modifier needs minimum-one wording if such a character is allowed; do not silently grant unlimited targets.
Reader and criticals: the Reader’s replacement uses the recorded face, never a naturally rolled confirmation; expanded critical failure applies to attack tests and existing explicit critical-test rules, not automatic failure on every skill or save.

Highlighted Panels
 At 3rd level, with the power of reading into the future, no one
surprises you anymore. As long as you can see 2 seconds into a
creature's future, you take half damage on a failure, and no damage
on a success against saving throws caused by that creature.

Predictable Strikes
 At 6th level, your opponents become extremely easy to read after
being affected by your technique, and you learned how to exploit
that. Once per turn when you hit an attack using G Warstaff against a
creature that you can see 3 seconds into the future, you can turn
that attack into a critical. You cannot roll for a black flash using
this feature.

Precognition
 At 11th level, you can glimpse your own future, knowing exactly what
to do and when. When you roll with advantage against a creature
because of your technique, your critical range is increased by half
your CE modifier (rounded down). Whenever a creature rolls with
disadvantage against you because of your technique, the roll’s
critical fail range is increased by half of your CE modifier (rounded
down). For example, if you have a +4 CE modifier and they roll a
natural 3 on their roll, it counts as a critical failure.

Domain Expansion: Storyboard Dominion
 At 18th level, you have mastered your technique allowing you to
expand your domain. All creatures in your domain count as having 6
seconds marked and at initiative 20, you can see 6 seconds into the
future of all creatures.

Greater Foresight
Prerequisite: Must have Predictable Strikes
 Your foresight ability has reached even greater potential, reaching
the point some consider enlightened. Whenever you have advantage
because of your technique, you can roll 3 dice instead of 2 and keep
the highest roll. Also, whenever a creature has disadvantage on
attack rolls against you because of your technique, they roll 3 dice
instead of 2 and keep the lowest roll.
