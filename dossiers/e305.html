Tutankhamun’s Meteoric Iron Dagger

Metaphysical Law: Matter born outside the local cursed-energy ecology is only partially subject to its rules.

FormDagger
EngineForeign Matter
Attunement1 hour*

Field notes. Subject: an immigrant weapon. Three truths govern the dagger. First, what the system cannot recognize, it cannot stop: the star-fallen iron ignores up to 4 points of AC granted by Cursed Technique, CE reinforcement, cursed armor, or barrier, the local cursed-energy ecology simply has no papers for it. Second, it cuts the system's own flesh best: an extra 3d8 force against barriers, shikigami, constructs, and technique-made objects, and a Starfall Cut, 3 CE once per turn, that adds 6d8 force on a hit, rolled as maximum damage against barriers and constructs. Third, foreignness is the price of admission: divination hunts the dagger at disadvantage, and it can neither store CE nor serve a Domain, naturalize it and you lose what made it dangerous. Mark the boundary the book draws: it bypasses reinforcement; it does not unmake technique. The immigrant cuts. It does not conquer.

Features & procedures

Properties & techniques

Exact player-facing procedures

Relic profile

1d6 piercing · Finesse, Light · +4 to attack and damage rolls

Jujutsu origin

The historical blade's meteoritic iron becomes the key jujutsu fact. Its material entered Earth's cursed-energy environment from outside it, so barriers and reinforcement do not fully 'recognize' the metal when deciding what should be stopped.

Foreign Matter

When attacking a creature whose AC is increased by a Cursed Technique, CE reinforcement, cursed armor, or barrier, ignore up to 4 points of that magical/CE-derived AC bonus.

Barrier Shear

The dagger deals an additional 3d8 force damage to barriers, shikigami, cursed constructs, and objects created by a Cursed Technique.

Starfall Cut — 3 CE, once per turn

On a hit, deal an additional 6d8 force damage. If the target is a barrier or cursed construct, roll those 6d8 as maximum damage instead of rolling.

Unfamiliar Signature

Divination or detection effects trying to identify the dagger's cursed-energy signature make their check at disadvantage.

Binding Vow — No Native Circuit

The dagger cannot store CE and cannot be used as a battery or focus for a Domain. Its anti-barrier qualities come from remaining foreign to the system.

Designer’s note. It bypasses reinforcement; it does not nullify techniques like the Inverted Spear of Heaven.

Rules integration

Cursed-tool attunement

Campaign canon

Cursed-tool attunement: you have PB attunement slots (2–6). This tool occupies one slot and requires a one-hour ritual. Attunement ends voluntarily over a short rest, on death, or when you replace it while over capacity. Distance does not break attunement. Cursed-tool attunement is separate from magic-item attunement.
