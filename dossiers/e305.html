Tutankhamun’s Meteoric Iron Dagger

Metaphysical Law: matter born outside the world is not fully recognized by the world’s cursed-energy rules. Star-fallen metal forged before ordinary iron belonged to Egypt — barrier reinforcement and CE-hardening are treated as one grade weaker against the blade.

Features & procedures

Properties & techniques

Exact player-facing procedures

Star-Fallen Iron

Barrier reinforcement and CE-hardening are treated as one grade weaker against the blade.

Anchor severing

The dagger gains advantage on checks made to damage or sever barrier anchors.

Foreign to the system

It does not nullify techniques; it simply interacts with them as if it were partly foreign to the system enforcing them.

Rules integration

Cursed-tool attunement

Campaign canon

Cursed-tool attunement: you have PB attunement slots (2–6). This tool occupies one slot and requires a one-hour ritual. Attunement ends voluntarily over a short rest, on death, or when you replace it while over capacity. Distance does not break attunement. Cursed-tool attunement is separate from magic-item attunement.
Balance & counterplay. It weakens barriers rather than nullifying techniques — targets that do not rely on barrier reinforcement or CE-hardening lose little to it. Fight it in the open, not behind walls.
