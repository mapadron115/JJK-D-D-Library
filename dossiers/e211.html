Widow's Key

1d6 bludgeoning, versatile d8. It can interact with mundane locks as thieves' tools using Tool attack bonus.

FormOversized black key The whole argument in one object: everything sealed is merely a lock that hasn't met its sorcerer.

Features & procedures

Properties & techniques

Exact player-facing procedures

Cursed Locksmith

The survey. Touch a cursed barrier for two CE and test your Tool check against its creator's DC: learn one property and cut a temporary keyhole. The ward is not broken — merely introduced. action, 2 CE: touch a cursed barrier/ward and make Tool check vs creator DC; success learns one property and creates a temporary 'keyhole'.

Turn the Key

The entry. Three CE at the keyhole, a contested Tool check: success opens a five-foot passage until your next turn starts. Mark the limits — no Domain collapses, nothing permanently dispelled. The Key passes through; it does not conquer. action, 3 CE at a keyhole: contested Tool check against the barrier creator's Tool check (invented) — if the creator is absent, roll against the creator's DC (invented); success opens a 5-ft passage until start next turn. Cannot collapse a Domain or permanently dispel a technique.

Deadbolt

The reverse: two CE as a reaction seals a door you touch — armored, stubborn, holding until your next turn ends. The locksmith keeps keys; it also keeps locks. reaction, 2 CE: seal a door/opening you touch; AC 15, HP 5×PB until end next turn.

Feature

One standing law: master keys bow to refinement and to the unbreakable. Some doors were sealed by hands this tool must not outrank. Master keys remain subject to refinement and explicit unbreakable laws.

Field notes. Subject: a key that learned to be a weapon. Three truths govern it. First, it is a barrier-thief's cursed energy soaked into steel: it does not break cursed barriers but convinces them they were always doors — every lock, to the Key, merely a lock that hasn't met its sorcerer yet. Second, the theft is honest: it learns a property before it cuts the keyhole; it opens a passage, not a ruin — no Domain collapses, nothing permanently dispelled. Third, it is symmetric: the same hand that opens can deadbolt, sealing what it once slipped through. Classify this correctly: not a siege engine but a thief's argument, made eloquent — the claim that every sealed thing was built, somewhere, to be opened.

Rules integration

Cursed-tool attunement

Campaign canon

Cursed-tool attunement: you have PB attunement slots (2–6). This tool occupies one slot and requires a one-hour ritual. Attunement ends voluntarily over a short rest, on death, or when you replace it while over capacity. Distance does not break attunement. Cursed-tool attunement is separate from magic-item attunement.
Balance & counterplay. Excellent infiltration without replacing Domain clashes or anti-technique artifacts.
