Features & procedures

Properties & techniques

Exact player-facing procedures

Cursed Locksmith

action, 2 CE: touch a cursed barrier/ward and make Tool check vs creator DC; success learns one property and creates a temporary 'keyhole'.

Turn the Key

action, 3 CE at a keyhole: contested Tool check against the barrier creator's Tool check (invented) — if the creator is absent, roll against the creator's DC (invented); success opens a 5-ft passage until start next turn. Cannot collapse a Domain or permanently dispel a technique.

Deadbolt

reaction, 2 CE: seal a door/opening you touch; AC 15, HP 5×PB until end next turn.

Feature

Master keys remain subject to refinement and explicit unbreakable laws.

The fiction. Registry notes hold that the Widow's Key is not a weapon that learned to be a key but a key that learned to be a weapon. Scholars classify it as a barrier-thief's CE soaked into the steel: it does not break cursed barriers, it convinces them they were always doors. The oversized black key is the technique's whole fiction in one object — everything sealed is, to the Key, merely a lock that hasn't met its sorcerer yet.

Rules integration

Cursed-tool attunement

Campaign canon

Cursed-tool attunement: you have PB attunement slots (2–6). This tool occupies one slot and requires a one-hour ritual. Attunement ends voluntarily over a short rest, on death, or when you replace it while over capacity. Distance does not break attunement. Cursed-tool attunement is separate from magic-item attunement.
Balance & counterplay. Excellent infiltration without replacing Domain clashes or anti-technique artifacts.
