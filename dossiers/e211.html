Widow's Key

1d6 bludgeoning, versatile d8. It can interact with mundane locks as thieves' tools using Tool attack bonus.

FormOversized black key
RoleBarrier utility / infiltration
EngineLock
Attunement1 hour*
Properties & techniquesExact player-facing procedures

Cursed Locksmith

action, 2 CE: touch a cursed barrier/ward and make Tool check vs creator DC; success learns one property and creates a temporary 'keyhole'.

Turn the Key

action, 3 CE at a keyhole: contested Tool check; success opens a 5-ft passage until start next turn. Cannot collapse a Domain or permanently dispel a technique.

Deadbolt

reaction, 2 CE: seal a door/opening you touch; AC 15, HP 5×PB until end next turn.

Feature

Master keys remain subject to refinement and explicit unbreakable laws.

Balance & counterplay. Excellent infiltration without replacing Domain clashes or anti-technique artifacts.
