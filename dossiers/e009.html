Cursed Spirit Manipulation

An unlimited stored collection, bounded active roster, sacrificial defense, Uzumaki, and extracted actions.

The fiction. Jujutsu scholars classify it as a digestion, not a technique of command: the sorcerer's cursed energy recognizes curses as food, and each orb swallowed teaches the body one more way to be inhabited. The prevailing theory holds that the taste — registry notes refuse to elaborate — is the price of the bargain: nothing commands what it has not first digested. Uzumaki, the compression of everything absorbed into a single blast, is simply the stomach made honest.

Full progression

Feature map

1Cursed Spirit Absorption.

Absorb

As an action or reaction after a non-Humanoid creature within 120 feet reaches 0 HP, spend 1 CE to turn it into an orb and eat it — this is automatic. A living creature with CR lower than your PB instead makes a Wisdom save (it must have the Cursed Spirit type); on failure it becomes an orb. Legendary resistance can prevent this normally.

Eating the orb

Eating an orb is part of the absorption. You may safely consume orbs of creatures with CR equal to or lower than your level. For a higher-CR orb, make a Constitution save (DC 5 + the creature’s CR): on success you absorb it and may use it after a short or long rest; on failure you gain 2 levels of exhaustion and take necrotic damage equal to the creature’s CR in d12s.

Collection strain

Check collection strain only when the number stored first crosses a multiple of 10; losing spirits does not let the same threshold trigger again. When it triggers, make a DC 10 Wisdom save: on failure you gain exhaustion — one level if you fail by 5 or less, two levels if you fail by more than 5, three levels if you fail by 10 or more.

Stored and summoned spirits

Stored spirits are unlimited and recorded by exact stat block and CR. Summoning costs CE equal to CR rounded up (minimum 1). You can have active spirits up to your CE modifier (minimum 1). They act immediately after you; one bonus action gives all of them the same simple order, while an action can give distinct or complex orders. Otherwise they Dodge.

Limits

Spirits cannot use legendary, lair, or mythic actions. Spellcasting and Innate Techniques are allowed, but any CE cost is paid from your pool. A spirit reduced to 0 HP is permanently exorcised.

3Curse-Infused Spirits.

Curse-Infused Spirits

Strength, Dexterity, and Constitution each gain your CE modifier, to a maximum of 22. Recalculate their attack, damage, AC, HP, and save DC only where that score normally applies. Sacrificial defense requires an active spirit: X is its CR rounded up (minimum 1) — spend X CE to reduce one damage instance by Xd20, destroying the spirit.

Resilience

Your spirits regain all of their HP at the end of a short rest, are immune to being frightened or charmed, and you know if one is exorcised even when it is far away.

6Perfect Control.

Perfect Control

Collection strain is now checked when your stored count first crosses a multiple of 20 instead of 10. You can now summon cursed spirits as a bonus action.

Pinnacle expression

Maximum, Domain, or equivalent.

Uzumaki: 11 — Maximum Uzumaki. choose and permanently consume at least two stored or active spirits. There is no creature cap; count them before rolling. The cone grows and damage scales exactly as printed. Afterward choose one action from one consumed spirit as an Extracted Action. You may hold one; it is used once and then lost. Pay 3 CE for a bonus action, 4 for an action, 5 for a reaction. A legendary action becomes a normal action costing 10 CE; lair/mythic actions remain ineligible.

Domain — Procession of a Hundred Demons: 18 — Womb Profusion. summoning costs 0 CE but active capacity remains. At initiative 20 every chosen hostile is engulfed by an independent curse procession and automatically takes 8d10 necrotic damage. An Undead target with CR below your PB is absorbed instead. A protected creature makes a Charisma save, taking half damage and avoiding absorption on a success. Ordinary summoned-spirit attacks roll normally.

Innate Technique Feats

Build-defining options.

These are optional character-building feats. You must meet each listed prerequisite before selecting it.

Prerequisite: Cursed Spirit Absorption and CE score 16+

Cursed Spirit Embodiment

Action; spend CE equal to the absorbed spirit’s CR, rounded up (minimum 1), and transform for up to half your character level in hours. Replace your Strength, Dexterity, Constitution, AC, current HP, movement, senses, and creature actions with the spirit’s profile; retain your Intelligence, Wisdom, Charisma, personality, alignment, and any proficiency bonus that is higher. You cannot use Cursed Spirit Manipulation features or legendary, lair, or mythic actions while transformed. At 0 spirit HP you revert, and excess damage carries over to your original HP.

Prerequisite: Perfect Control

Defensive Summoning

Reaction when hit, before damage: spend 5 CE plus the chosen stored spirit’s summon cost and manifest it in an adjacent legal space. It becomes the attack’s target; compare the original attack roll with its AC and apply damage to it. Area effects still affect every creature normally.

Prerequisite: Maximum: Uzumaki and CE score 20

Uzumaki Versatility

Bonus action: permanently consume exactly two stored spirits to form a miniature Uzumaki for 1 minute. Action or reaction when a target comes within 60 feet, 5 CE: ranged CE attack; hit deals Xd12 necrotic, X = combined CR rounded up. Ignore resistance, treat immunity as resistance. Undetected target grants advantage. Extract one action using the revised Uzumaki rules.

Prerequisites: Must have Maximum: Uzumaki

Minimum: Uzumaki

Due to your extended studies, you have made a smaller version of Uzumaki that's more deadly and much harder to dodge. As a bonus action, you can spend two cursed spirits to create a miniature Uzumaki that fits in your hand. It can't be seen by the creature unless its passive Perception is 25 or higher, or the creature succeeds a Perception check. As an action or reaction for 5 Cursed Energy, you can release all the condensed energy from this Uzumaki at a target within 30 feet. Make a ranged cursed energy attack roll, on a hit the target takes Xd12 of necrotic damage(X being the combined CR of the 2 curses). Like Maximum Uzumaki, this ignores resistance and treats immunity like resistance. You have advantage on your attack roll if the target has not detected your Minimum Uzumaki. Similar to Maximum Uzumaki, you can obtain actions, reactions, bonus actions, and legendary actions of the creature's technique.

Rules integration

Edge cases and shared systems.

Rules priority: the reviewed rulings, explicit codex clarifications, and the level progression always control when original wording conflicts. Legacy Domain auto-hit language, action terminology, event timing, missing saves, and older scaling never override revised player rules.
Why Tier IV: the user’s real collection depends on play, but even an average campaign produces a permanent, customizable monster library. The tier reflects asset growth and GM workload, not an assumption of legendary captures.
