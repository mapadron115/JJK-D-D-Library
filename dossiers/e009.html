At-the-table character reference

Maximum: Uzumaki: action; permanently consume at least two stored cursed spirits. The initial 30-foot cone deals 2d12 necrotic damage; each spirit beyond the first two adds 5 feet to the cone and 1d12 necrotic damage. Creatures make a Dexterity save, taking half damage on success. Apply the revised extraction and roster limitations below.

Completed rules

  • Absorb: as an action or reaction after a non-Humanoid creature within 120 feet reaches 0 HP, spend 1 CE to turn it into an orb; this is automatic. A living creature with CR lower than PB instead makes a Wisdom save and must have the Cursed Spirit type. On failure it becomes an orb. Legendary resistance can prevent this normally.
  • Eating an orb is part of the absorption. Use the printed high-CR Constitution save and consequences. Check collection strain only when the number stored first crosses a multiple of 10, or 20 after Perfect Control; losing spirits does not let the same threshold trigger again.
  • Stored spirits are unlimited and recorded by exact stat block and CR. Summoning costs CE equal to CR rounded up, minimum 1. You can have active spirits up to CE modifier (minimum 1). They act immediately after you; one bonus action gives all of them the same simple order, while an action can give distinct or complex orders. Otherwise they Dodge.
  • Spirits cannot use legendary, lair, or mythic actions. Spellcasting and Innate Techniques are allowed, but any CE cost is paid from your pool. A spirit reduced to 0 is permanently exorcised.
  • Curse-Infused Spirits: Strength, Dexterity, and Constitution each gain CE modifier to a maximum of 22. Recalculate their attack, damage, AC, HP, and save DC only where that score normally applies. Sacrificial defense requires an active spirit; X is its CR rounded up (minimum 1), costing X CE and reducing one damage instance by Xd20 before the spirit is destroyed.
  • Uzumaki: choose and permanently consume at least two stored or active spirits. There is no creature cap; count them before rolling. The cone grows and damage scales exactly as printed. Afterward choose one action from one consumed spirit as an Extracted Action. You may hold one; it is used once and then lost. Pay 3 CE for a bonus action, 4 for an action, 5 for a reaction. A legendary action becomes a normal action costing 10 CE; lair/mythic actions remain ineligible.
  • Domain — Procession of a Hundred Demons: summoning costs 0 CE but active capacity remains. At initiative 20 every chosen hostile is engulfed by an independent curse procession and automatically takes 8d10 necrotic damage. An Undead target with CR below your PB is absorbed instead. A protected creature makes a Charisma save, taking half damage and avoiding absorption on a success. Ordinary summoned-spirit attacks roll normally.

Power identity

Why Tier IV: the user’s real collection depends on play, but even an average campaign produces a permanent, customizable monster library. The tier reflects asset growth and GM workload, not an assumption of legendary captures.

Recovered character-build options

Innate Technique Feats 3

These are optional character-building feats. You must meet each listed prerequisite before selecting it.

Cursed Spirit Embodiment

Pinnacle

Prerequisite: Cursed Spirit Absorption and CE score 16+

Action; spend CE equal to the absorbed spirit’s CR, rounded up (minimum 1), and transform for up to half your character level in hours. Replace your Strength, Dexterity, Constitution, AC, current HP, movement, senses, and creature actions with the spirit’s profile; retain your Intelligence, Wisdom, Charisma, personality, alignment, and any proficiency bonus that is higher. You cannot use Cursed Spirit Manipulation features or legendary, lair, or mythic actions while transformed. At 0 spirit HP you revert, and excess damage carries over to your original HP.

Defensive Summoning

High impact

Prerequisite: Perfect Control

Reaction when hit, before damage: spend 5 CE plus the chosen stored spirit’s summon cost and manifest it in an adjacent legal space. It becomes the attack’s target; compare the original attack roll with its AC and apply damage to it. Area effects still affect every creature normally.

Uzumaki Versatility

Pinnacle

Prerequisite: Maximum: Uzumaki and CE score 20

Bonus action: permanently consume exactly two stored spirits to form a miniature Uzumaki for 1 minute. Action or reaction when a target comes within 60 feet, 5 CE: ranged CE attack; hit deals Xd12 necrotic, X = combined CR rounded up. Ignore resistance, treat immunity as resistance. Undetected target grants advantage. Extract one action using the revised Uzumaki rules.

Cursed Spirit Manipulation
You are blessed with an amazing technique to control Cursed Spirits.
Cursed Energy
Starting at 1st level, you can harness your negative feelings into a
usable source of energy, called cursed energy. These feelings can be
harnessed into attacks or abilities. The number of cursed energy you
possess is based on your class, some classes use a modifier that
being charisma or wisdom (Player’s choice)
Your cursed energy DC is 8 + user cursed energy ability modifier +
proficiency bonus.
You regain all spent cursed energy on a long rest.
Cursed Spirit Absorption
Beginning at 1st level, you know the taste that no one will ever
know, the one of curses. As an action or reaction for 1 Cursed
Energy, you may exorcise a non-humanoid creature with 0 hit points,
or a creature whose CR is less than your proficiency modifier within
120 feet of you and transform its soul into a small black orb for you
to eat. After that, you can summon it as an ally to fight for you.
You can summon your Cursed Spirits as an action by spending Cursed
Energy equal to their CR. You may summon as many cursed spirits at

Cursed Spirit. They lose their free will and follow each and every
command you give them. If they are reduced to 0 hit points they die
instantly, and cannot be revived by any means. They cannot use
actions on their own. You can spend a bonus action to command the
summoned creatures to attack, use magic, their innate technique, and
an action to do something more specific, such as 'protect that
creature, grab that, defend that area, etc.
