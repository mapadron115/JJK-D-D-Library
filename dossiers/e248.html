Core law

At-the-table character reference

Recovered printed values & campaign clarifications

Maximum: Wing King · level 11:

at 11th level, you learn to condense your blood into wings resembling veins as a bonus action for 8 CE, gaining the following benefits for 1 minute: your movement is quadrupled; you gain a flying speed equal to half your movement; you gain advantage on Dexterity saving throws; you can replace an attack on your turn with a Rot Technique or one line of blood attack. As a free action on your turn, you can unleash lines of blood—three ranged Cursed Energy attacks against a target within 60 feet, each dealing 4d10 + your CE modifier acid or necrotic damage; you can also replace an attack on your turn with a line of blood attack. While using Wing King, you cannot use Decay.

Core law

Completed rules

Rotten Blood:

Starting at 1st level, you possess toxic blood. You gain resistance to poison and acid damage and have advantage on saving throws against the poisoned condition. When you have less than your hit point maximum, you can cause your unarmed strikes or melee weapon attacks to deal an additional 1d8 acid damage once per turn. This increases to 2d8 at 3rd level, 3d8 at 6th level, and 4d8 at 11th level.

Blood Bags:

Once as part of a long rest, you can spend $100 and hit points up to your proficiency bonus to fill a Blood Bag with your own blood. This Blood Bag counts as a source of hit points for your Rot features. A Blood Bag lasts 5 days outside of refrigeration before it goes bad. You can have a number of Blood Bags equal to your Constitution modifier at once.

Rot Techniques:

Starting at 1st level, you can harness your blood in a manner fit for battle. When you use a Rot technique or feature, you can ignore the HP cost if you have an alternate source of blood. You gain four techniques and two additional techniques at 6th and 11th level. Blood Spit. As an action for 1 CE and 2 HP, you force a creature within 15 feet to make a Constitution save or take 2d6 acid damage and be blinded until the end of their turn on a fail. Rot Burst. Once per turn on a hit with a melee attack, you can spend 1 CE and 1 HP to deal an additional 2d8 acid damage. Rotten Blood Beam. As an action for 3 CE and 4 HP, you can make a ranged cursed energy attack against a target within 30 feet. On a hit, the target takes 2d8 piercing and 2d10 acid damage and must make a Constitution saving throw or have their movement reduced by 10 feet until the end of their next turn. Corrosive Wave. As an action for 3 CE and 5 HP, you launch waves of your rotten blood in a 15 feet cone. Creatures in range must make a Constitution saving throw or take 5d8 acid damage or half as much damage on a success. Blood Shield. As a reaction, you spend CE up to your modifier and twice that amount in HP. For each CE spent, the Blood Shield gains 10 hit points, which absorbs the damage and you take remaining damage. If this reduces a melee attack to 0, the attacker must make a Constitution save or take 2d8 acid damage. Homing Blood Missile. As an action for 4 CE and 5 HP you can shoot a seeking blood missile at a target within 60 feet. On a hit, the target takes 5d10 + your CE modifier in acid damage. You can reroll the attack roll using your bonus action or reaction. Blood Soak. As a bonus action for 3 CE and 3 HP, you can coat your limbs in your blood. For the next minute, your walking speed increases by 10 ft and your unarmed strikes deal 2d6 + CE modifier acid damage on a hit. Crimson Spear. As an action for 2 CE and 3 HP, you form your blood into a spear. Make a ranged cursed energy attack roll with a range of 30/90 feet. On a hit the target takes 1d6 piercing and 2d10 + CE modifier acid damage. Crimson Rain. As an action for 2 CE and 3 HP, you pick a point within 60 ft. Creatures within 15 feet must make a Dexterity Saving throw taking 1d10 piercing and 2d10 acid damage on failed save or half as much on a success.

Decay:

At 3rd level, as a bonus action for 3 Cursed Energy, you can cause all creatures that have taken damage from your technique in the last minute to form black floral patterns on their body as they have been infected with your blood. At the beginning of your turn, they take your Rotten Blood damage. This lasts for 1 minute unless you spend 3 cursed energy to continue the effect or end it as a free action. At 6th level, creatures also gain the poisoned condition for the duration and Decay also reduces a creature’s hit point maximum. A creature’s maximum hit points return to normal after a long rest.

Scarlet Rot:

At 6th level, your technique now ignores resistance to acid damage and a creature that fails a saving throw against your technique is poisoned for 1 minute and can make a Constitution save at the end of their turn to end the effect on themselves. You gain immunity to poison and acid damage as well as the poisoned condition. At 18th level, your technique treats immunity to acid damage as resistance.

Innate Technique Feats

Build-defining options.

These are optional character-building feats. You must meet each listed prerequisite before selecting it.

Prerequisite: Must have Rot Techniques

Blood Production

You have found a way to link your blood to Cursed Energy. For 1 CE, you can create a sufficient source of blood worth 10 HP that can only be used for your Rot features. This does not regain HP.

Prerequisite: Must have Scarlet Rot

Condensed Decay

You have learned to increase the potency of your blood’s properties. When you use a Rot Technique, you can spend additional HP up to twice your CE modifier to increase the damage dice of acid dealt by your technique by one tier for every 2 HP spent (d8<d10<d12) to a max of d12.

Prerequisite: Must have Maximum: Wing King

Blackened Blood

Your blood has become bubbling and black. A creature that fails a Constitution saving throw against your technique by 5 or more gains a level of exhaustion. A creature cannot gain more than 3 levels of exhaustion from this feature.

Prerequisite: Must have Maximum: Wing King

Wing King Improvement

You have mastered your Maximum technique, granting you the following benefits while using Wing King: you gain the Evasion feature; you can now make four lines of blood attacks as a free action on your turn; you can use a Rot Technique as a bonus action.

Rules integration

Edge cases and shared systems.

Technique list: Adopt all nine techniques as the operative list: 4 known at 1st, all 9 known by 11th (stale count sentence corrected).
Costs: "CE up to your modifier" caps CE spendable. HP costs are costs, not damage; they cannot be reduced by resistance and are spent even on a miss unless the feature says otherwise. Blood Shield HP cost is twice the CE spent, retained as printed. All saves use the standard CE save DC. Rotten Blood rider: the below-maximum-HP gate is checked per turn when the triggering attack lands, not locked in.
Decay: Decay recurring damage = current Rotten Blood rider dice, dealt at start of YOUR turn, no save/attack roll, as printed. Infection requires the creature to have taken damage from YOUR technique within the last minute.
Blood Bags: $100 cost and 5-day spoilage retained as printed; refrigeration pauses spoilage as written. Bag HP spent in place of the user's HP for Rot feature costs, does not heal. Under the campaign's finite baseline RCT, bag HP cannot be regenerated by RCT — it is stored spent HP.
Scarlet Rot and Wing King: Scarlet Rot poison-on-failed-save stacks with Decay's poisoned condition (redundant while both apply). Wing King is the named Maximum under the adopted V.6 Maximum Output framework. "Movement quadrupled" multiplies base walking speed before the fly-speed derivation (fly = half of quadrupled movement). Blood Production: 10 HP per 1 CE is an efficient economy by design.
Domain HP siphon: Rot Technique HP costs are drawn from chosen creatures in the domain instead of you; you choose which creatures and how the cost splits, dealt as unpreventable HP expenditure (not damage).
Boundary: nine techniques vs "four + two + two" progression — adopt all nine as the operative list (flagged for zack). Decay: the 6th-level hit-point-maximum reduction amount needs zack's call. Blood Bag: whether the $100 bag must be created by the Rot user needs zack's call. Domain HP-siphon split procedure needs zack's call.
Audit status: the audit records (AT192–AT195) were approved in the 2026-09-10 mass approval (983/983 decided; no rejections). Remaining open points above are table rulings, not approval gaps.
