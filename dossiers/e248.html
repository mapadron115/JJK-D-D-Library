Rot

The Death Paintings’ technique: rotten blood as a weapon of corrosion and infection, black floral rot blooming from every wound, crowned by wings that take the fight to the sky.

Power tierTier IV · Special Grade
Combat roleStriker / blaster
Core engineRotten Blood + HP-cost economy
Domain · pinnacleMind Palace of Corrupted Ego (L18)

Field notes. Subject: a body that turned its own decay into doctrine. Three truths govern Rot. First, the price is the honesty: every technique costs HP as well as CE, and the blood was never free, the sorcerer simply refused to let even their decay be barren. Second, the blood infects: Rotten Blood arms your strikes with a growing acid rider while you stand below maximum HP, and at three the Decay ritual marks every creature you've wounded, bleeding them at the start of your turns from black floral wounds. Third, the corruption ascends: Scarlet Rot grants immunity and ignores acid resistance, Wing King condenses the blood into veined wings with quadrupled movement and flight, and the Level 18 Mind Palace siphons your HP costs from the creatures trapped inside. Mark this: the garden was always the argument, the Domain is where it ends.

Full progression

Feature map

1Rotten Blood · Rot Techniques.

Passive · Resistance to poison and acid; advantage on saves vs poisoned · Below max HP: unarmed/melee attacks deal +1d8 acid once per turn (2d8 at 3rd, 3d8 at 6th, 4d8 at 11th) · Blood Bag: 1/long rest, $100 + up to PB HP; substitutes HP costs for Rot features; lasts 5 days; carry up to Con modifier.

The economy is stated up front and never softened: power costs CE and HP both, every time. Below maximum HP, your strikes carry an acid rider that grows from 1d8 to 4d8: and the Blood Bag, filled at a long rest for $100 and a tithe of your own blood, is the honest merchant's answer to the price.

Starting at 1st level, you possess toxic blood. You gain resistance to poison and acid damage and have advantage on saving throws against the poisoned condition. When you have less than your hit point maximum, you can cause your unarmed strikes or melee weapon attacks to deal an additional 1d8 acid damage once per turn. This increases to 2d8 at 3rd level, 3d8 at 6th level, and 4d8 at 11th level. Once as part of a long rest, you can spend $100 and hit points up to your proficiency bonus to fill a Blood Bag with your own blood. This Blood Bag counts as a source of hit points for your Rot features. A Blood Bag lasts 5 days outside of refrigeration before it goes bad. You can have a number of Blood Bags equal to your Constitution modifier at once. Starting at 1st level, you can harness your blood in a manner fit for battle. When you use a Rot technique or feature, you can ignore the HP cost if you have an alternate source of blood. You gain four techniques and two additional techniques at 6th and 11th level.

Rot Techniques (each costs CE + HP) · Blood Spit: action, 1 CE + 2 HP; 15 ft, Con save or 2d6 acid + blinded until end of their turn · Rot Burst: 1/turn on a melee hit, 1 CE + 1 HP; +2d8 acid · Rotten Blood Beam: action, 3 CE + 4 HP; ranged attack, 30 ft; 2d8 piercing + 2d10 acid; Con save or −10 speed until end of your next turn · Corrosive Wave: action, 3 CE + 5 HP; 15-ft cone; Con save or 5d8 acid (half) · Blood Shield: reaction; spend CE (up to modifier) + twice that in HP; 10 shield HP per CE; melee attacker reduced to 0: Con save or 2d8 acid · Homing Blood Missile: action, 4 CE + 5 HP; 60 ft; on hit 5d10 + CE modifier acid; bonus action or reaction to reroll the attack · Blood Soak: bonus action, 3 CE + 3 HP; 1 minute; +10 speed; unarmed +2d6 + CE modifier acid · Crimson Spear: action, 2 CE + 3 HP; ranged attack 30/90; 1d6 piercing + 2d10 + CE modifier acid · Crimson Rain: action, 2 CE + 3 HP; point within 60 ft, 15-ft radius; Dex save, 1d10 piercing + 2d10 acid (half).

Blood Spit. As an action for 1 CE and 2 HP, you force a creature within 15 feet to make a Constitution save or take 2d6 acid damage and be blinded until the end of their turn on a fail.

Rot Burst. Once per turn on a hit with a melee attack, you can spend 1 CE and 1 HP to deal an additional 2d8 acid damage.

Rotten Blood Beam. As an action for 3 CE and 4 HP, you can make a ranged cursed energy attack against a target within 30 feet. On a hit, the target takes 2d8 piercing and 2d10 acid damage and must make a Constitution saving throw or have their movement reduced by 10 feet until the end of their next turn.

Corrosive Wave. As an action for 3 CE and 5 HP, you launch waves of your rotten blood in a 15 feet cone. Creatures in range must make a Constitution saving throw or take 5d8 acid damage or half as much damage on a success.

Blood Shield. As a reaction, you spend CE up to your modifier and twice that amount in HP. For each CE spent, the Blood Shield gains 10 hit points, which absorbs the damage and you take remaining damage. If this reduces a melee attack to 0, the attacker must make a Constitution save or take 2d8 acid damage.

Homing Blood Missile. As an action for 4 CE and 5 HP you can shoot a seeking blood missile at a target within 60 feet. On a hit, the target takes 5d10 + your CE modifier in acid damage. You can reroll the attack roll, after seeing the roll but before knowing the outcome (invented), using your bonus action or reaction.

Blood Soak. As a bonus action for 3 CE and 3 HP, you can coat your limbs in your blood. For the next minute, your walking speed increases by 10 ft and your unarmed strikes deal 2d6 + CE modifier acid damage on a hit.

Crimson Spear. As an action for 2 CE and 3 HP, you form your blood into a spear. Make a ranged cursed energy attack roll with a range of 30/90 feet. On a hit the target takes 1d6 piercing and 2d10 + CE modifier acid damage.

Crimson Rain. As an action for 2 CE and 3 HP, you pick a point within 60 ft. Creatures within 15 feet must make a Dexterity Saving throw taking 1d10 piercing and 2d10 acid damage on failed save or half as much on a success.

3Decay.

Bonus action · 3 CE · Infect all creatures damaged by your technique in the last minute, 1 minute · At the start of your turns they take your Rotten Blood dice; 3 CE to extend, free action to end · 6th: also poisoned; max HP reduced by damage dealt (restored on long rest).

The blood learns infection: for 3 CE, every creature wounded by your technique in the last minute blooms black floral rot and bleeds your Rotten Blood dice at the start of your turns. At 6th level the rot also poisons and gnaws at hit-point maximums. This is not damage anymore; it is a garden being planted in the enemy.

At 3rd level you have learned to use your blood infectiously. As a bonus action for 3 Cursed Energy, you can cause all creatures that have taken damage from your technique in the last minute to form black floral patterns on their body as they have been infected with your blood. At the beginning of your turn, they take your Rotten Blood damage. This lasts for 1 minute unless you spend 3 cursed energy to continue the effect or end it as a free action. At 6th level, creatures also gain the poisoned condition for the duration and Decay also reduces a creature’s hit point maximum by the Decay damage dealt (invented). A creature’s maximum hit points return to normal after a long rest.

6Scarlet Rot · Rot Techniques.

Passive (6th) · Technique ignores acid resistance · Failed saves vs your technique: poisoned 1 minute (Con save ends at end of their turn) · Immune to poison, acid damage, and the poisoned condition · 18th: treats acid immunity as resistance.

Mastery turns the poison inward's opposite: immunity to poison and acid, acid resistance ignored by your technique, and every failed save poisons for a minute. The sorcerer has become the one thing their own blood cannot kill.

At 6th level, you have proved your mastery over your technique. Your technique now ignores resistance to acid damage and a creature that fails a saving throw against your technique is poisoned for 1 minute and can make a Constitution save at the end of their turn to end the effect on themselves. You gain immunity to poison and acid damage as well as the poisoned condition. At 18th level, your technique treats immunity to acid damage as resistance.

11Maximum: Wing King · Rot Techniques.

Bonus action · 8 CE · 1 minute · Movement quadrupled; fly speed = half of that; advantage on Dex saves · Replace an attack with a Rot Technique; free-action blood lines: three ranged attacks at 60 ft, each 4d10 + CE modifier acid or necrotic · Cannot use Decay.

The Maximum is ascension: 8 CE condenses the blood into veined wings, movement quadrupled, flight at half that, advantage on Dexterity saves, and free-action blood lines dealing 4d10 + modifier per strike. While the wings are spread, Decay is set aside: even rot must sometimes yield the sky.

At 11th level, you learn to condense your blood into wings resembling veins as a bonus action for 8 CE, gaining the following benefits for 1 minute: your movement is quadrupled; you gain a flying speed equal to half your quadrupled walking speed (invented); you gain advantage on Dexterity saving throws; you can replace an attack on your turn with a Rot Technique or one line of blood attack. As a free action on your turn, you can unleash lines of blood. These lines of blood take the shape of three ranged Cursed Energy attacks against a target within 60 feet. Each attack deals 4d10 + your CE modifier acid or necrotic damage. You can also replace an attack on your turn with a line of blood attack. While using Wing King, you cannot use Decay.

18Domain Expansion: Mind Palace of Corrupted Ego.

Full-turn action · 20 CE · 60-ft radius, up to 1 min, 800 clash (Tier IV) · All creatures in the domain suffer Decay; extra Decay damage at initiative 20 · Your Rot Techniques' HP costs drawn from willing creatures in the domain · 18th: treats acid immunity as resistance.

A palace built from a single corrupted mind: corridors of black marble veined with something green and pulsing, portraits whose faces have rotted off their frames, chandeliers of bone dripping slow wax. The geometry flatters the builder, every vista arranged to suggest grandeur, but the grandeur is gangrenous, and the rot has opinions. It is beautiful the way a wound is beautiful, right before it kills.

The Domain is the argument's end: all creatures within suffer Decay, initiative 20 brings additional Decay damage, and, the crown of it, your Rot Techniques' HP costs are drawn from chosen creatures inside instead of you. The self, crowned, insists the rot was a garden all along.

Deploying the Domain costs a full-turn action and 20 CE (invented): 60-foot radius, up to 1 minute, 800 clash points (Tier IV) (invented). At 18th level you have mastered your technique, allowing you to expand your domain. All creatures within your domain are under the effects of Decay and take additional Decay damage at initiative 20. Additionally, your Rot Techniques use hit points from willing creatures within your domain (invented). At 18th level, your technique treats immunity to acid damage as resistance.

Innate Technique Feats

Build-defining options.

These are optional character-building feats. You must meet each listed prerequisite before selecting it.

Prerequisite: Must have Rot Techniques

Blood Production

The economy, solved elegantly: 1 CE becomes 10 HP of usable blood for your Rot features. It cannot heal, it is currency, not medicine.

You have found a way to link your blood to Cursed Energy. For 1 CE, you can create a sufficient source of blood worth 10 HP that can only be used for your Rot features. This does not regain HP.

Prerequisite: Must have Scarlet Rot

Condensed Decay

The rot learns to clot: spend up to 4 HP on a Rot Technique hit, and the target's speed bleeds away five feet per two HP spent. The garden tightens around the limbs.

When you hit a creature with a Rot Technique, you may spend up to 4 HP. The target’s speed is reduced by 5 feet for every 2 HP spent until the end of its next turn, as your blood clots around its limbs.

Prerequisite: Must have Maximum: Wing King

Blackened Blood

The blood remembers what it touched: for an hour you know the direction and distance of anything your techniques wounded, with advantage on tracking. Nothing bled by you is ever truly lost to you.

Your blackened blood marks what it touches. You always know the direction and distance of any creature damaged by your Rot Techniques within the last hour, and you have advantage on Wisdom (Survival) checks to track them.

Prerequisite: Must have Maximum: Wing King

Wingbeat

The wings become weather: a bonus action beats every creature within 15 ft against a Strength save, hurling them ten feet and prone. The sky, too, can be a shove.

While using Wing King, you may beat your veined wings as a bonus action. Each creature within 15 feet of you must make a Strength saving throw against your CE save DC or be pushed 10 feet away from you and knocked prone.

Rules integration

Edge cases and shared systems.

Technique list: Adopt all nine techniques as the operative list: 4 known at 1st, all 9 known by 11th (stale count sentence corrected).
Costs: "CE up to your modifier" caps CE spendable. HP costs are costs, not damage; they cannot be reduced by resistance and are spent even on a miss unless the feature says otherwise. Blood Shield HP cost is twice the CE spent, retained as printed. All saves use the standard CE save DC. Rotten Blood rider: the below-maximum-HP gate is checked per turn when the triggering attack lands, not locked in.
Decay: Decay recurring damage = current Rotten Blood rider dice, dealt at start of YOUR turn, no save/attack roll, as printed. Infection requires the creature to have taken damage from YOUR technique within the last minute.
Blood Bags: $100 cost and 5-day spoilage retained as printed; refrigeration pauses spoilage as written. Bag HP spent in place of the user's HP for Rot feature costs, does not heal. Under the campaign's finite baseline RCT, bag HP cannot be regenerated by RCT — it is stored spent HP.
Scarlet Rot and Wing King: Scarlet Rot poison-on-failed-save stacks with Decay's poisoned condition (redundant while both apply). Wing King is the named Maximum under the adopted V.6 Maximum Output framework. "Movement quadrupled" multiplies base walking speed before the fly-speed derivation (fly = half of quadrupled movement). Blood Production: 10 HP per 1 CE is an efficient economy by design.
Domain HP siphon: Rot Technique HP costs are drawn from chosen creatures in the domain instead of you; you choose which creatures and how the cost splits, dealt as unpreventable HP expenditure (not damage).
Boundary: nine techniques vs "four + two + two" progression — adopt all nine as the operative list (flagged for zack). Decay: the 6th-level hit-point-maximum reduction amount needs zack's call. Blood Bag: whether the $100 bag must be created by the Rot user needs zack's call. Domain HP-siphon split procedure needs zack's call.
Audit status: the audit records (AT192–AT195) were approved in the 2026-09-10 mass approval (983/983 decided; no rejections). Remaining open points above are table rulings, not approval gaps.
