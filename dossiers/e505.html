Record

Classification & Risk

An Imaginary Cursed Spirit, Special Grade (CR 17+ band). Born of a cursed object; devastating beyond reason despite crude, playful motions. This is why sorcerers die upon encountering Special Grades.

Stats

Ability scores & traits

STR 24 (+7) · DEX 14 (+2) · CON 24 (+7) · INT 6 (−2) · WIS 12 (+1) · CHA 16 (+3). HP 320 (32d10 + 144), AC 18, speed 40 ft. Rapid Regeneration: regains 20 hit points at the start of its turn; this trait does not function on a turn in which it took radiant damage. Overbearing Presence: each creature that starts its turn within 30 ft. must succeed on a DC 19 Wisdom saving throw or be frightened for 1 minute (repeat the save at the end of each of its turns); frightened creatures have disadvantage on Constitution saving throws to maintain concentration.

Actions

Actions

Multiattack. Two Crude Slam attacks. Crude Slam. Melee Attack: +13 to hit, reach 10 ft. Hit: 3d10 + 7 bludgeoning damage; each other creature within 10 ft. of the target must succeed on a DC 18 Strength saving throw or be knocked prone as the impact sends bodies flying. Cursed Energy Arrow. Ranged Attack: +13 to hit, range 120 ft. Hit: 4d10 force damage — a condensed arrow of cursed energy.

Risk

Special

Damage that would annihilate lesser curses means nothing to it — wounds close as if injury were a rumor. Do not close with it without a plan to outlast the regeneration; do not stand in the open within arrow range. Retreat and regroup is a valid exorcism strategy against a newborn god.

Rules integration

Edge cases and shared systems.

Campaign canon notes

Field rule — perception: At Grade 2 and above this spirit’s cursed energy is dense enough that no Invisibility trait is assigned; standard perception applies. It remains invisible to ordinary non-sorcerers.
