Core law

What the technique actually does.

Core law, empathy. Every empathic feature costs CE — pain is the flavor, CE is the price. The discipline of Azarath is the art of spending CE before the feeling spends you. Roleplay the emotion all you want; the mechanics only ever ask for CE.

Field notes. Subject: a monastery with a bomb inside. Three truths govern her. First: the power is not hers, it is her father's, filtered through a lifetime of meditation — every surge of power is Trigon's inheritance laundered through discipline. Second: the soul-self is the pressure valve; projection keeps the feeling moving instead of pooling. Third: the calm is load-bearing. Spend the feeling or drown in it — those are the only options Azarath ever offered. Mark the economy: pain is income, and she is the richest person on every battlefield she walks onto.

Full progression

Level 1 through 20.

1Empathic Sense, Soul-Self

Passive · You sense the emotions of creatures within 60 feet and have advantage on Insight checks. Action · 1 CE · Project your soul-self: a raven-shaped astral form that travels up to 120 feet from you for 10 minutes. It is intangible and invisible to most senses, can scout and observe, but cannot interact or attack yet.

She feels the room before she enters it, and the raven goes where she can't: through walls, over battlefields, back with its eyes full. The soul leaves so the body doesn't have to.

3Empathic Bolt

Action · 2 CE · Ranged CE attack: 2d10 psychic damage on a hit. You may spend 2 additional CE to deal +1d8. The bolt carries the feeling with it; the target knows exactly whose pain this is.

Pain, itemized and delivered: the worse the day has been, the heavier the bolt. Azarath teaches that suffering shared is suffering weaponized.

6Soul-Self Strikes

While your soul-self is projected, it can attack: ranged CE attack originating from the soul-self's position, 2d10 force damage on a hit. It can also deliver your touch-range empathic features.

The raven learns talons: it strikes from angles the body never could, and the hands it lends are made of everything she's been holding in.

11Shared Pain

Reaction · 2 CE · When an ally within 30 feet takes damage, take the damage instead, then lash the pain back: the attacker takes psychic damage equal to half the damage you took. The interpose is never free; the CE is the point, and pain shared is pain returned.

She steps into it on purpose: the wound arrives, the gauge fills, and somewhere in the monastery a bell rings. Empathy, filed as a combat maneuver.

18Azarath Ascendant

Passive · Your soul-self's range extends to 300 feet, and your empathic features cost 1 less CE (minimum 1 CE). Bonus action · 1 CE · Enter White Raven for 1 minute: your empathic features deal +1d8 and you have advantage on saves against fear and charm.

The discipline wins: the white cloak, the steady hands, the inheritance finally housebroken. Trigon's daughter, Azarath's graduate — the bomb, defused from the inside.

Pinnacle expression

Maximum, Domain, or equivalent.

Maximum, Daughter of Trigon (10 CE): stop filtering. For 1 minute: four arms, fly 60 feet, resistance to all damage except radiant, your unarmed strikes deal 4d10 force damage, and creatures within 60 feet that see you make a WIS save or are frightened of you (save repeats at the end of each of their turns). As an action on each of your turns: hellfire wave — creatures in a 30-foot radius make a Constitution save, taking 6d10 fire plus 6d10 psychic damage on a failure (half on a success). When you activate it, make a WIS save (DC 18): on a failure, Trigon steers for 1 round and you attack the nearest creature. The monastery doors are open. Something is walking out.

Feats

Build-defining options.

Still Point

Bonus action · 2 CE · Recite the mantra and vanish from every sense but sight for 1 minute: you cannot be detected by blindsight, tremorsense, empathic sense, or divination, and you have advantage on Stealth checks. The calm is a choice, and she makes it the hard way.

Grief Eater

Reaction · 2 CE · When a creature within 60 feet fails a death saving throw, absorb their pain: they stabilize, and you take 2d10 psychic damage that cannot be reduced. Someone has to carry it. She volunteers.

Nevermore

Action · 2 CE · Unfurl the dread: creatures within 30 feet make a WIS save or are frightened of you for 1 minute (save ends). Once a raven says it, it stays said.

Trigon's Bargain

Once per turn, take 1d8 psychic damage that cannot be reduced or prevented to reduce the CE cost of your next empathic feature by 2 (minimum 1 CE). The inheritance accepts direct deposits.

Rules integration

Edge cases and shared systems.

Codex rulings: Direct rolled technique/weapon attacks can use the campaign's Black Flash procedure when otherwise eligible. Passive damage, hazards, summons acting independently, and initiative-20 Domain events cannot.
Generic Maximum Output applies only to ordinary features, never named Maxima or Domains.
Emotion is flavor, not a resource: roleplay it freely, but every empathic feature is paid in CE.
