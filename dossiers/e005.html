Boogie Woogie

Instant positional exchange with escalating range, hostile swaps, multi-swaps, and deceptive tempo.

The fiction. Jujutsu scholars classify Boogie Woogie as reinforcement that escaped the body — the user's CE was too restless to sit in muscle, so it answered a mind that thinks in beats and feints by making distance itself negotiable. The clap is not the trigger so much as the downbeat: the prevailing theory holds that the swap answers an intent committed to a rhythm, which is why the user must mean it before their hands ever meet.

Full progression

Feature map

1Boogie:

The swap

By clapping for 1 CE, exchange exactly two targets you can perceive within 30 feet: yourself with a willing creature or an eligible object. You may use this as an action, bonus action, or reaction (a reaction costs 2 additional CE). The swap is teleportation: no opportunity attacks; it preserves facing, conditions, momentum, and altitude, and a creature unsupported after the swap falls. Each destination must fit the arriving target, and worn or carried objects cannot be selected separately.

Eligible objects

An eligible object is Small or larger and contains CE. As an action for 1 CE, you can imbue an ordinary object so it counts; this lasts 1 minute and does not make it a reusable magic item.

3Woogie:

Woogie

You can now move hostile creatures, and you can make two creatures other than yourself swap places (remote pairs). Every unwilling creature in a swap makes a Charisma save: if either member of a pair succeeds, that pair does not move and the CE is still spent. A creature that succeeds is immune to your unwilling swaps until the start of your next turn. A swapped unwilling creature loses its reaction until the start of its next turn.

6Clapping With Soul:

Clapping With Soul

Your range extends to 45 feet, and the CE cost of every Boogie Woogie use is reduced by 1. Once per turn, you may use Boogie with no action (following the once-per-turn legacy free-action rule). The normal action, bonus-action, and reaction options remain available, but no creature can be moved by your Boogie Woogie more than twice in one turn.

11Round of Applause.

Round of Applause

Your range extends to 60 feet. Round of Applause rides on the triggering Boogie Woogie clap: when you use your reaction to clap and trigger Boogie Woogie, you may choose two disjoint pairs among up to four targets and resolve each pair separately at no added CE cost — a target cannot occur in both pairs, and failed or illegal pairs do not prevent the other pair. When you switch places with an ally who was in range of an enemy right before the switch, your next attack roll has advantage; the same applies if you teleport an enemy into range or an ally into range.

Pinnacle expression

Maximum, Domain, or equivalent.

Domain — Standing Ovation: 18 — Standing Ovation. range covers the barrier and ordinary swaps cost no CE but retain their timing and saves. At initiative 20, choose any number of disjoint legal pairs among chosen hostiles, willing creatures, and eligible objects; every pair swaps automatically without CE. A protected hostile makes a Charisma save and prevents its pair on a success. No target can appear in more than one pulse pair.

Innate Technique Feats

Build-defining options.

These are optional character-building feats. You must meet each listed prerequisite before selecting it.

Prerequisite: Boogie

Boogie Down

When you are hit by an attack, you can use your reaction to swap yourself with a willing creature or eligible object within range, following all normal Boogie Woogie rules and paying its normal CE cost. The attack instead targets whoever arrives in your space; compare the original attack roll against its AC and resolve damage normally.

Prerequisite: Woogie

Beguiling Tactics

An unwilling creature swapped by Woogie makes an Intelligence save; failure stuns it until the start of its next turn. After its first success it has advantage on later saves that encounter. As an action, fake-clap with Performance or Deception contested by Insight; on a win it has disadvantage on its next Beguiling save before end of your next turn.

Prerequisite: Round of Applause and CE score 20

Maximum: Best Friend

At the start of combat, choose one willing ally you can perceive. You fight as a duet: once per round, when either of you is targeted by an attack, you can use your reaction to swap the two of you with no CE cost, following all normal Boogie Woogie range and legality rules. The attack targets whoever arrives in the targeted space; compare the original attack roll against their AC and resolve damage normally.

Rules integration

Edge cases and shared systems.

Rules priority: the reviewed rulings, explicit codex clarifications, and the level progression always control when original wording conflicts. Legacy Domain auto-hit language, action terminology, event timing, missing saves, and older scaling never override revised player rules.
Why Tier II: the ceiling is player ingenuity, but the technique changes positions rather than directly supplying damage, defenses, minions, or permanent resources.
