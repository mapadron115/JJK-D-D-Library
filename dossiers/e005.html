Boogie Woogie

Instant positional exchange with escalating range, hostile swaps, multi-swaps, and deceptive tempo.

Field notes. Subject: a dancer for whom distance is negotiable, and every fight is choreography. Three truths govern the clapper. First, the swap is teleportation, not movement: one clap, 1 CE, exactly two perceived targets within 30 feet, no opportunity attacks, facing and momentum preserved. At first the dance requires consent: himself with a willing creature or a CE-bearing object. Second, the tempo turns hostile: Woogie moves unwilling creatures and swaps remote pairs, a Charisma save resists, the swapped lose their reactions. Third, the rhythm becomes reflex: Clapping With Soul stretches the range to 45 feet, cheapens every use by 1 CE, and grants a no-action clap once per turn. Round of Applause is the finale: one reaction clap, two disjoint pairs. And the Domain confesses the doctrine: Standing Ovation is a rhythm with no downbeat, unlimited free Boogie, no initiative-20 event. The dance never pauses for the audience.

Feature map

1Boogie:

The swap

Action, bonus action, or reaction (+2 CE for reaction) · 1 CE · Clap to exchange exactly two targets you can perceive within 30 ft: yourself with a willing creature or an eligible object · Teleportation (no opportunity attacks; preserves facing, conditions, momentum, altitude) · Each destination must fit the arriving target; worn or carried objects cannot be selected separately.

The downbeat: one clap, 1 CE, and exactly two targets exchange places, the sorcerer with a willing creature or a CE-bearing object, within thirty feet. It is teleportation, not movement, no opportunity attacks, momentum and facing preserved. The dance begins with consent. By clapping for 1 CE, exchange exactly two targets you can perceive within 30 feet: yourself with a willing creature or an eligible object. You may use this as an action, bonus action, or reaction (a reaction costs 2 additional CE). The swap is teleportation: no opportunity attacks; it preserves facing, conditions, momentum, and altitude, and a creature unsupported after the swap falls. Each destination must fit the arriving target, and worn or carried objects cannot be selected separately.

Eligible objects

Action · 1 CE · Imbue an ordinary Small-or-larger object so it counts as eligible for Boogie Woogie for 1 minute (does not make it a reusable magic item).

An eligible object is Small or larger and contains CE. As an action for 1 CE, you can imbue an ordinary object so it counts; this lasts 1 minute and does not make it a reusable magic item.

3Woogie:

Woogie

Passive · You may now move hostile creatures and swap two creatures other than yourself · Each unwilling creature makes a Charisma save: if either member of a pair succeeds, that pair does not move (CE still spent) · Success grants immunity to unwilling swaps until the start of your next turn · A swapped unwilling creature loses its reaction until the start of its next turn.

The tempo turns hostile: unwilling creatures may now be moved, and two strangers may be made to trade places. A Charisma save resists; the swapped lose their reactions. The dance no longer asks. You can now move hostile creatures, and you can make two creatures other than yourself swap places (remote pairs). Every unwilling creature in a swap makes a Charisma save: if either member of a pair succeeds, that pair does not move and the CE is still spent. A creature that succeeds is immune to your unwilling swaps until the start of your next turn. A swapped unwilling creature loses its reaction until the start of its next turn.

6Clapping With Soul:

Clapping With Soul

Passive · Range extends to 45 ft · Every Boogie Woogie use costs 1 less CE · Once per turn, use Boogie Woogie with no action · No creature can be moved by your Boogie Woogie more than twice in one turn.

The range stretches to 45 feet, every use costs 1 less, and once per turn the clap needs no action at all. The rhythm has become reflex, but no one may be moved more than twice in a turn. Even improvisation keeps time. Your range extends to 45 feet, and the CE cost of every Boogie Woogie use is reduced by 1. Once per turn, you may use Boogie with no action (following the once-per-turn legacy free-action rule). The normal action, bonus-action, and reaction options remain available, but no creature can be moved by your Boogie Woogie more than twice in one turn.

11Round of Applause.

Round of Applause

Passive · Range extends to 60 ft · When you use your reaction to clap, resolve two disjoint pairs among up to four targets at no added CE cost · If the swap changes ally/enemy adjacency (yourself or a target), your next attack roll has advantage.

The finale: on a reaction clap, two disjoint pairs swap at once, no added cost. Four targets, two exchanges, one downbeat. Your range extends to 60 feet. Round of Applause rides on the triggering Boogie Woogie clap: when you use your reaction to clap and trigger Boogie Woogie, you may choose two disjoint pairs among up to four targets and resolve each pair separately at no added CE cost, a target cannot occur in both pairs, and failed or illegal pairs do not prevent the other pair. When you switch places with an ally who was in range of an enemy right before the switch, your next attack roll has advantage; the same applies if you teleport an enemy into range or an ally into range.

Pinnacle expression

Maximum, Domain, or equivalent.

Domain Expansion, Standing Ovation:

Footlights ignite in a perfect ring, and the world becomes a theater in the round, velvet curtains rising on all sides to reveal an audience of empty seats that applaud anyway. The stage floor is polished to a mirror, doubling every combatant, and the spotlight follows movement the way a predator follows scent. All the world is a stage, and the stage has opinions about where you stand.

A rhythm with no downbeat: inside the Domain, Boogie is a free action any number of times, within its normal rules, and there is no initiative-20 event. The Heian law of this room: the dance never pauses for the audience. 18: full-turn action, 20 CE; 60-foot-radius enclosed Domain centered on you; 1 minute; Burnout 10 rounds. Heian philosophy: the Domain is a rhythm with no downbeat, no initiative-20 event. While it stands, you may use Boogie as a free action any number of times, each within its normal rules.

Innate Technique Feats

Build-defining options.

These are optional character-building feats. You must meet each listed prerequisite before selecting it.

Prerequisite: Boogie

Boogie Down

Struck by an attack, he answers with a reaction clap, swapping himself out and letting the blow meet whoever arrives. The best defense is to not be where the blade lands. When you are hit by an attack, you can use your reaction to swap yourself with a willing creature or eligible object within range, following all normal Boogie Woogie rules and paying its normal CE cost. The attack instead targets whoever arrives in your space; compare the original attack roll against its AC and resolve damage normally.

Prerequisite: Woogie

Beguiling Tactics

The swapped are not merely moved but stunned, an Intelligence save, and the first success teaches them advantage thereafter. The fake-clap, Performance or Deception, keeps the audience guessing which beat is real. An unwilling creature swapped by Woogie makes an Intelligence save; failure stuns it until the start of its next turn. After its first success it has advantage on later saves that encounter. As an action, fake-clap with Performance or Deception contested by Insight; on a win it has disadvantage on its next Beguiling save before end of your next turn.

Prerequisite: Round of Applause and CE score 20

Maximum: Best Friend

The duet: one chosen ally, and once per round when either is targeted, a reaction swaps the two for no CE. Two dancers, one tempo, no missteps. At the start of combat, choose one willing ally you can perceive. You fight as a duet: once per round, when either of you is targeted by an attack, you can use your reaction to swap the two of you with no CE cost, following all normal Boogie Woogie range and legality rules. The attack targets whoever arrives in the targeted space; compare the original attack roll against their AC and resolve damage normally.

Rules integration

Edge cases and shared systems.

Rules priority: the reviewed rulings, explicit codex clarifications, and the level progression always control when original wording conflicts. Legacy Domain auto-hit language, action terminology, event timing, missing saves, and older scaling never override revised player rules.
Why Tier II: the ceiling is player ingenuity, but the technique changes positions rather than directly supplying damage, defenses, minions, or permanent resources.
