Instant positional exchange with escalating range, hostile swaps, multi-swaps, and deceptive tempo.

Medium • Reposition / control • Project pp. 57–59

Feature map

  • 1 — Boogie: self/ally/object swap.
  • 3 — Woogie: unwilling and remote pairs.
  • 6 — Clapping With Soul: range, cost, no-action use.
  • 11 — Round of Applause.
  • 18 — Standing Ovation.

Completed rules

  • One use exchanges exactly two targets you can perceive within range. Each destination must fit the arriving target. Worn or carried objects cannot be selected separately. The swap is teleportation, causes no opportunity attacks, and preserves facing, conditions, momentum, and altitude; a creature unsupported after the swap falls.
  • An eligible object is Small or larger and contains CE. Imbuing an ordinary object takes an action, costs 1 CE, lasts 1 minute, and does not make it a reusable magic item.
  • At level 3, every unwilling creature makes a Charisma save. If either member of a pair succeeds, that pair does not move and the CE is still spent. A creature that succeeds is immune to your unwilling swaps until the start of your next turn. A swapped unwilling creature loses its reaction until the start of its next turn.
  • The level-6 no-action use follows the once-per-turn legacy free-action rule. The normal action, bonus-action, or reaction options remain available, but no creature can be moved by your Boogie Woogie more than twice in one turn.
  • At level 11, choose two disjoint pairs among up to four targets and resolve each pair separately. A target cannot occur in both pairs. Failed or illegal pairs do not prevent the other pair.
  • Domain — Standing Ovation: range covers the barrier and ordinary swaps cost no CE but retain their timing and saves. At initiative 20, choose any number of disjoint legal pairs among chosen hostiles, willing creatures, and eligible objects; every pair swaps automatically without CE. A protected hostile makes a Charisma save and prevents its pair on a success. No target can appear in more than one pulse pair.

Power identity

Why Tier II: the ceiling is player ingenuity, but the technique changes positions rather than directly supplying damage, defenses, minions, or permanent resources.

Recovered character-build options

Innate Technique Feats 3

These are optional character-building feats. You must meet each listed prerequisite before selecting it.

Boogie Down

Standard

Prerequisite: Boogie

After you use Boogie, the first attack against you before the start of your next turn has disadvantage. Multiple swaps do not add more protected attacks.

Beguiling Tactics

High impact

Prerequisite: Woogie

An unwilling creature swapped by Woogie makes an Intelligence save; failure stuns it until the start of its next turn. After its first success it has advantage on later saves that encounter. As an action, fake-clap with Performance or Deception contested by Insight; on a win it has disadvantage on its next Beguiling save before end of your next turn.

Maximum: Best Friend

Pinnacle

Prerequisite: Round of Applause and CE score 20

At initiative choose one willing ally. Swapping that ally costs 0 CE. Its first attack after being swapped from an enemy’s reach expands critical range by 3. While you flank together, each of you adds PB damage to attacks and ends when combat does.

Boogie Woogie
You were born with a simple although tricky ability to swap places
with your opponents with a clap.
Cursed Energy
Starting at 1st level, you can harness your negative feelings into a
usable source of energy, called cursed energy. These feelings can be
harnessed into attacks or abilities. The number of cursed energy you
possess is based on your class, some classes use a modifier that
being charisma or wisdom (Player’s choice)
Your cursed energy DC is 8 + user cursed energy ability modifier +
proficiency bonus.
You regain all spent cursed energy on a long rest.
Boogie
At 1st level, you have created a unique technique to switch places
with your others, it's extremely simple but effective. You can switch
places with an ally or magical object within 30 feet of you by
clapping for 1 Cursed Energy. You may use this technique as an
action, bonus action, or reaction, however, it costs 2 additional
Cursed Energy if used as a reaction. In addition, you can imbue
cursed energy into an object making it into a common magic item for 1
minute.
Woogie
Starting at 3rd level, you have extended the grasp of your technique
as well as its versatility. Your Boogie gains the following bonuses:
● Hostile creatures may be transported
● You can make two creatures other than yourself swap places
● Enemies teleported are
disorientated and lose their reaction
Clapping With Soul
Beginning at 6th level, “The act of
applause is an acclamation of the soul”,
you have gained a deeper understanding
of your technique and how it can be
used, your Boogie gains the following
benefits:
● The range of this technique
extends to 45 feet.
● The Cursed Energy cost is reduced by 1 Cursed Energy for all
Boogie Woogie techniques.
● You can now use Boogie once per turn as a free action.
Round of Applause
When you reach 11th level, you have understood the true depths of
Boogie Woogie, your Boogie gains the following:
● The range of this technique extends to 60 feet
● When you switch places with an ally who was previously in range
of an enemy right before the switch, your next attack roll has
advantage, the same goes if you teleport an enemy into range or
an ally into range
● You may use Boogie on up to four creatures at once
Domain Expansion: Standing Ovation
If you reach 18th level, you have mastered the beat of Boogie
Woogie, you gain your domain expansion. While inside it, you may use
boogie as a free action an unlimited amount of times.
