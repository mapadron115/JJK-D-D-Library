Features & procedures

Properties & techniques

Exact player-facing procedures

Warded shell

While wearing this armor, you gain a +2 bonus to AC and resistance to necrotic damage. It can be any armor type.

Curse gradient

As a reaction to being hit by necrotic damage or a cursed technique, you can roll 4d4 and reduce the damage by that amount.

Rules integration

Edge cases and shared systems.

Campaign canon notes

Note: Curse gradient reduces damage after resistance applies (halve first, then subtract the 4d4). It triggers on the hit, so it works against damage that also carries other riders.
