Core law

What the technique actually does.

Zero CE / Anti-Energy

  • You have 0 CE maximum and cannot gain, steal, store, or spend CE. Effects that require a CE reserve treat you as having none.
  • Use the campaign Heavenly Restriction chassis for HP, martial dice, movement, and body improvements. This framework replaces only its Ideal/technique-like progression where necessary.
  • Anti-Energy has a pool equal to level + proficiency bonus. It returns on a short or long rest. It is not CE and cannot fuel normal cursed techniques.
  • Anti-Cursed swords are cursed tools that become blunt/ordinary against mundane flesh but exceptionally effective against CE constructs and techniques.

HR Die

The HR Die is the Heavenly Restriction martial-damage die from the Project class table (p. 136); it scales with character level — the full progression is given in the Level 1 entry below. It is not cursed energy: this character has 0 CE, and anti-energy abilities never spend a CE reserve. Use Heavenly Restriction class level when a multiclass exception is explicitly approved; otherwise your character level and class level are identical.

Full progression

Level 1–20 Progression

1Demon-Slayer Sword

Heavy greatsword, 2d6 bludgeoning/slashing. Against CE constructs, shikigami, barriers, or technique-created matter deal +1 HR Die. Reaction, spend 1 Anti-Energy to gain advantage on a save against an external CE effect by blocking with the flat.

HR Die progression: d6 at levels 1–4, d8 at levels 5–10, d10 at levels 11–16, d12 at levels 17–20.

3Black Form

Bonus action, spend 2 Anti-Energy, 1 minute: +10 speed, jump distance doubled, weapon attacks count as anti-technique. Once/turn on hit against an active technique user, force Concentration/Technique Focus check.

6Demon-Dweller Sword

Gain second sword. Action, 1 Anti-Energy: 60-ft anti-energy slash, ranged weapon attack, 2 HR Dice force. With willing ally's touch, it can borrow one damage type or cosmetic affinity from their technique for the slash without copying features.

9Demon-Destroyer Sword

Action, 2 Anti-Energy: touch one ongoing non-Domain CE effect, curse, transformation, mark, buff/debuff, or summoned control link and make Strength/Athletics vs source CE DC. Success ends one effect; cannot undo completed damage/death.

11Devil Union

Bonus action, 4 Anti-Energy, 1 minute, once/long rest: +20 speed, fly 40 ft, resistance to CE technique damage, and anti-cursed weapon hits suppress the target's technique reactions until next turn.

13Black Divider

Action, 2 Anti-Energy: enlarge Demon-Slayer into 20-ft line/arc, make one weapon attack against each creature/construct; CE constructs take +3 HR Dice.

15Demon-Slasher Katana

Selective 60-ft slash, action, 3 Anti-Energy: choose targets in a 60-ft line; make attack only against chosen creatures, 5 HR Dice slashing/force on hit, ignores mundane objects and allies.

18True Devil Union

Devil Union may be used twice/long rest and anti-cursed hits force a CE ability save; failure suppresses one named technique feature until start next turn. Domains still require anti-domain rules rather than swording them away automatically.

20Five-Leaf Completion

Anti-Energy maximum doubles. Start combat with PB Anti-Energy if lower. Once/round Demon-Destroyer costs 0 against a technique of 3 CE or less, and Devil Union no longer has a daily limit but still consumes Anti-Energy.

Pinnacle expression

Pinnacle / Maximum

Forbidden Tool Art — Infinite Slash Equinox (spend all remaining Anti-Energy, minimum 6): 120-ft selective line, one attack against each chosen CE-bearing creature/construct; on hit 8 HR Dice force/slashing and suppress one active Focus effect. Barriers take maximum dice. Once/long rest.

Feats

Technique Feats

Prerequisite: Demon-Slayer

Anti-Magic Swordsman

Your sword drinks curses aimed at others: when an ally within 5 ft must save against an external CE effect, you may use your reaction to interpose your anti-cursed sword — they use your save result in place of their own.

Prerequisite: Black Form

Bull Thrust

When you take the Dash action and move at least 20 feet in a straight line, you can use a bonus action to drive your anti-cursed sword into a creature you move adjacent to during the dash, making a single melee attack with the sword as the charge’s momentum carries the cut.

Prerequisite: Demon-Dweller

Sword Flight

Ride Demon-Slayer/Dweller for fly speed equal walking while Black Form is active.

Prerequisite: Devil Union

Liebe's Partner

When Devil Union ends, the departing devil's anti-magic discharges as a parting gift: as a reaction, you may end one ongoing CE effect on each hostile creature within 30 ft (as Demon-Destroyer, no check required).

Rules integration

Binding Rulings

This framework intentionally uses Heavenly Restriction rather than giving a no-CE character a contradictory Innate Technique.
Anti-Cursed effects suppress or cancel ongoing CE phenomena; they do not erase mundane physics or retroactively undo completed damage.
The PDF Heavenly Restriction already supports zero-CE martial progression, extreme movement, and bonus-action martial attacks; this framework layers the sword system on top rather than replacing the body chassis.
Celestial Body-style immunity to ordinary sure-hits still follows the campaign's HR rules; object-targeting Domains may still affect you.
