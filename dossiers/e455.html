Shikigami Feed

Shredded meal made from the ground-up bodies of medium grade curses, disgusting to you, medicine to your shikigami.

Field notes. Subject: rations for the war-dogs. Three truths govern it. First, it heals what the sorcerer cannot: an action to eat, and the shikigami regains 8d4+8 hit points, medicine for the bound servant, ground from the bodies of medium-grade curses. Second, the fed beast fights better: advantage on its very next attack roll or skill check, whichever comes first, even an army of shadows marches on its stomach. Third, the ration is not for the master: only a shikigami may eat it; sorcerers and other creatures gain nothing. Hence the maxim: feed the hound, and the hound remembers.

Cursed Meal

Action · No CE · A shikigami eats the meal to regain 8d4+8 hit points and gain advantage on its next attack roll or skill check (whichever comes first); shikigami only.

Shredded from the bodies of medium-grade curses, disgusting to you, medicine to the shikigami. A shikigami can eat this as an action to regain 8d4+8 hit points and gain advantage on the next attack roll or skill check it makes.

Campaign canon notes

Note: Only a shikigami can consume this meal, it has no listed effect for sorcerers or other creatures. The advantage applies to the shikigami's very next attack roll or skill check, whichever comes first.
