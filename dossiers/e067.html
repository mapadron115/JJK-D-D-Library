DJINN 6 • Ice / Stagnation

Valefor — Fox of Stagnation

Rank A
Canon master: SinbadBasis: Canon core • ice manipulation, Garufor techniques, molecular Stagnation

Weapon Equip • Level 5

Nine-Tailed Stagnation. Weapon Equip lets you freeze what your hands or weapon touch and shape ice instantly from nearby moisture.

Garufor Zairu

Action • 2 CE

Hurl a storm of ice beasts/picks in a 120-ft line, 20 ft wide. Dexterity save; 5 Djinn Dice cold and speed reduced by 20 ft until the end of your next turn on failure.

Stagnation

Reaction • 3 CE

When a creature, projectile, or visible effect moves within 120 ft, attempt to arrest its motion. Creature makes Constitution save; on failure its remaining movement becomes 0 and it cannot take reactions until its next turn. Mundane projectiles automatically freeze in place.

Garufor Kirestal

Action • 5 CE

Choose a creature within 180 ft or a 40-ft-radius point. Constitution save; 8 Djinn Dice cold. Failed targets are Restrained inside crystal ice; they repeat a Strength save at the end of each turn to break free.

Full Djinn Equip • Level 9

Fly 100 ft; cold resistance; ignore ice terrain. Hostile creatures within 30 ft lose 10 ft of speed. Once per round you may force a creature hit by your attack to make a Constitution save or lose reactions.

EXTREME MAGIC • LEVEL 13+

Palace of Still Winter

12 CE • Codex Extreme

Stop molecular motion across a 150-ft-radius zone within 300 ft. Constitution save; 16 Djinn Dice cold, half on success. Failure Stagnates the target: Restrained, Incapacitated, and unable to take reactions until the end of your next turn. Creatures failing by 5+ remain frozen for up to 1 minute, repeating the save at each turn’s end.

Anime-scale: Strategic scale: flash-freeze a harbor, city district, or army formation and lock the landscape into supernatural winter.
