Palimpsest

Record a prior layer of an object, location, or person and selectively restore pieces of that earlier state without becoming unrestricted time travel.

Power tierTier IV · Special Grade

Field notes. Subject: the archivist of what happened. Three truths govern her. First: the present is only the latest draft, Records capture objects, terrain, and positions at the instant of inscription, lasting minutes at first, an hour at height. Second: she revises selectively, Position Note returns a creature to its recorded space, Living Layer restores a wounded ally and strikes a fresh condition, Redaction removes one buff or aura from the page. Third: the First Draft is the whole page at once, the Maximum returns a thirty-foot radius toward its recorded layout, healing allies and unmaking hostile gains alike. Assess her correctly: she does not prevent your victories; she files them as errors and corrects them. Maxim: the eraser is mightier than the sword it deletes.

Full progression

Level Progression

1Revision Dice, Records, Position Note, Restore Object

Main resource

Passive · Records and Revision Dice are the drafts she keeps and the ink she spends.

The drafts she keeps, and the ink she spends to correct them.

Revision Dice

Passive · Revision Dice = PB + CE modifier (min 2), regained on a long rest · Die size scales d6/d8/d10/d12 at levels 1/6/11/18.

Her measure of red ink, bounded by skill and resolve, renewed only with rest, growing in weight with mastery.

Records

Passive · Maintain up to PB Records · A Record captures an unattended object, a 5-ft cube of terrain, or a creature’s position only at the instant of inscription · Records last 10 minutes at 1st level.

A Record is a draft preserved at the instant of inscription — object, terrain, or a creature's position — held against the present.

Position Note

Bonus action · 1 CE, 60 ft · Record a creature’s current space until start of your next turn · Reaction · 1 CE + one Revision Die · After it moves: a hostile makes a Dex save — on failure it returns to the recorded legal space, on success its remaining speed becomes 0 · Willing targets return automatically.

The margin note that moves the text: a recorded space, and a reaction that returns the hostile to it, or stops him mid-stride.

Restore Object

Action · 2 CE + one Revision Die · Restore a recorded unattended object or 5-ft terrain section toward its recorded state: HP/integrity equal to twice the die + twice your CE modifier · Can’t duplicate: if the recorded original still exists elsewhere, the restoration fails.

She reverts the object toward its recorded draft, integrity returned, but the technique cannot duplicate; if the original still exists, the revision fails.

3Persistent Margins —

Passive · Object/terrain Records last 1 hour; you may record an object held by a willing creature · When restoring a broken weapon/tool, also remove one mundane jam, bend, tear, or similar impairment.

Her drafts keep longer and reach further, and mundane impairments are struck from the page.

6Living Layer —

Action · 4 CE + 2 Revision Dice · Record a willing creature for 1 round; before the Record ends, restore it as a reaction: it heals both dice + CE modifier and may end one frightened, poisoned, grappled, or restrained condition gained after the Record · Each creature benefits once per short rest.

She extends the craft to the living page itself: a willing creature restored, healed, and one fresh condition excised.

11Redaction —

Action · 5 CE, 60 ft · Choose a creature or manifested technique effect; make a CE ability check contested by the creator’s CE check (or the target makes a CE save) · On success, suppress one temporary buff, summoned construct, aura, or movement-altering technique effect until end of your next turn · Can’t erase innate techniques, Domains, or permanent effects.

The editor's black bar: one temporary buff, summon, aura, or movement-alteration struck from the page; innate techniques and Domains are beyond the censor's reach.

18Maximum Technique: First Draft —

Maximum Technique · Action · 12 CE, once per long rest · Choose a 30-ft radius area you personally observed within the last minute: terrain and unattended objects return toward the recorded layout · Willing creatures may return to their recorded positions and heal 4 Revision Dice · Hostile creatures make a Dex save: on failure they return to their recorded position and lose one temporary buff gained since then · Never restores expended CE, class resources, Domain uses, or dead creatures.

The maximum: a chosen ground returned toward its recorded layout, allies healed and repositioned, hostiles unmade of their recent gains. What was spent stays spent; what died stays dead.

Innate Technique Feats

Technique Feats

Archivist’s Eye

She reads an object's revisions by touch, the alterations are footnotes to her, and she follows them. You can read an object's revisions: by touching an object for 1 minute, you learn whether it has been magically altered since you last touched it, and you gain advantage on checks to discern the alteration's nature.

Margin Walker

She lingers in the margin between records: untargetable, unacting, until the return. You can linger in the margin between records: when you use Position Note on yourself, you can delay your return until the start of your next turn. While in the margin you cannot be targeted or act; you then return as a free action.

Errata

Even a resisted revision leaves its mark: the creature's speed carries the subtraction of one Revision Die. When a creature succeeds on a save against Position Note or Redaction, it still subtracts one Revision Die from its speed until the end of the turn.

Rules integration

Table Rulings

Palimpsest restores recorded state; it does not reverse the entire timeline.
Restoration cannot duplicate consumables, ammunition, money, cursed tools, or creatures.
If a recorded destination is occupied or hazardous in a way the subject could not legally occupy, use the nearest legal unoccupied space.
