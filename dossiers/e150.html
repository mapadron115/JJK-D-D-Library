Utility Controller / Repositioner

Palimpsest

Record a prior layer of an object, location, or person and selectively restore pieces of that earlier state without becoming unrestricted time travel.

Power tierExceptional

The fiction. The prevailing theory holds that the technique is not inherited but remembered: the sorcerer's CE imprinted on a single object they could not bear to see ruined, and has been reproducing that instant of preservation ever since. Registry scholars classify it as a manuscript technique — reality as palimpsest, every layer a draft, and the user's energy the hand that refuses to let the latest draft be the last word.

Records & Revision Dice

Revision Dice

You have Revision Dice equal to your proficiency bonus + CE modifier (minimum 2), regained on a long rest. Die size scales d6/d8/d10/d12 at levels 1/6/11/18.

Records

You can maintain Records equal to your proficiency bonus. A Record captures a subject’s state at the instant of inscription. At 1st level, Records last 10 minutes and may capture an unattended object, a 5-foot cube of terrain, or a creature’s position only.

Position Note

Bonus Action • 1 CE • 60 feet. Record a creature’s current space until the start of your next turn. As a reaction after it moves, spend one Revision Die: a hostile target makes a Dexterity save. On failure it returns to the recorded legal space; on success its remaining speed becomes 0. Willing targets return automatically.

Restore Object

Action • 2 CE + one Revision Die. Restore a recorded unattended object or 5-foot terrain section toward its recorded state. Restore HP/integrity equal to twice the die + twice your CE modifier. This cannot duplicate an item: if the recorded original still exists elsewhere, the restoration fails.

3Persistent Margins —

object/terrain Records last 1 hour. You may record an object currently held by a willing creature. When restoring a broken weapon/tool, you may also remove one mundane jam, bend, tear, or similar impairment.

6Living Layer —

Action, 4 CE + 2 Revision Dice. Record a willing creature for 1 round. Before that Record ends, you may restore it as a reaction: it heals both dice + CE modifier and may end one frightened, poisoned, grappled, or restrained condition gained after the Record. Each creature may benefit once per short rest.

11Redaction —

Action, 5 CE. Choose a creature or manifested technique effect within 60 feet. Make a CE ability check contested by the creator’s CE ability check (or target makes a CE save). On success, suppress one temporary buff, summoned construct, aura, or movement-altering technique effect until the end of your next turn. You do not erase innate techniques, Domains, or permanent effects.

18Maximum Technique: First Draft —

Action, 12 CE, once per long rest. Choose a 30-foot-radius area you personally observed within the last minute. Terrain and unattended objects return toward that recorded layout. Willing creatures may return to their recorded positions and heal 4 Revision Dice. Hostile creatures make a Dexterity save; on failure they return to their recorded position and lose one temporary buff gained since then. This never restores expended CE, class resources, Domain uses, or dead creatures.

Innate Technique Feats

Technique Feats

Archivist’s Eye

You can read an object's revisions: by touching an object for 1 minute, you learn whether it has been magically altered since you last touched it, and you gain advantage on checks to discern the alteration's nature.

Margin Walker

You can linger in the margin between records: when you use Position Note on yourself, you can delay your return until the start of your next turn. While in the margin you cannot be targeted or act; you then return as a free action.

Errata

When a creature succeeds on a save against Position Note or Redaction, it still subtracts one Revision Die from its speed until the end of the turn.

Rules integration

Table Rulings

Palimpsest restores recorded state; it does not reverse the entire timeline.
Restoration cannot duplicate consumables, ammunition, money, cursed tools, or creatures.
If a recorded destination is occupied or hazardous in a way the subject could not legally occupy, use the nearest legal unoccupied space.
