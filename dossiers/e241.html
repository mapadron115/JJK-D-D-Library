Field notes. Subject: the instant of crossing, kept. Three truths govern the Knife. First, every cut is an account: striking a solid unattended surface banks Cut, to a maximum of PB, for the combat's length. Second, the door is borrowed twice: Door-Slit spends Cut on a bonus action for paired slits within 30 ft, Tiny objects and weapon tips slip through, while Step Through spends 3 CE and 2 Cut to open creature-sized passages until your next turn, each entry costing ten feet of movement. Third, the threshold has manners: it ignores cover but still demands a line, and it will not cross Domain barriers or sealed soul spaces. Mark this: a threshold only opens where the wall was already willing to be passed.

Rules integration

Cursed-tool attunement

Campaign canon

Cursed-tool attunement: you have PB attunement slots (2–6). This tool occupies one slot and requires a one-hour ritual. Attunement ends voluntarily over a short rest, on death, or when you replace it while over capacity. Distance does not break attunement. Cursed-tool attunement is separate from magic-item attunement.
Balance & counterplay. Portal utility stays tactical rather than becoming unrestricted teleportation.
