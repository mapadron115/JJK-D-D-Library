Features & procedures

Properties & techniques

Exact player-facing procedures

Door-Slit

bonus, 1 CE spend Cut: create paired slits on two surfaces within 30 ft until end turn; Tiny objects/weapon tips can pass.

Step Through

3 CE and 2 Cut: create creature-sized paired openings within 30 ft until start next turn; entering costs 10 ft movement.

Edge Case

attack through a slit ignores cover but still requires line from one opening to target.

Feature

Cannot cross Domain barriers, sealed soul spaces, or ranges beyond stated distance.

The fiction. The prevailing theory holds that the Threshold Knife is not a blade that cuts doors, but a blade that remembers being one — registry scholars theorize its forging poured a sorcerer's CE into the exact instant of crossing a threshold, and the steel has been replaying that instant ever since. Each paired slit is the same doorway, borrowed twice. It cannot cross Domains or sealed soul spaces because a threshold only opens where a wall was already willing to be passed.

Rules integration

Cursed-tool attunement

Campaign canon

Cursed-tool attunement: you have PB attunement slots (2–6). This tool occupies one slot and requires a one-hour ritual. Attunement ends voluntarily over a short rest, on death, or when you replace it while over capacity. Distance does not break attunement. Cursed-tool attunement is separate from magic-item attunement.
Balance & counterplay. Portal utility stays tactical rather than becoming unrestricted teleportation.
