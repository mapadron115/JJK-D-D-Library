Cursewrought Weapon

A Grade 3 cursed weapon, any ammunition or melee weapon.

FormWeapon (Any Ammunition or Melee Weapon)
GradeGrade 3
AttunementNot required

Field notes. Subject: a blade with one perfect purpose, and a talisman that never blinks. Three truths govern the weapon. First, it knows its prey: whenever it hits a Construct, a cursed corpse among them, or an object, the inlaid talisman floods the strike with cursed energy and the hit becomes a critical hit, a specialist's instrument, honed for throats of clay and stone. Second, the judgment is automatic: no action, no cursed energy, no command, the talisman decides for itself, because the curse knows its own kind better than any hand. Third, it asks nothing to wear: no attunement, and any melee weapon or ammunition may carry the inlay. A weapon that knows one thing perfectly is worth an armory of maybes; point it at what it was made to unmake.

Passive

Talisman-Imbued Strike

Passive · Hits against Constructs (e.g. Cursed Corpses) and objects are critical hits.

An inlaid talisman automatically imbues the weapon with cursed energy. Whenever this weapon or piece of ammunition hits a Construct (such as a Cursed Corpse) or an object, the hit is a critical hit.
