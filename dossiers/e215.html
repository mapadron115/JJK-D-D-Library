Graveyard Shift

1d8 slashing/bludgeoning versatile d10. Striking earth can create Grave Plots, 5-ft squares, max PB.

FormWorkman's shovel A gravedigger's memory made manifest, consecration as labor, not prayer.
RoleTerrain / anti-undead It plants hurried holy ground wherever the fight stands, and punishes what walks out of the earth.
EnginePlot Five-foot squares of claimed earth: difficult for the hostile, fatal for the walking dead.
Attunement1 hour* An hour's ritual, the shovel must trust you with its old job.
Features & procedures

Properties & techniques

Exact player-facing procedures

Dig In

One CE, replacing an attack: strike the earth and raise a Plot within reach, difficult terrain for hostiles, lasting until the long rest or its replacement. The tool knows its work; give it ground and it gives you ground. replace attack, 1 CE: create Plot within reach; difficult terrain to hostiles. Plots last until you finish a long rest or replace them (invented).

Six Feet

The pinned are buried: a hostile knocked prone in a Plot is speed zero until its next turn starts. The earth it lies on has already been measured for it. hostile knocked prone in Plot is also speed 0 until start next turn.

Night Crew

Two CE, a bonus: move between unoccupied Plots within sixty feet, the crew travels through its own diggings unseen. The graveyard is smaller than it looks when you know the tunnels. bonus, 2 CE: teleport from one Plot to another within 60 ft if both unoccupied.

Feature

The old job, still honored: cursed spirits and undead-like summons entering a Plot take a die of radiant or force, once a turn. It knows exactly what belongs in the earth, and it is offended to see it walking around. Cursed spirits and undead-like summons entering a Plot take 1 Tool Die radiant/force once/turn.

Field notes. Subject: a laborer. Three truths govern it. First, its cursed energy learned consecration as labor, not prayer: a shovel that spent years over open graves, planting hurried holy ground the way a workman plants fence posts. Second, the Plots are honest terrain: difficult for the hostile, a snare for the prone, a road for the wielder, the battlefield reshaped by an hour's digging. Third, the old job persists: the walking dead take radiant or force for crossing claimed earth, because the tool knows what belongs in the ground. Mark this: it is weak where no ground can be marked, a gravedigger with no earth is only a man with a shovel. Give it dirt, and it gives you doctrine.

Rules integration

Cursed-tool attunement

Campaign canon

Cursed-tool attunement: you have PB attunement slots (2–6). This tool occupies one slot and requires a one-hour ritual. Attunement ends voluntarily over a short rest, on death, or when you replace it while over capacity. Distance does not break attunement. Cursed-tool attunement is separate from magic-item attunement.
Balance & counterplay. Needs prepared ground and is weak on maps where digging/marking surfaces is impossible.
