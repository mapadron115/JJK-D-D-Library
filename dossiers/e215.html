Features & procedures

Properties & techniques

Exact player-facing procedures

Dig In

replace attack, 1 CE: create Plot within reach; difficult terrain to hostiles. Plots last until you finish a long rest or replace them (invented).

Six Feet

hostile knocked prone in Plot is also speed 0 until start next turn.

Night Crew

bonus, 2 CE: teleport from one Plot to another within 60 ft if both unoccupied.

Feature

Cursed spirits and undead-like summons entering a Plot take 1 Tool Die radiant/force once/turn.

The fiction. Registry tool-scholars describe Graveyard Shift as a gravedigger's memory made manifest: a workman's shovel that spent so many years over open graves its CE learned consecration as a labor, not a prayer. The Grave Plots are hurried holy ground, and the anti-undead rider is simply the tool's old job — it knows exactly what belongs in the earth, and it is offended to see it walking around.

Rules integration

Cursed-tool attunement

Campaign canon

Cursed-tool attunement: you have PB attunement slots (2–6). This tool occupies one slot and requires a one-hour ritual. Attunement ends voluntarily over a short rest, on death, or when you replace it while over capacity. Distance does not break attunement. Cursed-tool attunement is separate from magic-item attunement.
Balance & counterplay. Needs prepared ground and is weak on maps where digging/marking surfaces is impossible.
