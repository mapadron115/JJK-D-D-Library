Umbral Witchcraft

Tier V · CalamityWitch Time / Wicked Weaves / Infernal Demons

Core Engine — Witch Tempo. Gain 1 Tempo (max PB) when an attack misses you by 5+, you succeed a Dex save for no damage, or you perfect-dodge with a reaction feature. Tempo fuels Witch Time and Infernal finishers.

Lv.FeatureRules
1Bullet ArtsWeapons attached to hands/feet. Once/turn after melee attack make a 30-ft ranged attack for 1d6 piercing without ability modifier.
3Witch TimeReaction after avoiding an attack/save, spend 1 Tempo: attacker speed 0, no reactions, and you gain advantage against it until end next turn.
6Wicked Weave2 CE after two hits in same turn: giant infernal limb strikes, 3d10 force and Str save or launch 20 ft.
9Beast WithinBonus action 1 CE: transform movement form for round—Panther (speed +30), Crow (fly 50), or Bat (reaction resistance to one hit).
11Infernal Summon4 CE, action: summon one demon attack rather than full companion—Gomorrah bite 6d10, Madama punch 5d10 + launch, Phantasmaraneae 20-ft fire burst 5d8.
13Demon Masquerade5 CE, 1 minute: choose bound demon; fly/climb as appropriate, resistance one type, attacks +1d10 and a unique movement trait.
15Umbran ClimaxSpend 3 Tempo: for one turn every qualifying melee hit manifests Wicked Weave for +1d10 without CE; maximum once per attack.
18Queen Sheba ContractOnce/long rest, 10 CE + all Tempo: colossal infernal strike, 60-ft line or 30-ft burst, Str save, 12d12 force/fire and hurled 60 ft on failure.
20Witch Without EqualTempo max 10; first Witch Time each round costs 0 Tempo if triggered by a natural 20 save or attack missing by 10+. Maintain two Demon Masquerade traits simultaneously.
Maximum — Climax: Infernal Finale (10 CE + 5 Tempo): choose three different Infernal Summon attacks and resolve each against different targets or areas. A creature can be hit by at most two.

Feats

Moon of Mahaa-Kalaa: reaction parry melee for +2 AC.Bat Within: failed dodge can spend 1 Tempo to halve damage.Accessory Witch: bind one extra magical accessory effect.Pure Platinum: begin boss combat with 2 Tempo.
Shared rulings: Technique DC = 8 + PB + CE ability modifier. Direct rolled attacks may crit and use the campaign Black Flash procedure; autonomous hazards and Domain events cannot. Maximum Output may modify ordinary supported features, never named Maxima/Domains. Two-technique characters use the campaign's agreed character-level progression.
