Umbral Witchcraft

Witch Time / Wicked Weaves / Infernal Demons

Power tierTier V · Calamity
Core law

What the technique actually does.

Core Engine — Witch Tempo. Gain 1 Tempo (max PB) when an attack misses you by 5+, you succeed a Dex save for no damage, or you perfect-dodge with a reaction feature. Tempo fuels Witch Time and Infernal finishers.

The fiction. Jujutsu scholars classify Umbral Witchcraft as a perfect dodge remembered too many times: the user's CE imprinted on the fraction of a second before an attack that should have killed them, and now it reproduces that moment every time danger arrives. Witch Time is not the world slowing — registry notes insist it is the sorcerer's perception refusing to leave the one second in which they survived. The infernal finishers are simply what the demons agreed to in exchange for a host who never gets hit.

Full progression

Level 1 through 20.

L1Bullet Arts

Weapons attached to hands/feet. Once/turn after melee attack make a 30-ft ranged attack for 1d6 piercing without ability modifier.

L3Witch Time

Reaction after avoiding an attack/save, spend 1 Tempo: attacker speed 0, no reactions, and you gain advantage against it until end next turn.

L6Wicked Weave

2 CE after two hits in same turn: giant infernal limb strikes, 3d10 force and Str save or launch 20 ft.

L9Beast Within

Bonus action 1 CE: transform movement form for round—Panther (speed +30), Crow (fly 50), or Bat (reaction resistance to one hit).

L11Infernal Summon

4 CE, action: summon one demon attack rather than full companion—Gomorrah bite 6d10, Madama punch 5d10 + launch, Phantasmaraneae 20-ft fire burst 5d8.

L13Demon Masquerade

5 CE, 1 minute: choose bound demon; fly/climb as appropriate, resistance one type, attacks +1d10 and a unique movement trait.

L15Umbran Climax

Spend 3 Tempo: for one turn every qualifying melee hit manifests Wicked Weave for +1d10 without CE; maximum once per attack.

L18Queen Sheba Contract

Once/long rest, 10 CE + all Tempo: colossal infernal strike, 60-ft line or 30-ft burst, Str save, 12d12 force/fire and hurled 60 ft on failure.

L20Witch Without Equal

Tempo max 10; first Witch Time each round costs 0 Tempo if triggered by a natural 20 save or attack missing by 10+. Maintain two Demon Masquerade traits simultaneously.

Pinnacle expression

Maximum, Domain, or equivalent.

Maximum — Climax: Infernal Finale (10 CE + 5 Tempo): choose three different Infernal Summon attacks and resolve each against different targets or areas. A creature can be hit by at most two.

Feats

Build-defining options.

Moon of Mahaa-Kalaa

When a creature misses you with a melee attack, you may spend your reaction and 1 Tempo to parry the blow into another creature within 5 ft of you: reroll the attack against that creature.

Bat Within

When you take damage from an effect you could see, you may spend 1 Tempo as a reaction to burst into a swarm of bats: you take half damage and immediately move 15 ft without provoking, reforming at the destination.

Accessory Witch

Your accessories drink your Witch Time: once per turn, when you trigger Witch Time, you may also activate one accessory's effect as part of the same reaction without spending its normal action.

Pure Platinum

Your debut is flawless: the first time each combat you trigger Witch Time, you may extend its effects to one additional creature within 30 ft (speed 0, no reactions) until the end of your next turn.

Rules integration

Edge cases and shared systems.

Shared rulings: Technique DC = 8 + PB + CE ability modifier. Direct rolled attacks may crit and use the campaign Black Flash procedure; autonomous hazards and Domain events cannot. Maximum Output may modify ordinary supported features, never named Maxima/Domains. Two-technique characters use the campaign's agreed character-level progression.
