Conduit Mimic

Tier IV · Special GradePower absorption / Smoke / Neon / Video / Concrete

Core Engine — Sources. You maintain one active Conduit Mode. Switching requires touching/absorbing a meaningful source of that Mode as a bonus action. At higher levels you can bank sources for rapid switching.

Maximum — Conduit Symphony (10 CE, expend all four core caches): Smoke, Neon, Video, and Concrete erupt in a 40-ft radius: Dex save 12d8 mixed force/fire/radiant/bludgeoning; failure also blinded or restrained (your choice) until next turn.

Shared rulings: Technique DC = 8 + PB + CE ability modifier. Direct rolled attacks may crit and use the campaign Black Flash procedure; autonomous hazards and Domain events cannot. Maximum Output may modify ordinary supported features, never named Maxima/Domains. Two-technique characters use the campaign's agreed character-level progression.
Fully specified level-15 finishers

Karmic Finishers

Each finisher requires one action, 6 CE, and its matching active Mode; each Mode’s finisher is usable once per short rest. Use your CE save DC.

Smoke Orbital Drop

Point within 120 feet; 30-foot-radius area. Dexterity save: 8d10 fire damage and prone on failure; half damage and no prone on success.

Neon Radiant Sweep

90-foot-long, 10-foot-wide line. Dexterity save: 8d10 radiant damage and blinded until the end of your next turn on failure; half damage with no blindness on success.

Video Angel / Demon Barrage

Point within 120 feet; 20-foot-radius area. Dexterity save: 8d10 force damage and no reactions until the end of your next turn on failure; half damage and no reaction loss on success.

Concrete Fortress

Point within 90 feet; 20-foot-radius area. Dexterity save: 8d10 bludgeoning damage and restrained until the end of your next turn on failure; half damage and no restraint on success. A raised rampart remains for 1 minute, grants half cover, has AC 15 and HP equal to 5 × your character level, and disappears at 0 HP.
