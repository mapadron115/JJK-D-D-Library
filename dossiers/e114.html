Core law

What the technique actually does.

Core Engine — Sources. You maintain one active Conduit Mode. Switching requires touching/absorbing a meaningful source of that Mode as a bonus action.

The fiction. Jujutsu scholars classify Conduit Mimic as an imprinting that never fixed: the user's cursed energy never settled into a single expression, so it learned to borrow everyone else's — smoke, neon, video, concrete, each absorbed through touch because the technique has no shape of its own to stand on. The cached charges and karmic finishers are not theft; they are the closest this energy ever comes to remembering itself.

Full progression

Level 1 through 20.

1Smoke

Smoke Shot 60 ft, 2d8 fire; Smoke Dash bonus action 1 CE, teleport-like 20-ft movement through gaps.

3Conduit Absorption

After 1 minute contact with willing/incapacitated supernatural elemental user, make CE check. Success can unlock a GM-approved Mode after milestone; cannot copy Domains, innate passives, or whole CTs.

6Neon

Precision Beam 90 ft, 3d8 radiant; Neon Dash 40 ft including walls. Crit against marked weak point restrains speed to 0 until next turn.

9Video

Video Blade 4d8 force at 60 ft; bonus action invisibility until attack/end next turn for 2 CE; wings glide/fly 40 for round.

11Concrete

Concrete Barrage 4d10 bludgeoning at 60 ft; +2 AC while active; Boulder Dash moves 30 ft and may knock prone.

13Source Cache

Store one charge of each unlocked Mode. Spend cache to switch anywhere without environmental source; refill by draining matching source.

15Karmic Finishers

Each Mode gains 6 CE finisher: Smoke Orbital Drop, Neon Radiant Sweep, Video Angel/Demon Barrage, Concrete Fortress. Each deals exactly 8d10 damage with its own fully specified area, saving throw, control rider, and duration — detailed below. Each is usable once per short rest per Mode.

Each finisher requires one action, 6 CE, and its matching active Mode; each Mode’s finisher is usable once per short rest. Use your CE save DC.

Smoke Orbital Drop

Point within 120 feet; 30-foot-radius area. Dexterity save: 8d10 fire damage and prone on failure; half damage and no prone on success.

Neon Radiant Sweep

90-foot-long, 10-foot-wide line. Dexterity save: 8d10 radiant damage and blinded until the end of your next turn on failure; half damage with no blindness on success.

Video Angel / Demon Barrage

Point within 120 feet; 20-foot-radius area. Dexterity save: 8d10 force damage and no reactions until the end of your next turn on failure; half damage and no reaction loss on success.

Concrete Fortress

Point within 90 feet; 20-foot-radius area. Dexterity save: 8d10 bludgeoning damage and restrained until the end of your next turn on failure; half damage and no restraint on success. A raised rampart remains for 1 minute, grants half cover, has AC 15 and HP equal to 5 × your character level, and disappears at 0 HP.

18Hybrid Conduit

For 4 CE and two cached sources, combine two Modes for 1 minute; gain both movement/passive traits, but attacks remain from one Mode at a time.

20Second Son

Maintain two Modes freely after unlocking Hybrid. Source Cache holds two charges each. Once/long rest absorb one hostile elemental technique used on you and negate half its damage while gaining its damage type as a temporary Mode until combat ends. This temporary Mode grants the damage type and basic use only, under the level-3 copy exclusions (no Domains, innate passives, or whole cursed techniques).

Features & procedures
Pinnacle expression

Maximum, Domain, or equivalent.

Maximum — Conduit Symphony (10 CE, expend all four core caches): Smoke, Neon, Video, and Concrete erupt in a 40-ft radius: Dex save 12d8 mixed force/fire/radiant/bludgeoning; failure also blinded or restrained (your choice) until next turn.

Feats

Build-defining options.

Smoke & Mirrors

When you use Smoke Dash, you may leave a 10-ft-radius cloud of thick smoke in the space you departed: it heavily obscures the area until the start of your next turn. The first attack you make before the cloud fades has advantage if its target cannot see you.

Neon Runner

You run through enemies as light: when you Neon Dash, you may pass through hostile creatures' spaces — each must make a Dex save or take 2d8 radiant damage.

Digital Angel

When you activate Video flight, you may spend 2 CE to hard-light the wings into blades for the round: you may make one Video Blade attack at any point during the movement, and attacking does not end the flight early.

Concrete Skin

When you take bludgeoning, piercing, or slashing damage, you may spend 1 CE as a reaction to petrify the wound shut: reduce the damage by 1d10 + your CE modifier, and you cannot be moved against your will until the start of your next turn.

Rules integration

Edge cases and shared systems.

Shared rulings: Technique DC = 8 + PB + CE ability modifier. Direct rolled attacks may crit and use the campaign Black Flash procedure; autonomous hazards and Domain events cannot. Maximum Output may modify ordinary supported features, never named Maxima/Domains. Two-technique characters use the campaign's agreed character-level progression.
