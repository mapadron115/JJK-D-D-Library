Conduit Mimic

Power tierTier IV · Special Grade
Core law

What the technique actually does.

Core Engine — Sources. One Mode at a time: that is the leash on the borrower. He may be any element, but never two — until he learns to pay for the exception. You maintain one active Conduit Mode. Switching requires touching/absorbing a meaningful source of that Mode as a bonus action.

Field notes. Subject: a man with no signature. Three truths govern him. First: his technique is an absence wearing other people's clothes — smoke, neon, video, concrete — each absorbed by touch because he has nothing of his own to stand on. Second: he may borrow, but never steal whole — no Domains, no innate passives, no complete cursed techniques; the archive loans him instruments, never the symphony. Third: preparation is his only armor — the cache he filled yesterday is the arsenal he fights with today. Classify him correctly: he is not the strongest element in the room. He is the room, briefly convinced it is an element.

Full progression

Level 1 through 20.

1Smoke

Smoke is the first disguise because it is the oldest alibi: be somewhere else before the question is finished. Twenty feet of gone. Smoke Shot (action): ranged CE attack, 60 ft, 2d8 fire; Smoke Dash bonus action 1 CE, teleport-like 20-ft movement through gaps.

3Conduit Absorption

The borrowing begins with a handshake — a minute of contact, a check of the will, and the technique learns a new shape it may wear. It will never learn a whole soul. After 1 minute contact with willing/incapacitated supernatural elemental user, make CE check. Success can unlock a GM-approved Mode after milestone; cannot copy Domains, innate passives, or whole CTs.

6Neon

Light with intent. The precision beam trades Smoke's mercy for ninety feet of exactness — and against a marked weak point, it stops the enemy cold. Precision Beam (action): ranged CE attack, 90 ft, 3d8 radiant; Neon Dash 40 ft including walls. Crit against marked weak point restrains speed to 0 until next turn.

9Video

The broadcast made flesh. He vanishes into the static between channels, and his wings are only suggestions of flight — until they cut. Video Blade (action): ranged CE attack, 60 ft, 4d8 force; bonus action invisibility until attack/end next turn for 2 CE; wings glide/fly 40 for round.

11Concrete

The heaviest answer. Concrete does not dodge; it endures, and everything it moves through learns to be flat. Concrete Barrage (action): ranged CE attack, 60 ft, 4d10 bludgeoning; +2 AC while active; Boulder Dash moves 30 ft; a creature you move through makes a Strength save or is knocked prone.

13Source Cache

The archive goes in the pocket. One charge of each Mode, carried everywhere — the sorcerer who never again needs to be near the source. Store one charge of each unlocked Mode. Spend cache to switch anywhere without environmental source; refill by draining a matching source: refilling one charge requires 1 minute of contact with a matching environmental source, or an action to drain a willing or incapacitated elemental user per Conduit Absorption.

15Karmic Finishers

Every borrowed element has a final argument. Six CE each, once per short rest per Mode — the finishers are the price of fluency. Each Mode gains 6 CE finisher: Smoke Orbital Drop, Neon Radiant Sweep, Video Angel/Demon Barrage, Concrete Fortress. Each deals exactly 8d10 damage with its own fully specified area, saving throw, control rider, and duration — detailed below. Each is usable once per short rest per Mode.

Each finisher requires one action, 6 CE, and its matching active Mode; each Mode’s finisher is usable once per short rest. Use your CE save DC.

Smoke Orbital Drop

Point within 120 feet; 30-foot-radius area. Dexterity save: 8d10 fire damage and prone on failure; half damage and no prone on success.

Neon Radiant Sweep

90-foot-long, 10-foot-wide line. Dexterity save: 8d10 radiant damage and blinded until the end of your next turn on failure; half damage with no blindness on success.

Video Angel / Demon Barrage

Point within 120 feet; 20-foot-radius area. Dexterity save: 8d10 force damage and no reactions until the end of your next turn on failure; half damage and no reaction loss on success.

Concrete Fortress

Point within 90 feet; 20-foot-radius area. Dexterity save: 8d10 bludgeoning damage and restrained until the end of your next turn on failure; half damage and no restraint on success. A raised rampart remains for 1 minute, grants half cover, has AC 15 and HP equal to 5 × your character level, and disappears at 0 HP.

18Hybrid Conduit

Two coats worn at once. For four CE he stops choosing between his borrowed selves — though even then, his hands may throw only one element at a time. For 4 CE and two cached sources, combine two Modes for 1 minute; gain both movement/passive traits, but attacks remain from one Mode at a time.

20Second Son

The masterpiece of the borrower: two Modes worn freely, two charges cached each — and once per day he catches an enemy's own technique out of the air and tries it on for size. Note the limit: the damage type and basic use only. The soul stays its owner's. Maintain two Modes freely after unlocking Hybrid. Source Cache holds two charges each. Once/long rest absorb one hostile elemental technique used on you and negate half its damage while gaining its damage type as a temporary Mode until combat ends. This temporary Mode grants the damage type and basic use only, under the level-3 copy exclusions (no Domains, innate passives, or whole cursed techniques).

Features & procedures
Pinnacle expression

Maximum, Domain, or equivalent.

Maximum — Conduit Symphony (10 CE, expend all four core caches; action): Smoke, Neon, Video, and Concrete erupt in a 40-ft radius: Dex save 12d8 mixed force/fire/radiant/bludgeoning; failure also blinded or restrained (your choice) until next turn.

Feats

Build-defining options.

Smoke & Mirrors

He exits through himself. The departed space keeps a cloud as his alibi, and the first strike from inside it is struck from nowhere. When you use Smoke Dash, you may leave a 10-ft-radius cloud of thick smoke in the space you departed: it heavily obscures the area until the start of your next turn. The first attack you make before the cloud fades has advantage if its target cannot see you.

Neon Runner

Light does not ask permission. When he dashes as neon, the field itself becomes a corridor — and the corridor burns whoever stands in it. You run through enemies as light: when you Neon Dash, you may pass through hostile creatures' spaces — each must make a Dex save or take 2d8 radiant damage.

Digital Angel

The wings remember they were once blades. Two CE, and the broadcast grows teeth that do not ground him. When you activate Video flight, you may spend 2 CE to hard-light the wings into blades for the round: you may make one Video Blade attack at any point during the movement, and attacking does not end the flight early.

Concrete Skin

He petrifies the wound shut — concrete's oldest lesson: the best response to being broken is to become the break. When you take bludgeoning, piercing, or slashing damage, you may spend 1 CE as a reaction to petrify the wound shut: reduce the damage by 1d10 + your CE modifier, and you cannot be moved against your will until the start of your next turn.

Rules integration

Edge cases and shared systems.

Shared rulings: Technique DC = 8 + PB + CE ability modifier. Direct rolled attacks may crit and use the campaign Black Flash procedure; autonomous hazards and Domain events cannot. Maximum Output may modify ordinary supported features, never named Maxima/Domains. Two-technique characters use the campaign's agreed character-level progression.
