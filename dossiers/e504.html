Cursed Spirit — Field Entry

Rainbow Dragon

Field notes. Subject: a cathedral that breathes. Three truths govern the Rainbow Dragon. First: its hide is a sermon on humility. Prismatic scales turn nonmagical blades aside and refract the strike back as radiant light, a grade-two cursed tool splintered on impact, and the dragon barely noticed. Second: it is passive until threatened, which is why it is so deadly. It does not posture, does not warn, does not grant second chances; it merely waits, and an agitated dragon's patience is a door already closed. Third: its roar fractures the air like glass, a sixty-foot cone of thunder that deafens what it does not shatter. Assess it correctly: this is not a beast to be hunted. It is a building you do not enter unless the building has already decided to let you.

Record

Classification & Risk

An Imaginary Cursed Spirit, Grade 1 (CR 8–16 band). Nests in abandoned temples or deep caverns steeped in old grudges. Surprisingly passive until threatened; most of an escort party was slaughtered when it was agitated.

Stats

Ability scores & traits

STR 24 (+7) · DEX 12 (+1) · CON 22 (+6) · INT 8 (−1) · WIS 14 (+2) · CHA 12 (+1). HP 240 (23d12 + 92), AC 19, speed 40 ft., fly 60 ft. Prismatic Hide: resistance to bludgeoning, piercing, and slashing damage from nonmagical attacks; a creature that hits it with a melee weapon attack takes 1d6 radiant damage as the scales refract the force back. Passive Nature: will not attack unless threatened or approached aggressively.

Actions

Actions

Multiattack. One Bite and one Tail attack. Bite. Melee Attack: +11 to hit, reach 10 ft. Hit: 3d10 + 6 piercing damage. Tail. Melee Attack: +11 to hit, reach 15 ft. Hit: 2d12 + 6 bludgeoning damage. Shattering Roar (recharge 5–6). A 60-ft. cone: each creature there must succeed on a DC 17 Constitution saving throw or take 6d10 thunder damage and be deafened for 1 minute (half damage, not deafened, on success) as its roar fractures the air like glass.

Risk

Special

A fully trained sorcerer struck it head-on with a grade-two weapon and the cursed tool splintered on impact — the dragon barely acknowledged the attack. Do not engage an agitated Rainbow Dragon without overwhelming force and a retreat plan; observation from a safe distance is the recommended study method.

Campaign canon notes

Field rule — perception: At Grade 2 and above this spirit’s cursed energy is dense enough that no Invisibility trait is assigned; standard perception applies. It remains invisible to ordinary non-sorcerers.
