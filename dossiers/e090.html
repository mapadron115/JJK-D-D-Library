Field notes. Subject: the Djinn of refusal. Other Djinn answer force with force; Dantalion's cursed energy does not block what arrives, it relocates the arrival, drawing paired circles that insist two distant points were always one. Three truths govern him. First, his generosity is structural: Short Transfer moves you and one willing creature 120 ft, or carries a Large object instead, the door held open. Second, his defense is misdirection: Damage Transfer redirects an attack, line, cone, or burst to an unoccupied point within 300 ft, and even Extremes meet an opposed check, damage reduced 3 Djinn Dice on a loss. Third, the Palace Between Moments: a 200-ft zone whose failures are banished to a pocket space for a minute or relocated within 1,000 ft, or a catastrophe redirected after an opposed check. Mark this: he does not win the fight. He moves it somewhere else.
