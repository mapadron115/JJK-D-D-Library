DJINN 71 • Light / Space-Time

Dantalion — Gatekeeper of Space

Rank S+
Canon master: Koumei RenBasis: Canon core • Dante Al-Thais / Big Dipper Transfer Circle, damage transfer, mountain teleportation

Weapon Equip • Level 5

Big Dipper Transfer Fan. Weapon Equip lets the user draw linked transfer constellations with the vessel and fold distance between them.

Short Transfer

Bonus Action • 2 CE

Open a pair of circles and teleport yourself plus one willing creature up to 120 ft. You can instead move a projectile/object of Large size or smaller.

Damage Transfer

Reaction • 4 CE

When an attack, line, cone, or burst affects a point within 120 ft, open paired circles and redirect it to an unoccupied point within 300 ft. For an Extreme, Domain effect, or boss technique, make an opposed technique check; on a loss reduce the damage by 3 Djinn Dice instead.

Dante Al-Thais

Action • 6 CE

Draw a Big Dipper Transfer Circle up to 100 ft across. Teleport up to ten willing creatures, one Gargantuan object, or a 60-ft-radius section of terrain to another circle within 1 mile. Unwilling creatures in transported terrain make Charisma saves to remain behind. Using it on mountain-scale mass imposes Extreme Fatigue even without an Extreme.

Full Djinn Equip • Level 9

Fly 110 ft; hover; once per turn teleport 40 ft without an action after using a Djinn ability. Transfer circles can be maintained simultaneously up to PB and may be used as doors by allies. Spatial barriers do not block your teleport unless they explicitly seal teleportation/space-time magic.

EXTREME MAGIC • LEVEL 13+

Palace Between Moments

16 CE • Codex Extreme

Choose a 200-ft-radius zone within 500 ft. Willing creatures/objects are automatically transferred; unwilling creatures make Charisma saves. On failure choose: banish them into a stable pocket space for up to 1 minute (repeat save at end of turns) or relocate them anywhere within 1,000 ft. You may instead use the Extreme to redirect one battlefield-scale attack or environmental catastrophe to an empty destination after an opposed technique check.

Anime-scale: Strategic scale: relocate an army, fortress section, or mountain; remove a catastrophe from one battlefield and make space itself the answer.
