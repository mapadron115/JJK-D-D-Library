Features & procedures

Exact player-facing procedures.

Weapon Equip • Level 5

Tectonic Crown.

The weapon is wrapped in a private gravity. Strikes buckle ground and pin targets, the blade no longer cuts so much as sentences, and the sentence is always down.

Weapon Equip wraps the weapon in localized gravitational force; strikes can buckle ground and pin targets.

Action • 2 Reserve

Downforce

The simplest royal command: one creature, one hundred and fifty feet away, is told to kneel. The save is a formality; failure means prone, speedless, flightless until his next turn. Gravity is the one edict no subject can veto.

One creature within 150 ft makes a Strength save. Failure: prone, speed 0, and cannot fly until the end of your next turn.

Action • 4 Reserve

Plate Step

He edits the battlefield's geography, raising, collapsing, splitting a sixty-foot radius into walls, fissures, and twenty-foot cliffs. The crown's first lesson: a king does not fight on the terrain. He composes it.

Raise or collapse terrain in a 60-ft-radius point within 180 ft. Dexterity save; 6 Djinn Dice bludgeoning/force, half on success. Create walls, fissures, or 20-ft elevation changes.

Bonus Action • 3 Reserve

Gravity Orbit

For one minute he walks with his own court in attendance, unattended objects orbiting like retainers, each spendable as shield or missile. The sovereign's doctrine: everything near the throne is already in service.

For 1 minute, fly 60 ft and orbit up to PB unattended objects around you; spend one object to reduce an incoming ranged hit by 2 Djinn Dice or launch it for 3 Djinn Dice force.

Full Djinn Equip • Level 9

He becomes unmovable the way mountains are unmovable: flight, tremorsense through the ground he rules, immunity to being knocked prone, the strength of a Gargantuan in lifting and shoving, and the terrain he creates now comes in double measure. The crown sits heavier; the realm grows to fit it.

Fly 90 ft; tremorsense 120 ft while touching ground; cannot be knocked prone; count as Gargantuan for lifting and forced movement. Terrain you create can be twice normal size.

14 Reserve • Codex Extreme from canon feat

Tectonic Coronation

The coronation itself: a five-hundred-foot earthquake delivered within a thousand feet, fissures splitting the field, fortifications answered at full measure. This is the honest version of the fiction, plates shifted, regions remade. A conqueror's final argument, made of the ground everyone is standing on.

Trigger a 500-ft-radius earthquake centered within 1,000 ft. Dexterity save; 16 Djinn Dice bludgeoning/force, half on success. Failure knocks prone; fissures split the field and buildings/fortifications take maximum dice.

Anime-scale: Strategic scale: manipulate tectonic plates to cause a regional earthquake. This is explicitly the canon-scale inspiration for the rework.

Field notes. Subject: the crown that fell and kept ruling. Three truths govern this weight. First: the technique never strikes the earth; it takes the throne over it, cursed authority expressed not as force but as gravity. Second: a crown is weight, not gold; the sorcerer demands the ground acknowledge its ruler, and the ground, being honest, complies. Third: at the pinnacle, the land itself is the instrument, tectonic plates moved like chess pieces, because the one who wears the Falling Crown does not conquer territory; he becomes the terrain. Mark the maxim: he does not attack the land. He outranks it.

Rules integration

Djinn Die, Equip & Extreme Magic

Damage-type chooser: when a Djinn ability lists two damage types, the Djinn King chooses which applies when the damage is dealt.
Independent Djinn Reserve: 14 (Rank B). Refreshes on a long rest. Every Djinn cost below, Weapon Equip, Full Equip, abilities, reactions, Extreme Magic, spends this Djinn’s Reserve, never your CE. If this Djinn’s Reserve is empty it simply cannot fuel more until you rest; your other Djinn keep their own separate Reserves.
Weapon Equip · level 5: bonus action, 2 Reserve, lasts 10 minutes.
Full Djinn Equip · level 9: bonus action, 4 Reserve, lasts 1 minute. Only one Equip can be active.
Deep Synchronization · level 11: once per short rest, one Equip activation costs 1 Reserve less.
Extreme Magic · level 13: action; requires that Djinn’s Full Equip; pay the listed Reserve cost from this Djinn’s Reserve; usable once per long rest per Djinn. Full Equip ends afterward, and you cannot Equip that Djinn again until you finish a short rest.
King Vessel · level 17: switch directly from one Full Equip to another as a bonus action, paying the new Equip cost. At level 20, once per long rest, immediately transition from one Djinn’s Extreme into another Djinn’s Full Equip.
Attack / save: use your CE attack modifier and CE save DC unless this Djinn explicitly says otherwise.
