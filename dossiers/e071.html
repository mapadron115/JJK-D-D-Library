DJINN 11 • Strength / Gravity

Gusion — Falling Crown

Rank B
Canon master: BarbarossaBasis: Canon core • Gravity Magic and tectonic-plate manipulation

Weapon Equip • Level 5

Tectonic Crown. Weapon Equip wraps the weapon in localized gravitational force; strikes can buckle ground and pin targets.

Downforce

Action • 2 CE

One creature within 150 ft makes a Strength save. Failure: prone, speed 0, and cannot fly until the end of your next turn.

Plate Step

Action • 4 CE

Raise or collapse terrain in a 60-ft-radius point within 180 ft. Dexterity save; 6 Djinn Dice bludgeoning/force, half on success. Create walls, fissures, or 20-ft elevation changes.

Gravity Orbit

Bonus Action • 3 CE

For 1 minute, fly 60 ft and orbit up to PB unattended objects around you; spend one object to reduce an incoming ranged hit by 2 Djinn Dice or launch it for 3 Djinn Dice force.

Full Djinn Equip • Level 9

Fly 90 ft; tremorsense 120 ft while touching ground; cannot be knocked prone; count as Gargantuan for lifting and forced movement. Terrain you create can be twice normal size.

EXTREME MAGIC • LEVEL 13+

Tectonic Coronation

14 CE • Codex Extreme from canon feat

Trigger a 500-ft-radius earthquake centered within 1,000 ft. Dexterity save; 16 Djinn Dice bludgeoning/force, half on success. Failure knocks prone; fissures split the field and buildings/fortifications take maximum dice.

Anime-scale: Strategic scale: manipulate tectonic plates to cause a regional earthquake. This is explicitly the canon-scale inspiration for the rework.
