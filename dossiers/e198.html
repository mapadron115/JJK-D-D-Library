What the technique actually does.

Character engine

Gain Echoes from darkness, successful hiding, and ceiling/floor transitions. Echoes power echolocation, swoops, and a low-HP injury threat that still respects campaign injury rules.

Technique save DC: 8 + PB + chosen CE ability modifier. Technique attack: PB + chosen CE ability modifier.

Full progression

Level progression

1Bat House Sense

Blindsight 20 ft; spend Echo for 60 ft until end turn. First attack from unseen position each turn +1 die slashing.

3Razor Swoop

Action, 2 CE: move up to speed straight without OAs and make one technique melee attack against a passed target for 3 dice + CE mod.

6Ceiling of Wings

Bonus, 2 CE, Focus, 20-ft swarm zone for 1 minute; dim becomes darkness to enemies; moving creatures are known; enemy Dash can grant Echo once/round.

11Neck-Seeking Blade

Razor Swoop hitting target below one-quarter HP may spend 3 Echoes; Con save or hit becomes critical and uses normal injury thresholds—never automatic decapitation above 0 HP.

18Domain Expansion: House Where Dawn Was Tested

Full-turn action, 20 CE, enclosed 60-foot radius centered on you, up to 1 minute, Technique Focus. The interior becomes a black cave. At initiative 20 every unguarded light source is suppressed until the next initiative 20; a creature adjacent to a light may use an action before the event to Guard that Light, preserving it. Gain 1 Echo per light suppressed, up to PB. While a hostile is in total darkness, moving more than 5 feet reveals its current position to you until the end of its turn. This is a rule-Domain with no automatic damage; anti-domain protection does not create light, but it continues to suppress any separate sure-hit effect a later feature might add.

Rules integration

Edge cases and shared systems.

Domain procedure This entry uses the repaired campaign Domain rules. The level-18 feature above is complete: its printed action, CE cost, radius, duration, initiative-20 law, fallback protection, and counterplay replace any legacy blanket auto-hit wording.
Counterplay Bright defended light and open spaces reduce Echo generation. The execution rider still obeys the campaign's injury thresholds.
