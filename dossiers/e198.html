Core law

What the technique actually does.

Character engine

Every Echo is a placed sound-location on the map, a node you can swap to, hear through, and strike from. Nodes are map assets, never damage currency.

Place an Echo: bonus action, 1 CE, place an Echo node on a surface or point you can see within 60 feet. You may maintain up to PB nodes; placing beyond the cap dismisses the oldest. A node lasts until dismissed or until you finish a long rest. You always know the exact position of each node, and you can hear through a node as though standing in its space. Enemies cannot perceive nodes without magic, they are your sounds, not theirs.

Technique save DC: 8 + PB + chosen CE ability modifier. Technique attack: PB + chosen CE ability modifier.

Field notes. Subject: cursed energy that learned the dark was never empty, only listening. Three truths govern the House. First: the House is geometry, not currency. Every Echo is a placed sound on the map, a node to swap to, hear through, and strike from; nodes are assets, never damage. Second: the bat commutes through its own fear. The CE places its fear of the dark onto the map and then travels through it, swap as a bonus action, strike with advantage from where the target cannot see, the sound carrying the body. Third: terror has a floor plan, and the sorcerer memorized it. Razor Swoop strikes from the node; the Ceiling of Wings turns dim light to darkness for enemies and tracks every moving thing inside; Neck-Seeking Blade swaps mid-strike against the nearly dead. Maximum: the House Where Dawn Was Tested, a black cave, PB nodes free on opening, swaps costing nothing, movement in total darkness revealed. Consider: you cannot corner something whose home address is everywhere it has ever been afraid.

Full progression

Level progression

1Bat House Sense

The senses, rebuilt. Blindsight twenty feet; swap as a bonus action to a node, the sound carries you; first attack each turn from unseen has advantage. This is the technique's foundation, and it is not an attack. It is an address book, and the bearer lives at every address. Blindsight 20 ft. Swap: as a bonus action, teleport to an unoccupied space within 5 feet of one of your Echo nodes, the sound carries you. From the Sound: the first attack you make each turn from a position where the target cannot see you has advantage.

3Razor Swoop

The strike, delivered by the architecture. Move up to speed in a straight line without provoking, one technique melee attack, no reactions on a hit, with advantage if the swoop began within five feet of a node. The bat strikes from its own sound. Mark this: the swoop's speed is not the weapon. The starting point is. Action, 2 CE: move up to your speed in a straight line without provoking opportunity attacks, then make one technique melee attack (2 + your CE modifier slashing); on a hit, the target cannot take reactions until the start of your next turn. If you began the swoop within 5 feet of an Echo node, the attack has advantage, the bat strikes from its own sound.

6Ceiling of Wings

The sky fills with wings. Bonus action, 2 CE, Focus: a twenty-foot swarm zone for a minute, dim light becomes darkness for enemies inside, every moving creature's position known, nodes placed without actions. The House expands upward. The dark was never empty; now it is furnished. Bonus action, 2 CE, Focus: a 20-foot swarm zone for 1 minute. Dim light becomes darkness for your enemies inside; you know the position of every moving creature inside; and you may place Echo nodes inside the zone without spending actions (still 1 CE each).

11Neck-Seeking Blade

The executioner's courtesy. When Razor Swoop hits a target below one-quarter HP, swap with a node within thirty feet mid-attack, the strike comes from the sound itself, and a failed Constitution turns the hit critical. Normal injury thresholds; never automatic decapitation above 0 HP. The technique is precise about this: the bat is an executioner, not a butcher. When your Razor Swoop hits a target below one-quarter HP, you may swap with an Echo node within 30 feet of the target as part of the attack (no action), the strike comes from the sound itself. On a hit, the target makes a Constitution save; on failure the hit becomes critical. It uses normal injury thresholds, never automatic decapitation above 0 HP.

18Domain Expansion: House Where Dawn Was Tested

The cave, perfected. The interior becomes black; PB nodes placed free anywhere inside on opening; swaps cost no action once per round; every hostile moving more than five feet in total darkness reveals its position until the end of its turn. The Domain is the technique's thesis proven at scale: the dark belongs to the one who mapped it. Full-turn action, 20 CE, enclosed 60-foot radius centered on you, up to 1 minute, Technique Focus. The interior becomes a black cave. When the Domain forms, you may immediately place up to PB Echo nodes anywhere inside it at no cost. While the Domain stands, swapping between nodes costs no action (once per round), and every hostile inside in total darkness that moves more than 5 feet reveals its current position to you until the end of its turn. This is a rule-Domain with no automatic damage; anti-domain protection does not destroy your nodes, but it hides a protected creature’s movement from them.

Innate Technique Feats

Innate Technique Feats

4 optional build choices

Roost Walker

The ceiling becomes a chair. Climb speed equal to walking, hang hands-free from any climbable surface, and while hanging unseen, From the Sound applies to every attack that turn, not just the first. The roost is the bat's office. It does its best work upside down. You gain a climb speed equal to your walking speed and can hang hands-free from any surface you could climb, talons and tail holding you fast. While hanging hands-free and unseen, From the Sound applies to every attack you make that turn, not just the first. Releasing your grip deliberately is silent: you have advantage on Dexterity (Stealth) checks to land unheard when you drop on purpose.

Blood Echo

The wounded leave a trail. A bloodied creature moving within thirty feet of a node leaves a sound-track followable until the end of the next turn. The House does not chase. It listens, and the bleeding are the loudest things in the dark. A bloodied creature moving within 30 feet of one of your Echo nodes leaves a sound-track you can follow until the end of your next turn.

Silent Wing

The swoop, unheard. Treated as unseen for the attack; enemies cannot use reactions triggered by the movement; those who never perceived the bearer do not learn its location until after. The bat's doctrine in one feature: the strike should arrive before the rumor of it. When you use Razor Swoop, your passage stays unrevealed until the attack resolves: you are treated as unseen for the swoop's attack, and enemies cannot use reactions triggered by your movement during it. Creatures that had not perceived you before the swoop do not learn your location until after damage is rolled. If the attack misses, you may immediately Swap to an Echo node within 30 feet as part of the same action, gone on the wingbeat.

Vanishing Wing (11+)

The blow, declined. When hit, reaction and 1 CE swap with a node within thirty feet, the attack misses if the new position is beyond reach or sight. I have watched swords pass through the space where the bat was standing. The space, it turns out, was only ever a rumor. When you are hit by an attack, you may use your reaction and spend 1 CE to swap with an Echo node within 30 feet; the attack misses if your new position is beyond the attacker's reach or out of its line of sight.

Rules integration

Edge cases and shared systems.

Domain procedure This entry uses the repaired campaign Domain rules. The level-18 feature above is complete: its printed action, CE cost, radius, duration, node law, fallback protection, and counterplay replace any legacy blanket auto-hit wording.
Counterplay Bright defended light and open ground starve the House of nodes, spread out, deny surfaces, and listen: a swap always arrives from a placed sound. The execution rider still obeys the campaign’s injury thresholds.
