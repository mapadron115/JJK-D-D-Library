Core law

What the technique actually does.

Character engine

Every Echo is a placed sound-location on the map — a node you can swap to, hear through, and strike from. Nodes are map assets, never damage currency.

Place an Echo: bonus action, 1 CE — place an Echo node on a surface or point you can see within 60 feet. You may maintain up to PB nodes; placing beyond the cap dismisses the oldest. A node lasts until dismissed or until you finish a long rest. You always know the exact position of each node, and you can hear through a node as though standing in its space. Enemies cannot perceive nodes without magic — they are your sounds, not theirs.

Technique save DC: 8 + PB + chosen CE ability modifier. Technique attack: PB + chosen CE ability modifier.

Full progression

Level progression

1Bat House Sense

Blindsight 20 ft. Swap: as a bonus action, teleport to an unoccupied space within 5 feet of one of your Echo nodes — the sound carries you. The first attack you make from an unseen position each turn deals +1 die slashing.

3Razor Swoop

Action, 2 CE: move up to your speed in a straight line without provoking opportunity attacks, then make one technique melee attack against a creature you passed for 3 dice + CE modifier. If you began the swoop within 5 feet of an Echo node, the attack has advantage — the bat strikes from its own sound.

6Ceiling of Wings

Bonus action, 2 CE, Focus: a 20-foot swarm zone for 1 minute. Dim light becomes darkness for your enemies inside; you know the position of every moving creature inside; and you may place Echo nodes inside the zone without spending actions (still 1 CE each).

11Neck-Seeking Blade

When your Razor Swoop hits a target below one-quarter HP, you may swap with an Echo node within 30 feet of the target as part of the attack (no action) — the strike comes from the sound itself. On a hit, the target makes a Constitution save; on failure the hit becomes critical. It uses normal injury thresholds — never automatic decapitation above 0 HP.

18Domain Expansion: House Where Dawn Was Tested

Full-turn action, 20 CE, enclosed 60-foot radius centered on you, up to 1 minute, Technique Focus. The interior becomes a black cave. When the Domain forms, you may immediately place up to PB Echo nodes anywhere inside it at no cost. While the Domain stands, swapping between nodes costs no action (once per round), and every hostile inside in total darkness that moves more than 5 feet reveals its current position to you until the end of its turn. This is a rule-Domain with no automatic damage; anti-domain protection does not destroy your nodes, but it hides a protected creature’s movement from them.

Innate Technique Feats

Innate Technique Feats

4 optional build choices

Roost Walker

You gain a climb speed equal to your walking speed and can hang hands-free from any surface you could climb, talons and tail holding you fast. While hanging this way your hands are free, and the first attack you make from an unseen hanging position each turn deals +1 die of slashing damage, as with Swap. Releasing your grip deliberately is silent: you have advantage on Dexterity (Stealth) checks to land unheard when you drop on purpose.

Blood Echo

A bloodied creature moving within 30 feet of one of your Echo nodes leaves a sound-track you can follow until the end of your next turn.

Silent Wing

When you use Razor Swoop, your passage stays unrevealed until the attack resolves: you are treated as unseen for the swoop's attack, and enemies cannot use reactions triggered by your movement during it. Creatures that had not perceived you before the swoop do not learn your location until after damage is rolled. If the attack misses, you may immediately Swap to an Echo node within 30 feet as part of the same action, gone on the wingbeat.

Vanishing Wing (11+)

When you are hit by an attack, you may use your reaction and spend 1 CE to swap with an Echo node within 30 feet; the attack misses if your new position is beyond the attacker's reach or out of its line of sight.

Rules integration

Edge cases and shared systems.

Domain procedure This entry uses the repaired campaign Domain rules. The level-18 feature above is complete: its printed action, CE cost, radius, duration, node law, fallback protection, and counterplay replace any legacy blanket auto-hit wording.
Counterplay Bright defended light and open ground starve the House of nodes — spread out, deny surfaces, and listen: a swap always arrives from a placed sound. The execution rider still obeys the campaign’s injury thresholds.
