At initiative 20 every unguarded light source is suppressed until the next initiative 20; a creature adjacent to a light may use an action before the event to Guard that Light, preserving it. Gain 1 Echo per light suppressed, up to PB. While a hostile is in total darkness, moving more than 5 feet reveals its current position to you until the end of its turn. This is a rule-Domain with no automatic damage; anti-domain protection does not create light, but it continues to suppress any separate sure-hit effect a later feature might add.

Domain procedure

This entry uses the repaired campaign Domain rules. The level-18 feature above is complete: its printed action, CE cost, radius, duration, initiative-20 law, fallback protection, and counterplay replace any legacy blanket auto-hit wording.

Character engine

Gain Echoes from darkness, successful hiding, and ceiling/floor transitions. Echoes power echolocation, swoops, and a low-HP injury threat that still respects campaign injury rules.

Technique save DC: 8 + PB + chosen CE ability modifier. Technique attack: PB + chosen CE ability modifier.

Counterplay

Bright defended light and open spaces reduce Echo generation. The execution rider still obeys the campaign's injury thresholds.

Adaptation note. This is original JJK 5e homebrew inspired by Camazotz and the named tradition. The folklore/myth supplies the motif; the resource engine, CE economy, progression, feats, Maximum and Domain procedures are game mechanics created for this codex.
