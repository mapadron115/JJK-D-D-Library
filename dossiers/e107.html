Core law

What the technique actually does.

Soul Stock

  • Soul Stock maximum = level × CE ability modifier (minimum level). You begin with a GM-approved starting stock equal to level.
  • Absorbing a soul requires a defeated eligible living/cursed creature and a deliberate Consume action; mindless constructs are ineligible.
  • Each stored soul has a Grade: Lesser, Standard, Elite, Named. Most are Lesser/Standard. Only PB Named/Elite souls may be retained at once.
  • Spending a soul is permanent until you absorb another.

The fiction. Jujutsu scholars classify Crimson Legion as hunger that achieved sentience — the sorcerer's cursed energy does not merely consume, it archives: every soul taken is filed, kept, and worn as both armor and audience. A stored soul is extra life because the technique refuses to concede that anything it has swallowed is truly gone. The prevailing theory holds that the Level Zero release is not a tactic but a promise finally honored — the graveyard emptied, every tenant set loose at once, because the user finally decided to stop keeping records.

Full progression

Level 1–20 Progression

L1Blood Drinker

Action over helpless/0-HP eligible creature, 1 CE: absorb one soul. Bonus action spend 1 soul to heal 2 Crimson Dice. This healing is not RCT.

Crimson Die progression: d6 at levels 1–4, d8 at levels 5–10, d10 at levels 11–16, d12 at levels 17–20. The original expansion repeatedly references a Crimson Die without printing its value. This reviewed edition completes that omission using the same four-step progression explicitly established by Vector Authority in the same book. This is a codex clarification, not a claimed quotation from the source.

L3Black Hound

Action, 2 CE: manifest a shadow familiar for 1 minute. It occupies your space/reach and once/turn after your attack deals +2 Crimson Dice necrotic to one target you hit.

L6Extra Life

When reduced to 0 HP but not conceptually/soul destroyed, reaction spend 3 souls: drop to 1 HP instead and immediately heal 2 Crimson Dice. Once/round.

L9Named Familiar

Action, 3 CE: manifest one stored Elite/Named soul for 1 minute using a simplified companion stat block. It acts after you, one action/round, and cannot use Domains/Maxima.

L11Vampiric Body

Resistance necrotic and nonmagical B/P/S; climb 30 ft; transform into mist/shadow as bonus action 2 CE until next turn, allowing movement through 1-inch gaps but no attacks.

L13Control Art Restriction Level One

Bonus action, 5 CE, 1 minute: release up to PB familiars at once; command all with one bonus action. While active Extra Life costs 2 souls.

L15True Vampire

Blood Drinker can absorb a qualifying creature reduced to 0 by you as a reaction once/round. Named Familiar can use one approved signature feature from its life.

L18Control Art Restriction Level Zero

Action, 10 CE: release any number of stored souls into a 120-ft-radius army for up to 10 minutes. Abstract them as up to CE modifier swarms + Named Familiars, not hundreds of initiatives. While Level Zero is active, you cannot use Extra Life because the souls are outside you. When it ends, surviving released souls return; destroyed ones are lost.

L20Everywhere and Nowhere — Optional Schrödinger Pinnacle

Story prerequisite only. Permanently consume all souls except one special paradox soul. Thereafter you cannot maintain Soul Stock/Level Zero, but once/long rest when killed or removed from space, reappear at start of your next turn in a space within 120 ft that you can meaningfully perceive/identify, at half HP. This replaces, not stacks with, the army playstyle.

Pinnacle expression

Pinnacle / Maximum

Maximum Technique — Sea of the Dead (12 CE, requires 30+ souls): release 30 souls at once as a 60-ft-radius tide until end next turn. Hostiles make Str save, taking 10 Crimson Dice necrotic and becoming Restrained by the dead on failure; half/no restraint on success. The 30 souls are expended regardless.

Feats

Technique Feats

Prerequisite: Black Hound

Baskerville

Your hound hunts with a body now: Black Hound may grapple creatures up to Large using your CE attack bonus for the grapple check, and while it holds a victim you may spend 1 soul as a bonus action to have it drag them 15 ft.

Prerequisite: level 3

Casull & Jackal

Gain paired cursed firearms; ranged attacks 60/240 ft use Dex or CE ability and qualify for technique riders, but not for automatic soul consumption.

Prerequisite: Extra Life

No Life King

They watched you refuse to die: when Extra Life triggers, you may spend 1 additional soul to let the moment land — each hostile within 10 ft that witnessed it makes a Wis save, becoming Frightened of you until the end of your next turn on a failure.

Prerequisite: Named Familiar

Named Dead

The named dead still talk: you may spend 10 minutes communing with one stored Elite/Named soul, asking three questions about its life (answered truthfully for what it knew) or gaining advantage on one History, Arcana, or Insight check drawing on its expertise.

Rules integration

Binding Rulings

Soul Stock is a campaign resource and should come from meaningful defeated creatures, not farming livestock.
Extra Life fails against explicit soul erasure, concept erasure, or effects that state no resurrection/return.
Level Zero intentionally sacrifices defensive immortality for army-scale action economy.
The Schrödinger pinnacle is optional and mutually exclusive with the classic soul-army endpoint.
