Disaster Tides

Hydrokinesis anchored by level-3 Domain access, a sea-shikigami roster, extreme defense, and flooding.

The fiction. Jujutsu scholars classify Disaster Tides as a reinforcement that overshot into something else entirely: the user's cursed energy imprinted on the memory of water that would not be refused — a tide that took and never gave back. Registry notes describe CE that behaves like the sea itself: it pools patiently, gathers pressure, and when it answers, it floods. The shikigami are not summoned from elsewhere; scholars hold they are the user's own drowned resolves given teeth, kept in the deep between Domains.

Full progression

Feature map

1Tides of Disaster.

Tides of Disaster

You know the Shape Water cantrip. As an action for 1 CE, throw water you create: one ranged CE attack, range 60/120 feet; on a hit it deals 1d8 cold damage. Damage increases to 2d8 at 5th level, 3d8 at 11th, and 4d8 at 17th. The technique creates its own water; Shape Water still requires existing water.

3Horizon of Captivating Skandha / Death Swarm.

Horizon of Captivating Skandha

Your Domain opens at 3rd level: two free uses per long rest, then 20 CE per use (full Domain rules in the Pinnacle expression section below).

Death Swarm

Only while the Domain is open, take an action and choose any mixture of shikigami up to your CE modifier in active count, paying each printed cost (use the appendix profiles). They appear in unoccupied spaces you can see, treat swim speed as fly speed inside the Domain, and attack normally.

Abyssal Feeding Frenzy

At initiative 20, the Domain’s spectral school engulfs every chosen hostile: each automatically takes 8d10 piercing or cold damage and is restrained by the feeding current until the next initiative 20. When an ability lists two damage types, the user chooses which applies when the damage is dealt. A protected creature makes a Dexterity save, taking half damage and avoiding restraint on success. This event uses no active shikigami and is not a summon attack.

6Water Defensive Ring.

Water Defensive Ring

As an action for 6 CE — or as a reaction to incoming damage for 8 CE — create a ring of water around you granting temporary HP equal to your CE modifier × 15. Apply incoming damage to the ring first. Its benefits last while any of its temporary HP remain; it ends when those HP reach 0, when dismissed, or after 10 minutes. The reaction is used after damage is rolled but before it is applied. While the ring is active, you gain immunity to cold damage and resistance to bludgeoning, slashing, and piercing damage. You cannot create another ring while one persists.

11Flash Flood.

Attack

As an action for 8 CE, choose one target within 30 feet and make a CE attack roll. On a hit it takes 10d8 cold damage and is pushed 80 feet away from you; the push deals no extra collision damage.

Area

As an action for 10 CE, choose a point within 30 feet; creatures in a 10-foot-radius circle make a Dexterity save, taking 8d12 cold damage on failure or half on success. The water fills a physically enclosed space 10 feet deep and remains for 1 hour, then becomes ordinary water that drains naturally. A creature too large for the space is not crushed.

18Maximum: Tsunami.

Maximum: Tsunami

As an action for 10 CE, cast the 8th-level Tsunami spell using your CE save DC and no material components. It requires Technique Focus for the spell’s normal duration.

Pinnacle expression

Maximum, Domain, or equivalent.

Early Domain: the Domain has two free uses per long rest; later uses cost 20 CE. All normal Domain timing, clash, and Burnout rules still apply. If cast with no hostile creature inside, it can remain as a noncombat environment indefinitely. Once a hostile creature enters, its normal combat duration begins and cannot become indefinite again.

Innate Technique Feats

Build-defining options.

These are optional character-building feats. You must meet each listed prerequisite before selecting it.

Prerequisite: Tides of Disaster

Hydrokinesis

Action, 2 CE: make a ranged CE attack against a creature within 30 feet, firing a high-pressure lance of water. On a hit, it takes 2d10 cold + 2d6 piercing damage and must make a Constitution save against your CE DC. On a failure, it is knocked prone and takes the rolled damage again as the jet bores through.

Prerequisite: Horizon of Captivating Skandha

Shoal

You may activate Death Swarm outside the Domain with its normal action, target, summon capacity, and any listed CE cost. It has no sure-hit; attacks and saves resolve normally.

Prerequisite: Water Defensive Ring

Typhoon

For 4 additional CE, the Ring creates a 20-foot-radius sphere storm for the Ring's duration (1 minute with Technique Focus; the storm is stationary unless moved by a stated effect). A creature makes a Strength save once when the storm appears and whenever it ends a turn there, taking 2d6 bludgeoning on failure. As a bonus action each turn, make a 60-foot 4d6 lightning strike: each creature in the area makes a Dexterity save, taking the damage on failure, or half on success. The area moves with you and is difficult terrain.

Rules integration

Edge cases and shared systems.

Rules priority: the reviewed rulings, explicit codex clarifications, and the level progression always control when original wording conflicts. Legacy Domain auto-hit language, action terminology, event timing, missing saves, and older scaling never override revised player rules.
Why Tier III: free early Domain access and a selectable summon roster change encounter expectations even before its late-game flood and tsunami tools appear.

Water Defensive Ring
Beginning at 6th level, you can make a ring of water that protects
you from any incoming attack. As an action for 6 Cursed Energy, you

Flash Flood
Starting at 11th level, with your studies you have learned how to
condense the water that you create and use it as a ball of mass
destruction. As an action for 8 Cursed Energy, you can choose one
target within 30 feet of you. Make a cursed energy attack roll
against it. On a hit, it will take 10d8 cold damage and be pushed 80
feet away from you. You may instead as an action for 10 Cursed Energy
choose a point within 30 feet of you. All creatures within a 10-foot
radius circle of that point must make a Dexterity saving throw. On a
failure they receive 8d12 cold damage, if the target succeeds on the
test they take half damage. If the area is enclosed it is filled with
10 feet deep water.
Maximum: Tsunami
At 18th level, you have reached the pinnacle of manipulating your
tides, creating a massive wave that destroys everything in its path.
As an action for 10 Cursed Energy, you can cast the Tsunami spell as
an 8th-level spell.
