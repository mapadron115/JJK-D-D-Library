DJINN 49 • Light

Crocell — Radiant Beast

Rank A
Canon master: SinbadBasis: Canon affinity/form • detailed techniques are largely unseen, so combat kit is extrapolated

Weapon Equip • Level 5

Radiant Saber Bracelet. Weapon Equip forms Crocell’s saber and coats movement in predatory light.

Flash Pounce

Bonus Action • 2 CE

Move or teleport up to 80 ft to a space you can see. Your next melee hit this turn deals +3 Djinn Dice radiant.

Predator Light

Action • 3 CE

Become Invisible until the end of your next turn or until you attack; while invisible this way, your speed increases by 40 ft and you can move through enemy spaces.

Radiant Rush

Action • 5 CE

Become a streak of light through a 300-ft line, 30 ft wide. Dexterity save; 9 Djinn Dice radiant/slashing, half on success. You appear anywhere along or at the end of the line.

Full Djinn Equip • Level 9

Fly 160 ft; +50-ft walking speed; radiant resistance; advantage on Perception based on hearing/smell. In bright light, Hide as a bonus action and attacks against you beyond 30 ft have disadvantage.

EXTREME MAGIC • LEVEL 13+

Sun-Beast Stampede

13 CE • Codex Extreme

A colossal radiant beast charges through a 700-ft-long, 100-ft-wide line. Dexterity save; 17 Djinn Dice radiant/force, half on success. Failure Blinds until the end of your next turn. You may appear at any point along the line after resolution.

Anime-scale: Strategic scale: cross and scour an entire battlefield as a horizon-filling burst of light.
