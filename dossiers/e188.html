White Silence

Yuki-onna inspiration. Failed cold saves and lingering in your Snowfield add Frost: 1 slows, 2 removes reactions, 3 Frostbinds with speed 0 for one turn before resetting to 1.

Combat roleCold controller / concealment assassin
Power tierTier IV · Special Grade
Core law

Character engine

Failed cold saves and lingering in your Snowfield add Frost: 1 slows, 2 removes reactions, 3 Frostbinds with speed 0 for one turn before resetting to 1.

Technique save DC: 8 + PB + chosen CE ability modifier. Technique attack: PB + chosen CE ability modifier.

The fiction. Jujutsu scholars describe White Silence as loneliness that learned to be weather: the user's cursed energy imprinted on a winter that took and took, and now the cold it exhales is not an attack but an environment the victim is invited to drown in. The prevailing theory holds that Frost is the body remembering, layer by layer — first the slowing, then the stillness, then the surrender — what it felt like to be left out in it. Nothing here hurries; the Snowfield does not chase, because the storm has never once needed to.

Full progression

Level progression

1Breath of White

Breath of White (action, 1 CE): exhale a 15-foot cone of cold; creatures make a Constitution save. On a failure it deals 2 Frost Dice of cold damage and adds +1 Frost; on a success it deals half damage and adds no Frost. The damage gains +1 die at 5th, 11th, and 17th level.

3Snowfield

Snowfield (action, 2 CE, Focus): create a 20-foot radius of snow for 1 minute. The area is lightly obscured and difficult terrain for enemies; a hostile ending its turn there makes a save or gains Frost.

6Woman in the Storm

While you are in your Snowfield, you may as a bonus action teleport 20 feet between snow-obscured spaces; your next technique attack that turn has advantage.

11Warmth Stolen Gently

Warmth Stolen Gently (action, 3 CE): target a Frostbound creature within 10 feet; it makes a Constitution save against 4 dice of cold damage. On a failure, you heal yourself or an ally for half the damage dealt.

18Domain Expansion: Village Lost Beneath Snow

Full-turn action, 20 CE, enclosed 90-foot radius centered on you, up to 1 minute, Technique Focus. The interior is a blizzard village; visibility beyond 30 feet is heavily obscured. At initiative 20, each chosen hostile that is not adjacent to a meaningful heat source, substantial shelter, or another creature automatically gains 1 Frost. Those three precautions are explicit environmental counterplay and prevent the Domain event entirely for that creature. A creature protected by an anti-domain defense that is otherwise exposed makes a Constitution save; success prevents the Frost. Ordinary cold attacks and Snowfield saves still resolve normally.

Feats

Build-defining options.

Footprints Vanish

You leave no tracks in snow, ice, or frost that a mundane tracker could follow, and you have advantage on Dexterity (Stealth) checks in cold terrain or inside your Snowfield. When you use Woman in the Storm to teleport between snow-obscured spaces, you arrive without sound: creatures relying on hearing have disadvantage on Wisdom (Perception) checks to notice your arrival until you take an action. While you stand motionless in falling snow or a blizzard, you are treated as invisible to creatures more than 30 feet away that lack magical sight.

Kiss of Winter

As a bonus action for 1 CE, you can press a winter's kiss to a willing creature's brow: until the end of your next turn it ignores Frost accumulation and treats your Snowfield as normal terrain.

Snowblind

When a creature reaches 2 Frost, you may use your reaction to blind it with driven snow until the end of its next turn unless it succeeds on a Constitution save against your CE DC.

First Flurry (6+)

When you roll initiative, you may fling the battle's first chill: one creature you can see within 30 feet must succeed on a Constitution save against your CE DC or gain 1 Frost.

Rules integration

Domain procedure

This entry uses the repaired campaign Domain rules. The level-18 feature above is complete: its printed action, CE cost, radius, duration, initiative-20 law, fallback protection, and counterplay replace any legacy blanket auto-hit wording.
Rules integration

Counterplay

Heat, shelter, clustering and leaving Snowfield all matter. Frost is staged control, not instant petrification/freezing.
