Domain Expansion: Village Lost Beneath SnowFull-turn action, 20 CE, enclosed 90-foot radius centered on you, up to 1 minute, Technique Focus. The interior is a blizzard village; visibility beyond 30 feet is heavily obscured. At initiative 20, each chosen hostile that is not adjacent to a meaningful heat source, substantial shelter, or another creature automatically gains 1 Frost. Those three precautions are explicit environmental counterplay and prevent the Domain event entirely for that creature. A creature protected by an anti-domain defense that is otherwise exposed makes a Constitution save; success prevents the Frost. Ordinary cold attacks and Snowfield saves still resolve normally.

Domain procedure

This entry uses the repaired campaign Domain rules. The level-18 feature above is complete: its printed action, CE cost, radius, duration, initiative-20 law, fallback protection, and counterplay replace any legacy blanket auto-hit wording.

Character engine

Failed cold saves and lingering in your Snowfield add Frost: 1 slows, 2 removes reactions, 3 Frostbinds with speed 0 for one turn before resetting to 1.

Technique save DC: 8 + PB + chosen CE ability modifier. Technique attack: PB + chosen CE ability modifier.

Counterplay

Heat, shelter, clustering and leaving Snowfield all matter. Frost is staged control, not instant petrification/freezing.
