Core law

Character engine

The staged verdict: failed cold saves and time spent in her Snowfield add Frost, one slows, two strip reactions, three Frostbinds at speed zero for a turn before the track resets to one. Failed cold saves and lingering in your Snowfield add Frost: 1 slows (speed halved, invented), 2 removes reactions, 3 Frostbinds with speed 0 for one turn before resetting to 1.

Technique save DC: 8 + PB + chosen CE ability modifier. Technique attack: PB + chosen CE ability modifier.

Field notes. Subject: loneliness that learned to be weather. Three truths govern her. First: Frost is the body remembering, layer by layer, the slowing, then the stillness, then the surrender, what it felt like to be left out in it. Failed saves and lingering in her Snowfield add it one degree at a time. Second: nothing here hurries. The Snowfield does not chase; it obscures, it hinders, it waits, and the storm has never once needed to catch what it can simply outlast. Third: her mercy is as cold as her cruelty. Warmth Stolen Gently takes from the Frostbound and gives to an ally, the same breath that numbs can spare. Consider her as winter itself: not an attack but an environment, and you are invited to drown in it. The village beneath the snow was lost slowly. So is everyone she touches.

Full progression

Level progression

1Breath of White

The exhale: one CE for a fifteen-foot cone of cold, two d8 scaling with level, and a failed Constitution save adds one Frost to the damage. Breath of White (action, 1 CE): exhale a 15-foot cone of cold; creatures make a Constitution save. On a failure it deals 2d8 cold damage (invented: a Frost Die is a d8) and adds +1 Frost; on a success it deals half damage and adds no Frost. The damage gains +1d8 at 5th, 11th, and 17th level (invented).

3Snowfield

The weather itself: two CE for a twenty-foot radius of snow for a minute, lightly obscured, difficult for enemies, and a hostile ending its turn there saves or gains Frost. Snowfield (action, 2 CE, Focus): create a 20-foot radius of snow for 1 minute. The area is lightly obscured and difficult terrain for enemies; a hostile ending its turn there makes a save or gains Frost.

6Woman in the Storm

The storm's daughter: inside her Snowfield, a bonus action carries her twenty feet between snow-obscured spaces, and her next technique attack that turn has advantage. While you are in your Snowfield, you may as a bonus action teleport 20 feet between snow-obscured spaces; your next technique attack that turn has advantage.

11Warmth Stolen Gently

Three CE against a Frostbound creature within ten feet: four d8 of cold, and on a failure she heals herself or an ally for half of what was dealt. Warmth Stolen Gently (action, 3 CE): target a Frostbound creature (a creature at Frost 3, invented) within 10 feet; it makes a Constitution save against 4d8 of cold damage (invented). On a failure, you heal yourself or an ally for half the damage dealt.

18Domain Expansion: Village Lost Beneath Snow

A blizzard village, ninety feet, one minute: at initiative twenty, every exposed hostile automatically gains one Frost, unless it stands near heat, shelter, or another living body, the Domain's own honest counterplay. Full-turn action, 20 CE, enclosed 90-foot radius centered on you, up to 1 minute, Technique Focus. The interior is a blizzard village; visibility beyond 30 feet is heavily obscured. At initiative 20, each chosen hostile that is not adjacent to a meaningful heat source, substantial shelter, or another creature automatically gains 1 Frost. Those three precautions are explicit environmental counterplay and prevent the Domain event entirely for that creature. A creature protected by an anti-domain defense that is otherwise exposed makes a Constitution save; success prevents the Frost. Ordinary cold attacks and Snowfield saves still resolve normally.

Feats

Build-defining options.

Footprints Vanish

The assassin's passage: no tracks in snow, advantage on Stealth in the cold, silent arrivals, and invisibility beyond thirty feet to the unmagical eye while motionless in falling snow. You leave no tracks in snow, ice, or frost that a mundane tracker could follow, and you have advantage on Dexterity (Stealth) checks in cold terrain or inside your Snowfield. When you use Woman in the Storm to teleport between snow-obscured spaces, you arrive without sound: creatures relying on hearing have disadvantage on Wisdom (Perception) checks to notice your arrival until you take an action. While you stand motionless in falling snow or a blizzard, you are treated as invisible to creatures more than 30 feet away that lack magical sight.

Kiss of Winter

A mercy for the willing: one CE and a brow-kiss buys immunity to Frost and easy passage through her Snowfield until the end of her next turn. As a bonus action for 1 CE, you can press a winter's kiss to a willing creature's brow: until the end of your next turn it ignores Frost accumulation and treats your Snowfield as normal terrain.

Snowblind

At two Frost, her reaction may blind the victim with driven snow until the end of its next turn. When a creature reaches 2 Frost, you may use your reaction to blind it with driven snow until the end of its next turn unless it succeeds on a Constitution save against your CE DC.

First Flurry (6+)

The battle's opening chill: at initiative, one creature within thirty feet saves or begins the fight at one Frost. When you roll initiative, you may fling the battle's first chill: one creature you can see within 30 feet must succeed on a Constitution save against your CE DC or gain 1 Frost.

Domain procedure

Rules integration

Counterplay

Heat, shelter, clustering and leaving Snowfield all matter. Frost is staged control, not instant petrification/freezing.
