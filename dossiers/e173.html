Gorgon’s Regard

Combat roleSight Controller / Anti-Martial
TierTier IV · Special Grade
Core law

Character fantasy

Force every opponent to choose between fighting blind and risking progressive petrification. Sight, facing, mirrors, and cover become real tactical resources.

Combat loop

  1. Unveil the Gaze and force enemies to choose whether to look.
  2. Stack Calcification on creatures that risk direct sight.
  3. Punish averted enemies with movement and snake attacks.
  4. Finish heavily calcified targets with petrification or exploit their immobility.

The fiction. Registry scholars classify the Gorgon's Regard as spite given eyes: the prevailing theory holds the user's cursed energy imprinted on the moment they were forced to look away — and on the fury of being seen as something monstrous for looking. The calcification, scholars note, is not inflicted on others so much as shared: a petrification the user's CE learned in a gaze that was turned on them first, now turned outward because the energy could not keep holding it.

Core law

Averted Gaze & Calcification

Unveil:

bonus action, 2 CE, Focus, up to 1 minute. Your eyes and serpentine hair become cursed focal points. At the start of a hostile creature’s turn within 60 feet that can see your face, it chooses Avert or Look.

Avert:

until start of its next turn it cannot make opportunity attacks against you, has disadvantage on attacks against you, and cannot benefit from sight-based advantage against you.

Look:

make Constitution save. Failure gains 1 Calcification, maximum 4. A creature that spends a full round unable to see you removes 1 Calcification at the end of its turn.

Stages:

1 = speed −10; 2 = disadvantage on Dexterity saves; 3 = restrained; 4 = petrified until all Calcification is removed by a feature capable of ending petrification, technique suppression, or 1 minute after Unveil ends.

Full progression

Level progression

1The Gorgon’s Eyes

Gain Unveil. You also gain Serpent Lash: a melee CE attack with 10-foot reach that deals 2 + your CE modifier piercing damage. Against a creature that is Averting, you have advantage on Serpent Lash once per turn.

3Bronze Reflection

You may project the Gaze through one polished reflective surface within 30 feet; creatures can avert from that surface as though it were your face. Action, 2 CE: force one creature with 2+ Calcification within 30 feet to save Constitution; failure adds 1 Calcification, success removes none.

6Stone-Blooded

Gain resistance to poison and advantage on saves against petrification. Shell-Crack: when you hit a creature with Calcification 3+ with Serpent Lash, you may shatter its calcifying shell — it sheds 1 Calcification and falls prone.

11House of Mirrors

Maintain up to PB reflected Gaze surfaces within 60 feet, each lasting while Unveil does. A creature can be forced to choose Avert/Look only once at the start of its turn regardless of how many reflections it sees. When a creature reaches Calcification 4, gain PB temporary HP.

18Domain Expansion: Garden of the Unblinking Queen

Full-turn action, 20 CE, 90-foot radius, 1 minute. Every surface becomes polished black stone reflecting your eyes. At initiative 20 each chosen hostile automatically gains 1 Calcification. A protected creature makes a Constitution save, gaining none on success. During their own turns, creatures still choose Avert or Look normally; anti-domain protection affects only the initiative-20 guaranteed stage, not ordinary reflected sight. Petrified creatures appear as statues in the garden and return to their original spaces when the Domain ends. Clash bookkeeping: 800 clash points (Tier IV). A 100+ point advantage collapses the weaker Domain; collapse deals no damage. Barrier AC 12 + PB; barrier HP = 10 × max(1, your CE modifier) + 1,600. Technique Burnout: 5 rounds.

Feats

Build-defining options.

Snake Crown

You gain blindsight 10 feet from the snakes’ senses and advantage on Perception based on vibration or scent.

Do Not Look Away

When an Averting creature voluntarily moves away from you, use reaction and 1 CE to move 10 feet toward it.

Mirror-Bearer

You can make one weapon, shield, or carried object you touch count as a polished reflection for Bronze Reflection.

Cracking Stone

Prerequisite: Calcification 3. Once per round, when a creature with Calcification 3+ is hit by an attack you did not make, you may use your reaction to shatter its calcifying shell: it sheds 1 Calcification and falls prone. This may spare an ally from petrification — or deny an enemy its stony armor — without spending your own attack.

Rules integration

Building the character

Works for a frontline controller, not only a caster. A Warrior with high CE stat can force horrible choices in melee; Support Specialist can play the gaze as pure control.
Rules integration

Counterplay

Blindness, opaque cover, smoke, fighting from beyond 60 feet, reflective counterplay, and willingly Averting all reduce the technique’s ceiling.
