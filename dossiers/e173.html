Gorgon’s Regard

Force every opponent to choose between fighting blind and risking progressive petrification. Sight, facing, mirrors, and cover become real tactical resources.

InspirationMedusa / the Gorgons
RoleSight Controller / Anti-Martial
TierTier IV · Special Grade
Core statCHA or WIS
Scaling dieGorgon Die

Character fantasy

Force every opponent to choose between fighting blind and risking progressive petrification. Sight, facing, mirrors, and cover become real tactical resources.

Combat loop

  1. Unveil the Gaze and force enemies to choose whether to look.
  2. Stack Calcification on creatures that risk direct sight.
  3. Punish averted enemies with movement and snake attacks.
  4. Finish heavily calcified targets with petrification or exploit their immobility.

Averted Gaze & Calcification

  • Unveil: bonus action, 2 CE, Focus, up to 1 minute. Your eyes and serpentine hair become cursed focal points. At the start of a hostile creature’s turn within 60 feet that can see your face, it chooses Avert or Look.
  • Avert: until start of its next turn it cannot make opportunity attacks against you, has disadvantage on attacks against you, and cannot benefit from sight-based advantage against you.
  • Look: make Constitution save. Failure gains 1 Calcification, maximum 4. A creature that spends a full round unable to see you removes 1 Calcification at the end of its turn.
  • Stages: 1 = speed −10; 2 = disadvantage on Dexterity saves; 3 = restrained; 4 = petrified until all Calcification is removed by a feature capable of ending petrification, technique suppression, or 1 minute after Unveil ends.

Innate Technique FeatsOptional character-building feats

Snake Crown

You gain blindsight 10 feet from the snakes’ senses and advantage on Perception based on vibration or scent.

Do Not Look Away

When an Averting creature voluntarily moves away from you, use reaction and 1 CE to move 10 feet toward it.

Mirror-Bearer

You can make one weapon, shield, or carried object you touch count as a polished reflection for Bronze Reflection.

Cracking Stone

Prerequisite: Calcification 3. Your weapon and unarmed attacks against restrained-by-Calcification targets ignore resistance to bludgeoning, piercing, and slashing damage.

Building the character

Works for a frontline controller, not only a caster. A Warrior with high CE stat can force horrible choices in melee; Support Specialist can play the gaze as pure control.

Technique die: Gorgon Die follows the shared d6 → d8 → d10 → d12 progression.

Counterplay

Blindness, opaque cover, smoke, fighting from beyond 60 feet, reflective counterplay, and willingly Averting all reduce the technique’s ceiling.

Adaptation note. This is original JJK 5e homebrew inspired by Medusa / the Gorgons. The myth/folklore provides imagery and thematic rules; the combat engine, progression, CE economy, feats, and Domain/Maximum procedures are game design for this codex.
