Features & procedures

Properties & techniques

Exact player-facing procedures

Half-remembered curse

Passive · A conventional weapon constantly imbued with cursed energy becomes a semi cursed tool; weaker than a full cursed tool of the same grade and more prone to breaking.

Not forged, but steeped. A katana carried through enough cursed energy begins to hold it the way cloth holds smoke. The result is real — it can touch what mundane steel cannot — but it is a lesser thing than a true cursed tool, and it breaks sooner.

Hungry steel

Passive · If not imbued with cursed energy at least once per week (+1 week per grade above 4th), the tool's effect is reduced by one grade.

The steeping fades. A semi cursed tool left unfed forgets itself grade by grade: feed it weekly, plus one extra week of grace per grade above 4th, or watch its edge dull back toward the mundane.

Two roads to the forge

Creation · Requirements: Cursed Imbuement, Cursed Enhancement, Tool Maker, or Basic Cursed Energy Manipulation. CE road: using Cursed Imbuement or Cursed Energy Manipulation, the tool is created after the listed CE has been channeled through the weapon. Time road: using Cursed Enhancement, the tool is created after wielding it in combat every day for the listed time. Tool Makers pay half cost (rounded up) on either road.

Pour energy into the steel, or carry the steel into battle until the steel learns. The CE road is measured in cursed energy spent through the weapon; the time road is measured in days of combat use. Tool Makers, who understand the material, pay half on either road, rounded up.

Field notes. Subject: the apprenticeship of objects. Registry theory holds that a cursed tool is not made but convinced, and the semi grades are the convincing in progress. Two truths govern them. First, they are honest about their station: weaker than true tools of the same grade, quicker to break, and honest in the only way that matters, they cost time or energy rather than luck. Second, they are hungry because they are unfinished; a finished tool holds its curse the way a vow holds its price, but a semi tool must be reminded weekly what it is becoming, or it slides back a grade toward mere steel. Mark this: every legendary weapon on this registry began as someone's daily carry. The difference between a katana and a cursed tool is mostly persistence.

Rules integration

Cursed-tool attunement

Campaign canon

Cursed-tool attunement: you have PB attunement slots (2–6). A semi cursed tool occupies one slot and requires a one-hour ritual. Attunement ends voluntarily over a short rest, on death, or when you replace it while over capacity. Distance does not break attunement. Cursed-tool attunement is separate from magic-item attunement.
Source fidelity: the creation table, the weekly-feeding decay, and the Tool Maker half-cost are reproduced verbatim from V6 §5.3. The separate crafting workstream owns the interactive crafting tool; this dossier is the rules reference.
