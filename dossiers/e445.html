Hand Sword

A blade with a will to wander, deadly in the hand, and still dangerous a hundred feet away.

Field notes. Subject: a retainer in steel. Three truths govern it. First, in the hand it is loyal: +2 to attack and damage, and advantage against being disarmed, it refuses to leave its master. Second, loosed, it becomes the hawk: a bonus action sends it up to twenty-five feet with one strike per turn, within a hundred feet of you, the hand's strength traded for the mind's reach, your cursed energy aims it, and the wound runs 2d10 deep. Third, the economy of command: one action of standing orders, and it fights on alone, needing nothing further from you. Hence the maxim: the general who can strike at a hundred feet need not attend the battle.

Exact player-facing procedures

Cursed Edge

Passive · +2 to attack and damage rolls · Advantage on ability checks and saving throws against being disarmed of this weapon.

In the hand it is the loyal retainer: the strike is +2, and the blade clings, advantage against any hand that would take it. When attacking with the Cursed Hand Sword you gain a +2 to attack and damage rolls, and advantage on ability checks or saving throws against being disarmed of this weapon.

Remote Control

Bonus action · Remotely control the sword: it moves up to 25 ft and makes one attack per turn, active within 100 ft · Remote attacks use your CE modifier; deal 2d10 slashing (no ability modifier).

Loosed, it becomes the hawk. The hand's strength is traded for the mind's reach, your cursed energy aims it, and the wound runs deep. You can choose to remotely control the sword to move and attack as a bonus action. The weapon moves up to 25 feet and makes one attack per turn, and remains active while within 100 ft of you. When attacking remotely, use your Cursed Energy modifier for attack rolls, and deal 2d10 slashing damage (no ability modifier).

Standing Orders

Action · Command it to travel somewhere or target a creature · It then carries out the order on its own without further actions from you.

The final economy: command it once and spend nothing after. A blade that obeys once obeys forever. You can command it to travel somewhere or target a creature as an action; the sword then carries out the order on its own, without needing further actions from you.

Edge cases and shared systems.

Campaign canon notes

Note: If the sword moves beyond 100 ft of you, remote control lapses; regain control when it is back in range. It cannot make more than one attack per turn even if you spend additional bonus actions.
