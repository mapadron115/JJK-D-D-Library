Features & procedures

Properties & techniques

Exact player-facing procedures

Feature

Spend Gaze reaction to grant adjacent ally +2 AC against one attack.

Gorgoneion

action, 2 CE, 15-ft cone Wis save; frightened until end next turn; creatures immune to frightened instead have speed -10 on failure.

Mirror of War

3 CE reaction vs ranged technique attack that misses you: make Tool attack against original attacker; on hit deal 2 Tool Dice force, not original technique.

Release: Shield Before the Host

7 CE, 1 minute: allies within 10 ft gain half cover from ranged attacks.

The fiction. Jujutsu scholars describe the Aegis as cursed energy that turned a survivor's instinct into doctrine. Every missed blow, every fear refused, becomes Gaze — the accumulated proof, stored in the mantle, that the wielder cannot be struck down or frightened away. The prevailing theory holds the tool's original sorcerer died facing something the shield refused to look away from, and the Aegis has been showing that same Gaze back at every enemy since.

Rules integration

Cursed-tool attunement

Campaign canon

Cursed-tool attunement: you have PB attunement slots (2–6). This tool occupies one slot and requires a one-hour ritual. Attunement ends voluntarily over a short rest, on death, or when you replace it while over capacity. Distance does not break attunement. Cursed-tool attunement is separate from magic-item attunement.
Balance & counterplay. Reflection creates new bounded force damage rather than copying arbitrary enemy techniques.
