Mirror of War

Three CE and a reaction for the Aegis's coldest honesty: when a ranged technique misses the bearer, the shield answers the attacker with its own Tool attack, two dice of force, not the enemy's technique returned. Reflection, bounded by the registry's rule: new damage, never borrowed. 3 CE reaction vs ranged technique attack that misses you: make Tool attack against original attacker; on hit deal 2 Tool Dice force, not original technique.

Release: Shield Before the Host

Action · 7 CE · 1 minute: allies within 10 ft gain half cover against ranged attacks.

Seven CE and an action for one minute of the host under the shield: every ally within ten feet gains half cover against ranged attacks. The doctrine's final form, the survivor's instinct, extended to the whole formation. 7 CE, 1 minute, action to activate (invented): allies within 10 ft gain half cover from ranged attacks.

Field notes. Subject: a survivor's instinct, made doctrine. Three truths govern him. First, the Aegis banks proof: every attack that misses the bearer, every fear or charm refused, becomes Gaze, the accumulated evidence, stored in the mantle, that the wielder cannot be struck down or frightened away. Second, the proof is spent as protection for others, not the self: a reaction and a banked Gaze grants an adjacent ally two AC against one attack, and the Release extends half cover against ranged attacks to every ally within ten feet for a minute. Third, the Gorgoneion is the doctrine made offensive: two CE and an action, a fifteen-foot cone, and the failed save is frightened, or, for the fearless, slowed by ten feet, because the mantle shows its enemies what it has already survived. Mark the tool's thesis: the best defense is a record of every defense that already worked.

Rules integration

Cursed-tool attunement

Campaign canon

Cursed-tool attunement: you have PB attunement slots (2–6). This tool occupies one slot and requires a one-hour ritual. Attunement ends voluntarily over a short rest, on death, or when you replace it while over capacity. Distance does not break attunement. Cursed-tool attunement is separate from magic-item attunement.
Balance & counterplay. Reflection creates new bounded force damage rather than copying arbitrary enemy techniques.
