Cursed Spirit — Field Entry

Kaoru Zenin

Field notes. Subject: the examination it never passed, now given infinite retakes. Three truths govern Kaoru Zenin. First: its bitterness is methodical, not blind. Its Grudge adds to every attack roll against the spellcasting, the confident, the clan-proud — promise draws its aim the way lanterns draw moths. Second: it is a fortress that flies. Guns fused with cursed muscle fire three detonating volleys a round; the Localized Explosion unmakes twenty feet of ground at a time; and from forty feet above the field, its bullets arrive with the certainty of a proctor's verdict. Third: its body is also its doctrine — a bonus action thickens its skin, hardens its hide, slicks the ground, or raises cover. Consider: it failed twenty examinations of sorcery. It now administers its own, and nobody has passed yet.

Classification & Risk

A Vengeful Cursed Spirit, Grade 1 (CR 8–16 band). Innate Technique: Cursed Transmutation. Guns sprout from shoulders, ribs, spine; its bullets detonate on impact. It fights not with blind rage but methodical bitterness, targeting sorcerers showing promise, confidence, or clan pride.

Stats

Ability scores & traits

STR 16 (+3) · DEX 18 (+4) · CON 18 (+4) · INT 14 (+2) · WIS 14 (+2) · CHA 12 (+1). HP 180 (19d10 + 76), AC 17, speed 30 ft., fly 40 ft. (lifts itself on transmuted air; not fast, but it doesn’t need to be). Grudge (vengeful): +1 to attack rolls against creatures with spellcasting or clan standing — promise, confidence, and clan pride draw its aim.

Actions

Actions

Multiattack. Three Gunfire attacks. Gunfire. Ranged Attack: +10 to hit, range 120 ft. Hit: 2d8 + 5 piercing damage, and the bullet detonates: each creature within 5 ft. of the target must succeed on a DC 15 Dexterity saving throw or take 1d8 fire damage. Localized Explosion (action). A 20-ft. radius Kaoru can see: each creature there must succeed on a DC 16 Dexterity saving throw or take 4d10 fire damage (half on success) as transmuted air detonates.

Risk

Special

Cursed Transmutation (bonus action, one effect): Thicken — +2 AC until its next turn; Hardened Shell — resistance to bludgeoning, piercing, and slashing damage until its next turn; Slick Ground — a 15-ft. square becomes difficult terrain and each creature there must succeed on a DC 14 Dexterity saving throw or fall prone; Cover — raise a 10-ft. wall granting half cover. From above, every shot is a reminder of the examinations it never passed.

Campaign canon notes

Field rule — perception: At Grade 2 and above this spirit’s cursed energy is dense enough that no Invisibility trait is assigned; standard perception applies. It remains invisible to ordinary non-sorcerers.
