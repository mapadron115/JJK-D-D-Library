Masamune / magic / wing / despair

Power tierTier V · Calamity
Core law

What the technique actually does.

Core Engine — Despair. Enemies gain Despair when they fail saves against your signature magic, witness you reduce an ally to 0, or suffer a critical Masamune hit (max 3). At 1: -5 speed; 2: disadvantage vs Frightened; 3: your first attack each round against them gains advantage.

The fiction. Jujutsu scholars classify the One-Winged Calamity as cursed energy that learned despair is a weapon. The prevailing theory holds that the technique does not cause fear so much as it harvests it: enemies who witness ruin, who fail against the magic, who survive a Masamune strike, all leave something behind, and at three measures of Despair the battlefield is fighting on the user's behalf. It is one person's CE, imprinting the sight of calamity onto everyone present until terror does the cutting for it.

Level 1 through 20.

10-ft reach cursed odachi, 1d10 slashing. 1 CE on hit adds 1d8 force and may pull/push 10 ft.

2 CE, 60 ft, Dex save: 3d8 force; three dark orbs may split targets.

L6Octaslash

3 CE: make three Masamune attacks as an action; each after first deals only weapon dice, no ability modifier.

L9Heartless Angel

5 CE, action, one creature within 60 ft Con save: 6d10 necrotic; on failure cannot regain HP until start next turn.

L11One Wing

4 CE, 1 minute: fly 60, +10 speed, Masamune +1d8, immune Frightened.

L13Hell's Gate

3 CE after falling at least 20 ft: plunge to point below; 15-ft radius Dex save, 6d10 piercing/force and prone.

L15Pale Horse

6 CE, 90 ft, Con save: 8d10 necrotic plus Poisoned until end next turn; failed by 5+ also 1 Despair.

L18Domain — Nibelheim's Burning Memory

Initiative 20: each hostile chooses to FACE or FLEE. FACE: Wis save, 8d8 psychic on failure. FLEE: moved 30 ft by the Domain toward its boundary and gains 1 Despair. At 3 Despair, FACE save has disadvantage.

L20Safer Sephiroth

One Wing no daily restriction; 80-ft flight; at 3 Despair Masamune crits 19–20. Once/long rest survive lethal damage at 1 HP and immediately gain One Wing.

Pinnacle expression

Maximum, Domain, or equivalent.

Maximum — Supernova (12 CE): ritual action; designate a 30-ft sphere within 150 ft. At start of your next turn, if not disrupted, cosmic illusion/energy detonates: Con save, 14d10 fire/force, half success, plus 2 Despair on failure. It does not literally destroy a solar system.

Feats

Build-defining options.

Long Blade

Once per turn, when you miss with Masamune, you may extend the swing into a second creature within 15 ft of the first, making a new attack roll at disadvantage.

Chosen by Jenova

When you would be subjected to a disease, poison, or transformation effect, you may spend a reaction to let Jenova's cells devour it: you are unaffected, and you learn the effect's nature and source.

Peerless SOLDIER

When you use Octaslash, you may move up to 10 ft between each attack without provoking opportunity attacks, and each slash may target a different creature. This movement does not count against your speed for the turn, letting you carve a path through a scattered formation in a single action.

Black Materia

Supernova charge cannot be interrupted by ordinary damage once/long rest.

Rules integration

Edge cases and shared systems.

Shared rulings: Technique DC = 8 + PB + CE ability modifier. Direct rolled attacks may crit and use the campaign Black Flash procedure; autonomous hazards and Domain events cannot. Maximum Output may modify ordinary supported features, never named Maxima/Domains. Two-technique characters use the campaign's agreed character-level progression.
