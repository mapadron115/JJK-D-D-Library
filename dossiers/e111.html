Core law

What the technique actually does.

Core Engine, Despair. The engine in full: Despair accrues, failed saves against his signature magic, witnessing him drop an ally to 0, a critical Masamune hit, max 3. At 1: -5 speed. At 2: disadvantage against Frightened. At 3: his first attack each round against them has advantage. Despair is not inflicted. It is collected, like rainwater. Enemies gain Despair when they fail saves against your signature magic, witness you reduce an ally to 0, or suffer a critical Masamune hit (max 3). At 1: -5 speed; 2: disadvantage vs Frightened; 3: your first attack each round against them gains advantage.

Field notes. Subject: cursed energy that learned despair is a weapon. Three truths govern him. First, he does not cause fear so much as harvest it: enemies gain Despair when they fail saves against his signature magic, when they witness him reduce an ally to 0, or when a Masamune critical lands, three measures maximum, each with its own toll. Second, the battlefield fights on his behalf at three Despair: -5 speed at one, disadvantage against Frightened at two, and his first attack each round with advantage at three. Third, his instruments are distance and spectacle, a 10-ft reach odachi, 60-ft flares, a 90-ft pale horse, a Domain that offers its victims a choice. Hence the maxim: he does not defeat his enemies. He lets them watch.

18Domain, Nibelheim's Burning Memory

The Domain offers its victims a choice at initiative 20: FACE or FLEE. FACE: Wis save, 8d8 psychic on failure. FLEE: moved 30 ft toward the boundary, +1 Despair. At 3 Despair, the FACE save is at disadvantage. Even the Domain is a harvest, it only asks which crop you prefer to be. Initiative 20: each hostile chooses to FACE or FLEE. FACE: Wis save, 8d8 psychic on failure. FLEE: moved 30 ft by the Domain toward its boundary and gains 1 Despair. At 3 Despair, FACE save has disadvantage.

One Wing loses its daily restriction; flight becomes 80 ft; at 3 Despair the Masamune crits on 19–20. And once per long rest, lethal damage is survived at 1 HP, with One Wing unfolding immediately. The safer form is not safer for him. It never was. One Wing no daily restriction; 80-ft flight; at 3 Despair Masamune crits 19–20. Once/long rest survive lethal damage at 1 HP and immediately gain One Wing.

Pinnacle expression

Maximum, Domain, or equivalent.

The Maximum is a ritual of cosmic theater: 12 CE, action, designate a 30-ft sphere within 150 ft; at the start of his next turn, if not disrupted (30+ damage from a single source forces a Concentration-style CE save, DC 10 or half damage; failure disrupts the charge), it detonates, Con save, 14d10 fire or force, half on success, +2 Despair on failure. It does not literally destroy a solar system. The dossier is honest about this. The victims rarely care. Maximum, Supernova (12 CE; action): ritual action (an action performed as a ritual over the turn); designate a 30-ft sphere within 150 ft. At start of your next turn, if not disrupted (taking 30+ damage from a single source before detonation forces a Concentration-style CE save, DC 10 or half the damage taken; failure disrupts the charge), cosmic illusion/energy detonates: Con save, 14d10 fire/force, half success, plus 2 Despair on failure. It does not literally destroy a solar system.

This movement does not count against your speed for the turn, letting you carve a path through a scattered formation in a single action.
