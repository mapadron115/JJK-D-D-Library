Field entry

Journal Entry field report, converted to a table-ready stat block

Field notes. Subject: the watched feeling, given legs. It chooses one creature as prey, and that creature can never perceive it, automatically failing every attempt to see it, while the curse gains advantage hiding from the very eyes it haunts. Here is the inversion to classify correctly: everyone else can see it plainly. The victim’s certainty that nothing is there is the whole of its armor. It suppresses its residual energy (a DC 13 Wisdom check to track it at all), stalks for days to ripen the paranoia, and strikes from the blind side, its claws bite 2d6 deeper when the target cannot see them coming. Then the paradox that should reassure you: once properly located, it is weak. One solid exorcism ends the haunting. The art is the finding, never the fight.

An Imaginary Cursed Spirit, Grade 4 (CR 1/8–1/2 band). Stalks a chosen victim for extended periods, fostering the persistent feeling of being watched; weak and easily exorcised once properly located.

STR 8 (−1) · DEX 16 (+3) · CON 10 (+0) · INT 6 (−2) · WIS 12 (+1) · CHA 8 (−1). HP 18 (4d8), AC 14, speed 30 ft. Skills Stealth +6. Invisibility. Unseen by Target: the curse chooses one creature as its prey; that creature automatically fails any Wisdom (Perception) check to see it, and the curse has advantage on Dexterity (Stealth) checks against it. Suppressed Residuals: it suppresses its residual cursed energy — tracking it by energy detection requires a DC 13 Wisdom (Perception or Insight) check.

Campaign canon notes

Field rule — low-grade perception: Per the C&C field rules, Grade 3 and lower curses are invisible to creatures without cursed energy. A creature in a life-or-death situation at less than half its hit point maximum may temporarily or permanently gain the ability to see them. This spirit carries the Invisibility trait below.
