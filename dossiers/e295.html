Jujutsu origin

Tyrfing's legend already behaves like a binding vow: extraordinary killing power purchased with a compulsory death whenever the sword is drawn. In this setting, generations of retelling hardened that condition into the weapon itself.

Blood-Hungry Edge

Once per turn on a hit, Tyrfing deals an additional 3d10 necrotic damage. While its Blood Debt is unpaid, attacks with Tyrfing score a critical hit on a 19–20.

Blood Debt

Drawing Tyrfing begins the debt. The blade cannot be willingly sheathed, dismissed, or abandoned until a non-trivial creature dies within 60 ft of the wielder after the draw. Summoned fodder, helpless livestock, and deliberately manufactured sacrifices do not satisfy the curse.

Unpaid Price

If 1 minute passes after drawing Tyrfing without satisfying the Blood Debt, the wielder takes 12d10 necrotic damage and their maximum HP is reduced by the same amount. This damage ignores resistance and immunity. The effect repeats every minute until a valid death pays the debt.

Binding Vow — A Sword Drawn for Death

Tyrfing receives its critical range and additional damage from the certainty that a death will occur. The curse does not care whose.

Designer’s note. Tyrfing is the more explosive of the two Norse death-swords: enormous pressure now, horrific payment if the wielder tries to bluff the vow.

Rules integration

Cursed-tool attunement

Campaign canon

Cursed-tool attunement: you have PB attunement slots (2–6). This tool occupies one slot and requires a one-hour ritual. Attunement ends voluntarily over a short rest, on death, or when you replace it while over capacity. Distance does not break attunement. Cursed-tool attunement is separate from magic-item attunement.
