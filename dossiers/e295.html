Tyrfing

Metaphysical Law: a drawn Tyrfing must be paid for with a life. Once Tyrfing leaves its sheath, it cannot be willingly sheathed until a creature dies within the encounter — a Binding Vow that makes the sword monstrously powerful precisely because it cannot be drawn safely.

Features & procedures

Properties & techniques

Exact player-facing procedures

Death Debt

Once Tyrfing leaves its sheath, it cannot be willingly sheathed until a creature dies within the encounter.

Escalating damage

While the Debt is unpaid, Tyrfing gains escalating bonuses to damage each round.

Punishes stalling

Its strikes ignore mundane hardness and heavily punish defensive stalling.

The debt turns inward

If combat ends without a death, the curse turns inward and selects the nearest valid Bearer the debt.

Binding Vow. The sword is monstrously powerful precisely because it cannot be drawn safely.

Rules integration

Cursed-tool attunement

Campaign canon

Cursed-tool attunement: you have PB attunement slots (2–6). This tool occupies one slot and requires a one-hour ritual. Attunement ends voluntarily over a short rest, on death, or when you replace it while over capacity. Distance does not break attunement. Cursed-tool attunement is separate from magic-item attunement.
Balance & counterplay. Drawing it is a commitment to a killing — the price is paid whether the enemy falls or the bearer does. Defensive stalling only sharpens its strikes, so the answer is to end the fight on your terms before the Debt compounds.
