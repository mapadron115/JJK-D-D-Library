Bloodlord

Blood counters / vampirism / aristocratic domination

Power tierTier IV · Special Grade
Core law

What the technique actually does.

Core Engine — Blood. Blood is the cellar's ledger: a maximum of ten, minted once per turn when he deals slashing or necrotic damage to a living target, or heals from a hostile creature. It fuels his vampiric regeneration and his commands. The aristocrat's energy will not obey until it has been fed — and it feeds only on what he takes from others. Blood max 10. Gain 1 once/turn when you deal slashing/necrotic damage to a living target or heal from a hostile creature. Blood fuels vampiric regeneration and commands.

Field notes. Subject: an aristocrat at a very old table. Three truths govern him. First, the appetite is etiquette: Blood accrues only from wounds he deals to the living or healing he steals from hostiles — never from his own veins — capped at ten, and every command he issues is tribute paid to an energy that recognizes exactly one hierarchy. Second, the table is the hierarchy: his commands forbid approach, his Spawn rise again, his court drinks from others' deaths. Third, the vintage compounds: Blood seasons his damaging features die by die, three Blood buys five dice of healing, and at the peak the cellar holds fifteen — with flight and armored flesh past ten, and one last refusal of death. The vampirism is not the fantasy. The fantasy is the birthright, and the blood is just how it keeps score.

Full progression

Level 1 through 20.

1Vampiric Thirst

The first sip. On a melee hit, one energy buys an extra die of necrotic and heals him the same roll — once per turn. The table's oldest custom: drink, and be restored. On melee hit spend 1 CE: +1d8 necrotic and heal same roll, once/turn.

3Blood Needle

The pouring tap. One energy, sixty feet, two dice of piercing and necrotic — and on a hit, one Blood minted. The aristocrat does not hunt. He draws. 1 CE, 60-ft technique attack, 2d8 piercing/necrotic; on hit gain 1 Blood.

6Aristocrat's Command

The dismissal. Two energy and one Blood: a failed Wisdom save, and the target may not willingly approach him nor strike one named creature until his next turn ends. Some guests are simply not received. 2 CE + 1 Blood, Wis save: target cannot willingly move closer to you or attack one named creature until end next turn.

9Vampire Spawn

The raising of the household. Four energy and three Blood summon one blood shikigami for a minute — armored, thirty hit points, a three-dice bite that heals itself by half. The table seats its own. 4 CE + 3 Blood: summon one blood shikigami for 1 minute, AC 15, HP 30, bite 3d8 and heals itself half.

11Blood Magic

The seasoning. When he casts a damaging feature he may spend up to his proficiency in Blood, each adding a die of necrotic — once per turn. The vintage, applied. Spend up to PB Blood when casting damaging feature; each adds 1d8 necrotic, max once/turn.

13Markov Immortality

The cellar's reserves. As a bonus action, three Blood buys five dice of healing — halved if radiant has touched him since his last turn. Old blood keeps; holy light spoils it. Bonus action 3 Blood heal 5d10. Radiant damage since last turn halves it.

15Lord of Innistrad

The court gathers. He may keep two Spawn, and allies within fifteen feet gain a die of temporary flesh the first time each combat they drop a hostile to zero. The aristocrat's table is generous — to those seated at it. Maintain two Spawn. Allies within 15 ft gain 1d8 temp HP first time they reduce hostile to 0 each combat.

18Domain — Castle Markov

At the twentieth count the banquet hall opens: hostile living creatures make a Constitution save — two dice of hit points lost on failure — and he distributes the total taken as healing among himself and his Spawn, capped at six dice. The host drinks first. The host drinks most. Initiative 20: hostile living creatures Con save; failure loses 2d10 HP, and you distribute total lost HP as healing among yourself/Spawn, capped 6d10.

20Ancient Vampire

The ancient's table. His Blood maximum becomes fifteen and combat opens at three; past ten Blood he flies at sixty and turns aside the mundane blows; once per long rest, at zero hit points, he may spend all his Blood — at least five — to remain at one. Death has been sending invitations for centuries. He keeps declining. Blood max 15; begin combat at 3. At 10+ Blood fly 60 and resist B/P/S. Once/long rest at 0 HP spend all Blood minimum 5 to remain at 1.

Pinnacle expression

Maximum, Domain, or equivalent.

Maximum — Exsanguinate (10 CE + up to 10 Blood; action): The whole vintage, drunk at once. Ten energy and up to ten Blood: creatures of his choice within thirty feet make a Constitution save — eight dice of necrotic plus one per two Blood, half on success — and he heals up to half the total dealt, capped at his maximum. The cellar, emptied in a single toast. creatures of your choice in 30 ft Con save, 8d10 necrotic +1d10 per 2 Blood spent; half success. Heal up to half total damage dealt, capped at your maximum HP.

Feats

Technique Feats

Vampiric Link

His thirst is efficient; nothing is wasted. When his vampiric healing would exceed his maximum, the excess becomes temporary hit points — up to five plus his level, for a minute, his veins visibly darkening. The stolen-blood shield, recalculated each time. Your thirst is efficient; nothing is wasted. Whenever one of your vampire features would heal you above your maximum HP, the excess becomes temporary HP instead, up to a maximum of 5 + your level. This stolen-blood shield lasts 1 minute and visibly darkens your veins, and it does not stack with itself — recalculate the total each time.

Blood Artist

Every death near him is a vintage. Once per round, when a creature within thirty feet dies — his own summon included — his reaction harvests its final pulse for one Blood. Friend or foe; he does not discriminate. No death pays twice. Every death near you is a vintage. Once per round, when a creature within 30 feet of you dies — including one of your own summons — you may use your reaction to harvest its final pulse, gaining 1 Blood. This works on any death you witness, friend or foe; Sorin does not discriminate. You cannot gain Blood twice from the same death.

Ancient Grudge

When a creature damages him, his reaction may taste its blood from afar — learning its current hit points and whether it bleeds below half. The aristocrat keeps a cellar book on everyone who has ever cut him. when a creature damages you, you may use your reaction to taste its blood from afar: learn its current HP total and whether it is below half.

Sanguine Bond

He forges a bond with a chosen enemy: one Blood as a bonus action marks a creature within sixty feet until his next turn ends — and the first time each turn he heals himself, the marked one takes his proficiency in necrotic. His wounds close; theirs open in sympathy. Foreign healing muddies the link and breaks it. You forge a sanguine bond with a chosen enemy: as a bonus action for 1 Blood, mark one creature you can see within 60 feet until the end of your next turn. The first time each turn you heal yourself while the mark persists, the marked creature takes necrotic damage equal to your proficiency bonus — your wounds close and theirs open in sympathy. The bond breaks early if the target receives magical healing from another source; foreign blood muddies the link.

Rules integration

Edge cases and shared systems.

Shared rulings: Technique DC = 8 + PB + CE ability modifier. Direct rolled attacks may crit and use the campaign Black Flash procedure when eligible. Tokens, autonomous summons, passive damage, and initiative-20 Domain effects cannot Black Flash. Maximum Output modifies ordinary supported techniques, never named Maxima or Domains.
