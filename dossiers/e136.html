Bloodlord

Blood counters / vampirism / aristocratic domination

Power tierTier IV · Special Grade
Core law

What the technique actually does.

Core Engine — Blood. Blood max 10. Gain 1 once/turn when you deal slashing/necrotic damage to a living target or heal from a hostile creature. Blood fuels vampiric regeneration and commands.

Full progression

Level 1 through 20.

1Vampiric Thirst

On melee hit spend 1 CE: +1d8 necrotic and heal same roll, once/turn.

3Blood Needle

1 CE, 60-ft technique attack, 2d8 piercing/necrotic; on hit gain 1 Blood.

6Aristocrat's Command

2 CE + 1 Blood, Wis save: target cannot willingly move closer to you or attack one named creature until end next turn.

9Vampire Spawn

4 CE + 3 Blood: summon one blood shikigami for 1 minute, AC 15, HP 30, bite 3d8 and heals itself half.

11Blood Magic

Spend up to PB Blood when casting damaging feature; each adds 1d8 necrotic, max once/turn.

13Markov Immortality

Bonus action 3 Blood heal 5d10. Radiant damage since last turn halves it.

15Lord of Innistrad

Maintain two Spawn. Allies within 15 ft gain 1d8 temp HP first time they reduce hostile to 0 each combat.

18Domain — Castle Markov

Initiative 20: hostile living creatures Con save; failure loses 2d10 HP, and you distribute total lost HP as healing among yourself/Spawn, capped 6d10.

20Ancient Vampire

Blood max 15; begin combat at 3. At 10+ Blood fly 60 and resist B/P/S. Once/long rest at 0 HP spend all Blood minimum 5 to remain at 1.

Pinnacle expression

Maximum, Domain, or equivalent.

Maximum — Exsanguinate (10 CE + up to 10 Blood): creatures of your choice in 30 ft Con save, 8d10 necrotic +1d10 per 2 Blood spent; half success. Heal up to half total damage dealt, capped at your maximum HP.

Feats

Technique Feats

Vampiric Link

Your thirst is efficient; nothing is wasted. Whenever one of your vampire features would heal you above your maximum HP, the excess becomes temporary HP instead, up to a maximum of 5 + your level. This stolen-blood shield lasts 1 minute and visibly darkens your veins, and it does not stack with itself — recalculate the total each time.

Blood Artist

Every death near you is a vintage. Once per round, when a creature within 30 feet of you dies — including one of your own summons — you may use your reaction to harvest its final pulse, gaining 1 Blood. This works on any death you witness, friend or foe; Sorin does not discriminate. You cannot gain Blood twice from the same death.

Ancient Grudge

when a creature damages you, you may use your reaction to taste its blood from afar: learn its current HP total and whether it is below half.

Sanguine Bond

You forge a sanguine bond with a chosen enemy: as a bonus action for 1 Blood, mark one creature you can see within 60 feet until the end of your next turn. The first time each turn you heal yourself while the mark persists, the marked creature takes necrotic damage equal to your proficiency bonus — your wounds close and theirs open in sympathy. The bond breaks early if the target receives magical healing from another source; foreign blood muddies the link.

Rules integration

Edge cases and shared systems.

Shared rulings: Technique DC = 8 + PB + CE ability modifier. Direct rolled attacks may crit and use the campaign Black Flash procedure when eligible. Tokens, autonomous summons, passive damage, and initiative-20 Domain effects cannot Black Flash. Maximum Output modifies ordinary supported techniques, never named Maxima or Domains.
