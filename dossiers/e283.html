Core law

At-the-table character reference

Recovered printed values & campaign clarifications

Shadow Strike · level 1:

when you make an attack roll, you can spend 1 cursed energy to target a creature's shadow that exists within their space. If the creature is unaware of your technique, you automatically hit as they don't guard their shadow. The target takes your attack in necrotic damage and you deal additional damage equal to your Cursed Energy modifier. This increases to twice your Cursed Energy modifier at 3rd level, thrice at 6th level, and four times at 11th level.

Awareness procedure:

if your attack roll is higher than the creature's passive Perception, they are unaware of how they were damaged. If your attack roll is lower, then they see you attack their shadow and become aware of your technique. A creature that is aware of your technique can make a Dexterity saving throw to try and avoid your assault on their shadow. On a failure, you automatically hit. On a success, you roll against their AC. You cannot use this technique if you cannot see the target's shadow.

Dark Binding · level 3:

when you make a weapon attack, you can spend 3 cursed energy to target a creature's shadow. On a hit, instead of dealing damage you release your grip on the weapon used to make the attack, leaving it to pin the target's shadow in place. The creature is grappled and restrained and is unable to move or be moved until the weapon is removed. The weapon can be removed as an attack.

Core law

Completed rules

Void Steps · level 6:

you have learned to become one with the shadows, gaining darkvision out to 200 ft and can see through magical darkness within this range. You can spend 2 CE to cast Pass Without Trace. When you take the Hide action in dim light or darkness, you become invisible until you attack, use a technique, or enter an area of bright light.

Shadows of Mortality · level 11:

you can use Shadow Strike as a free action once per turn for 1 CE. This special attack deals critical damage on a hit if the target has not taken a turn in combat or is surprised. You cannot roll for a black flash in this way.

Shadow visibility:

"cannot use if you cannot see the target's shadow" is a hard requirement — darkness, cover of the shadow, or lack of light can deny the technique; the GM adjudicates shadow visibility per scene. Creatures without shadows (some constructs, incorporeal) are immune by the visibility requirement.

Pinnacle expression

Maximum, Domain, or equivalent.

Maximum: Heavenly Star of Darkness: Prerequisite: Shadows of Mortality. As an action for 10 cursed energy, you invoke primordial darkness, forming a dark sun 300 ft in the sky that inhabits a 30 ft sphere for 1 minute. A creature that enters the 30 ft sphere the dark sun inhabits takes 4d6 necrotic damage and again at the start of their turns. At the end of the minute, you can spend 1 cursed energy to increase the duration by 1 minute. The technique ends if you become incapacitated. The dark sun radiates magical darkness within 600 ft of it that you can see normally in and any light from a technique costing less than 5 cursed energy is suppressed within this area. Any creature within that range with a Cursed Energy score or Passive perception higher than your Cursed Energy save DC can see through this magical darkness as if it were dim light. You can use Shadow Strike against a creature within this area without spending cursed energy and the target has disadvantage on Dexterity saves to protect their shadow. If you spend cursed energy on Shadow Strike against a creature within this area, they are considered vulnerable to the damage.

Domain Expansion: Zero Hour: 18 — At 18th level, you have mastered your technique allowing you to expand your domain. Within your domain, the shadows of all creatures are always visible to you and stretch towards you so they are always at your feet and able to be targeted. At initiative 20, three creatures of your choice suffer the full effect of Shadow Strike.

Build-defining options.

These are optional character-building feats. You must meet each listed prerequisite before selecting it.

Prerequisite: Must have Shadow Strike

Shadow User

You have furthered the uses of your shadow choosing one of the following features (This feat can be taken multiple times). Shadow Skulker: As an action for 2 CE you physically enter the shadows gaining full cover for X rounds or until you exit from a shadow within 10 feet. As an action while the technique is active, you can move into a shadow within 10 ft. You can hear and see through the shadow you entered. If the shadow is sufficiently illuminated, you exit the shadow. (X = CE mod). Shadow Envoy: As an action for 2 cursed energy, you can imbue your cursed energy into a creature's shadow within 5 ft. To attempt this technique without them noticing, you must make a Cursed Energy check against their passive perception. You can imbue conditions for when the technique is triggered. You can choose to leave either a melee attack with a range of 10 ft using Shadow Strike or a simple message. If you choose to leave a Shadow Strike, it targets a specific creature under a condition you set, uses your Cursed Energy modifier and is made at advantage if the target is unaware. If you choose a message, you can choose to leave five words. When you create the message, you can choose any number of creatures who can see the message in the shadow. Two shadow spells below 5th level. The CE cost equals the spell's level.

Prerequisite: Must have Dark Binding

Lingering Bindings

Your binding no longer needs to hold your weapon hostage. When you use Dark Binding, you can spend an additional 2 cursed energy: the target's shadow is pinned by your cursed energy alone, and you keep your weapon. The binding lasts for 1 minute while you concentrate (the target remains grappled and restrained), and the target can use its action to make a Strength check against your CE save DC to tear free. You can maintain only one such binding at a time.

Rules integration

Edge cases and shared systems.

Rules priority: the reviewed rulings, explicit codex clarifications, and character reference above always control when original wording conflicts. The audit dispositions for AT203 are adopted as a new campaign technique with the exact changes noted above.
Two-stage awareness gate: the awareness procedure is retained exactly — passive Perception comparison first, then a Dexterity save for the aware. The automatic hit against the unaware is a printed exception; it still requires the 1 CE spend and a visible shadow. The necrotic conversion replaces the attack's damage type; the CE-modifier multiplier scales at 3/6/11 as printed.
Black Flash HOLD: whether the automatic hit against unaware targets can trigger Black Flash is an audit HOLD (AT203) — a hit with no attack roll is made; the proposed default is no Black Flash without a d20 roll, pending zack's call for uniformity.
