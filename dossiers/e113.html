Prime Conduit

Electrokinesis / electromagnetism / ionic powers

Power tierTier IV · Special Grade
Core law

What the technique actually does.

Core Engine — Charge. Charge max = level + CE modifier. Absorb electricity as an action from a meaningful source to regain PB Charge; grid-scale sources may fully refill once per scene. Charge is separate from CE.

Full progression

Level 1 through 20.

1Lightning Bolt

60-ft ranged technique attack, 1 Charge, 2d8 lightning. You resist lightning and can safely drain ordinary electrical systems.

3Shock Grenade

1 CE + 1 Charge, 15-ft burst, Dex save 3d8 lightning/force.

6Induction Grind

Move along power lines/rails at double speed; Static Thrusters grant controlled glide. Electromagnetic Pulse: 2 CE pushes 20 ft in cone.

9Bio-Leech / Healing Pulse

Choose karmic expression after long rest. Leech: grappled enemy Con save, drain 3d8 HP and regain 2 Charge. Heal: spend 2 Charge to heal touched ally 3d8.

11Lightning Tether

1 Charge: 60-ft grapple line; pull yourself to terrain or Str save to pull Large-or-smaller target 20 ft.

13Ionic Power

Spend 3 Charge + 4 CE: Ionic Freeze (30-ft cone, 6d8 cold + restrained on failed Con) or Ionic Drain (20-ft radius 5d8 necrotic and heal half).

15Lightning Storm

6 CE + 4 Charge, Focus: 60-ft moving storm for 3 rounds; bonus action strike 10-ft point for 5d10 lightning Dex save.

18Ionic Storm

8 CE + 6 Charge: call a direct sky bolt at visible target; Dex save 12d10 lightning, half success; adjacent creatures take 4d10.

20Beast of the Grid

Charge max doubles; when standing in an active city grid, regain 1 Charge/round. Lightning immunity; Ionic Storm once/long rest costs no Charge.

Pinnacle expression

Maximum, Domain, or equivalent.

Maximum — Empire Thunderhead (10 CE + 8 Charge): 60-ft-radius storm for one round. Initiative-like immediate event: choose up to PB strike points; each 10-ft burst deals 8d10 lightning, Dex half. One creature can be affected once.

Feats

Build-defining options.

Precision:

Lightning Bolt ignores half cover and crits 19–20 if you did not move.

Polarity Wall:

reaction 1 Charge for +3 AC vs projectile.

Thunder Drop:

falling 20+ ft creates 10-ft 2d8 burst.

Karmic Overload:

once/long rest Charge costs become 0 for one round.

Rules integration

Edge cases and shared systems.

Shared rulings: Technique DC = 8 + PB + CE ability modifier. Direct rolled attacks may crit and use the campaign Black Flash procedure; autonomous hazards and Domain events cannot. Maximum Output may modify ordinary supported features, never named Maxima/Domains. Two-technique characters use the campaign's agreed character-level progression.
