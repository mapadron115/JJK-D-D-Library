Prime Conduit

Electrokinesis / electromagnetism / ionic powers

Power tierTier IV · Special Grade
Core law

What the technique actually does.

Core Engine, Charge. Understand the economy before the artillery. His Charge is not courage and not cursed energy, it is stored weather, and weather must be borrowed from somewhere that has it. Charge max = level + CE modifier. Absorb electricity as an action from a meaningful source to regain PB Charge; grid-scale sources may fully refill once per scene. Charge is separate from CE.

Field notes. Subject: the city, wearing a man. Three truths govern him. First: he is not the lightning, he is the wire it travels, the substation where the storm pays its toll; his Charge is weather kept in a jar, and jars run dry. Second: he drinks only where the grid is generous, a live socket, a power line, a city humming beneath his feet, which means a wise enemy draws him onto dead ground. Third: when the current turns inward it can close a wound or crack one open; leech or pulse, he chooses after dawn, and the choice holds until morning. Mark the final accounting: at the top of his art he does not throw lightning, he becomes the grid itself, and the sky answers before it is asked.

Full progression

Level 1 through 20.

1Lightning Bolt

The handshake comes first. Every Conduit learns the bolt before the storm: sixty feet of authority, straight as a telegraph line, and the discipline of never being burned by the wire he holds. 60-ft ranged technique attack, 1 Charge, 2d8 lightning. You resist lightning and can safely drain ordinary electrical systems.

3Shock Grenade

He learns to throw the storm instead of being it. One CE, one Charge, the price of turning a direct line into a scatter of ruin. 1 CE + 1 Charge, 15-ft burst, Dex save 3d8 lightning/force.

6Induction Grind

The grid becomes a road. I have watched him ride a rail line like a courier with urgent news, and the empire's own infrastructure never once asked his business. Move along power lines/rails at double speed; Static Thrusters grant controlled glide: while falling you descend 60 ft per round and may move horizontally up to your speed; you take no falling damage if you land during the glide. Electromagnetic Pulse: action, 2 CE, 30-ft cone; creatures in the cone make a Strength save or are pushed 20 ft away from you, half push on success.

9Bio-Leech / Healing Pulse

Every morning he signs the same contract: the current will either take or give, and it will do so at his word. Choose at dawn; the storm does not negotiate at dusk. Choose karmic expression after long rest. Leech: grappled enemy Con save, drain 3d8 HP and regain 2 Charge. Heal: spend 2 Charge to heal touched ally 3d8.

11Lightning Tether

The leash runs both ways. He may reel himself to the mountain, or reel the mountain to himself, the tether does not care which end is the prisoner. 1 Charge: 60-ft grapple line; pull yourself to terrain or Str save to pull Large-or-smaller target 20 ft.

13Ionic Power

Cold or rot, the two faces of stolen motion. The same current that freezes the air can drain the living; I have seen both ends of the wire. Spend 3 Charge + 4 CE: Ionic Freeze (30-ft cone, 6d8 cold + restrained on failed Con) or Ionic Drain (20-ft radius 5d8 necrotic and heal half).

15Lightning Storm

Three rounds of his own weather. He walks beneath a sky he carries, and the bonus action is the thunder deciding where to land. 6 CE + 4 Charge, Focus: 60-ft moving storm for 3 rounds; bonus action strike 10-ft point for 5d10 lightning Dex save.

18Ionic Storm

The sky answers before it is asked. Twelve dice of heaven's own vocabulary, and the ground around the target keeps the echo. 8 CE + 6 Charge: call a direct sky bolt at visible target; Dex save 12d10 lightning, half success; adjacent creatures take 4d10.

20Beast of the Grid

The final truth of the substation: stand in a live city and he does not spend, he channels. The grid feeds him now, not the other way around. Charge max doubles; when standing in an active city grid, regain 1 Charge/round. Lightning immunity; Ionic Storm once/long rest costs no Charge.

Pinnacle expression

Maximum, Domain, or equivalent.

Maximum, Empire Thunderhead (10 CE + 8 Charge; action): 60-ft-radius storm for one round. Initiative-like immediate event: choose up to PB strike points; each 10-ft burst deals 8d10 lightning, Dex half. One creature can be affected once.

Feats

Build-defining options.

Conductive Arc

He aims at the metal, not the man. The arc does the aiming afterward, a lesson in who really chooses the target. You may fire Lightning Bolt at an unattended metal object or conductive surface within range instead of a creature: the charge arcs outward, each creature within 10 ft of the point makes a Dex save, taking the bolt's damage on a failure.

Polarity Shift

He does not catch the arrow; he persuades it to be disloyal. One Charge as a reaction, and another man's aim becomes his instrument. When a creature within 30 ft makes a ranged weapon attack, you may spend 1 Charge as a reaction to bend the projectile's path: redirect the attack to a different creature within 15 ft of the original target, making a new attack roll.

Thunder Drop

The fall is the attack. I have watched him turn twenty feet of gravity into a detonation, the landing itself filed as a weapon. When you fall at least 20 ft, you may spend 1 Charge as a reaction just before landing: you take no falling damage, and the grounded impact erupts, each creature within 10 ft of where you land makes a Dexterity save, taking 2d8 lightning damage on a failure. Structures and unattended metal objects in the burst take double dice.

Karmic Overload

The vault emptied at once. It is the gambler's last hand: everything discharged, everything at zero, and the field rewritten in a single gesture. You may discharge all remaining Charge at once (minimum 6) as an action: each creature within 20 ft makes a Con save, taking 1d8 lightning per 2 Charge discharged on a failure (half on success); you are left at 0 Charge.

Rules integration

Edge cases and shared systems.

Shared rulings: Technique DC = 8 + PB + CE ability modifier. Direct rolled attacks may crit and use the campaign Black Flash procedure; autonomous hazards and Domain events cannot. Maximum Output may modify ordinary supported features, never named Maxima/Domains. Two-technique characters use the campaign's agreed character-level progression.
