████ and Light

Form killing intent into bars of light — a conjured radiant blade, burst barrages, dawn-fire, and hyperspeed movement.

TierTier III

Feature map

Level-by-level features, as the figure refined them.

L1

Light Constructs

Bonus action, 2 CE

You learn the Light cantrip. As a bonus action for 2 cursed energy, you mold killing intent into a bar of light in a free hand. This weapon lasts for 1 minute and counts as a simple melee weapon with which you are proficient. It deals 2d8 radiant damage on a hit and has the finesse, light, and thrown properties (range 20/60). You can use your Cursed Energy modifier for attack and damage rolls using this weapon. If you drop the weapon or throw it, it dissipates and reappears in your hand.

L3

Rain of Light

Action, 3 CE

In either a 15 ft cone or a 10 ft radius around yourself, all creatures make a Dexterity saving throw. On a failed save, they take 3d10 radiant damage and are restrained by bars of light piercing their body. A restrained creature can repeat the save at the end of their turn, escaping on a success. Damage increases to 5d10 at 6th level, 8d10 at 11th level, and 10d10 at 18th level. The cone's range increases by 15 ft and the radius increases by 5 ft at each of 6th, 11th, and 18th level (cumulative — invented clarification).

L3

████ of Dawn

Action, 2 CE

Any magical darkness within 30 feet of you is dispelled. Each hostile creature within 30 feet of you must make a Constitution saving throw: on a failure they take 2d10 + your CE modifier radiant damage and are blinded until the end of their turn; on a success they take half as much damage and are not blinded. A creature behind total cover is unaffected. Damage increases to 3d10 at 6th level, 5d10 at 11th level, and 8d10 at 18th level.

L6

Hyperspeed

Passive

You learn to imbue your body and constructs with high speed. You gain resistance to radiant damage.

L6

Light Beam

1 CE per attack, on hit

When you make an attack with your Light Construct, you can spend 1 CE to make the attack at advantage and launch the bar, making a ranged spell attack against a creature within 60 ft. When you hit a creature with a melee attack using your Light Construct, you can teleport 10 ft to an unoccupied space you can see as your body becomes a beam of light for a moment — once per turn (invented cap).

L6

Laser Light Techniques

Additional CE, up to your CE modifier

When you use a Light Technique, you can spend additional CE up to your CE modifier. You deal an additional damage die of the same size as the technique's own damage die per CE spent (invented clarification). This increases to 2 additional dice per CE at 11th level and 3 additional dice at 18th level.

Pinnacle expression

Maximum, Domain, or equivalent.

Capstone material.

Maximum Technique

Maximum: Light Speed

Action, 8 CE

You instantaneously move in a 5 ft wide, 120 feet long line. All creatures in that line must make a Constitution saving throw or take 18d10 radiant damage and be knocked prone on a failed save, or take half as much damage and not be knocked prone on a successful save. For every creature, object, or structure you move through, you must make a Constitution saving throw against your Cursed Energy save DC; on a failed save, you take 20 bludgeoning damage. If used at Maximum Output, objects and structures take maximum damage and creatures with a Constitution score lower than your CE score automatically fail the saving throw. Maximum Output (defined): when you activate this Maximum, you may spend additional CE, up to your CE modifier, to push it to Maximum Output; the printed Maximum Output effects then apply (invented definition — the C&C source uses the term but never defines it). At 18th level the line increases to 200 ft, and at 20th level to 300 ft.

Domain Expansion

Domain Expansion: Reverse Transcendence

Full-turn, 20 CE

At initiative 20, all creatures in your domain take 6d12 radiant damage. While within your domain, your movement speed doubles and all movement on your turn is considered an instantaneous teleport and cannot be reacted to, as your every move is at light speed. As an action on your turn, you can teleport anywhere within your domain; choose one creature within 5 ft of the point you start from or end up at, and that creature takes 4d12 radiant damage.
