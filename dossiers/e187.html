Chooser of the Slain

Valkyrie inspiration. Record dramatic ally Deeds when they crit, survive death, rescue someone, or turn a fight. Spend the recorded heroism later to protect or empower the same warrior.

Combat roleRescue support / heroic finisher
Power tierTier IV · Special Grade
Core law

Character engine

The ledger's rules: dramatic ally Deeds, crits, deaths survived, rescues, fights turned, recorded automatically on witness, spent back onto the same warrior, capped at PB and cleared each long rest. Record dramatic ally Deeds when they crit, survive death, rescue someone, or turn a fight. Recording is automatic (no action) when you witness the event (invented); "turn a fight" is GM-adjudicated, the guideline is that the ally’s action directly drops a hostile to 0 HP or ends the encounter’s primary threat (invented). Spend the recorded heroism later to protect or empower the same warrior. Stored Deeds cap at PB; clear all unspent Deeds on a long rest.

Technique save DC: 8 + PB + chosen CE ability modifier. Technique attack: PB + chosen CE ability modifier.

Field notes. Subject: an accountant of valor. Three truths govern her. First: glory is a ledger that must balance. She records only what she witnesses, the critical strike, the rescued friend, the death survived by a margin, and spends each record back onto the same warrior who earned it. Second: she is a rescue that never ended. Her maneuvers carry the fallen from the field, not because she was fast enough once, but because she was not. The grief is legible in the finest maneuver. Third: her Domain is a hall, not a slaughterhouse. Hall Where the Worthy Rise grants the chosen their moments, movement, a strike, a hand extended, and asks nothing of the enemy. Consider her as the valkyrie in miniature: she does not win the battle. She decides, deed by deed, who walks out of it.

Full progression

Level progression

1Ride to the Fallen

The first rescue: one Deed and one CE carry her twenty feet to a wounded Deed-holder without provoking, softening the blow by a d8 plus her modifier. Ride to the Fallen (reaction, 1 CE): spend one of that creature's Deeds to move 20 feet toward it without provoking opportunity attacks, and reduce the triggering damage by one d8 + your CE modifier (invented: the die is a d8).

The empowering: two CE and one Deed name an ally Chosen for a minute, ten feet of speed and a Valor Die of d8 on one damage roll per turn. Chosen Warrior (bonus action, 2 CE): spend one Deed to empower one ally for 1 minute, +10 feet of speed and +1 Valor Die (a d8, invented) to one damage roll per turn.

Reaction · 2 CE · When a creature within 30 ft drops to 0 HP: fly to it and carry it clear; it accrues no failed death saves from ordinary movement or the environment through its next turn.

The valkyrie's own flight: two CE as a reaction to reach a creature at zero HP within thirty feet and carry it clear, no failed death saves from movement or the environment through its next turn. Carry from the Field (reaction, 2 CE): when a creature within 30 feet drops to 0 HP, fly to it and carry it clear. It does not accrue failed death saves from ordinary movement or the environment through its next turn.

Two of the warrior's Deeds plus three CE refuse the killing blow: the Chosen stays at one HP, moves half speed as a reaction, and gains 2d8 plus her modifier in temporary HP, once per creature per long rest. Einherjar's Second Breath: when a Chosen Warrior would drop to 0 HP, you may spend two of its Deeds + 3 CE: it stays at 1 HP, as a reaction moves up to half its speed, and gains temporary HP equal to 2d8 + your CE modifier (invented). Once per creature per long rest.

18Domain Expansion: Hall Where the Worthy Rise

Domain Expansion · Full-turn action · 20 CE · Enclosed 60-ft radius centered on you · up to 1 minute · Technique Focus · Beneficial rule-Domain (no automatic damage): when it opens, every willing ally inside gains a temporary Deed (vanish when it ends) · At initiative 20, choose one willing creature inside with a Deed: it immediately moves up to half its speed without provoking, then makes one weapon attack, takes the Help action, or stabilizes a creature (never activates a Domain, Maximum, Extreme Magic, another extra-turn feature, or grants another action); spend one of its Deeds after it resolves · Hostiles receive no automatic damage or failure.

A mead-hall vast as a canyon, its rafters lost in golden haze, its long tables set for a feast no living mouth will eat. Banners hang motionless in air that does not stir, each bearing a name the wind has not yet learned to forget. Light falls in shafts like spears planted in the floor, and in that light the worthy are already standing.

A beneficial rule-Domain: sixty feet, one minute, no automatic harm. Every willing ally inside is granted a temporary Deed, and at initiative twenty one Deed-holder moves half speed and takes one chosen action, a strike, a Help, a stabilizing hand. Full-turn action, 20 CE, enclosed 60-foot radius centered on you, up to 1 minute, Technique Focus. This is a beneficial rule-Domain rather than a lethal sure-hit. When it opens, record one temporary Deed for every willing ally inside; these temporary Deeds vanish when the Domain ends. At initiative 20 choose one willing creature inside with a Deed. It may immediately move up to half its speed without provoking and then either make one weapon attack, take the Help action, or stabilize a creature. The granted action cannot activate a Domain, Maximum, Extreme Magic, another extra-turn feature, or anything that grants another action. Spend one of that creature’s Deeds after it resolves. Hostiles receive no automatic damage or failure.

Feats

Build-defining options.

Winged Escort

Flight that carries: one willing creature borne without slowing, half cover from below, and Ride to the Fallen extended to thirty feet. When you fly, under your own power or by Carry from the Field, you may carry one willing creature without any reduction to your flying speed. While carrying a creature this way, your Ride to the Fallen reaction move extends to 30 feet toward a Deed-holder, and the carried creature gains half cover against attacks made from below you. You cannot carry a creature larger than you or heavier than your carrying capacity.

Saga-Keeper

Her own legend recorded: her crits, her brushes with death, her rescues, Deeds stored under her own name. You may record Deeds from your own dramatic moments, your critical hits, your brushes with death, your rescues, as though you were one of the allies you watch; these Deeds are stored under your name and spent normally.

Shieldmaiden

Interposition over absorption: Ride to the Fallen may impose disadvantage on the triggering attack instead, or grant advantage on a save against a non-attack effect. When you use Ride to the Fallen, you may forgo the damage reduction to instead impose disadvantage on the triggering attack roll, decided before the roll is made, your interposition ruins the strike's line rather than absorbing it. If the attack still hits, you may still spend the creature's Deed after damage is rolled to reduce it by one die + your CE modifier, completing the same 20-foot reaction move. Against effects that call for a saving throw instead of an attack roll, you may grant the protected creature advantage on the save instead.

Last Seat in Valhalla (11+)

At eleventh level, a spent Deed buys a rerolled death save, the ally uses the new result. When an ally fails a death save, you may spend one of their Deeds as a reaction to let them reroll it; they use the new result.

Rules integration

Domain procedure

This entry uses the repaired campaign Domain rules. The level-18 feature above is complete: its printed action, CE cost, radius, duration, initiative-20 law, fallback protection, and counterplay replace any legacy blanket auto-hit wording.
Rules integration

Counterplay

Exhaust Deeds before the decisive exchange and separate the Valkyrie from allies. It amplifies earned heroics rather than generating free extra turns.
