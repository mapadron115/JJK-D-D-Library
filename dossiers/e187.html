Domain Expansion: Hall Where the Worthy RiseFull-turn action, 20 CE, enclosed 60-foot radius centered on you, up to 1 minute, Technique Focus. This is a beneficial rule-Domain rather than a lethal sure-hit. When it opens, record one temporary Deed for every willing ally inside; these temporary Deeds vanish when the Domain ends. At initiative 20 choose one willing creature inside with a Deed. It may immediately move up to half its speed without provoking and then either make one weapon attack, take the Help action, or stabilize a creature. The granted action cannot activate a Domain, Maximum, Extreme Magic, another extra-turn feature, or anything that grants another action. Spend one of that creature’s Deeds after it resolves. Hostiles receive no automatic damage or failure.

Domain procedure

This entry uses the repaired campaign Domain rules. The level-18 feature above is complete: its printed action, CE cost, radius, duration, initiative-20 law, fallback protection, and counterplay replace any legacy blanket auto-hit wording.

Character engine

Record dramatic ally Deeds when they crit, survive death, rescue someone, or turn a fight. Spend the recorded heroism later to protect or empower the same warrior.

Technique save DC: 8 + PB + chosen CE ability modifier. Technique attack: PB + chosen CE ability modifier.

Counterplay

Exhaust Deeds before the decisive exchange and separate the Valkyrie from allies. It amplifies earned heroics rather than generating free extra turns.

Adaptation note. This is original JJK 5e homebrew inspired by Valkyrie and the named tradition. The folklore/myth supplies the motif; the resource engine, CE economy, progression, feats, Maximum and Domain procedures are game mechanics created for this codex.
