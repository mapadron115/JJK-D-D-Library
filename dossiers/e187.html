Chooser of the Slain

Valkyrie inspiration. Record dramatic ally Deeds when they crit, survive death, rescue someone, or turn a fight. Spend the recorded heroism later to protect or empower the same warrior.

Combat roleRescue support / heroic finisher
Power tierTier IV · Special Grade
Core law

Character engine

Record dramatic ally Deeds when they crit, survive death, rescue someone, or turn a fight. Recording is automatic (no action) when you witness the event (invented); "turn a fight" is GM-adjudicated — the guideline is that the ally’s action directly drops a hostile to 0 HP or ends the encounter’s primary threat (invented). Spend the recorded heroism later to protect or empower the same warrior. Stored Deeds cap at PB; clear all unspent Deeds on a long rest.

Technique save DC: 8 + PB + chosen CE ability modifier. Technique attack: PB + chosen CE ability modifier.

The fiction. Jujutsu scholars describe it as a rescue that never ended — the sorcerer's cursed energy learned to record the exact shape of a valiant moment: the critical strike, the rescued friend, the death survived by a margin. Those records are spent back onto the same warriors because the technique believes glory is a ledger that must balance. The prevailing theory holds that the technique's truest grief is legible in its finest maneuver: it carries the fallen from the field because it was not fast enough, once.

Full progression

Level progression

1Ride to the Fallen

Ride to the Fallen (reaction, 1 CE): spend one of that creature's Deeds to move 20 feet toward it without provoking opportunity attacks, and reduce the triggering damage by one d8 + your CE modifier (invented: the die is a d8).

3Chosen Warrior

Chosen Warrior (bonus action, 2 CE): spend one Deed to empower one ally for 1 minute — +10 feet of speed and +1 Valor Die (a d8, invented) to one damage roll per turn.

6Carry from the Field

Carry from the Field (reaction, 2 CE): when a creature within 30 feet drops to 0 HP, fly to it and carry it clear. It does not accrue failed death saves from ordinary movement or the environment through its next turn.

11Einherjar's Second Breath

Einherjar's Second Breath: when a Chosen Warrior would drop to 0 HP, you may spend two of its Deeds + 3 CE: it stays at 1 HP, as a reaction moves up to half its speed, and gains temporary HP equal to 2d8 + your CE modifier (invented). Once per creature per long rest.

18Domain Expansion: Hall Where the Worthy Rise

Full-turn action, 20 CE, enclosed 60-foot radius centered on you, up to 1 minute, Technique Focus. This is a beneficial rule-Domain rather than a lethal sure-hit. When it opens, record one temporary Deed for every willing ally inside; these temporary Deeds vanish when the Domain ends. At initiative 20 choose one willing creature inside with a Deed. It may immediately move up to half its speed without provoking and then either make one weapon attack, take the Help action, or stabilize a creature. The granted action cannot activate a Domain, Maximum, Extreme Magic, another extra-turn feature, or anything that grants another action. Spend one of that creature’s Deeds after it resolves. Hostiles receive no automatic damage or failure.

Feats

Build-defining options.

Winged Escort

When you fly — under your own power or by Carry from the Field — you may carry one willing creature without any reduction to your flying speed. While carrying a creature this way, your Ride to the Fallen reaction move extends to 30 feet toward a Deed-holder, and the carried creature gains half cover against attacks made from below you. You cannot carry a creature larger than you or heavier than your carrying capacity.

Saga-Keeper

You may record Deeds from your own dramatic moments — your critical hits, your brushes with death, your rescues — as though you were one of the allies you watch; these Deeds are stored under your name and spent normally.

Shieldmaiden

When you use Ride to the Fallen, you may forgo the damage reduction to instead impose disadvantage on the triggering attack roll, decided before the roll is made — your interposition ruins the strike's line rather than absorbing it. If the attack still hits, you may still spend the creature's Deed after damage is rolled to reduce it by one die + your CE modifier, completing the same 20-foot reaction move. Against effects that call for a saving throw instead of an attack roll, you may grant the protected creature advantage on the save instead.

Last Seat in Valhalla (11+)

When an ally fails a death save, you may spend one of their Deeds as a reaction to let them reroll it; they use the new result.

Rules integration

Domain procedure

This entry uses the repaired campaign Domain rules. The level-18 feature above is complete: its printed action, CE cost, radius, duration, initiative-20 law, fallback protection, and counterplay replace any legacy blanket auto-hit wording.
Rules integration

Counterplay

Exhaust Deeds before the decisive exchange and separate the Valkyrie from allies. It amplifies earned heroics rather than generating free extra turns.
