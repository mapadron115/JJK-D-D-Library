DJINN 24 • Heat / Ice / Lightning

Cerberus — Severity & Fascination

Rank S
Canon master: Mira Dianus ArteminaBasis: Canon core • tri-element Djinn, Cerbero Hadisa, Cerbero Jairu Ranquesa, Cerbero Kalb Volquesa

Weapon Equip • Level 5

Threefold Trident. Weapon Equip becomes a three-pronged vessel; each attack can be fire, cold, or lightning.

Triune Fang

Action • 3 CE

Make three Djinn attacks at creatures within 180 ft, one fire, one cold, one lightning. Each hit deals 3 Djinn Dice. A creature hit by all three also takes 2 Djinn Dice force.

Cerbero Hadisa

Action • 5 CE

Flames converge from all directions on a 70-ft-radius point within 180 ft. Dexterity save; 8 Djinn Dice fire, half on success. Failure also blocks reactions until next turn.

Cerbero Jairu Ranquesa

Action • 5 CE

Summon three elemental hounds for 1 minute. Each occupies a space within 60 ft and can be commanded together as a bonus action; their attacks deal 3 Djinn Dice of fire, cold, or lightning and can grapple on a failed Strength save.

Full Djinn Equip • Level 9

Fly 110 ft; resistance to fire, cold, and lightning. Once per turn after dealing one element, your next different element against the same target deals +2 Djinn Dice and applies: fire = burning, cold = −20 speed, lightning = no reactions.

EXTREME MAGIC • LEVEL 13+

Cerbero Kalb Volquesa

14 CE • Canon name / Codex effect because canon attack was interrupted

A 180-ft-radius threefold calamity erupts within 300 ft. Dexterity save; 6 Djinn Dice fire + 6 cold + 6 lightning, half on success. Failure also burns, reduces speed to 0, and prevents reactions until the start of your next turn.

Anime-scale: Strategic scale: an army-scale composite storm. Canon never showed the completed effect, so the exact mechanics are deliberately labeled extrapolation.
