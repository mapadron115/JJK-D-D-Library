Features & procedures

Exact player-facing procedures.

Weapon Equip • Level 5

Threefold Trident.

Weapon Equip becomes a three-pronged vessel; each attack can be fire, cold, or lightning.

Action • 3 Reserve

Triune Fang

Make three Djinn attacks at creatures within 180 ft, one fire, one cold, one lightning. Each hit deals 3 Djinn Dice. A creature hit by all three also takes 2 Djinn Dice force.

Action • 5 Reserve

Cerbero Hadisa

Flames converge from all directions on a 70-ft-radius point within 180 ft. Dexterity save; 8 Djinn Dice fire, half on success. Failure also blocks reactions until next turn.

Action • 5 Reserve

Cerbero Jairu Ranquesa

Summon three elemental hounds for 1 minute. Each occupies a space within 60 ft and can be commanded together as a bonus action; their attacks deal 3 Djinn Dice of fire, cold, or lightning and can grapple on a failed Strength save.

Full Djinn Equip • Level 9

Fly 110 ft; resistance to fire, cold, and lightning. Once per turn after dealing one element, your next different element against the same target deals +2 Djinn Dice and applies: fire = burning, cold = −20 speed, lightning = no reactions.

14 Reserve • Canon name / Codex effect because canon attack was interrupted

Cerbero Kalb Volquesa

A 180-ft-radius threefold calamity erupts within 300 ft. Dexterity save; 6 Djinn Dice fire + 6 cold + 6 lightning, half on success. Failure also burns, reduces speed to 0, and prevents reactions until the start of your next turn.

Anime-scale: Strategic scale: an army-scale composite storm. Canon never showed the completed effect, so the exact mechanics are deliberately labeled extrapolation.

Rules integration

Djinn Die, Equip & Extreme Magic

Independent Djinn Reserve: 18 (Rank S). Refreshes on a long rest. Every Djinn cost below — Weapon Equip, Full Equip, abilities, reactions, Extreme Magic — spends this Djinn’s Reserve, never your CE. If this Djinn’s Reserve is empty it simply cannot fuel more until you rest; your other Djinn keep their own separate Reserves.
Weapon Equip · level 5: bonus action, 2 Reserve, lasts 10 minutes.
Full Djinn Equip · level 9: bonus action, 4 Reserve, lasts 1 minute. Only one Equip can be active.
Deep Synchronization · level 11: once per short rest, one Equip activation costs 1 Reserve less.
Extreme Magic · level 13: action; requires that Djinn’s Full Equip; pay the listed Reserve cost from this Djinn’s Reserve; usable once per long rest per Djinn. Full Equip ends afterward, and you cannot Equip that Djinn again until you finish a short rest.
King Vessel · level 17: switch directly from one Full Equip to another as a bonus action, paying the new Equip cost. At level 20, once per long rest, immediately transition from one Djinn’s Extreme into another Djinn’s Full Equip.
Attack / save: use your CE attack modifier and CE save DC unless this Djinn explicitly says otherwise.
