Field notes. Subject: a revolver that holds its duels against fate. Two truths govern the Last Word. First: the weapon does not forgive bad luck, it collects it. Every miss against a creature you can see banks 1 Certainty, and the steel was cursed by the conviction that a bullet deserves to arrive eventually. Second: certainty is spent, not stored. Spend it to ignore cover, to add the Tool Die after the roll, and at full PB, Dead Certain, to spend it all on one hit that cannot critically strike or Black Flash, because the debt is paid in full, not in fireworks. The Ricochet Clause traces one bank shot around hard cover within long range, the tool's one courtesy to geometry. I have watched bearers miss four times and smile, because the fifth shot was already decided. Mark this: the Last Word is not a gun. It is a patient creditor, and every trigger pull is either a deposit or a collection.

Rules integration

Cursed-tool attunement

Campaign canon

Cursed-tool attunement: you have PB attunement slots (2–6). This tool occupies one slot and requires a one-hour ritual. Attunement ends voluntarily over a short rest, on death, or when you replace it while over capacity. Distance does not break attunement. Cursed-tool attunement is separate from magic-item attunement.
Balance & counterplay. Missing builds certainty, so repeated bad luck becomes pressure without turning every shot into an automatic hit.
