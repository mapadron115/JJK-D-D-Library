Features & procedures

Properties & techniques

Exact player-facing procedures

Feature

Spend 1 Certainty before an attack to ignore half and three-quarters cover.

Feature

Spend 2 Certainty after rolling but before the result to add one Tool Die to the attack roll.

Dead Certain

at PB Certainty, spend all on a hit to add PB Tool Dice; this damage cannot critically hit or Black Flash.

Ricochet Clause

2 CE: trace one bank shot around a hard surface; total path must remain within long range.

Rules integration

Cursed-tool attunement

Campaign canon

Cursed-tool attunement: you have PB attunement slots (2–6). This tool occupies one slot and requires a one-hour ritual. Attunement ends voluntarily over a short rest, on death, or when you replace it while over capacity. Distance does not break attunement. Cursed-tool attunement is separate from magic-item attunement.
Balance & counterplay. Missing builds certainty, so repeated bad luck becomes pressure without turning every shot into an automatic hit.
