Features & procedures

Properties & techniques

Exact player-facing procedures

Passive

Marked Steel

The knife counts as a 4th grade cursed tool and can cut low-grade curses or weak barriers that ordinary steel cannot harm. It otherwise functions as a mundane dagger.

3 uses

Cut a Barrier

While within 5 feet of a barrier, you can spend half a minute to discreetly cut a 5 ft hole in a Basic Curtain or similarly weak barrier, without alerting the one who placed it. You can use the knife in this way three times before it breaks and becomes unusable.

Rules integration

Edge cases and shared systems.

Campaign canon notes

Grade note: the source lists the knife as Grade 3 but states that it counts as a 4th grade cursed tool. It is treated as Grade 3 for acquisition and attunement purposes and as Grade 4 for power comparisons.
