Basic Cursed Tool Knife

A Grade 3 cursed tool, an ordinary steel dagger etched with faint markings.

FormWeapon (Dagger)
GradeGrade 3
AttunementNot required

Field notes. Subject: the quiet cut. An ordinary steel dagger etched with faint markings, counted as a fourth-grade tool, carried as a third, and its true work is not the fighting but the entering. Three truths govern it. First, the markings make it real to what ordinary steel cannot harm: low-grade curses, and weak barriers that would turn a mundane edge. Second, its signature is patience: within five feet of a barrier, half a minute of quiet work cuts a five-foot hole in a Basic Curtain or its like, discreetly, without alerting the one who placed it. Third, discretion is finite: three such cuts, and the knife breaks, becoming unusable. Mark this: the loudest weapons announce their wars. This one files its paperwork in silence and is gone before the alarm learns its name.

Passive

Marked Steel

Passive · Counts as a 4th-grade cursed tool · Can cut low-grade curses and weak barriers ordinary steel cannot harm; otherwise a mundane dagger.

The markings are the whole of it: counted as a fourth-grade tool, real to low-grade curses and weak barriers alike. Otherwise, a mundane dagger, asking for nothing. The knife counts as a 4th grade cursed tool and can cut low-grade curses or weak barriers that ordinary steel cannot harm. It otherwise functions as a mundane dagger.

3 uses

Cut a Barrier

3 uses · Within 5 ft of a barrier: spend half a minute to discreetly cut a 5-ft hole in a Basic Curtain or similarly weak barrier without alerting its placer · Breaks after the third use.

The quiet work: five feet from the barrier, half a minute of discretion, a five-foot hole in a Basic Curtain, and the placer never learns. Three uses, and the knife spends itself; the third cut is its last. While within 5 feet of a barrier, you can spend half a minute to discreetly cut a 5 ft hole in a Basic Curtain or similarly weak barrier, without alerting the one who placed it. You can use the knife in this way three times before it breaks and becomes unusable.
