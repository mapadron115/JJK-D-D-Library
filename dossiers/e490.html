Field entry

Journal Entry field report, converted to a table-ready stat block

Field notes. Subject: an assassin who hires gravity as the blade. Its bite is a joke, a 1d4 whisper, so it does not bite at all; it climbs (difficult walls, ceilings, upside down, no check needed) to where eyes rarely go, and lets the fall do the killing. Mark the technique: a drop of ten feet or more onto a victim’s space turns the pathetic bite into a strike with advantage, 2d6+2 bludgeoning, and a Strength save or the victim is knocked prone. The small sky, falling. And note the coroner’s wisdom recorded in the file: its kills are filed as falling debris, flower pots, loose fixtures. Check the ceiling before you declare an accident.

Record

Classification & Risk

An Imaginary Cursed Spirit, Grade 4 (CR 1/8–1/2 band). Its bite can hardly injure — so it positions itself high above a target and drops suddenly, crushing the victim beneath it.

Stats

Ability scores & traits

STR 6 (−2) · DEX 15 (+2) · CON 10 (+0) · INT 3 (−4) · WIS 10 (+0) · CHA 6 (−2). HP 16 (3d10), AC 12, speed 20 ft., climb 30 ft. Spider Climb. The curse can climb difficult surfaces, including upside down on ceilings, without needing to make an ability check. Invisibility.

Actions

Actions

Bite. Melee Attack: +4 to hit, reach 5 ft. Hit: 1d4 + 2 piercing damage — hardly capable of serious injury.

Risk

Special

Death from Above. If the Cruller drops at least 10 ft. onto a creature’s space, it makes its Bite attack with advantage; on a hit it deals 2d6 + 2 bludgeoning damage instead, and the target must succeed on a DC 11 Strength saving throw or be knocked prone. Deaths are commonly misattributed to flower pots or loose fixtures — check ceilings before declaring an accident.

Campaign canon notes

Field rule — low-grade perception: Per the C&C field rules, Grade 3 and lower curses are invisible to creatures without cursed energy. A creature in a life-or-death situation at less than half its hit point maximum may temporarily or permanently gain the ability to see them. This spirit carries the Invisibility trait below.
