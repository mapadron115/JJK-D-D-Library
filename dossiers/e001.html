Ryu · complete dragon companion profile

The Project names and prices Ryu but does not provide a readable stat block. The following explicitly labeled codex conversion completes that missing player-facing rule.

Summon:

level 11; action, 10 CE; open space within 30 feet; 1-hour duration. Large dragon shikigami; AC 13 + PB; HP 10 × character level; speed 30 feet, fly 60 feet.

Command:

Ryu acts immediately after you; command its action with your bonus action, otherwise it Dodges. It uses your CE attack modifier and CE save DC.

Bite:

melee CE attack, reach 10 feet; hit: 2d10 + CE modifier piercing damage.

Tempest Breath (recharge 5–6):

30-foot cone; Dexterity save; 5d8 lightning damage on failure, half on success.

If reduced to 0 HP, Ryu vanishes and cannot be summoned again until you complete a long rest. It has no independent CE, Multiattack, Domain Expansion, or Maximum Technique.

Core law

Completed rules

Kaichi:

the horn lasts until dismissed or resummoned. Once on the summoning turn and once on each of your turns thereafter, when you take the Attack action you may make one melee CE attack with it (10-foot reach) as part of that action. Damage is 1d6 at level 1, 1d8 at 3, 1d10 at 6, 1d12 at 11, and 3d6 at 18. It is not a separate creature.

Reiki:

the bonus-action option doubles walking speed until the end of your next turn. The reaction’s +5 AC applies only against the triggering attack, after seeing its roll but before knowing the result.

Kirin:

spend 1 to PB CE. Gain 5 temporary HP per CE; your speed becomes 0 until the start of your next turn. Existing exhaustion levels remain but impose no effects while any of these temporary HP remain.

Ryu:

use the complete Ryu dragon-companion profile in the character reference above. It appears in an open space within 30 feet, acts immediately after you, and remains for 1 hour, until dismissed, or until reduced to 0 HP. A bonus action commands it; otherwise it Dodges. If reduced to 0 HP, it cannot be summoned again until a long rest.

Pinnacle expression

Maximum, Domain, or equivalent.

Domain — Fourfold Omen: 18 — Auspicious Beasts’ Realm: all four gifts harmonize. while active, speed is doubled; Reiki’s reaction costs no CE; and on expansion gain Kirin temporary HP as if PB CE were spent without losing movement. At initiative 20, the Domain manifests both an antlered omen and a storm dragon around each chosen hostile. The target automatically takes 4d10 force plus 4d10 lightning damage, is pushed up to 20 feet, and cannot take reactions until the next initiative 20. A protected target makes a Dexterity save, taking half of the combined damage with no push or reaction loss on a success. Fourfold Omen is one Domain event and uses no active or stored summon; ordinary Kaichi and Ryu attacks roll normally.

Innate Technique Feats

Build-defining options.

These are optional character-building feats. You must meet each listed prerequisite before selecting it.

Prerequisite: Kaichi

Double Up

One Kaichi activation may create two drill-horn manifestations; pay and track each horn separately. Both persist under the normal Kaichi duration. Horns are not summoned creatures, do not require a summon command, and do not consume creature or manifestation capacity. When you take the Attack action, resolve each available horn attack as part of that action; this never grants a second action.

Prerequisite: Reiki

Zero Friction

Gain +2 AC only while Reiki’s movement increase is active. It does not stack with itself or remain during a turn in which the speed benefit is suppressed.

Prerequisite: Kirin and CE score 20

Maximum: Kirin’s Flames

While Kirin is active, each melee hit against you deals fire damage to its attacker equal to three times the HP you actually lost after reductions and temporary HP. This requires no reaction, cannot trigger from reflected damage, and cannot recursively trigger itself.

Rules integration

Edge cases and shared systems.

Rules priority: the reviewed rulings, explicit codex clarifications, and character reference above always control when original wording conflicts. Legacy Domain auto-hit language, action terminology, event timing, missing saves, and older scaling never override revised player rules.
Why Tier II: Ryu is a serious companion and the four modes cover many situations, but each mode has a readable job and the technique does not acquire permanent outside assets.

Auspicious Beast Summon
By covering your face you can become a medium for the spirit of the
four auspicious beasts. These four beasts gift you great abilities.
Cursed Energy
Starting at 1st level, you can harness your negative feelings into a
usable source of energy, called cursed energy. These feelings can be
harnessed into attacks or abilities. The number of cursed energy you
possess is based on your class, some classes use a modifier that
being charisma or wisdom (Player’s choice)
Your cursed energy DC is 8 + user cursed energy ability modifier +
proficiency bonus.
You regain all spent cursed energy on a long rest.
Auspicious Beast #1: Kaichi
Beginning at 1st level, as an action using 1 Cursed Energy, you
summon a yellow drill horn. This horn can be used to do an additional
attack dealing 1d6 piercing damage. The damage increases to 1d8 at
3rd level, 1d10 at 6th level, 1d12 at 11th level, and 3d6 at 18th
level.
You may only have one Kaichi active at a time.
Auspicious Beast #2: Reiki
At 3rd level, you summon dark water to assist yourself. You can
either use a bonus action to use 1 Cursed Energy and double your
movement for 1 until the end of your next turn. Alternatively, as a
reaction, for 2 Cursed Energy, you can add a +5 to your AC.
