Auspicious Beast Summon

Four discrete beast gifts: a weapon, a burst of movement or defense, temporary endurance, and Ryu.

Field notes. Subject: a sorcerer who wears four answers, and reaches for each only when the question is asked. Three truths govern the medium. First, every gift answers a need, never a whim: Kaichi the horn is 1 CE of striking reach; Reiki the dark water is speed doubled or a blow refused at +5 AC, each bought cheap, because each asks for little. Second, Kirin is the honest trade: endurance purchased with stillness, temporary HP at the price of all movement. Third, Ryu is reserved for the need that had no other answer, 10 CE, an hour of real dragon, gone until a long rest if destroyed. Mark the Domain: Auspicious Beasts’ Realm is not a fifth gift but all four worn at once, every beast constant, every cost waived, Kaichi and Ryu unable to miss. Never waste an answer on the wrong question.

Full progression

Feature map

1Kaichi:

The horn

The first answer is the simplest: a horn for the moments that require reaching. I have watched sorcerers with whole arsenals answer fewer questions than this one drill of yellow light. As an action for 1 CE, you summon Kaichi, a yellow drill horn. The horn lasts until dismissed or resummoned. Once on the summoning turn and once on each of your turns thereafter, when you take the Attack action you may make one melee CE attack with it (10-foot reach) as part of that action. Kaichi is a manifestation, not a separate summoned creature.

Damage

1d6 at level 1, 1d8 at 3rd, 1d10 at 6th, 1d12 at 11th, and 3d6 at 18th.

3Reiki:

Reiki

The second answer is refusal in two forms, flee faster, or be struck not at all. Dark water, the registry calls it; I call it the only defense that never needs to explain itself. As a bonus action for 1 CE, double your walking speed until the end of your next turn. Alternatively, as a reaction for 2 CE, add +5 to your AC. The reaction's +5 applies only against the triggering attack, after seeing its roll but before knowing the result.

6Kirin:

Kirin

The third answer is the honest trade: endurance purchased with stillness. The kirin asks the sorcerer to stand take it, and then makes taking it survivable. As a bonus action, spend 1 to PB CE. Gain 5 temporary HP per CE spent; your speed becomes 0 until the start of your next turn. Existing exhaustion levels remain but impose no effects while any of these temporary HP remain.

11Ryu:

Summon

The fourth answer is the one kept sheathed: a dragon, summoned for 10 CE, real enough to die, and dead until a long rest. Consider what that price teaches about when to spend it. As an action for 10 CE, summon Ryu in an open space within 30 feet. It remains for 1 hour, until dismissed, or until reduced to 0 HP.

Profile

Large dragon shikigami; AC 13 + PB; HP 10 × character level; speed 30 feet, fly 60 feet. It uses your CE attack modifier and CE save DC. It has no independent CE, Multiattack, Domain Expansion, or Maximum Technique.

Command

Ryu acts immediately after you. Command its action with your bonus action; otherwise it Dodges.

Bite

Melee CE attack, reach 10 feet; on a hit, 2d10 + CE modifier piercing damage.

Tempest Breath (recharge 5-6)

30-foot cone; Dexterity save; 5d8 lightning damage on failure, half on success.

If reduced to 0 HP, Ryu vanishes and cannot be summoned again until you complete a long rest.

Pinnacle expression

Maximum, Domain, or equivalent.

Domain Expansion, Auspicious Beasts’ Realm:

The barrier opens onto a menagerie that predates architecture, a temple courtyard where the stone lanterns breathe and the clouds have learned to walk on four legs. Auspicious beasts drift between pillars of carved jade, their scales catching light that has no source, and the air tastes of incense and wet earth after rain. It is less a battlefield than a royal procession that has decided, politely, to include you.

Mark what the Domain reveals: not a fifth gift, but all four worn at once, every beast constant, every cost waived, Kaichi and Ryu unable to miss. Heian philosophy in one room: the Domain is a law, not an event. 18: full-turn action, 20 CE; 60-foot-radius enclosed Domain centered on you; 1 minute; Burnout 10 rounds. Heian philosophy: the Domain is a law, not an event, there is no initiative-20 pulse. While it stands, all of your Auspicious Beasts remain constantly active with none of their usual side effects or costs, and attacks made by Kaichi and Ryu cannot miss (damage still rolls normally). A creature protected by an anti-domain defense may make a Dexterity saving throw vs your CE save DC when a beast attacks it (invented); on a success that attack rolls normally instead of hitting automatically.

Innate Technique Feats

Build-defining options.

These are optional character-building feats. You must meet each listed prerequisite before selecting it.

Prerequisite: Kaichi

Double Up

The horn, doubled, two manifestations, two prices. Ambition, posted twice. One Kaichi activation may create two drill-horn manifestations; pay and track each horn separately. Both persist under the normal Kaichi duration. Horns are not summoned creatures, do not require a summon command, and do not consume creature or manifestation capacity. When you take the Attack action, resolve each available horn attack as part of that action; this never grants a second action.

Prerequisite: Reiki

Zero Friction

Reiki’s speed and Kaichi’s reach were always one answer divided in two. This feat reunites them: strike mid-sprint, and carry the momentum through the horn. While Reiki's movement increase is active, you can resolve your Kaichi horn attack at any point during your movement instead of only as part of the Attack action. If you move at least 20 feet in a straight line immediately before the attack, a hit also shoves the target 10 feet away from you, carrying your momentum through the horn.

Prerequisite: Kirin and CE score 20

Maximum: Kirin’s Flames

Kirin’s endurance, inverted: every melee hit taken returns fire threefold. The trade no longer asks the sorcerer to stand still, it dares the enemy to strike. While Kirin is active, each melee hit against you deals fire damage to its attacker equal to three times the HP you actually lost after reductions and temporary HP. This requires no reaction, cannot trigger from reflected damage, and cannot recursively trigger itself.

Rules integration

Edge cases and shared systems.

Rules priority: the reviewed rulings, explicit codex clarifications, and the level progression always control when original wording conflicts. Legacy Domain auto-hit language, action terminology, event timing, missing saves, and older scaling never override revised player rules.
Why Tier II: Ryu is a serious companion and the four modes cover many situations, but each mode has a readable job and the technique does not acquire permanent outside assets.
