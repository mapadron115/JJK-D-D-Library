Core law

What the technique actually does.

Character engine

Draw directional Wind Roads across the battlefield. Allies ride the flow, enemies pay extra movement against it, and intersections later become teleporting Crossroads.

Technique save DC: 8 + PB + chosen CE ability modifier. Technique attack: PB + chosen CE ability modifier.

Full progression

Level progression

1Plumed Road

Action, 1 CE: 30-ft ×5-ft Wind Road for 1 minute. Allies moving with it +10 speed; enemies against it pay double. Full traversal generates 1 Plume once/turn.

3Serpent of the Updraft

Bonus, 2 CE, 1 minute: 40-ft flight; end each turn within 10 ft of a Wind Road or descend safely 20 ft.

6Breath of Civilization

Action, 2 CE: along one Road, allies may reaction move 10 ft; hostiles Dex save or pushed 15 ft. Allies moved gain +PB to next ability check before end next turn.

11Morning-Star Crossing

Road intersections are Crossroads; once/turn willing creature entering one may teleport to another within 60 ft for 10 ft movement; cannot cross Domain barriers.

18Domain Expansion: City Beneath the Feathered Sky

Full-turn action, 20 CE, enclosed 90-foot radius centered on you, up to 1 minute, Technique Focus. Six free Wind Roads appear when the Domain opens and do not count against your normal cap. At initiative 20 you may rotate the direction of every Domain Road, then choose one disclosed law until the next event. Ascent: a willing creature that moves at least 10 feet with a Road gains a 20-foot fly speed until the end of that movement. Descent: each chosen hostile that moved against a Road since the previous initiative 20 is knocked prone; a protected creature makes a Strength save to remain standing. Exchange: each willing creature currently at a Crossroad may move 15 feet without provoking. The Domain does not make your attacks automatic.

Innate Technique Feats

Innate Technique Feats

4 optional build choices

Precious Plumes

When you generate a Plume from a full traversal, you may instead immediately redraw one of your Wind Roads, keeping its length but choosing a new position and direction anchored within 30 feet of you.

Tailwind Archer

When you make a ranged attack along the direction of a Wind Road, you may have the attack emerge from a Crossroad within 60 feet of you instead, originating there and ignoring cover between you and the target.

Cloud Serpent

While flying with Serpent of the Updraft, you may use a bonus action to trace a Wind Road up to 30 feet long along your flight path. It counts against your normal maximum.

Four Directions (11+)

When you or a willing ally teleports between Crossroads, you may instead swap the positions of two willing creatures standing at two different Crossroads within range.
