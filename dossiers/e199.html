Feathered Serpent inspiration. Draw directional Wind Roads across the battlefield. Allies ride the flow, enemies pay extra movement against it, and intersections later become teleporting Crossroads.

Milestone featurePlayer-facing rules
Plumed RoadAction, 1 CE: 30-ft ×5-ft Wind Road for 1 minute. Allies moving with it +10 speed; enemies against it pay double. Full traversal generates 1 Plume once/turn.
Serpent of the UpdraftBonus, 2 CE, 1 minute: 40-ft flight; end each turn within 10 ft of a Wind Road or descend safely 20 ft.
Breath of CivilizationAction, 2 CE: along one Road, allies may reaction move 10 ft; hostiles Dex save or pushed 15 ft. Allies moved gain +PB to next ability check before end next turn.
Morning-Star CrossingRoad intersections are Crossroads; once/turn willing creature entering one may teleport to another within 60 ft for 10 ft movement; cannot cross Domain barriers.
Domain Expansion: City Beneath the Feathered SkyFull-turn action, 20 CE, enclosed 90-foot radius centered on you, up to 1 minute, Technique Focus. Six free Wind Roads appear when the Domain opens and do not count against your normal cap. At initiative 20 you may rotate the direction of every Domain Road, then choose one disclosed law until the next event. Ascent: a willing creature that moves at least 10 feet with a Road gains a 20-foot fly speed until the end of that movement. Descent: each chosen hostile that moved against a Road since the previous initiative 20 is knocked prone; a protected creature makes a Strength save to remain standing. Exchange: each willing creature currently at a Crossroad may move 15 feet without provoking. The Domain does not make your attacks automatic.

Domain procedure

This entry uses the repaired campaign Domain rules. The level-18 feature above is complete: its printed action, CE cost, radius, duration, initiative-20 law, fallback protection, and counterplay replace any legacy blanket auto-hit wording.

Character engine

Draw directional Wind Roads across the battlefield. Allies ride the flow, enemies pay extra movement against it, and intersections later become teleporting Crossroads.

Technique save DC: 8 + PB + chosen CE ability modifier. Technique attack: PB + chosen CE ability modifier.

Counterplay

Break Focus, deny intersections, and fight where Wind Roads cannot be conveniently aligned.
