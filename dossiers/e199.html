Core law

What the technique actually does.

Character engine

Draw directional Wind Roads across the battlefield. Allies ride the flow, enemies pay extra movement against it, and intersections later become teleporting Crossroads.

Technique save DC: 8 + PB + chosen CE ability modifier. Technique attack: PB + chosen CE ability modifier.

Full progression

Level progression

1Plumed Road

As an action for 1 CE, you draw a 30-foot by 5-foot Wind Road (both endpoints within 60 feet of you) that lasts 1 minute. Maintain up to 3 roads. Allies moving with the Road's direction gain +10 speed; enemies moving against it pay double movement. Fully traversing a Road generates 1 Plume, once per turn; hold up to PB Plumes; unspent Plumes fade after 1 minute.

Ride the Wind

Bonus action, spend 1 Plume: one willing creature within 60 feet gains +20 feet speed until the end of its turn, usable only for movement along a Wind Road's direction.

3Serpent of the Updraft

As a bonus action for 2 CE, you gain a 40-foot fly speed for 1 minute; end each turn within 10 feet of a Wind Road or descend safely 20 feet.

6Breath of Civilization

As an action for 2 CE, along one Wind Road, each ally may use its reaction to move 10 feet, and each hostile makes a Dexterity save vs your Technique DC or is pushed a fixed 15 feet along the Road's direction. Allies moved this way gain +PB to their next ability check before the end of your next turn.

11Morning-Star Crossing

Wind Road intersections become Crossroads. Once per turn, a willing creature entering a Crossroad may teleport to another Crossroad within a fixed 60 feet, spending 10 feet of movement. Teleports cannot cross Domain barriers.

18Domain Expansion: City Beneath the Feathered Sky

Full-turn action, 20 CE, enclosed 90-foot radius centered on you, up to 1 minute, Technique Focus. When the Domain opens, six Wind Roads appear free of charge — these Domain Roads do not count against your normal maximum of 3. At initiative 20 you may rotate the direction of every Domain Road, then choose one disclosed law until the next event. Ascent: a willing creature that moves at least 10 feet with a Road gains a 20-foot fly speed until the end of that movement. Descent: each chosen hostile that moved against a Road since the previous initiative 20 is knocked prone; a protected creature makes a Strength save to remain standing. Exchange: each willing creature currently at a Crossroad may move 15 feet without provoking. The Domain does not make your attacks automatic.

Innate Technique Feats

Innate Technique Feats

4 optional build choices

Precious Plumes

When you generate a Plume from a full traversal, you may instead immediately redraw one of your Wind Roads, keeping its length but choosing a new position and direction anchored within 30 feet of you.

Tailwind Archer

When you make a ranged attack along the direction of a Wind Road, you may have the attack emerge from a Crossroad within 60 feet of you instead, originating there and ignoring cover between you and the target.

Cloud Serpent

While flying with Serpent of the Updraft, you may use a bonus action to trace a Wind Road up to 30 feet long along your flight path. It counts against your normal maximum.

Four Directions (11+)

When you or a willing ally teleports between Crossroads, you may instead swap the positions of two willing creatures standing at two different Crossroads within range.
