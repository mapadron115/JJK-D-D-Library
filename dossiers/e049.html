FLAME OF FRENZY
Core law

What the technique actually does.

Rules are written to plug into the finalized campaign glossary: normal actions stay normal, direct attacks roll, and only named Domain events bypass the initial roll.

Self-risk meter

Madness

You begin at 0 Madness. Frenzy features add the listed Madness after resolving. At the end of a short rest reduce Madness by 2; long rest resets to 0 unless a story effect says otherwise.

Escalation

Madness Thresholds

At 3+, add +1 Frenzy Die to damaging Frenzy features. At 5+, speed +10 and advantage vs frightened/charmed. At 7+, add +2 dice instead and you have disadvantage on checks to distinguish friend from foe under stress. At 10, Lord-state rules trigger.

Action · 1 CE · +1 Madness

Frenzied Burst

Ranged CE attack. Hit deals 2 Frenzy Dice fire/psychic, split as you choose. A creature hit twice by Frenzy before end of your next turn makes a Wisdom save or becomes frightened until end of its next turn.

Damage identity

Yellow Flame

Frenzy fire is cursed fire with a psychic component. Immunity to ordinary fire does not prevent the psychic half; mindless creatures ignore fear and identity riders but still take fire damage.

When you grapple a creature, spend 3 CE and +1 Madness to force a Wisdom save. Failure takes 5 Frenzy Dice psychic/fire and is stunned until end of your current turn; once per target per round.

Action · 6 CE · +2 Madness · Focus: for up to 1 minute, a flaming void-head hovers over you. At start of each of your turns it fires PB bolts; distribute them among targets within 90 feet. Each bolt is a ranged CE attack for 2 Frenzy Dice.

18The One GreatDomain Expansion and 2 more features

Domain Expansion

At Madness 6+, unlock One Great — No More Distinction.

Lord’s Emergence

If you would die at Madness 8+, you may immediately set Madness to 10 and enter Lord state instead, returning to 1 HP. Once per long rest.

Chaos Crown

At Madness 10, your head becomes a black-yellow flame for up to 1 minute. You are immune to frightened/charmed, Frenzy dice become d12, and you gain 30-foot flight; at end of each turn make Wisdom save DC 10 + rounds elapsed or the GM chooses your movement and one non-suicidal hostile action next turn.

May Chaos Take the World

You may voluntarily enter Lord state as a bonus action for 5 CE and + enough Madness to reach 10. The control save begins at DC 10.

While Lord state is active, your Frenzy damage ignores resistance and treats immunity as resistance.

Last Ember of Self

Once per Lord state, after failing the control save, choose to succeed instead and take 4d12 psychic damage that ignores reduction.

Pinnacle expression

Domain, Maximum, or equivalent.

Not every technique needs a Domain. When one exists, its sure-hit is a unique autonomous initiative-count-20 event unless the Domain is explicitly rule-based.

One Great — No More Distinction

Level 18 · Madness 6+ · Full-turn action · 20 CE · 90-foot radius · 1 minute. The barrier is a horizonless field where silhouettes melt into the same yellow flame.

Initiative 20 — Melt the Boundary: every chosen hostile automatically takes 4d12 fire + 4d12 psychic. Until the next initiative 20 it cannot receive Help, flanking, borrowed reactions, or buffs originating from another creature. A protected creature makes a Wisdom save; success halves damage and prevents the isolation rider.

Ordinary attacks still roll. The Domain does not erase identities, souls, or techniques outright.

Rules integration

Edge cases and shared systems.

These rulings are designed around the revised Player Guide rather than the raw PDF where the two differ.

Madness is not a condition; immunity to frightened does not prevent gaining it.
Mindless creatures take damage but ignore fear, identity, and social isolation riders.
Lord-state forced action can never compel deliberate suicide or self-mutilation.
Frenzy patches and Domain pulses do not Black Flash.
Technique DC: 8 + proficiency bonus + your chosen Cursed Energy ability modifier (Wisdom or Charisma unless your class says otherwise).
Direct criticals: rolled weapon, unarmed, and CE attacks can critical normally. After a qualifying critical, roll the campaign's unmodified Black Flash confirmation d20; a natural 20 triples the completed critical damage. Autonomous Domain events, damage-over-time, hazards, and unrolled pulses cannot Black Flash.
Maximum Output: unless a feature says otherwise, double that feature's base CE cost and enhance one supported axis—damage, range, duration, or accuracy/DC—using the campaign's Maximum Output rules. Named Maximum Techniques and Domains do not receive generic Maximum Output.
Technique Focus: effects marked Focus occupy the shared Technique Focus slot. Opening a Domain ends other Focus effects unless the Domain expressly preserves them.
Two-technique characters: these guides assume each technique progresses from character/class level as agreed by the campaign. Multiclassing itself does not grant another Innate Technique.
