Scarlet Hex

Probability / chaos magic / hexes / reality alteration

Power tierTier X · Reality Hazard
Core law

What the technique actually does.

Core Engine, Chaos Dice. The ledger of her habit. PB dice per long rest, each spent after a visible roll, and each spend adds Instability: at five, every spend surges; at ten, the event writes itself and the count resets. You have PB Chaos Dice, refreshed long rest. Spend after a visible roll to add/subtract one die. Each die spent adds 1 Instability. At 5 Instability, every further spend triggers a Chaos Surge; at 10, involuntary reality event and Instability resets.

Chaos Surge table (d8). The price of the habit, itemized: roll d8 when a Surge triggers. Some entries are mercy, an extra bonus action, attacks at disadvantage, and some are the bill arriving. Memorize it. When you trigger a Chaos Surge, roll d8: 1: Gravity hiccup: each creature within 20 ft of you makes a Dexterity save or falls prone. 2: Chromatic backlash: you take 2d8 force damage as probability snaps back. 3: Stolen seconds: you gain one additional bonus action this turn. 4: Probability fog: attacks against you have disadvantage until the start of your next turn. 5: Echo cast: one random creature within 60 ft must repeat its last d20 result on its next d20 roll. 6: Matter itch: one unattended object within 30 ft animates harmlessly for 1 minute. 7: Surge dividend: regain one spent Chaos Die. 8: Wild doubling: the next Chaos Die you spend counts as two dice for the add/subtract, adding 2 Instability. 10 Instability: an involuntary reality event, the GM warps one scene element (never instant death or permanent PC agency loss without consent); Instability then resets to 0.

Field notes. Subject: the editor with the red pen. There are techniques I catalogue, techniques I respect, and techniques I read the way one reads a weather report before a sea voyage, carefully, and with a hand on the rail. The Hex is the third kind. Two truths govern it. First: the dice are load-bearing, Chaos Dice, d8s to d12s, are not a resource but a weather system; the Surges are not accidents but climate. Second: the revision is real, she does not break rules but rewrites the scene in which rules apply, and the one law she cannot touch is the player's consent. I have planned against many things. I am still planning against this one.

Full progression

Level 1 through 20.

1Hex

Reaction · 1 CE · When a creature within 60 ft makes an attack roll, ability check, or saving throw: subtract 1d6 from the roll after it is made; once per round.

The first theft: one CE, one reaction, one die subtracted from someone else's certainty, after the roll, once per round. The world rolls; she edits.

Focus. Wherever this dossier says Focus, it means you are concentrating (the concentration condition).

3Telekinesis

Action · 2 CE · Move a Large-or-smaller object or creature 30 ft (unwilling creature makes a Strength save); you may hurl the target for 3d8 force damage.

The unthreading turns physical: two CE to move thirty feet of Large-or-smaller matter, or hurl it for three dice. Hands made of revoked physics. I have watched matter moved this way and found myself checking that the floor was still the floor.

6Probability Collapse

Free (after a visible roll) · Spend 1 Chaos Die · Force one attack, save, or check within 60 ft to be rerolled; the new result stands; once per round.

The editor's veto: spend a Chaos Die to force any roll within sixty feet to be taken again, the new result stands, whatever it is. Any roll. Including, I note with professional discomfort, mine. Once per round.

9Chaos Construct

Action · 3 CE · Create one: barrier (40 Integrity, AC 12 + PB), binding bands (restrained; escape vs your CE save DC), flight (40 ft), or a melee CE weapon (4d8 force); lasts 1 minute (requires Focus). Chaos Dice become d8s (d10s at L17).

She weaves the unthreaded into things: a barrier, bands, flight, or a four-dice weapon, held for a minute on Focus. The dice grow with her, d8s now, d10s at seventeen. I advise treating every object she has touched as a loaded argument.

11Mental Hex

Action · 4 CE · Target makes a Wisdom save; on a failure it is Charmed, Frightened, or trapped in a sensory illusion (your choice) until the end of its next turn; it repeats the save if damaged.

The weave turns inward: four CE and a failed Wisdom save, and the target is Charmed, Frightened, or lost in illusion until its next turn ends. Damage buys it another save. The mind, unthreaded, is as editable as the matter. I find this the most alarming sentence I have written this month.

13Rewrite Wound

Reaction · 5 CE + 1 Instability · When a creature within 30 ft takes damage: change the damage to half.

The kindest theft: five CE and one Instability, spent as a reaction, and someone else's wound becomes half itself. Probability selects the merciful outcome.

15Localized Reality

Action · 6 CE + 2 Instability · Alter one 20-ft cube for 1 minute: gravity, terrain state, weather, harmless appearance, or one resistance/vulnerability relation (GM-approved).

A twenty-foot cube of her own law for a minute, gravity, terrain, weather, or a resistance rewritten. The loom weaves a room. I have fought in rooms. I have never before fought in someone else's draft of the room.

18Domain, House of M

Domain · At initiative 20 declare one Rule (e.g. “flight fails”); affected creatures make a Charisma save or obey it until the next initiative 20. Rules cannot kill, erase techniques, or leave the Domain. Clash 1,000; burnout 3 rounds.

The Domain: at initiative twenty she declares a Rule, flight fails, fire cannot burn, and failures on a Charisma save obey it until the next count. Tier X, a thousand clash points; the Rules may not kill, erase, or leave the Domain. A thousand points. I have clashed Domains before. I do not propose to clash this one.

Clash bookkeeping: 1,000 clash points (Tier X). A 1,000+ point advantage collapses the weaker Domain; collapse deals no damage. Barrier AC 12 + PB; barrier HP = 10 × max(1, your CE modifier) + 2,000. Technique Burnout: 3 rounds.

20Scarlet Witch

Special · Once per long rest: 10 CE + 5 Instability for one GM-adjudicated, scene-level reality revision (never a PC's identity or agency without that player's consent). Chaos Dice become d12s; pool grows by 2.

The dice are d12s and two more join the pool; and once per long rest, ten CE and five Instability buy one scene-level revision, with the player's consent the only law she cannot rewrite. There is a line she will not cross. I have marked it on every map I own, and I stay behind it.

Pinnacle expression

Maximum, Domain, or equivalent.

Maximum, No More (12 CE + all Chaos Dice; action): choose one supernatural category currently manifested in a 60-ft radius—summons, barriers, flames, weapons created by techniques, clones, etc. Each source contests your CE check (see Rules, Shared Resolutions); on loss its manifestation ends. This does not permanently erase the underlying concept.

Feats

Build-defining options.

Lucky Hex

Bad luck is fungible. I have begun to suspect that luck, near her, is not random but merely unassigned. Once per short rest, when a hostile within 60 ft rolls a natural 1, you may use your reaction to spend one Chaos Die and redirect the failed roll onto a different target of your choice within range.

Chaos Vision

The past, near her, leaves fingerprints. I have stopped assuming my own past is clean. The weaver inspects the weave. As an action, you can read probability residue: learn what reality-altering or illusion effects have occurred within 60 ft in the last hour.

Red Thread

Cover is a rumor she declines to hear. I have started standing very still and thinking very precisely about where I am. When you use Hex, you may spend 1 additional CE to thread it through probability itself, targeting a creature you cannot see as long as you know its exact location within 60 ft.

What Hex?

The lightning rod is a vase. She can decline even her own chaos. I cannot decide whether that reassures me or terrifies me, so I have decided to be both. Once per long rest, when a Chaos Surge would trigger, you may use your reaction to ground it in an unattended object within 30 ft instead: the object warps harmlessly (changes color, floats briefly) and the Surge is spent.

Nexus Being

She pins the unpinned: a reaction and a Chaos Die to hold a creature's probability in place against teleport or displacement, contested if opposed. as a reaction when a creature within 30 ft would be teleported or forcibly displaced by a supernatural effect, you may spend 1 Chaos Die to pin its probability in place (contested against the effect's DC if opposed).

Witch's Grief

Grief, weaponized at range. Once per short rest, when you drop below 25% of your maximum HP, reality shudders: each hostile within 30 ft must succeed on a Wisdom save or roll its next attack, check, or save twice and take the worse.

Rules integration

Edge cases and shared systems.

Codex rulings: Direct rolled technique/weapon attacks can use the campaign's Black Flash procedure when otherwise eligible. Passive damage, hazards, summons acting independently, and initiative-20 Domain events cannot. Generic Maximum Output applies only to ordinary features, never named Maxima or Domains.
