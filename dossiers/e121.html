Scarlet Hex

Probability / chaos magic / hexes / reality alteration

Power tierTier X · Reality Hazard
Core law

What the technique actually does.

Core Engine — Chaos Dice. The ledger of her habit. PB dice per long rest, each spent after a visible roll — and each spend adds Instability: at five, every spend surges; at ten, the event writes itself and the count resets. You have PB Chaos Dice, refreshed long rest. Spend after a visible roll to add/subtract one die. Each die spent adds 1 Instability. At 5 Instability, every further spend triggers a Chaos Surge; at 10, involuntary reality event and Instability resets.

Chaos Surge table (d8). The price of the habit, itemized: roll d8 when a Surge triggers. Some entries are mercy — an extra bonus action, attacks at disadvantage — and some are the bill arriving. Memorize it. When you trigger a Chaos Surge, roll d8: 1 — Gravity hiccup: each creature within 20 ft of you makes a Dexterity save or falls prone. 2 — Chromatic backlash: you take 2d8 force damage as probability snaps back. 3 — Stolen seconds: you gain one additional bonus action this turn. 4 — Probability fog: attacks against you have disadvantage until the start of your next turn. 5 — Echo cast: one random creature within 60 ft must repeat its last d20 result on its next d20 roll. 6 — Matter itch: one unattended object within 30 ft animates harmlessly for 1 minute. 7 — Surge dividend: regain one spent Chaos Die. 8 — Wild doubling: the next Chaos Die you spend counts as two dice for the add/subtract, adding 2 Instability. 10 Instability: an involuntary reality event — the GM warps one scene element (never instant death or permanent PC agency loss without consent); Instability then resets to 0.

Field notes. Subject: the editor with the red pen. There are techniques I catalogue, techniques I respect, and techniques I read the way one reads a weather report before a sea voyage — carefully, and with a hand on the rail. The Hex is the third kind. Two truths govern it. First: the dice are load-bearing — Chaos Dice, d8s to d12s, are not a resource but a weather system; the Surges are not accidents but climate. Second: the revision is real — she does not break rules but rewrites the scene in which rules apply, and the one law she cannot touch is the player's consent. I have planned against many things. I am still planning against this one.

Full progression

Level 1 through 20.

1Hex

The first theft: one CE, one reaction, one die subtracted from someone else's certainty — after the roll, once per round. The world rolls; she edits. For 1 CE, use your reaction when a creature within 60 ft makes an attack roll, ability check, or saving throw: subtract 1d6 from the roll after it is made. You can do this once per round.

3Telekinesis

The unthreading turns physical: two CE to move thirty feet of Large-or-smaller matter, or hurl it for three dice. Hands made of revoked physics. I have watched matter moved this way and found myself checking that the floor was still the floor. For 2 CE, move a Large-or-smaller object or creature 30 ft; an unwilling creature makes a Strength save. You may hurl the target, dealing 3d8 force damage.

6Probability Collapse

The editor's veto: spend a Chaos Die to force any roll within sixty feet to be taken again — the new result stands, whatever it is. Any roll. Including, I note with professional discomfort, mine. Once per round. You may spend a Chaos Die to force a reroll of one attack, save, or check within 60 ft; the new result stands. Once per round.

9Chaos Construct

She weaves the unthreaded into things: a barrier, bands, flight, or a four-dice weapon, held for a minute on Focus. The dice grow with her — d8s now, d10s at seventeen. I advise treating every object she has touched as a loaded argument. For 3 CE, create a barrier (40 Integrity, AC 12 + PB), binding bands (restrained; escape vs your CE save DC), flight (40 ft), or a weapon (melee CE attack, 4d8 force) — lasting 1 minute (requires Focus). Your Chaos Dice become d8s at this level, and grow to d10s at level 17.

11Mental Hex

The weave turns inward: four CE and a failed Wisdom save, and the target is Charmed, Frightened, or lost in illusion until its next turn ends. Damaging it breaks the spell — literally. The mind, unthreaded, is as editable as the matter. I find this the most alarming sentence I have written this month. For 4 CE, target a creature; it makes a Wisdom save. On a failure it is Charmed, Frightened, or trapped in a sensory illusion (your choice) until the end of its next turn; it repeats the save if damaged.

13Rewrite Wound

The kindest theft: five CE and one Instability, spent as a reaction, and someone else's wound becomes half itself. Probability selects the merciful outcome. For 5 CE + 1 Instability, use your reaction when a creature within 30 ft takes damage: change the damage to half, as probability selects a less-lethal outcome.

15Localized Reality

A twenty-foot cube of her own law for a minute — gravity, terrain, weather, or a resistance rewritten. The loom weaves a room. I have fought in rooms. I have never before fought in someone else's draft of the room. For 6 CE + 2 Instability, alter one 20-ft cube for 1 minute — gravity, terrain state, weather, harmless appearance, or one resistance/vulnerability relation approved by your GM.

18Domain — House of M

The Domain: at initiative twenty she declares a Rule — flight fails, fire cannot burn — and failures on a Charisma save obey it until the next count. Tier X, a thousand clash points; the Rules may not kill, erase, or leave the Domain. A thousand points. I have clashed Domains before. I do not propose to clash this one. At initiative 20 choose one declared Rule such as “flight fails,” “fire cannot burn,” or “weapons are heavy.” Affected creatures make a Charisma save; on a failure they obey the Rule until the next initiative 20. Rules cannot say “you die,” erase cursed techniques permanently, or alter rules outside the Domain. Clash bookkeeping: 1,000 clash points (Tier X). A 100+ point advantage collapses the weaker Domain; collapse deals no damage. Barrier AC 12 + PB; barrier HP = 10 × max(1, your CE modifier) + 2,000. Technique Burnout: 3 rounds.

20Scarlet Witch

The dice are d12s and two more join the pool; and once per long rest, ten CE and five Instability buy one scene-level revision — with the player's consent the only law she cannot rewrite. There is a line she will not cross. I have marked it on every map I own, and I stay behind it. Your Chaos Dice become d12s and your pool grows by 2. Once per long rest, you may declare one major, scene-level reality revision with GM adjudication; it costs 10 CE and 5 Instability, and it cannot overwrite another PC’s identity or agency without that player’s consent.

Pinnacle expression

Maximum, Domain, or equivalent.

Maximum — No More (12 CE + all Chaos Dice; action): choose one supernatural category currently manifested in a 60-ft radius—summons, barriers, flames, weapons created by techniques, clones, etc. Each source contests your CE check (see Rules — Shared Resolutions); on loss its manifestation ends. This does not permanently erase the underlying concept.

Feats

Build-defining options.

Lucky Hex

She spends another's misfortune: when a hostile rolls a natural one within sixty feet, a reaction and a Chaos Die move the failure to a target of her choosing. Bad luck is fungible. I have begun to suspect that luck, near her, is not random but merely unassigned. once per short rest, when a hostile within 60 ft rolls a natural 1, you may use your reaction to spend one Chaos Die and redirect the failed roll onto a different target of your choice within range.

Chaos Vision

She reads the residue: an action to learn what reality-altering or illusion effects the last hour has left within sixty feet. The past, near her, leaves fingerprints. I have stopped assuming my own past is clean. The weaver inspects the weave. as an action, you can read probability residue: learn what reality-altering or illusion effects have occurred within 60 ft in the last hour.

Red Thread

For one more CE, the Hex threads through probability itself — striking a target she cannot see, so long as she knows its exact place within sixty feet. Cover is a rumor she declines to hear. I have started standing very still and thinking very precisely about where I am. when you use Hex, you may spend 1 additional CE to thread it through probability itself, targeting a creature you cannot see as long as you know its exact location within 60 ft.

What Hex?

Once per long rest, a Surge that would have happened does not: a reaction grounds it in an unattended object, which warps harmlessly and spends the Surge. The lightning rod is a vase. She can decline even her own chaos. I cannot decide whether that reassures me or terrifies me, so I have decided to be both. once per long rest, when a Chaos Surge would trigger, you may use your reaction to ground it in an unattended object within 30 ft instead: the object warps harmlessly (changes color, floats briefly) and the Surge is spent.

Nexus Being

She pins the unpinned: a reaction and a Chaos Die to hold a creature's probability in place against teleport or displacement, contested if opposed. as a reaction when a creature within 30 ft would be teleported or forcibly displaced by a supernatural effect, you may spend 1 Chaos Die to pin its probability in place (contested against the effect's DC if opposed).

Witch's Grief

Below a quarter of her health, reality flinches with her: hostiles within thirty feet save or roll their next d20 twice and take the worse. Grief, weaponized at range. once per short rest, when you drop below 25% of your maximum HP, reality shudders: each hostile within 30 ft must succeed on a Wisdom save or roll its next attack, check, or save twice and take the worse.

Rules integration

Edge cases and shared systems.

Codex rulings: Direct rolled technique/weapon attacks can use the campaign's Black Flash procedure when otherwise eligible. Passive damage, hazards, summons acting independently, and initiative-20 Domain events cannot. Generic Maximum Output applies only to ordinary features, never named Maxima or Domains.
