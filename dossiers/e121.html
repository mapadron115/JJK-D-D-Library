Scarlet Hex

Probability / chaos magic / hexes / reality alteration

Power tierTier X · Reality Hazard
Core law

What the technique actually does.

Core Engine — Chaos Dice. You have PB Chaos Dice (d6→d8 at 9→d10 at 17), refreshed long rest. Spend after a visible roll to add/subtract one die. Each die spent adds 1 Instability. At 5 Instability, every further spend triggers a Chaos Surge; at 10, involuntary reality event and Instability resets.

Full progression

Level 1 through 20.

1Hex

1 CE, 60 ft: reaction subtract 1d6 from attack/check/save after roll. Once/round.

3Telekinesis

2 CE: move Large-or-smaller object/creature 30 ft; unwilling Str save. Hurl for 3d8 force.

6Probability Collapse

Spend Chaos Die to force reroll of one attack/save/check within 60 ft; new result stands. Once/round.

9Chaos Construct

3 CE: barrier, binding bands, flight, or weapon; 4d8-equivalent effect, 1 minute Focus.

11Mental Hex

4 CE, Wis save: Charmed, Frightened, or trapped in sensory illusion until end next turn; repeat save if damaged.

13Rewrite Wound

5 CE + 1 Instability: reaction change damage just taken by creature within 30 ft to half, as probability selects a less-lethal outcome.

15Localized Reality

6 CE + 2 Instability: alter one 20-ft cube for 1 minute—gravity, terrain state, weather, harmless appearance, or one resistance/vulnerability relation approved by GM.

18Domain — House of M

At initiative 20 choose one declared Rule such as “flight fails,” “fire cannot burn,” or “weapons are heavy.” Affected creatures make Cha save; failure obeys Rule until next initiative 20. Rules cannot say “you die,” erase CTs permanently, or alter rules outside Domain. Clash bookkeeping: 1,000 clash points (Tier X). A 100+ point advantage collapses the weaker Domain; collapse deals no damage. Barrier AC 12 + PB; barrier HP = 10 × max(1, your CE modifier) + 2,000. Technique Burnout: 3 rounds.

20Scarlet Witch

Chaos Dice become d12, pool +2. Once/long rest declare one major scene-level reality revision with GM adjudication; costs 10 CE, 5 Instability, and cannot overwrite another PC's identity/agency without consent.

Pinnacle expression

Maximum, Domain, or equivalent.

Maximum — No More (12 CE + all Chaos Dice): choose one supernatural category currently manifested in a 60-ft radius—summons, barriers, flames, weapons created by techniques, clones, etc. Each source contests your CE check; on loss its manifestation ends. This does not permanently erase the underlying concept.

Feats

Build-defining options.

Lucky Hex

once per short rest, when a hostile within 60 ft rolls a natural 1, you may use your reaction to spend one Chaos Die and redirect the failed roll onto a different target of your choice within range.

Chaos Vision

as an action, you can read probability residue: learn what reality-altering or illusion effects have occurred within 60 ft in the last hour.

Red Thread

when you use Hex, you may spend 1 additional CE to thread it through probability itself, targeting a creature you cannot see as long as you know its exact location within 60 ft.

What Hex?

once per long rest, when a Chaos Surge would trigger, you may use your reaction to ground it in an unattended object within 30 ft instead: the object warps harmlessly (changes color, floats briefly) and the Surge is spent.

Nexus Being

as a reaction when a creature within 30 ft would be teleported or forcibly displaced by a supernatural effect, you may spend 1 Chaos Die to pin its probability in place (contested against the effect's DC if opposed).

Witch's Grief

once per short rest, when you drop below 25% of your maximum HP, reality shudders: each hostile within 30 ft must succeed on a Wisdom save or roll its next attack, check, or save twice and take the worse.

Rules integration

Edge cases and shared systems.

Codex rulings: Direct rolled technique/weapon attacks can use the campaign's Black Flash procedure when otherwise eligible. Passive damage, hazards, summons acting independently, and initiative-20 Domain events cannot. Generic Maximum Output applies only to ordinary features, never named Maxima or Domains.
