Scarlet Hex

Tier X · Reality HazardProbability / chaos magic / hexes / reality alteration

Core Engine — Chaos Dice. You have PB Chaos Dice (d6→d8 at 9→d10 at 17), refreshed long rest. Spend after a visible roll to add/subtract one die. Each die spent adds 1 Instability. At 5 Instability, every further spend triggers a Chaos Surge; at 10, involuntary reality event and Instability resets.

Maximum — No More (12 CE + all Chaos Dice): choose one supernatural category currently manifested in a 60-ft radius—summons, barriers, flames, weapons created by techniques, clones, etc. Each source contests your CE check; on loss its manifestation ends. This does not permanently erase the underlying concept.
