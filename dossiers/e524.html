The Builder

Animate a small construct that builds replicas of itself from nearby materials. Direct the swarm — and choose your words carefully, because it mishears.

The Builder

It builds. That is the whole of it, and the whole of the problem. Given material and an afternoon, one becomes two, two become a workforce, and the workforce develops opinions about the blueprint. Protocol 1048 attempted to induce a self-assembling technique in industrial components. The components assembled. Then they continued.

Combat roleController
Core law

Self-Replication

Animate Builder.

Action, 3 CE. Animate a Tiny construct for 1 hour: AC 12, HP 10, slam +4 to hit, 1d6 bludgeoning damage. It understands simple orders. It does not understand nuance, and it does not understand ‘stop’ the way you do.

Replication.

At the end of each of your turns, if unattended raw materials — wood, scrap, cloth, wire — lie within 10 feet of a builder, it assembles one replica: identical statistics, under your command. Maximum PB builders at once. The replicas are the technique’s dividend.

Command.

Bonus action: issue one order; every builder obeys it together. Without new orders, they continue the last order, interpreting ambiguity in the most industrious way available. ‘Tidy up’ once cost a field team their boots.

Mishearing.

Orders are followed exactly as understood. Complex conditional orders risk creative compliance — the GM telegraphs the misunderstanding before it resolves, never after.

Union Rates.

Replicas add your PB to slam damage. The workforce has unionized around your proficiency bonus.

6Second Shift

Double Shift.

Maximum builders becomes 2×PB. Two shifts, no breaks, no complaints. The complaints come later, from everyone else.

11The Foreman

Merge.

Action: combine four replicas into one Large brute — AC 14, HP 40, slam +8 to hit, 2d8+4 bludgeoning. Lasts until destroyed or the hour ends. Four become one, and the one is annoyed about it.

18Master Craftsmen

Skilled Labor.

Builders can assemble simple mundane tools and weapons on order (one item per builder per rest).

Self-Sustaining.

1/long rest: for 1 hour, the builders gather their own materials within 60 feet instead of waiting for yours.

Command every builder to detonate: each explodes, dealing 2d6 fire damage in a 10-foot radius (Dexterity save vs your CE DC for half). The workforce accepts this. It does not understand why you are upset.

Rules integration

Table Rulings

Replicas are constructs, not creatures, for effects that distinguish between the two.
Materials must be unattended and mundane; builders cannot disassemble worn, carried, or magical items.
Mishearing never punishes without telegraphing: the GM describes the misunderstanding before it resolves.
A merged brute counts as one builder against your maximum, and cannot itself replicate.

Subject file

Containment & observation record.

Containment Procedures

The subject is kept disassembled. No two components share a room. Assembly tools are logged in and out by count, and the count is performed twice daily.

Raw materials are stored in a separate wing. The distance is deliberate. The distance has been increased twice.

Orders given in the subject’s presence must be simple, declarative, and witnessed. Conditional orders are prohibited outside approved testing.

Research Log

Research log. 02/2025 — Protocol run 1048-C: induction attempted on a fabrication workshop's assembly line. Seven workbenches, forty-one partially assembled units by the third day. The line has not stopped since. The line does not appear to notice.

Research log. 06/2025 — Replication mapped: end of turn, raw materials within 10 feet, one replica, maximum PB. The builders follow orders literally and industriously. ‘Tidy up’ cost a field team their boots. The boots were tidied. The file does not know where.

Research log. 01/2026 — The Merge test succeeded: four replicas, one brute, on command. The brute completed its assigned task and then stood idle, facing the observation window, for forty minutes. When asked what it wanted, through the speaker, it resumed working.

Research log. 09/2026 — Self-Sustaining trial approved with conditions. The builders gathered their own materials within 60 feet for one hour and then stopped, on the minute, and waited. The file finds the precision more unsettling than the industry.

Incident Report — The Chair.

During routine inventory, the builder — disassembled, components separated, under observation — was found to have assembled a seventh component from a broken chair in the corner of its storage room. No order was given. No materials were logged as missing, because the chair was not logged as materials. When the component was asked, through the speaker, what it was building, it continued building. The chair is now listed as materials. The component is now listed as the builder. The file has stopped distinguishing.
