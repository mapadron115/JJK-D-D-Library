Features & procedures

Properties & techniques

Exact player-facing procedures

Cursed edge

You have a +4 bonus to attack rolls and damage rolls made with this weapon.

Reaction

Heavenly reprisal

When you are hit by an attack, you can use your reaction to make a melee or thrown attack with this weapon at advantage. On a hit, the creature is knocked prone. If thrown, Hiten returns to your hand after the attack.

Rules integration

Edge cases and shared systems.

Campaign canon notes

Timing: the retaliation resolves after the triggering hit's damage is applied. The prone effect applies on a hit regardless of damage dealt. The thrown return is automatic and costs no action.
