Core law

What the technique actually does.

Core Engine, Fear Marks. Fear Marks are her specimen jars: a creature gains one when it fails a Wisdom save against her, three at most. She may harvest a marked creature's fear into a Nightmare Blueprint, a custom shikigami wearing the shape of what its victim dreads. The cabinet fills one terror at a time. A creature gains a Fear Mark when it fails a Wis save against you, max 3. You may harvest a marked creature's fear into a Nightmare Blueprint, creating custom shikigami flavored around what it fears.

Field notes. Subject: a naturalist of other people's fears. Three truths govern them. First, fear is the collection: a creature that fails a Wisdom save against her gains a Fear Mark, no more than three, and she may harvest a marked creature's terror into a Nightmare Blueprint, a custom shikigami flavored around what it fears. Second, the specimens must be fed: the nightmares have opinions about the menu, and the technique is not cruelty but appetite, each Blueprint is someone else's terror, catalogued and weaponized. Third, three marks complete the specimen: at three the fear's theme is recorded, the Blueprint is built at rest, and in her Unwritten Nightmare the hostile must choose, Reveal its fear to her, or Run and be marked for the attempt. I have watched collectors of less dangerous things with the same careful hands.

Full progression

Level 1 through 20.

1Nightmare Touch

The first pin through the specimen. One energy, sixty feet, a Wisdom save: two dice of psychic on failure, and one Fear Mark set. The collection begins with a touch. Action, 1 CE, 60 ft, Wis save: 2d8 psychic and 1 Fear Mark on failure.

3Dream Token

The live specimen. One Fear Mark spent on a creature within sixty feet raises a one-hit-point Nightmare until her next turn ends, acting on her turn without an action, quick, psychic, brief. Pinned, but still biting. Action, spend 1 Fear Mark from a creature within 60 ft to create a 1-HP Nightmare until the end of your next turn; it acts on your turn without requiring an action (speed 30 ft; melee CE attack for 2d8 psychic).

6Blueprint

The taxonomy. Once a creature bears three Marks she records its fear theme, and at rest she builds one Blueprint, Hunter, Swarm, Cage, Doppelganger, or Abyss, each with its own movement or control rider. Five drawers in the cabinet, each labeled in someone else's handwriting. After a creature reaches 3 Marks, record one fear theme. At rest build one Blueprint: Hunter, Swarm, Cage, Doppelganger, or Abyss, each with a distinct movement/control rider, Hunter: +20 ft speed and advantage on checks to pursue the marked creature; Swarm: becomes a swarm of fragments that can occupy other creatures’ spaces and ignores difficult terrain; Cage: as an action, restrain one creature within 30 ft (Strength save negates) for 1 minute; Doppelganger: takes the shape of a creature the victim trusts, and attacks against it have disadvantage until it attacks; Abyss: projects a 15-ft-radius zone of darkness that is difficult terrain, and creatures inside make a Wisdom save or are frightened while inside.

9Nightmare Shikigami

The exhibit, fully mounted. Four energy summons one Blueprint for a minute, armored, eight times her level in flesh, a three-dice psychic strike on her bonus-action command, one at a time. The specimen, at working size. 4 CE: summon one Blueprint for 1 minute (AC 15, HP 8×level; its attacks use your CE attack bonus for 3d8 psychic); commanding it is a bonus action; one active.

11Exile Thought

The preserving jar. Three energy: a failed Intelligence save phases one reaction, summon, or Focus effect into nightmare until her next turn ends, ongoing durations still ticking. She does not destroy her enemies' tools. She shelves them. 3 CE, Int save: one reaction, summon, or Focus effect is phased into nightmare until end next turn; ongoing durations continue.

13Fear Made Flesh

The adaptation. Her Nightmare gains one trait drawn from its source fear, flight, burrowing, ten feet of reach, one resistance, or an added attack die, the game master approving the thematic fit. The specimen grows truer to its subject. Nightmare gains one trait from source fear: fly, burrow, reach +10, resistance one type, or +1 attack die. GM approves thematic fit.

15Nightmare Harvest

The collection made permanent. When a marked creature drops to zero, she preserves all its Marks as permanent Dream tokens, her proficiency's worth stored. Some specimens are too fine to release. When marked creature drops to 0, preserve all its Marks as permanent Dream tokens, max PB stored.

18Domain, The Unwritten Nightmare

At the twentieth count the gallery opens, and each hostile chooses: Reveal, a Wisdom save, six dice of psychic on failure, and she learns its fear theme for the round; or Run, the Domain moves it thirty feet to her chosen space and marks it. The exhibition is interactive. Initiative 20 each hostile chooses REVEAL or RUN. Reveal: Wis save, failure takes 6d10 psychic and grants you its fear theme for round. Run: Domain moves it 30 ft to a space of your choice and gives 1 Mark.

20Fear Has No Face

The collection doubles. She may keep two Nightmare Shikigami, their flesh rising to ten times her level, and once per long rest she may summon one from a stored Dream token for nothing. The cabinet, at full capacity. Maintain two Nightmare Shikigami; Blueprint HP becomes 10×level. Once/long rest summon one from stored Dream token for 0 CE.

Pinnacle expression

Maximum, Domain, or equivalent.

Maximum, Nightmare Incarnate (10 CE + 3 stored Dreams; action): The three prized specimens, fused. Ten energy and three stored Dreams: one Huge nightmare for a minute, heavy armor, a hundred and eighty hit points, two five-dice strikes, three chosen Blueprint traits. The centerpiece of the collection, and it is awake. fuse three Blueprints into one Huge nightmare for 1 minute: AC 18, HP 180, two 5d10 attacks, and three selected Blueprint traits.

Feats

Technique Feats

Dream Eater

When her Nightmare wounds a creature bearing three Fear Marks, she may strip all its Marks to grant the Nightmare two more dice of psychic for a minute. The specimen, fed on its own subject. when your Nightmare damages a creature with 3 Fear Marks, you may remove all its Marks to grant the Nightmare +2d8 psychic on its attacks for 1 minute.

Faceless Fear

Her Nightmare is never what it was a moment ago. As a bonus action it may alter its appearance freely, face, voice, stature, becoming anyone the marked creature fears or trusts, statistics unchanged. A creature that sees through it is immune to the trick for a day, but the fear remains: the Marks stay. Presentation, not transformation, claws remain claws. Your Blueprint is never what it was a moment ago. Your summoned Nightmare Shikigami can alter its appearance freely as a bonus action, face, voice, stature, becoming anyone the marked creature fears or trusts, without changing its statistics. A creature that sees through the disguise (Investigation vs. your technique DC) is immune to this trick for 24 hours, but the fear remains: it keeps its Fear Marks. This is presentation, not transformation, claws remain claws.

Mill the Mind

When a creature reaches three Fear Marks, she may immediately loose Exile Thought against it as a reaction, unspent, once per round. The cabinet's door swings shut the moment the specimen is complete. when a creature reaches 3 Fear Marks, you may immediately use Exile Thought against it as a reaction without spending CE. Once per round.

Night Terror

When a creature bearing at least one Fear Mark moves, her Nightmare may move up to its speed as a reaction to follow, once per round. The specimen does not lose its subject. when a creature with at least 1 Fear Mark moves, your Nightmare may move up to its speed as a reaction to follow it. Once per round.

Rules integration

Edge cases and shared systems.

Shared rulings: Technique DC = 8 + PB + CE ability modifier. Direct rolled attacks may crit and use the campaign Black Flash procedure when eligible. Tokens, autonomous summons, passive damage, and initiative-20 Domain effects cannot Black Flash. Maximum Output modifies ordinary supported techniques, never named Maxima or Domains.
