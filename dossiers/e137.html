Nightmare Weaver

Fear marks / nightmare shikigami / exile / dream theft

Power tierTier V · Special Grade+
Core law

What the technique actually does.

Core Engine — Fear Marks. A creature gains a Fear Mark when it fails a Wis save against you, max 3. You may harvest a marked creature's fear into a Nightmare Blueprint, creating custom shikigami flavored around what it fears.

The fiction. Jujutsu scholars classify Nightmare Weaver as fear that learned to hunt: the user's own nightmares were so dense with cursed energy that they slipped the boundary of the mind and kept going. Now the sorcerer's CE harvests the fears of others the way a scholar collects specimens — each Blueprint is someone else's terror, catalogued and weaponized. The prevailing theory holds the technique is not cruelty but appetite: the nightmares must be fed, and they have opinions about the menu.

Full progression

Level 1 through 20.

1Nightmare Touch

1 CE, 60 ft, Wis save: 2d8 psychic and 1 Fear Mark on failure.

3Dream Token

Spend 1 Fear Mark from a creature within 60 ft to create a 1-HP Nightmare until the end of your next turn; it acts on your turn without requiring an action (speed 30 ft; melee CE attack for 2d8 psychic).

6Blueprint

After a creature reaches 3 Marks, record one fear theme. At rest build one Blueprint: Hunter, Swarm, Cage, Doppelganger, or Abyss, each with a distinct movement/control rider — Hunter: +20 ft speed and advantage on checks to pursue the marked creature; Swarm: becomes a swarm of fragments that can occupy other creatures’ spaces and ignores difficult terrain; Cage: as an action, restrain one creature within 30 ft (Strength save negates) for 1 minute; Doppelganger: takes the shape of a creature the victim trusts, and attacks against it have disadvantage until it attacks; Abyss: projects a 15-ft-radius zone of darkness that is difficult terrain, and creatures inside make a Wisdom save or are frightened while inside.

9Nightmare Shikigami

4 CE: summon one Blueprint for 1 minute (AC 15, HP 8×level; its attacks use your CE attack bonus for 3d8 psychic); commanding it is a bonus action; one active.

11Exile Thought

3 CE, Int save: one reaction, summon, or Focus effect is phased into nightmare until end next turn; ongoing durations continue.

13Fear Made Flesh

Nightmare gains one trait from source fear: fly, burrow, reach +10, resistance one type, or +1 attack die. GM approves thematic fit.

15Nightmare Harvest

When marked creature drops to 0, preserve all its Marks as permanent Dream tokens, max PB stored.

18Domain — The Unwritten Nightmare

Initiative 20 each hostile chooses REVEAL or RUN. Reveal: Wis save, failure takes 6d10 psychic and grants you its fear theme for round. Run: Domain moves it 30 ft to a space of your choice and gives 1 Mark.

20Fear Has No Face

Maintain two Nightmare Shikigami; Blueprint HP becomes 10×level. Once/long rest summon one from stored Dream token for 0 CE.

Pinnacle expression

Maximum, Domain, or equivalent.

Maximum — Nightmare Incarnate (10 CE + 3 stored Dreams; action): fuse three Blueprints into one Huge nightmare for 1 minute: AC 18, HP 180, two 5d10 attacks, and three selected Blueprint traits.

Feats

Technique Feats

Dream Eater

when your Nightmare damages a creature with 3 Fear Marks, you may remove all its Marks to grant the Nightmare +2d8 psychic on its attacks for 1 minute.

Faceless Fear

Your Blueprint is never what it was a moment ago. Your summoned Nightmare Shikigami can alter its appearance freely as a bonus action — face, voice, stature — becoming anyone the marked creature fears or trusts, without changing its statistics. A creature that sees through the disguise (Investigation vs. your technique DC) is immune to this trick for 24 hours, but the fear remains: it keeps its Fear Marks. This is presentation, not transformation — claws remain claws.

Mill the Mind

when a creature reaches 3 Fear Marks, you may immediately use Exile Thought against it as a reaction without spending CE. Once per round.

Night Terror

when a creature with at least 1 Fear Mark moves, your Nightmare may move up to its speed as a reaction to follow it. Once per round.

Rules integration

Edge cases and shared systems.

Shared rulings: Technique DC = 8 + PB + CE ability modifier. Direct rolled attacks may crit and use the campaign Black Flash procedure when eligible. Tokens, autonomous summons, passive damage, and initiative-20 Domain effects cannot Black Flash. Maximum Output modifies ordinary supported techniques, never named Maxima or Domains.
