Nightmare Weaver

Tier V · Special Grade+Fear marks / nightmare shikigami / exile / dream theft

Core Engine — Fear Marks. A creature gains a Fear Mark when it fails a Wis save against you, max 3. You may harvest a marked creature's fear into a Nightmare Blueprint, creating custom shikigami flavored around what it fears.

Lv.FeatureRules
1Nightmare Touch1 CE, 60 ft, Wis save: 2d8 psychic and 1 Fear Mark on failure.
3Dream TokenSpend 1 Fear Mark from creature within 60 ft to create 1-HP Nightmare until end next turn; it attacks for 2d8 psychic.
6BlueprintAfter a creature reaches 3 Marks, record one fear theme. At rest build one Blueprint: Hunter, Swarm, Cage, Doppelganger, or Abyss, each with a distinct movement/control rider.
9Nightmare Shikigami4 CE: summon one Blueprint for 1 minute, AC 15, HP 8×level, attack 3d8 psychic; one active.
11Exile Thought3 CE, Int save: one reaction, summon, or Focus effect is phased into nightmare until end next turn; ongoing durations continue.
13Fear Made FleshNightmare gains one trait from source fear: fly, burrow, reach +10, resistance one type, or +1 attack die. GM approves thematic fit.
15Nightmare HarvestWhen marked creature drops to 0, preserve all its Marks as permanent Dream tokens, max PB stored.
18Domain — The Unwritten NightmareInitiative 20 each hostile chooses REVEAL or RUN. Reveal: Wis save, failure takes 6d10 psychic and grants you its fear theme for round. Run: Domain moves it 30 ft to a space of your choice and gives 1 Mark.
20Fear Has No FaceMaintain two Nightmare Shikigami; Blueprint HP becomes 10×level. Once/long rest summon one from stored Dream token for 0 CE.
Maximum — Nightmare Incarnate (10 CE + 3 stored Dreams): fuse three Blueprints into one Huge nightmare for 1 minute: AC 18, HP 180, two 5d10 attacks, and three selected Blueprint traits.

Technique Feats

Dream Eater: Nightmare damage grants 1d8 temp HP once/turn.Faceless Fear: Blueprint can alter appearance freely.Mill the Mind: 3 Marks also -1d8 next Int save.Night Terror: Nightmare speed +10.
Shared rulings: Technique DC = 8 + PB + CE ability modifier. Direct rolled attacks may crit and use the campaign Black Flash procedure when eligible. Tokens, autonomous summons, passive damage, and initiative-20 Domain effects cannot Black Flash. Maximum Output modifies ordinary supported techniques, never named Maxima or Domains.
