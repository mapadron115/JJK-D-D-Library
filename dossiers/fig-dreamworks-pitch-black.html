Nightmare King

Field notes. Subject: Pitch Black, the Nightmare King. Three truths govern the technique. First: fear is the crop — black sand is only the soil; every nightmare, every frightened heartbeat, is a harvest he has been tending since before lanterns. Second: the dark is the accomplice — he does not chase, he waits where the light already gave up, and the frightened come to him the way rivers come to the sea. Third: feeding is the engine — terror taken in becomes terror spent, and the books always balance in his favor. And mark the inversion: the one thing that ever unmade him was not courage but belief — a child's "I believe" spoken like a counterspell. Fear is strong. Being believed in is stronger.

Feature map

Level-by-level features

L1

Fear Sand

Action · 2 CE · 1 minute.

Shape black sand into a weapon or a wall for 1 minute. Weapon: a melee weapon (attack with your CHA modifier) dealing 1d8 + your CHA modifier psychic damage. Wall: a 10-ft long, 10-ft high wall of swirling sand within 30 ft — lightly obscures and grants half cover. One construct at a time.

L1

Nightmare Touch

Action · 3 CE · melee.

Your touch carries the dark with it: 2d8 psychic damage, and the target must succeed on a WIS save vs your CE DC or be frightened of you for 1 minute (it repeats the save at the end of each of its turns).

L3

Feed on Fear

Passive.

When you deal damage to a frightened creature, you regain hit points equal to half the damage dealt (once per turn). Your Fear Sand weapon deals an additional 1d8 psychic damage to frightened creatures. (Terror is a crop. He harvests.)

L6

One with the Dark

Passive.

While in dim light or darkness, you can take the Hide action as a bonus action, you have advantage on Stealth checks, and creatures have disadvantage on Wisdom (Perception) checks to notice you. (The dark was never empty. It was him.)

L11

Nightmare Steeds

Action · 4 CE · 1 hour.

Conjure a Large Nightmare — a horse of black sand with ember eyes. It acts on your initiative under your command: hit points equal to 5 × your level, speed 50 ft, and it can be ridden. As a bonus action, command it to attack: melee attack with your CHA modifier, dealing 2d6 + your CHA modifier psychic damage, and the target must succeed on a WIS save vs your CE DC or be frightened of you until the end of your next turn.

L14

The Fear Grows Teeth

Passive.

Creatures frightened by you have disadvantage on saving throws against your technique features, and when such a creature fails a save against your technique, it takes an additional 2d8 psychic damage. (Fear, fed, grows teeth.)

Maximum Technique

Eternal Nightmare

Action · 10 CE.

Each creature of your choice within 60 ft makes a WIS save vs your CE DC: failure means 6d8 psychic damage and frightened for 1 minute (it repeats the save at the end of each of its turns); success means half damage and no frightened condition. For 1 minute, whenever a creature frightened by this effect takes damage from any source, it takes an additional 1d8 psychic damage — the nightmare feeds on everything.

Domain Expansion

The Long Dark: Every Dream Ends Here

Full-turn action · 20 CE · 800 clash points · burnout 5 rounds · duration 2 minutes · 30-ft radius · disclosed.

The light goes out like a snuffed candle. The walls sweat black sand, and every shadow in the room stands up wearing a face you almost remember. Above, something with too many legs gallops across a sky that isn't there. You are asleep. You have always been asleep.

Inside the domain, the sure-hit is The Dream You Can't Wake From: creatures inside are frightened of you (no save) and take 2d8 psychic damage at the start of each of their turns while they remain inside. The domain's disclosed rule is Belief Breaks It — a creature that spends its action affirming belief (in a Guardian, a memory, a courage) ends the frightened condition on itself. (He ruled the dark for centuries. This is the room where he kept it.)

Level 20 · Capstone

The Boogeyman

Passive · once-per-long-rest reaction.

You are fear itself now: creatures within 30 ft can be frightened by your technique even if they cannot see you — your presence is enough. You have advantage on Intimidation checks against any creature that has ever heard the name Boogeyman (the DM decides; most humanoids qualify). Once per long rest, when a creature frightened by you drops to 0 hit points, you may use your reaction to devour its fading terror: regain 4d8 hit points and end one condition affecting you. (Every culture has a name for him. The names are all correct.)

Innate Technique Feats

Build-defining options.

These are optional character-building feats. You must meet each listed prerequisite before selecting it.

Prerequisite: Fear Sand

Sand in the Gears

Your sand finds machinery delicious. As an action, you may clog one nonmagical mechanism within 30 ft — a lock, a crossbow, a trap, a mill wheel — disabling it for 1 minute (or until cleaned as an action). (His sand never learned to be gentle. It learned to be thorough.)

Prerequisite: Nightmare Touch

Sweet Dreams

Lay a gentle hand on a sleeping willing creature and give it a true dream — while it sleeps within 1 mile of you, you can speak with it in its dreams, as many words as you like, and it remembers on waking. (Even the Nightmare King knows the difference. He simply prefers the other kind.)

Prerequisite: One with the Dark

The Dark Is Listening

While hidden in dim light or darkness, choose one shadow within 60 ft that you can see — for 1 minute, you hear as though you stood within it. (Every shadow is his ear, if he bothers to listen.)

Prerequisite: Feed on Fear

Terror, Bottled

When a creature frightened by you succeeds on a save to end the frightened condition, you may use your reaction to bottle the fading fear (maximum 3 bottles). As an action, shatter a bottle at a point within 30 ft — each creature within 10 ft makes a WIS save vs your CE DC or is frightened of you until the end of your next turn. (Waste not. Fear keeps.)
