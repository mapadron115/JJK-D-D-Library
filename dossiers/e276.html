Moon Dregs

Your technique forms a poison unique to you and comes with your own shikigami to help manifest it.

Power tierTier II · Versatile
Combat roleController
Core engineLunar Jellyfish shikigami + poison riders
DomainLiberation Rite of Water and Land (18)

Field notes. Subject: fragility, armed. Three truths govern this technique. First, the shikigami is the user's own softness weaponized: the lunar jellyfish has no shell, no claws, no speed, nothing but a poison unique to its sorcerer, because its bearer once had nothing else either. Second, the toxin is a tide that only rises: from 2d6 at 3rd level to 6d10 at 18th, then the save that poisons for a minute under exhaustion's weight, while the jellyfish grows teeth, from two attacks to three, from cover to full shelter. Third, the jellyfish is also the wall: it can occupy a creature's space and grant it cover, take attacks in its place, and the Maximum turns the poison into a crippling neurotoxin no cursed technique can wash out. Mark this: the Domain is the sea made air, toxin on every breath, guaranteed stings, a school of tiny jellyfish at the user's command. Everything that touches it will wish it hadn't.

Full progression

Feature map

1Lunar Jellyfish.

The summoning: an action for eight hours of drifting company, dodge by default, commanded by bonus action, resummoned for five. Starting at 1st level, you can use your action to summon a Jellyfish shikigami, which lasts for 8 hours and uses your proficiency bonus. You can spend 1 additional cursed energy to change the shikigami's size between tiny and large as an action. Moon Dregs only takes the dodge action unless you command Moon Dregs using your bonus action each turn. You can summon Moon Dregs for free once per long rest and again for 5 cursed energy. When Moon Dregs is destroyed, it cannot be summoned again for 1 minute.

3Poisonous Cursed Energy.

The toxin seeps in: once per turn, a melee hit may carry the poison for one energy, a die that only grows. At 3rd level, your cursed energy has toxic properties which seep into Moon Dregs and empower it. Once per turn, when you or Moon Dregs hit a creature with a melee attack on your turn, you can spend 1 cursed energy to cause the attack to deal an extra 2d6 poison damage. This increases to 4d6 at 6th level, 6d8 at 11th level and 6d10 at 18th level. This damage is not amplified by a critical hit. You can replace any of your attacks on your turn with an attack from Moon Dregs.

6Noxious Poison.

The poison turns corrosive: a Constitution save or a minute of poisoning under exhaustion's weight, and the jellyfish learns the wall. At 6th level, your cursed energy has become more corrosive and noxious, further seeping into Moon Dregs. When you use Poisonous Cursed Energy, a creature must make a Constitution saving throw or be poisoned for 1 minute. They can repeat the saving throw at the end of their turns, ending the effect on a success. While poisoned in this way, the target has a temporary level of exhaustion. Moon Dregs can now make two attacks when it takes the attack action and can grant a creature three quarters cover while occupying a creature's space, and full cover at 11th level.

11Cleansing of Purity.

The bond deepens: the user's modifier armors the jellyfish's body and mind, and its attacks come in threes. At 11th level, you can add your Cursed Energy modifier to Moon Dregs' AC as well as its Strength, Dexterity, and Constitution scores. Moon Dregs now makes three attacks when it takes the Attack action.

18Domain Expansion: Liberation Rite of Water and Land.

Water where there should be air. The light goes green and dim, pressure builds against the ears, and slow currents tug at clothing and hair with the insistence of hands. Shapes move in the murk beyond the barrier's edge, schooling and circling, patient as creditors. To breathe here is to swallow the sea in small, deliberate mouthfuls.

The sea made air: toxin on every breath at initiative 20, guaranteed stings, and a school of tiny hunters at command. Finally at 18th level, you have created a domain embedded with your technique. The air is filled with toxins and appears as if you are underwater, counting as difficult terrain unless you have a swim speed. Creatures are still able to breathe normally. At initiative 20, all creatures in your domain take 8d6 poison or acid damage, you choose the damage type each time this damage triggers, applying to every creature damaged by that tick. Moon Dregs attacks are imbued with your guaranteed hit and there are a number of tiny jellyfish shikigami swimming around equal to your cursed energy modifier that are also imbued with your guaranteed hit. You can command them as an action. They count as Moon Dregs, can only make 1 attack, and have 30 hitpoints.

Pinnacle expression

Maximum, Domain, or equivalent.

Maximum: Crippling Neurotoxin, the poison that stuns, exhausts threefold, paralyzes the deeply failed, and ignores every cure. Maximum: Crippling Neurotoxin: feat-gated (prerequisite: Cleansing of Purity), when you use Poisonous Cursed Energy, you can spend 8 additional cursed energy to enhance the poison. If a creature fails their save against Noxious Poison they are stunned until the end of their next turn and poisoned for 8 hours. On a successful save, the target is poisoned for 1 minute. A creature poisoned in this way is treated as being under 3 temporary levels of exhaustion. If a creature fails the save by 5 or more, they are paralyzed until the end of their next turn and poisoned for 24 hours. This poison ignores cursed techniques that grant resistance, immunity, or other reductions to poison damage and the poisoned condition. This poison cannot be removed by cursed techniques or reverse cursed technique.

Innate Technique Feats

Build-defining options.

These are optional character-building feats. You must meet each listed prerequisite before selecting it.

Prerequisite: Must have Lunar Jellyfish

Immortal Jellyfish

True to the name: destroyed, the jellyfish reverts to a polyp, one hit point, regrowing each turn toward half its strength. True to its name, your jellyfish refuses to die. When Moon Dregs is destroyed, you can spend 5 CE as a reaction to revert it to its polyp stage instead, it shrinks to Tiny size with 1 hit point. At the start of each of your turns, the polyp regains hit points equal to your CE modifier until it reaches half of Moon Dregs' hit point maximum, at which point it returns to full size.

Prerequisite: Must have Poisonous Cursed Energy

Adorable Poison Jar

The venom milked and bottled: two energy for a vial, weapon-applied or thrown, with the same poison in either hand. You have learned to milk Moon Dregs' venom into a jar. As an action for 2 CE, you can harvest a vial of jellyfish venom. You can apply it to a weapon as an action, the next hit forces a Constitution saving throw against your CE save DC, poisoning the target for 1 minute and dealing 2d6 poison damage on a failed save, or throw the vial as an improvised weapon (range 20/60 ft), shattering it in a 5 ft radius with the same effect.

Prerequisite: Must have Cleansing of Purity

Maximum: Crippling Neurotoxin

The feat-form of the crippling doctrine, granted to those who have learned the Cleansing. You have finally learned to amplify your poisonous cursed energy into a crippling neurotoxin that locks down nerves. When you use Poisonous Cursed Energy, you can spend 8 additional cursed energy to enhance the poison. If a creature fails their save against Noxious Poison they are stunned until the end of their next turn and poisoned for 8 hours. On a successful save, the target is poisoned for 1 minute. A creature poisoned in this way is treated as being under 3 temporary levels of exhaustion. If a creature fails the save by 5 or more, they are paralyzed until the end of their next turn and poisoned for 24 hours. This poison ignores cursed techniques that grant resistance, immunity, or other reductions to poison damage and the poisoned condition. This poison cannot be removed by cursed techniques or reverse cursed technique.

Rules integration

Edge cases and shared systems.

Recovered: Lunar Jellyfish shikigami stat block (V.6 PDF p. 177, transcribed verbatim, resolves the audit HOLD). Moon Dregs · Tiny shikigami (unaligned). AC 11 + your proficiency bonus. HP 10 × your level. Speed 5 ft., fly 40 ft. (hover), swim 40 ft. STR 16 (+3), DEX 14 (+2), CON 15 (+2), INT 4 (−3), WIS 13 (+1), CHA 5 (−3). Skills: Athletics +5. Damage Resistances: acid, cold. Damage Immunities: bludgeoning, poison. Condition Immunities: blinded. Senses: blindsight 10 ft., passive Perception 11. Languages: —. Challenge: uses your proficiency bonus. Defensive Shikigami: Moon Dregs can occupy the space of a willing or grappled creature, granting it half cover and allowing Moon Dregs to take attacks in place of the creature. Toxic Body: when a creature hits Moon Dregs with a melee attack within 5 feet, they take 1d4 poison damage as it splashes out when injured; this increases to 1d8 at 11th level. Actions, Sting: melee weapon attack, +3 + your proficiency to hit, reach 10 ft., one target; hit: 10 (2d8 + 3) piercing damage. Tendrils: melee weapon attack, +3 + your proficiency to hit, reach 5 ft., one target; hit: 6 (1d6 + 3) bludgeoning damage and the creature is grappled (escape DC 15); until the grapple ends, the creature is restrained and Moon Dregs cannot restrain another target.
Rules priority: the reviewed rulings, explicit codex clarifications, and the level progression always control when original wording conflicts. Legacy Domain auto-hit language, action terminology, event timing, missing saves, and older scaling never override revised player rules.
Temporary exhaustion: "temporary levels of exhaustion" end when the poisoning ends; they are tracked separately from real exhaustion and cannot kill.
Attack replacement: the attack-replacement lets the user trade their own attacks for the jellyfish's, using the jellyfish's attack bonus from the recovered stat block above.
Domain damage type (ruled): the V.6 text's "8d6 poison or acid damage" never states who chooses the type. Ruling: the domain's user chooses poison or acid each time the initiative-20 damage triggers; the choice applies to every creature damaged by that tick. (Simpler than per-creature choice, and matches the technique's theme of the user tailoring their own poison.)
Domain conversion: initiative-20 timing matches; clashes use the deterministic 1–1,000 point system; barrier statistics per D007; burnout per D010.
