Features & procedures

Properties & techniques

Exact player-facing procedures

Action

Heavenly binding

While wielding this weapon, you can use your action to make an attack roll using your Cursed Energy modifier against a target within 50 ft. On a hit, the creature is bound by the chains and cannot use cursed energy until it escapes.

Escape

The bound creature can use its action to attempt escape with an Athletics or Acrobatics check against a DC of 20 + half the target's current cursed energy (rounded up). On a success, it breaks free; the chains remain in your possession.

Indestructible binding

These chains become indestructible when physically binding a creature with strong cursed energy — they cannot be cut, broken, or destroyed while the binding holds.

Rules integration

Edge cases and shared systems.

Campaign canon notes

Escape DC: computed as 20 + ceil(target's current CE ÷ 2) at the moment of the escape attempt, so it falls as the target spends energy. "Cannot use cursed energy" suppresses techniques, CE-fueled features, and CE expenditure while bound; it does not remove the target's CE pool.
