Features & procedures

Properties & techniques

Exact player-facing procedures

Protect

reaction near ally grants +2 AC once/round. Lead — first ally following your movement gains +10 speed. Endure — first time bloodied gain 2 Tool Dice temp HP.

Sword of Promised Light

action, 4 CE, 60-ft ×5-ft line, Dex save, 4 Tool Dice radiant/slashing, half success.

Scabbard's Grace

while carrying its scabbard, advantage vs disease/poison and death saves.

Release: Once and Future Blade

8 CE, action to activate (invented), once/long rest: 120-ft line, 8 Tool Dice, then allies in line may move 15 ft safely.

The fiction. Registry scholars treat the blade as a technique that outlived its oath — metal that was present when a resolve was spoken with enough cursed energy behind it to learn the shape of keeping a promise. The prevailing theory holds the oath-breaking suppression is the tool's honesty: it serves the sworn word, not the wielder, and a broken vow starves it the way a broken circuit starves a lamp. It is someone else's resolve, loaned to anyone whose own can stand the comparison.

Rules integration

Cursed-tool attunement

Campaign canon

Cursed-tool attunement: you have PB attunement slots (2–6). This tool occupies one slot and requires a one-hour ritual. Attunement ends voluntarily over a short rest, on death, or when you replace it while over capacity. Distance does not break attunement. Cursed-tool attunement is separate from magic-item attunement.
Balance & counterplay. The Oath makes its leadership fantasy behavioral rather than a free pile of bonuses.
