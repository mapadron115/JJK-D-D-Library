Features & procedures

Properties & techniques

Exact player-facing procedures

Protect

reaction near allyThree oaths, three doctrines of command, chosen each dawn. Protect wards another with your reaction; Lead lends swiftness to whoever follows your advance; Endure refuses to fall the first time the blood comes. One blade; the wielder decides which virtue it enforces. grants +2 AC once/round. Lead, first ally following your movement gains +10 speed. Endure, first time bloodied gain 2 Tool Dice temp HP.

Sword of Promised Light

The blade remembers being drawn for something. When the Oath holds, four CE and an action become a sixty-foot verdict of radiant light, and those standing behind the line will find it carries them forward. action, 4 CE, 60-ft ×5-ft line, Dex save, 4 Tool Dice radiant/slashing, half success.

Scabbard's Grace

The older wisdom travels in the sheath, not the sword. Carry the scabbard and the body's small treacheries, poison, disease, the last door, lose their certainty. while carrying its scabbard, advantage vs disease/poison and death saves.

Release: Once and Future Blade

Once a day, the loan is repaid in full: a hundred and twenty feet of the blade's whole argument, eight dice of it, and the allies caught in its light are not harmed but repositioned, the way a king's charge repositions his host. This is the Release used as it was meant: a leader's weapon, even in its violence. 8 CE, action to activate (invented), once/long rest: 120-ft line, 8 Tool Dice, then allies in line may move 15 ft safely.

Field notes. Subject: someone else's resolve, loaned. Three truths govern him. First, the blade serves the sworn word, not the wielder: choose an Oath after each long rest, Protect, Lead, or Endure, and every property the sword owns flows from the keeping of it. Second, the Oath is a daily discipline of command: the blade grants what it grants (a warded ally, a hastened step, a second wind when bloodied) because the bearer behaves as the word demands, and the moment he does not, the properties starve until a short rest, the way a broken circuit starves a lamp. Third, the scabbard is the greater teacher: while it rides with him, disease, poison, and death itself find him harder to claim. Consider the paradox: the mightiest sword in the catalogue wins its wars by making its bearer behave.

Rules integration

Cursed-tool attunement

Campaign canon

Cursed-tool attunement: you have PB attunement slots (2–6). This tool occupies one slot and requires a one-hour ritual. Attunement ends voluntarily over a short rest, on death, or when you replace it while over capacity. Distance does not break attunement. Cursed-tool attunement is separate from magic-item attunement.
Balance & counterplay. The Oath makes its leadership fantasy behavioral rather than a free pile of bonuses.
