Field entry

Journal Entry field report, converted to a table-ready stat block

Field notes. Subject: stillness as a weapon. It is not a display that came alive; it is a hunter that learned the one thing no prey suspects — to be scenery. Its False Appearance makes it indistinguishable from a shop mannequin while motionless, and its rigid body shrugs off bludgeoning and piercing damage that would fell its frail look-alike. Almost mindless, barely more than a waiting shape — but its slam needs no cunning, only a turned back. Classify this correctly: the disguise IS the technique. Operational rule, learned the hard way — tap every mannequin on the display floor before you walk past it.

Record

Classification & Risk

A Cursed Corpse (natural), Grade 4 (CR 1/8–1/2 band). Forms in abandoned shopping centers or storage facilities. Its defense is its disguise; its body resists damage far better than its fragile appearance suggests.

Stats

Ability scores & traits

Construct. STR 14 (+2) · DEX 8 (−1) · CON 16 (+3) · INT 3 (−4) · WIS 8 (−1) · CHA 3 (−4). HP 34 (4d10 + 12), AC 15, speed 20 ft. False Appearance. While motionless, indistinguishable from an ordinary mannequin. Rigid Body: resistance to bludgeoning and piercing damage. Invisibility does not apply — as a physical cursed corpse it is plainly visible.

Actions

Actions

Slam. Melee Attack: +5 to hit, reach 5 ft. Hit: 1d8 + 3 bludgeoning damage.

Risk

Special

Exhibits little intelligence or complex behavior — it waits, disguised, and strikes when approached. Sweep display floors carefully: tap every mannequin before turning your back on it.

Edge cases and shared systems.

Campaign canon notes

Field rule — perception: At Grade 2 and above this spirit’s cursed energy is dense enough that no Invisibility trait is assigned; standard perception applies. It remains invisible to ordinary non-sorcerers.
