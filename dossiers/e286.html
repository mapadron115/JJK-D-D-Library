Tool Manipulation

You can control tools you imbue with cursed energy.

Power tierTier III · Apex
Combat roleStriker
Core engineTool imbuement and Floating Tools
DomainTsukumogami Spirit World (L18)

The fiction. The prevailing theory holds that Tool Manipulation awakened in a sorcerer who treated their tools as companions rather than equipment — and their cursed energy, asked to love something, answered by giving the tools enough of itself to fly. The floating weapons are not puppets, registry notes insist; they are what happens when CE is poured into an object someone genuinely cherished. The Tsukumogami Spirit World is the technique's final argument: that a tool kept long enough and well enough stops being an object at all.

Full progression

Feature map

L1Guided Control

Beginning at 1st level, you can spend CE up to your CE modifier as a bonus action to imbue a tool you are holding with cursed energy. This imbuement lasts an hour for each cursed energy spent and gains the following benefits: you gain a +2 bonus to attack rolls using this tool or a +1 bonus to AC if the tool is armor or used defensively; the damage die increases by 1 tier and again at 6th and 11th level (D4<D6<D8<D10<2d6<2d8).

L3Floating Tool

At 3rd level, when you imbue a tool, you can spend an additional 2 CE to create a Floating Tool. You can command a Floating Tool as an action to hover up to 35 feet in any direction and make a melee attack using your CE modifier. The Floating Tool deals its normal weapon damage or 1d4 bludgeoning, slashing, or piercing if it is an improvised weapon. You can only have one floating tool at a time and it falls to the ground if it is more than 120 ft from you.

A Floating Tool has AC equal to your CE save DC and hit points equal to 5 x your proficiency bonus + 10 for each grade above 4th if it is a cursed tool. A Floating Tool reduced to 0 HP is destroyed unless it is a cursed tool, in which case it loses the benefits of Floating Tool. You can spend 2 CE as a bonus action to return the effect of Floating Tool to the cursed tool by touching it.

L6Wind Scythe

At 6th level you have learned to use tools offensively. As an action for 3 CE, you can create a blast of wind from an imbued tool you are holding. Or from a Floating Tool within 60 ft for 1 additional CE. This creates a 10 feet wide, 60 feet long line. Creatures within range must make a Strength saving throw, taking 3d10 bludgeoning damage and pushed back 10 feet on a failed save or half as much on a success. At 11th level, your Wind Scythe now deals 6d10 bludgeoning damage and a creature that fails their save is pushed back 20 feet and knocked prone.

Tool Improvement. Additionally, your specialty grants you the following Tool Improvements. Floating Tool Improvement: the cost to create a Floating Tool becomes 1 CE; when you command a Floating Tool, they can move up to 40 feet; you gain a flying speed of 60 feet by mounting a Floating Tool; you can have 2 Floating Tools active at the same time, commanding both as part of the same action. Combat Tool Improvement: you gain a +1 bonus to attack and damage rolls when using an imbued tool for each CE spent. As a reaction, you can cause one of your imbued tools to protect you. It grants you a bonus to AC or reduces damage depending on its size (Tiny +1/1d4, Small +2/2d4, Medium +3/2d6, Large +4/2d8, Huge +5/2d10). If the tool has a bonus to AC or damage rolls, it is included in the AC and reduction respectively.

L11Tool Mastery

At 11th level, you have mastered your respective style, granting you the following: Floating Tool Mastery. You can command two Floating Tools using your bonus action or one Floating Tool as a free action on your turn. You and other creatures also gain advantage on Dexterity saves while riding a Floating Tool. Combat Tool Mastery. Your mastery over imbued tools allows you to manipulate the flow of combat with exceptional precision. When you use Combat Tool Improvement, the AC bonus it grants increases by 1, and the damage reduction provided increases by one additional die. In addition, you gain one extra reaction each round that can only be used to activate Combat Tool Improvement.

When your or a creature you can see makes an attack using your imbued tool, you can use your reaction to grant that attack roll advantage.

Maximum: Tsukumogami.

Prerequisite: Tool Mastery. As an action for 8 CE, you can imbue a cursed tool with sentience, causing it to come to life and become a Tsukumogami for 1 hour. Your Tsukumogami is always under the effects of Floating Tool and can freely move by itself. It does not count against the number of Floating Tools you can have at once, but you can only have one Tsukumogami at a time. The Tsukumogami can take an action on your turn while you are holding it. That action can be used only to take the Attack action (one weapon attack only), Dash, Disengage, Hide, or Use an Object action. If you use Wind Scythe or Combat Tool Improvement's reaction with your Tsukumogami, you can roll three additional dice.

L18Domain Expansion: Tsukumogami Spirit World

Finally at 18th level you have learned to manifest your domain. All weapon attacks made by creatures other than you become a guaranteed miss as you can manipulate all tools within your domain, causing them to come to life and obey your commands. At initiative 20, you choose one of your imbued tools to gain the benefits of Floating Tool and have all Floating Tools move 60 ft and attack a creature within range or have all creatures suffer the effects of Wind Scythe. You can control a number of Floating Tools equal to your CE modifier (min of 2) within your domain.

Innate Technique Feats

Build-defining options.

These are optional character-building feats. You must meet each listed prerequisite before selecting it.

Prerequisite: Must have Guided Control

Tools of the Craft

You can imbue a set of tools with your cursed energy, such as artisan's tools or thieves' tools (1 CE, lasting until your next long rest). You can use your CE modifier in place of the normal ability modifier for ability checks made using the imbued tools. Additionally, you can channel a technique through them: when you use Wind Scythe, you can originate the line from an imbued tool you can see within 60 feet of you instead of one you hold or a Floating Tool.

Prerequisite: Must have Floating Tool Improvement

Eyes in the Skies

Your Floating Tool is a perfect scouting perch. While you are riding a Floating Tool at least 20 feet above the ground, you can use your action to survey the area below: you learn the location of every creature within 120 feet of you that isn't behind total cover from your vantage. Allies who can see or hear you ignore disadvantage on Perception checks to notice those creatures until the start of your next turn.

Rules integration

Edge cases and shared systems.

Rules priority: the reviewed rulings, explicit codex clarifications, and the level progression always control when original wording conflicts. The audit dispositions for AT238 are adopted as a V.6 addition with the exact changes noted above.
Floating Tool leash: the 120-ft leash — crossing it drops the tool with no save — is kept as printed (AT238). Floating Tool HP references "grade above 4th," which uses the campaign's cursed-tool grade scale.
