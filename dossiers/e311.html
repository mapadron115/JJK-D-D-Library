Features & procedures

Properties & techniques

Exact player-facing procedures

Satanel — Negative Circuit

The black form channels ordinary cursed energy and applies Apocalypse Marks.

Satan — Reversed Circuit

The white form internally reverses CE into positive energy and applies Catastrophe Marks.

Armageddon

Requires 5 Apocalypse Marks and 5 Catastrophe Marks on the same target. The wielder must complete the activation chant. The marks collapse into one another for a catastrophic finishing strike. Afterward, the wielder suffers severe stat and technique suppression, while Antinomy itself remains weakened for days.

Rules integration

Cursed-tool attunement

Campaign canon

Cursed-tool attunement: you have PB attunement slots (2–6). This tool occupies one slot and requires a one-hour ritual. Attunement ends voluntarily over a short rest, on death, or when you replace it while over capacity. Distance does not break attunement. Cursed-tool attunement is separate from magic-item attunement.
Balance & counterplay. Armageddon needs 5 Apocalypse and 5 Catastrophe Marks on one target plus a completed chant — disrupt the mark buildup or interrupt the chant and the finisher never fires. Even a successful Armageddon leaves the wielder suppressed for days; punish the aftermath.
