Jujutsu origin

Antinomy is built around a contradiction that should be impossible in ordinary jujutsu engineering: one cursed tool houses a negative cursed-energy circuit and a reversed positive-energy circuit. A stabilizing Binding Vow forbids the two currents from occupying the blade simultaneously. The only place they are allowed to coexist is inside a marked target.

Satanel — Black Circuit

Antinomy begins in Satanel. Once per turn on a hit, spend 2 CE to deal an additional 4d10 necrotic damage and place 1 Apocalypse Seal on the target, maximum 5.

Principle of Antinomy — Bonus Action

Invert the weapon's internal circuit, switching between Satanel and Satan. The sword never holds both states at once.

Satan — White Circuit

Once per turn on a hit, spend 2 CE to deal an additional 4d10 radiant damage and place 1 Catastrophe Seal on the target, maximum 5. Creatures of your choice within 10 ft of the struck target make a Strength save against your Relic DC; on a failure they are pushed 15 ft and knocked prone. While Satan is active, you have resistance to bludgeoning, piercing, and slashing damage.

Armageddon Requirement

One target must carry 5 Apocalypse Seals and 5 Catastrophe Seals. The seals last until the end of combat or until Armageddon is attempted.

Armageddon Chant

Spend your action and bonus action chanting. Your speed becomes 0 until the start of your next turn and you cannot activate another Cursed Technique, Maximum, Reversal, or Domain during the chant. If incapacitated before your next turn, the chant fails but the seals remain.

Armageddon

On your next turn, make one Antinomy attack against the sealed target with advantage. On a hit, in addition to the weapon's normal damage, deal 20d12 radiant + 20d12 necrotic + 10d12 force damage. This damage ignores resistance and immunity to radiant and necrotic damage. An effect that would reduce the Armageddon damage to 0 instead reduces it by half.

Aftermath — 24 Hours

For 24 hours after Armageddon, treat all six of your ability scores as half their normal values for attacks, checks, saves, DCs, and derived statistics. You cannot activate Cursed Techniques, Maximum Techniques, Reversals, Domains, or cursed-tool techniques during this period.

Aftermath — 7 Days

For 7 days, Antinomy loses its +4 bonus and all special abilities and functions as an ordinary greatsword.

Binding Vow — Mutual Exclusion

Satanel and Satan may never occupy the blade together. The ten seals move the contradiction into the enemy instead. Armageddon is the one permitted instant where all ten collapse into one point, and the week-long ruin of the weapon is the price that allows it.

Designer’s note. The sword itself performs the reversal needed for Satan; the wielder does not need to know Reverse Cursed Technique.

Rules integration

Cursed-tool attunement

Campaign canon

Cursed-tool attunement: you have PB attunement slots (2–6). This tool occupies one slot and requires a one-hour ritual. Attunement ends voluntarily over a short rest, on death, or when you replace it while over capacity. Distance does not break attunement. Cursed-tool attunement is separate from magic-item attunement.
