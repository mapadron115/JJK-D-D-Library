Antinomy

A greatsword housing two mutually exclusive circuits — Satanel's necrotic seals and Satan's radiant seals — that must both be planted (5 each) on one target before the wielder chants and unleashes Armageddon: 20d12 radiant + 20d12 necrotic + 10d12 force.

Antinomy

Metaphysical Law: Negative CE and positive energy may coexist only long enough to annihilate the thing containing both.

FormGreatsword
EngineArmageddon
Attunement1 hour*

Field notes. Subject: a reckoning. This blade keeps two ledgers — Satanel’s black seals, Satan’s white — and the wielder is the clerk who writes five entries in each. Three truths govern it. First, contradiction is the engine: neither circuit can kill; only their meeting can, and it may only meet inside the marked. Second, the blade charges for its own arithmetic: a full action and a bonus action to speak the sum aloud, rooted in place, no other technique while the chant stands. Third — the doctrine the registry fears — the sum is never free. The wielder’s six measures halve for a day; the blade sleeps as common steel for a week. Ten seals, planted by hand, for one instant where heaven and hell agree. Mark this: the greatest damage in the archive is not priced in CE.

Features & procedures

Properties & techniques

Exact player-facing procedures

Relic profile

The shell is honest: three dice, heavy, two-handed, the plus-four of a masterwork. What you are holding is not a sword with a trick — it is a trick with a sword attached. 3d6 slashing · Heavy, Two-Handed · +4 to attack and damage rolls

Jujutsu origin

Understand the engineering before you admire the result. Two currents that should annihilate their housing, held apart by a vow — the contradiction is not a flaw in the design. It is the design. Antinomy is built around a contradiction that should be impossible in ordinary jujutsu engineering: one cursed tool houses a negative cursed-energy circuit and a reversed positive-energy circuit. A stabilizing Binding Vow forbids the two currents from occupying the blade simultaneously. The only place they are allowed to coexist is inside a marked target.

Satanel — Black Circuit

The first ledger: necrotic, seal by seal, once per turn. The clerk begins his entries in black — five marks, each costing coin. Antinomy begins in Satanel. Once per turn on a hit, spend 2 CE to deal an additional 4d10 necrotic damage and place 1 Apocalypse Seal on the target, maximum 5.

Principle of Antinomy — Bonus Action

The inversion. With a thought the blade turns its coat inside out, and black becomes white. Mark the law well: it is never both. The sword holds one truth at a time. Invert the weapon's internal circuit, switching between Satanel and Satan. The sword never holds both states at once.

Satan — White Circuit

The second ledger: radiant, with force enough to scatter bystanders and a hardness against harm while it stands. Five white seals to match the five black. Once per turn on a hit, spend 2 CE to deal an additional 4d10 radiant damage and place 1 Catastrophe Seal on the target, maximum 5. Creatures of your choice within 10 ft of the struck target make a Strength save against your Relic DC; on a failure they are pushed 15 ft and knocked prone. While Satan is active, you have resistance to bludgeoning, piercing, and slashing damage.

Armageddon Requirement

Ten seals on one body — the entire precondition, written plainly. The work is not the chant; the work is the ten honest hits that earn it. One target must carry 5 Apocalypse Seals and 5 Catastrophe Seals. The seals last until the end of combat or until Armageddon is attempted.

Armageddon Chant

Now the reckoning is spoken aloud: action and bonus action both, rooted in place, and no other technique may share the breath. If he falls before the sum is spoken, the seals keep — the debt is not forgiven, only deferred. Spend your action and bonus action chanting. Your speed becomes 0 until the start of your next turn and you cannot activate another Cursed Technique, Maximum, Reversal, or Domain during the chant. If incapacitated before your next turn, the chant fails but the seals remain.

Armageddon

The sum: twenty dice of radiance, twenty of rot, ten of raw force, struck with advantage, and no resistance permitted — even what would erase it merely halves it. This is the instant heaven and hell agree. On your next turn, make one Antinomy attack against the sealed target with advantage. On a hit, in addition to the weapon's normal damage, deal 20d12 radiant + 20d12 necrotic + 10d12 force damage. This damage ignores resistance and immunity to radiant and necrotic damage. An effect that would reduce the Armageddon damage to 0 instead reduces it by half.

Aftermath — 24 Hours

The wielder’s price, first installment: a day with every measure halved, and every technique silent. One who spends this much of himself should pray the sum was sufficient. For 24 hours after Armageddon, treat all six of your ability scores as half their normal values for attacks, checks, saves, DCs, and derived statistics. You cannot activate Cursed Techniques, Maximum Techniques, Reversals, Domains, or cursed-tool techniques during this period.

Aftermath — 7 Days

The blade’s price: a week as common steel, stripped of its fours and its miracles. The archive’s rule is absolute — nothing that deals in Armageddon is ever cheap twice. For 7 days, Antinomy loses its +4 bonus and all special abilities and functions as an ordinary greatsword.

Binding Vow — Mutual Exclusion

The vow that makes the impossible merely expensive: the two currents never meet in the steel, only in the marked. The week of ruin is not a flaw in the bargain. It is the bargain. Satanel and Satan may never occupy the blade together. The ten seals move the contradiction into the enemy instead. Armageddon is the one permitted instant where all ten collapse into one point, and the week-long ruin of the weapon is the price that allows it.

Designer’s note. The sword itself performs the reversal needed for Satan; the wielder does not need to know Reverse Cursed Technique.

Rules integration

Cursed-tool attunement

Campaign canon

Cursed-tool attunement: you have PB attunement slots (2–6). This tool occupies one slot and requires a one-hour ritual. Attunement ends voluntarily over a short rest, on death, or when you replace it while over capacity. Distance does not break attunement. Cursed-tool attunement is separate from magic-item attunement.
