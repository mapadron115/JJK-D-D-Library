Core law

What the technique actually does.

Core Engine — Preparation Slots. After long rest prepare PB Schemes: a spell matrix, armor module, Doombot protocol, trap, countermeasure, or stolen-energy contingency from the codex list. Doom wins by having the right answer, not one infinite spell list.

Scheme codex. Every prepared Scheme uses its full procedure: Spell Matrix (action, 3 CE, 60 ft): ranged CE attack, 3d8 force or necrotic. Armor Module (bonus action, 2 CE): install one Latverian Arsenal module you did not choose at level 9 for 1 minute. Doombot Protocol (action, 3 CE): deploy a Doombot decoy as per level 6. Trap (action, 2 CE): set a 10-ft-square arcane mine within 60 ft; the first creature to enter makes a Dexterity save, taking 4d8 force on failure, half on success; lasts 8 hours. Countermeasure — Counterspell (reaction, 2 CE): make a technique contest (see Rules — Shared Resolutions) against a technique used within 60 ft; if you win, the technique is countered. Stolen-Energy Contingency (reaction, 2 CE): when you succeed on a save against a technique, gain a Siphon token as if from Power Siphon.

The fiction. Registry scholars describe the technique as preparation fetishized into metaphysics: the user's cursed energy shaped itself around the moment of having the right answer ready — and now refuses to admit a moment unprepared. The prevailing theory holds that the Doombots are not decoys but the sorcerer's own refusal to die unready, wearing his face so he never has to.

Full progression

Level 1 through 20.

1Doom Armor

You wear Doom Armor: AC 16 + Dexterity (maximum +2), an integrated gauntlet you can fire (60 ft, 2d8 force), and an environmental seal.

3Arcane Bolt

For 1 CE, fire an arcane bolt at a creature within 60 ft, dealing 2d10 force or necrotic damage. You may prepare a Counterspell Scheme from the codex below: as a reaction, contest a technique for 2 CE (see Rules — Shared Resolutions).

6Doombot

For 3 CE, summon one 1-HP Doombot decoy for 1 minute. It can move, speak, make a gauntlet attack for 2d8, and impersonate you; only one at a time.

9Latverian Arsenal

Choose two armor modules: flight (50 ft), a force shield (reaction, +3 AC), a tractor beam, micro-missiles (15-ft burst, 4d8), or a mystic sensor.

11Power Siphon

As a reaction, spend 3 CE after you succeed on a save against a technique: gain a Siphon token. Within 1 minute, you may spend the token to add 3d10 matching damage or gain +2 on a technique contest (see Rules — Shared Resolutions).

13Master of Science & Sorcery

You may maintain one technological Focus effect and one magical Focus effect at the same time.

15Time Platform

For 6 CE, once per long rest: rewind only yourself to the position and hit points you had at the start of your previous turn; spent resources do not return.

18Domain — Castle Doom

At initiative 20, choose one of your prepared Schemes; the Domain executes its defensive version autonomously: suppress a projectile lane (a 10-ft-wide, 60-ft-long zone where ranged attacks have disadvantage for the round); deploy cover (a 10-ft wall with 30 Integrity); bind one target within 60 ft that fails a Strength save (restrained until the next initiative 20); or fire a 6d10 gauntlet blast (ranged CE attack, 120 ft; Dexterity save, half on success). You may change the Scheme each round.

20Doom Above All

You prepare twice your proficiency bonus in Schemes; the first Scheme you use each round costs 1 less CE. You may maintain two Doombots. Once per long rest, you may declare a plausible preparation against a revealed threat and retroactively fill one unused Scheme slot.

Pinnacle expression

Maximum, Domain, or equivalent.

Maximum — Power Cosmic Theft Protocol (12 CE): after a creature within 30 ft expends a Maximum/ultimate, reaction contest (see Rules — Shared Resolutions). Success stores an Echo until long rest; use once at half original dice/effect scale for 8 CE. Never copies Domains, identities, permanent passives, or reality-author privileges.

Feats

Build-defining options.

Diplomatic Immunity

Doom's sovereignty is itself a technique. When you formally invoke your rank, title, or the authority of Latveria, you have advantage on Charisma (Persuasion) and Charisma (Intimidation) checks for the next 10 minutes, and creatures that recognize your authority cannot willingly target you with hostile technique effects until you or an ally acts against them. This immunity is void against beings that do not recognize mortal titles — Domains, Calamity-grade horrors, and the genuinely insane.

Hidden Doombot

Once per long rest, when you would be reduced to 0 HP, you may reveal that the 'you' just defeated was a Doombot all along — provided it is logically possible: you must have had a Doombot deployed this combat or an unspent Scheme slot, and no effect may have explicitly prevented the swap. You instead appear unharmed at a location of your choice within 60 feet, leaving the Doombot behind as sparking wreckage. This revelation costs no action; it was planned for long before the battle began.

Mystic Armor

when you succeed on a save against a technique while wearing Doom Armor, you may spend 1 CE as a reaction to siphon its residue, gaining a Siphon token as if from Power Siphon.

DOOM Does Not Beg

once per short rest, when you fail a Wisdom or Charisma save, you may use your reaction to file the attack as intelligence: you learn the source's technique and one named feature it used, and you gain advantage on saves against that source for 1 minute.

Rules integration

Edge cases and shared systems.

Codex rulings: Direct rolled technique/weapon attacks can use the campaign's Black Flash procedure when otherwise eligible. Passive damage, hazards, summons acting independently, and initiative-20 Domain events cannot. Generic Maximum Output applies only to ordinary features, never named Maxima or Domains.
