Core law

What the technique actually does.

Core Engine — Preparation Slots. The whole technique in one card. PB Schemes after every long rest — matrices, modules, protocols, traps, countermeasures, stolen-energy contingencies. Doom's thesis: victory is filed, not improvised. After long rest prepare PB Schemes: a spell matrix, armor module, Doombot protocol, trap, countermeasure, or stolen-energy contingency from the codex list. Doom wins by having the right answer, not one infinite spell list.

Scheme codex. The filing cabinet, itemized: Spell Matrix, Armor Module, Doombot Protocol, Trap, and the rest — each with its full procedure. Read it as a menu, not a manual. Every prepared Scheme uses its full procedure: Spell Matrix (action, 3 CE, 60 ft): ranged CE attack, 3d8 force or necrotic. Armor Module (bonus action, 2 CE): install one Latverian Arsenal module you did not choose at level 9 for 1 minute. Doombot Protocol (action, 3 CE): deploy a Doombot decoy as per level 6. Trap (action, 2 CE): set a 10-ft-square arcane mine within 60 ft; the first creature to enter makes a Dexterity save, taking 4d8 force on failure, half on success; lasts 8 hours. Countermeasure — Counterspell (reaction, 2 CE): make a technique contest (see Rules — Shared Resolutions) against a technique used within 60 ft; if you win, the technique is countered. Stolen-Energy Contingency (reaction, 2 CE): when you succeed on a save against a technique, gain a Siphon token as if from Power Siphon.

Field notes. Subject: a sovereign who has already won. Three truths govern him. First: he does not cast spells so much as execute preparations — PB Schemes readied after every long rest from the codex, and his whole art is the doctrine that the right answer, filed in advance, beats the infinite spell list. Second: everything he wears is a contingency — the armor, the gauntlet, the decoy that can be him when he is not; Power Siphon turns a survived technique into a token of its own refutation. Third: at the summit the preparations cheat time itself — the Time Platform rewinds him a turn, and Doom Above All lets him declare a preparation retroactively, once per day, against a threat already revealed. Classify him correctly: you are not fighting a sorcerer. You are fighting his filing system.

Full progression

Level 1 through 20.

1Doom Armor

The throne is worn: AC sixteen plus Dexterity, an integrated gauntlet at sixty feet for two dice, and a seal against the environment. The king arrives armored against the weather of war. You wear Doom Armor: AC 16 + Dexterity (maximum +2), an integrated gauntlet you can fire (60 ft, 2d8 force), and an environmental seal.

3Arcane Bolt

One CE for two dice of force or necrotic at sixty feet — and the Counterspell Scheme, filed for two CE as a reaction: the first rehearsed answer. For 1 CE, fire an arcane bolt at a creature within 60 ft, dealing 2d10 force or necrotic damage. You may prepare a Counterspell Scheme from the codex below: as a reaction, contest a technique for 2 CE (see Rules — Shared Resolutions).

6Doombot

Three CE for a one-HP double that moves, speaks, fires his gauntlet, and passes for him — one at a time. The sovereign is never where you killed him. For 3 CE, summon one 1-HP Doombot decoy for 1 minute. It can move, speak, make a gauntlet attack for 2d8, and impersonate you; only one at a time.

9Latverian Arsenal

Two modules chosen: flight, a reactive shield, a tractor beam, micro-missiles, or a mystic sensor. The armor is a menu, and he orders twice. Choose two armor modules: flight (50 ft), a force shield (reaction, +3 AC), a tractor beam, micro-missiles (15-ft burst, 4d8), or a mystic sensor.

11Power Siphon

The refutation filed as treasure: survive a technique with three CE in hand as a reaction, and take a Siphon token — three dice of its own damage, or +2 on a contest, within the minute. As a reaction, spend 3 CE after you succeed on a save against a technique: gain a Siphon token. Within 1 minute, you may spend the token to add 3d10 matching damage or gain +2 on a technique contest (see Rules — Shared Resolutions).

13Master of Science & Sorcery

One technological Focus and one magical Focus, maintained at once. The two doctrines stop taking turns. You may maintain one technological Focus effect and one magical Focus effect at the same time.

15Time Platform

Six CE, once per long rest: he rewinds himself — position and hit points — to the start of his previous turn. Spent resources do not return; the platform is a refund, not a loan. For 6 CE, once per long rest: rewind only yourself to the position and hit points you had at the start of your previous turn; spent resources do not return.

18Domain — Castle Doom

The Domain executes a prepared Scheme on its own authority at initiative twenty: a suppressed projectile lane, deployed cover, a bound target, or an autonomous firing. The castle defends its king without being asked. At initiative 20, choose one of your prepared Schemes; the Domain executes its defensive version autonomously: suppress a projectile lane (a 10-ft-wide, 60-ft-long zone where ranged attacks have disadvantage for the round); deploy cover (a 10-ft wall with 30 Integrity); bind one target within 60 ft that fails a Strength save (restrained until the next initiative 20); or fire a 6d10 gauntlet blast (ranged CE attack, 120 ft; Dexterity save, half on success). You may change the Scheme each round.

20Doom Above All

Twice his proficiency in Schemes, the first each round one CE cheaper, two Doombots at once — and once per day, a plausible preparation declared retroactively against a revealed threat. The filing system finally admits it is prophecy. You prepare twice your proficiency bonus in Schemes; the first Scheme you use each round costs 1 less CE. You may maintain two Doombots. Once per long rest, you may declare a plausible preparation against a revealed threat and retroactively fill one unused Scheme slot.

Pinnacle expression

Maximum, Domain, or equivalent.

Maximum — Power Cosmic Theft Protocol (12 CE): after a creature within 30 ft expends a Maximum/ultimate, reaction contest (see Rules — Shared Resolutions). Success stores an Echo until long rest; use once at half original dice/effect scale for 8 CE. Never copies Domains, identities, permanent passives, or reality-author privileges.

Feats

Build-defining options.

Diplomatic Immunity

The rank is the technique. Invoke Latveria formally and ten minutes of advantage on Persuasion and Intimidation follow — sovereignty, worn like armor. Doom's sovereignty is itself a technique. When you formally invoke your rank, title, or the authority of Latveria, you have advantage on Charisma (Persuasion) and Charisma (Intimidation) checks for the next 10 minutes, and creatures that recognize your authority cannot willingly target you with hostile technique effects until you or an ally acts against them. This immunity is void against beings that do not recognize mortal titles — Domains, Calamity-grade horrors, and the genuinely insane.

Hidden Doombot

Once per long rest, the 'you' just killed was a Doombot all along — provided one was plausibly deployed. The sovereign's death is always a draft. Once per long rest, when you would be reduced to 0 HP, you may reveal that the 'you' just defeated was a Doombot all along — provided it is logically possible: you must have had a Doombot deployed this combat or an unspent Scheme slot, and no effect may have explicitly prevented the swap. You instead appear unharmed at a location of your choice within 60 feet, leaving the Doombot behind as sparking wreckage. This revelation costs no action; it was planned for long before the battle began.

Mystic Armor

The armor learns: succeed on a save and one CE as a reaction siphons the residue into a Siphon token. Survival, filed as income. when you succeed on a save against a technique while wearing Doom Armor, you may spend 1 CE as a reaction to siphon its residue, gaining a Siphon token as if from Power Siphon.

DOOM Does Not Beg

Once per short rest, a failed Wisdom or Charisma save becomes intelligence: he learns the source's technique and a named feature, and gains advantage on the saves against it. He does not endure attacks. He catalogues them. once per short rest, when you fail a Wisdom or Charisma save, you may use your reaction to file the attack as intelligence: you learn the source's technique and one named feature it used, and you gain advantage on saves against that source for 1 minute.

Rules integration

Edge cases and shared systems.

Codex rulings: Direct rolled technique/weapon attacks can use the campaign's Black Flash procedure when otherwise eligible. Passive damage, hazards, summons acting independently, and initiative-20 Domain events cannot. Generic Maximum Output applies only to ordinary features, never named Maxima or Domains.
