Core law

What the technique actually does.

Core Engine — Preparation Slots. After long rest prepare PB Schemes: a spell matrix, armor module, Doombot protocol, trap, countermeasure, or stolen-energy contingency from the codex list. Doom wins by having the right answer, not one infinite spell list.

Full progression

Level 1 through 20.

AC 16 + Dex max 2, integrated gauntlet 60 ft 2d8 force, environmental seal.

3Arcane Bolt

1 CE, 60 ft, 2d10 force/necrotic. Prepare Counterspell Scheme: reaction technique contest for 2 CE.

6Doombot

3 CE, summon one 1-HP decoy for 1 minute. It can move, speak, gauntlet attack for 2d8, and impersonate you; one at a time.

9Latverian Arsenal

Choose two modules: flight 50, force shield reaction +3 AC, tractor beam, micro-missiles 15-ft 4d8, or mystic sensor.

11Power Siphon

Reaction 3 CE after succeeding save vs technique: gain Siphon token. Spend token within minute to add 3d10 matching damage or +2 to technique contest.

13Master of Science & Sorcery

Maintain one technological and one magical Focus effect simultaneously.

15Time Platform

6 CE, once/long rest: rewind only yourself to position/HP at start previous turn; resources do not return.

18Domain — Castle Doom

Initiative 20 choose one prepared Scheme; the Domain executes its defensive version autonomously: suppress projectile lane, deploy cover, bind one failed-Str target, or fire 6d10 gauntlet blast Dex save. Change Scheme each round.

20Doom Above All

Prepare twice PB Schemes; first Scheme each round costs 1 less CE. Maintain two Doombots. Once/long rest declare a plausible preparation against a revealed threat and retroactively fill one unused Scheme slot.

Pinnacle expression

Maximum, Domain, or equivalent.

Maximum — Power Cosmic Theft Protocol (12 CE): after a creature within 30 ft expends a Maximum/ultimate, reaction contest. Success stores an Echo until long rest; use once at half original dice/effect scale for 8 CE. Never copies Domains, identities, permanent passives, or reality-author privileges.

Feats

Build-defining options.

Diplomatic Immunity

Doom's sovereignty is itself a technique. When you formally invoke your rank, title, or the authority of Latveria, you have advantage on Charisma (Persuasion) and Charisma (Intimidation) checks for the next 10 minutes, and creatures that recognize your authority cannot willingly target you with hostile technique effects until you or an ally acts against them. This immunity is void against beings that do not recognize mortal titles — Domains, Calamity-grade horrors, and the genuinely insane.

Hidden Doombot

Once per long rest, when you would be reduced to 0 HP, you may reveal that the 'you' just defeated was a Doombot all along — provided it is logically possible: you must have had a Doombot deployed this combat or an unspent Scheme slot, and no effect may have explicitly prevented the swap. You instead appear unharmed at a location of your choice within 60 feet, leaving the Doombot behind as sparking wreckage. This revelation costs no action; it was planned for long before the battle began.

Mystic Armor

when you succeed on a save against a technique while wearing Doom Armor, you may spend 1 CE as a reaction to siphon its residue, gaining a Siphon token as if from Power Siphon.

DOOM Does Not Beg

once per short rest, when you fail a Wisdom or Charisma save, you may use your reaction to file the attack as intelligence: you learn the source's technique and one named feature it used, and you gain advantage on saves against that source for 1 minute.

Rules integration

Edge cases and shared systems.

Codex rulings: Direct rolled technique/weapon attacks can use the campaign's Black Flash procedure when otherwise eligible. Passive damage, hazards, summons acting independently, and initiative-20 Domain events cannot. Generic Maximum Output applies only to ordinary features, never named Maxima or Domains.
