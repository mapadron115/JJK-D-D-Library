King of Rock

An instrument that once belonged to a world-wide famous musician, the crowd's roar still lives in the strings.

Field notes. Subject: the roar still in the strings. Three truths govern it. First, the crowd's memory is rentable: one coin of cursed energy buys an hour of legend, +3 to every Charisma (Performance) check played upon the instrument. Second, in the right hands it ceases to be an instrument and becomes a focus: a sorcerer attuned to it whose innate technique is music gains +2 to the technique's save DC and attack rolls. Third, the roar chooses its vessel: attunement is required, a one-hour ritual to borrow a legend's voice. Hence the maxim: play to the crowd, and the crowd fights with you.

Properties & techniques

Exact player-facing procedures

Legendary Performance

Free · Spend 1 CE · +3 bonus to Charisma (Performance) checks made with this instrument for 1 hour.

One coin of cursed energy, and for an hour the instrument remembers its glory days. You can spend 1 CE to gain a +3 bonus to Charisma (Performance) checks to perform with this instrument for 1 hour.

Muse's Blessing

Passive · While attuned, a creature whose innate technique relates to music gains +2 to that technique's CE save DC and CE attack rolls.

But in the hands of a sorcerer whose technique is music, it stops being an instrument and becomes a focus. If attuned to by a creature with an Innate Technique relating to music, they gain a +2 bonus to their Cursed Energy save DC and Cursed Energy attack rolls relating to their Innate Technique.

Rules integration

Edge cases and shared systems.

Campaign canon notes

Cursed-tool attunement: you have PB attunement slots (2–6). This tool occupies one slot and requires a one-hour ritual. Attunement ends voluntarily over a short rest, on death, or when you replace it while over capacity. Distance does not break attunement. Cursed-tool attunement is separate from magic-item attunement.
Note: Muse's Blessing only applies to technique features tied to the music-related Innate Technique, not to general CE attacks or other techniques. The Performance bonus applies to the instrument itself, whichever kind it takes.
