Field entry

Journal Entry field report, converted to a table-ready stat block

Field notes. Subject: the fox. Weak in every raw measure, and the cleverest Grade 3 in the ward’s files, with the intellect to use it. It passes through walls and floors like water (incorporeal movement; 1d10 force only if it ends a turn inside solid matter), hides as a bonus action with advantage, and prefers to be a rumor rather than a fight. Its weapon is patience: stalk a creature for twenty-four hours and the victim must make a Wisdom save or gain a level of exhaustion that no rest can lift while the haunting continues. Mark this for the pursuit teams, chasing it through walls is exactly what it wants. It understands fear, line of sight, and exhaustion. Hunt it with a plan, or not at all.

Field rule — low-grade perception: Per the C&C field rules, Grade 3 and lower curses are invisible to creatures without cursed energy. A creature in a life-or-death situation at less than half its hit point maximum may temporarily or permanently gain the ability to see them. This spirit carries the Invisibility trait below.
