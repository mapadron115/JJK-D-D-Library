Feature map

1Flow of the Dragon.

At 1st level you have formed a powerful connection between you and the earth that keeps you grounded. While your feet are touching the ground, you add your proficiency bonus to melee damage rolls and have advantage on ability checks and saving throws against being forcefully moved.

3Ley Line Command.

At 3rd level, as a bonus action for 3 CE while your feet are touching the ground, you form a Ley Line connection for 1 minute. You gain the following benefits:

  • You are immune to magical effects that would forcefully move or teleport you.
  • You reduce all damage taken by your proficiency bonus.
  • As a reaction to taking damage, you can spend 1 CE reinforce your body with cursed energy and gain resistance to the triggering damage type until the beginning of your next turn.

You lose these benefits while your feet are not touching the ground. At 11th level, you are immune to being grappled, restrained, or knocked prone while your Ley Line is active.

6Earth Dragon Soul.

At 6th level, you have learned to greater siphon the earth's might. Once per turn when you hit with a melee attack while your Ley Line is active, you deal an additional 1d8 damage. This increases to 2d8 at 11th level and 3d8 at 18th level. If you have not moved since the start of your last turn, you can do this extra damage twice per turn and your speed is reduced to 0 until the start of your next turn. Additionally, when you gain resistance to a damage type with your Ley Line as a reaction, you can instead spend 3 CE to make the resistance last until the Ley Line ends.

11World Anchor.

At 11th level, you gain temporary hit points equal to your proficiency bonus + your Cursed Energy modifier at the beginning of your turn while your feet are touching the ground. When you start your turn at half your hit point maximum or less while your Ley Line is active, you can spend 3 CE to gain resistance to all damage until the start of your next turn.

18Domain Expansion: Of Primordial World.

Finally at 18th level, you have learned to expand your domain. Creatures within your domain cannot teleport or fly more than 10 ft off the ground, otherwise they find themselves getting crushed into the ground and take 5d10 force damage. A creature can take this damage once per turn. Additionally, you are always considered under Ley Line.

Pinnacle expression

Maximum, Domain, or equivalent.

Maximum: Dragon Pulse: prerequisite: must have World Anchor. As a Full Turn Action (Action + Bonus Action + Movement) for 8 CE, you stomp your foot forcing all creatures within 120 ft that are touching the ground or at least 30 ft above the ground to make a Strength saving throw as you cause a massive shockwave that shatters the earth around you. On a failed save, a creature takes 10d10 bludgeoning damage and is knocked prone and stunned until the end of your next turn. On a successful save, a creature takes half as much damage and is not stunned. Objects and structures automatically fail this saving throw and take maximum damage. For every creature that fails this save, you gain 5 temporary hit points.

Domain Expansion: Of Primordial World: 18 — creatures within your domain cannot teleport or fly more than 10 ft off the ground, otherwise they find themselves getting crushed into the ground and take 5d10 force damage. A creature can take this damage once per turn. Additionally, you are always considered under Ley Line.

Innate Technique Feats

Build-defining options.

These are optional character-building feats. You must meet each listed prerequisite before selecting it.

Prerequisite: Must have Ley Line Command

Ley Line Extension

You have learned to extend the benefits of your connection with the earth to others. When you create a Ley Line you can give a number of creatures equal to your Cursed Energy modifier the benefits of Flow of the Dragon while they are within 30 ft and touching the same ground as you.

Prerequisite: Must have Earth Dragon Soul

Ground Control

As an action for 3 CE, you stomp your foot and transform the ground within 30 ft in one of the following ways:

  • The ground becomes difficult terrain for all creatures other than yourself.
  • All creatures touching the ground other than you must make a Dexterity saving throw. On a failed save they are knocked prone.
  • You grant creatures of your choice within range three quarters cover until the end of your next turn with earthen walls.
  • You move one creature touching the ground with a wave of earth up to 20 ft horizontally to a point within range. An unwilling creature must make a Dexterity saving throw to avoid the effect.
Rules integration

Edge cases and shared systems.

Grounded requirement: "feet touching the ground" is literal. Vehicle floors and carried platforms are not automatically the earth; a natural surface or a structure physically connected to the earth qualifies, including a building floor. Suspending contact suspends the benefits but does not reset the 1-minute Ley Line duration.
Ley Line resistance timing: the reaction applies before the triggering damage and lasts through the start of your next turn; at 6th level you may instead spend 3 CE to extend it until the Ley Line ends.
Stationary double damage: Earth Dragon Soul's stationary bonus may apply twice in a turn if you have not moved since the start of your prior turn; using the second locks your speed at 0 through your next turn. Teleportation or forced displacement breaks stationary eligibility even if your voluntary speed was 0.
Damage reduction: the proficiency-bonus reduction applies once per resolved damage event, before resistance; it does not stack per damage type. Earth Dragon Soul's damage type matches the triggering weapon or unarmed strike.
World Anchor: the temporary hit points replace, not stack. The 3 CE low-hit-point defense is paid before the relevant damage.
Domain law: the Domain blocks attempted teleportation and forces flyers down to legal ground, dealing 5d10 force damage once per turn; anti-domain protection suppresses enforcement, not ordinary terrain. Being always under Ley Line waives activation but not the feet-on-ground condition.
Ley Line Extension: shares only Flow of the Dragon with up to your CE modifier in creatures within 30 ft touching the same ground — not the entire Ley Line.
Ground Control: the chosen terrain lasts 1 minute (campaign ruling).
Dragon Pulse: the 5 temporary hit points per failed save form one summed pool. The source's "touching the ground or at least 30 ft above the ground" is preserved literally, leaving a 10–29 ft band — a flagged oddity, not a silent correction.
