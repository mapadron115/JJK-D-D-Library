Completed rules

  • Forbidden Zone: use a full action and 1 CE to create a fixed 30-foot-radius sphere centered on your space. It lasts 1 minute using Technique Focus. A creature you regard as an ally has advantage on its d20 rolls only while inside. Changing allegiance after activation does not change who qualifies.
  • Bless: as an action for 3 CE, choose one willing creature within 60 feet. For up to 1 minute using Technique Focus, it has the Zone’s advantage while on the same plane. Because both use Focus, you cannot sustain Bless and a separate Zone simultaneously.
  • Arena: each allied damage roll made inside adds CE modifier (minimum 0). When a hostile creature attempts to cross the Zone boundary in either direction, it makes a Charisma save. On failure, 5 feet of movement is spent and it remains on the original side. Forced movement makes the same save; on failure it stops at the boundary. One creature saves at most once per turn.
  • Maximize Output: when the Zone begins, choose one allied creature inside. The first time on each of its turns that it rolls damage for a CE attack or CE save effect, it may treat that feature’s printed base damage dice as maximum. Critical, Black Flash, item, and other added dice are still rolled. Change the chosen ally as a bonus action.
  • Hallowed Zone: every ally inside gains all Zone benefits. At initiative 20, choose up to PB willing allies inside; each chosen ally designates one hostile in the Zone, and a hallowed afterimage automatically deals PBd10 + your CE modifier force or radiant damage to that target. A protected target makes a Dexterity save for half. Several allies may designate the same target. All ordinary allied attacks and saving-throw features resolve normally.

Power identity

Why Tier III: it has almost no solo offense, but ordinary party play multiplies its advantage, damage, and output across several characters—a direct encounter-design pressure.

Recovered character-build options

Innate Technique Feats 3

These are optional character-building feats. You must meet each listed prerequisite before selecting it.

Blessing of Resilience

High impact

Prerequisite: Bless

When Zone is established, allies inside gain 5 temporary HP; re-entering does not refresh it. While inside, once per turn each ally adds PB to one damage roll. ends immediately on leaving.

Blessing of Combat

High impact

Prerequisite: Arena

Once per turn while inside your Zone, each ally may add 1d4 to one attack or damage roll after rolling but before outcome. The same roll cannot receive both this die and another Zone die.

Maximum: Shrine of the Gods

Pinnacle

Prerequisite: Arena, Bless, and CE score 20

Corrected prerequisite replaces Self-Embodiment of Perfection. Action, 10 CE: roll d10 and manifest Zone for that many rounds without its normal continuing action or Technique Focus requirement. Roll once; the duration cannot be refreshed or extended.

Cursed Energy
Beginning at 1st level, you can harness your negative feelings into
a usable source of energy, called cursed energy. These feelings can
be harnessed into attacks or abilities. The number of cursed energy
you possess is based on your class, some classes use a modifier that
being charisma or wisdom (Player’s choice)
Your cursed energy DC is 8 + user cursed energy ability modifier +
proficiency bonus.
Forbidden Zone
Starting at 1st level, through chanting, and dancing you bring out
the maximum potential of those around you. As a full action (Action +
Bonus Action + Movement) for 1 CE, you create a 30ft sphere. All
allies within the Sphere gain advantage on all their rolls.
Bless
At 3rd level, you gain better control over your zone further,
allowing you to focus it on a single person. For 3 CE you pick a
creature, that creature is under the effects of your Zone for up to a
minute. You may either dismiss this as an action, or you can fail a
concentration after being hit.
Arena
Beginning at 6th level, all allied creatures in your Zone deal
additional damage equal to your Cursed Energy Modifier. In addition,
you may choose to not allow hostile creatures to enter or leave your
Zone.
Maximize Output
Starting at 11th level, after setting up your Zone, you can choose
to maximize an ally's output. When a creature does a Cursed Energy
attack, they deal maximum damage instead of rolling.
Domain Expansion: Hallowed Zone
At 18th level, you gain the peak of your technique. All allies in
your domain are under your Zone, and their attacks become sure hits,
as if they were the ones who cast the domain.
