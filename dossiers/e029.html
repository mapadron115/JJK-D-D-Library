Solo Forbidden Area

A ritual support zone granting sweeping advantage, extra damage, border control, maximum output, and a party-shaped hallowed pulse.

Core law

Completed rules

Forbidden Zone:

use a full action and 1 CE to create a fixed 30-foot-radius sphere centered on your space. It lasts 1 minute using Technique Focus. A creature you regard as an ally has advantage on its d20 rolls only while inside. Changing allegiance after activation does not change who qualifies.

Bless:

as an action for 3 CE, choose one willing creature within 60 feet. For up to 1 minute using Technique Focus, it has the Zone’s advantage while on the same plane. Because both use Focus, you cannot sustain Bless and a separate Zone simultaneously.

Arena:

each allied damage roll made inside adds CE modifier (minimum 0). When a hostile creature attempts to cross the Zone boundary in either direction, it makes a Charisma save. On failure, 5 feet of movement is spent and it remains on the original side. Forced movement makes the same save; on failure it stops at the boundary. One creature saves at most once per turn.

Maximize Output:

when the Zone begins, choose one allied creature inside. The first time on each of its turns that it rolls damage for a CE attack or CE save effect, it may treat that feature’s printed base damage dice as maximum. Critical, Black Flash, item, and other added dice are still rolled. Change the chosen ally as a bonus action.

Pinnacle expression

Maximum, Domain, or equivalent.

Hallowed Zone: 18 — Hallowed Zone. every ally inside gains all Zone benefits. At initiative 20, choose up to PB willing allies inside; each chosen ally designates one hostile in the Zone, and a hallowed afterimage automatically deals PBd10 + your CE modifier force or radiant damage to that target. A protected target makes a Dexterity save for half. Several allies may designate the same target. All ordinary allied attacks and saving-throw features resolve normally.

Innate Technique Feats

Build-defining options.

These are optional character-building feats. You must meet each listed prerequisite before selecting it.

Prerequisite: Bless

Blessing Relay

Action, 3 CE while your Zone is active: designate one willing creature inside it as a relay for up to 1 minute, using your Technique Focus alongside the Zone. Allies you regard as such within 30 feet of the relay gain the Zone's advantage as if standing inside it. The relay itself gains no benefit while relaying.

Prerequisite: Arena

Blessing of Combat

Once per turn when an ally inside your Zone hits a hostile creature with an attack, they may push that hostile up to 10 feet as part of the hit. If the push carries it across the Zone boundary, it makes the Arena crossing Charisma save as normal.

Prerequisite: Arena, Bless, and CE score 20

Maximum: Shrine of the Gods

Action, 10 CE: establish your Zone centered on yourself for 1 minute using Technique Focus. It moves with you, applying the Arena boundary and ally benefits from its current position. You cannot sustain Bless separately while it moves.

Rules integration

Edge cases and shared systems.

Rules priority: the reviewed rulings, explicit codex clarifications, and character reference above always control when original wording conflicts. Legacy Domain auto-hit language, action terminology, event timing, missing saves, and older scaling never override revised player rules.
Why Tier III: it has almost no solo offense, but ordinary party play multiplies its advantage, damage, and output across several characters—a direct encounter-design pressure.

Additional material.

Cursed Energy
Beginning at 1st level, you can harness your negative feelings into
a usable source of energy, called cursed energy. These feelings can
be harnessed into attacks or abilities. The number of cursed energy
you possess is based on your class, some classes use a modifier that
being charisma or wisdom (Player’s choice)
Your cursed energy DC is 8 + user cursed energy ability modifier +
proficiency bonus.
Forbidden Zone
Starting at 1st level, through chanting, and dancing you bring out
the maximum potential of those around you. As a full action (Action +
Bonus Action + Movement) for 1 CE, you create a 30ft sphere. All
allies within the Sphere gain advantage on all their rolls.
Bless
At 3rd level, you gain better control over your zone further,
allowing you to focus it on a single person. For 3 CE you pick a
creature, that creature is under the effects of your Zone for up to a
minute. You may either dismiss this as an action, or you can fail a
concentration after being hit.
Arena
Beginning at 6th level, all allied creatures in your Zone deal
additional damage equal to your Cursed Energy Modifier. In addition,
you may choose to not allow hostile creatures to enter or leave your
Zone.
Maximize Output
Starting at 11th level, after setting up your Zone, you can choose
to maximize an ally's output. When a creature does a Cursed Energy
attack, they deal maximum damage instead of rolling.
Domain Expansion: Hallowed Zone
At 18th level, you gain the peak of your technique. All allies in
your domain are under your Zone, and their attacks become sure hits,
as if they were the ones who cast the domain.
