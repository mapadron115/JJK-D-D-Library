Solo Forbidden Area

A ritual support zone granting sweeping advantage, extra damage, border control, maximum output, and a party-shaped hallowed pulse.

Field notes. Subject: the warden who draws a line and keeps it. Three truths govern him. First: the sanctuary is fixed, a full action and a coin raise a thirty-foot sphere of law for a minute, and every ally inside rolls with advantage; the guest list is written at activation and never amended. Second: the threshold is defended, a hostile crossing the boundary saves Charisma or spends five feet of movement to remain where it was; forced movement is stopped at the line the same way, one save per creature per turn. Third: the Domain is the sanctuary perfected, a law, not an event, with no initiative-twenty pulse; every ally inside stands under the Ritual Zone, and their techniques become sure hits as if each had cast the Domain themselves. Assess him correctly: he has almost no offense of his own, his party is the weapon, and the Zone is the whetstone.

Rules integration

Edge cases and shared systems.

Rules priority: the reviewed rulings, explicit codex clarifications, and the level progression always control when original wording conflicts. Legacy Domain auto-hit language, action terminology, event timing, missing saves, and older scaling never override revised player rules.
Why Tier III: it has almost no solo offense, but ordinary party play multiplies its advantage, damage, and output across several characters—a direct encounter-design pressure.

Cursed Energy
Beginning at 1st level, you can harness your negative feelings into
a usable source of energy, called cursed energy. These feelings can
be harnessed into attacks or abilities. The number of cursed energy
you possess is based on your class, some classes use a modifier that
being charisma or wisdom (Player’s choice)
Your cursed energy DC is 8 + user cursed energy ability modifier +
proficiency bonus.
Forbidden Zone
Starting at 1st level, through chanting, and dancing you bring out
the maximum potential of those around you. As a full action (Action +
Bonus Action + Movement) for 1 CE, you create a 30ft sphere. All
allies within the Sphere gain advantage on all their rolls.
Bless
At 3rd level, you gain better control over your zone further,
allowing you to focus it on a single person. For 3 CE you pick a
creature, that creature is under the effects of your Zone for up to a
minute. You may either dismiss this as an action, or you can fail a
concentration after being hit.
Arena
Beginning at 6th level, all allied creatures in your Zone deal
additional damage equal to your Cursed Energy Modifier. In addition,
you may choose to not allow hostile creatures to enter or leave your
Zone.
Maximize Output
Starting at 11th level, after setting up your Zone, you can choose
to maximize an ally's output. When a creature does a Cursed Energy
attack, they deal maximum damage instead of rolling.
Domain Expansion: Hallowed Zone
At 18th level, you gain the peak of your technique. All allies in
your domain are under your Zone, and their attacks become sure hits,
as if they were the ones who cast the domain.
