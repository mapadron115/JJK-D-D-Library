Features & procedures

Exact player-facing procedures.

Weapon Equip • Level 5

Magnetic Blade Ring.

Razor-sharp blades manifest around the vessel, steered magnetically. The admiral's first muster: the fleet is small, but it already answers to signals.

Weapon Equip creates razor-sharp Vepar blades and lets the user steer them magnetically.

On hit • 2 Reserve

Polarity Cut

On a hit, the blade carries the fleet's doctrine, extra slashing lightning, and a metal-bearing target shoved or dragged thirty feet on a failed save. Magnetism's basic order: come here, or go there.

Add 3 Djinn Dice slashing/lightning and push or pull a metal-bearing target 30 ft on a failed Strength save.

Bonus Action • 3 Reserve

Sword Orbit

For one minute, a guard of floating blades attends him, each spendable as three measures of armor against one attack, or two Djinn Dice added to one weapon hit. The escort squadron: defense and offense, interchangeable, as all good naval assets are.

Create PB floating blades for 1 minute. Spend a blade to gain +3 AC against one attack or add 2 Djinn Dice to one weapon hit.

Action • 5 Reserve

Vepar Istella

Hundreds of swords rain into a hundred-and-twenty-foot point, the failed left bleeding into their next turn. The thousand-sword shower: the fleet's first broadside, and broadside is the honest word for it.

Hundreds of swords rain into a 120-ft-radius point within 300 ft. Dexterity save; 10 Djinn Dice slashing/lightning, half on success. Failed targets also bleed for 2 Djinn Dice at the start of their next turn.

Full Djinn Equip • Level 9

He takes the sky with his navy: flight, command over unattended metal within one hundred and eighty feet, magnetic attacks that ignore cover, and a replacement blade summoned free each turn, up to his full measure. The admiral's logistics: the fleet replenishes itself mid-battle. Losses are a paperwork problem.

Fly 120 ft; manipulate unattended metal within 180 ft; magnetic sword attacks ignore half and three-quarters cover. Once per turn summon one replacement Sword Orbit blade for free, up to PB.

14 Reserve • Codex Extreme

Sea of Ten Thousand Blades

The sky fills for a full minute, two hundred and fifty feet of magnetized swords, an opening volley and then a second wave at initiative twenty. Battlefield as sword storm; canon's hundreds of blades expanded to Extreme scale. The navy, holding formation: this is not an attack. It is a blockade of the air itself.

A 250-ft-radius sky fills with magnetized swords for 1 minute. On activation creatures save Dexterity against 16 Djinn Dice slashing/lightning, half on success. At initiative count 20 on the next round, surviving enemies in the zone save again against 6 Djinn Dice slashing/lightning, half on success, as the second wave falls.

Anime-scale: Strategic scale: turn an entire battlefield into a sword storm. Canon shows hundreds of blades cutting through high-tier guardians; this expands that language to Extreme scale.

Field notes. Subject: the admiral of a flying navy. Three truths govern this fleet. First: the soul sealed in this vessel was a fleet-commander in life, and its barrier-self still treats every blade as a ship, magnetism its discipline, lightning its voice. Second: the djinn never learned to give an order to a single soldier; ten thousand swords fly as one because that is the only scale it understands. Third: the Sea of Ten Thousand Blades is not an attack but a navy holding formation, the sky, administered as a fleet. Mark the maxim: other sorcerers throw blades. This one musters them.

Rules integration

Djinn Die, Equip & Extreme Magic

Damage-type chooser: when a Djinn ability lists two damage types, the Djinn King chooses which applies when the damage is dealt.
Independent Djinn Reserve: 16 (Rank A). Refreshes on a long rest. Every Djinn cost below, Weapon Equip, Full Equip, abilities, reactions, Extreme Magic, spends this Djinn’s Reserve, never your CE. If this Djinn’s Reserve is empty it simply cannot fuel more until you rest; your other Djinn keep their own separate Reserves.
Weapon Equip · level 5: bonus action, 2 Reserve, lasts 10 minutes.
Full Djinn Equip · level 9: bonus action, 4 Reserve, lasts 1 minute. Only one Equip can be active.
Deep Synchronization · level 11: once per short rest, one Equip activation costs 1 Reserve less.
Extreme Magic · level 13: action; requires that Djinn’s Full Equip; pay the listed Reserve cost from this Djinn’s Reserve; usable once per long rest per Djinn. Full Equip ends afterward, and you cannot Equip that Djinn again until you finish a short rest.
King Vessel · level 17: switch directly from one Full Equip to another as a bonus action, paying the new Equip cost. At level 20, once per long rest, immediately transition from one Djinn’s Extreme into another Djinn’s Full Equip.
Attack / save: use your CE attack modifier and CE save DC unless this Djinn explicitly says otherwise.
