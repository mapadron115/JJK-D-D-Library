DJINN 42 • Lightning / Magnetism

Vepar — Sea of Magnetic Swords

Rank A
Canon master: SinbadBasis: Canon core • Vepar Istella (Thousand Sword Shower); broader kit extrapolated

Weapon Equip • Level 5

Magnetic Blade Ring. Weapon Equip creates razor-sharp Vepar blades and lets the user steer them magnetically.

Polarity Cut

On hit • 2 CE

Add 3 Djinn Dice slashing/lightning and push or pull a metal-bearing target 30 ft on a failed Strength save.

Sword Orbit

Bonus Action • 3 CE

Create PB floating blades for 1 minute. Spend a blade to gain +3 AC against one attack or add 2 Djinn Dice to one weapon hit.

Vepar Istella

Action • 5 CE

Hundreds of swords rain into a 120-ft-radius point within 300 ft. Dexterity save; 10 Djinn Dice slashing/lightning, half on success. Failed targets also bleed for 2 Djinn Dice at the start of their next turn.

Full Djinn Equip • Level 9

Fly 120 ft; manipulate unattended metal within 180 ft; magnetic sword attacks ignore half and three-quarters cover. Once per turn summon one replacement Sword Orbit blade for free, up to PB.

EXTREME MAGIC • LEVEL 13+

Sea of Ten Thousand Blades

14 CE • Codex Extreme

A 250-ft-radius sky fills with magnetized swords for 1 minute. On activation creatures save Dexterity against 16 Djinn Dice slashing/lightning, half on success. At initiative count 20 on the next round, surviving enemies in the zone save again against 6 Djinn Dice as the second wave falls.

Anime-scale: Strategic scale: turn an entire battlefield into a sword storm. Canon shows hundreds of blades cutting through high-tier guardians; this expands that language to Extreme scale.
