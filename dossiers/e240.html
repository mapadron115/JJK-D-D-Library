Impact Wave

Feature

Spend Mass on a weapon hit to push 5 ft each; collision with solid obstacle adds 1 Tool Die once/turn.

Anchor

bonus, 1 CE: spend 1 Mass to become immune to forced movement until next turn, speed -10.

Feature

Rules integration

Cursed-tool attunement

Campaign canon

Cursed-tool attunement: you have PB attunement slots (2–6). This tool occupies one slot and requires a one-hour ritual. Attunement ends voluntarily over a short rest, on death, or when you replace it while over capacity. Distance does not break attunement. Cursed-tool attunement is separate from magic-item attunement.
Balance & counterplay. Designed to play with terrain without recursive push-resource loops.
