Field notes. Subject: a miser of force. Four truths govern this hammer. First, force is stored, never spent freely: every shove, throw, and displacement becomes Mass, to a maximum of PB, kept until combat ends. Second, stored force is returned with interest: Mass spent on a hit pushes 5 ft per point, and a collision with solid ground adds a Tool Die once per turn, terrain becomes the second weapon. Third, the same instinct turns inward: Anchor spends 1 Mass for immunity to forced movement until your next turn, at the cost of ten feet of speed, force remembered becomes force refused. Fourth, the ledger balances: no forced-movement loop may mint more than 1 Mass per turn. Mark this: the hammer does not create force. It hoards it.

Rules integration

Cursed-tool attunement

Campaign canon

Cursed-tool attunement: you have PB attunement slots (2–6). This tool occupies one slot and requires a one-hour ritual. Attunement ends voluntarily over a short rest, on death, or when you replace it while over capacity. Distance does not break attunement. Cursed-tool attunement is separate from magic-item attunement.
Balance & counterplay. Designed to play with terrain without recursive push-resource loops.
