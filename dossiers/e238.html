Features & procedures

Properties & techniques

Exact player-facing procedures

Recall Line

returning axe passes in straight line; one creature in line Dex save or 1 Tool Die slashing, once/round.

Permafrost

2 CE on hit: add Frostmark and 1 Tool Die cold; at 2 marks target speed -10.

Shatter Mark

melee hit consumes 2 marks for +2 Tool Dice cold and prone on Str fail.

Feature

The recall cannot hit the same creature both outbound and inbound in same attack sequence.

Rules integration

Cursed-tool attunement

Campaign canon

Cursed-tool attunement: you have PB attunement slots (2–6). This tool occupies one slot and requires a one-hour ritual. Attunement ends voluntarily over a short rest, on death, or when you replace it while over capacity. Distance does not break attunement. Cursed-tool attunement is separate from magic-item attunement.
Balance & counterplay. A compact Grade 1 weapon with a satisfying throw/recall loop rather than a full second technique.
