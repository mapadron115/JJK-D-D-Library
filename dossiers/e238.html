Recall Line

2 CE on hit: add Frostmark and 1 Tool Die cold; at 2 marks the target’s speed is reduced by 10 while both marks persist (invented).

Feature

Passive · The recall cannot hit the same creature both outbound and inbound in the same attack sequence.

The recall cannot hit the same creature both outbound and inbound in same attack sequence.

Field notes. Subject: winter that refuses the throw. Three truths govern this axe. First, the return is the technique: thrown 30/90, it comes back on a bonus action, and the Recall Line turns the return path itself into a weapon, one creature in the line faces a Dexterity save or 1 Tool Die of slashing, once per round. Second, the cold is the tax: hits lay Frostmarks, two per target at most, and Permafrost spends 2 CE to add a mark and cold, at two marks the target's speed bleeds away by ten. Third, the shatter is the purpose: a melee hit may consume both marks for +2 Tool Dice cold and prone on a failed Strength save. Mark this: the axe does not forgive distance, and winter is only ever distance made permanent.

Rules integration

Cursed-tool attunement

Campaign canon

Cursed-tool attunement: you have PB attunement slots (2–6). This tool occupies one slot and requires a one-hour ritual. Attunement ends voluntarily over a short rest, on death, or when you replace it while over capacity. Distance does not break attunement. Cursed-tool attunement is separate from magic-item attunement.
Balance & counterplay. A compact Grade 1 weapon with a satisfying throw/recall loop rather than a full second technique.
