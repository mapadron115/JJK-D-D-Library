The Tickle Doctrine

The file contains a warning, entered by three separate researchers, in three separate hands, all saying the same thing: do not let it touch you when you are sad. It will help. That is the problem — in the field, help is a weapon, and joy is the one nobody armors against.

Combat roleController
Core law

Euphoria

Tickle.

Action, 2 CE, 30 ft: Wisdom save vs your CE DC or be overcome with joy — incapacitated until the end of its next turn, then disadvantage on attacks and checks for 1 minute (lingering giggles). Success: disadvantage for 1 round.

Lingering Giggles.

The disadvantage persists after the incapacitation ends. Joy is sticky. The file has measured the stickiness at one minute.

Contagion.

At higher thresholds, joy spreads to onlookers. Laughter, the file notes, was always the most contagious thing in the building.

Rules integration

Table Rulings

Incapacitation from joy is not charm; charm immunity doesn’t apply, but creatures immune to incapacitation are unaffected.
Contagion spreads only the disadvantage, never the incapacitation.
Comfort cannot end conditions imposed by deities or artifacts (GM discretion).
Constructs and undead save with advantage — they don’t get the joke.

Subject file

Containment & observation record.

Containment Procedures

No sad staff on rotation. This is a formal rule, entered after Session 14, and it is enforced with unusual tenderness.

Exposure is limited to fifteen minutes per session. The subject does not understand limits. The subject understands play.

An orange residue is cleaned from the cell daily. The residue smells faintly of oranges. No one has identified the source. No one has tried very hard.

Research Log

Research log. 03/2025 — Acquisition: recovered after a ‘laughter incident’ at a care facility — thirty residents, zero injuries, one staff member who ‘hadn’t laughed like that in years’ and wept afterward. The subject was found in the common room, being petted.

Research log. 08/2025 — The euphoria is mapped: Wisdom save, incapacitation then lingering disadvantage, contagious at threshold. The weaponization proposal passed review in eleven minutes — the fastest approval in the file’s history, and the one the file’s author is least proud of.

Research log. 02/2026 — Comfort confirmed: the effect reverses cleanly — frightened and charmed conditions end on contact. The subject prefers this application. When deployed offensively, it goes still afterward, in a way the handlers describe as ‘disappointed.’

Research log. 10/2026 — Hysterics logged: a hostile cursed spirit failed its save by 9 and took psychic damage from laughing. The file classifies joy as a damage type with a straight face and moves on.

Incident Report — Session 14.

A grieving researcher entered the cell off-rotation, alone, in violation of every posted rule. Nine minutes of contact. She emerged improved — affect up, productivity doubled for a week. Her recommendation, entered in her own hand: do not repeat. Her reason, redacted at her request. The file respects the redaction. The file has never respected a redaction before, and the file would like that noted as the actual incident.
