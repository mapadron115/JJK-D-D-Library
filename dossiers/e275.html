Missile Fist

Materialize giant fists of cursed energy and replace your attacks with missile fists — ranged necrotic strikes that grapple, restrain, and crush at growing range.

Core law

At-the-table character reference

Recovered printed values & campaign clarifications

Maximum Technique: Brutal Bombardment · level 11:

Full-Turn Action (Action + Bonus Action + Movement) for 6 CE; one creature within 50 ft makes a Strength save. On failure it takes 3d8 + 3d8 necrotic damage for every attack you can make as an action and is knocked prone, plus 4d10 bludgeoning if it is a creature; half necrotic and no bludgeoning on success. Objects and structures take maximum damage. Splash: all creatures within 5 ft of the target (+5 ft per Missile Fist) save or take the wave's necrotic damage (half on success); objects auto-fail. Spend CE up to your CE modifier for +3d8 per CE, each counting as an additional Missile Fist for splash range.

Core law

Completed rules

Missile Impact:

as a bonus action for 1 CE, materialize giant fists of cursed energy. Until the end of your turn, you can replace any of your attacks with a Missile Fist, making a ranged Cursed Energy attack against a creature within 20 ft. A Missile Fist does 1d8 + your CE modifier necrotic damage on a hit. As part of the bonus action you use to activate this technique, you can make one additional Missile Fist attack. If you use Missile Fist against a creature within 5 ft, you can instead make a melee Cursed Energy attack that can use your Strength or Dexterity modifier for the attack and damage rolls. Damage rises to 2d8 at 6th level, 3d8 at 11th level, and 4d8 at 18th level.

Devastating Barrage:

when you use your Missile Fist, you can spend additional cursed energy up to your Cursed Energy modifier to make an additional Missile Fist attack per cursed energy spent. The range of your Missile Fists increases to 30 ft, increasing by 20 ft again at 6th, 11th, and 18th level (50 / 70 / 90 ft).

Grapple and restrain riders:

if you hit a creature that is Large or smaller with a Missile Fist, instead of dealing damage, you can choose to grapple it for 1 minute. If you hit a grappled creature that is Large or smaller with a Missile Fist, instead of dealing damage, you can choose to restrain it for 1 minute. A creature can make a Strength saving throw against your CE save DC at the end of their turn to escape your technique. On a success, they can remove one of the Missile Fists, removing either the Grappled or Restrained conditions. These conditions end if you become Incapacitated.

Unrelenting Fury:

as an action for 3 CE, you create a 30 ft cone of Missile Fists. All creatures within the cone must make a Strength saving throw. On a failed save, they take 2d8 necrotic damage for every attack you can make as an action and they are knocked prone. On a successful save, a creature takes half as much damage and is not knocked prone. You can spend your bonus action to increase the damage by 2d8. You can spend cursed energy up to your CE modifier to increase damage by 2d8 per CE.

Full progression

Feature map

1Missile Impact.

Starting at 1st level, you can spend 1 cursed energy as a bonus action to materialize giant fists of cursed energy. Until the end of your turn, you can replace any of your attacks with a Missile Fist, making a ranged Cursed Energy attack against a creature within 20 ft. A Missile Fist does 1d8 + your CE modifier necrotic damage on a hit. As part of the bonus action you use to activate this technique, you can make one additional Missile Fist attack. If you use Missile Fist against a creature within 5 ft, you can instead make a melee Cursed Energy attack that can use your Strength or Dexterity modifier for the attack and damage rolls. The damage of your Missile Fist increases to 2d8 at 6th level, 3d8 at 11th level, and 4d8 at 18th level.

3Devastating Barrage.

At 3rd level, when you use your Missile Fist, you can spend additional cursed energy up to your Cursed Energy modifier to make an additional Missile Fist attack per cursed energy spent. Additionally, the range of your Missile Fists increases to 30 ft. This range increases by 20 ft again at 6th, 11th, and 18th level. If you hit a creature that is Large or smaller with a Missile Fist, instead of dealing damage, you can choose to grapple it for 1 minute. If you hit a grappled creature that is Large or smaller with a Missile Fist, instead of dealing damage, you can choose to restrain it for 1 minute. A creature can make a Strength saving throw against your CE save DC at the end of their turn to escape your technique. On a success, they can remove one of the Missile Fists, removing either the Grappled or Restrained conditions. These conditions end if you become Incapacitated.

6Unrelenting Fury.

At 6th level, you have learned that a strong offense is the only thing that matters. As an action for 3 CE, you create a 30 ft cone of Missile Fists. All creatures within the cone must make a Strength saving throw. On a failed save, they take 2d8 necrotic damage for every attack you can make as an action and they are knocked prone. On a successful save, a creature takes half as much damage and is not knocked prone. You can spend your bonus action to increase the damage by 2d8. You can spend cursed energy up to your CE modifier to increase damage by 2d8 per CE.

11Maximum: Brutal Bombardment.

At 11th level, you can combine your Missile Fists into a larger, more brutal attack. As a Full-Turn Action Action (Action + Bonus Action + Movement) for 6 CE, you create a wave of Missile Fists. A creature within 50 ft must make a Strength saving throw. On a failed save, the target takes 3d8 + 3d8 necrotic damage for every attack you can make as an action (counting as an additional Missile Fist for range) and is knocked prone. If the target is a creature, the target takes an additional 4d10 bludgeoning damage from being crushed. On a successful save, a creature takes half as much necrotic damage and no bludgeoning damage. If the target is an object or structure, it instead takes maximum damage. All creatures within 5 ft of the target (+5 ft per Missile Fist) must make a Strength saving throw or similarly be crushed, taking the necrotic damage of the Missile Fist wave on a failed save or half as much damage on a successful save. Objects and structures automatically fail this save. You can spend cursed energy up to your CE modifier to increase the damage by 3d8 per CE spent, counting as an additional Missile Fist.

18Domain Expansion: Unrelenting Ring of Spirit.

At 18th level you have learned to expand your domain. At initiative 20, all creatures take 8d8 necrotic damage and are knocked prone by giant Missile Fists. As an action, you can target any creature of your choice within your domain with a single Missile Fist without spending cursed energy.

Innate Technique Feats

Build-defining options.

These are optional character-building feats. You must meet each listed prerequisite before selecting it.

Prerequisite: Must have Missile Impact

Exploding Fighting Spirit

You have learned to channel your passion for battle and fighting spirit into your cursed technique. When you hit a creature with Missile Fist, all creatures within 5 ft (other than you) take thunder damage equal to your CE modifier from the shockwaves. You can use this effect a number of times each turn equal to your proficiency bonus. When you hit a target with a Missile Fist, you gain a +1 bonus to attack rolls against that target. This bonus can stack and lasts until the end of your turn.

Prerequisite: Must have Devastating Barrage

Armored Missile Fist

As a bonus action for 2 CE, you cover your hands in Missile Fists, gaining the following benefits for 1 minute: ● You gain a +2 bonus to AC while holding no weapons or shields. ● You can use your CE modifier for attack and damage rolls using your unarmed strikes. ● Your unarmed strikes deal your Missile Fist damage. ● The range of your unarmed strikes becomes 10 ft.

Prerequisite: Must have Unrelenting Fury

Titan's Grasp

As a reaction for 3 CE, when you or a creature within 30 ft would take damage, you interpose a Missile Fist, reducing the damage by your Missile Fist damage. Roll the user's current Missile Fist damage dice (Xd8 + CE modifier at the user's level) and reduce the incoming damage by the result.

Rules integration

Edge cases and shared systems.

Rules priority: the reviewed rulings, explicit codex clarifications, and character reference above always control when original wording conflicts. Legacy Domain auto-hit language, action terminology, event timing, missing saves, and older scaling never override revised player rules.
Grapple/restrain choice: the grapple/restrain rider is a forgo-damage choice declared on a hit, before damage is rolled. Each successful end-of-turn Strength save removes exactly one Missile Fist (one condition); the creature chooses which condition is removed when both apply.
Attack counting: "per attack you can make as an action" counts the user's Attack-action attack count (Extra Attack etc.), not the Devastating Barrage bonus attacks.
Domain conversion: initiative-20 timing matches; clashes use the deterministic 1–1,000 point system; barrier statistics per D007; burnout per D010. Brutal Bombardment is the technique's Maximum under the adopted V.6 Maximum Output rules.
