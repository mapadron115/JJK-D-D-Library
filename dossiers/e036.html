Original Ritual Codex
Sorcerer with technique

Siphon

A close-range cursed technique that turns consecutive hits into stolen Cursed Energy, wagers that energy inside a merciless colosseum, and rewards the last combatant standing.

Combat roleMartial striker
Temporary CE cap4 × level
Core law

Hit cleanly. Take what remains.

One counter and one temporary resource drive the entire technique. Critical hits, extra attacks, and accuracy matter because every qualifying hit advances the chain.

Upper Tier IV.

Siphon begins narrower than a calamity technique: it needs accurate martial pressure and something with CE to steal. Its Domain, renewable CE, Spoils, and victorious encore loop make it Special Grade without making every encounter begin at full power.

Qualifying hit

Advance the Siphon Counter

A weapon attack, cursed-tool attack, unarmed strike, or technique-enhanced weapon attack that hits a creature advances the counter by 1. A critical hit counts as one hit unless a feature expressly says otherwise.

Threshold

Trigger and begin again

When the Siphon Counter reaches its threshold, Siphon activates and the counter returns to 0. Later hits begin a new chain. Siphon may activate multiple times in one turn.

Reset

The chain breaks

  • You miss a qualifying attack.
  • You become unconscious.
  • You end your turn without attempting a qualifying attack.

Continuity

The chain survives

  • Changing targets or moving.
  • Using actions between attacks.
  • Hitting a target with no CE; the drain simply yields nothing.

Technique statistics

Use normal CE mathematics

Spoil attacks use your CE attack modifier. Spoil saves use your CE save DC. Siphon itself needs no save: its drain follows a qualifying hit.

CE attack = PB + CE modifier
CE save DC = 8 + PB + CE modifier

Black Flash interaction:

a critical remains one hit, but an additional attack created by Perfected Siphon is another qualifying hit. Siphon’s CE drain occurs after the triggering attack and is not part of that attack’s damage.

Field notes. Subject: the gladiator who taxes every blow. Three truths govern him. First: every clean hit is a debt, Siphon converts qualifying strikes into stolen cursed energy, banked as Temporary CE; the technique never asks for damage, only for contact, and the debt-collector's memory is perfect. Second: the Domain is a closed room where someone must pay, the colosseum pools stolen energy as Tribute, draining the victims' Gladiator hit points with it, and the last competitor standing cashes out: Victor's Momentum scaled to the pool, no Burnout, one Colosseum Spoil. Third: the rhythm perfects, the threshold of consecutive qualifying hits shortens with mastery, and the stolen power is spendable: extra force dice on his own strikes, or fed into the Pool to fatten the prize. Assess him correctly: he does not fight to end the fight. He fights to be the one the arena pays.

Core law

Arena Bestiary

Each beast is a fully targetable shikigami, not a rider effect. Its body and tactics scale with the champion who won it.

Shared Arena Beast rules

Manifestation, commands, and scaling

Manifest the trophy

When you gain an Arena Beast Spoil, it appears at full HP in an unoccupied space you can see within 30 feet. If no such space exists, it appears in the nearest legal space. It vanishes when the Spoil ends, when reduced to 0 HP, or when you become unconscious. A vanished beast makes no death saves and cannot be manifested again from that Spoil.

Independent turn

The beast acts immediately after your turn and has its own movement, action, bonus action, and reaction. You command it mentally without an action and no shared language is required. With no command, it moves to protect you and takes the Dodge action. It can take any ordinary action its body could reasonably perform.

Borrowed technique math

Its proficiency bonus equals yours. Its attacks use your CE attack modifier and its effects use your CE save DC. PBdX means a number of dX equal to your PB; add your CE modifier only once where stated. Its attacks are cursed attacks. Calculate AC and HP when it manifests.

Hit points and recovery

In an HP formula, M = your CE modifier, minimum 1. A beast can be healed only by an effect able to heal a shikigami or cursed spirit; it has no Hit Dice. Temporary HP and other ordinary creature rules otherwise apply.

Separate combatant

The beast is allied to you but is not you. Its attacks do not advance your Siphon Counter and CE it removes does not become your Temporary CE unless an ability expressly says otherwise. When an ability advances your counter twice, resolve each advance in order, including any trigger and reset between them.

Recharge and boundaries

After a beast uses a Recharge ability, roll a d6 at the start of each of its turns; the ability returns on the listed result. A beast has no CE pool, cannot pay your costs, cannot use your class features or feats, and cannot expand a Domain.

Why these are powerful:

a beast is a victory lap, earned only after the Siphon user wins their own Domain. It adds a complete allied turn for a short window, but opponents can attack, control, banish, or outmaneuver it.

Core law

Build the rejected champion.

Siphon works on any martial chassis, but it rewards accuracy, durability, and enough attacks to complete repeated chains.

Physical shell

Strength and Constitution

A Superhuman ancestry and other durable physical options support the close-range loop. Athletics helps inside an arena whose Favor directly rewards control.

Class

Warrior is the cleanest fit

Martial weapons, armor, Extra Attack, and frontline durability let Siphon begin feeding without requiring a technique attack roll.

Priority

Accuracy before raw damage

A miss resets the counter. Advantage, reach, control, and reliable attack bonuses often generate more CE than one larger damage die.

Rejected clan heir

Turn shame into appetite

Your clan treated a poor natural reserve as weakness and Siphon as vulgar. Every stolen point proves that inherited capacity was never the same thing as mastery.

Debt collector

Energy is only being held

You regard CE as a debt carried temporarily by whoever stands across from you. A clean hit is simply collection.

Arena champion

Calmer under pressure

Crowds, blood, humiliation, and impossible odds feel familiar. The worse the contest becomes, the more certain you are that the arena will remember your name.

Complete loop:

chain hits → steal CE → meet the Domain threshold → wager the room’s CE → become last standing → record the full Cashout Score → absorb what your reserve can hold → gain Momentum and a Spoil → reopen the Amphitheater.

Full progression

The arena learns your name.

The technique keeps its original numbers. Later levels accelerate its loop rather than replacing it with unrelated abilities.

Pinnacle expression

Amphitheater of the Living Dead

Red sand, broken marble, rusted gates, dead spectators—and a binding rule that recognizes no argument except remaining standing.

Opening procedure Expansion

  1. Check the required Temporary CE before paying.
  2. Pay the activation cost using ordinary CE, Temporary CE, or both.
  3. Choose competitors in the listed radius; the rule applies without a save.
  4. Each competitor receives Gladiator HP equal to 4 × your character level.

The Domain lasts 1 minute and otherwise follows the shared Domain clash and ending rules.

Gladiator HP replaces ordinary harm Contest protection

While a competitor has Gladiator HP, all damage removes Gladiator HP instead of normal HP. Excess damage at 0 does not spill over. Ordinary healing and RCT cannot restore Gladiator HP.

At 0, the competitor is transported to the spectral stands. It can perceive and speak but is incapacitated, untargetable, and unable to affect the arena until the Domain ends.

Contribution pulse Initiative 20 · sole sure-hit

You contribute the current Drain Rate. Each active hostile competitor not protected from the Domain also contributes it automatically. CE actually lost enters the Colosseum Pool; for every point a competitor cannot pay, it instead loses 1 Gladiator HP. A protected hostile makes a Charisma save, contributing nothing on a success or suffering the complete contribution on a failure. This pulse—not ordinary attacks or Siphon—is the Domain’s sole sure-hit event.

The audience chooses its champion Favor

Gladiator Favor equals the Pool divided by 5, rounded down, maximum PB. Add Favor to weapon damage, Athletics and Intimidation checks, and checks or saves made to resist being shoved, grappled, restrained, or knocked prone.

Cash out Caster victory

When only you remain active, the Domain ends. Record the entire Pool as your Cashout Score, then absorb as much of it as your Temporary CE cap permits. Excess CE is consumed by Victor’s Momentum rather than stored. Gain the matching Momentum package and one Spoil, and suffer no Domain Burnout.

The arena rejects you Another victor

If another competitor is last, the Domain ends, the Pool is lost, no Spoil is awarded, and you suffer normal Domain Burnout.

A broken wager No victor

If the duration expires, the Domain is dismissed, or every competitor reaches 0 simultaneously, the Pool is lost and normal Burnout applies.

Innate Technique Feats

The champion chooses an appetite.

These optional feats sharpen consistency, extraction, arena growth, or victory rewards. Each occupies a normal feat choice, may be taken only once, and the technique has no additional feat list beyond these four.

Prerequisite: Siphon

Early Trigger

Bonus action: reset your Siphon Counter to 0. If it was at least 2 - or at least 1 from level 6 - Siphon triggers immediately against the last creature you hit since your last turn, draining as normal. If the counter was lower, nothing happens.

Prerequisite: Siphon, level 4

Redistributed Drain

Bonus action: transfer any amount of your Temporary CE to a willing creature within 30 feet. It gains that Temporary CE, up to its own cap, and you lose it; transferred CE expires on its normal schedule.

Prerequisite: Amphitheater of the Living Dead

Blood-Price Champion

Once per round when one of your qualifying attacks removes Gladiator HP from a hostile competitor, add 1 CE to the Colosseum Pool after the damage resolves. If that attack was a critical hit, add your PB instead. This contribution does not drain either combatant and cannot itself change Favor until the Pool is recalculated.

Prerequisite: Colosseum Spoils

Curator of Spoils

You may lend a Spoil you hold to a willing creature: it gains the Spoil's benefit until your next long rest, and you cannot benefit from it meanwhile. Reclaim it as a bonus action while within 60 feet.
