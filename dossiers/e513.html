Hide and Seek

A Conjurer's mansion with no address — a fourth-dimensional estate folded out of sight, entered through doors only the keyholder can hang. Inside: quiet rooms, deep vaults, and a long rest no one can interrupt. And Scream: a window that closes on whatever it touches, filing it away inside. Knov's hair went white learning what hunts in the dark. The mansion is his answer.

Feature map

Level-by-level features

L1

The Mansion Door

As an action, spend 2 CE and press your palm to a surface: conjure a 5-foot doorway that opens into your mansion — a furnished, lit, 30-foot-cube hall that exists in no dimension anyone can name. When you hang the door, you designate who may pass; all others meet a wall. The door lasts until you dismiss it or finish a long rest. You can maintain one door at a time.

The safest room in the world is the one with no address.

L3

Scream

As an action, spend 3 CE: conjure a hand-sized portal that snaps shut on a creature within 30 feet. The target makes a Dexterity saving throw against your CE save DC — on a failure, it is teleported into your mansion's holding room, whose door is locked by your will alone. On a success, nothing happens. An unwilling creature can attempt to break the holding room's door from inside (thieves' tools or a Strength check against your CE save DC). The window closes. Whatever it touched is filed away.

L6

More Rooms

The mansion grows. Whenever you finish a long rest inside it, you may add one room — a vault, a war room, a bathhouse, a cell, guest quarters. Long rests taken inside the mansion cannot be interrupted by anything outside it, and divination and scrying cannot find creatures inside — the mansion isn't in this dimension. Square footage is a state of mind.

L11

Paired Doors

You can maintain two doors at once. Step through one and out the other, so long as both are within 300 feet of each other. Hanging a second door costs an action and 2 CE as normal. Distance is a rumor the keyholder doesn't repeat.

L15

Keyholder's Step

As a bonus action, spend 1 CE: step into any of your doors and exit from any other door you can perceive within 500 feet — or step out of the mansion itself through any of your doors. You can bring willing creatures holding your hand. The house has hallways. They just aren't all in the same world.

L20

The Estate, Perfected

The mansion becomes a true estate — up to twelve rooms, each with a declared property you name when it is built: a sealed vault immune to divination and teleportation, a sickroom, a training hall, guest quarters that lock from the inside. You may hang a door on thin air, no surface required.

Exile. Scream perfected: you may close it on the empty air around a creature instead of the creature itself — on a failed Dexterity save, it is held outside reality for 1 minute (it can repeat a Charisma saving throw against your CE save DC at the end of each of its turns to return early). There are worse places than the mansion. One of them is nowhere.

Pinnacle expression

Reversal and Domain.

Capstone material

Reversal: Seek and Find

The inversion of hiding. When a creature within 60 feet of you attempts to hide — turning invisible, stepping into illusion, teleporting away, slipping into an extradimensional space — you may spend your reaction and 2 CE to tear the hiding off: conjure a portal on the concealment itself and drag the creature into the open in an unoccupied space adjacent to you. For 1 minute, it cannot benefit from being hidden or invisible against you. Hide and seek has two halves. This is the other one.

Domain Expansion: The Fourth-Dimensional Estate

18th level. Sure-hit domain — the sure-hit is the mansion, everywhere at once. Activation: full turn, 20 CE (D008 baseline for a normal Domain). Clash: 800 points; burnout 5 rounds; duration 2 min (Tier IV mapping); radius 120 ft.

Everywhere Is Inside. The domain's interior is the mansion — every creature inside is already within your walls. As a bonus action, you may relocate any number of willing creatures to any unoccupied spaces within the domain. At the start of each of your turns, each hostile creature must succeed on a Dexterity saving throw against your CE save DC or be swallowed by a Scream portal into a holding room — restrained there, repeating the save at the end of each of its turns, freed when the domain ends. You didn't walk into a domain. You walked into his house.

Feats

Five feats, filed away.

Hide and Seek teaches five feats — five doors the keyholder cut for special occasions. Each is optional, and you must meet its listed prerequisite before taking it.

Prerequisite: The Mansion Door

Doormat

Hang a door flat on a floor as a trap: the next creature to step on it — other than those you designated — falls into the mansion's cell. Spotting the trap takes a Perception or Investigation check against your CE save DC. It lasts until triggered or you finish a long rest.

Prerequisite: The Mansion Door

Mail Slot

Cut a letter-sized portal paired to any of your doors. It passes objects no larger than a scroll case and whispered messages, silently, in either direction — a dead drop with no address and no courier.

Prerequisite: More Rooms

The Vault

Designate one room as a time-locked vault: perishables placed inside do not decay, volatile items cannot detonate, and nothing stored there can be scried or located by divination. What goes into the Vault waits, perfectly, until you come back for it.

Prerequisite: Paired Doors

Spare Key

Name one creature your key-bearer. It may open and pass through your doors freely, and once per long rest it may hang a new door itself — the CE cost is drawn from your pool, with your standing permission.

Prerequisite: Scream

Catch, Don't Kill

Close a Scream portal on an incoming ranged attack or damaging effect you can see: spend your reaction and 3 CE, and the attack is swallowed into the mansion instead of striking — you take no damage. The portal closes on the arrow mid-flight.

Rules integration

Edge cases and shared systems.

Campaign canon notes

Cursed Energy baseline: CE pool is based on class; CE ability is Charisma or Wisdom (player's choice). CE save DC = 8 + CE ability modifier + proficiency bonus. Regain all spent CE on a long rest.
The mansion is extradimensional: it cannot be entered except through your doors, and destroying a door does not destroy the mansion — occupants are stranded inside until you hang another door. You cannot hang a door on a creature.
Scream is not subtle: the portal's snap is audible within 30 feet, and the holding room's door can be forced from inside (thieves' tools or Strength check vs. your CE save DC). A creature with no way to breathe still needs air — the mansion provides it.
Releasing prisoners: you may release any creature from the mansion through any of your doors as a free action on your turn. Creatures you never release are simply guests with no exit.
Anti-smuggling: paired doors transport creatures and objects, not ongoing effects — a creature mid-teleport or under a delayed blast arrives with its problems intact.
Domain activation defaults: rendered as full turn + 20 CE per the D008 normal-Domain baseline, with Tier IV clash stats (800 / 5-round burnout / 2 min) and a 120-ft radius.
Reversal vs. Maximum: Hide and Seek's pinnacle is written as a Reversal (Seek and Find), not a Maximum Technique; it occupies the technique's pinnacle slot.
