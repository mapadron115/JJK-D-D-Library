The Downward Stair

Sixty feet down, the knocking starts. The file has measured it. The file has never gone down to look. This is the correct decision, and the file would like that noted, in writing, where the next team will see it.

Combat roleUtility
Core law

The Descent

Descend.

Action, 3 CE: open a 10-foot stairwell descending 60 feet into an extradimensional shaft, lasting 10 minutes. You and willing creatures may descend; reopen from inside as an action; close as a bonus action.

The Shaft.

The shaft is dark, cold, and faintly wrong. Sounds from below do not correspond to anything above. This is normal. Probably.

What Knocks.

Flavor, sparing: at the GM’s discretion, something knocks from below. It has never come up. The file intends to keep it that way.

Subject file

Containment & observation record.

Containment Procedures

The stairwell is kept closed. Descents are logged. No one goes below sixty feet — this is the only measurement in the file that has never been revised.

Knocking is logged by time and duration, not investigated. Investigation was tried once. The file references the maintenance request and moves on.

Emergency exits are designated in pairs. No one descends alone. The file does not explain the pairing rule. The pairing rule explains itself.

Research Log

Research log. 06/2024 — Acquisition: recovered from a condemned building scheduled for demolition. The demolition crew reported ‘extra stairs.’ The building had four floors. The stairs had no bottom that the crew could find, and the crew stopped looking at sixty feet, independently, without being told.

Research log. 12/2024 — The descent is mapped: 60 feet (120 at threshold), extradimensional, ten minutes, reopenable from inside. The knocking begins at sixty feet — measured, timed, consistent. The file has never sent anyone down to look. The file considers this its finest decision.

Research log. 05/2025 — The Trap test: a weighted dummy was left in an open stairwell. It fell 6d6 worth, by the file’s measurement, and was recovered at the entrance when the stairwell closed — prone, dusty, and, per the recovery team, ‘relieved.’

Research log. 02/2026 — The Bottom: one volunteer, one descent with no bottom, 10d6 of falling, emergence one mile east in a drainage ditch. The volunteer’s statement consists of eleven words: ‘It knocked the whole way down. Don’t ask me what it was.’ The file has not asked.

Incident Report — The Survey.

A survey team — three professionals, full equipment, explicit orders not to exceed sixty feet — exceeded sixty feet. They returned in eleven minutes. They were quieter. Not silent: quieter, the way a room is quieter after someone leaves it. Their equipment logs show ninety feet of descent and four minutes unaccounted for. None of them will describe the four minutes. All three requested transfer within the week. All three were granted it. The sixty-foot rule is now the only rule in the file that carries no exceptions, no thresholds, and no review date.
