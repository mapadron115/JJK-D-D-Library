Feature map

Base

Cursed Lotus

As a bonus action for 2 CE, you create a blue lotus of cursed energy on your body for 1 minute. The next time you take damage, the lotus explodes and showers all creatures of your choice within 10 ft of you with blue petals of cursed energy. They must make a Dexterity saving throw against your technique save DC (8 + proficiency bonus + CE modifier), taking 4d4 slashing damage on a failure or half as much on a success. You can only have one lotus active at once.

Innate Technique Feats

Build-defining options.

Feats for this technique

Double Bloom — Prerequisite: Cursed Lotus

[Licensed fill] You can maintain two lotuses at once (each costs its own 2 CE and bonus action to create). Only one lotus can detonate per round.

Petal Screen — Prerequisite: Cursed Lotus

[Licensed fill] When your lotus explodes, you may have the petals swirl defensively instead of offensively: chosen creatures within 10 feet of you gain half cover until the start of your next turn, and no damage is dealt.

Seed Petal — Prerequisite: level 6

[Licensed fill] As an action for 2 CE, press a petal onto a willing creature. The next time that creature takes damage within 1 minute, a smaller lotus detonates around it: creatures of your choice within 10 feet of it make a Dexterity save against your technique save DC, taking 2d4 slashing damage on a failure or half on a success. You can have a number of seeds active equal to your proficiency bonus.

Rules integration

Edge cases and shared systems.

Campaign canon notes

Note: The detonation is automatic — the source requires no action or reaction to trigger it. The first instance of damage you take while the lotus is active sets it off.
Note: The source names no save DC; this dossier uses the campaign-standard technique save DC (8 + proficiency bonus + CE modifier).
Note: The explosion only affects creatures of your choice, so you can spare yourself and allies. Anyone you do not choose is unharmed.
Note: The lotus is consumed by detonation. An unspent lotus fades after 1 minute, and creating a new lotus while one is active ends the old one.
