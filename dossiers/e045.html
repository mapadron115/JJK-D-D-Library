X Tier is not Tier VI and it is not Calamity Grade.

It is a unique classification for Dimensional Authorship because the technique edits the rules used to measure other techniques: reality, action economy, dice, dimensions, and even what counts as the current scene. X Tier remains player-facing and selectable; its label tells the campaign to treat rule authorship as a premise, not to compress it back into the Tier I–V budget.

SNAP. THE NEXT PANEL IS DIFFERENT.
Technique foundation

Reality has an edit history.

Dimensional Authorship uses normal CE statistics. It deliberately avoids a Trust Value subsystem: reputation may be part of the character’s story, but the Innate Technique runs on CE and works even when nobody knows the user’s name.

CE attackPB + CE modifier
CE save DC8 + PB + CE modifier
Active EditsPB; 2× PB at 11; 3× PB at 18
Default duration1 minute
Reality Die

The art gains depth

Your Reality Die is d6 at levels 1–5, d8 at 6–10, d10 at 11–17, and d12 at 18–20. Features abbreviate it as RD.

Snap Range

Where the edit cursor reaches

Snap Range is 60 feet at level 1, 120 feet at level 6, 300 feet at level 11, and 600 feet at level 18. Total cover still blocks an ordinary Edit unless a feature supplies an image, dimensional route, or Domain origin.

Activation

A deliberate mark, not one finger

A snap is the iconic motion, but any deliberate hand sign, tap, blink, or traced X works. Restraining one hand does not switch off the technique. Incapacitation and Technique Burnout do.

Active Edit

Persistent revisions share one ledger

Flattened matter, manifested props, stored energy, Private Panels, and other effects labeled Active Edits count against capacity. Creating one beyond capacity ends an existing Edit of your choice. Instantaneous movement, damage, and a one-turn condition do not use capacity.

Planar

A defined two-dimensional state

A Planar creature has negligible depth but occupies its space normally. Its speed is reduced by 10 feet, it cannot take reactions, and it may move only along contiguous solid surfaces, including walls and ceilings. It can squeeze through a one-inch gap. If no surface supports it, it is restrained until it touches one. Returning to 3D ends those effects.

Targets

People resist; scenery usually does not

Willing creatures and unattended nonmagical objects receive an Edit automatically. An unwilling creature, attended object, cursed tool, barrier, summon, or CE construct uses the feature’s attack or save. Artifacts and campaign-defining objects are not casually deleted, but they can still be moved, flattened, sealed, or contested when the feature says so.

Dimensional return

No creature is left in an illegal square

When an effect returns a creature or object, use its departure space if legal; otherwise use the nearest unoccupied space chosen by the effect’s user. Returning never embeds a target inside solid matter. A target that cannot return appears at the nearest legal point on the same plane.

Table boundary, not a power nerf: this technique may rewrite local physics, statistics, dice, initiative, terrain, and event order. It cannot dictate another player character’s thoughts, choices, identity, or permanent backstory without that player’s consent. A freeform sentence can wreck the encounter; it does not overrule the people at the table.

Levels 1–18

From panel trick to authorial privilege.

The progression grants enormous breadth early and then begins breaking game structures on purpose. Every feature has timing, range, cost, resistance, and return language.

1Dimension at Your FingertipsPanel Strike, Panel Step, Paper Defense, and Fold the Panel.
Attack replacement · 1 CE

Panel Strike

When making a weapon or unarmed attack, spend 1 CE and use that attack bonus or your CE attack bonus. On a hit, deal the attack’s normal damage plus 2 RD. Alternatively, make a ranged CE attack within Snap Range that deals 2 RD + CE modifier force damage.

After either hit choose one: move the target 20 feet along a legal path; make it Planar until the start of your next turn; or turn one nonmagical object it holds into harmless paper until the start of its next turn. A worn object cannot be chosen.

Bonus action · 1+ CE

Panel Step

Teleport 30 feet to an unoccupied space you can see, a point reflected in a visible image, or a point on a contiguous surface. Each additional CE, maximum PB, adds 10 feet. At level 6 the base and additions double; at level 11 they become 120 and 30 feet; at level 18 the base destination may be any point in Snap Range. For +1 CE bring one willing adjacent creature.

Reaction · 2 CE

Paper Defense

When you take damage, reduce that instance by PB RD + CE modifier, then become Planar and move up to 15 feet along a surface without provoking. If reduced to 0, the triggering attack or effect misses you and applies no on-hit rider. You return to 3D at the start of your next turn.

Bonus action · 2 CE

Fold the Panel

Choose one Planar creature or object within Snap Range. A creature makes a Strength save. On failure it takes PB RD + CE modifier force damage, is folded into a hand-sized square, and is restrained until the end of your next turn; on success it takes half damage and is not folded. You may carry a folded target, but cannot place it in an extradimensional container. It unfolds in its space when the effect ends.

3The Scene Supplies Its Own PropsManifest images, recast matter, and make impossible trajectories.
Action · 1–5 CE · Active Edit

Prop Extraction

Touch or target within Snap Range an image depicting a nonliving object and pull out a three-dimensional copy. CE determines maximum size: Tiny 1, Small 2, Medium 3, Large 4, Huge 5. The prop lasts 1 hour, has AC 15 and 10 HP per CE spent, and performs every mundane function its image clearly communicates.

A prop weapon may use any ordinary weapon profile or your CE attack bonus and 2 RD damage. A depicted vehicle has speed 60 feet, carries a reasonable number of creatures for its size, and uses the same AC and HP. Props cannot create CE, cursed or magic items, living creatures, permanent wealth, or consumables with supernatural effects.

Action · 3+ CE · Active Edit

Matter Recast

Transform one nonliving object within Snap Range into a different object of equal or smaller size for 10 minutes. Three CE affects Large or smaller; each additional CE increases the limit one size, to Gargantuan. An attended object makes a Dexterity save using its holder’s bonus.

Mass, rigidity, and moving parts change to support the new form. A truck may become a sword, a door may become a shield, and rubble may become a motorcycle. A recast weapon uses your CE attack bonus, has reach appropriate to its size, and deals 3 RD + CE modifier. It returns safely when the Edit ends.

Once per turn · 1 CE

Perfect Trajectory

After a ranged weapon attack, Panel Strike, thrown prop, or forced movement misses its intended endpoint, spend 1 CE to redraw its route. Reroll the attack, or bend the forced movement around corners while preserving its remaining distance. If the rerolled attack hits, you may have the projectile or target continue 15 feet after impact and strike an unattended object or structure for the same rolled dice, without the flat modifier.

6Scene TransitionChange rendering style, freeze an event, or isolate a private fight.
Bonus action · 3 CE · Active Edit

Style Shift

For 1 minute, switch once during each of your turns without an action between two forms.

Ink Form: you are Planar without its speed or reaction penalties, move along any surface, pass through one-inch gaps, cannot be grappled by a creature lacking CE, and resist bludgeoning, piercing, and slashing damage.

Rendered Form: gain a flying speed equal to walking speed, +2 AC, and once per turn add 2 RD to one damaging attack or technique.

Reaction · 3 CE

Freeze Frame

When a creature within Snap Range begins movement, declares an action, or becomes the target of an effect, suspend it in a frozen panel. A willing creature is suspended automatically; an unwilling creature makes an Intelligence save. On failure it vanishes, its turn ends, and the declared activity remains committed but unresolved.

At the start of its next turn it returns, resolves the suspended activity first if still legal, and then takes that turn normally. If the activity is no longer legal, its action and paid resources are lost. Effects outside the panel cannot affect it while suspended.

Action · 6 CE · Technique Focus

Private Panel Battlespace

Choose a 30-foot-radius area within Snap Range and up to PB creatures in it. Willing creatures enter automatically; hostile creatures make Charisma saves. Transfer the selected creatures into a two-dimensional pocket scene for 3 rounds. You choose its visible geometry, though it must fit within a 60-foot square and cannot contain an inherently lethal hazard on arrival.

Time continues outside. Effects cannot cross the panel boundary, and creatures outside cannot target those within. Inside, you may orient gravity independently for each surface at the start of your turn and use Panel Step between any two visible points for 0 CE. When the Battlespace ends, every occupant returns under the dimensional-return rule. Only one Private Panel may exist at a time.

11Fourth-Dimensional AccessBottle attacks, revise continuity, cross scenes, and inspect the past.
Reaction · 4 CE · Active Edit

Energy Recast

When an attack, projectile, line, cone, or burst would deal damage at a point within 30 feet of you, make a CE ability check against the source’s save DC. If it has none, use 8 + its PB + its relevant ability modifier. On success, negate that effect’s damage and on-hit riders for every target and compress it into a harmless prop token you hold.

The token stores the original damage type and dice, capped at 10 RD + CE modifier. As a bonus action within 1 minute, throw it to a point within Snap Range: one creature makes a Dexterity save, taking the stored damage on failure or half on success; alternatively create a 20-foot-radius burst, with every creature making that save. Only one token may be stored.

Reaction · 5 CE · once per round

Continuity Edit

After any creature you perceive within Snap Range rolls a d20, but before the result is narrated, roll one RD and add or subtract it from the d20 result. This may turn a hit into a miss, change a save, alter a check, or affect initiative if used when initiative is rolled. It cannot change the face actually rolled for critical hits, natural 1s, or Black Flash confirmation.

Action · 6+ CE

Cross-Scene Cut

Teleport to any location on the same plane that you have personally visited or can currently perceive through a live image, recording, reflection, or remote sense. Bring up to PB willing creatures within 10 feet for +1 CE each. A static image of an unfamiliar destination requires a DC 15 CE ability check; on failure you arrive at a safe point chosen by the GM within 1 mile. At level 18, you may cross planes to a known destination with a DC 20 check.

Action · 5 CE

Fourth-Dimensional Archive

Touch a location or object and name an event connected to it. Reality displays a truthful reconstruction of up to 10 minutes from that event, even if no surviving witness observed it. You and chosen creatures can walk through the reconstruction without changing it. Ask the GM up to PB factual questions that the scene can answer.

The Archive shows events, physical evidence, and spoken words—not unexpressed thoughts or a guaranteed future. At level 18, the event may come from any point in the target’s history; before then it must have occurred within the last year.

18The Author Is PresentRewrite dice, turns, positions, and the physical grammar of a scene.
Once per round · no action

Authorial Privilege

Once per round—even during another creature’s turn—spend the listed CE and apply one Rewrite below. This is neither an action nor a reaction and may interrupt after its trigger but before resolution. Extra turns do not refresh it.

Redline Result · 5 CE

Write the die’s face

After a perceived d20 is rolled but before its result resolves, set the face to any number from 1 to 20. It counts as the natural face for critical hits and natural 1s. The separate unmodified Black Flash confirmation d20 cannot be rewritten.

Cut Turn · 8 CE

Remove the panel

When a hostile creature within Snap Range begins its turn, it makes a Charisma save. On failure its turn ends immediately and unused movement, actions, bonus actions, and reactions from that turn are lost.

Insert Panel · 8 CE

Add a full turn

Choose a willing creature within Snap Range. It immediately takes a full turn, interrupting the current event. A creature cannot receive another inserted turn until the end of its next normal turn.

Reblock Scene · 5 CE

Everyone was standing elsewhere

Teleport up to PB creatures or movable objects within Snap Range to legal spaces elsewhere in that range. Willing creatures and objects move automatically; each hostile creature makes a Charisma save and remains on a success.

Scene Law · 6 CE

Change one rule until initiative 20

Choose a point within Snap Range and a 60-foot-radius scene around it. Until the next initiative count 20, select one law:

  • Directional Gravity: gravity pulls in a direction you choose. On creation, unsecured creatures make Dexterity saves or fall 60 feet that way and land prone against a surface.
  • Compressed or Extended Distance: all movement, reach, ranges, and areas measured wholly inside are doubled or halved.
  • Damage Palette: choose two damage types; every instance of either type inside becomes the other.
  • Foreground / Background: choose whether nonmagical cover inside provides no cover or total cover, regardless of thickness. Creatures remain visible as silhouettes.
Passive

Three margins—and no limit in the Domain

Active Edit capacity becomes three times PB outside your Domain and unlimited inside it. You can perceive and physically interact across your own 2D, 3D, and pocket-dimensional boundaries.

Maximum Technique

Final Draft: Publish Reality.

The user stops editing individual panels and publishes one sentence as the governing truth of a scene.

Full-turn action · 15 CE

Publication

Choose a point within 300 feet. A 120-foot-radius scene centered there obeys one Published Sentence for up to 3 rounds while you maintain Technique Focus. The sentence takes effect after the full-turn action completes. Only one Final Draft may exist at a time.

When it ends, you suffer normal Maximum-Technique Burnout. There is no once-per-rest limit; if you can pay the cost and survive repeated Burnout, you may publish again.

Sentence grammar

One subject, one rule

A freeform sentence contains no more than twelve words and establishes one local physical, dimensional, or game-state rule. It may change terrain, distance, gravity, damage behavior, visibility, object identity, dimensional access, or when an event resolves.

It cannot dictate a player character’s choices, memories, or permanent identity. It cannot simply state that a creature is dead; use The Next Panel Is Blank when erasure is the intended mechanic.

Published Sentence

The Next Panel Is Blank

Choose up to PB creatures. Each hostile makes a Charisma save. On failure it is erased from the scene until the Maximum ends. It cannot act, perceive, be targeted, maintain Focus, or affect the world. On return it takes 20d12 force damage. On success it remains and takes half that damage immediately.

Published Sentence

Only the Author Is Three-Dimensional

Chosen hostile creatures make Dexterity saves. On failure they become Planar, restrained, and unable to affect a creature or object you have not made Planar. They repeat the save at the end of each turn. You remain 3D and can interact with every layer.

Published Sentence

Violence Has Not Been Drawn Yet

Damage and hostile conditions inside are recorded but do not apply while the sentence lasts. When it ends, you choose the order in which every recorded instance resolves. A creature reduced to 0 during this resolution falls only after all earlier instances in your chosen order finish.

Published Sentence

Distance Is a Typographical Error

Every point inside is adjacent to every other point for you and creatures you choose. Your movement between points costs 5 feet, melee reach covers the scene, and your effects may originate anywhere inside. Other creatures measure distance normally.

Freeform adjudication

Power is not grounds for veto

The GM translates the sentence literally and uses the strongest comparable preset as its mechanical benchmark. Ambiguity produces the most direct reasonable interpretation, not a hidden punishment. The GM may ask for one clarification before publication; after that, the sentence stands for its duration.

Example legal sentences: “Every projectile becomes a harmless paper flower before impact.” “Gravity points toward the red door.” “All exits open onto this room.” “Fire heals objects and burns living creatures.” “Nobody can be farther than five feet from me.” These are intentionally encounter-warping.

Level 18 · Open-edged Domain

The Unpublished World Behind the Panels.

A white drafting space overlays reality. Perspective lines pierce buildings, discarded versions of the battlefield hang in the margins, and everyone can see the hand that decides what becomes canon.

Expansion

The fourth wall opens

As a full-turn action, spend 20 CE to impose a fixed 175-foot-radius open-edged Domain for 1 minute. It has no enclosing shell and creatures may enter or leave. Its open geometry is an explicit X Tier exception supported by the loss of confinement. Clashes, anti-domain techniques, refinement, ending, and Burnout use the core rules.

Editor’s room

Every surface is an image

Snap Range covers the Domain, every visible surface counts as a usable image, and Active Edit capacity is unlimited. Once during each of your turns, Panel Step between any two points inside without an action or CE. Ordinary attacks, techniques, summons, and saves still resolve normally.

Author on deadline

Two rewrites, first one free

You may use Authorial Privilege twice per round inside the Domain. The first use each round costs 0 CE; the second costs normally. The same Rewrite may be chosen twice if its individual target restriction permits it.

Sole sure-hit event

Canon Revision owns initiative 20

At initiative count 20 after clash checks, choose Cut, Fold, or Recast separately for every chosen hostile creature inside. Canon Revision is the Domain’s only automatic hostile effect. It is not an attack, cannot critically hit, and cannot trigger Black Flash.

Initiative 20 · Canon Revision · sure-hit

The next edition replaces the target.

CUT

An unprotected creature is removed from reality until the next initiative count 20. On return it takes 10d12 force damage, appears in any legal space you choose inside the Domain, and cannot take reactions until the end of that turn. A protected creature makes a Charisma save and avoids the Cut entirely on a success.

FOLD

An unprotected creature takes 12d12 force damage, becomes Planar, and is restrained and incapacitated until the next initiative count 20. A protected creature makes a Dexterity save, taking half damage with no Planar state or conditions on a success.

RECAST

An unprotected creature takes 6d12 psychic damage and receives two clauses you choose until the next initiative count 20: AC becomes 10; all speeds become 0; its attack and technique damage dice become d4s; its CE costs double; or it cannot take reactions. A protected creature makes a Wisdom save, taking half damage and receiving no clauses on a success.

Anti-domain protection: Simple Domain, Hollow Wicker Basket, Falling Blossom Emotion, and a contested clash suppress Canon Revision’s guarantee only for the protected creature and supply the listed save. They do not remove the open scene, free Panel Step, image origins, Active Edits, or normally rolled Authorial Rewrites.

Rules reference

Even a broken rule needs exact punctuation.

These rulings stop the technique’s deliberate excess from becoming accidental ambiguity.

Criticals

Rolled strikes remain real attacks

A weapon-delivered or ranged Panel Strike may critically hit. Double its weapon and Reality Dice, not flat modifiers. It then follows the campaign’s normal, uncapped Black Flash confirmation procedure.

Automatic effects

No roll means no critical

Fold saves, Energy Recast releases, Maximum sentences, Canon Revision, and other automatic or saving-throw effects cannot critically hit or trigger Black Flash.

Reversal

No Cursed Technique Reversal

Dimensional Authorship has no Reversal. Switching from 3D to 2D, restoring an Edit, healing through a sentence, or converting energy into matter are forward applications. RCT does not invent an “objective reality” opposite.

Result rewriting

Redline Result versus Continuity Edit

Continuity Edit changes a numerical total but not the die’s natural face. Redline Result changes the face itself and can create or remove a critical hit or natural 1. Neither may alter the separate unmodified d20 rolled to confirm Black Flash. A die can be affected by only one of your result-rewriting features.

Extra turns

A full turn means a full turn

Insert Panel grants movement, action, bonus action, and normal turn-start or turn-end effects. It refreshes reactions as a turn normally would. It can recharge “once on each of your turns” features, but does not refresh “once per round,” Authorial Privilege, legendary actions, Domain pulses, or a resource explicitly tied to the normal initiative cycle.

Cut Turn

Start-of-turn effects happen first

Resolve effects explicitly occurring at the start of the target’s turn, then Cut Turn. On failure, end the remainder. End-of-turn effects still occur because a turn ended.

Images

Depiction establishes function

A recognizable photo, drawing, poster, screen, reflection, map, graffiti figure, or projected image qualifies. Pure text does not unless the freeform Maximum declares it visual.

Copies

Props are not permanent assets

A manifested key can open the depicted mundane lock profile; a copied ID can resemble the image; a weapon can function. Created money, evidence, signatures, food, fuel, and consumed supplies vanish when the Edit ends and are visibly unreal to CE perception.

Energy Recast

One effect becomes one token

Success negates the entire triggering attack or area’s damage and on-hit riders, not only damage to you. Ongoing terrain, a summoned attacker, or the source creature remains. Damage exceeding the storage cap is negated but not stored. A failed check leaves the effect unchanged and still spends CE and the reaction.

Maximum Output

Enhance the chosen activation

Maximum Output doubles an Edit’s complete CE cost. Damage mode adds half its original Reality Dice, rounded up; accuracy adds half the original CE attack bonus or half PB to a DC; range increases each listed distance by half; duration increases by half. Authorial Privilege, Final Draft, and Domain Expansion are ineligible.

Burnout

Existing pages do not all vanish

Technique Burnout prevents new Edits and ends effects requiring Technique Focus. Timed props, recast objects, stored tokens, Planar states, and Published Sentences that do not require Focus persist for their stated duration unless their feature says otherwise.

Counters

Specific authority can contest an Edit

Technique Extermination, stronger dimensional authority, an artifact’s explicit immunity, or a successful Domain clash can suppress an Edit. Generic immunity to frightened, prone, charm, or mundane transmutation does not grant blanket immunity to dimensional relocation or reality editing.

Precedence

The sentence is specific; the game remains legible

A Published Sentence overrides ordinary rules only inside its area and duration. A more specific named feature, Domain clash result, or artifact rule can contest it. If two reality writers impose incompatible specific rules, use a Domain clash when either is a Domain; otherwise both make opposed CE ability checks at initiative count 20, and the losing rule is suppressed until the next count 20.

Six X-Tier Technique feats

Bad drafts become signature techniques.

Ordinary Innate Techniques offer up to four dedicated feats. Dimensional Authorship deliberately breaks that structure: these six feats are independent choices and the complete X-Tier list. Each occupies a normal feat choice and may be taken only once.

Prerequisite: Dimensional Authorship

Clean Linework

Snap Range increases by 30 feet, Active Edit capacity increases by PB, and Paper Defense rolls one additional RD. You may make the activation mark while both hands are occupied.

Prerequisite: Panel Step, level 6

Smash Cut

Once per turn after Panel Step, spend 2 CE to make one weapon attack or Panel Strike without an action. On a hit add one RD and move yourself and the target up to 15 feet in the same direction.

Prerequisite: Prop Extraction, level 8

Living Illustration

As an action, spend 5 CE and choose a creature depicted in an image within Snap Range. You manifest it in an unoccupied space beside that image for 1 hour; it counts as one Active Edit. It has AC 15 + half PB, HP equal to 10 × PB, speed 40 feet, and one attack using your CE attack bonus that deals 2 RD + your CE modifier in an appropriate damage type. It acts immediately after you and Dodges unless you command it with a bonus action. If reduced to 0 HP, dismissed without an action, or no longer within Snap Range at the end of your turn, it folds back into its source image.

Prerequisite: Dimensional Authorship, level 4

Unreliable Narrator

As an action, spend 3 CE to place a false caption, sound, image, creature, object, or apparent terrain change inside a 30-foot cube within Snap Range for 10 minutes. The scene counts as one Active Edit and can include touch, temperature, scent, and convincing CE texture. A creature that uses an action to investigate makes an Intelligence save against your Technique DC, recognizing the false scene on a success. Physical interaction does not automatically reveal it; the fiction supplies expected resistance but cannot deal damage, restrain a creature, or bear real weight.

Prerequisite: Dimensional Authorship, level 14

Continuity Error

When you are reduced to 0 HP, killed by damage, or subjected to an incapacitating condition, use your reaction and spend 8 CE to restore your HP, CE, position, dimensions, and conditions to their state at the start of your previous turn. Other creatures, spent items, and scene changes are not reversed. The first use per long rest is safe; each later use before that rest inflicts 2 levels of exhaustion that cannot be prevented or removed until the scene ends.

Prerequisite: Authorial Privilege, level 18

Executive Editor

You may use Authorial Privilege a second time in the same round by paying 5 CE beyond that Rewrite’s normal cost. The second use cannot choose Insert Panel or Cut Turn outside your Domain. Inside Final Draft or your Domain, that restriction is removed: X may insert or delete a second turn in the same round, with every ordinary cost, save, and once-per-round limit of the chosen Rewrite still applying.

Campaign use

Do not build a weaker X by accident.

The technique is supposed to change what adventures are possible. Its best campaign is one that treats this authority as a premise rather than repeatedly inventing reasons it fails.

Striker

Weapon, coin, or bare cursor

Panel Strike works through weapon and unarmed attacks or as its own ranged CE attack. Strength, Dexterity, and pure CE builds all remain viable.

Controller

Change routes before damage

Planar states, Freeze Frame, directional gravity, Private Panels, and Reblock Scene often win encounters without spending the largest damage feature.

Creator

Bring the answer out of the scenery

Prop Extraction and Matter Recast reward maps, advertisements, photographs, blueprints, screens, and player sketches. Ask what the scene depicts before deciding what you lack.

Investigator

The past is another dimension

Cross-Scene Cut and Fourth-Dimensional Archive can bypass travel mysteries and false testimony. Design investigations around consequences, competing truths, and what characters do with proof.

CE economy

Overpowered does not mean free

Cheap movement and attacks can be used all day with a large CE Reserve, while turn insertion, repeated Rewrites, Final Draft, and Domain can consume dozens of CE in one encounter. The player chooses whether reality breaks constantly or catastrophically.

Encounter advice

Use objectives that survive authority

Multiple locations, civilians, political consequences, rival reality writers, promises, hidden costs, voluntary restraints, and enemies who want the character to edit something all remain meaningful. Merely adding immunity to every boss turns a distinctive technique into blank text.

Suggested campaign conversation: agree that freeform sentences are adjudicated generously and literally; players do not rewrite one another’s agency; the GM may present another X Tier rule-author or equally explicit dimensional opposition; and nobody expects ordinary encounter math to survive once the level-18 author starts publishing new rules.
