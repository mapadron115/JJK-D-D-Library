Feature map

Level-by-level features, as the figure refined them.

L1

Nerve Strike

On unarmed hit · 2 CE · once per turn per creature.

Your claws find the nerve and the body forgets itself. When you hit a creature with an unarmed strike, you may spend 2 CE: the target makes a CON save against your CE save DC. On a failure it is stunned until the end of your next turn; on a success its speed is halved and it cannot take reactions until the end of your next turn.

L3

Leopard Blitz

Passive.

Your speed increases by 10 ft. If you move at least 20 ft in a straight line toward a creature, your first unarmed strike against it that turn has advantage. You may Dash as a bonus action by spending 1 CE — the leopard does not ask permission to close.

L6

Scroll-Denied Fury

Passive · Fury pool (maximum PB).

Being denied is fuel — it always was. Whenever a creature succeeds on a saving throw against one of your technique features, or an attack you make misses, gain 1 Fury (maximum PB). As part of an unarmed strike, you may spend any amount of Fury: the strike deals an extra 1d8 force damage per Fury spent. Fury empties when combat ends.

L11

Nerve Cascade

Passive.

One locked nerve teaches the next. Once per turn, when a creature fails its saving throw against your Nerve Strike, you may immediately make one unarmed strike against a different creature within 5 ft of it — Nerve Strike may apply to this strike without counting against its once-per-turn limit.

L15

"You Don't Know"

Reaction · when reduced to 0 hit points · once per long rest.

They told him what he could not be for twenty years; he stopped listening. When you drop to 0 hit points, you may use your reaction to drop to 1 hit point instead, gain 2 Fury, and ignore the effects of exhaustion until combat ends. Nerve Strike costs you no CE until the end of your next turn — denial, answered.

L18

Chor Gom Breaker

Reaction.

No prison built holds him twice. When you are grappled, restrained, or stunned by another creature's effect, you may use your reaction to end the condition and gain 1 Fury. If the condition came from a spell or technique, its originator takes psychic damage equal to your level — the lock breaks backward.

Pinnacle expression

Maximum, Domain, or equivalent.

Capstone material.

Maximum Technique

Seven-Point Shutdown

Action · 10 CE · unarmed strike.

The full-body lock — seven points, one motion. Make an unarmed strike; on a hit, the target makes a CON save against your CE save DC at disadvantage. On a failure it is paralyzed for 1 minute (repeating the save at the end of each of its turns, ending the effect on a success) and takes 4d10 necrotic damage as its own nerves turn against it. On a success, half damage and stunned until the end of your next turn instead. You name each point as you strike it.

Level 20 · Capstone

The Leopard Unbound

Passive.

The last chain was never the prison — it was the doubt. Your speed increases by an additional 10 ft, your Fury maximum becomes 2 × PB, and Nerve Strike is no longer limited to once per turn per creature. When you spend Fury on an unarmed strike, the first Fury spent each turn costs 0 — the leopard's first bite is always free.

Innate Technique Feats

Build-defining options.

These are optional character-building feats. You must meet each listed prerequisite before selecting it.

Prerequisite: Nerve Strike

Deadened Nerves

The same hand that locks a nerve can quiet one. As an action, you may lay Nerve Strike's touch on a willing creature (no saving throw, no CE cost): it gains temporary hit points equal to your level + your DEX modifier and suppresses the effects of one level of exhaustion for 1 hour. Mercy, delivered with the same precision as cruelty.

Prerequisite: Leopard Blitz

Run Up the Walls

A straight line is a suggestion. When you take the Dash action (including Leopard Blitz's bonus-action Dash), you may move across vertical surfaces and liquids without falling, provided you end the movement on solid ground — momentum is just another kind of floor. The palace walls learned this first.

Prerequisite: Scroll-Denied Fury

Denied Again

His fury was never only his. When an ally within 30 ft misses with an attack, or a creature succeeds on a saving throw against an ally's feature, you may use your reaction to gain 1 Fury — their denial feeds you too. Twenty years of practice being angry on everyone's behalf.

Prerequisite: "You Don't Know"

Not Done Yet

Refusing to fall is, itself, an attack. When "You Don't Know" triggers, each hostile creature within 15 ft that can see you must make a WIS save against your CE save DC or be frightened of you until the end of your next turn. The leopard gets up. Everyone watches.
