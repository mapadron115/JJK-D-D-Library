Golden Slumber

Field notes. Subject: the Sandman, Guardian of Sleep. He has never spoken a word, and the technique has never needed him to. Two truths govern it. First: sleep is not surrender — it is the one country every living thing visits nightly, and he is its oldest border guard; nothing crosses into a sleeper's mind without passing him first. Second: dreams are medicine — a good dream knits what the waking day tore, and his sand is the needle. Pitch Black taught the world what nightmares are for. The Sandman is the older answer: the night was kind first, and he intends to keep it that way.

Feature map

Level-by-level features, as the figure refined them.

L1

Dreamsand

Action · 2 CE · 1 minute.

Shape golden dreamsand into a protective form for 1 minute (one at a time): a shield (you or an adjacent ally gain half cover), a rope (50 ft, supports 500 lb), a net (restrain one Medium or smaller creature; it escapes with a STR check vs your CE DC), or a lantern (sheds bright light in a 20-ft radius and dim light for 20 ft beyond). The sand is warm to the touch and cannot be shaped into a weapon — it never learned how.

L1

Lullaby Sand

Action · 3 CE · one creature within 30 ft.

Golden sand settles over the target's eyes. It makes a WIS save vs your CE DC: on a failure it falls into a deep, peaceful slumber — unconscious for 1 minute. Any damage wakes it; a creature can use its action to wake it gently, no check needed. While it slumbers this way it cannot be frightened or charmed — the dream holds it safe. A creature that does not sleep is unaffected.

L3

Good Dreams

Action · 3 CE · up to PB willing creatures within 30 ft.

You lay a good dream over each target like a blanket: each gains temporary hit points equal to 1d8 + your WIS modifier (2d8 + WIS at 11th level), and you end either the frightened or the charmed condition on each of them — the good dream replaces the bad one.

L6

Guardian's Watch

Passive.

You keep watch in your sleep. When you take a long rest, your dreamsand drifts over sleeping allies within 60 ft: they cannot be surprised while they sleep, and you wake instantly — no groggy round — the moment a hostile creature enters that radius. You still gain the rest's full benefit.

L11

Dreamcraft

Action · 4 CE · 1 hour.

Conjure a Large companion of golden sand that lasts 1 hour — choose its form: a Dreamsteed (rideable, speed 60 ft; its rider cannot be frightened or charmed while mounted), a Sandskiff (a floating platform carrying up to four creatures, fly 40 ft), or a Sentinel (stands guard; as a reaction when an adjacent ally is attacked, it interposes and the attack targets the Sentinel instead; it has hit points equal to 5 × your level). It acts on your initiative under your command and can take the Dash, Disengage, or Help action. It cannot attack — it was never built for that.

L14

Sand-Speech

Passive · wisp: action, 2 CE.

You have never spoken, and never needed to. As a passive, you shape words and images in golden sand to communicate with any creature that can see you within 60 ft — no shared language required; the images carry the meaning. As an action (2 CE), release a sand-wisp that travels up to 1 mile and returns within 10 minutes, replaying what it witnessed as silent moving sand-images.

Maximum Technique

The Golden Dawn

Action · 10 CE.

Golden sand rises over the battlefield like a sunrise. Each hostile creature within 60 ft makes a WIS save vs your CE DC: on a failure it falls into a deep slumber — unconscious for 1 minute (damage wakes it; an ally can wake it with an action). Each ally within 60 ft ends the frightened, charmed, and blinded conditions on itself and gains temporary hit points equal to 3d8 + your WIS modifier. The night was kind first. This is the proof.

Level 20 · Capstone

The Eternal Guardian

Passive · 1/long rest reaction.

You are the last light that never goes out. While you are conscious, allies within 30 ft of you cannot be frightened or charmed — your dreams guard theirs. Once per long rest, when an ally within 60 ft of you drops to 0 hit points, you may use your reaction: golden sand catches them — they drop to 1 hit point instead and fall into a peaceful slumber for 1 minute (unconscious; an ally can wake them with an action). While they slumber, they regain 1d8 + your WIS modifier hit points at the start of each of their turns.

Innate Technique Feats

Build-defining options.

These are optional character-building feats. You must meet each listed prerequisite before selecting it.

Prerequisite: Dreamsand

Sandwright's Hands

Golden sand, given a moment, learns any trade. When you shape Dreamsand, you may instead shape any set of artisan's tools or mundane adventuring gear — thieves' tools, a climber's kit, a healer's kit (3 uses), a disguise kit — that lasts for 1 hour. The sand remembers the shape of useful things.

Prerequisite: Lullaby Sand

The Restful Hour

Ten quiet minutes under your sand count as a full short rest. As a ritual (10 minutes, 2 CE), you settle up to PB willing creatures into restorative naps: each may spend hit dice as though finishing a short rest. A creature can benefit once per long rest. Even warriors need tucking in.

Prerequisite: Guardian's Watch

Nightlight

You keep a small lantern-wisp of golden sand — a firefly that never burns out. It follows you (or one ally you choose), shedding bright light in a 20-ft radius. While an ally stands in its light, it has advantage on saving throws against being frightened or charmed. Where your light falls, the dark has no vote.

Prerequisite: Dreamcraft

The Sandship

When you conjure your Dreamcraft, you may shape it as a great sand-vessel — part galleon, part dream. It carries up to six creatures and flies at 60 ft for the duration. Wonder, with seating.
