Feature map

Level-by-level features, as the figure refined them.

L1

Inner Peace

Passive · Stillness pool (maximum 2 × PB).

You carry a pool of Stillness (maximum 2 × PB; it begins at 0). At the start of each of your turns, if you took no damage since the start of your last turn, gain 1 Stillness. As a bonus action you may Center yourself: you cannot attack until the start of your next turn, and you gain 2 Stillness. You may spend Stillness on this technique's features; unspent Stillness fades when combat ends.

L3

Skadoosh

Action · 3 CE + 1 Stillness.

You release a shockwave of chi in a 15-ft cone. Each creature in the area makes a DEX save against your CE save DC: 2d8 + your WIS modifier force damage on a failure, half as much on a success, and failures are pushed 10 ft away from you. At 11th level the cone becomes 20 ft and the damage becomes 3d8 + your WIS modifier.

L6

Panda Style

Passive.

Your body is the weapon and the room is the arsenal. Improvised weapons you wield deal 1d8 + STR bludgeoning damage, and you may treat any unattended object within 5 ft as drawn without spending an action. When you take bludgeoning damage from a melee attack or a fall, you may use your reaction to halve it and bounce up to 10 ft in a straight line without provoking opportunity attacks.

L11

Dumpling-Fueled Recovery

Bonus action · 2 CE + 1 Stillness · requires food.

You inhale a meal mid-battle and fight on the fuel. Regain hit points equal to 1d8 + your WIS modifier + your level, and end one of the following on yourself: the poisoned condition, or one level of exhaustion not caused by this feature. You must have rations, a dumpling, or equivalent food on hand — this feature consumes it.

L15

The Scroll Is Blank

Passive.

There is no secret ingredient — it is just you. You are immune to being frightened, and whenever you succeed on a saving throw against being charmed you gain 1 Stillness. Once per long rest, when you fail a WIS or CHA saving throw, you may choose to succeed instead — the Dragon Warrior believes in himself now.

L18

Chi Master

Passive.

Your Stillness maximum becomes 3 × PB. As a bonus action, spend any amount of Stillness: one ally within 30 ft gains temporary hit points equal to 5 × the Stillness spent — your chi steadies them. You may also spend 2 Stillness as a bonus action to end the blinded, deafened, poisoned, or restrained condition on yourself.

Pinnacle expression

Maximum, Domain, or equivalent.

Capstone material.

Maximum Technique

Wuxi Finger Hold

Action · 10 CE · melee, one creature.

The legendary pinky hold. Make an unarmed strike; on a hit, the target makes a CON save against your CE save DC at disadvantage. On a failure, chi detonates outward from the held point: 8d10 force damage, and the target is stunned until the end of your next turn. On a success, half damage and no stun. You whisper: "Skadoosh." A creature reduced to 0 hit points by this Maximum disperses into golden petals — no corpse remains.

Level 20 · Capstone

True Dragon Warrior

Passive · 1/long rest nova · bonus action · 1 minute.

You stop performing the Dragon Warrior and simply are one. Your Stillness no longer fades when combat ends. Once per long rest, as a bonus action, enter the Dragon Warrior state for 1 minute: Skadoosh costs no Stillness and its cone becomes 30 ft; Panda Style's bounce requires no reaction; and the first time each round you would spend Stillness, you spend 0 instead. When the state ends, your Stillness empties — even legends must eat.

Innate Technique Feats

Build-defining options.

These are optional character-building feats. You must meet each listed prerequisite before selecting it.

Prerequisite: Inner Peace

The Pond Is Calm

Stillness is contagious. When you Center yourself, each ally within 15 ft who can see you gains advantage on their next saving throw against being frightened or charmed before the start of your next turn — they borrow your breathing. Still water steadies every boat.

Prerequisite: Skadoosh

Skadoosh, Downward

The shockwave does not care which way is up. When you use Skadoosh, you may aim the cone straight down instead: you take no damage, are launched 20 ft straight up, and land safely without falling damage, while each creature within 10 ft of your launch point must save or be knocked prone. Getting there is half the battle.

Prerequisite: Panda Style

The Room Fights Too

When you hit a creature with an improvised weapon, you may shatter it against them: the attack deals an extra 1d6 bludgeoning damage, and you choose a second creature within 10 ft of the target, which takes 1d6 bludgeoning damage as the debris finds it. The Dragon Warrior never fights alone — the furniture helps.

Prerequisite: Dumpling-Fueled Recovery

Secret Ingredient Soup

Ten quiet minutes and a pot. During a short rest you may cook for up to PB creatures: each creature that eats regains an extra 1d8 hit points and gains advantage on its next saving throw against poison or disease within the next 24 hours. There is no secret ingredient — but there is soup.
