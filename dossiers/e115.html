Blacklight

Biomass / consumption / shapeshifting / viral weapons

Power tierTier V · Calamity
Core law

What the technique actually does.

Passive · Biomass max = 5 × level · Consuming defeated biological creatures restores Biomass (5 minor, 10 standard, 20 elite; the GM prevents farming) · Biomass fuels healing, consumed-form traits, disguise, and utility — never direct damage.

Core Engine, Biomass. The currency is the fallen. Biomass maxes at five per level, and every point of it was once something living, understand that before spending it on anything else. Biomass max = 5 × level. Consuming defeated biological creatures restores Biomass by threat (5 minor, 10 standard, 20 elite; GM prevents farming). Biomass fuels healing, consumed-form traits, disguise, and utility forms, it is never spent on direct damage.

Field notes. Subject: hunger that learned to write. Three truths govern him. First: the meal is never fuel, it is identity; the biomass is memory, and every form he wears is a creature he has eaten, described in its own voice. Second: his economy runs backward, Biomass spends on healing, armor, and disguise, never on direct damage; the claws are already his, the healing must be bought. Third: disguise is his deepest appetite, he does not wear the face he ate, he becomes the life that wore it, minus only what the registry forbids him to copy. Mark this: he is not a fighter who eats. He is an appetite that learned, one corpse at a time, how to be a man.

Full progression

Level 1 through 20.

1Consume

Action · Target a helpless or 0-HP biological creature: Consume it, gaining Biomass (5/10/20 by threat) and one Disguise memory profile · You may spend up to your PB in Biomass per turn to heal an equal number of HP.

Everything begins at the helpless and the fallen. The Consume is his first and final lesson: the dead are not wasted, they are filed. Note the registry's footnote, the GM watches the farm. As an action, target a helpless or 0-HP biological creature: you Consume it, gaining Biomass (5 for a minor threat, 10 for a standard threat, 20 for an elite; your GM prevents farming) and one Disguise memory profile. You may spend up to your proficiency bonus in Biomass per turn to heal an equal number of hit points.

3Claws / Musclemass

Bonus action · Switch between consumed forms: Claws (1d8 slashing, and you can climb with them) or Musclemass (unarmed strikes deal 1d10, advantage on Athletics checks).

Two drafts of the same author: the climber's hooks or the laborer's shoulders, worn on a bonus action. Describe each as something eaten, the technique insists on the citation. As a bonus action, switch between your consumed forms: Claws (1d8 slashing, and you can climb with them) or Musclemass (your unarmed strikes deal 1d10, and you have advantage on Athletics checks). Each form is a trait of something you have eaten, describe it as such.

6Blade / Hammerfist

Passive · Blade form: 1d12 slashing · Hammerfist: 1d10; for 2 CE you can slam the ground — creatures in a 10-ft radius make a Str save or fall prone and are pushed 10 ft (the slam deals no damage to creatures); structures and unattended objects take 3d8 bludgeoning.

The drafts grow ambitious: a twelve-sided argument, or a hammer that shakes the ground without touching the men standing on it. Precision is the luxury of the well-fed. You gain two new consumed forms. Blade: a 1d12 slashing form. (The old spend of +2d8 damage per CE is removed.) Hammerfist: 1d10; for 2 CE you can slam the ground, creatures in a 10-ft radius make a Strength save or fall prone and are pushed 10 ft; structures and unattended objects take 3d8 bludgeoning damage. The slam deals no damage to creatures.

9Whipfist

Passive · Whipfist form: 20-ft reach, 1d8 damage; on a hit you may spend 1 CE to grapple the target and pull it 20 ft · Shield form: as a reaction, spend 5 Biomass to reduce damage you take by 2d10 + your level.

Reach is appetite extended. Twenty feet of tendril that can fetch what it touches, and the Shield form, which is the appetite learning to refuse. You gain the Whipfist form: 20-ft reach, 1d8 damage; on a hit you may spend 1 CE to grapple the target and pull it 20 ft. You also gain the Shield form: as a reaction, spend 5 Biomass to reduce damage you take by 2d10 + your level.

11Perfect Disguise

Passive · Consumed Disguise profiles copy the target’s appearance, voice, mundane memories, and proficiencies · They never copy cursed techniques, CE pools, Domains, souls, or supernatural bloodline features.

The impersonation matures: appearance, voice, even the borrowed life's mundane memories, everything except what cannot be eaten: technique, energy, Domain, soul. Your consumed Disguise profiles now copy the target’s appearance, voice, and mundane memories and proficiencies. They never copy cursed techniques, CE pools, Domains, souls, or supernatural bloodline features.

13Armor

Bonus action · 10 Biomass · 1 minute: +3 AC, resistance to bludgeoning, piercing, and slashing, speed reduced by 10 ft · Critical melee hits against you shed 1d4 Biomass.

He wears the meal as a fortress. Ten Biomass for a minute of shell, the price paid in what he has consumed, to keep consuming. For 10 Biomass, as a bonus action, grow armor for 1 minute: +3 AC, resistance to bludgeoning, piercing, and slashing, and your speed is reduced by 10 ft. Critical melee hits against you shed 1d4 Biomass.

15Viral Ascendancy

Action · 5 CE · 1 minute: you may Consume as a bonus action at 30 ft (target helpless or 0 HP), and you may hold up to three consumed profiles’ traits at once, swapping between them as a bonus action.

The author holds three drafts at once. For five CE he stops choosing between the things he has eaten, and the Consume itself becomes a bonus action at thirty feet. For 5 CE, for 1 minute: you may Consume as a bonus action at 30 ft (the target must be helpless or at 0 HP), and you may hold up to three consumed profiles’ traits at once, swapping between them as a bonus action.

18Critical Mass

Passive · While you hold 75+ Biomass: your attacks deal +1d8 damage and your speed increases by 10 ft · When reduced to 0 HP, spend 30 Biomass to remain at 1 HP instead (once per round).

At seventy-five Biomass he stops being a man carrying an appetite and becomes the appetite carrying a man. The extra die, the speed, and one refusal of death per round, priced at thirty. While you hold 75 or more Biomass, your attacks deal +1d8 damage and your speed increases by 10 ft. When you are reduced to 0 HP, you may spend 30 Biomass to remain at 1 HP instead; you can do this once per round.

20Blacklight Apex

Passive · Biomass maximum doubles · Once per turn you may switch forms freely · You may Consume elite targets as a bonus action at 0 HP · Once per long rest, if your body is destroyed you may reform from 50 Biomass, unless your soul or concept has been erased.

The apex predator's math: the Biomass maximum doubles, forms change freely, elites are eaten at a thought, and once per long rest, even destruction is negotiable. Only erasure of the soul is final. Your Biomass maximum doubles. Once per turn you may switch forms freely. You may Consume elite targets as a bonus action when they are at 0 HP. Once per long rest, if your body is destroyed, you may reform from 50 Biomass, unless your soul or concept has been erased.

Pinnacle expression

Maximum, Domain, or equivalent.

Maximum, Viral Cataclysm (10 CE + one consumed elite profile, expended): release a 50-ft-radius sphere viral bloom grown from the consumed elite's biomass. Each creature in the area makes a Constitution save; on failure it is infected with Viral Mark for 1 minute (no damage, the bloom is the vector, not the weapon). When an infected creature drops to 0 HP, you may Consume it from 30 ft as a reaction and gain one of its traits for 1 hour. Success: no infection.

Feats

Build-defining options.

Parkour Predator

The city is a second meal. He climbs without hands and leaps from the climb, the predator reads architecture the way the scholar reads a page. You gain a climb speed equal to your walking speed, and your claws let you climb sheer surfaces without needing free hands. When you take the Dash action, your long jump distance is doubled, and you may launch a jump directly out of a climb without landing first.

Patsy

He consumes intentions along with flesh. Five Biomass to reproduce a memory under questioning, the dead testify for him now. You consume intentions along with flesh: when you Consume a creature, you learn one short-term goal or plan it held (GM's discretion), and you may spend 5 Biomass to perfectly reproduce one specific memory under questioning for 10 minutes.

Adaptive Biomass

His body eats what hurts it. Five Biomass to learn the wound, and the next strike of that kind feeds him instead of felling him. Your body eats what hurts it: when you take damage of a type, you may spend 5 Biomass as a reaction to analyze it, the next time you take that damage type this combat, you may convert up to 10 of it into Biomass instead of losing HP.

Pack Leader

He tears loose a piece of himself and it learns to hunt. Fifteen Biomass for a hound with his proficiency in its teeth, the pack is the man, divided. As an action, spend 15 Biomass to tear free a piece of yourself that grows into an infected hound: use the wolf stat block, but it has 1 HP and adds your PB to its attack and damage rolls. It obeys your commands and acts immediately after you in initiative; after 10 minutes, or when reduced to 0 HP, it collapses into 5 Biomass you may reabsorb by touching it.

Rules integration

Edge cases and shared systems.

Shared rulings: Technique DC = 8 + PB + CE ability modifier. Direct rolled attacks may crit and use the campaign Black Flash procedure; autonomous hazards and Domain events cannot. Maximum Output may modify ordinary supported features, never named Maxima/Domains. Two-technique characters use the campaign's agreed character-level progression.
