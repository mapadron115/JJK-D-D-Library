Power tierTier V · Calamity
Core law

What the technique actually does.

Core Engine — Biomass. Biomass max = 5 × level. Consuming defeated biological creatures restores Biomass based on threat (5 minor, 10 standard, 20 elite; GM prevents farming). Biomass fuels healing, forms, and Devastators.

Full progression

Level 1 through 20.

1Consume

Action on helpless/0-HP biological target: gain Biomass and one Disguise memory profile. Spend up to PB Biomass/turn to heal equal HP.

3Claws / Musclemass

Bonus action switch form. Claws 1d8 slashing + climbing; Musclemass unarmed 1d10 and advantage Athletics.

6Blade / Hammerfist

Blade 1d12 slashing, +2d8 once/turn for 1 CE. Hammerfist 1d10; 2 CE ground slam 10-ft 3d8 and prone.

9Whipfist

20-ft reach, 1d8; on hit spend 1 CE to grapple/pull 20 ft. Shield form reaction spend 5 Biomass reduce damage 2d10+level.

11Perfect Disguise

Consumed profiles copy appearance, voice, mundane memories/proficiencies. They never copy CTs, CE pools, Domains, souls, or supernatural bloodline features.

13Armor

10 Biomass, bonus action, 1 minute: +3 AC, resistance B/P/S, speed -10. Critical melee hits against you shed 1d4 Biomass.

15Devastators

5 CE + 20 Biomass: Tendril Barrage (30-ft radius 8d10 piercing Dex half) or Groundspike Graveyard (40-ft cone 8d10 piercing, prone on fail).

18Critical Mass

At 75+ Biomass, attacks +1d8 and speed +10. When reduced to 0 HP, spend 30 Biomass to remain at 1 HP once/round.

20Blacklight Apex

Biomass max doubles. Switch forms freely once/turn. Consume elite targets as bonus action when at 0 HP. Once/long rest reform after bodily destruction from 50 Biomass unless soul/concept erased.

Pinnacle expression

Maximum, Domain, or equivalent.

Maximum — Viral Cataclysm (10 CE + 40 Biomass): 50-ft radius eruption, Con save 12d10 necrotic/piercing, half success. Failure infects target with Viral Mark for 1 minute; when it drops to 0 HP you may Consume it from 30 ft as reaction.

Feats

Build-defining options.

Parkour Predator:

climb speed = speed and long jumps doubled.

Patsy:

advantage on deception while using consumed identity.

Adaptive Biomass:

after taking same damage type 3 times, resistance until combat ends once/long rest.

Pack Leader:

create one weak infected companion from 15 Biomass for 10 minutes.

Rules integration

Edge cases and shared systems.

Shared rulings: Technique DC = 8 + PB + CE ability modifier. Direct rolled attacks may crit and use the campaign Black Flash procedure; autonomous hazards and Domain events cannot. Maximum Output may modify ordinary supported features, never named Maxima/Domains. Two-technique characters use the campaign's agreed character-level progression.
