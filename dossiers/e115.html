Blacklight

Biomass / consumption / shapeshifting / viral weapons

Power tierTier V · Calamity
Core law

What the technique actually does.

Core Engine — Biomass. Biomass max = 5 × level. Consuming defeated biological creatures restores Biomass by threat (5 minor, 10 standard, 20 elite; GM prevents farming). Biomass fuels healing, consumed-form traits, disguise, and utility forms — it is never spent on direct damage.

Full progression

Level 1 through 20.

1Consume

As an action, target a helpless or 0-HP biological creature: you Consume it, gaining Biomass (5 for a minor threat, 10 for a standard threat, 20 for an elite; your GM prevents farming) and one Disguise memory profile. You may spend up to your proficiency bonus in Biomass per turn to heal an equal number of hit points.

3Claws / Musclemass

As a bonus action, switch between your consumed forms: Claws (1d8 slashing, and you can climb with them) or Musclemass (your unarmed strikes deal 1d10, and you have advantage on Athletics checks). Each form is a trait of something you have eaten — describe it as such.

6Blade / Hammerfist

You gain two new consumed forms. Blade: a 1d12 slashing form. (The old spend of +2d8 damage per CE is removed.) Hammerfist: 1d10; for 2 CE you can slam the ground — creatures in a 10-ft radius make a Strength save or fall prone and are pushed 10 ft; structures and unattended objects take 3d8 bludgeoning damage. The slam deals no damage to creatures.

9Whipfist

You gain the Whipfist form: 20-ft reach, 1d8 damage; on a hit you may spend 1 CE to grapple the target and pull it 20 ft. You also gain the Shield form: as a reaction, spend 5 Biomass to reduce damage you take by 2d10 + your level.

11Perfect Disguise

Your consumed Disguise profiles now copy the target’s appearance, voice, and mundane memories and proficiencies. They never copy cursed techniques, CE pools, Domains, souls, or supernatural bloodline features.

13Armor

For 10 Biomass, as a bonus action, grow armor for 1 minute: +3 AC, resistance to bludgeoning, piercing, and slashing, and your speed is reduced by 10 ft. Critical melee hits against you shed 1d4 Biomass.

15Viral Ascendancy

For 5 CE, for 1 minute: you may Consume as a bonus action at 30 ft (the target must be helpless or at 0 HP), and you may hold up to three consumed profiles’ traits at once, swapping between them as a bonus action.

18Critical Mass

While you hold 75 or more Biomass, your attacks deal +1d8 damage and your speed increases by 10 ft. When you are reduced to 0 HP, you may spend 30 Biomass to remain at 1 HP instead; you can do this once per round.

20Blacklight Apex

Your Biomass maximum doubles. Once per turn you may switch forms freely. You may Consume elite targets as a bonus action when they are at 0 HP. Once per long rest, if your body is destroyed, you may reform from 50 Biomass — unless your soul or concept has been erased.

Pinnacle expression

Maximum, Domain, or equivalent.

Maximum — Viral Cataclysm (10 CE + one consumed elite profile, expended): release a 50-ft viral bloom grown from the consumed elite's biomass. Each creature in the area makes a Constitution save; on failure it is infected with Viral Mark for 1 minute (no damage — the bloom is the vector, not the weapon). When an infected creature drops to 0 HP, you may Consume it from 30 ft as a reaction and gain one of its traits for 1 hour. Success: no infection.

Feats

Build-defining options.

Parkour Predator

You gain a climb speed equal to your walking speed, and your claws let you climb sheer surfaces without needing free hands. When you take the Dash action, your long jump distance is doubled, and you may launch a jump directly out of a climb without landing first.

Patsy

You consume intentions along with flesh: when you Consume a creature, you learn one short-term goal or plan it held (GM's discretion), and you may spend 5 Biomass to perfectly reproduce one specific memory under questioning for 10 minutes.

Adaptive Biomass

Your body eats what hurts it: when you take damage of a type, you may spend 5 Biomass as a reaction to analyze it — the next time you take that damage type this combat, you may convert up to 10 of it into Biomass instead of losing HP.

Pack Leader

As an action, spend 15 Biomass to tear free a piece of yourself that grows into an infected hound: use the wolf stat block, but it has 1 HP and adds your PB to its attack and damage rolls. It obeys your commands and acts immediately after you in initiative; after 10 minutes, or when reduced to 0 HP, it collapses into 5 Biomass you may reabsorb by touching it.

Rules integration

Edge cases and shared systems.

Shared rulings: Technique DC = 8 + PB + CE ability modifier. Direct rolled attacks may crit and use the campaign Black Flash procedure; autonomous hazards and Domain events cannot. Maximum Output may modify ordinary supported features, never named Maxima/Domains. Two-technique characters use the campaign's agreed character-level progression.
