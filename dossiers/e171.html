Bestiary Technique #09

Proof Without Capture

Every sighting leaves evidence—and every piece of evidence becomes a resource for disappearing better, corrupting pursuit, and manufacturing the wrong trail.

InspirationBigfoot / Sasquatch
RoleScout / Stealth Skirmisher
TierTier II · Versatile
Core statDEX or WIS
Scaling dieEvidence Die

Character fantasy

Every sighting leaves evidence—and every piece of evidence becomes a resource for disappearing better, corrupting pursuit, and manufacturing the wrong trail.

Combat loop

  1. Allow witnesses to glimpse you, then break line of sight.
  2. Turn sightings, tracks, and residue into Evidence.
  3. Spend Evidence to Hide, falsify trails, or create decoy sightings.
  4. Stay elusive enough that pursuit continually feeds your stealth engine.

Evidence

  • Evidence cap: PB + 2. Gain 1 Evidence once per creature per encounter when it clearly sees you and you later break its line of sight for at least one full turn. You may also gain 1 when a creature succeeds on a check to track you. Evidence vanishes on long rest.
  • Impossible Specimen: your tracks, hair, silhouette, and CE residue are always just ambiguous enough to be argued over. This grants no automatic invisibility; it fuels the features below.

Innate Technique FeatsOptional character-building feats

Wood Ape

Gain proficiency in Athletics and Survival; if already proficient in either, gain expertise in that skill.

Tree Line

You may climb natural surfaces at full speed and gain half cover while at least 10 feet above the ground among dense branches/foliage.

No Clear Photo

A creature using a divination, camera, scope, or recording device to identify you must beat your CE DC with its relevant check if you have at least 1 Evidence.

Track the Tracker

When a creature succeeds on tracking you and grants Evidence, learn its direction and approximate distance for 1 hour.

Building the character

Scout is ideal. This technique is not invisibility—it is pursuit warfare. It shines in wilderness missions, urban rooftops, and any campaign where scouting matters.

Technique die: Evidence Die follows the shared d6 → d8 → d10 → d12 progression.

Counterplay

Maintain uninterrupted line of sight, use area denial, coordinate trackers, and prevent the user from repeatedly cycling sight → disappearance.

Adaptation note. This is original JJK 5e homebrew inspired by Bigfoot / Sasquatch. The myth/folklore provides imagery and thematic rules; the combat engine, progression, CE economy, feats, and Domain/Maximum procedures are game design for this codex.
