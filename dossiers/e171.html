Core law

Character fantasy

Every sighting leaves evidence—and every piece of evidence becomes a resource for disappearing better, corrupting pursuit, and manufacturing the wrong trail.

Combat loop

Sighting, evidence, trail, vanish, the cryptid's fourfold evasion.

  1. Allow witnesses to glimpse you, then break line of sight.
  2. Turn sightings, tracks, and residue into Evidence.
  3. Spend Evidence to Hide, falsify trails, or create decoy sightings.
  4. Stay elusive enough that pursuit continually feeds your stealth engine.

Field notes. Subject: cursed energy that learned to be a rumor. I have followed the false trails and found they all point backward, toward a creature that was never quite there. Three truths govern it. First: being seen is the fuel, Evidence is gained when a creature clearly sees you and you later break its sight, or when it succeeds at tracking you; the glimpse becomes the disappearance. Second: ambiguity is the armor, the Impossible Specimen's tracks, hair, and residue are always just arguable enough, and every failed attempt to pin the rumor down feeds it. Third: the trail points both ways, False Trails three hundred feet long, decoy sightings that punish investigation, and a Maximum that ends the argument entirely. Mark the final ruling: no specimen exists, because the technique spent every sighting proving it.

Feats

Build-defining options.

Ambush Sign

Designate ten feet of False Trail as the ambush point, the rumor bites. When you create a False Trail, you may designate one 10-foot segment of it as your ambush point. The first hostile creature to willingly enter that segment provokes an opportunity attack from you if you are hidden within 10 feet of it.

Tree Line

Climb at full speed; the high branches grant half cover. You may climb natural surfaces at full speed and gain half cover while at least 10 feet above the ground among dense branches/foliage.

No Clear Photo

Divination, camera, scope, recorder, all must beat your DC while you hold Evidence. A creature using a divination, camera, scope, or recording device to identify you must beat your CE DC with its relevant check if you have at least 1 Evidence.

Track the Tracker

Whoever tracks you grants Evidence, and tells you where they are for an hour. When a creature succeeds on tracking you and grants Evidence, learn its direction and approximate distance for 1 hour.

Rules integration

Building the character

Scout is ideal. This technique is not invisibility—it is pursuit warfare. It shines in wilderness missions, urban rooftops, and any campaign where scouting matters.
Rules integration

Counterplay

Maintain uninterrupted line of sight, use area denial, coordinate trackers, and prevent the user from repeatedly cycling sight → disappearance.
