Feature map

1Pain Killer.

Starting at 1st level, you can use your technique to stop injuries from getting worse. As a bonus action for 1 CE, you can make a symbol with your hand and touch a creature within 5 feet. If a creature is taking damage over time, you cause that damage to stop and the creature gains temporary hit points equal to your CE modifier + your level for 1 hour. This does not stop new effects from affecting the target. You also gain proficiency in Medicine.

3Practiced Hands.

At 3rd level, you can use Pain Killer on all willing creatures within 5 feet as a free action on your turn, spending 1 CE for each creature. This increases to 10 feet at 6th level and 15 feet at 11th level.

6Pain Breaker.

At 6th level, when you use Pain Killer on a creature, they also lose one condition of your choice and if they are making death saves they are immediately stabilized. You now gain Expertise in Medicine.

11Analgesic.

At 11th level, when a creature that you can see within 10 feet would take damage, as a reaction for 4 CE you can halve the damage.

18Domain Expansion: Medical Clinic.

Finally at 18th level you have learned to expand your domain. At initiative 20, you and all creatures of your choice fall under the effects of Pain Killer. You can use Analgesic as a free action for no cursed energy without a reaction, but only once per damage instance.

Pinnacle expression

Maximum, Domain, or equivalent.

Maximum: Panacea: feat-gated (prerequisite: Analgesic) — as a reaction to a creature within 10 feet (including yourself) falling to 0 hit points you can spend 4 CE and cause them to instead remain at 10 hit points and gain the benefits of Pain Killer.

Domain Expansion: Medical Clinic: 18 — at initiative 20, you and all creatures of your choice fall under the effects of Pain Killer. You can use Analgesic as a free action for no cursed energy without a reaction, but only once per damage instance.

Innate Technique Feats

Build-defining options.

These are optional character-building feats. You must meet each listed prerequisite before selecting it.

Prerequisite: Must have Pain Killer

Dull Pain

You can dull pain before it ever lands. As a bonus action for 1 CE, you can touch a creature and numb its nerves for 1 hour — it gains advantage on saving throws against being frightened and on Constitution saving throws made to maintain concentration, as pain can no longer distract it.

Prerequisite: Must have Practiced Hands

Field Poultice

You have learned to pack your medicine to go. As an action for 2 CE, you can prepare a poultice of cursed energy that holds your technique. A creature can use an action to press the poultice onto itself or another creature within 5 ft, gaining the effects of Pain Killer for 1 hour. The poultice loses its potency after 24 hours.

Prerequisite: Must have Analgesic

Maximum: Panacea

As a reaction to a creature within 10 feet (including yourself) falling to 0 hit points you can spend 4 CE and cause them to instead remain at 10 hit points and gain the benefits of Pain Killer.

Rules integration

Edge cases and shared systems.

Rules priority: the reviewed rulings, explicit codex clarifications, and the level progression always control when original wording conflicts. Legacy Domain auto-hit language, action terminology, event timing, missing saves, and older scaling never override revised player rules.
Free action read: "free action" is the campaign's free action (no action cost, once per turn).
Domain sure-hit scope (unresolved HOLD): the Domain says creatures "fall under the effects of Pain Killer," but Pain Breaker's condition removal and death-save stabilization are a separate 6th-level feature. It is unclear whether the Domain's guaranteed effect includes Pain Breaker's removal/stabilization or only the 1st-level feature's damage-stop and THP. Do not assume; needs a ruling before play.
THP stacking: temporary hit points from repeated applications do not stack per the standard THP rules.
Domain conversion: initiative-20 timing already matches; clashes use the deterministic 1–1,000 point system; barrier statistics per D007; burnout per D010. The sure-hit is the base Pain Killer effect (damage-over-time stop plus THP) applied to the user and chosen creatures; Analgesic's free, costless, reactionless use is kept as printed, limited to once per damage instance.
