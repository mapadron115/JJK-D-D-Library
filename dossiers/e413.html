Cursed Lantern

A Grade 3 cursed item, a small paper lantern inlaid with paper tags.

FormCursed Item
GradeGrade 3
AttunementNot required

Field notes. Subject: a paper watchman that never blinks. Three truths govern the lantern. First, vigilance can be bought for a copper's worth of fuel: 1 cursed energy buys an hour of light, the cheapest sentry a sorcerer can hire. Second, the lantern speaks two dialects, it glows brighter within 30 feet of cursed energy, and sputters violently within 30 feet of a cursed spirit, shikigami, or cursed corpse, the difference between a footprint and the foot that made it. Third, it watches without watching: the detection is passive, asking no hand no eye, so the bearer can fight while the lamp keeps guard. Trust the sputter more than the glow; residual energy is history, but a sputtering lamp is the present tense.
