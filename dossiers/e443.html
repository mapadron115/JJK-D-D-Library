Cursed Tool

Collar of the Chained

A cursed collar that quickens the cursed spirit or shikigami wearing it.

Field notes. Subject: a leash that lends wings. Three truths govern it. First, the collar serves no sorcerer directly: it quickens the cursed spirit or shikigami that wears it, ten feet of walking speed, which is to say it quickens what you command, power lent rather than owned. Second, mark the paradox, for it is the whole doctrine: the instrument of restraint exists to grant speed. The bound beast runs faster for being bound. Third, there is no attunement and no cost to pay; this is logistics, not tactics. It asks nothing and gives exactly one thing. Hence the maxim: the master who quickens his hounds quickens his own victory.
