Osmothèque

Distill emotions, memories, and biological traces into supernatural perfumes. Prepare formulas before battle, then deploy them as buffs, debuffs, tracking tools, and sensory tricks.

Power tierHigh

The fiction. Registry scholars describe Osmotheque as memory with a sense of smell: the user's cursed energy learned to read the emotional residue everyone leaves behind, and distilled it into something the hands can carry. The formulas work because nobody ever finished grieving — the grief is simply bottled instead.

Level Progression

Main resource

Scent Notes

Scent Notes

After each long rest, prepare Scent Notes equal to your proficiency bonus + CE modifier (minimum 3). A Note is spent when you use a Formula. You may recover one Note during a short rest by spending 2 CE and ten minutes distilling nearby scents.

Scent Die

Your Scent Die scales d6 at 1st, d8 at 6th, d10 at 11th, d12 at 18th.

Formula — Courage Accord

Bonus Action • 1 Note + 1 CE • 30 feet. One willing creature gains a Scent Veil: temporary HP equal to your CE modifier + twice your proficiency bonus (a measured dose; no roll). It also has advantage on its next saving throw against frightened/charmed before the end of your next turn. Bloom: when the Veil is fully depleted by damage, it breaks loudly — each hostile within 10 feet of the veiled creature makes a Wisdom save vs your CE save DC or has disadvantage on its next attack roll before the end of its next turn. One bloom per Veil.

Formula — Funeral Myrrh

Action • 1 Note + 2 CE • 30 feet. One creature makes a Wisdom save. On failure subtract your Scent Die from its next attack roll or saving throw before the end of your next turn (minimum total result 1).

Formula — Hunter’s Trace

Bonus Action • 1 Note + 1 CE. Mark the scent of one creature/object you can perceive within 30 feet. For 1 hour you have advantage on checks to track it and know its direction while within 120 feet unless the trail is completely sealed.

3New Formulas —

Veil of Familiarity: 1 Note + 2 CE, one creature gains advantage on Deception/Stealth checks to pass as belonging in the immediate environment for 10 minutes (does not alter appearance). Anesthetic Fog: Action, 1 Note + 2 CE, 10-foot-radius cloud for 1 round; Constitution save or speed -10 and no reactions until next turn.

6Bottled Memory —

2 Notes + 3 CE, touch a willing creature/object and capture up to 1 minute of recent sensory impression. Releasing it recreates an illusory scent/sound/feeling replay in a 10-foot area for up to 1 minute. Also gain Counter-Scent: reaction, 1 Note + 2 CE, let an ally within 30 feet reroll a save against charmed/frightened/poisoned.

11Synesthetic Poison —

Action, 2 Notes + 4 CE, 30 feet. Constitution save; failure takes 2 Scent Dice poison/psychic damage and choose blinded, deafened, or speed halved until the end of its next turn. Success halves damage and avoids condition. Group Accord: Courage Accord may affect up to PB creatures if you spend one Note per target; each target gains its own Scent Veil and its own bloom.

18Maximum Technique: Perfect Accord —

Action, 10 CE + 3 Notes, 1 minute, 30-foot aura. When activated choose Courage, Dread, and Clarity. Allies entering/start in aura gain temporary HP equal to your CE modifier + twice your proficiency bonus once per round and advantage against frightened/charmed. Hostiles entering/start make Wisdom save or subtract one Scent Die from their first attack/save that round. You and allies also have advantage on Perception/Investigation relying on senses.

Innate Technique Feats

Technique Feats

Perfumer’s Case

You can decant a formula into a thrown vial: as an action, throw one prepared bottle up to 30 feet. It shatters on impact, applying its effect in a 10-foot radius (single-target formulas allow a Dexterity save for half). A thrown Courage Accord is a splash of fortitude: each creature in the radius that fails the Dexterity save gains half the Veil’s temporary HP (rounded down); every creature’s Scent Veil still blooms when depleted.

Cold Extraction

You can gather Hunter’s Trace from a scene up to 24 hours old if physical residue remains.

Acquired Taste

A creature that fails its save against one of your formulas develops a craving for it: for the next hour, you can use a bonus action and 1 CE to release a trace of that scent. The craving creature must make a Wisdom save or use its reaction to move up to half its speed toward you by the safest route.

Rules integration

Table Rulings

Formulas are cursed sensory effects; a creature without a normal sense of smell can still be affected unless it is explicitly immune to sensory or mind-affecting cursed techniques.
Hunter’s Trace gives direction, not exact distance or line of sight.
Bottled Memory reproduces sensory impressions, not objective truth; illusions, disguises, and mistaken perceptions remain part of the recording.
