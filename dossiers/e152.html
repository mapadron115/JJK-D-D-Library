Osmothèque

Distill emotions, memories, and biological traces into supernatural perfumes. Prepare formulas before battle, then deploy them as buffs, debuffs, tracking tools, and sensory tricks.

Power tierTier III · Apex

Field notes. Subject: the distiller of what was felt. Three truths govern her. First: emotion is material, Scent Notes prepared after rest become Courage Accord's temporary-HP veil, Funeral Myrrh's dulling pall, or Hunter's Trace's unerring direction. Second: preparation is power, every formula is bottled before the battle, because the nose cannot be hurried mid-strike. Third: the bouquet widens, at height she bottles memories whole, poisons the senses themselves, and the Maximum fills a thirty-foot aura with courage for allies and dread for enemies. Assess her correctly: she fights before the fight, in the laboratory of grief. Maxim: the nose remembers what the mind forgives.

Scent Notes

Passive · After each long rest, prepare Scent Notes equal to PB + CE modifier (min 3) · A Note is spent when you use a Formula · During a short rest, spend 2 CE and 10 minutes distilling nearby scents to recover 1 Note.

Her inventory is measured: proficiency plus resolve, minimum three, and even a short rest may distill one more from the air itself.

Scent Die

Passive · Your Scent Die scales d6 (1st) → d8 (6th) → d10 (11th) → d12 (18th).

The weight of a formula's effect, growing with mastery.

Formula — Courage Accord

The fortifying perfume: a veil of temporary health and a steadied nerve, and when the veil breaks, it blooms loudly into the enemy's face.

Formula — Funeral Myrrh

The dulling pall: one failed save, and the target's next attack or save carries the subtraction of her craft.

Formula — Hunter’s Trace

The tracking accord: the quarry's direction known, the nose does not forget what it has marked.

The cruelest accord: poison for the senses, and Courage Accord decanted for the whole party.

Maximum Technique · Action · 10 CE + 3 Notes · 30-ft aura, 1 minute · Choose Courage, Dread, and Clarity: allies entering or starting in the aura gain temp HP equal to your CE modifier + 2×PB (once per round) and advantage vs frightened/charmed; hostiles entering or starting inside make a Wisdom save or subtract 1 Scent Die from their first attack or save that round; you and allies have advantage on sense-based Perception/Investigation.

The maximum: courage, dread, and clarity in three accords; allies veiled and steadied, hostiles dulled, every sense sharpened.

Innate Technique Feats

Technique Feats

Perfumer’s Case

She decants her formulas into thrown vials: thirty feet, ten-foot radius, and even Courage Accord may be splashed. You can decant a formula into a thrown vial: as an action, throw one prepared bottle up to 30 feet. It shatters on impact, applying its effect in a 10-foot radius (single-target formulas allow a Dexterity save for half). A thrown Courage Accord is a splash of fortitude: each creature in the radius that fails the Dexterity save gains half the Veil’s temporary HP (rounded down); every creature’s Scent Veil still blooms when depleted.

Cold Extraction

The patient perfumer: a day-old scene still yields its trace, if the residue remains. You can gather Hunter’s Trace from a scene up to 24 hours old if physical residue remains.

Acquired Taste

The final cruelty of the craft: the failed victim craves the scent, and the craving walks him toward her. A creature that fails its save against one of your formulas develops a craving for it: for the next hour, you can use a bonus action and 1 CE to release a trace of that scent. The craving creature must make a Wisdom save or use its reaction to move up to half its speed toward you by the safest route.

Rules integration

Table Rulings

Formulas are cursed sensory effects; a creature without a normal sense of smell can still be affected unless it is explicitly immune to sensory or mind-affecting cursed techniques.
Hunter’s Trace gives direction, not exact distance or line of sight.
Bottled Memory reproduces sensory impressions, not objective truth; illusions, disguises, and mistaken perceptions remain part of the recording.
