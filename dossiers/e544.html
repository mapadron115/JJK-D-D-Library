Core law

What the technique actually does.

Core law, the 2.5-microsecond distortion. When cursed energy strikes within 2.5 microseconds of a physical hit, space itself distorts and the impact multiplies by 2.5 — the Black Flash, the most coveted accident in jujutsu. Your technique courts the accident on purpose. Spark Strikes replace your attacks; each consecutive hit landed tightens the timing window for the next spark; missing any attack breaks the chain and the count resets to zero. There is no pool, no meter, no tracker beyond the count — the table already counts hits. The chain is arithmetic, not accounting.

Field notes. Subject: a spark-chaser. Three truths govern the red. First: the Flash cannot be forced, only courted — every clean hit is an invitation, and the distortion decides whether to attend. Second: missing is the only sin — the chain is trust between fist and timing, and a whiff betrays it. Third: the cascade is real — each Black Flash teaches the next one its shape, and the masters stop throwing punches and start conducting lightning.

Progression

Levels 1–18.

1Chasing the Spark

When you take the Attack action, you may replace one attack with a Spark Strike: melee CE attack, 1 CE, 1d8 bludgeoning damage on a hit. Count your consecutive Spark Strike hits landed — missing any attack resets the count to 0. After each Spark Strike hit, roll a d20: if the result equals or exceeds 21 minus your chain count, the strike sparks into a Black Flash.

The first hit is a question. The second is a louder question. Somewhere down the chain, the universe answers in red.

Black Flash · The strike's damage is multiplied by 2.5 (round down), and the distortion lingers — the target has disadvantage on saving throws until the end of your next turn.

For 2.5 microseconds, the world bends around your fist. Everything after is just paperwork.

3See the Gap

Bonus action · 1 CE · For 1 minute, you perceive cursed energy distortions within 60 feet: you sense invisible creatures, you know when a creature begins charging a technique (and whether it costs an action, bonus action, or reaction), and you have advantage on initiative rolls.

The technique's second sense has nothing to do with hitting. The world is full of tiny lightnings that never quite land — and now you can see every one.

6Inside the Arc

Passive · Your Spark Strike now deals 1d10 bludgeoning damage. When you spark a Black Flash, you enter the cascade until the end of your next turn: missing an attack does not reset your chain count, and your spark threshold is widened by an additional 2.

The foundation levels up with you — and the first spark now pulls the next one toward you like weather.

Action · 2 CE · Chain Arc: make a Spark Strike; if it hits, the distortion leaps to a second creature within 15 feet of the first, which takes 2d8 force damage (Dexterity save for half).

Lightning was never a single-target phenomenon. It just needed permission to branch.

11Step Inside

Action · 8 CE · Once per short rest · For 1 minute, you fight inside the distortion: your Black Flash spark threshold is 15 or higher on the d20 regardless of chain count, missing never breaks your chain, and each Black Flash you land extends the duration by 1 round (to a maximum of 1 additional minute). The short rest is the price — the distortion cannot be lived in cheaply.

High noon for the spark-chaser. For sixty seconds, you don't chase the lightning. You are the storm it was trying to reach.

18The Spark Remembers

Passive · Your chain count no longer resets when you miss — the distortion holds you now. Your spark threshold can never be worse than 15 or higher on the d20; your chain count only ever widens it further.

The master stops counting. The fist and the timing have been introduced; they no longer need a chaperone.

Pinnacle expression

Maximum, Domain, or equivalent.

Maximum, Crimson Distortion (action, 10 CE): you become the distortion for 1 minute. Every Spark Strike automatically sparks into a Black Flash — no roll, no threshold, nothing breaks the chain. Your chain count is treated as 10 for all purposes. When the minute ends, you suffer one level of exhaustion: the body was never meant to live inside the lightning, only to visit. The exhaustion is the price — the Maximum is not.

Feats

Build-defining options.

Ride the Red

When you spark a Black Flash, you may teleport up to 30 feet to an unoccupied space you can see as part of the strike. The distortion folds space; you simply ride the fold. The spark, but as movement.

The Gap Wears You

Reaction · 2 CE · When a melee attack hits you, slip 2.5 microseconds sideways: halve the damage taken, and the attacker takes 2d8 force damage as the distortion answers. The spark, but as armor.

Reverse in the Red

When you spark a Black Flash, you may forgo the ×2.5 damage to invert the distortion instead: a creature you touch regains 3d8 + WIS hit points. Negative times negative. The spark, but as medicine.

Spark-Sight

Once per short rest, when an attack would hit you, declare you saw it coming in the distortion — it misses instead. You were 2.5 microseconds ahead the whole time. The spark, but as foresight.
