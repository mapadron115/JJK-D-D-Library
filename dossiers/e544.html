Black Flash Chaining

Core law, the 2.5-microsecond distortion. When cursed energy lands within 2.5 microseconds of a physical hit, space distorts — the Black Flash, the most coveted accident in jujutsu, and the one thing no technique can simply grant. This is the discipline of chasing it: widen the road to the critical, chain one Black Flash into the next, and let the distortion compound until you are fighting inside it. The procedure is the rules' own (Section VI). The chain is yours.

Core law

What the technique actually does.

Core law, the 2.5-microsecond distortion. Black Flash is not a technique — it is a procedure (rules, Section VI): a critical hit, a confirmation roll, triple the completed critical damage, and the Zone. This technique does not replace it, widen its confirmation roll, or change its damage. It courts it. Your attacks critical more often, so more of them reach the confirmation roll. When a Black Flash lands, you gain Chain — and the Chain makes the next Black Flash hit harder. Missing an attack breaks the Chain. There is no pool, no meter — the Chain is a count of consecutive Black Flashes, and the table already counts what lands. The chain is arithmetic, not accounting.

Field notes. Subject: a spark-chaser. Three truths govern the red. First: the Flash cannot be forced, only courted — every clean hit is an invitation, and the distortion decides whether to attend. Second: missing is the only sin — the chain is trust between fist and timing, and a whiff betrays it. Third: the cascade is real — each Black Flash teaches the next one its shape, and the masters stop throwing punches and start conducting lightning.

Progression

Levels 1–18.

1Chasing the Spark

Your weapon and unarmed strike attack rolls are critical hits on a 19–20 for the purpose of Black Flash confirmation. When you land a Black Flash, you gain 1 Chain. Your Chain resets to 0 when you miss an attack. While you have Chain, your Black Flashes deal an additional 1d6 force damage per Chain link.

The first Black Flash is a question. The second is a louder question. Somewhere down the chain, the universe answers in red.

3See the Gap

Bonus action · 1 CE · For 1 minute, you perceive cursed energy distortions within 60 feet: you sense invisible creatures, you know when a creature begins charging a technique (and whether it costs an action, bonus action, or reaction), and you have advantage on initiative rolls.

The technique's second sense has nothing to do with hitting. The world is full of tiny lightnings that never quite land — and now you can see every one.

6Inside the Arc

When you land a Black Flash, you enter the cascade until the end of your next turn: missing an attack does not reset your Chain.

The foundation levels up with you — and the first Black Flash now pulls the next one toward you like weather.

When you land a Black Flash, you may spend 2 CE: the distortion leaps to a second creature within 15 feet of the target, which takes 2d8 force damage (Dexterity save for half).

Lightning was never a single-target phenomenon. It just needed permission to branch.

11Step Inside

Action · 8 CE · Once per short rest · For 1 minute, you fight inside the distortion: missing an attack never resets your Chain, and the first time you land a Black Flash each round, you may immediately make one additional attack with a weapon or an unarmed strike. Each Black Flash you land extends the duration by 1 round (to a maximum of 1 additional minute). The short rest is the price — the distortion cannot be lived in cheaply.

High noon for the spark-chaser. For sixty seconds, you don't chase the lightning. You are the storm it was trying to reach.

18The Spark Remembers

Passive · Your Chain never resets when you miss — the distortion holds you now. Your weapon and unarmed strike attack rolls are critical hits on an 18–20 for the purpose of Black Flash confirmation. When you land a Black Flash, you gain 2 Chain instead of 1.

The master stops counting. The fist and the timing have been introduced; they no longer need a chaperone.

Pinnacle expression

Maximum, Domain, or equivalent.

Maximum, Crimson Distortion (action, 10 CE): you become the distortion for 1 minute. Every qualifying critical hit you land automatically confirms as a Black Flash — no confirmation roll. Your Chain is treated as 10 for all purposes. When the minute ends, you suffer one level of exhaustion: the body was never meant to live inside the lightning, only to visit. The exhaustion is the price — the Maximum is not.

Feats

Build-defining options.

Ride the Red

When you land a Black Flash, you may teleport up to 30 feet to an unoccupied space you can see as part of the strike. The distortion folds space; you simply ride the fold. The spark, but as movement.

The Gap Wears You

Reaction · 2 CE · When a melee attack hits you, slip 2.5 microseconds sideways: halve the damage taken, and the attacker takes 2d8 force damage as the distortion answers. The spark, but as armor.

Reverse in the Red

When you land a Black Flash, you may forgo the Black Flash's triple damage — deal normal critical damage instead — to invert the distortion: a creature you touch regains 3d8 + WIS hit points. Negative times negative. The spark, but as medicine.

Spark-Sight

Once per short rest, when an attack would hit you, declare you saw it coming in the distortion — it misses instead. You were 2.5 microseconds ahead the whole time. The spark, but as foresight.

Rules integration

Edge cases and shared systems.

Black Flash procedure: this technique uses the Black Flash procedure (rules, Section VI) without altering it. Confirmation rolls remain unmodified d20s. Black Flash damage remains triple the completed critical-hit damage. The Zone advances only on natural Black Flashes per the rules.
Automatic Black Flashes (Crimson Distortion): per the rules, an automatic or forced Black Flash grants Stage 1 if you are outside the Zone and resets the current stage's duration if you are in it, but it never advances the Zone or restores CE.
Expanded critical range: the technique's 19–20 (L1) and 18–20 (L18) critical ranges apply to weapon and unarmed strike attack rolls for the purpose of Black Flash confirmation. Forced or expanded critical ranges qualify when the triggering feature permits Black Flash; this technique permits it.
Chain: Chain is a count of consecutive Black Flashes. It resets to 0 when you miss an attack, except as modified by Cascade, Step Inside, or The Spark Remembers. Chain is not a pool and cannot be spent.
Chain Arc: the leap triggers on a Black Flash you land; you choose whether to spend the 2 CE when the Black Flash lands.
