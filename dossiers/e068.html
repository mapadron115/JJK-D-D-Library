Features & procedures

Exact player-facing procedures.

Weapon Equip • Level 5

Amol Dherrsaiqa — Roaring Flame Sword.

The first permission. He wraps his blade in the kiln's breath and finds it longer, hotter, and unbothered by the usual wards, the furnace grants reach the way a teacher grants rope: only to the student who has stopped wasting it.

Weapon Equip surrounds the blade in a concentrated vortex of flame and allows its size to expand without becoming unwieldy.

Bonus Action • 3 Reserve

Amol Dherrsaiqa

The same flame, drawn tighter. For one minute his steel carries the kiln's concentrated answer, more heat, longer arm, and fire that treats resistance as a rumor. Austerity's arithmetic: do less, for longer, with everything committed.

For 1 minute your weapon deals +2 Djinn Dice fire once per turn, gains +10-ft reach, and its fire ignores resistance.

Action • 3 Reserve

Amol Berka

He stops swinging the fire and starts stationing it. A hundred-foot wall of flame is not an attack but a boundary, cross it and be weighed, begin your turn inside it and be found wanting. The kiln does not chase; it waits, and the waiting is the point.

Raise a 100-ft-long, 30-ft-high wall of flame for 1 minute. Crossing or starting in it deals 5 Djinn Dice fire; it provides heavy obscurement through the flames.

Action • 5 Reserve

Flame Minister’s Cut

The long sentence. Two hundred and forty feet of concentrated heat that even stone must answer at full measure, barriers and constructs have no evasions to hide behind. The ascetic's blade has, finally, one paragraph, and it ends everything in the row.

Release a 240-ft line, 30 ft wide. Dexterity save; 9 Djinn Dice fire, half on success. Barriers, constructs, and objects take maximum dice.

Full Djinn Equip • Level 9

He stops carrying the fire and becomes its vessel in full. He flies, burns nothing of himself, and his flame now treats immunity the way it always treated resistance, as a suggestion. The kiln has accepted the iron; the iron is no longer iron.

Fly 100 ft; fire resistance; your flame weapon can grow to Huge scale without penalty. Fire damage you deal treats immunity as resistance and resistance as none.

12 Reserve • Canon Extreme

Amol Al-Bador Saiqa

The minister himself takes the field, the phoenix-blade driven through five hundred feet of whatever dared be built there. Fortress defenses are not contested; they are audited, and found burnable. The proof of austerity, delivered at the scale only proof deserves.

Manifest the enormous flaming minister/phoenix and drive its blade through a 500-ft-long, 80-ft-wide path. Dexterity save; 18 Djinn Dice fire/radiant, half on success. Barriers and structures take maximum dice and lose damage thresholds for this attack.

Anime-scale: Strategic scale: carve through fortress-class defenses or contest another Extreme Magic head-on.

Field notes. Subject: the kiln that walks. Three truths govern this flame. First: the vessel's cursed energy does not summon Amon's fire, it proves worthy of carrying it, and austerity is the proof. Second: what is stripped away is not fuel but argument; the fewer things he wants, the truer the strike. Third: the flame ignores resistance the way devotion ignores reason, it was never a contest of quantities, only of permission. Assess him not as a pyromancer but as an ascetic whose weapon happens to burn: the forge's discipline is that everything extraneous leaves the blade first, then the battlefield. Mark the maxim: austerity is not denial. It is what remains after everything burnable has been offered to the flame.

Rules integration

Djinn Die, Equip & Extreme Magic

Damage-type chooser: when a Djinn ability lists two damage types, the Djinn King chooses which applies when the damage is dealt.
Independent Djinn Reserve: 16 (Rank A). Refreshes on a long rest. Every Djinn cost below, Weapon Equip, Full Equip, abilities, reactions, Extreme Magic, spends this Djinn’s Reserve, never your CE. If this Djinn’s Reserve is empty it simply cannot fuel more until you rest; your other Djinn keep their own separate Reserves.
Weapon Equip · level 5: bonus action, 2 Reserve, lasts 10 minutes.
Full Djinn Equip · level 9: bonus action, 4 Reserve, lasts 1 minute. Only one Equip can be active.
Deep Synchronization · level 11: once per short rest, one Equip activation costs 1 Reserve less.
Extreme Magic · level 13: action; requires that Djinn’s Full Equip; pay the listed Reserve cost from this Djinn’s Reserve; usable once per long rest per Djinn. Full Equip ends afterward, and you cannot Equip that Djinn again until you finish a short rest.
King Vessel · level 17: switch directly from one Full Equip to another as a bonus action, paying the new Equip cost. At level 20, once per long rest, immediately transition from one Djinn’s Extreme into another Djinn’s Full Equip.
Attack / save: use your CE attack modifier and CE save DC unless this Djinn explicitly says otherwise.
