The Hope Diamond

A curse told into existence: Borrowed Fortune (reaction, 3/long rest) adds +10 to a d20 roll within 30 ft, each use banking 1 Doom. The GM can later spend Doom to impose −10 on a roll; overflow at 3 Doom detonates into 8d10 psychic damage.

The Hope Diamond

Properties & techniques

Exact player-facing procedures

Relic profile

3 Fortune uses per long rest · Doom 0–3

Jujutsu origin

Its 'curse' is useful here precisely because much of the popular curse narrative was amplified by sensational retelling. Jujutsu theory treats that as the point: enough people expected catastrophe around the stone that expectation eventually acquired cursed energy and became self-enforcing.

Borrowed Fortune — Reaction

After you or a creature within 30 ft rolls a d20 but before the result is resolved, add +10 to the roll. You can do this 3 times per long rest. Each use adds 1 Doom to the diamond, maximum 3.

Collected Doom

The GM may spend 1 Doom after the wearer or a creature within 30 ft rolls a d20 to impose -10 on that roll. Doom can only be removed by being spent this way; a long rest does not erase it.

Blue Catastrophe

If the diamond is at 3 Doom and would gain another, it instead releases the overflow: the wearer takes 8d10 psychic damage, and every creature within 15 ft takes half of the damage the wearer took (after any mitigation). Doom then becomes 0.

Binding Vow — Luck Must Balance

Every +10 the diamond gives creates a future -10. Its technique cannot manufacture net probability.

Designer’s note. Borrowed Fortune cannot modify Black Flash confirmation rolls, Domain clashes, or other rolls the GM rules are metaphysical rather than probabilistic.

Rules integration

Cursed-tool attunement

Campaign canon

Cursed-tool attunement: you have PB attunement slots (2–6). This tool occupies one slot and requires a one-hour ritual. Attunement ends voluntarily over a short rest, on death, or when you replace it while over capacity. Distance does not break attunement. Cursed-tool attunement is separate from magic-item attunement.
