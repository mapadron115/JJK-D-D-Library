The Hope Diamond

Metaphysical Law: fortune can be bought only by moving misfortune elsewhere. A curse made true by people repeating the story — the wearer may turn a failed d20 roll into a success, but each correction stores an equal “Misfortune Debt.”

Rules integration

Cursed-tool attunement

Campaign canon

Cursed-tool attunement: you have PB attunement slots (2–6). This tool occupies one slot and requires a one-hour ritual. Attunement ends voluntarily over a short rest, on death, or when you replace it while over capacity. Distance does not break attunement. Cursed-tool attunement is separate from magic-item attunement.
Balance & counterplay. Every success is borrowed — the GM holds the debt and spends it on unrelated outcomes, so heavy use guarantees the misfortune lands somewhere worse. The counterplay is patience: let the debt accumulate, then collect.
