Core law

The axe keeps the sun's hours.

Rhitta has no Heat pool. Its power is the campaign hour: the GM announces the current Solar Station (the same five stations as the Sunshine technique, e039); the station sets the axe's fire, light, and release as the Reference table shows. There is nothing to accrue, bank, or spend — noon is noon.

The fiction. Cursed-tool scholars classify Rhitta as devotion given an edge: forged for a wielder whose power rose and fell with the sun, the axe kept the hours when its master could not. There is no heat pool because there was never a hoard — only faith that noon returns. Plain steel at night is the axe telling the truth about itself.

Features & procedures

Exact player-facing procedures.

Divine Axe:

1d12 slashing, Heavy, Two-Handed unless Strength 20+.

Solar Station:

Rhitta reads the campaign's sun (same clock as Sunshine, e039 — the GM announces the station; it changes at the start of the next round when a boundary is crossed; clouds, roofs, and mundane darkness do not change it). Add your station rank in d6 fire damage to every hit with the axe (Night +0, Low Sun +1d6, High Sun +2d6, Near-Noon +3d6, Zenith +4d6).

Stored Sun:

Low Sun — shed dim light 10 ft. High Sun and higher — shed bright light 20 ft (30 ft at Near-Noon and Zenith) and gain fire resistance while wielding Rhitta. Night — the axe is at its heaviest: it is Two-Handed regardless of Strength and your speed is reduced by 10 ft while you wield it.

Heat Wave (3 CE, action, requires Low Sun or higher):

30-ft cone, Dexterity save; fire damage equal to 2 Tool Dice + your station rank in Tool Dice (half on success).

Sunshine's shadow:

if the wielder has the Sunshine technique (e039), Rhitta treats the station as one rank higher (maximum Zenith). Rhitta works for any wielder, but beside Sunshine it becomes monstrous.

Tool Die:

d6 (1–4) → d8 (5–10) → d10 (11–16) → d12 (17–20), shared Sacred Treasure progression.

Pinnacle expression

Sacred Treasure Release — Charge & Fire.

Sacred Treasure Release — Charge & Fire (level 13+, 6 CE; action, once per long rest, requires High Sun or higher): release the stored sun in a 60-ft line or a 20-ft radius. Dexterity save; fire/radiant damage equal to (5 + twice your station rank) Tool Dice (High Sun 9, Near-Noon 11, Zenith 13), half on success.

Rules integration

Tool Die & Sacred Treasure attunement

Station ranks: Night 0, Low Sun 1, High Sun 2, Near-Noon 3, Zenith 4 (the 10 rounds of true noon). Only effects that truly move the sun, change cosmic time, or explicitly suppress solar power change the station.
Attunement: spend 1 hour and one cursed-tool attunement slot. A Sacred Treasure supplements your Innate Technique; it does not grant a second one.
Activation: activated properties spend the wielder’s own CE. If a non-sorcerer is explicitly allowed to wield the treasure, its Tool save DC is 8 + proficiency bonus + one chosen mental ability modifier.
Sacred Treasure Release: normally requires level 13 or higher and is usable once per long rest; pay the specific treasure’s listed CE cost. An explicit exception in its individual entry takes precedence.
