SACRED TREASURE • Escanor

Divine Axe Rhitta

Special Grade

Heat-storing divine axe

Rhitta works with any heat source, but becomes monstrous beside Sunshine or another high-output fire/solar CT.

  1. Divine Axe: 1d12 slashing, Heavy, Two-Handed unless Str 20+. While carrying stored Heat, it deals +1 fire per Heat.
  2. Charge: whenever you deal fire/radiant damage or take fire/radiant damage after resistance, gain 1 Heat, maximum PB + CE modifier (minimum 3). Heat persists until long rest.
  3. Solar Reservoir: as an action, spend CE up to PB to gain equal Heat. A Sunshine user may instead bank unused solar output according to that technique's guide.
  4. Release Heat (free on hit): spend any Heat; each point adds 1 Tool Die fire to that hit, maximum PB Heat per attack.
  5. Heat Wave (3 CE + Heat): action; spend 1–PB Heat, 30-ft cone, Dex save; 1 Tool Die fire per Heat + 2 Tool Dice.
  6. Stored Sun: while at maximum Heat, shed bright light 30 ft and gain fire resistance.
  7. Sacred Treasure Release — Charge & Fire (level 13+, 6 CE): release all Heat in a 60-ft line or 20-ft radius. Dex save; 1 Tool Die per Heat + 5 Tool Dice fire/radiant. On success half. Heat becomes 0.
