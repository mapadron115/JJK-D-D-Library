Divine Axe Rhitta

Rhitta works with any heat source, but becomes monstrous beside Sunshine or another high-output fire/solar CT.

FormHeat-storing divine axe
GradeSpecial Grade
Features & procedures

Exact player-facing procedures.

Divine Axe:

1d12 slashing, Heavy, Two-Handed unless Str 20+. While carrying stored Heat, it deals +1 fire per Heat.

Charge:

whenever you deal fire/radiant damage or take fire/radiant damage after resistance, gain 1 Heat, maximum PB + CE modifier (minimum 3). Heat persists until long rest.

Solar Reservoir:

as an action, spend CE up to PB to gain equal Heat. A Sunshine user may instead bank unused solar output according to that technique's guide.

Release Heat (free on hit):

spend any Heat; each point adds 1 Tool Die fire to that hit, maximum PB Heat per attack.

Heat Wave (3 CE + Heat):

action; spend 1–PB Heat, 30-ft cone, Dex save; 1 Tool Die fire per Heat + 2 Tool Dice.

Stored Sun:

while at maximum Heat, shed bright light 30 ft and gain fire resistance.

Sacred Treasure Release — Charge & Fire (level 13+, 6 CE):

release all Heat in a 60-ft line or 20-ft radius. Dex save; 1 Tool Die per Heat + 5 Tool Dice fire/radiant. On success half. Heat becomes 0.

Rules integration

Tool Die & Sacred Treasure attunement

Attunement: spend 1 hour and one cursed-tool attunement slot. A Sacred Treasure supplements your Innate Technique; it does not grant a second one.
Activation: activated properties spend the wielder’s own CE. If a non-sorcerer is explicitly allowed to wield the treasure, its Tool save DC is 8 + proficiency bonus + one chosen mental ability modifier.
Sacred Treasure Release: normally requires level 13 or higher and is usable once per long rest; pay the specific treasure’s listed CE cost. An explicit exception in its individual entry takes precedence.
