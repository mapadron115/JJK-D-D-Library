Core law

What the technique actually does.

Core Engine — Supremacy. Supremacy is his tribute economy: zero to ten, minted when hostiles fail against him, when he counters or steals magic, when a major foe falls below half. He spends it to dominate, to copy, and to amplify — and the dossier is explicit that he sits beyond a normal shared progression budget. The court has one seat, and he is already in it. Supremacy 0–10. Gain 1 when a hostile fails a save against you, you successfully counter/steal magic, or you reduce a major foe below half HP. Spend it to dominate, copy, and amplify. Bolas is intentionally beyond a normal shared progression budget.

Field notes. Subject: a court with one seat. Three truths govern him. First, tribute is paid in failure: Supremacy accrues when a hostile fails a save against him, when he counters or steals magic, when he drives a major foe below half — the failures of others, minted. Second, he annexes rather than learns: his Spellbreaker does not merely blunt enemy techniques but captures their halved damage for his Hoard, and a successful break lets him store the technique itself as an Echo. Third, the court acknowledges no peers: claw, breath, and winged flight; domination and ultimatum; a Domain with a thousand clash points and a burnout of three rounds. The dossier notes it plainly — he is intentionally beyond a normal shared progression budget. Assess him as what he is: not a sorcerer with a dragon's technique, but a dragon condescending to sorcery.

Full progression

Level 1 through 20.

1Elder Dragon

The dragon's body, worn like a title. A claw for a die of ten, forty feet of flight for one energy per ten minutes, resistance to fire and the psychic — and two energy buys a fifteen-foot cone of fire and force. The court opens with the obvious. Claw 1d10, flight 40 for 1 CE/10 min, resistance fire/psychic. 2 CE breath 15-ft cone 3d8 fire/force.

3Mind Shatter

The word that unmakes thought. Two energy, a Wisdom save: three dice of psychic on failure, and the victim's next mental save at disadvantage. He does not argue with minds. He adjourns them. 2 CE, Wis save: 3d10 psychic and disadvantage next Int/Wis/Cha save on failure.

6Spellbreaker

The court's veto. As a reaction for three energy, he contests a technique within sixty feet — and a win halves its damage or suppresses one non-damaging rider. Other sorcerers defend themselves. He overrules. Reaction 3 CE: technique contest (see Rules — Shared Resolutions) against feature within 60 ft. Win reduces its damage by half or suppresses one non-damaging rider.

9Slave of Bolas

The first vassal. Four energy and two Supremacy: a failed Wisdom save Charms the target until his next turn ends, and it immediately spends its reaction on his chosen move or basic attack. The court always has room for one more subject. 4 CE + 2 Supremacy, Wis save: Charmed until end next turn and immediately uses reaction to move or make one basic attack chosen by you.

11God-Pharaoh's Theft

The annexation. After his Spellbreaker succeeds, two Supremacy stores an Echo of the broken technique until his long rest — usable once at half dice for its cost plus two. He does not study enemy magic. He nationalizes it. After Spellbreaker succeeds, spend 2 Supremacy to store an Echo of the technique until long rest; use once at half dice/scale for its CE cost +2.

13Ruinous Ultimatum

The edict. Six energy names three targets or objects within sixty feet: a Dexterity save against six dice of force and necrotic, structures taking maximum dice. When the court speaks, the architecture listens. 6 CE: choose three targets/objects within 60 ft; each Dex save 6d10 force/necrotic, structures take max dice.

15Planeswalker

The throne moves. Four energy carries him and his proficiency's worth of willing creatures up to a mile to a known place — or a hundred and twenty feet in the heat of combat. Distance is a courtesy he extends to himself. 4 CE: teleport yourself and PB willing creatures up to 1 mile to a known location; combat use limited to 120 ft.

18Domain — Meditation Realm

At the twentieth count the court convenes, and he chooses its business: Discard — an Intelligence save or lose a reaction and a named option; Destroy — a Dexterity save against seven dice of force; Dominate — a Wisdom save or be moved twenty feet and made to interact. A thousand clash points back the proceedings. Initiative 20 choose DISCARD, DESTROY, or DOMINATE. Discard: Int save or lose reaction + one named option until next count 20. Destroy: Dex save 7d10 force. Dominate: Wis save or forced 20-ft movement and one basic interaction. Clash bookkeeping: 1,000 clash points (Tier X). A 100+ point advantage collapses the weaker Domain; collapse deals no damage. Barrier AC 12 + PB; barrier HP = 10 × max(1, your CE modifier) + 2,000. Technique Burnout: 3 rounds. Campaign gating: antagonist/BBEG-only by default. Coordinator-approved PC use requires the power-down: stored Echo cap 1, no double-Echo action, Ascension resistance limited to two damage types.

20Dragon-God

The god-pharaoh ascendant. His Supremacy ceiling rises to fifteen, his wings to eighty feet, his claw carrying two more dice of force — and he may store his proficiency in Echoes, spending ten Supremacy once per long rest to loose two different ones in a single action. The court, at full session. Supremacy max 15; fly 80, claw +2d10 force. Store PB Echos. Once/long rest, spend 10 Supremacy to use two different stored Echos in the same action.

Pinnacle expression

Maximum, Domain, or equivalent.

Maximum — God-Pharaoh's Ascension (12 CE + 10 Supremacy; action): Three rounds of godhood. Twelve energy and ten Supremacy: resistance to all but radiant and psychic, a hundred and twenty feet of flight, two added to his technique DC and attacks — and once per turn, a stored Echo used without consuming it. Afterward every Echo is erased. Even gods pay for the coronation. for 3 rounds, resistance all but radiant/psychic, flight 120, +2 to technique DC/attacks, and once/turn use a stored Echo without consuming it. Afterwards all Echos are erased.

Feats

Technique Feats

Cruel Intellect

He sifts pain for intelligence. When he deals psychic damage, one Supremacy as a bonus action reveals one of the target's named options — the interrogation conducted through the wound. when you deal psychic damage to a creature, you may spend 1 Supremacy as a bonus action to sift the pain: learn one of the target's currently available named options.

Dragon's Hoard

When his Spellbreaker halves a technique's damage, he may capture the suppressed half — and within a minute, loose it as a fifteen-foot cone of force as a bonus action. The court's taxes are collected in full. when your Spellbreaker halves a technique's damage, you may capture the suppressed half: as a bonus action within 1 minute, release it as a 15-ft cone dealing the captured dice as force damage (Dexterity save halves).

Schemer

His Mind Shatter reads as it wounds. Dealing psychic damage with it skims the victim's surface thoughts — ten minutes of advantage on Deception and Intimidation against that creature, and one fear or desire learned. A psychic echo, not a leash; it fades when turned on another. Your Mind Shatter does more than wound — it reads. When you deal psychic damage to a creature with Mind Shatter, you skim its surface thoughts in the same instant: for the next 10 minutes you have advantage on Charisma (Deception) and Charisma (Intimidation) checks against that creature, and you learn one fear or desire it holds. This is a psychic echo, not ongoing telepathy, and it fades if you use it against another creature.

The Ravager

His breath may be exhaled as a lingering miasma instead of a cone — a twenty-foot cloud until his next turn begins, the save repeated by all within. The dragon does not merely breathe. He weathers. your breath can be exhaled as a lingering miasma instead of a cone: a 20-ft-radius cloud lasting until the start of your next turn; creatures entering or starting their turn inside make the save, taking half damage on a success.

Planeswalker Spark

When he teleports in combat he may arrive striking — one melee attack against a creature within reach of his destination, part of the same action. The throne lands like a siege engine. when you teleport in combat, you may arrive striking: make one melee attack against a creature within reach of your destination as part of the same action.

Bolas's Citadel

Below half health, his stolen magic strains to be free: once per round, a used Echo may also strike one additional creature within thirty feet of the original target at half dice. Even wounded, the court collects double. while below half HP, your stolen magic strains to be free: once per round, when you use a stored Echo, you may have it also target one additional creature within 30 ft of the original target at half dice.

Rules integration

Edge cases and shared systems.

Shared rulings: Technique DC = 8 + PB + CE ability modifier. Direct rolled attacks may crit and use the campaign Black Flash procedure when eligible. Tokens, autonomous summons, passive damage, and initiative-20 Domain effects cannot Black Flash. Maximum Output modifies ordinary supported techniques, never named Maxima or Domains.
