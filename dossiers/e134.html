Core law

What the technique actually does.

Core Engine — Supremacy. Supremacy is his tribute economy: zero to ten, minted when hostiles fail against him, when he counters or steals magic, when a major foe falls below half. He spends it to dominate, to copy, and to amplify — and the dossier is explicit that he sits beyond a normal shared progression budget. The court has one seat, and he is already in it. Supremacy 0–10. Gain 1 when a hostile fails a save against you, you successfully counter/steal magic, or you reduce a major foe below half HP. Spend it to dominate, copy, and amplify. Bolas is intentionally beyond a normal shared progression budget.

Field notes. Subject: the court that annexes. I have faced dragons. I have faced emperors. The Supremacy is what happens when the two stop being separate job descriptions. Two truths govern it. First: the court does not argue — Mind Shatter adjourns minds, Spellbreaker overrules techniques, and the Echoes nationalize whatever was broken; he does not study enemy magic, he annexes it. Second: the throne is mobile — a mile's teleport with his chosen, eighty feet of wings at full session, and a Domain where the twentieth count convenes Discard, Destroy, or Dominate under a thousand clash points. I have won battles by being the larger court in the room. Against this one, I am not the larger court. I have written that sentence three times to make sure I meant it.

Full progression

Level 1 through 20.

1Elder Dragon

The dragon's body, worn like a title. A claw for a die of ten, forty feet of flight for one energy per ten minutes, resistance to fire and the psychic — and two energy buys a fifteen-foot cone of fire and force. The court opens with the obvious. I note the obvious the way one notes the edge of a cliff: respectfully, and from a measured distance. Claw 1d10, flight 40 for 1 CE/10 min, resistance fire/psychic. 2 CE breath 15-ft cone 3d8 fire/force.

3Mind Shatter

The word that unmakes thought. Two energy, a Wisdom save: three dice of psychic on failure, and the victim's next mental save at disadvantage. He does not argue with minds. He adjourns them. I have adjourned many councils. I have never seen it done to a mind, mid-thought, as a combat action. 2 CE, Wis save: 3d10 psychic and disadvantage next Int/Wis/Cha save on failure.

6Spellbreaker

The court's veto. As a reaction for three energy, he contests a technique within sixty feet — and a win halves its damage or suppresses one non-damaging rider. Other sorcerers defend themselves. He overrules. I have built my doctrine on the counterstroke. His counterstroke is a gavel. Reaction 3 CE: technique contest (see Rules — Shared Resolutions) against feature within 60 ft. Win reduces its damage by half or suppresses one non-damaging rider.

9Slave of Bolas

The first vassal. Four energy and two Supremacy: a failed Wisdom save Charms the target until his next turn ends, and it immediately spends its reaction on his chosen move or basic attack. The court always has room for one more subject. I have recruited soldiers. He recruits the enemy, mid-battle, as a procedural matter. 4 CE + 2 Supremacy, Wis save: Charmed until end next turn and immediately uses reaction to move or make one basic attack chosen by you.

11God-Pharaoh's Theft

The annexation. After his Spellbreaker succeeds, two Supremacy stores an Echo of the broken technique until his long rest — usable once at half dice for its cost plus two. He does not study enemy magic. He nationalizes it. Every technique used against him becomes his reserve. I have begun to consider the wisdom of using techniques at all in his presence. After Spellbreaker succeeds, spend 2 Supremacy to store an Echo of the technique until long rest; use once at half dice/scale for its CE cost +2.

13Ruinous Ultimatum

The edict. Six energy names three targets or objects within sixty feet: a Dexterity save against six dice of force and necrotic, structures taking maximum dice. When the court speaks, the architecture listens. I have given orders that buildings obeyed. His orders, I note, are not suggestions to the masonry. 6 CE: choose three targets/objects within 60 ft; each Dex save 6d10 force/necrotic, structures take max dice.

15Planeswalker

The throne moves. Four energy carries him and his proficiency's worth of willing creatures up to a mile to a known place — or a hundred and twenty feet in the heat of combat. Distance is a courtesy he extends to himself. I have moved armies. He moves the court, entire, as a single gesture. The gesture frightens me more than the army did. 4 CE: teleport yourself and PB willing creatures up to 1 mile to a known location; combat use limited to 120 ft.

18Domain — Meditation Realm

At the twentieth count the court convenes, and he chooses its business: Discard, Destroy, or Dominate. A thousand clash points back the proceedings. I have convened war councils. His council convenes inside the Domain, on schedule, with the force of law. I do not propose to attend. Initiative 20 choose DISCARD, DESTROY, or DOMINATE. Discard: Int save or lose reaction + one named option until next count 20. Destroy: Dex save 7d10 force. Dominate: Wis save or forced 20-ft movement and one basic interaction. Clash bookkeeping: 1,000 clash points (Tier X). A 100+ point advantage collapses the weaker Domain; collapse deals no damage. Barrier AC 12 + PB; barrier HP = 10 × max(1, your CE modifier) + 2,000. Technique Burnout: 3 rounds. Campaign gating: antagonist/BBEG-only by default. Coordinator-approved PC use requires the power-down: stored Echo cap 1, no double-Echo action, Ascension resistance limited to two damage types.

20Dragon-God

The god-pharaoh ascendant. His Supremacy ceiling rises to fifteen, his wings to eighty feet, his claw carrying two more dice of force — and he may store his proficiency in Echoes, loosing two different ones in a single action once per long rest. The court, at full session. I have seen full sessions. This one, I confess, I would rather read about. Supremacy max 15; fly 80, claw +2d10 force. Store PB Echos. Once/long rest, spend 10 Supremacy to use two different stored Echos in the same action.

Pinnacle expression

Maximum, Domain, or equivalent.

Maximum — God-Pharaoh's Ascension (12 CE + 10 Supremacy; action): Three rounds of godhood. Twelve energy and ten Supremacy: resistance to all but radiant and psychic, a hundred and twenty feet of flight, two added to his technique DC and attacks — and once per turn, a stored Echo used without consuming it. Afterward every Echo is erased. Even gods pay for the coronation. for 3 rounds, resistance all but radiant/psychic, flight 120, +2 to technique DC/attacks, and once/turn use a stored Echo without consuming it. Afterwards all Echos are erased.

Feats

Technique Feats

Cruel Intellect

He sifts pain for intelligence. When he deals psychic damage, one Supremacy as a bonus action reveals one of the target's named options — the interrogation conducted through the wound. when you deal psychic damage to a creature, you may spend 1 Supremacy as a bonus action to sift the pain: learn one of the target's currently available named options.

Dragon's Hoard

When his Spellbreaker halves a technique's damage, he may capture the suppressed half — and within a minute, loose it as a fifteen-foot cone of force as a bonus action. The court's taxes are collected in full. when your Spellbreaker halves a technique's damage, you may capture the suppressed half: as a bonus action within 1 minute, release it as a 15-ft cone dealing the captured dice as force damage (Dexterity save halves).

Schemer

His Mind Shatter reads as it wounds — skimming surface thoughts, learning one fear or desire. A psychic echo, not a leash. He wounds and reads in the same gesture. I have interrogated prisoners. He interrogates the wound itself. I find I prefer my method, and I find that preference is fear. Your Mind Shatter does more than wound — it reads. When you deal psychic damage to a creature with Mind Shatter, you skim its surface thoughts in the same instant: for the next 10 minutes you have advantage on Charisma (Deception) and Charisma (Intimidation) checks against that creature, and you learn one fear or desire it holds. This is a psychic echo, not ongoing telepathy, and it fades if you use it against another creature.

The Ravager

His breath may be exhaled as a lingering miasma instead of a cone — a twenty-foot cloud, the save repeated by all within. The dragon does not merely breathe. He weathers. I have weathered sieges. I have not been the weather. your breath can be exhaled as a lingering miasma instead of a cone: a 20-ft-radius cloud lasting until the start of your next turn; creatures entering or starting their turn inside make the save, taking half damage on a success.

Planeswalker Spark

When he teleports in combat he may arrive striking — one melee attack as part of the same action. The throne lands like a siege engine. I have braced against charges. The charge that arrives from nowhere, striking, is a category I had not budgeted for. when you teleport in combat, you may arrive striking: make one melee attack against a creature within reach of your destination as part of the same action.

Bolas's Citadel

Below half health, his stolen magic strains to be free: once per round, a used Echo may also strike one additional creature within thirty feet of the original target at half dice. Even wounded, the court collects double. while below half HP, your stolen magic strains to be free: once per round, when you use a stored Echo, you may have it also target one additional creature within 30 ft of the original target at half dice.

Rules integration

Edge cases and shared systems.

Shared rulings: Technique DC = 8 + PB + CE ability modifier. Direct rolled attacks may crit and use the campaign Black Flash procedure when eligible. Tokens, autonomous summons, passive damage, and initiative-20 Domain effects cannot Black Flash. Maximum Output modifies ordinary supported techniques, never named Maxima or Domains.
