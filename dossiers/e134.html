Core law

What the technique actually does.

Core Engine — Supremacy. Supremacy 0–10. Gain 1 when a hostile fails a save against you, you successfully counter/steal magic, or you reduce a major foe below half HP. Spend it to dominate, copy, and amplify. Bolas is intentionally beyond a normal shared progression budget.

Full progression

Level 1 through 20.

1Elder Dragon

Claw 1d10, flight 40 for 1 CE/10 min, resistance fire/psychic. 2 CE breath 15-ft cone 3d8 fire/force.

3Mind Shatter

2 CE, Wis save: 3d10 psychic and disadvantage next Int/Wis/Cha save on failure.

6Spellbreaker

Reaction 3 CE: technique contest against feature within 60 ft. Win reduces its damage by half or suppresses one non-damaging rider.

9Slave of Bolas

4 CE + 2 Supremacy, Wis save: Charmed until end next turn and immediately uses reaction to move or make one basic attack chosen by you.

11God-Pharaoh's Theft

After Spellbreaker succeeds, spend 2 Supremacy to store an Echo of the technique until long rest; use once at half dice/scale for its CE cost +2.

13Ruinous Ultimatum

6 CE: choose three targets/objects within 60 ft; each Dex save 6d10 force/necrotic, structures take max dice.

15Planeswalker

4 CE: teleport yourself and PB willing creatures up to 1 mile to a known location; combat use limited to 120 ft.

18Domain — Meditation Realm

Initiative 20 choose DISCARD, DESTROY, or DOMINATE. Discard: Int save or lose reaction + one named option until next count 20. Destroy: Dex save 7d10 force. Dominate: Wis save or forced 20-ft movement and one basic interaction. Clash bookkeeping: 1,000 clash points (Tier X). A 100+ point advantage collapses the weaker Domain; collapse deals no damage. Barrier AC 12 + PB; barrier HP = 10 × max(1, your CE modifier) + 2,000. Technique Burnout: 3 rounds. Campaign gating: antagonist/BBEG-only by default. Coordinator-approved PC use requires the power-down: stored Echo cap 1, no double-Echo action, Ascension resistance limited to two damage types.

20Dragon-God

Supremacy max 15; fly 80, claw +2d10 force. Store PB Echos. Once/long rest, spend 10 Supremacy to use two different stored Echos in the same action.

Pinnacle expression

Maximum, Domain, or equivalent.

Maximum — God-Pharaoh's Ascension (12 CE + 10 Supremacy): for 3 rounds, resistance all but radiant/psychic, flight 120, +2 to technique DC/attacks, and once/turn use a stored Echo without consuming it. Afterwards all Echos are erased.

Rules integration

Edge cases and shared systems.

Shared rulings: Technique DC = 8 + PB + CE ability modifier. Direct rolled attacks may crit and use the campaign Black Flash procedure when eligible. Tokens, autonomous summons, passive damage, and initiative-20 Domain effects cannot Black Flash. Maximum Output modifies ordinary supported techniques, never named Maxima or Domains.
