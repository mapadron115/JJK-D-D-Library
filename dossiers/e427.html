Property

Oni strength

Your Strength is 17 while you wear these gauntlets. They have no effect on you if your Strength is 17 or higher without them.

Observer’s account

Field notes. Subject: borrowed strength, and the ceiling it keeps. Three truths govern these gauntlets. First, the strength is a loan, not a gift: the Oni’s accumulated fear pours into the wearer’s arms, but the lender sets the terms, Strength 17, no more, no less. Second, a loan is only valuable to the poor in strength: one already 17 or mightier by nature gains nothing, for you cannot improve a man already stronger than the Oni’s fear. Third, the cleverest use is strategic poverty: give them to the weakest arm on your side, and the gap closes for the price of an attunement slot. Hence the maxim: borrow muscle from fear itself, but know that fear, like any creditor, caps the account.

Campaign canon notes

Cursed-tool attunement: you have PB attunement slots (2–6). This tool occupies one slot and requires a one-hour ritual. Attunement ends voluntarily over a short rest, on death, or when you replace it while over capacity. Distance does not break attunement. Cursed-tool attunement is separate from magic-item attunement.
Set, not boosted: the gauntlets set Strength to 17: they do not stack with and cannot raise a natural 17+.
