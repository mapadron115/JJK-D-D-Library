Shrine

Long-range invisible cuts, adaptive Cleave, Divine Flame, a four-armed perfect form, and Malevolent Shrine.

The fiction. Registry notes describe Shrine as severance that no longer bothers with a blade: the user's cursed energy learned the shape of disassembly itself, so a Dismantle needs no projectile — only a destination, which is why no eye can follow it. Cleave is that same certainty made adaptive; where the world resists being cut, the energy remembers the structure and returns, patient as erosion. The prevailing theory holds that Divine Flame is perceptible precisely because it is the exception — the one feature in the kit that still deigns to travel.

Full progression

Feature map

1Dismantle.

Dismantle. Replaces one attack in the Attack action. Spend 1 to PB CE per replaced attack and make a ranged CE attack at 150/600 feet. Damage is Xd8 slashing, where X is the CE spent on that replaced attack. Adjacent hostile creatures impose no disadvantage if the target cannot perceive the cut; long range still does.

3Cleave.

Cleave. Take an action, spend 1 to 10 CE, and make one ranged CE attack within 150 feet for (PB + CE spent)d10 slashing. A hit marks the target’s structure for 1 minute. A later Cleave hit while marked applies one penetration step: immunity becomes resistance, or resistance is ignored. Further hits do not add more steps, and a different target begins unadapted.

6Divine Flame: Open.

Divine Flame. X is CE spent, from 1 to twice PB. Choose a point at 150/600 feet; radius is 5 × X and damage Xd12 fire with a Dexterity save for half. The incantation and projectile are perceptible even when Dismantle is not.

11Perfect Form.

Perfect Form. CE ability, Strength, and Constitution each increase by 2 and their maxima rise by 2. The extra bonus action is an explicit feature exception, but each bonus action must perform a different named feature unless that feature says otherwise. The extra attack joins the Attack action.

Once per turn, Perfect Form may remove the charging action from one Maximum Output use. It does not waive CE, Technique Focus, level, Output Rank, Burnout, or any fatal consequence; it cannot accelerate Domain Expansion.

Pinnacle expression

Maximum, Domain, or equivalent.

Malevolent Shrine — Slaughter Cycle: 18 — Malevolent Shrine. at initiative 20, the shrine itself inundates every hostile creature with calibrated and indiscriminate cuts. Each automatically takes 10d10 slashing plus PBd10 force damage. A protected creature makes a Dexterity save, taking half of the combined damage on a success. Objects and structures take maximum damage. This is one autonomous Domain event, costs no CE, and is neither Cleave nor Dismantle; it cannot critically hit or trigger Black Flash.

Innate Technique Feats

Build-defining options.

These are optional character-building feats. You must meet each listed prerequisite before selecting it.

Prerequisite: Dismantle

Spiderweb

For 1 additional CE, replace Dismantle’s attack with a 30-foot cone. Creatures make Dexterity saves, taking the normal Dismantle damage on failure or half on success. Roll once for the area.

Prerequisite: Perfect Form

Form of Battle

While you grapple a creature, your Dismantle and Cleave need no ranged attack roll against it - the cuts ride your grip. Resolve them as melee CE attacks instead, with Cleave's mark and penetration applying normally. You cannot target any other creature with those slashes while the grapple holds.

Prerequisite: Malevolent Shrine and CE score 20

Maximum: World Cutter

Action with an audible chant and free hand: pay Cleave/Dismantle’s cost +5 CE and make its attack. It cuts occupied space, ignores cover, barriers, technique-created AC, resistance, and immunity. Resolve against base armor/natural/unarmored AC. A miss still expends CE; it cannot combine with Chanting or Maximum Output.

Rules integration

Edge cases and shared systems.

Rules priority: the reviewed rulings, explicit codex clarifications, and the level progression always control when original wording conflicts. Legacy Domain auto-hit language, action terminology, event timing, missing saves, and older scaling never override revised player rules.
Why Tier V: the ordinary package begins as scalable artillery and ends with repeatable, adaptive, map-wide disassembly. That calamity ceiling is the technique’s lore identity, not a reason to prohibit it.
