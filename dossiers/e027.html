Completed rules

  • Dismantle: replaces one attack in the Attack action. Spend 1 to PB CE per replaced attack and make a ranged CE attack at 150/600 feet for Xd8 slashing. Adjacent hostile creatures impose no disadvantage if the target cannot perceive the cut; long range still does.
  • Cleave: take an action, spend 1 to 10 CE, and make one ranged CE attack within 150 feet for (PB + CE spent)d10 slashing. A hit marks the target’s structure for 1 minute. A later Cleave hit while marked applies one penetration step: immunity becomes resistance, or resistance is ignored. Further hits do not add more steps, and a different target begins unadapted.
  • Divine Flame: X is CE spent, from 1 to twice PB. Choose a point at 150/600 feet; radius is 5 × X and damage Xd12 fire with a Dexterity save for half. The incantation and projectile are perceptible even when Dismantle is not.
  • Perfect Form: CE ability, Strength, and Constitution each increase by 2 and their maxima rise by 2. The extra bonus action is an explicit feature exception, but each bonus action must perform a different named feature unless that feature says otherwise. The extra attack joins the Attack action.
  • Once per turn, Perfect Form may remove the charging action from one Maximum Output use. It does not waive CE, Technique Focus, level, Output Rank, Burnout, or any fatal consequence; it cannot accelerate Domain Expansion.
  • Malevolent Shrine — Slaughter Cycle: at initiative 20, the shrine itself inundates every hostile creature with calibrated and indiscriminate cuts. Each automatically takes 10d10 slashing plus PBd10 force damage. A protected creature makes a Dexterity save, taking half of the combined damage on a success. Objects and structures take maximum damage. This is one autonomous Domain event, costs no CE, and is neither Cleave nor Dismantle; it cannot critically hit or trigger Black Flash.

Power identity

Why Tier V: the ordinary package begins as scalable artillery and ends with repeatable, adaptive, map-wide disassembly. That calamity ceiling is the technique’s lore identity, not a reason to prohibit it.

Recovered character-build options

Innate Technique Feats 3

These are optional character-building feats. You must meet each listed prerequisite before selecting it.

Spiderweb

High impact

Prerequisite: Dismantle

For 1 additional CE, replace Dismantle’s attack with a 30-foot cone. Creatures make Dexterity saves, taking the normal Dismantle damage on failure or half on success. Roll once for the area.

Form of Battle

Pinnacle

Prerequisite: Perfect Form

While a creature is grappled by you, your attacks against it have advantage and it has disadvantage on saves against your features. Both benefits end immediately when the grapple ends.

Maximum: World Cutter

Pinnacle

Prerequisite: Malevolent Shrine and CE score 20

Action with an audible chant and free hand: pay Cleave/Dismantle’s cost +5 CE and make its attack. It cuts occupied space, ignores cover, barriers, technique-created AC, resistance, and immunity. Resolve against base armor/natural/unarmored AC. A miss still expends CE; it cannot combine with Chanting or Maximum Output.

Shrine
You are born without a limit. Taking the world for yourself, with
abilities that hold great power.
Cursed Energy
Beginning at 1st level, you can harness your negative feelings into
a usable source of energy, called cursed energy. These feelings can
be harnessed into attacks or abilities. The number of cursed energy
you possess is based on your class, some classes use a modifier that
being charisma or wisdom (Player’s choice)
Your cursed energy DC is 8 + user cursed energy ability modifier +
proficiency bonus.
Dismantle
Starting at 1st level, you have to learn
the basics of your innate technique, which
is to cut things. When you take the attack
action, you can spend Cursed Energy up to
your proficiency bonus, to replace one of
those attacks with an attempt to dismantle
a creature or object within 150/600 feet
of you. Make a ranged Cursed Energy Attack
Roll against your target. On a hit, Xd8
slashing damage(with X being the number of
cursed energy spent).
When you gain the extra attack feature,
you can replace multiple attacks with
dismantle, but you must spend Cursed
Energy for each attack. If your target can't see this attack, being
within 5 feet of a hostile creature doesn't impose disadvantage on
this attack roll.
Cleave
At 3rd level, you learn how to cleave living targets apart in one
fell swoop. As an action for up to 10 Cursed Energy, you choose a
target within 150 feet of you. Make a ranged Cursed Energy Attack. On
a hit, the target takes a number of d10s equal to your proficiency
bonus + the amount of Cursed Energy spent in slashing damage.
If used a second time against the same creature, this attack ignores
any kind of resistance, since it adapts to the target's body. Being
within melee range of a creature who can’t see this technique doesn’t
impose disadvantage on the attack roll.
Divine Flame Open
At 6th level, you can utter an incantation to create an arrow of
fire. As an action, you can spend a number of Cursed Energy up to
your proficiency bonus times two, choosing a point within 150/600
feet of you. Each creature within X times 5 feet of that point must
make a dexterity saving throw. On a failed save, they take Xd12 fire
damage. On a successful save, they take half as much damage.
(Every time x is mentioned it's supposed to be your Cursed Energy
spent)
Perfect Form
Beginning at 11th level, your body transforms to reach peak output.
Your CE Stat, Strength, and Constitution scores all increase by 2,
including their maximums.
Additionally, you gain 2 extra arms right below your original ones,
as well as a mouth on your stomach. They are fully functional and can
act independently of your original limbs. They give you the following
benefits:
● You can take an additional bonus action during your turns.
● You can grapple or shove a creature as a bonus action.
● You can make an additional attack during your attack action.
● Once per turn, you can use Maximum Output on one of your cursed
Techniques without needing to wait for a round or spend
additional actions.
Domain Expansion: Malevolent Shrine
At last at 18th level, you have reached
the pinnacle of your innate technique,
making you finally be able to expand
your domain expansion. At initiative 20,
the domain cuts everything with cleave
and dismantle, causing whoever is within
the area of effect to take damage from a
cleave and a dismantle, which both count
as if the maximum amount of Cursed
Energy was used in it.
Additionally, objects within the domain
take this damage at initiative 20.
