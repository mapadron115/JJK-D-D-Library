+1 Cursed Weapon

A Grade 3 cursed weapon, any weapon of any kind, imbued with a curse.

FormWeapon (Any)
GradeGrade 3
AttunementNot required

Field notes. Subject: the same road, one step higher. Where the basic cursed weapon merely crosses the threshold, steel made real to curses, this one crosses it with a sharper edge: plus one to strike and to wound, woven into the same curse-imbued rite. Two truths govern it. First, it inherits everything the lesser blade owns: no attunement, any weapon of any kind, and the power to wound curses, shikigami, and all that shrug off mundane steel. Second, the plus-one is honest work, not glory: it does not change what the weapon can touch, only how surely and how hard it touches. Mark this: the student’s first blade teaches the hand; the second teaches ambition. Mastery is the first lesson, repeated with better steel.

Passive

Curse-Imbued Strike

The familiar rite, refined: plus one to attack and damage, and the same curse-imbued standing, real to curses, shikigami, and all that ignore mundane steel. No attunement. The threshold, kept, and the edge, improved. You gain a +1 bonus to attack and damage rolls made with this weapon. The weapon is considered a cursed weapon: its attacks can damage curses, shikigami, and any creature that resists or ignores damage from non-cursed physical weapons.
