Golden Fault

Damage creates Fractures. You decide whether to mend those breaks into stronger seams or exploit them before they can heal.

RoleDefender / Support Bruiser
Power tierHigh
Main resourceLacquer tokens
Full progression

Level Progression

1Fracture Sense, Lacquer Pot, Mend the Break, Exploit the Break

Fracture Sense

Once per round when a creature, object, construct, or barrier you can see within 60 feet takes damage, you may designate that damage as a Fracture without using an action. A target can hold up to 3 Fractures from you (5 at 11th level). Fractures last for 1 minute. A creature can hold Fractures whether it is willing or hostile.

Lacquer Pot

You have a pool of Lacquer tokens with a maximum equal to your proficiency bonus + your CE ability modifier (minimum 2). You begin each long rest with a number of tokens equal to your proficiency bonus.

Mend the Break

Bonus Action • 2 CE • 30 feet. Choose a willing creature bearing one of your Fractures, consume the Fracture and expend one Lacquer token. The target regains HP equal to 5 + your CE modifier. An object, construct, or barrier instead regains HP equal to twice that (10 + twice your CE modifier). This healing cannot restore a destroyed limb by itself.

Exploit the Break

Once per turn when you hit a hostile creature that bears one of your Fractures, you may consume one Fracture and expend one Lacquer token. Add +5 to the attack’s damage, and the target cannot regain HP until the start of your next turn.

3Seal the Seam, Reinforced Seam —

Seal the Seam

An ally’s bonus action, no CE: the ally consumes one of its own Fractures and one Lacquer token to heal 5 + your CE modifier HP. Beautiful Scar does not trigger on an ally’s self-seal.

Reinforced Seam

After you Mend a creature, it gains +1 AC or advantage against forced movement until the start of your next turn. If you Mend the same creature twice before the end of your next turn, it also gains 5 temporary HP.

6Living Kintsugi —

your Mend may end bleeding and stabilize automatically. By spending 4 CE and two Lacquer tokens, you may repair a non-severed limb injury by one step if the target has received appropriate mundane treatment. Once a creature benefits from this limb repair, it cannot do so again until a long rest.

11Catastrophic Break —

Your targets may now hold up to 5 Fractures each. When you hit, you may consume up to 3 Fractures at once, expending one Lacquer token per Fracture. If at least 3 are consumed, the target also makes a Constitution save; on a failure its AC is reduced by 2 until the start of your next turn.

18Maximum Technique: Golden Reassembly —

Action, 12 CE, once per long rest. Choose up to your proficiency bonus creatures/objects within 30 feet. Willing targets heal 15 + twice your CE modifier and may repair one non-severed limb injury; hostile targets instead detonate every Fracture they bear, taking 5 force damage per Fracture and falling prone on a failed Constitution save.

Feats

Technique Feats

Museum-Grade Repair

Your golden seams hold stronger than the original: when you fully repair an object, construct, or cursed tool, you can lace the break with gold. The next time it would take damage, it takes none instead, once per object.

Beautiful Scar

A creature you Mend yourself (an ally’s self-seal with Seal the Seam does not count) gains advantage on its next saving throw before the end of your next turn. Once per creature per short rest.

Shatterpoint

When a hostile creature reaches its maximum number of Fractures, you may immediately move 10 feet without provoking opportunity attacks. Your next attack against it before the end of the turn has advantage.

Rules integration

Table Rulings

Fractures are technique marks, not physical objects and cannot be scraped off; ending your technique removes them.
Healing from this technique still obeys any anti-healing effects already on the target.
A repaired cursed tool regains HP/integrity, not expended charges or once-per-rest abilities.
