Feature map

Level-by-level features, as the figure refined them.

L1

Reaper's Sickles

Action, 1 CE (manifest) · Bonus action (join/split)

Manifest a pair of cursed-energy sickles (light, finesse, 1d6 slashing each). As a bonus action join them into a greatsickle (two-handed, reach 10 ft., 2d6 slashing) or split it back. Persist until dismissed or you fall unconscious.

L1

Death's Mark

Bonus action

Mark one creature you can see within 120 ft. as your due: you always know its direction and distance, and it has disadvantage on Dexterity (Stealth) against your Wisdom (Perception). One mark at a time; lasts until a long rest or you mark another.

L3

The Whistle

Action, 2 CE

Each creature of your choice within 60 ft. that can hear you makes a Wisdom save or is frightened of you for 1 minute (repeat at the end of each of its turns). A creature already frightened of you takes 2d6 psychic (3d6 at 11th).

L6

"Why Did You Run?"

Reaction (pursuit) · Bonus action, 1 CE (teleport)

When your marked target moves, use your reaction to move up to your speed toward it without provoking opportunity attacks. As a bonus action for 1 CE, teleport up to 120 ft. to an unoccupied space you can see within 15 ft. of your marked target.

L11

Wounds That Do Not Close

— (passive)

Creatures damaged by your sickles can't regain hit points until the end of your next turn; magical healing (incl. RCT) restores half unless the healer spends 2 extra CE. Your marked target has disadvantage on saves vs. your Whistle.

Domain Expansion

The Last Whistle

Full turn, 20 CE

A rules domain — no sure-hit; the rules are the weapon. Darkness: a forest road under a white moon, the whistle looping endlessly. No Running: no creature inside except you can willingly end its move farther from you than where it began; teleportation fails. The Whistle Counts: at initiative 20, each creature of your choice inside makes a Wisdom save or gains one Due counter (max 3) — one: frightened while inside; two: speed halved and no reactions; three: Constitution save at the start of each of its turns or drop to 0 HP. Face Him: hitting you with an attack removes one Due counter. Counters vanish when the domain ends or a creature leaves its radius.
