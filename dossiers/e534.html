Shadow Eminence

By day, an unremarkable face in the crowd. By night, the Eminence in Shadow — a slime-clad god of theatrics who announces his apocalypses before he performs them. His innermost world is a second sun. He has never once received the credit. That is exactly how he wants it.

Innate Technique • The Eminence in Shadow

Shadow Eminence

Field notes. Subject: Cid Kagenou, who does not exist. Five truths govern him. First: the mob is the mask. Slouched shoulders, average grades, a face the eye slides off — he has practiced being unremarkable the way other men practice the sword. Second: the slime is the suit. It remembers being experimented on, and it pours over him like spilled midnight, hardening into whatever the scene requires. Third: the power is the secret. He is absurdly, unfairly strong, and no one will ever know. Fourth: the name is the technique. Every blow gets a title. Every title gets shouted. Fifth: the world is the weapon. His innermost self, laid over reality, is a sphere of light — and everything inside it is already his.

Feature map

Level-by-level features

L1

Slime Bodysuit

Bonus action · 1 CE · Manifest the slime suit until dismissed. While worn: +1 AC; as a bonus action, reshape it into any melee weapon (uses that weapon's profile, counts as a class weapon) or extend tendrils (10-ft reach on unarmed strikes).

The slime remembers everything. It pours over him like spilled midnight and hardens into whatever the scene requires — blade, armor, wings that are not yet wings. He wears apocalypse as a bodysuit and calls it fashion.

L1

Mob Camouflage

Passive · Advantage on Deception checks to appear harmless or weak. The first attack you make each combat has advantage.

Nobody looks twice at the mob — which is why the first blow always lands. He has never been suspected. He has never been thanked. Both facts delight him equally.

L1

Overcharged Strike

No action · 1 Atomic · When you hit with your slime weapon: add 1d8 force. (Atomic: maximum PB; gain 1 when you hit a creature with your slime weapon, once per turn.)

A little extra. Just a taste. The mob would never.

L3

Atomic Discharge

Action · 2+ Atomic · 60-ft range, 10-ft radius atomic blast · 2d10 force per Atomic spent, Dexterity save for half.

The small apocalypse. He cups it in his palm like a stage magician palming a coin, and the coin is a sun. Veterans of his performances learn to watch his hands the way sailors watch the horizon.

L6

Slime Wings & Threads

Bonus action · 2 CE · Sprout slime wings (fly 60 ft, 1 minute) or slime threads (grapple and restrain a creature within 30 ft; escape vs your CE DC).

The suit is never only a suit. Sometimes it is wings, black and slick as an oil spill at midnight. Sometimes it is threads, fine as lies, strong as verdicts. He ties up battlefields the way stagehands tie up curtains.

L11

Domain Expansion: I am Atomic

He speaks the words, and the world obliges. A sphere of blue-white light blooms outward from his outstretched hand, laced through with pink like veins of dawn — his innermost world laying itself over the real one, neat as a tablecloth. Inside it, the air hums at a pitch that makes teeth ache. Everything in the light is already his.

Full-turn action · 20 CE · 120-ft radius (OPEN — no barrier) · Duration 3 minutes · Clash 1,000 points (Tier V) · Technique Burnout: 3 rounds. You must declare "I am Atomic." aloud — the declaration is part of the technique.

Detonation (sure-hit, on opening). Every hostile in range takes 8d10 force + 8d10 radiant; Dexterity save, half on success.

Residual fire (sure-hit). At initiative count 20 of each round the Domain persists, atomic fire washes the area: 2d10 force to every hostile in range, no save.

It is open. There is nowhere to run that is not already inside it.

L18

Atomic Mastery

Passive · The Domain costs 15 CE and its radius becomes 180 ft. Atomic maximum becomes PB + 2. Gain 2 Atomic per qualifying hit instead of 1.

The theatrics were never waste. Every named technique, every pose, every shouted declaration was practice — and practice, compounded daily, is indistinguishable from divinity. The sphere grows wider. The light grows less patient.

L20

True Eminence

Full-turn action · 1/long rest · Open the Domain for 10 CE with the opening detonation maximized (no roll). Your Mob Camouflage cannot be pierced by divination short of a god's attention.

Ask him his name and he will tell you he is nobody. The slime disagrees. The crater disagrees. The second sun over the capital disagrees. But the mob? The mob was never there.

Feats

Four feats, named aloud.

Shadow Eminence teaches four feats. Each is optional, and you must meet its listed prerequisite before taking it. All names must be spoken.

Prerequisite: Slime Bodysuit

Theatrical Naming

When you give an attack an over-the-top name aloud, gain 1 Atomic (once per turn). The name must be spoken. The name must be absurd. The Atomic does not care about your dignity.

Prerequisite: Slime Bodysuit

Slime Arsenal

New morphs for the suit. Slime buckler: as a reaction, +2 AC against one attack. Slime bow: 60-ft ranged shots dealing 1d8 piercing. Constricting tendrils: as a bonus action, grapple a creature within 15 ft.

Prerequisite: Mob Camouflage

Mob's Escape

After you use Atomic Discharge or drop a creature to 0 hit points: take the Hide action as a bonus action with advantage, and leave a slime decoy of yourself in your space. The decoy lasts 1 minute, cannot act or move, and is obviously you to anyone who knew your identity all along — which is no one.

Prerequisite: Slime Wings & Threads

Shadow Step

2 Atomic · Bonus action: take the Dash action; opportunity attacks against you have disadvantage this turn. He was never slow. He was merely uninterested in arriving.

Rules integration

Edge cases and shared systems.

Campaign canon notes

Cursed Energy baseline: CE pool is based on class; CE ability is Charisma or Wisdom (player's choice). CE save DC = 8 + CE ability modifier + proficiency bonus. Regain all spent CE on a long rest.
Atomic pool: maximum equals your proficiency bonus (PB + 2 from Atomic Mastery). Gain 1 Atomic when you hit a creature with your slime weapon, once per turn (2 per hit from Atomic Mastery). Unspent Atomic resets to 0 when you finish a long rest.
Slime weapon: reshaping the suit into a weapon is a bonus action; the weapon uses the chosen profile's damage die and properties and counts as a class weapon for your features. Dismissing the suit is a free action.
The declaration: "I am Atomic." is a verbal component of the Domain — if you cannot speak, you cannot open it. The opening detonation's Dexterity save DC is your CE save DC.
Open Domain: I am Atomic manifests directly onto reality with no barrier — there is no barrier AC or HP to attack, and it cannot be entered or left by breaking a wall. It is simply there, a sphere of his inner world, until the duration ends.
Domain clash bookkeeping: 1,000 clash points (Tier V). A 100+ point advantage collapses the weaker Domain; collapse deals no damage. Technique Burnout: 3 rounds.
Perfect Mob identity: divination effects used to identify you as the source of an attack or of the Domain fail unless the caster already knew your identity. Mundane investigation faces the same wall: the mob was never there.
