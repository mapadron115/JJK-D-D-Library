Feature map

Level-by-level features

Field notes. Subject: momentum, stockpiled. One rotating arm, wound like a flywheel, and a doctrine I have not seen elsewhere: everyone can see the punch coming, and that is the point. Three truths govern the Cyclotron. First, power here is stored, not spent, each bonus-action wind-up banks one wind, up to ten, and the punch compounds with every rotation. Second, visibility is the doctrine itself: while the arm spins, attacks against the user have advantage, and the technique makes no apology for it, the wind-up is a threat made in public. Third, the wind is fungible: the same stored rotation can be a melee punch, a thirty-foot shockwave line, split between two targets, or bled off into survival at 1d8 per wind. Hence the maxim: the arm never stops, so the enemy never rests.

L1

Ripper Cyclotron

Bonus action · Wind your arm: gain 1 wind (max 10) · While you hold any winds: attacks against you have advantage · Release as part of the Attack action: unarmed strike, on hit 1d12 bludgeoning per wind held (winds spent) · Winds decay after 1 minute without winding or releasing.

The core of the game, and the dossier states it like physics: Enhancer aura, one rotating arm, compounding returns. Everyone can see the punch coming, and that’s the point.

Release. Rev it while they watch.

L3

Shockwave Release

When you release, instead of a melee punch: each creature in a 30-ft line originating from you: Dex save vs your CE save DC or take your wound punch's damage (half on success) · Winds spent.

The punch doesn’t need a fist at the end of it. The dossier names the trade honestly. Range, at the cost of the sure thing.

L6

Bleed-Off

No action · When you take damage while holding winds: lose any number of winds to reduce the damage by 1d8 per wind lost.

A wound arm is a loaded spring, and springs can vent. Offense, converted to survival at the exchange rate of the moment.

L11

Split Winding

When you release a melee punch: split winds between two targets within reach (min 1 each) · Separate unarmed strike against each, each dealing 1d12 per wind assigned · Winds spent.

The rotation divides. The dossier’s own arithmetic. One wind-up, two endings.

L18

Ground Zero

Passive · Max winds rise to 15 · When you release a melee punch with 10+ winds: each creature within 15 ft of the target (other than you): Str save vs your CE save DC or take half the punch's damage and be knocked prone (no damage on success).

The ceiling was always a suggestion. A fully committed punch is an area event wearing a fist’s disguise. The arm stops. Everything else keeps moving.

L20

The Arm Never Stops

Once per long rest · Bonus action · For 1 minute: winding is a free action once per turn (in addition to your bonus-action wind), no maximum winds, winds never decay · When it ends: gain 1 level of exhaustion.

Once per long rest, for one minute, the rotation becomes perpetual. Mark the price. The dossier says so. The shoulder was not built for forever.

Pinnacle expression

Maximum and Domain.

Capstone material

Maximum: Full Rotation

Action · 8 CE · Instantly gain winds up to your maximum, then release: unarmed strike with advantage · On hit: 1d12 bludgeoning per wind, and the target Str save vs your CE save DC or be pushed 20 ft and knocked prone.

The Maximum is the whole wind-up in a single motion. The whole fight was the wind-up for this.

Domain Expansion: Centrifuge

Full turn · 20 CE · 18th level · Clash 800, burnout 5 rounds, duration 2 min, radius 120 ft · Sure-hit: wound-arm strikes automatically hit; once per turn, gain 2 winds as part of your movement · At the start of each enemy's turn: Str save vs your CE save DC or be pulled 15 ft toward you.

The room itself winds up. The walls begin a slow rotation, accelerating, the world becoming a blur at the edges while the center holds impossibly still. Wind screams in a tightening spiral, dust and debris orbiting like a galaxy with a fist at its heart.

The sure-hit Domain, and its fantasy is the room itself winding up. The Cyclotron’s endgame is not a bigger punch, it is a world that spins with the arm.

Sure-hit: the rotation is inescapable. Winding costs you nothing extra. Come closer. The arm is ready.

Rules integration

Edge cases and shared systems.

Campaign canon notes

Cursed Energy baseline: CE pool is based on class; CE ability is Charisma or Wisdom (player's choice). CE save DC = 8 + CE ability modifier + proficiency bonus. Regain all spent CE on a long rest.
Winding economy: winding is a bonus action granting 1 wind (maximum 10; 15 from L18). Release is part of the Attack action: 1d12 bludgeoning per wind. While holding any winds, attacks against you have advantage (telegraphed). Winds decay after 1 minute without winding or releasing.
No declaration required: unlike Jajanken (e055), Ripper Cyclotron needs no spoken declaration, Phinks doesn't announce. The telegraphing is physical, not verbal; silence impedes nothing.
Shockwave Release: a 30-foot line originating from you; Dexterity save for half damage. Splitting winds (L11) applies only to melee releases, not to the shockwave line.
Domain activation defaults: rendered as full turn + 20 CE per the D008 normal-Domain baseline, with Tier III clash stats (800 / 5-round burnout / 2 min) and a 120-ft radius.
Maximum vs. Reversal: Ripper Cyclotron's pinnacle is written as a Maximum Technique (Full Rotation), not a Reversal; it occupies the technique's pinnacle slot.
