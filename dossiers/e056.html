Ripper Cyclotron
Arm-Wound Enhancement Engine

Wind the arm, announce the threat, and make every rotation matter. Simple enough to understand in one sentence, dangerous enough that nobody wants to let you reach fifteen.

Power tierTier III · Apex
Core engineArm Rotations
Core law

What the technique actually does.

Rules are written to plug into the finalized campaign glossary: normal actions stay normal, direct attacks roll, and only named Domain events bypass the initial roll.

Bonus action

Wind the Arm

Gain one Rotation by making a full clockwise wind. As an action, gain up to PB Rotations. Rotations persist for 1 minute while you remain conscious and the arm is capable of moving.

On unarmed/melee hit

Ripper Cyclotron

Before rolling an eligible attack, declare stored Rotations. On a hit, spend the declared Rotations and add one Cyclotron Die per Rotation; on a miss, lose half the declared Rotations, rounded up. Named overrides replace that miss rule when applicable.

Counterplay

Visible Threat

Rotations are obvious. A creature that can see you knows approximately how wound-up the technique is, though not exact damage math unless familiar with it.

Full progression

Level 1 through 20.

Milestones use the campaign’s familiar 1 / 3 / 6 / 11 / 18 structure and add an explicit level-20 mastery feature.

1One Turn, One Promise

Ripper Cyclotron

Gain Wind the Arm (bonus action: gain one Rotation with a full clockwise wind, or an action for up to PB Rotations; Rotations persist 1 minute while you remain conscious and the arm can move) and the Ripper Cyclotron: before an eligible attack declare all stored Rotations; on a hit add one Cyclotron Die per Rotation spent; on a miss lose half the declared Rotations, rounded up.

Reinforced Shoulder

Advantage on checks/saves against disarm or injury to the winding arm while at least one Rotation is stored.

3Calibrated Impact

Partial Release

Spend only the Rotations you declare rather than automatically dumping all.

Cyclotron Guard

Reaction, spend 1 Rotation to reduce incoming melee damage by one Cyclotron Die + PB.

Backhand Release

You may deliver Cyclotron through any proficient melee weapon, but bonus dice remain bludgeoning/force.

6Fifteen Turns

Fast Wind

Once per turn after you hit with an unarmed or melee attack, gain one Rotation without an action.

Fifteenth Rotation

An attack spending 15+ Rotations treats resistance to its bludgeoning/force bonus as normal damage and forces Strength save or prone.

Counter-Wind

If a creature misses you with a melee attack, use reaction to gain PB Rotations instead of Cyclotron Guard.

11Rotor Core

No-Action Wind

At start of each of your turns, if you have at least one free hand, gain one Rotation.

Shockwave Punch

When spending 8+ Rotations, creatures in a 15-foot cone behind the target make Dex saves, taking half the Cyclotron bonus damage on failure or quarter on success. They cannot critical or Black Flash.

Twenty-Turn Threat

Ordinary rotation cap becomes 20.

18Maximum: Heaven-Spinning Fist

Maximum Technique

Action · 10 CE; requires 15+ Rotations. Make one melee attack. On hit, add all spent Cyclotron Dice, ignore resistance/immunity to the bonus damage, and the target makes an injury save if the single hit reaches the campaign limb threshold. On miss all Rotations are lost.

Thirty Rotations

Cap becomes 30.

No Domain

The entire technique is a self-imposed winding condition; no innate barrier develops.

20No Ceiling

Overwind

You may exceed 30 Rotations. Each Rotation beyond 30 immediately deals one Cyclotron Die of unavoidable force damage to you and imposes -1 to the attack roll that eventually releases them per 5 excess Rotations.

Perfect Calibration

Misses now lose only one-quarter of declared Rotations, round up.

World-Shaking Release

An attack spending 40+ Rotations creates a 30-foot-radius shockwave for half bonus damage, Dex half again. The direct hit remains the only part that can critical/Black Flash.

Pinnacle expression

Domain, Maximum, or equivalent.

Not every technique needs a Domain. When one exists, its sure-hit is a unique autonomous initiative-count-20 event unless the Domain is explicitly rule-based.

No natural Domain Ripper Cyclotron remains intentionally simple. Its “domain” is the psychological space created when everyone at the table watches the rotation count climb.

Innate Technique Feats

Four build-defining options.

These broaden the law instead of merely inflating the same number.

Prerequisite: Ripper Cyclotron

Southpaw Cyclotron

Choose left or right arm each time you begin winding. Rotations belong to that arm; you may maintain separate totals, but your global cap is shared.

Prerequisite: level 3

Backlash Wind

The wound arm lashes out on its own. As a reaction when a creature misses you with a melee attack, you may spend 2 Rotations to make one unarmed strike against it.

Prerequisite: Ripper Cyclotron

Counting Out Loud

Sometimes the count is the weapon. As a bonus action, wind theatrically without storing a Rotation — a feinted count. One creature that can see you contests its Insight against your Deception or Intimidation; on a failure it has disadvantage on its next attack roll against you before the start of your next turn, flinching from the expected blow.

Prerequisite: level 6

Fifteen Is Plenty

When you spend exactly 15 Rotations on an attack that triggers Shockwave Punch, you may shape the shockwave into a 30-foot line instead of a cone, and creatures in the line have disadvantage on the Dexterity save. Precision earns its own reward.

Rules integration

Edge cases and shared systems.

These rulings are designed around the revised Player Guide rather than the raw PDF where the two differ.

Cyclotron bonus dice are part of the direct attack and double on a critical.
Shockwave copied damage does not critical or Black Flash.
Rotations require a functioning winding limb; restraint can prevent new winding but does not erase stored Rotations.
Overwind self-damage cannot be reduced, redirected, or converted into fuel.
Technique DC: 8 + proficiency bonus + your chosen Cursed Energy ability modifier (Wisdom or Charisma unless your class says otherwise).
Direct criticals: rolled weapon, unarmed, and CE attacks can critical normally. After a qualifying critical, roll the campaign's unmodified Black Flash confirmation d20; a natural 20 triples the completed critical damage. Autonomous Domain events, damage-over-time, hazards, and unrolled pulses cannot Black Flash.
Maximum Output: unless a feature says otherwise, double that feature's base CE cost and enhance one supported axis—damage, range, duration, or accuracy/DC—using the campaign's Maximum Output rules. Named Maximum Techniques and Domains do not receive generic Maximum Output.
Technique Focus: effects marked Focus occupy the shared Technique Focus slot. Opening a Domain ends other Focus effects unless the Domain expressly preserves them.
Two-technique characters: these guides assume each technique progresses from character/class level as agreed by the campaign. Multiclassing itself does not grant another Innate Technique.
