Feature map

Workshop ledger of judgments rendered, in the order they were entered. The List is never wrong; it is only early.

L1

The List

Bonus action · no CE.

As a bonus action, mark one creature you can see as naughty or nice — no saving throw; this is observation, not magic. You may maintain marks on a number of creatures equal to your proficiency bonus; a mark lasts 1 minute or until you dismiss it (no action), and you may change a creature's mark by marking it again. Naughty: your weapon attacks against the marked creature deal an extra 1d8 damage. Nice: when marked, and at the start of each of its turns while the mark lasts, it gains temporary hit points equal to your Wisdom modifier + your proficiency bonus (these do not stack). Ledger note: the ink is permanent. The judgment is not — marks may be revised.

L3

Dual Curved Swords

Passive.

When you take the Attack action with a scimitar, you may make one additional scimitar attack as a bonus action. This attack follows the normal rules for fighting with two weapons. Ledger note: two swords, both curved, both certain. The workshop does not issue straight blades to the Guardian.

L3

Portal Tattoos

Bonus action · 2 CE.

The ink on your arms remembers every door. As a bonus action, spend 2 CE to teleport up to 60 ft to an unoccupied space you can see, stepping through a tattoo portal. You may do this a number of times equal to your proficiency bonus per long rest. Ledger note: the yetis maintain the far ends. Tip your yeti.

L6

Center of Wonder

Passive · 30-ft aura.

You are the Guardian of Wonder, and wonder is the opposite of fear. Allies within 30 ft of you are immune to the frightened condition and gain +1d4 to saving throws against being charmed. The aura is inactive while you are unconscious. Ledger note: fear cannot enter the workshop. It has tried. The door is small and the wonder is large.

L11

Judgment

Passive.

The List grows teeth. Once per turn, when you hit a creature marked naughty with a weapon attack, it must make a Wisdom saving throw against your CE save DC or be stunned until the end of your next turn — judgment, delivered. Ledger note: the naughty are always surprised. The nice never are.

L14

The Reading of the List

Action · 4 CE · 1/long rest.

Once per long rest, as an action, you read aloud from the List: choose any number of creatures you can see within 60 ft and mark each as naughty or nice (your choice per creature), up to your normal mark limit. Ledger note: the workshop falls silent for the reading. Even the elves stop.

Maximum Technique

The Final Judgment

Action · 10 CE.

The Guardian renders his verdict on the whole battlefield at once. Choose any number of creatures you can see within 60 ft and declare each naughty or nice — they are marked accordingly, following The List's limits. Each naughty creature must make a Wisdom saving throw against your CE save DC: on a failure it takes 8d8 radiant damage and is stunned until the end of your next turn; on a success it takes half damage and is not stunned. Each nice creature gains temporary hit points equal to 4d8 + your Wisdom modifier and ends the frightened and charmed conditions on itself. Ledger note: naughty or nice — the List has decided, the swords have seconded, and wonder itself holds the door.

Level 20 · Capstone

Guardian of Wonder

Passive · 1/long rest intervention.

You are wonder's guardian, not its servant. Your Center of Wonder aura extends to 60 ft. The List no longer has a mark limit, and you may mark a creature as a free action once per turn. Once per long rest, when an ally within your aura would drop to 0 hit points, wonder intervenes: they drop to 1 hit point instead and gain temporary hit points equal to your Wisdom modifier + your proficiency bonus — a last-minute addition to the nice list. Ledger note: the final entry in every ledger is the same — wonder wins.

Innate Technique Feats

Build-defining options.

These are optional character-building feats. You must meet each listed prerequisite before selecting it.

Prerequisite: The List

He Sees You When You're Sleeping

The List does not require line of sight, only knowledge. You may mark a creature you cannot see if you know its true name and it is on the same plane of existence as you — the mark otherwise follows the normal rules of The List. Distance is no excuse. He knows when you are awake.

Prerequisite: Portal Tattoos

Plus One

Your portals fit two. When you use Portal Tattoos, you may bring one willing creature within 5 ft of you along to the destination — it steps through the ink at your heels. The yetis have been notified. They are fine with it.

Prerequisite: Center of Wonder

Borrowed Wonder

As a bonus action, choose one ally within 30 ft: for 1 minute, they radiate your wonder in a 15-ft radius — allies (other than you) within that radius gain the benefits of your Center of Wonder. A creature cannot benefit from two instances of Center of Wonder at once. Wonder, shared, does not diminish.

Prerequisite: Judgment

Guilt by Association

Judgment is contagious. When a creature fails its saving throw against your Judgment stun, you may immediately mark one creature of your choice within 30 ft of it as naughty — no action required. The List takes note of accomplices.
