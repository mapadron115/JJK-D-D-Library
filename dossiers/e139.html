Core law

What the technique actually does.

Core Engine, Blueprints & Powerstones. Blueprints and Powerstones are his war chest: after each long rest he prepares his proficiency plus Intellect in Blueprints, and his Powerstones, capped at his proficiency, each stand in for two energy on his artifact features. Ten minutes of repair work restores a stone, twice per short rest. The campaign is planned; the battle is merely executed. Prepare PB+Int modifier Blueprints after long rest. Powerstones max PB; spend them instead of up to 2 CE each on Artifact features. Regain one Powerstone after 10 minutes repairing/building, max twice/short rest.

Field notes. Subject: the foundry that plans. I respect logistics the way other men respect courage, courage wins battles, logistics wins the war after the battle. The Artificer is logistics given a will and a workshop. Two truths govern it. First: the toolkit is the army, Blueprints prepared at one or two energy, Powerstones as petty cash, Thopters as loose coins, and the Titan Engine as the war chest given legs; nothing is improvised, everything is inventoried. Second: the factory is the Domain, at the twentieth count one prepared Blueprint executes as an autonomous battlefield version under a thousand clash points, and the machinery does not pause for the enemy's convenience. I have out-planned many foes. This one out-plans me as a standing condition. I am revising my definition of 'prepared.'

Full progression

Level 1 through 20.

1Artificer's Blueprint

The toolkit, inventoried. Six answers, packed before the question, Beam, Shield, Sensor, Grapnel, Drone, Utility Construct. I pack answers too. His answers, I note, are all prepared before I have finished asking the question. Prepare devices (1–2 CE each): Beam (action, 1 CE, 120 ft): ranged CE attack, 2d8 force. Shield (reaction, 1 CE): +2 AC against the triggering attack. Sensor (action, 1 CE): detect CE effects, hidden creatures, and traps within 60 ft for 10 minutes. Grapnel (bonus action, 1 CE): pull yourself up to 40 ft to a surface within 60 ft, or pull a Medium-or-smaller object to you. Drone (action, 2 CE): deploy a Tiny drone for 1 hour (fly 40 ft, AC 14, HP 10); it scouts and relays what it sees. Utility Construct (action, 2 CE): assemble a Small construct for 1 hour (AC 13, HP 20, speed 25 ft); it carries, holds, or performs simple tasks.

3Powerstone

The jars of calm. Stones, each substituting two energy toward an artifact activation, never toward a Domain or Maximum. The plan's petty cash. I have kept war chests. His small change, I observe, is denominated in calm itself. Create PB stones. One substitutes 2 CE toward an artifact activation, but never Domain/Maximum.

6Thopter Foundry

The sky's small change. Two energy fields his proficiency in one-hit-point Thopters, a three-dice swarm strike, or half cover for an ally. The foundry's loose coins, spent wisely. I have spent loose coins. His buy air superiority by the handful. 2 CE: PB 1-HP Thopters for 1 minute; swarm attack 3d8 force, or grant one ally half cover.

9Mightstone / Weakstone

The twin stones. Three energy and a minute of Focus: Might adds two dice to a weapon or construct strike once per turn; Weak forces a hostile within thirty feet to save or suffer minus two to attacks and damage. One stone builds; the other unbuilds. 3 CE: choose Might (+2d8 weapon/construct damage once/turn) or Weak (hostile within 30 ft Str save, -2 attacks/damage) for 1 minute Focus.

11Tolarian Academy

The workshop in the field. During a short rest he raises a temporary Academy: two more prepared Blueprints, all Powerstones restored once per long rest. Even campaigns need a rear echelon. His rear echelon, I note, is portable, and it follows him into the field. During short rest construct temporary workshop. Prepared Blueprints +2 and restore all Powerstones once/long rest.

13Prototype Portal

The borrowed design. Four energy duplicates one studied tool's activation, half dice, half range, half duration, once, then the Blueprint burns. Reverse-engineering, weaponized. Show him your tool for ten minutes and he will show you your tool, smaller, once. I have stopped demonstrating. 4 CE: duplicate one non-legendary cursed tool activation you personally analyzed for 10 minutes; use once at reduced scale (half damage dice rounded up, half range/area, half duration), then Blueprint burns until long rest.

15Titan Engine

The siege's crown. Six energy and two Powerstones summon a Large mech, heavy armor, eighty hit points, two three-dice strikes, rideable. The war chest, given legs. I have ridden horses into battle. He rides the treasury. 6 CE + 2 Powerstones: summon Large mech for 1 minute, AC 18, HP 80, two 3d10 attacks, rideable.

18Domain, The Sylex Workshop

At the twentieth count the factory floor opens, and one prepared Blueprint executes as an autonomous battlefield version, beam, wall, repairs, or lattice. A thousand clash points keep the machinery running. The factory does not pause for the enemy's convenience. I have timed offensives to machinery. His machinery, I note, is the offensive. Initiative 20 choose one prepared Blueprint and execute an autonomous battlefield version: 6d10 beam (ranged CE attack, 120 ft); 50-Integrity wall (three 10-ft panels, 1 minute); repair constructs 5d10 (up to three constructs within 60 ft); or deploy a restraining lattice (20-ft cube within 60 ft; Strength save or restrained until the next initiative 20). Clash bookkeeping: 1,000 clash points (Tier X). A 100+ point advantage collapses the weaker Domain; collapse deals no damage. Barrier AC 12 + PB; barrier HP = 10 × max(1, your CE modifier) + 2,000. Technique Burnout: 3 rounds.

20Urza Planeswalker

The archive complete. Blueprint cap doubles, Powerstones to ten, and once per long rest he may declare one plausible device the situation requires and draft it, subject to adjudication. The general's requisition form, signed by reality. I have signed requisitions. His are countersigned by the situation itself. Blueprint cap doubles; Powerstones 10. Once/long rest declare one plausible device needed for the current problem and create a temporary Blueprint, subject to GM numerical adjudication.

Pinnacle expression

Maximum, Domain, or equivalent.

Maximum, Golgothian Sylex (12 CE + all Powerstones): The plan's price. Twelve energy and every Powerstone: one round of charging, then sixty feet of fourteen-dice force and radiance, maximum dice against constructs and barriers, and he takes six dice of unpreventable ruin while every prepared combat Blueprint burns until his long rest. Victory, itemized. one-round charge, then 60-ft radius 14d12 force/radiant, Con half; constructs/barriers take maximum dice. You also take 6d12 unpreventable damage and all prepared combat Blueprints burn until long rest.

Feats

Technique Feats

Mox Engineer

When one of his constructs is destroyed, his reaction salvages the wreckage, one final attack or activation before it collapses. Nothing in his workshop dies without filing its last report. I have debriefed the dying. His debriefs are filed by the wreckage, on schedule. When one of your constructs, Thopters, or Blueprint devices is destroyed, you can use your reaction to salvage its wreckage: it makes one final attack or activation at its current position before collapsing.

Academy Rector

He may open his Academy's workshop to allies: one willing ally attunes to a single prepared device and uses it once. Good supply serves the whole army. I have supplied armies. His supply, I observe, is a curriculum. You can open your Tolarian Academy workshop to allies: during a short rest, one willing ally can attune to a single prepared Blueprint device and use it once before your next long rest.

Master Transmuter

As an action for two energy he may transmute one prepared Blueprint into another, the plan, revised under fire. I revise plans under fire. His revisions, I note, are manufactured on the spot, to specification. As an action for 2 CE, you can transmute one prepared Blueprint into another you know, rebuilding the device in the field. The new device must serve a similar function (a sensor array can become a grapnel, not a Titan Engine), and a burned Blueprint cannot be transmuted.

Blind Seer

His hands read machines the way scholars read books, name, properties, command words, whether trapped. Function, not history. True artifacts resist, yielding one property of his choice. Even the seer respects a locked drawer. I have picked locks. He reads them. I am revising my definition of 'secure.' Your hands read machines the way scholars read books. By touching a cursed tool and concentrating for 1 minute, you learn its name, its activated properties, its command words, and whether it is trapped or cursed, as the identify spell, but only for crafted objects and devices. This reveals function, not history; it will not tell you who made it or why. True artifacts and legendary tools resist: from such an item you learn only one property of your choice.

Mishra's Rival

His Titan Engine may mount one of his other Blueprints as a hardpoint, beam, shield, or sensor integrated, usable as a bonus action while the Engine stands. The siege's crown, jeweled. Your Titan Engine can mount one of your other Blueprints as a hardpoint: while the Engine is active, one prepared device (beam, shield, or sensor) is integrated into it and can be used as a bonus action on your turn.

The Brothers' War

His Titan Engine may carry his smaller machines into battle, four Thopters or one drone docked, untargetable while docked, deployable as a bonus action. The war chest travels with its loose change. Your Titan Engine can carry your smaller machines into battle: up to four Thopters or one drone can dock with the Engine. While docked they cannot be targeted separately, and the Engine can deploy them as a bonus action.

Rules integration

Edge cases and shared systems.

Shared rulings: Technique DC = 8 + PB + CE ability modifier. Direct rolled attacks may crit and use the campaign Black Flash procedure when eligible. Tokens, autonomous summons, passive damage, and initiative-20 Domain effects cannot Black Flash. Maximum Output modifies ordinary supported techniques, never named Maxima or Domains.
