Core law

What the technique actually does.

Core Engine — Blueprints & Powerstones. Prepare PB+Int modifier Blueprints after long rest. Powerstones max PB; spend them instead of up to 2 CE each on Artifact features. Regain one Powerstone after 10 minutes repairing/building, max twice/short rest.

Full progression

Level 1 through 20.

1Artificer's Blueprint

Prepare devices: beam 2d8, shield +2 AC reaction, sensor, grapnel, drone, or utility construct. 1–2 CE each.

3Powerstone

Create PB stones. One substitutes 2 CE toward an artifact activation, but never Domain/Maximum.

6Thopter Foundry

2 CE: PB 1-HP Thopters for 1 minute; swarm attack 3d8 force, or grant one ally half cover.

9Mightstone / Weakstone

3 CE: choose Might (+2d8 weapon/construct damage once/turn) or Weak (hostile within 30 ft Str save, -2 attacks/damage) for 1 minute Focus.

11Tolarian Academy

During short rest construct temporary workshop. Prepared Blueprints +2 and restore all Powerstones once/long rest.

13Prototype Portal

4 CE: duplicate one non-legendary cursed tool activation you personally analyzed for 10 minutes; use once at reduced scale, then Blueprint burns until long rest.

15Titan Engine

6 CE + 2 Powerstones: summon Large mech for 1 minute, AC 18, HP 80, two 3d10 attacks, rideable.

18Domain — The Sylex Workshop

Initiative 20 choose one prepared Blueprint and execute an autonomous battlefield version: 6d10 beam, 50-Integrity wall, repair constructs 5d10, or deploy restraining lattice Str save. Clash bookkeeping: 1,000 clash points (Tier X). A 100+ point advantage collapses the weaker Domain; collapse deals no damage. Barrier AC 12 + PB; barrier HP = 10 × max(1, your CE modifier) + 2,000. Technique Burnout: 3 rounds.

20Urza Planeswalker

Blueprint cap doubles; Powerstones 10. Once/long rest declare one plausible device needed for the current problem and create a temporary Blueprint, subject to GM numerical adjudication.

Pinnacle expression

Maximum, Domain, or equivalent.

Maximum — Golgothian Sylex (12 CE + all Powerstones): one-round charge, then 60-ft radius 14d12 force/radiant, Con half; constructs/barriers take maximum dice. You also take 6d12 unpreventable damage and all prepared combat Blueprints burn until long rest.

Feats

Technique Feats

Mox Engineer

When one of your constructs, Thopters, or Blueprint devices is destroyed, you can use your reaction to salvage its wreckage: it makes one final attack or activation at its current position before collapsing.

Academy Rector

You can open your Tolarian Academy workshop to allies: during a short rest, one willing ally can attune to a single prepared Blueprint device and use it once before your next long rest.

Master Transmuter

As an action for 2 CE, you can transmute one prepared Blueprint into another you know, rebuilding the device in the field. The new device must serve a similar function (a sensor array can become a grapnel, not a Titan Engine), and a burned Blueprint cannot be transmuted.

Blind Seer

Your hands read machines the way scholars read books. By touching a cursed tool and concentrating for 1 minute, you learn its name, its activated properties, its command words, and whether it is trapped or cursed — as the identify spell, but only for crafted objects and devices. This reveals function, not history; it will not tell you who made it or why. True artifacts and legendary tools resist: from such an item you learn only one property of your choice.

Mishra's Rival

Your Titan Engine can mount one of your other Blueprints as a hardpoint: while the Engine is active, one prepared device (beam, shield, or sensor) is integrated into it and can be used as a bonus action on your turn.

The Brothers' War

Your Titan Engine can carry your smaller machines into battle: up to four Thopters or one drone can dock with the Engine. While docked they cannot be targeted separately, and the Engine can deploy them as a bonus action.

Rules integration

Edge cases and shared systems.

Shared rulings: Technique DC = 8 + PB + CE ability modifier. Direct rolled attacks may crit and use the campaign Black Flash procedure when eligible. Tokens, autonomous summons, passive damage, and initiative-20 Domain effects cannot Black Flash. Maximum Output modifies ordinary supported techniques, never named Maxima or Domains.
