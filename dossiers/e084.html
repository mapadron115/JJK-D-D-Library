Rank S

Weapon Equip • Level 5

Water God’s Hairpin. Weapon Equip becomes Vinea’s thrusting water sword and can manufacture high-pressure spirals around the blade.

Vainel Arros

Action • 2 CE

A spiraling water spear rips through a 220-ft line, 15 ft wide. Dexterity save; 6 Djinn Dice bludgeoning/piercing and pushed 40 ft on failure.

Tidal Membrane

Reaction • 3 CE

Wrap yourself or an ally within 60 ft in a water membrane. Reduce incoming damage by 4 Djinn Dice and push the attacker or projectile 30 ft away if possible.

Vainel Al-Salos

Action • 5 CE

Water spears rain into a 120-ft-radius point within 300 ft. Dexterity save; 10 Djinn Dice piercing/bludgeoning, half on success. The ground becomes difficult terrain until your next turn.

Full Djinn Equip • Level 9

Fly 100 ft; swim 160 ft; breathe water; resistance to cold and bludgeoning from water. Water techniques can originate from any water you can see within 500 ft, and large natural water sources increase their area by 25%.

EXTREME MAGIC • LEVEL 13+

Vainel Ganezza

14 CE • Canon Extreme

Gather enough water to drown a city district. Choose a 300-ft cone tsunami or reshape it into a 600-ft-long, 80-ft-wide spear. Strength save; 18 Djinn Dice bludgeoning/piercing, half on success. Failure carries targets up to 150 ft and knocks prone.

Anime-scale: Strategic scale: flood a city or form a colossal spear capable of attacking giant entities.
