Field notes. Subject: the Djinn of water and grief. I have interviewed the wielder twice, and the room felt like a harbor at ebb: Vinea answers not to command but to sorrow, and its works all push the world away or drown it. Three truths govern her. First, the spears strike and then exile: Vainel Arros hurls the struck forty feet, the Membrane shoves the attacker back, every blow is also a dismissal. Second, the water keeps its own company: in Full Equip you may strike from any water you can see within 500 ft, because the sorrow stands wherever water stands. Third, Vainel Ganezza is not an attack but a flood-policy, a 300-ft cone tsunami or a 600-ft spear that carries its victims 150 ft and leaves them prone. Mark this: the loneliest Djinn does not fight you. It empties the room of everyone, including you.
