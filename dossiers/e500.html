Field entry

Journal Entry field report, converted to a table-ready stat block

Field notes. Subject: a Binding Vow wearing a woman’s shape. On manifestation, a vow-like phenomenon takes hold of the entire scene: neither she nor any other creature can initiate violence, her attacks cannot be made, and attacks against her automatically miss, until her question is answered: “Am I pretty?” Consider the terrible symmetry, every answer is a chosen consequence, not an escape. Affirm, and she lingers, unveils the slit mouth, and asks again; the vow renews. Deny or evade, and it dissolves into immediate hostility: enraged for a minute (+2 to attacks and damage), shocking speed (Dash as a bonus action), scissors striking at +8 for 2d8+4. I have watched sorcerers freeze at the question. Choose knowing the folklore. There is no safe answer, only the consequence you would rather survive.
