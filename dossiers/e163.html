Core law

Character fantasy

See disasters before they fully exist, stockpile Omens, and spend those warnings to move allies, spoil enemy timing, or make a foretold opening unavoidable.

Combat loop

  1. Mark a dangerous creature or situation with Omen Sight.
  2. Gain Omen when the marked threat meaningfully advances a danger you predicted.
  3. Spend Omen on warnings, pre-emptive movement, defensive corrections, and predicted openings.
  4. Escalate major threats through the Disaster Clock until you understand exactly what is about to go wrong.

The fiction. Registry notes describe the technique as a warning system built from regret: the user's cursed energy imprinted on every disaster they saw coming and could not stop, and now stockpiles the seeing so it is never unarmed again. The prevailing theory holds that the Omens are not prophecies but apologies — each one a memory of something that went wrong, offered up before it goes wrong again.

Full progression

Level progression

L1Eyes Before the Storm

Omen cap

Proficiency bonus. You begin combat with 1 Omen if you are not surprised. Omens vanish on a short or long rest.

Omen Sight

Bonus action, 1 CE, 60 feet. Mark one creature, unstable object, barrier, or obvious environmental danger for 1 minute. You may maintain one mark.

Gain Omen

Once per round when a marked threat hits with an attack, forces a saving throw, spends 3+ CE on one activation, causes a creature to fall prone/restrained/unconscious, or materially worsens a marked environmental hazard, gain 1 Omen.

Eyes Before the Storm

Spend 1 Omen as a reaction when a creature you can see within 60 feet is attacked or forced to make a saving throw: that creature may immediately move up to 10 feet without provoking opportunity attacks. If the movement takes it out of the attack’s reach or range, behind total cover, or out of the effect’s area, the triggering attack misses, or the creature automatically succeeds on the saving throw. You must declare this before the result is declared.

L3Disaster Clock

Disaster Clock

One marked creature may carry a 0–3 Clock. The first time each round it uses an action, bonus action, reaction, or movement to directly advance the same dangerous objective, add 1 tick. At 3 ticks you may ask the GM which one of the following it is currently best positioned to do before the end of its next turn: attack, move/reposition, defend, or activate a technique. The answer is truthful but does not force its behavior.

As an action and 2 CE, place a Clock at 1 tick on a marked threat you have observed for at least one round. When a Clock reaches 3, your next attack against that creature before the end of your next turn has advantage and ignores reactions that would impose disadvantage or move the creature.

L6Winged Intercession

Manifest black wings as a bonus action for 2 CE for 10 minutes: 40-foot fly speed. Once per round, when you spend an Omen to protect another creature, you may fly up to half your speed toward it without provoking opportunity attacks. If you end adjacent to it, you may interpose yourself — you become the target of the triggering attack or effect instead.

L11Catastrophe Map

Omen cap becomes PB + CE modifier (minimum PB), and you may maintain three Omen Sight marks. Spend 2 Omens when a marked creature scores a critical hit to make it a normal hit. When a marked creature begins Domain Expansion, Maximum Technique, or another full-turn charge you can perceive, gain 1 Omen immediately.

L18Point Pleasant

You learn Domain Expansion: Point Pleasant — Before the Bridge Falls. Full-turn action, 20 CE, 90-foot radius, 1 minute. Every surface shows red-eyed afterimages of near futures. At initiative 20, name Attack, Movement, or Technique for each hostile creature. Until the next initiative 20, the first time that creature performs the named category, every ally you choose may move up to 10 feet without provoking opportunity attacks, and the next attack roll made against the named creature before the next initiative 20 has advantage. This Domain predicts consequences — it never compels the target to perform the named action. Clash bookkeeping: 500 clash points (Tier III). A 100+ point advantage collapses the weaker Domain; collapse deals no damage. Barrier AC 12 + PB; barrier HP = 10 × max(1, your CE modifier) + 500. Technique Burnout: 10 rounds.

Feats

Build-defining options.

Bridgewatcher

When initiative is rolled, choose one visible structure, vehicle, barrier, or terrain feature. You know whether it is currently stable, damaged, trapped, or likely to catastrophically fail within the next minute. If it fails during the encounter, gain 2 Omens.

Scarlet Afterimage

When Eyes Before the Storm moves a creature, leave a red afterimage in its old space until the start of its next turn. The first hostile attack made through or into that space is made with disadvantage.

Bad Feeling

Once per short rest, after the GM describes a location but before the party commits to entering it, spend 1 CE to ask whether an immediate physical or cursed threat is present within 300 feet. The answer is yes/no/unclear; it does not identify the threat.

Prophet of Impact

Prerequisite: Disaster Clock. When a Clock reaches 3, you may spend 2 Omens to mark one 10-foot square you can see. If the marked creature enters that square before your next turn, it falls prone and cannot take reactions until the end of that movement.

Rules integration

Building the character

Best on Support Specialist, Strategist, or a mobile Scout. Prioritize your CE stat and reaction economy. The technique is strongest in parties that can exploit movement and information rather than simply asking you to deal damage.
Rules integration

Counterplay

Blind or isolate the user, force multiple unrelated threats at once, or stop marked creatures from telegraphing the same objective. Omen generation is capped per round and prediction does not mind-control anyone.
