Core law

Character fantasy

See disasters before they fully exist, stockpile Omens, and spend those warnings to move allies, spoil enemy timing, or make a foretold opening unavoidable.

Combat loop

Mark, gain, spend, escalate, the watcher's fourfold duty.

  1. Mark a dangerous creature or situation with Omen Sight.
  2. Gain Omen when the marked threat meaningfully advances a danger you predicted.
  3. Spend Omen on warnings, pre-emptive movement, defensive corrections, and predicted openings.
  4. Escalate major threats through the Disaster Clock until you understand exactly what is about to go wrong.

Field notes. Subject: a warning system built from regret. I have watched the red-eyed watcher mark a bridge and count the ticks until the disaster arrived on schedule. Three truths govern it. First: the seeing must be paid for, Omens are stockpiled only when a marked threat meaningfully advances the danger you predicted; prophecy unearned is prophecy unspent. Second: the warnings are humble, one Omen buys ten feet of movement, a moved ally, a spoiled timing; the technique does not rewrite fate, it nudges it. Third: the clock is the counsel, the Disaster Clock reads a creature's objective tick by tick until, at three, you may ask what it is best positioned to do. Mark the sorrow at the center: the Omens are not prophecies. They are apologies, offered up before it goes wrong again.

Full progression

Level progression

1Eyes Before the Storm

Omen cap

The stockpile has a ceiling; the debt has a schedule. Proficiency bonus. You begin combat with 1 Omen if you are not surprised. Omens vanish on a short or long rest.

Omen Sight

One mark, one minute, sixty feet, the watching begins. Bonus action, 1 CE, 60 feet. Mark one creature, unstable object, barrier, or obvious environmental danger for 1 minute. You may maintain one mark.

Gain Omen

The threat must move first: warnings are earned only when the danger advances. Once per round when a marked threat hits with an attack, forces a saving throw, spends 3+ CE on one activation, causes a creature to fall prone/restrained/unconscious, or materially worsens a marked environmental hazard, gain 1 Omen.

Eyes Before the Storm

One Omen, one reaction: a creature moved out of harm's way before the blow lands. Spend 1 Omen as a reaction when a creature you can see within 60 feet is attacked or forced to make a saving throw: that creature may immediately move up to 10 feet without provoking opportunity attacks. If the movement takes it out of the attack’s reach or range, behind total cover, or out of the effect’s area, the triggering attack misses, or the creature automatically succeeds on the saving throw. You must declare this before the result is declared.

3Disaster Clock

Disaster Clock

Tick by tick, the future becomes legible, ask the GM what it is best positioned to do. One marked creature may carry a 0–3 Clock. The first time each round it uses an action, bonus action, reaction, or movement to directly advance the same dangerous objective, add 1 tick. At 3 ticks you may ask the GM which one of the following it is currently best positioned to do before the end of its next turn: attack, move/reposition, defend, or activate a technique. The answer is truthful but does not force its behavior.

As an action and 2 CE, place a Clock at 1 tick on a marked threat you have observed for at least one round. When a Clock reaches 3, your next attack against that creature before the end of your next turn has advantage and ignores reactions that would impose disadvantage or move the creature.

6Winged Intercession

Bonus action · 2 CE · Manifest black wings for 10 minutes: 40-ft fly speed · Once per round, when you spend an Omen to protect another creature, fly up to half your speed toward it without provoking; if you end adjacent, interpose yourself — you become the target of the triggering attack or effect.

Manifest black wings as a bonus action Black wings, forty feet, the omen learns to arrive. for 2 CE for 10 minutes: 40-foot fly speed. Once per round, when you spend an Omen to protect another creature, you may fly up to half your speed toward it without provoking opportunity attacks. If you end adjacent to it, you may interpose yourself, you become the target of the triggering attack or effect instead.

Omen cap becomes PB + CE modifier Three marks, a larger stockpile, and critical hits rewritten as ordinary ones. (minimum PB), and you may maintain three Omen Sight marks. Spend 2 Omens (a reaction, invented) when a marked creature scores a critical hit to make it a normal hit. When a marked creature begins Domain Expansion, Maximum Technique, or another full-turn charge you can perceive, gain 1 Omen immediately.

18Point Pleasant

Domain Expansion · Full-turn action · 20 CE · 90-ft radius, 1 minute · Every surface shows red-eyed afterimages of near futures · At initiative 20, name Attack, Movement, or Technique for each hostile: until the next initiative 20, the first time it performs the named category, every ally you choose may move up to 10 ft without provoking, and the next attack roll against the named creature has advantage (it never compels the target to perform the named action) · Clash 500 (Tier III); Technique Burnout 10 rounds.

The sky over Point Pleasant is the color of a bruise, and every window in the town reflects two red eyes. The bridge stands whole, for now, and the air smells of rain that has not fallen yet. On every surface, afterimages of wings, of falling, of the moment before.

You learn Domain Expansion: Point Pleasant, Before the Bridge Falls. The Domain names the disaster aloud, and the named futures start coming true. Full-turn action, 20 CE, 90-foot radius, 1 minute. Every surface shows red-eyed afterimages of near futures. At initiative 20, name Attack, Movement, or Technique for each hostile creature. Until the next initiative 20, the first time that creature performs the named category, every ally you choose may move up to 10 feet without provoking opportunity attacks, and the next attack roll made against the named creature before the next initiative 20 has advantage. This Domain predicts consequences, it never compels the target to perform the named action. Clash bookkeeping: 500 clash points (Tier III). A 100+ point advantage collapses the weaker Domain; collapse deals no damage. Barrier AC 12 + PB; barrier HP = 10 × max(1, your CE modifier) + 500. Technique Burnout: 10 rounds.

Feats

Build-defining options.

Bridgewatcher

Initiative is rolled; the watcher already knows which structures will fail. When initiative is rolled, choose one visible structure, vehicle, barrier, or terrain feature. You know whether it is currently stable, damaged, trapped, or likely to catastrophically fail within the next minute. If it fails during the encounter, gain 2 Omens.

Scarlet Afterimage

A moved creature leaves its red ghost behind, and the ghost takes the next attack. When Eyes Before the Storm moves a creature, leave a red afterimage in its old space until the start of its next turn. The first hostile attack made through or into that space is made with disadvantage.

Bad Feeling

One CE to ask the GM what the location is hiding, prophecy as due diligence. Once per short rest, after the GM describes a location but before the party commits to entering it, spend 1 CE to ask whether an immediate physical or cursed threat is present within 300 feet. The answer is yes/no/unclear; it does not identify the threat.

Prophet of Impact

At three ticks, mark the square where the disaster will land. Prerequisite: Disaster Clock. When a Clock reaches 3, you may spend 2 Omens to mark one 10-foot square you can see. If the marked creature enters that square before your next turn, it falls prone and cannot take reactions until the end of that movement.

Rules integration

Building the character

Best on Support Specialist, Strategist, or a mobile Scout. Prioritize your CE stat and reaction economy. The technique is strongest in parties that can exploit movement and information rather than simply asking you to deal damage.
Rules integration

Counterplay

Blind or isolate the user, force multiple unrelated threats at once, or stop marked creatures from telegraphing the same objective. Omen generation is capped per round and prediction does not mind-control anyone.
