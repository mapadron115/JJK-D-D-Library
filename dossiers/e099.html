01Accelerator — A Certain Magical Index

Vector Authority

Tier V · CalamityVectors / reflection / calculation / wings

You do not create force—you rewrite direction and magnitude. The technique is strongest when the user understands what is touching them and weakest when an interaction slips outside their current calculation model.

Calculation Load

  • Your Vector Die is d6 at levels 1–4, d8 at 5–10, d10 at 11–16, and d12 at 17–20.
  • Maintain a Vector Filter as a bonus action for 1 CE; it lasts until dismissed, unconscious, Technique Burnout, or you fail a Calculation check triggered by an unknown vector.
  • Known physical vectors—ordinary weapons, falls, mundane projectiles, wind, pressure, and straightforward CE blasts—can be reflected. Conceptual, soul, internal, teleporting, sure-hit, or deliberately misdirected effects require specific adaptation before the Filter can answer them.
  • Calculation check = Intelligence (Arcana) or your CE ability check against DC 10 + the effect's CE spent (maximum +10) or a GM-set complexity DC. Success adds that interaction to your Known Vector list until your next long rest.

Pinnacle / Maximum

Maximum Technique — Celestial Vector Collapse (10 CE): choose a point within 180 ft. Creatures in a 40-ft radius make Str saves, taking 12 Vector Dice force on failure, half on success; failures are dragged to the exact center, then blasted 40 ft upward. The effect also tears unsecured terrain and projectiles into the collapse.

Technique Feats

Prerequisite: level 3

Passive Filter

Your first Vector Filter reflection each round costs 0 CE against mundane physical attacks; against CE attacks it still costs normally.

Prerequisite: level 6

Kihara Counterstudy

After a creature bypasses your Filter once, gain advantage on the next Calculation check made specifically against that bypass method.

Prerequisite: level 9

Borrowed Vector

Reaction when an ally within 30 ft willingly moves: redirect up to 20 ft of that movement to yourself or another willing creature.

Prerequisite: Black Wings

White Wings

When Black Wings would end, spend 4 CE to continue for 1 additional round and immediately end one frightened, charmed, restrained, or prone condition on yourself.

Binding Rulings

  • Reflection is not universal immunity. Internal effects already inside the body, soul effects, Domains, teleportation, and interactions the Filter has not learned use their normal rules until analyzed.
  • Reflection cannot reflect a reflected attack recursively.
  • Vector Touch is a direct rolled attack and can Black Flash. Passive reflections and Domain pulses cannot.
  • Maximum Output can enhance a normal Vector ability, not Black/Platinum Wings, the Domain, or the named Maximum.
