Vector Authority

Power tierTier V · Calamity

Core law

What the technique actually does.

Calculation Load

The engine’s account book: the Vector Die that scales with level, the Filter maintained as a bonus action, and the ledger of known versus unknown vectors. Every calculation below is drawn against this balance.

  • Your Vector Die is the technique's core damage die; it scales with character level — the full progression is given in the Level 1 entry below.
  • Maintain a Vector Filter as a bonus action for 1 CE; it lasts until dismissed, unconscious, Technique Burnout, or you fail a Calculation check triggered by an unknown vector.
  • Known physical vectors—ordinary weapons, falls, mundane projectiles, wind, pressure, and straightforward CE blasts—can be reflected. Conceptual, soul, internal, teleporting, sure-hit, or deliberately misdirected effects require specific adaptation before the Filter can answer them.
  • Calculation check = Intelligence (Arcana) or your CE ability check against DC 10 + the effect's CE spent (maximum +10) or a GM-set complexity DC. Success adds that interaction to your Known Vector list until your next long rest.

Field notes. Subject: calculation made into skin. Three truths govern this technique. First, nothing touches him unanswered: every vector that reaches his skin is read, understood, and returned — the Filter is a customs house at the border of his body. Second, he cannot answer what he has not studied: unknown vectors demand a Calculation check, because his CE has no mouth of its own; it speaks only through the vectors it is given. Third, his answer is always proportionate and never invents force — he redirects what arrives, never creating it from nothing. Hence the maxim: do not strike him with your weapon; you are only lending it to him for a moment.

Full progression

Level 1–20 Progression

1One-Way Road

The first lesson: attacks do not get through; they get returned. A reaction and 1 CE turns a strike back on its sender or reroutes a line — and his touch learns the same language, pushing and pulling with force dice. Let the enemy’s violence do the work of yours. Vector Filter: when a known external attack/effect would hit you, reaction and 1 CE to force the attacker to reroll the attack against itself or redirect a line/projectile up to 60 ft. For save effects, gain advantage and take no damage on a success. Vector Touch: 1 CE on a weapon/unarmed hit adds one Vector Die force and pushes/pulls 10 ft.

Vector Die progression: d6 at levels 1–4, d8 at levels 5–10, d10 at levels 11–16, d12 at levels 17–20. This progression is explicitly defined in the original Ten Heresies expansion; every feature that refers to a Vector Die uses the value shown for your character level.

3Vector Rewrite

He rewrites himself the way he rewrites attacks: gravity and momentum become suggestions — +20 speed, walls and ceilings as roads, leaps that ignore their own length. Against mundane steel he no longer needs to raise a hand; the Filter answers on its own, once a round. Bonus action, 2 CE: alter your own gravity/momentum for 1 minute; +20 speed, wall/ceiling movement, standing leaps use running distance. Reflection no longer uses your reaction against mundane weapon/projectile attacks you can perceive; it still costs 1 CE once per round.

6Atmospheric Weapon

Now he arms the air itself: compressed wind as a 60-ft line, and bioelectric rewrite on a grappled touch — 3 CE to turn a held man’s nervous system into a lightning rod. If the Filter is the shield, this is the moment it learned to be a sword. Action, 3 CE: compress air into a 60-ft line, Dex save, 4 Vector Dice slashing/force. Bioelectric Rewrite: on a successful grapple/touch attack, 3 CE, Con save; failure takes 3 Vector Dice lightning and loses reactions until next turn.

9Advanced Filter

Mastery means restraint. He carries PB adaptations through the long rest, permits harmless vectors — light, sound, air, allies’ touch — to pass unexamined, and checks unknown effects without spending his reaction. The customs house learns manners. You can maintain PB adaptation entries through long rest and deliberately permit harmless vectors such as light, sound, oxygen, allies' touch, and chosen healing. Once/round when an unknown effect contacts your Filter, attempt the Calculation check without spending a reaction.

11Vector Transformation

He stops answering attacks and starts answering battlefields: a point within 90 ft violently attracts or repels, hurling bodies 30 ft on a failed save. He may also convert reflected damage into force, lightning, or thunder. Command, not defense — he is appointing directions. Action, 5 CE: choose a point within 90 ft; 20-ft radius violently attracts or repels. Str save, 6 Vector Dice force and move 30 ft; success half/no movement. You may also convert one damage type from a reflected attack into force, lightning, or thunder.

13Black Wings

The transformation: bonus action, 6 CE, a minute of 80-ft flight — and the Filter’s first cost each round drops to zero. He has stopped paying tolls at his own border. Note the price of heaven: once per long rest, no more. Bonus action, 6 CE, 1 minute, once/long rest: fly 80 ft, Vector Filter's first CE cost each round is 0, Vector Touch adds two dice, and you can reflect one failed-save external damage effect as though you had succeeded.

15Plasma Geometry

From atmospheric vectors he forges a plasma spear or sphere — 120 ft, 8 Vector Dice of lightning and fire, blindness on failure. Mark the constraint written in the margin: no vacuum, no weapon. Even his math needs something to calculate with. Action, 7 CE: create a plasma spear or sphere from atmospheric vectors, 120 ft, Dex save, 8 Vector Dice lightning/fire; failed save also Blinded until next turn. Cannot be used in vacuum or where the GM rules insufficient atmosphere exists.

18Domain Expansion — One-Way World

The Domain is the Filter expanded to law: a 175-ft barrier where every hostile’s movement is decided at initiative 20 — PUSH, PULL, or REVERSE, each struck by the Domain’s autonomous vector law. He does not chase his enemies; he appoints their direction, and the field obeys. 175-ft barrier. At initiative 20, choose PUSH, PULL, or REVERSE. Every hostile creature is struck by the Domain's autonomous vector law: Str save; failure takes 8 Vector Dice force and moves 40 ft in the chosen direction (REVERSE instead turns its own movement vector against it, knocking prone and dealing the damage). Anti-domain protection restores the save normally; your ordinary attacks do not auto-hit. Clash bookkeeping: 1,000 clash points (Tier V). A 100+ point advantage collapses the weaker Domain; collapse deals no damage. Barrier AC 12 + PB; barrier HP = 10 × max(1, your CE modifier) + 2,000. Technique Burnout: 3 rounds.

20Platinum Calculation

The final calculus: the Filter answers any analyzed phenomenon, bizarre CE interactions included, until he says otherwise. Black Wings loses its daily limit, and Platinum Wings grants +2 per Vector Die. He is no longer defending a body — he is administering a law. Vector Filter applies to any external phenomenon you have successfully analyzed, including bizarre CE interactions, until explicitly changed. Once/round make a Calculation check as no action. Black Wings no longer has a daily limit, but still costs CE; when activated at 10 CE it becomes Platinum Wings and Vector Die damage gains +2 per die.

Pinnacle expression

Pinnacle / Maximum

The Maximum is the Filter turned inside out: one point within 180 ft becomes a collapse — 12 Vector Dice, bodies dragged to the exact center, then blasted 40 ft skyward, terrain and projectiles torn in with them. This is not an attack. It is a tax levied on the whole neighborhood. Maximum Technique — Celestial Vector Collapse (10 CE; action): choose a point within 180 ft. Creatures in a 40-ft radius make Str saves, taking 12 Vector Dice force on failure, half on success; failures are dragged to the exact center, then blasted 40 ft upward. The effect also tears unsecured terrain and projectiles into the collapse.

Feats

Technique Feats

Prerequisite: level 3

Passive Filter

He extends his customs house over his allies: once per round, a known physical vector aimed at an ally within 15 ft is reflected as though it targeted him. Command extends its protection outward. You have learned to share your calculations: once per round, when an ally within 15 ft would be hit by a known physical vector, you may spend your reaction and 1 CE to extend your Filter over them, reflecting the attack as if it targeted you.

Prerequisite: level 6

Kihara Counterstudy

He learns from the one who got through: once per long rest, a creature that bypassed his Filter finds the same bypass path turned against it — the reflection travels the attacker’s own road home, and cannot be dodged the second time. Once you have witnessed a creature bypass your Filter, you learn the path it took: once per long rest, when that creature attacks you with the same bypass method, you may spend a reaction and 2 CE to reflect the attack back along its own bypass path — it cannot use that method to avoid the reflection.

Prerequisite: level 9

Borrowed Vector

An ally’s willing movement becomes his resource: redirect up to 20 ft of it to himself or another willing creature. Even generosity is a vector, and he is its master. Reaction when an ally within 30 ft willingly moves: redirect up to 20 ft of that movement to yourself or another willing creature.

Prerequisite: Black Wings

White Wings

When Black Wings would end, he may spend 4 CE to shed them as a single vector discharge — 4 Vector Dice and a 20-ft push to all within 20 ft. A wing that ends as a weapon. Nothing of his is ever simply discarded. When Black Wings would end, you may spend 4 CE to shed the wings in a single vector discharge instead: each creature within 20 ft makes a Str save, taking 4 Vector Dice force and being pushed 20 ft on a failure, or half damage and no push on a success. Black Wings then ends.

Rules integration

Binding Rulings

Reflection is not universal immunity. Internal effects already inside the body, soul effects, Domains, teleportation, and interactions the Filter has not learned use their normal rules until analyzed.
Reflection cannot reflect a reflected attack recursively.
Vector Touch is a direct rolled attack and can Black Flash. Passive reflections and Domain pulses cannot.
Maximum Output can enhance a normal Vector ability, not Black/Platinum Wings, the Domain, or the named Maximum.
