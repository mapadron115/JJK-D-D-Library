Vector Authority

You do not create force—you rewrite direction and magnitude. The technique is strongest when the user understands what is touching them and weakest when an interaction slips outside their current calculation model.

Power tierTier V · Calamity
Combat roleVectors / reflection / calculation / wings
Core law

What the technique actually does.

Calculation Load

Passive · Your Vector Die scales with character level (full progression in the Level 1 entry) · Maintain a Vector Filter as a bonus action for 1 CE: lasts until dismissed, unconscious, Technique Burnout, or a failed Calculation check · Known physical vectors can be reflected; conceptual, soul, internal, teleporting, sure-hit, or deliberately misdirected effects need specific adaptation · Calculation check: Int (Arcana) or CE ability vs DC 10 + the effect’s CE spent (max +10) or a GM-set complexity DC; success adds the interaction to your Known Vector list until your next long rest.

The engine’s account book: the Vector Die that scales with level, the Filter maintained as a bonus action, and the ledger of known versus unknown vectors. Every calculation below is drawn against this balance.

Field notes. Subject: calculation made into skin. Three truths govern this technique. First, nothing touches him unanswered: every vector that reaches his skin is read, understood, and returned, the Filter is a customs house at the border of his body. Second, he cannot answer what he has not studied: unknown vectors demand a Calculation check, because his CE has no mouth of its own; it speaks only through the vectors it is given. Third, his answer is always proportionate and never invents force, he redirects what arrives, never creating it from nothing. Hence the maxim: do not strike him with your weapon; you are only lending it to him for a moment.

Full progression

Level 1–20 Progression

1One-Way Road

Reaction · 1 CE · When a known external attack/effect would hit you: force the attacker to reroll the attack against itself or redirect a line/projectile up to 60 ft; against save effects: advantage and no damage on success. Vector Touch: 1 CE on a weapon/unarmed hit: +1 Vector Die force and push/pull 10 ft (Str save negates the push/pull).

The first lesson: attacks do not get through; they get returned. Let the enemy’s violence do the work of yours.

Passive · Vector Die scales with character level: d6 (1–4) → d8 (5–10) → d10 (11–16) → d12 (17–20).

3Vector Rewrite

Bonus action · 2 CE · 1 minute: alter your gravity/momentum: +20 speed, move along walls/ceilings, standing leaps use running distance. The Filter answers mundane weapon/projectile attacks without your reaction (still 1 CE, once/round).

He rewrites himself the way he rewrites attacks: gravity and momentum become suggestions. Against mundane steel he no longer needs to raise a hand; the Filter answers on its own.

6Atmospheric Weapon

Action · 3 CE · 60-ft line of compressed air: Dex save, 4 Vector Dice slashing/force. Bioelectric Rewrite: on a successful grapple/touch attack, 3 CE: Con save or 3 Vector Dice lightning and lose reactions until next turn.

Now he arms the air itself. If the Filter is the shield, this is the moment it learned to be a sword.

9Advanced Filter

Passive · Maintain PB adaptation entries through a long rest; permit harmless vectors (light, sound, oxygen, allies' touch, chosen healing). Once/round: attempt the Calculation check vs an unknown effect without spending a reaction.

Mastery means restraint. The customs house learns manners.

11Vector Transformation

Action · 5 CE · A point within 90 ft, 20-ft radius, violently attracts or repels: Str save or 6 Vector Dice force and moved 30 ft (success: half, no movement). Reflected damage may be converted to force, lightning, or thunder.

He stops answering attacks and starts answering battlefields. Command, not defense — he is appointing directions.

13Black Wings

Bonus action · 6 CE · Once per long rest · 1 minute: fly 80 ft; the Filter's first CE cost each round drops to 0; Vector Touch adds two dice; reflect one failed-save external damage effect as though you had succeeded.

The transformation. He has stopped paying tolls at his own border. Note the price of heaven: once per long rest, no more.

15Plasma Geometry

Action · 7 CE · 120 ft: plasma spear or sphere: Dex save, 8 Vector Dice lightning/fire, Blinded until next turn on failed save. Requires atmosphere (no vacuum).

From atmospheric vectors he forges a plasma spear or sphere. Mark the constraint written in the margin: no vacuum, no weapon. Even his math needs something to calculate with.

18Domain Expansion, One-Way World

Domain · 175-ft barrier · 1,000 clash points · 3-round burnout · At initiative 20 choose PUSH, PULL, or REVERSE: every hostile makes a Str save or takes 8 Vector Dice force and moves 40 ft in the chosen direction (REVERSE knocks prone instead). Anti-domain protection restores the save normally.

When the barrier closes, the world acquires a grain, like wood. Every surface shows faint chevrons pointing somewhere you did not choose, and the air itself leans, pressing at your back or your chest. To move against it is to swim upstream in a river you cannot see.

The Domain is the Filter expanded to law. He does not chase his enemies; he appoints their direction, and the field obeys.

20Platinum Calculation

Passive · The Filter answers any analyzed external phenomenon, including bizarre CE interactions, until explicitly changed. Once/round: Calculation check as no action. Black Wings: no daily limit (still costs CE); at 10 CE becomes Platinum Wings: +2 damage per Vector Die.

The final calculus. He is no longer defending a body — he is administering a law.

Pinnacle expression

Pinnacle / Maximum

The Maximum is the Filter turned inside out. This is not an attack. It is a tax levied on the whole neighborhood. Maximum Technique, Celestial Vector Collapse (10 CE; action): choose a point within 180 ft. Creatures in a 40-ft radius make Str saves, taking 12 Vector Dice force on failure, half on success; failures are dragged to the exact center, then blasted 40 ft upward. The effect also tears unsecured terrain and projectiles into the collapse.

Black Wings then ends. A wing that ends as a weapon. Nothing of his is ever simply discarded.

Rules integration

Binding Rulings

Reflection is not universal immunity. Internal effects already inside the body, soul effects, Domains, teleportation, and interactions the Filter has not learned use their normal rules until analyzed.
Reflection cannot reflect a reflected attack recursively.
Vector Touch is a direct rolled attack and can Black Flash. Passive reflections and Domain pulses cannot.
Maximum Output can enhance a normal Vector ability, not Black/Platinum Wings, the Domain, or the named Maximum.
