Vector Authority

You do not create force—you rewrite direction and magnitude. The technique is strongest when the user understands what is touching them and weakest when an interaction slips outside their current calculation model.

Power tierTier V · Calamity
Combat roleVectors / reflection / calculation / wings
Core law

What the technique actually does.

Calculation Load

  • Your Vector Die is the technique's core damage die; it scales with character level — the full progression is given in the Level 1 entry below.
  • Maintain a Vector Filter as a bonus action for 1 CE; it lasts until dismissed, unconscious, Technique Burnout, or you fail a Calculation check triggered by an unknown vector.
  • Known physical vectors—ordinary weapons, falls, mundane projectiles, wind, pressure, and straightforward CE blasts—can be reflected. Conceptual, soul, internal, teleporting, sure-hit, or deliberately misdirected effects require specific adaptation before the Filter can answer them.
  • Calculation check = Intelligence (Arcana) or your CE ability check against DC 10 + the effect's CE spent (maximum +10) or a GM-set complexity DC. Success adds that interaction to your Known Vector list until your next long rest.

The fiction. Jujutsu scholars classify Vector Authority as calculation made into skin: the user's cursed energy learned to read the language of every force that touches them and answer by rewriting it mid-sentence. Registry notes describe the technique as fundamentally reactive — it cannot answer what it does not understand, because the CE has no mouth of its own; it speaks only through the vectors it is given. The prevailing theory holds that the Filter's weakness is the price of its honesty: it must be surprised at least once by every new interaction, and the Calculation checks are the technique's memory learning to be surprised less often.

Full progression

Level 1–20 Progression

1One-Way Road

Vector Filter: when a known external attack/effect would hit you, reaction and 1 CE to force the attacker to reroll the attack against itself or redirect a line/projectile up to 60 ft. For save effects, gain advantage and take no damage on a success. Vector Touch: 1 CE on a weapon/unarmed hit adds one Vector Die force and pushes/pulls 10 ft.

Vector Die progression: d6 at levels 1–4, d8 at levels 5–10, d10 at levels 11–16, d12 at levels 17–20. This progression is explicitly defined in the original Ten Heresies expansion; every feature that refers to a Vector Die uses the value shown for your character level.

3Vector Rewrite

Bonus action, 2 CE: alter your own gravity/momentum for 1 minute; +20 speed, wall/ceiling movement, standing leaps use running distance. Reflection no longer uses your reaction against mundane weapon/projectile attacks you can perceive; it still costs 1 CE once per round.

6Atmospheric Weapon

Action, 3 CE: compress air into a 60-ft line, Dex save, 4 Vector Dice slashing/force. Bioelectric Rewrite: on a successful grapple/touch attack, 3 CE, Con save; failure takes 3 Vector Dice lightning and loses reactions until next turn.

9Advanced Filter

You can maintain PB adaptation entries through long rest and deliberately permit harmless vectors such as light, sound, oxygen, allies' touch, and chosen healing. Once/round when an unknown effect contacts your Filter, attempt the Calculation check without spending a reaction.

11Vector Transformation

Action, 5 CE: choose a point within 90 ft; 20-ft radius violently attracts or repels. Str save, 6 Vector Dice force and move 30 ft; success half/no movement. You may also convert one damage type from a reflected attack into force, lightning, or thunder.

13Black Wings

Bonus action, 6 CE, 1 minute, once/long rest: fly 80 ft, Vector Filter's first CE cost each round is 0, Vector Touch adds two dice, and you can reflect one failed-save external damage effect as though you had succeeded.

15Plasma Geometry

Action, 7 CE: create a plasma spear or sphere from atmospheric vectors, 120 ft, Dex save, 8 Vector Dice lightning/fire; failed save also Blinded until next turn. Cannot be used in vacuum or where the GM rules insufficient atmosphere exists.

18Domain Expansion — One-Way World

175-ft barrier. At initiative 20, choose PUSH, PULL, or REVERSE. Every hostile creature is struck by the Domain's autonomous vector law: Str save; failure takes 8 Vector Dice force and moves 40 ft in the chosen direction (REVERSE instead turns its own movement vector against it, knocking prone and dealing the damage). Anti-domain protection restores the save normally; your ordinary attacks do not auto-hit. Clash bookkeeping: 1,000 clash points (Tier V). A 100+ point advantage collapses the weaker Domain; collapse deals no damage. Barrier AC 12 + PB; barrier HP = 10 × max(1, your CE modifier) + 2,000. Technique Burnout: 3 rounds.

20Platinum Calculation

Vector Filter applies to any external phenomenon you have successfully analyzed, including bizarre CE interactions, until explicitly changed. Once/round make a Calculation check as no action. Black Wings no longer has a daily limit, but still costs CE; when activated at 10 CE it becomes Platinum Wings and Vector Die damage gains +2 per die.

Pinnacle expression

Pinnacle / Maximum

Maximum Technique — Celestial Vector Collapse (10 CE; action): choose a point within 180 ft. Creatures in a 40-ft radius make Str saves, taking 12 Vector Dice force on failure, half on success; failures are dragged to the exact center, then blasted 40 ft upward. The effect also tears unsecured terrain and projectiles into the collapse.

Feats

Technique Feats

Prerequisite: level 3

Passive Filter

You have learned to share your calculations: once per round, when an ally within 15 ft would be hit by a known physical vector, you may spend your reaction and 1 CE to extend your Filter over them, reflecting the attack as if it targeted you.

Prerequisite: level 6

Kihara Counterstudy

Once you have witnessed a creature bypass your Filter, you learn the path it took: once per long rest, when that creature attacks you with the same bypass method, you may spend a reaction and 2 CE to reflect the attack back along its own bypass path — it cannot use that method to avoid the reflection.

Prerequisite: level 9

Borrowed Vector

Reaction when an ally within 30 ft willingly moves: redirect up to 20 ft of that movement to yourself or another willing creature.

Prerequisite: Black Wings

White Wings

When Black Wings would end, you may spend 4 CE to shed the wings in a single vector discharge instead: each creature within 20 ft makes a Str save, taking 4 Vector Dice force and being pushed 20 ft on a failure, or half damage and no push on a success. Black Wings then ends.

Rules integration

Binding Rulings

Reflection is not universal immunity. Internal effects already inside the body, soul effects, Domains, teleportation, and interactions the Filter has not learned use their normal rules until analyzed.
Reflection cannot reflect a reflected attack recursively.
Vector Touch is a direct rolled attack and can Black Flash. Passive reflections and Domain pulses cannot.
Maximum Output can enhance a normal Vector ability, not Black/Platinum Wings, the Domain, or the named Maximum.
