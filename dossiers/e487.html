Classification & Risk

A Disease Cursed Spirit, ungraded. Latches onto weakened bodies; minor effects individually, but enough to create openings in moments that matter.

Stats

Ability scores & traits

STR 3 (−4) · DEX 10 (+0) · CON 10 (+0) · INT 1 (−5) · WIS 8 (−1) · CHA 4 (−3). HP 7 (2d6), AC 10, fly 20 ft. Invisibility. Plague (disease curse): its effects carry a minor sickness.

Actions

Actions

Sneezing Fit (recharge 5–6). One creature within 15 ft. must succeed on a DC 10 Constitution saving throw or be poisoned until the end of its next turn (sudden sneezing, distraction).

Risk

Special

Multiplication. In crowded, stressed environments the spirit multiplies: if a creature fails its Sneezing Fit save by 5 or more, a new Viral Flu with full hit points appears in an unoccupied space within 5 ft. Left alone it burns itself out quickly — exorcise the cluster before it spreads.

Rules integration

Edge cases and shared systems.

Campaign canon notes

Field rule — low-grade perception: Per the C&C field rules, Grade 3 and lower curses are invisible to creatures without cursed energy. A creature in a life-or-death situation at less than half its hit point maximum may temporarily or permanently gain the ability to see them. This spirit carries the Invisibility trait below.
