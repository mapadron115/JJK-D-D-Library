Core law

What the technique actually does.

Core law, resonant frequency. Every creature and object has a resonant frequency — the pitch at which its physical structure and its cursed energy circulation come apart. Your strikes tune you to it. Each hit you land on a target attunes you further: count hits landed per target. Attunement fades if you go 1 minute without landing a hit on that target. There is no pool, no tracker beyond the count, no engine — the technique is arithmetic, not accounting.

Field notes. Subject: a tuner, not a hammer. Three truths govern the pitch. First: everything breaks at its own frequency and no other — force is a confession of ignorance; the right note needs no strength at all. Second: cursed energy circulates, and circulation has rhythm — disturb the rhythm and the techniques built on it stutter, cost more, and eventually stop. Third: patience is the whole technique. The first hit is a question. The shatter is the answer.

Progression

Levels 1–18.

1First Tuning

When you take the Attack action, you may replace one attack with a Tuning Strike: melee or ranged CE attack (60 ft), 1 CE, 1d8 thunder damage on a hit. Each hit attunes the target — count hits landed.

The strike is barely a blow. It is a question asked at speed, and the target's structure answers whether it wants to or not.

Passive · A target with 2 or more hits of attunement has disadvantage on Constitution saving throws made to maintain concentration. Their cursed energy circulation is already wavering; holding a technique together gets harder with every note.

You do not need to break the dam to trouble the river. Two hits and their focus starts to slip.

3Listen

Bonus action · 1 CE · Sense resonant frequencies within 60 feet for 1 minute. You learn the structural weak points of objects (their break DCs), the damage vulnerabilities of creatures (if any), and whether any creature you perceive is concentrating on an effect. Choose one creature you perceive: you immediately count as having 1 hit of attunement on it.

The technique's second sense has nothing to do with hitting. Close your eyes. Everything hums, and the hum tells you exactly where it is thinnest.

6Breaking Point

Action · 2 CE · Shatter: choose one attuned target within 60 feet. It takes thunder damage based on hits landed — 1–2 hits: 2d8; 3–4 hits: 4d8; 5 or more hits: 6d8. Objects that are not being worn or carried are broken outright if the damage exceeds twice their hit point maximum.

This is what the tuning was for. One clean note, and the structure remembers it was always going to come apart.

Passive · A target with 3 or more hits of attunement has its cursed energy flow jammed: its technique CE costs increase by 1 (minimum 1 CE). Your Tuning Strike now deals 1d10 thunder damage.

The foundation levels up with you. The strikes hit harder, and the jamming starts — their circulation is yours to tax now.

11Resonant Cascade

When you Shatter a target with 5 or more hits of attunement, the resonance leaps: each other attuned target within 30 feet of the primary takes thunder damage equal to half the shatter damage, and until the end of your next turn their technique CE costs increase by 2. The five-hit setup is the price of the cascade — no shortcut, no substitute.

One breaking note finds the next. The room learns the pitch all at once, whether it was listening or not.

18The Perfect Pitch

You have reached the peak expression of the technique: the Maximum below. Your attunement no longer fades after 1 minute — once tuned, a target stays tuned until it takes a short rest.

The hunter stops counting. The pitch is simply known now, the way you know your own name.

Pinnacle expression

Maximum, Domain, or equivalent.

Maximum, Perfect Pitch (action, 10 CE): you sound the breaking frequency of everything you have tuned, all at once. Each attuned target within 120 feet takes 2d10 thunder damage per hit of attunement landed on it (Constitution save for half). Targets with 3 or more hits suffer total CE-flow collapse until the end of your next turn: disadvantage on all saving throws, and their technique CE costs are doubled. Creatures in range with no attunement take 4d10 thunder damage (Constitution save for half) as the pitch washes over them. The setup is the limiter — the Maximum is only as devastating as your patience made it.

Feats

Build-defining options.

Glass Singer

Action · 2 CE · Tune an object instead of breaking it: restore hit points to a damaged structure or object (2d10), perfectly tune an instrument, or shatter a lock's pins without harming the door. The same pitch that breaks can also mend — it was always about intent, never about force.

Dissonance

Reaction · 2 CE · When a creature hits you with an attack, emit a counter-frequency: the attacker takes 2d8 thunder damage and must make a Constitution save or have disadvantage on attack rolls until the end of its next turn. Resonance, worn as armor — the blow arrives already unmade.

Sympathetic Ear

Passive · You hear cursed energy flow the way others hear footsteps. You sense invisible creatures within 30 feet, have advantage on Insight checks to detect lies (you hear the heartbeat stutter), and can hear through walls and doors by their vibration. Listening, sharpened into sight.

Dead Air

Action · 3 CE · Create a 20-foot-radius zone of destructive interference for 1 minute: the area is magically silenced, and creatures inside have disadvantage on Constitution saves to maintain concentration. The pitch, inverted — instead of breaking things with sound, you unmake the sound itself.
