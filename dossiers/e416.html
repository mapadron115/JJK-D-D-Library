Lesser Warding Talisman

A Grade 3 cursed item, a paper talisman, expended on use.

FormCursed Item
GradeGrade 3
AttunementNot required

Field notes. Subject: a one-minute fortress folded into paper. Three truths govern the warding talisman. First, the wall arrives by command: as an action you channel cursed energy into the charm and a flat black barrier, a 5-foot square within 10 feet, stands for 1 minute, then the talisman is spent. Second, it is an honest wall: AC 15, 30 hit points, it fails its saving throws automatically and is immune only to poison and psychic damage, a barricade, not a spell, and I respect it for refusing to pretend otherwise. Third, it is an emergency, not a plan: one minute of cover, one use, no attunement, spend it where the alternative is bleeding. A minute of wall, raised before the blow, is worth an hour of regret spent after it.

Action

Raise the Black Barrier

Action · Create a flat black barrier (5-ft square) within 10 ft of you · Lasts 1 minute, then the talisman is expended · Barrier: AC 15, 30 HP, auto-fails saves, immune to poison and psychic damage.

As an action, you channel cursed energy into the paper charm to create a flat black barrier in the shape of a 5 ft square within 10 feet of you. The barrier lasts 1 minute, then the talisman is expended. The barrier has AC 15, 30 hit points, automatically fails saving throws, and has immunity to poison and psychic damage.
