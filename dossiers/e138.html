Core law

What the technique actually does.

Core Engine, Trophy Marks. Trophy Marks are his wall of honors: defeating a dangerous beast or monster, or studying one for an hour and passing its Nature, earns a Trophy Blueprint, and he equips his proficiency's worth at rest. Each is a summon trait, not a perfect copy. The hunter's memory, given teeth. Defeating a dangerous beast/monster, or successfully studying one (observing the beast for 1 hour outside combat and succeeding on an Intelligence (Nature) check vs DC 10 + the beast’s CR), earns a Trophy Blueprint. Equip PB Blueprints at rest. Each represents a summon trait rather than a perfect copy.

Field notes. Subject: a hunter who wears his kills as colleagues. Three truths govern him. First, a death is never wasted: defeating a dangerous beast, or studying one for an hour outside combat and besting its Nature, earns a Trophy Blueprint, his proficiency's worth equipped at rest, each a trait rather than a copy. Second, the pack is the weapon: one bonus action commands all his beasts, two may combine their strikes with advantage, and the marked prey cannot hide from them by any obscurity. Third, honor the hunt: the Apex trait is granted only after a worthy kill, and in his Great Hunt he names one Prey for the spectral stampede. The prevailing theory holds the Trophy is not a souvenir but a refusal to waste a death, the beast is gone, so the technique keeps its useful parts, and summons them as tribute.

The scars, worn outward. His Beast gains one equipped trait, Flight, Pack Tactics, Charge, Venom, Armor, Burrow, Amphibious, or Reach. The hunter's memory, itemized. Beast gains one equipped trait: Flight, Pack Tactics, Charge, Venom, Armor, Burrow, Amphibious, or Reach.

9Primal Might

The hunter's own blood answers. Three energy for a minute: ten feet of speed, advantage on Strength, his weapon attacks carrying an extra die, and his beasts share the speed. The pack runs as one body. 3 CE, 1 minute: self +10 speed, advantage Str, weapon attacks +1d8; beasts gain same speed bonus.

11Two Beasts

The pair hunts. He may keep two summons, sharing one command at most two attacks per round between them. Two sets of teeth, one will. Maintain two summons; they share one command collectively make at most two attacks/round.

13Apex Trophy

The worthy kill's honor. After felling a major monster, the game master may grant an Apex trait, a breath weapon, regeneration, magic resistance, huge size, or the like, one equipped at a time. The wall's centerpiece, earned in the field. After defeating major monster, GM may grant an Apex trait: breath, 30-ft cone, 6d10, Dexterity save for half, once per short rest; regeneration, 5 HP per round; magic resistance, advantage on saves against magical effects; huge size, reach +5 ft, +1d8 damage; etc.; only one Apex equipped.

15Overrun

The charge. Five energy, once per short rest: all his beasts and chosen allies within thirty feet move half their speed and make one basic melee attack as a reaction. The whole hunting party, loosed. 5 CE: all beasts and allies of choice within 30 ft move half speed and make one basic melee attack as reaction; once/short rest.

18Domain, The Great Hunt

At the twentieth count he names the Prey, and spectral beasts stampede down a twenty-foot-wide line ending at it, a Dexterity save against seven dice of bludgeoning and prone, the marked Prey at disadvantage. The final hunt needs no tracking. It has arrived. Initiative 20 designate Prey. Spectral beasts stampede through a 20-ft-wide line ending at Prey: Dex save 7d10 bludgeoning and prone; marked Prey has disadvantage.

20Apex Predator

The pack complete. Three active beasts, one of them carrying two normal Trophy traits plus the Apex, and the first Beast Call of each combat costs nothing. The hunter, at the head of everything he has ever respected. Three active beasts; one may carry two normal Trophy traits plus Apex. First Beast Call each combat costs 0 CE.

Feats

Technique Feats

Pack Bond

When he commands his beasts, two may combine their attacks on one target, a single roll with advantage, both beasts' damage on a hit. The pack's oldest tactic: one throat, many teeth. when you command your beasts, you may have two of them combine attacks on one target: make a single attack roll with advantage, dealing both beasts' damage on a hit.

Primal Hunter

His beasts do not hunt with eyes alone. The marked creature gains nothing from invisibility, obscurement, or hiding against them, scent and tremor betray it, and they have advantage to locate it, nor can it surprise the pack while the mark lasts. The mark still ends at the hour. Your beasts do not hunt with eyes alone. A creature marked by your Hunter's Mark cannot benefit from invisibility, obscurement, or being Hidden against your beasts, scent, sound, and the tremor of its steps betray it. Your beasts have advantage on Wisdom (Perception) checks to locate the marked target, and it cannot surprise your pack while the mark persists. The mark still ends after one hour.

Trample

A charging beast is a siege engine. When one with the Charge trait moves twenty feet straight at a creature and lands a melee hit, the target saves its Strength or is pushed ten feet, and the beast may keep moving. The hunt does not stop for obstacles. A charging beast is a siege engine. When one of your beasts with the Charge trait moves at least 20 feet straight toward a creature and hits it with a melee attack, the target must succeed on a Strength save against your technique DC or be pushed 10 feet directly away from the beast. The beast may continue its movement after the push if it has speed remaining.

Unnatural Predation

Once per short rest, he may command a beast within sixty feet of an enemy that harmed an ally to answer it with an attack as a reaction. The pack protects its own. reaction command beast to attack an enemy that harms an ally (the beast must be within 60 ft of that enemy) once/short rest.

Rules integration

Edge cases and shared systems.

Shared rulings: Technique DC = 8 + PB + CE ability modifier. Direct rolled attacks may crit and use the campaign Black Flash procedure when eligible. Tokens, autonomous summons, passive damage, and initiative-20 Domain effects cannot Black Flash. Maximum Output modifies ordinary supported techniques, never named Maxima or Domains.
