Wildspeaker

Tier IV · Special GradeHunt marks / beast trophies / primal summons

Core Engine — Trophy Marks. Defeating or successfully studying a dangerous beast/monster earns a Trophy Blueprint. Equip PB Blueprints at rest. Each represents a summon trait rather than a perfect copy.

Lv.FeatureRules
1Beast Call2 CE: summon basic Beast, AC 14, HP 8+2×level, bite 2d8; lasts 1 minute.
3Hunter's Mark1 CE bonus action mark visible creature; your beasts gain +PB damage once/round against it and track it for hour.
6Trophy TraitBeast gains one equipped trait: Flight, Pack Tactics, Charge, Venom, Armor, Burrow, Amphibious, or Reach.
9Primal Might3 CE, 1 minute: self +10 speed, advantage Str, weapon attacks +1d8; beasts gain same speed bonus.
11Two BeastsMaintain two summons; they share one command and collectively make at most two attacks/round.
13Apex TrophyAfter defeating major monster, GM may grant an Apex trait: breath cone, regeneration, magic resistance, huge size, etc.; only one Apex equipped.
15Overrun5 CE: all beasts and allies of choice within 30 ft move half speed and make one basic melee attack as reaction; once/short rest.
18Domain — The Great HuntInitiative 20 designate Prey. Spectral beasts stampede through a 20-ft-wide line ending at Prey: Dex save 7d10 bludgeoning and prone; marked Prey has disadvantage.
20Apex PredatorThree active beasts; one may carry two normal Trophy traits plus Apex. First Beast Call each combat costs 0 CE.
Maximum — Caller of the Pack (10 CE): summon five 1-HP spectral Trophy beasts for 3 rounds in addition to normal summons; resolve as one swarm, 8d10 physical damage/round against a marked target, Dex half.

Technique Feats

Beastmaster: summon HP +level.Primal Hunter: marked target cannot hide from beasts.Trample: beast Charge pushes 10 ft.Unnatural Predation: reaction command beast to attack enemy that harms ally once/short rest.
Shared rulings: Technique DC = 8 + PB + CE ability modifier. Direct rolled attacks may crit and use the campaign Black Flash procedure when eligible. Tokens, autonomous summons, passive damage, and initiative-20 Domain effects cannot Black Flash. Maximum Output modifies ordinary supported techniques, never named Maxima or Domains.
