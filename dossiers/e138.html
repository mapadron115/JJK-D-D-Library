Wildspeaker

Hunt marks / beast trophies / primal summons

Power tierTier IV · Special Grade
Core law

What the technique actually does.

Core Engine — Trophy Marks. Defeating or successfully studying a dangerous beast/monster earns a Trophy Blueprint. Equip PB Blueprints at rest. Each represents a summon trait rather than a perfect copy.

The fiction. Jujutsu scholars classify Wildspeaker as cursed energy with a hunter's memory — the user's CE learned the shape of every beast that ever bested them, or earned their respect, and now wears those shapes the way a hunter wears scars. The prevailing theory holds that a Trophy Mark is not a souvenir but a refusal to waste a death: the beast is gone, so the technique keeps its useful parts and summons them as tribute. The Great Hunt is simply what happens when that refusal finally finds a worthy Prey.

Full progression

Level 1 through 20.

L1Beast Call

2 CE: summon basic Beast, AC 14, HP 8+2×level, bite 2d8; lasts 1 minute.

L3Hunter's Mark

1 CE bonus action mark visible creature; your beasts gain +PB damage once/round against it and track it for hour.

L6Trophy Trait

Beast gains one equipped trait: Flight, Pack Tactics, Charge, Venom, Armor, Burrow, Amphibious, or Reach.

L9Primal Might

3 CE, 1 minute: self +10 speed, advantage Str, weapon attacks +1d8; beasts gain same speed bonus.

L11Two Beasts

Maintain two summons; they share one command and collectively make at most two attacks/round.

L13Apex Trophy

After defeating major monster, GM may grant an Apex trait: breath cone, regeneration, magic resistance, huge size, etc.; only one Apex equipped.

L15Overrun

5 CE: all beasts and allies of choice within 30 ft move half speed and make one basic melee attack as reaction; once/short rest.

L18Domain — The Great Hunt

Initiative 20 designate Prey. Spectral beasts stampede through a 20-ft-wide line ending at Prey: Dex save 7d10 bludgeoning and prone; marked Prey has disadvantage.

L20Apex Predator

Three active beasts; one may carry two normal Trophy traits plus Apex. First Beast Call each combat costs 0 CE.

Pinnacle expression

Maximum, Domain, or equivalent.

Maximum — Caller of the Pack (10 CE): summon five 1-HP spectral Trophy beasts for 3 rounds in addition to normal summons; resolve as one swarm, 8d10 physical damage/round against a marked target, Dex half.

Feats

Technique Feats

Pack Bond

when you command your beasts, you may have two of them combine attacks on one target: make a single attack roll with advantage, dealing both beasts' damage on a hit.

Primal Hunter

Your beasts do not hunt with eyes alone. A creature marked by your Hunter's Mark cannot benefit from invisibility, obscurement, or being Hidden against your beasts — scent, sound, and the tremor of its steps betray it. Your beasts have advantage on Wisdom (Perception) checks to locate the marked target, and it cannot surprise your pack while the mark persists. The mark still ends after one hour.

Trample

A charging beast is a siege engine. When one of your beasts with the Charge trait moves at least 20 feet straight toward a creature and hits it with a melee attack, the target must succeed on a Strength save against your technique DC or be pushed 10 feet directly away from the beast. The beast may continue its movement after the push if it has speed remaining.

Unnatural Predation

reaction command beast to attack enemy that harms ally once/short rest.

Rules integration

Edge cases and shared systems.

Shared rulings: Technique DC = 8 + PB + CE ability modifier. Direct rolled attacks may crit and use the campaign Black Flash procedure when eligible. Tokens, autonomous summons, passive damage, and initiative-20 Domain effects cannot Black Flash. Maximum Output modifies ordinary supported techniques, never named Maxima or Domains.
