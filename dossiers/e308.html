Relic profile

AC 18 · HP 120 · Damage Threshold 10 · Speed 180 ft · 2 seats · Fatal Momentum 0–6

Jujutsu origin

The modern curse is not treated as proof that every story surrounding the car was historical fact. In-world, the repeated association of the car with fatality, wreckage, and inherited bad luck is exactly what created the cursed vehicle after the fact.

Cursed Chassis

Add +4 to driving checks made with the vehicle. A creature proficient with land vehicles may use Dexterity + PB + 4.

Fatal Momentum

At the end of a round in which the car moves at least 120 ft, gain 1 Fatal Momentum, maximum 6. If it ends a round at 0 speed, all Momentum is lost.

Ram

If the car moves at least 60 ft directly toward a creature or object, the driver may make a vehicle attack. On a hit, deal 4d10 + 2d10 per Fatal Momentum bludgeoning damage and knock a Huge-or-smaller creature prone. The car takes half the damage dealt after Damage Threshold.

Dead Man's Route — Action, 4 CE, once per long rest

Declare a destination within 5 miles. For 10 minutes, the car ignores nonmagical difficult terrain, has advantage on driving checks, and cannot have its speed reduced below half by an external effect unless the source wins a contested check against the driver's result.

Don't Stop

During Dead Man's Route, voluntarily stopping before reaching the destination requires a Wisdom save with DC 12 + current Fatal Momentum. On a failure, the driver must continue moving by the safest available route.

Terminal Wreck

If the car is destroyed while at 4+ Momentum, creatures within 20 ft make a Dexterity save against the driver's Relic DC, taking 10d10 force damage on a failure or half on a success.

Binding Vow — The Route Wants Completion

Dead Man's Route gains its reality-bending pathfinding because the driver surrenders the uncomplicated right to stop.

Designer’s note. The car can be repaired by normal means; a creature may also spend 1 CE and 10 minutes to restore 10 HP to its cursed chassis.

Rules integration

Cursed-tool attunement

Campaign canon

Cursed-tool attunement: you have PB attunement slots (2–6). This tool occupies one slot and requires a one-hour ritual. Attunement ends voluntarily over a short rest, on death, or when you replace it while over capacity. Distance does not break attunement. Cursed-tool attunement is separate from magic-item attunement.
