Little Bastard

James Dean's cursed Porsche: it gains Fatal Momentum (0–6) by moving 120+ ft a round, fuels rams with it, and can declare a Dead Man's Route, ten minutes of unstoppable pathfinding that punishes stopping.

Little Bastard

Metaphysical Law: Once momentum becomes fate, stopping becomes more dangerous than continuing.

FormCursed Vehicle
EngineFatal Momentum
Attunement1 hour*

Field notes. Subject: a dare the machine makes with its driver. Three truths govern it. First, speed is a resource: move 120 feet in a round and the car banks 1 Fatal Momentum, to six at most, end a round stopped and the account empties. Second, momentum is a weapon: a 60-foot straight run lets the driver ram for 4d10 plus 2d10 per Momentum, knocking the Huge-or-smaller prone, the chassis taking half, after threshold. Third, the Route is a ritual of surrender: an action and 4 CE, 1/long rest, names a destination five miles out, ten minutes ignoring difficult terrain at advantage, speed no force can halve uncontested, but stopping demands a Wisdom save at DC 12 + Momentum, failure meaning the driver keeps moving by the safest route. Mark the terminal clause: destroyed at 4+ Momentum, it detonates, 10d10 force across twenty feet. Stopping was always the dangerous part.

Properties & techniques

Exact player-facing procedures

Relic profile

AC 18 · HP 120 · Damage Threshold 10 · Speed 180 ft · 2 seats · Fatal Momentum 0–6

Jujutsu origin

The modern curse is not treated as proof that every story surrounding the car was historical fact. In-world, the repeated association of the car with fatality, wreckage, and inherited bad luck is exactly what created the cursed vehicle after the fact.

Cursed Chassis

Passive · +4 to driving checks with the vehicle; a land-vehicle-proficient creature may use Dexterity + PB + 4.

Add +4 to driving checks made with the vehicle. A creature proficient with land vehicles may use Dexterity + PB + 4.

Fatal Momentum

Passive · At the end of a round in which the car moves at least 120 ft: gain 1 Fatal Momentum (max 6); ending a round at 0 speed loses all Momentum.

At the end of a round in which the car moves at least 120 ft, gain 1 Fatal Momentum, maximum 6. If it ends a round at 0 speed, all Momentum is lost.

Ram

Free (during movement) · Car moves at least 60 ft directly toward a creature or object: vehicle attack (DEX + PB + 4); on hit: 4d10 + 2d10 per Fatal Momentum bludgeoning damage, Huge-or-smaller targets knocked prone; the car takes half the damage dealt after Damage Threshold 10.

If the car moves at least 60 ft directly toward a creature or object, the driver may make a vehicle attack (Dexterity + proficiency bonus + 4, the dossier's driving bonus). On a hit, deal 4d10 + 2d10 per Fatal Momentum bludgeoning damage and knock a Huge-or-smaller creature prone. The car takes half the damage dealt after Damage Threshold.

Dead Man's Route — Action, 4 CE, once per long rest

Action · 4 CE, once per long rest · Declare a destination within 5 miles; for 10 minutes: ignore nonmagical difficult terrain, advantage on driving checks, and speed can't drop below half from an external effect unless the source wins a contested check against the driver's result.

Declare a destination within 5 miles. For 10 minutes, the car ignores nonmagical difficult terrain, has advantage on driving checks, and cannot have its speed reduced below half by an external effect unless the source wins a contested check against the driver's result.

Don't Stop

Passive (during Dead Man's Route) · Voluntarily stopping before the destination requires a Wisdom save (DC 12 + current Fatal Momentum); on a failure, the driver must continue moving by the safest available route.

During Dead Man's Route, voluntarily stopping before reaching the destination requires a Wisdom save with DC 12 + current Fatal Momentum. On a failure, the driver must continue moving by the safest available route.

Terminal Wreck

Passive · If the car is destroyed at 4+ Momentum: creatures within 20 ft make a DEX save vs the driver's Relic DC; 10d10 force damage on a failure, half on a success.

If the car is destroyed while at 4+ Momentum, creatures within 20 ft make a Dexterity save against the driver's Relic DC, taking 10d10 force damage on a failure or half on a success.

Binding Vow — The Route Wants Completion

Dead Man's Route gains its reality-bending pathfinding because the driver surrenders the uncomplicated right to stop.

Designer’s note. The car can be repaired by normal means; a creature may also spend 1 CE and 10 minutes to restore 10 HP to its cursed chassis.

Rules integration

Cursed-tool attunement

Campaign canon

Cursed-tool attunement: you have PB attunement slots (2–6). This tool occupies one slot and requires a one-hour ritual. Attunement ends voluntarily over a short rest, on death, or when you replace it while over capacity. Distance does not break attunement. Cursed-tool attunement is separate from magic-item attunement.
