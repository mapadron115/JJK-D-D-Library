Properties & techniques

Exact player-facing procedures

Relic profile

1d10 slashing · +4 to attack and damage rolls · Autonomous movement 60 ft

Jujutsu origin

Freyr's sword is remembered as capable of fighting on its own. The jujutsu interpretation turns 'worthiness' into a Binding Vow: the sword will spend its own stored cursed technique only for a wielder who continues to act in accordance with a declared duty.

Self-Fighting Blade

As a bonus action, command the sword to fly up to 60 ft and make one attack against a creature within 5 ft of it. Use your CE modifier for the attack and damage rolls if it is better than Strength or Dexterity. The autonomous attack deals 2d10 slashing damage before modifiers.

Hero's Oath

At the end of a long rest, declare one concrete duty for the coming day. While you are actively honoring it — the GM judges whether your actions honor the declared duty; knowingly acting against it shuts down the sword's autonomous features until the next long rest — the sword's autonomous attack requires no bonus action once per turn, but you must spend 2 CE each time it attacks this way.

Interposing Steel — Reaction, 2 CE

If the sword is within 30 ft of you or a creature named in your Hero's Oath, move it up to 30 ft and grant the target +4 AC against one attack.

Summer's Edge — 4 CE, once per turn

On a hit, deal an additional 5d8 damage; the wielder chooses fire or slashing on each hit.

Binding Vow — The Gift Cannot Be Reclaimed

If you willingly give permanent ownership of the sword to another creature, you can never attune to or command it again. If you knowingly betray your Hero's Oath, autonomous features shut down until your next long rest.

Designer’s note. The sword's strongest 'extra action' is paid with both CE and conduct, keeping the self-fighting fantasy without granting a free second character.

Rules integration

Cursed-tool attunement

Campaign canon

Cursed-tool attunement: you have PB attunement slots (2–6). This tool occupies one slot and requires a one-hour ritual. Attunement ends voluntarily over a short rest, on death, or when you replace it while over capacity. Distance does not break attunement. Cursed-tool attunement is separate from magic-item attunement.
