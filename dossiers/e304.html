Features & procedures

Properties & techniques

Exact player-facing procedures

Worthy Hand

The sword can hover, move, and attack independently beside its attuned wielder.

Full synchronization

At full synchronization, it gains its own turn immediately after the wielder.

Conduct judged

Acts of cowardice, betrayal, or abandonment of a declared duty suppress its autonomous abilities.

Given away

If the wielder willingly gives the sword away, they can never attune to it again.

Rules integration

Cursed-tool attunement

Campaign canon

Cursed-tool attunement: you have PB attunement slots (2–6). This tool occupies one slot and requires a one-hour ritual. Attunement ends voluntarily over a short rest, on death, or when you replace it while over capacity. Distance does not break attunement. Cursed-tool attunement is separate from magic-item attunement.
Balance & counterplay. Its autonomy is conditional on conduct — cowardice, betrayal, or abandoning a declared duty suppresses its abilities. The sword judges the wielder, not the enemy; force the wielder into an unworthy choice and the blade goes quiet.
