Feature map

Level-by-level features

Field notes. Subject: a loan officer with a shikigami. I have watched this technique and come away with three truths. First, the loan is a gift that indebts: Potclean latches on and the debtor’s current and maximum CE both rise by twice your level, you arm your enemy in order to ruin him. Second, interest compounds on their own spending: the first time each round a debtor spends CE, the debt grows by the amount spent. Every technique they cast is a deposit, into your account. Third, bankruptcy is the verdict: a bankrupt creature can neither spend nor regain CE until a long rest, and the lender profits at every stage, the bailout pockets the interest, the repossession regains it early. Hence the maxim: the house does not need to win the fight. The house only needs the books to balance.

L1

A.P.R. (Potclean)

Action · 2 CE · Conjure Potclean; it flies to a creature within 60 ft · Make a CE-ability attack roll vs its AC; on a hit it latches: the debtor's current and max CE both increase by twice your level (the principal) · Whenever you deal damage to the debtor, add it to the debt · At the start of the debtor's turn, interest accrues: new debt = (old debt + damage dealt since last tick) × 1.10, rounded down · Bankruptcy: debt exceeds the debtor's max CE → can't spend CE until a long rest; Potclean returns · Damage the debtor deals to you shrinks the debt · One loan at a time; Potclean can't be damaged while a loan is active.

The firm opens its doors: Potclean, a pot-bellied little lender. You have just made your enemy richer. That is the trap.

The counterplay. He's not fighting you. He's financing you.

L3

Audit

Passive · You know the debtor's current debt, max CE, and rounds until bankruptcy at the current rate · Sense the debtor's direction and distance within 1 mile.

The books are always open to you. Do the math out loud; the table gets to watch the number climb. The loan is a tether, and the lender always knows the balance.

L6

Branch Offices

Passive · Maintain loans on up to two debtors at once, each with its own ledger, interest, and bankruptcy.

Potclean splits its attention. The firm is growing, and every new client is a new ruin.

L11

Garnish Wages

Passive · The first time each round a debtor spends CE, the debt grows by the amount spent.

Owing changes how spending feels. Their own power feeds what they owe. Every technique they cast is a deposit, into your account.

L18

Chapter 7 Bankruptcy

Passive · A bankrupt creature can't spend or regain CE until it finishes a long rest · Your loans' principal becomes three times your level.

Bankruptcy hardens. Total liquidation: the debtor is left with an empty account and a full ledger.

L20

Bailout

Once per long rest · Action · Forgive an active loan: the debtor is freed, and you regain CE equal to half the forgiven debt (up to your max).

He’ll even let you off. For a fee. The house always wins, including when it loses the client.

Pinnacle expression

Reversal and Domain.

Capstone material

Reversal: Repossession

Action · 2 CE · The debtor takes psychic damage equal to 10% of the outstanding debt (rounded down, no save) · You regain CE equal to half the damage dealt · The loan continues; the debt is unchanged.

The loan, inverted: the creditor collects. You don’t have to wait for bankruptcy to get paid.

Domain Expansion: Ledger of Ruin

Full turn · 20 CE · 18th level · Clash 800, burnout 5 rounds, duration 2 min, radius 120 ft · Sure-hit: the lending itself — every hostile creature in the domain is instantly lent the principal (no attack roll), each with its own debt · Interest ticks on every debtor at the start of each of their turns.

The world becomes a counting house. Vast ledgers hang open in the air, their pages turning in a wind of numbers, and golden figures flicker above every head like prices at auction. The ground is polished dark wood, and somewhere a great abacus clicks, counting down something no one wants named.

The sure-hit Domain, and its fantasy is the lending itself. Inside the ledger, every account is already open, the sure-hit is the loan.

Everyone signs. Bankruptcy inside the domain follows the normal rules — the domain just makes sure nobody starts at zero.

Feats

Five feats, compounding.

Hakoware teaches five applications beyond the ledger itself. Each is optional, and you must meet its listed prerequisite before taking it.

Prerequisite: A.P.R. (Potclean)

Payment Plan

The debtor may use its action to pay down the debt 1-for-1 with its own CE. Owing becomes a negotiation, and every payment is a turn not spent fighting you. The lender profits from the installments and the distraction alike.

Prerequisite: Audit

Credit Check

Spend 1 CE and glance at any creature within sixty feet: read its books, current and maximum CE, with no loan required. You’d be amazed what people leave lying around. Foreknowledge is the cheapest instrument in the firm.

Prerequisite: A.P.R. (Potclean)

Predatory Lending

Seal a Binding Vow: once Potclean latches, you can’t dismiss it for a minute, and in exchange, interest rises to fifteen percent per round, compounding on old debt plus fresh damage. The vow is the rate hike; the restriction is the price of the arithmetic.

Prerequisite: A.P.R. (Potclean)

Debt Transfer

As an action, spend 1 CE: move an active loan to a different creature within 60 feet of the debtor (CE-ability attack vs. AC). The full outstanding debt follows. Someone else's problem now. The ledger doesn’t care whose name is on it, only that the numbers keep compounding.

Prerequisite: Chapter 7

Collateral

When a debtor goes bankrupt, seize collateral: one nonmagical object they’re holding or wearing comes to your hand. The debt’s paid. The watch is yours. Liquidation leaves nothing on the table, not even the table.

Rules integration

Edge cases and shared systems.

Campaign canon notes

Cursed Energy baseline: CE pool is based on class; CE ability is Charisma or Wisdom (player's choice). CE save DC = 8 + CE ability modifier + proficiency bonus. Regain all spent CE on a long rest.
The formula (the fantasy): at the start of each debtor's turn, new debt = (old debt + damage you dealt since last tick) × 1.10, rounded down (× 1.15 with Predatory Lending). Damage the debtor deals to you reduces the debt 1-for-1. Debt can't go below 0; the loan (principal) is separate from the debt.
Bankruptcy: debt exceeding the debtor's maximum CE → bankrupt: can't spend CE until a long rest (L18 Chapter 7: can't spend or regain CE). Potclean returns to you; the loan closes. Forgiving via Bailout (L20) closes it early instead.
Potclean is untouchable: while any loan is active, Potclean can't be damaged, targeted, or destroyed by anyone, debtor included. Dismissing it yourself (no action) closes all loans with no bankruptcy.
One loan at a time (two at L6, Branch Offices). Each debtor has a separate ledger and interest schedule.
Garnish Wages (L11): only the first CE expenditure each round feeds the debt; the debt grows by the amount spent, then spending resolves normally.
Domain activation defaults: rendered as full turn + 20 CE per the D008 normal-Domain baseline, with Tier III/IV clash stats (800 / 5-round burnout / 2 min) and a 120-ft radius.
Reversal vs. Maximum: Hakoware's pinnacle is written as a Reversal (Repossession), not a Maximum Technique; it occupies the technique's pinnacle slot.
