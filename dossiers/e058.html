Hakoware: Bankruptcy, Chapter Seven
A.P.R. Debt Domain

Attach an indestructible accountant, convert violence into loans, compound the balance at initiative 20, and force a debtor into cursed-energy bankruptcy.

Power tierTier IV · Special Grade control
Combat roleRule-domain creditor / CE suppression
Core engineDebt & Solvency
Core law

What the technique actually does.

Rules are written to plug into the finalized campaign glossary: normal actions stay normal, direct attacks roll, and only named Domain events bypass the initial roll.

Calculated threshold

Solvency

For Hakoware, a creature’s Solvency equals maximum HP + 5 × maximum CE. A creature with no CE still uses maximum HP. Recalculate only when a long-duration effect changes those maxima, not every time HP/CE is spent.

On hit · 2 CE

A.P.R. — Potclean

After a direct melee hit, choose to deal no HP damage. Instead conjure A.P.R. on the target for 1 minute and add Debt equal to the damage the hit would have dealt. A.P.R. is intangible/indestructible except by technique removal or Domain rules.

Mutual conversion

Loan Combat

While A.P.R. is attached, direct damage you deal to the debtor increases Debt instead of reducing its HP. Direct damage the debtor deals to you reduces Debt instead of your HP until Debt reaches 0; excess damage after Debt hits 0 affects HP normally.

Initiative 20

Interest

At initiative 20 each round while debtor is within Hakoware range, increase Debt by 10%, rounded up. This is not damage and cannot be prevented by resistance.

Threshold

Bankruptcy

When Debt exceeds Solvency, A.P.R. becomes I.R.S. The target suffers Technique Burnout and cannot voluntarily spend CE for PB rounds. It can still use mundane/class features not requiring CE.

Full progression

Level 1 through 20.

Milestones use the campaign’s familiar 1 / 3 / 6 / 11 / 18 structure and add an explicit level-20 mastery feature.

1The Loan Shark

Hakoware

Gain the Hakoware engine: Solvency (a creature’s threshold equals maximum HP + 5 × maximum CE), A.P.R. — Potclean (after a direct melee hit, 2 CE: deal no HP damage, conjure A.P.R. for 1 minute and add Debt equal to the damage the hit would have dealt), Loan Combat (damage you deal to the debtor increases Debt instead of HP; damage the debtor deals to you reduces Debt first), Interest (at initiative 20 each round, Debt increases by 10%, rounded up), and Bankruptcy (when Debt exceeds Solvency, the target suffers Technique Burnout and cannot voluntarily spend CE for PB rounds).

Debt Tracker

You always know the rough direction and current Debt of your active A.P.R. target while on the same plane.

Scaling

Technique Die d6; Hakoware range 50 feet; one active A.P.R.

3Bankruptcy Court

Rule Domain

Unlock Bankruptcy Court — Chapter Seven at level 3. This is a nonlethal rule-based Domain like Deadly Sentencing/Idle Death Gamble, not a lethal initiative-20 sure-hit. Clash bookkeeping: 800 clash points (Tier IV). A 100+ point advantage collapses the weaker Domain; collapse deals no damage. Barrier AC 12 + PB; barrier HP = 10 × max(1, your CE modifier) + 1,600. Technique Burnout: 5 rounds.

Court Opening Balance

When the Domain opens, choose competitors. Each hostile automatically receives an A.P.R. with opening Debt equal 2 × your level + PB. Violence is not forbidden; it is financially reinterpreted by Loan Combat.

Compounding Clock

The Domain resolves Interest at initiative 20 every round and displays Debt/Solvency openly to all competitors.

Scaling

Technique Die remains d6.

6Multiple Accounts

Two Debtors

Maintain A.P.R. on up to two creatures outside the Domain; inside, all chosen competitors are legal.

Collection Punch

Once per turn when you hit a debtor, choose to deal up to half normal damage to HP and convert the other half to Debt, instead of all-or-nothing.

Repossession

When a debtor spends 3+ CE on one activation, reaction: increase its Debt by 5 × CE spent, then pay 1 CE yourself.

Scaling

Technique Die becomes d8; Hakoware range extends to 100 feet.

11Compound Terms

Aggressive Interest

Outside the Domain, Interest becomes 15% at initiative 20. Inside Bankruptcy Court, choose 10% normal or 15% while also increasing your own CE costs by 1 until next initiative 20.

Payment Plan

Bonus action: voluntarily reduce a debtor’s Debt by up to 20 and gain temporary CE equal one-fifth the amount forgiven, maximum PB CE. Debtor must be within range.

A.P.R. Immunity

A.P.R. cannot be harmed, grappled, or physically removed; only technique suppression/exorcism, unconsciousness of the creditor, or explicit anti-technique effects end it.

Scaling

Technique Die becomes d10; Hakoware range extends to 150 feet.

18I.R.S. — Individual Ren Suppressor

Severe Bankruptcy

Bankruptcy duration becomes 1 minute. At end of each of its turns, target may make Constitution or CE ability save; success after at least 3 rounds ends it.

Maximum: Predatory Lending

Action · 10 CE: choose all current debtors. Double each current Debt, then each may immediately pay any amount of current CE to reduce Debt by 5 per CE. This Maximum cannot itself trigger Bankruptcy until after each target gets that payment chance.

Court of No Escape

Bankruptcy Court range becomes 120 feet and competitors cannot leave by ordinary movement; teleportation/barrier escape makes a Domain clash or technique check rather than being automatically prohibited.

Scaling

Technique Die becomes d12; Hakoware range extends to 300 feet.

20Chapter Seven — Final Notice

Thirty-Day Judgment

When a creature goes Bankrupt after its Debt reached at least twice Solvency, you may choose the severe canon-style sentence: its CE spending is suppressed for 30 days. This requires GM approval for named/story characters and ends if Hakoware is exorcised. You can choose the normal 1-minute sentence instead.

Same-Plane Ledger

Track Debt direction and whether A.P.R. remains active anywhere on the same plane.

Perfect Creditor

The first Repossession each round costs 0 CE.

Scaling

Technique Die remains d12.

Pinnacle expression

Domain, Maximum, or equivalent.

Not every technique needs a Domain. When one exists, its sure-hit is a unique autonomous initiative-count-20 event unless the Domain is explicitly rule-based.

Bankruptcy Court — Chapter Seven Action · 10 CE · 60-foot radius · up to 1 minute. A cartoonish financial tribunal overlays the battlefield. This is a rule-based nonlethal Domain: its procedure is the sure-hit equivalent.

On opening, chosen hostiles automatically receive A.P.R. and opening Debt equal 2 × level + PB. Every initiative 20, after clash checks, all active debts compound by 10% (or the aggressive rate). A creature whose Debt exceeds Solvency goes Bankrupt immediately.

Ordinary attacks remain possible, but Loan Combat converts creditor↔debtor damage until the debt is repaid. Anti-domain protection does not cancel the court automatically; it allows the protected creature to make a Charisma save when A.P.R. would first attach, avoiding opening Debt on success. It must still obey ordinary Loan Combat if later tagged by a normal hit.

Innate Technique Feats

Four build-defining options.

These broaden the law instead of merely inflating the same number.

Prerequisite: Hakoware

Kind Creditor

When you forgive Debt with Payment Plan or manually before level 11, the debtor gains temporary HP equal PB; cannot affect yourself.

Prerequisite: level 6

Repo Agent

Repossession can trigger when a debtor activates a cursed tool costing CE or feeds CE into a barrier, not only an innate technique.

Prerequisite: level 11

Foreclosure Notice

As an action for 2 CE, serve notice on one creature within Hakoware range bearing your A.P.R. It makes a Wisdom save against your Technique DC; on a failure it is frightened of you until the end of its next turn, and on a success its speed is reduced by 10 feet until the end of its next turn as the debt drags at its heels.

Prerequisite: Bankruptcy

No Defaulting

A Bankrupt creature cannot hide its CE signature from you and has disadvantage on checks to deceive you about its current CE state.

Rules integration

Edge cases and shared systems.

These rulings are designed around the revised Player Guide rather than the raw PDF where the two differ.

Debt is a contest number, not damage, so it does not interact with resistance, temp HP, criticals, or Black Flash.
Solvency uses maximum values to avoid recalculating every attack.
If the creditor becomes unconscious, A.P.R. ends and Debt disappears unless a pinnacle feature explicitly says otherwise.
Bankruptcy suppresses voluntary CE spending; passive non-CE ancestry/class traits continue.
Technique DC: 8 + proficiency bonus + your chosen Cursed Energy ability modifier (Wisdom or Charisma unless your class says otherwise).
Direct criticals: rolled weapon, unarmed, and CE attacks can critical normally. After a qualifying critical, roll the campaign's unmodified Black Flash confirmation d20; a natural 20 triples the completed critical damage. Autonomous Domain events, damage-over-time, hazards, and unrolled pulses cannot Black Flash.
Maximum Output: unless a feature says otherwise, double that feature's base CE cost and enhance one supported axis—damage, range, duration, or accuracy/DC—using the campaign's Maximum Output rules. Named Maximum Techniques and Domains do not receive generic Maximum Output.
Technique Focus: effects marked Focus occupy the shared Technique Focus slot. Opening a Domain ends other Focus effects unless the Domain expressly preserves them.
Two-technique characters: these guides assume each technique progresses from character/class level as agreed by the campaign. Multiclassing itself does not grant another Innate Technique.
