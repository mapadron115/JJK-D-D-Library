Codex Gigas

Metaphysical Law: Every truth taken from the Codex purchases an equal truth about the reader.

FormGrimoire
EngineDevil's Index
Attunement1 hour*

Field notes. Subject: a witness you cannot interview without testifying. Three truths govern the book. First, sight is purchased, never free: an action and 2 CE let the reader study a creature, barrier, tool, or technique within 60 feet and — on an Arcana check against its CE save DC — learn two of its seven secrets: current and maximum CE, highest and lowest saves, resistances, active effects, barrier integrity. Second, answers leave fingerprints: Answer in the Margin takes ten minutes and 5 CE, once per long rest, and returns one truthful answer — but the book reads back, branding the reader with Notice, a cumulative −1 to saves against curses, possession, and fear, to a maximum of three. Third, the only solvent is confession: as an action, the reader may speak one personally meaningful secret aloud and erase a single Notice. The book is an interrogator, not a library — every question is answered, and every answer opens the asker.

Features & procedures

Properties & techniques

Exact player-facing procedures

Relic profile

+4 to Arcana, History, and Religion checks concerning curses, barriers, vows, or cursed objects

Jujutsu origin

The manuscript's immense physical presence, demonic imagery, and centuries of legend accumulated into a knowledge-type cursed object. The dangerous part is not that a devil lives in the book; it is that the book has been treated as though something on the other side of the page is always reading back.

Devil's Index — Action, 2 CE

Study one creature, barrier, cursed tool, or visible technique within 60 ft. Make an Arcana check against the source's CE save DC. On a success, learn two of the following: current CE, CE maximum, highest saving throw, lowest saving throw, damage resistances/immunities, current active technique effects, or barrier HP/AC.

Answer in the Margin — 5 CE, once per long rest

Spend 10 minutes asking one concrete question concerning a curse, ritual, historical sorcerer, barrier, or cursed object. The book returns a truthful answer to the extent that the information exists in the campaign. You gain 1 Notice.

Notice

Maximum 3. For each Notice, you suffer a cumulative -1 on saving throws against curses, possession, and fear. As an action, you may willingly reveal a personally meaningful secret aloud to erase 1 Notice.

Page of Repetition — 4 CE

Once per long rest after successfully identifying a technique with Devil's Index, gain advantage on the next saving throw you make against that technique before your next long rest.

Binding Vow — Reciprocal Knowledge

The Codex cannot answer without learning. A reader who refuses to become legible cannot use Answer in the Margin.

Designer’s note. The book is an intelligence relic first and a combat tool second.

Rules integration

Cursed-tool attunement

Campaign canon

Cursed-tool attunement: you have PB attunement slots (2–6). This tool occupies one slot and requires a one-hour ritual. Attunement ends voluntarily over a short rest, on death, or when you replace it while over capacity. Distance does not break attunement. Cursed-tool attunement is separate from magic-item attunement.
