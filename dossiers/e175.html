Weight of the Heart

Offer enemies power that comes with literal metaphysical weight. Every accepted gift adds Heart Weight — and Weight punishes automatically: suppression, crushing jaws, and at 5+, Devour.

Combat roleTemptation Punisher / Executioner
TierTier IV · Special Grade
Core engineAutomatic Weight track
Core law

Character fantasy

Offer enemies power that comes with literal metaphysical weight. The technique never decides morality — it gives tempting shortcuts and lets targets make their own hearts heavy. Weight is not a currency you spend. Weight is the punishment, and it falls automatically along its track.

Combat loop

  1. Place the Scale on a target with Weigh Heart.
  2. Offer useful Temptations on its turn — they cost you no actions.
  3. Every accepted boon adds Heart Weight, and Weight punishes automatically: suppression, crushing jaws, Devour.
  4. The Scale is patient — a target that refuses every gift has chosen to fight you without help.

Field notes. Subject: a usurer of power. Three truths govern him. First: he never decides who is guilty — he only offers the loan. Every Temptation is a gift with the terms written on it, and the target signs with its own hand. Weight is the confession, not the sentence. Second: the scale is patient because it does not chase. Refuse everything and you fight him unaided; accept, and the jaws open on a schedule you set yourself. Third: Weight cannot be spent, only suffered. There is no counter-offer, no repayment plan — the track falls automatically, stage by stage, until Devour. Assess him as a creditor: he wins not when you lose, but the moment you take his help. The dead have never once needed to be hurried to their judgment — and he has learned that pace.

Core law

The Scale & Temptations

Weigh Heart:

The first move is an invitation. Two CE, sixty feet, a Wisdom save — and the target is told exactly what the gifts cost. bonus action, 2 CE, 60 feet: the target makes a Wisdom save. On failure it becomes Weighed for 1 minute (maximum one creature). It knows every Temptation’s benefit and that accepting adds Heart Weight.

Heart Weight:

Weight is confession, not currency. Only a knowingly accepted benefit adds it — never refusal, never ordinary violence, never a trick. 0–6. Weight is not moral alignment and cannot be created by secretly reinterpreting actions. Only a target knowingly accepting your offered mechanical benefit adds Weight — never refusal, never ordinary actions, never violence.

Temptation:

Four boons, offered free on the target's turn: Haste, Force, Plenty, Defiance. Each acceptance is one Weight, added after the boosted event resolves. once per round when the Weighed target could use one of the options below, you may offer it without spending any action. It freely accepts or refuses: Haste (+15 feet of movement this turn), Force (+1 Scale Die to one damage roll), Plenty (reduce one CE cost by 2, minimum 1), Defiance (+1 Scale Die to one failed save). Accepting adds 1 Heart Weight after the boosted event resolves.

The Weight Track (automatic):

The punishment is automatic. No spending, no choices — the stages expand with level and the track falls on its own. Weight punishes on its own — no spending, no choices; the stages the track can reach expand as you level (see Level progression below).

The Patient Scale (the fallback):

The technique never stalls. Temptations cost no actions, the scale reads the target's exact CE and HP, and Crocodile Maw is a complete combat action even at zero Weight. This technique never stalls. Temptations cost you no actions; while a creature is Weighed you always know its exact current CE and HP (the scale is honest); and Crocodile Maw is a complete combat action at 0 Weight. Keep the Scale on the refuser and let the fight come to you.

Full progression

Level progression

1Feather and Heart

The scale opens with its first two verdicts: Measured, which lends his attacks necrotic weight once per turn, and Found Wanting, which erodes the target's saves. Gain Weigh Heart and Temptations. Weight stages 1–2 are active from the start: 1 — Measured: your attacks deal +1 Scale Die necrotic to the Weighed target once per turn. 2 — Found Wanting: it subtracts 1 from saves against your technique.

3Crocodile Maw

The devourer's jaws. Three CE for a melee strike that scales with the Weight already gathered — the jaws take what the track allows, no decision required. Action, 3 CE: make a melee CE attack against a Weighed target. On hit: 2 Scale Dice + CE modifier piercing/necrotic, plus 1 additional Scale Die per 2 Heart Weight — no decision; the jaws take what the Weight allows.

6Heavier Than the Feather

The temptations double and the track deepens to six stages: slowed, suppressed, crushed at the turn's start, doomed beneath the Maw. You may offer up to two different Temptations per round, but a creature may accept only one per turn. The track deepens — stages 3–6 awaken: 3 — Slowed: speed −10 feet while moving away from you. 4 — Suppressed: it cannot regain CE. 5 — Crushing Jaws: at the start of its turn it takes 1 Scale Die necrotic and loses 1 CE if it has any, automatically. 6 — Doomed: your Crocodile Maw has advantage against it and ignores resistance to its necrotic portion.

11Hall Without a Judge

The scale widens to many targets and learns the deliberate assumption of burden — Weight moved, willingly, from one bearer to another. You may maintain Weighed on up to PB creatures. Once per round when a target refuses a Temptation, you may immediately offer a different Temptation; refusal itself never adds Weight. Action, 4 CE: move up to 2 Heart Weight from one willing Weighed ally onto yourself, or from yourself onto another willing Weighed creature — the deliberate assumption of burden.

18Maximum Technique: Devour the Heavy Heart

The final verdict. Ten CE against a target at five Weight or more: ten Scale Dice necrotic, CE drained, technique sealed — and if the devoured falls, the heart's cursed imprint is consumed and his CE is restored by its Weight. Action, 10 CE. One Weighed creature within 30 feet with 5+ Heart Weight makes Constitution save. Failure takes 10 Scale Dice necrotic, loses CE equal to its Heart Weight, and cannot use its Innate Technique until end of its next turn (this lockout does not stack with other technique-suppression effects and does not bypass immunities or rules governing technique suppression); success takes half damage and loses no CE/technique access. If this reduces it to 0 HP, a colossal crocodilian-lion-hippopotamus maw consumes the cursed imprint of the Heart: you regain CE equal to its Heart Weight and all Weight on that creature ends. A creature can be targeted by this Maximum only once per long rest.

Feats

Build-defining options.

Weigh the Dead

Even the dead can be weighed — a corpse's heaviest bargain, read within the hour. As an action and 2 CE, you may Weigh the heart of a corpse dead no more than 1 hour; it makes no save. You learn one significant burden it knowingly accepted in life — a bargain, betrayal, or sacrifice — as the GM describes. This offers no Temptations and adds no Heart Weight.

Sweet Temptation

A fifth boon, offered in the moment of harm: Safety, which halves damage at the price of one Weight. Add Safety: when you or an ally within 30 feet would take damage from the Weighed creature, you may offer it Safety as a Temptation without spending an action (once per round). Accept: the damage is halved and it gains 1 Heart Weight. Refuse: the damage lands in full.

Borrow the Burden

The sorcerer takes an ally's Weight as his own — generosity as damage control. When an ally within 30 feet accepts a Temptation, you may take the Heart Weight instead of them once per round. Temptations accepted by the hostile Weighed creature (such as Safety) do not trigger this feat — it covers allies’ acceptances only.

Devour Excess

A target at maximum Weight leaks power with every great expenditure; the scale drinks it as temporary CE. When a 6-Weight creature spends 3+ CE, gain 1 temporary CE, maximum PB temporary CE, expiring after 1 minute. Temporary CE never raises your CE maximum.

Rules integration

Building the character

Support Specialist, Strategist, or a melee Warrior. This technique is strongest against enemies who need to win now and cannot afford to refuse useful boons.
Technique die: Scale Die follows the shared d6 → d8 → d10 → d12 progression.
Rules integration

Counterplay

Refuse Temptations, remove or suppress the Weigh mark, or force the user to spread the Scale across several unmarked targets. No one gains Weight for ordinary actions — and Weight is never spent, only suffered.
