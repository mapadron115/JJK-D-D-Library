Weight of the Heart

Offer enemies power that comes with literal metaphysical weight. The technique never decides morality—it gives tempting shortcuts and lets targets make their own hearts heavy.

Combat roleTemptation Punisher / Executioner
TierTier IV · Special Grade
Core law

Character fantasy

Offer enemies power that comes with literal metaphysical weight. The technique never decides morality—it gives tempting shortcuts and lets targets make their own hearts heavy.

Combat loop

  1. Place the Scale on a target.
  2. Offer useful Temptations during their turn.
  3. Every accepted boon adds Heart Weight.
  4. Exploit heavy targets with crushing jaws, CE suppression, and a finishing Devour.

Core law

The Scale & Temptations

Weigh Heart:

bonus action, 2 CE, 60 feet, target makes Wisdom save. Failure becomes Weighed for 1 minute, maximum one creature until level 11. It knows every Temptation’s benefit and that accepting adds Heart Weight.

Heart Weight:

0–6. It is not moral alignment and cannot be created by secretly reinterpreting actions. Only a target knowingly accepting your offered mechanical benefit adds Weight.

Temptation:

once per round when the Weighed target could use one of the options below, you may offer it without an action. It freely accepts/refuses: Haste +15 feet movement this turn; Force +1 Scale Die to one damage roll; Plenty reduce one CE cost by 2 (minimum 1); Defiance +1 Scale Die to one failed save. Accepting gives 1 Heart Weight after the boosted event resolves.

Full progression

Level progression

1Feather and Heart

Gain Weigh Heart and Temptations. Against a target with 2+ Weight, your attacks deal +1 Scale Die necrotic once per turn. At 4+ Weight, it subtracts 1 from saves against you.

3Crocodile Maw

Action, 3 CE, melee CE attack against a Weighed target. Hit: 2 Scale Dice + CE modifier piercing/necrotic, plus one additional Scale Die per 2 Heart Weight. On hit at 4+ Weight, it cannot regain CE until start of your next turn.

6Heavier Than the Feather

Offer up to two different Temptations per round, but a creature may accept only one per turn. At 3 Weight, speed −10 while moving away from you. At 5 Weight, your Crocodile Maw has advantage and ignores resistance to its necrotic portion.

11Hall Without a Judge

Maintain PB Weighed targets. Once per round when a target refuses a Temptation, you may immediately offer a different Temptation; refusal itself never adds Weight. Action, 4 CE: move up to 2 Heart Weight from one willing Weighed ally onto yourself or from yourself onto another willing Weighed creature, reflecting deliberate assumption of burden.

18Maximum Technique: Devour the Heavy Heart

Action, 10 CE. One Weighed creature within 30 feet with 5+ Heart Weight makes Constitution save. Failure takes 10 Scale Dice necrotic, loses CE equal to its Heart Weight, and cannot use its Innate Technique until end of its next turn (this lockout does not stack with other technique-suppression effects and does not bypass immunities or rules governing technique suppression); success takes half damage and loses no CE/technique access. If this reduces it to 0 HP, a colossal crocodilian-lion-hippopotamus maw consumes the cursed imprint of the Heart: you regain CE equal to its Heart Weight and all Weight on that creature ends. A creature can be targeted by this Maximum only once per long rest.

Feats

Build-defining options.

Scribe of the Scale

Gain proficiency or expertise in Insight. You always know a Weighed creature’s exact Heart Weight.

Sweet Temptation

Add Safety: reduce one incoming damage instance by one Scale Die + CE modifier; accepting adds 1 Weight.

Borrow the Burden

When an ally within 30 feet accepts a Temptation, you may take the Heart Weight instead of them once per round.

Devour Excess

When a 6-Weight creature spends 3+ CE, gain 1 temporary CE, maximum PB temporary CE, expiring after 1 minute. Temporary CE never raises your CE maximum.

Rules integration

Building the character

Support Specialist, Strategist, or a melee Warrior. This technique is strongest against enemies who need to win now and cannot afford to refuse useful boons.
Technique die: Scale Die follows the shared d6 → d8 → d10 → d12 progression.
Rules integration

Counterplay

Refuse Temptations, remove/suppress the Weigh mark, or force the user to spread attention across several unmarked targets. No one gains Weight for ordinary actions.
Source inspiration

What was adapted.

  • Adaptation note. This is original JJK 5e homebrew inspired by Ammit and the weighing of the heart. The myth/folklore provides imagery and thematic rules; the combat engine, progression, CE economy, feats, and Domain/Maximum procedures are game design for this codex.
