Straw Doll Technique

Nail volleys, body-fragment Resonance, planted Hairpins, and direct technique disruption through the target’s body.

Field notes. Subject: the sorcerer whose wounds are all one wound. Three truths govern her. First: the doll is sympathetic, when a creature within thirty feet bleeds slashing or piercing, a successful sleight-of-hand binds a fragment into the doll, and doll and victim are one body; striking the doll connects automatically, and the fragment is consumed. Second: the rite is priced in threes, the wound is weighed against the victim's maximum: below a quarter buys PBd6 and spoiled attacks, a quarter to half buys more dice and stolen reactions, half or more buys the most dice and a repeating stun save. Third: the nails are punctuation, nails as punctuation, hairpins planted and detonated by proximity, the Maximum calling every planted nail home at once. Assess her correctly: Resonance needs ammunition, a wound, and a successful theft. Deny her any one of the three, and the doll is only straw.

Rules integration

Edge cases and shared systems.

Rules priority: the reviewed rulings, explicit codex clarifications, and the level progression always control when original wording conflicts. Legacy Domain auto-hit language, action terminology, event timing, missing saves, and older scaling never override revised player rules.
Why Tier II: Resonance can bypass ordinary distance and defense, but it needs ammunition, a triggering injury, a successful acquisition, and a consumed link.
