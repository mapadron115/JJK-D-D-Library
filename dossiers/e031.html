Straw Doll Technique

Nail volleys, body-fragment Resonance, planted Hairpins, and direct technique disruption through the target’s body.

The fiction. The prevailing theory holds that the Straw Doll is not a tool but a wound the sorcerer refused to close: their CE imprinted on the moment of being injured, and now it insists every target's injury belongs to the same doll. Resonance works because the user's energy never learned to distinguish their own spilled blood from anyone else's. The nails are just punctuation — the sentence was written in the body the first time the user bled.

Full progression

Feature map

1Nail Projectile.

Nail Projectile. The 1-CE action consumes two nails and makes two separate ranged CE attacks at 20/60 feet. Paying 4 additional CE consumes two more nails and makes two more attacks. Nails remain where they land until recovered.

3Resonance.

Acquire Resonance. As a reaction when a creature within 30 feet takes slashing or piercing damage, make Dexterity (Sleight of Hand) against its AC. On success, pay 3 CE and bind a supernatural fragment into your straw doll; no literal severed limb is required. One doll holds one target’s link for 1 minute.

Classify the triggering single damage instance against target maximum HP: Minor below 25%; Medium 25–49%; Major 50% or more. This replaces the source table’s missing 10–24% band. Once per turn as an action, strike the linked doll; it automatically connects and consumes the fragment.

Minor deals PBd6 and disadvantage on attacks until end of next turn; Medium deals (CE modifier + PB)d8 and removes reactions until end of next turn; Major deals (twice CE modifier + PB)d10 and forces the printed repeating Constitution save against stun. A new link requires a new successful acquisition.

6Hairpin.

Hairpin. As a bonus action, choose X from 1 to PB, pay X CE, and plant X nails in one object within reach. They last 1 hour. As a bonus action, or reaction when a creature comes within 5 feet, pay X CE again and detonate: 10-foot radius, Dexterity save, 6d8 + (X − 1)d8 force, half on success. All planted nails are consumed.

10Nail Recovery.

As a bonus action while touching an embedded nail’s target, a successful DC 10 Charisma check recovers that one nail.

11Improved Resonance.

Improved Resonance. Use an action, pay 6 CE, and make a melee CE attack against a creature with one listed condition. On hit, apply the printed prone, damage, and 1-minute CE-suppression effect. Affected creatures repeat the Charisma save without disadvantage at the end of each turn; on a failed activation save, CE is spent and the feature fails.

18Resonance: maximum HP reduction.

From level 18, Resonance damage also reduces the target’s maximum HP by the same amount until a long rest.

Pinnacle expression

Maximum, Domain, or equivalent.

Domain — Grand Effigy Rite: 18 — Wara Ningyo / maximum-HP damage. at initiative 20 every chosen hostile automatically takes 8d10 piercing or necrotic damage and receives a Resonance Effigy until the next initiative 20. The first time an effigied creature takes damage before then, it takes an additional PBd10 psychic damage and is restrained until the end of that turn; the Effigy then ends. A protected creature makes a Constitution save, taking half initial damage and receiving no Effigy on a success. This event needs no fragment, nail, condition, attack roll, or Improved Resonance activation.

Innate Technique Feats

Build-defining options.

These are optional character-building feats. You must meet each listed prerequisite before selecting it.

Prerequisite: Nail Projectile

Nail Volley

Action: spend X CE and consume X nails, X from 1 to CE modifier. Create a cone 10 × X feet long. Creatures make Dexterity saves, taking Xd4 piercing on failure or half on success. All nails land in the area and may be recovered normally.

Prerequisite: Hairpin

1000 Needles

When planting Hairpin nails, pay 1 extra CE per nail. A creature failing any detonation save is restrained for 1 minute. At each turn’s end it chooses Athletics or Acrobatics against CE DC; success ends it, failure deals 1d8 piercing. Multiple nails do not stack the condition or damage.

Prerequisite: Improved Resonance

Maximum: Last Nail in the Coffin

Action, 10 CE while a Resonance link persists: every nail you planted with Hairpin uproots and streaks unerringly to the linked target, detonating on arrival as Hairpin - one Dexterity save, 6d8 + (X - 1)d8 force for X nails, half on success - regardless of where they were planted. All nails are consumed and the link ends.

Rules integration

Edge cases and shared systems.

Rules priority: the reviewed rulings, explicit codex clarifications, and the level progression always control when original wording conflicts. Legacy Domain auto-hit language, action terminology, event timing, missing saves, and older scaling never override revised player rules.
Why Tier II: Resonance can bypass ordinary distance and defense, but it needs ammunition, a triggering injury, a successful acquisition, and a consumed link.
