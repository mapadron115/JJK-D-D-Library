Feature map

Level-by-level features, as the figure refined them. Every death is entered in the ledger; every entry is a lesson.

L1

Nine Lives

Passive · life pool (maximum 9).

You keep a ledger of lives, and the ledger begins at nine. When you are reduced to 0 hit points, you may use your reaction — once per round — to burn one life: you drop to 1 hit point instead of falling. Burned lives are gone; you regain one life when you finish a long rest, to a maximum of nine. The first eight deaths taught him everything. The ninth is the one he means to keep.

L1

The Legend's Blade

Reaction · when a creature misses you with a melee attack.

When a creature misses you with a melee attack, you may use your reaction to make one rapier attack against it. On a hit, the attack deals an extra 1d8 damage — the riposte is the whole point of the parry. He does not block so much as redirect, with commentary.

L3

Fear Me, If You Dare

Bonus action · 2 CE.

As a bonus action, choose one creature within 30 ft that can see and hear you. It must make a WIS save against your CE save DC or be frightened of you for 1 minute; it repeats the save at the end of each of its turns, ending the effect on a success. The legend arrives before the cat does. Sometimes the legend is enough.

L6

Softpaws

Passive.

Your climb speed equals your walking speed. You take no damage from falling, and you always land on your feet. You may move through the spaces of hostile creatures. Gravity is a suggestion. Doors are a suggestion. Everything is a suggestion.

L11

Lives Behind Him

Passive · triggers when you burn a life.

Whenever you burn a life with Nine Lives, choose one Lesson the dead lives taught you: you gain +2 AC until the start of your next turn; or you have advantage on your next saving throw before the end of your next turn; or you regain 2d8 + your CHA modifier hit points. Every death was a teacher. He was, finally, a good student.

L14

The Duelist's Challenge

Bonus action · 2 CE · once per short rest.

As a bonus action, you formally challenge one creature within 60 ft that can see and hear you to single combat. For 1 minute: you have advantage on melee attack rolls against the challenged creature; it has disadvantage on attack rolls against creatures other than you; and you may use The Legend's Blade against it whenever it hits you with a melee attack, not only when it misses. The duel ends early if the creature drops to 0 hit points, if you attack a different creature, or if you move more than 60 ft from it. He has dueled wolves, giants, and Death himself. He insists on the formalities.

L18

Death Keeps His Word

Passive.

Death respects a legend kept. Once per long rest, when you burn a life, you may spare it — the life is not lost, though you still drop to 1 hit point and still choose a Lesson. Additionally, while you have 3 or fewer lives remaining, your weapon attacks deal an extra 1d8 damage. He told Death to come for him last. Death agreed. Death keeps his appointments.

Pinnacle expression

Maximum, Domain, or equivalent.

Capstone material. He carries no Domain — a duelist's whole world is the six feet between two blades.

Maximum Technique

Nine Lives, One Cut

Action · 5 CE · burn up to 3 lives.

You spend lives the way other duelists spend breath — all at once. Burn up to 3 lives and make one rapier attack for each life burned, each against a creature within your reach. Each attack that hits deals an extra 3d8 damage. If all of the attacks hit a single creature, that creature takes an extra 4d8 damage and must make a CON save against your CE save DC or be stunned until the end of your next turn — every death he ever died, delivered in a single cut. The wolf taught him what a cut can mean. This is the answer.

Level 20 · Capstone

Live It Well

Passive · 1/long rest ally rescue.

The ninth life is not for hoarding. You regain 2 lives when you finish a long rest instead of 1. When you burn a life, each ally within 30 ft who can see you gains temporary hit points equal to your CHA modifier + your proficiency bonus, and advantage on its next saving throw before the end of your next turn. Once per long rest, when an ally within 30 ft of you drops to 0 hit points, you may use your reaction to spend one of your lives: the ally drops to 1 hit point instead. He spent eight lives on himself. The last one is for everyone else.

Innate Technique Feats

Build-defining options.

These are optional character-building feats. You must meet each listed prerequisite before selecting it.

Prerequisite: Nine Lives

Every Death a Teacher

The ledger records more than the fact of dying. When you burn a life, note the damage type of the attack or effect that reduced you to 0 hit points; until the end of your next turn, you have resistance to that damage type. The lesson is specific. The next identical blow lands softer.

Prerequisite: The Legend's Blade

Boots, Hat, and Flourish

The riposte need not draw blood to end a fight. When you use The Legend's Blade, you may forgo the extra 1d8 damage: instead, the attacker must make a DEX save against your CE save DC or drop one weapon it is holding at its feet. A disarmed duelist is a lecture in progress.

Prerequisite: Fear Me, If You Dare

Fear Me, All of You

The legend is contagious. When a creature fails its save against Fear Me, If You Dare, each enemy within 10 ft of it must also make a WIS save against your CE save DC or be frightened of you until the end of your next turn. One witness tells the story; the story does the rest.

Prerequisite: Softpaws

The Thief of Del Mar

Passing through is passing close. When you move through a hostile creature's space, you may make one Sleight of Hand check as part of that movement to lift a small loose object it carries, contested by its Perception. You also have advantage on Stealth checks made to hide in a space you moved through this turn. He was a wanted cat long before he was a legend.
