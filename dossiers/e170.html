Core law

Character fantasy

Build a hidden network of Tremor Paths beneath the fight, then erupt with acid, electricity, dragging strikes, and collapsing ground.

Combat loop

  1. Burrow to create Tremor Paths.
  2. Turn chosen segments into hazards or ambush routes.
  3. Erupt, drag, and vanish back into the network.
  4. Force enemies to spend actions finding or collapsing the ground beneath them.

The fiction. Registry notes describe the technique as a worm's patience made human: the sorcerer's CE learned to hunt the way the Death Worm hunts — never seen, always beneath, the ground itself the mouth. The Tremor Paths are the residue of that patience: tunnels of CE that remember where the body passed, waiting to erupt, because the prevailing theory holds the worm never truly emerges — it just lets the desert decide where it ends.

Full progression

Level progression

L1Buried Static

Tremor Die

Your Tremor Die follows the shared d6 → d8 → d10 → d12 progression.

Burrow

Bonus action, 2 CE, Focus, up to 1 minute. Gain burrow speed 20 feet through earth, sand, soil, snow, or comparable loose terrain; you cannot burrow through worked stone/metal. You may end your turn underground, but you cannot attack or perceive beyond tremorsense 30 feet while fully buried.

Tremor Path

Every 10-foot segment you burrow remains marked for 10 minutes, maximum number of segments = 2 × PB. You know every creature standing on a Path within 120 feet.

Path counterplay

A creature can use an action to inspect a suspected segment with Investigation/Survival against your CE DC. On success it identifies it; dealing 10 × PB damage to the ground, filling it, or reshaping the terrain collapses that segment.

Buried Static

Erupt: spend 1 CE when leaving a Path to make one melee CE attack against a creature within 10 feet; hit deals 1 Tremor Die acid or lightning in addition to normal unarmed damage. Live Wire: action, 2 CE, choose one Path segment within 60 feet; creatures standing on it make Dexterity save or take 2 Tremor Dice lightning, half on success.

L3Caustic Tunnel

When creating a Path, designate it Acidic for +1 CE. First hostile to enter/stand on it each round makes Dexterity save, taking 2 Tremor Dice acid and having speed reduced 10 feet until end of turn on failure. Maintain PB Acidic segments.

L6Drag Under

After Erupt hits a Large or smaller creature, spend 2 CE. It makes Strength save; failure pulls it into an adjacent Path space, knocks it prone, and restrains it until the start of your next turn. It remains on the surface—the technique does not bury a helpless creature alive.

L11Network Predator

Burrow speed 40 feet, tremorsense 60, Path cap 3 × PB. As a bonus action and 2 CE while on a Path, emerge from any connected Path segment within 120 feet. Once per round when a creature triggers an Acidic segment, you may also trigger Live Wire there for 1 CE without an action.

L18Maximum: Olgoi-Khorkhoi

Action, 10 CE, Focus, 1 minute. Become Huge, gain burrow 60, tremorsense 120, resistance to bludgeoning/piercing/slashing, and may burrow through worked stone at half speed. Erupt becomes a 15-foot burst: Dexterity save, 5 Tremor Dice acid + 5 Tremor Dice lightning, half on success; failed creatures are knocked prone. Once per turn after moving underground 30+ feet, collapse a 20-foot-square Path area; creatures there make Dex save or fall prone and become restrained by rubble until they use an action to escape. You cannot create new Acidic segments during the Maximum; you weaponize the network already built.

Feats

Build-defining options.

Sand Listener

As an action, press yourself to one of your Tremor Path segments and read its memory: learn the number, size categories, and direction of travel of creatures that crossed that segment in the last 10 minutes.

False Tremor

As an action and 1 CE, create a fake Path signature within 60 feet that lasts 10 minutes and fools ordinary tremor detection.

Venom Static

When one creature takes both acid and lightning damage from you in the same round, its nerves short-circuit: it cannot take reactions until the start of your next turn.

Buried Ambush

If combat begins while you are underground in one of your existing Paths, you may spend 1 CE to Erupt against a creature within 10 feet of your position before the first round begins, resolving the attack before initiative order starts.

Rules integration

Building the character

Strategist and Juggernaut both work: one emphasizes traps, the other eruption durability. The technique needs compatible terrain, so a player should discuss how often campaigns will occur on natural ground.
Rules integration

Counterplay

Fight on elevated platforms, worked metal/stone, or water; collapse detected tunnels; ready actions for eruptions.
