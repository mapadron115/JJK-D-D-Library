Bestiary Technique #08

Red Sand Below

Build a hidden network of Tremor Paths beneath the fight, then erupt with acid, electricity, dragging strikes, and collapsing ground.

InspirationMongolian Death Worm
RoleAmbusher / Hazard Controller
TierTier III · Apex
Core statCON or CE stat
Scaling dieTremor Die

Character fantasy

Build a hidden network of Tremor Paths beneath the fight, then erupt with acid, electricity, dragging strikes, and collapsing ground.

Combat loop

  1. Burrow to create Tremor Paths.
  2. Turn chosen segments into hazards or ambush routes.
  3. Erupt, drag, and vanish back into the network.
  4. Force enemies to spend actions finding or collapsing the ground beneath them.

Tremor Paths

  • Burrow: bonus action, 2 CE, Focus, up to 1 minute. Gain burrow speed 20 feet through earth, sand, soil, snow, or comparable loose terrain; you cannot burrow through worked stone/metal. You may end your turn underground, but you cannot attack or perceive beyond tremorsense 30 feet while fully buried.
  • Tremor Path: every 10-foot segment you burrow remains marked for 10 minutes, maximum number of segments = 2 × PB. You know every creature standing on a Path within 120 feet.
  • Path counterplay: a creature can use an action to inspect a suspected segment with Investigation/Survival against your CE DC. On success it identifies it; dealing 10 × PB damage to the ground, filling it, or reshaping the terrain collapses that segment.

Innate Technique FeatsOptional character-building feats

Sand Listener

While touching natural ground, tremorsense increases by 15 feet.

False Tremor

As an action and 1 CE, create a fake Path signature within 60 feet that lasts 10 minutes and fools ordinary tremor detection.

Venom Static

When one creature takes both acid and lightning damage from you in the same round, subtract 1d4 from its next saving throw before your next turn.

Buried Ambush

If combat begins while you are underground in one of your existing Paths, you have advantage on initiative.

Building the character

Strategist and Juggernaut both work: one emphasizes traps, the other eruption durability. The technique needs compatible terrain, so a player should discuss how often campaigns will occur on natural ground.

Technique die: Tremor Die follows the shared d6 → d8 → d10 → d12 progression.

Counterplay

Fight on elevated platforms, worked metal/stone, or water; collapse detected tunnels; ready actions for eruptions.

Adaptation note. This is original JJK 5e homebrew inspired by Mongolian Death Worm. The myth/folklore provides imagery and thematic rules; the combat engine, progression, CE economy, feats, and Domain/Maximum procedures are game design for this codex.
