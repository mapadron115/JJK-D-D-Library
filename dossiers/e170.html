Red Sand Below

Hunt the way the worm hunts — never seen, always beneath. Build a hidden network of Tremor Paths beneath the fight, then erupt with acid, electricity, dragging strikes, and collapsing ground.

Core law

Character fantasy

Build a hidden network of Tremor Paths beneath the fight, then erupt with acid, electricity, dragging strikes, and collapsing ground.

Combat loop

Burrow, mark, erupt, wire — the desert's fourfold verdict.

  1. Burrow to create Tremor Paths.
  2. Turn chosen segments into hazards or ambush routes.
  3. Erupt, drag, and vanish back into the network.
  4. Force enemies to spend actions finding or collapsing the ground beneath them.

Field notes. Subject: a worm's patience, made human. I have read the Tremor Paths like a map and found the hunting doctrine written underneath the desert. Three truths govern it. First: knowledge is buried — every burrowed segment remembers where you passed, and you know every creature standing on your Paths within one hundred twenty feet. Second: the ground testifies — Erupt from a Path for acid or lightning, wire a segment live, drag the large and the unwary down and leave them prone. Third: the patient are protected — burrowed movement, tremorsense, paths that can be collapsed only by deliberate violence against the earth itself. Mark the prevailing theory, which I hold to be true: the worm never truly emerges. It just lets the desert decide where it ends.

Full progression

Level progression

1Buried Static

Tremor Die

The desert's own unit of measure. Your Tremor Die follows the shared d6 → d8 → d10 → d12 progression.

Burrow

Twenty feet through loose earth — but never through worked stone. Bonus action, 2 CE, Focus, up to 1 minute. Gain burrow speed 20 feet through earth, sand, soil, snow, or comparable loose terrain; you cannot burrow through worked stone/metal. You may end your turn underground, but you cannot attack or perceive beyond tremorsense 30 feet while fully buried.

Tremor Path

Every segment remembers where you passed; you know who stands on them. Every 10-foot segment you burrow remains marked for 10 minutes, maximum number of segments = 2 × PB. You know every creature standing on a Path within 120 feet.

Path counterplay

Ten times PB damage, or fill it, or reshape it — the paths can be collapsed. A creature can use an action to inspect a suspected segment with Investigation/Survival against your CE DC. On success it identifies it; dealing 10 × PB damage to the ground, filling it, or reshaping the terrain collapses that segment.

Buried Static

Erupt from a Path, or wire a segment live — the ground testifies. Erupt: spend 1 CE (no action, invented) when leaving a Path to make one melee CE attack against a creature within 10 feet; hit deals 1 Tremor Die acid or lightning in addition to normal unarmed damage. Live Wire: action, 2 CE, choose one Path segment within 60 feet; creatures standing on it make Dexterity save or take 2 Tremor Dice lightning, half on success.

Feats

Build-defining options.

Sand Listener

Press yourself to a segment and read its memory: number, size, direction. As an action, press yourself to one of your Tremor Path segments and read its memory: learn the number, size categories, and direction of travel of creatures that crossed that segment in the last 10 minutes.

False Tremor

A fake signature within sixty feet — the desert learns to lie. As an action and 1 CE, create a fake Path signature within 60 feet that lasts 10 minutes and fools ordinary tremor detection.

Venom Static

Acid and lightning in one round — the nerves short-circuit, and reactions die. When one creature takes both acid and lightning damage from you in the same round, its nerves short-circuit: it cannot take reactions until the start of your next turn.

Buried Ambush

Combat begins while you are underground — the first strike lands before the first round. If combat begins while you are underground in one of your existing Paths, you may spend 1 CE to Erupt against a creature within 10 feet of your position before the first round begins, resolving the attack before initiative order starts.

Rules integration

Building the character

Strategist and Juggernaut both work: one emphasizes traps, the other eruption durability. The technique needs compatible terrain, so a player should discuss how often campaigns will occur on natural ground.
Rules integration

Counterplay

Fight on elevated platforms, worked metal/stone, or water; collapse detected tunnels; ready actions for eruptions.
