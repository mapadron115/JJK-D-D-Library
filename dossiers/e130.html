Core law

What the technique actually does.

Core Engine, Graveyard. The entire technique is one muster roll. Corpse cards are recorded from meaningful deaths within sixty feet, capped at half her level plus proficiency, and they are abstractions, not bodies to drag. She spends them to raise the dead, fuel her curses, or sacrifice for power. The grave is a bank, and she is its only teller. When a meaningful creature dies within 60 ft, you may record one Corpse card, max level/2 + PB. Corpse cards are abstractions, not literal bodies. Spend them to create undead, fuel curses, or sacrifice for power.

Field notes. Subject: a general with a filing system for the dead. Three truths govern her. First, death is the treasury: when a meaningful creature dies within sixty feet, her energy records the corpse as a Corpse card, an abstraction, not a body, kept like a draft in the war chest. Second, nothing the graveyard gives is free: every bargain exacts its price, a summon sacrificed for energy, every Corpse card spent at the Maximum's muster. Third, the dead are not commanded but owed: the raised are tribute, and even their endings are re-filed, when one of her undead falls, her Triumph harvests its final moment back into the ledger. I have read that verse for decades: the general who accounts for every soldier never truly loses an army.

Full progression

Level 1 through 20.

1Raise Dead

The first enlistment. Two energy and one card buys a zombie, modest armor, her level in its flesh, a necrotic bite, and her commands ride on a single bonus action. She may keep no more than her proficiency's worth standing. Small armies, honestly kept. For 2 CE + 1 Corpse card, summon a Zombie (AC 12, HP 5 + your level, attack 1d8 + PB necrotic). Your commands share one bonus action; you may have at most PB undead active.

3Dark Bargain

She sells the fallen twice. As a bonus action she sacrifices one of her summons, one energy regained, or two dice of temporary flesh. The technique's oldest rule: everything from the grave is spent, never wasted. As a bonus action, sacrifice one of your summons: regain 1 CE or gain 2d8 temporary hit points.

6Death's Majesty

The officer corps. Her undead strike with her proficiency added to damage, and for three energy one Corpse card may be promoted to an elite Wight, double the flesh, a heavier bite. The ledger rewards seniority. Your undead add your proficiency bonus to damage, once per round total. For 3 CE, you may turn one Corpse card into an elite Wight with twice a Zombie’s HP and a 2d8 attack.

9Diabolic Tutor

Cashing corpses for knowledge returned. Once per short rest, two Corpse cards recover one expended technique feature costing four energy or less, never a Domain, never a Maximum. The dead, it turns out, keep excellent records. Once per short rest, spend 2 Corpse cards to recover one expended technique feature costing 4 CE or less, excluding Domains and Maxima.

11Mass Reanimation

The full muster. Five energy and up to her proficiency in cards raises that many zombies in a single action, and the active cap stretches by two. When the filing system works, the grave empties in a single breath. As an action, for 5 CE + up to PB Corpse cards, summon that many Zombies, this action replaces the normal per-Zombie creation actions; they still obey your active cap + 2.

13Contract of Four Demons

Four charters, one signed each long rest: a wider cap, crueler curses against the wounded, sacrifices that heal deeper, or death magic that ignores necrotic resistance. Every general chooses her terms before the campaign. Each long rest, choose one pact: +2 undead cap, +2 curse DC against wounded creatures, your sacrifices heal 4d8 instead, or your death magic ignores necrotic resistance.

15Dreadhorde General

The command matures. Her active cap doubles, and her dead move as one body, attacks resolved in two grouped swarms, so the table never drowns in the muster. A general's courtesy to everyone else at the war council. Your active undead cap becomes 2×PB. Your undead move together and resolve attacks in two grouped swarms to keep play fast.

18Domain, The Chain Veil

At the twentieth count the veil falls across the field: every hostile standing within five feet of her dead makes a Constitution save, six dice of necrotic on failure, and each failure raises a temporary zombie beside the fallen. The grave recruits at the scene. At initiative 20: every hostile standing within 5 ft of one of your undead makes a Constitution save, taking 6d10 necrotic damage on a failure, or half on a success; each failed save creates a temporary 1-HP Zombie adjacent to it until the next initiative 20.

20Death Cannot Hold Me

The final exemption, written in her own hand. Reduced to zero, she may spend four Corpse cards to stand at one hit point instead, once per long rest, and her capacity doubles while her Wights stop costing extra slots. Death, it seems, could not collect. When you are reduced to 0 HP, you may sacrifice 4 Corpse cards to remain at 1 HP instead, once per long rest. Your Corpse capacity doubles; elite Wights no longer cost extra active slots.

Pinnacle expression

Maximum, Domain, or equivalent.

Maximum, Rise of the Dreadhorde (10 CE + all Corpse cards, minimum 5; action): The whole account spent at once. Ten energy and every Corpse card she holds, at least five, muster into a single Army shikigami: fifteen hit points per card, an armored hide, up to four necrotic volleys, one minute of war. When the filing system empties, the field is full. create a single Army shikigami with HP 15 per Corpse spent, AC 17, and one 3d10 necrotic attack per 3 Corpse cards (max 4 attacks). Lasts 1 minute.

Feats

Technique Feats

Gravecrawler

One of her dead refuses the ledger's final entry. A chosen zombie, destroyed, rises at the start of her next turn with one hit point, position and orders kept, elite rank surrendered. Death may file its paperwork; this one appeals. One of your dead refuses to stay down. Choose one Zombie under your control as your Gravecrawler: the first time it is destroyed each combat, it rises again at the start of your next turn with 1 HP, dragging itself from its own corpse. It keeps its position and your commands but loses any elite upgrades. You can maintain only one Gravecrawler at a time; you may choose another during a short rest.

Cruel Bargain

When she sacrifices a summon she may detonate its corpse instead of taking energy or flesh, ten feet of necrotic ruin, Dexterity to halve. The technique's economy has always had a second market. when you sacrifice a summon with Dark Bargain, you may detonate its corpse instead of regaining CE or temp HP: creatures within 10 ft make a Dexterity save, taking 2d8 necrotic on a failure.

Necromancer's Command

Her orders no longer need her voice. Commands reach her dead telepathically across a hundred and twenty feet, and a bonus action may set one to aid an ally or wrestle with Athletics. The general's reach, extended. you may issue commands to your undead telepathically within 120 ft, and command one undead to use the Help action or make a Strength (Athletics) check as your bonus action.

Liliana's Triumph

The ledger balances itself. When one of her undead is destroyed, her reaction harvests its final moment, one more Corpse card, as if a meaningful death had occurred. The army is never smaller than the grave allows. when one of your undead is destroyed, you may use your reaction to harvest its final moment: record a Corpse card from its remains, as if a meaningful creature had died.

Rules integration

Edge cases and shared systems.

Shared rulings: Technique DC = 8 + PB + CE ability modifier. Direct rolled attacks may crit and use the campaign Black Flash procedure when eligible. Tokens, autonomous summons, passive damage, and initiative-20 Domain effects cannot Black Flash. Maximum Output modifies ordinary supported techniques, never named Maxima or Domains.
