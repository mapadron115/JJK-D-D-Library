Power tierTier V · Calamity
Core law

What the technique actually does.

Core Engine — Graveyard. When a meaningful creature dies within 60 ft, you may record one Corpse card, max level/2 + PB. Corpse cards are abstractions, not literal bodies. Spend them to create undead, fuel curses, or sacrifice for power.

Full progression

Level 1 through 20.

1Raise Dead

2 CE + 1 Corpse: summon Zombie (AC 12, HP 5+level, attack 1d8+PB necrotic). Commands share one bonus action; max PB active.

3Dark Bargain

Bonus action sacrifice one summon: regain 1 CE or gain 2d8 temp HP.

6Death's Majesty

Your undead add PB to damage once per round total. 3 CE turns one Corpse into an elite Wight with twice Zombie HP and 2d8 attack.

9Diabolic Tutor

Once/short rest spend 2 Corpse cards to recover one expended technique feature costing 4 CE or less, excluding Domain/Maximum.

11Mass Reanimation

5 CE + up to PB Corpse cards: summon that many Zombies without counting against normal creation action economy; still obey active cap +2.

13Contract of Four Demons

Choose one pact each long rest: +2 undead cap, +2 curse DC against wounded creatures, sacrifice heals 4d8 instead, or death magic ignores necrotic resistance.

15Dreadhorde General

Active undead cap becomes 2×PB. They move together and resolve attacks in two grouped swarms to keep play fast.

18Domain — The Chain Veil

Initiative 20: every hostile standing within 5 ft of one of your undead makes Con save, taking 6d10 necrotic on failure, half success; each failed save creates a temporary 1-HP Zombie adjacent to it until next count 20.

20Death Cannot Hold Me

When reduced to 0, sacrifice 4 Corpse cards to remain at 1 once/long rest. Corpse capacity doubles; elite Wights no longer cost extra active slots.

Pinnacle expression

Maximum, Domain, or equivalent.

Maximum — Rise of the Dreadhorde (10 CE + all Corpse cards, minimum 5): create a single Army shikigami with HP 15 per Corpse spent, AC 17, and one 3d10 necrotic attack per 3 Corpse cards (max 4 attacks). Lasts 1 minute.

Rules integration

Edge cases and shared systems.

Shared rulings: Technique DC = 8 + PB + CE ability modifier. Direct rolled attacks may crit and use the campaign Black Flash procedure when eligible. Tokens, autonomous summons, passive damage, and initiative-20 Domain effects cannot Black Flash. Maximum Output modifies ordinary supported techniques, never named Maxima or Domains.
