Dreadhorde

Graveyard / undead tokens / sacrifice / death magic

Power tierTier V · Calamity
Core law

What the technique actually does.

Core Engine — Graveyard. When a meaningful creature dies within 60 ft, you may record one Corpse card, max level/2 + PB. Corpse cards are abstractions, not literal bodies. Spend them to create undead, fuel curses, or sacrifice for power.

Full progression

Level 1 through 20.

1Raise Dead

For 2 CE + 1 Corpse card, summon a Zombie (AC 12, HP 5 + your level, attack 1d8 + PB necrotic). Your commands share one bonus action; you may have at most PB undead active.

3Dark Bargain

As a bonus action, sacrifice one of your summons: regain 1 CE or gain 2d8 temporary hit points.

6Death's Majesty

Your undead add your proficiency bonus to damage, once per round total. For 3 CE, you may turn one Corpse card into an elite Wight with twice a Zombie’s HP and a 2d8 attack.

9Diabolic Tutor

Once per short rest, spend 2 Corpse cards to recover one expended technique feature costing 4 CE or less, excluding Domains and Maxima.

11Mass Reanimation

For 5 CE + up to PB Corpse cards, summon that many Zombies without spending the normal creation action economy; they still obey your active cap + 2.

13Contract of Four Demons

Each long rest, choose one pact: +2 undead cap, +2 curse DC against wounded creatures, your sacrifices heal 4d8 instead, or your death magic ignores necrotic resistance.

15Dreadhorde General

Your active undead cap becomes 2×PB. Your undead move together and resolve attacks in two grouped swarms to keep play fast.

18Domain — The Chain Veil

At initiative 20: every hostile standing within 5 ft of one of your undead makes a Constitution save, taking 6d10 necrotic damage on a failure, or half on a success; each failed save creates a temporary 1-HP Zombie adjacent to it until the next initiative 20.

20Death Cannot Hold Me

When you are reduced to 0 HP, you may sacrifice 4 Corpse cards to remain at 1 HP instead, once per long rest. Your Corpse capacity doubles; elite Wights no longer cost extra active slots.

Pinnacle expression

Maximum, Domain, or equivalent.

Maximum — Rise of the Dreadhorde (10 CE + all Corpse cards, minimum 5): create a single Army shikigami with HP 15 per Corpse spent, AC 17, and one 3d10 necrotic attack per 3 Corpse cards (max 4 attacks). Lasts 1 minute.

Feats

Technique Feats

Gravecrawler

One of your dead refuses to stay down. Choose one Zombie under your control as your Gravecrawler: the first time it is destroyed each combat, it rises again at the start of your next turn with 1 HP, dragging itself from its own corpse. It keeps its position and your commands but loses any elite upgrades. You can maintain only one Gravecrawler at a time; you may choose another during a short rest.

Cruel Bargain

when you sacrifice a summon with Dark Bargain, you may detonate its corpse instead of regaining CE or temp HP: creatures within 10 ft make a Dexterity save, taking 2d8 necrotic on a failure.

Necromancer's Command

you may issue commands to your undead telepathically within 120 ft, and command one undead to use the Help action or make a Strength (Athletics) check as your bonus action.

Liliana's Triumph

when one of your undead is destroyed, you may use your reaction to harvest its final moment: record a Corpse card from its remains, as if a meaningful creature had died.

Rules integration

Edge cases and shared systems.

Shared rulings: Technique DC = 8 + PB + CE ability modifier. Direct rolled attacks may crit and use the campaign Black Flash procedure when eligible. Tokens, autonomous summons, passive damage, and initiative-20 Domain effects cannot Black Flash. Maximum Output modifies ordinary supported techniques, never named Maxima or Domains.
