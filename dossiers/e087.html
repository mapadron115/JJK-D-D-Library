Field notes. Subject: the Djinn who listened until listening became cutting. I have sat across from a Caim vessel and felt watched in the way only the truly attentive can manage: his cursed energy had learned the world's seams by hearing them. Three truths govern him. First, he hears everything worth knowing: Listening Current gives 120-ft blindsight and 60-ft tremorsense for a minute, with advantage on vibration-based Perception, the battlefield narrated before it moves. Second, what he hears he can cut: Absolute Edge adds 4 Djinn Dice slashing/cold, ignores slashing resistance, and pierces non-force armor, the near-molecular water edge, because the listening never stopped getting finer. Third, the Tide: Absolute Tide expands a 260-ft ring of invisible cutting water that ignores ordinary cover and deals maximum dice to structures. A thousand years of paying attention, speaking all at once.
