Caim — Listening Closely & Absolute Sharpness

Canon master:Takeruhiko Yamato

Features & procedures

Exact player-facing procedures.

Weapon Equip • Level 5

Absolute Water Edge.

Weapon Equip shapes water into a near-molecular cutting edge around the vessel.

Bonus Action • 2 Reserve

Listening Current

For 1 minute gain 120-ft blindsight and 60-ft tremorsense while touching a solid/liquid surface; advantage on Perception checks involving vibration.

On hit • 2 Reserve

Absolute Edge

Add 4 Djinn Dice slashing/cold and ignore resistance to slashing for this hit. Magical armor bonuses do not apply to the target’s AC against this attack unless they are force/energy based.

Action • 5 Reserve

Pressure Cut

A razor current travels in a 300-ft line, 10 ft wide. Dexterity save; 9 Djinn Dice slashing/cold, half on success. Ignore ordinary cover and cut through nonmagical objects in the path.

Full Djinn Equip • Level 9

Fly 110 ft; swim 140 ft; crit range improves by 1 against creatures you can sense with Listening Current; water/slashing attacks ignore resistance and treat immunity as resistance.

13 Reserve • Codex name for canon-unnamed Extreme

Absolute Tide

A 260-ft-radius ring of invisible cutting water expands from a point within 300 ft. Dexterity save; 17 Djinn Dice slashing/cold, half on success. Ordinary cover offers no protection; structures and objects take maximum dice.

Anime-scale: Strategic scale: shave through fortress districts, forests, or mass formations with a single expanding cutting tide.

The fiction. Registry scholars describe Caim as attention that overshot — a Djinn who learned to listen so closely it began hearing the seams in the world, and then discovered that seams can be cut. The water edge is honest: near-molecular, because the listening never stopped getting finer. Absolute Tide is simply what happens when a thousand years of paying attention decides to speak all at once.

Rules integration

Djinn Die, Equip & Extreme Magic

Independent Djinn Reserve: 14 (Rank B). Refreshes on a long rest. Every Djinn cost below — Weapon Equip, Full Equip, abilities, reactions, Extreme Magic — spends this Djinn’s Reserve, never your CE. If this Djinn’s Reserve is empty it simply cannot fuel more until you rest; your other Djinn keep their own separate Reserves.
Weapon Equip · level 5: bonus action, 2 Reserve, lasts 10 minutes.
Full Djinn Equip · level 9: bonus action, 4 Reserve, lasts 1 minute. Only one Equip can be active.
Deep Synchronization · level 11: once per short rest, one Equip activation costs 1 Reserve less.
Extreme Magic · level 13: action; requires that Djinn’s Full Equip; pay the listed Reserve cost from this Djinn’s Reserve; usable once per long rest per Djinn. Full Equip ends afterward, and you cannot Equip that Djinn again until you finish a short rest.
King Vessel · level 17: switch directly from one Full Equip to another as a bonus action, paying the new Equip cost. At level 20, once per long rest, immediately transition from one Djinn’s Extreme into another Djinn’s Full Equip.
Attack / save: use your CE attack modifier and CE save DC unless this Djinn explicitly says otherwise.
