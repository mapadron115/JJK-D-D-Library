Paper Fan

An ancient, well-kept ceremonial fan, delicate to look at, cruel in the hand.

Field notes. Subject: the courtier's grudge. Three truths govern it. First, the gentleness is the deception: a +1 war fan with finesse, 1d8 on a hit, a courtier's instrument, kept sharp. Second, the grudge is priced: as a reaction to taking damage, two coins of cursed energy answer the blow with a spell attack at thirty feet, 4d8 slashing, and the attacker rooted where it stands until the end of its next turn. Third, the fan borrows your authority: the strike uses your spell attack modifier, melee or ranged as you please. Hence the maxim: the lightest weapon carries the longest memory.

Cursed Fan

Passive · Functions as a +1 War Fan with the finesse property · On hit: 1d8 slashing damage.

Delicate to look at, cruel in the hand, a courtier's instrument, kept sharp. This paper fan can be used as a +1 War Fan with the addition of the finesse property, and on a hit it deals 1d8 slashing damage.

Retribution

Reaction · 2 CE · When you take damage: make a melee or ranged spell attack against the creature that damaged you, if within 30 ft · On hit: 4d8 slashing and the target's speed becomes 0 until the end of its next turn.

Here is the grudge, priced: a reaction and two coins of energy, and the blow that found you is answered, in full, and the attacker rooted where it stands. As a reaction to taking damage, you can spend 2 CE to make a melee or ranged spell attack against the creature that damaged you if they are within 30 feet. On a hit, you deal 4d8 slashing damage and the target's movement speed is reduced to 0 until the end of its next turn.
