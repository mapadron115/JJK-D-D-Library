Field notes. Subject: the Djinn of severance. This is what happens when 'I am right' becomes a weapon instead of a belief, the registry's note, and the truest sentence in it. Three truths govern her. First, her power spends nothing on flesh: every drop goes to separation, Belior Zakera builds total sensory illusion and rewrites ten minutes of memory, because what is a lie but a badly severed perception. Second, her scythe cuts connections, not bodies: Belior Goldoreza severs one sense on a failed Wisdom save; Soul Separation displaces the soul from the body, leaving it Incapacitated. Third, Belior Zauto is a soul-execution: failed ordinary targets have their souls severed and banished for a minute; even failing bosses take 18 Djinn Dice and are displaced. Mark the cruelty as data, not flavor, the point of the technique is the parting, and the parting is total.
