Features & procedures

Exact player-facing procedures.

Weapon Equip • Level 5

Belior Goldoreza — Severing Funeral Scythe.

Weapon Equip manifests the funeral scythe and lets its cuts sever senses and spiritual connections rather than only flesh.

Action • 4 Reserve

Belior Zakera

One creature within 120 ft or creatures in a 30-ft-radius point make Wisdom saves. Failure creates a total sensory illusion and can alter/rewrite a short-term memory for up to 1 minute. Affected creatures repeat the save when suffering major contradictory harm.

On hit • 3 Reserve

Belior Goldoreza

Add 4 Djinn Dice psychic/necrotic. Target makes Wisdom save; on failure choose one sense to sever until the end of your next turn (sight, hearing, spatial balance = no reactions, or pain = disadvantage on concentration/technique checks).

Action • 6 Reserve

Soul Separation

One creature within 90 ft makes a Charisma save. Failure: its soul is displaced from its body until the end of your next turn; the body is Incapacitated and the soul can perceive but not act normally. Legendary/boss creatures also take 8 Djinn Dice psychic/necrotic.

Full Djinn Equip • Level 9

Fly 110 ft; advantage against charm, fear, illusion, and possession. Attacks affect incorporeal creatures normally. If you also possess Zagan, once per turn you may use one of Zagan’s base abilities without changing Equip by paying +1 Reserve, mirroring Hakuryuu’s combined Life-Djinn use.

16 Reserve • Canon Extreme

Belior Zauto

Choose one creature within 300 ft or all enemies in a 120-ft radius. Charisma save. Ordinary failed targets have their souls severed and banished for up to 1 minute; bodies fall Unconscious and repeat the save at each turn’s end. Major bosses/PCs that fail instead take 18 Djinn Dice psychic/necrotic and are soul-displaced/Incacitated until the end of your next turn. Success takes half damage and avoids displacement.

Anime-scale: Strategic scale: a soul-execution weapon, not a small psychic burst. It should terrify even characters who can tank ordinary damage.

The fiction. The prevailing theory holds that Belial's power is conviction made surgical — a djinn whose truth does not persuade but severs, cutting the threads between soul, senses, and memory the way a scythe cuts wheat. The vessel's cursed energy carries no force to spare for flesh; every drop of it is spent on separation, because the user's truth demanded that things come apart. Registry scholars note the cruelty is the point of the classification: this is what happens when "I am right" becomes a weapon instead of a belief.

Rules integration

Djinn Die, Equip & Extreme Magic

Independent Djinn Reserve: 20 (Rank S+). Refreshes on a long rest. Every Djinn cost below — Weapon Equip, Full Equip, abilities, reactions, Extreme Magic — spends this Djinn’s Reserve, never your CE. If this Djinn’s Reserve is empty it simply cannot fuel more until you rest; your other Djinn keep their own separate Reserves.
Weapon Equip · level 5: bonus action, 2 Reserve, lasts 10 minutes.
Full Djinn Equip · level 9: bonus action, 4 Reserve, lasts 1 minute. Only one Equip can be active.
Deep Synchronization · level 11: once per short rest, one Equip activation costs 1 Reserve less.
Extreme Magic · level 13: action; requires that Djinn’s Full Equip; pay the listed Reserve cost from this Djinn’s Reserve; usable once per long rest per Djinn. Full Equip ends afterward, and you cannot Equip that Djinn again until you finish a short rest.
King Vessel · level 17: switch directly from one Full Equip to another as a bonus action, paying the new Equip cost. At level 20, once per long rest, immediately transition from one Djinn’s Extreme into another Djinn’s Full Equip.
Attack / save: use your CE attack modifier and CE save DC unless this Djinn explicitly says otherwise.
