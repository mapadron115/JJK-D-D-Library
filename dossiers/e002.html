Recovered printed values & campaign clarifications

At-the-table character reference

Bird Strike · level 11: action, 10 CE; one of your crows attacks a target within its flying-speed range. The target makes a Dexterity save: 10d10 force damage and one revised limb-injury result on a failure, or half damage and no limb result on a success. The crow is destroyed either way.

Feature map

  • 1 — Cursed Crows: permanent raven bonds and remote sight.
  • 3 — Omen of Death: fear aura.
  • 6 — Raven’s Eye: enhanced awareness and quicker switching.
  • 11 — Bird Strike: sacrificial force attack.
  • 18 — Black Mahakala: the autonomous Murder’s Funeral pulse.

Completed rules

  • You begin with three cursed crows. Imbuing another raven is permanent until it dies or you release it. Use the Raven stat block with the Cursed Spirit type. You can direct up to PB + CE modifier crows at once (minimum 3); others remain bonded but inactive.
  • Remote sight has no range while both you and the crow are on the same plane. Your own body is blinded, deafened if you also listen through it, and cannot use reactions that depend on your body’s senses. At 6th level, changing the viewed crow is a bonus action and ending the link costs no action.
  • Omen of Death: creatures that succeed on the Wisdom save are immune to your Omen until the next dawn. A frightened creature repeats the save at the end of each turn.
  • Raven’s Eye: only active crows within 1 mile contribute; the passive Perception bonus is capped at PB.
  • Bird Strike: choose one active crow that can reach the target using its flying speed. The crow is destroyed after the save, hit or miss. A failed save deals printed damage and invokes one limb-damage result; success deals half damage and no limb result.
  • Domain — Murder’s Funeral: at initiative 20, choose up to three hostile creatures. Each is engulfed by a funeral murder and automatically takes 10d10 force or necrotic damage and becomes frightened of you until the next initiative 20. A protected creature makes a Wisdom save, taking half damage and avoiding frightened on a success. The event consumes no bonded crow, is not Bird Strike, and cannot critically hit.

Power identity

Why Tier I: exceptional intelligence gathering and one enormous finisher, paid for with visible, fragile assets and a simple command loop.

Seeing through a crow no longer blinds your body. You perceive from both locations, but a sight-based check uses one viewpoint chosen before rolling; physical cover and line of sight still apply from that viewpoint.

Scavenger

Once per turn after you damage a frightened creature with an attack, gain PB temporary HP. Temporary HP does not stack.

For each Bird Strike choose one: replace its d10 base dice with d12s, or make it a limb-tagged attack without targeted-strike disadvantage and subtract 1d6 from the target’s injury save. It still must meet the revised injury threshold.

Black Bird Manipulation
You were born with a simple ability many called weak, the ability to

Cursed Energy

usable source of energy, called cursed energy. These feelings can be
harnessed into attacks or abilities. The number of cursed energy you
possess is based on your class, some classes use a modifier that
being charisma or wisdom (Player’s choice)

proficiency bonus.
You regain all spent cursed energy on a long rest.
Cursed Crows
At 1st level, you have learned how to imbue crows with cursed energy
to use them as part of your technique. As an action for 1 Cursed
Energy, you can imbue a raven with cursed energy if it's restrained,
grappled, or incapacitated. It then becomes one of your cursed
ravens, it will become charmed by you and will follow your orders no
matter what. As an action, you can close your eyes giving you the
blinded condition unless you have blindsight, in exchange for being
able to see through one of your crows.
While seeing through your crows, you
can use your passive perception instead
of the crows. You can only see one crow
at a time and may switch between them
as an action or stop seeing through
them as a bonus action. You can start
with 3 cursed crows.

When you reach 3rd level, you are
constantly followed by an omen of

a bonus action, you force each creature
of your choice that you can see within
30 feet of you to make a Wisdom saving
throw. On a failed save, a creature
becomes frightened of you for 1 minute.
The frightened creature can repeat this
saving throw at the end of each of its
turns, ending the effect on itself on a
success.
Raven’s Eye
Starting at 6th level, your crows have been enhancing your sight.
You will gain +1 to your passive perception for every crow you
currently have. In addition, you can switch between crows as a bonus
action and may stop seeing through them at will.
Bird Strike
Beginning 11th level, your mastery of the Black Bird Manipulation
has made you develop its most powerful technique. As an action for 10
Cursed Energy, you can order one of your crows to launch themselves
at a creature from their flying speed range. The target must make a
Dexterity saving throw, on a success they will take half damage and
won't damage a limb. On a failure, however, they will receive 10d10
force damage and will get one of their limbs damaged, roll a d6 to
see the damaged limb.
Domain Expansion: Black Mahakala
If you reach 18th level, you gain your domain expansion. While
inside it, at Initiative 20, you may choose up to three targets to
suffer the full effects of Bird Strike.
