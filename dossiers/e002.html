Black Bird Manipulation

A reconnaissance network with fear control and a costly, lethal Bird Strike.

Field notes. Subject: a reconnaissance network that remembers every battlefield, and a flock that arrives ahead of death. Three truths govern the omen-keeper. First, the crows are memory, not muscle: three bonded at the start, permanent until death, charmed, obedient, and the sorcerer may see through one with no range limit on the same plane, blinded himself. Second, the fear is borrowed: Omen of Death forces a Wisdom save within 30 feet as a bonus action, frightening for a minute, but success grants immunity until dawn. Third, Bird Strike is the costly truth: 10 CE, 10d10 force with a limb-injury result, and the crow dies. Mark the Domain: in Black Mahakala the Murder Descends at every initiative 20: up to three creatures suffer the full Bird Strike, no cost, no roll, and the crows still die. The Domain does not change the price. It simply pays it on schedule.

Full progression

Feature map

1Cursed Crows:

Cursed crows

The flock is not an army; it is a memory with wings. Three bonded crows, permanent until death, charmed, obedient, and a sorcerer who sees through their eyes at the cost of his own. You begin with three cursed crows. As an action for 1 CE, you can imbue a restrained, grappled, or incapacitated raven, making it one of your cursed crows; the bond is permanent until it dies or you release it, and the crow is charmed by you and follows your orders. Use the Raven stat block with the Cursed Spirit type. You can direct up to PB + CE modifier crows at once (minimum 3); others remain bonded but inactive.

Remote sight

As an action, close your eyes to see through one crow (you are blinded unless you have blindsight). There is no range limit while you and the crow are on the same plane. Your own body is blinded, and deafened if you also listen through the crow, and cannot use reactions that depend on your body’s senses. You may use your passive Perception instead of the crow’s. Switching to another crow costs an action; ending the link costs a bonus action.

3Omen of Death:

Omen of Death

Crows gather where death is about to happen; the sorcerer merely arrives with the invitation. A bonus action, thirty feet, and the flock’s memory of every battlefield does the frightening. As a bonus action, force each creature of your choice that you can see within 30 feet to make a Wisdom save. On a failure it becomes frightened of you for 1 minute. A frightened creature repeats the save at the end of each of its turns, ending the effect on a success. A creature that succeeds on the save is immune to your Omen of Death until the next dawn.

6Raven’s Eye:

Raven’s Eye

The network matures into an instrument: every crow within a mile sharpens the sorcerer’s own senses, and switching between witnesses becomes a bonus action. The witness multiplies. You gain +1 to your passive Perception for every active crow you have within 1 mile; the bonus is capped at PB.

Quicker switching

Changing the crow you view through is now a bonus action, and ending the link costs no action.

11Bird Strike:

Bird Strike

The costly truth of the dossier: 10 CE, 10d10 force, a limb-injury result, and the crow dies. Every lethal strike spends a witness. Count the flock before you count the damage. As an action for 10 CE, order one active crow to attack a target it can reach using its flying speed. The target makes a Dexterity save: on failure it takes 10d10 force damage and suffers one revised limb-injury result; on success it takes half damage and no limb result. The crow is destroyed after the save, hit or miss.

Pinnacle expression

Maximum, Domain, or equivalent.

Domain Expansion, Black Mahakala:

The sky goes out like a snuffed lamp, and in its place a murder of crows becomes the ceiling, the walls, the weather. At the center squats a shadow in the shape of a wrathful god, crowned and many-armed, though its face is only ever the suggestion of a beak. The birds do not caw. Their silence is the loudest thing in the room.

The Murder Descends: at every initiative count 20, up to three chosen creatures suffer the full Bird Strike, no cost, no attack roll, and the crows still die. The Domain does not change the price of the strike. It simply pays it for him, on schedule. 18: full-turn action, 20 CE; 60-foot-radius enclosed Domain centered on you; 1 minute; Burnout 10 rounds. Sure-Hit Event, The Murder Descends: at initiative count 20 each round, choose up to three creatures inside; each suffers the full effects of Bird Strike at no cost, and the crows still die afterwards. An unprotected chosen target takes the complete printed result with no attack roll and no initial save. A creature protected by an anti-domain defense makes a Dexterity saving throw vs your CE save DC (invented); on a success it takes half damage and suffers no other rider.

Innate Technique Feats

Build-defining options.

These are optional character-building feats. You must meet each listed prerequisite before selecting it.

Prerequisite: Cursed Crow

Deathwatch

To mark a creature through a crow’s eyes is to borrow the flock’s patience: direction and distance for an hour, through invisibility, darkness, cover. Death, watched in advance. While seeing through a crow, you can use a bonus action to mark one creature the crow can see. For 1 hour you sense the marked creature's direction and distance through the bond, even through invisibility, darkness, or cover, while both of you remain on the same plane. Only one creature can be marked this way at a time.

Prerequisite: Omen of Death

Murder's Due

The frightened one is released, and the flock collects the debt, every nearby crow spending its reaction to close in. Mercy, weaponized. As a bonus action, end Omen of Death's frightened condition on one creature within 60 feet of you. Each of your active crows within 30 feet of it can use its reaction to fly up to its speed toward that creature without provoking opportunity attacks, converging on the scent of its fear.

Prerequisite: Bird Strike and CE score 20

Maximum: Kamikaze

When the crow dies in its strike, the death-cry becomes an Omen. The flock’s terror outlives the flock, fear, funded by a corpse. When your Bird Strike destroys its crow, the death-cry becomes an Omen of Death. Each creature within 15 feet of the target must make your Omen Wisdom save or become frightened of you until the end of your next turn. A creature that succeeds is immune to this effect until the next dawn.

Prerequisites: Must have Cursed Crow

All Seeing

The bond deepens until the sorcerer is no longer blinded while seeing through his crows, and every nearby crow lends him ten feet of blindsight. The network stops borrowing his eyes and gives him theirs. You have improved your ability to manage information. While you see through the crows you don’t gain the blinded condition. You gain 10 ft of blindsight for every cursed crow within 60 ft of you that is not incapacitated.

Prerequisites: Must have Omen of Death

Scavenger

The crows taught him their opportunism: strike what is already frightened, and the fear feeds him, temporary HP equal to his level, once per turn. You have learned from the opportunistic way of the crows. Once per turn when you hit a frightened creature with an attack, you gain temporary hit points equal to your level.

Rules integration

Edge cases and shared systems.

Rules priority: the reviewed rulings, explicit codex clarifications, and the level progression always control when original wording conflicts. Legacy Domain auto-hit language, action terminology, event timing, missing saves, and older scaling never override revised player rules.
Why Tier I: exceptional intelligence gathering and one enormous finisher, paid for with visible, fragile assets and a simple command loop.
