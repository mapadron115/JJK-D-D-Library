Black Bird Manipulation

A reconnaissance network with fear control and a costly, lethal Bird Strike.

The fiction. The prevailing theory holds that the user's cursed energy imprinted on crows — creatures that gather where death is about to happen — and learned to arrive ahead of it. Registry notes describe the Omen of Death as the flock's memory of every battlefield the sorcerer ever watched from too far away to stop.

Full progression

Feature map

1Cursed Crows:

Cursed crows

You begin with three cursed crows. As an action for 1 CE, you can imbue a restrained, grappled, or incapacitated raven, making it one of your cursed crows; the bond is permanent until it dies or you release it, and the crow is charmed by you and follows your orders. Use the Raven stat block with the Cursed Spirit type. You can direct up to PB + CE modifier crows at once (minimum 3); others remain bonded but inactive.

Remote sight

As an action, close your eyes to see through one crow (you are blinded unless you have blindsight). There is no range limit while you and the crow are on the same plane. Your own body is blinded — and deafened if you also listen through the crow — and cannot use reactions that depend on your body’s senses. You may use your passive Perception instead of the crow’s. Switching to another crow costs an action; ending the link costs a bonus action.

3Omen of Death:

Omen of Death

As a bonus action, force each creature of your choice that you can see within 30 feet to make a Wisdom save. On a failure it becomes frightened of you for 1 minute. A frightened creature repeats the save at the end of each of its turns, ending the effect on a success. A creature that succeeds on the save is immune to your Omen of Death until the next dawn.

6Raven’s Eye:

Raven’s Eye

You gain +1 to your passive Perception for every active crow you have within 1 mile; the bonus is capped at PB.

Quicker switching

Changing the crow you view through is now a bonus action, and ending the link costs no action.

11Bird Strike:

Bird Strike

As an action for 10 CE, order one active crow to attack a target it can reach using its flying speed. The target makes a Dexterity save: on failure it takes 10d10 force damage and suffers one revised limb-injury result; on success it takes half damage and no limb result. The crow is destroyed after the save, hit or miss.

Pinnacle expression

Maximum, Domain, or equivalent.

Domain — Murder’s Funeral: 18 — Black Mahakala: the autonomous Murder’s Funeral pulse. at initiative 20, choose up to three hostile creatures. The Domain holds their funerals early: a funeral murder gathers around each and croaks the Omen of Death made certain — each target hears its own dirge. Each automatically takes 10d10 force or necrotic damage as the dirge tears at them, and becomes frightened of you until the next initiative 20: they have heard how they end. A protected creature makes a Wisdom save, taking half damage and avoiding frightened on a success. The event consumes no bonded crow, is not Bird Strike, and cannot critically hit.

Innate Technique Feats

Build-defining options.

These are optional character-building feats. You must meet each listed prerequisite before selecting it.

Prerequisite: Cursed Crow

Deathwatch

While seeing through a crow, you can use a bonus action to mark one creature the crow can see. For 1 hour you sense the marked creature's direction and distance through the bond, even through invisibility, darkness, or cover, while both of you remain on the same plane. Only one creature can be marked this way at a time.

Prerequisite: Omen of Death

Murder's Due

As a bonus action, end Omen of Death's frightened condition on one creature within 60 feet of you. Each of your active crows within 30 feet of it can use its reaction to fly up to its speed toward that creature without provoking opportunity attacks, converging on the scent of its fear.

Prerequisite: Bird Strike and CE score 20

Maximum: Kamikaze

When your Bird Strike destroys its crow, the death-cry becomes an Omen of Death. Each creature within 15 feet of the target must make your Omen Wisdom save or become frightened of you until the end of your next turn. A creature that succeeds is immune to this effect until the next dawn.

Prerequisites: Must have Cursed Crow

All Seeing

You have improved your ability to manage information. While you see through the crows you don’t gain the blinded condition. You gain 10 ft of blindsight for every cursed crow within 60 ft of you that is not incapacitated.

Prerequisites: Must have Omen of Death

Scavenger

You have learned from the opportunistic way of the crows. Once per turn when you hit a frightened creature with an attack, you gain temporary hit points equal to your level.

Rules integration

Edge cases and shared systems.

Rules priority: the reviewed rulings, explicit codex clarifications, and the level progression always control when original wording conflicts. Legacy Domain auto-hit language, action terminology, event timing, missing saves, and older scaling never override revised player rules.
Why Tier I: exceptional intelligence gathering and one enormous finisher, paid for with visible, fragile assets and a simple command loop.

Bird Strike
Beginning 11th level, your mastery of the Black Bird Manipulation
has made you develop its most powerful technique. As an action for 10
Cursed Energy, you can order one of your crows to launch themselves
at a creature from their flying speed range. The target must make a
Dexterity saving throw, on a success they will take half damage and
won't damage a limb. On a failure, however, they will receive 10d10
force damage and will get one of their limbs damaged, roll a d6 to
see the damaged limb.

If you reach 18th level, you gain your domain expansion. While
inside it, at Initiative 20, you may choose up to three targets to
suffer the full effects of Bird Strike.
