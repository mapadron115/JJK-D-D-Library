Attunement: spend 1 hour and one cursed-tool attunement slot. A Sacred Treasure supplements your Innate Technique; it does not grant a second one.

Activation: activated properties spend the wielder’s own CE. If a non-sorcerer is explicitly allowed to wield the treasure, its Tool save DC is 8 + proficiency bonus + one chosen mental ability modifier.

SACRED TREASURE • Meliodas

Demon Sword Lostvayne

Special Grade

Ornamental short sword of physical division

Lostvayne is dangerous because clones inherit the wielder's techniques. Their raw output falls sharply, but hax, reactions, positioning, and utility often do not.

  1. Demon Sword: finesse shortsword, 1d6 slashing. Counts as a cursed tool and technique focus.
  2. Physical Clone (3 CE): action; create one clone in an empty space within 10 ft for 1 minute. It has your AC, speed, saves, senses, and 1 HP. It acts immediately after you.
  3. Clone Output: each clone deals only 25% of your weapon/technique damage (round down, minimum 1), cannot use Domains, Maximum Techniques, Extreme Magic, summons that persist independently, or create more clones. Save DCs are -2.
  4. Shared Reserve: clones spend YOUR CE. They do not receive separate resources, recharge features, reactions, or limited-use pools. You and all clones share one reaction per round.
  5. Command: no action required. A clone has one action, movement, and bonus action, but cannot use Extra Attack unless a later feature says otherwise.
  6. Fourfold Division (level 9+): you may maintain up to four clones. Creating additional clones costs 2 CE each after the first.
  7. Sacred Treasure Release — Thousand Reflections (level 13+, 7 CE): for 1 minute, maintain four clones without their maintenance counting against other summon limits; each clone's damage rises to 33%. Once/round, one clone may use your reaction independently, but still spends your CE.
