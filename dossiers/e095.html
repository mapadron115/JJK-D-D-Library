Demon Sword Lostvayne

Lostvayne is dangerous because clones inherit the wielder's techniques. Their raw output falls sharply, but hax, reactions, positioning, and utility often do not.

FormOrnamental short sword of physical division
GradeSpecial Grade
Features & procedures

Exact player-facing procedures.

Demon Sword:

finesse shortsword, 1d6 slashing. Counts as a cursed tool and technique focus.

Physical Clone (3 CE):

action; create one clone in an empty space within 10 ft for 1 minute. It has your AC, speed, saves, senses, and 1 HP. It acts immediately after you.

Clone Output:

each clone deals only 25% of your weapon/technique damage (round down, minimum 1), cannot use Domains, Maximum Techniques, Extreme Magic, summons that persist independently, or create more clones. Save DCs are -2.

Shared Reserve:

clones spend YOUR CE. They do not receive separate resources, recharge features, reactions, or limited-use pools. You and all clones share one reaction per round.

Command:

no action required. A clone has one action, movement, and bonus action, but cannot use Extra Attack unless a later feature says otherwise.

Fourfold Division (level 9+):

you may maintain up to four clones. Creating additional clones costs 2 CE each after the first.

Sacred Treasure Release — Thousand Reflections (level 13+, 7 CE):

for 1 minute, maintain four clones without their maintenance counting against other summon limits; each clone's damage rises to 33%. Once/round, one clone may use your reaction independently, but still spends your CE.

The fiction. The registry classifies Lostvayne not as a weapon but as a division of the self: the blade reads the wielder's soul and distributes the reading, never the whole — which is why the clones keep the user's techniques, reactions, and hax while their raw output thins to a quarter. The prevailing theory holds that the shared CE reserve is the point: the sword is a Sacred Treasure because it lets one soul fight a war on four fronts, and charges every front the same price.

Rules integration

Tool Die & Sacred Treasure attunement

Attunement: spend 1 hour and one cursed-tool attunement slot. A Sacred Treasure supplements your Innate Technique; it does not grant a second one.
Activation: activated properties spend the wielder’s own CE. If a non-sorcerer is explicitly allowed to wield the treasure, its Tool save DC is 8 + proficiency bonus + one chosen mental ability modifier.
Sacred Treasure Release: normally requires level 13 or higher and is usable once per long rest; pay the specific treasure’s listed CE cost. An explicit exception in its individual entry takes precedence.
