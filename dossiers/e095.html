Shared Reserve:

Fourfold Division (level 9+):

Sacred Treasure Release — Thousand Reflections (level 13+, 7 CE; bonus action):

Once/round, one clone may use your reaction independently, but still spends your CE.

Field notes. Subject: the blade that divides. The registry is right, not a weapon but a division of the self: Lostvayne reads the wielder's soul and distributes the reading, never the whole. Three truths govern it. First, the arithmetic is honest: each clone deals 25% of your damage (minimum 1), save DCs at −2, no Domains, Maxima, or Extreme Magic. Second, the price is shared: clones spend your CE, no separate resources, no separate recharge, one shared reaction per round, because one soul fights on four fronts at one shared price. Third, the command is free: no action required, each clone with its own action, movement, bonus action; from level 9, four clones; the Release, Thousand Reflections, raises damage to 33%, lets four clones ignore summon limits, and lends one clone your reaction once per round. Mark the maxim: Lostvayne does not multiply you. It itemizes you.
