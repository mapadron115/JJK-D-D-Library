Pyromantic Soul

Ember / ignition / impulsive casting / inferno

Power tierTier IV · Special Grade
Core law

What the technique actually does.

Core Engine — Ember. Ember 0–10, maximum 10. Ember is gained ONLY through recklessness — dealing fire damage to others never grants Ember.

Blood for fire: when you take damage from a source you caused (your own technique features catching you, your own burning path), gain 1 Ember. Once per turn. Damage taken as an Overdraw cost does not also trigger this.

Take it head-on: when you must make a Dexterity saving throw, you may declare you meet it head-on before rolling — make the save with disadvantage; whether you succeed or fail, gain 1 Ember. Once per turn.

Overdraw: when you activate a Pyromantic Soul feature, you may take damage equal to your character level (cannot be reduced) to gain 1 Ember. Once per turn.

Cooling: at the end of each of your turns in which you gained no Ember, lose 1 Ember. Caution starves the fire; there is no way to bank it.

Thresholds — automatic intensification (always on while Ember meets the threshold; no activation, no choice):

1+ Living Flame: your hair becomes living flame; shed bright light 10 ft and dim light 10 ft beyond; disadvantage on Dexterity (Stealth) checks.

3+ Kindled: once per turn, add +1d8 fire to one of your fire features.

5+ Raging: the bonus becomes +2d8.

7+ Unbound: your fire damage ignores resistance.

10 Inferno Unbound: the bonus becomes +3d8, and the fire will not be banked — at the start of each of your turns, flames erupt: every creature within 15 ft of you (including you) takes PBd8 fire, Dexterity save for half. You cannot suppress this.

Full progression

Level 1 through 20.

1Pyrobolt

Make a 60-ft technique attack for 1 CE, dealing 2d8 fire damage.

3Chandra's Ignition

For 2 CE, create a 15-ft burst centered on yourself; creatures in the area make a Dexterity save, taking 3d8 fire damage on a failure. You may choose to catch yourself in the burst; you make the save as normal, and if you take the damage you gain 1 Ember through Blood for fire.

6Flame Dash

As a bonus action, spend 1 CE: move 30 ft without provoking opportunity attacks and leave a burning path until your next turn; each creature takes 2d8 fire damage once. You may run through your own path; if you take its damage you gain 1 Ember (once per turn).

9Spontaneous Combustion

For 3 CE, target one creature within 60 ft; it makes a Constitution save, taking 5d8 fire damage on a failure and Burning for 1d8 at the end of its next turn.

11Regulator Off

When you Take it head-on, you gain 2 Ember instead of 1. (Your fire ignores resistance while Ember is 7+ — see Core law.)

13Elemental Incarnation

For 5 CE, for 1 minute: you gain fire immunity, a fly speed of 50, you may pass through occupied spaces as living flame, and your attacks deal +1d10 fire damage.

15Inferno

For 6 CE, create a 30-ft-radius inferno within 90 ft; creatures in the area make a Dexterity save, taking 8d10 fire damage on a failure, and unattended objects ignite. You may choose to catch yourself in the inferno to gain Ember through Blood for fire.

18Emblem of Flame

Taking it head-on no longer imposes disadvantage — you have learned to ride the blast. Your first fire technique each turn rolls one additional damage die.

20Torch of Defiance

Your Ember cannot drop below 3 while you are conscious. Once per long rest, you may cast Inferno for 0 CE with maximum dice.

Pinnacle expression

Maximum, Domain, or equivalent.

Maximum — Incandescent Cataclysm (10 CE, requires Ember 10): 60-ft cone or 30-ft sphere, Dexterity save 12d12 fire/radiant, half on success. Ember is a gate, not a cost — it does not become 0. After it resolves, your threshold intensifications are suppressed until the end of your next turn (the fire vents outward; it is not banked).

Feats

Technique Feats

Young Pyromancer

Your big magic sheds sparks that live. The first time each combat you cast a fire technique costing 3 or more CE, the excess flame coalesces into a 1-HP fire elemental token (AC 13, fly 30, touch 1d6 fire) that lasts 1 minute and acts on your initiative under your bonus-action command. It cannot leave a 30-foot radius around you — it is a spark, not a summon — and it winks out if you go a full round without dealing fire damage.

Shared Flame

as a bonus action, you can share your Living Flame with a willing creature: the next weapon attack it makes before the end of your next turn deals an extra 1d8 fire damage.

Reckless Spark

Pain is fuel. When you roll fire damage for one of your techniques, you may take damage equal to your proficiency bonus — this cannot be reduced or redirected — to reroll any number of the dice and keep the new results. You must accept the second roll. If this damage drops you to 0 HP, the reroll still resolves; Chandra's spark does not care what it burns, including her.

Can't Be Extinguished

once per long rest, when you take damage while Ember is 5+, use your reaction to erupt: the attacker takes PBd8 fire, Dexterity save for half, and you gain 1 Ember.

Rules integration

Edge cases and shared systems.

Shared rulings: Technique DC = 8 + PB + CE ability modifier. Direct rolled attacks may crit and use the campaign Black Flash procedure when eligible. Tokens, autonomous summons, passive damage, and initiative-20 Domain effects cannot Black Flash. Maximum Output modifies ordinary supported techniques, never named Maxima or Domains.
