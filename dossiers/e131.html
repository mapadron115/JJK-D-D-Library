Pyromantic Soul

Ember / ignition / impulsive casting / inferno

Power tierTier IV · Special Grade
Core law

What the technique actually does.

Core Engine — Ember. Ember 0–10. Gain 1 once/turn when you deal fire damage; gain an additional 1 when you willingly take damage or fail a restraint/control effect through reckless action. At 5+ your flames intensify; at 10 you can cash out spectacularly.

Full progression

Level 1 through 20.

1Pyrobolt

60-ft technique attack, 1 CE, 2d8 fire. At Ember 5+, +1d8 once/turn.

3Chandra's Ignition

2 CE: 15-ft burst centered on you, Dex save 3d8 fire; you are unharmed.

6Flame Dash

Bonus action 1 CE: move 30 ft without provoking and leave burning path until next turn, 2d8 fire once/creature.

9Spontaneous Combustion

3 CE, one creature 60 ft Con save, 5d8 fire and Burning 1d8 at end next turn on failure.

11Regulator Off

At Ember 7+, fire ignores resistance. You may add +2 dice to a fire feature by taking damage equal to level after resolving it.

13Elemental Incarnation

5 CE, 1 minute: fire immunity, fly 50, pass through occupied spaces as flame, attacks +1d10 fire.

15Inferno

6 CE: 30-ft radius within 90 ft, Dex save 8d10 fire; unattended objects ignite. Ember +2.

18Emblem of Flame

At Ember 10, hostile creatures starting within 15 ft take PB fire; your first fire technique each turn rolls one additional die.

20Torch of Defiance

Ember cannot drop below 3 while conscious. Once/long rest cast Inferno for 0 CE and maximum dice, then Ember becomes 0.

Pinnacle expression

Maximum, Domain, or equivalent.

Maximum — Incandescent Cataclysm (10 CE, Ember 10): 60-ft cone or 30-ft sphere, Dex save 12d12 fire/radiant, half success. Ember becomes 0; until end next turn you cannot use fire techniques above 2 CE.

Rules integration

Edge cases and shared systems.

Shared rulings: Technique DC = 8 + PB + CE ability modifier. Direct rolled attacks may crit and use the campaign Black Flash procedure when eligible. Tokens, autonomous summons, passive damage, and initiative-20 Domain effects cannot Black Flash. Maximum Output modifies ordinary supported techniques, never named Maxima or Domains.
