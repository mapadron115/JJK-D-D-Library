Field entry

Journal Entry field report, converted to a table-ready stat block

Field notes. Subject: a fight that punishes the fight itself. Unusually aggressive for a disease curse — it attacks on sight of a perceiving sorcerer — and its whole body is contested ground. Mark the perimeter: starting your turn within ten feet means a Constitution save or poisoned until your next turn begins (the Infectious Aura). Mark the artillery: Eject Phlegm, thirty feet, 2d6 poison, and a failed save brings the coughing fit — poisoned, and disadvantage on attack rolls while afflicted. It is not powerful; it is unapproachable. The calculus here admits no heroics — do not close with it if avoidable. Ranged exorcism, fast, before the coughing moves through the team like a rumor.

Record

Classification & Risk

A Disease Cursed Spirit, Grade 4 (CR 1/8–1/2 band). Attacks immediately if it realizes a person can perceive it. Not individually powerful, but its ability to hinder combat makes it a serious nuisance.

Actions

Actions

Eject Phlegm. Ranged Attack: +4 to hit, range 30 ft. Hit: 2d6 poison damage, and the target must succeed on a DC 12 Constitution saving throw or suffer a violent coughing fit — poisoned until the end of its next turn and with disadvantage on attack rolls while so afflicted.

Campaign canon notes

Field rule — low-grade perception: Per the C&C field rules, Grade 3 and lower curses are invisible to creatures without cursed energy. A creature in a life-or-death situation at less than half its hit point maximum may temporarily or permanently gain the ability to see them. This spirit carries the Invisibility trait below.
