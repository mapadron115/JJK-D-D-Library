Feast of curses

The recipe is unglamorous and exact: high-grade curse flesh, ground and shredded. A shikigami eats it as an action, 10d4+20 hit points restored, advantage on attacks and skill checks for one minute. Shredded food meal made from the ground-up bodies of high-grade curses. A shikigami can eat this as an action to regain 10d4+20 hit points and gain advantage on attack rolls and skill checks for 1 minute.

Shikigami only

And it is a key cut for one lock, no effect on creatures that are not shikigami, each portion consumed in the eating. Do not waste the dead on the living. This meal has no effect on creatures that are not shikigami. Each portion is consumed when eaten.

Field notes. Subject: a meal of ground high-grade curse flesh, served to shikigami. Two truths. First: it is triage in a bowl, eaten as an action, it restores 10d4+20 hit points, a surge of stolen vitality that cannot exceed the shikigami's maximum. Second: it is courage served alongside, advantage on attack rolls and skill checks for one minute, the beast fighting sharper and keener for having fed. Mark the exclusivity: this food knows its audience, no effect on anything that is not shikigami, and each portion is gone when eaten. The old kennel wisdom holds: feed your hounds on the enemy you slew yesterday, and they will remember the taste of tomorrow's. A master's pantry, kept in the currency of dead curses.

Edge cases and shared systems.

Campaign canon notes

Note: the shikigami spends its own action to eat; a master can feed an incapacitated shikigami by using their own action. Hit points restored cannot exceed the shikigami's maximum.
