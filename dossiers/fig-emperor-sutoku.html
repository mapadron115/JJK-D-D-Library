Feature map

Level-by-level features, as the figure refined them.

L1

Name Crown & Exile / Burden Upward

Action · 2 CE · Focus, 1 minute.

At character creation, choose INT or CHA as your CE ability for this technique; locked thereafter. Link two creatures within 60 feet as Crown and Exile. Unwilling targets make a Wisdom save vs your technique DC when named (zack-ruled) — a creature that succeeds cannot be made Crown or Exile for 1 minute; willing targets (including yourself) need no save. Once per round as a reaction, transpose one external non-damage benefit from the Crown to the Exile; once per round as a reaction, transpose one external non-damage penalty from the Exile to the Crown. The Crown loses what the Exile gains. Benefits transpose down; penalties transpose up. Damage, Stunned/Paralyzed/Unconscious, death, and Domain sure-hits are excluded.

L3

Exchange Offices

Bonus action · 1 CE.

Swap which linked creature is Crown and which is Exile. Once per round, the Exile may use the Crown's proficiency bonus on a d20 roll when the Crown's is higher.

L6

The Low Seat Speaks

Reaction.

When a beneficial effect would affect the Exile — one that could equally target the Crown — the Exile may refuse it. If the Crown accepts the effect, it redirects upward to the Crown, and Exchange Offices triggers immediately without costing your bonus action.

L11

Two Courts

Passive.

You may maintain two independent Crown–Exile pairs. You have one transposition reaction per pair per round.

L18

The Throne Has No Owner

Passive.

The first valid transposition per pair per round costs no reaction. Exchange Offices may be used once per round without spending a bonus action.

Pinnacle expression

Maximum, Domain, or equivalent.

Capstone material.

Maximum Technique

Sovereign Becomes Commoner

Action · 10 CE.

Choose one Axis — AC, walking speed, current CE, current HP percentage, or initiative count — among creatures within 60 feet. The single highest and single lowest exchange those values until the end of your next turn. Values are capped at each recipient's maxima. Max HP, ability scores, class features, and techniques are never exchanged.

Domain Expansion

Court of the Demon Sovereign: The World Turned Upside Down

Full-turn action · 20 CE · 1,000 clash points · burnout 3 rounds · duration 3 minutes · 60-ft enclosed.

At initiative 20, you announce one Axis (as the Maximum), rank every creature in the Domain publicly, and the highest and lowest swap values until the next initiative 20. The sure-hit is the ranking and transposition; ordinary attacks stay ordinary. Counterplay is printed: creatures may deliberately lower their Axis position before the count.

Level 20 · Capstone

The Seating Chart, Rewritten

Once per long rest · action · 5 CE.

Choose one Axis (as the Maximum). For 1 minute, all creatures within 60 feet are continuously ranked on that Axis — re-ranked at each initiative 20 — and once per round as a reaction you may swap any two creatures' current values on that Axis. Values are capped at maxima; max HP, ability scores, class features, and techniques are never exchanged.
