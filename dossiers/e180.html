Nine Tails, Nine Lies

Plan which of nine named lies to deploy — stolen voices, false roads, fox weddings, foxfire — each with one fixed effect. Lies are earned only when enemies waste real resources on a deception, and none of them are currency.

Combat roleIllusion / Trickster Controller
TierTier IV · Special Grade
Core engineNine named lies
Core law

Character fantasy

You are the fox who lies with intent. Nine named lies — each with one specific effect and its own price — are the entire technique. The fox plans which lie the moment demands; the only judgment at the table is whether a deception earned the next lie.

Combat loop

  1. Create a deception that matters tactically.
  2. When an enemy genuinely wastes effort because of it, claim the next lie.
  3. Deploy the lie the moment demands — each lie has one fixed effect.

Core law

Claim a Lie

Claim a Lie:

once per hostile creature per round, when a technique-created deception causes it to waste an attack, action, bonus action, reaction, at least 10 feet of movement, or at least 1 CE on a target, location, or effect that was not what it believed, you claim the next unearned lie in the fixed order below. Pure dialogue misunderstandings count only if they cause one of those concrete costs. Claims reset after a long rest — the fox must lie anew each day.

Full progression

Level progression

1First Tail

Gain the Claim-a-Lie engine. You may claim Lies 1–2. You begin with the First Lie already told: I. Paper Fox (action, 1 CE — one visual/audio illusion in a 5-foot space within 30 feet for 1 minute; it may move 30 feet on your turn; no physical force; Investigation against your CE DC after direct inspection).

3Three Tails

You may now claim Lies 1–4. II. Stolen Voice (bonus action, 1 CE — speak with any voice you have heard; hearers need Insight against your CE DC to doubt it). III. Foxfire — Kindle Foxfire: action, 1 CE — place a foxfire wisp (a floating blue flame) in a 5-foot space within 60 feet; it lasts 1 minute with Focus and sheds dim light 10 feet. On your turn you may move each wisp up to 30 feet, no action. Maximum wisps = PB. When a hostile starts its turn within 10 feet of a wisp or moves within 10 feet of one, it makes a Wisdom save against your technique DC — failure: it is lured until the end of its next turn (it must spend its movement moving toward the wisp by the most direct route, as though charmed by the light); success: it is immune to that wisp’s lure for 24 hours. IV. Fox Wedding (action, 2 CE — 15-foot cube of illusory rain, lanterns, or procession within 60 feet; lightly obscured for 1 minute with Focus). Luring Drift: when a creature becomes lured by your wisp, you may move that wisp up to 15 feet (no action) — the victim’s forced movement follows the light.

6Five Tails

You may now claim Lies 1–6, and you may maintain up to PB Paper Foxes. V. False Road (action, 2 CE — illusory path, bridge, or doorway up to 30 feet long within 60 feet for 1 minute; believers waste their movement on a failed Wisdom save). VI. Substitution (reaction when attacked, 2 CE — before the roll, swap with a Paper Fox within 30 feet; the attack destroys the illusion).

11Seven Tails

You may now claim Lies 1–8. VII. Possession’s Echo (action, 3 CE — one creature that can see an illusion; Wisdom save or it treats one 15-foot cube of your illusion as fully real for movement and targeting until the start of your next turn; damage from a contradiction grants a new save). VIII. The Hidden Ball (bonus action, 3 CE — attacks against you have disadvantage until the start of your next turn, and you may Hide as part of this bonus action). Once per round when you claim a lie, move one illusion or wisp 30 feet without spending an action.

18True Form — Nine Tails Ascendant

IX. Nine Tails Ascendant — replaces Domain. Bonus action, 10 CE, once per long rest, 1 minute. All nine lies are available regardless of claims. Paper Foxes cost 0 CE and you may maintain up to PB. Foxfire wisps are kindled as a bonus action for 0 CE. Substitution costs 0 CE. +10-ft speed; you leave no tracks and cannot be followed non-magically. This is not a Domain: it has no clash points, no barrier, and the Domain clash and Burnout rules do not apply. This is the combat loop's endgame: escalate toward the ninth lie without relying on the GM simply declaring an NPC “fooled.”

Feats

Build-defining options.

Fox Bridegroom

Gain proficiency or expertise in Deception; harmless cosmetic shapechanging can alter hair, eyes, ears, tail, and clothing appearance at will.

Blue Foxfire

You may kindle a foxfire wisp in the space of one of your Paper Foxes. The first creature that becomes lured by that wisp regards you as a trusted ally while lured, hearing an ally’s voice calling from the flame.

Clever Enough to Believe Yourself

When you fail a save against being frightened or charmed, you may use your reaction and spend 1 CE to make one of your Paper Foxes within 30 feet believe the lie instead: the condition ends on you and the illusion is destroyed.

Thousand-Year Trick

Prerequisite: level 11. The first time each round a creature identifies one of your illusions, you may treat that wasted inspection as fuel: it counts as a deception that wasted an action for the purpose of claiming your next lie.

Rules integration

Building the character

Charisma-heavy Strategist/Support Specialist or slippery Scout. It rewards players who create believable tactical lies, while Tail gain has objective triggers that keep it playable.
Rules integration

Counterplay

Probe illusions with low-cost actions, use area effects, communicate discoveries, and deny the fox its claims — a lie unearned is a lie unusable.
