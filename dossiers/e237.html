Features & procedures

Properties & techniques

Exact player-facing procedures

Lock-On

bonus mark one creature; attacks ignore half cover against it. The mark lasts 1 minute or until you mark a new creature (invented).

Open Path

action, 2 CE: unlock mundane barrier or make Tool check against cursed seal to open 5-ft passage for one round.

Close Heart

reaction, 2 CE: ally within 30 ft gains advantage vs charm/fear/possession save.

Release: Door Between Battles

8 CE, action to activate (invented), once/long rest: create paired doors within 120 ft for 1 minute; willing creatures can move between them for 10 ft movement; cannot cross Domain barrier.

Field notes. Subject: an answer to every closed thing. Four truths govern the Key. First, it names its target: a bonus-action Lock-On marks one creature, and your strikes ignore half cover against it, the Key decides what a door is. Second, it opens: for 2 CE, an action unbars mundane barriers or pits a Tool check against a cursed seal to carve a 5-ft passage for a round. Third, it closes: for 2 CE and a reaction, an ally within 30 ft gains advantage against charm, fear, or possession, some doors are worth keeping shut. Fourth, it multiplies: Door Between Battles pairs two doors within 120 ft for a minute, moving willing creatures between them for ten feet of movement, never through a Domain barrier. Mark this: it is a rescue given a handle.

Rules integration

Cursed-tool attunement

Campaign canon

Cursed-tool attunement: you have PB attunement slots (2–6). This tool occupies one slot and requires a one-hour ritual. Attunement ends voluntarily over a short rest, on death, or when you replace it while over capacity. Distance does not break attunement. Cursed-tool attunement is separate from magic-item attunement.
Balance & counterplay. Mobility and anti-control are bounded by line of effect and barrier rules.
