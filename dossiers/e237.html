Key of the Closed Sky

1d8 slashing/bludgeoning versatile d10. Originalized key-sword concept: opens cursed pathways but does not import another setting's cosmology.

FormKey-shaped sword
RoleLock / heart-barrier utility
EngineKey
Attunement1 hour*
Properties & techniquesExact player-facing procedures

Lock-On

bonus mark one creature; attacks ignore half cover against it.

Open Path

action, 2 CE: unlock mundane barrier or make Tool check against cursed seal to open 5-ft passage for one round.

Close Heart

reaction, 2 CE: ally within 30 ft gains advantage vs charm/fear/possession save.

Release: Door Between Battles

8 CE once/long rest: create paired doors within 120 ft for 1 minute; willing creatures can move between them for 10 ft movement; cannot cross Domain barrier.

Balance & counterplay. Mobility and anti-control are bounded by line of effect and barrier rules.
