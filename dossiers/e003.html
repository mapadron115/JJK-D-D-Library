Medium • Controller / striker • Project pp. 55–56

Recovered printed values & campaign clarifications

At-the-table character reference

Slicing Exorcism: action; ranged CE attack, 60/120 feet; 1d10 necrotic damage on a hit.

Flowing Red Scale · level 3: bonus action, 3 CE, 1 minute, Technique Focus; double speed, +2 AC, advantage on Dexterity saves and Strength checks, and one restricted extra action each turn: one weapon attack, Dash, Disengage, Hide, or Use an Object.

Convergence · level 6: spend additional CE up to your CE modifier and pay an equal amount of HP. Each extra CE adds one damage die matching that feature’s base damage die, or one round of duration. A suitable external blood supply waives only the HP cost.

Piercing Blood · level 11: action, 10 CE + 10 HP; 100-foot-long, 5-foot-wide line; Dexterity save for 10d8 piercing damage, half on success. Convergence may replace the save with a single CE attack as described in the revised ruling.

Completed rules

  • Constructs: every CE and HP creates one connected 5-foot segment in an open space within reach. A construct has AC 13, HP equal to your level per CE spent, and lasts 1 hour. It can form a wall, bridge, simple tool, or weapon shape but cannot create moving machinery. A suitable external blood source waives only the HP cost and is consumed.
  • Crimson Binding: choose one creature within 30 feet. On a failed Strength save it is restrained; it repeats the save at the end of each turn. The binding is an object with AC 13 and HP equal to twice your level. You may maintain one binding at a time. Slicing Exorcism is a ranged CE attack with a 60/120-foot range.
  • Flowing Red Scale: lasts 1 minute and requires Technique Focus. Its extra action follows the printed restricted-action list and cannot stack with another extra-action source.
  • Convergence: spend up to your CE modifier as printed and pay matching HP. Choose damage or duration when you use it. Damage adds that many of one additional die matching the affected feature’s base damage die; duration adds that many rounds, not turns of control. It is one damage amplifier.
  • Piercing Blood: a 100-foot-long, 5-foot-wide line. Convergence may change the entire line to one CE attack compared against each target’s AC; roll damage once. On a miss a target takes no damage.
  • Domain — Bloodbath: at initiative 20 each chosen hostile automatically takes 8d10 necrotic damage and is restrained by the blood tide until the next initiative 20. A protected creature makes a Constitution save, taking half damage with no restraint on success. While the Domain remains, when a technique would cost your HP, one creature damaged or restrained by that technique instead takes equal necrotic damage; this transfer cannot reduce a target below 1 HP and cannot avoid a cost unrelated to a target.

Power identity

Why Tier III: ordinary use already covers defense, hard control, action economy, terrain, and burst damage. Its strength comes from breadth and real HP risk, not a hypothetical single combo.

Recovered character-build options

Innate Technique Feats 7

These are optional character-building feats. You must meet each listed prerequisite before selecting it.

Blood Production

Standard

Prerequisite: Blood Manipulation

Bonus action, 1 CE: produce enough blood to replace up to 10 HP of one Blood Manipulation cost before the end of your next turn. Unused blood becomes inert and cannot be stockpiled.

Poisonous Blood

High impact

Prerequisite: Blood Manipulation

Once per turn, a creature hit by your blood attack with an attack total of 15+ or failing a save against your blood feature by 5+ becomes poisoned for 1 minute. It repeats a Constitution save against CE DC at the end of each turn.

Blood Crystallization

High impact

Prerequisite: Blood Construction

Bonus action, 3 CE, Technique Focus for 1 minute: resistance to bludgeoning, piercing, and slashing; unarmed die increases one size; reaction adds +5 AC against one attack. At each turn’s start roll d20; on 1–3 gain one exhaustion level. Ending the form requires no action.

Flowing Red Scale: Stack

Pinnacle

Prerequisite: Flowing Red Scale

While Flowing Red Scale is active, bonus action and 5 CE upgrades rather than stacks with it: triple base speed, +3 AC, advantage on Strength/Dexterity saves and Strength checks, one restricted extra action, and a second reaction each round. It shares the original duration and Technique Focus.

Blood Edge

High impact

Prerequisite: Convergence and CE score 20

Once on your turn with no action, spend 1–CE modifier CE and matching HP to form one proficient melee weapon for 1 minute. It is cursed; base damage is d10, rising one step per 2 CE to d12 then 2d8. A suitable outside blood source waives only the HP cost.

Maximum: Supernova

Pinnacle

Prerequisite: Piercing Blood and CE score 20

Action: create up to PB orbs in open spaces within 30 feet. Each costs 10 CE and 10 HP as Piercing Blood. An orb lasts 1 minute and detonates immediately or by your reaction: creatures within 10 feet make Dexterity saves, taking 10d8 piercing and prone on failure, half and no prone on success. Resolve overlapping orbs separately.

Maximum: Wing King

Pinnacle

Prerequisite: Piercing Blood and CE score 20

Bonus action, 10 CE, once per long rest: for 1 minute with Technique Focus, quadruple walking speed and gain flying speed equal to half that walking speed. Gain one restricted extra action that can make three ranged CE attacks, each dealing 3d10 + CE modifier necrotic on hit.

Rules priority: the reviewed rulings, explicit codex clarifications, and character reference above always control when original wording conflicts. Legacy Domain auto-hit language, action terminology, event timing, missing saves, and older scaling never override revised player rules.
Blood Manipulation
You were born with a complex ability held and practiced by the Kamo
family, you can control your blood.
Cursed Energy
Starting at 1st level, you can harness your negative feelings into a
usable source of energy, called cursed energy. These feelings can be
harnessed into attacks or abilities. The number of cursed energy you
possess is based on your class, some classes use a modifier that
being charisma or wisdom (Player’s choice)
Your cursed energy DC is 8 + user cursed energy ability modifier +
proficiency bonus.
You regain all spent cursed energy on a long rest.
Blood Constructs
Starting at 1st level, you have been blessed with the Innate
Technique of the Kamo clan, making you begin with the most basic
technique, the blood constructs. You may pull blood from an available
source to create a semi-solid construct. As an action for 1 Cursed
Energy and 1 hit point, you can create a small construct smaller than
a 5ft by 5ft space. For every extra 5ft the price increases by 1
cursed energy and hp. You may ignore the hp cost if you have some
alternate source of blood. These constructs have HP equal to level
times the Cursed Energy spent.
Battle Blood
Starting at 1st level, you can harness your blood in a manner fit
for battle. As an action, you gain the two following construct
options:
● Crimson Binding: As an action, you unleash a red blood net,
forcing the target within 30ft to do a Strength saving throw,
or be restrained. They may remake the save at the start of
their turn.
● Slicing Exorcism: As an action you rapidily spin a ring of blood
and unleash it as a projectile. Roll a CE attack, on hit you
deal 1d10 Necrotic Damage.
Flowing Red Scale
At 3rd level, this technique increases the user's body temperature,
pulse rate, and the number of red blood cells to give themselves
greatly increased energy and physical capabilities. You may spend 3
Cursed Energy and a bonus action to gain the following benefits for 1
minute:
● Your speed is doubled.
● You will gain a +2 bonus to your AC.
● You gain advantage on Dexterity saving throws and strength
checks.
● You will gain an additional action on each of your turns. That
action can be used only to take the Attack (one weapon attack
only), Dash, Disengage, Hide, or Use an Object action.
Convergence
At 6th level, you compress and condense blood to its absolute limit.
Before you use a cursed technique, you can spend additional Cursed
Energy(and an equal amount of hit points) up to your Cursed Energy
modifier to enhance the technique's potency. For each Cursed Energy
you use, the technique will receive one additional dice of damage or
1 additional turn of duration. You may ignore the hp cost if you have
some alternate source of blood.
Piercing Blood
Beginning at 11th level, you can compress your blood into a deadly
supersonic beam. As an action, for 10 cursed energy and 10 hit
points, you unleash a 100ft line which makes your opponent have to
make a Dexterity save taking 10d8 piercing on a failure or half as
much on a success. If convergence is active you may instead of having
it be a Dex save you can have it be an attack roll. You may ignore
the hp cost if you have some alternate source of blood.
Domain Expansion: Bloodbath
When you reach 18th level, you gain your domain expansion. While
inside it, at Initiative 20, all of your opponents suffer 4d12
necrotic damage as their blood attacks them from the inside. As well
as while in the domain your cursed technique abilities cost hit
points from the opponents you are targeting.
