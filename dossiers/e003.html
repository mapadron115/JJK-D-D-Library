Blood Manipulation

A broad martial toolkit that turns HP and outside blood into construction, control, speed, and output.

Field notes. Subject: an engineer of his own river, who spends his body the way a state spends its water. Three truths govern the Kamo heir. First, blood is water, and water is managed: every five-foot segment of construct costs 1 CE and 1 HP, an outside source waives the HP, never the CE. Second, the engineer opens his own sluices: Flowing Red Scale doubles his speed, adds +2 AC, and grants an extra action; Convergence matches extra CE with his own HP for dice or duration. Third, the river weaponized: Piercing Blood is 10 CE and 10 HP for a supersonic line, 10d8 piercing across 100 feet. Mark the Domain’s cruelty: in Bloodbath, every chosen creature’s own blood attacks it from inside, 4d12 necrotic at initiative 20, no roll, no save. Every body is a river. Every river can be turned.

Full progression

Feature map

1Blood Constructs / Battle Blood.

Blood Constructs

The river, dammed and directed: every five-foot segment of construct costs 1 CE and 1 HP, the currency posted honestly. An outside source waives the HP, never the CE. Water is free; work is not. As an action for 1 CE and 1 HP, pull blood from an available source to create a semi-solid construct: every 1 CE and 1 HP creates one connected 5-foot segment in an open space within reach. A construct has AC 13, HP equal to your level per CE spent, and lasts 1 hour. It can form a wall, bridge, simple tool, or weapon shape, but cannot create moving machinery. A suitable external blood source waives only the HP cost and is consumed.

Crimson Binding

As an action for 4 CE, unleash a red blood net at one creature within 30 feet. On a failed Strength save it is restrained; it repeats the save at the end of each turn, for up to 1 minute with Technique Focus. The binding is an object with AC 13 and HP equal to twice your level. You may maintain one binding at a time.

Slicing Exorcism

As an action for 1 CE, spin a ring of blood and unleash it: one ranged CE attack, range 60/120 feet; 1d10 necrotic damage on a hit.

3Flowing Red Scale.

Flowing Red Scale

The engineer opens the sluices: doubled speed, +2 AC, advantage on Dexterity saves and Strength checks, and one extra action per turn. For one minute, the body is a river in flood, at the river’s prices. As a bonus action for 3 CE, gain the following for 1 minute (requires Technique Focus): your speed is doubled; +2 bonus to AC; advantage on Dexterity saves and Strength checks; and one extra action on each of your turns, usable only for one weapon attack, Dash, Disengage, Hide, or Use an Object. The extra action cannot stack with another extra-action source.

6Convergence.

Convergence

The doctrine of over-engineering: before any technique, spend extra CE up to your CE modifier and match it in HP, each pair buys a damage die or a round of duration. Power, bought in the body’s own coin. Before you use a cursed technique, you may spend additional CE up to your CE modifier and pay an equal amount of HP. Each extra CE adds one damage die matching that feature’s base damage die, or one round of duration, choose damage or duration when you use it. A suitable external blood supply waives only the HP cost.

11Piercing Blood.

Piercing Blood

The river weaponized: 10 CE and 10 HP for a supersonic line, 100 feet long, 10d8 piercing on a failed save. This is what the body spends when it stops building and starts ending. As an action for 10 CE + 10 HP, unleash a 100-foot-long, 5-foot-wide line of supersonic blood. Each target makes a Dexterity save: 10d8 piercing damage on failure, half on success. Convergence may replace the save with a single CE attack compared against each target’s AC, roll damage once; on a miss a target takes no damage.

Pinnacle expression

Maximum, Domain, or equivalent.

Domain Expansion, Bloodbath:

The ground forgets it was ever stone and becomes a shallow, endless sea of blood, warm as a living body and thick enough to slow the step. It rains upward here, droplets rising from the surface to hang in the air like red lanterns, and every breath carries the copper tang of an old wound reopened. The horizon is not a line but a stain.

Their Blood Rebels: at every initiative count 20, each chosen creature’s own blood attacks it from inside, 4d12 necrotic, no roll, no initial save. The engineer’s final lesson: every body is a river, and every river can be turned. 18: full-turn action, 20 CE; 60-foot-radius enclosed Domain centered on you; 1 minute; Burnout 10 rounds. Sure-Hit Event, Their Blood Rebels: at initiative count 20 each round, every chosen creature’s blood attacks it from the inside for 4d12 necrotic damage, with no attack roll and no initial save. A creature protected by an anti-domain defense makes a Constitution saving throw vs your CE save DC (invented); on a success it takes half damage. While the Domain stands, your blood manipulation techniques may spend hit points from other creatures within the Domain to pay their HP costs.

Innate Technique Feats

Build-defining options.

These are optional character-building feats. You must meet each listed prerequisite before selecting it.

Prerequisite: Blood Manipulation

Blood Transfusion

The engineer learns to irrigate others: 1 CE and 5 HP become 2d6 + CE modifier of healing in a willing creature. His own river, lent out, never to himself. As an action, spend 1 CE and 5 HP to transfuse freshly produced blood into a willing creature within 5 feet. It regains 2d6 + your CE modifier hit points. You cannot target yourself with this transfusion.

Prerequisite: Blood Manipulation

Crimson Clot

A reaction to clot the blood in a wound he has dealt, the victim’s speed halved as the blood hardens. The river leaves dams behind it. Once per turn, when you damage a creature with a blood attack, you can use your reaction to clot the blood in the wound. The creature's speed is halved until the end of its next turn as the blood hardens around the injury. A creature with no blood is immune.

Prerequisite: Blood Construction

Blood Crystallization

Blood, frozen into armor: resistance to the three physical damage types, a larger unarmed die, +5 AC as a reaction, at the price of a d20 rolled each turn, exhaustion waiting on 1–3. The crystal is strong because the flesh beneath it is brittle. Bonus action, 3 CE, Technique Focus for 1 minute: resistance to bludgeoning, piercing, and slashing; unarmed die increases one size; reaction adds +5 AC against one attack. At each turn’s start roll d20; on 1–3 gain one exhaustion level. Ending the form requires no action.

Prerequisite: Flowing Red Scale

Red Current

While the Red Scale floods him, he may run on liquid and up walls, the slipstream carrying him past guards who expected gravity to do their work. While Flowing Red Scale is active, you can move across the surface of liquids and up vertical surfaces without falling; if you end your turn on neither solid ground nor a surface, you fall. Your rushing slipstream carries you past guards: your movement does not provoke opportunity attacks while Flowing Red Scale is active.

Prerequisite: Convergence and CE score 20

Blood Edge

A blade bled into being: 1 to CE-modifier CE, matching HP, for a cursed weapon rising from d10 to 2d8. The sword that costs its smith, and cannot be sundered without sundering him. Once on your turn with no action, spend 1–CE modifier CE and matching HP to form one proficient melee weapon for 1 minute. It is cursed; base damage is d10, rising one step per 2 CE to d12 then 2d8. A suitable outside blood source waives only the HP cost.

Prerequisite: Piercing Blood and CE score 20

Maximum: Supernova

Up to PB orbs, each a Piercing Blood waiting to happen, detonated on his reaction. The general’s calculus: one man’s blood, spent to wound many. Action: create up to PB orbs in open spaces within 30 feet. Each costs 10 CE and 10 HP as Piercing Blood. An orb lasts 1 minute and detonates immediately or by your reaction: creatures within 10 feet make Dexterity saves, taking 10d8 piercing and prone on failure, half and no prone on success. Resolve overlapping orbs separately.

Prerequisite: Piercing Blood and CE score 20

Maximum: Wing King

The river takes flight: quadrupled speed, a flying speed, an extra action for three ranged strikes. Blood was never meant to fly; that is exactly why it terrifies. Bonus action, 10 CE, once per long rest: for 1 minute with Technique Focus, quadruple walking speed and gain flying speed equal to half that walking speed. Gain one restricted extra action that can make three ranged CE attacks, each dealing 3d10 + CE modifier necrotic on hit.

Prerequisites: Must have Flowing Red Scale

Flowing Red Scale: Stack

The flood, deepened: 6 CE for a greater Red Scale. The sluices were never the limit, only the engineer’s caution was. The power of the Flowing Red Scale has reached new heights. As a bonus action for 6 CE or while Flowing Red Scale is active for 3 CE, you instead gain the following benefits for 1 minute: ●​ Your movement speed is tripled ●​ You gain a +4 bonus to AC ●​ You have advantage on Dexterity and Strength saving throws and Strength checks and once per round you can reroll a check or save ●​ You gain one extra action and reaction.

Rules integration

Edge cases and shared systems.

Rules priority: the reviewed rulings, explicit codex clarifications, and the level progression always control when original wording conflicts. Legacy Domain auto-hit language, action terminology, event timing, missing saves, and older scaling never override revised player rules.
Why Tier III: ordinary use already covers defense, hard control, action economy, terrain, and burst damage. Its strength comes from breadth and real HP risk, not a hypothetical single combo.
Appendix

Additional material.

Blood Manipulation
You were born with a complex ability held and practiced by the Kamo
family, you can control your blood.
Cursed Energy
Starting at 1st level, you can harness your negative feelings into a
usable source of energy, called cursed energy. These feelings can be
harnessed into attacks or abilities. The number of cursed energy you
possess is based on your class, some classes use a modifier that
being charisma or wisdom (Player’s choice)
Your cursed energy DC is 8 + user cursed energy ability modifier +
proficiency bonus.
You regain all spent cursed energy on a long rest.
Blood Constructs
Starting at 1st level, you have been blessed with the Innate
Technique of the Kamo clan, making you begin with the most basic
technique, the blood constructs. You may pull blood from an available
source to create a semi-solid construct. As an action for 1 Cursed
Energy and 1 hit point, you can create a small construct smaller than
a 5ft by 5ft space. For every extra 5ft the price increases by 1
cursed energy and hp. You may ignore the hp cost if you have some
alternate source of blood. These constructs have HP equal to level
times the Cursed Energy spent.
Battle Blood
Starting at 1st level, you can harness your blood in a manner fit
for battle. As an action, you gain the two following construct
options:
● Crimson Binding: As an action, you unleash a red blood net,
forcing the target within 30ft to do a Strength saving throw,
or be restrained. They may remake the save at the start of
their turn.
● Slicing Exorcism: As an action you rapidily spin a ring of blood
and unleash it as a projectile. Roll a CE attack, on hit you
deal 1d10 Necrotic Damage.
Flowing Red Scale
At 3rd level, this technique increases the user's body temperature,
pulse rate, and the number of red blood cells to give themselves
greatly increased energy and physical capabilities. You may spend 3
Cursed Energy and a bonus action to gain the following benefits for 1
minute:
● Your speed is doubled.
● You will gain a +2 bonus to your AC.
● You gain advantage on Dexterity saving throws and strength
checks.
● You will gain an additional action on each of your turns. That
action can be used only to take the Attack (one weapon attack
only), Dash, Disengage, Hide, or Use an Object action.
Convergence
At 6th level, you compress and condense blood to its absolute limit.
Before you use a cursed technique, you can spend additional Cursed
Energy(and an equal amount of hit points) up to your Cursed Energy
modifier to enhance the technique's potency. For each Cursed Energy
you use, the technique will receive one additional dice of damage or
1 additional turn of duration. You may ignore the hp cost if you have
some alternate source of blood.
Piercing Blood
Beginning at 11th level, you can compress your blood into a deadly
supersonic beam. As an action, for 10 cursed energy and 10 hit
points, you unleash a 100ft line which makes your opponent have to
make a Dexterity save taking 10d8 piercing on a failure or half as
much on a success. If convergence is active you may instead of having
it be a Dex save you can have it be an attack roll. You may ignore
the hp cost if you have some alternate source of blood.
Domain Expansion: Bloodbath
When you reach 18th level, you gain your domain expansion. While
inside it, at Initiative 20, all of your opponents suffer 4d12
necrotic damage as their blood attacks them from the inside. As well
as while in the domain your cursed technique abilities cost hit
points from the opponents you are targeting.
