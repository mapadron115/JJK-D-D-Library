+3 Cursed Weapon

The first-grade standard — a curse-imbued weapon for sorcerers facing what fists alone can't touch.

Field notes. Subject: the officer's issue. Three truths govern it. First, the +2's elder sibling: the same honest tool, one grade sharper — any weapon, imbued to wound curses and shikigami, carrying +3 to attack and damage. Second, first grade: the weapon for what fists alone cannot touch, now with authority. Third, it is issued, not bonded — no attunement, no ritual — because a standard must be pick-up-able. Hence the maxim: give the standard a sharper edge, and the whole army stands taller.

First-Grade Imbuement

The first-grade standard: any weapon, taught to wound what fists alone cannot touch. The quartermaster's answer to a harder war. This is any weapon that has been imbued with a curse, allowing it to damage curses or shikigami. You gain a +3 bonus to attack and damage rolls with this weapon.
