Features & procedures

Sacred Treasure Release — Zero-Miss Zone (level 13+, 7 CE):

Level 13+ · 7 CE · For 1 minute: reach 60 ft, opportunity attacks cost your reaction at most once per creature per round · Once per turn, turn one missed Courechouse attack into a hit (never a critical hit).

The release is the doctrine made law: sixty feet of reach, opportunity attacks rationed by creature rather than reaction, and once per turn a miss becomes a hit. The staff stops correcting the hand starts correcting the world. 1 minute. Reach becomes 60 ft, opportunity attacks do not consume your reaction more than once per creature per round, and once/turn one missed Courechouse attack into a hit. Field notes. Subject: a hunter’s impatience, preserved in wood and chain. Three truths govern this treasure. First, it answers the miss, not the strike: where most weapons spend their virtue on the blow, Courechouse spends it on the second chance, the reroll, the turned miss into a hit, the barrage that does not end. Second, it denies the enemy his geography: reach extends, cover is routed around, and no corner of the battlefield is truly beyond the hand that holds it. Third, it rewards the hunter’s habits, theft and drain techniques may ride its strikes, because a hunter’s weapon is also a hunter’s net. Mark the doctrine folded into every procedure: never let the prey have the first reach.

Rules integration

Tool Die & Sacred Treasure attunement

Attunement: spend 1 hour and one cursed-tool attunement slot. A Sacred Treasure supplements your Innate Technique; it does not grant a second one.
Activation: activated properties spend the wielder’s own CE. If a non-sorcerer is explicitly allowed to wield the treasure, its Tool save DC is 8 + proficiency bonus + one chosen mental ability modifier.
Sacred Treasure Release: normally requires level 13 or higher and is usable once per long rest; pay the specific treasure’s listed CE cost. An explicit exception in its individual entry takes precedence.
