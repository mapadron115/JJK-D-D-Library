Activation: activated properties spend the wielder’s own CE. If a non-sorcerer is explicitly allowed to wield the treasure, its Tool save DC is 8 + proficiency bonus + one chosen mental ability modifier.

Holy Staff Courechouse

Special Grade

Four-section chain staff of impossible reach and precision

Courechouse rewards a martial character who already has strong physical stats; Snatch makes it nastier, but the staff works without it.

  1. Four-Section Staff: 1d8 bludgeoning, finesse. Reach 10 ft; you may attack around half cover.
  2. Distorted Chain (1 CE): before an attack, extend reach to 30 ft and ignore three-quarters cover from physical obstacles that the chain can route around.
  3. Predatory Angle: once/turn when you miss a weapon attack, reroll it against a different creature within reach.
  4. Snatch Conduit: if you possess a theft/drain technique, its touch/range can originate from any creature struck by Courechouse this turn; normal costs/saves remain.
  5. Super Concentration (4 CE): bonus action, 1 minute. +10 speed, advantage on Perception, and once/turn make one additional Courechouse attack when taking the Attack action. You cannot be surprised while conscious.
  6. Hunter's Barrage (4 CE): action; choose up to PB creatures within 30 ft and make one attack against each. Missing one does not end the barrage.
  7. Sacred Treasure Release — Zero-Miss Zone (level 13+, 7 CE): 1 minute. Reach becomes 60 ft, opportunity attacks do not consume your reaction more than once per creature per round, and once/turn turn one missed Courechouse attack into a hit. This does not create critical hits.
