Cursed Speech

A vocal-strain engine ranging from forced movement to limb destruction, maximum-HP collapse, and death.

Core law

Completed rules

Read this section as the technique’s posted tolls: every word costs an action, every creature must hear it, language is irrelevant, and every CE spent tears the throat that spoke it. The law is honest about the price because the price is the point. A word normally uses your action; “as an attack” in the source does not replace one attack inside the Attack action. A creature must be able to hear you; language is irrelevant. Deafened creatures and targets in magical silence are unaffected. Unless a word invokes a spell attack or HP threshold, it uses a Charisma save. A target may choose to fail.

Vocal-cord HP are a separate pool: one third maximum HP, rounded up. Each CE spent on a word deals that much vocal damage after resolution. While above 0, regain 1 vocal HP at the start of each turn; a short or long rest restores the pool. Ordinary healing restores both normal and vocal pools by the same amount but never above either maximum. At 0 vocal HP you cannot use a word, do not regenerate vocal HP each turn, and suffer one exhaustion level until the pool rises above 0.

Against a target whose CR exceeds your level, vocal damage from that use is doubled after all other calculation. This is strain, not damage, and cannot be reduced. It preserves the lore distinction between commanding peers and overwhelming stronger beings.

Field notes. Subject: a voice that issues edicts the world cannot refuse. Three truths govern the speaker. First, the vocabulary is the whole technique: ten words, Sleep, Return, Run Away, Blast Away, Plummet, Get Twisted, Get Crushed, Don’t Move, Explode, Die, each with its own cost. Hearing is the only threshold; language is irrelevant. Second, the toll is posted in the flesh: a vocal-HP pool one third of maximum HP, every CE spent dealing that much unreducible strain; at zero, no words and one exhaustion level. The strain doubles against higher-CR targets, commanding peers versus overwhelming stronger beings, kept honest. Third, the words escalate: Interjection (a bonus action, even a reaction), Iron Chords (the pool doubles), Resounding Voice (a 35-foot cone, allies included). And the Domain is the room that remembers: Echo Chamber’s Last Word falls on every chosen creature at initiative 20: no cost, no save.

Full progression

Feature map

1Power of Words:

The ten commands

Ten words. That is the entire vocabulary of command: Sleep, Return, Run Away, Blast Away, Plummet, Get Twisted, Get Crushed, Don’t Move, Explode, Die, each with its own cost and procedure. The sorcerer who inherits this technique inherits a dictionary, and the dictionary is a weapon. You speak ten cursed words, Sleep, Return, Run Away, Blast Away, Plummet, Get Twisted, Get Crushed, Don’t Move, Explode, and Die, each with its own CE cost and effect, listed in the Word Binding procedure reference below. Outside them you can speak only in whatever meaningless concept you choose, such as sushi ingredients.

Vocal-cord HP

Your vocal cords are a separate pool equal to one third of your maximum HP, rounded up. Each CE you spend on a word deals that much vocal damage after the word resolves. While above 0, regain 1 vocal HP at the start of each turn; a short or long rest restores the pool. Ordinary healing restores both pools by the same amount but never above either maximum. At 0 vocal HP you cannot use words.

3Interjection:

Interjection

The words learn timing: one chosen word may fire as a bonus action, or as a reaction when a target moves, acts, summons, or aims at the speaker. Command, interrupting mid-sentence. Choose one word after a long rest. It may use a bonus action, or a reaction when a legal target begins movement, starts an action, summons a creature, or targets you. You still pay all CE and vocal strain.

Word enhancement

At levels 3, 6, 11, and 18 you unlock one additional CE of enhancement for each word (maximum four). At this level: one additional CE. A listed +1 DC per CE is an explicit technique exception. “X” in base damage means CE modifier; when an enhancement says “additional Xd10,” each added CE contributes one d10.

6Iron Chords.

Iron Chords

The throat is reinforced: the vocal-HP pool doubles to half the sorcerer’s maximum HP. The toll-keeper strengthens the bridge he must keep crossing. Your vocal cords strengthen: the vocal-HP pool becomes one half of your maximum HP, rounded up.

11Resounding Voice.

Resounding Voice

The voice becomes a broadcast: 5 extra CE turns a word’s area into a 35-foot cone, allies included, damage rolled once. Command, no longer aimed but declared. Pay 5 extra CE to replace a word’s normal target area with a 35-foot cone originating from you. Every creature in it that can hear you is affected, including allies; roll damage once. You also unlock your third CE of word enhancement (the fourth unlocks at level 18).

Pinnacle expression

Maximum, Domain, or equivalent.

Domain Expansion, Echo Chamber: The Last Word: at every initiative count 20, one known word falls on every chosen creature, no cost, no initial save. The Domain is a room that remembers the last thing said in it, and says it forever. 18: full-turn action, 20 CE; 60-foot-radius enclosed Domain centered on you; 1 minute; Burnout 10 rounds. Sure-Hit Event, The Last Word: at initiative count 20 each round, speak one word you know; every chosen creature falls under that word’s effect at no cost, with no initial save. The word’s effect is disclosed when the Domain opens and follows its normal rules; the event deals no damage. A creature protected by an anti-domain defense makes a Wisdom saving throw vs your CE save DC (invented); on a success it resists the word entirely.

Innate Technique Feats

Build-defining options.

These are optional character-building feats. You must meet each listed prerequisite before selecting it.

Prerequisite: Power of Words

Fighting Words

Those who fail a word’s save make a second Wisdom save, failure leaves them disadvantaged on attacks that spare the speaker. The words teach their victims manners. When a creature fails a save against your cursed word, it makes a second Wisdom save. On failure, for 1 minute it has disadvantage on attacks that do not include you as a target; it repeats the save at the end of each turn. A creature can be affected by only one Fighting Words at a time.

Prerequisite: Interjection

Hushed Word

The Interjection word, pitched as a whisper to one creature within 30 feet, only it hears, only it suffers. Command, narrowed to a single ear. When you use a word with Interjection, you can pitch it as a whisper aimed at one creature within 30 feet that can hear you. Only that creature hears and is affected by the word, even if it would normally reach further; you still pay its full CE and vocal strain.

Prerequisite: Resounding Voice

Megaphone

Resounding Voice, focused into a 5-foot-wide, 70-foot line. The broadcast becomes a lance. When you use Resounding Voice, you can focus the broadcast into a 5-foot-wide, 70-foot line originating from you instead of a cone. Only creatures in the line that can hear you are affected; all vocal-cord, target, and save rules remain.

Prerequisites: Must have Interjection

Quick Tongue

One more word falls under Interjection’s speed. The vocabulary of the instant grows. Your versatility and training have increased the amount of words you have and your speed. You can choose one more word to be under the effect of Interjection.

Rules integration

Edge cases and shared systems.

Rules priority: the reviewed rulings, explicit codex clarifications, and the level progression always control when original wording conflicts. Legacy Domain auto-hit language, action terminology, event timing, missing saves, and older scaling never override revised player rules.
Why Tier IV: vocal strain is real, but ordinary access to hard commands, technique removal by dismissal, lasting maximum-HP damage, and an execution word demands boss-aware preparation.
