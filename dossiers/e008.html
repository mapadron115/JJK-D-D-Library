A vocal-strain engine ranging from forced movement to limb destruction, maximum-HP collapse, and death.

Feature map

  • 1 — Power of Words: ten commands and vocal-cord HP.
  • 3 — Interjection: one quick command.
  • 6 — Iron Chords.
  • 11 — Resounding Voice.
  • 18 — Echo Chamber.

Completed rules

  • A word normally uses your action; “as an attack” in the source does not replace one attack inside the Attack action. A creature must be able to hear you; language is irrelevant. Deafened creatures and targets in magical silence are unaffected. Unless a word invokes a spell attack or HP threshold, it uses a Charisma save. A target may choose to fail.
  • Vocal-cord HP are a separate pool: one third maximum HP, or one half from level 6, rounded up. Each CE spent on a word deals that much vocal damage after resolution. While above 0, regain 1 vocal HP at the start of each turn; a short or long rest restores the pool. Ordinary healing restores both normal and vocal pools by the same amount but never above either maximum. At 0 vocal HP you cannot use a word, do not regenerate vocal HP each turn, and suffer one exhaustion level until the pool rises above 0.
  • Against a target whose CR exceeds your level, vocal damage from that use is doubled after all other calculation. This is strain, not damage, and cannot be reduced. It preserves the lore distinction between commanding peers and overwhelming stronger beings.
  • Enhancements: at levels 3, 6, 11, and 18 you unlock one additional CE of enhancement for each word (maximum four). A listed +1 DC per CE is an explicit technique exception. “X” in base damage means CE modifier; when an enhancement says “additional Xd10,” each added CE contributes one d10.
  • Interjection: choose one word after a long rest. It may use a bonus action, or a reaction when a legal target begins movement, starts an action, summons a creature, or targets you. You still pay all CE and vocal strain.
  • Resounding Voice: pay 5 extra CE to replace a word’s normal target area with a 35-foot cone originating from you. Every creature in it that can hear you is affected, including allies; roll damage once.
  • Domain — Absolute Edict: at initiative 20 choose one edict for every chosen hostile that can hear: Silence deals 8d10 thunder damage and prevents speech until the next initiative 20; Kneel deals 4d10 psychic damage, knocks the target prone, and sets its speed to 0 until then; or Flee deals 4d10 psychic damage, removes reactions, and moves the target up to its speed along the safest route you choose. A protected creature makes a Wisdom save, taking half damage and ignoring the edict’s rider on a success. Absolute Edict costs no CE or vocal HP and is not an activation of an ordinary command word.
Word Binding procedure
Sleep · 1 CE Cast sleep at 1st level. Each unlocked added CE adds 3d8 to its HP pool.
Return · 1 CE One summoned creature within 60 feet makes a Charisma save; on failure it is dismissed to its origin or becomes dormant. Added CE can raise DC as printed.
Run Away · 2 CE One creature within 60 feet; on failure it immediately uses its reaction to move up to its speed directly away by the safest route. Added CE chooses +1 DC or one extra target each.
Blast Away · 4 CE One creature within 60 feet; on failure push 60 feet and deal 1d8 force per 10 feet actually moved. Each unlocked added CE adds 10 feet.
Plummet · 6 CE One creature within 60 feet; on failure take CE modifier d10 force, fall prone, and speed becomes 0 for up to 1 minute. Repeat a Strength save at end of each turn. Each added CE adds 1d10.
Get Twisted · 8 CE One creature within 30 feet; failure deals printed HP loss and inflicts the chosen arm/leg result under the limb rules. Success causes half HP loss and no limb result.
Get Crushed · 10 CE One creature within 30 feet; failure loses CE modifier d12 current and maximum HP and is paralyzed for up to 1 minute. Repeat a Strength save at end of each turn; before each failed repeat, lose that maximum HP again. Lost maximum returns after a long rest.
Don’t Move · 12 CE Resolve power word stun against one creature within 60 feet. Each added CE raises its HP threshold by 25.
Explode · 14 CE Target within 60 feet and creatures within 5 feet make Charisma saves; failure takes 3d8 force + CE modifier d6 fire, success half. Each added CE adds 1d8 force.
Die · 16 CE One creature within 60 feet whose current HP is at or below 30 × CE modifier makes a Charisma save; on failure it dies. Each added CE gives +1 DC and +10 HP threshold.

Power identity

Why Tier IV: vocal strain is real, but ordinary access to hard commands, technique removal by dismissal, lasting maximum-HP damage, and an execution word demands boss-aware preparation.

Recovered character-build options

Innate Technique Feats 3

These are optional character-building feats. You must meet each listed prerequisite before selecting it.

Fighting Words

Standard

Prerequisite: Power of Words

When a creature fails a save against your cursed word, it makes a second Wisdom save. On failure, for 1 minute it has disadvantage on attacks that do not include you as a target; it repeats the save at the end of each turn. A creature can be affected by only one Fighting Words at a time.

Quick Tongue

Standard

Prerequisite: Interjection

Choose one additional cursed word you know. That word may be used with Interjection. Change the choice only when gaining a level.

Megaphone

High impact

Prerequisite: Resounding Voice

When using Resounding Voice, spend 3 additional CE to replace its area with a 70-foot cone. This replaces rather than adds to its range; all vocal-cord, target, and save rules remain.

Interjection
Beginning at 3rd, you have learned how to say one specific word
quicker than the others. You can choose one of your cursed words to
use as a Bonus Action or Reaction. You can change the chosen word at
the end of a long rest.
