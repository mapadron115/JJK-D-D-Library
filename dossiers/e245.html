Cursed Tool Arsenal

Six Realms Staff — Rikudōkon

Special Grade emotion-forged staff: six Realm weapon forms chosen by the wielder's genuine emotional state — never by choice. Record of Ragnarok adaptation; the Hell Realm form is campaign-original.

Cursed ToolSpecial GradeEmotion-selected weapon forms
Record of Ragnarok-inspired homebrew · Naraka Chains campaign-original · Source character: Buddha

Core Principle

The Six Realms Staff does not ask the wielder what weapon they want.
It asks: Who are you right now?

The Staff transforms according to the wielder's genuine dominant emotional state. The wielder may never freely choose a Realm. A player cannot simply claim an emotion, fake one, or repeatedly manufacture a state purely to access the mathematically best weapon form.

Binding Vow — The Heart Chooses. The hand may wield the weapon. Only the heart may choose it.

At the start of the wielder's turn, or immediately after a major emotional reversal in the scene, the Staff may transform once per round into the Realm matching the wielder's genuine emotional state. The GM adjudicates uncertain cases collaboratively with the player. The point is to reward character truth, not punish roleplay.

Grade: Special Grade cursed tool · Attunement: 1 hour · Slots: 1 cursed-tool slot.

Shared Cursed-Tool Procedure

The Six Realms Staff uses a scaling Tool Die based on the wielder's level.

Activated properties spend the wielder's Cursed Energy. A non-sorcerer permitted to attune to the Staff uses: Tool DC = 8 + Proficiency Bonus + chosen mental ability modifier.

The Staff's abilities are cursed-tool abilities, not additional Innate Techniques. They do not count against the wielder's Innate Technique limit.

The Six Realms

God Realm — Wish-Granting Wheel Kannon · Lokapala

Emotional trigger: Confident joy, expansive certainty, or the sincere feeling that the current problem can be overcome.

Form: Heavy halberd / crescent axe.

Gift: Once per turn when you hit with a direct weapon attack using Lokapala, add 1 Tool Die force damage. If you moved at least 10 feet directly toward the target before the attack, the target must also make a Strength saving throw. On a failure, push it 5 feet.

Animal Realm — Horse-Headed Kannon · Nirvana

Emotional trigger: Instinctive aggression, competitive exhilaration, or the urge to meet force with greater force.

Form: Massive spiked club.

Gift: When Nirvana hits a creature that has moved at least 10 feet since the end of its previous turn, add 1 Tool Die bludgeoning damage. The target must make a Strength saving throw. On a failure: if standing, it is knocked prone; if already prone, it is pushed 10 feet instead.

Human Realm — The Snare Is Not Empty · Akshara

Emotional trigger: Focused curiosity, compassion, careful judgment, or the desire to understand before destroying.

Form: Compact shortsword / vajra blade.

Gift: Once per round when a creature within reach misses you with a melee attack, you may use your Reaction and spend 1 CE to make one weapon attack against it. On a hit, the target cannot take reactions until the start of its next turn.

Asura Realm — Eleven-Headed Kannon · Ahimsa

Emotional trigger: Protective resolve, refusal to let someone else be harmed, or anger consciously directed into defense.

Form: Enormous devotional shield.

Gift: When you or a creature within 5 feet is hit, you may use your Reaction to reduce the damage by 2 Tool Dice + Proficiency Bonus. If this reduces the damage to 0, the protected creature may immediately move 5 feet without provoking opportunity attacks.

Hungry Ghost Realm — Salakayas

Emotional trigger: Hatred, grief sharpened into aggression, hunger for one particular enemy's defeat, or the conviction that one thing must be cut away.

Form: Lion-headed scythe.

Gift: Salakayas gains reach. Once per turn when it hits a creature already missing HP, add 1 Tool Die slashing damage. The target must make a Strength saving throw. On a failure, pull it 5 feet toward you. If the emotional state fueling Salakayas genuinely ends, the form's Gift ends as well.

Hell Realm — Naraka Chains

Campaign-original sixth combat expression

Emotional trigger: Accepting personal suffering in order to stop someone else, or sincerely believing that both combatants are already trapped inside the same hell.

Form: Chained staff / hooked restraint weapon.

Gift: When you hit a creature with a direct weapon attack, you may spend 2 CE to mark it until the start of your next turn. While marked, if either you or the marked creature voluntarily moves more than 20 feet away from the other, that creature takes 1 Tool Die force damage per 10 feet traveled beyond the 20-foot threshold. This effect never prevents movement. The target is always allowed to leave.

Sacred Release — Cycle of Samsara

Level requirement: 13+ · Action: Bonus action · Cost: 6 CE · Uses: Once per long rest · Duration: Up to 1 minute · Requires: Technique Focus.

During Cycle of Samsara, the Staff can respond immediately to genuine emotional changes without waiting for the start of your turn. It may still transform no more than once per round. Whenever the Staff leaves one Realm because the wielder's genuine emotional state changes, the departing form may perform one final Farewell.

Farewells

  • Lokapala — God Realm: One creature hit by Lokapala during the round is pushed 10 feet.
  • Nirvana — Animal Realm: Immediately attempt one Shove against a creature within reach.
  • Akshara — Human Realm: Move 10 feet without provoking opportunity attacks from one creature you hit during the round.
  • Ahimsa — Asura Realm: One creature within 5 feet gains temporary HP equal to your Proficiency Bonus.
  • Salakayas — Hungry Ghost Realm: One wounded creature you hit during the round loses its reactions until the start of its next turn.
  • Naraka Chains — Hell Realm: Immediately end one Naraka mark without dealing any movement-triggered damage from it.

A Farewell is not a separate turn and may never create more than one additional weapon attack.

Awakened Legacy — Shared Fate / Zero

This is not a normal progression feature. It is a narrative awakening state.

Trigger. If the Six Realms Staff is truly destroyed by Special Grade force, artifact-level destruction, or a major narrative event, and a compatible remnant, spirit, or being willingly accepts shared fate with the wielder, the broken Staff may undergo a one-time awakened transformation.

The fragments reform into: Parinirvana Sword — Zero. A seven-branched awakened cursed sword. This is not ordinary repair. It cannot be activated simply because the wielder wants the stronger weapon.

Parinirvana Sword — Zero

While Zero exists:

  • its direct weapon attacks count as Special Grade cursed-tool attacks;
  • once per turn, a direct hit deals an additional 1 Tool Die radiant or force damage.

During the awakened scene, after the wielder acts in accordance with the willing remnant's shared purpose, they may invoke:

Mahaparinirvana

Action. Cost: 8 CE. Make one direct weapon attack. On a hit, add PB Tool Dice force or radiant damage.

This attack may damage cursed spirits, cursed-object manifestations, soul-linked constructs, or partially incorporeal cursed entities normally when their defense depends on metaphysical attachment. It does not automatically bypass Domain Expansion, Infinity, absolute immunity, or unrelated defensive mechanics unless another legal interaction would already allow that.

After using Mahaparinirvana, Zero becomes dormant until the narrative bond that created it is meaningfully reaffirmed or restored. The intent is that this is not a repeatable equipment nuke.

Source note

The first five forms and the emotion-based transformation premise adapt the Record of Ragnarok weapon. Naraka Chains is explicitly campaign-original, because the source material does not provide a comparably complete sixth combat form suitable for this conversion.

The defining contrast is: Chastiefol asks "What tool do you need?" — Six Realms asks "Who are you right now?" The Staff's tactical weakness is also the heart of its identity: the wielder cannot guarantee access to the mathematically optimal form. The strongest form available is determined by the character's genuine emotional state, making the Staff a reflection of inner life rather than a normal transformable weapon menu.
