Features & procedures

Properties & techniques

Exact player-facing procedures

Deactivation field

When you attack with the Black Rope, you may activate it as part of the attack (no action). While active, all magic and cursed-energy features are deactivated — techniques, maintained effects, and magical properties shut down. The field lasts until you end it (no action) or the rope burns away.

Burning cost

For every hit you land while the effect is active, you take a cumulative −1 penalty to attack and damage rolls with the rope. When the penalty reaches −5, the Black Rope burns away and is destroyed.

Rules integration

Edge cases and shared systems.

Campaign canon notes

Note: the degradation is permanent and does not reset between activations — ending the field early is the only way to preserve the rope. Deactivated features are suppressed only while the field is active.
