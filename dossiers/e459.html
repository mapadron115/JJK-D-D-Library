Features & procedures

Properties & techniques

Exact player-facing procedures

Deactivation field

The rope's cruelty is procedural — it activates as part of an attack and ends at no action cost. While it holds, the enemy's magic simply does not exist: no techniques, no maintained effects, no enchanted properties. Suppression, not contest. When you attack with the Black Rope, you may activate it as part of the attack (no action). While active, all magic and cursed-energy features are deactivated — techniques, maintained effects, and magical properties shut down. The field lasts until you end it (no action) or the rope burns away.

Burning cost

But every landed hit while the field burns takes its toll from the rope itself — a permanent, cumulative −1 to its attack and damage that no rest restores. At −5, the rope is gone; the only way to spare it is to end the field while there is still rope left. For every hit you land while the effect is active, you take a cumulative −1 penalty to attack and damage rolls with the rope. When the penalty reaches −5, the Black Rope burns away and is destroyed.

Field notes. Subject: a whip that unmakes sorcery and, in payment, unmakes itself. Three truths. First: while the field is active, all magic and cursed energy die — techniques, maintained effects, magical properties — not resisted, not contested, simply switched off. It is the only negotiation the rope offers, and it is absolute. Second: every hit the rope lands while the field burns costs it a point of itself — a cumulative −1 to its own attack and damage, permanent, no rest restoring it. Third: the debt is finite — at −5 the rope burns away entirely, and ending the field early is the only mercy it affords itself. Assess it as a fuse, not a weapon: each swing shortens it. Strike only when the silence is worth the rope.

Campaign canon notes

Note: the degradation is permanent and does not reset between activations — ending the field early is the only way to preserve the rope. Deactivated features are suppressed only while the field is active.
