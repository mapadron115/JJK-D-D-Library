Feature map

Level-by-level features, as the figure refined them.

L1

Face the Shards

Reaction, 1 CE

Shards of a broken mirror hang around you. When you can see a ranged attack or damaging cursed-energy effect targeting you, spend your reaction and 1 CE: you take no damage and the attacker suffers its own effect (same roll vs. their AC, or their own save vs. their own DC). Damaging effects with neither an attack roll nor a save are suffered by the attacker at full damage with no save (invented). In the instant of return, the attacker sees the worst part of themselves in the glass. The Flinch: melee weapon attacks, unarmed strikes, falls, and other purely physical blows cannot be reflected — they shatter the shards instead. The Worst of You: whenever one of your reflections turns a creature’s own attack back upon it, it must succeed on a Wisdom save against your CE save DC or be frightened of you until the end of your next turn. A creature that succeeds is immune to this fear for 24 hours.

L3

Look Away

Reaction, 1 CE (instead of reflecting)

When you could reflect, avert the mirror instead: the effect falls into the cracks between the shards and vanishes — harming no one, rebounding nowhere.

L6

Crueler Reflection

+1 CE on a reflection

The mirror shows the attacker the worst part of themselves, magnified, and the wound follows the sight: spend 1 additional CE when you reflect and the reflected damage dice are doubled, as though the attack had critically hit.

L11

Someone Worth Shielding

Reaction, 1 CE

When an ally within 30 ft. is targeted by a reflectable effect, reflect it as though it targeted you. Area and line effects can be turned back onto their point of origin — the originator makes its own save or catches its own blast.

L15

Kept Wounds

No action (keep) · Action (open the ledger)

When you take damage from any source (even a physical blow), note it in a ledger of hurts (max 5 x character level; fades on long rest). As an action, open the ledger at a creature within 60 ft.: CE-ability attack roll vs. AC; on a hit the shards show it everything it ever did to you — the stored amount as psychic damage. The ledger empties to 0.

Domain Expansion

Hall of Broken Mirrors

Full turn, 20 CE

Sure-hit domain — the sure-hit is the reflection. Every surface is cracked glass showing each viewer their own flaws. While it stands, every attack or damaging effect that targets you rebounds onto its source automatically (no reaction, no CE): same roll vs. the attacker's AC, or their own save vs. their own DC. Every rebound carries The Worst of You (see Face the Shards). Reflected effects cannot be reflected again.
