Thought counters / telepathy / illusion / memory

Power tierTier IV · Special Grade
Core law

What the technique actually does.

Core Engine, Thought. The whole technique is one exchange rate. Thought has a ceiling, his proficiency plus Intellect, never less than three, and it is minted only by reading: a mind that fails against him, a technique correctly identified, a willing subject studied for an action. Everything he does is a purchase from that purse. Thought max = PB + Intelligence modifier (minimum 3). Gain 1 once/turn when a creature fails an Int/Wis save against you, you correctly identify a technique, or you spend an action reading a willing mind. Spend Thought to strengthen illusions, interrupt plans, and erase information.

Field notes. Subject: a cartographer of minds. Three truths govern him. First, attention is the coin of his realm: a failed save against him, a technique correctly named, a mind willingly opened, each is minted into Thought, and Thought spends on illusions, interruptions, and erasures. Mark this: no mechanic here strikes the body for its keep; his currency is attention, never blood. Second, the patient inherit the field, I have watched him win whole duels by letting the enemy spend itself on his defenses. Third, knowledge compounds into foresight: at the peak he declares a turn foreseen and forces its repetition, and inside his Infinite Library he steals a Page of the enemy's choices, predicting the next matching action. Consider the maxim: he does not fight the battle. He is already at the debrief.

Pinnacle expression

Maximum, Domain, or equivalent.

Maximum, Mindbreak (10 CE + 6 Thought; action): The sentence the whole library has been building toward. Ten energy, six Thought, one mind: ten dice of psychic ruin and Stun on a failed save, and if it kills, one memory of the scene is sealed, locked until healing or the story itself intervenes. Some pages are never returned. one creature within 60 ft makes Int save. Failure: 10d10 psychic and Stunned until end next turn; success half. If reduced to 0 by this, you may seal one memory of the scene until restored by RCT-equivalent mental healing or major narrative intervention.

Feats

Technique Feats

Mental Fortress

A probe turned back on its sender. When he succeeds on a mental save, one Thought spent as a reaction reads the surface thought of whatever targeted him, the interrogator, interrogated. when you succeed on an Intelligence or Wisdom save, you may spend 1 Thought as a reaction to reflect the probe: learn the surface thought of the creature that targeted you, as Telepath with no save.

False Memory

An illusion wearing the shape of something the victim already believes is almost real. Built from a mind he has read within the minute, his illusions contest harder and strike first, disadvantage on their first save, disadvantage on their first look. The best lies are the ones the mark half-remembers. An illusion built from the victim's own mind is almost real. When you create an illusion, such as Perfect Illusion or Illusory Double, to deceive a creature whose surface thoughts you have read within the last minute, you have advantage on the technique contest (see Rules, Shared Resolutions) to establish the illusion, and the target has disadvantage on its first saving throw against it, and it has disadvantage on its first Investigation check to see through the illusion, which wears the shape of something it already believes. This advantage does not stack with other advantage on the same roll.

Architect of Thought

He does not spend Thought; he mortgages it. A feature's effect can be set as a contingency, waiting up to a minute for a named trigger, one trap laid at a time, and the future springs it for him. when you spend Thought on a feature, you may set it as a contingency: the effect waits up to 1 minute for a trigger you name, then resolves. You can maintain one contingency at a time.

Unraveler

His unmaking broadens. Unsummon may target a persistent effect or a Focus-maintained feature instead of a summon, a won contest suppresses it until the controller's next turn. Nothing his enemies maintain is safe from review. your Unsummon can target a persistent magical effect or Focus-maintained feature within 60 ft instead of a summon or construct; on a won contest it is suppressed until the end of its controller's next turn.

Rules integration

Edge cases and shared systems.

Shared rulings: Technique DC = 8 + PB + CE ability modifier. Direct rolled attacks may crit and use the campaign Black Flash procedure when eligible. Tokens, autonomous summons, passive damage, and initiative-20 Domain effects cannot Black Flash. Maximum Output modifies ordinary supported techniques, never named Maxima or Domains.
