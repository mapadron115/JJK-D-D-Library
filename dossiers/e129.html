Mind Sculptor

Tier IV · Special GradeThought counters / telepathy / illusion / memory

Core Engine — Thought. Thought max = PB + Intelligence modifier (minimum 3). Gain 1 once/turn when a creature fails an Int/Wis save against you, you correctly identify a technique, or you spend an action reading a willing mind. Spend Thought to strengthen illusions, interrupt plans, and erase information.

18Domain — Infinite LibraryInitiative 20: choose one hostile. Int save; failure gives you one Page—Attack, Defense, Movement, or Technique. Until next initiative 20, the first matching action it attempts is predicted: impose disadvantage or grant advantage to its target.

Maximum — Mindbreak (10 CE + 6 Thought): one creature within 60 ft makes Int save. Failure: 10d10 psychic and Stunned until end next turn; success half. If reduced to 0 by this, you may seal one memory of the scene until restored by RCT-equivalent mental healing or major narrative intervention.

Technique Feats

Mental Fortress: +1 vs Int/Wis saves.False Memory: illusions gain advantage against a creature whose thoughts you read.Architect of Thought: Thought max +2.Unraveler: Unsummon range +30 ft.
Shared rulings: Technique DC = 8 + PB + CE ability modifier. Direct rolled attacks may crit and use the campaign Black Flash procedure when eligible. Tokens, autonomous summons, passive damage, and initiative-20 Domain effects cannot Black Flash. Maximum Output modifies ordinary supported techniques, never named Maxima or Domains.
