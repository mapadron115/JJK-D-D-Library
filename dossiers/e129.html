Mind Sculptor

Thought counters / telepathy / illusion / memory

Power tierTier IV · Special Grade
Core law

What the technique actually does.

Core Engine — Thought. Thought max = PB + Intelligence modifier (minimum 3). Gain 1 once/turn when a creature fails an Int/Wis save against you, you correctly identify a technique, or you spend an action reading a willing mind. Spend Thought to strengthen illusions, interrupt plans, and erase information.

Full progression

Level 1 through 20.

1Telepath

Telepathy 60 ft. 1 CE, Wis save: read surface thought until end next turn. Failure grants 1 Thought.

3Illusory Double

2 CE: create PB doubles for 1 minute, Focus. First attack against you each round has disadvantage; a miss destroys one.

6Unsummon

Reaction 3 CE when a summon/construct manifests within 60 ft: technique contest. Win dismisses it until end of its controller's next turn; mundane creature instead pushed 30 ft.

9Memory Mill

3 CE + 1 Thought, Int save: target loses access to one declared proficiency, prepared technique option, or reaction until end next turn. Never deletes permanent features.

11Mind Palace

Advantage Int saves and concentration/Focus checks. Spend 2 Thought to communicate telepathically with PB creatures within 1 mile for 10 minutes.

13Thoughtseize

4 CE, Wis save: learn target's currently available named combat options and choose one; it costs +2 CE or equivalent resource until end next turn.

15Perfect Illusion

5 CE: 30-ft cube multisensory illusion for 10 minutes, Focus. Investigation vs DC reveals it; you can make its terrain count as difficult terrain through psychosomatic hesitation.

18Domain — Infinite Library

Initiative 20: choose one hostile. Int save; failure gives you one Page—Attack, Defense, Movement, or Technique. Until next initiative 20, the first matching action it attempts is predicted: impose disadvantage or grant advantage to its target.

20Living Guildpact

Thought max 12; first Thought spent each round is free. Once/long rest, after seeing a creature take a turn, declare you foresaw it: force it to repeat its movement path and first action choice on its next turn if legally possible.

Pinnacle expression

Maximum, Domain, or equivalent.

Maximum — Mindbreak (10 CE + 6 Thought): one creature within 60 ft makes Int save. Failure: 10d10 psychic and Stunned until end next turn; success half. If reduced to 0 by this, you may seal one memory of the scene until restored by RCT-equivalent mental healing or major narrative intervention.

Feats

Technique Feats

Mental Fortress

when you succeed on an Intelligence or Wisdom save, you may spend 1 Thought as a reaction to reflect the probe: learn the surface thought of the creature that targeted you, as Telepath with no save.

False Memory

An illusion built from the victim's own mind is almost real. When you create an illusion — such as Perfect Illusion or Illusory Double — to deceive a creature whose surface thoughts you have read within the last minute, you have advantage on the technique contest or save against it, and it has disadvantage on its first Investigation check to see through the illusion, which wears the shape of something it already believes. This advantage does not stack with other advantage on the same roll.

Architect of Thought

when you spend Thought on a feature, you may set it as a contingency: the effect waits up to 1 minute for a trigger you name, then resolves. You can maintain one contingency at a time.

Unraveler

your Unsummon can target a persistent magical effect or Focus-maintained feature within 60 ft instead of a summon or construct; on a won contest it is suppressed until the end of its controller's next turn.

Rules integration

Edge cases and shared systems.

Shared rulings: Technique DC = 8 + PB + CE ability modifier. Direct rolled attacks may crit and use the campaign Black Flash procedure when eligible. Tokens, autonomous summons, passive damage, and initiative-20 Domain effects cannot Black Flash. Maximum Output modifies ordinary supported techniques, never named Maxima or Domains.
