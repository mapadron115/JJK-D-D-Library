Ten Shadows — Technique Chassis

Core manifestation rules

These rules apply to every tamed shadow unless a stat block explicitly overrides them.

Manifest

Call from shadow

As an action, spend the listed CE and manifest a tamed shikigami in an unoccupied space within 5 feet of you. It remains until reduced to 0 HP, dismissed without an action, or you die.

Initiative

Act as one

A shikigami shares your initiative and takes its turn immediately after yours. It moves and uses its reaction independently.

Command

One manifestation

As a bonus action, command one manifestation to take an action. Every uncommanded manifestation Dodges. A standing order guides behavior but never removes this recurring command cost.

Range

Telepathic bond

You can communicate with your shikigami within 120 feet. Beyond that range it follows its last instruction and prioritizes returning to you.

Scaling

Your cursed energy

A shadow uses the higher of its printed attack bonus or your CE attack bonus, and the higher of its printed save DC or your CE save DC. Its attacks count as cursed attacks.

Incapacitated

Instinct takes over

If you are incapacitated, each manifested shikigami may act normally without being commanded, protecting you according to its nature and last order.

Manifestation limit: You may normally maintain one manifestation at 1st level, two at 3rd level, and three at 11th level. The Divine Dogs together count as one manifestation and share one action and reaction. All active Gama likewise count collectively as one manifestation and share one action and reaction.

Mahoraga consumes your entire normal manifestation capacity. A fusion counts as one manifestation, although every component remains unavailable while merged.

Technique progression

The roster is earned through victory, not an artificial level gate. From level 1, you may invoke the taming ritual for any untamed shadow on the roster—including Mahoraga—if you can pay that ritual’s listed CE cost. The creature itself is the gate.

The taming ritual

The full roster is challengeable from level 1. There is no level prerequisite for a taming ritual; the listed CE cost, the no-outside-aid rule, and the creature waiting within the ritual are the gates.

Beginning the trial

Invoke the untamed

  • There is no level prerequisite. Use an action and pay the shikigami's listed CE cost.
  • It appears at full HP in an unoccupied space within 30 feet.
  • Roll initiative normally. It treats you as its primary enemy.
  • Your already-tamed shikigami are part of your technique and may participate.
Victory and interference

Win alone or earn nothing

  • Reducing the untamed shadow to 0 HP without outside aid permanently tames it.
  • If another creature directly attacks, heals, restrains, buffs, or otherwise assists, the taming is invalidated.
  • An interfering creature becomes a ritual participant and a valid target.
  • The ritual continues until the shikigami or every ritual participant is defeated.
  • CE is spent even if the ritual fails.

Mahoraga: The Eight-Handled Sword Divergent Sila Divine General uses its complete CR 25 stat block during and after taming. No diminished player version exists. Anyone capable of defeating it alone has earned the full measure of the Ten Shadows Technique.

Dispersal, recovery, and inheritance

Damage remains meaningful without making every ordinary defeat permanently erase part of the subclass.

Dispersal

Reduced to 0 HP

The shadow disperses and cannot be manifested again until you finish a long rest. Dismissing and resummoning a shadow never restores HP; it returns with the HP it had when dismissed.

Destruction

Permanent loss is exceptional

A shadow is permanently destroyed only when a rule, exorcising effect, binding vow, or major story event explicitly says so. Its inherited potential can then pass into a compatible surviving shadow or a defined Totality form.

Shadow Storage

The shadow is a flexible inventory, not a weightless bag or an undefined creature prison.

  • You may store or retrieve one object you are carrying as your normal object interaction.
  • Stored objects continue to count against your carrying capacity.
  • Only objects can be stored unless another feature explicitly permits a creature to enter.
  • An object must fit through a 5-foot opening and cannot be actively worn or carried by an unwilling creature.
  • For 1 CE, you may retrieve a stored object as a reaction or cause it to emerge from a visible shadow within 30 feet.

Shadow Wearing

Unlocked at 8th level, Shadow Wearing is the internal counterpart to the fusion systems learned later.

Activate

Wear one tamed shadow

As a bonus action, choose one tamed, available shikigami and pay half its manifestation cost, rounded up to a minimum of 1 CE. Its form lasts for 1 minute.

Availability

One body, one function

You can wear only one form at a time. Its source cannot be manifested or contribute to a fusion, and a manifested shadow or active fusion component cannot be worn.

Action economy

You wield the form

Wearing is not a manifestation and uses no manifestation slot. You may summon and command other shadows normally. Wearing attacks use your CE attack bonus and wearing saves use your CE save DC.

Progression split: Shadow Wearing develops inward at level 8; Wells Unknown Abyss develops outward at level 11. At level 17, a user who has tamed Mahoraga may unlock Wheel Wearing once per long rest.

Fusion systems

Wells Unknown Abyss provides stable recipes. Chimerafication is the late-game freedom to build something unprecedented.

Wells Unknown Abyss

As an action, merge two manifested shikigami through one of the fifteen recipes in the Fusion Catalog. No additional CE is required after both components are manifested. The recipe identifies the base and the exact donor package it gains.

  • The fusion occupies one manifestation slot.
  • Neither component can exist separately while merged.
  • Undo the fusion as a bonus action; neither component regains HP.
  • If the fusion reaches 0 HP, both components disperse until your next long rest.
Chimerafication

Prerequisites: Wells Unknown Abyss and a 20 CE ability score.

Choose one tamed shikigami as the base and up to three different tamed shikigami as additions. The donors need not be manifested beforehand, but you pay the normal manifestation cost of every participant.

  • The base retains its complete stat block.
  • Each donor contributes one named trait, action, bonus action, reaction, or special movement speed.
  • Each donor adds one-quarter of its maximum HP, rounded down, to the base's maximum HP.
  • Ability scores, AC, saving throws, and wholesale stat blocks cannot be donated.
  • A transferred Multiattack works only with the attacks actually named in its wording.
  • The fusion counts as one manifestation. All contributors remain unavailable separately.
  • If reduced to 0 HP, every contributor disperses until your next long rest.

Full freedom at the pinnacle: A tamed Mahoraga may serve as either the base or a donor. Conquering Mahoraga is already the required balancing event.

Three Innate Technique feats

Divine Dog Totality, Shadow Skulker, and Chimerafication are the complete optional feat list. “Up to four” is a ceiling, not a requirement that every inheritance invent a fourth choice.

Inheritance

Divine Dog Totality

Prerequisite: Divine Dogs and one Divine Dog permanently destroyed.

Manifest the surviving Dog as Divine Dog Totality. Totality is mutually exclusive with both original Dogs and uses the revised Totality stat block.

Shadow movement

Shadow Skulker

As an action for 2 CE, submerge into your shadow for consecutive turns equal to your Constitution modifier, minimum one.

  • You have total cover and perceive the space around your shadow.
  • Your shadow moves across connected surfaces at half your speed.
  • You may summon, dismiss, and command Ten Shadows shikigami.
  • You cannot personally attack, use RCT, expand a Domain, or activate other cursed techniques.
  • Emerge within 5 feet as a bonus action. You are forcibly ejected when the duration ends.
  • If no manifested shikigami remain, you are ejected at the end of your next turn.
Freeform fusion

Chimerafication

Prerequisite: Wells Unknown Abyss and a 20 CE ability score.

Build one base plus as many as three donor additions under the Chimerafication rules. Two ready-to-play presets—Zagroth and Hageshi—appear in the Fusion Catalog.

Domain Expansion

Chimera Shadow Garden uses Shadow Submersion at initiative count 20 as its sole sure-hit event. Every shikigami attack and ability continues to resolve normally.

Level 18 · 20 CE

Chimera Shadow Garden

Use the system's normal lethal-domain activation, barrier, range, duration, clash, and burnout rules. The interior becomes a bottomless garden of fluid shadow. Terrain is difficult for creatures of your choice, while you gain the benefits of freedom of movement.

Sole Sure-Hit Event: Shadow Submersion

At initiative 20, chosen hostile creatures touching the Domain's surfaces automatically become restrained until the next initiative 20. A creature protected from the sure-hit instead makes a Strength save, avoiding the restraint on a success. A restrained creature may use its action to attempt a Strength check against your CE save DC, ending the restraint early on a success.

Shadow Clones

As a bonus action, spend up to your CE modifier in CE to create that many clones. Each has your AC, 1 HP, your speed, and no attacks or class features. As a reaction before damage is dealt, swap with a clone within 60 feet and make it the attack's target.

Shikigami Copies

Once per round when you manifest a non-Mahoraga shikigami, create one perfect temporary copy without additional CE. Copies ignore the normal manifestation limit but share the normal command economy.

Free manifestations

Shikigami manifested within the Domain cost 0 CE. Any manifestation or copy created for free disappears when the Domain ends; a shadow that was already active before expansion remains afterward.

Technique accuracy

Shadow Submersion is the Domain's only sure-hit. Shikigami attacks, Flood, Mournful Roar, grapples, and all other summon abilities resolve normally using their revised attack and save rules.

Shikigami User interaction

This prevents the class from receiving an unrelated generic companion in addition to the full Ten Shadows roster.

If a Shikigami User selects Ten Shadows, the Divine Dogs replace the generic Shikigami Companion. Choose one currently available Ten Shadows manifestation as your Bonded Shadow after each long rest. Class features that specifically name “your companion” apply only to that Bonded Shadow, not the entire roster.

Eight-Handled Wheel

Track each distinct damage type or phenomenon separately through three adaptation stages. The first resolved exposure begins adaptation. The second grants resistance, or advantage against an effect that does not deal damage. The third grants immunity or a specific functional counter. Each later distinct hit or failed save from the same phenomenon advances it one stage after that instance resolves; multiple hits can accelerate adaptation.

Conditional Adaptation

When afflicted by a condition, Mahoraga recovers at the end of its next turn. Repeated applications advance that condition through the same three-stage process, eventually granting immunity for the remainder of the manifestation.

Obstacle Adaptation

Barriers, unreachable movement, spatial defenses, and other obstacles can be treated as phenomena. Upon completing adaptation, Mahoraga develops a DM-determined method of overcoming that exact obstacle.

Ten Shadows — Shadow Wearing

Official table wording

Level 8 Feature

Shadow Wearing

At 8th level, you learn to draw a tamed shikigami’s shadow over your own body rather than fully manifesting it.

  • Activation: As a bonus action, choose one tamed, available shikigami that is not currently manifested or contributing to a fusion.
  • Cost: Spend cursed energy equal to half that shikigami’s normal summon cost, rounded up. Minimum 1 CE.
  • Duration: 1 minute. It ends early if you become unconscious, dismiss it without an action, or activate another Shadow Wearing form.
  • Limit: You can wear only one shikigami at a time.
  • Source Lock: While wearing a shikigami, you cannot manifest it or use it as the base or donor of either fusion system.
  • Other Shadows: Wearing is not a manifestation and occupies no manifestation slot. You may manifest and command other shikigami normally.
  • Unavailable Shadows: If that shikigami is unavailable because it was reduced to 0 hit points and has not recovered, you cannot wear it.

Attack & Save Rules

Shadow Wearing is a body-overlap technique, so the user supplies the accuracy, control, and cursed energy output.

  • Attacks: Any attack caused by Shadow Wearing uses your Cursed Energy attack bonus unless the DM rules a weapon attack is more appropriate.
  • Saves: Any saving throw caused by Shadow Wearing uses your Cursed Energy save DC.
  • Your Actions: Wearing abilities use your actions, bonus actions, and reactions as written. They require no shikigami command.
  • Once per Activation: An expended once-per-activation ability returns only when you pay to begin a new activation of that form.
  • Body Risk: Shadow Wearing is your body bearing a shikigami’s function, not a second creature or a separate pool of hit points.
Design intent: Shadow Wearing gives Ten Shadows a mid-level identity between basic summoning and later shikigami fusion. It rewards creative use without replacing the importance of actual summons.

Wheel Wearing

Mahoraga Shadow Wearing

Prerequisite: 17th level, Shadow Wearing, and Mahoraga tamed and currently available.

As a bonus action, spend 10 CE to wear the shadow of the Eight-Handled Sword Divergent Sila Divine General Mahoraga. This form lasts for 1 minute, locks Mahoraga as its source shadow, and can be used only once per long rest.

DM note: This should feel like the final summit of Ten Shadows. It is not just another stance; it is controlled adaptation wearing the body of the user.

Adaptive Wheel

When you take damage or fail a saving throw against a phenomenon, you may open an adaptation track for that specific damage type, attack, technique, condition, or environmental obstacle. You may maintain a number of tracks equal to your proficiency bonus.

At the end of your turn, advance one track by one turn if you were exposed to its phenomenon since the end of your previous turn. A track cannot advance this way more than once per round.

Wheel TurnsEffect
1 TurnYou gain advantage on future saves/checks against that specific effect.
2 TurnsYou gain resistance to that specific damage or advantage on saves and checks against that effect. If neither applies, the DM removes one limited part of it.
3 TurnsYou become adapted. For the rest of Wheel Wearing, you are immune to that specific damage or condition, or can ignore or counteract that exact obstacle as the DM determines.

Wheel Tools

  • Quick Adaptation: As a reaction when you are exposed to a phenomenon on an existing track, spend 3 CE to advance that track once immediately. You may do this a number of times equal to your proficiency bonus per long rest.
  • General’s Recovery: At the start of your turn, regain hit points equal to your proficiency bonus + your CE modifier.
  • Executioner’s Edge: Once per turn when you hit a cursed spirit or undead creature with a melee attack, deal an extra 3d12 radiant damage.
  • Strain of Adaptation: When Wheel Wearing ends, you cannot activate Shadow Wearing, manifest Mahoraga, or use either fusion system until the end of your next turn.
  • Heavy Strain: If you completed three or more adaptation tracks, gain 1 level of exhaustion when the form ends.

Campaign pacing

Suggested Progression

Levels 1–5

Summon, command, tame. Learn the basic shikigami rhythm and the danger of losing shadows.

Level 6

Shadow Storage expands the technique’s utility and makes the user feel like the shadow is part of their body.

Level 8

Shadow Wearing unlocks for every tamed and available shadow. No additional roster gate is imposed.

Level 11

Wells Unknown Abyss begins the external fusion route, while Shadow Wearing remains internal mastery.

Level 17

A user who has single-handedly tamed Mahoraga unlocks Wheel Wearing as a once-per-long-rest capstone.

Level 18

Chimera Shadow Garden completes the technique’s progression by turning the battlefield itself into living shadow.


Training Arc Advice

Begin with senses, movement, and defense. Heavy forms such as Mourn Tiger, Max Elephant, Round Deer, and Piercing Ox are not level-gated, but their costs and body-forward tactics make them natural later breakthroughs.

Balance Philosophy

Shadow Wearing should make the Ten Shadows user more versatile, not better at everything at once. The biggest limiter is choosing one form and giving up that shikigami as a summon during the duration.

Chimera Shadow: Wildebeast of Hell’s Savannah — Zagroth

Base: Divine Dog Totality

Additions: Piercing Ox + Mourn Tiger + Great Serpent

Type: Advanced freeform Chimerafication preset

Role: Ultimate striker / predator / high-speed execution unit

Summon Cost: 25 CE (2 + 8 + 9 + 6)

Maximum HP: Divine Dog Totality’s maximum HP + one-quarter of each donor’s maximum HP

Duration: 1 minute

Restriction: While Zagroth is active, Divine Dog Totality, Piercing Ox, Mourn Tiger, and Great Serpent cannot be summoned or used separately.

Defeat: If Zagroth reaches 0 HP, all four component shadows disperse until the summoner finishes a long rest.

Zagroth is the offensive counterpart to Hageshi.

Where Hageshi says: you shall not pass,

Zagroth says: you should have run sooner.

Lore

Zagroth is not shaped to defend territory. It is shaped to erase escape routes. Its four shadows knot together beneath a fleeing creature before the chimera erupts from the path ahead, as though the hunt had already decided where the prey would die.

1. Predator Core

Zagroth has advantage on attacks against a creature if the summoner or one of the summoner’s allies is within 5 feet of that creature.

Once per turn, if Zagroth hits a creature that has already taken damage this round, it deals an additional 2d6 slashing damage.

2. Oxbreaker Charge

If Zagroth moves at least 20 feet directly toward its target without turning during the same turn, its next melee hit deals an additional 1d12 piercing damage per 10 feet moved, capped at the summoner’s proficiency bonus in d12s. These dice disappear after the attack, hit or miss.

A Huge or smaller target must succeed on a Strength save against the summoner’s CE save DC or be knocked prone or pushed 20 feet, summoner’s choice.

3. Tiger Phase

Once per round, Zagroth can move through creatures or objects as difficult terrain. It takes 2d4 force damage if it ends its turn inside an object.

Once per turn, if it emerges from a creature’s space, shadow, or solid object before attacking, its next hit deals an additional 1d8 slashing damage.

4. Serpent Lash

Zagroth’s melee attacks have 15-foot reach.

Once per turn when Zagroth hits a Huge or smaller creature, that creature must succeed on a Strength save against the summoner’s CE save DC or become grappled. A prone target is restrained for the duration of the grapple instead. The escape DC equals the summoner’s CE save DC, and Zagroth can hold only one creature at a time.

5. Hell Savannah Pounce

Once per summon, as an action, Zagroth moves up to its speed without provoking opportunity attacks and may phase through one creature or solid obstacle during that movement. It then makes one melee attack with advantage.

On a hit, the attack gains Tiger Phase and Predator Core damage. It also gains Oxbreaker Charge dice based on the final uninterrupted straight-line distance Zagroth moved directly toward the target.

The target then makes one Strength save against the summoner’s CE save DC. On a failure, it is knocked prone, grappled, and restrained until the end of Zagroth’s next turn. On a success, it is pushed 10 feet and is not grappled.

Chimera Shadow: The Last Standing Guardian of the Forest — Hageshi

Base: Max Elephant

Additions: Round Deer + Nue + Rabbit Escape

Type: Advanced freeform Chimerafication preset

Role: Bulwark / interceptor / endurance wall / counter-charge defender

Summon Cost: 22 CE (10 + 7 + 3 + 2)

Maximum HP: Max Elephant’s maximum HP + one-quarter of each donor’s maximum HP

Duration: 1 minute

Restriction: While Hageshi is active, Max Elephant, Round Deer, Nue, and Rabbit Escape cannot be summoned or used separately.

Defeat: If Hageshi reaches 0 HP, all four component shadows disperse until the summoner finishes a long rest.

Theme

Hageshi is the summoner’s “wall” chimera.

It is designed to:

  • absorb frontal pressure,
  • remain stable under impossible force,
  • self-correct and self-heal,
  • hold space for the summoner and their allies,
  • and punish anything that tries to break through it head-on.

It is not the technique’s best offensive chimera.

It is one of its strongest defensive answers.

Lore

Hageshi resembles a walking forest boundary: elephantine legs rooted like ancient trunks, antlers branching through a canopy of storm feathers, and pale rabbits continually scattering from and reforming its hide. Its arrival marks the point where the technique stops asking how to win and asks only what must still be standing afterward.

1. Guardian Frame

When summoned, Hageshi gains 25 temporary HP.

It has advantage on checks and saves made to resist being moved or knocked prone, and it counts as one size larger when determining whether it can be grappled, shoved, or physically overrun.

2. Round Deer Core

At the start of each of Hageshi’s turns, it regains HP equal to twice the summoner’s proficiency bonus.

Once per round, when an ally starts its turn within 10 feet of Hageshi, that ally regains HP equal to the summoner’s proficiency bonus. This feature cannot restore a creature above half its maximum HP.

3. Rabbit Redistribution

Once per round, when Hageshi is hit, it can use its reaction before damage is rolled to partially disperse its structure and choose one benefit:

  • gain +4 AC against the triggering attack, potentially causing it to miss, or
  • gain resistance to the triggering attack’s damage.

After the attack resolves, Hageshi may move up to 10 feet without provoking opportunity attacks. The resistance does not stack with resistance from another source.

4. Elephant Bulwark

As a bonus action, Hageshi roots itself and chooses a facing until the start of its next turn.

While rooted:

  • its speed is 0, and it cannot be forcibly moved or knocked prone,
  • it has resistance to damage from attacks and effects originating in its frontal half,
  • allies directly behind it have three-quarters cover,
  • and when a creature moves at least 20 feet toward Hageshi and enters a space within 5 feet of it, that creature must make a Strength save against the summoner’s CE save DC. On a failure, it takes 4d6 bludgeoning damage and its speed becomes 0 for the turn; on a success, it takes half damage and can continue moving. A creature can trigger this save only once per turn.

5. Storm-Tusk Intercept

When a hostile creature Hageshi can see within 30 feet moves toward the summoner or one of the summoner’s allies, Hageshi can use its reaction to move up to half its speed without provoking opportunity attacks and make one Gore attack against that creature.

On a hit, the attack deals an additional 2d6 lightning damage, and the target must succeed on a Strength save against the summoner’s CE save DC or be pushed 10 feet and have its speed reduced to 0 for the rest of the turn.

Storm-Tusk Intercept and Rabbit Redistribution compete for the same reaction.

6. Last Standing Wall

Once per summon, as an action, Hageshi establishes a 20-foot-wide guard line across spaces it occupies or can reach and roots itself until the start of its next turn.

Until then:

  • the line counts as blocked by Hageshi’s pressure,
  • allies behind the line have three-quarters cover,
  • a hostile creature attempting to cross the line must make a Strength save against the summoner’s CE save DC,
  • on a failure, its speed becomes 0 and it takes 4d6 bludgeoning or piercing damage,
  • and on a success, it takes no damage but treats the line as difficult terrain.

A creature makes this save only once during this use, even if it attempts to cross the line more than once.
