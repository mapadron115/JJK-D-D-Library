Ten Shadows — Technique Chassis

Core manifestation rules

These rules apply to every tamed shadow unless a stat block explicitly overrides them.

Manifest

Call from shadow

As an action, spend the listed CE and manifest a tamed shikigami in an unoccupied space within 5 feet of you. It remains until reduced to 0 HP, dismissed without an action, or you die.

Initiative

Act as one

A shikigami shares your initiative and takes its turn immediately after yours. It moves and uses its reaction independently.

Command

One manifestation

As a bonus action, command one manifestation to take an action. Every uncommanded manifestation Dodges. A standing order guides behavior but never removes this recurring command cost.

Range

Telepathic bond

You can communicate with your shikigami within 120 feet. Beyond that range it follows its last instruction and prioritizes returning to you.

Scaling

Your cursed energy

A shadow uses the higher of its printed attack bonus or your CE attack bonus, and the higher of its printed save DC or your CE save DC. Its attacks count as cursed attacks.

Incapacitated

Instinct takes over

If you are incapacitated, each manifested shikigami may act normally without being commanded, protecting you according to its nature and last order.

Manifestation limit: You may normally maintain one manifestation at 1st level, two at 3rd level, and three at 11th level. The Divine Dogs together count as one manifestation and share one action and reaction. All active Gama likewise count collectively as one manifestation and share one action and reaction.

Mahoraga consumes your entire normal manifestation capacity. A fusion counts as one manifestation, although every component remains unavailable while merged.

Technique progression

The roster is earned through victory, not an artificial level gate. From level 1, you may invoke the taming ritual for any untamed shadow on the roster—including Mahoraga—if you can pay that ritual’s listed CE cost. The creature itself is the gate.

The taming ritual

The full roster is challengeable from level 1. There is no level prerequisite for a taming ritual; the listed CE cost, the no-outside-aid rule, and the creature waiting within the ritual are the gates.

Beginning the trial

Invoke the untamed

  • There is no level prerequisite. Use an action and pay the shikigami's listed CE cost.
  • It appears at full HP in an unoccupied space within 30 feet.
  • Roll initiative normally. It treats you as its primary enemy.
  • Your already-tamed shikigami are part of your technique and may participate.
Victory and interference

Win alone or earn nothing

  • Reducing the untamed shadow to 0 HP without outside aid permanently tames it.
  • If another creature directly attacks, heals, restrains, buffs, or otherwise assists, the taming is invalidated.
  • An interfering creature becomes a ritual participant and a valid target.
  • The ritual continues until the shikigami or every ritual participant is defeated.
  • CE is spent even if the ritual fails.

Mahoraga: The Eight-Handled Sword Divergent Sila Divine General uses its complete CR 25 stat block during and after taming. No diminished player version exists. Anyone capable of defeating it alone has earned the full measure of the Ten Shadows Technique.

Eight-Handled Sword Divergent Sila Divine General Mahoraga

Armor Class 22. Hit Points 400 + 5PB. Legendary Resistance · 3/day.

Mahoraga retains its complete adaptation wheel, Sword of Extermination, regeneration, full attack suite, legendary actions, and CR 25 taming profile. It consumes the user’s normal manifestation capacity and is never replaced with a reduced player-safe version.

Shadow Wearing

At level 8, wear the function of one available tamed shikigami. The chosen shadow cannot be manifested while worn. Shadow Wearing lasts for up to your Constitution modifier in turns unless a specific form ends sooner.

Well’s Unknown Abyss — 15 Stable Recipes

At level 11, combine two manifested shadows through one of fifteen predetermined recipes. The fusion counts as one manifestation, shares the usual command economy, and both ingredients are unavailable until the fusion ends.

Chimerafication

Choose one base shikigami and add up to three tamed shadows. The base remains intact. Each added shadow contributes one selected trait or ability and one-quarter of its maximum HP. The resulting chimera counts as one manifestation.

Chimera Shadow: Wildebeast of Hell’s Savannah — Zagroth

Components: Divine Dog Totality + Piercing Ox + Mourn Tiger + Great Serpent. Summon Cost: 11 CE. Duration: 1 minute.

Zagroth combines Predator Core, Oxbreaker Charge, Tiger Phase, Serpent Lash, and Hell Savannah Pounce into an ultimate execution-striker package.

Chimera Shadow: The Last Standing Guardian of the Forest — Hageshi

Components: Max Elephant + Round Deer + Nue + Rabbit Escape. Summon Cost: 9 CE. Duration: 1 minute.

Hageshi is the defensive counterpart: Guardian Frame, Round Deer Core, Rabbit Redistribution, Elephant Bulwark, Storm-Tusk Intercept, and Last Standing Wall make it the roster’s dedicated battlefield bulwark.
