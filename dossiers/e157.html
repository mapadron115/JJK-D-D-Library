Corrosive Threshold

Infect surfaces and prey with Corrosion, turn those marks into doors, then drag sufficiently degraded targets into a private hunting space where the geometry belongs to you.

RoleTerrain controller / abducting duelistSourceSCP Wiki

Core Engine — Corrosion

  • Corrosion Die: d6 → d8 → d10 → d12.
  • Corrosive Touch. Action or replacement for one attack, 1 CE, melee CE attack. Hit: 1 Corrosion Die + CE modifier necrotic damage and 1 Corrosion Mark. Damage increases to 2 dice at level 6, 3 at 11, and 4 at 18. A creature may hold up to 3 Marks for 1 minute.
  • Predigestion. Each Corrosion Mark reduces the creature’s speed by 5 feet and grants you +1 on Athletics checks to grapple or shove it, maximum +3. This does not reduce AC.
  • Surface Corrosion. As part of touching a solid unattended surface, spend 1 CE to place a Surface Mark on a 5-foot section. Maintain up to PB + CE modifier Surface Marks for 10 minutes. Corrosive Touch deals maximum damage to mundane unattended objects.
  • Threshold Walk. Bonus action, 1 CE: enter a Surface Mark within 5 feet and emerge from another Surface Mark within 30 feet. This is teleportation; you leave black residue but do not damage creatures merely by passing through.

Domain Expansion — House Beneath Every Surface

Expansion

Full-turn action, 20 CE, enclosed 175-foot-radius Domain for 1 minute. Its halls and rooms overlap the battlefield; every solid surface counts as one of your Surface Marks.

Predigestion Tide — sole sure-hit

At initiative count 20, every chosen hostile creature gains 1 Corrosion Mark and is pulled up to 10 feet toward the nearest solid surface. If it already had 3 Marks, it instead becomes restrained by sinking residue until the next initiative count 20. A protected creature makes a Strength save and avoids both effects on success.

Every wall is a mouth

Threshold Walk may begin and end at any solid surface in the Domain and costs no CE once on each of your turns. Carrying a willing creature costs 1 CE.

Pocket-space supremacy

Rotting World costs 3 CE inside the Domain. A target still receives its normal save; the Domain does not automatically abduct it.

Technique Feats

Corrosive Threshold

Black Mucus

A Surface Mark creates a 5-foot patch of difficult terrain for enemies. A creature that falls prone in it cannot stand without spending an additional 5 feet of movement.

Predatory Emergence

Predator Beneath

After Threshold Walk, you may remain partially submerged until the start of your next turn. You have half cover but your speed is 0 and you cannot make opportunity attacks while submerged.

Corrosive Touch, level 6

Liquefying Grip

When you apply the third Corrosion Mark to a creature, it cannot take reactions until the end of its next turn and you may attempt to grapple it as part of the same attack.

Corrosive Threshold, level 11

Recall Protocol

Once per short rest when damage would reduce you below one-quarter HP, use your reaction and 2 CE to teleport to any Surface Mark within 120 feet after the damage resolves.

Containment Counterplay

  • Sudden intense light: when an effect comparable to daylight or a blinding flash catches you while emerging from a surface, you must succeed on a Constitution save against the source’s DC or be forced to the nearest legal space and unable to Threshold Walk until the start of your next turn.
  • Lead or liquid layers: a Surface Mark separated from you by a substantial lead-lined layer or continuous body of liquid costs +2 CE to use as a Threshold Walk destination.
  • Complex layered structures: the GM may rule that several disconnected layered materials count as multiple thresholds rather than one. This is counterplay, not permanent immunity to the technique.
