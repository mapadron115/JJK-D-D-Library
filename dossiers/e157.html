Corrosive Threshold

Infect surfaces and prey with Corrosion, turn those marks into doors, then drag sufficiently degraded targets into a private hunting space where the geometry belongs to you.

Corrosion Die

d6 → d8 → d10 → d12.

Corrosive Touch.

Action or replacement for one attack, 1 CE, melee CE attack. Hit: 1 Corrosion Die + CE modifier necrotic damage and 1 Corrosion Mark. A creature may hold up to 3 Marks for 1 minute.

Predigestion.

Each Corrosion Mark reduces the creature’s speed by 5 feet and grants you +1 on Athletics checks to grapple or shove it, maximum +3. This does not reduce AC.

Surface Corrosion.

As part of touching a solid unattended surface, spend 1 CE to place a Surface Mark on a 5-foot section. Maintain up to PB + CE modifier Surface Marks for 10 minutes. Corrosive Touch deals maximum damage to mundane unattended objects.

Threshold Walk.

Bonus action, 1 CE: enter a Surface Mark within 5 feet and emerge from another Surface Mark within 30 feet. This is teleportation; you leave black residue but do not damage creatures merely by passing through.

3Predatory Emergence

Once per turn, the first melee hit you land before the end of the turn after Threshold Walk deals +2 Corrosion Dice. Threshold Walk range becomes 60 feet.

6Dragging Hand

Corrosive Touch damage increases to 2 dice. When you are grappling a creature with at least 2 Corrosion Marks, bonus action, 3 CE: it makes a Charisma save. On failure, you and it vanish into the pocket space until the start of your next turn, then return adjacent to any Surface Mark within 60 feet. No other creature can enter this short abduction.

11Rotting World

Corrosive Touch damage increases to 3 dice. Action, 6 CE, Technique Focus: a creature with 3 Corrosion Marks within 10 feet makes a Charisma save. On failure, you and it enter your pocket dimension for up to 1 minute. At the end of each of its turns, it may repeat the save, escaping on success. You may use a bonus action each turn to create one 10-foot wall, pit, bridge, or patch of difficult terrain there. Damage and attacks resolve normally.

18House Beneath Every Surface

Corrosive Touch damage increases to 4 dice. Threshold Walk costs 0 CE once per turn, range becomes 120 feet, and you gain the Domain below.

Pinnacle expression

Domain Expansion — House Beneath Every Surface

Expansion Full-turn action, 20 CE, enclosed 60-foot-radius Domain for 1 minute. Its halls and rooms overlap the battlefield; every solid surface counts as one of your Surface Marks.

Predigestion Tide — sole sure-hit At initiative count 20, every chosen hostile creature gains 1 Corrosion Mark and is pulled up to 10 feet toward the nearest solid surface. If it already had 3 Marks, it instead becomes restrained by sinking residue until the next initiative count 20. A protected creature makes a Strength save and avoids both effects on success.

Every wall is a mouth Threshold Walk may begin and end at any solid surface in the Domain and costs no CE once on each of your turns. Carrying a willing creature costs 1 CE.

Pocket-space supremacy Rotting World costs 3 CE inside the Domain. A target still receives its normal save; the Domain does not automatically abduct it.

Clash bookkeeping 800 clash points (Tier IV). In a Domain clash, a 100+ point advantage collapses the weaker Domain; collapse deals no damage. Barrier: AC 12 + PB; HP = 10 × max(1, your CE modifier) + 1,600. Burnout: 5 rounds.

When you apply the third Corrosion Mark to a creature, it cannot take reactions until the end of its next turn and you may attempt to grapple it as part of the same attack.

Once per short rest when damage would reduce you below one-quarter HP, use your reaction and 2 CE to teleport to any Surface Mark within 120 feet after the damage resolves.

Rules integration

Containment Counterplay

Sudden intense light: when an effect comparable to daylight or a blinding flash catches you while emerging from a surface, you must succeed on a Constitution save against the source’s DC or be forced to the nearest legal space and unable to Threshold Walk until the start of your next turn.
Lead or liquid layers: a Surface Mark separated from you by a substantial lead-lined layer or continuous body of liquid costs +2 CE to use as a Threshold Walk destination.
Complex layered structures: the GM may rule that several disconnected layered materials count as multiple thresholds rather than one. This is counterplay, not permanent immunity to the technique.
