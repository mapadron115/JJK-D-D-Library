Corrosive Threshold

Infect surfaces and prey with Corrosion, turn those marks into doors, then drag sufficiently degraded targets into a private hunting space where the geometry belongs to you.

Combat roleTerrain controller / abducting duelist

Research log. Subject: the doorway that digests. The subject was found in a basement where the walls had begun to lean toward it, politely, like plants toward light. Two truths govern this file. First: the Mark is the mouth, each one saps speed and feeds the subject's grip, and at three the quarry is held; the detonation that follows is only the swallowing. Second: the building is the body, every surface a potential door, every wall a mouth, and the Domain below a stomach the subject can enter at will and no one else can follow into. We have stopped leaning against walls in this wing. The walls have noticed our politeness and, we suspect, approved of it.

Full progression

Progression

1Corrosion / Slough / Threshold Walk

Corrosive Touch.

The preparation begins at the point of contact: fixed necrotic damage and one Corrosion Mark, the prey, softened. Action or attack replacement, 1 CE, melee CE attack. Hit: 1d8 + CE modifier necrotic damage (fixed) and 1 Corrosion Mark. A creature may hold up to 3 Marks for 1 minute.

Predigestion.

Mark analysis: each one saps five feet of speed and feeds the subject's grip, at three, the quarry is held. The subject does not chase. It seasons. Each Corrosion Mark reduces the creature’s speed by 5 feet and grants you +1 on Athletics checks to grapple or shove it, maximum +3. This does not reduce AC.

Surface Corrosion.

Environmental finding: every surface may carry a five-foot Mark, the network of doors growing with the subject's mastery. The room is no longer a room. It is a menu. As part of touching a solid unattended surface, spend 1 CE to place a Surface Mark on a 5-foot section. Maintain up to PB + CE modifier Surface Marks for 10 minutes. Corrosive Touch deals maximum damage to mundane unattended objects.

Threshold Walk.

The doors open: a bonus action, one coin of CE, and he is somewhere else, teleportation through his own residue. Bonus action, 1 CE: enter a Surface Mark within 5 feet and emerge from another Surface Mark within 30 feet. This is teleportation; you leave black residue but do not damage creatures merely by passing through.

Slough — the detonation.

Detonation event: two or more Marks within thirty feet may be triggered, failure burns necrotic per Mark and consumes them; three Marks deepen the odds against the prey. The subject prefers to let the seasoning finish before the swallowing. Bonus action, no CE: choose a creature within 30 feet bearing at least 2 Corrosion Marks. It makes a Constitution save; on failure it takes 5 necrotic damage × the Marks it bears and the Marks are consumed; on success it takes half damage and the Marks remain. If it bears 3 Marks, it makes the save with disadvantage.

3Predatory Emergence

Arrival weaponized: the first melee hit after the Walk carries additional necrotic dice, and the Walk now reaches sixty feet. It exits the wall already swinging. We have moved the observation post back another ten feet. Once per turn, the first melee hit you land before the end of the turn after Threshold Walk deals +2d8 necrotic. Threshold Walk range becomes 60 feet.

6Dragging Hand

Bonus action · 3 CE · When grappling a creature with at least 2 Corrosion Marks: it makes a Charisma save; on failure, you and it vanish into the pocket space until the start of your next turn, then return adjacent to any Surface Mark within 60 ft · No other creature can enter.

Abduction event: grappling the twice-marked, the subject spends three energy and drags the quarry into the pocket until its next turn, no other creature may follow. Where it takes them, we cannot observe. The subject returns alone, and does not explain itself. When you are grappling a creature with at least 2 Corrosion Marks, bonus action, 3 CE: it makes a Charisma save. On failure, you and it vanish into the pocket space until the start of your next turn, then return adjacent to any Surface Mark within 60 feet. No other creature can enter this short abduction.

11Rotting World

Action · 6 CE · Technique Focus · A creature with 3 Corrosion Marks within 10 ft: Charisma save or enter your pocket dimension with you for up to 1 minute (it repeats the save at the end of each of its turns, escaping on success) · Your bonus action each turn creates one 10-ft wall, pit, bridge, or patch of difficult terrain there · Damage and attacks resolve normally.

The long abduction: six CE, the thrice-marked, and a full minute in the pocket, where he authors a wall, a pit, a bridge per turn. Action, 6 CE, Technique Focus: a creature with 3 Corrosion Marks within 10 feet makes a Charisma save. On failure, you and it enter your pocket dimension for up to 1 minute. At the end of each of its turns, it may repeat the save, escaping on success. You may use a bonus action each turn to create one 10-foot wall, pit, bridge, or patch of difficult terrain there. Damage and attacks resolve normally.

18House Beneath Every Surface

Passive · Threshold Walk costs 0 CE once per turn; range becomes 120 ft · You gain the Domain below.

The Walk costs nothing once per turn and reaches a hundred and twenty feet, and he gains the House below. Threshold Walk costs 0 CE once per turn, range becomes 120 feet, and you gain the Domain below.

Pinnacle expression

Domain Expansion — House Beneath Every Surface

Research log. The interior resembles a residential structure, but every wall, floor, and ceiling is coated in a slow-moving residue that was not present at entry. The residue is warm. The rooms are larger than the building's exterior dimensions allow. The house is digesting. We are inside the house.

ExpansionDomain event: for one minute, every solid surface is the subject's Mark, at each twenty-count, every hostile gains a Mark and is pulled toward the walls, and the thrice-marked are restrained by the sinking residue. The room is digesting. We are inside the room. These two facts are related. Full-turn action, 20 CE, enclosed 60-foot-radius Domain for 1 minute. Its halls and rooms overlap the battlefield; every solid surface counts as one of your Surface Marks.

Predigestion Tide, sole sure-hit At initiative count 20, every chosen hostile creature gains 1 Corrosion Mark and is pulled up to 10 feet toward the nearest solid surface. If it already had 3 Marks, it instead becomes restrained by sinking residue until the next initiative count 20. A protected creature makes a Strength save and avoids both effects on success.

Every wall is a mouth Threshold Walk may begin and end at any solid surface in the Domain and costs no CE once on each of your turns. Carrying a willing creature costs 1 CE.

Pocket-space supremacy Rotting World costs 3 CE inside the Domain. A target still receives its normal save; the Domain does not automatically abduct it.

Clash bookkeeping 800 clash points (Tier IV). In a Domain clash, a 100+ point advantage collapses the weaker Domain; collapse deals no damage. Barrier: AC 12 + PB; HP = 10 × max(1, your CE modifier) + 1,600. Burnout: 5 rounds.

Black Mucus

Terrain finding: the Marks leave difficult ground, the fallen in it pay extra to stand. Even the floor is on the subject's side. We have filed a complaint with the floor. A Surface Mark creates a 5-foot patch of difficult terrain for enemies. A creature that falls prone in it cannot stand without spending an additional 5 feet of movement.

Predator Beneath

Submersion option: after the Walk the subject may remain half-submerged, half cover for the price of stillness. It waits inside the wall like a crocodile. We have stopped using the word 'wall' as if it meant safety. After Threshold Walk, you may remain partially submerged until the start of your next turn. You have half cover but your speed is 0 and you cannot make opportunity attacks while submerged.

Corrosive Touch, level 6

Liquefying Grip

Third-Mark effect: no reactions until the subject's next turn, and the grapple attempted in the same motion. The seasoning, the seizing, and the swallowing, all in one gesture. Efficient. We hate how efficient. When you apply the third Corrosion Mark to a creature, it cannot take reactions until the end of its next turn and you may attempt to grapple it as part of the same attack.

Corrosive Threshold, level 11

Recall Protocol

Once per short rest, the quartered threshold keeps him: two CE and the reaction, and he is at any Mark within a hundred and twenty feet. Once per short rest when damage would reduce you below one-quarter HP, use your reaction and 2 CE to teleport to any Surface Mark within 120 feet after the damage resolves.

Rules integration

Containment Counterplay

Sudden intense light: when an effect comparable to daylight or a blinding flash catches you while emerging from a surface, you must succeed on a Constitution save against the source’s DC or be forced to the nearest legal space and unable to Threshold Walk until the start of your next turn.
Lead or liquid layers: a Surface Mark separated from you by a substantial lead-lined layer or continuous body of liquid costs +2 CE to use as a Threshold Walk destination.
Complex layered structures: the GM may rule that several disconnected layered materials count as multiple thresholds rather than one. This is counterplay, not permanent immunity to the technique.
