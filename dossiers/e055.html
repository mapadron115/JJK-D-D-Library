JAJANKEN
Core law

What the technique actually does.

Rules are written to plug into the finalized campaign glossary: normal actions stay normal, direct attacks roll, and only named Domain events bypass the initial roll.

Bonus action · 1 CE

Charge Beat

Begin or continue the chant and gain one Charge Beat, up to the current cap. Until you release Jajanken or your next turn ends, AC -1 per stored Beat (maximum -3) because aura is concentrated away from defense.

Melee release

Rock

Replace one unarmed attack. On hit add one Jajanken Die per stored Beat, then clear all Beats. Rock uses Strength or your normal unarmed attack ability.

Ranged release

Paper

Ranged CE attack, 90 feet. On hit deal half the Rock bonus dice (round up) as force plus CE modifier. Paper is safer but intentionally less efficient.

Blade release

Scissors

Melee CE attack with 15-foot reach. On hit deal 75% of Rock’s bonus dice (round up) as slashing/force and ignore resistance to nonmagical slashing.

Maximum: Jajanken Triad

Action · 10 CE with at least 3 Beats: resolve Rock, Scissors, and Paper in any order against legal targets, using the same stored Beat count for each. Clear Beats after all three. Each attack rolls separately and can critical.

No Domain

Jajanken’s conditions are personal risk and declaration. It has no innate Domain.

Vow Seed

You understand how to stake future potential. The level-20 vow becomes available only after a major emotional/narrative threshold chosen with the GM.

20All the Power I’ll Ever HaveForbidden Vow: Future for Now and 2 more features

Forbidden Vow: Future for Now

Full-turn action. Declare that you sacrifice your future access to Jajanken and CE after this battle. For 1 minute: Strength, Dexterity, Constitution become at least 24; speed doubles; Jajanken Die becomes 2d12; Charge Beats are gained 2 at a time; you gain one restricted extra action for movement or one attack. When it ends, your CE maximum becomes 0 and this CT is lost until a narrative miracle explicitly restores it.

Adult Rock

During the vow, Rock can spend all Beats and 10 CE once to deal double its Jajanken bonus dice. This is a named pinnacle and cannot be Maximum Output.

No Cheap Contract

The sacrifice must be real. A campaign cannot pre-arrange automatic restoration merely to bypass the vow.

Domain, Maximum, or equivalent.

Not every technique needs a Domain. When one exists, its sure-hit is a unique autonomous initiative-count-20 event unless the Domain is explicitly rule-based.

No natural Domain

Jajanken is powered by telegraphing, risk, and commitment. Its ultimate expression is the Future for Now Binding Vow rather than a barrier sure-hit.

Rules integration

Edge cases and shared systems.

These rulings are designed around the revised Player Guide rather than the raw PDF where the two differ.

Charge Beats are technique state, not CE, and vanish when suppressed.
Rock/Scissors/Paper direct attack dice are part of the attack and double on criticals.
The Future for Now vow is intentionally campaign-altering; the guide does not assume restoration.
Paper and Scissors remain weaker than Rock by design, but their positional advantages are real.
Technique DC: 8 + proficiency bonus + your chosen Cursed Energy ability modifier (Wisdom or Charisma unless your class says otherwise).
Direct criticals: rolled weapon, unarmed, and CE attacks can critical normally. After a qualifying critical, roll the campaign's unmodified Black Flash confirmation d20; a natural 20 triples the completed critical damage. Autonomous Domain events, damage-over-time, hazards, and unrolled pulses cannot Black Flash.
Maximum Output: unless a feature says otherwise, double that feature's base CE cost and enhance one supported axis—damage, range, duration, or accuracy/DC—using the campaign's Maximum Output rules. Named Maximum Techniques and Domains do not receive generic Maximum Output.
Technique Focus: effects marked Focus occupy the shared Technique Focus slot. Opening a Domain ends other Focus effects unless the Domain expressly preserves them.
Two-technique characters: these guides assume each technique progresses from character/class level as agreed by the campaign. Multiclassing itself does not grant another Innate Technique.
