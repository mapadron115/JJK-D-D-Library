Jajanken

Gon Freecss's honest game — aura, a declared choice, and a fist that means it. Rock is a charging punch that grows the longer you dare to stand still and glowing; Paper is the same conviction thrown across the room as projected aura; Scissors is aura transmuted to a blade's edge. You always say which one is coming, out loud. That's the whole trick: they know, and it doesn't help.

Feature map

Level-by-level features

L1

Rock, Charging Fist

The core of the game — Enhancer aura packed into one honest fist. As an action, plant your feet and charge: gain 1 charge (maximum 3). You must declare "Jajanken… Rock!" aloud to begin charging. While you hold a charge, your speed is halved and attacks against you have advantage — you're standing still, glowing, telling everyone exactly what's coming.

Release. As part of the Attack action, make an unarmed strike. On a hit, it deals 2d12 bludgeoning damage, plus 2d12 for each charge held. The charges are spent. If you go 1 minute without charging or releasing, the charge fizzles. Simple. Devastating. Announced.

L3

Paper

Emission — the same conviction, thrown across the room. As an action, spend 1 CE and declare "Paper!": make a ranged cursed-energy attack (range 60 ft.), a flat palm of projected aura. On a hit: 3d8 bludgeoning damage. Paper needs no charge and no stillness — it's the answer for the enemy who won't come to you. The emission curves in flight: ignore half cover. Rock is honest. Paper is honest at a distance.

L6

Scissors

Transmutation — aura honed to an edge. As part of the Attack action, spend 1 CE and declare "Scissors!": your fingers become a blade of transmuted aura. Make a melee unarmed strike dealing 3d8 slashing damage. The blade is precise where Rock is heavy — it scores, it doesn't crush. Three choices. No bluffs.

L11

Held Gambit

You've learned to hold the punch, not just throw it. While holding a Rock charge, you may release it as a reaction when a creature enters your reach or hits you with a melee attack: resolve the Rock strike against that creature immediately. The charge is spent. A declared fist is also a loaded trap.

L15

Crater

The charge cap for Rock rises to 6, and a fully honest punch shakes the ground with it. When you release Rock with 4 or more charges, the shockwave spreads: each creature within 10 feet of the target (other than you) must succeed on a Strength saving throw against your CE save DC or take damage equal to half the Rock's total and be knocked prone. On a success: no damage, no prone. The fist lands once. The ground lands twice.

L20

Everything I Have

Once per long rest, as a bonus action: for 1 minute, the charge takes no time — charging Rock becomes a bonus action instead of an action, Paper and Scissors cost no CE, and holding a charge no longer halves your speed or grants advantage to attackers. When the minute ends, your CE pool empties to 0 and you cannot spend or regain CE until you finish a long rest. Gon gave everything for one fight, once. The technique remembers the price.

Pinnacle expression

Maximum and Domain.

Capstone material

Maximum: All-In Rock

The ultimate expression of Rock: everything, one fist, announced. As an action, spend 8 CE and declare your target aloud: your arm completes its final rotation in an instant — you are treated as holding 6 charges. Make an unarmed strike with advantage. On a hit: 12d12 bludgeoning damage, and the target must succeed on a Strength saving throw against your CE save DC or be pushed 30 feet and knocked prone. Structures and objects take double damage. You cannot whisper a Maximum.

Domain Expansion: The Honest Game

18th level. Sure-hit domain — the sure-hit is the declaration made inescapable. Activation: full turn, 20 CE (D008 baseline for a normal Domain). Clash: 800 points; burnout 5 rounds; duration 2 min (Tier III mapping); radius 120 ft.

Every declaration lands. The domain is a child's game with no way out. While it stands, your Jajanken strikes cannot miss — Rock, Paper, and Scissors resolve as though every attack roll succeeded — and Rock charges twice as fast (2 charges per action). Every creature inside hears your declaration, regardless of distance or noise. There is no sneaking a punch in the Honest Game.

Feats

Five feats, declared aloud.

Jajanken's feats are new applications of the three choices — each is optional, and you must meet its listed prerequisite before taking it.

Prerequisite: Rock, Charging Fist

Ground Slam

You may release a Rock charge downward instead of at a creature: each creature within 15 feet of you must succeed on a Strength saving throw against your CE save DC or take the Rock's damage and be knocked prone (half damage on a success, no prone). The charge is spent. Sometimes the honest target is the floor.

Prerequisite: Paper

Signal Paper

You may fire Paper straight into the sky as a signal instead of an attack: a palm of aura visible for miles in daylight, farther at night, shaping one short word if you form it. It deals no damage. Gon never needed subtlety.

Prerequisite: Scissors

Sunder

When you strike with Scissors, you may forgo damage to cut instead: destroy one nonmagical object the target holds or wears — a rope, a weapon, a strap, a mask. Against bindings or restraints worn by someone else, this works provided you can reach them.

Prerequisite: Rock, Charging Fist

Aftershock

When your released Rock hits, you may spend 1 CE to drive the shockwave through: choose one creature directly behind the target within 15 feet — it must succeed on a Dexterity saving throw against your CE save DC or take half the Rock's damage. The punch doesn't stop at the first body.

Prerequisite: Paper

Paper Mine

As an action, spend 1 CE to press a Paper charge into an object you touch: the next creature to pick up or handle the object triggers the blast (Dexterity saving throw against your CE save DC; 3d8 bludgeoning on a failure, half on a success). The mine lasts 1 hour or until triggered. Even an honest game has traps.

Rules integration

Edge cases and shared systems.

Campaign canon notes

Cursed Energy baseline: CE pool is based on class; CE ability is Charisma or Wisdom (player's choice). CE save DC = 8 + CE ability modifier + proficiency bonus. Regain all spent CE on a long rest.
The declaration is mandatory: Jajanken requires a spoken declaration — "Rock!", "Paper!", or "Scissors!" — to charge or release. While silenced or unable to speak, you cannot charge or release. Enemies always know which form is coming; that is the point.
Charging economy: each action spent charging grants 1 charge (maximum 3; 6 from L15). While holding a charge, your speed is halved and attacks against you have advantage. Charges fizzle after 1 minute without charging or releasing. Rock's release is part of the Attack action.
Paper and Scissors are attacks: Paper is a ranged cursed-energy attack (magical); Scissors is a melee unarmed strike dealing slashing damage (magical). All three forms count as magical for overcoming resistances.
Domain activation defaults: rendered as full turn + 20 CE per the D008 normal-Domain baseline, with Tier III clash stats (800 / 5-round burnout / 2 min) and a 120-ft radius.
Maximum vs. Reversal: Jajanken's pinnacle is written as a Maximum Technique (All-In Rock), not a Reversal; it occupies the technique's pinnacle slot.
