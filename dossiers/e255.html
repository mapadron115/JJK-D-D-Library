Core law

At-the-table character reference

Recovered printed values & campaign clarifications

Limb recreation is temporary:

Mantle of Courage's recreation of a lost limb is a temporary functional replacement for the duration, not true regeneration; it ends with Ignition.

Free action reading:

"Free action" is read as the campaign's free action (no action cost, once per turn).

Severe burns is a rider:

"Severe burns" has no condition definition beyond its printed effect (additional fire damage equal to your proficiency bonus from any fire source until a long rest); run it as a rider, not a formal condition.

Feat references:

The technique's feats reference these features by name: Ferocious Courage → Ignition; Blazing Heart → Mantle of Courage; Soulfire → Eternal Flames.

Domain framing:

Fire of the Heart resolves at initiative 20. Domain clashes use the deterministic 1–1,000 point system; barrier per D007; burnout per D010.

Field notes. Subject: the kept flame. Three truths govern him. First, the engine is conviction: the fire is not cast, it is carried — Ignition spends up to his CE modifier on the weapon, a bonus action, one minute, d6 per coin that climbs to d12 by the eighteenth. Second, the doctrine: the flame is a hearth that follows him — the Mantle of Courage, three CE, a ten-foot aura of fire and cold resistance, and enemies that enter it save or burn. Third, the absolution: the Domain, Fire of the Heart — full-turn, twenty CE, sixty feet — puts every weapon at maximum Ignition with the guaranteed hit, and at initiative 20 deals the Mantle’s damage to all creatures of his choice. The heart, allowed to be the loudest thing in the room. Maxim: courage is not the absence of fear. It is a blade that refuses to go cold.

Full progression

Feature map

1Ignition.

The foundation: a bonus action, up to his CE modifier in coin, one minute — the weapon ignited, a d6 of fire per CE spent, climbing to d12 by the eighteenth. The blade is merely the wick; conviction is the fuel. Bonus action while holding a melee weapon: spend up to your CE modifier (min of 1) to ignite the weapon for 1 minute, dealing 1d6 fire damage on a hit per ce spent. The weapon emits bright light for 5 feet and dim light for another 10 feet. The die rises to d8 at 6th, d10 at 11th, d12 at 18th.

3Mantle of Courage.

At the third, the Mantle of Courage: three CE, a free action, a ten-foot flame aura for a minute — fire and cold resistance, and enemies that enter or begin their turn inside save or burn. The hearth, worn like a banner. Ignore the weapon's detriments or penalties, or apply Ignition to unarmed strikes (potentially recreating a lost limb for the duration). While Ignition is active, spend 3 CE as a free action on your turn for a 10-foot flame aura (1 minute) granting fire and cold resistance; enemies entering or starting their turn there make a Constitution saving throw for Ignition damage, or half on success.

6Eternal Flames.

At the sixth, Eternal Flames: with three CE on Ignition, the blade reaches ten feet further, ignores fire resistance, and even a miss by two still burns half. Mark this: courage that misses is still courage. With at least 3 ce on Ignition: weapon range +10 feet, ignore fire resistance, and a miss by 2 or less still deals half Ignition damage (3 or less at 11th, 4 or less at 18th).

11Maximum: Conflagration.

The Maximum: Conflagration. Ten CE, an action, a thirty-foot cone — a Constitution save against a wall of flame. The hearth, briefly, becomes a wildfire. At 11th level, while your technique may be simple, that does not mean it isn't powerful. While holding a melee weapon you can spend 10 ce as an action to swing your weapon, creating a 30 feet cone of intense flame and heat. All creatures within range must make a Constitution saving throw, taking 10d12 fire damage and being knocked prone on a failed save or half as much on a successful save. If a creature fails by 5 or more, they gain severe burns and take additional fire damage equal to your proficiency bonus from any source of fire damage until they finish a long rest.

18Domain Expansion: Fire of the Heart.

The pinnacle: Fire of the Heart. Full-turn, twenty CE, sixty feet — every weapon at maximum Ignition with the guaranteed hit, and at initiative 20, all creatures of his choice suffer the Mantle’s damage. The heart, allowed to be the loudest thing in the room. Deploying the Domain costs a full-turn action and 20 CE (invented): 60-foot radius, up to 1 minute, 300 clash points (Tier II) (invented). Finally at 18th level you have learned to expand your domain. Any melee weapon you are holding is treated as having the maximum cursed energy used for Ignition and is imbued with your domain's guaranteed hit. At initiative 20, all creatures of your choice within your domain suffer the damage of your Mantle of Courage.

Innate Technique Feats

Build-defining options.

These are optional character-building feats. You must meet each listed prerequisite before selecting it.

Prerequisite: Must have Ignition

Ferocious Courage

When the blade lands, one CE lets the flames leap to another creature within fifteen feet — a Dexterity save against half his Ignition damage. Courage is contagious; so is fire. When you hit a creature with your ignited weapon, you may spend 1 CE to let the flames leap to another creature within 15 feet of the target. That creature must make a Dexterity saving throw against your CE save DC, taking half your Ignition damage on a failure and no damage on a success (invented).

Prerequisite: Must have Mantle of Courage

Blazing Heart

The Mantle may be draped over one willing ally within thirty feet instead of himself — the aura, the resistances, all of it. The hearth, shared. When you activate Mantle of Courage, you may drape its flames over one willing ally within 30 feet instead of yourself. That ally gains the aura, resistances, and other benefits of the Mantle for the duration.

Prerequisite: Must have Eternal Flames

Soulfire

Three CE, and the fire burns even the soul: all fire damage that turn ignores immunity and reduces maximum hit points by the fire dealt. What the flame takes, the body does not get back. You have learned to burn even the soul. When you hit with a melee attack, you can spend 3 CE to cause all fire damage dealt on your turn to ignore immunity to fire damage and reduce a creature's maximum hit points by the fire damage dealt (invented) until they complete a long rest.

Prerequisite: Must have Maximum: Conflagration

Technique Release

End Ignition early on a hit to discharge the remaining flame — every creature within ten feet of the target but himself saves or burns. The kept flame, spent all at once — the last lesson of courage is that some fires are meant to be used up. When you hit with an attack while Ignition is active, you may end Ignition early to discharge its remaining flame. Each creature within 10 feet of the target, other than you, must make a Dexterity saving throw against your CE save DC, taking your Ignition damage on a failure or half as much on a success.

Rules integration

Edge cases and shared systems.

Rules priority: the reviewed rulings, explicit codex clarifications, and the level progression always control when original wording conflicts. Legacy Domain auto-hit language, action terminology, event timing, missing saves, and older scaling never override revised player rules.
Why Tier II: a weapon-striker package with scaling fire dice, a resistance-granting aura, fire-immunity piercing, and max-HP reduction — potent and self-contained, but single-target and melee-bound.
Soulfire: its immunity bypass and max-HP reduction are kept as printed (explicit source text, once-per-turn CE cost); the max-HP reduction is removed by a long rest, not by RCT, per the printed wording.
Ignition die scaling: the per-ce fire die is d6 at 1st, d8 at 6th, d10 at 11th, d12 at 18th; Mantle of Courage and Fire of the Heart deal "your Ignition damage" at its current die.
Appendix

Additional material.

Blazing Courage
Through your beliefs, you set both your weapon and heart alight with
your blazing courage.

Cursed Energy
Starting at 1st level, you can harness your negative feelings into a
usable source of energy, called cursed energy. These feelings can be
harnessed into attacks or abilities. The number of cursed energy you
possess is based on your class, some classes use a modifier that
being charisma or wisdom (Player's choice)
Your cursed energy DC is 8 + user cursed energy ability modifier +
proficiency bonus.

Ignition
Starting at 1st level, your technique allows yourself to ignite your
weapon in flames, increasing your fire power. You can spend up to
your CE modifier (min of 1) as a bonus action while holding a melee
weapon, igniting said weapon in flames for 1 minute. For the
duration, the weapon deals 1d6 fire damage on a hit per ce spent, and
emits bright light for 5 feet and dim light for another 10 feet.
This increases to 1d8 per ce spent at 6th level and again to 1d10 at
11th level and 1d12 at 18th level.

Mantle of Courage
Starting at 3rd level, you can strengthen both yourself and your
weapon. When you use Ignition, you ignore any detriments or penalties
of the weapon or can use it on yourself for your unarmed strikes,
potentially recreating a lost limb for the duration.
While Ignition is active, you can spend 3 CE as a free action on your
turn. You emit an aura of flames within 10 feet of you for 1 minute,
giving you resistance to fire and cold damage. An enemy that enters
your mantle or starts their turn there must make a Constitution
saving throw, taking your Ignition damage on a failed save or half as
much on a successful save.

Eternal Flames
At 6th level, when you spend at least 3 ce on Ignition, you can
increase the weapon's range by 10 feet, ignore resistance to fire
damage and when you miss with a weapon attack by 2 or less, you deal
half your Ignition damage.
This increases to 3 or less at 11th level and 4 or less at 18th
level.

Maximum: Conflagration
At 11th level, while your technique may be simple, that does not
mean it isn't powerful. While holding a melee weapon you can spend 10
ce as an action to swing your weapon, creating a 30 feet cone of
intense flame and heat. All creatures within range must make a
Constitution saving throw, taking 10d12 fire damage and being knocked
prone on a failed save or half as much on a successful save.
If a creature fails by 5 or more, they gain severe burns and take
additional fire damage equal to your proficiency bonus from any
source of fire damage until they finish a long rest.

Domain Expansion: Fire of the Heart
Finally at 18th level you have learned to expand your domain. Any
melee weapon you are holding is treated as having the maximum cursed
energy used for Ignition and is imbued with your domain's guaranteed
hit. At initiative 20, all creatures of your choice within your
domain suffer the damage of your Mantle of Courage.
