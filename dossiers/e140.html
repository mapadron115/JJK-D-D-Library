Core law

What the technique actually does.

Core Engine, Oil. Oil is her autobiography in progress: ten counters at most, minted when she takes a new damage type, survives a condition for the first time that combat, or spends two energy to self-modify. At thresholds she chooses Evolutions, permanent for the day. Infection of the unwilling is temporary and combat-scoped. The body is a draft; the oil is the editor. Oil counters max 10 on yourself. Each of the following grants 1 Oil, and multiple can pay out in the same turn: taking a new damage type (once per combat per type); a condition on you ending (once per combat per condition); spending 2 CE to self-modify (bonus action, once per turn). At thresholds choose permanent-for-day Evolutions. Infection of unwilling creatures is temporary and combat-scoped.

Field notes. Subject: a patient revising their own manuscript in oil. Three truths govern them. First, the wound is a design note: Oil accrues when they take a new damage type, survive a condition for the first time that combat, or spend two energy to self-modify, cursed energy that stopped repairing the body and started editing it. Second, the draft is revised daily: at thresholds they choose Evolutions, permanent for the day, plating, limbs, wings, resistances, the flesh rewritten in the image of a thought the owner never dared finish. Third, it completes, it does not corrupt: infection of the unwilling is temporary and combat-scoped, and the horror the scholars name is precisely this, it does not ruin its user. It finishes them.

Full progression

Level 1 through 20.

1Glistening Oil

Attack option · natural weapon 1d8 · on a hit spend 1 CE: target Con save or gains 1 Infection (max 3); each Infection gives -5 speed.

The first coat. The manuscript's first annotation. Infection on an unwilling creature ends when combat ends. Spend 1 Oil (no action, once per turn): your natural weapon deals +1d8 damage until the end of the turn.

3Adapt

Passive · at Oil 3 choose one adaptation: +1 AC, climb 30, darkvision/sensors, natural weapon +1 damage die once per turn, or resistance to the last damage type until your next long rest.

The first revision. The draft improves where it was criticized.

6Proliferate

Action · 2 CE · choose PB creatures or objects within 30 ft already bearing your counter/mark · increase each by 1, respecting caps.

The spread. Good edits propagate.

9Compleat Limb

When you first reach Oil 5 (no action), manifest a modular limb (choose one; you may change the choice when you gain Oil): blade (your natural weapon becomes 2d8); cannon (action, ranged CE attack, 60 ft, 3d8 force); shield (reaction when you are attacked, before the hit is resolved: +2 AC against that attack); injector (bonus action, advantage on grapple checks for 1 minute).

The modular flesh. The body, given attachments.

11Perfected Form

Passive · choose a second Adaptation; the resistance adaptation can be maintained alongside another · at Infection 3 the victim also has disadvantage on Con Focus checks.

The second edit. The manuscript grows footnotes.

13Praetor Evolution

Passive · at Oil 7 choose one major evolution: wings 60, regeneration PB HP per turn, +10 ft reach, magic resistance (advantage on saves vs CT), or two modular limbs at once.

The major rewrite. The draft becomes a second edition.

15Corrupted Conversion

Action · 5 CE · one target at Infection 3 · Cha save: on failure Charmed until the end of your next turn and may be ordered one basic action · Infection then resets to 0.

The pressed convert. The annotation becomes an author.

18Domain, New Phyrexia

Initiative 20 · Proliferate all your counters once for free · then each hostile with Infection Con save: 5d10 necrotic damage per Infection on failure, capped 10d10.

At the twentieth count the clinic opens. The whole ward, treated at once.

20All Will Be One

Passive · Oil maximum 15 · maintain three normal Adaptations plus one Praetor Evolution · once per long rest, when you would die: become a 1-HP cocoon and reform next turn with 50 HP if the cocoon survives.

The final draft. The manuscript refuses its last page.

Ten energy at Oil ten, as a bonus action: for a minute every hit may apply Infection without energy, once per turn; at initiative ten each round she automatically Proliferates one chosen counter across her proficiency in targets, and she grows Large with two more armor. The editor, at full staff. for 1 minute, every hit can apply Infection without CE once/turn; at initiative 10 each round automatically Proliferate one chosen counter on PB targets. You become Large and gain +2 AC.

Toxic

Her oil hangs in the air around her: past Oil five she exudes a miasma, and a creature starting its turn within five feet saves its Constitution or gains one Infection, three at most. The manuscript has an atmosphere. Your oil hangs in the air around you: once you reach Oil 5, you exude a miasma. A creature that starts its turn within 5 feet of you must make a Constitution save or gain 1 Infection (maximum 3).

Viral Drake

Her Infection spreads like a virus: when she Proliferates, one creature bearing her Infection may leap the oil to another within ten feet, a failed Constitution save, one Infection gained. The footnote cites another footnote. Your Infection spreads like a virus: when you Proliferate, choose one creature bearing your Infection. One creature within 10 feet of it must make a Constitution save or gain 1 Infection as the oil leaps hosts.

Steel Thane

Her compleated plating can lock rigid: as a reaction to bludgeoning, piercing, or slashing damage she gains resistance to that instance, but her speed becomes zero until her next turn as the plating seizes. Armor has opinions about mobility. Your compleated plating can lock rigid: as a reaction when you take bludgeoning, piercing, or slashing damage, you gain resistance to that instance of damage, but your speed becomes 0 until the start of your next turn as the plating seizes.

Gitaxian Probe

A hair-thin probe of oil toward a creature within sixty feet: as a bonus action for one energy she learns its resistances, immunities, vulnerabilities, and how hurt it is, unharmed, bloodied, or critical. Near-invisible, leaving no Infection: observation, not corruption. Once probed, her Adapt may copy its resistance profile untested. The editor reads before revising. You extend a hair-thin probe of glistening oil toward one creature you can see within 60 feet: as a bonus action for 1 CE, you learn its damage resistances, immunities, and vulnerabilities, plus how hurt it is, unharmed, bloodied, or critical. The probe is near-invisible (Wisdom (Perception) vs. your technique DC to notice) and leaves no Infection; it is observation, not corruption. Once you have probed a creature, your Adapt feature may copy its resistance profile even if you have not yet taken that damage type.

Rules integration

Edge cases and shared systems.

Shared rulings: Technique DC = 8 + PB + CE ability modifier. Direct rolled attacks may crit and use the campaign Black Flash procedure when eligible. Tokens, autonomous summons, passive damage, and initiative-20 Domain effects cannot Black Flash. Maximum Output modifies ordinary supported techniques, never named Maxima or Domains.
