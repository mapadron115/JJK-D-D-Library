Core law

What the technique actually does.

Core Engine — Oil. Oil counters max 10 on yourself. Gain 1 when you take a new damage type, survive a condition for first time that combat, or spend 2 CE to self-modify. At thresholds choose permanent-for-day Evolutions. Infection of unwilling creatures is temporary and combat-scoped.

The fiction. Jujutsu scholars classify Compleation as the most heretical reinforcement theory on record — cursed energy that stopped repairing the body and started *editing* it, treating every new wound as a design note. The prevailing theory holds the oil is CE too dense and too ambitious to stay liquid: it coats, it adapts, it proliferates, and each threshold crossed is the sorcerer's flesh rewritten in the image of a thought the owner never dared finish. It does not corrupt its user; it completes them — and that is the horror.

Full progression

Level 1 through 20.

L1Glistening Oil

Natural weapon 1d8. On hit 1 CE, Con save or target gains 1 Infection (max 3); each gives -5 speed.

L3Adapt

At Oil 3 choose one: +1 AC, climb 30, darkvision/sensors, natural weapon +1 die once/turn, or resistance to last damage type until rest.

L6Proliferate

2 CE: choose PB creatures/objects within 30 ft already bearing your counter/mark; increase each by 1, respecting caps.

L9Compleat Limb

Oil 5: manifest modular limb—blade, cannon 60 ft 3d8, shield reaction +2 AC, or injector granting grapple advantage.

L11Perfected Form

Choose second Adaptation; resistance adaptation can be maintained alongside another. Infection 3 also imposes disadvantage on Con Focus checks.

L13Praetor Evolution

Oil 7 choose one major evolution: wings 60, regeneration PB HP/turn, reach +10, magic resistance (advantage saves vs CT), or two modular limbs.

L15Corrupted Conversion

5 CE, target at Infection 3 makes Cha save; failure becomes Charmed until end next turn and can be ordered one basic action. Infection then resets to 0.

L18Domain — New Phyrexia

Initiative 20 Proliferate all your counters once for free, then each hostile with Infection makes Con save; failure takes 5d10 necrotic/metal damage per Infection, capped 10d10.

L20All Will Be One

Oil max 15; maintain three normal Adaptations + one Praetor Evolution. Once/long rest when you would die, become a 1-HP cocoon and reform next turn with 50 HP if cocoon survives.

Pinnacle expression

Maximum, Domain, or equivalent.

Maximum — March of the Machine (10 CE, Oil 10): for 1 minute, every hit can apply Infection without CE once/turn; at initiative 10 each round automatically Proliferate one chosen counter on PB targets. You become Large and gain +2 AC.

Feats

Technique Feats

Toxic

Your oil hangs in the air around you: once you reach Oil 5, you exude a miasma. A creature that starts its turn within 5 feet of you must make a Constitution save or gain 1 Infection (maximum 3).

Viral Drake

Your Infection spreads like a virus: when you Proliferate, choose one creature bearing your Infection. One creature within 10 feet of it must make a Constitution save or gain 1 Infection as the oil leaps hosts.

Steel Thane

Your compleated plating can lock rigid: as a reaction when you take bludgeoning, piercing, or slashing damage, you gain resistance to that instance of damage, but your speed becomes 0 until the start of your next turn as the plating seizes.

Gitaxian Probe

You extend a hair-thin probe of glistening oil toward one creature you can see within 60 feet: as a bonus action for 1 CE, you learn its damage resistances, immunities, and vulnerabilities, plus how hurt it is — unharmed, bloodied, or critical. The probe is near-invisible (Wisdom (Perception) vs. your technique DC to notice) and leaves no Infection; it is observation, not corruption. Once you have probed a creature, your Adapt feature may copy its resistance profile even if you have not yet taken that damage type.

Rules integration

Edge cases and shared systems.

Shared rulings: Technique DC = 8 + PB + CE ability modifier. Direct rolled attacks may crit and use the campaign Black Flash procedure when eligible. Tokens, autonomous summons, passive damage, and initiative-20 Domain effects cannot Black Flash. Maximum Output modifies ordinary supported techniques, never named Maxima or Domains.
