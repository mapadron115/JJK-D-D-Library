Core law

What the technique actually does.

Core Engine, Oil. Oil is her autobiography in progress: ten counters at most, minted when she takes a new damage type, survives a condition for the first time that combat, or spends two energy to self-modify. At thresholds she chooses Evolutions, permanent for the day. Infection of the unwilling is temporary and combat-scoped. The body is a draft; the oil is the editor. Oil counters max 10 on yourself. Gain 1 when you take a new damage type, survive a condition for first time that combat, or spend 2 CE to self-modify. At thresholds choose permanent-for-day Evolutions. Infection of unwilling creatures is temporary and combat-scoped.

Field notes. Subject: a patient revising their own manuscript in oil. Three truths govern them. First, the wound is a design note: Oil accrues when they take a new damage type, survive a condition for the first time that combat, or spend two energy to self-modify, cursed energy that stopped repairing the body and started editing it. Second, the draft is revised daily: at thresholds they choose Evolutions, permanent for the day, plating, limbs, wings, resistances, the flesh rewritten in the image of a thought the owner never dared finish. Third, it completes, it does not corrupt: infection of the unwilling is temporary and combat-scoped, and the horror the scholars name is precisely this, it does not ruin its user. It finishes them.

Toxic

Her oil hangs in the air around her: past Oil five she exudes a miasma, and a creature starting its turn within five feet saves its Constitution or gains one Infection, three at most. The manuscript has an atmosphere. Your oil hangs in the air around you: once you reach Oil 5, you exude a miasma. A creature that starts its turn within 5 feet of you must make a Constitution save or gain 1 Infection (maximum 3).

Viral Drake

Her Infection spreads like a virus: when she Proliferates, one creature bearing her Infection may leap the oil to another within ten feet, a failed Constitution save, one Infection gained. The footnote cites another footnote. Your Infection spreads like a virus: when you Proliferate, choose one creature bearing your Infection. One creature within 10 feet of it must make a Constitution save or gain 1 Infection as the oil leaps hosts.

Her compleated plating can lock rigid: as a reaction to bludgeoning, piercing, or slashing damage she gains resistance to that instance, but her speed becomes zero until her next turn as the plating seizes. Armor has opinions about mobility. Your compleated plating can lock rigid: as a reaction when you take bludgeoning, piercing, or slashing damage, you gain resistance to that instance of damage, but your speed becomes 0 until the start of your next turn as the plating seizes.

Gitaxian Probe

A hair-thin probe of oil toward a creature within sixty feet: as a bonus action for one energy she learns its resistances, immunities, vulnerabilities, and how hurt it is, unharmed, bloodied, or critical. Near-invisible, leaving no Infection: observation, not corruption. Once probed, her Adapt may copy its resistance profile untested. The editor reads before revising. You extend a hair-thin probe of glistening oil toward one creature you can see within 60 feet: as a bonus action for 1 CE, you learn its damage resistances, immunities, and vulnerabilities, plus how hurt it is, unharmed, bloodied, or critical. The probe is near-invisible (Wisdom (Perception) vs. your technique DC to notice) and leaves no Infection; it is observation, not corruption. Once you have probed a creature, your Adapt feature may copy its resistance profile even if you have not yet taken that damage type.

Rules integration

Edge cases and shared systems.

Shared rulings: Technique DC = 8 + PB + CE ability modifier. Direct rolled attacks may crit and use the campaign Black Flash procedure when eligible. Tokens, autonomous summons, passive damage, and initiative-20 Domain effects cannot Black Flash. Maximum Output modifies ordinary supported techniques, never named Maxima or Domains.
