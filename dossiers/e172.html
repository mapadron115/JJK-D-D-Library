Visitors at the Window

Core law

Character fantasy

Call an almost-indestructible nuisance swarm that steals objects, clings to structures, breaks lights, rattles defenders, and keeps coming back.

Combat loop

  1. Manifest the Visitor Swarm around a contested structure or target.
  2. Spend commands on nuisance effects rather than raw damage.
  3. Force enemies to waste actions batting them away or securing equipment.
  4. Escalate the siege until the entire battlefield becomes a haunted house perimeter.

Core law

Visitor Swarm

Manifest:

action, 2 CE, Focus, 1 minute. The swarm occupies a 10-foot space within 30 feet, AC 15, HP = 5 × level, climb 30, hover 20. It is a single manifestation and never gains separate turns.

Movement:

at the end of your turn move it up to 30 feet without an action. It may occupy walls, ceilings, roofs, and creature spaces but cannot end inside a creature.

Command:

once per turn as a bonus action choose one Swarm Trick. At 11th level choose two different tricks instead.

Full progression

Level progression

1Window Visitors

Swarm Tricks: Rattle: one creature within swarm space makes Wisdom save or cannot take reactions until start of your next turn. Grab: contest your CE ability vs target Athletics/Acrobatics; on win one non-heavy held object is pulled 10 feet and dropped. Cling: one creature’s speed −10 until it leaves swarm space. Pellet: ranged CE attack from swarm, 30 feet, 1 Visitor Die bludgeoning.

3Night Siege

When swarm shares a space with a door, window, light source, vehicle, turret, or unattended object, command it to Sabotage: relevant mundane object becomes unusable until a creature spends an action repairing/clearing it; cursed/magical objects instead make a durability/CE check determined by the GM and are suppressed for only one round on failure.

6They Came Back

When swarm reaches 0 HP, it disperses instead of dying. At the start of your next turn spend 2 CE to reform it with half HP in a space within 30 feet of you. Once per short rest, reform for 0 CE. Pellet becomes 2 Visitor Dice.

11Every Window

Choose two tricks per bonus action and swarm space becomes 15 feet. Creatures inside have disadvantage on ranged attacks made through the swarm. When Grab succeeds, you may hand the item to yourself or an ally within 15 feet of the swarm instead of dropping it.

18Maximum: Siege at Kelly-Hopkinsville

Action, 8 CE, Focus, 1 minute. The swarm becomes a 30-foot-radius Visitor Perimeter centered on a point within 60 feet. It has no HP and cannot be destroyed, but moving 60 feet from the center ends it. At the end of each of your turns choose up to PB Tricks, each against a different target/object in the Perimeter. Pellet deals 3 Visitor Dice. Once per round when an enemy spends an action solely removing, repairing, securing, or identifying a Visitor effect, one ally you choose gains advantage on its next attack or check before your next turn.

Feats

Build-defining options.

Shiny Eyes

The swarm sheds dim light 10 feet when you choose and can see invisible creatures within its space.

Roof Scratcher

A creature starting its turn directly beneath the swarm on a ceiling makes Wisdom save or has disadvantage on its first attack that turn.

Tiny Thieves

Grab may target a worn pouch, keyring, magazine, potion, phone, talisman, or similarly small accessible object, but never armor, an attuned item, or an object physically integrated into the target.

No Bodies Found

When the swarm disperses, it leaves no corpse or permanent material — and it may complete one last delivery: it deposits one object it is holding in an unoccupied space within 60 feet of its position before vanishing.

Rules integration

Building the character

Support Specialist or Strategist. This is deliberately a low-damage technique with excellent action-economy harassment, perfect for defending houses, breaking enemy emplacements, and making ranged formations miserable.
Technique die: Visitor Die follows the shared d6 → d8 → d10 → d12 progression.
Rules integration

Counterplay

Area damage, leaving the swarm’s position, redundant equipment, and simply accepting some nuisance effects instead of wasting actions on them.
