Adaptive Hatred

The art in one sentence: survive it once, and decide whether it may ever hurt you twice. Survive something once, record what hurt you, and spend limited Adaptation Slots deciding which threats your body is allowed to stop caring about.

Combat roleAdaptive tank / bruiser

Research log. Subject: spite with a perfect memory. The subject has been injured 214 times under observation and has never once been injured the same way twice, it keeps a ledger, and the ledger is written in scar tissue. Two truths govern this file. First: the pain is the data, every blow is recorded by exact damage type, named condition, or recurring hazard, and vagueness is not entered; the body refuses to learn what was not precisely suffered. Second: the ledger is the evolution, recorded Threats become Data, Data becomes Adaptations installed in the flesh, and at sufficient suffering the subject hardens a Tag into immunity and outgrows the encounter entirely. It does not hold grudges. It holds records. The distinction has not comforted anyone on staff.

Progression

1Exposure / Adaptation

Adaptation Die

Every grudge needs its unit of measure. d6 → d8 → d10 → d12.

Threat Tag.

Ledger protocol: the pain must be named exactly, or it is not entered. Vagueness is rejected at intake. The subject's bookkeeping is more rigorous than ours, and we are a research laboratory. A Tag is one exact damage type, one named condition, or one recurring environmental hazard. “Fire damage,” “restrained,” and “vacuum” are valid; “this enemy” and “all cursed techniques” are not.

Exposure Record.

The record is written while the wound is fresh: reaction, one CE, one Data. When you take damage or fail a save that imposes a condition, use your reaction and 1 CE to record one relevant Threat Tag and gain 1 Data in that Tag, maximum 3. You can gain at most 1 Data in the same Tag per round. Data lasts until your next long rest.

Install Adaptation.

Expenditure event: one Data, one Slot, one refusal the flesh will honor. The subject spends its suffering the way other organisms spend energy. The exchange rate is obscene and the subject accepts it gladly. Bonus action, 1 CE, spend 1 Data and fill an Adaptation Slot until you replace it or finish a long rest. At level 1 you have 1 Slot. For a damage Tag, gain resistance to that damage type. For a condition Tag, gain advantage on saves against that condition and reduce any duration measured in rounds by 1, minimum 1. Environmental Tags grant an appropriate survival trait such as a climb/swim speed, water breathing, or tolerance to extreme temperature, subject to GM approval.

Biological Rule.

Localization finding: Adaptations live in the body, not in the technique's focus, the flesh keeps its own counsel. The immunity is biological, not technical. You cannot counterspell scar tissue. We checked, at some expense. Installed Adaptations are internal biology and do not use Technique Focus. You may replace one as a bonus action by paying the normal install cost and Data.

11Hardened Adaptation

Passive · Slots become three · A normal elemental or physical damage Tag becomes immunity; force, psychic, radiant, necrotic, soul, spatial, conceptual, and Domain-derived damage remain resistance and let you reduce one matching damage instance per round by PB Adaptation Dice · A standard condition Tag may become immunity, except unconscious or incapacitated from 0 HP or a Domain's unique rule.

Capacity increase: Slots become three. Working maxim, subject's own: resistance is a policy; immunity is a verdict. It said this to an interviewer, unprompted, while being burned. The interviewer has requested reassignment. A normal elemental/physical damage Tag becomes immunity. Force, psychic, radiant, necrotic, soul, spatial, conceptual, and Domain-derived damage instead remains resistance and lets you reduce one matching damage instance per round by PB Adaptation Dice. A standard condition Tag may become immunity, but never unconscious/incapacitated from 0 HP or a Domain’s unique rule.

Capacity increase: Slots become four. The subject has spoken the technique's true name aloud, at last. Designation on file. It does not fail, once per long rest, lethal damage is refused, the body heals, and a Hardened Adaptation installs itself mid-encounter. We have stopped scheduling lethality trials. Pinnacle expression

Maximum Technique — Abhorrent Evolution

Activation Activation event: two Tags consumed at once, the body eats its own history to outrun the present. It spends its past to survive its now. The economics are desperate and the subject considers them routine. Level 18, bonus action, 10 CE. Consume all stored Data in at least two different Tags. For 1 minute, gain two temporary Adaptation Slots and may immediately install one Basic Adaptation for each consumed Tag without further CE.

Predatory body Morphology event: Large, fast, newly certain, overgrowth wears a predator's outline. The subject is now, dimensionally, the largest thing in the ward. It has begun to act like it knows. Become Large if space permits, gain +10 feet speed, and your unarmed strikes deal an additional Adaptation Die.

Cost of overgrowth Cost analysis: every evolution invoices the body afterward, exhaustion, silence, an empty ledger. The subject pays for its apotheosis in the only currency it respects: vulnerability, briefly, and then it begins saving again. When the Maximum ends, lose every temporary Slot and all Data consumed to activate it. You gain one level of exhaustion and cannot record Data until the end of your next turn.

No Domain required Architectural note: no Domain was raised, because none was needed, survival itself is the pinnacle. The subject's level-18 budget is spent entirely on refusing to die. We find this philosophically complete and tactically exhausting. Adaptive Hatred intentionally uses its level-18 power budget on biological survival and a named Maximum rather than inventing a barrier that the source concept does not need.

Technique Feats

Adaptive Hatred

Lent Loathing

Hatred, it turns out, can be charitable. Once per round, when you take damage from a Threat Tag for which you have an installed Adaptation, you may use your reaction and 1 CE to lend that Adaptation’s benefit to one ally you can see until the start of your next turn: resistance for a damage Tag, or advantage on saves against a condition Tag. Your own Adaptation is unaffected.

Reactive Morphology

Hostile Evolution

Retaliation finding: every blow received becomes a blow returned. The subject does not counterattack. It returns fire with the attacker's own postage. Our insurance carrier has been notified. Once per turn, after a creature damages you with a Tag for which you have an installed Adaptation, you may use your reaction and 1 CE to make one unarmed strike against that creature. On a hit it takes an additional Adaptation Die of damage of the Tag’s damage type, or force damage if the Tag is a condition or environmental hazard.

Adaptive Hatred, level 6

Keter Genome

Acceleration event: the body now outpaces the ledger, installing what it merely remembers. Learning has become reflex. The subject is no longer studying pain; it is fluent in it. Once per round, when you take damage from a Threat Tag for which you hold at least 1 Data but have no installed Adaptation, you may use your reaction and 1 CE to immediately replace one installed Adaptation with an Adaptation to that Tag without spending Data.

Adaptive Hatred, level 11

Consume and Rebuild

Dietary finding: even the flesh may eat to learn. The subject incorporates meaningful quantities of matter during rest. We have stopped asking what it eats. The quartermaster's reports are redacted at the subject's request, which it made politely, in writing. Regain PB Adaptation Dice HP and gain 1 Data for one environmental or material Threat Tag that the consumed matter reasonably demonstrates; GM adjudicates the Tag.

Rules integration

Table Rulings

You must actually experience a threat to record it. Seeing fire, reading about a Domain, or touching a harmless sample does not generate Data.
One enormous attack still provides only 1 Data because Exposure Record is a reaction. Multiattack can generate additional Data only across later rounds.
Hardened immunity is intentionally powerful but expensive in Data and Slots. Exotic categories remain resistance rather than immunity so soul, spatial, conceptual, and Domain threats still matter.
Adaptations disappear on long rest unless a feat says otherwise. This prevents the campaign from turning into a permanent list of every immunity ever encountered.
