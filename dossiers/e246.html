Comedian

Fumihiko Takaba’s technique: reality bends around the user’s idea of comedy, damage becomes a gag, gags become battlefield control, and Stand Up Comedy turns the whole fight into the bit.

Power tierTier IV · Special Grade
Combat roleController / tank

Core law

Completed rules

Confidence:

The engine of the whole technique is written here, and it is a confession: this power runs on conviction alone. Understand the joke and you can kill it, the bit survives only inside a mind that has never doubted the punchline.

A creature that knows how your technique works can make a contested Charisma (Persuasion) or Wisdom (Insight) check as an action to deconstruct your humor against your Charisma (Performance). If they roll higher, your technique deactivates as you lose confidence in your comedy and cannot use your technique. You can make a DC 15 Wisdom saving throw at the end of your turn to regain control of your technique and cause it to reactivate as normal.

Field notes. Subject: a stage that devours the world. Three truths govern Comedian. First, conviction is the whole engine: cursed energy too earnest to distinguish funny from true, and anyone who understands the joke can deconstruct it with a contested check, shutting the technique down until a DC 15 Wisdom save recovers the bit. Second, harm becomes theater: a reaction and 1 CE turn any damaging hit into a gag, the umbrella raised, the peel slipped on, and punchline damage cannot kill, because comedy isn't funny if someone dies. Third, the bit conquers the field: gags hurl, conjure, and punchline; Comedic Foil bars interfering techniques within 300 ft; and the Level 18 Domain turns the fight into a cutaway where the audience boos anyone who breaks the gag. Mark this: it survives only inside a mind that never doubts the punchline.

Full progression

Feature map

1Comedy.

Action · 1 CE · Activate for 1 minute, concentration · Reaction · 1 CE: when damaged, take no damage · Proficiency in Performance (Expertise at 11th).

The technique activates for 1 CE and a concentration, and immediately rewrites the rules of harm: a reaction and 1 CE turn any damaging hit into a gag, the umbrella raised, the peel slipped on. Reality bends; the sorcerer merely suggests the direction.

Beginning at 1st level, reality bends around you to fit your idea of comedy. As an action for 1 cursed energy, you activate your technique for 1 minute while maintaining concentration. When you are damaged, you can use your reaction (invented) to spend 1 cursed energy to take no damage as some funny phenomena allows you to avoid damage (Eg. you open an umbrella to block an attack, you slip on a banana peel to dodge, etc). You also gain proficiency in Performance and at 11th level you gain Expertise.

3Commit to the Bit.

Active-technique gags · Blasting Off: on a melee hit, 1 CE; hurl target up to 60 ft away, prone (scales at 6th/11th/18th) · Props: bonus action, 2 CE; conjure CE-modifier items in a 5-ft cube, last 1 minute (scales) · Powerful Punchline: action, 3 CE; 30-ft sphere within 80 ft / 60-ft line / 30-ft cone; Str, Dex, or Con save; PB + CE modifier d8s bludgeoning, piercing, slashing, or psychic + rider; cannot kill (drops to 1 HP).

The toolbox of the bit: Blasting Off hurls a melee-hit target up to 60 ft and prone, Props conjures whatever the joke needs, and Powerful Punchline deals damage that cannot kill, because comedy isn't funny if someone dies.

At 3rd level, you have learned to use your comedy to supplement your abilities. You gain access to the following gags while your technique is active.

Blasting Off. On a hit with a melee attack, you can spend 1 CE to cause the target to go flying up to 60 ft away from you and fall prone. This distance increases at 6th, 11th, and 18th level.

Props. As a bonus action for 2 CE, you create a number of items equal to your CE modifier that fit in a 5 ft cube that help with your current joke or bit. These cubes increase in size at 6th, 11th, and 18th level. These props last for 1 minute or until your technique deactivates.

Powerful Punchline. As an action for 3 CE, you let loose a good punchline related to a joke you tell or a bit you have going. Choose a range and source of the punchline:

  • 30 ft diameter sphere within 80 ft
  • 60 ft long, 5 ft wide line
  • 30 ft cone

All creatures within range must make a Strength, Dexterity, or Constitution saving throw based on the source of the punchline. On a failed save, a target takes your proficiency bonus + your CE modifier in d8s of bludgeoning, piercing, slashing, or psychic damage and an additional effect depending on the punchline. This damage cannot kill a person since comedy isn’t funny if someone dies. A creature reduced to 0 hit points by this damage is instead left at 1 hit point (invented).

6Comedic Foil.

Passive (while technique active) · Creature starting its turn within 300 ft: CE save or cannot use interfering cursed techniques until its next turn · You may bar creatures from initiative and strip CE-sensing within 300 ft · Blasting Off 90 ft; Props cubes 10 ft.

The audience of one becomes an audience of three hundred: creatures starting their turn within 300 ft must save or lose any technique that would interfere with the joke. The sorcerer may also bar late arrivals from initiative and blind enemies to cursed energy itself, distractions are not tolerated during the act.

At 6th level, a creature that starts their turn within 300 ft of you while your technique is active must make a Cursed Energy saving throw. On a failure, they cannot use any cursed techniques that would interfere with your current joke until their next turn. Additionally, you can choose to not allow other creatures to interfere with your combat while your technique is active. This stops other creatures from rolling for initiative or affecting the battle in any way as some random reason prevents them from joining in. You can also choose to remove a creature’s ability to sense cursed energy while they are within 300 ft of you while your technique is active to stop them from being distracted from your jokes.

Commit to the Bit improvements: Blasting Off now sends the target up to 90 ft away from you; your Props cubes increase to a 10 ft cube.

11Maximum: Peak Comedy.

Action · 10 CE · Comedy at maximum for 1 minute · Once per turn free Gag; free damage negation CE-modifier times per round; double Punchline ranges; 2 CE for +PB damage dice · Action: scene-change teleport of creatures within 100 ft into a cutaway (Wis save vs CE DC resists); targets in the cutaway must fit attacks to the gag · Blasting Off 120 ft; Props 15-ft cubes.

Maximum efficiency, ten minutes of it: free gags once per turn, free damage negation up to your CE modifier per round, doubled Punchline ranges, and a scene-change cutaway that teleports the unwilling. This is the headliner set, everything the bit can do, all at once.

At 11th level, you can activate your technique at maximum efficiency. As an action for 10 cursed energy, you activate Comedy for 1 minute. During this minute you gain the following improvements: once per turn you can use your Gags without spending cursed energy; you can use Comedy’s negation for free a number of times equal to your CE modifier each round; the number of props you can create becomes twice your CE modifier (thrice your CE modifier at 18th level). The range and radius of your Powerful Punchline are doubled and you can spend 2 cursed energy to deal additional damage dice equal to your proficiency bonus; as an action you completely change the scene and scenario into one fitting of the joke you tell, teleporting creatures within 100 ft into a cutaway (unwilling creatures make a Wisdom save vs your CE DC to resist the teleport, invented), and a target in the cutaway must make a Wisdom save or cannot attack unless they make it fit into the cutaway gag.

Commit to the Bit improvements: Blasting Off now sends the target up to 120 ft away from you; your Props cubes increase to a 15 ft cube.

18Domain Expansion: Stand Up Comedy.

Full-turn action · 20 CE · 60-ft radius, up to 1 min, 800 clash (Tier IV) · Initiative-20: force a cutaway · Creatures acting against the gag take 8d8 psychic (audience boos), once per round.

A theater assembles itself from nothing: red curtains, footlights, rows of seats filled with an audience that laughs a half-beat before the joke lands. The spotlight follows the sorcerer like a loyal dog, and the air smells of popcorn and sawdust and greasepaint. Everything is slightly too bright, slightly too loud, and the exits have all become part of the set.

The Domain is the whole act as architecture: the audience boos 8d8 psychic damage on any creature that acts against the gag, and a full-turn joke forces a Charisma contest the losers pay for in the same coin. Inside this theater, the only safe heckle is laughter.

Deploying the Domain costs a full-turn action and 20 CE (invented): 60-foot radius, up to 1 minute, 800 clash points (Tier IV) (invented). At 18th level, you have finally learned to expand your domain. At initiative 20, you can force a cutaway. A creature within your domain cannot attack you unless they can make it fit into the cutaway gag. If a creature does not act according to your jokes and gags, the audience boos them and they take 8d8 psychic damage. A creature can only take this damage once per round.

While within your domain · Blasting Off 200 ft; Props 20-ft cubes; Peak Comedy props at 3× CE modifier · Full Turn Action (Action + Bonus Action + Movement): Charisma (Persuasion) joke; creatures in the domain may roll Wisdom (Insight) to counter; those who don't roll or roll lower take 8d8 psychic.

Commit to the Bit improvements: Blasting Off now sends the target up to 200 ft away from you; your Props cubes increase to a 20 ft cube.

Peak Comedy improvement: the number of props you can create becomes thrice your CE modifier.

As a Full Turn Action (Action + Bonus Action + Movement) while within your domain, you can tell a joke and make a Charisma (Persuasion) check. All creatures within your domain can make a Wisdom (Insight) check to criticize or attack it. A creature that chooses not to roll or rolls lower than you takes 8d8 psychic damage.

Innate Technique Feats

Build-defining options.

These are optional character-building feats. You must meet each listed prerequisite before selecting it.

Prerequisite: Must have Comedy

Straight Man

Every comedian needs a straight man: one willing ally within 30 ft of the target may deliver your Gag through you, at your DC. The bit travels; the bill stays yours.

Choose one willing ally who can see and hear you. That ally may deliver one of your Gags as if they were you, using your CE save DC, though you still pay any CE cost. The ally must be within 30 feet of the Gag’s target.

Prerequisite: Must have Commit to the Bit

Super Gag

Premium material: Written on your Face turns an enemy's intentions into readable doodles, and Costume Switch dresses the battlefield in cosmetic absurdity. None of it is functional, that is precisely the point.

You have stepped up your game with some premium material you read online. You gain the following gags while your technique is active: Written on your Face. As an action for 3 CE, you target a creature within 30 ft that you can see. For 1 minute or when your technique ends, the target’s emotions and intentions are literally written on their face. The target has disadvantage on attacks against anyone who can see their face. Anyone that can see their face knows their general emotions and can tell whenever they are scheming or planning something by reading the doodles on their face. Costume Switch. As an action for 2 CE, you apply a wardrobe change to a number of creatures equal to your CE modifier within 30 ft of you that you can see. This effect is purely cosmetic and cannot change their AC or movement and cannot give them any conditions. You can also change a property about them, such as giving them multiple eyes or arms. These changes are nonfunctional and a creature can take an action to make a Wisdom (Perception) or Intelligence (Investigation) against your CE save DC inspect these changes. On a success, they realize the changes are fake. This feature lasts for a number of minutes equal to your CE modifier before disappearing.

Prerequisite: Must have Peak Comedy

Maximum: Laugh Track

The invisible audience does the work now: your Gags may originate from any point within 30 ft, and no one can tell they came from you without beating your save DC. The whole theater is the ventriloquist's dummy.

While your technique is active, your invisible audience can deliver your Gags for you. A Gag you use may originate from any point within 30 feet of you that you choose; creatures cannot tell the Gag came from you without succeeding on a Wisdom (Perception) check against your CE save DC.

Prerequisite: Must have Comedy

Lasting Laughs

For 2 extra CE, the act runs ten minutes instead of one, a full set rather than a single sketch. Peak Comedy, mark this, is excluded: the headliner set refuses an encore.

Even when you’re the one telling the joke, you can’t stop laughing. For an additional 2 CE when you activate Comedy, you can make the technique last 10 minutes. This does not work with Peak Comedy.
