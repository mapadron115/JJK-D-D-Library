Mobility Controller / Skirmisher

Right of Way

Paint supernatural traffic lanes onto the battlefield. Allies exploit legal movement while enemies accumulate Citations for crossing, rushing, or ignoring your signals.

Power tierTier III · Apex

Field notes. Subject: the surveyor who deputizes the ground. Three truths govern him. First: order is a weapon, Green grants allies speed and safe passage; Yellow and Red tax every voluntary enemy step with Citations. Second: the fine compounds, cashed Citations scale from lost reactions to zero speed to prone with no bonus actions, and three Citations is the maximum the law allows. Third: the grid expands, one Lane becomes two, then three; Intersections punish crossings; the Domain declares a single global Signal, and its automatic Citations are the sure-hit. Assess him correctly: he never strikes you; he merely makes every step you take into evidence against you. Maxim: the road is never neutral; it only pretends to be.

Traffic Lane

Bonus action · 1 CE, 30 ft · Create a 5-ft-wide, 30-ft-long Lane on a surface · Lasts 1 minute · Maintain 1 Lane (2 at 6th, 3 at 11th) · Choose its direction and current Signal: Green, Yellow, or Red.

One bonus action, one coin of CE: five feet wide, thirty long, one minute of law painted onto the ground.

Green Signal

Passive · Willing creatures moving with the Lane’s direction gain +10 ft speed for that movement · Their first 10 ft moved inside the Lane each turn does not provoke opportunity attacks.

The benevolent signal: allies traveling with the current gain speed, and their first steps ignore the enemy's reach.

Yellow Signal

Passive · A hostile that voluntarily moves 10+ ft inside the Lane gains 1 Citation at the end of that movement (max 1 from Yellow per round).

The warning signal: the hostile who rushes through it anyway is fined for the voluntary step.

Red Signal

Passive · The first time each round a hostile enters or crosses a Red Lane, it makes a Dexterity save: failure ends its current movement and gives it 1 Citation; success allows movement normally.

The absolute signal: the first crossing each round demands the save, and failure ends the movement with a Citation.

Cash Citation

Bonus action · 60 ft · Spend one creature’s Citations (a creature holds at most 3): 1 Citation: speed −10 ft until start of your next turn, no reactions · 2 Citations: speed 0 until start of your next turn · 3 Citations: Str save or prone, speed 0 until start of your next turn, no bonus actions · Deals no damage.

The fines, collected: the law takes no blood, only liberty.

3Signal Controller —

Passive · Once per turn, change one Lane’s Signal without using an action · Reaction · No CE · When a hostile gains a Citation: one willing creature in that Lane may move 5 feet.

She is the authority, not the paint: signals change at her word, and every hostile Citation buys one ally five feet.

6Intersection —

Passive · Maintain 2 Lanes (40 ft each) · A hostile crossing a Lane intersection: Dexterity save; on failure it gains 1 Citation and its movement ends.

Two lanes cross, and the crossing becomes a trap.

11Priority Vehicle —

Passive · Maintain 3 Lanes · You and one ally (chosen at the start of your turn) may move through hostile creature spaces as difficult terrain in a Green Lane · Your first attack after moving 20+ ft in a Lane has advantage.

Her chosen vehicles pass through hostile spaces as through traffic, and the first strike after the journey has advantage.

18Domain Expansion: Metropolitan Gridlock —

Domain Expansion · 10 CE · The Domain becomes a luminous road network · At initiative 20, choose Green, Yellow, or Red as the global Signal until the next count 20: every hostile that moved 10+ ft since the prior count gains 1 Citation — this automatic Citation is the sure-hit and deals no damage · Your own Lanes remain available for their normal effects.

Avenue after avenue of stopped traffic, brake lights burning red in the dark like a field of warning lanterns. Steam rises from the asphalt, horns sound from nowhere and everywhere, and the traffic lights cycle through colors no city ever issued. The crosswalks are empty. The city is waiting for someone to jaywalk.

The city at rush hour: one global Signal for all hostiles, and the automatic Citation is the sure-hit — no damage, only the law.

Innate Technique Feats

Technique Feats

Emergency Lane

One lane may be declared an emergency: allies speed along it, and the first hostile each turn is pushed along its direction. As a bonus action, declare one of your Lanes an emergency lane until the start of your next turn: willing creatures traveling along it gain 10 feet of speed, and the first hostile creature each turn that enters it must make a Strength save or be pushed 10 feet along the lane's direction.

Traffic Camera

She sees every vehicle on her roads, speed and Citations known at a glance. You know the current speed and number of Citations of every creature standing in one of your Lanes.

Repeat Offender

The thrice-fined lose the cover of the crowd: her attacks ignore their half cover until the fines are paid. When a creature reaches 3 Citations, your attacks against it ignore half cover until its Citations are spent.

Rules integration

Table Rulings

A Lane is a technique zone, not physical paint. It can follow flat/sloped surfaces but cannot float unsupported before 18th level.
Forced movement never grants Yellow Citations because it is not voluntary. It may still cross Red and trigger the save.
A creature cannot be given more than 3 Citations; excess Citations are lost.
