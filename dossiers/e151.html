Mobility Controller / Skirmisher

Right of Way

Paint supernatural traffic lanes onto the battlefield. Allies exploit legal movement while enemies accumulate Citations for crossing, rushing, or ignoring your signals.

Power tierHigh

Field notes. Subject: the surveyor who deputizes the ground. Three truths govern him. First: order is a weapon — Green grants allies speed and safe passage; Yellow and Red tax every voluntary enemy step with Citations. Second: the fine compounds — cashed Citations scale from lost reactions to zero speed to prone with no bonus actions, and three Citations is the maximum the law allows. Third: the grid expands — one Lane becomes two, then three; Intersections punish crossings; the Domain declares a single global Signal, and its automatic Citations are the sure-hit. Assess him correctly: he never strikes you; he merely makes every step you take into evidence against you. Maxim: the road is never neutral; it only pretends to be.

Main resource

Lanes & Citations

Lanes and Citations: the roads she paints, and the fines she collects.

Traffic Lane

One bonus action, one coin of CE: five feet wide, thirty long, one minute of law painted onto the ground. Bonus Action • 1 CE. Create a 5-foot-wide, 30-foot-long Lane on a surface within 30 feet. It lasts 1 minute. You can maintain one Lane at 1st level. Choose its direction and current Signal: Green, Yellow, or Red.

Green Signal

The benevolent signal: allies traveling with the current gain speed, and their first ten feet ignore the enemy's reach. Willing creatures moving in the Lane’s direction gain +10 feet speed for that movement. The first 10 feet they move inside the Lane each turn does not provoke opportunity attacks.

Yellow Signal

The warning signal: the hostile who rushes through it anyway is fined — one Citation for the voluntary step. A hostile creature that voluntarily moves 10+ feet while inside the Lane gains 1 Citation at the end of that movement. A creature can gain only one Citation from Yellow each round.

Red Signal

The absolute signal: the first crossing each round demands the save — failure ends the movement and issues the Citation. The first time each round a hostile creature enters or crosses a Red Lane, it makes a Dexterity save. Failure ends its current movement and gives it 1 Citation; success allows movement normally.

Cash Citation

The fines, collected: one Citation steals reactions and speed, two steal movement, three steal footing and the bonus action — and the law takes no blood, only liberty. Bonus Action • 60 feet. Spend one creature’s Citations (a creature can hold at most 3 Citations): 1 Citation: its speed is reduced by 10 feet until the start of your next turn and it cannot take reactions; 2 Citations: its speed becomes 0 until the start of your next turn; 3 Citations: it makes a Strength save — on a failure it is knocked prone, its speed becomes 0 until the start of your next turn, and it cannot take bonus actions. Cash Citation deals no damage at any tier.

3Signal Controller —

She is the authority, not the paint: signals change without action, and every hostile Citation buys one ally five feet. once per turn change one Lane’s Signal without using an action. Reaction when a hostile gains a Citation: one willing creature in that Lane may move 5 feet.

6Intersection —

Two lanes cross, and the crossing becomes a trap: the hostile pays the Citation and stops at the corner. maintain 2 Lanes, each up to 40 feet. When a hostile crosses the point where two of your Lanes intersect, it makes a Dexterity save; on a failure it gains 1 Citation and its movement ends.

11Priority Vehicle —

Three lanes, and her chosen vehicles pass through hostile spaces as through traffic — the first strike after twenty feet of travel has advantage. maintain 3 Lanes. You and one ally chosen at the start of your turn may move through hostile creature spaces while in a Green Lane (spaces are difficult terrain). The first attack you make after moving at least 20 feet in a Lane has advantage.

18Domain Expansion: Metropolitan Gridlock —

The city at rush hour: at initiative count twenty, one global Signal for all hostiles, and the automatic Citation is the sure-hit — no damage, only the law. 10 CE. The Domain becomes a luminous road network. At initiative count 20, choose Green, Yellow, or Red as the global Signal until the next count 20. Every hostile creature that moved 10+ feet since the prior count gains 1 Citation; this automatic Citation is the sure-hit and deals no damage. Your own Lanes remain available for their normal effects.

Innate Technique Feats

Technique Feats

Emergency Lane

One lane may be declared an emergency: allies speed along it, and the first hostile each turn is pushed along its direction. As a bonus action, declare one of your Lanes an emergency lane until the start of your next turn: willing creatures traveling along it gain 10 feet of speed, and the first hostile creature each turn that enters it must make a Strength save or be pushed 10 feet along the lane's direction.

Traffic Camera

She sees every vehicle on her roads — speed and Citations known at a glance. You know the current speed and number of Citations of every creature standing in one of your Lanes.

Repeat Offender

The thrice-fined lose the cover of the crowd: her attacks ignore their half cover until the fines are paid. When a creature reaches 3 Citations, your attacks against it ignore half cover until its Citations are spent.

Rules integration

Table Rulings

A Lane is a technique zone, not physical paint. It can follow flat/sloped surfaces but cannot float unsupported before 18th level.
Forced movement never grants Yellow Citations because it is not voluntary. It may still cross Red and trigger the save.
A creature cannot be given more than 3 Citations; excess Citations are lost.
