Core law

What the technique actually does.

Core Engine, Lands. The technique is one long act of annexation. As a bonus action she claims a twenty-foot patch of natural ground within sixty feet as a Land, capped at her proficiency, persisting for the scene. On or beside her Land, her techniques cost less and the earth itself may be woken. The map is drawn; the map is her. As a bonus action attune a 20-ft patch of natural ground within 60 ft as a Land, max PB. Lands persist for the scene. Standing on or adjacent to your Land improves your techniques and lets you animate it.

Field notes. Subject: a surveyor whose map annexed the surveyor. Three truths govern her. First, territory is an extension of the self: as a bonus action she attunes a twenty-foot patch of natural ground as a Land, no more than her proficiency's worth, and standing on or beside it discounts her techniques and lets her wake it. Second, the land answers because it believes it is her: the elementals she raises are not allies but the ground itself, risen. Third, held ground compounds: her claims widen to thirty feet and overlap, her allies quicken upon them, and at the peak she may treat the entire battlefield within a hundred and twenty feet as her Land for a minute. She does not stand on the ground. Consider: she stands in herself.

Rules integration

Edge cases and shared systems.

Shared rulings: Technique DC = 8 + PB + CE ability modifier. Direct rolled attacks may crit and use the campaign Black Flash procedure when eligible. Tokens, autonomous summons, passive damage, and initiative-20 Domain effects cannot Black Flash. Maximum Output modifies ordinary supported techniques, never named Maxima or Domains.
