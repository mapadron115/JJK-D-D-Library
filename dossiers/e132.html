Core law

What the technique actually does.

Core Engine — Lands. As a bonus action attune a 20-ft patch of natural ground within 60 ft as a Land, max PB. Lands persist for the scene. Standing on or adjacent to your Land improves your techniques and lets you animate it.

The fiction. The prevailing theory holds that Worldwaker is an awakening that went sideways: the sorcerer's cursed energy could not distinguish the ground it stood on from a part of its own body, and it has been annexing terrain ever since. Registry scholars describe each Land as a claim staked in trauma — the user's CE marked the earth the way a cornered animal marks territory. The elementals are not summoned allies; they are the land itself, answering because it thinks it is the sorcerer.

Full progression

Level 1 through 20.

L1Leyline

While you stand on one of your Lands, once per turn you may reduce the CE cost of a Worldwaker feature by 1 (minimum 1). You may also shape mundane plants and stone within a 10-ft cube.

L3Awaken Land

For 2 CE, one of your Lands becomes an Elemental for 1 minute (AC 14, HP 10 + 2×your level, slam 2d8 + PB); you may maintain at most one.

L6Vine Snare

For 2 CE, target a 20-ft square touching one of your Lands; creatures in the area make a Strength save or are Restrained until the end of their next turn, and the area becomes difficult terrain.

L9Elemental Bond

You may maintain two Awakened Lands. When one of them hits, you may move 10 ft without provoking opportunity attacks or gain 1d8 temporary hit points.

L11World Shaper

For 3 CE, raise a wall, ramp, or trench from one of your Lands — up to six 5-ft cubes; each structure has AC 15 and 15 HP.

L13Animist's Awakening

For 5 CE, animate up to PB of your Lands for one round; each makes one 3d8 slam as an autonomous coordinated event, then all but your normal active elementals return to terrain.

L15Vastwood

Your Lands become 30-ft patches and may overlap. Allies standing on them gain +10 speed and advantage against forced movement.

L18Domain — Zendikar Risen

At initiative 20, choose QUAKE, GROWTH, or ELEMENTAL: Quake — creatures make a Dexterity save, taking 6d10 damage on a failure and falling prone; Growth — creatures make a Strength save or are Restrained; Elemental — create two 20-HP land spirits until the next initiative 20.

L20Soul of the World

Your Land maximum becomes 10. Once per long rest, for 1 minute, treat the entire battlefield within 120 ft of you as your Land.

Pinnacle expression

Maximum, Domain, or equivalent.

Maximum — Worldwake (10 CE): every Land erupts. Choose up to 6; each creates a 15-ft burst for 5d10 bludgeoning/nature damage, Dex half. A creature can be hit by at most two bursts.

Feats

Technique Feats

Lotus Cobra

when you attune a new Land, you may read its memory: learn what creatures have crossed it within the last hour.

Elvish Guidance

as a bonus action, one ally standing on your Land may move up to 10 ft without provoking opportunity attacks.

Living Terrain

as a bonus action, you can coax your Land to shelter one willing creature standing on it: ranged attacks against that creature have disadvantage until it leaves the Land or the start of your next turn.

Nature's Claim

structures damaged by your land effects become unstable: the ground in a 15-ft radius around them is difficult terrain until repaired.

Rules integration

Edge cases and shared systems.

Shared rulings: Technique DC = 8 + PB + CE ability modifier. Direct rolled attacks may crit and use the campaign Black Flash procedure when eligible. Tokens, autonomous summons, passive damage, and initiative-20 Domain effects cannot Black Flash. Maximum Output modifies ordinary supported techniques, never named Maxima or Domains.
