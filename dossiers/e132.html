Power tierTier IV · Special Grade
Core law

What the technique actually does.

Core Engine — Lands. As a bonus action attune a 20-ft patch of natural ground within 60 ft as a Land, max PB. Lands persist for the scene. Standing on or adjacent to your Land improves your techniques and lets you animate it.

Full progression

Level 1 through 20.

1Leyline

While on a Land, once/turn reduce a Worldwaker feature by 1 CE (minimum 1). Shape mundane plants/stone in 10-ft cube.

3Awaken Land

2 CE: one Land becomes Elemental for 1 minute, AC 14, HP 10+2×level, slam 2d8+PB; max one.

6Vine Snare

2 CE, 20-ft square touching Land, Str save or Restrained until end next turn; area difficult terrain.

9Elemental Bond

Maintain two Awakened Lands. When one hits, move 10 ft without provoking or gain 1d8 temp HP.

11World Shaper

3 CE: raise wall/ramp/trench from Land, up to six 5-ft cubes; structures AC 15, 15 HP each.

13Animist's Awakening

5 CE: animate up to PB Lands for one round; each makes one 3d8 slam as autonomous coordinated event, then all but normal active elementals return to terrain.

15Vastwood

Your Lands become 30-ft patches and may overlap. Allies on them gain +10 speed and advantage vs forced movement.

18Domain — Zendikar Risen

Initiative 20 choose QUAKE, GROWTH, or ELEMENTAL: Quake Dex save 6d10 + prone; Growth Str save Restrained; Elemental creates two 20-HP land spirits until next count 20.

20Soul of the World

Land max 10. Once/long rest treat the entire battlefield within 120 ft as your Land for 1 minute.

Pinnacle expression

Maximum, Domain, or equivalent.

Maximum — Worldwake (10 CE): every Land erupts. Choose up to 6; each creates a 15-ft burst for 5d10 bludgeoning/nature damage, Dex half. A creature can be hit by at most two bursts.

Rules integration

Edge cases and shared systems.

Shared rulings: Technique DC = 8 + PB + CE ability modifier. Direct rolled attacks may crit and use the campaign Black Flash procedure when eligible. Tokens, autonomous summons, passive damage, and initiative-20 Domain effects cannot Black Flash. Maximum Output modifies ordinary supported techniques, never named Maxima or Domains.
