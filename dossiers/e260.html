Core law

At-the-table character reference

Recovered printed values & campaign clarifications

Temporary and copy tools:

Temporary and copy tools cost 2 CE as a reaction and cannot replicate technique features.

Bonus stacking:

2 CE buys +2 to a single saving throw; only one instance per roll, and your CE modifier applies once per roll.

Adaptation costs:

Adaptation costs 1 CE as a reaction and grants resistance (not immunity) at base; a new damage source requires a new spend.

Eight-Handled Sword costs:

The sword costs 10 CE to manifest (2 CE per additional target). It has AC 18 and 50 hit points, and can be dispelled as a free action.

Divine Adaptation costs:

Divine Adaptation costs 5 CE to activate, plus 3 CE to adapt to a condition. Adaptation transfers only on an RCT heal; the same immunity cannot stack.

Zenith costs:

Zenith costs 20 CE to activate, once per long rest, for 1 minute; the 12th-level spell exclusion applies. No Special Grade cost entry is printed — none is invented.

The fiction. Jujutsu scholars classify it as survival given claws — not a copy of Mahoraga but an answer to him, cursed energy that learned the divine general's only lesson: nothing gets to kill you twice the same way. The prevailing theory holds each adaptation is a trauma the CE refused to discard, worn on the body like a scar that fights back; the Zenith is that instinct finished — a minute in which the sorcerer stops being a person and becomes an *ending*. Nothing here is inherited; it is spite, metabolized into biology.

Full progression

Feature map

1Dark Claw.

Bonus action for 1 CE: grow a large black claw for 1 minute. Unarmed strikes deal +1d8 slashing, use your CE modifier for attack and damage, and you gain +2 AC.

3Adaptation.

Reaction for 1 CE when you take damage: gain resistance to that damage type until the end of your next turn (1 minute at 6th; immunity for 1 minute at 11th).

6Eight-Handled Sword.

Action for 10 CE (2 CE per additional target): summon Mahoraga's massive sword for 1 minute — 2d12 slashing, CE modifier for attack and damage, +1 to attack rolls while wielding.

11Divine Adaptation.

Spend 5 CE to activate Divine Adaptation, plus 3 CE per condition adapted, to become immune to one condition (blinded, charmed, deafened, frightened, poisoned, stunned) for 1 minute; spend 2 CE to deal +2d8 of an adapted damage type with unarmed strikes for 1 minute.

Pinnacle expression

Maximum, Domain, or equivalent.

Domain Expansion — Hall of the Five Clawed Dragon: 18 — full-turn action, 20 CE; 60-foot-radius enclosed Domain centered on you; 1 minute; Burnout 10 rounds. While the Domain stands, all of your Claw attacks are guaranteed hits, and you may swap any number of your Claw options at the beginning of your turn. Sure-Hit Event — Hall of the Five Clawed Dragon: at initiative count 20 each round, your claws stab through space at one chosen creature inside — a guaranteed hit that ignores distance and deals critical damage, which cannot trigger Black Flash. A creature protected by an anti-domain defense makes a Dexterity saving throw vs your CE save DC (invented); on a success it takes half damage.

Innate Technique Feats

Build-defining options.

These are optional character-building feats. You must meet each listed prerequisite before selecting it.

Prerequisite: Must have Dark Claw

Wheel of Adaptation

You manifest Mahoraga's wheel above your head, turning with every adaptation you complete. As a bonus action for 2 CE, you can turn the wheel toward one ally you can see within 30 ft, lending them your hard-won resilience — they gain the resistance or immunity of one of your active adaptations until the end of your next turn.

Prerequisite: Must have Adaptation

Mahoraga's Fury

When you are reduced to half your hit points or fewer, Mahoraga's fury surfaces through your claw. As a bonus action for 2 CE, you can slam the Dark Claw into the ground, erupting jagged claws in a 15 ft radius around you. Each creature in the area must make a Dexterity saving throw, taking your unarmed strike damage on a failed save or half as much on a success. Once you use this eruption, you can't use it again until you finish a short rest.

Prerequisite: Must have Eight-Handled Sword

Perfect Adaptation

Your adaptation is now perfect — nothing learned is wasted. As an action for 3 CE, you can discharge one of your adaptations: choose a damage type you have adapted to during this combat and release it in a 30 ft cone. Each creature in the area must make a Dexterity saving throw, taking 1d8 damage of the chosen type for each damage type you have adapted to this combat (maximum 5d8) on a failed save, or half as much on a success. Your adaptation to the discharged type ends.

Prerequisite: Must have Claw

Close Combat Specialist

When you hit a creature with a claw attack while within 5 ft, you can attempt to grapple the creature as a bonus action. While grappling a creature, you can cause the creature to take your claw damage at the start of their turn.

Rules integration

Edge cases and shared systems.

Rules priority: the reviewed rulings, explicit codex clarifications, and the level progression always control when original wording conflicts. Legacy Domain auto-hit language, action terminology, event timing, missing saves, and older scaling never override revised player rules.
Why Tier III: reactive damage-type immunity, condition immunity, a 2d12 CE-scaling sword, and a Domain pinnacle — a premier adaptive striker.
Adaptation transfer: adaptation transfers only on an RCT heal; it does not pass through other healing. The same immunity cannot stack from multiple adaptations.
Temporary tools: temporary and copied tools cannot replicate technique features.
Zenith: 20 CE, once per long rest, 1 minute; the 12th-level spell exclusion applies. No Special Grade cost entry is printed and none is invented.
Appendix

Additional material.

Dark Claw
You can channel the power of the cursed spirit Mahoraga into your body.

Cursed Energy
Starting at 1st level, you can harness your negative feelings into a
usable source of energy, called cursed energy. These feelings can be
harnessed into attacks or abilities. The number of cursed energy you
possess is based on your class, some classes use a modifier that
being charisma or wisdom (Player's choice)
Your cursed energy DC is 8 + user cursed energy ability modifier +
proficiency bonus.

Dark Claw
Starting at 1st level, you can channel the power of the cursed spirit
Mahoraga into your body. As a bonus action, you can spend 1 CE to
grow a large black claw from your hand. This claw lasts for 1 minute.
While the claw is active, your unarmed strikes deal an additional 1d8
slashing damage, and you can use your CE modifier for attack and
damage rolls with your unarmed strikes. Additionally, you gain a +2
bonus to AC as the claw deflects attacks.

Adaptation
Starting at 3rd level, your body begins to adapt to threats. When you
take damage from a source, you can spend 2 CE as a reaction to begin
adapting to that damage type. You gain resistance to that damage type
until the end of your next turn. At 6th level, this resistance lasts
for 1 minute. At 11th level, you gain immunity to that damage type
for 1 minute instead of resistance.

Eight-Handled Sword
At 6th level, you can manifest the Eight-Handled Sword Divergent Sila
Divine General Mahoraga's blade. As an action, you can spend 5 CE to
summon a massive sword for 1 minute. The sword deals 2d12 slashing
damage on a hit, and you can use your CE modifier for attack and
damage rolls. While wielding the sword, you gain a +1 bonus to attack
rolls.

Divine Adaptation
At 11th level, your adaptation becomes divine. When you use
Adaptation, you can spend an additional 3 CE to adapt to a condition
as well as a damage type. Choose one of the following conditions:
blinded, charmed, deafened, frightened, poisoned, stunned. You become
immune to that condition for 1 minute. Additionally, when you adapt to
a damage type, you can spend 2 CE to deal an additional 2d8 damage of
that type with your unarmed strikes for 1 minute.

Zenith
At 18th level, you have reached the zenith of Mahoraga's power. As an
action, you can spend 15 CE to transform for 1 minute. You gain the
following benefits:
- Your unarmed strikes deal an additional 2d10 slashing damage.
- You can use your CE modifier for attack and damage rolls.
- You gain a +3 bonus to AC.
- You gain immunity to two damage types of your choice.
- You gain immunity to two conditions of your choice.
- When you take damage, you can use your reaction to adapt without
spending cursed energy.
