Feature map

Level-by-level features, as the figure refined them.

L1

Claim the Fallen

Passive.

Tier IV · Trophy striker · suggested CE ability STR or CHA (chosen at inheritance; locked thereafter) · Primary verb: Claim. Empires write the histories. The defeated keep the heads. The Bronze Head treats every victory as a transfer, witnessed by Heaven: what falls in her presence becomes hers — and she fights with what she has taken. Trophies. When you reduce a creature to 0 hit points with an attack, or when a creature within 30 ft of you drops a weapon, standard, or focus (destroyed, disarmed, or surrendered), you may claim it as a Trophy (no action required). You can hold a number of Trophies equal to your proficiency bonus. A Trophy is a mote of cursed energy shaped like what was taken; claiming a magic weapon does not grant its properties unless a feature says so. Trophies persist until you dismiss them (no action) or finish a long rest.

When you claim a Trophy, you gain temporary hit points equal to your proficiency bonus + the defeated creature's proficiency bonus (if it had one). (The taking feeds her. It always has.)

L1

The Spoils Fight

Passive.

While you hold at least one Trophy, your melee weapon attacks deal additional force damage equal to the number of Trophies you hold (maximum +6). (She fights with what she has taken, and she has taken so much.)

L3

Wear the Taken

Bonus action · 2 CE · 1 minute (ends if you dismiss your Trophies).

Choose one Trophy you hold. You don its aspect until the effect ends, gaining one benefit based on what was taken (GM's call, chosen at claim): the swiftness of a cavalry standard (+10 ft speed), the steadiness of an archer's bow (advantage on ranged attack rolls), the weight of a champion's blade (+2 to melee damage rolls), or the rank of a commander's seal (advantage on Charisma checks against soldiers). You may only wear one aspect at a time. (She does not imitate the defeated. She outranks them.)

L3

The Kandake's Tithe

Action · 3 CE.

One creature you can see within 30 ft may willingly render you tribute — a weapon, an object, a secret spoken aloud, a promise. If it does, you gain it as a Trophy (even without a defeat), and the giver cannot willingly act against you until the end of your next turn. A creature that declines suffers no effect. (Tribute rendered willingly counts. Samos taught her that a dictated peace is also a conquest.)

L6

The Threshold

Bonus action · 5 CE · 1 minute (concentration).

You set a Trophy beneath your feet — a 15-ft radius Threshold centered on yourself, moving with you. While it stands: you cannot be moved against your will; you have advantage on saving throws against being frightened or charmed; and any enemy that starts its turn inside the Threshold must succeed on a Wisdom saving throw against your technique save DC or have disadvantage on attack rolls against you until the start of its next turn. The rules are disclosed — a creature that understands them may choose to fight accordingly. (The bronze head under the stairs. The ground is hers.)

L11

Tithe of the Dying

Passive.

Your claim range extends to 60 ft, and you may now claim a Trophy from any creature reduced to 0 hit points within range — even if you did not defeat it, even if an ally did. Additionally, when you claim a Trophy, you may immediately transfer its temporary hit points from Claim the Fallen to an ally within 30 ft instead of taking them yourself. (The queen's share. The host eats because she takes.)

L18

The Treaty of Samos

Action · 8 CE · once per long rest.

You dictate terms to one creature you can see within 60 ft that is below half its hit point maximum: a single demand, spoken aloud, no longer than a sentence (e.g., "yield your weapon," "order the retreat"). The creature chooses: accept — it complies to the best of its ability for 1 minute (it will not harm itself to comply); or refuse — it takes 8d10 psychic damage (no save; the refusal is witnessed) and you claim a Trophy from it as if it had been defeated. Heaven enforces the witnessing, not the compliance. A creature that cannot understand you is unaffected. (She ended a war with a sentence.)

Maximum Technique

The Head Under the Throne

Action · 10 CE · requires at least 3 Trophies.

You expend all Trophies you hold and make one melee weapon attack against a creature you can see within your reach. On a hit, it deals an additional 2d10 force damage per Trophy expended. On a miss, it deals half the damage it would have dealt on a hit (rounded down). The Trophies are expended whether the attack hits or misses. (Every head she ever took, in a single strike. The inventory, presented.)

Domain Expansion

Meroë: The Palace of Taken Things

Full-turn action · 20 CE · 800 clash points · burnout 5 rounds · duration 2 minutes · 40-ft radius · disclosed.

The throne room at Meroë: walls hung with taken standards, the threshold set with the bronze head. Inside this domain, the sure-hit is Everything Breaks Into Her Hands — whenever a weapon, standard, focus, or similar object held by a hostile creature inside the domain is destroyed, dropped, disarmed, or surrendered, you immediately claim it as a Trophy (ignoring your normal range, and exceeding your Trophy cap by up to 3 while the domain stands). Additionally, while the domain stands, your Spoils Fight bonus is doubled (maximum +12). (Fight her in her Palace, and you arm her with every swing.)

Level 20 · Capstone

The Record, Corrected

Once per long rest.

When you claim a Trophy from a creature of CR 10 or higher (or a player-character-tier NPC), you may correct the record: name one fact about the defeat, spoken aloud. It becomes true in every written account not yet fixed in print — histories, reports, ledgers update as if it had always been so. (Magical writing and Heaven-witnessed records are unaffected; the Samos price still holds — she may correct any record except the authorship of her own victory.) Use it sparingly. It is the closest thing she has to a prayer.
