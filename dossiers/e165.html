Core law

Character fantasy

Build Pursuit by running down fleeing prey, then spend it on dive-bombs, horn launches, pursuit reactions, and impossible aerial combinations.

Combat loop

  1. Move in a meaningful new way to gain Pursuit.
  2. Mark prey so fleeing enemies become fuel instead of escaping.
  3. Spend Pursuit on attacks that launch, frighten, or reposition.
  4. Keep moving—ending turns planted in one place lets the hunt cool down.

The fiction. Registry notes classify the Leeds Infernal as hunger made aerodynamic: the Jersey Devil's CE imprinted on the chase itself, and now the technique only exists while the hunt is moving. Pursuit is the technique's memory of running down fleeing prey, and the cooling rule is its one honest law — stop running, and the hunger forgets you. Scholars call it a predator's awakening: a moment of absolute bloodlust that the cursed energy has been reproducing mid-flight ever since.

Full progression

Level progression

L1Pine Barrens Predator

Pursuit cap

PB. Gain 1 Pursuit once per turn when you move at least 20 feet before hitting a creature, descend at least 10 feet, leap a gap, Dash, or willingly pass within 5 feet of a hostile without stopping. The same movement event grants only 1.

Cooling

If you end your turn having moved less than 10 feet, lose all Pursuit.

Fleeing prey is fuel: when a hostile creature you can see willingly moves out of your reach or takes the Disengage action, gain 1 Pursuit (once per round).

Cryptid Body

Your unarmed strikes may deal bludgeoning, piercing, or slashing and can use Strength or Dexterity. Wings, hooves, horns, and elongated limbs are visible manifestations, not separate creatures.

Pine Barrens Predator

You can glide: during your turn, after moving at least 10 feet, you may fly up to 30 feet but must end on a surface that can support you. Hoofbeat: after moving 20+ feet toward a target, on a hit you may spend 1 Pursuit (no action): push the target 10 feet; it makes a Strength save, knocked prone on failure. Horn Toss: spend 1 Pursuit after a melee hit; target makes Strength save or is launched 10 feet upward or horizontally.

L3Marked Quarry

Bonus action, 1 CE: mark one creature you can see for 1 minute. Once per round when it voluntarily moves 10+ feet away from you, gain 2 Pursuit and may use your reaction to move up to half your speed toward it. Devil’s Cry: action, 2 CE, 30-foot cone; Wisdom save or frightened until the end of your next turn. The shriek’s fantasy is terror, not a thunderbolt — it deals no damage.

L6True Flight

Gain 50-foot fly speed while conscious. Pursuit cap becomes PB + 2. Dive Rake: if you descend 20+ feet before a melee hit, spend 1 Pursuit (no action): the target makes a Strength save; on failure it is knocked prone and frightened until the end of your next turn, and you may move through its space to an unoccupied space within 10 feet without provoking from it. You may spend up to 2 additional Pursuit: each forces one additional creature within 5 feet of the target to make the same save or be knocked prone — the dive-bomb scatters the formation.

L11Night Pursuit

Pursuit cap becomes 2 × PB. Once per turn after you spend Pursuit, move 10 feet without provoking. Marked Quarry no longer costs CE against a creature you damaged this turn. When a Quarry teleports or is forcibly moved, you may spend 2 Pursuit to move up to your speed toward its new position after the movement resolves.

L18Maximum: The Leeds Devil Takes Flight

Action, 10 CE, Focus, 1 minute. Become Large, fly speed 80 feet, reach +5 feet, Pursuit cap 12, and begin each turn with at least 3 Pursuit. Once on each turn when you move 30+ feet, make one unarmed attack without an action against a creature you pass within reach; this attack cannot itself grant Pursuit. Devil’s Cry becomes a 60-foot cone; creatures that fail the save are frightened for 1 minute (repeating the save at the end of each of their turns) and cannot take reactions while frightened. When the Maximum ends, lose all Pursuit and gain 1 exhaustion level.

Feats

Build-defining options.

Roofline Hunter

Climbing costs no extra movement and you may run across vertical surfaces during your turn if you end on a legal surface or remain flying.

Hellish Whinny

When a creature fails its save against Devil’s Cry, its panic betrays it: for 1 minute you always know its direction and distance, and it cannot become hidden from you. This does not let you see it while invisible, but you know which space it occupies.

Near Miss

When a creature misses you with an opportunity attack, gain 1 Pursuit, once per round. The hunt feeds on near misses.

Broken-Bridle Pursuit

Prerequisite: Marked Quarry. When your Quarry imposes restrained, grappled, or prone on you, gain 2 Pursuit after the condition is applied.

Rules integration

Building the character

Warrior and Scout shells both work. A Strength build plays like a flying bruiser; Dexterity plays like a skirmisher. The technique deliberately rewards terrain, height, and aggressive movement over standing still and taking the Attack action every round.
Rules integration

Counterplay

Difficult terrain, immobilization, cramped rooms, readied attacks, and forced stationary objectives all attack the Pursuit engine directly.
