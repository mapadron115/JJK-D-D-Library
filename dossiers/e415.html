Lesser Sealing Talisman

A Grade 3 cursed item — a paper talisman, expended on use.

FormCursed Item
GradeGrade 3
AttunementNot required

Field notes. Subject: a lock that is also a wall, bought with a slip of paper. Three truths govern the sealing talisman. First, denial is a weapon: pressed onto an item, container, or door, it places a Minor Magical Lock that holds for 8 hours at DC 18 — eight hours is a night, and a night is often the whole war. Second, the seal cannot be peeled: the talisman cannot be removed while active, so the sealed thing stays sealed against hands that found it first. Third, it is spent in the spending: once the 8 hours end, the paper is gone — a consumable, asking no attunement and no cursed energy, only judgment about what is worth sealing. Seal what the enemy must open before you do, and you have already won the first engagement.

Consumable

Minor Magical Lock

Apply the talisman to an item, container, or door to place a Minor Magical Lock on it for 8 hours (DC 18 to open). The talisman cannot be removed while active, and it is expended after the 8 hours are complete.
