Completed rules

  • You gain the named Simple Domain feat even if you lack its normal prerequisite. Its radius is 20 feet. You may activate it without CE PB times per long rest, as either a bonus action or a reaction to a Domain or sure-hit effect; further uses pay the feat’s normal cost.
  • Batto Sword Draw: as an action for 3 CE, choose a continuous path no longer than half your speed and a number of creatures up to Dexterity modifier (minimum one) that come within weapon reach of that path. Make one weapon attack with advantage and compare it with each chosen AC; roll damage once for those hit. You teleport along the path, do not provoke, and finish in a legal space just beyond the farthest target. If none exists, finish at the last legal space.
  • Evening Moon: while your Simple Domain is active and you wield a melee weapon, you are in the stance. Once per round when one or more hostile creatures enter its radius, use a reaction to attack each entering creature once. On a hit, choose: spend 1 to PB CE to make the hit critical and add that many weapon dice; or gain +5 AC and +5 to saves against CE effects until the start of your next turn. The defensive choice does not require CE.
  • Moonlight Veil: as a bonus action, imbue one wielded melee weapon for 1 minute using Technique Focus. Add CE modifier to its attack and damage rolls. If you hold only a broken weapon or fight unarmed, the veil supplies a spectral weapon using the original weapon’s base die, or 1d6 for unarmed, with no magic-item properties.
  • Perfect Shadow Style: Simple Domain no longer costs CE or has a use limit, and you may move without ending it. As a bonus action, add 60 feet to the range of one New Shadow Style technique used before the end of the current turn; for Simple Domain, this increases radius by 60 feet until the start of your next turn.

Power identity

Why Tier I: its late-game anti-Domain mastery is exceptional, but its whole package is a disciplined melee system with limited off-scene reach and no independent resource engine.

Recovered character-build options

Innate Technique Feats 3

These are optional character-building feats. You must meet each listed prerequisite before selecting it.

Augmented Vow

High impact

Prerequisite: True New Shadow Style: Simple Domain

When opening Simple Domain, state one symmetrical conduct rule and one consequence no harsher than 5d10 damage or one condition until end of the breaker’s next turn. Every creature inside, including you, knows it and is bound for CE modifier rounds or until leaving. It cannot compel impossible actions, surrender, self-harm, or permanent loss.

Instantaneous Attack

Standard

Prerequisite: Batto Sword Draw

While your Simple Domain is active, use an action to prepare. The next time a hostile creature enters your weapon reach before the Domain ends, use your reaction to make one weapon attack. The preparation is then consumed.

First Strike

High impact

Prerequisite: Evening Moon Sword Drawing

Once when initiative is rolled, with no action choose one: activate Simple Domain and pay its cost, or make one weapon attack against a creature already within reach. This occurs after initiative is set and before the first turn.

New Shadow Style
You are born with no innate technique, but in order to make up you
learn a special secret. A domain for the weak.
Cursed Energy
Starting at 1st level, you can harness your negative feelings into a
usable source of energy, called cursed energy. These feelings can be
harnessed into attacks or abilities. The number of cursed energy you
possess is based on your class, some classes use a modifier that
being charisma or wisdom (Player’s choice)
Your cursed energy DC is 8 + user cursed energy ability modifier +
proficiency bonus.
True New Shadow Style: Simple Domain
Beginning at 1st level, you gain the New Shadow Style: Simple Domain
feat with the following benefits:
● You can activate New Shadow Style: Simple Domain at no cost a
number of times equal to your proficiency modifier
● You can activate it as a reaction at no extra cost
● You can activate this as a bonus action
● The area of effect is a 20-foot radius instead of 15 feet
Batto Sword Draw
At 3rd level, for 3 Cursed Energy, you have learned the fastest
technique of the New Shadow Style. As an action while wielding a
melee weapon, you can make one attack roll with
an advantage against a number of targets equal to
the Dexterity modifier of your choice within half
of your movement speed and reach. In addition,
you end your turn behind the farthest target out
of them all. This movement does not provoke
attacks of opportunity.
Evening Moon Sword Drawing
Once you reach 6th level, you enter a crouched
stance. When a creature enters the area of your
New Shadow Style: Simple Domain you will make a
weapon attack against this creature or creatures.
On a hit, you deal critical damage plus an
additional amount of weapon dice equal to the
cursed energy you choose to use for Evening Moon
Sword Drawing, or you can choose to gain a +5
bonus to your AC and saving throws against Cursed
Energy attacks.
New Shadown Style: Moonlight Veil
Starting at 11th level, you can use a bonus action to imbue your
weapon with your simple domain. You can add your Cursed Energy stat
bonus to strikes using your weapon both to hit and damage. Weapons
include unarmed strikes, and this can be used to replace a broken
weapon.
Perfect Shadow Style
Finally, at 18th level, you manage to reach the peak of New Shadow
Style. Your New Shadow Style: Simple Domain gains the following
benefits:
● New Shadow Style: Simple Domain can be used for free
● You can freely move and maintain New Shadow Style: Simple Domain
● The range of any of your New Shadow Style techniques can be
increased by 60 feet as a bonus action
