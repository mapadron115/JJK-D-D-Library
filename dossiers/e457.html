Momentum Edge

The blade is a miser of force, whatever it took from the last cut, it lends to the next, and only once per turn. Interest paid in thirds: Once per turn, you can gain a bonus to an attack or damage roll made with this cursed tool, equal to 1/3 of the slashing damage dealt by your last attack with it (rounded down).

Field notes. Subject: a blade of old bone that remembers the weight of its last cut. Three truths. First: the Dragon Bone never strikes the first blow well, it is a weapon of the second strike, and the third, each drawing interest on the last. A duel with this blade is won on the swing before. Second: it is honest about what it borrows, only slashing damage actually dealt, after resistances and reductions, and only a third of it, rounded down. A glancing touch lends nothing; the house keeps the change. Third: it forgives failure as quickly as it compounds victory, a whiffed swing starves the edge, and the bonus falls to nothing. Hence the maxim: wield this blade like an avalanche, or do not wield it at all. It rewards momentum, never promise.

Campaign canon notes

"Your last attack" [INVENTED]: the source does not define it. Read as your most recent attack made with this weapon. Only the slashing damage actually dealt by that attack counts, use the total after resistances and reductions, then divide by 3 and round down. If your last attack dealt no slashing damage, the bonus is +0.
