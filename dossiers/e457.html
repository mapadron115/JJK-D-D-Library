Momentum Edge

Once per turn, you can gain a bonus to an attack or damage roll made with this cursed tool, equal to 1/3 of the slashing damage dealt by your last attack with it (rounded down).

Edge cases and shared systems.

Campaign canon notes

"Your last attack" [INVENTED]: the source does not define it. Read as your most recent attack made with this weapon. Only the slashing damage actually dealt by that attack counts — use the total after resistances and reductions, then divide by 3 and round down. If your last attack dealt no slashing damage, the bonus is +0.
