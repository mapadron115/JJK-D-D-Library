Rockstar

You can amplify sound and launch it as a blast of energy.

Power tierTier III · Apex
Combat roleBlaster
Core engineSound Blast volleys + Sound Wave
DomainHall of Fame (18)

Field notes. Subject: a crescendo with a technique attached. Three truths govern it. First: this is not a loud voice but a multiplied one, the technique never makes one bigger blast; it makes the action repeat, because a performance is never a single note. Second: the Sound Wave is not damage with a side effect, the deafen and the push are the point. The sorcerer silences the enemy’s ears the way a headliner drowns the crowd, denying them even the dignity of hearing the answer. Third: the Epic Solo is what happens when the performer stops playing at the enemy and starts playing the room, every hostile that ends a turn near the soloist pays in thunder for standing too close to the speakers. I have watched a user fight, and there is no counter-argument in this technique. There is only decibels.

Full progression

Feature map

1Sound Amplification.

Action (holding an instrument you're proficient in) · 1 CE · Ranged CE attack, 30 ft (60 ft at 6th): 2d10 thunder (3d10/4d10/6d10 at 6th/11th/18th); creature hit: Str save or pushed 10 ft · You gain Performance + one instrument proficiency.

The instrument is the excuse, not the source, one coin of cursed energy per blast, a ranged technique attack that hurls thunder and shoves the target ten feet back. Starting at 1st level, you gain proficiency in Performance and one instrument of your choice. While holding an instrument you are proficient in, you can spend 1 CE as an action to play it and launch a Sound Blast at a target within 30 feet. Make a ranged Cursed Energy attack roll, on a hit you deal 2d10 thunder damage and if the target is a creature, it must succeed on a Strength saving throw or be pushed back 10 feet. This technique's damage increases to 3d10 at 6th level, 4d10 at 11th level, and 6d10 at 18th level.

3Jacked Audio.

Upgrade · Your Sound Blast action launches up to two Sound Blasts (1 CE each); up to three at 11th.

The technique’s real engine is repetition: by the third level the action launches two blasts, by the eleventh three, the volume of the set, not the size of the note. At 3rd level, when you use your action to launch a Sound Blast, you can launch up to two Sound Blasts, spending 1 Cursed Energy for each. At 11th level you can make up to three Sound Blast attacks as part of your action.

6Resonating Music.

Action · 5 CE · 15-ft cone (30 ft at 11th): Con save or Sound Blast damage + PB d10s thunder + pushed 20 ft + deafened to end of their next turn, half damage on success.

At the sixth level the blast becomes weather, a fifteen-foot cone that pushes twenty feet and deafens, and the range of everything doubles as the venue grows. At 6th level, you have learned to apply your Sound Blast to an area. As an action for 5 cursed energy, you can launch a Sound Wave in a 15 feet cone. All creatures within range must make a Constitution saving throw or take your Sound Blast damage + additional d10s equal to your proficiency bonus and be pushed back 20 feet and deafened until the end of their next turn on a failed save or half as much damage on a successful save. The range of your Sound Blast now increases to 60 feet. The range of your Sound Wave increases to 30 feet at 11th level.

11Maximum: Epic Solo.

Bonus action · 8 CE (+ extra CE up to half PB) · Solo for 1 min: your attacks +2d10 thunder (+2d10 more per extra CE); creatures failing saves vs your technique have dis on their next attack or check (expires end of their next turn); hostiles ending their turn within 15 ft of you take CE-mod + PB thunder.

The Maximum is the refusal to leave the stage: for one minute every attack carries extra thunder, failed saves buy the enemy disadvantage, and the air within fifteen feet of the soloist burns anyone who ends their turn in it. At 11th level, as a bonus action, you can spend 8 cursed energy to start a solo. For the next minute, your attacks deal an additional 2d10 thunder damage and if a creature fails its saving throw against your technique it gains disadvantage on its next attack roll or ability check. The disadvantage expires at the end of the target's next turn if unused. You can also spend additional cursed energy up to half your proficiency bonus to deal an additional 2d10 thunder per CE spent. A hostile creature that ends its turn within 15 feet of you during your solo takes thunder damage equal to your CE modifier + your proficiency bonus.

18Domain Expansion: Hall of Fame.

Domain (18th level) · Initiative 20: creatures of your choice take your Sound Amplification damage and are deafened for 1 min.

The domain opens with a chord that has no source, and the barrier shimmers like heat above a stage. Ranks of phantom amplifiers rise in a coliseum ring, their cones breathing light with every beat, and the air itself thickens into something you feel in your teeth. It is a concert hall built from volume alone, and the applause never stops.

The Domain is the encore nobody asked for, at initiative twenty, every chosen creature inside takes the full blast and is deafened for a minute. At 18th level, you mastered your technique allowing you to expand your domain. At initiative 20, all creatures of your choice within your domain take your Sound Amplification damage and are deafened for 1 minute.

Innate Technique Feats

Build-defining options.

These are optional character-building feats. You must meet each listed prerequisite before selecting it.

Prerequisite: Must have Resonating Music

Musical Climax

The sorcerer learns what every performer knows, the drop matters more than the beat. Charge the technique over rounds of concentration, then throw the sound from anywhere within sixty feet, shaping the wave into a line. You've learned that just playing isn't enough, you need to build up to the best part. As an action you can start playing your instrument to charge your technique, concentrating for up to a number of rounds equal to your CE modifier, after which you must release it. When you release a Sound Blast or Sound Wave charged this way, you can originate it from any unoccupied point within 60 feet of you that you can see, throwing your sound, and you can shape a Sound Wave into a 60-foot line instead of a cone.

Prerequisite: Must have Resonating Music

Emotional Melodies

Five coins buy a minute of vibe, and the vibe is a weapon: happy charms, sad pins them prone and still, angry turns them berserk against their own side. As a bonus action for 5 cursed energy, you can introduce a vibe of your choice for 1 minute while concentrating. You can only have one Vibe active at a time. Deafened creatures automatically succeed. Happy Vibes: on a hit with your Sound Blast, the target makes a Charisma saving throw or be Charmed and only hear you and your music until the Vibe ends; they can repeat the save at the beginning of their turn. Sad Vibes: on a hit with your Sound Blast, the target makes a Charisma saving throw or fall prone and their speed becomes 0 until the Vibe ends; they can repeat the save at the beginning of their turn. Angry Vibes: on a hit with your Sound Blast, the target makes a Charisma saving throw or becomes berserk until the Vibe ends, a berserk creature uses their reaction to make a melee attack at the closest creature at the start of their turn and must use their action to attack; they can repeat the save at the beginning of their turn.

Prerequisite: Must have Maximum: Epic Solo

Rock Squad

The band gets back together mid-solo, allies ending their turns near the soloist carry the rhythm into their next strike, pushing what they hit. Looks like the band is getting back together! While your Epic Solo is active, you can conduct your allies' strikes. When an allied creature ends their turn within 15 feet of you during your Epic Solo, their next attack before the start of your next turn carries your rhythm: on a hit, the target is pushed 10 feet away from the ally, as if struck by your Sound Blast.

Rules integration

Edge cases and shared systems.

Rules priority: the reviewed rulings, explicit codex clarifications, and the level progression always control when original wording conflicts. Legacy Domain auto-hit language, action terminology, event timing, missing saves, and older scaling never override revised player rules.
Jacked Audio stacking: Jacked Audio's multi-blast is the technique's Extra-Attack-equivalent and does not stack with Extra Attack, one attack action per turn.
Sound Blast classification: a Sound Blast is a technique attack, not a weapon attack.
Epic Solo disadvantage expiry: the disadvantage rider does not specify expiry if unused; proposed default is it expires at the end of the target's next turn.
Epic Solo aura: the 15-ft aura damage (CE mod + PB, no save, no attack roll) is kept as printed; it is not a sure-hit Domain effect and does not trigger Black Flash. The aura deals flat damage and cannot critically hit.
Rock Squad dice: "additional d4 in thunder damage equal to your proficiency bonus" is read as a number of d4s equal to PB.
Musical Climax corrections: the printed prerequisite "Sound Waves" is a transcription error, corrected to Resonating Music. Charging costs actions and concentration; the release must occur or the charges are lost when concentration breaks. "Ignore resistances" applies to the released Blast/Wave only, not to the whole turn's damage, needs confirmation.
Emotional Melodies: berserk uses the printed procedure (reaction attack at start of turn plus compelled action attack); it does not grant extra actions. The Sad vibe's prone + speed 0 with repeat saves is retained as printed; it is strong control and intentionally so.
Domain conversion: resolves at initiative 20 per approved Domain timing; deafened lasts 1 minute as printed. Domain clashes use the deterministic 1–1,000 point system.
