Observer’s account

Field notes. Subject: purity as a fence. A pouch holds 1d4 + 1 pinches; an action scatters up to five, each covering five feet, and cursed spirits of Grade 3 or lower will not cross the line. Three truths. First, the ward is a refusal, not a wall: unwilling, not unable, a compelled spirit or anything Grade 2 or higher crosses freely. Second, the line is fragile as faith: wind, water, or a careless boot breaks it, and a broken line deters nothing. Third, its power is in the buying of breath: it does not kill, it delays, five feet of salt is five feet of time. Mark this: the oldest magic is a boundary drawn on the ground. Hence the maxim: when you cannot outfight them, outlast the line you drew.

Campaign canon notes

Unwilling, not unable: the line deters, it does not restrain, a spirit compelled by a stronger effect, or any Grade 2 or higher spirit, can cross freely.
Line integrity: the salt line persists until physically disturbed (wind, water, trampling), the source states no duration, so a broken line no longer deters. [INVENTED]
