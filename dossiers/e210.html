Hour Hand

1d8 finesse piercing. Your first successful hit each round advances Beat 1→2→3→1; missing all attacks in a round resets to 1. Apply the benefit of the Beat you are on when the hit lands, then advance (invented).

FormRapier A duelist's rapier — patience given steel, wound tighter with every landed blow.
RoleTempo duelist The fight as a measure of music, the blade as its baton — the hand that keeps time wins.
EngineBeat A three-count rhythm the wielder holds; the tool rewards the hand that does not drop it.
Attunement1 hour* An hour's ritual — the mechanism must learn your tempo.
Features & procedures

Properties & techniques

Exact player-facing procedures

Beat 1

The count begins. Your first successful hit each round carries extra speed — the opening note, struck and gone. +10 speed until end turn.

Beat 2

The count deepens. The second hit lands with an extra die of force — the mechanism wound tighter, the swing carrying the first blow's momentum. hit adds 1 Tool Die force.

Beat 3

The count resolves. The third hit robs the target of reactions and lets you step ten feet clear — the phrase ended, the blade already elsewhere. target loses reactions and you may step 10 ft after hit.

Borrowed Second

Two CE after the third beat: one more attack as a bonus action — the measure extended by a borrowed bar. It cannot borrow from itself; the rhythm forbids it. 2 CE after Beat 3: make one additional weapon attack as bonus action; cannot trigger itself.

Broken Rhythm

The instrument's judgment. Stunned or incapacitated, the count resets — not because the tool forgot, but because it refuses to play for a hand that lost the tempo. being stunned/incapacitated resets Beat.

Field notes. Subject: a metronome with an edge. Three truths govern it. First, each landed blow winds the mechanism tighter: a three-beat cadence — speed, then force, then denial and distance — that the wielder must hold the way a musician holds time. Second, the reward is earned, never granted: miss every attack in a round and the count unwinds to one; the blade has no patience for a hand that stops playing. Third, the rhythm is readable: opponents can hear the count coming, and that is the design — a predictable cadence creates windows a wise enemy will disrupt. I have watched duelists lose on the third beat more than the first. The tool's maxim, unstated: keep time, or be kept.

Rules integration

Cursed-tool attunement

Campaign canon

Cursed-tool attunement: you have PB attunement slots (2–6). This tool occupies one slot and requires a one-hour ritual. Attunement ends voluntarily over a short rest, on death, or when you replace it while over capacity. Distance does not break attunement. Cursed-tool attunement is separate from magic-item attunement.
Balance & counterplay. Predictable three-beat cadence creates windows opponents can read and disrupt.
