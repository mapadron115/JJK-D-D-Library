Features & procedures

Properties & techniques

Exact player-facing procedures

Beat 1

+10 speed until end turn.

Beat 2

hit adds 1 Tool Die force.

Beat 3

target loses reactions and you may step 10 ft after hit.

Borrowed Second

2 CE after Beat 3: make one additional weapon attack as bonus action; cannot trigger itself.

Broken Rhythm

being stunned/incapacitated resets Beat.

The fiction. Registry notes describe the Hour Hand as a duelist's patience given steel: the rapier's CE rewards a rhythm the wielder holds — each landed blow winds the mechanism tighter, each missed round lets it unwind. Broken Rhythm is the instrument's judgment: the beat resets not because the tool forgot, but because it refuses to play for a hand that lost the tempo.

Rules integration

Cursed-tool attunement

Campaign canon

Cursed-tool attunement: you have PB attunement slots (2–6). This tool occupies one slot and requires a one-hour ritual. Attunement ends voluntarily over a short rest, on death, or when you replace it while over capacity. Distance does not break attunement. Cursed-tool attunement is separate from magic-item attunement.
Balance & counterplay. Predictable three-beat cadence creates windows opponents can read and disrupt.
