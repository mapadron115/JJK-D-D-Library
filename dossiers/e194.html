Red Lioness Fever

Sekhmet inspiration. Consecutive damaging turns build Heat 0–5. Heat grants speed and damage but at 4+ also burns the user, creating a deliberate escalation/cooling decision.

Combat roleEscalating berserker / plague-fire striker
Power tierTier IV · Special Grade
Core law

Character engine

Consecutive damaging turns build Heat 0–5. Heat grants speed and damage but at 4+ also burns the user, creating a deliberate escalation/cooling decision.

Technique save DC: 8 + PB + chosen CE ability modifier. Technique attack: PB + chosen CE ability modifier.

Full progression

Level progression

1Lioness Heat

Heat 1–2: +5 speed. 3+: first hit/turn +1 die fire. 5: +2 dice. At Heat 4+ take PB unavoidable damage end turn; bonus action lower Heat by 1.

3Breath of Pestilence

Action, 2 CE, 15-ft cone Con save; 2 dice fire or necrotic; failure also reduces healing by PB until end next turn.

6Blood-Red Draught

Action (or bonus with prepared red drink), 1 CE: reduce Heat by 3, heal 1 die + CE mod, end frightened. Once/short rest.

11Eye of Ra

At Heat 5 attacks ignore fire resistance; when self-damage occurs, enemies within 10 ft Dex save or take 1 die fire.

18Maximum: Lady of Slaughter, Lady of Healing

7 CE, 3 rounds. Slaughter locks Heat 5 and doubles self-damage for stronger first two hits; Mercy locks Heat 0 and lets action heal 3 dice + CE mod and cleanse poison/fear. This healing is not Reverse Cursed Technique and cannot exceed the target’s normal maximum healing from technique sources per the campaign injury rules.

Rules integration

Counterplay

Deny consecutive damaging turns and make the user choose between Heat momentum and self-preservation.
