Red Lioness Fever

Core law

Character engine

Consecutive damaging turns build Heat 0–5. Heat grants speed and damage but at 4+ also burns the user, creating a deliberate escalation/cooling decision.

Technique save DC: 8 + PB + chosen CE ability modifier. Technique attack: PB + chosen CE ability modifier.

The fiction. Registry scholars describe the Fever as the cursed energy of a berserker who once had to be stopped by being handed something red to drink — the technique remembers both the slaughter and the draught that ended it. The Heat is not rage but the desert sun carried in the blood, climbing with every consecutive blow until the body itself begins to burn. And the red drink still works, because this energy was taught mercy exactly once, and it never unlearned the taste.

Full progression

Level progression

1Lioness Heat

You track Heat from 0 to 5, built by consecutive damaging turns: gain 1 Heat at the end of each of your turns in which you dealt damage; if you end a turn without dealing damage, Heat resets to 0 (invented). At Heat 1–2 you gain +5 speed. At Heat 3 or higher, your first hit each turn deals +1d8 of fire damage (invented: the die is a d8); at Heat 5 this becomes +2d8 (invented). At Heat 4 or higher you take unavoidable damage equal to your proficiency bonus at the end of each of your turns; as a bonus action you may lower your Heat by 1.

3Breath of Pestilence

As an action for 2 CE, you exhale a 15-foot cone of pestilence. Each creature in the cone makes a Constitution save, taking 2d8 of fire or necrotic damage (invented; your choice) on a failure. A creature that fails also has its healing reduced by your proficiency bonus until the end of your next turn.

6Blood-Red Draught

As an action (or a bonus action if you have a prepared red drink) for 1 CE, you drink to reduce your Heat by 3, regain 1d8 of hit points (invented) plus your CE modifier, and end the frightened condition on yourself. You can use this once per short rest.

11Eye of Ra

While you are at Heat 5, your attacks ignore fire resistance. Whenever you take your Heat self-damage, each enemy within 10 feet of you makes a Dexterity save or takes 1 die of fire damage.

18Maximum: Lady of Slaughter, Lady of Healing

As an action (invented), you spend 7 CE for a 3-round Maximum with two modes. Slaughter locks your Heat at 5 and doubles your Heat self-damage, and your first two hits each turn deal one additional die of fire damage (invented); Mercy locks your Heat at 0 and lets you use your action to heal 3d8 + your CE modifier (invented) and cleanse poison and fear. This healing is not Reverse Cursed Technique and cannot exceed the target’s normal maximum healing from technique sources per the campaign injury rules.

Feats

Build-defining options.

Lion's Pursuit

At Heat 3+, when you take the Dash action you may leave a 5-foot-wide trail of fire along your path: creatures that enter it before the start of your next turn take 1 fire die.

Red Beer Ritual

You may administer your Blood-Red Draught to an adjacent willing ally instead of drinking it yourself: the ally gains the healing (1 die + your CE modifier) and the end of the frightened condition, while your Heat still falls by 3 as the ritual's cooling passes through another's body. A use spent this way counts against your once-per-short-rest limit, and the ally must drink as part of your action. You cannot split a single draught's effects between two creatures.

Fever Claw

When you hit a creature with a melee attack, you may spend 1 CE to deliver your plague by claw: the target's healing is reduced by your PB until the end of your next turn, as with Breath of Pestilence.

Two Faces of the Sun (11+)

Once during your Maximum's 3-round duration, you may switch between Slaughter and Mercy as a bonus action, the sun turning its other face. Your Heat immediately becomes 5 for Slaughter or 0 for Mercy to match the new mode, and the spent round still counts toward the duration. You cannot switch again for the rest of the Maximum.

Rules integration

Counterplay

Deny consecutive damaging turns and make the user choose between Heat momentum and self-preservation.
