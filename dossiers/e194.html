Character engine

Consecutive damaging turns build Heat 0–5. Heat grants speed and damage but at 4+ also burns the user, creating a deliberate escalation/cooling decision.

Technique save DC: 8 + PB + chosen CE ability modifier. Technique attack: PB + chosen CE ability modifier.

Counterplay

Deny consecutive damaging turns and make the user choose between Heat momentum and self-preservation.

Adaptation note. This is original JJK 5e homebrew inspired by Sekhmet and the named tradition. The folklore/myth supplies the motif; the resource engine, CE economy, progression, feats, Maximum and Domain procedures are game mechanics created for this codex.
