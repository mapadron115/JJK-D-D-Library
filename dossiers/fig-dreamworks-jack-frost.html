Innate Technique • Rise of the Guardians

Belief

Field notes. Subject: Jack Frost, winter's apprentice, three centuries unseen. Three truths govern the technique. First: belief is the fuel — no crowd, no storm; one child saying his name, and the wind remembers how to carry him. Second: play is the method — snowballs, not siege engines; frost on glass, not walls of ice; a snow day declared unilaterally against the calendar. Third: the wind is the road — he does not fly so much as accept lifts from an old friend. And mark the inversion: the boy nobody could see became the one children look for first. Being believed is a kind of armor no cold can crack.

Feature map

Level-by-level features

L1

Belief

Passive · tracked as a resource.

Track Belief, maximum equal to your proficiency bonus + your CHA modifier; you always count as one believer. Gain 1 Belief when a creature within 60 ft that can see you expresses belief in you — an ally's free action ("I believe!"), a delighted cheer, a child shouting your name. At Belief 3+ your tricks sharpen; at Belief 6+ they sing (see features). While no creature but yourself believes in you, the world is heavier: your technique features cost +1 CE.

L1

Wind-Rider

Bonus action · 1 CE.

You gain a fly speed equal to your walking speed + 10 ft. It lasts until the end of your turn — or for 1 minute while your Belief is 3 or higher. (He does not fly. The wind carries him, and the wind likes him.)

L3

Snowball Barrage

Action · 2 CE.

Conjure winter in your hands and hurl it: three ranged attacks (range 30/60 ft), each dealing 1d6 + your CHA modifier cold damage on a hit. A creature hit must succeed on a CON save vs your CE DC or be unable to take reactions until the start of your next turn — snow in the face. While your Belief is 6 or higher, hurl four snowballs instead. (This is not Ice Formation. This is a snowball fight, and he fights to win.)

L6

Frostbite Patterns

Action · 2 CE · touch a window, mirror, or smooth surface.

Breathe frost across the surface. For 1 hour, you may use an action to see and hear through the frosted surface as though you stood before it (range 1 mile). Alternatively, inscribe a message in the frost that only your believers can read. (Every winter window he ever touched was a letter. This is how you read them.)

L11

Whiteout

Action · 5 CE · Concentration, up to 1 minute.

Snow swallows a 60-ft radius sphere centered on you. The area is heavily obscured to every creature you choose to exclude; your believers see through it clearly. Hostile creatures treat the area as difficult terrain. You and your believers ignore both effects. (A snow day, declared unilaterally.)

L14

The Wind Remembers

Reaction · 2 CE.

When you take damage from an attack you can see, ride the gust aside: move up to half your fly speed without provoking opportunity attacks, and the attacker has disadvantage on attack rolls against you until the start of your next turn. (He was never where the blow landed.)

Pinnacle expression

Maximum, Domain, or equivalent.

Capstone material.

Maximum Technique

Eternal Snow Day

Action · 10 CE.

A blizzard blankets a 1-mile radius for 8 hours, or until you dismiss it. Visibility drops to 60 ft; hostile creatures treat all terrain as difficult; tracks vanish within minutes. You and your believers ignore the storm entirely, and while it lasts you may use a bonus action to step from one snow-covered point to another within the mile — appearing wherever the snow falls. (School's out. Indefinitely.)

Level 20 · Capstone

Guardian of Childhood

Passive · once-per-long-rest reaction.

Children and the childlike always count as your believers, and they can always see you — no illusion, invisibility, or trick of light hides you from a believing child. Once per long rest, when an ally within 60 ft drops to 0 hit points, you may use your reaction to catch them on the wind: they stabilize and gain temporary hit points equal to twice your current Belief. (Jamie said his name, and the wind answered.)

Innate Technique Feats

Build-defining options.

These are optional character-building feats. You must meet each listed prerequisite before selecting it.

Prerequisite: Wind-Rider

Updraft Ally

When you Wind-Ride, one willing creature you are touching rides the same gust — it gains your fly speed until the end of your turn (or the full minute, if extended). (The wind likes him. It can be introduced to his friends.)

Prerequisite: Frostbite Patterns

Windowpane Whispers

Your frosted surfaces carry sound both ways — while observing through one, you may also speak, and your voice is heard clearly by anyone near the surface. (He spent three hundred years leaving messages on glass. Now he can wait for the reply.)

Prerequisite: Whiteout

The Storm Provides

While Whiteout persists, you may use a bonus action to raise the snow into shelter — up to three allies inside the storm gain half cover until the start of your next turn. (A snow day protects its own.)

Prerequisite: Snowball Barrage

A Snowball with Your Name on It

Once per turn, you may forgo one snowball's damage to mark its target instead — you and your believers always know the marked creature's direction for 1 hour. (The snow never forgets where you are.)
