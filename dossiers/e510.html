Emperor's Chains

Five chains spill from the fingers of one hand, a jailer, a judge, a dowser, a healer, and the scarlet-eyed emperor that drives them all. Kurapika's Specialist Hatsu is built on a single terrible promise: the middle chain may only ever close around the ones who took everything from him, and if it closes around anyone else, the stake already buried in his own heart will finish the job. Every chain is a tool; the vow is the weapon. And behind it all burn scarlet eyes that spend his life like coin.

Emperor's Chains

Field notes. Subject: a courtroom that walks on five fingers. Three truths govern the chains. First: the vow is the weapon. Chain Jail binds one designated group, and one only; loose it against any other creature, and the stake buried in your own heart finishes the job. The chains are legislation with a death clause. Second: each finger is an office. The middle finger jails; the ring finger dowses direction and reads lies from the pulse; the thumb mends, two dice per CE spent; the index steals aura and analyzes it through a dolphin only she can see; the pinky passes sentence with a Judgment stake buried in the heart. Third: power is paid in lifespan. Emperor Time burns an hour of life per round, godhood, metered like rent, and after death, the strongest Nen persists: the protocol keeps her conscious a minute past zero, and her chains working a day past the grave.

Feature map

Level-by-level features

L1

Chain Jail

The core statute. The middle chain lashes sixty feet to grapple and Zetsu-lock the designated, no CE spent, no technique features answered. And beneath it, the Binding Vow of the Designated: mis-aim the jail, and you die without a save. Canon keeps its promises. The core of the technique. As a bonus action, conjure the five chains from the fingers of one hand, they persist until dismissed, count as conjured cursed objects, and are invisible to creatures that cannot perceive cursed energy. As an action, lash the middle-finger chain at a creature within 60 feet: make a melee attack using your CE ability (the chain strikes at reach). On a hit, the target is grappled and Zetsu-locked: it cannot spend or regain CE, and all of its innate-technique features go quiet while bound. Escape: as an action, Athletics or Acrobatics against your CE save DC; the chain itself can be attacked (AC 15, hit points equal to 5 × your level).

The Binding Vow of the Designated. When you take this technique, name the group the chains are sworn against, a defined organization or bloodline, GM-approved. Chain Jail may only ever target a member of that group. If you loose it against any other creature, the stake buried in your own heart (see Judgment Chain) drives home: you die, no saving throw. Canon keeps its promises.

The ring finger carries the Dowsing Chain: as an action, attune to a creature you have met, you know its direction and approximate distance within 1 mile; the attunement lasts until you finish a long rest, attune to a different creature, or dismiss it (no action required) (invented); as a reaction when a creature within 30 feet of you speaks, read its pulse through the chain and learn whether it deliberately lied. The thumb carries the Holy Chain: as an action (invented), touch a creature and spend CE, it regains 2d6 hit points per CE spent (maximum 5 CE per touch). The pinky chain is sealed until L3. The thumb mends what the other fingers break.

The index finger carries the Steal Chain: a syringe-tipped chain that steals the target's aura. As an action, make a melee attack using your CE ability against a creature within 30 feet; on a hit the syringe tip pierces it, the target cannot spend or regain CE until the end of your next turn, and you steal one use of one Innate Technique feature it used within the last minute. The syringe then becomes Stealth Dolphin, a dolphin construct only you can see: it analyzes the stolen feature and tells you its name and mechanics. While you hold the stolen ability, Emperor Time remains active and its lifespan cost accrues normally, holding someone's stolen power is never free. You may invoke the stolen feature once as an action using your CE, or lend it to a willing creature you touch: they may invoke it once, after which the stolen aura returns to its owner. Canon (HxH): the function is canon; all mechanical numbers are invented.

L3

Judgment Chain

The pinky carries the gavel. A stake driven into the heart, a single rule dictated, break it and the heart is crushed, no save. The judge was judged first: the same stake rests in her own heart from the moment she took the technique. As an action, spend 3 CE and drive the pinky chain's stake into a creature within 5 feet (melee attack using your CE ability). On a hit, the stake lodges in the target's heart and you dictate a single rule, one sentence, phrased as a prohibition or demand the target is capable of obeying. If the target breaks the rule, the stake crushes its heart: it dies, no saving throw. The stake remains until you dismiss it or the target dies, and you always know whether the rule stands or has fallen.

The same stake rests in your own heart from the moment you take this technique, it is the enforcer of the Binding Vow of the Designated, and it cannot be removed by any means short of renouncing the technique entirely. The judge is judged first.

L6

Emperor Time

Scarlet eyes, full power. Every chain at its strongest, two chains wielded at once, and the meter running: one hour of life per round, the lifespan ledger marked, the hair whitening shade by shade. Every second of godhood, paid in years. As a bonus action, your eyes turn scarlet. While Emperor Time burns, every chain works at full power: the Dowsing Chain cannot be fooled by any mundane or magical deception; the Holy Chain heals 3d6 per CE and can reattach a severed limb with a 5-CE touch; Chain Jail's escape DC increases by 2; and you may wield two chains at once, resolving two different chain actions in a single turn (paying each cost).

The price is real. Every round Emperor Time remains active burns 1 hour of your life, mark it on the Lifespan Ledger. For every full 24 hours burned, your hit point maximum is permanently reduced by 5 and your hair whitens a shade. The GM keeps the true total; you keep the tally. Every second of godhood, paid in years.

L11

Twin Fetters

The chains learn to hunt in pairs, a second designated member lashed with the same attack, reach doubled, and three chains at once under Emperor Time. The vow has long arms. Your chains learn to hunt in pairs. When you hit with Chain Jail, you may spend 2 CE to lash a second member of the designated group within 30 feet of the first, resolve the same attack roll against it. Chain Jail's reach extends to 120 feet, and the chain can now grapple Huge or smaller creatures.

Additionally, while Emperor Time burns you may wield three chains at once instead of two. The vow has long arms.

L18

The Oathbound Arsenal

Years of feeding the vow have made it heavy. Escape DCs climb, the chain's flesh doubles, the Dowsing Chain tracks any designated member ever met regardless of distance. The vow compounds like interest. Years of feeding the vow have made it heavy. Against the designated group, your chains are merciless: Chain Jail's escape DC increases by an additional 2 (stacks with Emperor Time), the chain's hit points double, and the Dowsing Chain can track any designated member you have ever met regardless of distance (same plane).

Judgment Chain can now hold a number of simultaneous rules equal to your proficiency bonus across all staked creatures, and you may dictate conditional rules, "if you ever speak my name to them, the stake falls." The vow compounds like interest.

L20

Post-Mortem Protocol

Nen does not always stop at death, especially Nen bound by vow. Conscious a minute past zero, Emperor Time burning free, advantage on every chain, and if she falls, the chains carry out her last command for a day. The strongest Nen is written after death. Nen does not always stop at death, especially Nen bound by vow. The first time you drop to 0 hit points, you do not fall: you remain conscious for 1 minute, Emperor Time burns with no lifespan cost, and your chain attacks are made with advantage. If you die while the protocol runs, your chains persist for 24 hours, carrying out the last command you gave them.

Once the protocol has run its course, it cannot trigger again until you finish a long rest. Canon: the strongest Nen is written after death.

Pinnacle expression

Maximum and Domain.

Capstone material

Maximum: The Unappealable Sentence

The vow, executed perfectly. No attack roll, no save: one designated member bound and sentenced for one full minute, unappealable by force, teleportation, or dispelling. There is no appeal. The ultimate expression of the vow. As an action, spend 8 CE and burn 12 hours of life immediately: choose one member of the designated group you can see within 120 feet. No attack roll, no saving throw, the chains find them unerringly. The target is bound by Chain Jail and a Judgment rule of your dictation is set upon it simultaneously. The binding cannot be escaped for 1 minute by any means, not by force, not by teleportation, not by dispelling; it ends only if you dismiss it or if you die. The Judgment rule persists after the minute by its normal rules. There is no appeal.

Domain Expansion: The Scarlet Courtroom

A courtroom with no walls and chains bearing eyes. Every designated member inside is automatically jailed, each with a hovering stake awaiting a rule; outsiders pass through the jail like smoke but are lashed for entering. Even the domain obeys the vow. 18th level. Sure-hit domain, the sure-hit is the vow, executed perfectly. Activation: full turn, 20 CE (D008 baseline for a normal Domain). Clash: 1000 points; burnout 3 rounds; duration 3 min (Tier V mapping); radius 120 ft.

The Docket. The air fills with hanging chains, and every link bears an eye. When the domain forms, every member of the designated group inside is automatically bound by Chain Jail, no attack roll, no save, and a hovering Judgment stake hangs over each of them, ready to receive a rule you dictate as a bonus action on your turns. The vow holds even here: creatures outside the designated group cannot be jailed, the chains pass through them like smoke, though the chains still lash them for 4d10 bludgeoning when they enter or start their turn inside (Dexterity save for half). Even the domain obeys the vow.

Rules integration

Edge cases and shared systems.

Campaign canon notes

Cursed Energy baseline: CE pool is based on class; CE ability is Charisma or Wisdom (player's choice). CE save DC = 8 + CE ability modifier + proficiency bonus. Regain all spent CE on a long rest.
The Binding Vow of the Designated: name the group when you take the technique (a defined organization or bloodline, GM-approved). Loosing Chain Jail against any other creature kills you, no saving throw, no resurrection bargaining; the stake in your own heart enforces it. Renouncing the technique entirely is the only way to remove the stake.
Zetsu-lock: while bound by Chain Jail, a creature cannot spend or regain CE and its innate-technique features are suppressed. It can still take actions and attack; escaping the grapple ends the lock.
Chain statistics: the Chain Jail chain has AC 15 and hit points equal to 5 × your level (doubled against the designated group at L18). Conjured chains are invisible to creatures that cannot perceive cursed energy.
Lifespan Ledger: 1 hour of life per round of Emperor Time (plus flat costs where printed). Every full 24 hours permanently reduces your hit point maximum by 5. The GM tracks the true total; the player tracks the tally.
Judgment rules: one sentence, capable of being obeyed; breaking the rule means death with no saving throw; you always know whether a set rule stands or has fallen.
Post-mortem note: if the user dies, the chains persist for 24 hours carrying out the last command given (canon post-mortem Nen), but the vow's death clause still kills the user first.
Domain activation defaults: rendered as full turn + 20 CE per the D008 normal-Domain baseline, with Tier V clash stats (1000 / 3-round burnout / 3 min) and a 120-ft radius.
Maximum vs. Reversal: Emperor's Chains' pinnacle is written as a Maximum Technique (The Unappealable Sentence), not a Reversal; it occupies the technique's pinnacle slot.
