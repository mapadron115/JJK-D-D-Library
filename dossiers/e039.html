Sunshine

One heavenly furnace, expressed through two incompatible vessels. Pride Incarnate turns the body into an ever-growing monarch and reaches a fatal, unsurpassable noon. Solar Grace circulates the same power with angelic precision, multiplying suns without sacrificing control.

The sky determines the die.

Solar clock

Use the campaign’s sun, not the wall clock

Solar noon is the moment the primary sun reaches its highest point, not necessarily 12:00. If exact hours are not tracked, divide the sunlit day into quarters and let the GM announce when the one-hour Near-Noon window and ten-round Zenith begin. When a boundary is crossed during combat, the station changes at the start of the next round.

Weather and shelter

The sun need not be visible

Clouds, roofs, mundane darkness, being underground, and ordinary magical darkness do not change the station. An effect that truly moves the sun, changes cosmic time, transports you to a sunless plane, or explicitly suppresses heavenly or solar power can change it. A visual illusion of daylight cannot.

10-minute rite · one memory

Solar Memory

While the actual station is rank 1–3, spend 10 uninterrupted minutes to store that rank. You cannot store borrowed or Domain-created sunlight and cannot store Zenith. Maintain one stored rank; a new rite replaces it. The memory remains until released, overwritten, or its vessel is destroyed.

As a bonus action, spend 2 CE to release it for 1 minute using Technique Focus. Your effective rank is the higher of the actual and stored ranks. When the release ends, the memory is expended. From level 11, releasing a memory no longer requires Technique Focus.

Level 1 · replaces one attack · X CE

Cruel Sun

When you take the Attack action, replace one attack and spend X CE, from 1 to PB. Make a ranged CE attack at 120/480 feet. On a hit, deal (X + 1) Solar Dice + CE modifier fire or radiant damage. Choose the type when the attack is made.

At level 11, range becomes 300/1,200 feet and X may be up to twice PB. At level 18, range becomes 600/2,400 feet and X has no ceiling other than available CE. Cruel Sun can critically hit and qualify for Black Flash.

Night does not delete the character: rank 0 uses d4s and removes daytime benefits; it never lowers purchased ability scores, HP, AC, class features, or weapon proficiencies. A Pride Incarnate at midnight is the character’s ordinary body, not a second underpowered character sheet.

Level 1 choice

Choose how the sun inhabits you.

The incarnation is selected when Sunshine is gained and normally cannot be switched. It changes features, not the shared Solar Station, Cruel Sun, or CE Reserve.

Escanor version · Pride Incarnate

The body rises with the sun

While your effective rank is at least 1, your Strength score becomes the listed floor if it was lower: 12 + (2 × rank). Add 2 to the floor at level 11 and another 2 at level 18. This temporary score governs attacks, damage, checks, saves, carrying, and jumping, but not permanent prerequisites.

When initiative is rolled, gain temporary HP equal to character level × effective rank. You gain it once for that combat; later rank changes do not refresh it.

Once per turn · on melee hit · 1 CE

Lion’s Heat

After you hit with a melee weapon, unarmed strike, or natural weapon carrying your CE, but before damage is rolled, spend 1 CE to add PB Solar Dice of fire or radiant damage. These dice are part of the triggering attack, double on a critical hit, and enter the completed critical total if it becomes a Black Flash.

The attack need not use an axe, Strength, or a cursed tool. Pride rewards close combat without prescribing the character’s weapon.

Mael version · Solar Grace

The body circulates the sun

While your effective rank is at least 1, you gain a flying speed of 20 + (10 × rank) feet and can hover. This can appear as wings, a solar halo, or unsupported flight. If rank becomes 0 while you lack another flying speed, you fall normally.

When one of your Sunshine areas affects multiple targets, you may choose fire or radiant separately for each affected creature. Pride Incarnate instead chooses one damage type for the entire activation.

Once per turn · on any qualifying hit · 1 CE

Solar Infusion

After your weapon, unarmed, natural, or CE attack carrying your CE hits, but before damage is rolled, spend 1 CE to add PB Solar Dice of fire or radiant damage. It can enhance Cruel Sun and other Sunshine attack rolls. The dice belong to the triggering attack and therefore interact normally with critical hits and Black Flash.

Solar Grace trades Pride’s Strength floor and temporary HP for a broader trigger, flight, and precise energy circulation.

One technique, two player versions: copying Sunshine copies the shared base expression unless the copying feature can copy a progression feature. A copied Cruel Sun does not automatically grant Pride Incarnate, Solar Grace, The One, or Greatest Sun.

Levels 3–6

Solar arsenal.

These are the complete procedures referenced by the progression. Unless stated otherwise, an area rolls damage once for all affected creatures.

Level 3 · after Cruel Sun · bonus action · 2 CE

Pride Flare

After a Cruel Sun attack resolves, center an explosion on the attack’s target or endpoint. Its radius is 10 + (5 × effective rank) feet. You and objects you wear or carry are unaffected. Every other creature in the area makes a Dexterity save, taking PB Solar Dice of fire or radiant damage on failure or half on success.

This is a separate saving-throw activation. It cannot critically hit or trigger Black Flash, and the original target can take both Cruel Sun and Pride Flare damage.

Level 6 · action · PB CE

Crazy Prominence

Make PB ranged CE attacks and distribute them among creatures within 150 feet. Each hit deals 2 Solar Dice; add your CE modifier to one hit of the activation. At level 11, range becomes 300 feet and each hit deals 3 Solar Dice. At level 18, range becomes 600 feet and each hit deals 4 Solar Dice.

Each attack may critically hit and attempt Black Flash independently. Solar Infusion or another once-per-turn rider applies only as often as that rider currently permits.

Pride · level 3 · bonus action · 2 CE

Sun-Rider

Move through the air up to 30 + (10 × effective rank) feet without spending speed or provoking opportunity attacks. You may pass through creature spaces but not solid objects and must end in an unoccupied space. If unsupported afterward, you fall normally.

Pride · level 3 · passive

Sun-Wrought Stature

At rank 2+, count as one size larger for carrying, lifting, grappling, and shoving. At rank 3+, you may physically become Large or return to normal size without an action; equipment resizes, occupied space becomes 10 feet, and melee reach increases by 5 feet. If there is insufficient room, gain only the virtual-size benefit.

Grace · level 3 · replaces one attack · 2 CE

Solar Fist

Project concentrated sunlight through a 60-foot-long, 5-foot-wide line. Creatures in it make a Dexterity save, taking 3 Solar Dice + CE modifier fire or radiant damage on failure or half on success. Damage becomes 5 Solar Dice at level 11 and 7 Solar Dice at level 18.

Solar Fist is a saving-throw effect rather than an attack and cannot critically hit or trigger Black Flash.

Grace · level 3 · passive

Fine Control

Choose any number of creatures whenever you create a Sunshine area. Chosen creatures automatically succeed on its saving throw and take no damage from that activation. This choice does not protect objects, structures, barriers, or creatures you did not choose.

Pride · level 6 · action · PB CE

Super Slash

Make PB melee weapon, unarmed, or natural-weapon attacks. During the sequence, you may move up to your speed in total; a creature you hit cannot make an opportunity attack against this movement until the sequence ends. These are ordinary rolled attacks, but Super Slash is not the Attack action and receives no attacks granted specifically by taking that action.

Each attack can critically hit and attempt Black Flash. Lion’s Heat remains once per turn until The One changes its limit.

Pride · level 6 · reaction · 2 CE

Prideful Endurance

When you take damage, roll PB Solar Dice and add your current Strength modifier. Reduce that damage by the total, to a minimum of 0. This reduction occurs before a limb-damage threshold is checked but cannot undo an explicit break, sever, or other consequence that has already resolved.

Grace · level 6 · once per turn · 2 CE

Sunspot

After a creature takes Sunshine damage, mark it with a visible sunspot for 1 minute without Technique Focus. Maintain one mark. It detonates at the first of the following: the creature starts its turn, voluntarily moves a total of 10 feet, or activates an Innate Technique feature. The creature makes a Dexterity save, taking PB Solar Dice on failure or half on success. On failure it is also blinded until the end of its next turn. The mark then ends.

The detonation is not an attack and cannot critically hit or trigger Black Flash.

Grace · level 6 · passive

Orbital Origin

Cruel Sun and each Crazy Prominence attack may originate from you or an unoccupied point you perceive within 60 feet. Determine range, cover, and line of effect from that origin. Origin range becomes 150 feet at level 11 and 300 feet at level 18. The origin cannot be inside a creature or solid object.

Solar Sovereignty: from level 6, you are immune to damage from your own Sunshine features and have resistance to fire and radiant damage while effective rank is at least 1. Passive heat may illuminate, scorch, or announce your presence in the fiction, but deals no unlisted damage and does not automatically destroy equipment or terrain.

Levels 11–18

Two answers to high noon.

Pride lets the vessel become stronger than survival. Grace keeps the vessel intact and turns refinement into repeatable celestial artillery.

Pride · level 11 · true Zenith

The One

Whenever your effective rank becomes 4, you enter The One without an action or CE. It lasts only while rank remains 4 and requires no Technique Focus. At level 11, Pride cannot store rank 4, so The One normally comes from the ten-round true Zenith. Your level-18 Domain can also create it.

  • Your Strength becomes 26 if lower. You become Large if smaller, gain 5 feet of melee reach, and gain 30 feet of speed.
  • When the form begins, gain temporary HP equal to five times your level. These replace lower temporary HP rather than stacking.
  • You have resistance to all damage, immunity to fire and radiant damage, immunity to frightened, and cannot be knocked prone or moved against your will.
  • When you take the Attack action, make one additional melee weapon, unarmed, or natural-weapon attack as part of it.
  • You may use Lion’s Heat once on each qualifying melee hit, up to PB times per turn, paying 1 CE each time.
The One · action · 5 CE

Divine Sword

Cut through a 30-foot cone with a bare hand or held weapon. Creatures in the cone make a Dexterity save, taking 10 Solar Dice + Strength modifier slashing or radiant damage on failure or half on success. Unattended objects, structures, barriers, and technique constructs take maximum rolled damage.

Divine Sword is a save effect and cannot critically hit or trigger Black Flash.

The One · replaces one attack · 5 CE

Divine Spear

Make one melee weapon, unarmed, or natural-weapon attack with 15-foot reach. On a hit, add 10 Solar Dice of piercing or radiant damage. The target then makes a Constitution save; on failure it is stunned until the end of its next turn.

The added dice are part of the attack, double on a critical, and enter the completed critical total for Black Flash.

Grace · level 11

Perfect Grace

Your Solar Grace flying speed doubles. Whenever you roll Solar Dice, reroll each 1 or 2 once and use the new result. At rank 4, Solar Infusion may be used once on each qualifying hit, up to PB times per turn, paying 1 CE per hit. Fine Control may exempt any creatures you choose, as normal.

Solar Authority applies to both incarnations: all Sunshine damage ignores resistance, and at rank 3+ matching immunity becomes resistance.

Grace · level 11 · named Maximum · action · 10 CE

Maximum: Greatest Sun

Choose a point you perceive within 600 feet. A miniature sun grows as it crosses the sky and detonates there. Its radius is 30 feet if the point is within 150 feet of you, or 60 feet if farther away. Creatures you choose in the area make Dexterity saves, taking (10 + PB) Solar Dice + CE modifier fire or radiant damage on failure or half on success. Unattended objects, structures, and barriers take maximum rolled damage.

This is a named Maximum Technique. It cannot critically hit, trigger Black Flash, or receive generic Maximum Output or Chanting.

Pride · level 18 · fatal vow

The One Ultimate

When The One would end, you may refuse its end without an action, provided you are conscious and the technique is not being forcibly suppressed. Record your maximum HP as your Last Dawn maximum and enter The One Ultimate. The final vow persists through the Technique Burnout caused by the Pride Domain that triggered it; that Burnout does not disable Sunshine while the Ultimate remains.

  • You retain every benefit of The One, your Strength becomes 28 if lower, and your Sunshine damage ignores both resistance and immunity.
  • At the start of each turn after the first, reduce current and maximum HP by twice your character level. This is not damage, bypasses temporary HP, and cannot be prevented or restored while the Ultimate lasts. Reaching 0 maximum HP kills you.
  • At the start of each turn, gain one restricted extra action for one melee attack, Cruel Sun, Dash, or Disengage. Distinct extra-action features can coexist normally.
  • The Ultimate has no arbitrary round limit. It ends when you dismiss it, become incapacitated, die, or lose Sunshine. When it ends for any reason, your body burns away and you die. Ordinary RCT cannot prevent or reverse this consequence; campaign-level resurrection may restore you if it can recreate the body.
The One Ultimate · full-turn action · all current CE, minimum 15

Final Prominence

Choose a point within 1,200 feet and spend all current CE. A final sun fills a 150-foot-radius sphere. You may exempt yourself and a number of creatures up to your CE modifier, minimum 0. Every other creature makes a Dexterity save, taking (20 + CE spent)d12 + Last Dawn maximum HP fire or radiant damage on failure or half on success. Unattended objects, structures, and barriers take maximum dice plus the flat damage.

Final Prominence is instantaneous, cannot critically hit or trigger Black Flash, and cannot receive Maximum Output or Chanting. After it resolves—even if every target avoids or negates it—the Ultimate ends and its fatal consequence occurs.

Grace · level 18

Sun Without Horizon

Your effective rank can never be lower than 2 while Sunshine is available, even at night or on a sunless plane. During each true Zenith, if conscious and not in Technique Burnout, you may automatically store rank 4 in your internal Solar Memory, replacing a lower stored rank if desired. This is the only way Solar Grace can preserve true Zenith. Release it using the normal 2 CE bonus action; it lasts 1 minute and is then expended.

Grace · level 18

Absolute Circulation

Solar Infusion may be used up to PB times per turn at every station, once per qualifying hit and for 1 CE each. Maintain up to PB Sunspots. Once per Crazy Prominence activation, you may mark each different creature hit by paying 2 CE per mark, up to available capacity.

Greatest Sun overcharge

No artificial ceiling

When using Greatest Sun, spend any additional CE beyond its 10 CE cost. Each additional CE adds one Solar Die to its damage. For every 5 additional CE, increase its radius by 5 feet. The full payment remains one named Maximum activation and is not copied by Maximum Output.

Level 18 · Domain Expansion

The barrier decides that it is noon.

Each incarnation receives its own complete Domain and a distinct initiative-count-20 sure-hit event. Both use the campaign’s 60-foot complete-Domain baseline, clash procedure, barrier statistics, and Technique Burnout.

Pride Domain

Throne Beneath a Merciless Noon

Full-turn action, 20 CE: create an enclosed 60-foot-radius Domain centered on you for 1 minute. Its interior is a glassed plain below one overwhelming sun. Your effective rank becomes 4 and you enter The One for the Domain’s duration.

Pride arena

The throne strengthens; it does not aim

Your rank-4 body, The One, and ordinary Sunshine features function throughout the Domain, but their attacks and saving throws resolve normally. Sovereign Pressure at initiative count 20 is this Domain’s sole sure-hit event.

Initiative 20 · Sovereign Pressure

Kneel beneath the only sun

Each chosen hostile creature automatically takes 8 Solar Dice + CE modifier radiant damage, falls prone, and cannot take reactions until the next initiative count 20. A creature protected from the sure-hit instead makes a Constitution save, taking half damage and suffering neither rider on success. Creatures immune to prone suffer the damage and reaction loss only.

Grace Domain

Empyrean of the Greatest Sun

Full-turn action, 20 CE: create an enclosed 60-foot-radius Domain centered on you for 1 minute. Its interior is a weightless golden firmament filled with orbiting suns. Your effective rank becomes 4 for the duration.

Grace firmament

Every horizon becomes an origin

Cruel Sun, Crazy Prominence, and Greatest Sun may originate from any unoccupied point inside the barrier, and Fine Control continues to protect any creatures you choose. These attacks and saves resolve normally. Celestial Procession is the sole sure-hit event.

Initiative 20 · Celestial Procession · sure-hit

One sun for every condemned soul

Each chosen hostile creature automatically takes (PB + 1)d12 + CE modifier fire or radiant damage and gains a Procession Sun until the next initiative count 20. The first time before then that it voluntarily moves at least 10 feet or activates an Innate Technique feature, the sun detonates for PBd12 radiant damage and blinds it until the end of its next turn; the Procession Sun then ends.

A creature protected from the sure-hit makes a Dexterity save. On a failure it suffers the complete result; on a success it takes half initial damage and gains no Procession Sun. This Domain mark is a self-contained event and does not use or replace your ordinary Sunspot capacity.

Domain ending: the artificial station ends with the barrier. Pride’s The One therefore ends. At level 18, Pride may invoke The One Ultimate in the instant before Domain Burnout would disable the technique; this is the fatal vow’s one explicit Burnout exception. Solar Grace cannot store the Domain’s artificial Zenith.

Rules reference

No heat haze in the procedures.

Use these rulings whenever Sunshine intersects critical hits, Maximum Output, multiple suns, storage, other class features, or narrative daylight.

Technique math

Normal CE statistics

CE attack = PB + CE modifier
CE save DC = 8 + PB + CE modifier

Pride’s weapon and unarmed attacks retain their normal attack statistic unless another character feature changes it.

Solar damage

One declared type

Pride chooses fire or radiant for an entire activation. Grace may choose separately for each affected creature. This choice does not make one damage instance both types and does not bypass defenses until Solar Authority says it does.

Multiple suns

One primary station

On a world with several suns, the GM identifies the primary sun when the campaign or scene begins. Secondary suns do not add ranks or dice. A plane with no meaningful sun is Night unless Sun Without Horizon or a Domain overrides it.

Criticals and Black Flash

Preserve the Project ruling

Cruel Sun, Lion’s Heat, Solar Infusion, Crazy Prominence, Super Slash, and Divine Spear use attack rolls and may participate in critical hits. A rider declared after the hit but before damage belongs to that attack, so its dice double on the critical. After any qualifying critical, roll the second unmodified d20; a natural 20 makes Black Flash and triples the entire completed critical damage. There is no frequency cap.

Save effects

No attack roll, no critical

Pride Flare, Solar Fist, Sunspot, Divine Sword, Greatest Sun, Final Prominence, and Domain pulse saving throws cannot critically hit or Black Flash. A Domain’s automatic hit is not a critical unless another rule explicitly declares it one.

Maximum Output

Overcharge one ordinary activation

Maximum Output doubles the full cost of the chosen activation and applies one current core mode. For Cruel Sun, Damage adds half of its original X + 1 dice, rounded up. For Lion’s Heat or Solar Infusion it adds half PB Solar Dice, rounded up. For Pride Flare it adds half PB Solar Dice or increases the final radius by half, depending on chosen mode.

Maximum Output sequences

Distribute one enhancement

On Crazy Prominence or Super Slash, Accuracy applies to every attack in that activation. For Crazy Prominence, Damage adds half the activation’s original Solar Dice, rounded up, distributed among successful attacks as you choose. Super Slash supplies no technique damage dice of its own, so Damage adds nothing and never copies weapon dice. The One, Greatest Sun, The One Ultimate, Final Prominence, and either Domain are named pinnacle features and cannot receive Maximum Output.

Storage

No recursive sunlight

Only the actual celestial station can be stored. A released memory, artificial sun, illusion, Maximum, or Domain cannot charge another memory. Two memories never stack; use only the highest effective rank.

Pride vessel

Any bonded object

Pride stores Solar Memory in one weapon or worn object bonded during the storage rite. If it is destroyed or leaves your possession, the memory is inaccessible but not automatically erased; rebonding requires another rite. Actual sunlight and all non-storage features still function.

Grace vessel

Internal and undroppable

Grace stores Solar Memory internally. It cannot be stolen or disarmed, but is suppressed whenever Sunshine itself is suppressed. Zenith storage at level 18 still occurs only once per actual solar Zenith.

Sunspot stacking

One mark per creature

A creature can carry only one of your Sunspots. Applying another replaces the earlier mark without detonating it. The Grace Domain ignores your total mark capacity but not this per-creature rule.

Technique Focus

Most sunlight is instantaneous

Only a released Solar Memory before level 11 uses Technique Focus. The One, Sunspot, and ordinary attack applications do not. Domains and Technique Burnout follow their own universal procedures.

Changing size

The space must exist

If Pride cannot fit as Large, it gains virtual size but does not expand its occupied space or reach. Returning to normal size never crushes or ejects another creature. Worn and held equipment resizes but gains no extra damage dice merely from size.

No Reversal

Absence is intentional

Sunshine has no Cursed Technique Reversal. RCT remains available through universal rules but does not create moonlight, cold, darkness, or an inverted incarnation. The technique already expresses two legitimate directions without inventing an inversion.

Ultimate life burn

Not damage, healing, or an injury

The One Ultimate’s maximum-HP loss cannot be reduced, transferred, absorbed by temporary HP, healed by RCT, or avoided through damage immunity. If an effect suppresses Sunshine, the form ends and its stated death occurs. A resurrection that recreates the body returns the character with their ordinary pre-vow maximum HP unless that effect says otherwise.

Action stacking

Specific actions remain distinct

Cruel Sun and Solar Fist replace attacks only inside the Attack action. Crazy Prominence and Super Slash are technique actions, not Attack actions. The One’s additional attack joins the Attack action. The One Ultimate’s restricted extra action is a distinct explicit action and can coexist with other differently named extra actions.

Adaptation boundary

Sunshine is the power—not the entire source character

  • Pride does not force arrogance, a personality change, a particular gender, an axe, or a weak alternate character sheet at night.
  • Grace does not automatically grant angel ancestry, Ark, reincarnation magic, commandments, or non-Sunshine spells.
  • Passive flavor heat supplies light and spectacle but no hidden damage, regeneration denial, curse removal, or equipment destruction.
  • A character may describe existing class, ancestry, tool, feat, and RCT features through the chosen solar aesthetic without receiving duplicate mechanics.
Four Innate Technique feats

Every sun chooses what it nourishes.

Two feats are shared; one belongs to Pride and one to Grace. Each occupies a normal feat choice, may be taken only once, and this is Sunshine’s complete four-feat list.

Prerequisite: Solar Memory

Twin Solar Memory

You may maintain two stored solar ranks at once. Pride uses two separately bonded weapons or worn objects; Grace holds two internal memories. Releasing one does not expend the other, but only one memory can be active at a time. A stored true Zenith rank fills both slots and replaces both prior memories.

Prerequisite: Sunshine

Corona of Command

While your effective rank is at least 1, chosen allies within 10 feet have advantage on saves against being frightened. At the start of each of your turns, choose one such ally; it gains temporary HP equal to PB + effective rank. At rank 4 the aura becomes 30 feet and the temporary HP also adds your CE modifier.

Prerequisite: Pride Incarnate

Night-Prowling Lion

At effective rank 0, your Strength has a floor of 14, increasing to 16 at level 11 and 18 at level 18. You may use Lion’s Heat at rank 0, treating its Solar Dice as d4s. This does not raise your effective rank, create sunlight, enable The One, or activate any other feature that requires daylight.

Prerequisite: Solar Infusion, level 6

Grace Bestowed

When a willing creature within 120 feet hits with a weapon, unarmed, natural, or CE attack carrying its own CE, use your reaction and pay Solar Infusion’s normal 1 CE to add your current Solar Infusion damage to that hit. This use counts against every per-turn Solar Infusion limit and receives the same station and Perfect Grace multipliers.

Character guide

Build the vessel you want to play.

The technique supports maximal builds, imperfect builds, and characters whose strongest hour is deliberately inconvenient.

Pride ability priorities

Strength need not begin at 20

Constitution improves survival and CE ability powers Cruel Sun and saves. The solar Strength floor lets a modest starting Strength become formidable in daylight while a naturally exceptional Strength remains meaningful at night.

Pride weapons

Rhitta is an inspiration, not a tax

Heavy weapons suit the fantasy, but a sword, spear, staff, gauntlet, improvised weapon, natural weapon, or bare hand can carry Lion’s Heat and Super Slash.

Pride planning

Schedule the impossible

Before level 18, The One is earned through actual campaign time. Negotiate noon confrontations, preserve Near-Noon for night operations, and decide whether a final vow is worth more than the character’s future.

Grace ability priorities

CE governs the celestial weapon

Wisdom or Charisma improves attack rolls, saving throws, and flat technique damage. Dexterity and Constitution still matter, but constant flight and Fine Control let a non-maximal stat line fight intelligently.

Grace positioning

Move the origin, not only the user

Orbital Origin defeats cover and opens crossfire without granting attacks through solid walls. Preserve distance for Greatest Sun’s larger radius and use Fine Control to keep allies inside an otherwise catastrophic area.

Grace planning

Preserve one real Zenith

At level 18, true noon becomes a portable campaign resource rather than a rest recharge. Spend it when d12 Solar Dice, multiplied infusions, and a Greatest Sun must coexist outside the Domain.

Shared combat loop: establish the effective station → attack with Cruel Sun or the character’s normal offense → add the incarnation rider → detonate or multiply suns → decide whether the current light should be spent, preserved, or escalated into a pinnacle.

Design record

Lore translated, not transplanted.

The source material establishes the governing ideas; the numbers, action economy, domains, storage rule, and player protections are campaign mechanics.

Pride references
