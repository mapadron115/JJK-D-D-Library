Kinetic Transmission

A living ten-speed cursed engine that stores motion as Gear, spends only the power a Shift requires, reverses hostile technique transmission, and ultimately imposes an open mechanical Domain upon reality.

Tier V endpoint.

The ordinary engine begins as an Apex martial technique, reaches Special Grade through Redline, and becomes Calamity only after Burn Out and the open-barrier Zugzwang Engine are earned. Its ceiling is intentionally exceptional; its procedure now uses one tracked number.

Core engine

Ten Gears. One number.

Maximum Revolutions and Transmission Limit are removed. Current Gear is both stored motion and the amount available for every Shift.

Activation

Wind-Up Engine

As a bonus action, spend 1 CE to activate for 1 minute. Set Current Gear equal to PB. The technique ends if you become incapacitated or dismiss it without an action; remaining Gear is then lost.

Current Gear: 0–10

Generation

One end-of-turn check

  • Gain 1 Gear if you moved at least 15 feet.
  • Gain 1 Gear if you hit a hostile creature.
  • From level 3, doing both grants 1 additional Gear, for 3 total.

Harmless practice and defenseless objects cannot generate Gear.

Lowering Gear

Spend only what the Shift needs

When a Shift says to lower Gear by X, choose any positive X up to Current Gear. Reduce Current Gear by X after declaring the effect. A Shift never automatically empties the engine; Gear reaches 0 only when you deliberately spend everything remaining.

Technique mathematics

Choose Wisdom or Charisma

Choose Wisdom or Charisma as the technique’s CE ability when you gain Kinetic Transmission.

CE attack = PB + CE modifier
CE save DC = 8 + PB + CE modifier

Acceleration Shift

Convert Gear into movement

Without using an action, lower Gear by X. Your speed increases by 15 feet per Gear until the end of the current turn.

Impact Shift

Transmit the collision

Once per turn after a qualifying melee hit but before damage, lower Gear by X. Add X × (Drive Die + CE modifier) force damage. A target no more than one size larger makes a Strength save or is pushed 10 feet per Gear in a direction you choose.

Black Flash

Part of the completed attack

Because Impact Shift is declared after the hit but before damage, it becomes part of that attack’s critical damage and is multiplied by Black Flash under the comprehensive rules.

No separate spending ceiling: Current Gear is the limit. A character at Gear 7 can distribute those seven Gears among every legal Shift they perform, but cannot spend an eighth until Gear is generated again.

Levels 1–18

From motion to law.

The core progression keeps the technique’s original breadth while expressing every numerical application through Current Gear.

1

Wind-Up Engine

Activate, generate Gear, accelerate, and transmit impact.

Activation and generation

Bonus action, 1 CE, 1 minute. Begin at Gear equal to PB. At turn end gain 1 for moving 15 feet and 1 for hitting a hostile creature.

First two Shifts

Acceleration Shift buys 15 feet per Gear. Impact Shift adds one Drive Die plus CE modifier and 10 feet of possible push per Gear.

3

Clutch Control

Faster generation, repeated impacts, impossible footwork, and braking.

Continuous Cycle

If you moved at least 15 feet and hit a hostile creature, gain 3 Gears at the end of the turn instead of 2.

Clutchless Shifting

You may use Impact Shift more than once during your turn. Each use lowers Gear separately; Current Gear is the only total limit.

Inertial Footwork

After using Acceleration Shift, difficult terrain costs no extra movement, you may change direction freely, cross liquids and vertical surfaces, and opportunity attacks against you have disadvantage.

Brake Shift

When you take damage or are forcibly moved, use your reaction and lower Gear by X. For every Gear, choose one: reduce damage by one Drive Die + CE modifier; reduce forced movement by 15 feet; or reduce effective falling distance by 30 feet. Different Gears may select different options.

6

Velocity Override

Mark creatures and objects, then rewrite their permitted movement.

Transmission Marks

Whenever you hit a creature, or touch a willing creature or object, mark it without an action for 1 minute. Maintain marks up to PB. Applying another beyond the limit ends one existing mark of your choice.

Override

As a bonus action—or reaction when a marked target moves, falls, or attacks—pay 3 CE and lower Gear by X. The target must be within 120 feet. An unwilling creature makes a Constitution save.

Up-Shift

Move the target 20 feet per Gear in any direction without opportunity attacks. A solid collision deals two Drive Dice per Gear and knocks it prone. For a creature collision, the other creature makes a Dexterity save; failure deals the collision damage and knocks both prone.

Down-Shift

Reduce speed by 15 feet per Gear until the beginning of your next turn. The target cannot react, and its AC and Dexterity saves fall by 1 per Gear, maximum PB. At speed 0 it is restrained for the duration.

11

Perfect Transmission & Redline

Greater statistics, efficient shifting, and a temporary maximum-output engine.

Perfect Transmission

Strength, Dexterity, and CE scores and their maximums increase by 2. Once per turn after an ordinary Acceleration, Impact, Brake, or Override Shift lowers Gear, regain 1 of the Gears spent. This refund does not apply to Burn Out, Dead Ignition, or Relativistic Break.

Maximum Technique: Redline

While active, use a bonus action and spend 10 CE. Set Current Gear to 10. Redline lasts PB rounds, and Current Gear returns to 10 at the beginning of each of your turns.

Redline benefits

  • Speed is tripled; movement does not provoke.
  • Move across vertical surfaces, liquids, and open air; stop or reverse instantly.
  • You cannot be forcibly moved unless you choose.
  • Gain one additional action for one attack, Dash, Disengage, or Use an Object.
  • Once per turn, use Velocity Override without CE or an action; Gear still lowers normally.
  • Once per round, use Brake Shift without consuming your reaction; Gear still lowers normally.

Recoil

When Redline ends, take two Drive Dice of force damage per round it remained active. This damage ignores resistance, immunity, and reduction. Gain one exhaustion level, Kinetic Transmission ends, and suffer normal Technique Burnout.

18

First Ignition

Diagnose an open Domain and answer the next expansion with Zugzwang Engine.

Conceptual prerequisite

You must personally witness or correctly diagnose an open-barrier Domain. Level 18 provides the output; the observed Domain provides the blueprint.

Reactive blossom

The next time you perceive another creature begin Domain Expansion, use your reaction and pay the normal 20 CE to answer with Zugzwang Engine. Resolve any clash normally. Afterward, you may expand it normally with a full-turn action.

Cursed Technique Reversal

Burn Out

Positive energy gives a marked hostile technique negative inertia, making its activation collapse backward through the pathways that produced it.

Requirements

RCT, an active engine, and a mark

You must possess Reverse Cursed Technique, have Kinetic Transmission active, perceive the marked target within 120 feet, and retain the reaction, CE, Healing Reserve, and Gear required by the chosen application.

Reversal Feedback trigger: the marked creature activates an Innate Technique feature.

Reaction and cost: 4 CE, 20 points from your RCT Healing Reserve, and lower Gear by X from 1 to your Current Gear.

The target makes a Constitution save. On failure, the feature fails, its action and CE remain spent, and all of its Innate Technique features are burned out until the end of its next turn. On success, the feature resolves and it takes half backlash with no Burnout.

Backlash

Negative-Inertia Recoil

Deal X × (Drive Die + CE modifier) force damage. This is separate save-based damage and cannot critically hit or Black Flash.

Severe failure

Engine Seizure

If you begin the reaction at Gear 10, lower all 10 Gears, and the target fails by 5 or more, its Innate Technique remains burned out for up to 1 minute. It repeats the Constitution save at the end of each turn, ending the seizure on success.

Domain countermeasure

Dead Ignition

When a marked target begins Domain Expansion, use your reaction at Gear 10. Spend 8 CE and 40 RCT Reserve, then lower all 10 Gears. The target saves with advantage. Failure prevents stabilization, spends its CE, deals 10 × (Drive Die + CE modifier) force, and causes normal Domain Burnout. Success opens the Domain and deals half backlash.

Limits by fiction: Burn Out cannot interrupt a resolved effect, ordinary reinforcement, Black Flash, Heavenly Restriction, passive biology, or an independently operating cursed tool. Removing the Transmission Mark severs the reversal connection.

Open-barrier Domain

Perpetual Motion: Zugzwang Engine

An immense mechanical chessboard is imposed directly upon reality. Its open escape route is the binding vow supporting a 175-foot signature sure-hit event.

Expansion

Open Circuit

Full-turn action, 20 CE, 1 minute. The Domain is centered on a fixed King Gear at the point of expansion. Its 175-foot radius does not move with you. Creatures can enter or leave normally; there is no enclosing shell to attack.

Ending and counterplay

A conceptual anchor

Ordinary attacks cannot damage the King Gear. The Domain ends through duration, dismissal, your death or incapacitation, a successful Domain clash, or an effect expressly able to extinguish a Domain. Anti-domain techniques suppress Compulsory Gear’s guarantee normally.

Continuous Redline

The world powers the engine

Kinetic Transmission and Redline remain active without CE upkeep or personal Redline recoil. Current Gear becomes 10 at the beginning of each of your turns.

Absolute Transmission

Everything bears the mark

Every creature and movable object in range counts as marked. Velocity Override costs no CE but retains its normal saving throw and still lowers Gear by the chosen amount. Compulsory Gear is the Domain’s sole sure-hit event.

Conservation Law

Every decision moves the King

Whenever a hostile creature moves at least 10 feet or uses an action, bonus action, or reaction, you may immediately move 10 feet without spending movement or provoking. This needs no reaction, can trigger repeatedly, and does not retrigger itself.

Initiative 20 · sole sure-hit

Compulsory Gear

At initiative count 20, every hostile creature in the Domain suffers exactly one Compulsory Gear of your choice for that creature. A creature cannot suffer both during the same event. A creature protected from the sure-hit uses the save described by its selected Gear.

Zero Gear

An unprotected creature takes 10d12 force, becomes paralyzed, and becomes immovable except through Kinetic Transmission until the next initiative count 20. A protected creature makes a Constitution save, suffering the complete result on a failure or half damage with no condition on a success.

Terminal Gear

An unprotected creature takes 10d12 force, moves up to 120 feet along your chosen path, and falls prone. Until the next initiative count 20, it takes 4d12 force whenever it uses an action, bonus action, reaction, or moves at least 10 feet. A protected creature makes a Dexterity save, suffering the complete result on a failure or half initial damage with no movement, prone, or recurring damage on a success.

When the engine stops

Burnout without Redline recoil

When the Domain ends, its Continuous Redline ends without applying Redline’s personal recoil damage or exhaustion. You still suffer the comprehensive Domain Burnout: technique lock and 2 exhaustion levels for 1 minute, both ending immediately if you land a Black Flash.

Four Innate Technique feats

Advanced applications

Each feat applies the same ten-Gear language instead of creating another subsystem. These four are the complete list and each may be taken only once.

Prerequisite: Kinetic Transmission

Flywheel Counter

When you take bludgeoning, piercing, slashing, force, or thunder damage, use your reaction and spend 2 CE. Roll PB Drive Dice and add CE modifier once, reducing the damage by the total. Gain 1 Gear per Drive Die, maximum Gear 10. If inactive, Kinetic Transmission activates at Gear 0 before these Gears are gained.

Prerequisite: Velocity Override, level 6

Suspended Interval

Projectile: reaction and 3 CE; stop one moving physical projectile within 60 feet for up to 1 minute. Maintain up to PB and release any number as a bonus action, making ranged CE attacks.

Creature: action and 5 CE; a marked creature within 60 feet makes a Constitution save or becomes restrained, immovable, and unable to react until the end of your next turn.

Prerequisite: Clutch Control, CE 18+

Vector Skip

When using Acceleration Shift, pay 1 additional CE. For every Gear lowered, teleport 20 feet instead of gaining 15 feet of speed. Bring one willing adjacent creature by lowering one additional Gear. Your first Impact Shift that turn adds one Drive Die.

Prerequisite: Redline, CE 20+, breakthrough

Relativistic Break

While at Gear 10 during Redline, as a full-turn action spend 10 CE and lower all 10 Gears. Travel instantly through a 300-foot, 15-foot-wide line. Creatures make Dexterity saves: failure takes 20d12 force, moves to the end, falls prone, and cannot react; success takes half only. Damage ignores resistance; immunity becomes resistance. Structures automatically fail and take maximum damage. Redline ends; take 4 Drive Dice unavoidable recoil and suffer Technique Burnout.

At the table

Four decisions

The complete technique can be run without remembering any hidden caps.

Build

Activate at Gear equal to PB. Move and hit; resolve one end-of-turn Gear increase.

Shift

Choose X, lower Current Gear by that amount, and use the matching movement, damage, defense, or control formula.

Reverse

Keep priority targets marked and preserve CE, RCT Reserve, a reaction, and enough Gear for Burn Out.

Expand

At level 18, open Zugzwang Engine, refill to Gear 10 each turn, and impose one Compulsory Gear on every hostile creature.
