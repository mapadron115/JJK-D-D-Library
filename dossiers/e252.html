Disaster Vermin

A living-swarm horror package: insect swarms that are actual insects rather than shikigami, pouch shikigami at your side, swarms that merge into an Almighty Swarm, and Parthenogenesis, an egg that births you again if you die within the hour.

Power tierTier III · Apex
Combat roleSupport / controller
Core engineVermin of Disaster
Domain · pinnacleBrood Cathedral (L18)

Field notes. Subject: the brood. Three truths govern her. First, the flesh is her own: these are actual insects made from her cursed energy, not shikigami, what she commands is a piece of her own body, dispersed. Second, the congregation: one swarm, then a proficiency bonus of swarms, then the Almighty Swarm, the many merging into one, larger with every joining, pooling wounds and bites. Strength is not added here; it is multiplied by gathering. Third, the thesis: Parthenogenesis. An egg on a wall, eight coins, cheaper for every creature devoured, die within the hour and the egg births her again at half health, her energy, none of her gear. The body was never the point; the brood was. Maxim: kill the sorcerer and the nest remains. The nest was never in one place.

Full progression

Feature map

1Vermin of Disaster.

The beginning: two coins, an action, a space within five feet, and the first insects of her own energy. They dodge without orders. They are not trained; they are loyal. Beginning at 1st level, as an action for 2 cursed energy you create an Insect Swarm in a space you can see within 5 ft of you. You can command the swarm as a bonus action, otherwise it takes the dodge action. Your Insect Swarm is made up of actual insects created from your cursed energy, not shikigami and you can only have one Insect Swarm summoned at a time.

Insect Swarm, stat block (invented interim). The V6 source prints no swarm statistics; every value below is campaign-designed, not source-verified. Medium swarm of Tiny beasts (actual insects, not shikigami). AC 12 (invented). Hit points equal to twice your level (invented). Speed 20 ft., climb 20 ft. (invented). Damage resistances bludgeoning, piercing, slashing (invented). Bites. Melee attack: +PB + your CE modifier to hit (invented), reach 5 ft., one target; Hit: 1d6 + your CE modifier piercing damage (invented). The swarm is commanded as a bonus action; if not commanded, it takes the Dodge action (per Vermin of Disaster).

3Earthen Insect Trance.

At the third, the nest expands: swarms up to her proficiency bonus, all commanded in one bonus action, plus the pouch shikigami, two at a time, riding the same command. At 3rd level, your mastery over insects has grown. You can have a number of Insect Swarms summoned equal to your proficiency bonus and can command any number of them as a bonus action. Additionally, you gain access to an insect shikigami with a large pouch. As an action, you can summon up to 2 shikigami, spending 2 cursed energy per shikigami to summon them in an unoccupied space within 10 ft of you. You can command the Shikigami and Insect Swarm as part of the same bonus action.

6Almighty Swarm.

At the sixth, the congregation: swarms within five feet merge, growing a size per swarm, pooling hit points and damage dice. The many become one, and the one is terrible. At 6th level, you have learned the power of the almighty swarm. As part of the bonus action to command your Insect Swarms, you can order different swarms to move closer and combine into one Vermin Swarm if within 5 ft of each other, becoming 1 size larger for every additional swarm. The Vermin Swarm gains the combined hit points and damage dice of all the swarms together. The individual Insect Swarms that make up the Vermin Swarms still count against your maximum number of controlled swarms.

11Parthenogenesis.

The thesis, made flesh: an egg on a wall, eight coins, cheaper for every creature devoured in the last eight hours. Die within the hour and the egg births her again, half health, her CE, none of her gear. The body was never the point. At 11th level, you have learned that your instinctual hunger is not without reason, but exists to stimulate the desire to multiply. As an action for 8 cursed energy, you create an insect egg affixed to a wall/ceiling. The cost to create the egg decreases by 1 CE per real creature devoured by you or a swarm in the last 8 hours (minimum cost 1). The egg has an AC equal to your Cursed Energy save DC and hit points equal to twice your level. Only one egg can be active at a time, creating a new one ends the old (proposed). If you die within an hour of creating the egg, the egg is consumed and births an identical copy of you in a legal adjacent space, with current hit points equal to half your hit point maximum. Your cursed energy is passed on after your death, giving your new body your remaining cursed energy from when you died. You do not regain any gear you had beforehand.

18Domain Expansion: Brood Cathedral of the Crawling God.

A cathedral built by and for the swarm: arches of chitin, pillars of compacted husk, stained glass made from ten thousand iridescent wings filtering a sickly amber light. The pews are empty cocoons, split open, and the air thrums with wingbeat, a single chord held without pause. Something vast moves in the dark behind the altar, laying, always laying.

The pinnacle: Brood Cathedral of the Crawling God. Full-turn, twenty CE, sixty feet, and its congregation is the battlefield itself. At initiative 20, three chosen enemies take 6d10 as eggs burst from their skin, and everything that dies inside is devoured and becomes a swarm that does not count against her cap. The battlefield feeds the nest. Deploying the Domain costs a full-turn action and 20 CE (invented): 60-foot radius, up to 1 minute, 500 clash points (Tier III) (invented). Finally at 18th level you have learned to expand your domain. At initiative 20, up to 3 creatures of your choice within your domain take 6d10 necrotic damage as eggs fester and burst from their skin, three distinct targets, 6d10 each, once per target. A creature that dies within your domain is devoured and becomes an Insect Swarm; one corpse becomes one swarm, not recursive own-swarm death spawning. This Insect Swarm does not count against your maximum number of controlled swarms until the domain ends; when the domain ends, excess swarms over your normal cap are dismissed (proposed).

Innate Technique Feats

Build-defining options.

These are optional character-building feats. You must meet each listed prerequisite before selecting it.

Prerequisite: Must have Vermin of Disaster

Vermin Bulwark

The swarm as shield: when a blow lands within five feet of her insects, they take it instead. The nest has always known how to lose parts to save the whole. As a reaction when a creature within 5 feet of one of your Insect Swarms is hit by an attack, you may have the swarm interpose itself; the swarm takes the damage instead of the target.

Prerequisite: Must have Almighty Swarm

Vermin Techniques

Three new intimacies: the Cloak, standing inside the swarm, its wounds become her buffer; the Grasping Hand, a grapple through the insects for three CE, the victim pulled into the swarm; and Transport Via Bugs, six CE, a link between two great swarms, and she steps through. The nest is armor, hand, and road. You have learned to better control the combined actions of your Vermin Swarms, allowing you to learn the following ways to use your Swarms: Insect Cloak. While you are occupying the same space as one of your Vermin Swarms, you can treat the Swarm's hit points as temporary hit points for yourself. Grasping Hand. You can spend 3 CE to replace one of your attacks on your turn with a grapple check through one of your Insect Swarms against a creature within 5 ft of the swarm. Instead of an Athletics check, you make a Cursed Energy (Arcana) check. The grapple gains a +1 bonus and +5 ft of range per size category above Medium the swarm is. On a success, the creature is grappled and can be pulled into the Insect Swarm. Transport Via Bugs. As an action for 6 CE, you form a link between one Large or larger Vermin Swarm within 10 ft and connect it to another Large or larger Vermin Swarm at any distance. Before the end of your turn, you can spend 5 ft of movement to walk into the space of the Insect Swarm within 5 ft, reappearing within 5 ft of the destination Insect Swarm.

Prerequisite: Must have Parthenogenesis

Festering Life Sword

Six CE, a bonus action, and a weapon becomes the Festering Life Sword, poison on a failed save, lasting a day or until she dies. The brood’s hatred, given an edge. As a bonus action for 6 CE, you transform a weapon you are holding. It loses all previous benefits and becomes the Special Grade Cursed Tool: Festering Life Sword, 1d8 slashing (invented); on a hit the target makes a Constitution save vs your CE DC or is poisoned until the end of your next turn (invented). You can only have one weapon transformed this way at a time and this tool disappears when you finish a long rest or die.

Prerequisite: Must have Vermin of Disaster

Reinforced Vermin

Two CE on the command to harden one swarm, a size larger, two extra damage dice while unbloodied. The nest, when asked, grows chitin. As part of the bonus action you take to command your Insect Swarms, you can spend 2 cursed energy to reinforce one of your Insect Swarms with cursed energy for 1 minute. While reinforced, their size category increases by one and they deal an additional 2 damage dice on a successful Bites attack (only +1 if bloodied). If you use multiple Reinforced Insect Swarms to form a Vermin Swarm, it only gains these benefits once.

Rules integration

Edge cases and shared systems.

Stat blocks: the Insect Swarm combat block is filled in the Vermin of Disaster feature above, every value is invented campaign design (the V6 source prints no swarm statistics), not verified source material. The pouch shikigami (Earthen Insect Trance) combat block remains on hold: do not run its attacks until the block is recovered.
Creature categories: keep creature categories distinct, actual insect swarms are not shikigami, and shikigami-feeding effects cannot heal an actual insect swarm. Pouch shikigami share the command bonus action with the Insect Swarms.
Almighty Swarm: the combined swarm counts all components against the proficiency-bonus cap; sum CURRENT and MAX hit points separately; the Vermin Swarm replaces its components, not duplicates them. Largest normal category is Gargantuan unless a specific oversized profile applies. Reinforcement applies only once when merging multiples; bloodied means half hit points or more lost.
Egg: the cost discount counts real devoured creatures once in the last 8 hours, no discount from harmless vermin breeding or a cooperative farm. The source gives rebirth, not two independent copies. Explicit permadeath and soul prohibitions still win over the egg.
Insect Cloak: uses the actual swarm hit points as a buffer, not copied temporary hit points that refill when moving out and back in.
Grasping Hand: 3 CE attack replacement; Cursed Energy (Arcana) grapple, +1 and +5 ft per size category above Medium; legal opposed escape against the check DC (proposed: CE save DC).
Transport Via Bugs: 6 CE action links a Large-or-larger swarm within 10 ft to another existing Large-or-larger swarm at any distance; only the caster transits, spending 5 ft of movement before the end of their turn. Proposed: the destination must be on the same plane; unlimited distance on that plane is preserved. The feat does not grant the entire party a portal unless separately expanded.
Festering Life Sword: replaces the weapon's benefits rather than stacking; one active at a time; ends on a long rest or death. No permanent harvest from the temporary sword.
Imagery note: no canon Disaster Vermin user exists; the dossier image is a thematic manga panel (the Grasshopper Curse).
