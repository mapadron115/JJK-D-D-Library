At-the-table character reference

Recovered printed values & campaign clarifications

Domain Expansion: Brood Cathedral of the Crawling God · level 18:

finally at 18th level you have learned to expand your domain. At initiative 20, up to 3 creatures of your choice within your domain take 6d10 necrotic damage as eggs fester and burst from their skin. A creature that dies within your domain is devoured and becomes an Insect Swarm. This Insect Swarm does not count against your maximum number of controlled swarms until the domain ends. Adopted: three distinct targets and 6d10 each, once per target; one corpse becomes one swarm, not recursive own-swarm death spawning. On Domain end, excess over normal cap dismissed (proposal).

Parthenogenesis egg · level 11:

as an action for 8 cursed energy, you create an insect egg affixed to a wall/ceiling. The cost to create the egg decreases by 1 CE per creature devoured by you or a swarm in the last 8 hours (min cost 1). The egg has an AC equal to your Cursed Energy save DC and hit points equal to twice your level. If you die within an hour of creating the egg, it births an identical copy of you from it with current hit points equal to half your hit point maximum. Adopted: egg cost discount counts real devoured creatures once in last 8 hours; proposed one active egg — creating a new one ends the old. On qualifying death: consume the egg, spawn in a legal adjacent space at ceil(maxHP/2), retain remaining CE, no equipment, no fresh rest resources.

Core law

Completed rules

Vermin of Disaster:

Beginning at 1st level, as an action for 2 cursed energy you create an Insect Swarm in a space you can see within 5 ft of you. You can command the swarm as a bonus action, otherwise it takes the dodge action. Your Insect Swarm is made up of actual insects created from your cursed energy, not shikigami and you can only have one Insect Swarm summoned at a time.

Earthen Insect Trance:

At 3rd level, your mastery over insects has grown. You can have a number of Insect Swarms summoned equal to your proficiency bonus and can command any number of them as a bonus action. Additionally, you gain access to an insect shikigami with a large pouch. As an action, you can summon up to 2 shikigami, spending 2 cursed energy per shikigami to summon them in an unoccupied space within 10 ft of you. You can command the Shikigami and Insect Swarm as part of the same bonus action.

Almighty Swarm:

At 6th level, you have learned the power of the almighty swarm. As part of the bonus action to command your Insect Swarms, you can order different swarms to move closer and combine into one Vermin Swarm if within 5 ft of each other, becoming 1 size larger for every additional swarm. The Vermin Swarm gains the combined hit points and damage dice of all the swarms together. The individual Insect Swarms that make up the Vermin Swarms still count against your maximum number of controlled swarms.

Innate Technique Feats

Build-defining options.

These are optional character-building feats. You must meet each listed prerequisite before selecting it.

Prerequisite: Must have Vermin of Disaster

Vermin Bulwark

As a reaction when a creature within 5 feet of one of your Insect Swarms is hit by an attack, you may have the swarm interpose itself; the swarm takes the damage instead of the target.

Prerequisite: Must have Almighty Swarm

Vermin Techniques

You have learned to better control the combined actions of your Vermin Swarms, allowing you to learn the following ways to use your Swarms: Insect Cloak. While you are occupying the same space as one of your Vermin Swarms, you can treat the Swarm's hit points as temporary hit points for yourself. Grasping Hand. You can spend 3 CE to replace one of your attacks on your turn with a grapple check through one of your Insect Swarms against a creature within 5 ft of the swarm. Instead of an Athletics check, you make a Cursed Energy (Arcana) check. The grapple gains a +1 bonus and +5 ft of range per size category above Medium the swarm is. On a success, the creature is grappled and can be pulled into the Insect Swarm. Transport Via Bugs. As an action for 6 CE, you form a link between one Large or larger Vermin Swarm within 10 ft and connect it to another Large or larger Vermin Swarm at any distance. Before the end of your turn, you can spend 5 ft of movement to walk into the space of the Insect Swarm within 5 ft, reappearing within 5 ft of the destination Insect Swarm.

Prerequisite: Must have Parthenogenesis

Festering Life Sword

As a bonus action for 6 CE, you transform a weapon you are holding. It loses all previous benefits and becomes the Special Grade Cursed Tool: Festering Life Sword. You can only have one weapon transformed this way at a time and this tool disappears when you finish a long rest or die.

Rules integration

Edge cases and shared systems.

STAT-BLOCK HOLD: swarm and pouch-shikigami combat blocks are MISSING in this text extract and remain hold. Do not run swarm attacks, Bites damage dice, or grapple contests until the blocks are filled.
Creature categories: keep creature categories distinct — actual insect swarms are not shikigami, and shikigami-feeding effects cannot heal an actual insect swarm. Pouch shikigami share the command bonus action with the Insect Swarms.
Almighty Swarm: the combined swarm counts all components against the proficiency-bonus cap; sum CURRENT and MAX hit points separately; the Vermin Swarm replaces its components, not duplicates them. Largest normal category is Gargantuan unless a specific oversized profile applies. Reinforcement applies only once when merging multiples; bloodied means half hit points or more lost.
Egg: the cost discount counts real devoured creatures once in the last 8 hours — no discount from harmless vermin breeding or a cooperative farm. Proposed: one active egg; creating a new one ends the old. The source gives rebirth, not two independent copies. Explicit permadeath and soul prohibitions still win over the egg.
Domain: three distinct targets, 6d10 each, once per target. One corpse becomes one swarm — not recursive own-swarm death spawning.
Insect Cloak: uses the actual swarm hit points as a buffer, not copied temporary hit points that refill when moving out and back in.
Grasping Hand: 3 CE attack replacement; Cursed Energy (Arcana) grapple, +1 and +5 ft per size category above Medium; legal opposed escape against the check DC (proposed: CE save DC).
Transport Via Bugs: 6 CE action links a Large-or-larger swarm within 10 ft to another existing Large-or-larger swarm at any distance; only the caster transits, spending 5 ft of movement before the end of their turn. Proposed: the destination must be on the same plane; unlimited distance on that plane is preserved. The feat does not grant the entire party a portal unless separately expanded.
Festering Life Sword: replaces the weapon's benefits rather than stacking; one active at a time; ends on a long rest or death. No permanent harvest from the temporary sword.
Imagery note: no canon Disaster Vermin user exists; the dossier image is a thematic manga panel (the Grasshopper Curse).

category increases by one and they deal an additional 2 damage dice
