Features & procedures

Exact player-facing procedures.

Weapon Equip • Level 5

Resonant Royal Edge.

Weapon Equip vibrates at supernatural frequency; its strikes carry sonic force beyond the physical blade.

On hit • 2 Reserve

Resonant Edge

Add 3 Djinn Dice thunder. Until the end of your next turn the target gains no benefit from invisibility or silence against you.

Bonus Action • 2 Reserve

Echo Kingdom

For 1 minute gain 120-ft blindsight based on vibration and sound; you can identify moving creatures through nonmagical walls up to 5 ft thick.

Action • 5 Reserve

Commanding Note

120-ft cone; Constitution save. 7 Djinn Dice thunder and pushed 60 ft on failure; half and no push on success. Failed creatures are Deafened for 1 minute.

Full Djinn Equip • Level 9

Fly 90 ft; thunder resistance; immunity to mundane deafness and advantage against auditory illusions. Your voice can be heard clearly up to 1 mile when you choose.

12 Reserve • Codex Extreme

King’s Resonance

Release a sovereign note in a 200-ft radius. Constitution save; 15 Djinn Dice thunder, half on success. Failure also Stuns until the end of your next turn and Deafens for 1 minute. Structures take maximum dice and brittle objects shatter.

Anime-scale: Strategic scale: break fortress walls, scatter formations, or silence an entire city district with a single resonance.

Rules integration

Djinn Die, Equip & Extreme Magic

Independent Djinn Reserve: 14 (Rank B). Refreshes on a long rest. Every Djinn cost below — Weapon Equip, Full Equip, abilities, reactions, Extreme Magic — spends this Djinn’s Reserve, never your CE. If this Djinn’s Reserve is empty it simply cannot fuel more until you rest; your other Djinn keep their own separate Reserves.
Weapon Equip · level 5: bonus action, 2 Reserve, lasts 10 minutes.
Full Djinn Equip · level 9: bonus action, 4 Reserve, lasts 1 minute. Only one Equip can be active.
Deep Synchronization · level 11: once per short rest, one Equip activation costs 1 Reserve less.
Extreme Magic · level 13: action; requires that Djinn’s Full Equip; pay the listed Reserve cost from this Djinn’s Reserve; usable once per long rest per Djinn. Full Equip ends afterward, and you cannot Equip that Djinn again until you finish a short rest.
King Vessel · level 17: switch directly from one Full Equip to another as a bonus action, paying the new Equip cost. At level 20, once per long rest, immediately transition from one Djinn’s Extreme into another Djinn’s Full Equip.
Attack / save: use your CE attack modifier and CE save DC unless this Djinn explicitly says otherwise.
