Core law

Character engine

Place cursed Anchor Threads between surfaces. Movement through the visible network is taxed and can trigger pulls, restraints, and geometry-based traps.

Technique save DC: 8 + PB + chosen CE ability modifier. Technique attack: PB + chosen CE ability modifier.

The fiction. Jujutsu scholars classify it as an ambush that learned patience: the user's CE weaves Anchor Threads the way a spider does — not to chase, but to make the world come to them. The prevailing theory holds that the threads are the sorcerer's distrust made structural: every crossing taxed, every surface claimed, until the battlefield is one completed web and the prey's movement belongs to the spinner.

Full progression

Level progression

1Earth-Spider Thread

Earth-Spider Thread (action, 1 CE): create two thread anchors within 30 feet. Threads last 1 minute, and you can maintain up to PB threads at once (invented). A hostile crossing a thread pays +10 feet of movement unless it succeeds on a Dexterity save; on a failed save you may use your reaction to snap the thread for 1d8 of slashing damage (invented: the die is a d8) and a 5-foot pull.

3Ceiling Hunter

Ceiling Hunter: gain a climb speed equal to your speed and the ability to occupy ceilings. As a bonus action, travel 20 feet along your connected thread network without provoking opportunity attacks.

6Loom Trap

Loom Trap: with 3 anchors and +2 CE, form a 15-foot triangular zone. The first hostile entering it or starting its turn inside makes a Dexterity save vs your CE save DC (invented) or is restrained until it spends an action and succeeds on a Strength save. The trap has AC 13 and HP equal to 3 × your level.

11Buried Spider

Buried Spider: place anchors through earth or stone you touch, up to 10 feet deep. Once per turn, when you hit a restrained target, you may consume an anchor to deal +2d8 of damage (invented) and drag the target 10 feet.

18Domain Expansion: Castle Under Eightfold Silk

Full-turn action, 20 CE, enclosed 60-foot radius centered on you, up to 1 minute, Technique Focus. Six indestructible Domain Anchors appear in legal spaces you choose. At initiative 20 choose any three Anchors; they define a triangular Law-Web until the next initiative 20. Each chosen hostile inside the triangle treats all movement as difficult terrain. The first time an unprotected hostile leaves the triangle before the next event, it is pulled to the triangle’s center and restrained until the end of its current turn. A protected creature instead makes a Dexterity save when leaving; success avoids both pull and restraint. The Domain event deals no automatic damage, and ordinary thread attacks still roll.

Feats

Build-defining options.

Silk Architect

When you create Anchor Threads, you may anchor one end to a willing creature within 30 feet instead of a surface; the thread moves with the creature, and you always know its direction and distance while the thread lasts.

Tremor Reader

While you are touching one of your Anchor Threads, you gain blindsight with a 10-foot radius centered on every anchor connected to that thread, reading movement through the web as though you stood at each anchor. A creature moving inside this tremor-sense cannot benefit from being unseen against your thread-snapping reactions. The sense is passive, but it ends for any anchor whose thread is cut or dismissed.

Spider Drop

When you drop at least 10 feet from the ceiling onto a creature, you may forgo your attack to land on it instead: it must succeed on a Strength save against your CE DC or be knocked prone and grappled by you.

Eightfold Knot (11+)

As an action, you may collapse a Loom Trap into a portable snare that lasts 1 hour; as a bonus action you can throw it at a point within 30 feet to instantly reform the trap there.

Rules integration

Domain procedure

This entry uses the repaired campaign Domain rules. The level-18 feature above is complete: its printed action, CE cost, radius, duration, initiative-20 law, fallback protection, and counterplay replace any legacy blanket auto-hit wording.
Rules integration

Counterplay

Outside the Domain, threads and web zones are visible and destructible. Area damage and alternate routes erase prepared geometry.
