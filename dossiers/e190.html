Domain Expansion: Castle Under Eightfold SilkFull-turn action, 20 CE, enclosed 60-foot radius centered on you, up to 1 minute, Technique Focus. Six indestructible Domain Anchors appear in legal spaces you choose. At initiative 20 choose any three Anchors; they define a triangular Law-Web until the next initiative 20. Each chosen hostile inside the triangle treats all movement as difficult terrain. The first time an unprotected hostile leaves the triangle before the next event, it is pulled to the triangle’s center and restrained until the end of its current turn. A protected creature instead makes a Dexterity save when leaving; success avoids both pull and restraint. The Domain event deals no automatic damage, and ordinary thread attacks still roll.

Character engine

Place cursed Anchor Threads between surfaces. Movement through the visible network is taxed and can trigger pulls, restraints, and geometry-based traps.

Technique save DC: 8 + PB + chosen CE ability modifier. Technique attack: PB + chosen CE ability modifier.

Counterplay

Outside the Domain, threads and web zones are visible and destructible. Area damage and alternate routes erase prepared geometry.
