Core law

Character engine

Place cursed Anchor Threads between surfaces. Movement through the visible network is taxed and can trigger pulls, restraints, and geometry-based traps.

Technique save DC: 8 + PB + chosen CE ability modifier. Technique attack: PB + chosen CE ability modifier.

Field notes. Subject: a sorcerer who never chases, and therefore never loses a chase. Three truths govern the Loom. First: the ambush is patient. The Anchor Threads do not strike, they tax, restrain, and pull; the enemy's movement becomes the spider's supply line, and every crossing is invoiced. Second: the battlefield is the weapon. Ceilings, stone, the earth itself, every surface is an anchor point, which means the terrain has quietly changed allegiances before the first blow lands. Third: geometry is strategy. Three anchors make a triangle that holds a body; six anchors make a Domain that holds a war. I have watched prey study the threads for a way through and find only the arithmetic of their own footsteps. The Tsuchigumo does not defeat you. It files you into its ledger, and waits for the balance to come due.

Full progression

Level progression

1Earth-Spider Thread

The first tax of the hunt. Two anchors, one line between them, and suddenly the ground is no longer neutral, the hostile pays in footsteps or pays in blood when the thread snaps. Mark this: the thread is not a wall. It is a toll gate, and every toll gate teaches the prey which roads it no longer owns. Earth-Spider Thread (action, 1 CE): create two thread anchors within 30 feet. Threads last 1 minute, and you can maintain up to PB threads at once (invented). A hostile crossing a thread pays +10 feet of movement unless it succeeds on a Dexterity save; on a failed save you may use your reaction to snap the thread for 1d8 of slashing damage (invented: the die is a d8) and a 5-foot pull.

3Ceiling Hunter

The spider's second truth: the battlefield has a roof, and it is his. I have watched the Tsuchigumo vanish upward and reappear twenty feet along his own web, untouchable, unhurried. The prey watches the floor; the predator owns the ceiling. That asymmetry is the whole plan. Ceiling Hunter: gain a climb speed equal to your speed and the ability to occupy ceilings. As a bonus action, travel 20 feet along your connected thread network without provoking opportunity attacks.

6Loom Trap

Three anchors, and the geometry closes like a mouth. The triangle is the smallest prison ever built: enter it and it holds you until you spend the effort of escape. Consider, most traps are placed where the enemy might go. The Loom Trap is placed where the enemy already is. Loom Trap: with 3 anchors and +2 CE, form a 15-foot triangular zone. The first hostile entering it or starting its turn inside makes a Dexterity save vs your CE save DC (invented) or is restrained until it spends an action and succeeds on a Strength save. The trap has AC 13 and HP equal to 3 × your level.

11Buried Spider

The web descends into the ground itself. Anchors placed through stone mean the earth is no longer innocent, a restrained target dragged ten feet deeper into the Loom's arithmetic. I have catalogued many snares; this is the only one that digs. Buried Spider: place anchors through earth or stone you touch, up to 10 feet deep. Once per turn, when you hit a restrained target, you may consume an anchor to deal +2d8 of damage (invented) and drag the target 10 feet.

18Domain Expansion: Castle Under Eightfold Silk

The ledger becomes law. Six indestructible anchors, three chosen at a time, and the triangle is no longer a suggestion, movement inside it costs double, leaving it drags you back to the center. The sure-hit does not wound; it relocates. That is the Tsuchigumo's mercy: you are not killed in the Castle. You are filed. Full-turn action, 20 CE, enclosed 60-foot radius centered on you, up to 1 minute, Technique Focus. Six indestructible Domain Anchors appear in legal spaces you choose. At initiative 20 choose any three Anchors; they define a triangular Law-Web until the next initiative 20. Each chosen hostile inside the triangle treats all movement as difficult terrain. The first time an unprotected hostile leaves the triangle before the next event, it is pulled to the triangle’s center and restrained until the end of its current turn. A protected creature instead makes a Dexterity save when leaving; success avoids both pull and restraint. The Domain event deals no automatic damage, and ordinary thread attacks still roll.

Feats

Build-defining options.

Silk Architect

The web learns to walk. By anchoring a thread to a willing creature instead of stone, the spider takes its toll road with it, the line moves with its bearer, and the spinner always knows where the road leads. Terrain was never the point; the claim was. When you create Anchor Threads, you may anchor one end to a willing creature within 30 feet instead of a surface; the thread moves with the creature, and you always know its direction and distance while the thread lasts.

Tremor Reader

Touch the web and you hear through it. Every anchor becomes an ear; every crossing becomes a footstep the spinner reads. I have watched prey tiptoe across a battlefield they thought was empty. The Tsuchigumo heard each toe, and smiled from the ceiling. While you are touching one of your Anchor Threads, you gain blindsight with a 10-foot radius centered on every anchor connected to that thread, reading movement through the web as though you stood at each anchor. A creature moving inside this tremor-sense cannot benefit from being unseen against your thread-snapping reactions. The sense is passive, but it ends for any anchor whose thread is cut or dismissed.

Spider Drop

The ceiling hunter's punctuation. Drop ten feet onto the prey and let the landing be the argument, prone and grappled, crushed by the weight of the geometry above. The spider does not dive. It collects. When you drop at least 10 feet from the ceiling onto a creature, you may forgo your attack to land on it instead: it must succeed on a Strength save against your CE DC or be knocked prone and grappled by you.

Eightfold Knot (11+)

The trap learns to travel. Collapsed into a portable snare, the Loom Trap is carried like a folded letter and thrown like a verdict, the triangle opens wherever it lands. Patience, it turns out, can be packed. As an action, you may collapse a Loom Trap into a portable snare that lasts 1 hour; as a bonus action you can throw it at a point within 30 feet to instantly reform the trap there.

Rules integration

Domain procedure

This entry uses the repaired campaign Domain rules. The level-18 feature above is complete: its printed action, CE cost, radius, duration, initiative-20 law, fallback protection, and counterplay replace any legacy blanket auto-hit wording.
Rules integration

Counterplay

Outside the Domain, threads and web zones are visible and destructible. Area damage and alternate routes erase prepared geometry.
