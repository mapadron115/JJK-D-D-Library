Features & procedures

Exact player-facing procedures.

Weapon Equip • Level 5

Nyoi Rentou — Refined Blade at Will.

Weapon Equip becomes a double-purpose scythe/hammer. Its size and weight can change instantly at the moment of impact.

Bonus Action • 1 Reserve

Expanding Edge

Until the end of your turn, increase the weapon’s reach to 30 ft or make it Huge. The weapon’s weight never penalizes its King while the effect is active.

On hit • 2 Reserve

Cruel Scythe

Add 4 Djinn Dice slashing/force. You may instead project the slash to a creature within 120 ft using the same attack roll.

Action • 5 Reserve

Hammer of Grief

Smash a point within 150 ft. Creatures in a 60-ft radius make Strength saves; 9 Djinn Dice force, half on success. Failure knocks prone and creates a 20-ft-deep crater.

Full Djinn Equip • Level 9

Fly 110 ft on spike-like wings; oversized weapon mastery. Once per turn after hitting, sweep a second creature within 30 ft for 3 Djinn Dice slashing/force without another attack roll.

12 Reserve • Canon Extreme

Lelazzo Madraga

A vast Metal Vessel symbol spreads above a point within 300 ft and Leraje’s crushing power descends across a 180-ft radius. Strength save; 18 Djinn Dice force/bludgeoning, half on success. Failure knocks prone and Restrains until the end of your next turn as the area is pulverized into a deep crater. Structures take maximum dice.

Anime-scale: Strategic scale: pulverize a city-block-sized battlefield or crush giant enemies beneath a crater-forming Strength Magic impact.

Rules integration

Djinn Die, Equip & Extreme Magic

Independent Djinn Reserve: 16 (Rank A). Refreshes on a long rest. Every Djinn cost below — Weapon Equip, Full Equip, abilities, reactions, Extreme Magic — spends this Djinn’s Reserve, never your CE. If this Djinn’s Reserve is empty it simply cannot fuel more until you rest; your other Djinn keep their own separate Reserves.
Weapon Equip · level 5: bonus action, 2 Reserve, lasts 10 minutes.
Full Djinn Equip · level 9: bonus action, 4 Reserve, lasts 1 minute. Only one Equip can be active.
Deep Synchronization · level 11: once per short rest, one Equip activation costs 1 Reserve less.
Extreme Magic · level 13: action; requires that Djinn’s Full Equip; pay the listed Reserve cost from this Djinn’s Reserve; usable once per long rest per Djinn. Full Equip ends afterward, and you cannot Equip that Djinn again until you finish a short rest.
King Vessel · level 17: switch directly from one Full Equip to another as a bonus action, paying the new Equip cost. At level 20, once per long rest, immediately transition from one Djinn’s Extreme into another Djinn’s Full Equip.
Attack / save: use your CE attack modifier and CE save DC unless this Djinn explicitly says otherwise.
