Keening Before Death

Banshee inspiration. Death Notes create Laments tied to endangered creatures. Spend those omens to warn allies, bolster survival, or make your wail more dangerous around people already near death.

Combat roleDeath-warning support / sonic debuffer

Core law

Character engine

Death Notes create Laments tied to endangered creatures. Spend those omens to warn allies, bolster survival, or make your wail more dangerous around people already near death.

Technique save DC: 8 + PB + chosen CE ability modifier. Technique attack: PB + chosen CE ability modifier.

Full progression

Level progression

1Warning Keen

Reaction, 1 CE: spend target's Lament to add 1 die to its AC against one attack or to a save, after seeing the roll but before outcome.

3Piercing Wail

Action, 2 CE, 30-ft cone Con save: 2 dice thunder; failure deafened and disadvantage on next attack; targets whose Lament you hold take +1 die.

6Mourning Veil

Action, 2 CE, Focus, 20-ft aura 1 minute: allies advantage on death saves; when one succeeds, spend its Lament to restore 1 HP once/creature/long rest.

11The Cry Arrives First

On initiative, if a visible creature is below half HP gain its Lament. You cannot be surprised by a creature whose Lament you hold.

18Domain Expansion: House That Heard the Cry

Full-turn action, 20 CE, enclosed 60-foot radius centered on you, up to 1 minute, Technique Focus. This is an omen-support Domain. At initiative 20 every creature inside below half HP generates one temporary Lament for you even if you are at your normal cap; excess temporary Laments vanish at the end of the round. Then choose one creature whose Lament you hold. Until the next initiative 20 it receives Forewarning: +2 AC and +2 to saving throws, but it has disadvantage on attacks against you. This is not mind control and causes no automatic damage. If the chosen creature is unwilling and protected by an anti-domain defense, it may make a Wisdom save to reject Forewarning entirely.

Rules integration

Domain procedure

This entry uses the repaired campaign Domain rules. The level-18 feature above is complete: its printed action, CE cost, radius, duration, initiative-20 law, fallback protection, and counterplay replace any legacy blanket auto-hit wording.
Rules integration

Counterplay

Healthy targets generate fewer Laments. Silence/deafening can impede active sonic abilities, though supernatural omen sensing remains.
