Domain Expansion: House That Heard the CryFull-turn action, 20 CE, enclosed 60-foot radius centered on you, up to 1 minute, Technique Focus. This is an omen-support Domain. At initiative 20 every creature inside below half HP generates one temporary Lament for you even if you are at your normal cap; excess temporary Laments vanish at the end of the round. Then choose one creature whose Lament you hold. Until the next initiative 20 it receives Forewarning: +2 AC and +2 to saving throws, but it has disadvantage on attacks against you. This is not mind control and causes no automatic damage. If the chosen creature is unwilling and protected by an anti-domain defense, it may make a Wisdom save to reject Forewarning entirely.

Character engine

Death Notes create Laments tied to endangered creatures. Spend those omens to warn allies, bolster survival, or make your wail more dangerous around people already near death.

Technique save DC: 8 + PB + chosen CE ability modifier. Technique attack: PB + chosen CE ability modifier.

Counterplay

Healthy targets generate fewer Laments. Silence/deafening can impede active sonic abilities, though supernatural omen sensing remains.
