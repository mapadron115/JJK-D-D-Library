Features & procedures

Properties & techniques

Exact player-facing procedures

Property

Serpent staff

This curved staff is obviously a cursed corpse in the shape of a snake. Its stiffened body lets it be used as a quarterstaff.

Action

Release the snake

As an action, throw the staff so it lands in an unoccupied space within 10 feet of you. It becomes a Giant Venomous Snake (Construct) in that space, under your control. It shares your Initiative count and takes its turn immediately after yours.

Property

Mental command

On your turn, you can mentally command the snake (no action required) if it is within 60 feet of you and you do not have the Incapacitated condition. You decide what action it takes and where it moves, or issue a general command such as attacking your enemies or guarding a location. Without commands, it defends itself.

Bonus action

Recall

As a bonus action, command the snake to revert to staff form in its current space. You cannot use the staff's property again for 1 hour. If the snake is reduced to 0 hit points, it dies and reverts to staff form, the staff then shatters and is destroyed. If it reverts before losing all hit points, it regains all of them.

Observer’s account

Field notes. Subject: a viper on a leash, borrowed. The staff is a cursed corpse shaped as a snake, stiff as a quarterstaff until thrown, when it wakes as a Giant Venomous Snake under your mental command. Three truths. First, the weapon has a temper you may borrow, not own: command it within 60 feet, no action required, and it strikes where you point. Second, the leash has two ends: recall it as a bonus action, but leave it an hour before it serves again, and know that if the snake dies, the staff dies with it, shattered forever. Third, mercy preserves it: revert the snake before 0 hit points and it returns whole, healed entire. Hence the maxim: a borrowed fang is a fortune, spend it like one, and recall it before the enemy spends it for you.

Campaign canon notes

The snake is a construct: it counts as a cursed corpse (construct), relevant to construct-targeting effects and to critical hits from tools like the Cursed Demon's bane line.
Death is permanent: if the snake form dies, the staff is destroyed outright. Reverting it before 0 HP keeps it, and fully heals it.
