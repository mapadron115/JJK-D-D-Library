Features & procedures

Properties & techniques

Exact player-facing procedures

Bonus action

Coat the blade

As a bonus action for 2 cursed energy, you coat the blade of this dagger with poison. The poison remains for 1 minute or until an attack using this weapon hits a creature.

On hit

Cursed poison

The first creature hit while the blade is coated must succeed on a DC 13 Constitution saving throw or take 2d8 poison damage and have the Poisoned condition for 1 minute.

Observer’s account

Field notes. Subject: a viper that makes its own venom. A bonus action and 2 CE, and the blade sweats poison for a minute — the first landed hit forces a DC 13 Constitution save or takes 2d8 poison and the Poisoned condition for a full minute. Three truths. First, the economy favors the patient: missed coated attacks keep their poison, so the coating is never wasted on the wind — only on flesh. Second, the venom is rented, not owned: one minute, then the blade is clean again, and the next dose costs again. Third, poison is a tax on the enemy’s actions: the Poisoned condition degrades everything they attempt for a minute, long after the wound. Hence the maxim: a blade that poisons itself has cut the supply line between the hunter and the hunt.
