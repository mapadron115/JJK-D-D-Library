Feature map

Level-by-level features, as the figure refined them.

L1

Foxfire

action, 1 CE

Tier V · Controller / Illusionist · Suggested CE ability: CHA · Primary verb: Enchant. Your technique save DC = 8 + your proficiency bonus + your CHA modifier. Your technique attack bonus = your proficiency bonus + your CHA modifier.

Ranged spell attack (+PB + CHA to hit), 120 ft: 2d10 fire + 1d6 psychic damage — the flame burns the idea of you; the psychic portion aches in memory first. Foxfire ignores half and three-quarters cover (it curves around obstacles, the way desire does). Flammable objects ignited by foxfire burn blue-white and cannot be extinguished by mundane means for 1 minute.

L1

Nine Shadows

bonus action, 2 CE per shadow

Create up to nine illusory duplicates — her court personas, given temporary flesh. Each shadow: AC 15, HP equal to 5 × her CHA modifier (minimum 10), speed 30 ft, and it may cast foxfire using her attack bonus, dealing half damage. Shadows act on her initiative, directly after her. When a shadow is destroyed, she takes 5 psychic damage (feedback — the persona felt it). She may have at most nine shadows active. Re-summoning a destroyed shadow costs the full 2 CE again. (The ninth tail is never manifested as a shadow.)

L3

The Court Wants

action, 3 CE

One creature within 60 ft that can see her: WIS save vs technique DC. On failure, the creature is charmed for 1 hour — and she appears to it as what it most desires or trusts (the reflection, not an illusion; detect magic shows nothing, because there is nothing to detect). While charmed this way, the victim becomes a courtier: Tamamo can perceive through its senses as a free action. The victim re-saves when it takes damage from her or witnesses her harming someone it loves.

L3

Never Where You Think

reaction, 2 CE

When Tamamo is targeted by an attack, she may swap positions with one of her shadows within 60 ft — the attack targets the shadow instead, resolved against its AC. If no shadow is in range, she may instead impose disadvantage on the attack (a flicker of eight tails, and the blade finds the wrong woman).

L6

Sessho-seki's Breath

action, 5 CE

She exhales the Killing Stone's vapor: a 30-ft cone, CON save vs technique DC or 6d8 poison damage and poisoned for 1 minute (re-save at the end of each turn). The vapor smells of hot rock and old perfume. Vegetation in the cone dies instantly and does not regrow for a year.

L6

Wear the Shadow

bonus action, 3 CE

She assumes one of her shadows' forms completely — voice, memories, mannerisms, scent. For 1 hour: +10 to Deception checks made as that persona, and divination effects (including true seeing below 7th level) perceive the shadow's identity, not hers. She has used this to be three different advisors in three different capitals in a single week.

L11

The Smile That Ruins Kingdoms

action, 8 CE

Every creature of her choice within 60 ft that can see her: WIS save vs technique DC or charmed for 1 minute. While charmed by this, victims regard her spoken wishes as suggestions from a beloved ruler — they will obey any instruction that isn't obviously self-destructive (as suggestion, no concentration required, but one instruction per victim). Re-save when ordered to harm someone they love. Daji smiled, and the Shang fell. The mechanism has not changed. Only the kingdoms are larger.

L11

Tails as Lives

passive

When Tamamo would be reduced to 0 HP, a tail is severed instead: all active shadows are destroyed, she is restored to half her maximum HP, and she teleports up to 120 ft in a flash of foxfire (leaving no trace — not even ash). Usable once per long rest. Narratively, she has nine tails to spend; mechanically, the table only ever sees her spend one. The second time should not happen in the same campaign.

L18

The Ninth Tail

action, 10 CE

She reveals what was never shown. For 1 minute, all nine tails manifest openly — the sky bruises violet, the air smells of ruin and plum wine: - shadows may be re-summoned as a bonus action for 1 CE each (no action required); - foxfire deals double dice; - creatures have disadvantage on saves against The Court Wants and The Smile That Ruins Kingdoms; - she gains truesight 60 ft (the ninth tail sees). When it ends, one shadow — one persona, one life — is banished for a century (GM's choice which; she will mourn it theatrically and mean it not at all). Power has a price. Hers is just denominated in centuries.

Domain Expansion

"THE GILDED CAGE OF NINE TAILS" (disclosed rule-domain)

20 CE, full-turn action. 60-ft radius. Clash 1,000 · burnout 3 rounds. · duration 3 minutes

A Heian court at midnight — lanterns, silk screens, the scent of plum wine, nine shadows moving behind every screen. The sure-hit is etiquette: every hostile within perceives Tamamo as their most trusted beloved, and the court's first law is spoken by Heaven itself — none raise a hand against the lady. Mechanically: a hostile that wishes to attack Tamamo (or include her in an area effect) must first succeed on a WIS save vs technique DC at the start of its turn; on failure, it cannot target her that turn (it may act otherwise freely — the cage is gilded, not locked). Creatures that fail by 5 or more are charmed until the start of their next turn as well. This is a rule-domain compulsion — nonlethal, save-gated, Heaven-witnessed. It does not make her invincible. It makes hitting her cost the one thing no warrior wants to spend: certainty.

Level 20 · Capstone

The Pattern Eternal (capstone, passive)

capstone, passive

She stops pretending to be one woman. - Narrative phylactery: if slain, she reforms in 1d10 days wherever three or more people speak her story with fear — Daji, Bao Si, Tamamo, the fox-wife, any telling of the pattern. Destroying her permanently requires what no army has managed in three thousand years: making the world stop telling the story. (Sun's network has a standing project. It is not going well.) - The court is forever: victims of The Court Wants no longer re-save when damaged — the charm lasts until remove curse (6th level or higher), a year passes, or she releases them. Her courtiers are hers. - The twelfth tail: once per century, she may grow a tenth shadow — a persona with no history, no story, nothing the world has ever feared. Perfect cover. She is saving it. The GM should be faintly terrified of what she's saving it for.
