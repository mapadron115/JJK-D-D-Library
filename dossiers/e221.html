Features & procedures

Properties & techniques

Exact player-facing procedures

Feature

Attacks against Rune-markedOnce the Rune is placed, the spear's argument is settled: distance and cover are legal fictions it no longer recognizes. Aim without apology. target ignore disadvantage from long range and half cover.

Never Lost

The throw is not a parting; it is a loan of an instant. By the turn's end it is home, only an effect of equal standing can detain it. after thrown attack, Gungnir returns at end of turn unless trapped by Special Grade effect.

Odin's Cast

Three CE buys the legendary cast: three hundred feet, three extra dice, and the true payload, for one minute the target cannot Hide from the one who marked him. To be seen is to be struck. 3 CE: throw up to 300 ft; on hit +3 Tool Dice and target cannot Hide from you for 1 minute.

Release: Spear Over the Host

For one minute the spear commands more than its bearer: each hit on the marked one becomes an order, and an ally sixty feet away answers with a step toward the named fate. The host moves because the spear has already decided where the war is. 7 CE, action to activate (invented): for 1 minute, once/turn after you hit marked target, one ally within 60 ft may reaction move 10 ft toward it.

Field notes. Subject: a vow that learned to fly. Three truths govern him. First, the Rune is the spear asking the world's permission to keep a promise it no longer remembers making: mark one creature, only ever one, and the mark holds a minute or until the spear changes its mind. Second, against the marked, distance becomes a rumor: long range and half cover cease to signify, because the throw was decided before it was made. Third, it returns because it was never thrown; it was sent. Odin's Cast carries it three hundred feet and leaves the wound visible, the target cannot Hide from the hand that named him. Mark this: it does not hunt the way other spears hunt. It concludes.

Rules integration

Cursed-tool attunement

Campaign canon

Cursed-tool attunement: you have PB attunement slots (2–6). This tool occupies one slot and requires a one-hour ritual. Attunement ends voluntarily over a short rest, on death, or when you replace it while over capacity. Distance does not break attunement. Cursed-tool attunement is separate from magic-item attunement.
Balance & counterplay. 'Never misses' becomes relentless targeting assistance rather than literal automatic hits.
