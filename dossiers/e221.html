Features & procedures

Properties & techniques

Exact player-facing procedures

Feature

Attacks against Rune-marked target ignore disadvantage from long range and half cover.

Never Lost

after thrown attack, Gungnir returns at end of turn unless trapped by Special Grade effect.

Odin's Cast

3 CE: throw up to 300 ft; on hit +3 Tool Dice and target cannot Hide from you for 1 minute.

Release: Spear Over the Host

7 CE, action to activate (invented): for 1 minute, once/turn after you hit marked target, one ally within 60 ft may reaction move 10 ft toward it.

The fiction. Registry notes describe Gungnir not as a weapon that was made, but as one that was decided — the prevailing theory holds that its first wielder's resolve, “it will not miss,” was poured into the steel so completely that the spear learned the sentence as its nature. Scholars classify it among the rare tools that carry a vow in their grain: the rune-mark is the spear asking the world's permission to keep a promise it no longer remembers making. It returns to the hand because it was never thrown. It was sent.

Rules integration

Cursed-tool attunement

Campaign canon

Cursed-tool attunement: you have PB attunement slots (2–6). This tool occupies one slot and requires a one-hour ritual. Attunement ends voluntarily over a short rest, on death, or when you replace it while over capacity. Distance does not break attunement. Cursed-tool attunement is separate from magic-item attunement.
Balance & counterplay. 'Never misses' becomes relentless targeting assistance rather than literal automatic hits.
