Clarent

Features & procedures

Properties & techniques

Exact player-facing procedures

Feature

Spend Grievance on hits against that creature for +1 Tool Die each, max 2.

Usurper's Challenge

bonus, 1 CE: mark visible creature as Leader for 1 minute; only one.

Red Treason

3 CE, spend 3 Grievance on hit: Wis save or frightened of you and cannot Help allies until end next turn.

Feature

Grievance disappears when target is defeated or after short rest.

The fiction. Scholars classify Clarent as a grudge that learned swordplay: the blade's CE imprinted on the moment its bearer was struck by someone they had trusted, and it has been keeping score ever since. The Grievance is not anger — registry notes describe it as the wound's own bookkeeping, owed to whoever stands where the betrayer stood.

Rules integration

Cursed-tool attunement

Campaign canon

Cursed-tool attunement: you have PB attunement slots (2–6). This tool occupies one slot and requires a one-hour ritual. Attunement ends voluntarily over a short rest, on death, or when you replace it while over capacity. Distance does not break attunement. Cursed-tool attunement is separate from magic-item attunement.
Balance & counterplay. Needs a relationship or declared leader to become exceptional.
