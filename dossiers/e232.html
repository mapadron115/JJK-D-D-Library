Spend Grievance on hitsThe ledger is cashed only against its debtor: each banked Grievance becomes a die on hits against the named one, two at most. The blade's arithmetic is precise and personal. against that creature for +1 Tool Die each, max 2.

Usurper's Challenge

One CE and a bonus action to declare the court: a visible creature is marked Leader for a minute, one at a time, for the blade keeps a single account open. Red Treason

On hit · 3 CE + 3 Grievance · Wis save or the target is frightened of you and cannot take the Help action for its allies until the end of your next turn.

Three CE and three banked Grievance on a hit: the traitor must save or be frightened of the bearer and stripped of the ability to Help his allies. The punishment fits the crime, the one who broke faith loses the fellowship. 3 CE, spend 3 Grievance on hit: Wis save or frightened of you and cannot Help allies until end next turn.

Feature

Passive · Grievance marks vanish when the debtor is defeated or you finish a short rest.

Grievance disappearsThe blade's own mercy clause: the marks vanish when the debtor falls or the short rest comes. Clarent is a grudge, not a monument, it does not outlive its cause. when target is defeated or after short rest.

Field notes. Subject: a grudge that learned swordplay. Three truths govern him. First, the Grievance is not anger but bookkeeping: when a creature that aided or buffed the bearer within the last day turns its hand against him, the blade enters two marks against it, one mark when a declared Leader strikes an ally, and the wound keeps its own accounts. Second, the marks are spent only against the debtor: up to two extra dice per hit, aimed at the one the ledger names, Clarent does not generalize its grievances. Third, the grudge is mortal by design: the marks die with the target or at the short rest, and Red Treason, three CE, three Grievance, a failed Wisdom save, leaves the traitor frightened and unable to Help his allies until the next turn ends. Hence the doctrine: the blade punishes the betrayal, not the battle. Mark the bearer it chose: someone who was struck by a trusted hand, and has been keeping score ever since.

Rules integration

Cursed-tool attunement

Campaign canon

Cursed-tool attunement: you have PB attunement slots (2–6). This tool occupies one slot and requires a one-hour ritual. Attunement ends voluntarily over a short rest, on death, or when you replace it while over capacity. Distance does not break attunement. Cursed-tool attunement is separate from magic-item attunement.
Balance & counterplay. Needs a relationship or declared leader to become exceptional.
