Stormfather Catastrophe

Typhon inspiration. Build Cataclysm by using different destructive phenomena; spend it to layer fire, lightning, thunder, poison and forced movement instead of repeating one best attack.

Combat roleDisaster artillery / transforming controller
Power tierTier V · Calamity
Core law

Character engine

The engine in one line: Cataclysm accrues only from difference, the first damage each round with a phenomenon unlike the last, and is spent to layer riders rather than repeat a best attack. You gain 1 Cataclysm the first time each round you damage with a phenomenon different from the one you last used; maximum Cataclysm = PB. Spend Cataclysm to layer fire, lightning, thunder, poison and forced movement instead of repeating one best attack.

Technique save DC: 8 + PB + chosen CE ability modifier. Technique attack: PB + chosen CE ability modifier.

Field notes. Subject: a storm that refuses to repeat itself. Three truths govern him. First: variety is his fuel. Cataclysm accrues only when each new phenomenon differs from the last, fire after lightning, thunder after poison, and the moment he repeats himself, the engine idles. Second: he never throws the same spear twice by design. Riders layer onto storms, ignite, reaction denial, a push, a venom, so every blast lands differently than the last, and the enemy cannot brace for a pattern. Third: the mountain endures what the sky cannot. Below half health the storm digs in, harder to hurt, harder to move, and his Cataclysm cap climbs. Consider him as a chorus of a hundred throats, each singing a different disaster: the catastrophe is not any single bolt. It is that no two bolts are ever the same.

Full progression

Level progression

1Hundred-Throated Storm

The opening volley: a thirty-foot line or fifteen-foot cone of fire, lightning, thunder, or poison for one CE, two d8 scaling with level, and thunder that pushes ten feet. Hundred-Throated Storm (action, 1 CE): unleash a 30-foot line or 15-foot cone of fire, lightning, thunder, or poison; creatures in the area make a Dexterity save. It deals 2d8 of the chosen damage type, plus 1d8 at 5th, 11th, and 17th level (invented: the technique die is a d8). A storm of thunder may push 10 feet.

3Serpent Limbs

The storm grows limbs. Two CE for a minute: ten feet of speed, a climb speed, wings at high Cataclysm, and a Cataclysm spent after twenty feet of movement buys a grappling melee strike. Serpent Limbs (bonus action, 2 CE, 1 minute): gain +10 feet of speed and a climb speed, plus storm-wings at higher Cataclysm. After moving 20 feet, you may spend 1 Cataclysm for a melee technique strike dealing 2d8 of damage (invented) and a grapple. On a hit the target is grappled with no separate check; it may use its action to escape, DC = your CE save DC (invented).

6Many-Mouthed Tempest

Passive · When you unleash a Storm, spend up to 3 Cataclysm to add different riders: ignite, reaction denial, 15-ft push, or poisoned until the end of the target's next turn.

The layering begins: up to three Cataclysm spent on a Storm buy different riders, ignite, reaction denial, a fifteen-foot push, or venom until the target's next turn ends. When you unleash a Storm, you may spend up to 3 Cataclysm to add different riders: ignite, reaction denial, a 15-foot push, or poisoned until the end of the target’s next turn.

11Mountain-Buried Monster

Mountain-Buried Monster: Reaction · 3 CE · Reduce incoming damage by 3d8 + your CE modifier (invented) and create rubble terrain · Passive: while below half HP, your Cataclysm cap increases by 2 and you gain 30 ft of flight.

The mountain answers. Three CE as a reaction reduces incoming damage by three d8 plus his modifier and raises rubble, and below half HP his Cataclysm cap climbs by two and he gains thirty feet of flight. Mountain-Buried Monster (reaction, 3 CE): reduce incoming damage by 3d8 + your CE modifier (invented) and create rubble terrain. While you are below half HP, your Cataclysm cap increases by 2 and you gain 30 feet of flight.

18Maximum: Siege of Heaven

Maximum Technique: Siege of Heaven · 8 CE · Lasts 3 rounds · Once per long rest · Activating it costs an action (invented); you assume a Huge storm form · At initiative 20 each round, choose a different catastrophe: eruption, thunderhead, hurricane, or venom rain — each creates a 30-ft zone; creatures make a save against 4d8 of damage plus a rider (invented).

Eight CE, three rounds, once per long rest: a Huge storm form, and at initiative twenty a different catastrophe each round, eruption, thunderhead, hurricane, venom rain, each a thirty-foot zone of four d8 and a rider. Maximum: Siege of Heaven (8 CE, lasts 3 rounds, once per long rest): activating it costs an action (invented); you assume a Huge storm form. At initiative 20 each round, choose a different catastrophe: eruption, thunderhead, hurricane, or venom rain. Each catastrophe creates a 30-foot zone; creatures make a save against 4d8 of damage plus a rider (invented).

Feats

Build-defining options.

Polyphonic Horror

Two phenomena failed in one round earn a Wisdom save against fright, the storm speaking with many throats. When a creature fails saves against two different Typhon phenomena in one round, you may use your reaction and spend 1 Cataclysm to force it to make a Wisdom save against your CE DC; on a failure it is frightened of you until the end of its next turn, as the storm speaks with many throats.

Tireless Coils

Drive the limbs into the ground: immobile, immovable, and advantaged on shoves and grapples until his next turn. As a bonus action, you can drive your serpent limbs into the ground: until the start of your next turn you cannot move, but you cannot be moved against your will and you have advantage on Strength checks and saves to shove or grapple.

Volcanic Prison

Two Cataclysm and a grappled foe buy cooling magma, restrained, burning, until the end of its next turn. As an action while a creature is grappled by your Serpent Limbs, you may spend 2 Cataclysm to encase it in cooling magma: it must succeed on a Strength save against your CE DC or be restrained until the end of your next turn, taking 1 fire die at the start of each of its turns while restrained.

Last Challenge of Olympus (11+)

The death he refuses quietly: at zero HP, erupt, every Cataclysm spent as a die of fire, lightning, or thunder in fifteen feet, before he falls. When you drop to 0 HP, you may use your reaction to spend any amount of Cataclysm and erupt: each creature within 15 feet must make a Dexterity save against your CE DC, taking 1 fire, lightning, or thunder die per Cataclysm spent (your choice) on a failure, half on success. You then fall unconscious as normal.

Rules integration

Counterplay

Spread out, resist varied damage, and force defensive CE spending. The Maximum is not a Domain and can be stopped by incapacitating the user.
