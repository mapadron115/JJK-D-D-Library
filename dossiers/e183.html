Stormfather Catastrophe

Typhon inspiration. Build Cataclysm by using different destructive phenomena; spend it to layer fire, lightning, thunder, poison and forced movement instead of repeating one best attack.

Combat roleDisaster artillery / transforming controller
Power tierTier V · Calamity
Core law

Character engine

You gain 1 Cataclysm the first time each round you damage with a phenomenon different from the one you last used; maximum Cataclysm = PB (PB+2 below half HP). Spend Cataclysm to layer fire, lightning, thunder, poison and forced movement instead of repeating one best attack.

Technique save DC: 8 + PB + chosen CE ability modifier. Technique attack: PB + chosen CE ability modifier.

Full progression

Level progression

1Hundred-Throated Storm

Action, 1 CE: 30-ft line or 15-ft cone; Dex save; 2 dice fire/lightning/thunder/poison, +1 die at 5/11/17. Thunder may push 10 ft.

3Serpent Limbs

Bonus, 2 CE, 1 minute: +10 speed, climb speed, storm-wings at higher Cataclysm; after moving 20 ft spend 1 Cataclysm for a 2-die melee technique strike and grapple.

6Many-Mouthed Tempest

Spend up to 3 Cataclysm on a Storm to add different riders: ignite, reaction denial, 15-ft push, or poisoned until end next turn.

11Mountain-Buried Monster

Reaction, 3 CE: reduce incoming damage by 3 dice + CE mod and create rubble terrain. Below half HP, Cataclysm cap +2 and gain 30-ft flight.

18Maximum: Siege of Heaven

8 CE, 3 rounds, once/long rest. Huge storm form; initiative 20 choose a different catastrophe each round: eruption, thunderhead, hurricane, venom rain. Each is a 30-ft zone, save for 4 dice plus rider.

Feats

Build-defining options.

Polyphonic Horror

failing saves against two different Typhon phenomena in one round can frighten until end next turn.

Tireless Coils

+10 speed; standing costs 5 ft; advantage vs forced movement while transformed.

Volcanic Prison

grappled target takes one die fire when it willingly teleports/escapes.

Last Challenge of Olympus (11+)

first time/long rest below one-quarter HP, immediately gain PB Cataclysm.

Rules integration

Counterplay

Spread out, resist varied damage, and force defensive CE spending. The Maximum is not a Domain and can be stopped by incapacitating the user.
