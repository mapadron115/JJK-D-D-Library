Stormfather Catastrophe

Typhon inspiration. Build Cataclysm by using different destructive phenomena; spend it to layer fire, lightning, thunder, poison and forced movement instead of repeating one best attack.

Combat roleDisaster artillery / transforming controller
Power tierTier V · Calamity
Core law

Character engine

You gain 1 Cataclysm the first time each round you damage with a phenomenon different from the one you last used; maximum Cataclysm = PB. Spend Cataclysm to layer fire, lightning, thunder, poison and forced movement instead of repeating one best attack.

Technique save DC: 8 + PB + chosen CE ability modifier. Technique attack: PB + chosen CE ability modifier.

The fiction. Jujutsu scholars classify it as appetite given inheritance: the user's cursed energy modeled itself on the storm that never repeats a strike — fire, lightning, thunder, poison — and learned hunger for difference. The prevailing theory holds that Cataclysm is the technique's memory of being one disaster among hundreds, and its refusal to ever be only one thing twice.

Full progression

Level progression

L1Hundred-Throated Storm

Hundred-Throated Storm (action, 1 CE): unleash a 30-foot line or 15-foot cone of fire, lightning, thunder, or poison; creatures in the area make a Dexterity save. It deals 2 dice of the chosen damage type, plus 1 die at 5th, 11th, and 17th level. A storm of thunder may push 10 feet.

L3Serpent Limbs

Serpent Limbs (bonus action, 2 CE, 1 minute): gain +10 feet of speed and a climb speed, plus storm-wings at higher Cataclysm. After moving 20 feet, you may spend 1 Cataclysm for a melee technique strike dealing 2 dice of damage and a grapple.

L6Many-Mouthed Tempest

When you unleash a Storm, you may spend up to 3 Cataclysm to add different riders: ignite, reaction denial, a 15-foot push, or poisoned until the end of the target’s next turn.

L11Mountain-Buried Monster

Mountain-Buried Monster (reaction, 3 CE): reduce incoming damage by 3 dice + your CE modifier and create rubble terrain. While you are below half HP, your Cataclysm cap increases by 2 and you gain 30 feet of flight.

L18Maximum: Siege of Heaven

Maximum: Siege of Heaven (8 CE, lasts 3 rounds, once per long rest): you assume a Huge storm form. At initiative 20 each round, choose a different catastrophe: eruption, thunderhead, hurricane, or venom rain. Each catastrophe creates a 30-foot zone; creatures make a save against 4 dice of damage plus a rider.

Feats

Build-defining options.

Polyphonic Horror

When a creature fails saves against two different Typhon phenomena in one round, you may use your reaction and spend 1 Cataclysm to force it to make a Wisdom save against your CE DC; on a failure it is frightened of you until the end of its next turn, as the storm speaks with many throats.

Tireless Coils

As a bonus action, you can drive your serpent limbs into the ground: until the start of your next turn you cannot move, but you cannot be moved against your will and you have advantage on Strength checks and saves to shove or grapple.

Volcanic Prison

As an action while a creature is grappled by your Serpent Limbs, you may spend 2 Cataclysm to encase it in cooling magma: it must succeed on a Strength save against your CE DC or be restrained until the end of your next turn, taking 1 fire die at the start of each of its turns while restrained.

Last Challenge of Olympus (11+)

When you drop to 0 HP, you may use your reaction to spend any amount of Cataclysm and erupt: each creature within 15 feet must make a Dexterity save against your CE DC, taking 1 fire, lightning, or thunder die per Cataclysm spent (your choice) on a failure, half on success. You then fall unconscious as normal.

Rules integration

Counterplay

Spread out, resist varied damage, and force defensive CE spending. The Maximum is not a Domain and can be stopped by incapacitating the user.
