Dark Dimension

A martial darkness technique that rewards reading opponents, absorbing hostile techniques, and concentrating impossible cutting power through a sword.

Power tierTier IV · Special Grade
Combat roleDarkness / katana / ki / dimensional cuts
Core law

What the technique actually does.

Ki Read

The engine’s eye: bonus action, 1 CE, a minute of advantage on Insight and Perception within 30 ft, and he cannot be surprised by what he can sense. Darkness is delivered through a single-edged blade, or manifested as one if unarmed.

  • As a bonus action, enter Ki Read for 1 CE and 1 minute. You gain advantage on Insight/Perception against creatures within 30 ft and cannot be surprised by creatures you can sense.
  • Darkness techniques may be delivered through a sword or similar single-edged weapon. If unarmed, manifest a dark blade for the same mechanics (katana: 1d8/1d10 versatile slashing).
  • Darkness is unusually slow as a projectile but excellent at absorbing or cutting supernatural effects.

Field notes. Subject: attention weaponized. Three truths govern him. First, his darkness is not inherited but remembered, his CE imprinted on a moment of absolute perception, a swordsman reading every killing intent in the room, and now it swallows what it reads. Second, his darkness is slow as a projectile but peerless at absorption: technique contests consume ranged attacks and lines before they land. Third, the dimensional cut is the same instinct taken one step further, a blade so certain of its target that space stopped being an obstacle. Hence the maxim: he does not chase. He reads, and the cut arrives where the reading ends.

Full progression

Level 1–20 Progression

1Dark Cloaked Slash

On a weapon hit · 1 CE · Add one Dark Die necrotic or force · Ki Read also grants +2 initiative while active.

The opening stroke. He is dangerous twice: at the draw, and before it.

Dark Die progression: d6 at levels 1–4, d8 at levels 5–10, d10 at levels 11–16, d12 at levels 17–20. The original expansion repeatedly references a Dark Die without printing its value. This reviewed edition completes that omission using the same four-step progression explicitly established by Vector Authority in the same book. This is a codex clarification, not a claimed quotation from the source.

3Black Hole

Reaction · 2 CE · When a ranged attack or line effect crosses within 10 ft: technique contest vs the attacker · Success: consume the projectile/effect before it hits you · Failure: reduce its damage by 2 Dark Dice.

His first answer to distance. The darkness does not dodge. It eats.

6Avidya Wild Slash

Action · 3 CE · One weapon attack vs every hostile within reach, +2 Dark Dice per hit · Move up to 10 ft total between attacks.

The doctrine of the many. Attention, divided without dilution.

9Mana Zone

Action · 4 CE · Focus, 1 minute · 30-ft aura: know the location of moving creatures inside; your Dark techniques may originate from any point in the aura.

He declares his ground. The field becomes his hand.

11Dimension Slash

Action · 6 CE · 120-ft line: technique attack vs each creature/object in the line, 7 Dark Dice force or slashing · Ignores mundane cover and slashing resistance; barriers take maximum dice.

The cut that made his name. Space was an obstacle. It is not anymore.

13Black Moon

Bonus action · 3 CE · While Mana Zone is active: 15-ft selective nullification field around you until your next turn · Hostile CE projectiles/effects entering it make a technique contest or are erased; allied effects pass untouched.

Darkness that discriminates.

15Iai: Death Draw

Reaction · 4 CE · When a creature within Mana Zone attacks, teleports, or enters your reach: move up to 15 ft and make one weapon attack with advantage · +4 Dark Dice on hit; can Black Flash.

The perfect counter. He does not answer the threat. He was already answering.

18Maximum Technique, Dark Cloaked Dimension Sever (Empowered)

Maximum Technique, empowered form · Action · 10 CE · Dimension Slash becomes a 300-ft line at 12 Dark Dice · Cuts barrier seams and spatial constructs (Domains still use normal clash/counter rules).

The Maximum's empowered form — the Equinox form. The longest sentence his blade has ever written.

20Captain's Black Moon

Passive · Mana Zone: 60-ft radius, no longer occupies Technique Focus · Once/round: Black Hole or Iai costs 0 CE · Dimension Slash attacks within the zone crit on 19–20.

The zone perfected. The captain’s darkness no longer asks permission.

Pinnacle expression

Pinnacle / Maximum

The Maximum is the cut aimed at what cannot be cut: a 150-ft line at 10 Dark Dice, and one chosen barrier, summoned construct, or terrain structure must make a technique stability save or be split open. At 18th level it takes its empowered Equinox form: a 300-ft line at 12 Dark Dice, cutting barrier seams and spatial constructs outright. Note the honest boundary: creatures are not automatically severed. Space yields; flesh still argues. Maximum Technique, Dark Cloaked Dimension Sever (10 CE; action): 150-ft line, 10 Dark Dice force/slashing. One chosen barrier, summoned construct, or terrain structure hit must also make a technique stability save (see Rules, Shared Resolutions) or be split/opened. Equinox form (level 18): 300-ft line, 12 Dark Dice force/slashing; barrier seams and spatial constructs are cut outright (Domains still use normal clash/counter rules). This does not automatically sever creatures.

Feats

Technique Feats

Prerequisite: Ki Read

Ki Savant

While Ki Read is active, a bonus action focuses on one sensed creature: he learns whether it concentrates on a technique, with advantage on his first Black Hole contest against its effects this round. Reading precedes erasing.

Prerequisite: level 3

Dark Cloaked Blade

His weapon counts as magical and cursed while the technique is available, and may deal necrotic instead of its normal physical type. The blade is no longer merely steel with intentions.

Prerequisite: Black Hole

Black Hole Mastery

He learns to catch rather than merely erase: when Black Hole consumes a projectile or line, a reaction and 2 CE holds it for one round, then releases it as a 60-ft line with its original dice. What he swallows, he may return.

Prerequisite: level 11

Surpass Your Limits

At 0 HP, before falling, he may spend all remaining CE (minimum 5) to release one final Dimension Slash along his facing, then fall. The last thing he ever does is the truest thing he knows.

Rules integration

Binding Rulings

Dimension Slash bypasses physical cover; it does not bypass every supernatural defense or Domain by declaration.
Black Moon is a selective anti-technique field and uses contests rather than automatic deletion against equal/greater effects.
Iai is a rolled weapon attack and can Black Flash.
This technique does not need a Domain; Mana Zone/Black Moon already occupy its battlefield-control identity.
