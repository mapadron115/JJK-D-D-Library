Core law

What the technique actually does.

Ki Read

The engine’s eye: bonus action, 1 CE, a minute of advantage on Insight and Perception within 30 ft, and he cannot be surprised by what he can sense. Darkness is delivered through a single-edged blade, or manifested as one if unarmed.

  • As a bonus action, enter Ki Read for 1 CE and 1 minute. You gain advantage on Insight/Perception against creatures within 30 ft and cannot be surprised by creatures you can sense.
  • Darkness techniques may be delivered through a sword or similar single-edged weapon. If unarmed, manifest a dark blade for the same mechanics.
  • Darkness is unusually slow as a projectile but excellent at absorbing or cutting supernatural effects.

Field notes. Subject: attention weaponized. Three truths govern him. First, his darkness is not inherited but remembered, his CE imprinted on a moment of absolute perception, a swordsman reading every killing intent in the room, and now it swallows what it reads. Second, his darkness is slow as a projectile but peerless at absorption: technique contests consume ranged attacks and lines before they land. Third, the dimensional cut is the same instinct taken one step further, a blade so certain of its target that space stopped being an obstacle. Hence the maxim: he does not chase. He reads, and the cut arrives where the reading ends.

Dark Die progression: d6 at levels 1–4, d8 at levels 5–10, d10 at levels 11–16, d12 at levels 17–20. The original expansion repeatedly references a Dark Die without printing its value. This reviewed edition completes that omission using the same four-step progression explicitly established by Vector Authority in the same book. This is a codex clarification, not a claimed quotation from the source.

Pinnacle expression

Pinnacle / Maximum

The Maximum is the cut aimed at what cannot be cut: a 150-ft line at 10 Dark Dice, and one chosen barrier, summoned construct, or terrain structure must make a technique stability save or be split open. Note the honest boundary: creatures are not automatically severed. Space yields; flesh still argues. Maximum Technique, Dark Cloaked Dimension Sever (10 CE; action): 150-ft line, 10 Dark Dice force/slashing. One chosen barrier, summoned construct, or terrain structure hit must also make a technique stability save (see Rules, Shared Resolutions) or be split/opened. This does not automatically sever creatures.

Rules integration

Binding Rulings

Dimension Slash bypasses physical cover; it does not bypass every supernatural defense or Domain by declaration.
Black Moon is a selective anti-technique field and uses contests rather than automatic deletion against equal/greater effects.
Iai is a rolled weapon attack and can Black Flash.
This technique does not need a Domain; Mana Zone/Black Moon already occupy its battlefield-control identity.
