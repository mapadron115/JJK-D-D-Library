04Yami Sukehiro — Black Clover

Dark Dimension

Tier IV · Special GradeDarkness / katana / ki / dimensional cuts

A martial darkness technique that rewards reading opponents, absorbing hostile techniques, and concentrating impossible cutting power through a sword.

Ki Read

  • As a bonus action, enter Ki Read for 1 CE and 1 minute. You gain advantage on Insight/Perception against creatures within 30 ft and cannot be surprised by creatures you can sense.
  • Darkness techniques may be delivered through a sword or similar single-edged weapon. If unarmed, manifest a dark blade for the same mechanics.
  • Darkness is unusually slow as a projectile but excellent at absorbing or cutting supernatural effects.

Pinnacle / Maximum

Maximum Technique — Dark Cloaked Dimension Sever (10 CE): 150-ft line, 10 Dark Dice force/slashing. One chosen barrier, summoned construct, or terrain structure hit must also make a technique stability save or be split/opened. This does not automatically sever creatures.

Technique Feats

Prerequisite: Ki Read

Ki Savant

Ki Read radius becomes 60 ft and grants advantage on initiative instead of +2.

Prerequisite: level 3

Dark Cloaked Blade

Your weapon counts as magical/cursed while technique is available and may deal necrotic instead of its normal physical type.

Prerequisite: Black Hole

Black Hole Mastery

On a successful Black Hole contest, gain 1 CE once/round, representing reclaimed hostile output.

Prerequisite: level 11

Surpass Your Limits

Once/long rest when you begin your turn below 25% HP, immediately regain PB CE and end Technique Burnout.

Binding Rulings

  • Dimension Slash bypasses physical cover; it does not bypass every supernatural defense or Domain by declaration.
  • Black Moon is a selective anti-technique field and uses contests rather than automatic deletion against equal/greater effects.
  • Iai is a rolled weapon attack and can Black Flash.
  • This technique does not need a Domain; Mana Zone/Black Moon already occupy its battlefield-control identity.

Source Notes

The fictional ability is the inspiration; all JJK 5e numbers and progression procedures are homebrew.
