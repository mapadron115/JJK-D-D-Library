Dark Dimension

A martial darkness technique that rewards reading opponents, absorbing hostile techniques, and concentrating impossible cutting power through a sword.

Power tierTier IV · Special Grade
Combat roleDarkness / katana / ki / dimensional cuts
Core law

What the technique actually does.

Ki Read

  • As a bonus action, enter Ki Read for 1 CE and 1 minute. You gain advantage on Insight/Perception against creatures within 30 ft and cannot be surprised by creatures you can sense.
  • Darkness techniques may be delivered through a sword or similar single-edged weapon. If unarmed, manifest a dark blade for the same mechanics.
  • Darkness is unusually slow as a projectile but excellent at absorbing or cutting supernatural effects.

Dark Die

Levels 1–4 d6
Levels 5–10 d8
Levels 11–16 d10
Levels 17–20 d12

The original expansion repeatedly references a Dark Die without printing its value. This reviewed edition completes that omission using the same four-step progression explicitly established by Vector Authority in the same book. This is a codex clarification, not a claimed quotation from the source.

Full progression

Level 1–20 Progression

1Dark Cloaked Slash

On a weapon hit spend 1 CE to add one Dark Die necrotic/force. Ki Read also grants +2 initiative while active.

3Black Hole

Reaction, 2 CE when a ranged attack or line effect crosses within 10 ft: technique contest against attacker. Success consumes the projectile/effect before it hits you; failure reduces its damage by 2 Dark Dice.

6Avidya Wild Slash

Action, 3 CE: make one weapon attack against every hostile within reach. On hit each takes normal weapon damage +2 Dark Dice. You may move 10 ft between attacks total.

9Mana Zone

Action, 4 CE, Focus, 1 minute: 30-ft aura. You know the location of moving creatures in it and your Dark techniques may originate from any point in the aura.

11Dimension Slash

Action, 6 CE: 120-ft line, technique attack against each creature/object in line, 7 Dark Dice force/slashing. Ignores mundane cover and resistance to slashing; barriers take maximum dice.

13Black Moon

While Mana Zone is active, bonus action 3 CE to create a 15-ft selective nullification field around you until next turn. Hostile CE projectiles/effects entering it make technique contest or are erased; allied effects are untouched.

15Iai: Death Draw

Reaction, 4 CE when a creature within Mana Zone attacks, teleports, or enters your reach: move up to 15 ft and make one weapon attack with advantage. On hit add 4 Dark Dice; this can Black Flash.

18Equinox

Maximum-style technique, 10 CE: Dimension Slash becomes a 300-ft line and 12 Dark Dice. It cuts barrier seams and spatial constructs, though Domains still require normal clash/counter rules.

20Captain's Black Moon

Mana Zone radius 60 ft and no longer occupies Technique Focus for you. Once/round Black Hole or Iai costs 0 CE. Dimension Slash attacks within Mana Zone crit on 19–20.

Pinnacle expression

Pinnacle / Maximum

Maximum Technique — Dark Cloaked Dimension Sever (10 CE): 150-ft line, 10 Dark Dice force/slashing. One chosen barrier, summoned construct, or terrain structure hit must also make a technique stability save or be split/opened. This does not automatically sever creatures.

Feats

Technique Feats

Prerequisite: Ki Read

Ki Savant

Ki Read radius becomes 60 ft and grants advantage on initiative instead of +2.

Prerequisite: level 3

Dark Cloaked Blade

Your weapon counts as magical/cursed while technique is available and may deal necrotic instead of its normal physical type.

Prerequisite: Black Hole

Black Hole Mastery

On a successful Black Hole contest, gain 1 CE once/round, representing reclaimed hostile output.

Prerequisite: level 11

Surpass Your Limits

Once/long rest when you begin your turn below 25% HP, immediately regain PB CE and end Technique Burnout.

Rules integration

Binding Rulings

Dimension Slash bypasses physical cover; it does not bypass every supernatural defense or Domain by declaration.
Black Moon is a selective anti-technique field and uses contests rather than automatic deletion against equal/greater effects.
Iai is a rolled weapon attack and can Black Flash.
This technique does not need a Domain; Mana Zone/Black Moon already occupy its battlefield-control identity.
