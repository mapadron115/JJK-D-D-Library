Cursed Tool

Sword of Extermination

A Special Grade cursed shortsword — against cursed spirits, a single failed save is annihilation.

FormWeapon (Shortsword)
GradeSpecial Grade
AttunementNot required

Field notes. Subject: a death warrant made steel. Three truths govern this blade. First, it does not wound cursed spirits — it judges them: one hit, one failed save, and the thing is exorcised, destroyed outright regardless of its remaining hit points. Against spirits it is not a weapon but a verdict. Second, the verdict is narrow: sorcerers, shikigami, and all other flesh fall outside its law, and for them it is merely a fine shortsword. Third, the DC is thirty. Most things cannot answer the question the blade asks, and a failed answer admits no appeal. Mark the maxim: the blade asks only once. Answer wrong, and there is no second question.

Features & procedures

Properties & techniques

Exact player-facing procedures

Cursed edge

The iron of the pulpit: +4 to strike and to wound. The exorcism is the sermon; this is the blade that carries it. You have a +4 bonus to attack and damage rolls made with this cursed tool.

Extermination

This is the whole argument of the sword. On a hit, a cursed spirit faces a DC 30 Constitution save — fail, and it is not wounded but ended, destroyed outright regardless of remaining hit points. Note the jurisdiction: the law applies to cursed spirits only. On a hit, a cursed spirit must make a DC 30 Constitution saving throw. On a failed save it is exorcised instantaneously — destroyed outright, regardless of its remaining hit points. This property has no effect on creatures that are not cursed spirits.

Rules integration

Edge cases and shared systems.

Campaign canon notes

Instant destruction: exorcism here means outright destruction; a cursed spirit immune to being instantly destroyed (by stat block or GM ruling) instead takes the weapon damage normally and ignores this property. Sorcerers, shikigami, and other creature types are unaffected.
