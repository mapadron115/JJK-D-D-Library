Features & procedures

Properties & techniques

Exact player-facing procedures

Cursed edge

You have a +4 bonus to attack and damage rolls made with this cursed tool.

Extermination

On a hit, a cursed spirit must make a DC 30 Constitution saving throw. On a failed save it is exorcised instantaneously — destroyed outright, regardless of its remaining hit points. This property has no effect on creatures that are not cursed spirits.

Rules integration

Edge cases and shared systems.

Campaign canon notes

Instant destruction: exorcism here means outright destruction; a cursed spirit immune to being instantly destroyed (by stat block or GM ruling) instead takes the weapon damage normally and ignores this property. Sorcerers, shikigami, and other creature types are unaffected.
