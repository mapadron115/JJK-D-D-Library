Flame body / flight / heat / nova

Core law

What the technique actually does.

Passive · Heat 0–10 · Gain 1 when you deal 10+ fire damage in a turn or begin your turn in significant flame · Each condition grants 1 Heat; both can pay out in the same turn · Lose 1 after a round dealing no fire damage · At Heat 5 gain fire immunity (applies from L1; Superheated Body at L6 adds +1d8 fire damage) · At Heat 10: no additional effect before L18 (Nova Instability).

Core Engine, Heat. The ledger of his own temperature. Heat rises when he deals ten or more fire damage in a turn or stands in real flame, and cools after a quiet round. At five he is immune; at ten he risks the nova. Heat 0–10. Gain 1 when you deal 10+ fire damage in a turn or begin your turn in significant flame; lose 1 after a round dealing no fire damage. At Heat 5 gain fire immunity; at 10 risk Nova Instability.

Field notes. Subject: a promise made of temperature. Three truths govern him. First: the flame is not his weapon but his identity, combustion learned as a state of being, rising and falling with his aggression; the Heat ledger, zero to ten, is the measure of how close he stands to his own ceiling. Second: everything escalates with Heat, at five he is immune to his own element, at eight his fire ignores resistance, at ten he adds two dice to every technique but begins to burn himself. Third: the nova is the contract's last clause, twelve dice in a forty-foot radius, Heat zeroed, exhaustion taken. Mark the maxim: he does not spend his fire. He becomes it, briefly, and pays the temperature of the becoming.

Full progression

Level 1 through 20.

1Flame On!

Bonus action · 1 CE · 1 minute: you are aflame — fire resistance, melee attackers take PB fire damage once per turn, and you can hurl a fire bolt (60 ft, 2d8).

Focus. Wherever this dossier says Focus, it means you are concentrating (the concentration condition).

The ignition. The body declares itself a hearth.

3Jet Flight

Passive · While aflame: fly speed 50 ft · When you take the Dash action, leave a 5-ft fire trail until your next turn; enemies crossing it take 1d8 fire damage.

He commutes by his own combustion. The road behind him is also a weapon.

6Fire Sculpting & Superheated Body

Action · 2 CE · 15-ft cone or 60-ft line of flame: creatures in the area make a Dex save, taking 4d8 fire on failure · Or harmless shaped flame or light.

The flame learns obedience. Art and artillery share the same four dice.

Passive · At Heat 5+: fire immunity, your attacks deal +1d8 fire damage · Creatures grappling you take 2d8 fire damage.

At Heat five he stops negotiating with fire.

11Plasma Burst & White Flame

Action · 4 CE · 20-ft burst within 90 ft: creatures in the area make a Dex save, taking 6d10 fire on failure.

The harvest. Range is the flame's apology for its hunger.

Passive · At Heat 8+: your fire ignores resistance and treats immunity as resistance · Ordinary flammable terrain ignites instantly.

At Heat eight the fire stops respecting the rules, and the terrain itself catches at his passing.

18Nova Instability & Nova Flame

Action · 10 CE, at Heat 10 · Creatures in a 40-ft radius make a Con save, taking 12d10 fire/radiant on failure (half on success) · Your Heat becomes 0 and you gain exhaustion.

The last clause of the contract. He spends the whole treasury at once.

Passive · At Heat 10: +2 fire dice to your damaging techniques, but you take unpreventable damage = your PB at the end of each turn unless you vent 2 Heat as a bonus action.

The ceiling is reached. The star eats itself.

20Living Star

Passive · Heat cannot fall below 3 while Flame On is active · Fly speed becomes 90 · The first fire technique you use each round gains +1 damage die · Once per long rest, Nova Flame causes no exhaustion.

The star has learned to survive its own dawn.

Pinnacle expression

Maximum, Domain, or equivalent.

Maximum, Supernova (10 CE, Heat 10; action): Nova Flame above. It is deliberately catastrophic but local, not literal stellar output.

Feats

Build-defining options.

Hotshot

Precision is the flame's rare courtesy. At Heat 5+, your fire bolt can be fired as a focused lance: one creature within 120 ft, ignoring half and three-quarters cover. This uses your action and deals normal fire bolt damage.

Skywriter

He signs the sky. Propaganda as a technique. As a bonus action, you may write a message, sketch a map, or conjure an image in the air, legible up to 1 mile away at night or 300 ft by day, that burns for 1 minute. You may also sculpt a flame-duplicate of yourself that lasts until the start of your next turn: the first attack made against you while it burns has disadvantage, as the attacker strikes the wrong target.

Thermal Updraft

He carries a passenger in his own weather, fire that has learned manners. While flying with Jet Flight, you may carry one willing creature of your size or smaller without reducing your speed, cradling them in a heat-shielded updraft that grants them your fire resistance for the ride. Your fiery aura does not harm your passenger, and you may release them as a free action, if released within 30 ft of the ground, they drift down safely and land on their feet.

Flame Cage

He does not chase. He files the perimeter. As an action for 2 CE, you raise a 10-ft-radius, 10-ft-tall ring of white-hot flame that lasts 1 minute (Focus). Any creature that enters the ring's wall or ends its turn there takes 3d8 fire damage, or half on a successful Dexterity save. The cage restrains through threat as much as flame: enemies inside must choose between burning through the wall or staying trapped.

Rules integration

Edge cases and shared systems.

Codex rulings: Direct rolled technique/weapon attacks can use the campaign's Black Flash procedure when otherwise eligible. Passive damage, hazards, summons acting independently, and initiative-20 Domain events cannot. Generic Maximum Output applies only to ordinary features, never named Maxima or Domains.
