Core law

What the technique actually does.

Passive · Heat 0–10 · Gain 1 when you deal 10+ fire damage in a turn or begin your turn in significant flame · Lose 1 after a round dealing no fire damage · At Heat 5 gain fire immunity; at 10 risk Nova Instability.

Core Engine, Heat. The ledger of his own temperature. Heat rises when he deals ten or more fire damage in a turn or stands in real flame, and cools after a quiet round. At five he is immune; at ten he risks the nova. Heat 0–10. Gain 1 when you deal 10+ fire damage in a turn or begin your turn in significant flame; lose 1 after a round dealing no fire damage. At Heat 5 gain fire immunity; at 10 risk Nova Instability.

Field notes. Subject: a promise made of temperature. Three truths govern him. First: the flame is not his weapon but his identity, combustion learned as a state of being, rising and falling with his aggression; the Heat ledger, zero to ten, is the measure of how close he stands to his own ceiling. Second: everything escalates with Heat, at five he is immune to his own element, at eight his fire ignores resistance, at ten he adds two dice to every technique but begins to burn himself. Third: the nova is the contract's last clause, twelve dice in a forty-foot radius, Heat zeroed, exhaustion taken. Mark the maxim: he does not spend his fire. He becomes it, briefly, and pays the temperature of the becoming.

Full progression

Level 1 through 20.

1Flame On!

Bonus action · 1 CE · 1 minute: you are aflame — fire resistance, melee attackers take PB fire damage once per turn, and you can hurl a fire bolt (60 ft, 2d8).

The ignition: one CE, one minute aflame, resistance, a retributive aura for melee attackers, and a sixty-foot bolt. The body declares itself a hearth. As a bonus action, spend 1 CE: for 1 minute you are aflame, you gain fire resistance, melee attackers take fire damage equal to your proficiency bonus once per turn, and you can hurl a fire bolt (60 ft, 2d8).

3Jet Flight

Passive · While aflame: fly speed 50 ft · When you take the Dash action, leave a 5-ft fire trail until your next turn; enemies crossing it take 1d8 fire damage.

He commutes by his own combustion, fifty feet while aflame, and the Dash leaves a five-foot trail of consequence. The road behind him is also a weapon. While aflame, you gain a fly speed of 50 ft. When you take the Dash action, you leave a 5-ft fire trail until your next turn; enemies crossing it take 1d8 fire damage.

6Fire Sculpting

Action · 2 CE · 15-ft cone or 60-ft line of flame: creatures in the area make a Dex save, taking 4d8 fire on failure · Or harmless shaped flame or light.

The flame learns obedience: a fifteen-foot cone or a sixty-foot line for two CE, or harmless shaped light. Art and artillery share the same four dice. For 2 CE, create a 15-ft cone or 60-ft line of flame; creatures in the area make a Dexterity save, taking 4d8 fire damage on a failure. Alternatively, you may create harmless shaped flame or light.

9Superheated Body

Passive · At Heat 5+: fire immunity, your attacks deal +1d8 fire damage · Creatures grappling you take 2d8 fire damage.

At Heat five he stops negotiating with fire. Immunity, an extra die on every attack, and two dice of grief for anyone grappling him. At Heat 5 or higher, you gain immunity to fire and your attacks deal +1d8 fire damage. Creatures grappling you take 2d8 fire damage.

11Plasma Burst

Action · 4 CE · 20-ft burst within 90 ft: creatures in the area make a Dex save, taking 6d10 fire on failure.

The harvest: four CE for a twenty-foot burst at ninety feet, six dice with a Dexterity save. Range is the flame's apology for its hunger. For 4 CE, create a 20-ft burst within 90 ft; creatures in the area make a Dexterity save, taking 6d10 fire damage on a failure.

13White Flame

Passive · At Heat 8+: your fire ignores resistance and treats immunity as resistance · Ordinary flammable terrain ignites instantly.

At Heat eight the fire stops respecting the rules, resistance ignored, immunity reduced to resistance, and the terrain itself catches at his passing. At Heat 8 or higher, your fire ignores resistance and treats immunity as resistance; ordinary flammable terrain ignites instantly.

15Nova Instability

Passive · At Heat 10: +2 fire dice to your damaging techniques, but you take unpreventable damage = your PB at the end of each turn unless you vent 2 Heat as a bonus action.

The ceiling is reached: at Heat ten he gains two dice on everything, and pays his proficiency in unpreventable damage each turn unless he vents. The star eats itself. At Heat 10, you add +2 fire dice to your damaging techniques, but you take unpreventable damage equal to your proficiency bonus at the end of each of your turns unless you vent 2 Heat as a bonus action.

18Nova Flame

Action · 10 CE, at Heat 10 · Creatures in a 40-ft radius make a Con save, taking 12d10 fire/radiant on failure (half on success) · Your Heat becomes 0 and you gain exhaustion.

The last clause of the contract: ten CE at Heat ten, forty feet of twelve-dice reckoning, then Heat zeroed and exhaustion taken. He spends the whole treasury at once. As an action, for 10 CE, at Heat 10: creatures in a 40-ft radius make a Constitution save, taking 12d10 fire/radiant damage on a failure, or half damage on a success. Your Heat becomes 0 and you gain exhaustion.

20Living Star

Passive · Heat cannot fall below 3 while Flame On is active · Fly speed becomes 90 · The first fire technique you use each round gains +1 damage die · Once per long rest, Nova Flame causes no exhaustion.

Heat never falls below three while he burns; his fly speed doubles to ninety; every round's first technique gains a die; and once per day, the nova costs no exhaustion. The star has learned to survive its own dawn. Your Heat cannot fall below 3 while Flame On is active. Your fly speed becomes 90. The first fire technique you use each round gains +1 damage die. Once per long rest, Nova Flame causes no exhaustion.

Pinnacle expression

Maximum, Domain, or equivalent.

Maximum, Supernova (10 CE, Heat 10; action): Nova Flame above. It is deliberately catastrophic but local, not literal stellar output.

Feats

Build-defining options.

Hotshot

At Heat five the bolt narrows to a lance: one target at one hundred twenty feet, cover halved in relevance. Precision is the flame's rare courtesy. at Heat 5+, your fire bolt can be fired as a focused lance: one creature within 120 ft, ignoring half and three-quarters cover. This uses your action and deals normal fire bolt damage.

Skywriter

He signs the sky. A bonus action's worth of flame-calligraphy, messages legible a mile away by night, burning for a minute. Propaganda as a technique. You shape flame and smoke with photographic precision. As a bonus action, you may write a message, sketch a map, or conjure an image in the air, legible up to 1 mile away at night or 300 ft by day, that burns for 1 minute. You may also sculpt a flame-duplicate of yourself that lasts until the start of your next turn: the first attack made against you while it burns has disadvantage, as the attacker strikes the wrong target.

Thermal Updraft

He carries a passenger in his own weather. The updraft shields the guest with his resistance and spares them his aura, fire that has learned manners. While flying with Jet Flight, you may carry one willing creature of your size or smaller without reducing your speed, cradling them in a heat-shielded updraft that grants them your fire resistance for the ride. Your fiery aura does not harm your passenger, and you may release them as a free action, if released within 30 ft of the ground, they drift down safely and land on their feet.

Flame Cage

Two CE for a ten-foot ring of white flame, a minute of Focus: enter or linger and pay three dice. He does not chase. He files the perimeter. As an action for 2 CE, you raise a 10-ft-radius, 10-ft-tall ring of white-hot flame that lasts 1 minute (Focus). Any creature that enters the ring's wall or ends its turn there takes 3d8 fire damage, or half on a successful Dexterity save. The cage restrains through threat as much as flame: enemies inside must choose between burning through the wall or staying trapped.

Rules integration

Edge cases and shared systems.

Codex rulings: Direct rolled technique/weapon attacks can use the campaign's Black Flash procedure when otherwise eligible. Passive damage, hazards, summons acting independently, and initiative-20 Domain events cannot. Generic Maximum Output applies only to ordinary features, never named Maxima or Domains.
