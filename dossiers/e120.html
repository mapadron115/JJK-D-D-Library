Human Torch

Flame body / flight / heat / nova

Power tierTier IV · Special Grade
Core law

What the technique actually does.

Core Engine — Heat. Heat 0–10. Gain 1 when you deal 10+ fire damage in a turn or begin your turn in significant flame; lose 1 after a round dealing no fire damage. At Heat 5 gain fire immunity; at 10 risk Nova Instability.

The fiction. The prevailing theory holds the technique is a body that mistook its own temperature for a promise — cursed energy that learned combustion not as an attack but as an identity, rising and falling like a flame fed on the user's aggression. Registry notes describe the nova ceiling as the reason it survives: the CE burns hottest when spent hottest, so the sorcerer is ever one bad round from becoming the thing they throw. Nothing here is inherited; it is self-immolation given discipline.

Full progression

Level 1 through 20.

L1Flame On!

As a bonus action, spend 1 CE: for 1 minute you are aflame — you gain fire resistance, melee attackers take fire damage equal to your proficiency bonus once per turn, and you can hurl a fire bolt (60 ft, 2d8).

L3Jet Flight

While aflame, you gain a fly speed of 50 ft. When you take the Dash action, you leave a 5-ft fire trail until your next turn; enemies crossing it take 1d8 fire damage.

L6Fire Sculpting

For 2 CE, create a 15-ft cone or 60-ft line of flame; creatures in the area make a Dexterity save, taking 4d8 fire damage on a failure. Alternatively, you may create harmless shaped flame or light.

L9Superheated Body

At Heat 5 or higher, you gain immunity to fire and your attacks deal +1d8 fire damage. Creatures grappling you take 2d8 fire damage.

L11Plasma Burst

For 4 CE, create a 20-ft burst within 90 ft; creatures in the area make a Dexterity save, taking 6d10 fire damage on a failure.

L13White Flame

At Heat 8 or higher, your fire ignores resistance and treats immunity as resistance; ordinary flammable terrain ignites instantly.

L15Nova Instability

At Heat 10, you add +2 fire dice to your damaging techniques, but you take unpreventable damage equal to your proficiency bonus at the end of each of your turns unless you vent 2 Heat as a bonus action.

L18Nova Flame

For 10 CE, at Heat 10: creatures in a 40-ft radius make a Constitution save, taking 12d10 fire/radiant damage on a failure, or half damage on a success. Your Heat becomes 0 and you gain exhaustion.

L20Living Star

Your Heat cannot fall below 3 while Flame On is active. Your fly speed becomes 90. The first fire technique you use each round gains +1 damage die. Once per long rest, Nova Flame causes no exhaustion.

Pinnacle expression

Maximum, Domain, or equivalent.

Maximum — Supernova (10 CE, Heat 10): Nova Flame above. It is deliberately catastrophic but local, not literal stellar output.

Feats

Build-defining options.

Hotshot

at Heat 5+, your fire bolt can be fired as a focused lance: one creature within 120 ft, ignoring half and three-quarters cover. This uses your action and deals normal fire bolt damage.

Skywriter

You shape flame and smoke with photographic precision. As a bonus action, you may write a message, sketch a map, or conjure an image in the air — legible up to 1 mile away at night or 300 ft by day — that burns for 1 minute. You may also sculpt a flame-duplicate of yourself that lasts until the start of your next turn: the first attack made against you while it burns has disadvantage, as the attacker strikes the wrong target.

Thermal Updraft

While flying with Jet Flight, you may carry one willing creature of your size or smaller without reducing your speed, cradling them in a heat-shielded updraft that grants them your fire resistance for the ride. Your fiery aura does not harm your passenger, and you may release them as a free action — if released within 30 ft of the ground, they drift down safely and land on their feet.

Flame Cage

As an action for 2 CE, you raise a 10-ft-radius, 10-ft-tall ring of white-hot flame that lasts 1 minute (Focus). Any creature that enters the ring's wall or ends its turn there takes 3d8 fire damage, or half on a successful Dexterity save. The cage restrains through threat as much as flame: enemies inside must choose between burning through the wall or staying trapped.

Rules integration

Edge cases and shared systems.

Codex rulings: Direct rolled technique/weapon attacks can use the campaign's Black Flash procedure when otherwise eligible. Passive damage, hazards, summons acting independently, and initiative-20 Domain events cannot. Generic Maximum Output applies only to ordinary features, never named Maxima or Domains.
