Human Torch

Flame body / flight / heat / nova

Power tierTier IV · Special Grade
Core law

What the technique actually does.

Core Engine — Heat. Heat 0–10. Gain 1 when you deal 10+ fire damage in a turn or begin your turn in significant flame; lose 1 after a round dealing no fire damage. At Heat 5 gain fire immunity; at 10 risk Nova Instability.

Full progression

Level 1 through 20.

1Flame On!

Bonus action 1 CE, 1 minute: fire resistance, melee attackers take PB fire once/turn, fire bolt 60 ft 2d8.

3Jet Flight

While aflame fly 50 ft. Dash leaves 5-ft fire trail until next turn, 1d8 to crossing enemies.

6Fire Sculpting

2 CE: 15-ft cone or 60-ft line, Dex save 4d8 fire; alternatively create harmless shaped flame/light.

9Superheated Body

At Heat 5+, immunity fire and attacks +1d8 fire. Grapplers take 2d8 fire.

11Plasma Burst

4 CE, 20-ft burst within 90 ft, Dex save 6d10 fire.

13White Flame

At Heat 8+, fire ignores resistance and treats immunity as resistance; ordinary flammable terrain ignites instantly.

15Nova Instability

At Heat 10, +2 fire dice to damaging techniques, but take PB unpreventable damage at end turn unless you vent 2 Heat as bonus action.

18Nova Flame

10 CE, Heat 10: 40-ft radius Con save 12d10 fire/radiant, half success. Heat becomes 0 and gain exhaustion.

20Living Star

Heat cannot fall below 3 while Flame On. Fly 90. First fire technique each round gains +1 die. Nova Flame no longer causes exhaustion once/long rest.

Pinnacle expression

Maximum, Domain, or equivalent.

Maximum — Supernova (10 CE, Heat 10): Nova Flame above. It is deliberately catastrophic but local, not literal stellar output.

Feats

Build-defining options.

Hotshot:

fire bolt crits 19–20 at Heat 5+.

Skywriter:

perfect smoke/fire shaping for communication and feints.

Thermal Updraft:

carry one willing creature while flying without speed loss.

Flame Cage:

Rules integration

Edge cases and shared systems.

Codex rulings: Direct rolled technique/weapon attacks can use the campaign's Black Flash procedure when otherwise eligible. Passive damage, hazards, summons acting independently, and initiative-20 Domain events cannot. Generic Maximum Output applies only to ordinary features, never named Maxima or Domains.
