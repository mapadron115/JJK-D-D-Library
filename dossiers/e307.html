Wedjat Eye of Horus

Metaphysical Law: The wearer’s blessing is paid for by the people attached to them.

FormAmulet
EngineWedjat Eye
Attunement1 hour*

Field notes. Subject: a master mason of flesh. Three truths govern the Eye. First, wholeness comes in six parts: six segments, 1d3 restored at dawn — one spent as a reaction to turn aside 3d10 + PB damage within 30 feet; two as an action to heal 4d10 plus the wielder's CE modifier; three to end a condition or mend a damaged limb; all six, in one reaction, when a creature within 30 feet would fall — it stands at 1 HP, then regains 6d10 and sheds one limb injury. Second, the work continues between battles: one who commands RCT may spend 3 CE in a short rest to restore a segment, once per day. Third, the ceiling is metaphysical: 63/64ths — the Eye restores what still holds a living pattern, but the truly dead are beyond its final fraction. And before any of that, it lends its wearer the sight to see the wound coming: +4 to sight-based Perception, advantage against blindness. Six parts of a whole. The seventh was never for sale.

Relic profile

6 Eye Segments · Regains 1d3 expended segments at dawn

Jujutsu origin

The Wedjat Eye is already a symbol of healing, protection, restoration, and recovered wholeness. In JJK terms, countless protective amulets acted like tiny low-grade cursed tools; this is the singular original pattern that learned how to force damaged bodies back toward the shape they are supposed to possess.

Protective Sight

Gain +4 to Perception checks relying on sight and advantage on saving throws against being blinded.

Eye of Protection — Reaction, 1 segment

Reduce damage dealt to you or a creature you can see within 30 ft by 3d10 + PB.

Eye of Restoration — Action, 2 segments

A creature within 5 ft regains 4d10 + your CE modifier HP. A wielder with no CE uses Wisdom instead.

Recovered Piece — Action, 3 segments

End one blinded, deafened, poisoned, paralyzed, or stunned condition, or repair one damaged limb.

Whole Eye — Reaction, all 6 segments

When you or a creature within 30 ft would be reduced to 0 HP, it instead remains at 1 HP, then heals 6d10 HP and removes one limb injury.

RCE Reassembly

A creature capable of RCT may spend 3 CE during a short rest to restore 1 expended Eye Segment, once per day.

Binding Vow — 63/64ths

The Eye can restore what still has a living pattern, but it cannot create the final missing fraction from nothing: it cannot resurrect a creature that is truly dead.

Designer’s note. A healing relic with a hard metaphysical ceiling rather than unlimited resurrection.

Rules integration

Cursed-tool attunement

Campaign canon

Cursed-tool attunement: you have PB attunement slots (2–6). This tool occupies one slot and requires a one-hour ritual. Attunement ends voluntarily over a short rest, on death, or when you replace it while over capacity. Distance does not break attunement. Cursed-tool attunement is separate from magic-item attunement.
