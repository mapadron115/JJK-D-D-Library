Exact player-facing procedures

Reaction

Sharpened resolve

When you fail a Constitution saving throw to maintain Concentration, you can spend 1 cursed energy as a reaction to succeed instead. Declare the reaction after seeing the failed roll; the concentration is maintained as if the save had succeeded.

Observer’s account

Field notes. Subject: a second hand on a fraying rope. The ring does one thing: when Concentration breaks, a failed save, 1 CE spent as a reaction knots it back together. Two truths. First, timing is the whole of it: the reaction is declared after the failure is seen, so no energy is ever wasted on a save you would have made. Second, it buys continuity, not immunity: the blow still lands, the damage still counts, only the thread of focus is spared. This is the economics of the long fight, where a sustained technique outlasts the sorcerer who drops it. As iron sharpens iron, so a friend sharpens a friend. Hence the maxim: the strongest concentration is the one that gets a second chance.
