Field entry

Journal Entry field report, converted to a table-ready stat block

Field notes. Subject: the tavern brawler as disease vector. Small, mottled, hot-headed, it has advantage on initiative because it has never, in its existence, waited for anything. Its punches are merely the delivery system: a failed Constitution save plants the burning rash, poisoned for a minute, saves repeated each turn, and every failed repeat deals another 1d6 poison as the rash ripens toward rupture. Confronted by a crowd, it answers with Infectious Burst (recharge 5–6): fifteen feet of 2d6 poison, the same rash seeded to many at once. It is not strong, and it never backs down, which is exactly why you isolate it before it flexes, and treat the rash before it bursts.

Record

Classification & Risk

A Disease Cursed Spirit, Grade 4 (CR 1/8–1/2 band). Harasses civilians for amusement; when challenged, always strikes first. Reckless aggression warrants caution during exorcism.

Stats

Ability scores & traits

STR 14 (+2) · DEX 12 (+1) · CON 14 (+2) · INT 5 (−3) · WIS 8 (−1) · CHA 8 (−1). HP 28 (5d8 + 5), AC 13, speed 30 ft. Invisibility. Plague (disease curse): its punches and bursts spread infection through physical contact. Hot-Headed: has advantage on initiative rolls.

Actions

Actions

Punch. Melee Attack: +5 to hit, reach 5 ft. Hit: 1d6 + 3 bludgeoning damage, and the target must succeed on a DC 11 Constitution saving throw or contract burning rash: poisoned for 1 minute, repeating the save at the end of each of its turns; each failed repeat deals an extra 1d6 poison damage as the rash worsens toward rupture. Infectious Burst (recharge 5–6, when facing multiple opponents). Each creature within 15 ft. must succeed on a DC 12 Constitution saving throw or take 2d6 poison damage and contract burning rash as above.

Risk

Special

Not particularly strong — but it never backs down, and the rash it spreads turns a skirmish into a triage. Isolate it before it flexes; treat the rash before it ruptures.

Campaign canon notes

Field rule — low-grade perception: Per the C&C field rules, Grade 3 and lower curses are invisible to creatures without cursed energy. A creature in a life-or-death situation at less than half its hit point maximum may temporarily or permanently gain the ability to see them. This spirit carries the Invisibility trait below.
