The Ultra Dragon

The heads argue. The heart decides. It has never once flown away from the others.

Power tierTier III · Blaster
Combat roleBlaster
Core engineFour elemental breaths, one shared heart
Domain—

Field notes. Subject: loyalty, with four heads and one heart. Three truths govern the Ultra Dragon. First: the heads argue and the heart decides — flame, spark, frost, and stone, four tempers sharing a single pulse, and not one of them has ever flown away from the others. Second: it chose its riders. None tamed the Ultra Dragon; they were auditioned by it, weighed head by head, and found worth carrying. Third: gentleness is not the absence of power but its discipline. A creature that could level a city block with one exhalation instead learns the exact weight of a rider's hand on the reins. Consider: the Ultra Dragon does not serve because it must. It serves because it decided to — and a decision made by four heads at once is not easily unmade.

Full progression

Feature map

1Four Heads, One Heart.

Four Heads, One Heart

You bear four draconic heads: Flame, Spark, Frost, and Stone. As an action, one head breathes a 15-foot cone; each creature in the cone makes a Dexterity saving throw against your CE save DC. Flame deals 1d8 fire, Spark 1d8 lightning, Frost 1d8 cold, Stone 1d8 bludgeoning (each head's damage increases by 1d8 at levels 6, 11, and 18). The heads share one hit point pool — you are a single creature. You gain darkvision out to 60 feet.

3Wings of the Quartet.

Wings of the Quartet

The sky accepts all four at once. You gain a flying speed of 60 feet. While flying, you may carry one willing creature of your size or smaller (2 at level 11, 4 at level 18).

6Twin Exhalation.

Twin Exhalation

Two tempers, one breath. When you take the breathe action, two heads breathe instead of one — two 15-foot cones, which may differ in element and direction. Resolve each cone separately.

11Fourfold Exhalation.

Fourfold Exhalation

The whole quartet sings at once. When you take the breathe action, all four heads breathe as a single action — four 15-foot cones, each in the direction of its head. Resolve each cone separately.

14Stormheart Retaliation.

Stormheart Retaliation

The heart answers every wound. As a reaction for 2 CE, when a creature damages you, one head of your choice breathes at it — a 15-foot cone resolved as your level 1 breath.

18Dragon's Oath.

Dragon's Oath

It chose its riders, and it keeps them. As a reaction, when an ally within 30 feet of you is hit by an attack, you interpose yourself — you take the hit instead, and the attack resolves against you.

20Elemental Cataclysm.

Elemental Cataclysm

Once per long rest, all four heads exhale as one: a 90-foot cone of mingled elements. Each creature in the cone makes a Dexterity saving throw against your CE save DC, taking 8d8 damage on a failure (2d8 fire + 2d8 lightning + 2d8 cold + 2d8 bludgeoning), or half as much on a success. A creature that fails is also knocked prone.

Innate Technique Feats

Build-defining options.

These are optional character-building feats. You must meet each listed prerequisite before selecting it.

Prerequisite: Must have Wings of the Quartet

Loyal Steed

The chosen are carried gently. While you are carrying riders, you may take the Dodge action as a bonus action, and your riders have advantage on saving throws to stay mounted.

Prerequisite: Must have Twin Exhalation

Elemental Weaving

Two tempers braided are more than their sum. When two heads breathe on the same area, the pairing takes on a combined effect: Flame + Frost fills the area with blinding steam (Constitution save or blinded for 1 round); Spark + Stone hurls jagged shrapnel (an additional 1d8 piercing damage); Flame + Spark raises a firestorm (targets have disadvantage on their Dexterity saving throws).

Prerequisite: Must have Four Heads, One Heart

All-Seeing

Four heads watch everywhere at once. You cannot be surprised, and you have advantage on Wisdom (Perception) checks.

Prerequisite: Must have the Stone head (Four Heads, One Heart)

Stone Belly

The stone head's gullet opens onto a small demiplane. You may store up to 500 pounds of objects within it, or shelter one willing or mindless creature — a sheltered creature can breathe normally inside.

Rules integration

Edge cases and shared systems.

One creature, four heads: the heads share a single hit point pool and a single set of conditions — you are one creature. Effects that target "a head" (such as Stone Belly) do not create separate targets.
Breath cones: each head's cone originates from that head. With Twin Exhalation and Fourfold Exhalation, the cones may point in different directions; each cone is resolved separately with its own saving throws.
Carrying: Wings of the Quartet carries willing creatures only — unwilling creatures cannot be grappled into the carry. A carried creature moves with you and can act normally on its own turn.
Elemental Weaving: the combined effects apply only where the two cones overlap on the same area. A creature caught in only one cone suffers that cone's normal effect.
Stone Belly: the demiplane holds air — a sheltered creature breathes normally. Only one creature may shelter inside at a time; objects stored do not count against this limit.
