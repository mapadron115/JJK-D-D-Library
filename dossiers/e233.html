Features & procedures

Properties & techniques

Exact player-facing procedures

Answering Cut

reaction spend 1 Answer when attacked in melee: make weapon attack after triggering attack resolves.

Truth's Edge

2 CE on hit: target Cha save or cannot knowingly speak a deliberate lie until start next turn; silence is allowed.

No Escape Clause

spend 2 Answer on opportunity hit to prevent teleport until end turn.

Release: Retaliation Without Delay

7 CE, 1 minute, bonus action to activate (invented): first Answering Cut each round does not spend Answer and adds 1 Tool Die.

The fiction. Scholars classify Fragarach as an answer that predates its question: the blade only strikes those who strike first because the curse it carries was born from a sorcerer who never drew first. Registry notes hold the truth-binding and the retaliatory cut are the same resolve — a refusal to lie and a refusal to be struck unanswered, made steel. It is called the Answerer, the prevailing theory goes, because it never learned to ask.

Rules integration

Cursed-tool attunement

Campaign canon

Cursed-tool attunement: you have PB attunement slots (2–6). This tool occupies one slot and requires a one-hour ritual. Attunement ends voluntarily over a short rest, on death, or when you replace it while over capacity. Distance does not break attunement. Cursed-tool attunement is separate from magic-item attunement.
Balance & counterplay. Counterplay is simple: do not feed its reactive conditions, use range, or force its reaction elsewhere.
