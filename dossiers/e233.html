Features & procedures

Properties & techniques

Exact player-facing procedures

Answering Cut

The doctrine in one procedure: a reaction and one banked Answer, and the cut answers the attack after it resolves. Second by principle, certain by nature. reaction spend 1 Answer when attacked in melee: make weapon attack after triggering attack resolves.

Truth's Edge

Two CE on a hit lays the older binding: the struck cannot knowingly speak a deliberate lie until the next turn — though silence remains his right. The blade that refuses to be struck unanswered also refuses to be answered untruthfully. 2 CE on hit: target Cha save or cannot knowingly speak a deliberate lie until start next turn; silence is allowed.

No Escape Clause

Two banked Answer on an opportunity hit, and the escape is denied: no teleport until the turn ends. The answer, once owed, must be delivered in person. spend 2 Answer on opportunity hit to prevent teleport until end turn.

Release: Retaliation Without Delay

Seven CE for one minute of the doctrine at full flood: each round's first Answering Cut costs nothing and carries an extra die. For this minute, the blade's patience is infinite and its memory is perfect. 7 CE, 1 minute, bonus action to activate (invented): first Answering Cut each round does not spend Answer and adds 1 Tool Die.

Field notes. Subject: an answer that predates its question. Three truths govern him. First, the Answer is earned only by being struck at or by being ready: a Readied action whose trigger occurs, or an enemy attacking a bearer who has not attacked it this round — the blade rewards the hand that waits, never the hand that starts. Second, the Answering Cut is the curse's whole doctrine: a reaction, one banked Answer, and the weapon attack lands after the triggering attack resolves — retaliation, not interruption; the answer arrives second by principle, and it arrives regardless. Third, the truth-binding and the cut are the same resolve: two CE on a hit and the struck cannot knowingly speak a deliberate lie until the next turn — silence permitted, evasion permitted, falsehood refused — because the sorcerer the curse was born from never drew first and never lied. It is called the Answerer, the registry holds, because it never learned to ask.

Rules integration

Cursed-tool attunement

Campaign canon

Cursed-tool attunement: you have PB attunement slots (2–6). This tool occupies one slot and requires a one-hour ritual. Attunement ends voluntarily over a short rest, on death, or when you replace it while over capacity. Distance does not break attunement. Cursed-tool attunement is separate from magic-item attunement.
Balance & counterplay. Counterplay is simple: do not feed its reactive conditions, use range, or force its reaction elsewhere.
