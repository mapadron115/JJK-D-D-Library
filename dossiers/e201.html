Core law

What the technique actually does.

Character engine

Manifest one of seven Terror Faces at a time. Cedar zones extend your aura, so the technique is about choosing which fear-law matters and shaping a forest that carries it.

Technique save DC: 8 + PB + chosen CE ability modifier. Technique attack: PB + chosen CE ability modifier.

Field notes. Subject: seven refusals, worn one at a time. Three truths govern the Terrors. First: these are not seven techniques. The bearer's CE learned the exact shapes of being feared, the way a forest learns the wind's direction, and fear, properly worn, is a kind of law. Each Face is an edict, Glare, Roar, Scent, Blaze, Maze, Majesty, Doom, a different law of fear. Second: the edict is chosen, not stacked. One Face at a time, switched as a bonus action, the technique is about choosing which fear-law matters, moment by moment. Third: the forest carries the face. Cedar zones extend the aura outward, so the battlefield itself enforces the chosen law; destroy the zones and the bearer shrinks back to his own fifteen feet. Maximum: the Cedar Forest Before the Axe, seven zones, one per Face, moved at initiative 20, the unprotected suffering failed-save results without the saving throw. The cedar is incidental; the forest was only the first thing that ever cowered.

Full progression

Level progression

1First Three Terrors

Passive · One Terror Face active at a time, 15-ft aura · Bonus action to switch Faces · Glare: Wis save or approach ends; Roar: Wis save or next attack −PB damage; Scent: unseen benefits defeated in aura.

The first three edicts. One Face at a time, switched as a bonus action, aura fifteen feet: Glare ends the approach of those who fail, Roar taxes the aggression of those who enter, Scent defeats the benefits of being unseen. Three refusals, and the battlefield has already learned the bearer's handwriting. You manifest one Terror Face at a time, choosing from the first three; switching Faces is a bonus action. Your active Face's aura is a 15-foot radius around you. Glare: a creature attempting to approach you (moving within your aura) makes a Wisdom save; on failure its movement toward you ends for the turn. Roar: the first time each round a creature enters your aura or starts its turn there, it makes a Wisdom save (invented); on a failed save, the creature's next attack before the end of its next turn deals PB less damage (minimum 1). Scent: defeats the benefits of being unseen within your aura.

3Cedar Forest

Action · 2 CE · Three 10-ft cedar zones within 60 ft · 1 minute · Half cover; active Terror Face aura radiates from each zone within 60 ft of you.

The law gains territory. Action, 2 CE: three ten-foot cedar zones within sixty feet for a minute, half cover, and the active Face's aura radiates from each zone within sixty feet of the bearer, no action, no anchor. The forest carries the face. I have watched a battlefield become a jurisdiction. As an action for 2 CE, you grow three 10-foot cedar zones within 60 feet for 1 minute; they provide half cover. The forest carries your face: your active Terror Face's aura effects also originate from a 15-foot radius around each cedar zone within 60 feet of you, no action, no anchoring cost. Destroy the zones and you shrink back to your own 15 feet.

6Fourth & Fifth Terrors

Passive · Blaze: hostile ending its turn in aura, Wis save or frightened until end of its next turn · Maze: aura is difficult terrain, no Dash.

Two more refusals enter the statute book. Blaze frightens the hostile that ends its turn in the aura; Maze makes the aura difficult terrain where none may Dash. The technique's vocabulary is growing, and each new word is a different way to say no. You unlock the fourth and fifth Faces. Blaze: the first hostile creature that ends its turn in your Face's aura makes a Wisdom save vs your Technique DC; on failure it is frightened of you until the end of its next turn; on success no effect. Maze: the Face's aura is difficult terrain and creatures inside it can't Dash.

11Sixth & Seventh Terrors

Passive · Majesty: allies have advantage on saves vs frightened · Doom: bloodied creatures entering aura, Wis save or frightened · When a cedar zone is destroyed, switch Face once per round with no action.

The final edicts, and they are the strangest. Majesty steadies the bearer's allies against fear, the law protects its own; Doom frightens the bloodied who enter the aura. And when a cedar zone falls, the bearer may switch Face once per round without action. The forest's destruction is itself a kind of legislation. You unlock the sixth and seventh Faces. Majesty: your allies have advantage on saves against being frightened. Doom: bloodied creatures entering your aura make a Wisdom save or are frightened. When a cedar zone is destroyed, you may switch your Face once per round without using an action.

18Domain Expansion: Cedar Forest Before the Axe

Full-turn action · 20 CE · Enclosed 90-ft radius, up to 1 min · Place seven 20-ft Terror zones (one per Face), non-overlapping; initiative-20 event moves each up to 10 ft; a chosen newly-covered hostile resolves its Face (max two per event); unprotected targets take the failed-save result automatically.

Cedars rise in every direction, trunks straight as temple pillars, their crowns braided into a roof that admits light only in green shafts. The air is resin and cold shadow, and the forest floor is soft with centuries of fallen needles that swallow every footstep. Seven clearings wait among the trees, each holding a different quality of stillness, and the axe has not yet fallen, which is the terror of it.

The legislature in full session. Seven twenty-foot zones, one per Face, placed non-overlapping; at initiative 20 each moves ten feet; the newly covered resolve their Face, two per event, and the unprotected suffer the failed-save result without the saving throw. The Domain is the technique's argument completed: seven laws, seven territories, and nowhere left to stand that is not governed. Full-turn action, 20 CE, enclosed 90-foot radius centered on you, up to 1 minute, Technique Focus. Place seven non-overlapping 20-foot Terror zones, one for each Face. At initiative 20 move each zone up to 10 feet. A chosen hostile newly covered by a Face immediately resolves that Face, to a maximum of two Faces during the event. For an unprotected target, a Face that normally calls for a saving throw applies its failed-save result automatically; a protected creature makes that Face’s normal save instead. Faces that are purely sensory or beneficial retain their normal wording. This event never stacks more than two Terror results on one creature at once.

Innate Technique Feats

Innate Technique Feats

4 optional build choices

Cedar Warden

The jurisdiction widens. The active Face's aura now radiates twenty feet from each cedar zone within one hundred twenty feet, the bearer's own fifteen feet unchanged. The warden's beat was always larger than his body. Now the map agrees. The forest carries your face farther and wider. Your active Terror Face's aura effects now originate from a 20-foot radius around each cedar zone within 120 feet of you (instead of 15 feet within 60 feet). Your own 15-foot personal aura is unchanged.

Terrible Countenance

The felled tree testifies. When a cedar zone is destroyed, switch Face immediately without action, and resolve the new Face against the destroyer. Mark this: the forest's death is not a loss. It is a change of edict, and the lumberjack is the first to hear it. When a Cedar zone is destroyed, you may immediately switch your Face without using an action and resolve the new Face against the creature that destroyed it.

Forest Voice

The law needs no voice. Switch Faces while silenced, gagged, or in magical silence, and while voiceless, the switch costs no action at all. The forest speaks where the bearer cannot. Consider: the Terrors were never spoken. They were always only ever shown. Switching your Terror Face is not a verbal act: you may change Faces even while silenced, gagged, or inside magical silence, and while you cannot speak, switching your Face costs no action at all. The forest speaks where your voice cannot.

Sevenfold Guardian (11+)

The visage splits. Once per short rest, maintain two Faces until the end of the next turn, both fear-laws holding at once, hostiles resolving both where applicable, no creature suffering more than one failed result on a single Face. Two refusals, simultaneous. The bearer has stopped choosing between edicts and started issuing them in pairs. Once per short rest, when you switch your Terror Face, you may maintain two Faces simultaneously until the end of your next turn, your visage splits and both fear-laws hold. Hostiles in your Aura resolve both Faces' effects where applicable, but a single creature cannot suffer more than one failed-save result from this overlap. When the duration ends, you keep the most recently chosen Face and the other fades.

Counterplay Destroy Cedar zones and force the user to choose which Terror Face matters. Seven options are breadth, not seven simultaneous passives.
