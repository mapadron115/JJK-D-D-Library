Seven Terrors of the Cedar Forest

Humbaba inspiration. Manifest one of seven Terror Faces at a time. Cedar zones extend your aura, so the technique is about choosing which fear-law matters and shaping a forest that carries it.

Core statWIS or CHA

Scaling died6 → d8 → d10 → d12
Level progression1 / 3 / 6 / 11 / 18
Milestone featurePlayer-facing rules
First Three TerrorsGlare stops approach on failed Wis save; Roar reduces first attack's damage on failed save; Scent defeats unseen benefits in aura when scent can reach.
Cedar ForestAction, 2 CE: three 10-ft cedar zones within 60 ft for 1 minute; half cover and count as Aura spaces while within 30 ft of you.
Fourth & Fifth TerrorsBlaze burns first hostile ending in Aura; Maze makes Aura difficult terrain and prevents Dash.
Sixth & Seventh TerrorsMajesty gives allies advantage vs frightened; Doom can frighten bloodied entrants. Destroying Cedar lets you switch Face once/round without action.
Domain Expansion: Cedar Forest Before the AxeFull-turn action, 20 CE, enclosed 90-foot radius centered on you, up to 1 minute, Technique Focus. Place seven non-overlapping 20-foot Terror zones, one for each Face. At initiative 20 move each zone up to 10 feet. A chosen hostile newly covered by a Face immediately resolves that Face, to a maximum of two Faces during the event. For an unprotected target, a Face that normally calls for a saving throw applies its failed-save result automatically; a protected creature makes that Face’s normal save instead. Faces that are purely sensory or beneficial retain their normal wording. This event never stacks more than two Terror results on one creature at once.

Domain procedure

This entry uses the repaired campaign Domain rules. The level-18 feature above is complete: its printed action, CE cost, radius, duration, initiative-20 law, fallback protection, and counterplay replace any legacy blanket auto-hit wording.

Character engine

Manifest one of seven Terror Faces at a time. Cedar zones extend your aura, so the technique is about choosing which fear-law matters and shaping a forest that carries it.

Technique save DC: 8 + PB + chosen CE ability modifier. Technique attack: PB + chosen CE ability modifier.

Counterplay

Destroy Cedar zones and force the user to choose which Terror Face matters. Seven options are breadth, not seven simultaneous passives.

Adaptation note. This is original JJK 5e homebrew inspired by Humbaba and the named tradition. The folklore/myth supplies the motif; the resource engine, CE economy, progression, feats, Maximum and Domain procedures are game mechanics created for this codex.
