What the technique actually does.

Character engine

Manifest one of seven Terror Faces at a time. Cedar zones extend your aura, so the technique is about choosing which fear-law matters and shaping a forest that carries it.

Technique save DC: 8 + PB + chosen CE ability modifier. Technique attack: PB + chosen CE ability modifier.

Full progression

Level progression

1First Three Terrors

Glare stops approach on failed Wis save; Roar reduces first attack's damage on failed save; Scent defeats unseen benefits in aura when scent can reach.

3Cedar Forest

Action, 2 CE: three 10-ft cedar zones within 60 ft for 1 minute; half cover and count as Aura spaces while within 30 ft of you.

6Fourth & Fifth Terrors

Blaze burns first hostile ending in Aura; Maze makes Aura difficult terrain and prevents Dash.

11Sixth & Seventh Terrors

Majesty gives allies advantage vs frightened; Doom can frighten bloodied entrants. Destroying Cedar lets you switch Face once/round without action.

18Domain Expansion: Cedar Forest Before the Axe

Full-turn action, 20 CE, enclosed 90-foot radius centered on you, up to 1 minute, Technique Focus. Place seven non-overlapping 20-foot Terror zones, one for each Face. At initiative 20 move each zone up to 10 feet. A chosen hostile newly covered by a Face immediately resolves that Face, to a maximum of two Faces during the event. For an unprotected target, a Face that normally calls for a saving throw applies its failed-save result automatically; a protected creature makes that Face’s normal save instead. Faces that are purely sensory or beneficial retain their normal wording. This event never stacks more than two Terror results on one creature at once.

Innate Technique Feats

Innate Technique Feats

4 optional build choices

Cedar Warden

You may originate one of your Face effects from a Cedar zone within 30 feet of you instead of from yourself, using your normal save DC.

Terrible Countenance

When a Cedar zone is destroyed, you may immediately switch your Face without using an action and resolve the new Face against the creature that destroyed it.

Forest Voice

Switching your Terror Face is not a verbal act: you may change Faces even while silenced, gagged, or inside magical silence. As a bonus action, you may project the switch through one of your Cedar zones within 30 feet, changing your Face and originating its effect from that zone's position instead of your own. The forest speaks where your voice cannot.

Sevenfold Guardian (11+)

Once per short rest, when you switch your Terror Face, you may maintain two Faces simultaneously until the end of your next turn — your visage splits and both fear-laws hold. Hostiles in your Aura resolve both Faces' effects where applicable, but a single creature cannot suffer more than one failed-save result from this overlap. When the duration ends, you keep the most recently chosen Face and the other fades.

Rules integration

Edge cases and shared systems.

Domain procedure This entry uses the repaired campaign Domain rules. The level-18 feature above is complete: its printed action, CE cost, radius, duration, initiative-20 law, fallback protection, and counterplay replace any legacy blanket auto-hit wording.
Counterplay Destroy Cedar zones and force the user to choose which Terror Face matters. Seven options are breadth, not seven simultaneous passives.
