Core law

What the technique actually does.

The whole technique is this paragraph: one bonus action to polarize, and every feature downstream keys off the mark. Polarity engine. As a bonus action for 1 CE, polarize one creature or metal object you can see within 60 feet: it counts as magnetized by you until the end of your next turn. You sense the direction and distance of all polarized metal within 120 feet.

Technique save DC: 8 + PB + chosen CE ability modifier. Technique attack: PB + chosen CE ability modifier.

Field notes. Subject: a law of physics with a grudge. Three truths govern it. First: everything here is downstream of a single tax, one bonus action, one cursed energy, and a creature or hunk of metal is polarized until the end of the user’s next turn. No polarity, no technique. Second: this is the only dossier in the archive that loots the enemy’s inventory mid-fight. Turn the Blade tears a metal projectile aside and sends it home; Crush constricts the armor the enemy chose to wear, the counterplay is printed in the file: drop your metal or be governed by it. Third: the Domain is the thesis stated as geography. At initiative twenty every armed hostile is dragged to the monopole, pinned, and disarmed. I have watched a user end a fight without striking a single blow. He simply collected everyone’s weapons first.

Full progression

Level progression

1Polarize & Push/Pull

Polarize (bonus action · 1 CE): magnetize one creature or metal object you can see within 60 ft to end of your next turn · Attract/Repel (action · 1 CE): move one polarized target within 60 ft up to 20 ft straight toward or away from you (unwilling: Str save negates); collision: both Dex save or prone; pulled into metal: restrained until it uses an action to pass a Str check vs your Technique DC.

The opening move is a tax and a shove, polarize by bonus action, then attract or repel the marked twenty feet, with the unwilling saving on Strength and the pulled sticking fast to metal. Polarize (bonus action, 1 CE): magnetize one creature or metal object you can see within 60 feet until the end of your next turn, as above.

Attract / Repel (action, 1 CE): choose one polarized target within 60 feet. Attract: pull it up to 20 feet in a straight line toward you. Repel: push it up to 20 feet directly away from you. An unwilling creature makes a Strength save to negate the movement.

If the target strikes a solid surface or another creature, both make a Dexterity save or fall prone. A creature pulled into a metal surface sticks fast, it is restrained until it uses an action to succeed on a Strength check against your Technique DC.

3Magnetic Flight & Scrap Barrage

Passive · Fly speed = your walking speed.

At the third level the user stops walking and starts throwing, flight at walking speed, and a coin per action hurls polarized scrap for piercing damage. Magnetic Flight: you gain a fly speed equal to your walking speed.

Action · 1 CE · Ranged technique attack, 60 ft: 2d8 + CE modifier piercing.

Scrap Barrage (action, 1 CE): make a ranged technique attack against a creature within 60 feet, hurling polarized scrap. On a hit it takes 2d8 + your CE modifier piercing damage.

6Turn the Blade & Crush

Reaction (a creature attacks you with a metal weapon or projectile) · 1 CE · Attacker Str save vs your Technique DC: fail = the attack misses (metal torn aside) · Projectile: immediately redirect — ranged technique attack vs a creature of your choice within 60 ft, dealing the projectile's normal damage on a hit.

The sixth level turns the enemy’s arsenal into the user’s, a reaction that tears metal attacks aside and redirects projectiles, and a bonus-action constriction of the armor the target chose to wear. Turn the Blade (reaction, 1 CE): when a creature attacks you with a metal weapon or a metal projectile, force the attacker to make a Strength save against your Technique DC. On a failure the attack misses as the metal is torn aside. If the triggering attack was a metal projectile, you may immediately redirect it, make a ranged technique attack against a creature of your choice within 60 feet, dealing the projectile's normal damage on a hit.

Bonus action · 2 CE · One polarized creature wearing metal armor or carrying a metal object: Str save or restrained to end of its next turn + 2d8 bludgeoning, half damage on success.

Crush (bonus action, 2 CE): one polarized creature wearing metal armor or carrying a metal object makes a Strength save. On a failure it is restrained until the end of its next turn as its own gear constricts, and it takes 2d8 bludgeoning damage. On a success it takes half damage and is not restrained.

11Polarity Field & Railgun

Action · 3 CE · Focus, 1 min · 15-ft emanation centered on you: attacks with metal weapons/projectiles vs creatures inside at disadvantage; polarized creatures inside at dis on saves vs your Attract, Repel, and Crush.

The eleventh level is area denial and artillery, a fifteen-foot field that disadvantages metal attacks and hostile saves against the pulls, and a once-per-turn railgun that accelerates scrap instead of retrieving it. Polarity Field (action, 3 CE, Focus, 1 minute): a 15-foot emanation centered on you. Attacks with metal weapons or metal projectiles against creatures inside the field have disadvantage. Polarized creatures inside the field have disadvantage on saving throws against your Attract, Repel, and Crush.

Passive · Once per turn, when you use Attract on a polarized metal object (not a creature): accelerate it instead — ranged technique attack vs a creature within 120 ft: 4d8 piercing on a hit.

Railgun: once per turn when you use Attract on a polarized metal object (not a creature), you may accelerate it instead of pulling it to you, make a ranged technique attack against a creature within 120 feet. On a hit it takes 4d8 piercing damage.

18Domain Expansion: The Master's Field

Full-turn action · 20 CE · 60-ft radius enclosed domain centered on you, up to 1 min, Technique Focus · Initiative 20: each hostile wearing/carrying metal: Str save or pulled 20 ft toward the center and restrained (pinned on the monopole) to the next initiative 20 + disarmed (weapons orbit the center) until it leaves or the domain ends; success: pulled 10 ft, not restrained · Counterplay: drop your metal, or break the user's Focus.

The air goes iron-tasting and every hair stands up. At the domain's center a single point of absolute stillness hangs in the air, a dark bead of nothing that pulls at fillings, buckles, and blood all at once, and faint lines of force radiate from it like the grain of wood. Metal does not fall here, it kneels.

The Domain is the monopole made manifest, full-turn, twenty coins, and at initiative twenty every armed hostile is dragged to the center, pinned, and disarmed until they leave. Full-turn action, 20 CE, enclosed 60-foot radius centered on you, up to 1 minute, Technique Focus. Sure-hit event at initiative 20: each hostile wearing metal armor or carrying a metal weapon or object makes a Strength save. On a failure it is pulled 20 feet toward the domain's center and restrained, pinned against the central monopole, until the next initiative 20, and its metal weapons are ripped from its grasp to orbit the center (it is disarmed until it leaves the domain or the domain ends). On a success it is pulled 10 feet and is not restrained. Counterplay: drop your metal before the count, or break the user's Focus.

20Electromagnetic Apex

Passive · Metal sense: 1 mile · Blackout Pulse (action · 5 CE, once per long rest): 60-ft burst within 120 ft — constructs Con save or stunned 1 round then shut down 1 min; nonmagical tech stops working 1 min (attended: bearer's save).

The twentieth level extends the sense to a mile and grants the Blackout Pulse, constructs stunned and shut down, nonmagical technology silenced for a minute. Your metal sense extends to 1 mile.

Blackout Pulse (action, 5 CE, once per long rest): a 60-foot burst centered on a point within 120 feet of you. Each construct in the burst makes a Constitution save; on a failure it is stunned for 1 round and then shut down for 1 minute. Nonmagical technological objects in the burst stop working for 1 minute (an attended object uses its bearer's save).

Pinnacle expression

Maximum, Domain, or equivalent.

The Maximum polarizes the battlefield itself, for one minute, every metal object and every armed creature within one hundred and twenty feet counts as marked, and the push-pull range doubles. Maximum, Iron Requiem (10 CE): as an action, for 1 minute every metal object and every creature wearing or carrying metal within 120 feet of you counts as polarized by you (no per-target cost), and your Attract and Repel range becomes 120 feet.

Feats

Build-defining options.

Prerequisite: level 11

Blood Iron

Once per long rest the technique reaches past metal entirely, polarize the iron in a living creature’s blood, and one effect ignores the metal requirement with the target saving at disadvantage. Once per long rest, you may Polarize the iron in a living creature's blood. One Attract, Repel, or Crush ignores the metal requirement entirely and works on any creature; the target has disadvantage on its saving throw against that effect.

Prerequisite: Magnetic Flight

Debris Throne

Flight becomes fortification, orbiting scrap grants +2 AC against ranged attacks, and a reaction flings a shard at anyone who misses. While you are flying, orbiting scrap grants you +2 AC against ranged attacks. Once per turn when a ranged attack misses you, you may use your reaction to fling a shard, make a ranged technique attack against the attacker, dealing 2d8 + your CE modifier piercing damage on a hit.

Prerequisite: Attract or Repel

Polarity Reversal

A saved pull is not a wasted one, reverse the field as part of the same effect and move the target ten feet the other way, no save. When a creature succeeds on its saving throw against your Attract or Repel, you may reverse the field as part of the same effect: it moves 10 feet in the opposite direction (no saving throw). You may do this once per turn.

Prerequisite: Crush

Polar Cage

The constriction learns permanence, one more coin shapes the crushed metal into a cage, and the restraint lasts a minute with a save each turn. When Crush restrains a target, you may spend 1 additional CE to shape its metal into a cage: the restraint lasts 1 minute instead of until the end of its next turn (the target repeats the Strength save at the end of each of its turns, ending the effect on a success).

Rules integration

Edge cases and shared systems.

Codex rulings: Direct rolled technique/weapon attacks can use the campaign's Black Flash procedure when otherwise eligible. Passive damage, hazards, summons acting independently, and initiative-20 Domain events cannot. Generic Maximum Output applies only to ordinary features, never named Maxima or Domains.
Counterplay: nonmetal weapons and armor, spread out against pulls, drop metal before the Domain's initiative-20 count, break Focus.
