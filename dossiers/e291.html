Core law

What the technique actually does.

The whole technique is this paragraph: one bonus action to polarize, and every feature downstream keys off the mark. Electromagnetic Signature. All creatures and objects made from conventional matter possess an electromagnetic signature and can be affected by this technique. Metallic and highly conductive targets are Magnetically Responsive: certain abilities gain stronger effects against them. Polarize (bonus action · 1 CE · 60 ft): assign one creature or object you can see a positive or negative polarity for 1 minute. An unwilling target does not save against becoming polarized; it saves against the effects subsequently applied to it. You can safely maintain polarized marks equal to your PB + 2; each mark beyond that deals you 1d6 psychic damage at the start of each of your turns while it remains active. You always know the direction and approximate distance of your polarized targets while they remain within 1 mile. Once per turn, you can polarize a Magnetically Responsive target without spending CE.

Technique save DC: 8 + PB + chosen CE ability modifier. Technique attack: PB + chosen CE ability modifier.

Field notes. Subject: a law of physics with a grudge. Three truths govern it. First: everything here is downstream of a single mark. No polarity, no technique — but polarity itself is no longer the tax it was, because everything carries a signature now; metal is only easier. Second: the only dossier in the archive that loots the enemy’s inventory mid-fight has learned to loot the battlefield itself. Field Deflection turns an attack aside and sends it home; Field Compression squeezes any polarized body, not just the armored ones; the old counterplay — drop your metal — is dead, and what replaces it is narrower: break Focus, leave the field, or hide your signature. Third: the Domain is the thesis stated as geography: every creature and object inside takes the mark, and at every initiative count 20 the whole room is pulled, pinned, and disarmed at once. I have watched a user end a fight without striking a single blow. He simply collected the battlefield first.

Focus. Wherever this dossier says Focus, it means you are concentrating (the concentration condition).

The opening move is a tax, a mark, and a shove — and the mark is the whole technique.

The field has a sense now: current, charge, and heartbeat, all visible.

18Domain Expansion: The Master's Field

Full-turn action · 20 CE · 60-ft closed domain · Focus, up to 1 min · Every creature and object inside is automatically polarized; you freely determine and change their polarities · Initiative 20 sure-hit vs every creature other than you: 6d10 force or lightning damage + Str save; on a failure, pulled up to 30 ft toward a point you choose and Restrained until the next initiative 20, and held weapons and unsecured objects are torn away and suspended in orbit; on a success, half damage, moves only 15 ft, not Restrained · Torn-away weapons and objects return when the domain ends or their bearer leaves it.

The sky goes black and hollow, a celestial sphere veined with luminous field lines, and everything inside hangs suspended between two enormous, invisible poles — dust and steel and soldiers all adrift in the same still current.

The Domain is the field made manifest.

20Full-Spectrum Perception & Blackout Pulse

Full-Spectrum Perception (passive): electromagnetic perception increases to 120 ft · Within 1 mile, determine the direction of major electrical systems, active technology, polarized targets, and significant electromagnetic disturbances · Within 120 ft, invisibility cannot conceal a creature's electromagnetic signature unless the effect specifically suppresses that signature · Blackout Pulse (action · 5 CE · once per long rest): point within 120 ft, 60-ft radius — constructs make a Con save or are Stunned until the end of your next turn and disabled for 1 min; unprotected technology ceases functioning for 1 min; living creatures make a Con save or lose their reactions and have disadvantage on Dex checks, Dex saves, and weapon attacks until the end of your next turn · You may exclude up to technique-modifier targets.

The sense extends to a mile; the Blackout Pulse silences the works of man — and staggers the living.

Pinnacle expression

Maximum, Domain, or equivalent.

The battlefield itself takes the mark. Maximum Technique, Iron Requiem (action · 10 CE · Focus, up to 1 min): every creature and object within 120 feet of you counts as polarized, polarized targets do not count against your maintained-mark limit, Attraction and Repulsion reach 120 feet and can each affect up to technique-modifier targets, you can use Attraction or Repulsion once per turn as a bonus action, all matter counts as Magnetically Responsive, and your flying speed doubles.

Feats

Build-defining options.

Prerequisite: level 11

Blood Iron

The mark reaches past metal into the current inside living things. Once per turn, when a living polarized creature fails a saving throw against your technique, you can spend 1 additional CE and choose one: it cannot take reactions until the end of its next turn; its speed is halved until the end of its next turn; it is Blinded until the beginning of its next turn; or it loses Focus if it fails an additional Constitution saving throw.

Prerequisite: Magnetic Flight

Debris Throne

Flight becomes fortification, orbiting material grants +2 AC against ranged attacks, and a reaction flings debris at anyone who misses. While you are flying, orbiting material grants you +2 AC against ranged attacks. When a ranged attack misses you, you can use your reaction to launch part of the debris at the attacker, making a ranged technique attack dealing 2d8 + technique modifier piercing or force damage on a hit.

Prerequisite: Attraction or Repulsion

Polarity Reversal

A saved pull is not a wasted one, reverse the field as part of the same effect and move the target ten feet the other way, no save. When a creature succeeds on its saving throw against your Attraction or Repulsion, you may reverse the field as part of the same effect: it moves 10 feet in the opposite direction (no saving throw). You may do this once per turn.

Prerequisite: Field Compression

Polar Cage

The constriction learns permanence, one more coin extends the restraint while you hold Focus. When Field Compression Restrains a target, you may spend 1 additional CE to extend the restraint for up to 1 minute while maintaining Focus. The creature repeats its Strength saving throw at the end of each of its turns, ending the effect on a success.

Prerequisite: Attraction or Repulsion

Magnetic Reflex

The field answers before thought does. Once on each creature's turn, you can activate Attraction or Repulsion as a free action, even when it isn't your turn. The CE cost still applies, and every other rule for the feature is unchanged.

Rules integration

Edge cases and shared systems.

Codex rulings: Direct rolled technique/weapon attacks can use the campaign's Black Flash procedure when otherwise eligible. Passive damage, hazards, summons acting independently, and initiative-20 Domain events cannot. Generic Maximum Output applies only to ordinary features, never named Maxima or Domains.
Counterplay: break the user’s Focus, leave the field’s radius, shield or conceal your electromagnetic signature; saving throws still gate every effect — polarity itself is never the attack.
