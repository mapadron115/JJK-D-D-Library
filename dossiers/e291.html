Master of magnetism: flight, force, and the iron inside the world.

Core law

What the technique actually does.

Polarity engine. As a bonus action for 1 CE, polarize one creature or metal object you can see within 60 feet: it counts as magnetized by you until the end of your next turn. You sense the direction and distance of all polarized metal within 120 feet.

Technique save DC: 8 + PB + chosen CE ability modifier. Technique attack: PB + chosen CE ability modifier.

The fiction. Jujutsu scholars classify Magnetic Manipulation as cursed energy that learned the world's hidden skeleton — iron, not as a substance but as a promise. The prevailing theory holds it awakened in a mind that could not accept being moved by others, so the energy learned to move everything else first: polarize a creature or a weapon, then attract, repel, or crush it, because the sorcerer's resolve expresses itself as a law of physics. Registry notes describe the central monopole of the Domain as the technique's thesis stated plainly: everything obeys, because the user will not.

Full progression

Level progression

1Polarize & Push/Pull

Polarize (bonus action, 1 CE): magnetize one creature or metal object you can see within 60 feet until the end of your next turn, as above.

Attract / Repel (action, 1 CE): choose one polarized target within 60 feet. Attract: pull it up to 20 feet in a straight line toward you. Repel: push it up to 20 feet directly away from you. An unwilling creature makes a Strength save to negate the movement.

If the target strikes a solid surface or another creature, both make a Dexterity save or fall prone. A creature pulled into a metal surface sticks fast — it is restrained until it uses an action to succeed on a Strength check against your Technique DC.

3Magnetic Flight & Scrap Barrage

Magnetic Flight: you gain a fly speed equal to your walking speed.

Scrap Barrage (action, 1 CE): make a ranged technique attack against a creature within 60 feet, hurling polarized scrap. On a hit it takes 2d8 + your CE modifier piercing damage.

6Turn the Blade & Crush

Turn the Blade (reaction, 1 CE): when a creature attacks you with a metal weapon or a metal projectile, force the attacker to make a Strength save against your Technique DC. On a failure the attack misses as the metal is torn aside. If the triggering attack was a metal projectile, you may immediately redirect it — make a ranged technique attack against a creature of your choice within 60 feet, dealing the projectile's normal damage on a hit.

Crush (bonus action, 2 CE): one polarized creature wearing metal armor or carrying a metal object makes a Strength save. On a failure it is restrained until the end of its next turn as its own gear constricts, and it takes 2d8 bludgeoning damage. On a success it takes half damage and is not restrained.

11Polarity Field & Railgun

Polarity Field (action, 3 CE, Focus, 1 minute): a 15-foot emanation centered on you. Attacks with metal weapons or metal projectiles against creatures inside the field have disadvantage. Polarized creatures inside the field have disadvantage on saving throws against your Attract, Repel, and Crush.

Railgun: once per turn when you use Attract on a polarized metal object (not a creature), you may accelerate it instead of pulling it to you — make a ranged technique attack against a creature within 120 feet. On a hit it takes 4d8 piercing damage.

18Domain Expansion: The Master's Field

Full-turn action, 20 CE, enclosed 60-foot radius centered on you, up to 1 minute, Technique Focus. Sure-hit event at initiative 20: each hostile wearing metal armor or carrying a metal weapon or object makes a Strength save. On a failure it is pulled 20 feet toward the domain's center and restrained — pinned against the central monopole — until the next initiative 20, and its metal weapons are ripped from its grasp to orbit the center (it is disarmed until it leaves the domain or the domain ends). On a success it is pulled 10 feet and is not restrained. Counterplay: drop your metal before the count, or break the user's Focus.

20Electromagnetic Apex

Your metal sense extends to 1 mile.

Blackout Pulse (action, 5 CE, once per long rest): a 60-foot burst centered on a point within 120 feet of you. Each construct in the burst makes a Constitution save; on a failure it is stunned for 1 round and then shut down for 1 minute. Nonmagical technological objects in the burst stop working for 1 minute (an attended object uses its bearer's save).

Pinnacle expression

Maximum, Domain, or equivalent.

Maximum — Iron Requiem (10 CE): as an action, for 1 minute every metal object and every creature wearing or carrying metal within 120 feet of you counts as polarized by you (no per-target cost), and your Attract and Repel range becomes 120 feet.

Feats

Build-defining options.

Prerequisite: level 11

Blood Iron

Once per long rest, you may Polarize the iron in a living creature's blood. One Attract, Repel, or Crush ignores the metal requirement entirely and works on any creature; the target has disadvantage on its saving throw against that effect.

Prerequisite: Magnetic Flight

Debris Throne

While you are flying, orbiting scrap grants you +2 AC against ranged attacks. Once per turn when a ranged attack misses you, you may use your reaction to fling a shard — make a ranged technique attack against the attacker, dealing 2d8 + your CE modifier piercing damage on a hit.

Prerequisite: Attract or Repel

Polarity Reversal

When a creature succeeds on its saving throw against your Attract or Repel, you may reverse the field as part of the same effect: it moves 10 feet in the opposite direction (no saving throw). You may do this once per turn.

Prerequisite: Crush

Polar Cage

When Crush restrains a target, you may spend 1 additional CE to shape its metal into a cage: the restraint lasts 1 minute instead of until the end of its next turn (the target repeats the Strength save at the end of each of its turns, ending the effect on a success).

Rules integration

Edge cases and shared systems.

Codex rulings: Direct rolled technique/weapon attacks can use the campaign's Black Flash procedure when otherwise eligible. Passive damage, hazards, summons acting independently, and initiative-20 Domain events cannot. Generic Maximum Output applies only to ordinary features, never named Maxima or Domains.
Counterplay: nonmetal weapons and armor, spread out against pulls, drop metal before the Domain's initiative-20 count, break Focus.
