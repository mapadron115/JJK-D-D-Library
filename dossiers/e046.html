Core law

What the technique actually does.

Rules are written to plug into the finalized campaign glossary: normal actions stay normal, direct attacks roll, and only named Domain events bypass the initial roll.

GRAVITY SOVEREIGNTY

Bonus action · 1 CE

Gravity Mark

Place a visible violet mark on one creature, unattended object, projectile, or 5-foot terrain section within Gravity Range. A hostile creature may make a Strength save to resist. A mark lasts 1 minute and records one vector: Pull, Push, Orbit, or Anchor. Re-marking can change its vector.

Clash bookkeeping: 1,000 clash points (Tier V). A 100+ point advantage collapses the weaker Domain; collapse deals no damage. Barrier AC 12 + PB; barrier HP = 10 × max(1, your CE modifier) + 2,000. Technique Burnout: 3 rounds.

Bonus action

Vector Command

Command up to PB perceived marks. Pull or Push moves a failed-save hostile 10 feet + 5 feet per PB. Orbit lifts or circles an object/creature around a chosen point; unwilling creatures save. Anchor reduces speed by 10 feet per mark and grants advantage against forced movement.

Movement rider

Gravity Collision

When your command drives a creature into a solid surface or another creature, it stops in the last legal space and falls prone — no damage; if driven into another creature, that creature makes a Dexterity save vs your Technique DC: failure — knocked prone too; success — pushed 5 ft aside, stays upright. One command repositions a creature once.

Passive

Redmane Weight

You may treat yourself as one size larger for grapples, shoves, carrying, and resisting forced movement while at least one self-mark is active. This never changes occupied space or reach.

The fiction. The prevailing theory holds that Gravity Sovereignty awakened the moment its user refused to watch something fall — CE too stubborn to let mass obey the world, so it made mass obey the user instead. The violet marks are not commands so much as arguments the technique keeps winning: pull, push, orbit, anchor, each one a ruling that the stars may only move where the sorcerer permits. Jujutsu scholars classify the final falling-star form as the technique completing its logic — a body that decided it too was just another object under its own law.

Full progression

Level 1 through 20.

Milestones use the campaign’s familiar 1 / 3 / 6 / 11 / 18 structure and add an explicit level-20 mastery feature.

L1Starscourge Fundamentals

Gravitas and 1 more feature. Range 30 feet, 2 Gravity Marks.

Gravitas

Gain Gravity Mark, Vector Command, Gravity Collision, and Redmane Weight. You can self-mark without a save or action as part of activating the technique once per turn.

Greatsword Conductor

Any weapon you wield can act as a gravity focus. Once per turn on a weapon hit, spend 1 CE to place a Gravity Mark on the target after damage.

L3Starcaller Arts

Starcaller Cry and 2 more features. Range 60 feet, 3 Gravity Marks.

Starcaller Cry

Action · 3 CE: creatures of your choice in a 20-foot-radius sphere within range make Strength saves. Failure pulls them to the center up to 30 feet and knocks them prone; success moves them 10 feet without prone.

Collapsing Stars

Action · 2 CE: create PB violet gravity orbs that each make a ranged CE attack within 90 feet. Each hit deals 2d8 force and pulls 10 feet. Several orbs may strike one target.

Cragblade

Bonus action · 2 CE · 1 minute: coat one weapon in gravity stone. Once per turn after hitting a marked creature, force a Strength save; failure pushes or pulls it 10 feet without spending Vector Command.

L6General of the Battlefield

Conqueror’s Orbit and 2 more features. Range 90 feet, PB Gravity Marks.

Conqueror’s Orbit

Marked projectiles and unattended objects up to Huge can orbit you. Reaction, 2 CE: when a physical projectile or line attack crosses within 20 feet, redirect one eligible marked projectile/object into its path, imposing disadvantage or granting +PB to the relevant save.

Meteor Sling

Action · 4 CE: choose up to PB marked unattended objects within range. Hurl each at a creature or space within 120 feet. Make one CE attack per object; hit deals 3d8 bludgeoning/force, and a Large+ object also knocks prone on failed Strength save.

Weightless Step

Once per turn, command one self-mark without an action to leap, hover, or move up to 20 feet in any direction. This is gravity-assisted movement, not teleportation.

L11Lord of the Wailing Dunes

Absolute Vector and 2 more features. Range 150 feet, PB+2 Gravity Marks.

Absolute Vector

Your Vector Command can move failed-save creatures 30 + 5×PB feet. Huge creatures no longer gain any special benefit from size; Gargantuan creatures have advantage instead.

Orbital Arsenal

Up to PB marked objects can remain in orbit without Technique Focus. Once per turn when you attack, launch one orbiting object as part of the same action for 2d8 damage on hit.

Maximum: Festival of Falling Stars

Action · 10 CE · Focus · 1 minute: create a 60-foot-radius orbital field. At the start of each of your turns, form three meteor masses. As a bonus action launch any number; each creates a 10-foot burst for 4d8, Dex half. Unlaunched meteors remain in orbit, maximum 9.

L18Domain of the Stars

Domain Expansion and 2 more features. Range 300 feet, PB+CE modifier Gravity Marks.

Domain Expansion

Unlock Wailing Firmament — Starcaller Cataclysm, described below.

Consort’s Meteor Rain

Action · 8 CE: vanish upward until the start of your next turn, becoming untargetable except by effects that can reach your declared altitude/space. On return choose a point within 300 feet; creatures in a 30-foot radius make Dex saves for 8d8 + CE modifier force, half on success, and are thrown 30 feet on failure.

Perfect Gravity

Your own Gravity Marks no longer count against your ordinary mark limit. You can stand, run, and stop on open air while the technique is active.

L20Prime Conqueror

Mass Bows to the General and 2 more features. Range sight, no ordinary mark cap during Prime Conqueror.

Mass Bows to the General

Once per turn issue Vector Command without an action in addition to any bonus-action command. You may command the same mark in both commands.

Heaven-Shattering Reentry

When Consort’s Meteor Rain returns, you may spend an additional 10 CE. Radius doubles to 60 feet and damage becomes 12d8; you take 4d8 unavoidable force from the impact. This is a named pinnacle and cannot receive Maximum Output.

Army of One

While below half HP, hostile creatures have disadvantage on saves against your Vector Command.

Pinnacle expression

Domain, Maximum, or equivalent.

Not every technique needs a Domain. When one exists, its sure-hit is a unique autonomous initiative-count-20 event unless the Domain is explicitly rule-based.

Wailing Firmament — Starcaller Cataclysm

Level 18 · Full-turn action · 20 CE · 90-foot radius · 1 minute · Focus. The barrier becomes a black-red desert beneath a sky crowded with arrested stars. Your ordinary attacks and Gravity Marks still roll/save normally.

Initiative 20 — Heaven Falls: choose any number of hostile creatures. Each unprotected target automatically takes 6d8 force, is pulled up to 40 feet toward a point you choose, then becomes suspended 20 feet above the nearest surface until initiative 20 of the next round. A protected creature makes a Strength save; success halves damage and prevents movement/suspension.

Suspended creatures are not paralyzed: they may act, but ordinary movement cannot change altitude unless they can fly, teleport, or defeat the gravity with an appropriate feature.

Innate Technique Feats

Four build-defining options.

These broaden the law instead of merely inflating the same number.

Prerequisite: Gravity Sovereignty

Redmane Greatbow

You can mark ammunition as part of firing it. Long-range weapon attacks against a marked creature ignore disadvantage from long range, and once per turn a hit may Pull the target 10 feet.

Prerequisite: level 6

Lion’s Gravity

When you succeed on a save against forced movement or prone, use your reaction to place an Anchor mark on yourself and Pull the source 15 feet toward you if it is within Gravity Range.

Prerequisite: Starcaller Cry

Starcaller’s Mark

What the cry cannot crush, it claims. Each creature that succeeds on its save against Starcaller Cry gains a Gravity Mark with the Anchor vector for 1 minute, with no additional save; a creature can carry only one such mark at a time.

Prerequisite: Maximum: Festival of Falling Stars

Grand Conjunction

You merge held meteors into a greater one. When you launch meteors as a bonus action, you may merge two or more into a single burst: it creates a 15-foot-radius burst dealing 4d8 per merged meteor, with a Dexterity save for half. Declare the merger before launching.

Rules integration

Edge cases and shared systems.

These rulings are designed around the revised Player Guide rather than the raw PDF where the two differ.

Gravity movement is forced movement and does not provoke opportunity attacks.
A creature can be affected by several marks, but one Vector Command asks it for only one save and repositions a creature once.
Teleportation ignores the path but does not automatically remove a mark unless it leaves Gravity Range or the effect says attachments are removed.
Gravity does not increase a weapon’s base die unless a named feature says so.
Technique DC: 8 + proficiency bonus + your chosen Cursed Energy ability modifier (Wisdom or Charisma unless your class says otherwise).
Direct criticals: rolled weapon, unarmed, and CE attacks can critical normally. After a qualifying critical, roll the campaign's unmodified Black Flash confirmation d20; a natural 20 triples the completed critical damage. Autonomous Domain events, damage-over-time, hazards, and unrolled pulses cannot Black Flash.
Maximum Output: unless a feature says otherwise, double that feature's base CE cost and enhance one supported axis—damage, range, duration, or accuracy/DC—using the campaign's Maximum Output rules. Named Maximum Techniques and Domains do not receive generic Maximum Output.
Technique Focus: effects marked Focus occupy the shared Technique Focus slot. Opening a Domain ends other Focus effects unless the Domain expressly preserves them.
Two-technique characters: these guides assume each technique progresses from character/class level as agreed by the campaign. Multiclassing itself does not grant another Innate Technique.
