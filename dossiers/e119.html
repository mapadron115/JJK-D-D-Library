Invisible Force

Invisibility / force fields / constructs / internal pressure

Power tierTier V · Calamity
Core law

What the technique actually does.

Core Engine — Field Integrity. Your force constructs share an Integrity pool equal to 5 × level + CE modifier × 5. Damage to barriers subtracts Integrity. Pool fully restores on short/long rest; when 0, all constructs collapse and you suffer Technique Burnout until end next turn.

The fiction. Registry scholars describe Invisible Force as boundary-keeping made into flesh: the user's CE answered a need to be unseen by learning to bend light away, and answered every later need — shelter, separation, self-defense — with the same solution: a wall that isn't there. The prevailing theory holds that the shared Integrity pool is one reserve spent two ways — hiding and holding — which is the technique's honest duality: nothing can be protected without drawing on the same strength that kept it hidden. Jujutsu scholars classify this as reinforcement that chose refusal over repair — the sorcerer's energy would rather not be touched at all.

Full progression

Level 1 through 20.

1Invisibility

For 2 CE (requires Focus), you become invisible for 10 minutes. For 3 CE, one additional willing creature also becomes invisible; if a target attacks, it is revealed until the start of its next turn, but the effect does not end.

3Force Screen

As a reaction, spend 1 CE to create a panel (AC 16) with up to PB×5 Integrity allocated from your pool, intercepting one attack or line effect. Unspent Integrity remains in your pool.

6Force Shapes

For 2 CE, create a platform, bridge, bubble, ram, or 10-ft wall within 60 ft for 1 minute (platform/bridge: 10-ft square; bubble: 10-ft sphere; each costs Integrity equal to 5 × its longest dimension in feet). The ram makes a melee CE attack (reach 15 ft), dealing 3d10 force damage and pushing the target 15 ft on a hit.

9Selective Dome

For 4 CE, create a 15-ft-radius dome with up to 40 Integrity allocated. Creatures you choose when creating it may pass through; others cannot, except by breaking through or teleporting.

11Flight

A force platform grants a 50-ft fly speed to you or one willing creature; 2 CE for 10 minutes.

13Internal Bubble

For 5 CE, target one creature within 60 ft; it makes a Constitution save. On a failure it takes 6d10 force damage and cannot speak until the end of its next turn, as a tiny field compresses its airway and body space. This is not automatic organ destruction.

15City Wall

As an action, spend 6 CE to create up to six connected 10-ft panels, allocating up to 80 Integrity among them. You may reshape them as a bonus action.

18Absolute Sphere

For 8 CE, enclose one Huge-or-smaller target; it makes a Dexterity save to avoid being trapped. You may allocate up to 100 Integrity to the sphere. While trapped, the target has total cover and cannot leave by ordinary movement.

20Unseen Architect

Your Integrity pool doubles. You may maintain a number of constructs equal to your proficiency bonus without Focus. The first Force Screen you create each round costs 0 CE. Your Invisibility can affect a number of creatures equal to your proficiency bonus for the base 3 CE.

Pinnacle expression

Maximum, Domain, or equivalent.

Maximum — Event Horizon Shell (10 CE; action): 30-ft sphere with 150 temporary Integrity for 1 minute. It can protect what is inside or imprison what is caught; creatures in the area when it forms make a Dexterity save, and on a success choose to be inside or outside the shell; at initiative 20 you may contract it 5 ft, dealing 5d10 force to hostile creatures touching its boundary.

Feats

Build-defining options.

Invisible Objects

When you activate Invisibility, objects you wear and carry share it — they flicker visible for a round when you attack, exactly as you do. Additionally, you may spend 1 extra CE to veil one unattended object or 10-ft surface you touch; it remains invisible for the full 10 minutes even if your own invisibility ends early, letting you hide a door, a trap, or an ally's hiding spot.

Hard-Light Step

When you are shoved, knocked prone, or begin to fall, you may use your reaction and spend 1 CE to flash-form a hard-light foothold beneath you: move up to 10 ft to an unoccupied space, including mid-air, without provoking opportunity attacks. If you were falling, you land safely on the platform instead, and it supports your weight until the start of your next turn.

Fine Control

Your force constructs gain fine articulation: any platform, bubble, wall, or dome you create can manipulate objects like a spectral hand. As a bonus action you may have a construct make a Dexterity (Sleight of Hand) check using your CE attack bonus, lift up to 10 lb, or operate a simple mechanism within 60 ft — picking a lock, turning a key, or pulling a lever from across the room.

Mother's Shield

when you intercept an attack targeting an ally with Force Screen, you may also slide the ally up to 10 ft to unoccupied ground as part of the same reaction.

Rules integration

Edge cases and shared systems.

Codex rulings: Direct rolled technique/weapon attacks can use the campaign's Black Flash procedure when otherwise eligible. Passive damage, hazards, summons acting independently, and initiative-20 Domain events cannot. Generic Maximum Output applies only to ordinary features, never named Maxima or Domains. Integrity is technique-construct HP, not Domain barrier HP: it is resolved against the approved barrier rules only when a construct functions as a Domain barrier.
