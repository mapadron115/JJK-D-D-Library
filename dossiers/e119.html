Invisible Force

Invisibility / force fields / constructs / internal pressure

Power tierTier V · Calamity
Core law

What the technique actually does.

Passive · Your force constructs share an Integrity pool = 5 × level + 5 × CE modifier · Damage to barriers subtracts Integrity · Pool fully restores on short/long rest · At 0, all constructs collapse and you suffer Technique Burnout until the end of your next turn.

Core Engine, Field Integrity. One ledger for every wall she will ever build. Damage to any barrier draws from this pool; when it empties, everything collapses and she suffers Technique Burnout until her next turn ends. Budget accordingly. Your force constructs share an Integrity pool equal to 5 × level + CE modifier × 5. Damage to barriers subtracts Integrity. Pool fully restores on short/long rest; when 0, all constructs collapse and you suffer Technique Burnout until end next turn.

Field notes. Subject: the architect of nothing. Three truths govern her. First: her answer to every need, shelter, separation, self-defense, is the same solution: a wall that isn't there, bent light and held force; invisibility is only the most honest form of it. Second: everything she builds shares one ledger, Field Integrity, five per level plus five per CE modifier point, and when the ledger is empty, the architecture collapses and the builder burns out. Third: the forms escalate in ambition, panel, shapes, dome, city wall, until at the summit she is no longer building walls but administering a worksite she cannot be seen in. Assess her as the rarest kind of sorcerer: one whose strongest weapon is the space she refuses to occupy.

Full progression

Level 1 through 20.

1Invisibility

Action · 2 CE (requires Focus): become invisible for 10 minutes · 3 CE: one additional willing creature also becomes invisible · If a target attacks, it is revealed only until the start of its next turn (the effect does not end).

The first and truest wall: herself, unseen. Two CE for ten minutes, three to bring a companion, and even striking only reveals her until her next turn. The absence persists. For 2 CE (requires Focus), you become invisible for 10 minutes. For 3 CE, one additional willing creature also becomes invisible; if a target attacks, it is revealed until the start of its next turn, but the effect does not end.

3Force Screen

Reaction · 1 CE · Create a panel (AC 16) with up to PB×5 Integrity from your pool, intercepting one attack or line effect · Unspent Integrity remains in your pool.

The absence learns to take a hit. One CE as a reaction: a panel with her Integrity behind it, stepped between the attack and its target. Whatever is not spent remains in the ledger. As a reaction, spend 1 CE to create a panel (AC 16) with up to PB×5 Integrity allocated from your pool, intercepting one attack or line effect. Unspent Integrity remains in your pool.

6Force Shapes

Action · 2 CE · Create within 60 ft for 1 minute: platform, bridge, bubble, ram, or 10-ft wall (platform/bridge: 10-ft square; bubble: 10-ft sphere; each costs Integrity = 5 × its longest dimension in feet) · The ram makes a melee CE attack (reach 15 ft): 3d10 force and push 15 ft on a hit.

She stops building walls and starts building things, platforms, bridges, bubbles, a ram with fifteen feet of argument. Two CE, sixty feet, one minute: the catalog of the invisible. For 2 CE, create a platform, bridge, bubble, ram, or 10-ft wall within 60 ft for 1 minute (platform/bridge: 10-ft square; bubble: 10-ft sphere; each costs Integrity equal to 5 × its longest dimension in feet). The ram makes a melee CE attack (reach 15 ft), dealing 3d10 force damage and pushing the target 15 ft on a hit.

9Selective Dome

Action · 4 CE · 15-ft-radius dome with up to 40 Integrity · Creatures you choose may pass; others cannot except by breaking through or teleporting.

The siege work begins: forty Integrity in a fifteen-foot dome, and she decides who may pass. Exclusion is the whole technique. For 4 CE, create a 15-ft-radius dome with up to 40 Integrity allocated. Creatures you choose when creating it may pass through; others cannot, except by breaking through or teleporting.

11Flight

Action · 2 CE · Grant a 50-ft fly speed to you or one willing creature for 10 minutes.

A platform becomes a road in the sky, fifty feet for two CE, for herself or a guest. The architect commutes by her own absence. A force platform grants a 50-ft fly speed to you or one willing creature; 2 CE for 10 minutes.

13Internal Bubble

Action · 5 CE · One creature within 60 ft makes a Con save: on failure takes 6d10 force damage and cannot speak until the end of its next turn.

The gentlest and cruelest application: a field inside the enemy's own chest, crushing breath into silence. Six dice, a failed save, and no speech until his next turn ends. For 5 CE, target one creature within 60 ft; it makes a Constitution save. On a failure it takes 6d10 force damage and cannot speak until the end of its next turn, as a tiny field compresses its airway and body space. This is not automatic organ destruction.

15City Wall

Action · 6 CE · Create up to six connected 10-ft panels, allocating up to 80 Integrity among them · You may reshape them as a bonus action.

Six panels, eighty Integrity, reshaped as a bonus action, the battlefield re-zoned at will. She does not conquer terrain. She redraws it. As an action, spend 6 CE to create up to six connected 10-ft panels, allocating up to 80 Integrity among them. You may reshape them as a bonus action.

18Absolute Sphere

Action · 8 CE · Enclose one Huge-or-smaller target (Dex save to avoid); allocate up to 100 Integrity to the sphere · While trapped the target has total cover and cannot leave by ordinary movement.

The perfect refusal: one target, enclosed, one hundred Integrity between it and the world. Dexterity to dodge the trap; nothing to dodge the walls. For 8 CE, enclose one Huge-or-smaller target; it makes a Dexterity save to avoid being trapped. You may allocate up to 100 Integrity to the sphere. While trapped, the target has total cover and cannot leave by ordinary movement.

20Unseen Architect

Passive · Integrity pool doubles · Maintain PB constructs without Focus · The first Force Screen you create each round costs 0 CE · Invisibility affects PB creatures for the base 3 CE.

The ledger doubles, the worksite runs without her Focus, the first screen of each round costs nothing, and her invisibility now takes the whole crew. The architect has become a municipality. Your Integrity pool doubles. You may maintain a number of constructs equal to your proficiency bonus without Focus. The first Force Screen you create each round costs 0 CE. Your Invisibility can affect a number of creatures equal to your proficiency bonus for the base 3 CE.

Pinnacle expression

Maximum, Domain, or equivalent.

Maximum, Event Horizon Shell (10 CE; action): 30-ft sphere with 150 temporary Integrity for 1 minute. It can protect what is inside or imprison what is caught; creatures in the area when it forms make a Dexterity save, and on a success choose to be inside or outside the shell; at initiative 20 you may contract it 5 ft, dealing 5d10 force to hostile creatures touching its boundary.

Feats

Build-defining options.

Invisible Objects

She veils what she carries, and for one more CE, what she touches. The equipment follows the same rule as the woman: seen only when it strikes. When you activate Invisibility, objects you wear and carry share it, they flicker visible for a round when you attack, exactly as you do. Additionally, you may spend 1 extra CE to veil one unattended object or 10-ft surface you touch; it remains invisible for the full 10 minutes even if your own invisibility ends early, letting you hide a door, a trap, or an ally's hiding spot.

Hard-Light Step

Gravity is merely a suggestion. When shoved, tripped, or falling, a reaction and one CE buy a foothold out of nothing, ten feet, mid-air if needed, without asking permission. When you are shoved, knocked prone, or begin to fall, you may use your reaction and spend 1 CE to flash-form a hard-light foothold beneath you: move up to 10 ft to an unoccupied space, including mid-air, without provoking opportunity attacks. If you were falling, you land safely on the platform instead, and it supports your weight until the start of your next turn.

Fine Control

Her constructs grow fingers. Platforms and domes manipulate like spectral hands, a bonus action's worth of Sleight at her attack bonus. Fortifications that pick pockets. Your force constructs gain fine articulation: any platform, bubble, wall, or dome you create can manipulate objects like a spectral hand. As a bonus action you may have a construct make a Dexterity (Sleight of Hand) check using your CE attack bonus, lift up to 10 lb, or operate a simple mechanism within 60 ft, picking a lock, turning a key, or pulling a lever from across the room.

Mother's Shield

The screen does two jobs now: it takes the hit and moves the ally ten feet to safety in the same reaction. Protection, filed as logistics. when you intercept an attack targeting an ally with Force Screen, you may also slide the ally up to 10 ft to unoccupied ground as part of the same reaction.

Rules integration

Edge cases and shared systems.

Codex rulings: Direct rolled technique/weapon attacks can use the campaign's Black Flash procedure when otherwise eligible. Passive damage, hazards, summons acting independently, and initiative-20 Domain events cannot. Generic Maximum Output applies only to ordinary features, never named Maxima or Domains. Integrity is technique-construct HP, not Domain barrier HP: it is resolved against the approved barrier rules only when a construct functions as a Domain barrier.
