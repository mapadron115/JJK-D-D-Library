Invisible Force

Tier V · CalamityInvisibility / force fields / constructs / internal pressure

Core Engine — Field Integrity. Your force constructs share an Integrity pool equal to 5 × level + CE modifier × 5. Damage to barriers subtracts Integrity. Pool fully restores on short/long rest; when 0, all constructs collapse and you suffer Technique Burnout until end next turn.
