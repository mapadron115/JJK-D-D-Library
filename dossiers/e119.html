Invisible Force

Invisibility / force fields / constructs / internal pressure

Power tierTier V · Calamity
Core law

What the technique actually does.

Core Engine — Field Integrity. Your force constructs share an Integrity pool equal to 5 × level + CE modifier × 5. Damage to barriers subtracts Integrity. Pool fully restores on short/long rest; when 0, all constructs collapse and you suffer Technique Burnout until end next turn.

Full progression

Level 1 through 20.

1Invisibility

2 CE, Focus, 10 minutes: invisible self. 3 CE affects one additional willing creature; attacks reveal target until start next turn but do not end effect.

3Force Screen

Reaction 1 CE: create panel with AC 16 and up to PB×5 Integrity allocated; intercept one attack/line. Unspent Integrity remains in pool.

6Force Shapes

2 CE: create platform, bridge, bubble, ram, or 10-ft wall within 60 ft for 1 minute. Ram attack 3d10 force and push 15 ft.

9Selective Dome

4 CE: 15-ft-radius dome, allocate up to 40 Integrity. Creatures chosen when created may pass; others cannot unless breaking/teleporting.

11Flight

Force platform grants 50-ft flight to self or one willing creature; 2 CE for 10 minutes.

13Internal Bubble

5 CE, one creature within 60 ft Con save. Failure: 6d10 force and cannot speak until next turn as tiny field compresses airway/body space. This is not automatic organ destruction.

15City Wall

Action 6 CE: create up to six connected 10-ft panels, allocating up to 80 Integrity. May reshape them as bonus action.

18Absolute Sphere

8 CE: enclose one Huge-or-smaller target, Dex save to avoid. Allocate up to 100 Integrity. While trapped, target has total cover and cannot leave by ordinary movement.

20Unseen Architect

Integrity doubles. Maintain PB constructs without Focus. First Force Screen each round costs 0 CE. Invisibility can affect PB creatures for base 3 CE.

Pinnacle expression

Maximum, Domain, or equivalent.

Maximum — Event Horizon Shell (10 CE): 30-ft sphere with 150 temporary Integrity for 1 minute. It can protect what is inside or imprison what is caught; at initiative 20 you may contract it 5 ft, dealing 5d10 force to hostile creatures touching its boundary.

Feats

Build-defining options.

Invisible Objects:

extend invisibility to carried/10-ft objects.

Hard-Light Step:

reaction create foothold and move 10 ft.

Fine Control:

force constructs can manipulate objects like spectral hands.

Mother's Shield:

Force Screen protecting an ally gains +10 Integrity.

Rules integration

Edge cases and shared systems.

Codex rulings: Direct rolled technique/weapon attacks can use the campaign's Black Flash procedure when otherwise eligible. Passive damage, hazards, summons acting independently, and initiative-20 Domain events cannot. Generic Maximum Output applies only to ordinary features, never named Maxima or Domains. Integrity is technique-construct HP, not Domain barrier HP: it is resolved against the approved barrier rules only when a construct functions as a Domain barrier.
