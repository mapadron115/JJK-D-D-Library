Omnitrix

Omnitrix: Codon Archive

A transformation technique with 19 original-series alien bodies, shared HP/CE/action economy, solo genome subjugation, Master Control, and Way Big as a pinnacle trial.

Cursed TechniqueTier V · Modular
Ben 10 original-series inspired homebrew

Continuity ruling

The roster is organized by what young Ben actually unlocked, rather than treating every form shown by a future counterpart as one of his summer transformations.

Way Big did not debut in Omniverse. Azmuth unlocks the To’kustar transformation in Ben 10: Secret of the Omnitrix, the canon movie intended as the original series’ chronological finale. It is therefore included here, but as the level-18 Maximum rather than a normal playlist body.

Core 10 · inherited

Original playlist

Heatblast, Wildmutt, Diamondhead, XLR8, Grey Matter, Four Arms, Stinkfly, Ripjaws, Upgrade, and Ghostfreak begin permanently subjugated. They require no ritual.

Eight candidates

Young Ben’s additions

Cannonbolt, Wildvine, Blitzwolfer/Benwolf, Snare-Oh/Benmummy, Frankenstrike/Benvicktor, Upchuck, Ditto, and Eye Guy become ritual candidates at their listed levels. Eligibility is not ownership.

Apex candidate

Way Big

Way Big becomes eligible at level 18 and has a unique heroic subjugation trial. Passing it unlocks the Maximum; reaching level 18 alone does not.

Optional candidates

Original Future forms

Spitter, Buzzshock, and Arctiguana first appear through future users in the 2005 series. Their optional profiles and ritual laws appear in the appendix; they are not counted among the base nineteen.

Core technique rules

Specific wording here overrides generic transformation defaults in the reviewed Player Codex.

Activation

Heroic Transformation

As a bonus action, spend 1 CE and choose a loaded, subjugated form. A mere Candidate can never be selected. You transform for up to 10 minutes. The transformation itself does not require Technique Focus. You may revert during your turn without an action, but cannot transform again until the start of your next turn unless a feature allows a direct change. You also revert at 0 HP, when unconscious or dead, during Time-Out, or when the technique is suppressed. If the full 10 minutes expire naturally, Instability resets to 0 and the dial enters Time-Out for 1 minute.

Shared body

No free healing or second sheet

Current and maximum HP, Hit Dice, CE, limited uses, exhaustion, conditions, injuries, and Technique Focus all persist through every change. Transforming, switching, or reverting restores none of them. Excess damage never disappears into another form.

Statistics

Genome Aptitude

A form lists a Primary and Secondary ability. If lower, Primary becomes 16 + PB (maximum 22) and Secondary becomes 14 + PB (maximum 20). These temporary scores never change your CE modifier, CE maximum, technique attack, or save DC.

Defense

Alien AC

Use the form’s printed AC or one compatible unarmored AC calculation, not worn armor or a shield. Worn gear merges. One held cursed tool may remain if the anatomy can wield it; nothing is copied.

Actions

Your class still matters

You retain PB, proficiencies, saving-throw proficiencies, feats, and anatomy-compatible class features. Natural attacks are cursed weapon attacks and work with Extra Attack. A form action that says “action” does not become multiple uses through Extra Attack.

Technique math

Attacks, checks, saves, and Black Flash

CE attack = PB + CE modifier; CE ability check = d20 + PB + CE modifier; CE save DC = 8 + PB + CE modifier. A natural attack uses the ability named by its form. Rolled natural and CE attacks may critically hit and use the campaign Black Flash procedure. Saving throws, passive damage, hazards, and the Way Big tremor cannot Black Flash.

Focus

One concentration economy

The body transformation does not use Technique Focus, but any alien ability expressly marked Focus does. Beginning another Focus effect ends the first. When damage is taken, make a Constitution save with DC 10 or half the damage, whichever is higher; failure ends Focus. Incapacitation, death, and Time-Out also end it. At most one continuing hard-control, invisibility, possession, or overclock effect can be maintained at once.

Playlist, Instability, and Time-Out

The dial is dependable when used normally. Its famous failures appear when the wielder forces rapid or emergency changes.

Active Playlist

Candidate, Subjugated, Loaded

A Candidate is known but unusable. A Subjugated genome is permanently owned. A Loaded genome is a subjugated form currently placed in the active dial and can be selected in initiative. Reconfigure the playlist after a long rest. At level 6, a one-minute Field Calibration may exchange one loaded form for one subjugated archived form once per short rest. A newly subjugated form may replace a loaded form immediately.

Instability 0–3

Pushing the prototype

Direct Quick Change, Emergency Selection, and features that say so add Instability. It begins at 0. Spending one complete round in base form clears 1; one minute in base form or a rest clears all. Instability is not CE and cannot be restored or transferred.

Mistransform

Selection at Instability 3

When selecting a form at Instability 3, either spend 1 additional CE to lock the choice or roll among your loaded slot numbers. The rolled form replaces the declared form. Normal transformations at Instability 0–2 never randomly fail.

Time-Out

The fourth point cannot be held

If you would gain a fourth Instability, finish resolving the triggering switch or feature, revert at the end of the current turn, reset Instability to 0, and cannot transform until the end of your next turn. Outside initiative, the lock lasts 1 minute.

Random slot die

Works at every capacity

Number loaded ordinary forms from 1 to capacity. Roll the smallest standard die that can show every slot and reroll results above capacity: d10 for ten, d12 for twelve, and d20 for fifteen or a complete ordinary archive. Way Big is never assigned a slot.

Level 3

Capture Mode

After one minute touching or closely scanning a willing, restrained, or incapacitated sapient creature with stable biology, make a CE ability check against its CE save DC or 10 + its PB. Success records an eligible Candidate; it still requires subjugation. The named milestone Candidates appear in the archive automatically, so a missing campaign creature never deletes progression. Any additional species requires a complete GM-approved profile and trial law before its ritual can begin.

Level 6

Quick Change and Genetic Sovereignty

While transformed, use a bonus action and spend 1 CE to change directly into another loaded form, gaining 1 Instability. Ghostfreak’s Identity Hazard ends permanently: the user’s soul and personality are the uncontested owner of every stored genome.

Level 9

Emergency Selection

Once per short rest, when an attack targets you or you must make a Strength, Dexterity, or Constitution save, use your reaction and spend 2 CE to transform before the roll resolves. Apply the new form’s AC, size, aptitude, resistance, and passive traits. Gain 2 Instability. You cannot also use a form reaction against the same trigger.

Level 13

Genetic Safeguard

Once per long rest, when damage would reduce you to 0 HP without killing you outright, transform into any loaded non-Way Big form, remain at 1 HP, and gain PB Omni Dice temporary HP. This requires no action or CE, adds 3 Instability, and cannot prevent soul destruction, conceptual erasure, or instant death.

Level 15

Precision Dial

Your first Quick Change on each of your turns adds no Instability. You no longer roll for a Mistransformation from an ordinary Quick Change; Emergency Selection and other explicitly unstable features can still force Time-Out.

Level 20

Master Control

Every ordinary genome you have subjugated is loaded; locked Candidates remain locked, and Way Big remains the separate apex. Transforming from base form costs 0 CE. Once during each of your turns, change between subjugated ordinary forms without an action, CE, or Instability. Ordinary transformations have no time limit. Emergency Selection may be used once per round rather than once per short rest, but still costs 2 CE and grants 1 Instability. Master Control never substitutes for a ritual victory. Way Big retains every cost, duration, Focus, weakness, and recovery rule printed in its profile.

Codon Subjugation Ritual

The first ten genomes are inherited already mastered. Beginning at level 3, every later Candidate must recognize the wielder through a solitary combat rite.

Ownership requires victory. Recording a genome, reaching its listed level, or gaining Master Control only makes it a Candidate. The wielder must reduce its Unbound Genome Echo to 0 HP—or complete that species’ Concession Condition—without outside aid. Only then is the transformation permanently Subjugated.

Begin the rite

Ten-minute invocation · 3 CE

While in base form with 0 Instability, spend 10 uninterrupted minutes invoking one eligible Candidate, then pay 3 CE. Way Big instead costs 6 CE to challenge. Choose a temporary ritual loadout up to Active Playlist capacity from your already Subjugated forms; your prior playlist returns when the Curtain falls, except that a victory may immediately replace one slot with the new form. You cannot begin another subjugation ritual until completing a long rest.

Solitary law

No outside aid

Any direct attack, damage, healing, temporary HP, buff, debuff, restraint, resource transfer, reaction, or other assistance from another creature immediately invalidates the rite and collapses the Curtain. Effects you personally create and control, your class features, feats, and your own cursed tools are allowed. External ongoing effects end when the Curtain forms.

The arena

Codon Crucible

A closed 100-foot-diameter Curtain forms around you. It is a ritual barrier, not a Domain: it has no sure-hit, clash, or Burnout. The Echo acts immediately after your turn. At initiative 20, after you if tied, resolve the Candidate’s Trial Law. The normal rite lasts 10 rounds.

Victory

Exorcism or concession

Reduce the Echo to 0 HP or satisfy its listed Concession Condition before the final round ends. The Echo kneels and dissolves into the dial; that genome is permanently Subjugated and may immediately replace one loaded form. HP, CE, resources, conditions, and Instability are not restored.

Failure

The cost remains paid

If the time expires, you abort as an action, outside aid intervenes, or you reach 0 HP, the Curtain and Echo disappear without unlocking the form. CE spent and all damage remain. Reaching 0 HP follows normal rules and does not automatically stabilize you. The same Candidate may be challenged again only after a long rest.

Suppression

No ritual shortcuts

The Candidate cannot be loaded, selected randomly, copied, worn, accessed through Emergency Selection, or used by Genetic Safeguard. Capture Mode cannot tame it. Master Control cannot tame it. Before paying CE, you may instead declare a Practice Rite; it costs the normal CE and allies may enter without collapsing it, but it can never grant subjugation.

Ritual adjudication order

  1. Resolve initiative 20’s Trial Law.
  2. Take the wielder’s turn, including transformations and personally controlled features.
  3. Take the Unbound Echo’s turn immediately afterward.
  4. Check 0 HP, the Concession Condition, outside interference, and the round limit—in that order.

Extensions and endpoints

The Omnitrix spends its high-level budget on transformation breadth and Master Control. It does not also receive an obligatory Domain.

Extension

Quick Change · level 6

Quick Change is the technique’s central extension: a direct rewrite from one loaded, Subjugated body to another. It uses the bonus action, CE cost, and Instability rules printed in the progression section. It never refreshes a form’s uses, HP, CE, temporary HP, reactions, or movement and cannot enter a Candidate form.

Maximum Output

Ordinary form features only

Once you satisfy the campaign’s Maximum Output prerequisite, it may modify an ordinary alien feature under the shared Maximum Output rules: double the feature’s CE cost, then choose the permitted range, damage, accuracy, or duration increase. It cannot modify Heroic Transformation, Capture Mode, Codon Subjugation, Quick Change, Genetic Safeguard, a passive trait, or anything inside Maximum Technique: Way Big.

No reversal

Technique Reversal

Codon Archive has no standard reversal. The formula already rewrites the user toward many biological opposites; reversing its cursed-energy flow does not create a coherent second effect. RCT still heals the shared body normally.

No Domain

Domain Expansion

This technique has no default Domain. Way Big occupies the level-18 pinnacle slot, and Master Control is its capstone. A campaign that awards a universal Domain option should build it separately and should not grant more turns, more bodies, or simultaneous forms.

Maximum

Way Big is not slot nineteen

Way Big is an apex transformation outside the normal playlist. It cannot be rolled as a mistransformation, copied by a clone, used by Emergency Selection, captured early, or made free by Master Control. Even at level 18, it remains locked until Azmuth’s Measure is passed. Its complete use rules are in its transformation profile.

Player rulings

Use these answers at the table instead of improvising how a biological rewrite interacts with ordinary JJK 5e rules.

QuestionRuling
Do I heal by changing?No. There is one current and maximum HP total. Temporary HP follows the normal rule that only one source applies; changing never preserves expired form-specific temporary HP.
Do resistances apply retroactively?No. Resolve an effect using the body present when that part of the effect occurs. Emergency Selection explicitly changes you before its triggering roll; ordinary reactions resolve only when their text says.
Can switching escape a grapple?Changing size does not automatically escape a restraint. A mundane grapple ends only if the new size makes the grappler more than one size smaller or the anatomy makes the hold physically impossible. Magical, cursed, and technique restraints persist until their own end condition.
What happens to movement?Movement spent during the turn remains spent. After a switch, subtract all movement already used from the new form’s speed, minimum 0. Changing into a flying or swimming form does not grant a fresh movement allotment.
What happens to ongoing form powers?Leaving a form immediately ends its personal stances, modes, flight grants, resistances, and any Focus effect it created. A detached wall, slime mass, or object remains only for its printed duration and cannot be actively directed after you leave.
Can Extra Attack repeat everything?No. It repeats a feature only when that feature says it replaces one attack or is a natural weapon. A feature labeled “action” consumes the entire action regardless of Extra Attack.
Do I get alien spellcasting?No. You gain only the statistics and features printed in a profile. Species lore never grants an unlisted immunity, language, tool proficiency, technique, memory, spell list, or independent CE pool.
What happens to equipment?Worn equipment merges and provides no benefit. One held cursed tool may remain only if the form can physically wield it. Ammunition, charges, attunement, and uses are never copied. Returning to base form restores merged equipment to its prior state.
Can I maintain several resources?Yes only where expressly printed, such as Upchuck’s Morsels, but switching never creates a second copy. A resource tied to a body follows that profile’s exit rule; if none is printed, it ends without refund when you leave.
Does a miss refund CE?No. Pay a feature’s CE when declared. A missed attack, successful enemy save, interrupted Focus, forced reversion, or failed check does not refund it unless the feature explicitly says activation could not begin.
How do conditions and death work?Conditions, injuries, exhaustion, death saves, soul effects, and marks remain on the same character. At 0 HP the transformation ends after the triggering effect resolves. If the character dies in alien form, they revert and remain dead.
Can a clone copy the Omnitrix?A clone receives only what its own feature expressly copies. It cannot create another archive, refill CE, duplicate playlist slots, or independently activate Way Big. Ditto’s bodies use the rules in Ditto’s profile and are not conventional summons or clones.
What enters a subjugation ritual?Your current HP, CE, conditions, injuries, limited uses, personally owned equipment, class features, and feats enter unchanged. The rite restores nothing. You may choose a temporary loadout from all Subjugated forms, but cannot include the Candidate being challenged.
Can an ally rescue me?Yes. Any outside intervention immediately collapses a true rite, removes the Echo, and prevents subjugation. This lets allies save a dying wielder without allowing them to help earn the transformation.
Does failing revoke anything?No. Failure never removes a previously Subjugated form. It only preserves the Candidate’s locked status, consumes the ritual CE, and leaves all damage and resources exactly where they ended.
Does level 20 unlock skipped trials?No. Master Control perfects access to forms you own; it does not manufacture victories. Every undefeated Candidate remains unusable until its ritual is completed.

Creating a captured genome

Capture Mode is a story hook, not permission to improvise a stronger twentieth class feature mid-session. Before a new species can even become a Candidate, the GM writes a complete profile with all seven items below. It remains unusable until its ritual is won.

1 · Body

Size, Alien AC, speeds, senses, Primary aptitude, Secondary aptitude, and anatomy limits.

2 · Basic offense

Normally one natural attack at 1 OD + ability modifier, or a comparable attack-replacement option.

3 · Identity features

Two to four active abilities with actions, ranges, targets, CE costs, saves, durations, and repeat-save timing.

4 · Damage budget

A typical 2 CE area deals PB OD; a 4–5 CE capstone may reach twice PB OD with meaningful restrictions.

5 · Real weakness

At least one exploitable biological, environmental, sensory, or anatomical limitation—not merely flavor text.

6 · Shared economy

No new HP total, CE reserve, turn, reaction, Focus slot, class resource, permanent summon, or resource refresh.

7 · Trial identity

Write one initiative-20 Trial Law and one measurable Concession Condition that teach the species’ core strength or weakness. Neither can require another player’s help.

Sphoeroid Body

You breathe air and water. You have advantage on checks and saves against forced movement, and you may inflate or deflate harmlessly as part of standing, squeezing, or using a feature in this profile.

Pressurized Spit · replaces one attack

Make a ranged CE attack at 60 feet. On a hit, deal 1 OD + CE modifier bludgeoning damage and push the target 5 feet. Spend 1 CE on hit to push it 15 feet instead and coat it until the end of your next turn, reducing its speed by 10 feet.

Slime Flood · action, 2 CE

Creatures in a 30-foot cone make a Strength save, taking PB OD bludgeoning damage, being pushed 15 feet, and falling prone on failure. Success halves damage and prevents both riders. The area becomes slippery difficult terrain until the start of your next turn.

Conductive Slurry

When a creature coated by Pressurized Spit next takes lightning damage, it takes an additional 2 OD lightning damage and the coating ends. A creature may use an action to scrape or wash off the coating before then.

Inflated Bulwark · reaction, 1 CE

When damage affects you, inflate and reduce the instance by PB OD + Constitution modifier. If the damage includes piercing, a failure, critical hit, or other trigger does not cause you to explode; you simply deflate after resolution.

Congealed Slime · balancing limitation

After taking cold damage, Pressurized Spit cannot spend CE for its enhanced push, and Slime Flood is unavailable until the end of your next turn. Your walking speed is also only 25 feet because of the form’s short limbs.

Canon anchor: future-user transformation first shown in Ben 10,000; Sphoeroid slime is projected by body inflation, launches heavy opponents, conducts electricity, and the species is amphibious. Spitter reference.

Living Battery

You have resistance to lightning damage and hold 0 Charge when entering the form, maximum PB. Charge cannot be created by an ally repeatedly shocking you outside a genuine encounter or hazard, and all remaining Charge is lost when you leave Buzzshock.

Electric Bolt · replaces one attack

Absorb Current · reaction, 1 CE

When lightning damage affects only you, reduce it by PB OD + Constitution modifier before resistance. If reduced to 0, gain 1 Charge. This cannot absorb a Domain event or a lightning effect that is simultaneously resolving against another creature.

Arc Chain · action, 2 CE

Choose a creature within 60 feet and up to PB − 1 additional creatures, each within 15 feet of the previous one. Each makes a Dexterity save, taking PB OD lightning damage and losing reactions until the start of its next turn on failure, or half damage with no rider on success.

Wire Travel · bonus action, 1 CE

Become electricity and move up to 60 feet through a continuous powered cable, conductive pipe, or occupied electrical system you touch, emerging into an open space. This movement does not provoke and cannot cross a barrier, broken circuit, or protected cursed device. A mundane device traversed may be powered or shut off until the end of your next turn.

Electrical Fission · level 15, action, 4 CE, Focus

Create one additional Buzzshock body in an adjacent space for up to 1 minute. Use Ditto’s One Character, Many Locations and Shared Pain and Area Effects rules, but only two bodies may exist and neither can use Electrical Fission. The bodies merge when adjacent without an action; leaving Buzzshock merges them immediately.

Power Depletion

While you have 0 CE, flying speed falls to 20 feet, Wire Travel and Electrical Fission are unavailable, and you have disadvantage on Strength checks against a larger creature. The JJK conversion uses lightning resistance plus reactive absorption instead of unconditional damage immunity.

Canon anchor: future-user transformation first shown in Ben 10,000; Nosedeenians store and project electricity, absorb energy, travel through and possess circuits, fly, and can fission after sufficient charge. Buzzshock reference.

Polar Frame

You ignore environmental penalties from extreme cold, have resistance to cold damage, and can cling to rough walls and ceilings. Ice you create is ordinary terrain to you unless another effect changes it.

Freeze Ray · replaces one attack

Make a ranged CE attack at 90 feet. On a hit, deal 1 OD + CE modifier cold damage and reduce the target’s speed by 10 feet until the start of your next turn. Multiple rays do not stack this reduction.

Deep Freeze · action, 2 CE

Creatures in a 30-foot cone make a Constitution save, taking PB OD cold damage and becoming restrained in ice until the end of your next turn on failure. Success halves damage and prevents restraint. A restrained creature or adjacent ally may use an action for a Strength check against your CE save DC to break the ice early.

Iceworks · action, 2 CE, Focus

Create up to three connected 5-foot ice panels on surfaces within 60 feet for up to 1 minute. They have AC 13 and a shared HP total of 4 × character level, are vulnerable to fire, and may form a wall, bridge, ramp, or simple nonmechanical shape. They cannot appear inside a creature’s space.

Cryo-Jet · bonus action, 1 CE

Propel yourself with a freeze ray, gaining a 40-foot flying speed until the end of the turn. You may carry one willing creature no larger than you, but doing so reduces the speed to 20 feet.

Flash-Freeze Terrain · action, 3 CE

Choose a 20-foot square of exposed liquid, lava, or ground within 60 feet. Nonmagical liquid freezes and ordinary fire is extinguished. The area becomes slippery difficult terrain for 1 minute. Creatures standing there make a Dexterity save, taking PB OD cold damage and falling prone on failure, or half damage without falling on success.

Thermal Shock

After taking fire damage, cold resistance ends, Freeze Ray rolls one fewer OD (minimum one), and Deep Freeze cannot restrain until the end of your next turn. Fire damage deals double damage to Iceworks.

Research and design record

The rules are original homebrew. Canon references establish roster boundaries and each form’s identity; prior campaign books establish the JJK 5e procedure.

Canon boundary

Primary continuity references

  • Cartoon Network’s official Secret of the Omnitrix presentation, identifying the movie as classic Ben 10 and featuring Way Big.
  • Ben 10 (2005 series) overview, including the statement that the movie was intended as the chronological finale.
  • Original Omnitrix transformation ledger, used to distinguish unlocks, scans, escapes, later off-screen unlocks, and future-only appearances.
  • Each alien profile links its own capability reference. Later-series capabilities are identified when they materially expand what the debut established.
System boundary

Campaign rules consulted

  • Jujutsu Kaisen 5e V.3.pdf: levels 1, 3, 6, 11, and 18 for Innate Technique features, CE math, actions, and class integration.
  • Ritual Codex — Reviewed Player Edition: Technique Focus, printed-cost procedure, Black Flash eligibility, Maximum Output, transformations, shared resources, and complete player wording.
  • Ten Shadows Technique Guide: the direct precedent for solo subjugation, outside-aid invalidation, permanent mastery after defeating the manifestation, and a large modular roster that still uses one player action economy.
  • War God’s Household: named scaling die, many forms, clear active-form limit, and no duplicated turns.
  • Conduit Mimic, Triple Core, and Remembrance Bearer: one active mode, limited equipped selection, reconfiguration, and modular progression.
Deliberate choice

Transformation, not Shikigami

The Omnitrix’s fiction is embodied change. An Unbound Echo exists only as the hostile examiner inside its subjugation ritual; winning does not turn it into a summon. In ordinary play every profile overwrites the same character, avoiding independent HP, extra turns, and Shikigami positioning.

Deliberate choice

One body, many aptitudes

Genome Aptitude makes a species exceptional at its defining physical or mental role without rewriting the character’s CE score. This follows the codex’s form-switching precedents while refusing free healing, new reserves, or nineteen character sheets.

Deliberate choice

Prototype pressure

Normal use is reliable. Instability appears through rapid switching and emergency overrides, preserving the original Omnitrix’s timeout and wrong-form texture without making the player’s basic class feature randomly unusable.

JJK progression

Inheritance first, domination afterward

The base ten function like the technique’s inherited starter vocabulary. Later genomes are not level-up gifts: their levels only reveal the Candidate. Codon Subjugation borrows Ten Shadows’ uncompromising solo-victory law, then gives each alien a Concession Condition so mastery comes from understanding the form rather than merely producing enough damage.
