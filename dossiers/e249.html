Cursed Corpse Creation

Masamichi Yaga’s technique: the art of building cursed corpses — hand-made soldiers animated by cursed cores, with Panda as the proof of concept. A long-lived, expensive, fully custom minion-master chassis.

Core law

Completed rules

Core:

Core sizes and capacities: Tiny Core $5,000 / 10 max CE; Small Core $30,000 / 25; Medium Core $100,000 / 50; Large Core $250,000 / 75; Huge Core $500,000 / 100; Gargantuan Core $1,000,000 / 150.

Imbuement:

While you have a core, you can imbue the core with cursed energy, allowing it to animate. You can imbue cursed energy into a core you touch as an action and grant it any amount of your cursed energy. However as shown in the table above it has a maximum amount it can handle. If the corpse uses cursed energy it comes from this pool and must be recharged by you. A tiny or small cursed corpse expends 1 CE for each day it is active. A medium or large consumes 5, huge consumes 10, and gargantuan consumes 20.

Body:

The third thing you need is a body. Your cursed corpse requires a core with a size matching the body. Body costs and time: Tiny $1,000 worth of materials / 30 min; Small $10,000 / 3 hours; Medium $50,000 / 24 hours; Large $75,000 / 72 hours; Huge $100,000 / 216 hours; Gargantuan $150,000 / 648 hours.

Completed Cursed Corpse:

After building your cursed corpse, it will use the following depending on what size it is. The cursed corpse inherits your proficiency bonus at the time of its creation. They regain their health normally on a short or long rest, and cannot be healed using Reversed Cursed Technique. Cursed corpses are created for a niche and you allocate their stats during creation. Each score is a 6 before allocation and you have 27 points that you allocate between scores. Every increase costs 1 point, except for 14 and above, which cost 2 points each. Score maximums by size: Tiny STR 12 / DEX 18 / CON 12 / INT 10 / WIS 10 / CHA 10; Small 14 / 16 / 14 / 9 / 9 / 9; Medium 16 / 14 / 16 / 8 / 8 / 8; Large 18 / 12 / 18 / 8 / 8 / 8; Huge 20 / 10 / 20 / 8 / 8 / 8; Gargantuan 24 / 8 / 24 / 6 / 6 / 6.

Commands:

You can command a number of cursed corpses up to your proficiency bonus as a bonus action to act on your turn to have them act. Additionally, you can command the same number of cursed corpses as an action to give them a complex command that allows them to function for much longer as long as they are following that command. A Cursed Corpse with the Autonomy Mutation does not require commands to act, but can be commanded as a free action on your turn.

The fiction. Jujutsu scholars classify Cursed Corpse Creation as the inverse of a normal technique — not energy shaped by the user, but energy taught to live without one, poured into a core until it wakes up and stays awake. Every corpse is the same question asked in wood and stuffing and metal: how much of a soul can cursed energy carry before the vessel stops being a puppet? The prevailing theory holds that Panda is the technique's only honest answer — proof that the question was never whether a corpse could live, but whether someone would raise one like a son.

Full progression

Feature map

L1Cursed Corpse Creation

Starting at 1st level, your technique allows you to master the process of creating cursed corpses. A cursed corpse requires a cursed core to function, cursed energy for fuel, and a body. You start with either 2 completed tiny cursed corpses or 1 small cursed corpse. Core. While you have enough money and access to materials, you can create up to a Small core as part of a long rest. At 3rd level, you can make a Medium Core, a Large at 6th level, Huge at 11th level, and Gargantuan at 18th level.

Body. You can create up to a Small Cursed Corpse. At 3rd level, you can make a Medium Cursed Corpse, a Large at 6th level, Huge at 11th level, and Gargantuan at 18th level.

L3Corpse Tactics

At 3rd level, you have learned new ways to command your cursed corpses during combat. You can use one of these tactics as part of the bonus action you take to command your Cursed Corpses and lasts until the start of your next turn. Cooperative Tactics. You give one of your Cursed Corpses advantage on an attack or saving throw on their turn. Offensive Tactics. You cause one of your Cursed Corpses to deal an additional damage die on a hit. Defensive Tactics. You let one of your Cursed Corpses take the Dodge action as a bonus action. Guerilla Tactics. You let one of your cursed Corpses take the Disengage action as a bonus action. Mobility Tactics. You let one of your Cursed Corpses take the Dash action as a bonus action.

L6Cursed Corpse Mutation

Starting at 6th level, your strong cursed energy has caused mutations to form. When you complete a cursed corpse, you roll a d20. On a roll of 20, your cursed corpse gains a random mutation.

Mutations (roll d12). 1 Quick Movement (+20 ft walking speed); 2 Adaptive Movement (30 ft climb or swim speed); 3 Skills (proficiency in 2 skills or 1 saving throw); 4 Hardened Frame (+3 AC); 5 Weapon Usage (wield weapons proficiently); 6 Toughness (max HP +2 x your level); 7 Energy Capacity (one core holds +30 CE); 8 Energy Usage (can use Basic CE Manipulation); 9 Improved Ability (+4 to an Ability and Max); 10 Autonomy (doesn’t require commands, sentient); 11 Core Capacity (can hold 1d4 additional cores); 12 Self Generation (regains 1 CE per hour).

L11Maximum: Prized Creation

At 11th level, you can put extra time and care into one of your favorite cursed corpses to make a masterpiece. You can choose one of your existing Cursed Corpses or when you make a new Cursed Corpse to be your Favorite. It gains a +4 bonus to two of its Ability scores and their maximums for that score. The damage die of all its attacks increases by one tier (D10<D12<2d8). It gains 3 Mutations of your choice. You cannot select the same Mutation multiple times.

L18Perfect Creations

At 18th level, you can command all Cursed Corpses under your control as a bonus action on your turn regardless of numbers or distance. Additionally, your Cursed Corpses can continue functioning for a week after running out of cursed energy.

Innate Technique Feats

Build-defining options.

These are optional character-building feats. You must meet each listed prerequisite before selecting it.

Prerequisite: Must make Cursed Corpse Creation

Patchwork Salvage

When one of your Cursed Corpses is destroyed, you may spend 1 hour harvesting its intact parts. You may transfer one mutation from the destroyed corpse to another of your Cursed Corpses.

Prerequisites: Must have Cursed Corpse Creation

Soul Imbuement

You have learned to copy a creature’s soul information into a Cursed Corpse. You can only have 1 soul imbued corpse at a time. This increases to 2 at 11th level and 3 at 18th level. It takes 3 long rests to imbue someone’s soul information into the core. You must have the person readily available or have a good memory of the person to turn their physical information into soul information. Once a corpse has been created, it takes 2 weeks to develop before it becomes sentient. It gains the Autonomy mutation and can choose a Cursed Energy feat as if it were a player. A corpse with this core is sentient and mimics the behavior of the soul they are imbued with, but it does not gain its memories.

Prerequisite: Must have Corpse Tactics

Cursed Tactician

Your Corpse Tactics orders can be relayed through your corpses. When you use Corpse Tactics, a corpse within range may pass the order to another of your corpses within 30 feet of it, which may pass it on further, extending your effective command range.

Prerequisite: Must have Cursed Corpse Mutation

Mutate Specialist

Once per long rest, when one of your Cursed Corpses would gain a random mutation, you may instead grant it a mutation already possessed by another of your corpses.

Prerequisite: Must make Cursed Corpse Creation

Expert Maker

You have spent your life learning your technique and learned tips and tricks that help you master the process. You can create a cursed core or body for 1/3 of the cost or time.

Rules integration

Edge cases and shared systems.

STAT-BLOCK HOLD: completed-corpse AC/HP/speeds/base attacks absent — flag STAT-BLOCK HOLD.
CE economy: core capacities are machine storage, not CE creation — transfer actual user CE by action and touch; never manufacture CE by charging. Tiny/Small 1 CE per active day, Medium/Large 5, Huge 10, Gargantuan 20.
Creation: Record the creator's PB at creation permanently unless a later upgrade explicitly changes it. corpses regain health normally on a short or long rest and cannot be healed using Reversed Cursed Technique. Bodies with cores get +1 core capacity per size.
Point-buy: 27-point allocation is exact: each score starts at 6, each increment costs 1 point, and increases to 14 or higher cost 2 points each — not standard 5e point-buy.
Construction: body-construction hours are work-hours, not a rest tax; rest can be taken while a body builds.
Commands: commanded bodies act in a declared order during the user's turn; complex commands are explicit finite tasks with conditions, not autonomous improvisation.
Expert Maker: Expert Maker chooses one-third cost OR one-third time per component, not both.
Mutations: Snapshot the creator's level for Toughness when gained. extra cores each need actual purchase, construction, and charging — stored energy is never duplicated. Self Generation restores 1 CE per hour into its own cores up to actual capacity. One Favorite at a time; proposed: changing a Favorite costs an action + 1 CE.
Perfect Creations: The one-week grace supplies basic operation only, not spendable CE for attacks. Refill starts a new grace period only after normal fuel is exhausted, not an hourly off/on exploit.
Soul Imbuement: 3 long rests + 2 weeks maturation; grants Autonomy and one qualified CE feat, no actual donor memories and no innate technique. A sentient corpse choosing feats must meet its own prerequisites.
No Domain: No Domain is printed — level 18 is Perfect Creations, not a missing standard Domain to invent.

Cursed Tactician
Prerequisite: Must have Corpse Tactics

 When you use Corpse Tactics, you can spend ce up to your proficiency
bonus to use an additional tactics option for each ce spent. You can
choose the same tactics option as part of the same bonus action, but
you cannot use the same tactics multiple times on the same cursed
corpse.

Mutate Specialist
Prerequisite: Must have Cursed Corpse Mutation

You have learned the process to purposely force mutations to occur.
When you roll for a mutation, you can add your Cursed Energy modifier
to the roll. If you succeed, you roll for an additional mutation for
every number higher than 20. You cannot gain the same mutation more
than once.
