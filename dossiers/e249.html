Cursed Corpse Creation

Masamichi Yaga’s technique: the art of building cursed corpses, hand-made soldiers animated by cursed cores, with Panda as the proof of concept. A long-lived, expensive, fully custom minion-master chassis.

Core law

Completed rules

Core:

Every soldier needs a well he drinks from. Mark the prices honestly: the table below is the floor of this technique’s ambition, a Gargantuan core costs a million and holds one hundred and fifty. Dream accordingly. Core sizes and capacities: Tiny Core $5,000 / 10 max CE; Small Core $30,000 / 25; Medium Core $100,000 / 50; Large Core $250,000 / 75; Huge Core $500,000 / 100; Gargantuan Core $1,000,000 / 150.

Imbuement:

A core without fuel is a cask without wine. Imbuement is the feeding: your own energy, poured in by touch, spent by the corpse until you refill it, the quartermaster’s eternal errand. While you have a core, you can imbue the core with cursed energy, allowing it to animate. You can imbue cursed energy into a core you touch as an action and grant it any amount of your cursed energy. However as shown in the table above it has a maximum amount it can handle. If the corpse uses cursed energy it comes from this pool and must be recharged by you. A tiny or small cursed corpse expends 1 CE for each day it is active. A medium or large consumes 5, huge consumes 10, and gargantuan consumes 20.

Body:

The third leg of the triad. Mark the time column harder than the cost column: coin can be found, but 648 hours is a decision to be an army-maker. The third thing you need is a body. Your cursed corpse requires a core with a size matching the body. Body costs and time: Tiny $1,000 worth of materials / 30 min; Small $10,000 / 3 hours; Medium $50,000 / 24 hours; Large $75,000 / 72 hours; Huge $100,000 / 216 hours; Gargantuan $150,000 / 648 hours.

Completed Cursed Corpse:

Once assembled, the corpse stands as its own account, it inherits your skill, heals like the living, and is built to purpose: you shape it in the 27-point allotment, and the shape dictates the war it fights. After building your cursed corpse, it will use the following depending on what size it is. The cursed corpse inherits your proficiency bonus at the time of its creation. They regain their health normally on a short or long rest, and cannot be healed using Reversed Cursed Technique. Cursed corpses are created for a niche and you allocate their stats during creation. Each score is a 6 before allocation and you have 27 points that you allocate between scores. Every increase costs 1 point, except for 14 and above, which cost 2 points each. Score maximums by size: Tiny STR 12 / DEX 18 / CON 12 / INT 10 / WIS 10 / CHA 10; Small 14 / 16 / 14 / 9 / 9 / 9; Medium 16 / 14 / 16 / 8 / 8 / 8; Large 18 / 12 / 18 / 8 / 8 / 8; Huge 20 / 10 / 20 / 8 / 8 / 8; Gargantuan 24 / 8 / 24 / 6 / 6 / 6.

Commands:

Strings and orders. They move when you tell them to, on your turn, for your bonus action, command is the technique’s second half, and the puppeteer who neglects it has built an audience, not an army. You can command a number of cursed corpses up to your proficiency bonus as a bonus action to act on your turn to have them act. Additionally, you can command the same number of cursed corpses as an action to give them a complex command that allows them to function for much longer as long as they are following that command. A Cursed Corpse with the Autonomy Mutation does not require commands to act, but can be commanded as a free action on your turn.

Field notes. Subject: the maker of soldiers. Four truths govern him. First, the triad: no corpse stands without core, body, and fuel, neglect any leg and the whole thing collapses, which is why the wise spend their coin and hours before the war, never during. Second, the ledger: a Gargantuan core costs a million and holds one hundred and fifty cursed energy; the arithmetic of power is exactly as crude as it sounds. Third, the strings: corpses move on the maker’s word, a bonus action for a number up to his proficiency bonus, command is the technique’s second half, and Autonomy is its release. Fourth, the honest answer: Panda. The technique asks how much soul energy can carry; the maker answered by raising one like a son. Maxim: you do not command corpses. You raise them, supply them, and, if you have earned it, release them.

Full progression

Feature map

1Cursed Corpse Creation.

Downtime (long rest) · Start with 2 Tiny or 1 Small completed corpse · Cores up to Small per long rest (Medium 3rd, Large 6th, Huge 11th, Gargantuan 18th).

Apprenticeship is arithmetic: one core, one body, one stream of your own energy. Small things at first, two Tiny corpses or one Small, and the maker’s hands learn on puppets before they ever build legions. Starting at 1st level, your technique allows you to master the process of creating cursed corpses. A cursed corpse requires a cursed core to function, cursed energy for fuel, and a body. You start with either 2 completed tiny cursed corpses or 1 small cursed corpse. Core. While you have enough money and access to materials, you can create up to a Small core as part of a long rest. At 3rd level, you can make a Medium Core, a Large at 6th level, Huge at 11th level, and Gargantuan at 18th level.

Downtime · Craft corpses up to Small (Medium 3rd, Large 6th, Huge 11th, Gargantuan 18th).

Body. Size is a progression the maker must earn: Small now, Medium at the third, Large at the sixth, Huge at the eleventh, Gargantuan at the eighteenth. The workshop grows with the sorcerer. You can create up to a Small Cursed Corpse. At 3rd level, you can make a Medium Cursed Corpse, a Large at 6th level, Huge at 11th level, and Gargantuan at 18th level.

3Corpse Tactics.

Bonus action (as part of command) · One tactic, lasts until the start of your next turn · Cooperative: advantage on one attack or save · Offensive: +1 damage die on a hit · Defensive: Dodge as a bonus action · Guerilla: Disengage as a bonus action · Mobility: Dash as a bonus action.

The strings become an instrument. Five tactics, one bonus action, cooperative, offensive, defensive, guerilla, mobility, and a good commander does not merely move his corpses; he conducts them. At 3rd level, you have learned new ways to command your cursed corpses during combat. You can use one of these tactics as part of the bonus action you take to command your Cursed Corpses and lasts until the start of your next turn. Cooperative Tactics. You give one of your Cursed Corpses advantage on an attack or saving throw on their turn. Offensive Tactics. You cause one of your Cursed Corpses to deal one additional die of the attack’s normal damage size and type on a hit (invented). Defensive Tactics. You let one of your Cursed Corpses take the Dodge action as a bonus action. Guerilla Tactics. You let one of your cursed Corpses take the Disengage action as a bonus action. Mobility Tactics. You let one of your Cursed Corpses take the Dash action as a bonus action.

6Cursed Corpse Mutation.

Passive (6th) · On completing a corpse, roll d20; on a 20 it gains a random mutation.

At the sixth, strong energy does what strong energy does: it refuses to be perfectly copied. On a perfect roll of 20, the maker finds a surprise in his own work, the art, developing its own opinions. Starting at 6th level, your strong cursed energy has caused mutations to form. When you complete a cursed corpse, you roll a d20. On a roll of 20, your cursed corpse gains a random mutation.

Mutation table (d12) · 1 +20 ft speed · 2 30-ft climb or swim · 3 proficiency in 2 skills or 1 save · 4 +3 AC · 5 weapon use · 6 +2x level max HP · 7 core +30 CE · 8 Basic CE Manipulation · 9 +4 to an Ability and max · 10 Autonomy (no commands, sentient) · 11 +1d4 extra cores · 12 regain 1 CE/hour.

Mutations (roll d12). The mutations are the dice talking, twelve deviations, from swiftness to sentience. Note the tenth: Autonomy. It is the rarest because it ends the puppeteer’s job. 1 Quick Movement (+20 ft walking speed); 2 Adaptive Movement (30 ft climb or swim speed); 3 Skills (proficiency in 2 skills or 1 saving throw); 4 Hardened Frame (+3 AC); 5 Weapon Usage (wield weapons proficiently); 6 Toughness (max HP +2 x your level); 7 Energy Capacity (one core holds +30 CE); 8 Energy Usage (can use Basic CE Manipulation); 9 Improved Ability (+4 to an Ability and Max); 10 Autonomy (doesn’t require commands, sentient); 11 Core Capacity (can hold 1d4 additional cores; each additional core adds its listed maximum CE to the corpse’s pool, invented); 12 Self Generation (regains 1 CE per hour).

11Maximum: Prized Creation.

Downtime · One Favorite corpse: +4 to two Ability scores and their maximums; damage dice up one tier; 3 chosen mutations (no repeats).

The Maximum: the maker chooses a favorite and lavishes it, +4 to two scores, a fiercer damage die, three chosen mutations. This is how you get a Panda: not luck, but love with a budget. At 11th level, you can put extra time and care into one of your favorite cursed corpses to make a masterpiece. You can choose one of your existing Cursed Corpses or when you make a new Cursed Corpse to be your Favorite. It gains a +4 bonus to two of its Ability scores and their maximums for that score. The damage die of all its attacks increases by one tier (D10<D12<2d8). It gains 3 Mutations of your choice. You cannot select the same Mutation multiple times.

18Perfect Creations.

Passive (18th) · Bonus action: command all corpses regardless of numbers or distance · Corpses function 1 week after CE runs out.

The pinnacle: every corpse answers at once, at any distance, and when the fuel runs dry, they march on for a week. An army that needs no logistics has made its maker obsolete, and that is the maker’s triumph. At 18th level, you can command all Cursed Corpses under your control as a bonus action on your turn regardless of numbers or distance. Additionally, your Cursed Corpses can continue functioning for a week after running out of cursed energy.

Innate Technique Feats

Build-defining options.

These are optional character-building feats. You must meet each listed prerequisite before selecting it.

Prerequisite: Must make Cursed Corpse Creation

Patchwork Salvage

Nothing is wasted in the workshop. When a corpse falls, an hour with the salvage grafts one of its mutations onto another. The fallen serve again, in parts. When one of your Cursed Corpses is destroyed, you may spend 1 hour harvesting its intact parts. You may transfer one mutation from the destroyed corpse to another of your Cursed Corpses.

Prerequisites: Must have Cursed Corpse Creation

Soul Imbuement

The old heresy, done properly: copy a soul’s information into the core, wait two weeks, and the corpse wakes sentient, behaving like its donor without remembering them. One at a time. Slow. This is where ‘corpse’ stops being the right word. You have learned to copy a creature’s soul information into a Cursed Corpse. You can only have 1 soul imbued corpse at a time. This increases to 2 at 11th level and 3 at 18th level. It takes 3 long rests to imbue someone’s soul information into the core. You must have the person readily available or have a good memory of the person to turn their physical information into soul information. Once a corpse has been created, it takes 2 weeks to develop before it becomes sentient. It gains the Autonomy mutation and can choose any Innate Technique feat the GM deems appropriate (invented) as if it were a player. A corpse with this core is sentient and mimics the behavior of the soul they are imbued with, but it does not gain its memories.

Prerequisite: Must have Corpse Tactics

Cursed Tactician

Orders relayed corpse to corpse, thirty feet per relay, the army becomes its own telegraph. Range is logistics; this feat buys it. Your Corpse Tactics orders can be relayed through your corpses. When you use Corpse Tactics, a corpse within range may pass the order to another of your corpses within 30 feet of it, which may pass it on further, extending your effective command range.

Prerequisite: Must have Cursed Corpse Mutation

Mutate Specialist

The maker who studies his accidents learns to repeat the good ones, once a day, grant a mutation another corpse already carries, rather than trusting the dice. Once per long rest, when one of your Cursed Corpses would gain a random mutation, you may instead grant it a mutation already possessed by another of your corpses.

Prerequisite: Must make Cursed Corpse Creation

Expert Maker

A lifetime of tips and tricks: a core or body at a third of the cost or a third of the time. Years of sawdust and solder, cashed in. You have spent your life learning your technique and learned tips and tricks that help you master the process. You can create a cursed core or body for 1/3 of the cost or time.

Rules integration

Edge cases and shared systems.

STAT-BLOCK HOLD: completed-corpse AC/HP/speeds/base attacks absent — flag STAT-BLOCK HOLD.
CE economy: core capacities are machine storage, not CE creation — transfer actual user CE by action and touch; never manufacture CE by charging. Tiny/Small 1 CE per active day, Medium/Large 5, Huge 10, Gargantuan 20.
Creation: Record the creator's PB at creation permanently unless a later upgrade explicitly changes it. corpses regain health normally on a short or long rest and cannot be healed using Reversed Cursed Technique. Bodies with cores get +1 core capacity per size.
Point-buy: 27-point allocation is exact: each score starts at 6, each increment costs 1 point, and increases to 14 or higher cost 2 points each — not standard 5e point-buy.
Construction: body-construction hours are work-hours, not a rest tax; rest can be taken while a body builds.
Commands: commanded bodies act in a declared order during the user's turn; complex commands are explicit finite tasks with conditions, not autonomous improvisation.
Expert Maker: Expert Maker chooses one-third cost OR one-third time per component, not both.
Mutations: Snapshot the creator's level for Toughness when gained. extra cores each need actual purchase, construction, and charging — stored energy is never duplicated. Self Generation restores 1 CE per hour into its own cores up to actual capacity. One Favorite at a time; proposed: changing a Favorite costs an action + 1 CE.
Perfect Creations: The one-week grace supplies basic operation only, not spendable CE for attacks. Refill starts a new grace period only after normal fuel is exhausted, not an hourly off/on exploit.
Soul Imbuement: 3 long rests + 2 weeks maturation; grants Autonomy and one qualified CE feat, no actual donor memories and no innate technique. A sentient corpse choosing feats must meet its own prerequisites.
No Domain: No Domain is printed — level 18 is Perfect Creations, not a missing standard Domain to invent.

Mutate Specialist
Prerequisite: Must have Cursed Corpse Mutation

You have learned the process to purposely force mutations to occur.
When you roll for a mutation, you can add your Cursed Energy modifier

every number higher than 20. You cannot gain the same mutation more
than once.
