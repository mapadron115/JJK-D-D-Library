Features & procedures

Exact player-facing procedures.

Titan Hammer:

requires Str 18 or Powerful Build to avoid disadvantage. 2d8 bludgeoning; Heavy, Two-Handed. Once/turn after moving 10 ft, add 1 Tool Die to a hit.

Creation Conduit:

if you possess an earth/terrain technique, its ranges increase by 30 ft and once/turn one damage roll gains +1 Tool Die while Gideon is held.

Ground Break (2 CE):

action; strike earth. 15-ft cone, Dex save, 3 Tool Dice bludgeoning and prone on failure.

Rising Earth (3 CE):

action; raise up to three 10-ft panels of stone within 60 ft, each AC 15 and HP equal 10 + 5×PB.

Lightning Rod — reaction, 3 CE:

when a CE/spell attack or line/cone/burst would damage you or an ally within 10 ft, redirect its energy into the ground. Reduce damage by 4 Tool Dice + CE modifier; any excess still applies. Ground in a 10-ft radius becomes hazardous difficult terrain.

Sacred Treasure Release — Mother's Answer (level 13+, 7 CE):

for 1 minute, earth techniques gain +2 Tool Dice and Ground Break radius doubles. Once during the release, use Lightning Rod without CE and with doubled reduction dice.

The fiction. Registry notes describe Gideon as a cursed tool built around one principle: that the earth is patient enough to absorb anything. The hammer's channeling is not the user's CE projected outward but hostile energy pulled downward and buried — the prevailing theory holds it was forged for a sorcerer who survived by grounding every strike instead of answering one. It answers its wielder with the patience of stone, and the stone, scholars note, is still owed.

Rules integration

Tool Die & Sacred Treasure attunement

Attunement: spend 1 hour and one cursed-tool attunement slot. A Sacred Treasure supplements your Innate Technique; it does not grant a second one.
Activation: activated properties spend the wielder’s own CE. If a non-sorcerer is explicitly allowed to wield the treasure, its Tool save DC is 8 + proficiency bonus + one chosen mental ability modifier.
Sacred Treasure Release: normally requires level 13 or higher and is usable once per long rest; pay the specific treasure’s listed CE cost. An explicit exception in its individual entry takes precedence.
