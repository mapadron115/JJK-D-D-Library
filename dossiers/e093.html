Sacred Treasure Release — Mother's Answer (level 13+, 7 CE):

Level 13+, 7 CE: for 1 minute, earth techniques gain +2 Tool Dice and Ground Break's radius doubles; once during the release, use Lightning Rod without CE and with doubled reduction dice. Field notes. Subject: the hammer that buries. Sorcerers try to out-strike Gideon and fail the way one fails to outlast a mountain: its principle is that the earth is patient enough to absorb anything. Three truths govern it. First, it is two weapons in one: the Titan Hammer strikes, the Creation Conduit extends earth techniques, ranges +30 ft, +1 Tool Die to one damage roll per turn. Second, its doctrine is downward: Ground Break splits the earth in a 15-ft cone; Rising Earth raises three stone panels; Lightning Rod pulls a hostile attack's energy into the ground, reducing it by 4 Tool Dice + CE modifier. Third, the Release, Mother's Answer at level 13+: earth techniques gain +2 Tool Dice, Ground Break doubles its radius, and once Lightning Rod costs nothing with doubled reduction. Mark the economy: Gideon never answers a strike. It files it.
