Attunement: spend 1 hour and one cursed-tool attunement slot. A Sacred Treasure supplements your Innate Technique; it does not grant a second one.

Activation: activated properties spend the wielder’s own CE. If a non-sorcerer is explicitly allowed to wield the treasure, its Tool save DC is 8 + proficiency bonus + one chosen mental ability modifier.

Sacred Treasure Release: normally requires level 13 or higher and is usable once per long rest; pay the specific treasure’s listed CE cost. An explicit exception in its individual entry takes precedence.

SACRED TREASURE • Diane

War Hammer Gideon

Grade 1 / Special Grade with Creation

Massive earth-channeling war hammer

A brutal tool on anyone strong enough to wield it, but its true purpose is amplifying earth manipulation and grounding hostile energy.

  1. Titan Hammer: requires Str 18 or Powerful Build to avoid disadvantage. 2d8 bludgeoning; Heavy, Two-Handed. Once/turn after moving 10 ft, add 1 Tool Die to a hit.
  2. Creation Conduit: if you possess an earth/terrain technique, its ranges increase by 30 ft and once/turn one damage roll gains +1 Tool Die while Gideon is held.
  3. Ground Break (2 CE): action; strike earth. 15-ft cone, Dex save, 3 Tool Dice bludgeoning and prone on failure.
  4. Rising Earth (3 CE): action; raise up to three 10-ft panels of stone within 60 ft, each AC 15 and HP equal 10 + 5×PB.
  5. Lightning Rod — reaction, 3 CE: when a CE/spell attack or line/cone/burst would damage you or an ally within 10 ft, redirect its energy into the ground. Reduce damage by 4 Tool Dice + CE modifier; any excess still applies. Ground in a 10-ft radius becomes hazardous difficult terrain.
  6. Sacred Treasure Release — Mother's Answer (level 13+, 7 CE): for 1 minute, earth techniques gain +2 Tool Dice and Ground Break radius doubles. Once during the release, use Lightning Rod without CE and with doubled reduction dice.
