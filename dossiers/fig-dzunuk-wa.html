Feature map

Level-by-level features, as the figure refined them.

L1

The Cry

passive aura

Tier V · Unaimable Tank · Suggested CE ability: CON · Primary verb: Take. Technique DC = 8 + proficiency bonus + CON modifier. She is Huge. She targets the nearest hostile creature with all offensive features — she cannot be directed to a specific target by any means. Devoured. When Dzunuk'wa reduces a creature to 0 HP — or stuffs it into the basket with Into the Basket — it is not killed — it is Devoured: removed into the basket, unreachable by revival, resurrection, scrying, or sending while she lives. If she is slain, the Devoured spill out at 0 HP, stable. A creature may be pulled free as an action with a successful STR contest against her (attempting to pull a creature free counts as an attack against her for Snatch's trigger — invented).

Her pursed lips never stop: hu-hu-hu. Creatures within 60 ft that can hear her must succeed on a WIS save vs her technique DC at the start of their turn or be frightened of her until the start of their next turn. A creature that succeeds is immune for 1 minute. (The willing-decline: a creature may stop its ears — cotton, wax, hands — becoming deafened; the deafened cannot hear the cry. The forest charges for the silence: deafened creatures have disadvantage on Perception.)

L1

The Forest Outlasts

passive

At the start of her turn, she regains 15 HP. If she took fire or radiant damage since her last turn, she does not regenerate that turn — the char must fall off first. Additionally, the first time each round she would take damage, reduce it by 10 (hide of winter). She cannot be banished, turned, or possessed — the forest does not leave.

L3

Snatch

reaction, 3 CE

When a creature within 15 ft of her misses her with an attack, or when she succeeds on a saving throw against its effect, she grabs it: the creature is grappled (escape DC = her technique DC). While grappled by her, the winter cold closes around it — it hears only hu-hu-hu.

L3

The Woods Close

bonus action, 2 CE

For 1 minute, the ground within 30 ft of her becomes difficult terrain for hostile creatures — roots, deadfall, the forest remembering it is hers. She ignores it. (She does not create the terrain. She reminds it.)

L6

She Eats the Vanguard

action, 5 CE

Bite: melee attack, +PB + STR to hit, reach 15 ft. On hit: 6d12 + STR bludgeoning damage. If this reduces the target to 0 HP, it is Devoured — and she regains HP equal to half the damage dealt, the basket settling heavier on her back.

L6

Into the Basket

action, 4 CE

Every creature grappled by her within 15 ft must succeed on a STR save vs her technique DC or be stuffed into the basket — Devoured alive. On a success, the grapple ends (the quick child, escaping — this time).

L11

The Basket Is Never Full

action, 7 CE

She shakes the basket. The Devoured within cry out (if the basket is empty, there is no cry) — every hostile creature within 60 ft that can hear must succeed on a WIS save vs her technique DC or be frightened for 1 minute (repeat the save at the end of each turn). She gains temporary HP equal to 10 × the number of Devoured in the basket (maximum 100). The basket settles. It is never full.

Maximum Technique

Maximum: WINTER'S MOTHER (action, 10 CE, once per long rest)

action, 10 CE, once per long rest

For 1 minute, she becomes famine itself: - She cannot be moved against her will, and her damage reduction (The Forest Outlasts) increases to 20. - At the start of each hostile creature's turn within 60 ft, it must succeed on a CON save vs her technique DC or gain 1 level of exhaustion (the campaign of the empty belly). A creature may instead kneel and drop its weapons — submitting — and is ignored by the famine for that turn. (The willing-decline: hunger respects surrender. It always has.) - Her Snatch reaction costs no CE.

Domain Expansion

HU-HU-HU — THE BASKET OF THE WILD WOMAN (action, 20 CE)

action, 20 CE

90-ft enclosed Domain. 1000 clash points · 3-round burnout · 3-minute duration (per zack's Tier V bookkeeping). The deep woods close over everything. Daylight thins to green dusk. Everywhere: hu-hu-hu. - Sure-hit (enemies): at initiative 20, the forest takes one step closer — each hostile creature inside takes 3d10 bludgeoning damage (branches, roots, the ground itself closing) and is pulled 15 ft toward her (the basket calls). This movement provokes no opportunity attacks — nothing in the forest interferes with the basket's dinner. - Sure-hit (allies of the forest): at the start of her turn, she regains 10 HP per hostile creature inside the Domain (maximum 50) — the woods feeding their hunger back to her. - Inside the Domain, her Snatch reach becomes 30 ft. The Domain ends early if she is reduced to 0 HP — the forest opens its hand. (It has never happened. The tuition file remains open.)

Level 20 · Capstone

SHE WAS ALWAYS HERE (passive, once per long rest)

passive, once per long rest

When she would be reduced to 0 HP, she is not. The forest refuses the accounting: she returns to half her maximum HP, every hostile creature within 30 ft must succeed on a WIS save vs her technique DC or be frightened for 1 minute, and the cry changes — one time, those who hear it understand the words underneath the hu-hu-hu: "I was here before your roads. I will be here after."
