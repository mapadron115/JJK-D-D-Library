Character engine

Maintain a Hand Pool equal to PB + CE modifier. Every spectral hand committed to offense, defense, grappling, or utility is unavailable for the other jobs until your next turn.

Technique save DC: 8 + PB + chosen CE ability modifier. Technique attack: PB + chosen CE ability modifier.

Counterplay

Pressure several axes at once. Hands spent defending, grappling, and manipulating cannot also become barrage attacks.

Adaptation note. This is original JJK 5e homebrew inspired by Hecatoncheires and the named tradition. The folklore/myth supplies the motif; the resource engine, CE economy, progression, feats, Maximum and Domain procedures are game mechanics created for this codex.
