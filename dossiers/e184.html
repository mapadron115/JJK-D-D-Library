Hundred-Handed Arsenal

Hecatoncheires inspiration. Maintain a Hand Pool equal to PB + CE modifier. Every spectral hand committed to offense, defense, grappling, or utility is unavailable for the other jobs until your next turn.

Combat roleAction-economy bruiser / projectile controller
Power tierTier IV · Special Grade
Core law

Character engine

Maintain a Hand Pool equal to PB + CE modifier. Every spectral hand committed to offense, defense, grappling, or utility is unavailable for the other jobs until your next turn.

Technique save DC: 8 + PB + chosen CE ability modifier. Technique attack: PB + chosen CE ability modifier.

Full progression

Level progression

1Hands Beyond Counting

Spend Hands to extend melee reach, add a Hand Die after a hit, or use reaction to give +2 AC against one attack.

3Stone Barrage

Action, 2 CE: spend up to PB Hands; make one ranged technique attack per Hand within 60 ft, max two attacks per target; each hit one die + CE mod (modifier once/target).

6Fifty Heads, Hundred Tasks

Hands remotely manipulate objects, bolster Strength checks, or maintain one 20-ft spectral grapple.

11Titan-Burying Volley

Once/turn after hitting a Large+ creature, spend 3 Hands; Str save or prone and restrained beneath spectral stones until it breaks free with an action.

18Maximum: Three Hundred Stones

7 CE, once/long rest: expend all Hands to bombard up to PB 15-ft squares within 120 ft; Dex save, scaling dice based on Hands spent; zones become difficult terrain.

Feats

Build-defining options.

Ambidexterity Is Meaningless

You can commit Hands to the Help action: each committed Hand aids a different creature you can see within 30 feet, granting the aid's benefit on the first attack roll or ability check that creature makes before the start of your next turn.

Jailor of Titans

You can maintain two 20-foot spectral grapples at once, paying the normal Hand cost for each and making separate contested checks for each.

Catch the Sky

defensive Hand reaction can protect against ranged technique attacks from 30 ft.

Briarean Barrage (11+)

When you use Stone Barrage, you may instead fuse the Hands into one massive fist: make a single melee technique attack with 30-foot reach, dealing one Hand Die per Hand spent plus your CE modifier once; on a hit the target must succeed on a Strength save against your CE DC or be knocked prone.

Rules integration

Counterplay

Pressure several axes at once. Hands spent defending, grappling, and manipulating cannot also become barrage attacks.
