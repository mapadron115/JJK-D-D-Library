DJINN 34 • Light (Darkness)

Furfur — Ecstasy & Rending Darkness

Rank S
Canon master: SinbadBasis: Canon affinity/title • specific combat techniques largely unseen, so mechanics are Codex extrapolation

Weapon Equip • Level 5

Eclipse Necklace. Weapon Equip manifests a black-edged weapon that can extinguish light and split radiance into destructive shadow.

Lightless Cut

On hit • 2 CE

Add 3 Djinn Dice radiant or necrotic. You may extinguish mundane and magical light of equal/lower tier in a 30-ft radius around the target.

Shadow Refraction

Reaction • 3 CE

When targeted by an attack, impose disadvantage and teleport up to 30 ft to a shadow or dim-light space you can see after the attack resolves.

Rending Night

Action • 5 CE

180-ft line, 30 ft wide. Dexterity save; 8 Djinn Dice radiant/necrotic, half on success. Failure Blinds until the end of your next turn.

Full Djinn Equip • Level 9

Fly 120 ft; see through magical darkness; resistance to radiant and necrotic. In dim light/darkness, you may Hide as a bonus action and teleport 30 ft between shadows once per turn.

EXTREME MAGIC • LEVEL 13+

Black Sun — Rending Ecstasy

14 CE • Codex Extreme

Create a supernatural eclipse in a 180-ft-radius zone within 300 ft. Constitution save; 16 Djinn Dice radiant/necrotic, half on success. Failure Blinds and Frightens until the end of your next turn. The zone remains magical darkness for 1 minute and moves with the chosen point only if the point is a creature.

Anime-scale: Strategic scale: black out and shred a district-sized battlefield. Kouji’s Half-Fallen Furfur may narratively mix white and black Rukh, but the core kit stays the same.
