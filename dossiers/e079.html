Features & procedures

Exact player-facing procedures.

Weapon Equip • Level 5

Eclipse Necklace.

Weapon Equip manifests a black-edged weapon that can extinguish light and split radiance into destructive shadow.

On hit • 2 Reserve

Lightless Cut

Add 3 Djinn Dice radiant or necrotic. You may extinguish mundane and magical light of equal/lower tier in a 30-ft radius around the target.

Reaction • 3 Reserve

Shadow Refraction

When targeted by an attack, impose disadvantage and teleport up to 30 ft to a shadow or dim-light space you can see after the attack resolves.

Action • 5 Reserve

Rending Night

180-ft line, 30 ft wide. Dexterity save; 8 Djinn Dice radiant/necrotic, half on success. Failure Blinds until the end of your next turn.

Full Djinn Equip • Level 9

Fly 120 ft; see through magical darkness; resistance to radiant and necrotic. In dim light/darkness, you may Hide as a bonus action and teleport 30 ft between shadows once per turn.

14 Reserve • Codex Extreme

Black Sun — Rending Ecstasy

Create a supernatural eclipse in a 180-ft-radius zone within 300 ft. Constitution save; 16 Djinn Dice radiant/necrotic, half on success. Failure Blinds and Frightens until the end of your next turn. The zone remains magical darkness for 1 minute and moves with the chosen point only if the point is a creature.

Anime-scale: Strategic scale: black out and shred a district-sized battlefield. Kouji’s Half-Fallen Furfur may narratively mix white and black Rukh, but the core kit stays the same.

Rules integration

Djinn Die, Equip & Extreme Magic

Independent Djinn Reserve: 18 (Rank S). Refreshes on a long rest. Every Djinn cost below — Weapon Equip, Full Equip, abilities, reactions, Extreme Magic — spends this Djinn’s Reserve, never your CE. If this Djinn’s Reserve is empty it simply cannot fuel more until you rest; your other Djinn keep their own separate Reserves.
Weapon Equip · level 5: bonus action, 2 Reserve, lasts 10 minutes.
Full Djinn Equip · level 9: bonus action, 4 Reserve, lasts 1 minute. Only one Equip can be active.
Deep Synchronization · level 11: once per short rest, one Equip activation costs 1 Reserve less.
Extreme Magic · level 13: action; requires that Djinn’s Full Equip; pay the listed Reserve cost from this Djinn’s Reserve; usable once per long rest per Djinn. Full Equip ends afterward, and you cannot Equip that Djinn again until you finish a short rest.
King Vessel · level 17: switch directly from one Full Equip to another as a bonus action, paying the new Equip cost. At level 20, once per long rest, immediately transition from one Djinn’s Extreme into another Djinn’s Full Equip.
Attack / save: use your CE attack modifier and CE save DC unless this Djinn explicitly says otherwise.
