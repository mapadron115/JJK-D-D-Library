Record

Classification & Risk

A Disease Cursed Spirit, Grade 4 (CR 1/8–1/2 band). Normally passive; only attacks if threatened. Prefers to infect non-sorcerers and exhaust them rather than kill.

Stats

Ability scores & traits

STR 4 (−3) · DEX 12 (+1) · CON 12 (+1) · INT 2 (−4) · WIS 10 (+0) · CHA 6 (−2). HP 21 (6d6), AC 11, fly 20 ft. (hover). Invisibility. Plague (disease curse): its motes and its death-burst carry sickness.

Actions

Actions

Mote Burst (only when provoked). Ranged Attack: +4 to hit, range 60 ft. Hit: 1d6 + 2 necrotic damage, and the target must succeed on a DC 11 Constitution saving throw or be unable to regain hit points until the end of its next turn (its energy sapped on contact).

Risk

Special

Death Burst. When the curse dies, its body bursts into a cloud: each creature within 10 ft. must succeed on a DC 11 Constitution saving throw or be cursed with insomnia for 24 hours — the creature gains no benefit from finishing a long rest. Exorcise from range, upwind, and do not cluster around the kill.

Rules integration

Edge cases and shared systems.

Campaign canon notes

Field rule — low-grade perception: Per the C&C field rules, Grade 3 and lower curses are invisible to creatures without cursed energy. A creature in a life-or-death situation at less than half its hit point maximum may temporarily or permanently gain the ability to see them. This spirit carries the Invisibility trait below.
