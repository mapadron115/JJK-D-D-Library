Core law

At-the-table character reference

Recovered printed values & campaign clarifications

Demonic Intellect · level 1:

you have extreme control of your emotions with a logical mindset allowing you to use Intelligence as your Cursed Energy modifier. You can spend 1 CE as a bonus action to overclock your brain for 1 minute, gaining the following benefits: you can spend 2 CE to take an action as a reaction; you gain advantage on Intelligence checks; you add your Intelligence modifier to AC and Dexterity saves; you gain the 2000 IQ Skill feat.

2000 IQ:

you can make an Intelligence (Arcana) check as a bonus action while viewing a technique in play to understand how it works. The DC is equal to the user’s Cursed Energy save DC. When making this check, treat your Intelligence score as a 22. If you instead spend a full turn action (Action + Bonus Action + Movement) attempting to understand a technique, you have advantage on the check.

Future and Past · level 3:

when you finish a long rest, roll two d20s and record the numbers rolled. You can spend 1 CE as a reaction to replace any attack roll, saving throw, or ability check you make, or a saving throw you force another creature to make with one of these foretelling rolls. You must choose to do so before the roll. Each foretelling roll can be used only once. When you finish a long rest, you lose any unused foretelling rolls. When you complete a short rest, you can spend 1 CE to gain another foretelling roll. You can only have one foretelling roll from a short rest at a time.

Overload · level 6:

as an action for 4 cursed energy you can touch a creature and force them to make an Intelligence saving throw. On a failure they take 5d10 psychic damage and are stunned until the end of their next turn, on a success they take half as much damage and aren’t stunned.

Laplace’s Demon · level 11:

as an action, you can spend 8 cursed energy to enter a state of elevated intellect and foresight for 1 hour. All rolls you make are at advantage and all rolls against you are at disadvantage as you are able to see through the truth of the world in its entirety. As a reaction, you can add your Intelligence modifier to an attack roll, skill check or saving throw made by you or a creature within 5 ft of you.

Laplace Threshold · level 18:

Domain Expansion. At initiative 20, all creatures of your choice within your domain gain a cumulative −2 penalty to all rolls against you as you begin to see the state of every atom. While inside the domain: you know all the hit points, conditions, resistances, immunities, and vulnerabilities of all creatures; invisible creatures are treated as visible to you; creatures cannot gain advantage against you from being unseen or hidden.

Core law

Completed rules

Intelligence as CE ability:

keep INT as selectable CE ability for this technique; apply actual INT modifier to compatible CE formulas rather than adding it to a second ability. Overclock grants the listed AC/DEX bonus once and lasts 1 minute without invented Focus.

Action as a reaction:

immediately after another creature finishes an action, bonus action or reaction, spend an available reaction and 2 CE to take one ordinary action. Resolve the previous event first. Cannot interrupt an unresolved hit, create a turn, refresh movement, or bundle a full-turn action requiring bonus action/movement. Attack action uses real Extra Attack as written. Bonus-action spells/features remain bonus actions and cannot be relabeled. Technique-only reactions from another feature cannot pay this unrestricted action unless specifically authorized.

2000 IQ procedure:

bonus-action Intelligence (Arcana) versus the observed caster’s CE DC; treat INT score as 22 only for that check (proposal: use max(actual score, 22) to avoid penalizing Hyper-Intellect 24). Full-turn observation grants advantage and cannot be purchased with a reaction action. The source only says understand the technique, not copy it or reveal every secret vow.

Foretelling dice:

keep two long-rest foretelling dice plus at most one short-rest die; unused long-rest dice expire on the next long rest; clear the separate short-rest die then too, so total held remains at most 3. Spending a stored die replaces the whole d20 determination, not adds to it or rerolls until favorable; it remains a reaction with its own 1 CE cost. It cannot replace an enemy attack because that category is not printed. A natural 20 foretelling die is a substituted die, not a genuinely rolled Black Flash confirmation.

Overload:

keep the touch Overload save with no extra attack roll and no extra Focus.

Level-11 state:

preserve the one-hour duration and 8 CE cost. Interpret all-roll advantage as d20 attacks/saves/checks, not damage/recharge/Chaos tables. The INT-addition reaction is added after the d20 is known but before the outcome; one additive INT bonus per such use, not a second granted reaction. The source gives no extra reaction merely from the level-11 state. Preserve the one-hour level-11 duration and 8 CE cost for Maximum Output accounting.

Combat IQ:

through refining your ability, you have become more skilled in using your technique in combat and can add your Intelligence modifier to Initiative rolls. Additionally, when using Demonic Intellect, you gain the following additional benefits: you add your proficiency bonus to all saving throws you make; you gain an additional reaction each round; once per round you can add your Intelligence modifier to a damage roll you make. Keep the initiative bonus always, and the other benefits only while Demonic Intellect is active. PB-to-all-saves adds proficiency once to an otherwise unproficient save; a proficient save does not add PB again. Refresh its one extra unrestricted reaction and damage-bonus use at caster turn start; ending/restarting overclock does not refresh either. The damage addition applies once to one damage roll, not each target independently.

Hyper-Intellect:

your Intelligence score increases by 3 and you gain proficiency in either History, Investigation, Arcana, Religion, or Nature. Your Intelligence score increases by 1 again at 6th, 11th, and 18th level. Your maximum score for Intelligence is now 24. Earned milestone increases apply when learned late; no four skills or permanent cap 26.

Calculate Scenario:

you focus your mind for 1 minute and spend 3 cursed energy. You ask a single question concerning a specific goal, event, or activity to occur within 7 days. The DM offers a truthful reply. The answer doesn’t take into account any possible circumstances that might change the outcome, such as additional glimpses of the future or the loss or gain of a companion. No combat-speed casting, guaranteed detailed blueprint, or retcon.

Laplace Threshold pressure:

keep −2 cumulative per resolved initiative 20, uncapped while the Domain persists. Scope: enemy attack rolls against the caster, checks directly opposed by the caster, and saves against caster effects; not enemy damage dice or unrelated exploration rolls. Each target has its own counter. Anti-domain protection blocks a new increment and suppresses existing penalties while protected; ending this Domain clears its counters, with no carryover to the next expansion. Preserve full printed tactical knowledge of Domain occupants, not automatic knowledge of their memories or undisclosed abilities. Knowing HP does not provide line of effect through a wall.

Your overclocked mind runs firing solutions for the whole field. While your Demonic Intellect overclock is active, you can spend 1 CE as a bonus action to feed an ally you can see within 30 ft a predicted opening — they gain advantage on their next attack roll before the end of your next turn.

Prerequisite: Must have Future and Past

As an action for 2 CE, you can choose one creature you can see and predict its immediate intent — the DM tells you what the creature plans to do on its next turn, including its intended target and general action. A creature with an Intelligence of 3 or lower reveals only its target.

Rules integration

Edge cases and shared systems.

Rules priority: the reviewed rulings, explicit codex clarifications, and character reference above always control when original wording conflicts. Legacy Domain auto-hit language, action terminology, event timing, missing saves, and older scaling never override revised player rules.
Why Tier IV: off-turn actions, stored d20 replacements, and a one-hour all-advantage state make Laplace’s Demon the campaign’s strongest support engine; with two available unrestricted reactions, two action-reactions cost 4 CE and can deliver two normal Attack actions off-turn—a deliberate source-power consequence, not infinite attacks.
Temporary 2000 IQ: the overclock grants only the feat’s observation procedure while overclocked; no permanent feat or Arcana proficiency is generated.
