Core law

Completed rules

Intelligence as CE ability:

Intelligence is the technique's cursed-energy ability, the actual modifier, applied once, never doubled. keep INT as selectable CE ability for this technique; apply actual INT modifier to compatible CE formulas rather than adding it to a second ability. Overclock grants the listed AC/DEX bonus once and lasts 1 minute without invented Focus.

Action as a reaction:

The overclock's signature purchase: two energy and a reaction to take a full action off-turn, never a full-turn bundle, never a turn itself. immediately after another creature finishes an action, bonus action or reaction, spend an available reaction and 2 CE to take one ordinary action. Resolve the previous event first. Cannot interrupt an unresolved hit, create a turn, refresh movement, or bundle a full-turn action requiring bonus action/movement. Attack action uses real Extra Attack as written. Bonus-action spells/features remain bonus actions and cannot be relabeled. Technique-only reactions from another feature cannot pay this unrestricted action unless specifically authorized.

2000 IQ procedure:

The observation rite: a bonus-action Arcana check against the caster's DC, the mind treated as 22 for the attempt. bonus-action Intelligence (Arcana) versus the observed caster’s CE DC; treat INT score as 22 only for that check (proposal: use max(actual score, 22) to avoid penalizing Hyper-Intellect 24). Full-turn observation grants advantage and cannot be purchased with a reaction action. The source only says understand the technique, not copy it or reveal every secret vow.

Foretelling dice:

Two banked futures per long rest, one more from a short rest, each a reaction-spent replacement, never a reroll. keep two long-rest foretelling dice plus at most one short-rest die; unused long-rest dice expire on the next long rest; clear the separate short-rest die then too, so total held remains at most 3. Spending a stored die replaces the whole d20 determination, not adds to it or rerolls until favorable; it remains a reaction with its own 1 CE cost. It cannot replace an enemy attack because that category is not printed. A natural 20 foretelling die is a substituted die, not a genuinely rolled Black Flash confirmation.

Overload:

The overclock turned outward: a touch, an Intelligence save, and the enemy's mind stunned by its own weight. keep the touch Overload save with no extra attack roll and no extra Focus.

Combat IQ:

The firing-solution suite: initiative sharpened always, and while overclocked, saves, a spare reaction, damage, all improved. through refining your ability, you have become more skilled in using your technique in combat and can add your Intelligence modifier to Initiative rolls. Additionally, when using Demonic Intellect, you gain the following additional benefits: you add your proficiency bonus to all saving throws you make; you gain an additional reaction each round; once per round you can add your Intelligence modifier to a damage roll you make. Keep the initiative bonus always, and the other benefits only while Demonic Intellect is active. PB-to-all-saves adds proficiency once to an otherwise unproficient save; a proficient save does not add PB again. Refresh its one extra unrestricted reaction and damage-bonus use at caster turn start; ending/restarting overclock does not refresh either. The damage addition applies once to one damage roll, not each target independently.

Hyper-Intellect:

The brain acclimates: Intelligence rises by three, then by one at the milestones, capped at 24. your Intelligence score increases by 3 and you gain proficiency in either History, Investigation, Arcana, Religion, or Nature. Your Intelligence score increases by 1 again at 6th, 11th, and 18th level. Your maximum score for Intelligence is now 24. Earned milestone increases apply when learned late; no four skills or permanent cap 26.

Calculate Scenario:

One question, one minute, three energy, a truthful answer about the coming seven days, with no retcons. you focus your mind for 1 minute and spend 3 cursed energy. You ask a single question concerning a specific goal, event, or activity to occur within 7 days. The DM offers a truthful reply. The answer doesn’t take into account any possible circumstances that might change the outcome, such as additional glimpses of the future or the loss or gain of a companion. No combat-speed casting, guaranteed detailed blueprint, or retcon.

Laplace Threshold pressure:

The Domain's arithmetic: −2 per initiative 20, cumulative and uncapped, tracked per target, against the user's rolls alone. keep −2 cumulative per resolved initiative 20, uncapped while the Domain persists. Scope: enemy attack rolls against the caster, checks directly opposed by the caster, and saves against caster effects; not enemy damage dice or unrelated exploration rolls. Each target has its own counter. Anti-domain protection blocks a new increment and suppresses existing penalties while protected; ending this Domain clears its counters, with no carryover to the next expansion. Preserve full printed tactical knowledge of Domain occupants, not automatic knowledge of their memories or undisclosed abilities. Knowing HP does not provide line of effect through a wall.

Field notes. Subject: a mind that calculated its way out of dying. Three truths govern it. First, the overclock is the engine: Intelligence becomes the cursed-energy ability, and for one energy the brain runs hot for a minute, actions bought as reactions, advantage on every Intelligence check, the modifier armoring both AC and Dexterity saves. Second, the future is banked like coin: two foretold d20s per long rest, a third from a short rest, each spent as a reaction to replace a roll outright, the residue, scholars say, of the instant this brain refused to stop working. Third, the pressure compounds: the 11th-level state grants an hour of all-advantage and all-disadvantage-against, and the Domain's Threshold stacks −2 on every roll against the user, initiative 20 after initiative 20, uncapped. Mark this: it treats the future as data because it once had to solve its way through the present. It has never cooled down.

Full progression

Feature map

1Demonic Intellect.

Passive · Use Intelligence as your CE modifier · Bonus action · 1 CE: overclock for 1 min — spend 2 CE to take an action as a reaction; adv on Int checks; add Int modifier to AC and Dex saves; 2000 IQ: bonus action + Int (Arcana) vs the user's CE save DC (treat Int as 22) to understand a technique in play (adv with a full-turn observation).

The foundation: Intelligence as the cursed-energy ability, and the overclock that buys off-turn actions and armors the body with thought. You use Intelligence as your Cursed Energy modifier. As a bonus action for 1 CE, you can overclock your brain for 1 minute, gaining the following benefits: you can spend 2 CE to take an action as a reaction; you gain advantage on Intelligence checks; you add your Intelligence modifier to AC and Dexterity saving throws; and you gain the 2000 IQ observation procedure. 2000 IQ: as a bonus action while viewing a technique in play, make an Intelligence (Arcana) check to understand how it works (DC equal to the user’s Cursed Energy save DC), treating your Intelligence score as 22 for the check. If you instead spend a full-turn action (Action + Bonus Action + Movement) observing, you have advantage on the check.

3Future and Past.

Passive · After a long rest: roll two d20s and record them · Reaction · 1 CE: replace any attack roll, save, or check you make (or a save you force) with one foretelling roll (before rolling; each used once) · Short rest: spend 1 CE to gain one more foretelling roll (one short-rest roll max).

The banked futures: two d20s recorded at dawn, each spendable as a reaction to replace a roll, the technique's coldest asset. When you finish a long rest, roll two d20s and record the numbers rolled. You can spend 1 CE as a reaction to replace any attack roll, saving throw, or ability check you make, or a saving throw you force another creature to make, with one of these foretelling rolls; you must choose to do so before the roll, and each foretelling roll can be used only once. When you finish a long rest, you lose any unused foretelling rolls. When you complete a short rest, you can spend 1 CE to gain another foretelling roll; you can only have one foretelling roll from a short rest at a time.

6Overload.

Action · 4 CE · Touch a creature: Int save or 5d10 psychic + stunned to end of its next turn, half damage on success · No attack roll.

The mind weaponized against other minds: a touch that stuns, or halves on the save. As an action for 4 cursed energy, touch a creature and force it to make an Intelligence saving throw. On a failure it takes 5d10 psychic damage and is stunned until the end of its next turn; on a success it takes half as much damage and is not stunned. No attack roll is needed.

11Laplace’s Demon.

Action · 8 CE · Elevated intellect for 1 hr: all your d20 rolls at advantage, all d20 rolls against you at disadvantage · Reaction: add your Int modifier to an attack roll, check, or save made by you or a creature within 5 ft (after the d20, before the outcome; no extra reaction granted).

The state of elevated intellect: an hour of advantage on everything and disadvantage on everything against, foresight as atmosphere. As an action for 8 cursed energy, enter a state of elevated intellect and foresight for 1 hour: all your d20 rolls (attacks, saves, checks) are at advantage and all d20 rolls against you are at disadvantage, as you see through the truth of the world in its entirety. As a reaction, you can add your Intelligence modifier to an attack roll, skill check, or saving throw made by you or a creature within 5 ft of you, added after the d20 is known but before the outcome; this grants one additive INT bonus per use, not an extra reaction, and the state itself grants no extra reaction.

18Laplace Threshold.

Domain (18th level) · Initiative 20: creatures of your choice take a cumulative −2 penalty to all rolls against you · Inside: you know all creatures' HP, conditions, resistances, immunities, vulnerabilities; invisible creatures are visible to you; being unseen/hidden grants no advantage vs you.

The Domain is omniscience with a ledger: cumulative −2 pressure at initiative 20, and every secret of every occupant laid open. Domain Expansion. At initiative 20, all creatures of your choice within your domain gain a cumulative −2 penalty to all rolls against you, as you begin to see the state of every atom. While inside the domain: you know all the hit points, conditions, resistances, immunities, and vulnerabilities of all creatures; invisible creatures are treated as visible to you; creatures cannot gain advantage against you from being unseen or hidden.

Pinnacle expression

Maximum, Domain, or equivalent.

Laplace Threshold, the state of every atom, seen, and the rolls against the seer taxed without limit. Domain Expansion, Laplace Threshold: 18: at initiative 20, all creatures of your choice within your domain gain a cumulative −2 penalty to all rolls against you as you begin to see the state of every atom (uncapped while the Domain persists; scope: enemy attack rolls against you, checks directly opposed by you, saves against your effects; each target tracks its own counter). While inside the domain: you know all the hit points, conditions, resistances, immunities, and vulnerabilities of all creatures; invisible creatures are treated as visible to you; creatures cannot gain advantage against you from being unseen or hidden.

Rules integration

Edge cases and shared systems.

Rules priority: the reviewed rulings, explicit codex clarifications, and the level progression always control when original wording conflicts. Legacy Domain auto-hit language, action terminology, event timing, missing saves, and older scaling never override revised player rules.
Why Tier IV: off-turn actions, stored d20 replacements, and a one-hour all-advantage state make Laplace’s Demon the campaign’s strongest support engine; with two available unrestricted reactions, two action-reactions cost 4 CE and can deliver two normal Attack actions off-turn—a deliberate source-power consequence, not infinite attacks.
Temporary 2000 IQ: the overclock grants only the feat’s observation procedure while overclocked; no permanent feat or Arcana proficiency is generated.

Domain Expansion: Laplace Threshold

The air grows crowded with the unseen made visible: faint traceries of motion hang where bodies have been, numbers ghost across surfaces like breath on glass, and every object wears a thin halo of its own future position. It is like standing inside a calculation, vast and silent, where nothing is hidden because hiding requires something the eye cannot follow. The demon does not blink.
