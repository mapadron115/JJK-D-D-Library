Deep Purple

A Conjurer's answer to being outnumbered: smoke, given orders. The user exhales soldiers — tireless, obedient, utterly disposable — that fight, scout, carry, and screen while the conjurer hides somewhere in the fog. Deep Purple doesn't win duels; it wins battlefields, one expendable private at a time. The pipe is just for show. Mostly.

Feature map

Level-by-level features

Field notes. Subject: an army, exhaled. I have watched this technique and the word that keeps returning is muster — not summoning, not conjuring: a muster, with all the discipline the word implies. Three truths govern the parade. First, the army is breathed out: 2 CE and an exhale puts PB smoke soldiers in unoccupied spaces within sixty feet, acting on your initiative on verbal orders alone. Second, the general is indistinguishable from his men: he dissolves into smoke and re-forms beside any soldier within a hundred and twenty feet, perceives through their eyes, and can wear one’s face as his own. Third, fog is logistics, not cover: the soldiers grapple, carry the wounded, hold ropes, run ten-mile couriers, filter poison from a drowning man’s lungs. Hence the maxim: find the general — he is the one who looks like every other soldier.

L1

Smoke, Obey

The muster begins with a breath: as an action, 2 CE, exhale — and PB smoke soldiers (minimum two) appear in unoccupied spaces within sixty feet. They act on your initiative, immediately after you, obeying verbal orders freely. An army that costs a breath and answers to a word: this is the technique’s whole economy. As an action, spend 2 CE and exhale: conjure smoke soldiers — a number equal to your proficiency bonus (minimum 2) — each appearing in an unoccupied space within 60 feet of you. The soldiers act on your initiative, immediately after you, and obey your verbal orders freely (no action required). Each soldier makes melee attacks using your CE ability modifier for the attack roll, dealing 1d6 + your CE ability modifier bludgeoning damage on a hit.

They don't need to win. They need to be there.

L3

Eyes in the Fog

One soldier becomes your eyes: bonus action, 1 CE, designate a soldier within a hundred and twenty feet — and perceive through it, seeing and hearing, while your own body goes blind and deaf. The general doesn’t need to be at the front. The front can be anywhere the fog is. One of your soldiers becomes your eyes. As a bonus action, spend 1 CE and designate a soldier within 120 feet: until you dismiss it, you perceive through that soldier — seeing and hearing what it does — while your own body is blinded and deafened. The soldier still fights and moves on your orders. The general doesn't need to be at the front.

L6

The Ranks Swell

The muster grows: the standing army becomes 5 × your character level, each conjuring still 2 CE per PB-sized batch. The breath is the same; the lungs are larger. The muster grows. The total number of smoke soldiers you can maintain at once becomes 5 × your character level; each conjuring still costs 2 CE per proficiency-bonus-sized batch.

Screen. When you are targeted by an attack you can see, you may spend your reaction and 1 CE: a soldier within 5 feet of you interposes itself — the attack targets the soldier instead, and the soldier dissipates. That's what they're for.

L11

Hands of Smoke

Your soldiers learn hands, not just fists: grapple and shove at your CE save DC, carry the wounded, open doors, hold ropes. An army is logistics wearing a uniform — and this one now does the army’s real work. Your soldiers learn hands, not just fists. They can grapple and shove (using your CE save DC for the contested check), carry — two soldiers together can lift and move a Medium or smaller creature at 20 feet per round — and perform simple tasks: open unlocked doors, drag the wounded clear, hold a rope, carry a stretcher. An army is logistics wearing a uniform.

L18

The General Vanishes

Bonus action, 3 CE: dissolve into smoke and re-form adjacent to any soldier within a hundred and twenty feet, bringing only what you wear and carry. Anyone watching sees only the fog shift. The general’s oldest trick — be everywhere the army is, and nowhere in particular. As a bonus action, spend 3 CE: you dissolve into smoke and re-form in any unoccupied space adjacent to one of your soldiers within 120 feet of you. You can bring nothing but what you wear and carry. Anyone watching sees only the fog shift. Find the general. He's the one that looks like every other soldier.

L20

The Fog Itself

Once per long rest, for one minute: become living smoke. Attacks and single-target effects cannot target you; pass through inch-wide gaps; resist nonmagical bludgeoning, piercing, and slashing. You cannot attack or spend CE except through your soldiers. The general abdicates the battlefield to his men — and becomes untouchable in the exchange. Once per long rest, as a bonus action: for 1 minute, you become living smoke. Attacks and single-target effects cannot target you; you can pass through gaps as narrow as 1 inch; you have resistance to bludgeoning, piercing, and slashing damage from nonmagical sources. You cannot attack or spend CE except through your soldiers. You may end the effect early as a bonus action. There is no general anymore. There's weather.

Pinnacle expression

Maximum and Domain.

Capstone material

Maximum: The Smoking Parade

The Maximum is the whole army at once: 5 CE, one minute, soldiers equal to 10 × your level appearing anywhere within a hundred and twenty feet, striking as one — every hostile within ten feet of the formation suffers at the start of your turns. A parade is not a maneuver. It is an announcement. As an action, spend 5 CE: for 1 minute, you conjure your full muster at once — soldiers equal to 10 × your character level, appearing in unoccupied spaces anywhere within 120 feet, at no further CE cost. They share your initiative and strike as one: at the start of each of your turns, every hostile creature within 10 feet of at least three of your soldiers takes 6d8 bludgeoning damage (Dexterity saving throw against your CE save DC for half). When the minute ends, every soldier dissipates and you gain one level of exhaustion — the pipe burns the lungs that feed it. The whole parade, all at once, and damn the cost.

Domain Expansion: The Barracks of Fog

The sure-hit Domain, and the sure-hit is the muster: full-turn activation, 20 CE, 800 clash, five-round burnout, two minutes, a hundred-and-twenty-foot radius. The Barracks of Fog does not summon an army into a Domain — the Domain is the barracks, and the muster never ends inside it. 18th level. Sure-hit domain — the sure-hit is the muster. Activation: full turn, 20 CE (D008 baseline for a normal Domain). Clash: 800 points; burnout 5 rounds; duration 2 min (Tier IV mapping); radius 120 ft.

Nowhere to Stand. The domain is thick with your soldiers — they are already everywhere. Every hostile creature inside except you is swarmed: at the start of each of your turns, each takes 4d8 bludgeoning damage (Dexterity saving throw against your CE save DC for half). Additionally, all creatures inside except you are blinded beyond 10 feet by smoke. Your soldiers within the domain do not count against your maintained total. You brought an army to a duel.

Feats

Five feats, drilled.

Deep Purple teaches five feats — five drills your smoke has rehearsed until they're reflex. Each is optional, and you must meet its listed prerequisite before taking it.

Prerequisite: Smoke, Obey

False General

Shape one soldier into your exact likeness — face, voice, mannerisms — indistinguishable without close inspection against your CE save DC. It still dissipates when struck. The decoy’s only armor is being believed. Shape one soldier into your exact likeness — face, voice, mannerisms. It mimics you well enough that no one can distinguish it from you without close inspection (an Investigation check against your CE save DC). It still dissipates when struck.

Prerequisite: Eyes in the Fog

Dead Drop

Detail one soldier as a courier: it carries a message or small object up to ten miles over twenty-four hours, traveling as drifting smoke, interceptable only by effects that target smoke, fog, or aura. The army’s postal service runs on weather. Detail one soldier as a courier: it can carry a written message or a small object up to 10 miles over 24 hours, traveling as drifting smoke. It cannot be intercepted except by effects that specifically target smoke, fog, or aura.

Prerequisite: Smoke, Obey

Secondhand Lungs

Shape a soldier into a breathing mask over a willing creature’s face: for one hour, immunity to inhaled poisons and gases, and underwater breathing — the soldier filters everything. The fog that chokes armies can also keep one man alive. Shape a soldier into a breathing mask fitted over a willing creature's face. For 1 hour, that creature is immune to inhaled poisons and gases and can breathe underwater — the soldier filters everything. The mask dissipates if struck.

Prerequisite: The Ranks Swell

Fog of War

As an action, 2 CE: the soldiers exhale a sixty-foot cube of heavy smoke, lasting a minute — heavily obscured, proof even against magical sight, dispersed only by strong wind. The fog of war, issued on demand. As an action, spend 2 CE: your soldiers exhale a 60-foot cube of heavy smoke that lasts 1 minute. The area is heavily obscured, and not even magical sight can pierce it. A strong wind disperses it early.

Prerequisite: The General Vanishes

The Last Soldier

Once per long rest: at 0 hit points, dissolve instead of falling — re-form adjacent to your most distant soldier within three hundred feet at 1 hit point. The fog always keeps one private in reserve. The general’s last order is to himself: survive. Once per long rest, when you drop to 0 hit points, you may dissolve instead of falling: you re-form adjacent to your most distant soldier within 300 feet at 1 hit point. The fog always keeps one private in reserve.

Rules integration

Edge cases and shared systems.

Campaign canon notes

Cursed Energy baseline: CE pool is based on class; CE ability is Charisma or Wisdom (player's choice). CE save DC = 8 + CE ability modifier + proficiency bonus. Regain all spent CE on a long rest.
Smoke soldier statistics: AC 13, 1 hit point (any damage dissipates them), speed 30 ft, immune to poison and psychic damage. They cannot be healed. They dissipate if you fall unconscious or finish a long rest.
Command economy: verbal orders are free; soldiers act on your initiative immediately after you. Complex conditional orders ("guard the door unless she speaks the word") are one standing order per soldier.
No soldier chains: smoke soldiers cannot conjure, command, or become other soldiers. The muster answers to you alone.
Eyes in the Fog: while perceiving through a soldier you are blinded and deafened to your own senses; damage to your body still breaks your concentration on nothing — the link is not concentration, but you cannot maintain it while unconscious.
Domain activation defaults: rendered as full turn + 20 CE per the D008 normal-Domain baseline, with Tier IV clash stats (800 / 5-round burnout / 2 min) and a 120-ft radius.
Maximum vs. Reversal: Deep Purple's pinnacle is written as a Maximum Technique (The Smoking Parade), not a Reversal; it occupies the technique's pinnacle slot.
