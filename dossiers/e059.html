Deep Purple
Smoke Army Technique

Exhale cursed smoke into ropes, rafts, decoys, soldiers, sensory screens, and an almost unbreakable prison. The strength is not one attack—it is never running out of answers.

Power tierTier IV · Special Grade
Combat roleBattlefield sculptor / swarm commander
Domain / pinnacleNone — Smoky Jail & 216 Battalion
Core law

What the technique actually does.

Rules are written to plug into the finalized campaign glossary: normal actions stay normal, direct attacks roll, and only named Domain events bypass the initial roll.

Equipment condition

Pipe Focus

You need a prepared pipe or equivalent focus to create new smoke. Losing it does not erase existing smoke. A replacement can be prepared during a short rest.

Resource on battlefield

Smoke Unit

Action or bonus action, 1 CE: create 5 Smoke Units in spaces within 30 feet. One Unit fills one 5-foot cube or forms one Small construct. Units last 1 hour and can be reclaimed when dismissed.

Bonus action

Shape Smoke

Reshape up to PB Smoke Units within range into obscuring cloud, rope/bridge, raft, wall, decoy silhouette, or other noncomplex object. Durability equals 5 HP per Unit, AC 12+PB.

Passive

Smoke Sense

You sense creatures disturbing your smoke within range, gaining rough location even when the cloud obscures sight.

Full progression

Level 1 through 20.

Milestones use the campaign’s familiar 1 / 3 / 6 / 11 / 18 structure and add an explicit level-20 mastery feature.

1Smoke on the Water

Smoke Units

Gain Pipe Focus, Smoke Units, Shape Smoke, and Smoke Sense.

Smoke Screen

A cube filled with at least one Unit is heavily obscured to creatures that cannot sense through your smoke; wind/area effects can disperse exposed smoke as adjudicated.

Scaling

Smoke Die d6; range 60 feet. Smoke Unit limit: 10.

3Deep Purple Constructs

Living Decoy

2 Smoke Units: create a humanoid decoy that can speak scripted phrases, move 30 feet on your bonus action, and make one harmless feint. Investigation/CE check vs your DC reveals it on close inspection or physical contact.

Smoke Rope

1 Unit per 20 feet: create rope/bridge able to support 500 pounds per Unit.

Color of Smoke

Make smoke appear like mundane fog, dust, grass, clothing, or a known person’s surface colors; physical contact still reveals smoke.

Scaling

Smoke Die remains d6; range extends to 90 feet. Smoke Unit limit: 20.

6Smoke Troopers

Trooper Core

Spend 2 Units + 1 CE to create one Trooper, cap PB×5. AC 12+PB, HP 4×level, speed 30. It acts after you under simple preset orders and makes one attack for 1 Smoke Die + PB force.

Quality Over Quantity

Five Troopers may fuse into one Elite Trooper: HP equal sum, attack deals PB Smoke Dice + CE modifier, and it can perform Help, grapple, or shove with your CE modifier.

Battlefield Orders

One bonus action commands all Troopers; different complex orders require your action.

Scaling

Smoke Die becomes d8; range extends to 120 feet. Smoke Unit limit: PB×10.

11Smoky Jail

Smoky Jail

Action · 8 CE + 20 Units · Focus: create up to 30-foot-radius smoke barrier for 1 minute. Barrier AC = CE DC, HP 20×PB, resistance to non-CE damage. Creatures cannot pass its surface without destroying/suppressing it or becoming microscopic/insubstantial enough to fit between particles.

Smoke Clone

10 Units: create one convincing duplicate with Elite Trooper stats. It may mimic your voice and weapon motions but cannot use your CT.

Deep Purple Recycle

When a construct ends intact, recover all its Units; destroyed constructs return half, round down.

Scaling

Smoke Die becomes d10; range extends to 180 feet.

18Deep Purple: 216

Maximum: Purple Haze Battalion

Action · 10 CE + at least 40 Units: for 1 minute abstract your army into up to six Trooper Swarms rather than hundreds of turns. Each swarm occupies 20-foot square, HP 10×level, and on its turn forces Dex save for 6 Smoke Dice force/bludgeoning, half on success. You can split/merge swarms as bonus action.

Atmospheric Control

Shape Smoke can affect any amount up to half your total active Units with one bonus action.

No Domain

Deep Purple already builds its own battlefield and prison; it has no innate Domain.

Scaling

Smoke Die becomes d12; range extends to 300 feet.

20Sea Hunter’s Atmosphere

Perfect Recycle

Destroyed constructs return all but one-quarter of their Units, round down.

Smoke Everywhere

Once per long rest, action: create 50 Smoke Units without CE as you empty your lungs completely. Gain one exhaustion level afterward.

Remote Command

Troopers with preset orders continue anywhere on the same plane for 1 hour; direct commands still require them within 1 mile.

Scaling

Smoke Die remains d12; range extends to a 1-mile network.

Pinnacle expression

Domain, Maximum, or equivalent.

Not every technique needs a Domain. When one exists, its sure-hit is a unique autonomous initiative-count-20 event unless the Domain is explicitly rule-based.

No natural Domain Deep Purple’s equivalent high-level battlefield control is Smoky Jail plus the 216-soldier Maximum. It can still learn generic barriers through class features.

Innate Technique Feats

Four build-defining options.

These broaden the law instead of merely inflating the same number.

Prerequisite: Deep Purple

Lung of the Sea

You can hold your breath for 30 minutes and have advantage on saves against inhaled poisons/gases.

Prerequisite: Living Decoy

Purple In

You may hide the CE signature of up to PB smoke constructs at once; active CE Perception vs your DC can reveal them.

Prerequisite: Smoke Troopers

Trooper Doctrine

Choose one preset doctrine after long rest: Guard (+1 AC), Pursue (+10 speed), or Rescue (can drag willing/unconscious allies without speed penalty). All Troopers use it.

Prerequisite: Smoky Jail

Jailhouse Rock

Your jail can roll. When you create Smoky Jail, you may anchor it to yourself so it moves with you, keeping its radius centered on you. A rolling jail’s barrier HP is reduced by 5 × PB (minimum 10) — mobility has a price in smoke.

Rules integration

Edge cases and shared systems.

These rulings are designed around the revised Player Guide rather than the raw PDF where the two differ.

Smoke Units are physical technique manifestations, not CE points; spending CE creates them, dismissing recovers the units but not CE.
Trooper swarms exist to protect table action economy; do not roll 216 individual turns.
Smoke Sense gives location, not automatic sight or identity.
Smoky Jail is not a Domain and does not cause Technique Burnout when it ends.
Technique DC: 8 + proficiency bonus + your chosen Cursed Energy ability modifier (Wisdom or Charisma unless your class says otherwise).
Direct criticals: rolled weapon, unarmed, and CE attacks can critical normally. After a qualifying critical, roll the campaign's unmodified Black Flash confirmation d20; a natural 20 triples the completed critical damage. Autonomous Domain events, damage-over-time, hazards, and unrolled pulses cannot Black Flash.
Maximum Output: unless a feature says otherwise, double that feature's base CE cost and enhance one supported axis—damage, range, duration, or accuracy/DC—using the campaign's Maximum Output rules. Named Maximum Techniques and Domains do not receive generic Maximum Output.
Technique Focus: effects marked Focus occupy the shared Technique Focus slot. Opening a Domain ends other Focus effects unless the Domain expressly preserves them.
Two-technique characters: these guides assume each technique progresses from character/class level as agreed by the campaign. Multiclassing itself does not grant another Innate Technique.
