Curse-Imbued

This is any weapon that has been imbued with a curse, allowing it to damage curses or shikigami. You gain a +2 bonus to attack and damage rolls with this weapon.

Note: Unlike ordinary weapons, a cursed weapon can harm cursed spirits and shikigami that would otherwise be immune or resistant to non-cursed damage.
