Core law

At-the-table character reference

Recovered printed values & campaign clarifications

Flames of Disaster:

bonus action, 1 CE; 15-foot cone; Dexterity save, half damage on success. Damage is 1d8 fire at levels 1–4, 2d8 at 5–10, 3d8 at 11–16, and 4d8 at 17–20.

Maximum Meteor:

action, 10 CE; mark the area, then resolve the attack at the start of your next turn. Creatures in the 120-foot-radius impact area make a Dexterity save: 10d4 bludgeoning + 10d6 fire damage, or half on success. Incapacitation before impact cancels the meteor and expends the CE.

Core law

Completed rules

Flames of Disaster is a bonus action and forces a Dexterity save for 1d8 fire at level 1, 2d8 at 5, 3d8 at 11, and 4d8 at 17; a successful save halves the damage. Ignited unattended objects burn normally; it does not automatically ignite creatures or worn objects.

Volcanoes:

create from one up to CE modifier (minimum one), paying 2 CE for each. Each is a 5-foot-diameter object in an unoccupied ground space within 120 feet, with AC 14, HP equal to five times your level, immunity to fire and poison, and a 1-minute duration using Technique Focus.

One bonus action commands any number of your volcanoes to erupt simultaneously. Choose a different 30-by-10-foot line for each and roll damage once for the activation. A creature in overlapping lines saves only once and takes damage once. On failure it is restrained by magma and repeats a Strength save at the end of each turn. The line remains difficult terrain until the volcano ends.

Ember Insects:

use the appendix swarm. It appears within 5 feet, acts after you, and lasts 1 minute. A bonus action commands it; otherwise it Dodges. Only one swarm can exist at a time.

Pinnacle expression

Maximum, Domain, or equivalent.

Maximum Meteor: 11 — Maximum: Meteor. the activation action pays 10 CE, lifts you up to 60 feet without provoking, and marks the 120-foot-radius impact area. You are restrained in the air while charging. At the start of your next turn, descend to an open point in the area and resolve a Dexterity save for 10d4 bludgeoning plus 10d6 fire damage, or half on success. If incapacitated before then, the materials disperse and the CE is lost.

Domain — Coffin Furnace: 18 — Coffin of the Iron Mountain. at initiative 20 the iron mountain closes its coffin: black iron seals around each chosen hostile as magma erupts within — each automatically takes 10d10 fire damage and is restrained, entombed, until the next initiative 20. While entombed, the target cannot see outside its coffin. A protected creature makes a Dexterity save, taking half damage and avoiding the coffin on a success. Fire immunity functions normally; ordinary flames, volcanoes, insects, and Meteor retain their rolls.

Innate Technique Feats

Build-defining options.

These are optional character-building feats. You must meet each listed prerequisite before selecting it.

Prerequisite: Flames of Disaster

Pyrokinesis

Repeatable; choose a different option each time: fire resistance, or two fire-damaging spells of level 1–4. Cast chosen spells with CE ability and CE equal to spell level; cantrips, if allowed, cost 1 CE. No spell may be selected twice.

Prerequisite: CE score 16+

Shooting Star

Bonus action, spend 1–PB CE to gain a 60-foot flying speed for that many rounds. The flight ends if incapacitated; if it ends aloft, normal falling rules apply.

Prerequisite: Maximum: Meteor

Hellscape

Action, 15 CE, Technique Focus for 1 minute: unattended nonmagical objects and ground in a 100-foot radius melt into lava. It is difficult terrain; entering or ending a turn there causes a Constitution save, 4d6 fire on failure, half on success, once per turn. Bonus action moves one 10-foot lava square up to 20 feet along a surface.

Rules integration

Edge cases and shared systems.

Rules priority: the reviewed rulings, explicit codex clarifications, and character reference above always control when original wording conflicts. Legacy Domain auto-hit language, action terminology, event timing, missing saves, and older scaling never override revised player rules.
Why Tier II: large numbers and a vast Meteor are visible encounter threats, but the technique remains a finite package of damage, terrain, and one defined summon.

DisasterFlames
You are born with a great power, with nigh-endless possibilities,
fire manipulation, allowing you to burn down everything in your way.
Cursed Energy
Starting at 1st level, you can harness your negative feelings into a
usable source of energy, called cursed energy. These feelings can be
harnessed into attacks or abilities. The number of cursed energy you
possess is based on your class, some classes use a modifier that
being charisma or wisdom (Player’s choice)
Your cursed energy DC is 8 + user cursed energy ability modifier +
proficiency bonus.
You regain all spent cursed energy on a long rest.
Flames of Disaster
Starting at 1st level, you have learned the basics of fire
manipulation, being able to launch a wave of flames from your hands
as well as the ability to cast Control Flames. As a bonus action for
1 Cursed Energy, you unleash a 15-foot cone which forces people to
make a Dexterity save. On a failure, they take 1d8 fire damage or
half as much damage on a success. The fire ignites any flammable
objects in the area that aren’t being worn or carried. Your damage
dice increases to 2d8 at 5th level, 3d8 at 11th level, and 4d8 at
17th level.

occupying a 5 ft. radius circle. As a bonus action, you may force

Ember Insects
At 6th level, you have learned how to create small insect shikigami
from your body. As an action for 5 Cursed Energy, you may create 1
Ember Insects Swarm 5ft. around you. These shikigami act immediately
after your turn.
Maximum: Meteor
When you reach 11th level, you have learned how to extract the
maximum out of your Disaster Flames, creating your maximum technique.
As an action for 10 Cursed Energy, you may fly up high in the air
gathering materials into a single gigantic meteor. In the following
turn as you reach the ground. Every creature except you since you
don't take the damage, in a 120 feet radius from you must attempt a
Dexterity saving throw. On a failure, they take 10d4 bludgeoning
damage + 10d6 fire damage. On a success, they take half the damage.
