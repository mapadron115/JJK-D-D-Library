Disaster Flames

Reliable fire waves, placed volcanoes, explosive shikigami, and a conspicuous Maximum Meteor.

Field notes. Subject: dread made geographic, a sorcerer whose energy remembers every catastrophe it ever feared. Three truths govern the arsonist of terrain. First, nothing here is created from nothing: the volcanoes are eruptions the energy remembers, 2 CE each, up to the CE modifier, each commanding a 30-by-10-foot line of 5d10 fire and magma restraint on one bonus action. Second, the disasters escalate: a pocket cone at will, the ember-insect swarm acting alone, and the largest memory the technique admits, Maximum Meteor: 10 CE, the sorcerer lifted 60 feet and restrained mid-air, a 120-foot-radius descent of 10d4 bludgeoning plus 10d6 fire. Third, the Domain buries: the Coffin of the Iron Mountain seals black iron around each chosen hostile at every initiative 20: 10d10 fire, restrained and entombed, blind inside its coffin. It does not burn its guests. It buries them, burning.

Feature map

1Flames of Disaster.

Flames of Disaster

Bonus action · 1 CE · 15-foot cone · Dexterity save: 1d8 fire damage (2d8 at 5th, 3d8 at 11th, 4d8 at 17th); half on success · Ignited unattended objects burn; creatures and worn objects are not automatically ignited.

The smallest catastrophe, on demand: a bonus action, 1 CE, a 15-foot cone, 1d8 fire, rising to 4d8 by 17th level. The sorcerer carries a disaster in his pocket and spends it like small change. As a bonus action for 1 CE, unleash a 15-foot cone of flame. Each creature makes a Dexterity save: 1d8 fire damage on failure, half on success. Damage is 1d8 at levels 1–4, 2d8 at 5–10, 3d8 at 11–16, and 4d8 at 17–20. Ignited unattended objects burn normally; it does not automatically ignite creatures or worn objects.

3Erupting Volcanoes.

Create volcanoes

Action · 2 CE each · Create 1 to CE modifier (minimum 1) volcanoes in unoccupied ground spaces within 120 feet · Each is a 5-foot-diameter object, AC 14, HP 5 × level, immune to fire and poison · 1 minute, uses Technique Focus.

The remembered eruptions, placed: up to CE-modifier volcanoes, 2 CE each, each commanding a 30-by-10-foot line of 5d10 fire and magma restraint on one bonus action. The ground gives way on schedule. As an action, create from one up to CE modifier (minimum one) volcanoes, paying 2 CE each. Each is a 5-foot-diameter object in an unoccupied ground space within 120 feet, with AC 14, HP equal to five times your level, immunity to fire and poison, and a 1-minute duration using Technique Focus.

Erupt

Bonus action · 0 CE · Command any number of your volcanoes to erupt, each targeting a different 30-by-10-foot line · Each creature in a line: Dexterity save, 5d10 fire damage + restrained by magma for 1 minute (Strength save at end of each turn to break); half damage and no restraint on success · Overlapping lines: one save, one damage roll · Lines remain difficult terrain until the volcano ends.

One bonus action commands any number of your volcanoes to erupt simultaneously. Choose a different 30-by-10-foot line for each and roll damage once for the activation: each creature in a line makes a Dexterity save, taking 5d10 fire damage and becoming restrained by magma for 1 minute on failure, or half damage and no restraint on success. A restrained creature repeats a Strength save at the end of each turn. A creature in overlapping lines saves only once and takes damage once. The line remains difficult terrain until the volcano ends.

6Ember Insects.

Ember Insects

Action · 5 CE · Create one ember-insect swarm (appendix profile) within 5 feet · Acts after you, lasts 1 minute · Bonus action to command it; otherwise it Dodges · Only one swarm at a time.

The catastrophe, given wings: a 5-CE swarm that acts after the sorcerer and Dodges unless commanded. Disaster, delegated. As an action for 5 CE, create one ember-insect swarm within 5 feet (use the appendix swarm profile). It acts after you and lasts 1 minute. A bonus action commands it; otherwise it Dodges. Only one swarm can exist at a time.

Pinnacle expression

Maximum, Domain, or equivalent.

Maximum Meteor: The largest memory the technique will admit to: 10 CE, the sorcerer lifted 60 feet and restrained in the air while charging, then a 120-foot-radius descent, 10d4 bludgeoning plus 10d6 fire, Dexterity save for half. Conspicuous by design: the sky is the announcement. 11: Maximum: Meteor. As an action, the activation pays 10 CE, lifts you up to 60 feet without provoking, and marks the 120-foot-radius impact area. You are restrained in the air while charging. At the start of your next turn, descend to an open point in the area and resolve a Dexterity save for 10d4 bludgeoning plus 10d6 fire damage, or half on success. If incapacitated before then, the materials disperse and the CE is lost.

Domain, Coffin Furnace:

An iron mountain rises on all sides, black metal veined with magma, and the sky is replaced by a lid of smoke and ember. The heat does not come in waves but as a constant pressure, like being held underwater in a furnace, and the ground pulses with a slow, volcanic heartbeat. It is a coffin in the most literal sense, a box built to contain fire and whatever the fire is eating.

The Coffin of the Iron Mountain: at every initiative count 20, black iron seals around each chosen hostile as magma erupts within, 10d10 fire, restrained and entombed until the next initiative 20, blind inside its coffin. The Domain does not burn its guests. It buries them, burning. 18: Coffin of the Iron Mountain. at initiative 20 the iron mountain closes its coffin: black iron seals around each chosen hostile as magma erupts within, each automatically takes 10d10 fire damage and is restrained, entombed, until the next initiative 20. While entombed, the target cannot see outside its coffin. A protected creature makes a Dexterity save, taking half damage and avoiding the coffin on a success. Fire immunity functions normally; ordinary flames, volcanoes, insects, and Meteor retain their rolls.

Innate Technique Feats

Build-defining options.

These are optional character-building feats. You must meet each listed prerequisite before selecting it.

Prerequisite: Flames of Disaster

Ashfall

The cone’s damage, snuffed and traded for a minute of choking ash, the area heavily obscured. Fire, spent as shadow. When you activate Flames of Disaster, you can snuff its damage to fill the cone's area with choking ash for 1 minute. The area is heavily obscured.

Prerequisite: Erupting Volcanoes

Volcanic Launch

A volcano erupts beneath the sorcerer himself, 2 CE, a bonus action, and he is launched up to 60 feet to safety. The disaster, ridden. As a bonus action, spend 2 CE to have one of your volcanoes within 30 feet erupt directly beneath you instead of along its line. You are launched up to 60 feet to an unoccupied space you can see and land safely without taking falling damage; the eruption still deals its normal damage along one 30-by-10-foot line of your choice originating from the volcano.

Prerequisite: Maximum: Meteor

Hellscape

15 CE and a minute of Focus: the ground in a 100-foot radius melts into lava, difficult terrain, 4d6 fire for entering or lingering. The battlefield, rezoned as a furnace. Action, 15 CE, Technique Focus for 1 minute: unattended nonmagical objects and ground in a 100-foot radius melt into lava. It is difficult terrain; entering or ending a turn there causes a Constitution save, 4d6 fire on failure, half on success, once per turn. Bonus action moves one 10-foot lava square up to 20 feet along a surface.

Prerequisites: Must have Flames of Disaster

Pyrokinesis

Training finds new applications for the old flame, resistance, reach, or refinement, taken more than once. The catastrophe, studied. Through training and understanding, you have found new applications to your flames. You gain the one of the following (This feat can be taken more than once): ●​ Resistance to Fire Damage and when you roll damage for a technique that deals fire damage, you can reroll any 1s, but must take the new result, even if the new roll is a 1. ●​ Two spells below 5th level dealing fire damage. The ce cost equals the spell’s level.

Rules integration

Edge cases and shared systems.

Rules priority: the reviewed rulings, explicit codex clarifications, and the level progression always control when original wording conflicts. Legacy Domain auto-hit language, action terminology, event timing, missing saves, and older scaling never override revised player rules.
Why Tier II: large numbers and a vast Meteor are visible encounter threats, but the technique remains a finite package of damage, terrain, and one defined summon.
