Recovered printed values & campaign clarifications

At-the-table character reference

Special Grade creation: voluntarily constructing a Special Grade cursed tool kills the creator immediately after the item is completed. Under the original Project wording, that death cannot be undone by ordinary Reverse Cursed Technique or any revival effect; the revised campaign ruling does not soften that explicit consequence.

Feature map

  • 1 — Construct: mundane matter.
  • 3 — Cursed Creation: graded items.

Completed rules

  • Mundane construction: as a bonus action, spend 1 to PB CE to create a nonmagical object you understand in an open space within 10 feet. Each CE creates up to 10 pounds and 1 cubic foot of matter. It is real and permanent. A complex object requires a blueprint, tool proficiency, or a studied example; no object can contain energy, active chemicals, information, or living tissue that you do not understand.
  • “Two properties” combines two understood mundane item designs into one object at double total CE and mass. Choose one base damage die and combine compatible weapon properties; if properties conflict—such as light and heavy—choose one. Created currency and raw valuables are recognizable as constructed matter under CE detection, but remain physically real—the economic consequences belong in the fiction, not a hidden ban.
  • Cursed or magic items: name the exact published or campaign item before creating it. The GM confirms its grade and whether its lore can exist in the campaign. It lasts for CE modifier hours, requires normal attunement, cannot supply wishes or permanent ability increases, and vanishes without leaving saleable components. Creating Special Grade still has the printed fatal consequence and cannot be undone by ordinary RCT.
  • Liquid Metal: the additional CE is chosen when manifested and cannot be raised later. It lasts 1 minute, uses Technique Focus, and can be dismissed with no action. Its reach applies to melee attacks made with it, not every attack you possess.
  • Insectoid Armor: lasts 1 minute, uses Technique Focus, and ends if you are incapacitated. Ability-score increases can exceed the usual maximum only by the printed +4. Its extra Dash is a once-per-turn no-action Dash; the critical bonus applies only to unarmed strikes.
  • Domain — Threefold Affliction: items created inside require no CE, but their attacks roll normally. At initiative 20 a Domain-created Perfect Sphere manifests in contact with each chosen hostile, automatically dealing 5d12 force damage, then vanishes. A protected creature makes a Dexterity save for half. User-created Special Grade items retain their fatal price.

Power identity

Why Tier IV: even a modest user can solve material, equipment, and access problems outside combat. The tier acknowledges narrative reach and adjudication load while preserving the lore’s frightening endpoint.

Recovered character-build options

Innate Technique Feats 3

These are optional character-building feats. You must meet each listed prerequisite before selecting it.

Subtle Constructs

Standard

Prerequisite: Construct

After creating an item, make Dexterity (Sleight of Hand) against each observer’s passive Perception. Against a creature you fool, your first attack or check using that item before end of next turn has advantage. The creation itself remains subject to line of sight and obvious physical evidence.

Imbue

Pinnacle

Prerequisite: Cursed Creation

When using Cursed Creation to construct a cursed tool, pay twice that tool’s normal CE cost to imbue the creation with the tool’s Technique for 7 days. There is no added one-item cap. The created tool uses the normal attunement rules and your CE attack/DC when its technique needs them; Creating a Special Grade tool still kills the creator afterward and cannot be reversed or revived.

Maximum: Perfect Sphere

Pinnacle

Prerequisite: Insectoid Armor and CE score 20

Action, 10 CE: create one 5-foot-radius sphere within 10 feet for 1 minute with Technique Focus. It is indestructible while maintained. Ending a turn adjacent or being rammed forces one Dexterity save per turn for 5d12 force, half on success. Bonus action moves it 30 feet; it destroys barriers and unattended nonmagical structures in its path.

Construction
Construction, as the name implies, allows the user to conjure objects
from nothing.
Cursed Energy
Starting at 1st level, you can harness your negative feelings into a
usable source of energy, called cursed energy. These feelings can be
harnessed into attacks or abilities. The number of cursed energy you
possess is based on your class, some classes use a modifier that
being charisma or wisdom (Player’s choice)
Your cursed energy DC is 8 + user cursed energy ability modifier +
proficiency bonus.
You regain all spent cursed energy on a long rest.
Construct
Beginning at 1st, you can create any kind of object you understand
with your technique, although without much efficiency. As a bonus
action for 1 cursed energy, you can create one non-magical object you
have a basic understanding and information of its properties.
You may also spend twice the cursed energy in this technique to
create one object with two properties. (Ex: You create a greatsword
mixed in with a spear, it would have both the Heavy, two-handed, and
the Versatile and thrown (20/60) properties. When mixing a light and
heavy weapon, choose which one of the properties it will maintain.)
Cursed Creation
At 3rd level, your construction technique has improved by a lot, you
may now even create cursed or magical objects. You can create a
Cursed Tool for 3 cursed energy + 1
for every grade it has above
common(cannot go higher than a Grade 1
weapon). These created cursed tools
will only last for a number of hours
equal to your Cursed Energy modifier,
vanishing right after the time passes.
If you so desire, you can make a
Special Grade(Legendary) Cursed Tool.
However, due to the backlash of your
technique, you will die afterward. You
cannot be revived in any way shape or
form after using this feature.
Liquid Metal
At 6th level, you have learned how to create liquid metal, a
substance with fluid form and changeable volume but stable physical
properties due to semi-autonomous cursed energy. As part of creating
a weapon with your construction, you may spend up to an additional 4
cursed energy to make them become fluid with volume. This allows you
to extend the range of melee weapons 10ft per cursed energy.
In addition, whenever you attack with them in this increased range
form their damage die tier will increase equal to the cursed energy
used. (1d8>1d10->1d12->2d6->2d8)
Insectoid Armor
At 11th level, you realize you need a way to efficiently use your
cursed energy despite being an inefficient technique. You have
learned how to create armor that simulates the efficiency of an
insect's bug body. As a bonus action for 10 cursed energy, you can
surround yourself in this armor and gain the following benefits:
● Your Strength, Dexterity, and Armor Class scores increase by
+4, this is the result of the efficiency of the bugs.
● You will gain resistance to non-magical bludgeoning, piercing
and slashing damage. This evolves to immunity at level 20.
● Wings will sprout from your back, making you gain a flying
speed equal to your walking speed. In addition, you can now use
your free action to dash 1/turn.
● Your unarmed strikes will deal two additional damage dice. In
addition, your unarmed strikes roll one additional damage dice
on a critical.
Domain Expansion: Threefold Affliction
Finally at 20th level, you have refined
your technique to the maximum point, now
gaining your domain expansion. While
inside your domain, you can now create any
of your items by spending no cursed
energy. All of these items will deal one
additional damage dice and attacks made
with them cannot miss. However, these
weapons will only last until the end of
the domain.
