Construction

Permanent matter creation, temporary cursed items, liquid metal, and a devastating insect armor.

Field notes. Subject: a builder. Her cursed energy remembers with perfect fidelity, every object she has ever understood, kept like a seal in wax, and in moments of resolve, it insists the memory is real. Classify this correctly: she does not conjure. She recalls, and the world agrees. Three truths govern her. First, understanding is the arsenal: what the mind holds clearly, the hand can build; what it holds vaguely, it cannot. Second, permanence is a matter of conviction, mundane things endure because she believes them utterly; borrowed legends fade because even she knows they are guests. Third, the final truth, written in the oldest ink: to build a Special Grade tool is to die in the making. Some convictions are fatal. The master knows which temples not to raise.

Full progression

Feature map

1Construct:

Mundane construction

Bonus action · Spend 1 to PB CE · Create a nonmagical object you understand in an open space within 10 ft: up to 10 lb and 1 cubic ft per CE, real and permanent · Complex objects require a blueprint, tool proficiency, or studied example · Cannot contain energy, active chemicals, information, or living tissue you do not understand.

As a breath, she spends a mote of energy, and what she understands stands in the world, real, permanent, true. Ten pounds per coin, a cubic foot per coin: the honest exchange rate of creation. But mark the law: nothing she does not understand, no hidden energy, no strange chemistry, no secrets, no living flesh. The mind is the mold; the mold cannot cast what it has never held. As a bonus action, spend 1 to PB CE to create a nonmagical object you understand in an open space within 10 feet. Each CE creates up to 10 pounds and 1 cubic foot of matter. It is real and permanent. A complex object requires a blueprint, tool proficiency, or a studied example; no object can contain energy, active chemicals, information, or living tissue that you do not understand.

Two properties

Passive (on creation) · Combine two understood mundane item designs into one object at double total CE and mass · Choose one base damage die and combine compatible weapon properties; conflicting properties (e.g. light and heavy) choose one · Created currency and valuables read as constructed under CE detection but remain physically real.

She may wed two designs into one, spear and greatsword, light and heavy reconciled, at double the cost. The chimera forge: where natures conflict, one must yield. Even in creation, there are contradictions no energy can marry. You may combine two understood mundane item designs into one object at double total CE and mass. Choose one base damage die and combine compatible weapon properties; if properties conflict, such as light and heavy, choose one. Created currency and raw valuables are recognizable as constructed matter under CE detection, but remain physically real.

3Cursed Creation:

Cursed or magic items

Bonus action (with creation) · Create a named cursed or magic item (name the exact item; GM confirms its grade and lore fit) · Cost: 3 CE + 1 per grade above common (up to Grade 1) · Lasts CE modifier hours; requires normal attunement; cannot supply wishes or permanent ability increases; vanishes without leaving saleable components.

Now she builds legends, named tools, graded relics, the weapons of history. She must name the legend truly, and the campaign must agree the legend may walk. The price is steep, the hours numbered, the attunement real. Borrowed fire warms, but it always goes out. All borrowed things return to the dark they came from. You can now create cursed or magic items. Name the exact published or campaign item before creating it; the GM confirms its grade and whether its lore can exist in the campaign. A created cursed tool costs 3 CE + 1 per grade above common (up to Grade 1), lasts for CE modifier hours, requires normal attunement, cannot supply wishes or permanent ability increases, and vanishes without leaving saleable components.

Special Grade

Passive · Voluntarily constructing a Special Grade cursed tool kills you immediately after the item is completed · That death cannot be undone by ordinary Reverse Cursed Technique or any revival effect.

To willingly build a Special Grade tool is to die the moment it is finished, no reversal, no revival, the old ink unsoftened. Understand the doctrine: some thresholds are not prices but endings. The wise builder knows which temples not to raise. The deadliest weapon she will ever make is the one she refuses to. Voluntarily constructing a Special Grade cursed tool kills you immediately after the item is completed. Under the original Project wording, that death cannot be undone by ordinary Reverse Cursed Technique or any revival effect; the revised campaign ruling does not soften that explicit consequence.

6Liquid Metal.

Liquid Metal

Passive (at creation) · Spend up to an additional 4 CE to make a created weapon fluid: each CE adds +10 ft melee reach and raises its damage die one tier (1d8 to 1d10 to 1d12 to 2d6 to 2d8) · Lasts 1 minute (Technique Focus); dismiss with no action.

She may bid a created weapon run like water, reach lengthening ten feet per coin, the edge climbing tier by tier, fluid for a minute. The river-blade: it strikes from where blades should not reach, and flows around the parry. Still water, applied to war. As part of creating a weapon with your construction, you may spend up to an additional 4 CE to make it fluid. Choose the additional CE when manifested; it cannot be raised later. Each CE extends the weapon’s melee reach by 10 feet and raises its damage die one tier (1d8 → 1d10 → 1d12 → 2d6 → 2d8). It lasts 1 minute, uses Technique Focus, and can be dismissed with no action. The reach applies to melee attacks made with it, not every attack you possess.

11Insectoid Armor.

Insectoid Armor

Bonus action · 10 CE (Technique Focus) · Surround yourself in insectoid armor for 1 minute: +4 Strength, Dexterity, and AC; resistance to nonmagical bludgeoning, piercing, and slashing (immunity at level 20); fly speed equal to walking speed; once per turn Dash with no action; unarmed strikes deal two additional damage dice (+1 more on crit) · Ends if incapacitated.

For ten coins and a minute, she does not build armor, she becomes the chrysalis. Strength, speed, and shell rise together; wings unfold; her very fists grow new dice. The mundane breaks upon her; at the peak, it does not even scratch. This is Construction's final argument: the greatest thing the builder ever makes is herself, revised. As a bonus action for 10 CE, surround yourself in insectoid armor for 1 minute (uses Technique Focus; ends if you are incapacitated): your Strength, Dexterity, and AC increase by +4 (this can exceed the usual maximum only by the printed +4); you gain resistance to nonmagical bludgeoning, piercing, and slashing damage (immunity at level 20); wings grant a flying speed equal to your walking speed; once per turn you may Dash with no action; your unarmed strikes deal two additional damage dice, plus one more die on a critical hit.

Pinnacle expression

Maximum, Domain, or equivalent.

Threefold Affliction, opened: sixty feet where making is law. Within, her creations cost nothing, the treasury of memory thrown open, every weapon dealt two extra dice, and no attack with them can miss. Heian philosophy: the Domain is not an event but a statute, with no thunderclap at the twentieth toll. Consider what she has done: turned the battlefield into her workshop, and the workshop's rule is that the builder never fails. Against this, only the old defenses avail, and even they merely beg the dice for mercy. Domain Expansion, Threefold Affliction:

The domain is a workshop built from memory, every tool and material the user has ever imagined laid out in triplicate on benches of dark wood. Three braziers burn at the corners, one for each affliction the old texts name, and the smoke from each smells different, grief, craving, and the slow rot of indifference. To stand inside is to stand inside someone's recollection of labor.

18: full-turn action, 20 CE; 60-foot-radius enclosed Domain centered on you; 1 minute; Burnout 10 rounds. Heian philosophy: the Domain is a law, not an event, no initiative-20 pulse. While it stands, you may create any of your Construction items by spending no cursed energy; every such item deals two additional damage dice, and attacks made with them cannot miss. These weapons last only until the Domain ends. A creature protected by an anti-domain defense may make a Dexterity saving throw vs your CE save DC when struck by such a weapon (invented); on a success the attack rolls normally instead of hitting automatically.

Innate Technique Feats

Build-defining options.

These are optional character-building feats. You must meet each listed prerequisite before selecting it.

Prerequisite: Construct

Subtle Constructs

She may build unseen, the dagger that was not there a breath ago, the tool no eye marked in the making. Against the fooled, the first use strikes with advantage. The quietest blade is the one that was never drawn, only remembered into being. After creating an item, make Dexterity (Sleight of Hand) against each observer’s passive Perception. Against a creature you fool, your first attack or check using that item before end of next turn has advantage. The creation itself remains subject to line of sight and obvious physical evidence.

Prerequisite: Cursed Creation

Imbue

At double the price, she may lend a created tool a Technique for seven days, a borrowed soul, housed in borrowed steel. No further limit; the only laws are attunement and her own strength. But the oldest price stands: a Special Grade soul still kills its maker. Some guests cannot be hosted. When using Cursed Creation to construct a cursed tool, pay twice that tool’s normal CE cost to imbue the creation with the tool’s Technique for 7 days. There is no added one-item cap. The created tool uses the normal attunement rules and your CE attack/DC when its technique needs them; Creating a Special Grade tool still kills the creator afterward and cannot be reversed or revived.

Prerequisite: Insectoid Armor and CE score 20

Maximum: Perfect Sphere

The Maximum: a five-foot sphere, perfect, indestructible, moved by a thought, thirty feet at a gesture, shattering walls and the unready alike, dealing ruin to all it touches or nears. The simplest shape, perfected. There is a lesson the masters carve above their doors: when you can build anything, build the thing nothing can break. Action, 10 CE: create one 5-foot-radius sphere within 10 feet for 1 minute with Technique Focus. It is indestructible while maintained. Ending a turn adjacent or being rammed forces one Dexterity save per turn for 5d12 force, half on success. Bonus action moves it 30 feet; it destroys barriers and unattended nonmagical structures in its path.

Rules integration

Edge cases and shared systems.

Rules priority: the reviewed rulings, explicit codex clarifications, and the level progression always control when original wording conflicts. Legacy Domain auto-hit language, action terminology, event timing, missing saves, and older scaling never override revised player rules.
Why Tier IV: even a modest user can solve material, equipment, and access problems outside combat. The tier acknowledges narrative reach and adjudication load while preserving the lore’s frightening endpoint.

Beginning at 1st, you can create any kind of object you understand
with your technique, although without much efficiency. As a bonus
action for 1 cursed energy, you can create one non-magical object you

You may also spend twice the cursed energy in this technique to

heavy weapon, choose which one of the properties it will maintain.)

Liquid Metal
At 6th level, you have learned how to create liquid metal, a
substance with fluid form and changeable volume but stable physical
properties due to semi-autonomous cursed energy. As part of creating
a weapon with your construction, you may spend up to an additional 4
cursed energy to make them become fluid with volume. This allows you
to extend the range of melee weapons 10ft per cursed energy.
In addition, whenever you attack with them in this increased range
form their damage die tier will increase equal to the cursed energy
used. (1d8>1d10->1d12->2d6->2d8)
Insectoid Armor
At 11th level, you realize you need a way to efficiently use your
cursed energy despite being an inefficient technique. You have
learned how to create armor that simulates the efficiency of an
insect's bug body. As a bonus action for 10 cursed energy, you can
surround yourself in this armor and gain the following benefits:
● Your Strength, Dexterity, and Armor Class scores increase by
+4, this is the result of the efficiency of the bugs.
● You will gain resistance to non-magical bludgeoning, piercing
and slashing damage. This evolves to immunity at level 20.
● Wings will sprout from your back, making you gain a flying
speed equal to your walking speed. In addition, you can now use
your free action to dash 1/turn.
● Your unarmed strikes will deal two additional damage dice. In
addition, your unarmed strikes roll one additional damage dice
on a critical.
Domain Expansion: Threefold Affliction
Finally at 20th level, you have refined
your technique to the maximum point, now
gaining your domain expansion. While
inside your domain, you can now create any
of your items by spending no cursed
energy. All of these items will deal one
additional damage dice and attacks made
with them cannot miss. However, these
weapons will only last until the end of
the domain.
