Sun-Eater Wings

Garuda inspiration. Generate Pinions by sustained flight and by helping creatures overcome poison, grapples, restraint, and serpent-like bindings. Spend Pinions on rescue movement and radiant strikes.

RoleAerial rescue / anti-binding support
Core statWIS or CHA
ResourcePinion
Scaling died6 → d8 → d10 → d12
Level progression1 / 3 / 6 / 11 / 18
Milestone featurePlayer-facing rules
Golden PinionsBonus, 1 CE, wings 10 minutes, flight = speed. Once/turn after flying 20 ft gain 1 Pinion (max PB); spend one on hit for +1 die radiant/slashing.
Enemy of VenomReaction, 1 CE: add one die to an ally's save against poison, grapple, restrained, or serpent-like effect; if successful gain 1 Pinion.
Wingbeat DeliveranceAction, 2 CE, spend Pinions to move creatures 10 ft; willing targets move safely, hostiles Str save. A target can be moved twice.
Sunward AscentBegin turn airborne with open overhead space to gain 1 Pinion. Spend 3 to end poisoned/restrained on self or touched ally unless superior Domain law.
Maximum: Wings That Eclipse the Serpent7 CE, 1 minute: fly 80/hover, poison/acid resistance, first Wingbeat target free each turn; first hit vs a creature actively poisoning/restraining another deals +3 dice radiant.

Character engine

Generate Pinions by sustained flight and by helping creatures overcome poison, grapples, restraint, and serpent-like bindings. Spend Pinions on rescue movement and radiant strikes.

Technique save DC: 8 + PB + chosen CE ability modifier. Technique attack: PB + chosen CE ability modifier.

Counterplay

Ground the user or fight under low ceilings. Peak Pinion generation needs movement and successful rescue/cleansing interactions.

Adaptation note. This is original JJK 5e homebrew inspired by Garuda and the named tradition. The folklore/myth supplies the motif; the resource engine, CE economy, progression, feats, Maximum and Domain procedures are game mechanics created for this codex.
