Sun-Eater Wings

Garuda inspiration. Generate Pinions by sustained flight and by helping creatures overcome poison, grapples, restraint, and serpent-like bindings. Spend Pinions on rescue movement and radiant strikes.

Combat roleAerial rescue / anti-binding support
Power tierTier IV · Special Grade
Core law

Character engine

Generate Pinions by sustained flight and by helping creatures overcome poison, grapples, restraint, and serpent-like bindings. Spend Pinions on rescue movement and radiant strikes.

Technique save DC: 8 + PB + chosen CE ability modifier. Technique attack: PB + chosen CE ability modifier.

Field notes. Subject: a sorcerer whose wings grow from the act of rescue itself. Three truths govern the Sun-Eater. First: mercy is the engine. Pinions are not bought, they are grown by sustained flight and by pulling others free of poison, grapple, restraint, and serpent-bindings; every unbinding leaves a feather behind. Second: the hatred is specific. This energy learned, before anything else, that being bound is the worst thing that can happen to a person, the whole technique is an argument against cages. Third: rescue becomes judgment. Spend the Pinions on radiant strikes, and the Maximum turns the rescuer into the serpent's eclipse, poisoners and restrainers struck down by the light they denied. I have watched him cross a battlefield not toward the enemy but toward the fallen, and arrive more dangerous than any charge. Consider: the Garuda does not fight his enemies. He collects his debts from them, feather by feather.

Full progression

Level progression

1Golden Pinions

The wings are the argument; the feathers are the receipts. Fly twenty feet and the technique drops one Pinion, already grown, finally released, to be spent on radiant or slashing judgment. Mark this: the sorcerer does not conjure ammunition. He molts it. As a bonus action for 1 CE, you grow golden wings for 10 minutes and gain a flying speed equal to your speed. Once per turn after you fly at least 20 feet, you gain 1 Pinion (maximum equal to your proficiency bonus). When you hit with an attack, you may spend 1 Pinion to deal +1d8 of radiant or slashing damage (invented: the die is a d8).

3Enemy of Venom

The rescuer's reflex. An ally failing against poison, grapple, restraint, the Garuda's reaction and one CE lends them a die, and if they stand, the feather returns. This is the whole technique in one exchange: someone else's freedom, paid for, and the wings grow stronger for it. When an ally makes a saving throw against poison, grapple, the restrained condition, or a serpent-like effect, you may use your reaction and spend 1 CE to add one d8 (invented) to their save. If the save succeeds, you gain 1 Pinion.

6Wingbeat Deliverance

Wingbeat Deliverance: Action · 2 CE · Spend 1 Pinion per creature (invented) to move up to PB creatures within 60 ft (invented) 10 ft each · Willing targets move safely; hostile targets make a Strength save · One target may be moved twice (20 ft total for 2 Pinions, invented).

The sky intervenes. For Pinions, up to PB creatures move ten feet, the willing carried safe, the hostile tested by Strength. One target may be moved twice. I have watched a battle's geometry redrawn in a single action: the ally pulled from the trap, the enemy pushed into it. As an action for 2 CE, you spend 1 Pinion per creature (invented) to move up to PB creatures within 60 feet of you (invented) 10 feet. Willing targets move safely; hostile targets make a Strength save. A single target can be moved twice by this effect (20 feet total for 2 Pinions, invented).

11Sunward Ascent

Passive · If you begin your turn airborne with open overhead space: gain 1 Pinion · Spend 3 Pinions to end the poisoned or restrained condition on yourself or a creature you touch (unless a superior Domain law prevents it).

Climb with open sky above and the ascent itself feeds you, one Pinion for beginning the turn airborne. Three Pinions buy the unbinding: poisoned or restrained ended by touch. The sun is the technique's senior partner here; the wings merely deliver its verdict. If you begin your turn airborne with open overhead space, you gain 1 Pinion. You may spend 3 Pinions to end the poisoned or restrained condition on yourself or a creature you touch, unless a superior Domain law prevents it.

18Maximum: Wings That Eclipse the Serpent

Maximum Technique · Action (invented) · 7 CE · 1 minute · Fly speed 80 with hover; resistance to poison and acid damage · The first creature you move with Wingbeat Deliverance each turn is moved free; the first time you hit a creature that is actively poisoning or restraining another creature, the hit deals +3d8 radiant (invented).

Mercy, at scale, becomes judgment. Eighty feet of hovering flight, poison and acid turned aside, the first rescue each turn free, and the first strike against one who poisons or restrains another burns with radiant fury. The serpent's eclipse is not metaphor. It is the shadow the rescuer casts over the ones who bind. As an action (invented), you spend 7 CE to become Wings That Eclipse the Serpent for 1 minute: you gain a fly speed of 80 with hover and resistance to poison and acid damage. The first creature you move with Wingbeat Deliverance each turn is moved for free, and the first time you hit a creature that is actively poisoning or restraining another creature, the hit deals +3d8 of radiant damage (invented).

Feats

Build-defining options.

Feather Shield

A feather interposed between an ally and the blow. One Pinion, one reaction, +2 AC, decided after the roll, before the result; and if the feather turns the strike, it returns to the wing unspent. The technique's thrift is exact: mercy given is mercy that comes home. When an ally within 15 feet is targeted by an attack, you may use your reaction and spend 1 Pinion to interpose a shield of pinion feathers, granting the ally +2 AC against that attack. Decide after seeing the attack roll but before knowing the result. If the attack misses because of this bonus, the feather returns to your wings and you regain the spent Pinion, up to your maximum.

Sky Ferry

The rescuer as transport. Carry one willing creature at full speed, and let them strike from above on your movement, one attack or cantrip, reaction-spent, the sky as a gun platform. The movement still draws opportunity attacks; the technique protects the passenger, not the itinerary. While flying with your Golden Pinions, you may carry one willing creature without reducing your flying speed. The carried creature may use its reaction during your movement to make one attack or cast a cantrip, striking from above; your movement still provokes opportunity attacks as normal. If you are forced to land while carrying a creature, you may spend 1 Pinion as a reaction to glide it safely to the ground within 15 feet of you, negating its falling damage.

Venom Breaker

The reflex sharpens. Enemy of Venom now reaches disease and corrosive ongoing effects where appropriate, the Garuda's enmity is not limited to the listed bindings. A rescuer who has learned what a cage is recognizes every new one on sight. Enemy of Venom can apply to disease/corrosive ongoing effects when appropriate.

Solar Plumage (11+)

The sun takes over the paperwork. Begin the turn in direct sunlight and the cleansing is free, poisoned or restrained ended without spending Pinions. Mark this: the technique's oldest ally is the light itself. The wings were always the sun's idea. When you start your turn in direct sunlight, you may end the poisoned or restrained condition on yourself without spending Pinions, as the sun's light does the cleansing for you.

Rules integration

Counterplay

Ground the user or fight under low ceilings. Peak Pinion generation needs movement and successful rescue/cleansing interactions.
