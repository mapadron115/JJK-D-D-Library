Sun-Eater Wings

Garuda inspiration. Generate Pinions by sustained flight and by helping creatures overcome poison, grapples, restraint, and serpent-like bindings. Spend Pinions on rescue movement and radiant strikes.

Combat roleAerial rescue / anti-binding support
Power tierTier IV · Special Grade
Core law

Character engine

Generate Pinions by sustained flight and by helping creatures overcome poison, grapples, restraint, and serpent-like bindings. Spend Pinions on rescue movement and radiant strikes.

Technique save DC: 8 + PB + chosen CE ability modifier. Technique attack: PB + chosen CE ability modifier.

The fiction. Jujutsu scholars classify Sun-Eater Wings as mercy that hardened into plumage: the user's CE imprinted on every moment they pulled someone free of something that held them, and now the wings grow from the act of rescue itself. The Pinions are not spent like currency — registry notes describe them as feathers the technique already grew, finally dropped. It is a sorcerer whose energy learned, before anything else, that being bound is the worst thing that can happen to a person.

Full progression

Level progression

L1Golden Pinions

As a bonus action for 1 CE, you grow golden wings for 10 minutes and gain a flying speed equal to your speed. Once per turn after you fly at least 20 feet, you gain 1 Pinion (maximum equal to your proficiency bonus). When you hit with an attack, you may spend 1 Pinion to deal +1 die of radiant or slashing damage.

L3Enemy of Venom

When an ally makes a saving throw against poison, grapple, the restrained condition, or a serpent-like effect, you may use your reaction and spend 1 CE to add one die to their save. If the save succeeds, you gain 1 Pinion.

L6Wingbeat Deliverance

As an action for 2 CE, you spend Pinions to move creatures 10 feet. Willing targets move safely; hostile targets make a Strength save. A single target can be moved twice by this effect.

L11Sunward Ascent

If you begin your turn airborne with open overhead space, you gain 1 Pinion. You may spend 3 Pinions to end the poisoned or restrained condition on yourself or a creature you touch, unless a superior Domain law prevents it.

L18Maximum: Wings That Eclipse the Serpent

You spend 7 CE to become Wings That Eclipse the Serpent for 1 minute: you gain a fly speed of 80 with hover and resistance to poison and acid damage. The first creature you move with Wingbeat Deliverance each turn is moved for free, and the first time you hit a creature that is actively poisoning or restraining another creature, the hit deals +3 dice of radiant damage.

Feats

Build-defining options.

Feather Shield

When an ally within 15 feet is targeted by an attack, you may use your reaction and spend 1 Pinion to interpose a shield of pinion feathers, granting the ally +2 AC against that attack. Decide after seeing the attack roll but before knowing the result. If the attack misses because of this bonus, the feather returns to your wings and you regain the spent Pinion, up to your maximum.

Sky Ferry

While flying with your Golden Pinions, you may carry one willing creature without reducing your flying speed. The carried creature may use its reaction during your movement to make one attack or cast a cantrip, striking from above; your movement still provokes opportunity attacks as normal. If you are forced to land while carrying a creature, you may spend 1 Pinion as a reaction to glide it safely to the ground within 15 feet of you, negating its falling damage.

Venom Breaker

Enemy of Venom can apply to disease/corrosive ongoing effects when appropriate.

Solar Plumage (11+)

When you start your turn in direct sunlight, you may end the poisoned or restrained condition on yourself without spending Pinions, as the sun's light does the cleansing for you.

Rules integration

Counterplay

Ground the user or fight under low ceilings. Peak Pinion generation needs movement and successful rescue/cleansing interactions.
