Grave-Mound Tenacity

Draugr inspiration. Choose a Grave Anchor. Enemies entering your guarded space, moving the Anchor, or escaping your reach generate Restlessness, which fuels resistance and retaliation.

Combat roleUndead tank / territory anchor
Power tierTier III · Apex
Core law

Character engine

Choose a Grave Anchor. Enemies entering your guarded space, moving the Anchor, or escaping your reach generate Restlessness, which fuels resistance and retaliation.

Technique save DC: 8 + PB + chosen CE ability modifier. Technique attack: PB + chosen CE ability modifier.

Full progression

Level progression

1Restless Corpse

When you are hit, you may spend Restlessness for resistance to that attack's physical damage. You may also spend 1 Restlessness on a melee hit to add +1 Grave Die of cold damage.

3Mound-Dweller

Mound-Dweller (action, 2 CE): raise a 15-foot Grave Mound around your Anchor for 1 minute. It is difficult terrain for enemies, and movement inside it generates Restlessness.

6Corpse-Swell

Corpse-Swell (bonus action, 2 CE, 1 minute): become Large if possible, gain advantage on Strength checks and saves, and +5 feet of reach. Targets you have grappled must pass a Charisma save to teleport.

11Too Angry to Die

When you drop to 0 HP inside your Mound, you may spend PB Restlessness + 4 CE to remain at 1 HP, stand up, and make one melee attack. Once per long rest.

18Domain Expansion: Barrow That Refuses the Dawn

Full-turn action, 20 CE, enclosed 60-foot radius centered on you, up to 1 minute, Technique Focus. Your Grave Anchor becomes the center of a sealed barrow. At initiative 20, each chosen hostile more than 15 feet from the Anchor is dragged 10 feet toward it and knocked prone. A creature protected from the sure-hit instead makes a Strength save; on success it is neither moved nor knocked prone. Gain 1 Restlessness for each hostile actually moved, up to your normal cap. Teleportation cannot cross the Domain barrier unless an effect explicitly crosses Domain barriers. Ordinary attacks still roll.

Feats

Build-defining options.

Burial Goods

You may maintain a second, portable Grave Anchor — a sealed burial token weighing 5 lb — alongside your main Anchor, though only one Anchor projects your Grave Mound at a time; switching which Anchor projects the Mound costs a bonus action and 1 CE. A hostile creature that moves while carrying or touching the portable Anchor generates Restlessness for you as though it were moving inside your guarded space, rewarding the thief's greed. If the portable Anchor is destroyed, you take psychic damage equal to one Grave Die per Restlessness you hold, up to a number of dice equal to your PB.

Anchor's Pull

When you hit a creature with an opportunity attack, you may spend 1 Restlessness to drag it 10 feet toward your Grave Anchor instead of dealing damage; a creature that enters your guarded space this way generates Restlessness as normal.

Corpse Weight

count one size larger for carrying/grapple/forced movement while swollen.

Unquiet Dead (11+)

When Too Angry to Die triggers, your rage infects the field: each hostile creature within 15 feet must succeed on a Wisdom save against your CE DC or be frightened of you until the end of your next turn.

Rules integration

Domain procedure

This entry uses the repaired campaign Domain rules. The level-18 feature above is complete: its printed action, CE cost, radius, duration, initiative-20 law, fallback protection, and counterplay replace any legacy blanket auto-hit wording.
Rules integration

Counterplay

Refuse the anchor fight. Ranged pressure and moving the Grave Anchor deny Restlessness and territory value.
