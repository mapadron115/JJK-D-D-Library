Grave-Mound Tenacity

Draugr inspiration. Choose a Grave Anchor. Enemies entering your guarded space, moving the Anchor, or escaping your reach generate Restlessness, which fuels resistance and retaliation.

Core law

Character engine

The deed and the fence: one object or ten-foot location named Anchor each long rest, hostiles entering the guarded space or moving more than ten feet inside it generate Restlessness, capped at PB. Designate one object or 10-foot location as your Grave Anchor when you finish a long rest (invented). Enemies entering your guarded space, moving the Anchor, or escaping your reach generate Restlessness, which fuels resistance and retaliation. Gain 1 Restlessness the first time each round a hostile enters your mound or guarded space, and 1 when a hostile willingly moves more than 10 feet inside it (invented); your Restlessness cannot exceed your PB (invented). A Grave Die is a d8 (invented).

Technique save DC: 8 + PB + chosen CE ability modifier. Technique attack: PB + chosen CE ability modifier.

Field notes. Subject: resentment with an address. Three truths govern him. First: he owns ground, and the technique is the deed. The Grave Anchor is his title; the mound is his fence; everything that enters uninvited makes him stronger, Restlessness is not fuel he gathers, it is his mood rising at the trespass. Second: he fights like a landlord, not a duelist. Violations buy resistance and cold retaliation; the mound is difficult terrain for everyone but him. Third: the dead do not leave, and neither does he. Too Angry to Die stands him back up inside his own barrow, because the technique believes that is simply what the dead are supposed to do when guests overstay. Assess him as a barrow with opinions: refuse the anchor fight, and he is a tenant with no rent to collect. Step inside it, and he is the law of that acre.

18Domain Expansion: Barrow That Refuses the Dawn

Domain Expansion · Full-turn action · 20 CE · Enclosed 60-ft radius centered on you · up to 1 minute · Technique Focus · Your Grave Anchor becomes the center of a sealed barrow · Sure-hit: at initiative 20, each chosen hostile more than 15 ft from the Anchor is dragged 10 ft toward it and knocked prone (a protected creature instead makes a Strength save: success is neither moved nor knocked prone); gain 1 Restlessness per hostile actually moved, up to your PB · Teleportation cannot cross the barrier unless explicitly allowed · Ordinary attacks still roll.

The world narrows to a single barrow mound beneath a sky that will not finish becoming morning. Standing stones ring the slope like teeth, and the mist moves against the wind, slow and deliberate, as though the field itself were breathing. No sun comes. The dark here is not absence but refusal, a night that has learned to hold its ground.

The barrow sealed: sixty feet, one minute, the Anchor at its heart. At initiative twenty, hostiles beyond fifteen feet are dragged ten feet closer and knocked prone, and each one moved breeds one Restlessness. Full-turn action, 20 CE, enclosed 60-foot radius centered on you, up to 1 minute, Technique Focus. Your Grave Anchor becomes the center of a sealed barrow. At initiative 20, each chosen hostile more than 15 feet from the Anchor is dragged 10 feet toward it and knocked prone. A creature protected from the sure-hit instead makes a Strength save; on success it is neither moved nor knocked prone. Gain 1 Restlessness for each hostile actually moved, up to your PB (invented: the Restlessness cap). Teleportation cannot cross the Domain barrier unless an effect explicitly crosses Domain barriers. Ordinary attacks still roll.

Feats

Build-defining options.

Burial Goods

A second, portable Anchor, a five-pound burial token, though only one projects the Mound at a time; destroy it and he bleeds psychic per Restlessness held. You may maintain a second, portable Grave Anchor, a sealed burial token weighing 5 lb, alongside your main Anchor, though only one Anchor projects your Grave Mound at a time; switching which Anchor projects the Mound costs a bonus action and 1 CE. A hostile creature that moves while carrying or touching the portable Anchor generates Restlessness for you as though it were moving inside your guarded space, rewarding the thief's greed. If the portable Anchor is destroyed, you take psychic damage equal to one Grave Die per Restlessness you hold, up to a number of dice equal to your PB.

Anchor's Pull

One Restlessness on an opportunity attack drags the victim ten feet toward the Anchor instead of dealing damage. When you hit a creature with an opportunity attack, you may spend 1 Restlessness to drag it 10 feet toward your Grave Anchor instead of dealing damage; a creature that enters your guarded space this way generates Restlessness as normal.

Corpse Weight

While swollen, he counts one size larger for carrying, grapples, and forced movement. count one size larger for carrying/grapple/forced movement while swollen.

Unquiet Dead (11+)

Too Angry to Die infects the field: hostiles within fifteen feet save or fear him until the end of his next turn. When Too Angry to Die triggers, your rage infects the field: each hostile creature within 15 feet must succeed on a Wisdom save against your CE DC or be frightened of you until the end of your next turn.
