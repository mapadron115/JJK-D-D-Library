Field notes. Subject: the crystal that thinks apart. What remains when a sorcerer's curiosity outlives the hand that holds it: cursed energy that thinks apart from the body, given a permanent address. Three truths govern it. First, it studies before it strikes: Analyze (bonus action, 1 CE) reveals two of HP band, CE band, AC, highest and lowest saves, or resistances within 60 ft. Second, it is a medium, not a weapon: Remote Eye lets you perceive through Aldan 300 ft away; Spell Relay originates a technique from its position; Soul Anchorage catches your consciousness at 0 HP, stable in the crystal until an ally reaches you. Third, the Release — Grand Calculation at level 13+: Analyze free once per turn, technique ranges +50%, and once per round a successful enemy save may be reduced by one Tool Die.
