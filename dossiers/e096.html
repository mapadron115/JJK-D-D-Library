Attunement: spend 1 hour and one cursed-tool attunement slot. A Sacred Treasure supplements your Innate Technique; it does not grant a second one.

Activation: activated properties spend the wielder’s own CE. If a non-sorcerer is explicitly allowed to wield the treasure, its Tool save DC is 8 + proficiency bonus + one chosen mental ability modifier.

Sacred Treasure Release: normally requires level 13 or higher and is usable once per long rest; pay the specific treasure’s listed CE cost. An explicit exception in its individual entry takes precedence.

SACRED TREASURE • Merlin

Morning Star Aldan

Special Grade

Floating crystal spell-focus and analytical vessel

Canon gives Aldan fewer explicit combat functions than the other treasures, so its deeper mechanics are a Codex extension built around its demonstrated role as Merlin's magical medium and information tool.

  1. Arcane Focus: Aldan floats within 5 ft and cannot be disarmed while you are conscious unless suppressed. Technique range increases by 15 ft.
  2. Analyze (1 CE): bonus action; choose creature/effect within 60 ft. Learn two of: current HP band, CE band, AC, highest save, lowest save, active resistance/immunity, technique category, or whether an effect is a barrier/summon/illusion.
  3. Remote Eye (2 CE): action; move Aldan up to 300 ft for 10 minutes and perceive through it. Your body is blinded/deafened while doing so.
  4. Soul Anchorage (4 CE, 1/long rest): reaction when reduced to 0 HP but not killed outright; consciousness enters Aldan until end of next turn. You are stable. If an ally reaches your body or Aldan before then, return at 1 HP; otherwise fall unconscious normally.
  5. Spell Relay (3 CE): once/turn originate a technique from Aldan's position instead of yours, provided the technique does not require your body to make physical contact.
  6. Sacred Treasure Release — Grand Calculation (level 13+, 7 CE): 1 minute. Analyze becomes free once/turn, technique ranges +50%, and once/round after a creature succeeds on a save against your technique, subtract one Tool Die from its result, potentially turning it into a failure.
