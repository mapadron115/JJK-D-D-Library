Properties & techniques

Exact player-facing procedures

Feature

On hit · Spend Charge for +1 Tool Die lightning each, max 2 per blow.

Spend Charge on hitEvery toll of the bell rings once, and the bell is finite. Spend the Charge with a general's measure, two dice at most per blow, and the storm will pay out exactly what was banked. for +1 Tool Die lightning each, max 2.

Thunder Recall

Bonus action · 2 CE · Recall from up to 300 ft; creatures in the 5-ft line: Dex save or 1 Tool Die thunder damage.

The recall is not a request; it is the hammer returning to its only recognized hand. Mark the line it travels, the thunder does not discriminate, and a careless master recalls his own ruin. bonus, 2 CE: recall from up to 300 ft; creatures in a 5-ft line Dex save or 1 Tool Die thunder.

Worthy Grip

Passive · Unattuned creatures treat it as immovable unless they beat the Tool DC with a Strength check.

Classify this correctly: it is not virtue the hammer tests, but recognition. The immovable grip is loyalty wearing the mask of weight, the tool simply has no idea who you are. unattuned creatures treat it as immovable unless they beat Tool DC with Strength check; this is a cursed attunement law, not moral judgment.

Release: Storm-Crowned Return

7 CE · 1 minute: flight 40 ft, thrown range doubles, first recall each turn is free.

For one minute, the bargain is at its grandest: flight, doubled range, a recall that costs nothing. The storm, having been fed motion all day, at last agrees to move the bearer instead. 7 CE, 1 minute: flight 40 ft, thrown range doubles, first recall/turn free.

Field notes. Subject: the storm made loyal. Three truths govern him. First, the hammer drinks motion and pays thunder: every step, every catch, every bolt taken is banked as Charge, and Charge is the only purse this weapon respects, idled too long, it disperses like an unspent charge of cavalry. Second, it was never thrown, only sent: the recall is not a trick of the hand but the weapon's nature, and the bearer who will not stand still finds it flies farther for him. Third, and most instructive: the Worthy Grip is no trial of virtue. The hammer simply does not recognize an unattuned hand, and immobility is how a thing with nothing left to give still stays loyal. Hence the maxim: courage in motion is the only currency this storm accepts.

Rules integration

Cursed-tool attunement

Campaign canon

Cursed-tool attunement: you have PB attunement slots (2–6). This tool occupies one slot and requires a one-hour ritual. Attunement ends voluntarily over a short rest, on death, or when you replace it while over capacity. Distance does not break attunement. Cursed-tool attunement is separate from magic-item attunement.
Balance & counterplay. Strong but still uses attack rolls, CE, and finite Charge.
