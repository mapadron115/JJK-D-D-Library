Features & procedures

Properties & techniques

Exact player-facing procedures

Feature

Spend Charge on hit for +1 Tool Die lightning each, max 2.

Thunder Recall

bonus, 2 CE: recall from up to 300 ft; creatures in a 5-ft line Dex save or 1 Tool Die thunder.

Worthy Grip

unattuned creatures treat it as immovable unless they beat Tool DC with Strength check; this is a cursed attunement law, not moral judgment.

Release: Storm-Crowned Return

7 CE, 1 minute: flight 40 ft, thrown range doubles, first recall/turn free.

The fiction. Registry notes describe Mjölnir as momentum given a body: the tool drinks the sorcerer's motion and returns it as thunder, which is why it flies farther for those who refuse to stand still. The prevailing theory holds that the Worthy Grip is not a test of virtue but of binding — the tool simply does not recognize a hand it has not been attuned to, and immobility is its way of staying loyal. Jujutsu scholars classify the Charge engine as the weapon's appetite: it feeds on the bearer's courage to keep moving, and spends it back as lightning.

Rules integration

Cursed-tool attunement

Campaign canon

Cursed-tool attunement: you have PB attunement slots (2–6). This tool occupies one slot and requires a one-hour ritual. Attunement ends voluntarily over a short rest, on death, or when you replace it while over capacity. Distance does not break attunement. Cursed-tool attunement is separate from magic-item attunement.
Balance & counterplay. Strong but still uses attack rolls, CE, and finite Charge.
