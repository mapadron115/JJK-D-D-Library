Cursed Spirit — Field Entry

Fly Head

The weakest catalogued curse: a drifting, buzzing nuisance drawn to negative emotion, containable in a talisman-wrapped box.

ClassificationImaginary Cursed Spirit
GradeUngraded
RiskLow to None

Field notes. Subject: the floor of the bestiary. Three truths govern the Fly Head. First, it is the weakest catalogued curse, below Grade 4, one hit point, Strength 1: so feeble that Jujutsu High keeps contained specimens for civilian sight tests. Second, it is a weather vane for dread: drawn to places of negative emotion, drifting aimlessly alone, growing bolder only in clusters, where Fly Heads gather, stronger things are not far. Third, the only threat is arithmetic: one is a boxed nuisance, re-sealed and released elsewhere; many become a swarm, and the swarm is Grade 4. Mark this: the least curse still teaches the first lesson. Look. Then look again.

Campaign canon notes

Field rule — low-grade perception: Per the C&C field rules, Grade 3 and lower curses are invisible to creatures without cursed energy. A creature in a life-or-death situation at less than half its hit point maximum may temporarily or permanently gain the ability to see them. This spirit carries the Invisibility trait below.
