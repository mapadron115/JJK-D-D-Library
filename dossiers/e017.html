Completed rules

  • The extra 1d6 necrotic applies to each hit with a natural weapon, unarmed strike, or deliberate touch attack. The 1-CE action targets one creature within reach; it makes the printed Constitution save. Constructs are immune to conversion, not to the damage.
  • A creature reduced to 0 by your Idle Transfiguration becomes a Transfigured Creature instead of dying. Its maximum HP becomes half its former maximum (minimum 1) and it returns at full HP. You may store up to level × max(1, CE modifier); active creatures are limited to CE modifier (minimum one). A bonus action extracts any number up to that capacity into open spaces within 10 feet or stores adjacent willing transfigured creatures.
  • During a rest, choose Tiny through Huge; size changes space and natural reach only. Set Intelligence, Wisdom, and Charisma to 4, then move the removed points among Strength, Dexterity, and Constitution, each to a maximum of 22. Remove supernatural features, spellcasting, techniques, and proficiencies. AC is 10 + Dexterity modifier; attacks use your PB plus their Strength or Dexterity and deal 1d6 plus that modifier. A bonus action gives all active creatures one simple order; an action gives distinct or complex orders.
  • Ally Alterations: a willing creature receives them during a rest. They are permanent until you reverse them, the creature dies, or Technique Extermination removes them. One creature can have up to PB, and Hardened Skin chooses one of bludgeoning, piercing, or slashing. This does not change its creature type.
  • Self-Alterations: each is a bonus action, costs its printed CE, and lasts 1 minute. You may maintain up to PB compatible alterations; apply the printed restriction column. Ending one costs no action. These do not require Technique Focus.
  • Body Clone: on creation, divide maximum HP and maximum CE evenly between bodies, assigning odd points to the original, and reduce current pools to their new maxima. The clone acts on your turn, shares your actions only as stated in the source, and cannot create another clone. On recombination, restore maxima and add its remaining current HP/CE. If it dies, the reserved maxima return after your next long rest, but its remaining current resources are lost.
  • Soul Multiplicity: consumed creatures are permanently destroyed. Body Repel has a 60/120-foot range and uses one CE attack; it adds 1d8 for every creature beyond the first two, with no hidden cap. A Polymorphic Soul Isomer uses the appendix profile and counts as one active summon.
  • Domain — Soul Crucible: at initiative 20 choose one fate for each chosen hostile. Rupture automatically deals 8d10 necrotic damage and reduces maximum HP by the damage dealt; the reduction lasts until a long rest or soul-capable RCT restores it. Grotesque Form automatically deals 4d10 necrotic damage and leaves the creature restrained and blinded until the next initiative 20. A protected creature makes a Constitution save, taking half damage with no maximum-HP reduction or conditions on a success. Soul Crucible is direct Domain contact, not an ordinary use of Transfiguration; ordinary uses retain their saves.

Power identity

Why Tier IV: soul rewriting and a permanent creature reserve are campaign systems, even when the player never pursues the strongest available bodies. The revision defines the system rather than pretending that potential makes it unusable.

Recovered character-build options

Innate Technique Feats 3

These are optional character-building feats. You must meet each listed prerequisite before selecting it.

Remote Transfiguration

Pinnacle

Prerequisite: Transfiguration

A deliberate bare-hand touch may place one detectable soul mark on a Humanoid indefinitely. While on the same plane and knowing its identity, use an action and pay the normal Transfiguration cost to affect it at any distance; it saves normally. The mark is consumed after the attempt and can be removed by Technique Extermination or explicit soul repair.

Awakening

Pinnacle

Prerequisite: Ally Alteration

When altering a willing Non-Sorcerer, make one DC 15 CE ability check per creature. Success and 10 CE awaken a latent, lore-consistent innate technique after 1d4+1 days. The GM records the technique; it uses the target’s character level and does not grant class features or a second technique. The permanent change can reshape a campaign.

Maximum: Spirit Body of Distorted Killing

Pinnacle

Prerequisite: Self-Embodiment of Perfection and CE score 20

Action, 10 CE: for 10 minutes your AC cannot be below 25 and you gain proficient elbow blades (1d12 + CE modifier piercing). On each blade attack, spend 1 CE for advantage and 15-foot reach. The form ends if incapacitated; AC bonuses do not raise the 25-floor unless they normally stack.

The soul, an interesting thing, your technique is to manipulate it.
Cursed Energy

possess is based on your class, some classes use a modifier that
being charisma or wisdom (Player’s choice)

Transfiguration
Starting at 1st level, your touch can alter a creature's very own
being, their soul. Whenever you hit a creature with a natural weapon,
unarmed strike, or touch them, you will deal an additional 1d6
Necrotic damage.
In addition, as an action for 1 Cursed Energy, you may force said
creature within your reach to make a Constitution saving throw. On a
failure, they will take 1d10 Necrotic Damage, which increases to 2d10
at 5th level, 3d10 at 9th level, 4d10 at 13th level, and 5d10 at 17th
level. On a success, the creature takes half as much damage.
If Necrotic damage from any features of your Idle Transfiguration
reduces a creature's total hit points to 0, the creature will become
one of your Transfigured Creatures. This does not work on Constructs.
Transfigured Creatures. You can have a number of transfigured
creatures equal to your level times your Cursed Energy modifier. All
of your transfigured creatures will follow all of your orders, and
their type is undead. Once you have gained a transfigured creature,
while on rest, you may modify the following things on the creature:
● Size. The size may be changed anywhere from tiny to huge.
● Scores. All of the creatures' ability scores will remain the
same but their mental scores will be decreased to 4. You may
redistribute the points removed from their mental stats into
their physical scores, being able to surpass their maximum.
● Primitiveness. Transformed creatures lose all their supernatural
abilities (such as using spells or cursed techniques), they
have no proficiencies, and their AC is 10 + Dexterity modifier.
They can only use natural weapons or unarmed attacks. They can
roll a d6 in place of the normal damage of their unarmed
strike.
To give your transfigured creatures simple orders such as to
attack a creature takes a bonus action, while more complex
orders like defending an area take an action.
You can safely store your transfigured creatures in your own body,
extracting any number of them as a bonus action.

Body Clone
At 6th level, you have learned how to create an almost perfect clone
of yourself. As an action for 6 Cursed Energy, you create a perfect
copy of yourself with half of your maximum hit points and half of
your max Cursed Energy. It is identical to you, is controlled by you,
acts on your turn, and you can see through its eyes or hear through
its ears. Your clone cannot use any of your Innate Technique features
except self-alteration. You can't make another clone after the first
one until it dies or you touch it and it automatically rejoins you
granting you back the rest of your max HP and max Cursed Energy
points.
Soul Multiplicity
Starting at 11th level, as an action for 5 Cursed Energy you may
spend 2 or more of your Transfigured Creatures, fusing them together
and creating one of the following effects:
Body Repel. You create a barrage of Transfigured creatures being
launched at high speeds. You launch off and Cursed Energy attack with
a range of 60/120 feet range, this deals 5d8 Necrotic damage. For
each additional Transfigured Creature, you will add 1d8 to the total
Necrotic damage.
Polymorphic Soul Isomer. You create a Polymorphic Soul Isomer. When
using this technique, you can spend 5 additional Cursed Energy to add
another Transfigured Creature to the fusion(minimum of 2), and each
transfigured creature counts as one soul.
Domain Expansion: Self-Embodiment of Perfection
Finally, at 18th level, you have studied your powers and have
finally learned your most powerful technique. You create a large
dimension filled with your body parts, making escaping death
impossible. At initiative 20 they will receive your transfiguration
touch damage rolled twice.
