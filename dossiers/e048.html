Core law

What the technique actually does.

Rules are written to plug into the finalized campaign glossary: normal actions stay normal, direct attacks roll, and only named Domain events bypass the initial roll.

DARK MOON COVENANT

Action or replace one attack · 1 CE

Lunar Bolt

The weapon: action or replace one attack, 1 CE — ranged CE attack within Lunar Range. On a hit: 2d8 cold or force, her choice, and one Lunar Sigil for 1 minute. 3d8 at L11, 4d8 at L18.

Ranged CE attack within Lunar Range. Hit deals 2d8 cold or force (your choice) and places one Lunar Sigil for 1 minute. 3d8 at L11, 4d8 at L18.

Debuff

Lunar Sigil

The debuff: a sigiled creature’s resistance to technique damage drops one step against her Dark Moon features — immunity to resistance to normal. She maintains up to PB Lunar Sigils; sigils can be removed by cleansing.

A creature with a sigil has its resistance to technique damage reduced one step against your Dark Moon features (immunity→resistance→normal). You can maintain up to PB Lunar Sigils; sigils can be removed by cleansing.

Reaction · 2 CE

Cold Moon Guard

Reaction, 2 CE: when a visible ranged technique attack or projectile targets her or crosses within 10 feet, create a small dark moon — reduce the attack roll by PB or add PB to her saving throw. If the effect misses or fails because of this, absorb it and gain one Moon Shard.

When a visible ranged technique attack or projectile targets you or crosses within 10 feet, create a small dark moon. Reduce the attack roll by PB or add PB to your saving throw. If the effect misses/fails because of this, absorb it and gain one Moon Shard.

Stored fragment

Moon Shard

The stored fragment: she holds up to PB Moon Shards. Spend one shard instead of 1 CE on a Dark Moon Covenant feature, or consume all shards when a feature says so. Shards vanish on a long rest.

You can hold up to PB Moon Shards. Spend one shard instead of 1 CE on a Dark Moon Covenant feature, or consume all shards when a feature says so. Shards vanish on a long rest.

Field notes. Subject: the sorcerer whose cursed energy formed in the image of a cold moon — a severance that never finished. Three truths govern her. First, the moon keeps what it cuts: Cold Moon Guard devours ranged sorcery outright, and the devoured attacks return as Moon Shards — stored fragments spent as cursed energy. Second, weakness is branded before it is exploited: Lunar Sigils step a creature’s resistance to technique damage down one rung — immunity to resistance to normal — so the covenant’s own cold strikes where it was forbidden. Third, nothing here is borrowed from any heaven: the spirits she calls are not summons but refusals to cut — the dead she could not bring herself to sever, kept in memory until memory learned to fight. Mark it: one person’s cold, deciding what the battlefield is allowed to contain.

Rules integration

Edge cases and shared systems.

These rulings are designed around the revised Player Guide rather than the raw PDF where the two differ.

Lunar Sigils are technique marks and can be suppressed or removed by appropriate cleansing.
Causal Severance affects external links, not identity, class levels, or self-owned CT access.
Absorbing a technique does not teach or copy it.
Spirit familiars are shikigami-like manifestations and do not independently use your other techniques.
Technique DC: 8 + proficiency bonus + your chosen Cursed Energy ability modifier (Wisdom or Charisma unless your class says otherwise).
Direct criticals: rolled weapon, unarmed, and CE attacks can critical normally. After a qualifying critical, roll the campaign's unmodified Black Flash confirmation d20; a natural 20 triples the completed critical damage. Autonomous Domain events, damage-over-time, hazards, and unrolled pulses cannot Black Flash.
Maximum Output: unless a feature says otherwise, double that feature's base CE cost and enhance one supported axis—damage, range, duration, or accuracy/DC—using the campaign's Maximum Output rules. Named Maximum Techniques and Domains do not receive generic Maximum Output.
Technique Focus: effects marked Focus occupy the shared Technique Focus slot. Opening a Domain ends other Focus effects unless the Domain expressly preserves them.
Two-technique characters: these guides assume each technique progresses from character/class level as agreed by the campaign. Multiclassing itself does not grant another Innate Technique.
