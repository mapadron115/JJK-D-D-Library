Core law

What the technique actually does.

Rules are written to plug into the finalized campaign glossary: normal actions stay normal, direct attacks roll, and only named Domain events bypass the initial roll.

Action or replace one attack · 1 CE

Lunar Bolt

Ranged CE attack within Lunar Range. Hit deals 2 Lunar Dice cold/force and places one Lunar Sigil for 1 minute.

Debuff

Lunar Sigil

A creature with a sigil has its resistance to technique damage reduced one step against your Dark Moon features (immunity→resistance→normal). Additional sigils do not stack this step unless a later feature says so.

Reaction · 2 CE

Cold Moon Guard

When a visible ranged technique attack or projectile targets you or crosses within 10 feet, create a small dark moon. Reduce the attack roll by PB or add PB to your saving throw. If the effect misses/fails because of this, absorb it and gain one Moon Shard.

Stored fragment

Moon Shard

You can hold up to PB Moon Shards. Spend one shard instead of 1 CE on a Dark Moon Covenant feature, or consume all shards when a feature says so. Shards vanish on a long rest.

Action · 10 CE: choose one creature or object within 120 feet and one external ongoing binding on it—summoner control, tracking curse, borrowed buff, remote command, possession tether, or barrier lock. Make a CE ability check against the source DC/refinement. Success suppresses that link for 1 minute. Pinnacle expression

Domain, Maximum, or equivalent.

Not every technique needs a Domain. When one exists, its sure-hit is a unique autonomous initiative-count-20 event unless the Domain is explicitly rule-based.

Age of Stars — Moon Beyond Causality

Level 18 · Full-turn action · 20 CE · 90-foot radius · 1 minute. Gravity dims, stars appear beneath everyone’s feet, and a silent Dark Moon moves through the barrier.

Initiative 20 — Dark Moon Passage: the Moon crosses every chosen hostile. Each unprotected target automatically takes 5d12 cold + 5d12 force, gains two Lunar Sigils, and all external buffs, summons attached to it, and remote-control links affecting it are suppressed until the next initiative 20. A protected creature makes a Wisdom save; success halves damage and prevents sigils/suppression.

This never switches off the target’s own innate technique merely for existing inside the Domain.

Rules integration

Edge cases and shared systems.

These rulings are designed around the revised Player Guide rather than the raw PDF where the two differ.

Lunar Sigils are technique marks and can be suppressed or removed by appropriate cleansing.
Causal Severance affects external links, not identity, class levels, or self-owned CT access.
Absorbing a technique does not teach or copy it.
Spirit familiars are shikigami-like manifestations and do not independently use your other techniques.
Technique DC: 8 + proficiency bonus + your chosen Cursed Energy ability modifier (Wisdom or Charisma unless your class says otherwise).
Direct criticals: rolled weapon, unarmed, and CE attacks can critical normally. After a qualifying critical, roll the campaign's unmodified Black Flash confirmation d20; a natural 20 triples the completed critical damage. Autonomous Domain events, damage-over-time, hazards, and unrolled pulses cannot Black Flash.
Maximum Output: unless a feature says otherwise, double that feature's base CE cost and enhance one supported axis—damage, range, duration, or accuracy/DC—using the campaign's Maximum Output rules. Named Maximum Techniques and Domains do not receive generic Maximum Output.
Technique Focus: effects marked Focus occupy the shared Technique Focus slot. Opening a Domain ends other Focus effects unless the Domain expressly preserves them.
Two-technique characters: these guides assume each technique progresses from character/class level as agreed by the campaign. Multiclassing itself does not grant another Innate Technique.
