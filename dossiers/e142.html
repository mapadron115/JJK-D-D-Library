Hive Evolution

Tier V · Special Grade+Hive slots / shared traits / shikigami swarm

Core Engine — Hive Traits. Every Sliver shares every equipped Hive Trait. Know 2 traits at level 1 and learn more through progression/discovery. Equip only a limited number at once to prevent exponential bookkeeping.

Lv.FeatureRules
1Sliver Brood2 CE summon 2 Slivers for 1 minute, AC 13, HP 5+level each, attack 1d6+PB. Traits: choose two of Claw (+1 die once/round), Carapace (+1 AC), Swift (+10 speed), Sinew (+PB damage once/round).
3Hive MindAll Slivers telepathically coordinate; they share one initiative/command and can Help one another without action once/round.
6Trait LibraryLearn Flying, Venom (Con save +1d8 poison), Reach +5, Regeneration 2 HP/turn, or Ward (+1 saves). Equip 3 traits.
9Brood GrowthSummon 3 Slivers; 3 CE. Resolve their attacks as one grouped roll if desired.
11Adaptive SliverReaction 2 CE after Hive takes damage type: gain resistance to it until start next turn, shared by all Slivers.
13Advanced TraitsLearn Double Strike (one extra attack total for hive), Leech (heal you 1d8 when hive damages), Psionic (damage psychic/force), or Diffusion (first AoE damage each round -PB). Equip 4.
15Sliver Queen6 CE summon Queen for 1 minute, AC 18, HP 100, two 3d10 attacks; counts as all Slivers and shares traits. Ordinary brood may remain.
18Domain — The First HiveInitiative 20 create one temporary Sliver token adjacent to each of up to PB hostiles. Tokens make one 3d8 shared-trait attack then dissolve at next count 20.
20Sliver OverlordEquip 6 traits. Brood becomes 5 Slivers. Once/turn switch one equipped trait as bonus action. Queen first use/long rest costs 0.
Maximum — Sliver Legion (10 CE): for 3 rounds all known Hive Traits become equipped simultaneously. Summon five temporary 1-HP Slivers; afterwards your equipped traits fall to 2 until short rest.

Technique Feats

Gemhide: sacrifice one Sliver to regain 1 CE.Hibernation: dismiss brood as reaction to halve AoE damage.Crystalline: +1 saves for hive.Necrotic: when Sliver dies, adjacent hostile takes 1d8 necrotic.
Shared rulings: Technique DC = 8 + PB + CE ability modifier. Direct rolled attacks may crit and use the campaign Black Flash procedure when eligible. Tokens, autonomous summons, passive damage, and initiative-20 Domain effects cannot Black Flash. Maximum Output modifies ordinary supported techniques, never named Maxima or Domains.
