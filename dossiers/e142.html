Hive Evolution

Hive slots / shared traits / shikigami swarm

Power tierTier V · Special Grade+
Core law

What the technique actually does.

Core Engine — Hive Traits. Every Sliver shares every equipped Hive Trait. Know 2 traits at level 1 and learn more through progression/discovery. Equip only a limited number at once to prevent exponential bookkeeping.

Full progression

Level 1 through 20.

1Sliver Brood

2 CE summon 2 Slivers for 1 minute, AC 13, HP 5+level each, attack 1d6+PB. Traits: choose two of Claw (+1 die once/round), Carapace (+1 AC), Swift (+10 speed), Sinew (+PB damage once/round).

3Hive Mind

All Slivers telepathically coordinate; they share one initiative/command and can Help one another without action once/round.

6Trait Library

Learn Flying, Venom (Con save +1d8 poison), Reach +5, Regeneration 2 HP/turn, or Ward (+1 saves). Equip 3 traits.

9Brood Growth

Summon 3 Slivers; 3 CE. Resolve their attacks as one grouped roll if desired.

11Adaptive Sliver

Reaction 2 CE after Hive takes damage type: gain resistance to it until start next turn, shared by all Slivers.

13Advanced Traits

Learn Double Strike (one extra attack total for hive), Leech (heal you 1d8 when hive damages), Psionic (damage psychic/force), or Diffusion (first AoE damage each round -PB). Equip 4.

15Sliver Queen

6 CE summon Queen for 1 minute, AC 18, HP 100, two 3d10 attacks; counts as all Slivers and shares traits. Ordinary brood may remain.

18Domain — The First Hive

Initiative 20 create one temporary Sliver token adjacent to each of up to PB hostiles. Tokens make one 3d8 shared-trait attack then dissolve at next count 20.

20Sliver Overlord

Equip 6 traits. Brood becomes 5 Slivers. Once/turn switch one equipped trait as bonus action. Queen first use/long rest costs 0.

Pinnacle expression

Maximum, Domain, or equivalent.

Maximum — Sliver Legion (10 CE): for 3 rounds all known Hive Traits become equipped simultaneously. Summon five temporary 1-HP Slivers; afterwards your equipped traits fall to 2 until short rest.

Feats

Technique Feats

Gemhide

A sliver's gemhide is condensed mana. As a bonus action, you may sacrifice one of your summoned Slivers within 30 feet: it dissolves into component mana and you regain 1 CE. You cannot sacrifice the Sliver Queen this way — her hide is load-bearing. A sacrificed sliver cannot be re-summoned until your next turn.

Hibernation

When your brood is caught in an area effect, instinct takes over. As a reaction when one or more of your Slivers would take damage from an area effect, you may dismiss any number of them into hibernation: they vanish into the hive-mind's dormant state, take half damage from the effect, and return at the start of your next turn in their previous spaces (or the nearest free space). Slivers reduced to 0 HP by the halved damage do not return — hibernation cannot save the already dead. Dormant slivers cannot act or be targeted.

Crystalline

Your Slivers' refractive hides turn blades aside: as a reaction when a Sliver is hit by an attack, another Sliver within 5 feet can interpose itself, becoming the target instead.

Necrotic

A dying sliver's final act is spite. When one of your Slivers is destroyed, one hostile creature adjacent to it takes 1d8 necrotic damage with no save, as rupturing ichor sprays outward. This is not an attack and cannot be redirected — it is simply what happens when sliver-flesh bursts. Slivers sacrificed cleanly, such as through Gemhide, do not burst; their mana is harvested before it can rupture.

Rules integration

Edge cases and shared systems.

Shared rulings: Technique DC = 8 + PB + CE ability modifier. Direct rolled attacks may crit and use the campaign Black Flash procedure when eligible. Tokens, autonomous summons, passive damage, and initiative-20 Domain effects cannot Black Flash. Maximum Output modifies ordinary supported techniques, never named Maxima or Domains.
