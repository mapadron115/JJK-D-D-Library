Core law

What the technique actually does.

Core Engine, Hive Traits. Hive Traits are the whole doctrine: every Sliver shares every equipped trait, no exceptions. The number equipped at once is limited, two at first, rising with mastery, purely to keep the brood countable. The smallest unit of this technique is not the Sliver. It is the hive. Every Sliver shares every equipped Hive Trait. Equip only a limited number at once to prevent exponential bookkeeping.

Field notes. Subject: a chorus that refused to be a crowd. Three truths govern them. First, no member is weaker than the whole: every Sliver shares every equipped Hive Trait, and the number equipped is capped only to keep the brood countable, rising from two to six. Second, the mind is one: the brood shares a single initiative and command, Aids one another freely, and a struck hive may answer with a shared resistance. Third, the brood is the body: the Queen counts as all Slivers and shares their traits, and at the peak the Overlord fields five with six traits equipped, swapping one as a bonus action. Scholars call it loneliness solved by multiplication: the user's energy refused the boundary between self and other. The Queen is not a ruler. She is the answer to the question of who the self is when there are dozens of them.

Full progression

Level 1 through 20.

1Sliver Brood

The first selves. Two energy summons two Slivers for a minute, quick, armored, striking on his technique attack, and he chooses two traits for the whole brood: Claw, Carapace, Swift, or Sinew. One bonus action commands them all. The chorus tunes up. 2 CE summon 2 Slivers for 1 minute (AC 13, HP 5+level each, speed 30 ft; attacks use your CE attack bonus, 1d6+PB). Commanding the brood is a bonus action. Traits: choose two of Claw (+1 die once/round), Carapace (+1 AC), Swift (+10 speed), Sinew (+PB damage once/round).

3Hive Mind

The single thought. His Slivers coordinate telepathically, one initiative, one command, and may Aid one another without spending an action, once per round. The chorus breathes together. All Slivers telepathically coordinate; they share one initiative/command can Help one another without action once/round.

6Trait Library

The shared wardrobe. He learns Flying, Venom, Reach, Regeneration, or Ward, and equips three traits at once. Every Sliver flies if one does; that is the whole point. Learn Flying, Venom (Con save +1d8 poison), Reach +5, Regeneration 2 HP/turn, or Ward (+1 saves). Equip 3 traits.

9Brood Growth

The family grows. Three energy summons three Slivers, their attacks resolvable as one grouped roll. The chorus adds voices. Summon 3 Slivers; 3 CE. Resolve their attacks as one grouped roll if desired.

11Adaptive Sliver

The reflex. As a reaction for two energy, after the hive takes a damage type, the whole brood gains resistance to it until his next turn begins. The chorus learns the key of whatever strikes it. Reaction 2 CE after Hive takes damage type: gain resistance to it until start next turn, shared by all Slivers.

13Advanced Traits

The deeper cuts. He learns Double Strike, Leech, Psionic, or Diffusion, and equips four traits at once. The harmony grows more complex; no voice is left out. Learn Double Strike (one extra attack total for hive), Leech (once per round, heal 1d8 when the hive damages a creature), Psionic (damage psychic/force), or Diffusion (first AoE damage each round -PB). Equip 4.

15Sliver Queen

The answer. Six energy summons the Queen for a minute, heavy armor, a hundred hit points, two three-dice strikes, and she counts as all Slivers, sharing every trait, while the ordinary brood may remain. The soloist, who is also the choir. 6 CE summon Queen for 1 minute, AC 18, HP 100, two 3d10 attacks; counts as all Slivers and shares traits. Ordinary brood may remain.

18Domain, The First Hive

At the twentieth count the original cell opens: one temporary Sliver token appears beside each of up to his proficiency in hostiles, each making one three-dice shared-trait strike before dissolving at the next count. The hive's first memory, reenacted. Initiative 20 create one temporary Sliver token adjacent to each of up to PB hostiles. Tokens make one 3d8 shared-trait attack then dissolve at next count 20.

20Sliver Overlord

The whole mind. He equips six traits, the brood becomes five Slivers, and once per turn he may swap one equipped trait as a bonus action, while the Queen's first summoning each long rest costs nothing. The chorus, conducting itself. Equip 6 traits. Brood becomes 5 Slivers. Once/turn switch one equipped trait as bonus action. Queen first use/long rest costs 0.

Pinnacle expression

Maximum, Domain, or equivalent.

Maximum, Sliver Legion (10 CE; action): Everything, at once. Ten energy: for three rounds every Hive Trait he knows is equipped simultaneously, and five temporary one-hit-point Slivers join, afterward his equipped traits fall to two until a short rest. The whole repertoire in a single chord, and the silence after. for 3 rounds all known Hive Traits become equipped simultaneously. Summon five temporary 1-HP Slivers; afterwards your equipped traits fall to 2 until short rest.

Feats

Technique Feats

Gemhide

A sliver's gemhide is condensed mana. As a bonus action he may sacrifice one summoned Sliver within thirty feet, never the Queen; her hide is load-bearing, dissolving it into one energy regained. The sacrificed cannot be re-summoned until his next turn. The chorus donates a voice to the cause. A sliver's gemhide is condensed mana. As a bonus action, you may sacrifice one of your summoned Slivers within 30 feet: it dissolves into component mana and you regain 1 CE. You cannot sacrifice the Sliver Queen this way, her hide is load-bearing. A sacrificed sliver cannot be re-summoned until your next turn.

Hibernation

When the brood is caught in an area effect, instinct takes over: as a reaction he may dismiss any number of Slivers into hibernation, half damage, returning at his next turn's start, the already-dead staying dead. Dormant slivers cannot act or be targeted. The chorus holds its breath. When your brood is caught in an area effect, instinct takes over. As a reaction when one or more of your Slivers would take damage from an area effect, you may dismiss any number of them into hibernation: they vanish into the hive-mind's dormant state, take half damage from the effect, and return at the start of your next turn in their previous spaces (or the nearest free space). Slivers reduced to 0 HP by the halved damage do not return, hibernation cannot save the already dead. Dormant slivers cannot act or be targeted.

Crystalline

Their refractive hides turn blades aside: as a reaction when a Sliver is hit, another within five feet may interpose itself and become the target. The chorus takes its wounds in rotation. Your Slivers' refractive hides turn blades aside: as a reaction when a Sliver is hit by an attack, another Sliver within 5 feet can interpose itself, becoming the target instead.

Necrotic

A dying sliver's final act is spite: when one is destroyed, one adjacent hostile takes a die of necrotic with no save, rupturing ichor, not an attack, unredirectable. Slivers sacrificed cleanly through Gemhide do not burst; their mana is harvested before it can rupture. Even the exits are choreographed. A dying sliver's final act is spite. When one of your Slivers is destroyed, one hostile creature adjacent to it takes 1d8 necrotic damage with no save, as rupturing ichor sprays outward. This is not an attack and cannot be redirected, it is simply what happens when sliver-flesh bursts. Slivers sacrificed cleanly, such as through Gemhide, do not burst; their mana is harvested before it can rupture.

Rules integration

Edge cases and shared systems.

Shared rulings: Technique DC = 8 + PB + CE ability modifier. Direct rolled attacks may crit and use the campaign Black Flash procedure when eligible. Tokens, autonomous summons, passive damage, and initiative-20 Domain effects cannot Black Flash. Maximum Output modifies ordinary supported techniques, never named Maxima or Domains.
