At-the-table character reference

Granite Blast: action, 1 CE; one ranged CE attack, range 150/600 feet. Damage progression: 3d8 at level 1; 6d8 at 3; 8d8 at 6; 10d8 at 11; 12d8 at 15; and 12d10 at 18. Long-range attacks have disadvantage.

Completed rules

  • Granite Blast is an action and one ranged CE attack at its printed 150/600-foot range. Long range imposes disadvantage normally. Its damage is 3d8 at level 1, 6d8 at 3, 8d8 at 6, 10d8 at 11, 12d8 at 15, and 12d10 at 18.
  • Grape Shot: spend the printed additional CE, choose from two targets up to the smaller of PB or the number of damage dice, and allocate at least one die to each. Make a separate CE attack for each target. A creature can receive only one portion from the same Granite Blast.
  • Guided Shell: after rolling but before the result is declared, spend 1 to 5 CE and add that amount to the attack. This is an explicit exception to the shared +2 accuracy ceiling; it affects only one attack roll and cannot also raise the save DC of that use.
  • Nuclear Shell: spend 1 CE and an action to create an obvious, audible charge. It is held until the end of your next turn and is lost if you become incapacitated or begin another Maximum Technique. Release with an action and no additional base cost: use a 150-foot cone, roll damage once, and apply the printed Dexterity save. “CE modifier dice” means add that many of Granite Blast’s current damage dice.
  • Shooting Range — Dead-Zone Salvo: at initiative 20, the Domain fires three artillery shells that you distribute among hostile creatures; one creature may receive more than one. Each shell deals 5d12 force damage automatically. A protected creature makes one Dexterity save against the combined shells assigned to it, taking half damage on a success. These shells are a single autonomous Domain event: they are not Granite Blast, cannot gain Guided Shell, and cannot critically hit.

Power identity

Why Tier III: it has little narrative reach and low complexity, but its expected—not merely optimized—damage output is an encounter-building fact from early levels onward.

Recovered character-build options

Innate Technique Feats 3

These are optional character-building feats. You must meet each listed prerequisite before selecting it.

Detonate

Standard

Prerequisite: Granite Blast

When activating Granite Blast, you may make it a melee CE attack with 5-foot reach instead of a ranged attack. All cost, damage, and other modifiers remain unchanged.

Canshot

High impact

Prerequisite: Grape Shot

Corrected prerequisite: Grape Shot. Once per Granite Blast activation after one blast hits, spend 3 CE; every other creature within 5 feet of that target takes half the damage actually dealt, with no additional attack or save. The original target is not hit twice.

Maximum: Antimatter Bomb

Pinnacle

Prerequisite: Nuclear Shell

When you activate Nuclear Shell, its cone may become a 100-foot-radius sphere centered on you. You are excluded; all other creatures save normally. Unattended nonmagical objects and structures in the sphere are reduced to dust. Pay Nuclear Shell’s normal cost and charge time.

cursed energy. As simple as it is, its power is undeniable.

being Charisma or Wisdom.
