Cursed Cannon

Extremely high, extremely legible ranged output with splitting, guidance, and a charged cone.

Field notes. Subject: a one-man battery. Three truths govern the cannon. First, there is no subtlety here: Granite Blast is an action and 1 CE for a ranged CE attack at 150/600 feet, 3d8 force at 1st level, 12d10 by 18th. Second, the battery is generous: Grape Shot divides one blast among up to PB targets for 1 extra CE; Guided Shell is spite made precise, 1–5 CE added to the roll, an explicit exception to the +2 accuracy ceiling. Energy this legible refuses, on principle, to miss. Third, the charge is announced: Nuclear Shell is built obvious and audible, held until the end of the next turn, then released as a 150-foot cone. Mark the Domain’s name: Il Gran Dolce, the sweet course, served at every initiative 20, up to three chosen creatures suffering the full blast with no roll and no save. Inside this room, it simply lands.

Full progression

Feature map

1Granite Blast.

Granite Blast

Action · 1 CE · Ranged CE attack, 150/600 ft · On hit: force damage = 3d8 (1st), 6d8 (3rd), 8d8 (6th), 10d8 (11th), 12d8 (15th), 12d10 (18th).

The foundation of the battery: an action, 1 CE, one ranged CE attack at 150/600 feet, 3d8 force at 1st level, 12d10 by 18th. Absolute legibility: no subtlety, no trickery, only the honest arithmetic of a beam. As an action for 1 CE, release a beam of cursed energy: one ranged CE attack, range 150/600 feet (long-range attacks have disadvantage normally). On a hit it deals force damage: 3d8 at level 1; 6d8 at 3rd; 8d8 at 6th; 10d8 at 11th; 12d8 at 15th; and 12d10 at 18th.

3Grape Shot.

Grape Shot

Passive (+1 CE on Granite Blast) · Split the blast among 2 up to PB targets (maximum = the number of damage dice), at least one die each · Separate CE attack per target; a creature can receive only one portion.

Generosity, artillery-style: for 1 extra CE, the blast is divided among up to PB targets, at least one die each. A blast too large for one target, shared. For 1 additional CE, split your Granite Blast among multiple targets: choose from two targets up to the smaller of your PB or the number of damage dice, allocating at least one die to each. Make a separate CE attack for each target. A creature can receive only one portion from the same Granite Blast.

6Guided Shell.

Guided Shell

Free action · After the roll but before the result: spend 1-5 CE and add that amount to the attack roll · Explicit exception to the shared +2 accuracy ceiling; affects one attack roll only; cannot also raise that use's save DC.

Spite, made precise: after the roll but before the result, spend 1–5 CE to add to the attack, an explicit exception to the shared +2 accuracy ceiling. Energy this legible refuses, on principle, to miss. After rolling but before the result is declared, spend 1 to 5 CE and add that amount to the attack roll. This is an explicit exception to the shared +2 accuracy ceiling; it affects only one attack roll and cannot also raise the save DC of that use.

11Nuclear Shell.

Nuclear Shell

Action · 1 CE · Create an obvious, audible charge (held until the end of your next turn; lost if incapacitated or you begin another Maximum) · Release with an action, no added base cost: 150-ft cone, damage rolled once (current Granite Blast dice + CE modifier dice of the same die); each creature makes the printed Dexterity save.

The held charge: an action and 1 CE to build it, obvious, audible, held until the end of the next turn, then released as a 150-foot cone of Granite Blast damage plus CE-modifier dice. The battery announces its intentions. It does not need to hide them. Spend 1 CE and an action to create an obvious, audible charge. It is held until the end of your next turn and is lost if you become incapacitated or begin another Maximum Technique. Release it with an action and no additional base cost: it becomes a 150-foot cone, roll damage once (your current Granite Blast damage plus additional dice equal to your CE modifier, each die matching Granite Blast's current damage die, d8, or d10 from level 18) and each creature makes the printed Dexterity save.

Pinnacle expression

Maximum, Domain, or equivalent.

Domain Expansion, Il Gran Dolce:

A grand dining hall materializes, all marble and candlelight, set for a dessert course that will never be served to anyone living. The tables are laid with confections too perfect to be real, sugar spun into architecture, and the chandeliers drip with something thicker than wax. It is the sweetest room you will ever die in.

The sweet course, served on schedule: at every initiative count 20, up to three chosen creatures suffer the full Granite Blast, no attack roll, no initial save. The battery no longer aims. Inside this room, it simply lands. 18: full-turn action, 20 CE; 60-foot-radius enclosed Domain centered on you; 1 minute; Burnout 10 rounds. Sure-Hit Event, Il Gran Dolce: at initiative count 20 each round, choose up to three creatures inside; each suffers the full effects of Granite Blast, with no attack roll and no initial save. A creature protected by an anti-domain defense makes a Dexterity saving throw vs your CE save DC (invented); on a success it takes half damage.

Rules integration

Edge cases and shared systems.

Rules priority: the reviewed rulings, explicit codex clarifications, and the level progression always control when original wording conflicts. Legacy Domain auto-hit language, action terminology, event timing, missing saves, and older scaling never override revised player rules.
Why Tier III: it has little narrative reach and low complexity, but its expected—not merely optimized—damage output is an encounter-building fact from early levels onward.
