Cursed Rope

Thirty feet of cord that hunts on a word, and refuses to let go on another.

Field notes. Subject: a cord that listens. Three truths govern it. First, two words are the whole doctrine: one spoken as an action sends the rope hunting, twenty feet, a Dexterity save against DC 15, or restrained, and a second, spoken as a bonus action, undoes it. Bind and release; a pair of syllables. Second, the snare is monogamous: one creature at a time, within the rope's thirty feet. Choose your catch. Third, it mends but never resurrects: AC 20, twenty hit points, one returned every five minutes while it endures, and true death at zero. Hence the maxim: the snare that listens is worth a hundred that merely lie in wait.

Features & procedures

Properties & techniques

Exact player-facing procedures

Binding Word

Hold one end, speak the word, and the other end hunts. Twenty feet of reach, one save, or restrained. This rope is 30 feet long and weighs 3 pounds. If you hold one end of the rope and use an action to speak its command word, the other end darts forward to entangle a creature you can see within 20 feet of you. The target must succeed on a DC 15 Dexterity saving throw or become restrained.

Release Word

The second syllable undoes the first: a bonus action frees the catch, or the catch may free itself, with an action and a strong pull. You can release the creature by using a bonus action to speak a second command word. A target restrained by the rope can use an action to make a DC 15 Strength or Dexterity check (target's choice). On a success, the creature is no longer restrained by the rope.

Living Cord

The cord is alive enough to mend, but cut it to zero and it dies like anything else. The rope has AC 20 and 20 hit points. It regains 1 hit point every 5 minutes as long as it has at least 1 hit point. If the rope drops to 0 hit points, it is destroyed.

Rules integration

Edge cases and shared systems.

Campaign canon notes

Note: The rope restrains only one creature at a time; its 30-foot length while you hold one end limits its working reach to the 20-foot entanglement range. The rope's self-repair does not restore it from destruction.
