The Unfinished Score

It is beautiful. That is the first thing every researcher writes, and the last thing some of them write. The score is incomplete. It wants to be finished. It has opinions about whose hands do the finishing, and it has been waiting for hands like yours.

Combat roleStriker
Core law

The Composition

The First Bars.

Bonus action, 2 CE: begin performing (concentration, up to 1 minute). While performing, your damaging techniques deal +1d6 psychic damage. If you take damage while performing: Wisdom save DC 12 or be compelled to keep performing — you cannot end it voluntarily; repeat the save at the end of each of your turns.

The Compulsion.

Compelled performance is not charm. It is the music’s gravity — the file distinguishes carefully, because the countermeasures differ and the outcomes differ more.

The Price.

Every bar costs. The file tracks the cost in the margins, in handwriting that deteriorates across the entries. The later entries are harder to read. The file has noticed.

The Draw.

The Final Note.

1/long rest: complete it — 10d10 psychic damage in a 60-ft radius (Wisdom save for half). You take 4d10 psychic yourself. The composer always pays.

The Unfinished Score

Earworm

Creatures damaged by your boosted techniques hear the melody for 1 hour: disadvantage on concentration saves. It follows them. It is catchy. That is the worst part.

The Unfinished Score

Unfinished Business

After failing three compulsion saves, the Score notices you — a GM hook, to be resolved in play. The file does not say what noticing entails. The file’s author declined to elaborate and has taken leave.

Subject file

Containment & observation record.

Containment Procedures

The score is kept incomplete. No one transcribes the missing bars. This is the first rule, the last rule, and the only rule that has never been broken — because everyone who has tried is in the file.

Performance is limited to supervised sessions. Humming is prohibited site-wide. The file acknowledges the absurdity and enforces it anyway.

Handwriting is monitored. Deterioration is logged. The log is the saddest document the file’s author has ever maintained, and she has maintained the Dispensary’s beverage log.

Research Log

Research log. 08/2024 — Acquisition: recovered from an estate sale, catalogued as ‘unfinished composition, composer unknown.’ The auctioneer hummed two bars during the viewing. The auctioneer has not hummed since, and does not remember the viewing.

Research log. 02/2025 — The boost is mapped: +1d6 psychic per damaging technique while performing, scaling with thresholds, concentration. The compulsion: Wisdom DC 12 when damaged, save each turn. The file notes the DC is ‘insultingly low’ and the compliance rate ‘total.’

Research log. 10/2025 — The Audience confirmed: 30 feet, frightened, Wisdom vs CE DC. A hostile cursed spirit spent three rounds approaching the music instead of attacking. The file classifies this as ‘the most polite combat on record’ and moves on.

Research log. 04/2026 — The Final Note performed once, under conditions the file describes as ‘necessary’ and declines to detail: 10d10 in 60 feet, 4d10 to the performer. The performer’s hands are displayed in the file. The file does not display them proudly. No second performance is scheduled.

Incident Report — Four Bars.

A researcher — trained musician, full briefing, explicit prohibition — completed four bars during a supervised session. What happened to his hands is in the file, with photographs. What happened to the session’s audio recording is also in the file: it plays four bars, then room tone, then, faintly, under the room tone, a fifth bar beginning. The recording has been destroyed. Three copies existed. The file is confident all three are destroyed. The file counts the copies daily.
