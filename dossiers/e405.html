Properties & techniques

Exact player-facing procedures

Action

Pluck a Bead

As an action, you can pluck one bead from the bracelet to gain Advantage on Strength (Athletics) checks for 1 minute. The plucked bead disappears immediately after it is used. Once all three beads have been plucked, the bracelet loses its magic.
