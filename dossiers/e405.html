Crystal Bracelet

A Grade 4 cursed item — three crystal beads on a leather bracelet.

FormCursed Item
GradeGrade 4
AttunementNot required

Field notes. Subject: the body, reminded. Three crystal beads on a leather cord — pluck one, and for a minute your limbs remember what it is to be strong. Two truths govern it. First, the strength is borrowed, not granted: advantage on Strength (Athletics) checks for one minute per bead, and each plucked bead is gone the instant it is spent. Second, the bracelet keeps a strict account: three beads, three minutes, and when the third is gone the cord is only leather. Mark this: the body already knows how to be strong — that is why the reminder works. What the bracelet sells is not power. It is timing.

Properties & techniques

Exact player-facing procedures

Action

Pluck a Bead

An action, a pluck, a minute of remembered strength — advantage on every Athletics check until the minute runs out. The bead vanishes in the using; the bracelet counts down to ordinary leather. As an action, you can pluck one bead from the bracelet to gain Advantage on Strength (Athletics) checks for 1 minute. The plucked bead disappears immediately after it is used. Once all three beads have been plucked, the bracelet loses its magic.
