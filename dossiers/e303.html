Relic profile

10 Sanctified Charges maximum

Jujutsu origin

The Grail is a vessel whose legend is overwhelmingly about sacred contact, restoration, and impossible grace. In jujutsu terms, its structure has become uniquely tolerant of positive energy: it can hold RCE without being destroyed by it.

Consecrated Reservoir

During a short or long rest, a creature may spend CE into the Grail, converting 1 CE into 1 Sanctified Charge. The Grail can accept no more than PB charges per long rest and holds 10.

Healing Draught — Action, 1 charge

A living creature touching the Grail regains 20 HP. A Cursed Spirit or other creature harmed by positive energy instead takes 4d10 radiant damage.

Restoration — Action, 3 charges

End one of the following on a touched creature: blinded, deafened, paralyzed, poisoned, stunned, or one ongoing non-Special-Grade curse. You may instead repair one damaged limb that has not been removed.

Make Whole — 5 charges, 1 minute

Restore a severed limb, remove one level of exhaustion, or attempt to end a Special Grade curse. For a Special Grade curse, make an Arcana check against the source's CE save DC.

Second Breath — Reaction, 10 charges

When a living creature within 5 ft would fall to 0 HP, it instead falls to 1 HP, then regains 50 HP and removes all non-permanent limb injuries.

Binding Vow — Vessel of Mercy

The Grail cannot use its positive energy to directly damage living humans. It may harm Cursed Spirits and other beings naturally destroyed by RCE.

Designer’s note. It does not resurrect the truly dead; it restores a living pattern that still exists.

Rules integration

Cursed-tool attunement

Campaign canon

Cursed-tool attunement: you have PB attunement slots (2–6). This tool occupies one slot and requires a one-hour ritual. Attunement ends voluntarily over a short rest, on death, or when you replace it while over capacity. Distance does not break attunement. Cursed-tool attunement is separate from magic-item attunement.
