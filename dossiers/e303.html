The Holy Grail

A consecrated vessel that banks cursed energy as Sanctified Charges and spends them as pure positive-energy medicine, healing draughts, limb restoration, even a last-breath reversal. It cannot harm the living; its violence is reserved for curses.

The Holy Grail

Metaphysical Law: What enters as cursed energy may leave purified.

FormVessel
EngineConsecrated Reservoir
Attunement1 hour*

Field notes. Subject: a hospital that eats cursed energy. Three truths govern the vessel. First, the exchange is honest: during a rest, 1 CE becomes 1 Sanctified Charge, no more than PB per long rest, ten at most. Second, the medicine climbs from draught to miracle: one charge, a touch restores 20 HP; three, a condition ended or a limb repaired; five and a minute, a limb regrown, exhaustion lifted, even a Special Grade curse contested; ten, a reaction when a life within five feet would end, it stands at 1 HP, takes back 50, and sheds its limb injuries. Third, mercy has a direction: the Grail cannot turn positive energy on living humans, but what positive energy destroys, curses and their kin, it strikes for 4d10 radiant. Mark the ceiling: it restores the living pattern. The truly dead stay dead.

Relic profile

10 Sanctified Charges maximum

Jujutsu origin

The Grail is a vessel whose legend is overwhelmingly about sacred contact, restoration, and impossible grace. In jujutsu terms, its structure has become uniquely tolerant of positive energy: it can hold RCE without being destroyed by it.

Consecrated Reservoir

Rest activity · During a short or long rest, a creature may spend CE into the Grail at 1 CE → 1 Sanctified Charge; the Grail accepts at most PB charges per long rest and holds 10.

During a short or long rest, a creature may spend CE into the Grail, converting 1 CE into 1 Sanctified Charge. The Grail can accept no more than PB charges per long rest and holds 10.

Healing Draught — Action, 1 charge

Action · Spend 1 charge · A living creature touching the Grail regains 20 HP; a Cursed Spirit or other creature harmed by positive energy instead takes 4d10 radiant damage.

A living creature touching the Grail regains 20 HP. A Cursed Spirit or other creature harmed by positive energy instead takes 4d10 radiant damage.

Restoration — Action, 3 charges

Action · Spend 3 charges · End one of the following on a touched creature: blinded, deafened, paralyzed, poisoned, stunned, or one ongoing non-Special-Grade curse; or repair a damaged (not removed) limb.

End one of the following on a touched creature: blinded, deafened, paralyzed, poisoned, stunned, or one ongoing non-Special-Grade curse. You may instead repair one damaged limb that has not been removed.

Make Whole — 5 charges, 1 minute

5 charges · 1 minute · Restore a severed limb, remove one level of exhaustion, or attempt to end a Special Grade curse (Arcana check against the source's CE save DC).

Restore a severed limb, remove one level of exhaustion, or attempt to end a Special Grade curse. For a Special Grade curse, make an Arcana check against the source's CE save DC.

Second Breath — Reaction, 10 charges

Reaction · Spend 10 charges · When a living creature within 5 ft would fall to 0 HP: it falls to 1 HP instead, then regains 50 HP and removes all non-permanent limb injuries.

When a living creature within 5 ft would fall to 0 HP, it instead falls to 1 HP, then regains 50 HP and removes all non-permanent limb injuries.

Binding Vow — Vessel of Mercy

Passive restriction · Cannot use positive energy to directly damage living humans; may harm Cursed Spirits and other beings naturally destroyed by RCE.

The Grail cannot use its positive energy to directly damage living humans. It may harm Cursed Spirits and other beings naturally destroyed by RCE.

Designer’s note. It does not resurrect the truly dead; it restores a living pattern that still exists.

Rules integration

Cursed-tool attunement

Campaign canon

Cursed-tool attunement: you have PB attunement slots (2–6). This tool occupies one slot and requires a one-hour ritual. Attunement ends voluntarily over a short rest, on death, or when you replace it while over capacity. Distance does not break attunement. Cursed-tool attunement is separate from magic-item attunement.
