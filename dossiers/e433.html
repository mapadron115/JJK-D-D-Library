Consumption

Pugilist surge

After you drink this potion, each unarmed strike you make deals an extra 1d6 necrotic damage on a hit. This effect lasts 10 minutes. This potion is a thick green fluid that tastes like spinach.

Observer’s account

Field notes. Subject: a meal that punches back. Thick, green, spinach-flavored — and for ten minutes every unarmed strike lands with cursed rot, an extra 1d6 necrotic on each hit. Two truths. First, the dose is the doctrine: one drink, one drinker, one ten-minute window — a weapon with an expiry date, so the fight must be brought to the enemy, not awaited. Second, it asks nothing of the drinker’s technique: no CE cost per swing, no attunement — the fists simply become cursed for a while, and even the untrained arm profits. Mark this: potions are logistics, not power. Hence the maxim: drink deep before the charge, and let your knuckles carry the rot the enemy did not plan for.
