Deal-Maker

Field notes. Subject: Rumpelstiltskin, deal-maker of Far Far Away. Three truths govern the technique. First: the boon is the bait, never the gift — healing, shelter, advantage, whatever the heart names, delivered at once and in full, because a hungry mark signs faster. Second: the price is the technique — deferred, denominated, and denominated again in the Fine Print, which is not deception but disclosure at a font size of the author's choosing. Third: the vow is the enforcement — Heaven itself witnesses every signature, so collection is not violence but arithmetic. And mark the inversion: the man who sells days never spends his own. He rents yours.

Feature map

Level-by-level features

L1

The Deal

Action · 2 CE.

Offer a contract to a creature you can communicate with (willing, or charmed by you): an immediate boon — healing of 2d8+CHA, 12 temporary hit points, a 1d6 Edge die for one roll within the hour, or cleansing blinded, deafened, frightened, or poisoned — against a deferred price: 3d8 psychic damage at dawn, a lost Hit Die, a taboo act, or an owed favor. One Deal per creature; you may hold active Deals equal to your proficiency bonus. A Deal is a binding vow — it cannot be dispelled, only paid or collected.

L1

Fine Print

Passive · noted secretly.

When you strike a Deal, choose a hidden clause the GM keeps: Doubles at Dawn (an unpaid price doubles each dawn), Transferable (you may transfer the Deal as a bonus action; the new debtor makes a WIS save vs your CE DC to refuse), Early Due (the price becomes payable the instant you speak the debtor's name), or In Kind (an unpaid price is taken in memories — one lost skill or tool proficiency). A target that spends 1 minute reading the contract and beats your CE DC with an INT check learns the Fine Print before signing.

L3

Signed in Ink

Passive · quill manifests for 1 CE.

Your Deals are sealed with a manifested quill. A debtor cannot dodge collection by silence or absence: the first time a debtor attempts to evade a due Debt — fleeing, refusing to answer, hiding — it automatically fails its next saving throw against your Debt Collection. Deals are binding vows, not spells: they cannot be counterspelled or dispelled by anyone but you.

L6

Debt Collection

Action · 4 CE · 60 ft.

Target a creature holding one of your due Debts: it pays the price now, or makes a CHA save vs your CE DC. On a failure you choose the terms — the price paid at double, or the Debt converts into a Binding Vow: one task you name, disclosed, witnessed by Heaven. On a success the price is paid as written. Either way, the Deal closes.

L11

All Magic Comes with a Price

Passive · 30-ft aura.

You sense every bargain struck within 1 mile — its shape, its parties, its price (never its Fine Print). When a creature within 30 ft of you regains hit points or gains temporary hit points from a source you did not provide, you may use your reaction to levy the market's toll: the creature pays 1 CE or takes 1d4 psychic damage.

L14

The Escape Clause

Reaction · once per long rest.

When you take damage or fail a saving throw, invoke the escape clause: one creature holding one of your Debts makes the saving throw in your place, or takes the damage instead. The price, as always, finds a payer.

Maximum Technique

The Ultimate Contract

Action · 10 CE · touch · the target must be willing or incapacitated.

Rewrite one day of the target's life. Choose one decision, event, or outcome from the last 24 hours and declare what happened instead; work with the GM to adjust the present accordingly. Creatures present for the original event make an INT save vs your CE DC — success means they remember both timelines. The target immediately owes you a Debt of your choosing, payable at once, no save. Table-consent feature: the rewritten day stands only if the GM and the affected players agree it is playable. (Shrek learned the price of a day he never lived. The contract does not care.)

Level 20 · Capstone

King for a Day

Once per long rest · action.

For 1 hour you are sovereign of the scene: crowds of neutral creatures treat you as royalty — they bow, offer hospitality, obey reasonable requests — and you have advantage on CHA checks against them. While it lasts, striking a Deal is a bonus action for you, and Debt Collection costs 2 CE. (For one day, Far Far Away was his. For one hour, anywhere is.)

Innate Technique Feats

Build-defining options.

These are optional character-building feats. You must meet each listed prerequisite before selecting it.

Prerequisite: The Deal

The Quill Remembers

He always knew what you wanted. That was the whole trick. When a creature accepts your Deal, you learn its heart's desire — the DM tells you one true want it would trade anything for. A creature whose desire you know never thinks to read your Fine Print.

Prerequisite: Fine Print

A Footnote's Weight

Your Fine Print may name a taboo — one simple act. If the debtor performs it before the price is paid, the Debt becomes due immediately, and you may use Debt Collection as a reaction. (Read the contract. No, really. Read it.)

Prerequisite: Debt Collection

The Broker's Cut

When you collect a Debt, you siphon the price itself: regain CE equal to half the psychic damage the collection dealt, up to your CHA modifier. (The house always takes its cut. He is the house.)

Prerequisite: All Magic Comes with a Price

Signed, Sealed

Once per long rest, when a creature within 30 ft of you strikes any bargain, vow, or deal that isn't yours, you may use your reaction to interpose your quill — you are added as witness and co-signatory. You learn its exact terms, and each party pays you a toll of 1 CE (or 1d4 psychic damage if they have none). (Every market needs its witness. He volunteers.)
