Core law

What the technique actually does.

Core law, every strike hits twice. Two animals, one law. The pistol shrimp snaps its claw and fires a bubble that collapses hotter than the sun's surface — the snap is not the weapon, the bubble's death is. The mantis shrimp clubs its prey at the speed of a bullet, and the strike is so fast a second bubble is born on impact — the punch lands, and then the void lands after it. The technique unites them: cursed energy manifests a cavitation bubble in every strike, and the bubble's collapse is the second hit. You do not hit twice. The water hits twice — once for you, once for the void.

Field notes. Three truths from the tide pool. First: the bubble is the blow. For the pistol shrimp, the claw is only the trigger; the collapse is what kills. For the mantis, the club is the blow and the bubble is the echo. The technique takes both — every strike carries its own collapse. Second: speed is armor. The mantis strike is too fast to see, which is why nothing ever blocks it — you do not survive the club by being tougher, you survive by never being where it lands. The technique's defense is the same: strike first, strike fast, let the shell handle the rest. Third: the eyes see what the claws cannot. Sixteen colors, polarized light — the mantis perceives a world its prey cannot imagine. The technique's sight is the same: you see the cursed energy moving before the technique is ever thrown.

Full progression

Level 1 through 20.

1Cavitation Strike

When you hit a creature with an unarmed strike or a melee weapon attack, you may spend 1 CE to collapse a cavitation bubble on the point of impact. The target takes an additional 1d6 thunder damage (2d6 at level 6, 3d6 at level 11) and must succeed on a Strength saving throw against your CE save DC or be pushed 10 feet away from you.

The bread and butter: every punch hits twice. The first hit is yours; the second belongs to the void. The push is the shockwave shoving the target back — the bubble dies outward, and everything near it learns which direction “out” is. One CE per bubble keeps the ledger honest: you pay for every second hit.

3Mantis Eyes · Carapace

Mantis Eyes. Passive · You perceive the world in sixteen colors. You can see invisible creatures and objects, see through magical darkness, and automatically discern visual illusions. You perceive cursed energy flows: you can tell whether a creature you can see possesses cursed energy, and you perceive cursed-energy constructs that would otherwise be invisible.

The mantis shrimp sees colors that have no names — sixteen receptors where you have three, polarized light where you have glare. The technique opens the same eyes. Nothing hides from you by being unseen: invisibility, darkness, illusion, all of it is just light arranged to lie, and you read light the way others read words.

Carapace. Bonus action · 2 CE · 1 minute · Hardened exoskeleton plates manifest over your body. Your AC increases by 2 while Carapace is active.

The shell is the other half of the animal. The shrimp is soft inside and armored outside, and the armor is not a metaphor — it is plates, grown in a breath, hard as the club itself. Raise it when the fight turns toward you. The shell does not make you invincible; it makes you the thing that survives its own first mistake.

6Snap Shot

When you take the Attack action, you may replace one attack with a Snap Shot. Snap your fingers: fire a cavitation bubble at a creature within 60 feet. Make a ranged CE attack. On hit: 3d8 thunder damage (4d8 at level 11), and the target must succeed on a Constitution saving throw against your CE save DC or be Deafened until the end of its next turn.

The pistol half, at range. The pistol shrimp never touches its prey — it snaps, and the bubble does the killing from inches away. You snap, and the bubble flies sixty feet. The crack is louder than a gunshot; the deafness is the sound arriving after the damage, the way thunder arrives after lightning. Mid power, versatile, still leaning on the L1 — the Snap is the bubble thrown, not the bubble ridden.

11Shellbreaker

Action · 6 CE · Make a single melee attack with advantage. On hit: your weapon damage + 6d10 bludgeoning damage. The target’s armor is cracked: its AC is reduced by 2 until the end of your next turn. If the target is an object or a construct, it takes double damage from this strike.

High power, not a finisher. The mantis club shatters shells, cracks aquarium glass, breaks what was built to not break. Shellbreaker is the same strike with the safety off — the advantage is the speed (too fast to dodge), the damage is the club, and the cracked AC is the shell failing. Three or four of these across a fight and the opponent’s armor is a suggestion. The doubling against objects and constructs is the technique remembering what shells are: things that break.

Pinnacle expression

Maximum, Domain, or equivalent.

The water around your fist boils before you move. The air shudders. For one instant, the world holds its breath — and then the strike lands, and the snap follows, and for a fraction of a second there is a light in your palm as bright as the surface of the sun, and everything near it learns what a bubble’s death feels like at full size.

Maximum: Supercavitation. Three modes — the mantis, the pistol, and both at once.

Boiling Strike (mantis). Action · 10 CE · Make one melee attack with advantage. On hit: your weapon damage + 8d10 bludgeoning + 4d10 thunder damage. The strike boils the air around the impact: each creature within 10 feet of the target (other than the target) takes 4d10 fire damage (Dexterity save for half).

Solar Snap (pistol). Action · 10 CE · Snap your fingers. Each creature in a 30-foot cone must make a Constitution saving throw against your CE save DC. Fail: 8d10 radiant damage and Blinded until the end of its next turn. Success: half damage, not Blinded.

Supernova (combined). Action · 20 CE · Unleash both at once. Make a Boiling Strike against a creature within reach, and simultaneously detonate a Solar Snap as a 30-foot sphere centered on that creature. Both effects apply; the Strike’s attack roll and the Snap’s saving throws are resolved independently — the Snap detonates whether or not the Strike hits.

The Maximum is both animals, and then both at once. The Boiling Strike is the mantis club at full speed — the air itself boils around the impact, and the fire is the water giving up. The Solar Snap is the pistol bubble at full size — sonoluminescence, light and heat like the sun’s surface for an instant, and the blindness is the light arriving with the damage. The Supernova is the two together, and the cost is the point: twenty CE, both effects, no discount. The combination is not efficient. It is final.

Feats

Build-defining options.

Prerequisite: Cavitation Strike

Spearer

Your strikes can switch to the spearer’s barbed spear. Your unarmed strikes gain 10-foot reach and deal piercing damage instead of bludgeoning. On a hit, you may spend 1 CE to drive the barb home: the target is Grappled (escape DC = your CE save DC) as the spear holds it. The mantis has two weapons — the club for smashing, the spear for holding. This is the second one.

Prerequisite: Snap Shot

Sonic Boom

Action · 4 CE · Snap with both claws at once. Each creature within a 20-foot radius of you must make a Constitution saving throw against your CE save DC. Fail: 4d8 thunder damage, knocked Prone, and Deafened until the end of its next turn. Success: half damage, not Prone, not Deafened. The Snap, but as control — the crack becomes a shockwave, and everything standing in it falls down.

Prerequisite: Carapace

Molt

Bonus action · 3 CE · You shed your carapace in an instant. End one condition on yourself (blinded, deafened, grappled, poisoned, or restrained) and gain temporary hit points equal to your level. If your Carapace is active, it ends — you shed the shell to shed the condition. The shrimp outgrows its shell and leaves it behind. So do you.

Prerequisite: Cavitation Strike

Jet Propulsion

Bonus action · 2 CE · You jet backward on a burst of water. You immediately move up to 30 feet in any direction without provoking opportunity attacks, and you gain a swimming speed equal to your walking speed for 1 minute. The shrimp does not run from danger. It jets — backward, fast, gone before the water settles.

Rules integration

Edge cases and shared systems.

Codex rulings: Direct rolled technique attacks can use the campaign's Black Flash procedure when otherwise eligible. Bubble-collapse damage is thunder damage; Boiling Strike's boil is fire damage; Solar Snap's flash is radiant damage.
Shellbreaker's AC reduction does not stack with itself; a new Shellbreaker replaces the old reduction. The reduction ends at the end of your next turn whether or not you attack again.
Supernova's Boiling Strike and Solar Snap resolve independently: the Snap detonates whether or not the Strike hits. The Snap's sphere is centered on the Strike's target, even if the Strike misses.
Molt ends Carapace if it is active — you shed the shell to shed the condition. You cannot Molt and benefit from Carapace in the same turn.
Mantis Eyes automatically discerns visual illusions — no check is required. It does not reveal invisible creatures' identities or intentions, only their presence and position.
Cavitation Strike's push moves the target directly away from you. If the target cannot be pushed (it is restrained, grappled by something immovable, or against a wall), it takes the damage but is not pushed.
