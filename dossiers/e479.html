Cursed Tool

Ring of Lost Love

A Special Grade cursed ring (requires attunement), the Queen of Curses dwells within, and sometimes she lends her strength.

FormCursed Tool (Ring)
GradeSpecial Grade
AttunementRequired

Field notes. Subject: a queen in residence. Three truths govern the ring. First, even idle, she lends her regard: while attuned, +2 to cursed energy save DC and to attack rolls, favor worn like a ribbon. Second, invited in, she dwells: for one minute, as an action, your speed grows by thirty feet, your armor becomes 20 plus proficiency, and fifteen coins of her own temporary energy answer to you. Third, borrowed crowns are never kept: once she has dwelt in you, she will not return for 1d6+2 days, the long absence is the only price the source admits. Mark this: there is no mechanical devil in the contract. The devil is the wait.

Features & procedures

Properties & techniques

Exact player-facing procedures

Queen's favor

Worn and unattended, she still lends her regard: +2 to cursed energy save DC and to attack rolls while attuned. Favor, worn like a ribbon. While attuned to this ring, you gain a +2 bonus to your Cursed Energy save DC and to attack rolls.

Action

Partial Possession

Invite her in. As an action, for one minute: +30 feet of speed, armor of 20 plus proficiency unless yours is higher, and fifteen temporary cursed energy that vanish when she leaves. Then the wait, 1d6+2 days before she will dwell in you again. The source names no other price, and none is invented: the absence is the cost. As an action, you allow the Queen of Curses to temporarily dwell within your body. For 1 minute: your walking speed increases by 30 ft, your AC becomes 20 + your proficiency bonus (unless your current AC is higher), and you gain 15 temporary Cursed Energy that disappears when the duration ends. Once you use the ring in this way, you cannot use it again for 1d6 + 2 days.

Rules integration

Edge cases and shared systems.

Campaign canon notes

Temporary CE: the 15 temporary cursed energy cannot be banked or carried past the minute, unspent energy is lost when Partial Possession ends. The source specifies no mechanical drawback for hosting the Queen; none is invented here, the long cooldown is the cost.
