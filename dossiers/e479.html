Features & procedures

Properties & techniques

Exact player-facing procedures

Queen's favor

While attuned to this ring, you gain a +2 bonus to your Cursed Energy save DC and to attack rolls.

Action

Partial Possession

As an action, you allow the Queen of Curses to temporarily dwell within your body. For 1 minute: your walking speed increases by 30 ft, your AC becomes 20 + your proficiency bonus (unless your current AC is higher), and you gain 15 temporary Cursed Energy that disappears when the duration ends. Once you use the ring in this way, you cannot use it again for 1d6 + 2 days.

Rules integration

Edge cases and shared systems.

Campaign canon notes

Temporary CE: the 15 temporary cursed energy cannot be banked or carried past the minute — unspent energy is lost when Partial Possession ends. The source specifies no mechanical drawback for hosting the Queen; none is invented here — the long cooldown is the cost.
