At-the-table character reference

Recovered printed values & campaign clarifications

Flagged chassis rule:

Fall damage from this technique ignores any type of fall damage reduction or immunity, exactly as printed. This is stronger than the campaign's normal handling and interacts with D002/D007 expectations; it is kept pending zack's final call, not corrected away.

Sweeping Gravity comparison:

Creatures with a Dexterity score below your CE save DC cannot make a Dexterity saving throw against your technique. The printed comparison (score vs. DC) is kept as-is; it is not reinterpreted as an ability check.

Light Fall override:

Light Fall's "take no fall damage when you land" protects those creatures even from the technique's own immunity-bypassing fall damage, as printed.

Shield reduction is flat:

Antigravity Shield's reduction (your CE modifier + your level) is a flat reduction, not a resistance; it stacks with resistance under the general stacking rules.

Domain framing:

Zero Gravity resolves at initiative 20. The auto-failed saves are the Domain's sure-hit effect; Domain clashes use the deterministic 1–1,000 point system. Barrier per D007; burnout per D010.

Dense Antigravity Shield dice:

The feat's reduction is half of the technique's maximum-height fall-damage dice, rolled (d12 dice at 11th level and above under Hard Fall), until the beginning of your next turn — not a flat value.

Core law

Completed rules

Antigravity:

Starting at 1st level, you have learned how to use your technique to manipulate gravity, allowing you to make a small gravitational field around yourself. As an action for 3 cursed energy, you can reverse gravity in a 10 foot-radius 30 foot-high cylinder centered on you for all creatures but yourself until the beginning of your next turn.

All creatures and objects that aren't anchored to the ground in the area fall upward. A creature can make a Dexterity saving throw to grab onto a fixed object it can reach, thus avoiding the fall. If a solid object (such as a ceiling) is encountered in this fall, falling objects and creatures strike it and take the remaining height as fall damage. If an object or creature reaches the top of the area without striking anything, it remains there, oscillating slightly, for the duration. At the end of the duration, affected objects and creatures fall back down, taking fall damage.

The height of the cylinder increases to 50 feet at 3rd level, 100 feet at 11th level and 200 feet at 18th level. Fall damage from this technique ignores any type of fall damage reduction or immunity. Fall damage die becomes d8 at 3rd level and d10 at 6th level.

Gravity Field:

At 3rd level, you gained the following benefits through practicing your technique. Light Fall. As a reaction for 1 CE to falling, you and creatures equal to your proficiency bonus within 120 feet have their rate of descent slowed to 60 feet per round and take no fall damage when you land. Focused System. When you use your innate technique, you can choose a number of creatures equal to your CE modifier + your proficiency bonus to be unaffected by your technique. Disorienting Gravity. Any creature that is lifted upwards by your Antigravity must make a Constitution saving throw. On a failure, they make any attacks at disadvantage and cannot maintain concentration while within your field.

Antigravity Shield:

At 6th level, you have learned to defend yourself from attacks using your antigravity. As a reaction for 2 CE, you can encase yourself in a field of antigravity, causing attack rolls against you to be made at disadvantage and any damage other than psychic is reduced by your CE modifier + your level until the beginning of your next turn. If you are taking the Dodge action, you can use this reaction on another creature within 5 feet.

Boosted System:

At 11th level, you master all aspects of your technique and gain the following improvements to your Antigravity. Lasting Field. You can spend additional CE up to your modifier to increase the range by 10 feet and the duration by 1 round per CE. Sweeping Gravity. Creatures with a Dexterity score below your CE save DC cannot make a Dexterity saving throw against your technique. Nauseating Gravity. If a creature fails a saving throw against your Disorienting Gravity, they are also stunned until the end of their next turn. Hard Fall. Fall damage from your field becomes d12 bludgeoning.

Pinnacle expression

Maximum, Domain, or equivalent.

Domain Expansion: Zero Gravity: 18 — At 18th level you have learned how to expand your domain. At initiative 20, all creatures of your choice are affected by and fail all saving throws against Antigravity, which can be released by you at will. The cylinder height rises to 200 feet.

Innate Technique Feats

Build-defining options.

These are optional character-building feats. You must meet each listed prerequisite before selecting it.

Prerequisite: Must have Gravity Field

Levitation System

As an action for 2 CE, you can cast Levitate. You can spend an additional 2 CE to force the creature to make the save at disadvantage.

Prerequisite: Must have Antigravity Shield

Inverted Shield

As a reaction when you are hit by a melee attack while your Antigravity Shield is active, you may spend 3 CE to invert the field. The attacker must make a Strength saving throw against your CE save DC or be pulled 10 feet toward you and knocked prone.

Prerequisite: Must have Boosted System

Antigravitational Flight

As an action for 3 CE, you can give yourself or another creature you touch a 60 feet hover speed for 10 minutes.

Rules integration

Edge cases and shared systems.

Rules priority: the reviewed rulings, explicit codex clarifications, and character reference above always control when original wording conflicts. Legacy Domain auto-hit language, action terminology, event timing, missing saves, and older scaling never override revised player rules.
Why Tier III: a repeatable zone of reversed gravity with save-denial, stun riders, d12 fall damage that ignores fall-damage defenses, and a defensive shield makes this a top-tier battlefield controller without calamity-scale output.
Flag — immunity bypass: the chassis rule that fall damage from this technique ignores any type of fall damage reduction or immunity is kept as printed pending zack's final call. Do not correct it away at the table without that call.
Flag — score comparison: Sweeping Gravity compares a Dexterity score to the CE save DC. Keep the printed comparison; do not reinterpret it as an ability check.
