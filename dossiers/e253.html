At-the-table character reference

Recovered printed values & campaign clarifications

Flagged chassis rule:

Fall damage from this technique ignores any type of fall damage reduction or immunity, exactly as printed. This is stronger than the campaign's normal handling and interacts with D002/D007 expectations; it is kept pending zack's final call, not corrected away.

Sweeping Gravity comparison:

Creatures with a Dexterity score below your CE save DC cannot make a Dexterity saving throw against your technique. The printed comparison (score vs. DC) is kept as-is; it is not reinterpreted as an ability check.

Light Fall override:

Light Fall's "take no fall damage when you land" protects those creatures even from the technique's own immunity-bypassing fall damage, as printed.

Shield reduction is flat:

Antigravity Shield's reduction (your CE modifier + your level) is a flat reduction, not a resistance; it stacks with resistance under the general stacking rules.

Domain framing:

Zero Gravity resolves at initiative 20. The auto-failed saves are the Domain's sure-hit effect; Domain clashes use the deterministic 1–1,000 point system. Barrier per D007; burnout per D010.

Dense Antigravity Shield dice:

The feat's reduction is half of the technique's maximum-height fall-damage dice, rolled (d12 dice at 11th level and above under Hard Fall), until the beginning of your next turn, not a flat value.

Field notes. Subject: the one who unmade down. Three truths govern him. First, reversal, not escape: the technique does not free you from the fall, it changes the direction. Everyone inside the cylinder falls twice: once toward the sky, once toward the ground. Second, the geometry is the tactic: the cylinder grows, thirty feet, fifty, a hundred, two hundred, and the taller the sky, the heavier the verdict. Mark the printed cruelty: this fall ignores even protections against falling. Third, the pinnacle is consent revoked: in Zero Gravity, chosen enemies simply fail their saves against the reversal, falling until he releases them. Down is no longer a fact. It is his decision. Maxim: down is a decision. He has learned to unmake it.

Full progression

Feature map

1Antigravity.

Action · 3 CE · 10-ft radius, 30-ft high cylinder centered on you (excludes you) until your next turn · Unanchored creatures/objects fall upward; Dex save to grab a fixed object · Fall damage ignores all reduction/immunity · Height rises to 50/100/200 ft at 3rd/11th/18th level (die d6/d8/d10/d12).

The first lesson is the cruelest geometry in the codex: a cylinder of un-down, ten feet wide and thirty high, and everyone inside falls twice, once toward the sky and once toward the ground. Mark this: the fall ignores even protections against falling. What was built to save you from the ground cannot save you from the sky. As an action for 3 cursed energy, you can reverse gravity in a 10 foot-radius 30 foot-high cylinder centered on you for all creatures but yourself until the beginning of your next turn.

All creatures and objects that aren't anchored to the ground in the area fall upward. A creature can make a Dexterity saving throw to grab onto a fixed object it can reach, thus avoiding the fall. If a solid object (such as a ceiling) is encountered in this fall, falling objects and creatures strike it and take the remaining height as fall damage. If an object or creature reaches the top of the area without striking anything, it remains there, oscillating slightly, for the duration. At the end of the duration, affected objects and creatures fall back down, taking fall damage.

The height of the cylinder increases to 50 feet at 3rd level, 100 feet at 11th level and 200 feet at 18th level (see Domain Expansion: Zero Gravity). Fall damage from this technique ignores any type of fall damage reduction or immunity. The fall damage die is a d6 at 1st and 2nd level (invented).

3Gravity Field.

Upgrades · Light Fall (Reaction · 1 CE, when falling): you + PB creatures within 120 ft descend 60 ft/round, no fall damage · Focused System: CE mod + PB creatures unaffected by your technique · Disorienting Gravity: lifted creatures Con save or attacks at disadvantage and can't maintain concentration.

At the third: refinement. Light Fall, one coin, a reaction, and falling allies within 120 feet land unharmed; Focused System, allies chosen out of the storm; Disorienting Gravity, lifted enemies lose their aim and their concentration. He learns not only how to drop the sky, but who is spared by it. At 3rd level you gain the following benefits through practicing your technique.

Light Fall. As a reaction for 1 CE to falling, you and creatures equal to your proficiency bonus within 120 feet have their rate of descent slowed to 60 feet per round and take no fall damage when you land.

Focused System. When you use your innate technique, you can choose a number of creatures equal to your CE modifier + your proficiency bonus to be unaffected by your technique.

Disorienting Gravity. Any creature that is lifted upwards by your Antigravity must make a Constitution saving throw. On a failure, they make any attacks at disadvantage and cannot maintain concentration while within your field.

The cylinder height rises to 50 feet and the fall damage die becomes d8.

6Antigravity Shield.

Reaction · 2 CE · Attacks against you at disadvantage; non-psychic damage reduced by CE modifier + level until your next turn · If you are Dodging, can shield another creature within 5 ft instead.

At the sixth, the fall becomes armor: a reaction, two CE, attacks at disadvantage, damage shaved by CE modifier plus level. The man who commands the drop can also command the miss. At 6th level you have learned to defend yourself from attacks using your antigravity. As a reaction for 2 CE, you can encase yourself in a field of antigravity, causing attack rolls against you to be made at disadvantage and any damage other than psychic is reduced by your CE modifier + your level until the beginning of your next turn. If you are taking the Dodge action, you can use this reaction on another creature within 5 feet.

The fall damage die becomes d10.

11Boosted System.

Upgrades · Lasting Field: spend up to CE modifier CE, +10 ft radius and +1 round duration per CE · Sweeping Gravity: Dex score below your CE save DC = no Dex save vs your technique · Nauseating Gravity: failed Disorienting save also stuns to end of their next turn · Hard Fall: d12 fall damage.

At the eleventh, mastery in four parts: a field bought wider and longer, a denial of Dexterity saves for the clumsy, a stun for the disoriented, and the fall grown to d12. The cylinder reaches a hundred feet, and everything in it is ammunition. At 11th level you master all aspects of your technique and gain the following improvements to your Antigravity.

Lasting Field. You can spend additional CE up to your modifier to increase the range, the field’s radius (invented), by 10 feet and the duration by 1 round per CE.

Sweeping Gravity. Creatures with a Dexterity score below your CE save DC cannot make a Dexterity saving throw against your technique.

Nauseating Gravity. If a creature fails a saving throw against your Disorienting Gravity, they are also stunned until the end of their next turn.

Hard Fall. Fall damage from your field becomes d12 bludgeoning.

The cylinder height rises to 100 feet.

18Domain Expansion: Zero Gravity.

Full-turn · 20 CE · 60-ft radius domain, up to 1 min · Initiative 20: creatures of your choice fail all saves vs Antigravity (you can release them at will) · Cylinder height 200 ft.

Up becomes a rumor: dust, debris, and the unprepared hang motionless in air gone suddenly indifferent, and the light falls wrong, pooling in corners like water. The ground is still there but it has lost its argument, a suggestion rather than a law, and every sound arrives flattened, as though the world were holding its breath. Falling is no longer a direction, it is a decision someone else makes.

The pinnacle: Zero Gravity. Full-turn, twenty CE, sixty feet, and at initiative 20, chosen enemies simply fail their saves against the reversal, falling until he releases them. The cylinder stands two hundred feet. Down is no longer a fact; it is his decision. Deploying the Domain costs a full-turn action and 20 CE (invented): 60-foot radius, up to 1 minute, 500 clash points (Tier III) (invented). At 18th level you have learned how to expand your domain. At initiative 20, all creatures of your choice are affected by and fail all saving throws against Antigravity, which can be released by you at will. The cylinder height rises to 200 feet.

Innate Technique Feats

Build-defining options.

These are optional character-building feats. You must meet each listed prerequisite before selecting it.

Prerequisite: Must have Gravity Field

Levitation System

The small, surgical version: Levitate for two CE, two more forces the save at disadvantage. Sometimes you do not need the whole sky, only one man’s share of it. As an action for 2 CE, you can cast Levitate. You can spend an additional 2 CE to force the creature to make the save at disadvantage.

Prerequisite: Must have Antigravity Shield

Inverted Shield

When his shielded self is struck in melee, three CE inverts the field, the attacker is pulled ten feet in and knocked prone on a failed Strength save. The wall reaches back. As a reaction when you are hit by a melee attack while your Antigravity Shield is active, you may spend 3 CE to invert the field. The attacker must make a Strength saving throw against your CE save DC or be pulled 10 feet toward you and knocked prone.

Prerequisite: Must have Boosted System

Antigravitational Flight

Three CE, a touch, ten minutes: sixty feet of hover for himself or another. The sky’s servant may lend the sky out. As an action for 3 CE, you can give yourself or another creature you touch a 60 feet hover speed for 10 minutes.

Prerequisite: Must have Antigravity Shield

Dense Antigravity Shield

Three more CE, and the shield thickens: reduce all damage by half the Antigravity’s maximum-height fall-damage dice. The densest sky is the best armor. For an additional 3 CE, your Antigravity Shield instead reduces all damage against you by half your Antigravity’s maximum height fall damage dice until the beginning of your next turn.

Rules integration

Edge cases and shared systems.

Rules priority: the reviewed rulings, explicit codex clarifications, and the level progression always control when original wording conflicts. Legacy Domain auto-hit language, action terminology, event timing, missing saves, and older scaling never override revised player rules.
Why Tier III: a repeatable zone of reversed gravity with save-denial, stun riders, d12 fall damage that ignores fall-damage defenses, and a defensive shield makes this a top-tier battlefield controller without calamity-scale output.
Flag, immunity bypass: the chassis rule that fall damage from this technique ignores any type of fall damage reduction or immunity is kept as printed pending zack's final call. Do not correct it away at the table without that call.
Flag, score comparison: Sweeping Gravity compares a Dexterity score to the CE save DC. Keep the printed comparison; do not reinterpret it as an ability check.
