Features & procedures

Properties & techniques

Exact player-facing procedures

Feature

On hit · Spend Sever: +1 Tool Die damage per Sever spent, max 2 per hit; only against the creature the Sever was banked from.

Spend Sever to addThe banked arguments are cashed as damage: two dice at most per hit, spent against the thing the blade was arguing with. A patient debater is rewarded. Tool Die damage, max 2/hit.

Monster-Cutter

On hit · 2 CE · Con save: the target cannot regain hit points until the start of your next turn.

Two CE on a hit and the crueler argument lands: until your next turn begins, the struck cannot regain hit points. Regeneration is a claim the blade disputes with steel. 2 CE on hit: Con save; target cannot regain HP until start your next turn.

Unmake Shape

3 CE + 2 Sever · The transformed target makes a Cha save or one transformation-granted movement or reach feature is suppressed until the end of your next turn.

Three CE and two banked Sever, and the cross-examination sharpens: the transformed target must save or lose a movement or reach its form granted. Not the form itself, only its privileges. 3 CE, spend 2 Sever: transformed target makes Cha save or one transformation-granted movement/reach feature is suppressed until end next turn.

Feature

Limitation · Harpe can never permanently remove an Innate Technique or a transformation.

Cannot permanently removeThe blade's own boundary, stated plainly: no permanent removal of an Innate Technique or a transformation. Harpe argues with what things become. It does not legislate what they are. an Innate Technique or transformation.

Field notes. Subject: a blade forged for the unkillable. Three truths govern him. First, the Sever marks are not wounds but arguments: each cut against a transformed, regenerating, or summoned thing is the tool's case that its state is a lie, and the blade banks those arguments, once per turn, for later use. Second, it argues the two simplest denials: Monster-Cutter forbids the struck from regaining hit points, and Unmake Shape suppresses a movement or reach the transformation granted, interruption, never erasure. Third, and most instructive: even this blade has a doctrine it will not violate. It cannot permanently remove an Innate Technique or a transformation. Some refusals are permanent, and Harpe, the registry notes, respects that. Assess it as the monster-hunter's cross-examination: devastating against what refuses to stay one thing, honest about what it cannot touch.

Rules integration

Cursed-tool attunement

Campaign canon

Cursed-tool attunement: you have PB attunement slots (2–6). This tool occupies one slot and requires a one-hour ritual. Attunement ends voluntarily over a short rest, on death, or when you replace it while over capacity. Distance does not break attunement. Cursed-tool attunement is separate from magic-item attunement.
Balance & counterplay. Excels against monstrous states without becoming universal technique negation.
