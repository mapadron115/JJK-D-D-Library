Properties & techniques

Exact player-facing procedures

Feature

Monster-Cutter

2 CE on hit: Con save; target cannot regain HP until start your next turn.

Unmake Shape

3 CE, spend 2 Sever: transformed target makes Cha save or one transformation-granted movement/reach feature is suppressed until end next turn.

Feature

Cannot permanently remove an Innate Technique or transformation.

Rules integration

Cursed-tool attunement

Campaign canon

Cursed-tool attunement: you have PB attunement slots (2–6). This tool occupies one slot and requires a one-hour ritual. Attunement ends voluntarily over a short rest, on death, or when you replace it while over capacity. Distance does not break attunement. Cursed-tool attunement is separate from magic-item attunement.
Balance & counterplay. Excels against monstrous states without becoming universal technique negation.
