Doubling raises Criticality

Each doubling raises a Criticality score.

Runaway discharge

The pulse

Archive Protocol. No activation without multiple barrier-capable sorcerers present.

Rules integration

Cursed-tool attunement

Campaign canon

Cursed-tool attunement: you have PB attunement slots (2–6). This tool occupies one slot and requires a one-hour ritual. Attunement ends voluntarily over a short rest, on death, or when you replace it while over capacity. Distance does not break attunement. Cursed-tool attunement is separate from magic-item attunement.
Balance & counterplay. The pulse ignores allegiance — a failed discharge strikes the wielder’s own side too, and the amplification demands barrier-capable supervision every activation. Push the Criticality and let the risk do the work.
