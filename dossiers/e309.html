The Demon Core

Metaphysical Law: The closer the system is pushed toward catastrophe, the more violently cursed energy multiplies.

FormCursed Object
EngineReflector Assembly
Attunement1 hour*

Field notes. Subject: a furnace that runs on nerve. Three truths govern it. First, catastrophe is the fuel: any CE-powered feature used within five feet may be pushed to Stage 1–4: +2/+4/+8/+12 damage dice, or +1 to +4 save DC. The closer the system is pushed toward failure, the more violently energy multiplies. Second, every amplification is audited: after the feature resolves, the operator checks Arcana against DC 14/18/22/26: failure releases a Runaway Reaction: 4d10 force per stage across 15 feet per stage, Constitution save for half, everyone except the operator, who takes it all. Third, hubris has its own tariff: a natural 1 doubles the pulse's dice and silences the Core for seven days. One mercy: a second pair of hands, an adjacent creature's reaction and 3 CE buys the operator advantage, once per activation. And the vow holds the line: remove the check, and the bonus goes with it.

Relic profile

Criticality Stage 0–4 · 5-ft operating radius

Jujutsu origin

Unlike many ancient relics, the Demon Core did not need millennia of folklore. Two fatal criticality accidents, the terror of invisible radiation, and the symbolic weight of early nuclear weapons loaded a very modern object with concentrated fear. The cursed technique mirrors criticality: power rises fastest at the exact point safety disappears.

Reflector Assembly

Passive · A creature within 5 ft activating a CE-powered feature may choose Criticality Stage 1–4 before resolving: +2/+4/+8/+12 damage dice of one existing type, or +1/+2/+3/+4 to its save DC (features with no damage roll or save DC can't be amplified).

A creature within 5 ft that activates a CE-powered feature may choose Criticality Stage 1–4 before resolving it. The feature receives one benefit based on stage: +2/+4/+8/+12 damage dice of one existing damage type, or +1/+2/+3/+4 to its save DC. A feature with no damage roll or save DC cannot be amplified.

Criticality Check

Passive · After the amplified feature resolves: Cursed Energy (Arcana) check — Intelligence (Arcana) for a wielder with no CE — DC 14/18/22/26 for stages 1–4.

After the amplified feature resolves, the operator makes a Cursed Energy (Arcana) check, or Intelligence (Arcana) if they have no CE. DC is 14 / 18 / 22 / 26 for stages 1–4.

Runaway Reaction

Passive · On a failed check: pulse of 15 ft radius per stage; every creature in the area (including the operator) takes 4d10 force damage per stage; a CON save vs the same DC halves it for everyone except the operator (operator takes full damage automatically).

On a failed Criticality Check, the Core releases a pulse in a radius of 15 ft per stage. Every creature in the area, including the operator, takes 4d10 force damage per stage; a Constitution save against the same DC halves the damage for everyone except the operator. The operator automatically takes full damage.

Prompt Criticality

Passive · Natural 1 on the check: double the Runaway Reaction damage dice and the Core becomes inert for 7 days.

On a natural 1 on the Criticality Check, double the Runaway Reaction damage dice and the Core becomes inert for 7 days.

Scram — Reaction

Reaction · A second creature adjacent to the Core may spend 3 CE to give the operator advantage on the Criticality Check (only one creature can Scram per activation).

A second creature adjacent to the Core may spend 3 CE to give the operator advantage on the Criticality Check. Only one creature can Scram per activation.

Binding Vow — Power Requires Proximity to Failure

Passive restriction · The Core can't be made safe: eliminating the Criticality Check also eliminates the bonus.

The Core cannot provide safe amplification. Any attempt to eliminate the Criticality Check also eliminates the bonus.

Designer’s note. Jujutsu Headquarters would classify transport of the Core itself as a Special Grade incident.

Rules integration

Cursed-tool attunement

Campaign canon

Cursed-tool attunement: you have PB attunement slots (2–6). This tool occupies one slot and requires a one-hour ritual. Attunement ends voluntarily over a short rest, on death, or when you replace it while over capacity. Distance does not break attunement. Cursed-tool attunement is separate from magic-item attunement.
