Property

Comprehend languages

While holding this monocle up to your eye, you can spend 2 cursed energy to gain the benefits of Comprehend Languages for 1 minute while looking through the monocle.

Observer’s account

Field notes. Subject: an interpreter for the price of breath. The monocle spends a sliver of cursed energy — 2 CE — and for one minute the world speaks the wearer’s tongue. Three truths. First, understanding is cheaper than violence: wars have been lost for want of a translator, and this lens is one you carry. Second, it is a glass, not a mind: lower the monocle and the translation dies; the effect lives only while you are looking through it. Third, the price is honest — 2 CE per minute of comprehension, a coin any sorcerer can afford. Consider what it means to read a warding inscription aloud before it finishes you. Hence the maxim: the sharpest blade on any field is the sentence understood in time.
