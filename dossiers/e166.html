May We Come In?

Make invitation the currency, the technique has never known anything but asking. Power grows only through genuine permission. Offer useful bargains, and each accepted bargain becomes a permanent threshold, a door you may always cross, a line you may always reach through.

Combat roleSocial Controller / Curse Specialist
TierTier IV · Special Grade
Core engineMap of Open Doors
Core law

Character fantasy

Power grows only through genuine permission. Every bargain a creature knowingly accepts becomes a permanent threshold, a door you may always cross, a line you may always reach through. The technique grows as a map of open doors. Doors are never currency; they are never spent.

Combat loop

Offer, open, knock, welcome, the guest's fourfold rite.

  1. Offer a target something genuinely useful.
  2. If it voluntarily accepts, record an Open Door to one Threshold it controls.
  3. Doors never close on their own, cross them, reach through them, know the welcomed.
  4. Engineer situations where accepting your help is tempting without ever forcing consent.

Field notes. Subject: the technique of a born outsider. I have asked its user why she never forces a door and received the only answer the technique permits: it has never known anything but asking. Three truths govern it. First: permission is permanent, an Open Door never fades, never spends, and closes only on genuine revocation; the map of open doors outlives the memory of the bargains. Second: the kindness is real, every Offer is concrete and useful, because the technique cannot grow on anything but a knowing yes; Cold Knock is the one honest breach, and it slams shut within a turn. Third: welcome curdles, three Doors make a creature Welcomed, and the welcomed who strike you find the hospitality turning in their hands. Mark the inversion: the most polite technique in the registry is also one of its most patient traps.

Full progression

Level progression

1The First Invitation

Offer

A concrete kindness, freely given, movement, Help, temp HP, or the dignity of standing up. Once per turn as a bonus action, spend 1 CE and make a concrete offer to one creature within 30 feet: 10 feet of movement without provoking opportunity attacks, Help on one check or attack before your next turn, PB temporary HP, or allowing it to end prone for free. If it knowingly and voluntarily accepts the benefit, record 1 Open Door: choose one Threshold the creature meaningfully controls. Maximum 3 Open Doors per creature. Charm, domination, threats of immediate technique harm, or mind control cannot open Doors.

Open Door

Permanent, unspent, unfading, the gift outlives the giver. An Open Door is permanent. It does not fade on a rest and it is never spent. It closes only when the granting creature genuinely revokes permission, a deliberate, communicated revocation (spoken to your face, sent by messenger, or sealed in a warding rite), never an accident, a trick, or a coerced retraction. A creature that does not know you hold its Door cannot revoke it. If revocation drops a Welcomed creature below 3 Doors, the welcome ends.

Cold Knock (the fallback)

When no one bargains, the technique wedges a door by force, the one honest breach. When no one will bargain, the technique does not stall. Once per short rest, when a hostile creature within 30 feet refuses or ignores your Offer, you may spend 2 CE to wedge a door by force: record 1 temporary Open Door to one Threshold it controls. It lasts until the end of your next turn, then slams shut, and the creature knows you forced it. A wedged door works like a genuine Door except it can never count toward Welcomed. Forced consent never becomes genuine; this is leverage, not permission.

Threshold

A home, a room, a ward, whatever a creature meaningfully controls. A home, room, vehicle, barrier, camp, ward, or space that a creature meaningfully controls can count as its Threshold. Domains are not automatically bypassed by this feature.

The First Invitation

Every Door marks its granter; direction within three hundred feet, and saves that slip by one. Each Open Door marks its granter. You know the direction to a creature whose Door you hold while it is within 300 feet. Once per turn when such a creature makes a save against your technique, subtract 1 from that save. You may ask permission to cross a mundane locked or guarded Threshold; if a creature whose Door you hold explicitly allows it, you can open or cross that Threshold as though physically authorized.

3Access

Your Doors deepen. The Doors deepen: cover ignored, Thresholds stepped through. Your attacks and technique effects ignore half cover created by a granter’s own possessions, summons, or mundane fortifications. As a bonus action and 2 CE, step through a held Door: teleport to an unoccupied space just inside a non-Domain Threshold you hold a Door for, provided it is within 300 feet.

6Welcome

When a single creature has granted you 3 Open Doors Three Doors and the welcome is permanent, until it curdles., three separate accepted bargains, it is Welcomed, and the welcome is permanent until revoked below 3. The Price of Welcome: once per turn (no action), when a Welcomed creature within 60 feet damages you with an attack or harmful effect, the welcome curdles, it makes a Wisdom save; on failure it cannot willingly move farther from you until the end of its next turn and has disadvantage on attack rolls against you until the end of its next turn. Action, 3 CE: a Welcomed creature within 60 feet makes a Wisdom save; on failure it is charmed by you until the end of your next turn and cannot willingly cross a Threshold away from you during that time. On success it is immune to this use until your next turn.

11Once Welcomed, Never Gone

Your Doors outlast memory: The Doors outlast memory itself, direction and distance, on the same plane. they persist until genuinely revoked, and you know the direction and approximate distance to every granter on the same plane. Once per short rest, when a granter activates a barrier, Curtain, or non-Domain ward, you may use your reaction and 3 CE to make a CE ability check against its save DC; on success, you personally count as authorized for that effect for 1 round. This never defeats a Domain barrier or nullifies its sure-hit.

18Domain Expansion: Please, We’re Cold Outside

A night so cold the stars look brittle, snow falling sideways through dark pines, and at its center one house with every window lit gold. Smoke rises from the chimney, and the smell of woodsmoke and stew drifts out, wrong and wonderful. The cold presses against the windows like something asking to be let in.

Full-turn action, 20 CE, 90-foot radius, 1 minute. A freezing night surrounds one warm house A freezing night around one warm house, hospitality, weaponized. . The Domain is rules-based. Outside the house, movement costs +5 feet per 5 feet and creatures cannot regain temporary HP. Anyone may enter the house by willingly crossing its open doorway; doing so immediately removes frightened, grants temporary HP equal to 5 × your proficiency bonus, and counts as voluntarily accepting your hospitality, you record 1 Open Door to that creature (maximum 3 per creature). Inside, violence is allowed normally. At initiative 20 the exterior cold intensifies: each creature outside gains one Cold stack; at 2 stacks speed −10 feet, at 3 disadvantage on Constitution saves, at 4 it cannot take reactions. Entering the house removes all Cold. Anti-domain protection prevents gaining Cold but does not remove the house or its voluntary bargain. The Domain never forces anyone to enter. Clash bookkeeping: 800 clash points (Tier IV). A 100+ point advantage collapses the weaker Domain; collapse deals no damage. Barrier AC 12 + PB; barrier HP = 10 × max(1, your CE modifier) + 1,600. Technique Burnout: 5 rounds.

Feats

Build-defining options.

Polite Stranger

The Offer travels by phone, speaker, intercom, a mile of politeness. You may deliver Offer through a phone, speaker, intercom, or other live two-way communication within 1 mile. The target must hear and voluntarily accept.

Take My Hand

One more kindness on the menu: stand, and walk ten feet toward the hand that offered. Add one Offer option: the target may stand from prone and move 10 feet toward you without provoking. If accepted, you may move 10 feet toward it as well.

Guest Right

Inside a Threshold whose controller invited you, no one surprises you. While inside a Threshold whose controller has invited you, you cannot be surprised, and you sense whenever a creature enters or leaves that Threshold.

The Eyes Are Wrong

Two Doors deep, and they can no longer read you, or hide from you. A creature that has granted you 2+ Open Doors has disadvantage on Insight checks against you and cannot benefit from being invisible against your technique senses.

Rules integration

Building the character

Charisma-heavy Support Specialist or Strategist is natural, but a martial character can weaponize the “help first, curse later” loop. This technique is intentionally strongest when the player is socially clever rather than when the GM makes NPCs foolish.
Rules integration

Counterplay

Refuse offers, communicate the danger, isolate the user from controlled spaces, deliberately revoke Doors you granted, and avoid granting explicit access. Forced or manipulated consent never opens a Door.
