Combat roleRegenerating Bruiser / Tank
TierTier IV · Special Grade
Core law

Character fantasy

Severe injury creates more spectral heads unless enemies cauterize the wound. More heads mean more defense, reach, reactions, and venomous pressure—but also more body to punish with area attacks.

Combat loop

  1. Take meaningful hits and turn surviving them into new Heads.
  2. Spend Head Dice on bites, blocks, and reactive counters.
  3. Force enemies to choose between weak cauterizing damage and letting your engine grow.
  4. At high level, turn the accumulated heads into a many-necked siege body.

The fiction. Registry scholars describe it as a grudge against death itself: the user's cursed energy, standing on the brink of ending, chose *more* instead of *healing* — and has chosen it ever since. Each spectral head is a separate refusal to die, a wound drafting reinforcements instead of mending. Fire and radiance cauterize the argument because they are the one language this body's stubbornness was never taught to speak over.

Core law

Head Count & Cauterization

Head Count:

begin with 2 Heads; maximum PB + 1. Heads are manifested necks/maws attached to your cursed body, not independent creatures.

Severing Trigger:

once per round when one damage instance deals at least 2 × your level HP to you and does not reduce you to 0, you may spend reaction +1 CE to mark a Neck Wound. At start of your next turn, if you have not taken fire or radiant damage since the trigger, the wound becomes +1 Head and you heal 1 Head Die + CON modifier. Fire/radiant “cauterizes” the pending wound and prevents the new Head.

Head Dice:

you have a number of Head Dice equal to current Heads, refreshing at start of your turn. Features spend these dice; unspent dice vanish at the next refresh.

Full progression

Level progression

1Many-Necked Body

Shed the Neck. Reaction — when you are hit by an attack, sever one of your Heads (Head Count −1, minimum 1). The offered neck lunges into the blow: the attack deals no damage to you. The severed neck becomes a Neck Wound under the Severing Trigger rules: at the start of your next turn it regrows (+1 Head, and you heal 1 Head Die + CON modifier) unless you took fire or radiant damage since the trigger. No damage threshold, no CE cost, no additional reaction to regrow — offering the neck is the cost. Bite: once per turn after an unarmed/weapon hit, spend 1 Head Die to add the die as piercing damage. If you have 4+ Heads, reach for Bite becomes 10 feet.

3Hydra Venom

When Bite adds a Head Die, spend 1 CE to force Constitution save. Failure is poisoned until end of target’s next turn. A poisoned target that is hit by another Bite before then takes one extra Head Die necrotic.

6Two From One

When a Neck Wound successfully regrows, you may spend 2 additional CE to gain 2 Heads instead of 1, still respecting cap. Head cap becomes PB + 3. Area damage that hits you consumes one extra Head Die from your current pool after resolving, representing multiple heads caught at once.

11Unkillable Neck

At start of turn, if you have 4+ Heads and a Head regrew since your last turn, heal one Head Die without spending CE. You may spend 2 Head Dice instead of a reaction to Shed the Neck. When you succeed on a death save, immediately create a pending Neck Wound that cannot be cauterized until after your next turn begins.

18Maximum: Ninefold Lernaean Body

Action, 10 CE, Focus, 1 minute. Head Count becomes 9, size Large, reach 15, and Head Dice are d12. Once each turn after taking the Attack action, spend 3 Head Dice to make three Bite CE attacks against one or several creatures in reach. At start of each turn in which a Head regrew, heal 2 Head Dice. Fire/radiant damage reduces this regeneration to one die for that turn. When the Maximum ends, Head Count becomes your normal cap; excess heads vanish without healing.

Feats

Build-defining options.

Swamp-Born

You gain a swim speed equal to your walking speed and can hold your breath for 1 hour. While fully submerged, fire and radiant damage lose their cauterizing edge: a Neck Wound marked on a turn you end underwater cannot be cauterized until after your next turn begins, giving the regrowth time to root. As a bonus action while submerged, you may spend 1 CE to exhale venom-tinged murk into a 15-foot radius around you, heavily obscuring it until the start of your next turn.

Guarding Heads

When an adjacent ally is hit, spend 1 Head Die and reaction to reduce its damage by the die + CON modifier.

Venom Reservoir

A creature poisoned by your Hydra Venom also has its speed reduced by 10 feet. Your necks track the venom's spread through the victim's body: your Bite's reach extends by 5 feet against a poisoned creature, and when it tries to move away from you, you may spend 1 CE as a reaction to force a Strength saving throw — on a failure its movement ends immediately. The speed penalty and the leash both end when the poison ends.

Heracles Problem

When an enemy deals fire/radiant damage solely to cauterize a pending Neck Wound, gain one Head Die of temporary HP after that damage resolves.

Rules integration

Building the character

Juggernaut or Warrior with high Constitution. The technique rewards taking serious hits but does not generate heads from trivial self-damage or chip damage.
Technique die: Head Die follows the shared d6 → d8 → d10 → d12 progression.
Rules integration

Counterplay

Cauterize pending wounds, use area damage, burst the user from high HP to 0 before regrowth, or deny reactions.
