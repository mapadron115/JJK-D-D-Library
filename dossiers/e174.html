Core law

Character fantasy

Severe injury creates more spectral heads unless enemies cauterize the wound. More heads mean more defense, reach, reactions, and venomous pressure—but also more body to punish with area attacks.

Combat loop

  1. Take meaningful hits and turn surviving them into new Heads.
  2. Spend Head Dice on bites, blocks, and reactive counters.
  3. Force enemies to choose between weak cauterizing damage and letting your engine grow.
  4. At high level, turn the accumulated heads into a many-necked siege body.

Field notes. Subject: a grudge against death, with a ledger of necks. Three truths govern her. First: she does not heal — she recruits. Where a wound closes on other sorcerers, hers drafts reinforcements, and every scar becomes a soldier. Second: her strength is a loan the enemy can cancel. Fire and radiance cauterize the wound before the draft is sealed, and she knows it — watch how she flinches from the torch she otherwise despises. Third: every muster has its supply problem. Area attacks punish the many-necked body she grows, so the heads she raises are also targets she feeds her enemies. Consider her correctly: not unkillable, but expensively killable — and the expense rises with every head you give her. Mark this: the hydra does not survive her wounds. She multiplies them.

Core law

Head Count & Cauterization

Head Count:

Two heads to begin with, and a cap she must earn. Mark the arithmetic: every head is both a weapon and a liability, and the count is the technique's whole balance sheet. begin with 2 Heads; maximum PB + 1. Heads are manifested necks/maws attached to your cursed body, not independent creatures.

Severing Trigger:

The wound drafts its own reinforcements. Note the conditions: a single heavy blow, a spent reaction, one cursed energy — and the enemy has until her next turn to cauterize the argument with fire or radiance. once per round when one damage instance deals at least 2 × your level HP to you and does not reduce you to 0, you may spend reaction +1 CE to mark a Neck Wound. At start of your next turn, if you have not taken fire or radiant damage since the trigger, the wound becomes +1 Head and you heal 1 Head Die + CON modifier. Fire/radiant “cauterizes” the pending wound and prevents the new Head.

Head Dice:

Heads are currency, refreshed each turn and spent by the round. Unspent dice vanish — a garrison that musters only what it will use. you have a number of Head Dice equal to current Heads, refreshing at start of your turn. Features spend these dice; unspent dice vanish at the next refresh.

Full progression

Level progression

1Many-Necked Body

The opening lesson: a neck is an offering. Shed the Neck turns a struck blow into a pending wound — the attack deals no damage, and the neck regrows unless the enemy thinks in fire. Shed the Neck. Reaction — when you are hit by an attack, sever one of your Heads (Head Count −1, minimum 1). The offered neck lunges into the blow: the attack deals no damage to you. The severed neck becomes a Neck Wound under the Severing Trigger rules: at the start of your next turn it regrows (+1 Head, and you heal 1 Head Die + CON modifier) unless you took fire or radiant damage since the trigger. No damage threshold, no CE cost, no additional reaction to regrow — offering the neck is the cost. Bite: once per turn after an unarmed/weapon hit, spend 1 Head Die to add the die as piercing damage. If you have 4+ Heads, reach for Bite becomes 10 feet.

3Hydra Venom

The heads learn poison. Spend one CE on a Bite to force a Constitution save, and venom stacked on venom turns a poisoned wound into necrotic pressure. When Bite adds a Head Die, spend 1 CE to force Constitution save. Failure is poisoned until end of target’s next turn. A poisoned target that is hit by another Bite before then takes one extra Head Die necrotic.

6Two From One

Regrowth becomes multiplication. Spend two CE on a regrowing wound and it returns doubled — though the wider body she grows catches more of every area attack. When a Neck Wound successfully regrows, you may spend 2 additional CE to gain 2 Heads instead of 1, still respecting cap. Head cap becomes PB + 3. Area damage that hits you consumes one extra Head Die from your current pool after resolving, representing multiple heads caught at once.

11Unkillable Neck

The technique stops asking permission. Heads regrow without the CE tithe, the neck may be shed with dice instead of a reaction, and even a refused death save becomes a pending wound no flame can touch until her next turn begins. At start of turn, if you have 4+ Heads and a Head regrew since your last turn, heal one Head Die without spending CE. You may spend 2 Head Dice instead of a reaction to Shed the Neck. When you succeed on a death save, immediately create a pending Neck Wound that cannot be cauterized until after your next turn begins.

18Maximum: Ninefold Lernaean Body

The siege form. Nine heads, Large, fifteen-foot reach, d12 Head Dice, and a bite that strikes threefold — but fire and radiance still dim the regeneration, and when the minute ends the excess heads vanish without healing. Action, 10 CE, Focus, 1 minute. Head Count becomes 9, size Large, reach 15, and Head Dice are d12. Once each turn after taking the Attack action, spend 3 Head Dice to make three Bite CE attacks against one or several creatures in reach. At start of each turn in which a Head regrew, heal 2 Head Dice. Fire/radiant damage reduces this regeneration to one die for that turn. When the Maximum ends, Head Count becomes your normal cap; excess heads vanish without healing.

Feats

Build-defining options.

Swamp-Born

The hydra's native element: water, where her wounds are safe from the flame. You gain a swim speed equal to your walking speed and can hold your breath for 1 hour. While fully submerged, fire and radiant damage lose their cauterizing edge: a Neck Wound marked on a turn you end underwater cannot be cauterized until after your next turn begins, giving the regrowth time to root. As a bonus action while submerged, you may spend 1 CE to exhale venom-tinged murk into a 15-foot radius around you, heavily obscuring it until the start of your next turn.

Guarding Heads

A neck interposed for another — the garrison's duty turned outward. When an adjacent ally is hit, spend 1 Head Die and reaction to reduce its damage by the die + CON modifier.

Venom Reservoir

Venom as a leash: the slowed, the tracked, the held. A creature poisoned by your Hydra Venom also has its speed reduced by 10 feet. Your necks track the venom's spread through the victim's body: your Bite's reach extends by 5 feet against a poisoned creature, and when it tries to move away from you, you may spend 1 CE as a reaction to force a Strength saving throw — on a failure its movement ends immediately. The speed penalty and the leash both end when the poison ends.

Heracles Problem

Spite, formalized: the enemy is punished for thinking like Heracles. When an enemy deals fire/radiant damage solely to cauterize a pending Neck Wound, gain one Head Die of temporary HP after that damage resolves.

Rules integration

Building the character

Juggernaut or Warrior with high Constitution. The technique rewards taking serious hits but does not generate heads from trivial self-damage or chip damage.
Technique die: Head Die follows the shared d6 → d8 → d10 → d12 progression.
Rules integration

Counterplay

Cauterize pending wounds, use area damage, burst the user from high HP to 0 before regrowth, or deny reactions.
