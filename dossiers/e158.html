With Many Voices

Archive perfect voices, breathe away short-term memory, lure confused enemies toward false cries, and convert Lost Moments into reliable ambush damage.

RoleAmbusher / memory debuffer / scoutSourceSCP Wiki

Core Engine — Voiceprints and Lost Moments

  • Echo Die: d6 → d8 → d10 → d12.
  • Voiceprint Archive. After hearing a creature speak a complete sentence within 60 feet, you may record its Voiceprint without an action. Maintain PB Voiceprints; recording another replaces one. You can reproduce an archived voice perfectly.
  • Thrown Voice. Bonus action, 1 CE: cause one Voiceprint to originate from any point within 60 feet for up to 1 minute. A suspicious listener may use an action for Insight against your CE DC; success reveals that the sound is anomalous, not who created it.
  • Amnestic Breath. Action, 2 CE, 15-foot cone. Creatures make Constitution saves; on failure gain 1 Lost Moment, maximum 3. A creature outside your amnestic effects may repeat the save at the end of each turn, removing 1 Lost Moment on success.
  • Lost Moment 1: the creature cannot take reactions against you or your auditory decoys. 2: it has disadvantage on Perception and Insight checks involving you and cannot benefit from Help on attacks against you. 3: whenever you move at least 10 feet, you become hidden from that creature until you attack, speak in your natural voice, or it succeeds on a Search action against your CE DC.
  • Predatory Strike. Once per turn when you hit a creature with at least 1 Lost Moment while you are hidden from it or it cannot react to you, add 1 Echo Die damage of the attack’s type. This becomes 2 dice at level 6, 3 at 11, and 4 at 18.

Domain Expansion — Corridor Without Memory

Expansion

Full-turn action, 20 CE, enclosed 175-foot-radius maze of dark bio-containment corridors for 1 minute.

Memory Break — sole sure-hit

At initiative count 20, each chosen hostile creature gains 1 Lost Moment, up to 3. A protected creature makes a Constitution save and gains none on success.

Voices everywhere

Thrown Voice may originate from any point in the barrier, requires no CE once per turn, and may use any Voiceprint you have ever archived during the current day.

Predator remains rolled

Bites, weapon attacks, ambushes, and Predatory Strike still use normal attack rolls. The Domain guarantees memory disruption, not damage.

Technique Feats

With Many Voices

Perfect Voiceprint

Voiceprint capacity becomes PB + CE modifier. You may also reproduce nonverbal sounds made by archived creatures, such as breathing, crying, or footsteps.

Troglobitic Senses

Dark Hunter

Your blindsight becomes 60 feet in darkness. Once per turn after moving at least 20 feet while hidden, add one Echo Die to your next Athletics or Acrobatics check before the end of the turn.

Amnestic Breath, level 6

No New Memories

A creature that fails Amnestic Breath by 5 or more gains 2 Lost Moments instead of 1. Once a creature suffers this effect, it cannot do so again until after a short or long rest.

With Many Voices, level 11

Pack Echo

When an ally within 30 feet attacks a creature with 2+ Lost Moments, use your reaction and 1 CE to make the target hear the ally from the wrong direction. That attack gains advantage; this does not make you hidden.

Table Rulings

  • Lost Moments do not erase campaign facts or force players to forget roleplay information. They model temporary inability to form reliable new combat memories.
  • Blindsight represents heat, airflow, vibration, and other nonvisual hunting senses; it does not see through solid walls.
  • Voiceprints cannot duplicate a verbal component, cursed chant, command word, or identity lock well enough to activate another creature’s supernatural feature unless the feature specifically accepts recordings.
  • Wind, sealed breathing gear, or effects that block inhaled agents may provide advantage or immunity against Amnestic Breath at the GM’s discretion.
