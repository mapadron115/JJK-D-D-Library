With Many Voices

Archive perfect voices, breathe away short-term memory, lure confused enemies toward false cries, and herd frightened prey toward the voices of their friends.

Combat roleAmbusher / memory debuffer / scout

Research log. Subject: the predator that unmakes the present tense. The subject was recorded for six hours and appears in none of the footage, the tapes contain only our own voices, describing it, with increasing alarm, to each other. Two truths govern this file. First: the erasure is tiered, breath deletes the present moment, the tiers strip reactions, then senses, then movement itself from the hollowed mind; at three Lost Moments the victim's own ten-foot steps leave it hidden from itself. Second: the voice is the trap, any sound the subject has archived may be projected, layered, and weaponized, and the hollowed will spend their own reactions walking toward it. We no longer debrief witnesses aloud. We write everything down, and we do not read it back in this wing.

Full progression

Progression

1Voiceprint / Amnestic Breath

Voiceprint Archive.

The collection begins with a single sentence heard within sixty feet, recorded without action, kept to proficiency's count, reproduced perfectly. After hearing a creature speak a complete sentence within 60 feet, you may record its Voiceprint without an action. Maintain PB Voiceprints; recording another replaces one. You can reproduce an archived voice perfectly.

Thrown Voice.

The voice, displaced: a bonus action, one coin of CE, and the archived voice speaks from any point within sixty feet, suspicion may name it anomalous, but never its author. Bonus action, 1 CE: cause one Voiceprint to originate from any point within 60 feet for up to 1 minute. A suspicious listener may use an action for Insight against your CE DC; success reveals that the sound is anomalous, not who created it.

Amnestic Breath.

Breath event: a fifteen-foot cone, two energy, and the failed save loses a Moment. The present, unbreathed. Witnesses describe the interval afterward as 'the part where nothing happened, loudly.' Action, 2 CE, 15-foot cone. Creatures make Constitution saves; on failure gain 1 Lost Moment, maximum 3. A creature outside your amnestic effects may repeat the save at the end of each turn, removing 1 Lost Moment on success.

Lost Moment 1:

Tier analysis: first the reactions against the subject, then the senses themselves, then, at three Moments, its ten-foot movements leave it hidden from the hollowed mind. The victim's own locomotion becomes unobservable to the victim. We have no precedent. We checked. the creature cannot take reactions against you or your auditory decoys. 2: it has disadvantage on Perception and Insight checks involving you and cannot benefit from Help on attacks against you. 3: whenever you move at least 10 feet, you become hidden from that creature until you attack, speak in your natural voice, or it succeeds on a Search action against your CE DC.

Predatory Strike.

Hunt pattern: strikes land on the hollowed, the hidden, the unreacting, and failed resistance herds the frightened toward the nearest projected voice. It does not chase. It calls, and the called come. Once per turn when you hit a creature with at least 1 Lost Moment while you are hidden from it or it cannot take reactions against you (no action): it makes a Wisdom save vs your CE DC. On failure it is frightened of the nearest point where you have a projected Voiceprint (or of you, if none) until the end of its next turn, and it cannot willingly move farther from that point while frightened.

3Troglobitic Senses

Physiology note: thirty feet of blindsight, a climber's speed, and the dark's own advantage in stealth. The body is built for a hunt conducted entirely in the erased present. It is, anatomically, a listener that eats. Gain blindsight 30 feet, a climb speed equal to walking speed, and advantage on Stealth in dim light or darkness. Bright light does not damage you but prevents advantage from this feature.

6Borrowed Distress

Passive · Once per round, when a creature with at least 1 Lost Moment hears one of your Voiceprints from a point it can reach: force a Wisdom save; on failure it must use its reaction (if available) to move up to half its speed toward the sound by the safest route · Predatory Strike no longer requires you to be hidden or the target unable to react — 1+ Lost Moment suffices.

Lure finding: the hollowed hear a Voiceprint and spend their own reactions walking toward it, and the Strike no longer requires the subject to be hidden. The victim funds its own capture. The economy of this hunt is flawless and we resent it. Once per round when a creature with at least 1 Lost Moment hears one of your Voiceprints from a point it can reach, force a Wisdom save. On failure it must use its reaction, if available, to move up to half its speed toward that sound by the safest route. Predatory Strike no longer requires you to be hidden or the target to be unable to react, at least 1 Lost Moment suffices.

11AMN-C227 Saturation

Bonus action · 4 CE · Technique Focus, 1 minute · A 15-ft aura surrounds you: a hostile ending its turn inside makes a Constitution save or gains 1 Lost Moment · A creature failing its Predatory Strike save is frightened for 1 minute (repeating the save at the end of each of its turns).

Aura finding: a fifteen-foot field where ending turns accrue Lost Moments, and the frightened stay frightened for the minute. Standing near the subject is, itself, a forgetting. We rotate observers at fifteen-foot intervals. Bonus action, 4 CE, Technique Focus for 1 minute: a 15-foot aura surrounds you. A hostile creature ending its turn in the aura makes a Constitution save or gains 1 Lost Moment. A creature that fails its Predatory Strike save is frightened for 1 minute, repeating the save at the end of each of its turns.

18Corridor Without Memory

Passive · Amnestic Breath becomes a 30-ft cone; a creature failing its Predatory Strike save is also stunned until the end of its next turn · You gain the Domain below.

Escalation: the Breath widens to thirty feet, failed Strikes stun, and the subject has manifested the Corridor below. The forgetting now has architecture. Amnestic Breath becomes a 30-foot cone. A creature that fails its Predatory Strike save is also stunned until the end of its next turn, and you gain the Domain below.

Pinnacle expression

Domain Expansion — Corridor Without Memory

Research log. The corridor is dark, bio-containment paneling, emergency lights that never quite turn on. The subject's voice comes from everywhere and nowhere, reciting entries from our own field journals, including entries we have not written yet. Time inside is not passing incorrectly. It is being eaten, on schedule.

ExpansionDomain event, designated the Corridor: for one minute, a maze of dark corridors, at each twenty-count, every hostile breaks a memory, and the projected voice speaks from anywhere in the barrier, once per turn, free. Time inside is not passing incorrectly. It is being eaten, on schedule. Full-turn action, 20 CE, enclosed 60-foot-radius maze of dark bio-containment corridors for 1 minute.

Memory Break, sole sure-hit At initiative count 20, each chosen hostile creature gains 1 Lost Moment, up to 3. A protected creature makes a Constitution save and gains none on success.

Voices everywhere Thrown Voice may originate from any point in the barrier, requires no CE once per turn, and may use any Voiceprint you have ever archived during the current day.

Predator remains rolled Bites, weapon attacks, ambushes, and Predatory Strike still use normal attack rolls. The Domain guarantees memory disruption, not damage.

Clash bookkeeping 500 clash points (Tier III). In a Domain clash, a 100+ point advantage collapses the weaker Domain; collapse deals no damage. Barrier: AC 12 + PB; HP = 10 × max(1, your CE modifier) + 500. Burnout: 10 rounds.

Innate Technique Feats

Technique Feats

With Many Voices

Perfect Voiceprint

Mimicry depth: beyond speech, breath, weeping, footsteps, and two archived sounds layered at once, calling from two directions. In trial 9 the subject held a conversation with itself, in our voices, about us. We have the transcript. We are not releasing the transcript. Your mimicry extends beyond speech: you can reproduce any nonverbal sound made by a creature whose voice you have archived, breathing, crying, footsteps, and layer two archived sounds at once, a voice calling from one direction and footsteps from another.

Troglobitic Senses

Dark Hunter

Projection finding: the voice hunts ahead, projected to a point, the subject hears through it as through its own ears. Its hearing is wherever its voice has been. The concept of 'elsewhere' is thinning in this file. Your voice hunts ahead of you: as a bonus action for 1 CE, project a Voiceprint to a point within 60 feet. Until the start of your next turn you can hear through that point as if you were there, and your blindsight extends 30 feet from it.

Amnestic Breath, level 6

No New Memories

Deep-erasure event: the failed victim loses the last minute itself, cannot describe the subject, the room, or its orders. For one hour, the witness is a perfect, polite absence of evidence. Our interview transcripts from this stage are blank pages with timestamps. We keep them anyway. A creature that fails its save against your Amnestic Breath also loses its short-term memory of the last minute: for the next hour it cannot describe you, recall the room's layout, or remember orders received in that time.

With Many Voices, level 11

Pack Echo

Pack finding: allies' strikes on the twice-hollowed are lured from the wrong direction, with advantage. The subject shares its hunting ground the way a conductor shares a baton. We are the orchestra. We did not audition. When an ally within 30 feet attacks a creature with 2+ Lost Moments, use your reaction and 1 CE to make the target hear the ally from the wrong direction. That attack gains advantage; this does not make you hidden.

Rules integration

Table Rulings

Lost Moments do not erase campaign facts or force players to forget roleplay information. They model temporary inability to form reliable new combat memories.
Blindsight represents heat, airflow, vibration, and other nonvisual hunting senses; it does not see through solid walls.
Voiceprints cannot duplicate a verbal component, cursed chant, command word, or identity lock well enough to activate another creature’s supernatural feature unless the feature specifically accepts recordings.
Wind, sealed breathing gear, or effects that block inhaled agents may provide advantage or immunity against Amnestic Breath at the GM’s discretion.
