Body Manipulation

Permanent body theft with a growing library of harvested traits and effective immortality.

The fiction. Jujutsu scholars classify Body Manipulation as the ultimate selfishness made manifest: the user's CE, faced with a dying body, learned not to reinforce it but to evacuate it — and has been evacuating ever since. The brain is the seat of the self, so the brain moves; everything else is a vessel, a library of borrowed traits filed away in Skill Theft. The forehead scar is where the CE refuses to heal, a seam the world keeps noticing no matter how perfect the vessel.

Full progression

Feature map

1Graverobber:

The transplant

When you have an intact, dead Humanoid whose body can physically contain your brain, you may spend an entire long rest transferring your brain into it, paying 1 CE once the rest is recovered. The prior body dies. If the vessel is unsuitable, the CE is not spent and the transfer fails safely.

The new body

Replace Strength, Dexterity, and Constitution with the vessel’s printed values; retain your Intelligence, Wisdom, and Charisma. Gain its ancestry traits, mundane proficiencies, movement, senses, size, and languages. You do not gain class levels, feats, spellcasting, CE, legendary, lair, or mythic actions, or an Innate Technique except through Skill Theft. Use the vessel’s normal maximum HP only if it is lower than your class-derived maximum; otherwise keep class HP. Your class Hit Dice and CE Reserve never change with bodies.

The scar

The forehead scar is visible. Noticing behavioral inconsistency uses the printed DC 30 Insight check (a creature who knew the vessel has advantage); a close medical inspection finds the surgical anomaly with DC 20 Medicine.

3/6/11/18Skill Theft:

Skill Theft

After inhabiting a body through a long rest, you may store one eligible feature it possessed — including an Innate Technique’s base feature — in a retained-feature slot. You have one slot at level 3, two at 6, three at 11, and four at 18. A stored feature remains available in later bodies. Changing a slot requires another successful transplant and a long rest. A copied technique uses your CE attack, save DC, CE pool, and Output Rank. Multiattack, regeneration, legendary resistance, boss phases, and features whose only purpose is to implement a monster’s challenge rating are ineligible.

18Immortality:

Immortality

Your biological age becomes that of each new vessel. This prevents death from old age; it does not prevent death, soul damage, brain destruction, or a failed opportunity to transfer.

Innate Technique Feats

Build-defining options.

These are optional character-building feats. You must meet each listed prerequisite before selecting it.

Prerequisite: Graverobber

Flawless Acting

After seven days and $2,500 of expenses, establish one false identity with clothing, records, profession, and affiliations. It cannot duplicate a real person. Investigation contests your Deception to expose inconsistencies; the identity grants no legal authority the fiction cannot support.

Prerequisite: Skill Theft

Memory Bank

You perfectly remember everything you experience. As an action, touch an object and store any continuous portion of one remembered experience in it; there is no added object or duration cap. A creature touching it may use an action to begin experiencing the memory from your point of view at normal speed and may stop at will. A memory grants information, not proficiency, statistics, or technique access.

Prerequisite: Immortality and CE score 20

Maximum: Backup

Prepare an eligible humanoid corpse over 8 hours, then use an action and 10 CE to designate it as your only backup. On death, resolve the Clone spell’s soul-transfer procedure using that body, without its gold or maturation time. Preparing a new backup destroys the old link; a trapped or unwilling soul cannot transfer.

Rules integration

Edge cases and shared systems.

Rules priority: the reviewed rulings, explicit codex clarifications, and the level progression always control when original wording conflicts. Legacy Domain auto-hit language, action terminology, event timing, missing saves, and older scaling never override revised player rules.
Why Tier IV: its combat power depends on available bodies, but its ordinary campaign reach—identity replacement, permanent feature collection, and immortality—requires Special Grade preparation even without optimization.
