Permanent body theft with a growing library of harvested traits and effective immortality.

High • Narrative / adaptation • Project pp. 60–61

Feature map

  • 1 — Graverobber: brain transplant.
  • 3/6/11/18 — Skill Theft: one through four retained features.
  • 18 — Immortality: age follows the current vessel.

Completed rules

  • An eligible vessel is an intact, dead Humanoid whose body can physically contain your brain. The transfer takes the entire long rest, costs 1 CE after the rest is recovered, and leaves the prior body dead. If the vessel is unsuitable, the CE is not spent and the transfer fails safely.
  • Replace Strength, Dexterity, and Constitution with the vessel’s printed values; retain Intelligence, Wisdom, and Charisma. Gain its ancestry traits, mundane proficiencies, movement, senses, size, and languages. Do not gain class levels, feats, spellcasting, CE, legendary/lair/mythic actions, or an Innate Technique except through Skill Theft.
  • Use the vessel’s normal maximum HP only if it is lower than the maximum supplied by your class; otherwise keep class-derived HP. Your class Hit Dice and CE Reserve never change with bodies.
  • Skill Theft: you have one retained-feature slot at level 3, two at 6, three at 11, and four at 18. After inhabiting a body through a long rest, you may store one eligible feature it possessed, including an Innate Technique’s base feature. A stored feature remains available in later bodies. Changing a slot requires another successful transplant and a long rest.
  • A copied technique uses your CE attack, save DC, CE pool, and Output Rank. Multiattack, regeneration, legendary resistance, boss phases, and features whose only purpose is to implement a monster’s challenge rating are ineligible.
  • The forehead scar is visible. Noticing behavioral inconsistency uses the printed DC 30 Insight check; a close medical inspection finds the surgical anomaly with DC 20 Medicine. A creature who knew the vessel has advantage.
  • At level 18, your biological age becomes that of each new vessel. This prevents death from old age; it does not prevent death, soul damage, brain destruction, or a failed opportunity to transfer.

Power identity

Why Tier IV: its combat power depends on available bodies, but its ordinary campaign reach—identity replacement, permanent feature collection, and immortality—requires Special Grade preparation even without optimization.

Recovered character-build options

Innate Technique Feats 3

These are optional character-building feats. You must meet each listed prerequisite before selecting it.

Flawless Acting

Standard

Prerequisite: Graverobber

After seven days and $2,500 of expenses, establish one false identity with clothing, records, profession, and affiliations. It cannot duplicate a real person. Investigation contests your Deception to expose inconsistencies; the identity grants no legal authority the fiction cannot support.

Memory Bank

High impact

Prerequisite: Skill Theft

You perfectly remember everything you experience. As an action, touch an object and store any continuous portion of one remembered experience in it; there is no added object or duration cap. A creature touching it may use an action to begin experiencing the memory from your point of view at normal speed and may stop at will. A memory grants information, not proficiency, statistics, or technique access.

Maximum: Backup

Pinnacle

Prerequisite: Immortality and CE score 20

Prepare an eligible humanoid corpse over 8 hours, then use an action and 10 CE to designate it as your only backup. On death, resolve the Clone spell’s soul-transfer procedure using that body, without its gold or maturation time. Preparing a new backup destroys the old link; a trapped or unwilling soul cannot transfer.
