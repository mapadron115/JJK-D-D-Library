Body Manipulation

Permanent body theft with a growing library of harvested traits and effective immortality.

Field notes. Subject: a tenant who has stopped pretending any house is his, and a library of borrowed traits that outlives every lease. Four truths govern the graverobber. First, the self is the brain: given an intact dead humanoid and one long rest, he moves, 1 CE, the prior body dies, the vessel’s physical scores become his. Second, the library grows with every tenancy: Skill Theft keeps one feature per body, one slot at 3rd level, four by 18th. Third, the rent can never be hidden: the forehead scar is where his CE refuses to heal, DC 30 Insight, DC 20 inspection. Fourth, the last truth: Immortality declines old age but not violence. He has not conquered death. He has declined to live anywhere it could find him.

Feature map

1Graverobber:

The transplant

Long rest · 1 CE · Transfer your brain into an intact, dead Humanoid whose body can physically contain it · The prior body dies · Unsuitable vessel: the CE is not spent and the transfer fails safely.

The self is the brain; everything else is a lease. Given an intact dead humanoid and one long rest, the tenant moves, 1 CE, the prior body dies, the vessel’s physical scores become his. Consider what a man learns about identity when he has signed this lease a hundred times. When you have an intact, dead Humanoid whose body can physically contain your brain, you may spend an entire long rest transferring your brain into it, paying 1 CE once the rest is recovered. The prior body dies. If the vessel is unsuitable, the CE is not spent and the transfer fails safely.

The new body

Passive · Replace Strength, Dexterity, and Constitution with the vessel's printed values; retain Intelligence, Wisdom, and Charisma · Gain its ancestry traits, mundane proficiencies, movement, senses, size, and languages · No class levels, feats, spellcasting, CE, legendary/lair/mythic actions, or Innate Technique (except via Skill Theft) · Class Hit Dice and CE Reserve never change.

Replace Strength, Dexterity, and Constitution with the vessel’s printed values; retain your Intelligence, Wisdom, and Charisma. Gain its ancestry traits, mundane proficiencies, movement, senses, size, and languages. You do not gain class levels, feats, spellcasting, CE, legendary, lair, or mythic actions, or an Innate Technique except through Skill Theft. Use the vessel’s normal maximum HP only if it is lower than your class-derived maximum; otherwise keep class HP. Your class Hit Dice and CE Reserve never change with bodies.

The scar

Passive · The forehead scar is visible · DC 30 Insight to notice behavioral inconsistency (advantage for creatures who knew the vessel) · DC 20 Medicine to find the surgical anomaly on close inspection.

The forehead scar is visible. Noticing behavioral inconsistency uses the printed DC 30 Insight check (a creature who knew the vessel has advantage); a close medical inspection finds the surgical anomaly with DC 20 Medicine.

3/6/11/18Skill Theft:

Skill Theft

Long rest (after a successful transplant) · Store one eligible feature the body possessed, including an Innate Technique's base feature · Slots: 1 at level 3, 2 at 6, 3 at 11, 4 at 18 · A copied technique uses your CE attack, save DC, CE pool, and Output Rank · Multiattack, regeneration, legendary resistance, boss phases, and monster-CR-only features are ineligible.

After inhabiting a body through a long rest, you may store one eligible feature it possessed, including an Innate Technique’s base feature, in a retained-feature slot. You have one slot at level 3, two at 6, three at 11, and four at 18. A stored feature remains available in later bodies. Changing a slot requires another successful transplant and a long rest. A copied technique uses your CE attack, save DC, CE pool, and Output Rank. Multiattack, regeneration, legendary resistance, boss phases, and features whose only purpose is to implement a monster’s challenge rating are ineligible.

18Immortality:

Immortality

Passive · Biological age becomes that of each new vessel; death from old age is prevented · Does not prevent death, soul damage, brain destruction, or a failed transfer.

The lease never expires: biological age resets with every vessel, and old age is declined. Mark the limits honestly, violence, soul damage, brain destruction, and a missed transfer still answer. He has not conquered death. He has declined to live anywhere it could find him. Your biological age becomes that of each new vessel. This prevents death from old age; it does not prevent death, soul damage, brain destruction, or a failed opportunity to transfer.

Innate Technique Feats

Build-defining options.

These are optional character-building feats. You must meet each listed prerequisite before selecting it.

Prerequisite: Graverobber

Flawless Acting

Seven days and $2,500 buy a false identity, clothing, records, profession, affiliations. The tenant furnishes the house. After seven days and $2,500 of expenses, establish one false identity with clothing, records, profession, and affiliations. It cannot duplicate a real person. Investigation contests your Deception to expose inconsistencies; the identity grants no legal authority the fiction cannot support.

Prerequisite: Skill Theft

Memory Bank

He remembers everything, and can store a remembered experience in a touched object. A tenant who has lived a hundred lives needs an archive. You perfectly remember everything you experience. As an action, touch an object and store any continuous portion of one remembered experience in it; there is no added object or duration cap. A creature touching it may use an action to begin experiencing the memory from your point of view at normal speed and may stop at will. A memory grants information, not proficiency, statistics, or technique access.

Prerequisite: Immortality and CE score 20

Maximum: Backup

Eight hours of preparation, 10 CE, and one designated corpse: on death, the lease transfers. The tenant keeps a spare house. Prepare an eligible humanoid corpse over 8 hours, then use an action and 10 CE to designate it as your only backup. On death, resolve the Clone spell’s soul-transfer procedure using that body, without its gold or maturation time. Preparing a new backup destroys the old link; a trapped or unwilling soul cannot transfer.

Rules integration

Edge cases and shared systems.

Rules priority: the reviewed rulings, explicit codex clarifications, and the level progression always control when original wording conflicts. Legacy Domain auto-hit language, action terminology, event timing, missing saves, and older scaling never override revised player rules.
Why Tier IV: its combat power depends on available bodies, but its ordinary campaign reach—identity replacement, permanent feature collection, and immortality—requires Special Grade preparation even without optimization.
