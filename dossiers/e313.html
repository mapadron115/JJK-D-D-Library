Properties & techniques

Exact player-facing procedures

Empowered edge

Plus four to strike and wound, the mundane excellence that carries the metaphysical one. A soul-cutting blade that could not hit would be philosophy; this one is a weapon. You have a +4 bonus to attack and damage rolls made with this cursed tool.

Soul split

Armor, wards, immunity, all declined, all at once. And every point of damage is carved from the target’s maximum until rest: the ledger of the self, not the flesh, keeps the score. Attacks made with this weapon ignore resistances and immunities. Damage dealt by this sword reduces the target's maximum hit points by the same amount until the target finishes a long rest.

Field notes. Subject: the wound that precedes the blow. The blade does not cut flesh first and the soul second, it cuts what the target is, and the flesh merely obeys. Three truths govern it. First, resistance is a property of the body, and this sword does not address the body: wards, armor, and immunity are arguments made to the wrong court. Second, the damage is a subtraction from the self, not the meat, every point struck reduces what the target can be, until a long rest restores the ledger. Third, the limitation is the oldest one: the wielder must still land the blow. The sword cuts souls; it does not aim itself. Mark this: most weapons wound what you have. This one wounds what you are.

Rules integration

Cursed-tool attunement

Campaign canon

Cursed-tool attunement: you have PB attunement slots (2–6). This tool occupies one slot and requires a one-hour ritual. Attunement ends voluntarily over a short rest, on death, or when you replace it while over capacity. Distance does not break attunement. Cursed-tool attunement is separate from magic-item attunement.
Balance & counterplay. Maximum-HP reduction lasts until a long rest; it does not stack with itself beyond damage dealt, and the wielder must still land the blow.
