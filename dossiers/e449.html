Features & procedures

Properties & techniques

Exact player-facing procedures

Flurry of Wings

When you make an attack as part of the Attack action using these knuckles, you can make an additional attack using them as part of the same Attack action.

Necrotic Sting

Once per turn, you can deal an additional 1d6 necrotic damage on a hit.

Wingbeat Feint

If you score a critical hit against a creature using these knuckles, that creature has disadvantage on attack rolls against you until the end of their turn.

Rules integration

Edge cases and shared systems.

Campaign canon notes

Note: The extra attack is part of the Attack action itself, not a bonus action — it stacks with Extra Attack and other bonus-action attacks.
