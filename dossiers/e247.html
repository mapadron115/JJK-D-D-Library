Seance

Granny Ogami’s technique: a séance art that channels the dead through the living, borrowed bodies, borrowed memories, and the ever-present risk that the dead take the wheel.

Power tierTier III · Apex
Combat roleSupport / controller
Core enginePhysical / Soul Information channeling
Domain · pinnacleSeance Parlor (L18)

Field notes. Subject: a boundary failure with a dinner invitation. Three truths govern Seance. First, the transaction is exact: a Full Turn Action, 2 CE per Information, 4 for both, a willing vessel, and a consumed fragment of the dead; the upkeep is 1 CE per turn, and no alternate source may pay it. Second, memory is the danger: Physical Information borrows the body, but Soul Information borrows the mind, and at each turn's start a Charisma save against the deceased decides who is driving. The technique never learned the difference between remembering someone and becoming them. Third, the dead arrive in force: Blessing deepens the channel, Ritual of Resurrection extends it through a minute of rite, Black Seance overloads it to the dead's full strength, and the Level 18 Parlor waives consumption and maintenance alike. Mark the warning: it is not the medium who invites the dead in.

Full progression

Feature map

1Seance.

The foundation is a transaction: a Full Turn Action, 2 CE for Physical or Soul Information, 4 for both, a willing vessel, and a consumed fragment of the dead. The body is borrowed; the upkeep is 1 CE per turn; and no alternate source of energy may pay the price, not even by binding vow.

Starting at 1st level, you have a very powerful and dangerous technique, and you begin to learn its basics. As a Full Turn Action you can spend 2 CE for Physical Information, 2 CE for Soul Information, or 4 CE for both (invented). You can target yourself or another willing creature within 15 ft for the seance of a dead humanoid with a CR or level no more than 1 higher than your level. You must have a small body part such as a finger of the deceased humanoid that you or the willing participant must consume. You or the target of the seance must spend 1 cursed energy at the beginning of the target’s turns while the technique is active to maintain its form, with the technique ending once the cursed energy runs out. You cannot use an alternate source of cursed energy, even with a binding vow. You lose access to your Innate Technique features while affected by this technique.

Physical Information. The target of the seance gains the appearance, racial traits, features, proficiencies, and Strength, Dexterity, and Constitution scores of the deceased humanoid and temporary hit points equal to your proficiency.

Soul Information. The target gains access to the full information the body has including all memories and one of their Cursed Technique or Innate Technique features. At the beginning of their turns, the target must make a Charisma saving throw against the deceased person (DC 8 + their proficiency bonus + Charisma modifier). On a failure, the dead humanoid takes control until the target’s cursed energy runs out.

3Blessing of the Grateful Dead.

The channel deepens: Physical Information now grants temporary HP of level + PB, and Soul Information yields two features of your choosing without costing the vessel's own Innate Technique. The dead grow generous, but they do not grow quiet.

At 3rd level, you have increased the potency of your seance. When channeling Physical Information, the target gains temporary hit points equal to your level + your proficiency. When channeling Soul Information, the target gains access to two Cursed Technique or Innate Technique features of your choice and does not lose access to their own Innate Technique features. The target cannot use Innate Technique features from different techniques at the same time.

6Ritual of Resurrection.

Patience becomes power: a minute-long ritual of full-turn actions, concentration held throughout, lets the transformation reach dead of CR or level equal to your level + PB. Break concentration and the rite begins again from nothing.

At 6th level, you have learned how to create a prolonged ritual to make your technique more powerful. When you use your technique, you can start a ritual instead of immediately shapeshifting. You must make a Full Turn Action (Action + Bonus Action + Movement) every turn for 1 minute to perform this ritual while maintaining concentration. If you lose concentration, the ritual stops and you must start over. After you have finished the ritual, you or a willing creature within 30 ft can transform into the dead humanoid if the CR or level is equal to or lower than your level + your proficiency bonus.

11Maximum: Black Seance.

The ritual completed at maximum: for 5 additional CE, Physical Information overloads into the dead's own hit points as temporary HP, or Soul Information overloads into all of the dead's technique features, Domain Expansion excluded, minimum CE costs only. The dead, at full strength, are magnificent and terrifying in equal measure.

At 11th level, when you complete Ritual of Resurrection you can spend an additional 5 cursed energy to cause the target of the Seance to gain one of the following benefits. Physical Information Overload. The target gains the hit points of the dead humanoid as temporary hit points if channeling Physical Information. Soul Information Overload. The target gains access to all the dead humanoid’s Cursed Technique and Innate Technique features if channeling Soul Information. You cannot use Domain Expansion this way and can only use the minimum required cursed energy for innate technique features. The target also cannot use the dead humanoid’s innate technique and its own at the same time.

18Domain Expansion: Seance Parlor.

A parlor materializes around a single round table, its wood dark with polish and age, thirteen chairs drawn close though only some are occupied. Candlelight gutters in air that does not move, throwing shadows that arrive before their objects, and the wallpaper's pattern seems to shift when unobserved, flowers becoming faces. The veil here is thin as wet paper, and something on the other side is pressing a hand against it.

The parlor is hospitality made absolute: at initiative 20 the Domain extends the seance to a willing creature, the body part need only be held rather than consumed, maintenance is waived, and possession saves auto-succeed. Every channeled feature carries the Domain's sure-hit, the guests arrive, and the doors lock from their side.

Deploying the Domain costs a full-turn action and 20 CE (invented): 60-foot radius, up to 1 minute, 500 clash points (Tier III) (invented). At 18th level you have mastered your technique allowing you to expand your domain. At initiative 20 you can choose a willing creature to gain the benefits of Seance. Although you must have the body part of the dead humanoid being called, you don’t need to consume it as long as you are holding it. Within your domain, you no longer have to spend cursed energy to maintain the seance and automatically succeed against a creature’s soul information. All innate technique features gained through Seance in your domain are imbued with your domain’s guaranteed hit. Seances caused by your domain end when your domain disappears.

Innate Technique Feats

Build-defining options.

These are optional character-building feats. You must meet each listed prerequisite before selecting it.

Prerequisite: Must have Seance

Bestial Seance

The medium's whimsy: channel a Beast of CR up to your PB instead of the dead, wearing its $200-per-CR costume as the price of admission. Damage the costume and the transformation follows it into ruin.

You have learned to bring a bit of whimsy to your usually ghastly technique. When you use Seance on yourself, you can choose to instead channel the Physical Information of a Beast with a CR equal to or less than your proficiency bonus. You gain all the creature’s traits, senses, movements and actions in addition to the normal effects of Physical Information. To use this technique, you must be wearing a costume of that specific animal, costing $200 x the Beast’s CR. You can wear this costume with armor, but if it takes an amount of damage (other than psychic) equal to 3 x your level, it becomes unusable and you lose any benefits from this feature.

Prerequisite: Must have Ritual of Resurrection

Warded Circle

The ritual's courtesy: trace a warding circle when the rite begins, and hostile feet find ten feet of difficult terrain and a Wisdom save before entering. The dead deserve a quiet room.

When you begin your Ritual of Resurrection, you may trace a warding circle around yourself. For the ritual’s duration, hostile creatures treat the 10-foot-radius circle as difficult terrain and must succeed on a Wisdom saving throw against your CE save DC to willingly enter it.

Prerequisite: Must have Maximum: Black Seance

Cling to Life

When the CE runs out, the desperate may pay in flesh instead: 5 HP at each turn's start, reducing maximum HP by the same, with only 15 restored per long rest. The spirit stays. The body keeps the receipt.

When a target runs out of cursed energy and can no longer maintain the seance, they can choose to forcefully hold the spirit within their body. At the beginning of the target’s turn, they instead spend 5 HP, reducing their maximum by the same amount. The target regains up to 15 of the lost hit point maximum after each long rest (invented).

Rules integration

Edge cases and shared systems.

Channeling: Channeling = Full Turn Action + consumed body part, both hard gates. Add: "no alternate CE source, even with a binding vow" as an explicit anti-circumvention rule.
Possession save: Possession-save DC uses the DECEASED's PB + Cha mod, not the user's. Body-part freshness unspecified — propose any preserved part works (DM check flagged).
Boundary: the soul-information possession save uses the DECEASED's proficiency bonus and Charisma modifier, not the user's. Whether the consumed body part must be fresh or preserved is flagged for zack's decision. Whether a possessing dead humanoid can act through the body beyond stated channel effects is flagged for zack's decision.
Soul Overload: Soul Overload = all channeled features incl. passives but channeled features are capped at minimum required CE. "Black Seance: different techniques cannot be used simultaneously" governs action-by-action use, not passive benefits.
Boundary: Black Seance: Domain Expansion explicitly banned. Whether possessing spirit may use reactions or bonus-action features not granted by channeling is flagged for zack's decision.
Domain: Domain opens/resolves at initiative 20. Held-not-consumed body part + waived maintenance + possession-save auto-success are Domain-only benefits.
Boundary: whether multiple willing creatures can be chosen across rounds is flagged for zack's decision.
Bestial Seance and Perfect Ritual: Bestial Seance grants the beast's printed actions; proposed legendary/lair actions excluded (DM check flagged). Perfect Ritual DC reduction (DC minus CE mod) has a proposed floor of minimum DC 1 (DM check flagged). The HP-maximum reduction is real and only 15 is restored per long rest.
Boundary: Bestial Seance: whether legendary and lair actions are included is flagged for zack's decision. Perfect Ritual: whether the DC reduction has a minimum floor of 1 is flagged for zack's decision.
Audit status: the audit records (AT199–AT202) were approved in the 2026-09-10 mass approval (983/983 decided; no rejections). Remaining open points above are table rulings, not approval gaps.
