Gallows Coin

Bonus action flip before attacking: Heads grants 1 Credit; Tails grants 1 Debt. Max PB each. Credits and Debts reset to 0 on a long rest (invented). You must accept the result.

FormBlack iron coin A debt made metal, a broken promise struck and circulated, still collecting.
RoleRisk / dueling Every flip re-enacts the choice between being owed and owing, fortune as a contract.
EngineDebt Luck as a ledger, Credits spent on the blade, Debts collected by the world.
Attunement1 hour* An hour's ritual, the coin must learn whose luck it is allowed to ruin.
Features & procedures

Properties & techniques

Exact player-facing procedures

Feature

On hit · Spend up to 2 Credit: +1 Tool Die each.

The honest side of the ledger: spend a Credit after a hit for an extra Tool Die, two at most. Heads is a loan the coin is willing to make. Spend Credit after a weapon hit for +1 Tool Die each, max 2.

Feature

Passive · Enemies deal +1 damage per Debt when hitting you (max your proficiency bonus).

The dishonest side: every Debt lets enemies add damage when they hit you, capped by proficiency. Tails is a loan the world collects, and it always collects. Debt gives enemies +1 damage per Debt when they hit you, max PB.

Pay the Hangman

On crit · Erase all Debt and add that many Tool Dice to the strike.

The settlement. Crit, and all Debt is erased, converted, die for die, into your strike. The curse rewards the perfect blow by canceling the account; the hangman is paid and the ledger burns. when you crit, erase all Debt and add that many Tool Dice.

Loaded Fate

2 CE before a flip · Flip twice and choose one result; the unchosen result becomes one Debt.

Two CE before the flip: flip twice and choose, but the unchosen result becomes Debt. Mark this: the tool lets you cheat fate, then bills you for the privilege. 2 CE before flip lets you flip twice and choose, but the unchosen result becomes one Debt.

Field notes. Subject: a ledger that flips. Three truths govern it. First, it was forged around a single broken promise: every flip re-enacts the choice between being owed and owing, Credits for the blade, Debts for the world, and luck, like guilt, always collects. Second, the risk is visible: enemies can read your Debts the way creditors read a ledger, and they will price their blows accordingly. Third, the escape is perfection: a critical hit pays the hangman all at once, erasing the account and turning it into damage, the curse's one mercy, reserved for the flawless. Assess it correctly: not a gambler's toy but a duelist's discipline, the bearer who flips must be willing to be collected.

Rules integration

Cursed-tool attunement

Campaign canon

Cursed-tool attunement: you have PB attunement slots (2–6). This tool occupies one slot and requires a one-hour ritual. Attunement ends voluntarily over a short rest, on death, or when you replace it while over capacity. Distance does not break attunement. Cursed-tool attunement is separate from magic-item attunement.
Balance & counterplay. Power comes with a visible liability opponents can exploit.
