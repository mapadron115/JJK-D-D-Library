Features & procedures

Properties & techniques

Exact player-facing procedures

Feature

Spend Credit after a weapon hit for +1 Tool Die each, max 2.

Feature

Debt gives enemies +1 damage per Debt when they hit you, max PB.

Pay the Hangman

when you crit, erase all Debt and add that many Tool Dice.

Loaded Fate

2 CE before flip lets you flip twice and choose, but the unchosen result becomes one Debt.

Rules integration

Cursed-tool attunement

Campaign canon

Cursed-tool attunement: you have PB attunement slots (2–6). This tool occupies one slot and requires a one-hour ritual. Attunement ends voluntarily over a short rest, on death, or when you replace it while over capacity. Distance does not break attunement. Cursed-tool attunement is separate from magic-item attunement.
Balance & counterplay. Power comes with a visible liability opponents can exploit.
