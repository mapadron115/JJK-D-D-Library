Feature

Spend Credit after a weapon hit for +1 Tool Die each, max 2.

Feature

Debt gives enemies +1 damage per Debt when they hit you, max PB.

Pay the Hangman

when you crit, erase all Debt and add that many Tool Dice.

2 CE before flip lets you flip twice and choose, but the unchosen result becomes one Debt.

Balance & counterplay. Power comes with a visible liability opponents can exploit.
