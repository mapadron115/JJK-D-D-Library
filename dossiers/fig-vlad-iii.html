Feature map

Level-by-level features, as the figure refined them.

L1

Cast Off Dread

Bonus action · 1 CE · Focus, 1 minute.

Your CE ability for this technique is Charisma. Externalize your terror as a Dread Shadow: a 10-foot-radius zone placed within 30 feet of you. Hostiles entering it or starting their turns inside make a Wisdom save. Failures become Dreaded until the end of their next turn: they cannot willingly move closer to the Shadow's center, and they have disadvantage on attacks against you while you are inside the Shadow. Dreaded is bespoke — immunity to Frightened grants advantage on the save, not immunity.

L3

Move the Terror

Bonus action · no additional CE.

Move the Dread Shadow up to 20 feet. A hostile whose space the Shadow would cover may use its reaction to step 5 feet out before making the save — yield ground or face the fear.

L6

Impaler's Lesson

Once per turn · 2 CE.

When you hit a Dreaded creature with a piercing weapon or a grapple adjacent to a solid surface, the target makes a Strength save or is pinned (speed 0 until the end of its next turn, or until it uses an action to beat your Athletics against your technique DC). Restraint, not dismemberment.

L11

Separate the Name

Reaction · 2 CE.

When you are targeted by a non-Domain mark, curse, scrying, tracking, or identity-dependent technique, redirect it to the Dread Shadow — if the effect can legally target a shadow construct. If it cannot, the CE is not spent.

L18

The Ruler Is Not the Legend

Passive.

Cast Off Dread no longer requires Focus, and the Shadow's radius becomes 15 feet. Once per round, move the Shadow up to 10 feet as a reaction (no additional CE). Effects redirected to the Shadow stay on it even if you leave.

Pinnacle expression

Maximum, Domain, or equivalent.

Capstone material.

Maximum Technique

Dracula Unbound

Action · 10 CE · 1 minute.

The Shadow becomes the autonomous curse: 40-foot fly speed, passage through mist, and +2 × PB necrotic damage after each of your piercing hits (healing you the same amount). Cost of control: each turn, if no hostile is Dreaded and Dracula has taken damage since your last turn, make a Charisma save vs your own technique DC — on a failure, Dracula chooses its own movement and target, prioritizing blood and fear over tactics.

Level 20 · Capstone

I Am the Dread

Once per long rest · action.

Recall the Dread Shadow into yourself. For 1 minute: hostiles within 30 feet that can see you make the Dreaded save at the start of their turns, as if standing inside the Shadow; Separate the Name costs no CE; Impaler's Lesson needs no solid surface. He spent his life casting it off. At the end, he wears it.
