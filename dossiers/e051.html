Feature map

Level-by-level features

Field notes. Subject: the sorcerer who gave his lightning standing orders. Three truths govern him. First, the program outruns the programmer: Whirlwind lets his body dodge before his mind catches up, he cannot be surprised, and blows simply miss. Second, the storm has two modes: Whirlwind’s reflexive defense and Speed of Lightning’s conscious offense, blinding movement and strikes that stop a heart for one beat. Third, the meter is the price: every second burns cursed energy, Godspeed drains 1 CE per turn, and the storm always runs out. Mark it: “I’ve set my own rules”, and the lightning obeys them faster than he can.

L1

Godspeed: Whirlwind

Bonus action · 2 CE · Enter Godspeed (Whirlwind) for up to 1 minute · While active: attacks against you have disadvantage, you can't be surprised, resistance to lightning damage · 1 CE upkeep at the start of each of your turns.

The program, given its orders: bonus action, 2 CE, his aura becomes electricity with one standing order, dodge. As a bonus action, spend 2 CE to transmute your aura into electricity and give it standing orders: dodge. You enter Godspeed (Whirlwind) for up to 1 minute. At the start of each of your turns, spend 1 CE or Godspeed ends, the storm eats aura the way fire eats air. While active: attacks against you have disadvantage (your body moves before thought), you cannot be surprised, and you have resistance to lightning damage.

L3

Speed of Lightning

The conscious mode: bonus action while Godspeed is active, switch from reflex to intent. The conscious mode. As a bonus action while Godspeed is active, switch to Speed of Lightning (switching back is also a bonus action; the 1 CE per-turn drain continues). Your speed increases by 30 feet, opportunity attacks against you have disadvantage, and your unarmed strikes carry the storm: +2d8 lightning damage. Once per turn, when such a strike hits, the target must succeed on a Constitution saving throw against your CE save DC or be stunned until the end of your next turn, the current stops the heart for one beat.

L6

The storm’s answer to distance: action, 2 CE, call the bolt down whether or not Godspeed is active. Killua's answer to distance. Each creature within 10 feet of the point must succeed on a Dexterity saving throw against your CE save DC or take 5d8 lightning damage (half on a success). Thunderbolt works whether or not Godspeed is active. The storm doesn't care whose hand throws it.

L11

Whirlwind takes orders, and now the orders include hitting back. Whirlwind takes orders, and now the orders include hitting back. The program was always "dodge." You just added "then punish."

L20

Once per long rest · Bonus action · For 1 minute: Godspeed costs no CE to maintain, Whirlwind and Speed of Lightning run simultaneously, +30 ft. additional speed · When it ends, gain 1 level of exhaustion.

Once per long rest, the meter breaks: for one minute Godspeed costs no CE to maintain, both modes run together, speed doubles again, and the body pays with exhaustion, because it was not built to be weather. Once per long rest, as a bonus action: for 1 minute, the meter breaks, Godspeed costs no CE to maintain, you benefit from Whirlwind and Speed of Lightning simultaneously (attacks against you have disadvantage, +30 ft. speed, lightning strikes with the stun rider), and your speed increases by an additional 30 feet. When the minute ends, you gain 1 level of exhaustion, the body was not built to be weather.

Rules integration

Edge cases and shared systems.

Campaign canon notes

Cursed Energy baseline: CE pool is based on class; CE ability is Charisma or Wisdom (player's choice). CE save DC = 8 + CE ability modifier + proficiency bonus. Regain all spent CE on a long rest.
The drain: entering Godspeed costs a bonus action and 2 CE; it lasts up to 1 minute. At the start of each of your turns, spend 1 CE or Godspeed ends immediately. Switching between Whirlwind and Speed of Lightning costs a bonus action and no extra CE. The two modes cannot be active together (except during The Storm, Perfected).
Whirlwind: attacks against you have disadvantage; you cannot be surprised. Speed of Lightning: +30 ft. speed; opportunity attacks against you have disadvantage; unarmed strikes deal +2d8 lightning; the stun rider applies once per turn per target.
Lightning resistance: while Godspeed is active (either mode), you have resistance to lightning damage. This does not stack with other sources of resistance.
Domain activation defaults: rendered as full turn + 20 CE per the D008 normal-Domain baseline, with Tier IV clash stats (800 / 5-round burnout / 2 min) and a 120-ft radius.
Maximum vs. Reversal: Godspeed's pinnacle is written as a Maximum Technique (Storm's Verdict), not a Reversal; it occupies the technique's pinnacle slot.
