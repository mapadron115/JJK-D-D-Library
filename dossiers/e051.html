Living Circuit
Programmed Lightning Nervous System

Turn cursed energy into bioelectric instructions. Lightning hurts, but the real technique is a nervous system that can move before conscious thought.

Power tierTier IV · Special Grade
Combat roleReaction speedster / interrupter
Core engineReflex Programs
Domain / pinnacleNone — Godspeed is the pinnacle
Core law

What the technique actually does.

Rules are written to plug into the finalized campaign glossary: normal actions stay normal, direct attacks roll, and only named Domain events bypass the initial roll.

Passive

Bioelectric Aura

You gain resistance to lightning damage and advantage on saves against paralysis caused by electricity. Your technique’s lightning is cursed bioelectricity, not a Kashimo-style charge placed in the enemy.

On melee hit · 1 CE

Lightning Palm

Add 1 Circuit Die lightning. Target makes Constitution save or loses reactions until start of its next turn.

Prepared trigger

Reflex Program

After a long rest write one trigger→response pair you can physically perform: “when attacked in melee → step back,” “when hostile enters 5 feet → strike,” “when projectile enters 10 feet → dodge.” A program never creates an action by itself until a feature explicitly grants an automatic response.

Full progression

Level 1 through 20.

Milestones use the campaign’s familiar 1 / 3 / 6 / 11 / 18 structure and add an explicit level-20 mastery feature.

1Nervous Current

Lightning Palm

Gain Bioelectric Aura, Lightning Palm, and one Reflex Program.

Neural Kickstart

Add PB to initiative. If already proficient in initiative from another feature, add half PB instead.

3Lightning Leaves the Body

Thunderbolt

Action · 3 CE: 60-foot ranged CE attack, 4 Circuit Dice lightning; hit target makes Con save or is stunned until end of current turn.

Neurostep

Bonus action · 1 CE: Dash and ignore opportunity attacks from creatures you damaged with lightning this turn.

Second Program

Maintain two Reflex Programs and change one after a short rest.

6Whirlwind

Automatic Response

Bonus action · 3 CE · Focus · 1 minute: activate Whirlwind. Choose up to PB stored Reflex Programs. Once per round when one trigger occurs, execute its legal movement or one basic attack without spending your reaction; further program responses that round use your reaction normally.

Threat Current

While Whirlwind is active, you cannot be surprised by a creature you can perceive and add PB to Dexterity saves.

Contact Stun

Once per turn when Lightning Palm hits a creature that already cannot take reactions, spend +1 CE to reduce its speed to 0 until start of its next turn.

11Godspeed

Speed of Lightning

Bonus action · 5 CE · Focus, 1 CE at start of each turn: speed doubles, running jump distance triples, no opportunity attacks, and gain one restricted extra action for Dash, Disengage, or one weapon/unarmed attack.

Whirlwind Mastery

Whirlwind automatic response can occur twice per round, but the second costs 1 CE.

Terrain Computation

Ignore nonmagical difficult terrain while Godspeed is active and run across walls/liquids during your turn if you end on support.

18Dual Godspeed

Kanmuru

Bonus action · 8 CE · Focus, 2 CE/turn: combine Whirlwind and Speed of Lightning. Speed triples instead of doubles, gain two automatic programmed responses per round, and one additional reaction usable only for movement, defense, or a basic attack.

Thunderclap Intercept

When a creature within your speed begins an action you have a program for, spend 2 CE to move up to half speed and resolve your programmed attack first. If it incapacitates the creature, its triggering action is lost.

No Domain

Living Circuit spends refinement inward. It has no innate Domain; its “sure-hit” fantasy is preemptive execution, which remains rolled and interruptible.

20Perfect Circuit

Zero-Latency Body

During Kanmuru, the first automatic response each round costs no CE and does not count against your two-response limit.

Program the Impossible

You may store up to PB + CE modifier Reflex Programs and rewrite any number during a short rest.

Living Lightning

Once per turn a direct attack made as a programmed response adds 2 Circuit Dice and can Black Flash normally.

Pinnacle expression

Domain, Maximum, or equivalent.

Not every technique needs a Domain. When one exists, its sure-hit is a unique autonomous initiative-count-20 event unless the Domain is explicitly rule-based.

No natural Domain Living Circuit’s peak is Kanmuru: cursed energy is spent on the body’s internal signal timing rather than an external barrier. Generic anti-domain skills remain available through class progression.

Innate Technique Feats

Four build-defining options.

These broaden the law instead of merely inflating the same number.

Prerequisite: Living Circuit

Assassin’s Hands

Your unarmed strikes may deal slashing damage and use Dexterity. Once per turn after Lightning Palm, move 5 feet without provoking.

Prerequisite: Thunderbolt

Recharge Discipline

During a short rest, if you have access to a safe electrical source or spend 5 CE in meditation, gain temporary CE equal PB that can only fuel Living Circuit — it cannot fund Maximum Techniques or Domains — and expires at next rest.

Prerequisite: Whirlwind

Programmed Guard

Add “when I fail a Dexterity save → reroute” as a valid program. Once per round spend 2 CE to add one Circuit Die to that failed save, potentially turning it into success.

Prerequisite: Godspeed

Heart Like a Switch

When initiative is rolled, activate either Whirlwind or Speed of Lightning without a bonus action; pay its normal CE cost.

Rules integration

Edge cases and shared systems.

These rulings are designed around the revised Player Guide rather than the raw PDF where the two differ.

Automatic responses cannot recursively trigger themselves.
A Reflex Program uses only information the character can perceive at the trigger moment.
Extra actions cannot activate another extra-action-granting feature unless that feature explicitly allows it.
Lightning Palm’s direct rider doubles on a critical; stun/lost-reaction conditions do not.
Technique DC: 8 + proficiency bonus + your chosen Cursed Energy ability modifier (Wisdom or Charisma unless your class says otherwise).
Direct criticals: rolled weapon, unarmed, and CE attacks can critical normally. After a qualifying critical, roll the campaign's unmodified Black Flash confirmation d20; a natural 20 triples the completed critical damage. Autonomous Domain events, damage-over-time, hazards, and unrolled pulses cannot Black Flash.
Maximum Output: unless a feature says otherwise, double that feature's base CE cost and enhance one supported axis—damage, range, duration, or accuracy/DC—using the campaign's Maximum Output rules. Named Maximum Techniques and Domains do not receive generic Maximum Output.
Technique Focus: effects marked Focus occupy the shared Technique Focus slot. Opening a Domain ends other Focus effects unless the Domain expressly preserves them.
Two-technique characters: these guides assume each technique progresses from character/class level as agreed by the campaign. Multiclassing itself does not grant another Innate Technique.
