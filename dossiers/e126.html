Core law

What the technique actually does.

Core Engine — Ring Charge & Will. Ring Charge = 20 + level, restored by a long-rest Power Battery ritual. Will tokens max PB; gain 1 by succeeding Wis/Cha save, protecting someone despite fear, or maintaining Focus after heavy damage.

The fiction. Jujutsu scholars classify Emerald Will as resolve made manifest — cursed energy too disciplined to dissipate, answering a wielder whose will has held when everything else failed. The ring and its charge are the technique's accounting, the prevailing theory holds: constructs are not built but permitted, given form in the exact measure the user's conviction sustains. Will tokens are the technique's memory — each one a recorded instance of courage, spent back as the energy returns to the shape that first made it.

Full progression

Level 1 through 20.

1Emerald Construct

Spend 1 Charge to create a weapon or projectile construct (60 ft, 2d8 force) or a simple tool. Your ring provides environmental life support.

3Flight & Shield

Spend 1 Charge per 10 minutes to fly at 60 ft. As a reaction, spend 1 Charge to gain +3 AC or reduce damage you take by 2d10 + your proficiency bonus.

6Complex Constructs

Spend 2 Charge + 1 CE to create a complex construct — Large or smaller, within 60 ft, lasting 10 minutes (Focus) — a vehicle, cage, giant hand, platform, and so on; its attacks use your CE attack bonus and deal 4d8 (+2d10 instead at Mecha scale), and it has 30 Integrity, AC 12 + PB.

9Willpower

Spend 1 Will to add 1d10 to a construct’s damage, a saving throw, or a Focus check, after the roll is made.

11Ring Scan

Analyze energy, language, or life signs within 1 mile; spend 2 Charge for a detailed supernatural scan: your CE ability check vs the target’s Stealth check or spell/technique save DC, target’s choice.

13Mecha Construct

Spend 5 Charge + 4 CE: for 1 minute you wear Large construct armor — +2 AC, fly 80, construct attacks deal +2d10, reach 15 ft.

15No Evil Shall Escape

While you hold 3 or more Will, you are immune to the Frightened condition and the first construct you create each round costs 1 less Charge.

18Domain — Sector 2814

At initiative 20, create one autonomous construct event: an 8d10 force blast (Dexterity save), a 40-Integrity wall, or a giant hand (Strength save or Restrained until the next initiative 20). Your ordinary attacks still roll.

20Indomitable Will

Your Charge maximum doubles and your Will maximum becomes 10. Once per long rest, when you reach 0 Charge, you may continue operating for 3 rounds fueled by will alone; you gain 1 exhaustion afterward.

Pinnacle expression

Maximum, Domain, or equivalent.

Maximum — Emerald Colossus (10 CE + 10 Charge; bonus action): summon Gargantuan construct for 3 rounds. Once each turn command it without action to slam: creatures in a 20-ft burst make a Strength save, taking 6d10 force on failure, half on success; or create 100-Integrity cover. One active effect at a time.

Feats

Build-defining options.

Creative Mind

Your constructs are never single-purpose. When you forge a Complex Construct, you may weave one additional utility function into it at no extra Charge: silent operation, a built-in light source, a sealed airtight compartment, wheels or skids, or a fine-manipulation limb. The utility shares the construct's Integrity and duration, and cannot duplicate another feature's damage. There is always one more use for the ring.

Ring Slinging

when you create a projectile construct, you may launch it from any point within 30 ft of you that you can see, rather than from yourself.

Fearless

when a creature attempts to Frighten you, you may use your reaction to forge the fear into a construct: create a 10-Integrity construct shaped like the source of the fear. If it still exists at the start of your next turn, you gain 1 Will.

Emergency Protocol

Your will outlives your body. Once per long rest, when you are reduced to 0 HP while wearing the ring, it flares on instinct: you are automatically stabilized and gain temporary HP equal to your remaining Will + your proficiency bonus, lasting 1 minute. You remain unconscious — but your constructs do not fall. Any you were maintaining persist for the minute, held together by refusal alone.

Rules integration

Edge cases and shared systems.

Codex rulings: Direct rolled technique/weapon attacks can use the campaign's Black Flash procedure when otherwise eligible. Passive damage, hazards, summons acting independently, and initiative-20 Domain events cannot. Generic Maximum Output applies only to ordinary features, never named Maxima or Domains.
