Core law

What the technique actually does.

Core Engine — Ring Charge & Will. Ring Charge = 20 + level, restored by a long-rest Power Battery ritual. Will tokens max PB; gain 1 by succeeding Wis/Cha save, protecting someone despite fear, or maintaining Focus after heavy damage.

Full progression

Level 1 through 20.

1Emerald Construct

Spend 1 Charge: weapon/projectile 60 ft 2d8 force or simple tool. Ring provides environmental life support.

3Flight & Shield

1 Charge/10 min fly 60. Reaction 1 Charge: +3 AC or 2d10+PB damage reduction.

6Complex Constructs

2 Charge + 1 CE: vehicle, cage, giant hand, platform, etc.; attacks 4d8 or Integrity 30.

9Willpower

Spend 1 Will to add 1d10 to construct damage, save, or Focus check after roll.

11Ring Scan

Analyze energy/language/life signs within 1 mile; 2 Charge for detailed supernatural scan, contested by concealment.

13Mecha Construct

5 Charge + 4 CE, 1 minute: Large armor, +2 AC, fly 80, construct attacks +2d10, reach 15.

15No Evil Shall Escape

At 3+ Will, immune Frightened and first construct each round costs 1 less Charge.

18Domain — Sector 2814

Initiative 20 create one autonomous construct event: 8d10 force blast Dex save, 40-Integrity wall, or giant hand Str save restrain until next initiative 20. Your ordinary attacks still roll.

20Indomitable Will

Charge max doubles, Will max 10. Once/long rest at 0 Charge, continue operating for 3 rounds fueled by will; gain 1 exhaustion afterward.

Pinnacle expression

Maximum, Domain, or equivalent.

Maximum — Emerald Colossus (10 CE + 10 Charge): summon Gargantuan construct for 3 rounds. Once each turn command it without action to slam 20-ft burst for 6d10 force or create 100-Integrity cover. One active effect at a time.

Feats

Build-defining options.

Creative Mind

Your constructs are never single-purpose. When you forge a Complex Construct, you may weave one additional utility function into it at no extra Charge: silent operation, a built-in light source, a sealed airtight compartment, wheels or skids, or a fine-manipulation limb. The utility shares the construct's Integrity and duration, and cannot duplicate another feature's damage. There is always one more use for the ring.

Ring Slinging

when you create a projectile construct, you may launch it from any point within 30 ft of you that you can see, rather than from yourself.

Fearless

when a creature attempts to Frighten you, you may use your reaction to forge the fear into a construct: create a 10-Integrity construct shaped like the source of the fear. If it still exists at the start of your next turn, you gain 1 Will.

Emergency Protocol

Your will outlives your body. Once per long rest, when you are reduced to 0 HP while wearing the ring, it flares on instinct: you are automatically stabilized and gain temporary HP equal to your remaining Will + your proficiency bonus, lasting 1 minute. You remain unconscious — but your constructs do not fall. Any you were maintaining persist for the minute, held together by refusal alone.

Rules integration

Edge cases and shared systems.

Codex rulings: Direct rolled technique/weapon attacks can use the campaign's Black Flash procedure when otherwise eligible. Passive damage, hazards, summons acting independently, and initiative-20 Domain events cannot. Generic Maximum Output applies only to ordinary features, never named Maxima or Domains.
