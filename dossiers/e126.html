Core law

What the technique actually does.

Core Engine, Ring Charge & Will. Two ledgers: Ring Charge, twenty plus level, restored by the long-rest Power Battery ritual, and Will, maxed at proficiency, earned by holding saves, protecting despite fear, or keeping Focus through heavy damage. Ring Charge = 20 + level, restored by a long-rest Power Battery ritual. Will tokens max PB; gain 1 by succeeding Wis/Cha save, protecting someone despite fear, or maintaining Focus after heavy damage.

Field notes. Subject: an architect whose only material is refusal to quit. Three truths govern him. First: the ring is the technique's accounting, Ring Charge for the building, Will tokens for the insisting, and the will is earned, never granted: by holding saves, by protecting despite fear, by keeping Focus through heavy damage. Second: the constructs are imagination with a ledger, a weapon at first level, a vehicle or cage at sixth, a mecha at thirteenth, an autonomous Domain event at eighteenth; each with its Integrity, its AC, its price. Third: the will outlives the body, at the summit he may run on empty Charge for three rounds fueled by nothing but refusal, and take the exhaustion after. Mark the paradox: the ring is the most honest weapon in the archive. It does exactly as much as he means.

Full progression

Level 1 through 20.

1Emerald Construct

Action · 1 Charge · Create a weapon or projectile construct (60 ft, 2d8 force) or a simple tool; your ring provides environmental life support.

One Charge for a weapon or projectile at sixty feet for two dice, or a simple tool. The ring provides life support besides; the architect arrives equipped.

Reaction · 1 Will · Add 1d8 to a saving throw or ability check you make.

Indomitable Spark. The will was never only for building. One token, one refusal: a die added to the save, the check, the moment of not-quitting. The ring's smallest miracle, and the first.

One Charge per ten minutes at sixty feet, and a reaction's worth of refusal: +3 AC or two dice plus proficiency off the damage. The ring answers emergencies.

Two Charge and one CE for the real drafting table: a Large-or-smaller vehicle, cage, hand, or platform, thirty Integrity, his CE attack bonus behind four dice. Imagination, itemized.

One Will, spent after the roll: a die added to a construct's damage, a save, or a Focus check. The ledger of insistence opens.

Charge doubles, Will caps at ten, and once per long rest, at zero Charge he runs three more rounds on will alone, taking exhaustion after. The body quits last.

One active effect at a time.

Feats

Build-defining options.

Creative Mind

His constructs are never single-purpose: one utility function woven free into every Complex Construct, silence, light, sealed air. The blueprint has margins, and he uses them. When you forge a Complex Construct, you may weave one additional utility function into it at no extra Charge: silent operation, a built-in light source, a sealed airtight compartment, wheels or skids, or a fine-manipulation limb. The utility shares the construct's Integrity and duration, and cannot duplicate another feature's damage. There is always one more use for the ring.

Ring Slinging

The projectile may launch from any point he can see within thirty feet, not from himself. The ring's arm is longer than his.

Fearless

An attempt to Frighten him becomes raw material: a reaction forges the fear into a ten-Integrity construct shaped like its source, and if it survives to his next turn, the fear is spent. He builds with the enemy's threats. If it still exists at the start of your next turn, you gain 1 Will.

Emergency Protocol

Once per long rest, at zero HP the ring flares on instinct: automatic stabilization and temporary HP equal to remaining Will plus proficiency. The will outlives the body, literally. The temporary HP lasts 1 minute. You remain unconscious, but your constructs do not fall. Any you were maintaining persist for the minute, held together by refusal alone.

Rules integration

Edge cases and shared systems.

Codex rulings: Direct rolled technique/weapon attacks can use the campaign's Black Flash procedure when otherwise eligible. Passive damage, hazards, summons acting independently, and initiative-20 Domain events cannot. Generic Maximum Output applies only to ordinary features, never named Maxima or Domains.
