Core law

What the technique actually does.

Core Engine, Ring Charge & Will. Two ledgers: Ring Charge, twenty plus level, restored by the long-rest Power Battery ritual, and Will, maxed at proficiency, earned by holding saves, protecting despite fear, or keeping Focus through heavy damage. Ring Charge = 20 + level, restored by a long-rest Power Battery ritual. Will tokens max PB; gain 1 by succeeding Wis/Cha save, protecting someone despite fear, or maintaining Focus after heavy damage.

Field notes. Subject: an architect whose only material is refusal to quit. Three truths govern him. First: the ring is the technique's accounting, Ring Charge for the building, Will tokens for the insisting, and the will is earned, never granted: by holding saves, by protecting despite fear, by keeping Focus through heavy damage. Second: the constructs are imagination with a ledger, a weapon at first level, a vehicle or cage at sixth, a mecha at thirteenth, an autonomous Domain event at eighteenth; each with its Integrity, its AC, its price. Third: the will outlives the body, at the summit he may run on empty Charge for three rounds fueled by nothing but refusal, and take the exhaustion after. Mark the paradox: the ring is the most honest weapon in the archive. It does exactly as much as he means.

Feats

Build-defining options.

Creative Mind

His constructs are never single-purpose: one utility function woven free into every Complex Construct, silence, light, sealed air. The blueprint has margins, and he uses them. Your constructs are never single-purpose. When you forge a Complex Construct, you may weave one additional utility function into it at no extra Charge: silent operation, a built-in light source, a sealed airtight compartment, wheels or skids, or a fine-manipulation limb. The utility shares the construct's Integrity and duration, and cannot duplicate another feature's damage. There is always one more use for the ring.

Ring Slinging

The projectile may launch from any point he can see within thirty feet, not from himself. The ring's arm is longer than his. when you create a projectile construct, you may launch it from any point within 30 ft of you that you can see, rather than from yourself.

Fearless

An attempt to Frighten him becomes raw material: a reaction forges the fear into a ten-Integrity construct shaped like its source, and if it survives to his next turn, the fear is spent. He builds with the enemy's threats. when a creature attempts to Frighten you, you may use your reaction to forge the fear into a construct: create a 10-Integrity construct shaped like the source of the fear. If it still exists at the start of your next turn, you gain 1 Will.

Emergency Protocol

Once per long rest, at zero HP the ring flares on instinct: automatic stabilization and temporary HP equal to remaining Will plus proficiency. The will outlives the body, literally. Your will outlives your body. Once per long rest, when you are reduced to 0 HP while wearing the ring, it flares on instinct: you are automatically stabilized and gain temporary HP equal to your remaining Will + your proficiency bonus, lasting 1 minute. You remain unconscious, but your constructs do not fall. Any you were maintaining persist for the minute, held together by refusal alone.

Rules integration

Edge cases and shared systems.

Codex rulings: Direct rolled technique/weapon attacks can use the campaign's Black Flash procedure when otherwise eligible. Passive damage, hazards, summons acting independently, and initiative-20 Domain events cannot. Generic Maximum Output applies only to ordinary features, never named Maxima or Domains.
