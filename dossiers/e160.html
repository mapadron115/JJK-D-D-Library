The Possessive Mask

One soul, many coats, and the mask always knows whose it is. Wear one body until it rots, tempt another into accepting the mask, then move your consciousness across humanoid hosts while keeping one shared HP, CE, and action economy.

Combat roleSocial controller / body-hopper / infiltrator
Core law

Host Integrity

Living Host

The costume has a wear-rating: Integrity, ticking down each turn. Host Integrity = PB + CE modifier + the host’s Constitution modifier, minimum 3. Lose 1 Integrity at the end of each of your turns while in a non-Prime living Host.

Using a Host

Continuity finding: the soul's statistics persist; the body's are borrowed. There is only ever one pool of health to spend. The subject is never two people. It is one person, wearing people. Use the Host’s size, STR, DEX, CON, mundane senses, movement modes, and natural weapons. Keep your own INT, WIS, CHA, HP, CE, PB, proficiencies, class features, and Innate Technique. You do not gain the Host’s class features, spellcasting, Innate Technique, attunements, or legendary actions.

Structural collapse

Failure mode: at zero Integrity the costume fails and the soul snaps home, to the Prime Host if it lives, else the mask falls free and waits. The subject describes this as 'changing rooms.' The rooms describe it differently. At 0 Integrity the Host can no longer move. Your consciousness immediately returns to the living Prime Host if it exists and is on the same plane; otherwise the mask falls free and you are incapacitated until a valid Host wears it.

Prime Host death

Critical dependency: lose the Prime, and the mask becomes the subject's whole world, dependent on occupation to act, restoration of a true Prime a campaign-level objective. Immortality, with a single point of failure. We have circled it in red. If the Prime Host dies while you are elsewhere, you do not immediately die. You become dependent on the mask and must occupy a valid Host to act. Restoring or replacing a true Prime Host becomes a campaign-level objective rather than free immortality.

Research log. Subject: identity reduced to a passenger. The subject was recovered as a porcelain mask with no wearer, it has since worn eleven. Interviews conducted through successive hosts confirm a single continuous consciousness; the mask owns the continuity, and the bodies are currency. Two truths govern this file. First: Temptation is groomed, not seized, one accepted touch at a time, until a host at three marks can be taken whole; even the unwilling receive their Charisma save, because the technique honors the shape of consent it then violates. Second: costumes wear thin, Host Integrity ticks down every turn, corpses keep better than the living, and every transfer is a small death of the vessel; damage follows the soul, never the meat. The current host has asked us to note that it is cooperating voluntarily. The mask, through the same mouth, has confirmed this. We are logging both statements without endorsement.

Full progression

Progression

1Mask / Temptation

Prime Host.

Prime Host identified: the original body, the one the mask calls home. All continuity, consciousness, energy, technique, belongs to the mask-bound soul, not to whichever body currently wears it. The host is a vehicle. The mask is the driver. The driver has never once asked directions. Your original body is your Prime Host. Your consciousness, CE, HP, class features, and technique belong to the mask-bound soul, not to whichever body currently wears it.

Manifest Mask.

Donning event: porcelain on the face, the performance can begin. Average donning time, four seconds. Average host compliance afterward, total. We time it anyway. Bonus action, 1 CE. A porcelain mask forms on your face. While worn by your Prime Host, gain telepathy 30 feet and proficiency in Deception or Persuasion; if already proficient, add PB again to checks with that skill.

Corrosive Secretion.

Ichor analysis: black, viscous, restraining, how the mask holds its audience still. Chemically unremarkable. Theatrically devastating. Once per turn, after you hit a creature with a melee attack, succeed on a grapple check, or touch an unattended object (no action), spend 1 CE. Creature: it makes a Constitution save vs your CE DC; on failure black ichor binds its limbs, its speed is halved and it cannot take reactions until the start of your next turn. Unattended nonmagical object: it corrodes, a Tiny or Small object is destroyed; a larger object gains a 1-foot-diameter hole or loses one component of your choice.

Temptation.

Grooming pattern: seduction in three marks, the slow work of making a host volunteer. Each mark is accepted. Each acceptance is documented. The paperwork of consent, completed just before consent becomes irrelevant. Action, 1 CE: a creature within 30 feet that can see or hear the mask makes a Wisdom save. On failure it gains 1 Temptation, maximum 3, for 1 minute. At 1 it has disadvantage on Insight against you; at 2 its speed is halved when willingly moving farther from the mask; at 3 it becomes a valid conscious target for Host Transfer. At the end of each turn it may repeat the Wisdom save, removing 1 Temptation on success.

Shared Vitality.

Damage routing: one ledger for all bodies, damage follows the soul, not the meat. Wounding the host wounds the subject. The subject considers this a fair trade. The hosts have not been consulted, except through the mask, which confirms they agree. Possessing another body never creates a second HP or CE pool. Damage to your current Host damages your normal HP.

3Host Transfer

Action, 3 CE: place the mask The curtain rises on a new body; the old one goes dark. on a willing or incapacitated humanoid-shaped creature within 5 feet, or a conscious living creature at 3 Temptation. An unwilling living target makes a Charisma save. On failure your consciousness moves into it and your Prime Host falls unconscious but stable. The host repeats the save at the end of each turn, ejecting you on success.

6Borrowed Bodies

Corpses, mannequins, statues. Casting note: the dead make excellent understudies, they never eject the subject. The subject prefers the living for the performance and the dead for the reliability. We find both preferences troubling. , and other humanoid-shaped nonliving vessels automatically accept Host Transfer. They use STR 16, DEX 12, CON 16, AC 13 + half PB (rounded down), speed 30 unless their construction suggests otherwise. They lose 1 Host Integrity per minute rather than per turn.

11Mind Under the Mask

Telepathy and Temptation range become At three Temptation, the mask can hear its audience thinking. 120 feet and 60 feet respectively. At 3 Temptation you may read a target’s surface thoughts while within telepathy range. When your current Host reaches 0 Integrity, use your reaction and 2 CE to transfer to another valid Host within 30 feet; otherwise return to the Prime Host.

18Theater of the Anguished Mask

A creature that fails its Corrosive Secretion save is restrained Ichor that restrains, and the stage set for the Domain below. until the start of your next turn instead, and unattended objects corrode at no CE cost. You gain the Domain below.

Pinnacle expression

Domain Expansion — Theater of the Anguished Mask

Expansion A black theater where every seat wears a face, and the audience cannot leave. Full-turn action, 20 CE, enclosed 60-foot-radius black theater for 1 minute. Every seat, curtain, and spotlight wears a smiling or grieving face.

Curtain Call, sole sure-hit At initiative count 20, the whole house is tempted at once. At initiative count 20, each chosen hostile creature gains 1 Temptation, up to 3. A protected creature makes a Wisdom save and gains none on success.

Remote Host Transfer Inside the theater, the mask need not even touch its next actor. Inside the Domain, a creature at 3 Temptation within 60 feet is a valid Host Transfer target without touch. An unwilling living creature still receives its normal Charisma save and repeats it at the end of its turns.

Masquerade movement Exit one Host, enter another, the performance never pauses. When you voluntarily leave a Host, you may choose another valid Host within 60 feet instead of returning to the Prime Host. Each transfer still pays its normal CE cost unless a feature says otherwise.

Clash bookkeeping 800 clash points (Tier IV). In a Domain clash, a 100+ point advantage collapses the weaker Domain; collapse deals no damage. Barrier: AC 12 + PB; HP = 10 × max(1, your CE modifier) + 1,600. Burnout: 5 rounds.

Innate Technique Feats

Technique Feats

The Possessive Mask

Honeyed Words

Social finding: a silver tongue earns its marks without spending an action. The subject talks its way into faces the way other organisms breathe. Interviewers are now rotated every ten minutes. Once per turn when you succeed on Deception, Persuasion, or Intimidation against a creature currently at 0 Temptation, you may spend 1 CE to give it 1 Temptation without using an action.

Corrosive Secretion

Black Ichor

Ichor persistence: where spilled on a surface, it lingers as a corrosive patch. The stage retains the performance. Our floors have begun to keep a memory of every show. When Corrosive Secretion corrodes an unattended surface, leave a 5-foot corrosive patch until the start of your next turn. A creature entering it for the first time on a turn has its speed halved until the start of your next turn, maximum once per turn.

Host Transfer, level 6

Borrowed Aptitude

Skill acquisition: every body brings its own training; the mask keeps the best of it. Eleven hosts, eleven proficiencies, one resume. The subject is, technically, the most qualified individual in the facility. When you enter a living Host, choose one skill in which that Host is proficient. You gain proficiency in it while possessing that body; if already proficient, you gain no additional expertise.

Host Transfer, level 11

Never Without a Face

Contingency observed: the prudent actor keeps a spare costume within a mile, a prepared vessel, designated during rest. The subject always has an understudy waiting in the wings. We have mapped three. There are, we suspect, more. During a short rest, designate one valid nonliving vessel, a corpse, mannequin, or statue you have prepared, within 1 mile as your contingency. The first time within the next 24 hours that a Host ejects you or reaches 0 Integrity, your consciousness transfers to the contingency vessel immediately without spending CE or an action, instead of returning to the Prime Host or falling free.

Rules integration

Table Rulings

Host Transfer is deliberately difficult against unwilling PCs: it requires three failed Temptation saves, then a Charisma save, and repeats every turn. It is powerful setup control rather than effortless character theft.
Possession does not heal you. All bodies share your current and maximum HP.
An inanimate Host receives the standard physical stat line above unless the GM provides an existing creature/object profile.
The mask’s telepathy may discover surface thoughts only where Mind Under the Mask says so; it is not unrestricted mind reading at level 1.
