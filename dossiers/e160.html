The Possessive Mask

Wear one body until it rots, tempt another into accepting the mask, then move your consciousness across humanoid hosts while keeping one shared HP, CE, and action economy.

RoleSocial controller / body-hopper / infiltratorSourceSCP Wiki

Core Engine — Mask, Temptation, and Hosts

  • Mask Die: d6 → d8 → d10 → d12.
  • Prime Host. Your original body is your Prime Host. Your consciousness, CE, HP, class features, and technique belong to the mask-bound soul, not to whichever body currently wears it.
  • Manifest Mask. Bonus action, 1 CE. A porcelain mask forms on your face. While worn by your Prime Host, gain telepathy 30 feet and proficiency in Deception or Persuasion; if already proficient, add PB again to checks with that skill.
  • Corrosive Secretion. Once per turn after a melee hit, grapple, or touch against an object, spend 1 CE to deal +1 Mask Die acid or necrotic damage. Unattended mundane objects take maximum damage from this die.
  • Temptation. Action, 1 CE: a creature within 30 feet that can see or hear the mask makes a Wisdom save. On failure it gains 1 Temptation, maximum 3, for 1 minute. At 1 it has disadvantage on Insight against you; at 2 its speed is halved when willingly moving farther from the mask; at 3 it becomes a valid conscious target for Host Transfer. At the end of each turn it may repeat the Wisdom save, removing 1 Temptation on success.
  • Shared Vitality. Possessing another body never creates a second HP or CE pool. Damage to your current Host damages your normal HP.

Host Integrity

Living Host

Host Integrity = PB + CE modifier + the host’s Constitution modifier, minimum 3. Lose 1 Integrity at the end of each of your turns while in a non-Prime living Host.

Using a Host

Use the Host’s size, STR, DEX, CON, mundane senses, movement modes, and natural weapons. Keep your own INT, WIS, CHA, HP, CE, PB, proficiencies, class features, and Innate Technique. You do not gain the Host’s class features, spellcasting, Innate Technique, attunements, or legendary actions.

Structural collapse

At 0 Integrity the Host can no longer move. Your consciousness immediately returns to the living Prime Host if it exists and is on the same plane; otherwise the mask falls free and you are incapacitated until a valid Host wears it.

Prime Host death

If the Prime Host dies while you are elsewhere, you do not immediately die. You become dependent on the mask and must occupy a valid Host to act. Restoring or replacing a true Prime Host becomes a campaign-level objective rather than free immortality.

Domain Expansion — Theater of the Anguished Mask

Expansion

Full-turn action, 20 CE, enclosed 175-foot-radius black theater for 1 minute. Every seat, curtain, and spotlight wears a smiling or grieving face.

Curtain Call — sole sure-hit

At initiative count 20, each chosen hostile creature gains 1 Temptation, up to 3. A protected creature makes a Wisdom save and gains none on success.

Remote Host Transfer

Inside the Domain, a creature at 3 Temptation within 60 feet is a valid Host Transfer target without touch. An unwilling living creature still receives its normal Charisma save and repeats it at the end of its turns.

Masquerade movement

When you voluntarily leave a Host, you may choose another valid Host within 60 feet instead of returning to the Prime Host. Each transfer still pays its normal CE cost unless a feature says otherwise.

Technique Feats

The Possessive Mask

Honeyed Words

Once per turn when you succeed on Deception, Persuasion, or Intimidation against a creature currently at 0 Temptation, you may spend 1 CE to give it 1 Temptation without using an action.

Corrosive Secretion

Black Ichor

When Corrosive Secretion damages an unattended surface, leave a 5-foot corrosive patch until the start of your next turn. A creature entering it for the first time on a turn takes 1 Mask Die acid damage, maximum once per turn.

Host Transfer, level 6

Borrowed Aptitude

When you enter a living Host, choose one skill in which that Host is proficient. You gain proficiency in it while possessing that body; if already proficient, you gain no additional expertise.

Host Transfer, level 11

Never Without a Face

Once per short rest when a Host ejects you or reaches 0 Integrity, you may transfer to another valid Host within 60 feet without spending CE.

Table Rulings

  • Host Transfer is deliberately difficult against unwilling PCs: it requires three failed Temptation saves, then a Charisma save, and repeats every turn. It is powerful setup control rather than effortless character theft.
  • Possession does not heal you. All bodies share your current and maximum HP.
  • An inanimate Host receives the standard physical stat line above unless the GM provides an existing creature/object profile.
  • The mask’s telepathy may discover surface thoughts only where Mind Under the Mask says so; it is not unrestricted mind reading at level 1.
