Features & procedures

Exact player-facing procedures.

Weapon Equip • Level 5

Gale Sovereign.

Tornadoes gather around hands and weapons, the cutting wind under precise control. The sovereign's first audience: the storm, presented at arm's length, awaiting orders.

Weapon Equip forms tornadoes around hands/weapons and grants precise control of cutting wind.

Action • 2 Reserve

Wind Blade

A hundred-and-eighty-foot blade of wind — the failed shoved or dragged thirty feet. The gale's basic vocabulary: cut, then relocate. The wind does not merely strike; it rearranges.

180-ft line, 15 ft wide. Dexterity save; 5 Djinn Dice slashing, half on success. Push or pull failed targets 30 ft.

Bonus Action • 2 Reserve

Sovereign Current

He rides the current one hundred and fifty feet without provocation, carrying a willing companion of his size or smaller. The treaty includes travel: when the storm has agreed to listen, it also agrees to carry.

Fly up to 150 ft without provoking. Carry one willing creature of your size or smaller in the current.

Action • 5 Reserve

Foraz Zora

Twin tornadoes combined into a mountain-rending gale — a hundred-and-twenty-foot point or a four-hundred-foot line, the failed hurled eighty feet — and it may even be spent as a reaction to contest another great wind or water. The sovereign's counterargument: my weather outranks yours.

Combine twin tornadoes into a mountain-rending gale. Choose a 120-ft-radius point within 300 ft or a 400-ft line 60 ft wide. Strength save; 10 Djinn Dice slashing/thunder and hurled 80 ft on failure, half on success. May contest another large wind/water projectile as a reaction by spending +2 Reserve.

Full Djinn Equip • Level 9

He becomes the sky's local government: flight, resistance to thunder and ranged blows, attacks from beyond thirty feet arriving exhausted — and command over ordinary wind, smoke, gas, and falling debris across three hundred feet. The contract, matured: the air itself is now administered.

Fly 160 ft; resistance to thunder and ranged weapon damage; ranged attacks from beyond 30 ft have disadvantage. You may redirect ordinary wind, smoke, gas, and falling debris in a 300-ft radius.

14 Reserve • Codex Extreme

Heaven-Splitting Typhoon

The typhoon erupts — two hundred and fifty feet of storm, the failed lifted two hundred feet and restrained within it, structures answered at full measure. Mountain sections erased, tsunamis countered, armies scoured from the field. The treaty's final clause: when the sovereign speaks at full volume, the sky obeys at full scale.

A 250-ft-radius typhoon erupts within 600 ft. Strength save; 18 Djinn Dice slashing/thunder, half on success. Failure lifts targets up to 200 ft and Restrains them in the storm until the end of your next turn. Structures take maximum dice.

Anime-scale: Strategic scale: erase a mountain section, counter a city-scale tsunami, or scour an army from the field.

Field notes. Subject: the storm that agreed to listen. Three truths govern this obedience. First: the power is borrowed, not born — a wind-djinn's authority poured into a metal vessel and commanded by contract, obedience rather than inheritance. Second: the wind was never theirs; it simply agreed, once, to listen — and the contract's terms are written in mountain-rending gales. Third: control here is not domination but delegation — the sovereign does not push the wind; he assigns it. Mark the maxim: he does not command the storm. He holds its leash, and the leash is a treaty.

Rules integration

Djinn Die, Equip & Extreme Magic

Damage-type chooser: when a Djinn ability lists two damage types, the Djinn King chooses which applies when the damage is dealt.
Independent Djinn Reserve: 16 (Rank A). Refreshes on a long rest. Every Djinn cost below — Weapon Equip, Full Equip, abilities, reactions, Extreme Magic — spends this Djinn’s Reserve, never your CE. If this Djinn’s Reserve is empty it simply cannot fuel more until you rest; your other Djinn keep their own separate Reserves.
Weapon Equip · level 5: bonus action, 2 Reserve, lasts 10 minutes.
Full Djinn Equip · level 9: bonus action, 4 Reserve, lasts 1 minute. Only one Equip can be active.
Deep Synchronization · level 11: once per short rest, one Equip activation costs 1 Reserve less.
Extreme Magic · level 13: action; requires that Djinn’s Full Equip; pay the listed Reserve cost from this Djinn’s Reserve; usable once per long rest per Djinn. Full Equip ends afterward, and you cannot Equip that Djinn again until you finish a short rest.
King Vessel · level 17: switch directly from one Full Equip to another as a bonus action, paying the new Equip cost. At level 20, once per long rest, immediately transition from one Djinn’s Extreme into another Djinn’s Full Equip.
Attack / save: use your CE attack modifier and CE save DC unless this Djinn explicitly says otherwise.
