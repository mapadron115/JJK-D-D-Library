Core law

What the technique actually does.

Character engine

Designate doors, bridges, hallway lines, or paired markers as Thresholds. Hostile crossings generate Vigilance and let the guardian intercept, relocate allies, and eventually account for every intrusion.

Technique save DC: 8 + PB + chosen CE ability modifier. Technique attack: PB + chosen CE ability modifier.

The fiction. Jujutsu scholars classify it as guardianship calcified into geometry — cursed energy that awakened the day its user decided some line must never be crossed, and has been standing on that line ever since. The prevailing theory holds the technique does not see people at all, only *crossings*: every designated threshold is a place the sorcerer's resolve was sworn once and keeps being sworn, on a loop, at anyone who tests it. The colossus is not summoned; it is the posture of refusal, given gates.

Full progression

Level progression

1Guardian Threshold

As an action for 1 CE, you designate a Threshold (a door, bridge, hallway line, or paired markers) for 10 minutes. You can maintain up to PB Thresholds at once (invented). The first hostile crossing each turn generates Vigilance (maximum PB, invented) and forces a Wisdom save; on a failure the creature's speed becomes 0 immediately after crossing. Unspent Vigilance vanishes when no Threshold is active or when you finish a long rest (invented).

3Bull-Lion Intercession

When an ally within 30 feet is hit by an attack, you may use your reaction and spend 1 Vigilance to interpose: you and the ally swap positions (the ally is drawn into the space you left; willing allies only). You become the attack’s target instead and take the hit in full with your stone body. If you are within 10 feet of one of your Thresholds when you intercept, the gate steadies you — you have resistance to the triggering attack’s damage.

6Five-Legged Perspective

You can see and hear 10 feet around every Threshold. As a bonus action for 2 CE, you teleport from one Threshold to another within 120 feet.

11Name on the Gate

You mark a number of creatures (or a category) equal to your proficiency bonus as Welcome. Welcome creatures gain +10 speed when crossing your Thresholds; other creatures that fail their Threshold save also take 2d8 of force damage (invented: the die is a d8), once per turn.

18Domain Expansion: Gate No Enemy Crosses Uncounted

Full-turn action, 20 CE, enclosed 60-foot radius centered on you, up to 1 minute, Technique Focus. Create four indestructible Domain Thresholds. At initiative 20, each chosen hostile that crossed any Threshold since the previous initiative 20 receives 1 Census Mark. A protected creature may make a Wisdom save to avoid that mark. Immediately spend any number of Census Marks, choosing separately for each mark: push that creature 10 feet toward the Threshold it crossed; give one ally +2 AC against that creature until the next initiative 20; or deal 1d8 force damage (invented: a Ward Die is a d8), to a maximum of 3 damage marks per creature per event. Unspent Census Marks vanish after the event. Ordinary attacks still roll.

Innate Technique Feats

Innate Technique Feats

4 optional build choices

Inscribed Gate

When you inscribe a Threshold, you may spend 1 Vigilance to render it invisible to hostile creatures. A hostile creature can spot it with a successful Wisdom (Perception) check against your technique save DC.

Winged Guardian

When you use Bull-Lion Intercession, you may instead teleport the ally from one Threshold to another Threshold within 30 feet of the first, rather than swapping positions with them; you still take the hit either way.

Palace Watch

As a bonus action, you may spend 2 Vigilance to relocate one Threshold you can see to a new valid anchor within 60 feet.

No Unrecorded Passage (11+)

Hostile invisible creatures that cross one of your Thresholds are revealed until the end of their turn: invisibility and similar concealment are suppressed for the crossing creature as the gate counts its passage. The creature may attempt a Wisdom save against your technique DC; on a success it remains unseen but still generates Vigilance as normal. You always know the exact space where the crossing occurred. This revelation pierces magical invisibility but not total cover or anti-divination effects.

Rules integration

Edge cases and shared systems.

Domain procedure This entry uses the repaired campaign Domain rules. The level-18 feature above is complete: its printed action, CE cost, radius, duration, initiative-20 law, fallback protection, and counterplay replace any legacy blanket auto-hit wording.
Counterplay Fight in open terrain, attack from multiple directions, or teleport without crossing marked lines. The guardian is strongest at actual chokepoints.
