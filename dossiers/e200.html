Core law

What the technique actually does.

Character engine

Designate doors, bridges, hallway lines, or paired markers as Thresholds. Hostile crossings generate Vigilance and let the guardian intercept, relocate allies, and eventually account for every intrusion.

Technique save DC: 8 + PB + chosen CE ability modifier. Technique attack: PB + chosen CE ability modifier.

Full progression

Level progression

1Guardian Threshold

Action, 1 CE, 10 minutes: first hostile crossing each turn grants Vigilance and Wis save; failure speed becomes 0 immediately after crossing.

3Bull-Lion Intercession

Reaction spend 1 Vigilance: reduce damage to ally within 30 ft by 1 die + CE mod and move it 5 ft toward nearest Threshold.

6Five-Legged Perspective

See/hear 10 ft around every Threshold; bonus action, 2 CE teleport from one Threshold to another within 120 ft.

11Name on the Gate

Mark PB creatures/category as Welcome. Welcome gains +10 speed crossing; others failing Threshold save also take 2 dice force once/turn.

18Domain Expansion: Gate No Enemy Crosses Uncounted

Full-turn action, 20 CE, enclosed 60-foot radius centered on you, up to 1 minute, Technique Focus. Create four indestructible Domain Thresholds. At initiative 20, each chosen hostile that crossed any Threshold since the previous initiative 20 receives 1 Census Mark. A protected creature may make a Wisdom save to avoid that mark. Immediately spend any number of Census Marks, choosing separately for each mark: push that creature 10 feet toward the Threshold it crossed; give one ally +2 AC against that creature until the next initiative 20; or deal 1 Ward Die force damage, to a maximum of 3 damage marks per creature per event. Unspent Census Marks vanish after the event. Ordinary attacks still roll.

Rules integration

Edge cases and shared systems.

Domain procedure This entry uses the repaired campaign Domain rules. The level-18 feature above is complete: its printed action, CE cost, radius, duration, initiative-20 law, fallback protection, and counterplay replace any legacy blanket auto-hit wording.
Counterplay Fight in open terrain, attack from multiple directions, or teleport without crossing marked lines. The guardian is strongest at actual chokepoints.
