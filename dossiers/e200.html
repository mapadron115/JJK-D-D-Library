Gate of the Protective Colossus

Lamassu inspiration. Designate doors, bridges, hallway lines, or paired markers as Thresholds. Hostile crossings generate Vigilance and let the guardian intercept, relocate allies, and eventually account for every intrusion.

Domain Expansion: Gate No Enemy Crosses UncountedFull-turn action, 20 CE, enclosed 60-foot radius centered on you, up to 1 minute, Technique Focus. Create four indestructible Domain Thresholds. At initiative 20, each chosen hostile that crossed any Threshold since the previous initiative 20 receives 1 Census Mark. A protected creature may make a Wisdom save to avoid that mark. Immediately spend any number of Census Marks, choosing separately for each mark: push that creature 10 feet toward the Threshold it crossed; give one ally +2 AC against that creature until the next initiative 20; or deal 1 Ward Die force damage, to a maximum of 3 damage marks per creature per event. Unspent Census Marks vanish after the event. Ordinary attacks still roll.

Character engine

Designate doors, bridges, hallway lines, or paired markers as Thresholds. Hostile crossings generate Vigilance and let the guardian intercept, relocate allies, and eventually account for every intrusion.

Technique save DC: 8 + PB + chosen CE ability modifier. Technique attack: PB + chosen CE ability modifier.

Counterplay

Fight in open terrain, attack from multiple directions, or teleport without crossing marked lines. The guardian is strongest at actual chokepoints.

Adaptation note. This is original JJK 5e homebrew inspired by Lamassu and the named tradition. The folklore/myth supplies the motif; the resource engine, CE economy, progression, feats, Maximum and Domain procedures are game mechanics created for this codex.
