Features & procedures

Exact player-facing procedures.

Weapon Equip • Level 5

Bararaq Saiqa — Lightning Sword.

Weapon Equip turns the vessel into Baal’s lightning sword. Weapon hits can conduct lightning through armor and nearby targets.

Action • 2 Reserve

Bararaq

Call a bolt at a point or creature within 180 ft. Dexterity save; 5 Djinn Dice lightning. On failure, the target cannot take reactions until the start of its next turn.

Reaction • 3 Reserve

Lightning Incarnation

When targeted by an attack or visible effect, become lightning and move up to 60 ft without provoking. If this movement leaves the effect’s area or attack reach, it misses you.

Action • 5 Reserve

Bararaq Saiqa

Draw lightning from the sky into the sword and release it in a 300-ft line, 40 ft wide. Dexterity save; 9 Djinn Dice lightning/thunder, half on success. Structures take maximum die results.

Full Djinn Equip • Level 9

Fly 120 ft; lightning resistance; your movement can run across air and vertical surfaces. Once per turn, a weapon hit or Baal ability arcs 2 Djinn Dice lightning to up to two creatures within 30 ft of the first target.

12 Reserve • Canon Extreme

Bararaq Inqerad-Saiqa

A gigantic sword of concentrated lightning descends on a point within 600 ft. Choose a 120-ft-radius impact or a 600-ft-long, 80-ft-wide blade-line. Dexterity save; 18 Djinn Dice lightning/thunder, half on success. Failed creatures are Stunned until the start of your next turn. Structures, barriers, and siege targets take maximum dice.

Anime-scale: Strategic scale: cleave a mountain ridge, pierce a colossal target, or erase an army formation across a battlefield.

The fiction. Registry scholars classify Djinn vessels as the rare technique that is not the sorcerer's own: a sealed fragment of Baal's wrath, and the wielder's cursed energy is merely the key that turns in the lock. The lightning was never generated — it was permitted, and each Equip is a renegotiation of that permission. The price, scholars note, is that wrath is a patient teacher: wield Baal long enough and the wielder's own temper starts to answer before the vessel does.

Rules integration

Djinn Die, Equip & Extreme Magic

Damage-type chooser: when a Djinn ability lists two damage types, the Djinn King chooses which applies when the damage is dealt.
Independent Djinn Reserve: 16 (Rank A). Refreshes on a long rest. Every Djinn cost below — Weapon Equip, Full Equip, abilities, reactions, Extreme Magic — spends this Djinn’s Reserve, never your CE. If this Djinn’s Reserve is empty it simply cannot fuel more until you rest; your other Djinn keep their own separate Reserves.
Weapon Equip · level 5: bonus action, 2 Reserve, lasts 10 minutes.
Full Djinn Equip · level 9: bonus action, 4 Reserve, lasts 1 minute. Only one Equip can be active.
Deep Synchronization · level 11: once per short rest, one Equip activation costs 1 Reserve less.
Extreme Magic · level 13: action; requires that Djinn’s Full Equip; pay the listed Reserve cost from this Djinn’s Reserve; usable once per long rest per Djinn. Full Equip ends afterward, and you cannot Equip that Djinn again until you finish a short rest.
King Vessel · level 17: switch directly from one Full Equip to another as a bonus action, paying the new Equip cost. At level 20, once per long rest, immediately transition from one Djinn’s Extreme into another Djinn’s Full Equip.
Attack / save: use your CE attack modifier and CE save DC unless this Djinn explicitly says otherwise.
