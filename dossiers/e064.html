Reaction • 3 CE

When targeted by an attack or visible effect, become lightning and move up to 60 ft without provoking. If this movement leaves the effect’s area or attack reach, it misses you.

Full Djinn Equip • Level 9

Fly 120 ft; lightning resistance; your movement can run across air and vertical surfaces. Once per turn, a weapon hit or Baal ability arcs 2 Djinn Dice lightning to up to two creatures within 30 ft of the first target.

EXTREME MAGIC • LEVEL 13+

Bararaq Inqerad-Saiqa

12 CE • Canon Extreme

A gigantic sword of concentrated lightning descends on a point within 600 ft. Choose a 120-ft-radius impact or a 600-ft-long, 80-ft-wide blade-line. Dexterity save; 18 Djinn Dice lightning/thunder, half on success. Failed creatures are Stunned until the start of your next turn. Structures, barriers, and siege targets take maximum dice.

Anime-scale: Strategic scale: cleave a mountain ridge, pierce a colossal target, or erase an army formation across a battlefield.
