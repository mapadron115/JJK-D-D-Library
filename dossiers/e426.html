Saturated Weapon

A weapon soaked in dense cursed energy until the saturation itself became a weapon.

FormWeapon (Any)
GradeGrade 3
AttunementNot required

Field notes. Subject: a wound that refuses to close, a blade steeped in cursed energy until the density itself became the weapon. Three truths govern it. First, every hit leaves a burning ledger: on a hit the weapon deals an additional 1d6 necrotic damage, the saturation bleeding out of the steel and into the flesh. Second, the wound vetoes recovery: the target cannot regain hit points until the end of its turn, regeneration and healing, all of it paused by the cursed saturation, and I have watched a single strike unravel an entire strategy of attrition. Third, the density asks nothing: no attunement, no action, no cursed energy, the violence is simply there, in the metal. Make them bleed what they cannot replace, and the fight ends before the first bandage.
