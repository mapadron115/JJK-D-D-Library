Features & procedures

Properties & techniques

Exact player-facing procedures

Storm-forged edge

Sealed weather makes for steady manners — +2 to hit and damage on every swing, whether or not the storm ever leaves the blade. When attacking with this weapon you gain a +2 bonus to attack and damage rolls.

Tempest surge

The release is the whole point: as an action for 3 CE, the edge exhales a sixty-foot cone of slashing force — DC 17 Dexterity, 8d6 on failure, half on success. Weather, aimed like a blade. As an action, you can spend 3 cursed energy to force creatures in a 60 ft cone to make a DC 17 Dexterity saving throw, taking 8d6 slashing damage on a failed save or half as much on a successful save.

Field notes. Subject: a scimitar with a storm folded into its edge. Two truths. First: the storm is sealed, not summoned — +2 to strike and wound on every ordinary swing, the blade's baseline manners, until the wielder pays 3 cursed energy and the sky comes out: a sixty-foot cone of slashing force at DC 17, 8d6 to those who fail, half to those who endure. Second: the tempest is a cone, and cones are honest — they punish the clustered and spare the dispersed. Mark the discipline: the surge is an action, not an ornament; the wielder who spends his turn on weather must have bought the moment first. I have watched squalls do less. Wield it as the sky fights: rarely, widely, and only when the field is worth the rain.
