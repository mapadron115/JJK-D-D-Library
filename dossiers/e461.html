Features & procedures

Properties & techniques

Exact player-facing procedures

Storm-forged edge

When attacking with this weapon you gain a +2 bonus to attack and damage rolls.

Tempest surge

As an action, you can spend 3 cursed energy to force creatures in a 60 ft cone to make a DC 17 Dexterity saving throw, taking 8d6 slashing damage on a failed save or half as much on a successful save.
