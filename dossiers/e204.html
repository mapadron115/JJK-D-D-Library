Pallbearer

2d6 bludgeoning, Heavy, Two-Handed. When a hostile creature you damaged since your last turn drops to 0 HP, gain 1 Burden, max PB, until combat ends.

FormExecutioner maul Broad-headed and patient — the shape of an ending, not an argument.
RoleBruiser / momentum It grows more dangerous the longer the fighting goes; the late fight is its native ground.
EngineBurden The weight of deaths the maul refuses to put down, carried as fuel.
Attunement1 hour* An hour's ritual of ownership — the tool must know whose arm carries its dead.
Features & procedures

Properties & techniques

Exact player-facing procedures

Feature

Mark this first: the weight is never yours to pay. The maul grows heavier with every Burden it collects, and the attuned arm alone is exempt — the debt lends its strength only to the one willing to carry it. Each Burden increases the maul's effective weight but never penalizes its attuned wielder; once/turn add one Tool Die if you have 2+ Burden.

Funeral March

The dead walk their own road, and the maul knows the way. Toward the bloodied, toward the finishing blow, it hurries its Bearer — ten feet a Burden, with no attack of opportunity daring to interrupt a funeral. bonus action, 1 CE: move 10 ft per Burden toward a bloodied enemy without opportunity attacks.

Interment

This is the Burden spent, the grave finally filled. Heave the maul down and spend everything: the spectral weight pins its target under all the dead at once — prone for the many, and for the heavy-laden, buried. 3 CE on hit, spend all Burden: Str save or prone; at 3+ Burden also restrained under spectral grave weight until it uses an action to escape.

Empty Casket

A paradox worth noting: when the fight ends and the maul has nothing to carry, it thanks its bearer with a mote of its own cursed energy. An empty casket is still a casket — the tool remembers being owed. if combat ends with no Burden, regain 1 CE once/short rest.

Field notes. Subject: a debt collector. Three truths govern it. First, the maul does not empower — it indebts; every Burden is a death it refuses to put down, lent as weight only to an arm willing to carry. Second, Burden is honest fuel: it grows only from real takedowns, never from time, so an enemy with no expendable dead starves it of its easiest harvest. Third, the spending is burial: Interment turns the whole ledger into one blow — the maul does not strike harder, it strikes with everyone. Mark the final mercy: a fight that ends with no Burden pays the wielder back a mote of energy, as though the tool were relieved not to have carried anyone. The maxim writes itself: the heaviest weapon is the one that remembers every grave.

Rules integration

Cursed-tool attunement

Campaign canon

Cursed-tool attunement: you have PB attunement slots (2–6). This tool occupies one slot and requires a one-hour ritual. Attunement ends voluntarily over a short rest, on death, or when you replace it while over capacity. Distance does not break attunement. Cursed-tool attunement is separate from magic-item attunement.
Balance & counterplay. It snowballs from actual takedowns, not passive time; bosses without adds deny its easiest fuel.
