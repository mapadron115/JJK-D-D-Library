Pallbearer

2d6 bludgeoning, Heavy, Two-Handed. When a hostile creature you damaged since your last turn drops to 0 HP, gain 1 Burden, max PB, until combat ends.

Features & procedures

Properties & techniques

Exact player-facing procedures

Feature

Each Burden increases the maul's effective weight but never penalizes its attuned wielder; once/turn add one Tool Die if you have 2+ Burden.

Funeral March

bonus action, 1 CE: move 10 ft per Burden toward a bloodied enemy without opportunity attacks.

Interment

3 CE on hit, spend all Burden: Str save or prone; at 3+ Burden also restrained under spectral grave weight until it uses an action to escape.

Empty Casket

if combat ends with no Burden, regain 1 CE once/short rest.

The fiction. Registry notes describe Pallbearer as a tool that remembers its work: the maul was present at enough killings that the weight of the dead soaked into the steel, and now it collects Burden the way a graveyard collects stones. The prevailing theory holds that the tool does not empower the wielder — it indebts them; every Burden is a death the maul refuses to put down, and it lends its weight only to an arm willing to carry.

Rules integration

Cursed-tool attunement

Campaign canon

Cursed-tool attunement: you have PB attunement slots (2–6). This tool occupies one slot and requires a one-hour ritual. Attunement ends voluntarily over a short rest, on death, or when you replace it while over capacity. Distance does not break attunement. Cursed-tool attunement is separate from magic-item attunement.
Balance & counterplay. It snowballs from actual takedowns, not passive time; bosses without adds deny its easiest fuel.
