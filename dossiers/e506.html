Cursed Spirit — Field Entry

Smallpox Deity

Field notes. Subject: the last rites, performed preemptively. Three truths govern the Smallpox Deity. First: acknowledgement is the attack. No gesture, no incantation, no warning, the moment it names you its target, the Coffin of the Plague Pit manifests: a coffin locks, an immense rock crushes, and smallpox takes hold in violence. Second: its presence alone is suffocating. A thirty-foot plague aura poisons the lungs, and its touch sows the disease itself, exhaustion that deepens at every dawn until the body surrenders. Third: it does not lose arguments. Legendary Resistance turns aside three failures a day, and any creature dropped to zero by its Domain dies instantly, no resistance, no counterplay, just inevitability. I have watched sorcerers prepare for this spirit. The prepared ones still bled. Do not let it acknowledge you. If it has, you are already inside the coffin.

Classification & Risk

A Disease Cursed Spirit, Special Grade (CR 17+ band). Old, ritualistic, reeking of decay. Its presence alone is suffocating; its technique is a funerary rite.

Stats

Ability scores & traits

STR 18 (+4) · DEX 10 (+0) · CON 24 (+7) · INT 14 (+2) · WIS 18 (+4) · CHA 18 (+4). HP 340 (27d12 + 162), AC 18, speed 30 ft. Plague Aura: each creature that starts its turn within 30 ft. must succeed on a DC 18 Constitution saving throw or be poisoned until the start of its next turn. Plague (disease curse). Legendary Resistance (3/Day): if it fails a saving throw, it can choose to succeed instead.

Actions

Actions

Plague Touch. Melee Attack: +12 to hit, reach 5 ft. Hit: 3d8 + 6 necrotic damage, and the target must succeed on a DC 18 Constitution saving throw or contract smallpox: one level of exhaustion, repeating the save at each dawn — three successes end it. Domain Expansion: Coffin of the Plague Pit (1/Day). The moment the deity acknowledges its target, its Domain manifests instantly: each creature within 60 ft. must succeed on a DC 20 Constitution saving throw. On a failure, a coffin of metal and wood locks around the victim (restrained), is crushed beneath an immense rock (8d10 bludgeoning damage, funerary-bell echo), and then smallpox takes hold violently (8d10 necrotic damage). A creature reduced to 0 hit points by this Domain dies instantly — no resistance or counterplay, just inevitability. On a success, half damage and not restrained.

The domain smells of old wood and older earth. The ground opens into rows of plague pits, and above each one a sealed coffin hangs in the air, swaying gently, while a single funerary bell tolls from somewhere that has no direction. The air is thick and sweet and wrong, the way sickrooms are.

Risk

Special

Witnesses report the execution of a bound, gagged criminal: straightjacket to prevent handsigns, yet the Domain needed no gesture — acknowledgement alone sufficed. Do not let it acknowledge you. If it has, you are already inside the coffin; spend everything on the saving throw, because there is no second one.

Field rule — perception: At Grade 2 and above this spirit’s cursed energy is dense enough that no Invisibility trait is assigned; standard perception applies. It remains invisible to ordinary non-sorcerers.
