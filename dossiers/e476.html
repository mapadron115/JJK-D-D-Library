Field notes. Subject: power, ripened. Three truths govern the peach. First, the ingredient is time: three years buys +2 to the CE maximum, six buys +3, nine buys +5: the fruit does not hurry, and neither may the eater. Second, the gift is permanent: once eaten, the increase to the cursed energy maximum never leaves; it is not a loan but a harvest. Third, the body remembers its one meal: each creature may benefit only once, however ripe the second peach. Mark the maxim: the orchard does not hurry. Neither should the thief. To wait nine years for a peach is its own kind of jujutsu, the discipline of leaving a weapon on the tree.

Ripened power

Consumable · No CE · Eat the peach: permanently +2 / +3 / +5 to your Cursed Energy maximum for 3 / 6 / 9 years ripened (highest tier, tiers don't stack); one use per creature.

One peach, one harvest. Eating it permanently raises your cursed energy maximum, +2 for three years of ripening, +3 for six, +5 for nine, taking the highest tier reached. And each creature reaps only once: the body remembers its single meal. Eating this peach permanently increases your Cursed Energy maximum. The increase depends on how long the fruit has ripened: 3 years → +2, 6 years → +3, 9 years → +5. Use the highest tier the peach has reached; tiers do not stack.

Campaign canon notes

Once per creature: each creature can benefit from an Eternal Peach only once, a second peach, however ripe, grants nothing further. The increase applies to the CE maximum (and current CE, up to the new maximum).
