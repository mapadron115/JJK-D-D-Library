Blood Spear

A Grade 3 cursed weapon, a blood-steeped spear.

FormWeapon (Spear)
GradeGrade 3
AttunementNot required

Field notes. Subject: a harvester that pays its wielder in the currency of the slain. Three truths govern the spear. First, it feeds only on finished work: when a melee attack with it reduces a target to 0 hit points, the death is not an ending but a transaction, the spear drinks the fallen. Second, the wage is borrowed vigor: 2d6 temporary hit points, vitality drained from the killed into the killer, armor made of somebody else's last breath. Third, it asks nothing in advance: no attunement, no cursed energy, no ritual, the spear's terms are pure piecework, paid on delivery. Choose your killing blow the way a reaper chooses his field: the spear only eats what you finish.
