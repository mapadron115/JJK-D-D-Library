Mythical Beast Amber

An electric martial engine whose once-ever release grows past human limits and ends in irreversible collapse.

Field notes. Subject: the battery that may be spent only once. Three truths govern him. First: his everyday lightning is an engine of accumulation, every strike lays down charge, and charge is voltage waiting for an order; the bolt cannot miss a marked target, but it cannot find one he has not touched, so positioning is the whole war. Second: his body is a capacitor with a hairline crack, the Release grows him past human limits minute by minute, immunity, speed, extra actions, priced in exhaustion that mounts with every deactivation save until the fifth minute, when the body collapses and no ordinary revival answers. Third: the technique is spent once in a life, marked permanently even if suppressed, never restored by RCT. Victory, for him, is decided before activation: choose the battlefield where one perfect burn is enough, because there will never be a second.

Domain Expansion: Lightning God’s Realm.

The sky splits open and does not close, a permanent wound of white-blue lightning that never quite strikes. The air crackles with static, hair rising, metal humming, and the clouds rotate slowly around a central eye of pure electric fury. It smells of ozone and burnt copper, the scent of the moment before the thunder, extended into eternity.

Innate Technique Feats

Build-defining options.

These are optional character-building feats. You must meet each listed prerequisite before selecting it.

Prerequisite: Electric Energy

Defensive Surge

The storm bites back uninvited. When a melee strike lands, his reaction forces the attacker to save or seize up, paralyzed, though the blow itself still lands. Lightning does not forgive a hand laid on it. Reaction when a melee attack hits you: the attacker makes a Constitution save. On failure it is paralyzed until the start of its next turn. This does not cancel the triggering hit and can affect a creature only once per turn.

Prerequisite: Lightning Discharge

Imbued Weapon

The weapon becomes a battery. Charge it with stored strikes, and a bonus action within sixty feet draws a five-foot line of lightning between him and it, every creature along the wire pays by the charge. Each melee weapon hit may place one positive charge in that weapon, maximum twice CE modifier for one weapon. Bonus action while it is within 60 feet: consume all charges to create a 5-foot-wide line between you and it. Creatures in the line make Dexterity saves, taking 1d12 lightning per charge, half on success.

Prerequisite: CE score 20 and Mythical Beast Amber Release

Final Discharge

If he deactivates before the fifth minute, he may vent the account's remainder: two d12 of lightning per full minute endured, thirty feet, Dexterity halves it, and the deactivation saves are still owed. Even the exit is a detonation. When you deactivate your Mythical Beast Amber Release with a bonus action before the minute-5 collapse, you can vent its remaining charge as a final blast. Each creature within 30 feet of you must make a Dexterity save against your CE DC, taking 2d12 lightning damage per full minute endured (maximum 8d12), or half on a success. You still resolve the Release's deactivation Constitution saves normally.

Rules integration

Edge cases and shared systems.

Rules priority: the reviewed rulings, explicit codex clarifications, and the level progression always control when original wording conflicts. Legacy Domain auto-hit language, action terminology, event timing, missing saves, and older scaling never override revised player rules.
Why Tier V: the everyday lightning kit is merely excellent. The tier belongs to the lore-defining, once-ever transformation that can fight at calamity tempo precisely because its cost is existential.

Mythical Beast Amber
You hold a technique that changes the very essence of your energy to
electricity. However, its true power is unknown even to you.
Cursed Energy

usable source of energy, called cursed energy. These feelings can be
harnessed into attacks or abilities. The number of cursed energy you
possess is based on your class, some classes use a modifier that
being charisma or wisdom (Player’s choice)

Electric Energy
Starting at 1st level, you may not know your cursed technique yet,
but you have gained insane mastery over your aura. Once per turn when
you deal damage with a melee weapon attack, you can spend 1 cursed
point to add 1d10 lightning damage to the total amount. This damage
increases to 2d10 at 3rd level, 3d10 at 6th level, 4d10 at 11th

At 3rd level, your cursed energy is always electrifying your whole
body, making your reactions be instant. Your eyes gain marks of
lightning around them, as a sign of your energy always refilling you.
You can add your Cursed Energy modifier to your initiative rolls, and
you may now use the Electric Energy feature twice per turn.
Also, whenever you deal lightning damage you can add one additional
damage dice.

action, you can release a lightning bolt which heads
straight for the accumulated charges dealing a number of
d12’s equal to the number of charges.
