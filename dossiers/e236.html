The fiction. The prevailing theory holds that the Blood-Red Muramasa's Hunger is not a property of the steel but the original bloodlust that was cursed into it: the blade's CE is appetite with a handle, and every wielder since has simply been the current hand it found. This is why sheathing the demon calms it and why neglect starves it — the sword does not want to be drawn; it wants to be fed, and it will punish a wielder who denies it. Registry notes add the Muramasa warning as scholarly consensus: the katana's power was never the edge. It was always the hunger behind it.

Rules integration

Cursed-tool attunement

Campaign canon

Cursed-tool attunement: you have PB attunement slots (2–6). This tool occupies one slot and requires a one-hour ritual. Attunement ends voluntarily over a short rest, on death, or when you replace it while over capacity. Distance does not break attunement. Cursed-tool attunement is separate from magic-item attunement.
Balance & counterplay. Intentionally dangerous: maximum output pressures the wielder as hard as the enemy.
