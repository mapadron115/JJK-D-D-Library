Temporal Archmage

Time counters / phase / delay / extra temporal windows

Power tierTier V · Calamity
Core law

What the technique actually does.

Core Engine — Time Counters. Max PB. Gain 1 when you Ready an action that successfully triggers, cause an enemy to waste an action/reaction, or begin your turn maintaining a delayed effect. Time Counters manipulate timing but do not grant unrestricted extra turns.

Full progression

Level 1 through 20.

1Phase

Reaction 2 CE: +3 AC against one attack; if it misses, briefly phase and move 10 ft without provoking.

3Delay

2 CE, reaction when creature within 60 ft uses a 1-action technique: contest. Win delays resolution until start of its next turn; resources remain spent.

6Temporal Step

Bonus action 1 Counter: teleport to a space occupied by you since start previous turn.

9Time Raveler

3 CE + 1 Counter: one creature Wis save; on failure loses reactions and cannot Ready until end next turn.

11Hero of Dominaria

At end turn, if you spent 3+ CE, regain one reaction usable only for Phase or a defensive temporal feature.

13Temporal Reversal

5 CE + 2 Counters, reaction: restore one creature within 30 ft to position and HP it had at start of current turn; spent resources/conditions caused by choices do not rewind.

15Stasis

6 CE, Con save: target is phased out until initiative count immediately before its next turn; while absent cannot affect/be affected.

18Domain — The Slow Sunset

Initiative 20 place three temporal markers among creatures. Enemy marker: Dex save or its speed/action timing is delayed—its action occurs at end of its turn after bonus/movement. Ally marker: may move half speed immediately. No extra full turns.

20Master of Time

Counter max 10. Once/long rest after completing your turn, spend 6 Counters to take a Temporal Window: move and take one action that cannot be Domain, Maximum, or another turn-granting effect.

Pinnacle expression

Maximum, Domain, or equivalent.

Maximum — Hero's Rewind (12 CE + 5 Counters): return battlefield creatures/objects within 60 ft to positions and HP from end of previous round. CE, limited uses, deaths already narratively resolved, and knowledge do not rewind.

Feats

Technique Feats

Mage of Zhalfir

you cannot be surprised, and once per short rest you may swap your initiative result with one willing ally after initiative is rolled.

Momentary Blink

as a bonus action, you can phase only your senses for an instant: see and hear through up to 10 ft of solid obstruction until the end of your turn.

Untap

when a delayed effect you created resolves, you may use your reaction to echo its timing: one willing creature within 30 ft may immediately use its reaction to move up to half its speed.

No, Not Yet

you can lend your Delay to others: as a reaction when an ally within 60 ft is targeted by a 1-action technique, you may contest it with Delay on their behalf (normal cost).

Rules integration

Edge cases and shared systems.

Shared rulings: Technique DC = 8 + PB + CE ability modifier. Direct rolled attacks may crit and use the campaign Black Flash procedure when eligible. Tokens, autonomous summons, passive damage, and initiative-20 Domain effects cannot Black Flash. Maximum Output modifies ordinary supported techniques, never named Maxima or Domains.
