Empty Herd

Open Feeding Wounds, drain cursed energy or vitality through them, and turn Satiety into healing, speed, and increasingly predatory attacks.

Scaling dieFeeding Die

Character fantasy

Open Feeding Wounds, drain cursed energy or vitality through them, and turn Satiety into healing, speed, and increasingly predatory attacks.

Combat loop

  1. Land puncture attacks and create Feeding Wounds.
  2. Feed from wounded targets to build Satiety.
  3. Spend Satiety to heal, move, sharpen senses, or empower strikes.
  4. Spread wounds across several targets so the predator always has another source.

Feeding Wounds & Satiety

  • Feeding Wound: once per turn after a piercing/slashing melee hit, spend 1 CE to mark the target for 1 minute. Maximum active wounds = PB. A target may use an action for Medicine or Constitution check vs your CE DC to end its wound.
  • Satiety: maximum PB + CE modifier (minimum PB). You begin at 0 and lose all after a short/long rest.
  • Feed: bonus action while within 5 feet of a wounded creature. It makes Constitution save. Failure: if it has CE, it loses 1 CE and you gain 2 Satiety; otherwise it takes 1 Feeding Die necrotic and you gain 1 Satiety. Success: no loss and you gain no Satiety. A creature can be Fed from only once per round.

Innate Technique FeatsOptional character-building feats

Livestock Sense

You can determine whether a creature you can smell within 30 feet currently has CE and whether it is above or below half HP.

Clean Puncture

A creature has disadvantage on its check to remove your Feeding Wound if you created it with a critical hit.

Packless Hunter

When no ally is within 15 feet of you, gain +1 AC against wounded creatures.

Starved Burst

When you begin your turn at 0 Satiety and below half HP, gain 2 Satiety. Once used, recharge on a short rest.

Building the character

Warrior or Scout. It rewards a player who moves between several injured targets instead of tunneling one boss forever. The CE drain is meaningful but gated behind hits, saves, proximity, and wound setup.

Technique die: Feeding Die follows the shared d6 → d8 → d10 → d12 progression.

Counterplay

Remove wounds, fight at range, keep CE expenditure low while marked, and deny the user access to several feeding targets.

Adaptation note. This is original JJK 5e homebrew inspired by Chupacabra. The myth/folklore provides imagery and thematic rules; the combat engine, progression, CE economy, feats, and Domain/Maximum procedures are game design for this codex.
