Empty Herd

The hunger feeds on schedule, whether you will it or not; you merely ride it. Open Feeding Wounds and drain blood, milk, or breath. Every feeding advances an automatic metamorphosis, gaunt predator, fed hunter, swollen apex, with fixed stage benefits and liabilities, never decisions to spend.

Core engineMetamorphosis track
Core law

Character fantasy

Become the hunger that drains the herd. Feed publicly and visibly; every feeding advances an automatic metamorphosis, gaunt predator, fed hunter, swollen apex. You never choose how to spend a feeding: the track is the body’s, not the mind’s.

Combat loop

Wound, feed, advance, molt, the predator's fourfold season.

  1. Land puncture attacks and create Feeding Wounds.
  2. Feed from wounded targets, each feeding advances the metamorphosis.
  3. Live inside the current stage: fixed benefits and fixed liabilities, no spend decisions.
  4. Spread wounds across several targets so the predator always has another source.

Field notes. Subject: the rare technique where the sorcerer is not the hunter. I have watched the metamorphosis advance and understood the user is merely riding, gaunt, fed, swollen apex, each stage a fixed appetite. Three truths govern it. First: the hunger keeps appointments, not accounts, feeding grants no currency, only stages on the track, and the track resets after a long rest without feeding. Second: the livestock is marked, Feeding Wounds are laid by piercing and slashing hits, and the wounded can be fed on from five feet, then thirty, then through a spectral tether. Third: the drain is the doctrine, CE siphoned on failed saves, the emptied unable to refill, the night ending with the pens standing empty. Mark the classification: they are not the hunter. They are the herd's worst season, and the hunger is borrowing their legs.

Level progression

1Predatory Metabolism

Feeding Wound

One piercing or slashing hit marks the livestock. Once per turn after a piercing/slashing melee hit, spend 1 CE to mark the target for 1 minute. Maximum active wounds = PB (PB + your CE modifier at 11th level). A target may use an action for Medicine or Constitution check vs your technique DC to end its wound.

The Metamorphosis Track (automatic)

Feeding grants no currency, only stages: Gaunt, Fed, swollen Apex. Feeding never grants a currency. Each time you Feed successfully, advance one stage (the track resets to Gaunt after a long rest without feeding). Stages are fixed: Gaunt Predator (0–1 feedings): advantage on Stealth in darkness; your attacks deal +2 damage against creatures at full HP, the hungry strike hardest first. Fed Hunter (2–3 feedings): +10 feet speed; the first time each round you Feed successfully, heal 5 HP; disadvantage on checks to hide or pass as harmless, the fed body shows. Swollen Apex (4+ feedings): your melee attacks deal +2 damage and you gain advantage on Intimidation; but your speed drops 10 feet, you have disadvantage on Dexterity saves, and you cannot benefit from the Hide action, nothing this swollen hides.

Feed

Five feet, a bonus action, a Constitution save, and the hunger advances. Bonus action while within 5 feet of a wounded creature. It makes a Constitution save. On failure: if it has CE, it loses 1 CE; otherwise it takes 4 necrotic damage (8 at 6th level, 12 at 11th). Either way, a failed save counts as one feeding and advances your metamorphosis. On success, no loss and no feeding. A creature can be Fed from only once per round.

Feats

Build-defining options.

Livestock Sense

Smell tells you who carries CE, and who is below half. You can determine whether a creature you can smell within 30 feet currently has CE and whether it is above or below half HP.

Spraying Wound

A critical hit marks a second creature for free, the wound sprays. Once per turn, when you score a critical hit with a piercing or slashing melee attack, you may mark a second creature within 5 feet of the target with a Feeding Wound without spending CE; it counts against your maximum active wounds.

Packless Hunter

Alone, the solitude becomes a weapon, drive the wounded from the herd. When no ally is within 15 feet of you, your solitude is a weapon: once per turn when you hit a wounded creature, you may drive it from the herd. It makes a Wisdom saving throw against your technique DC, becoming frightened of you until the end of your next turn on a failure.

Purging Molt

Regress one stage to slough tainted flesh, the molt purges poison and disease. As an action, regress one stage on the Metamorphosis track (minimum Gaunt Predator) to slough tainted flesh: end one poisoned condition or disease affecting you.

Rules integration

Building the character

Warrior or Scout. It rewards a player who moves between several injured targets instead of tunneling one boss forever. The CE drain is meaningful but gated behind hits, saves, proximity, and wound setup.
Rules integration

Counterplay

Remove wounds, fight at range, keep CE expenditure low while marked, and deny the user access to several feeding targets, the Gaunt stage is the weakest, so keep the predator hungry.
