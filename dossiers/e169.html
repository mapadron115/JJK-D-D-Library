Empty Herd

Open Feeding Wounds and drain blood, milk, or breath. Every feeding advances an automatic metamorphosis — gaunt predator, fed hunter, swollen apex — with fixed stage benefits and liabilities, never decisions to spend.

Combat roleDrain Striker / Sustain
TierTier III · Apex
Core engineMetamorphosis track
Core law

Character fantasy

Become the hunger that drains the herd. Feed publicly and visibly; every feeding advances an automatic metamorphosis — gaunt predator, fed hunter, swollen apex. You never choose how to spend a feeding: the track is the body’s, not the mind’s.

Combat loop

  1. Land puncture attacks and create Feeding Wounds.
  2. Feed from wounded targets — each feeding advances the metamorphosis.
  3. Live inside the current stage: fixed benefits and fixed liabilities, no spend decisions.
  4. Spread wounds across several targets so the predator always has another source.

Core law

Feeding Wounds & Metamorphosis

Feeding Wound:

once per turn after a piercing/slashing melee hit, spend 1 CE to mark the target for 1 minute. Maximum active wounds = PB. A target may use an action for Medicine or Constitution check vs your CE DC to end its wound.

The Metamorphosis Track (automatic):

Feeding never grants a currency. Each time you Feed successfully, advance one stage (the track resets to Gaunt after a long rest without feeding). Stages are fixed: Gaunt Predator (0–1 feedings): advantage on Stealth in darkness; your attacks deal +1 Feeding Die against creatures at full HP — the hungry strike hardest first. Fed Hunter (2–3 feedings): +10 feet speed; the first time each round you Feed successfully, heal one Feeding Die; disadvantage on checks to hide or pass as harmless — the fed body shows. Swollen Apex (4+ feedings): your melee attacks deal +1 Feeding Die and you gain advantage on Intimidation; but your speed drops 10 feet, you have disadvantage on Dexterity saves, and you cannot benefit from the Hide action — nothing this swollen hides.

Feed:

bonus action while within 5 feet of a wounded creature. It makes a Constitution save. On failure: if it has CE, it loses 1 CE; otherwise it takes 1 Feeding Die necrotic. Either way, a failed save counts as one feeding and advances your metamorphosis. On success, no loss and no feeding. A creature can be Fed from only once per round.

Full progression

Level progression

1Predatory Metabolism

Gain Feeding Wounds, Feed, and the Metamorphosis Track. The Gaunt Predator stage is active from the first feeding: advantage on Stealth in darkness, and your attacks deal +1 Feeding Die against creatures at full HP — the hungry strike hardest first.

3Night Scent

You know the direction of every wounded creature within 300 feet and have advantage on tracking them. Pounce: if you move 20+ feet toward a wounded target before hitting it, your first successful Feed after the hit needs no bonus action.

6Bloodless Drain

Feed drains CE equal to half PB (minimum 1) on a failed save. Against a creature with no CE, Feed deals 2 Feeding Dice. The track deepens automatically: reaching Fed Hunter grants +10 feet speed, heals you one Feeding Die the first time each round you Feed successfully, and imposes disadvantage on checks to hide or pass as harmless.

11Empty the Pen

Active Wound cap becomes PB + CE modifier. Once per turn when a wounded creature within 30 feet spends 3+ CE on one activation, you may use your reaction to Feed on it as though within 5 feet. Feeding from a creature at 0 CE also prevents it from regaining CE until the start of your next turn on a failed save.

18Maximum: Night of the Empty Pens

Action, 10 CE, Focus, 1 minute. Gain blindsight 60 feet, speed +30, climb 40. Your first Feed each round needs no bonus action and may target a wounded creature within 15 feet through a spectral feeding tether. Each successful Feed advances the metamorphosis by one additional stage. When you Feed successfully, heal one Feeding Die automatically. When the Maximum ends, the track resets to Gaunt.

Feats

Build-defining options.

Livestock Sense

You can determine whether a creature you can smell within 30 feet currently has CE and whether it is above or below half HP.

Spraying Wound

Once per turn, when you score a critical hit with a piercing or slashing melee attack, you may mark a second creature within 5 feet of the target with a Feeding Wound without spending CE; it counts against your maximum active wounds.

Packless Hunter

When no ally is within 15 feet of you, your solitude is a weapon: once per turn when you hit a wounded creature, you may drive it from the herd. It makes a Wisdom saving throw against your technique DC, becoming frightened of you until the end of your next turn on a failure.

Purging Molt

As an action, regress one stage on the Metamorphosis track (minimum Gaunt Predator) to slough tainted flesh: end one poisoned condition or disease affecting you.

Rules integration

Building the character

Warrior or Scout. It rewards a player who moves between several injured targets instead of tunneling one boss forever. The CE drain is meaningful but gated behind hits, saves, proximity, and wound setup.
Technique die: Feeding Die follows the shared d6 → d8 → d10 → d12 progression.
Rules integration

Counterplay

Remove wounds, fight at range, keep CE expenditure low while marked, and deny the user access to several feeding targets — the Gaunt stage is the weakest, so keep the predator hungry.
