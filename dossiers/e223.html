Vajra

Counts as mace or thrown focus, 1d6 bludgeoning, thrown 30/90. The codex uses the vajra motif respectfully as an indestructible thunderbolt implement, not a representation of ritual practice.

Features & procedures

Properties & techniques

Exact player-facing procedures

Diamond Strike

weapon damage counts magical and ignores resistance to nonmagical B/P/S.

Thunder Seal

2 CE on hit: +2 Tool Dice lightning and target cannot benefit from invisibility until next turn.

Barrier Thunder

action, 3 CE: Tool check against cursed barrier within 30 ft; on success deal 4 Tool Dice force to barrier only.

Release: Indestructible Thunderbolt

7 CE, 1 minute, bonus action to activate (invented): resistance lightning/thunder; Barrier Thunder rolls twice use higher.

The fiction. The codex treats the vajra as conviction given a handle: an implement forged around the idea that some things do not break, and some barriers do not get to stand. Registry notes describe its thunder as the residue of a thousand techniques that failed against it — the bolt remembers every wall it ever shattered.

Rules integration

Cursed-tool attunement

Campaign canon

Cursed-tool attunement: you have PB attunement slots (2–6). This tool occupies one slot and requires a one-hour ritual. Attunement ends voluntarily over a short rest, on death, or when you replace it while over capacity. Distance does not break attunement. Cursed-tool attunement is separate from magic-item attunement.
Balance & counterplay. Anti-barrier pressure damages barriers rather than casually deleting Domains.
