Features & procedures

Properties & techniques

Exact player-facing procedures

Diamond Strike

The first and quietest property: the strike is declared magical and mundane resistance is simply not consulted. The vajra's argument is older than armor. weapon damage counts magical and ignores resistance to nonmagical B/P/S.

Thunder Seal

Two CE lays the seal on the hit: two more dice of lightning, and the struck cannot hide — even from themselves — until the next turn. What the thunder touches stays visible. 2 CE on hit: +2 Tool Dice lightning and target cannot benefit from invisibility until next turn.

Barrier Thunder

Aimed at walls, never at men: a Tool check against a cursed barrier within thirty feet, four dice of force if it holds. This is the siege engine wearing a mace's clothes. action, 3 CE: Tool check against cursed barrier within 30 ft; on success deal 4 Tool Dice force to barrier only.

Release: Indestructible Thunderbolt

Seven CE buys a minute of the tool's true nature: resistance to the storm's own elements, and the Barrier Thunder striking twice for the higher roll. The unbreakable thing, briefly, unbreaks its wielder too. 7 CE, 1 minute, bonus action to activate (invented): resistance lightning/thunder; Barrier Thunder rolls twice use higher.

Field notes. Subject: conviction with a handle. Three truths govern him. First, the vajra does not break, and it extends the courtesy to its wielder's arguments: Diamond Strike renders the blow magical and indifferent to mundane resistance — some things are simply not permitted to be blunted. Second, its thunder is the residue of a thousand techniques that failed against it: Thunder Seal strips invisibility from the struck for a turn, because what has weathered every wall refuses to let anything hide behind one. Third, barriers are its native prey: Barrier Thunder is aimed at the wall and the wall only, and under the Release the strike rolls twice and takes the higher — the way a determined man asks his question twice. Mark this: the thunderbolt is not aimed at people. It is aimed at the idea that anything can stand between it and them.

Rules integration

Cursed-tool attunement

Campaign canon

Cursed-tool attunement: you have PB attunement slots (2–6). This tool occupies one slot and requires a one-hour ritual. Attunement ends voluntarily over a short rest, on death, or when you replace it while over capacity. Distance does not break attunement. Cursed-tool attunement is separate from magic-item attunement.
Balance & counterplay. Anti-barrier pressure damages barriers rather than casually deleting Domains.
