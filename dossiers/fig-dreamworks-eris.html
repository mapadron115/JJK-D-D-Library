Feature map

Level-by-level features, as the figure refined them.

L1

The Apple of Discord

Action · 2 CE · Spite pool (maximum 2 × PB).

You carry a pool of Spite (maximum 2 × PB; it begins at 0). You gain 1 Spite whenever a hostile creature you can see takes the Help action, benefits from flanking or Pack Tactics, or succeeds on a saving throw to maintain concentration — order, witnessed, offends you. Unspent Spite fades when combat ends. As an action, you produce a golden apple — for the fairest — and place or hurl it at a point within 60 ft. Each hostile creature within 20 ft of the apple that can see it must make a WIS save vs your CE DC. On a failure, the creature is spite-bound for 1 minute: it must attack the nearest creature other than you on each of its turns, and it may repeat the save at the end of each of its turns, ending the effect on a success. While two or more creatures are spite-bound by the same apple, you gain 1 Spite at the start of each of your turns. The apple is a real object (AC 10, 1 hit point) — destroying it ends the effect for creatures bound by it. There is always another apple.

L3

A Thousand Borrowed Faces

Action · 1 CE.

You are never yourself if someone else is more useful. You physically reshape into any humanoid you have seen — face, voice, build — for 1 hour or until you dismiss it (no action). This is no illusion: only truesight or a successful Insight check vs your CE DC pierces it. While wearing a specific person's face, their allies treat you as that person until you act with open hostility. You retain your own game statistics. Eris, famously, has never once been herself at a party.

L6

Sow Discord

Action · 4 CE + 1 Spite.

Pick two hostile creatures within 60 ft of each other that can see or hear you, and give them a reason. Each makes a WIS save vs your CE DC. On a failure, each regards the other as a hated rival for 1 minute: it has disadvantage on attack rolls against any creature except its rival, and whenever it can include its rival in an attack or effect, it must. It may repeat the save at the end of each of its turns. If both fail their initial saves, you gain 2 Spite — the harvest doubles.

L11

Petty Spite

Passive · reaction.

Your spite is no longer a feeling; it is a weapon. As a reaction when a hostile creature you can see succeeds on an attack roll, saving throw, or ability check, you may spend 2 Spite to force it to reroll and take the lower result — order offends you, personally. Additionally, once per turn when you deal damage to a creature that is concentrating, benefiting from a beneficial spell, or spite-bound, you deal an extra 2d8 psychic damage. The more orderly the target, the harder she hits.

L15

Goddess of Chaos

Passive.

You are immune to being charmed — no one turns Eris's own trick on Eris. Your Spite maximum becomes 3 × PB, and when you roll initiative with no Spite, you gain Spite equal to your PB; there is always something orderly nearby to ruin. Creatures have disadvantage on saving throws against your discord effects while they are concentrating or under the effect of a beneficial spell. She doesn't break rules. She breaks the people who follow them.

L18

Eris Unbound

Passive.

Chaos, perfected, is contagious. Your spite-bound and Sow Discord effects ignore immunity to the charmed condition — a creature immune to charm instead makes its saves with advantage: resistance, not refusal. When a creature spite-bound by you drops to 0 hit points, the discord spreads: one hostile creature within 30 ft of it must make a WIS save vs your CE DC or become spite-bound for 1 minute. The apple rolls on.

The Apple, Perfected

Action · 10 CE · 30-ft-radius sphere within 120 ft.

The wedding of Peleus, perfected as a weapon. You hurl the true Apple of Discord to a point within 120 ft: each hostile creature in a 30-ft-radius sphere must make a WIS save vs your CE DC at disadvantage. On a failure, it is spite-bound for 1 minute. Every failure also shatters order on the victim: its concentration ends, and one beneficial spell affecting it (your choice) ends. A creature that fails by 5 or more immediately uses its reaction to make one weapon attack against the nearest creature other than you. Troy fell for less.

Level 20 · Capstone

Discord Incarnate

Passive · 1/long rest reform.

You are chaos with a grudge and a perfect memory. You cannot be charmed, frightened, or possessed. Creatures spite-bound by you or bound by your Sow Discord cannot end the effect with their end-of-turn saves — only your incapacitation, your choice, or the apple's destruction ends it. Your Spite no longer fades when combat ends. Once per long rest, when you drop to 0 hit points, you dissolve into screaming static and reform in an unoccupied space within 60 ft, with hit points equal to your CHA modifier + your PB. She never loses. She just stops attending.

Innate Technique Feats

Build-defining options.

These are optional character-building feats. You must meet each listed prerequisite before selecting it.

Prerequisite: The Apple of Discord

A Gift, Obviously

Nobody suspects the present. When you produce the apple, you may disguise it as something desirable — a dropped healing potion, a discarded weapon, a sealed letter. The first hostile creature to touch it must make its WIS save against spite-bound at disadvantage, believing it has won something. The apple is always a gift. That is the whole trick.

Prerequisite: A Thousand Borrowed Faces

Wear the Rival

You don't just wear their face — you spend their reputation. While disguised as a specific creature, you may spend 1 Spite as an action to plant a rumor in the mind of one creature that can see you: it must make a WIS save vs your CE DC or believe the person you're impersonating has betrayed it — it is spite-bound toward the original for 1 minute. The frame is the weapon; the face is just delivery.

Prerequisite: Sow Discord

Friendly Fire Doctrine

Your discord doesn't just turn blades — it sharpens them. When a creature spite-bound by you or bound by your Sow Discord damages another creature, it deals an extra 1d8 psychic damage. They were always angry. You merely gave the anger a schedule.

Prerequisite: Petty Spite

The Ledger of Small Wrongs

Eris forgets nothing, especially slights. When a hostile creature damages you, you may mark it in your ledger (no action required). You have advantage on saving throws against the marked creature's effects, and it has disadvantage on saving throws against your discord effects. The ledger has no last page.
