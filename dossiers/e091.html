Sacred Treasure Framework

Design rule: Sacred Treasures do not grant an entire second Innate Technique. They are exceptional cursed tools that increase a user's ability to express what they already do, while still providing enough independent function that finding one is exciting for the “wrong” build.
Tool Die

d6 at levels 1–4, d8 at 5–10, d10 at 11–16, d12 at 17–20. Tool Dice scale with character level, not acquisition order.

Attunement

1 hour and one cursed-tool attunement slot. A Sacred Treasure may reject an incompatible wielder if the campaign wants the original lore's personalized nature.

CE

All activated properties spend the wielder's normal CE. A non-sorcerer uses 8 + PB + a chosen mental modifier as Tool DC.

Release

Sacred Treasure Release is normally a level-13+ mastery state, once per long rest unless stated otherwise. It is deliberately comparable to a powerful Maximum-style equipment feature.

Shared Rulings

Black Flash

Direct melee attacks with a Sacred Treasure can Black Flash under the campaign's normal rules. Save-only Release effects cannot.

Maximum Output

Activated tool abilities do not automatically qualify for Maximum Output. If an ability explicitly channels the wielder's Innate Technique, that underlying technique may use its normal Maximum Output rules.

Destruction

These are legendary objects. Ordinary damage should not destroy them. A deliberate Special Grade effect, artifact-breaking ability, or major narrative event may damage one, creating a repair quest rather than deleting it casually.

Two Techniques

Tool abilities are not Innate Techniques and therefore do not count against a character's CT limit. They still share actions, CE, concentration, and other normal resources.
