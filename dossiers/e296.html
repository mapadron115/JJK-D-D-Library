Dáinsleif

Metaphysical Law: A wound acknowledged by Dáinsleif does not accept restoration.

FormLongsword
EngineUnhealing Wound
Attunement1 hour*

Field notes. Subject: a judge whose sentences cannot be appealed. Three truths govern it. First, the stroke is fated: after a miss, 2 CE turns the miss into a hit — the natural 1 excepted, for even this blade cannot rewrite what fate refused to write. Second, the wound is final: once per turn on a hit, the sword deals an additional 3d8 necrotic, and the victim's maximum HP falls by the same amount — a wound ordinary healing and Reverse Cursed Technique cannot repair. Third, restoration demands ritual, not medicine: only a long rest, or a minute-long 5-CE RCT rite whose Arcana check beats the wielder's Relic DC, can lift the reduction. And once drawn in combat, the blade will not be sheathed until something falls to 0 HP. Consider: this is not soul-cutting. It is the cursing of the very act of healing.

Properties & techniques

Exact player-facing procedures

Relic profile

1d8 slashing (versatile 1d10) · +4 to attack and damage rolls

Jujutsu origin

The myth of Dáinsleif gives it three brutal qualities: a stroke that does not fail, wounds that do not heal, and a blade that demands death once drawn. The cursed tool expresses those ideas literally rather than as generic bonus damage.

Fated Stroke — 2 CE, once per turn

After you miss with Dáinsleif, you may spend 2 CE to turn the miss into a hit. A natural 1 cannot be changed this way.

Unhealing Wound

Once per turn when Dáinsleif hits, it deals an additional 3d8 necrotic damage. The target's maximum HP is reduced by the necrotic damage dealt. That reduction cannot be repaired by ordinary healing or Reverse Cursed Technique.

Breaking the Wound

A creature can remove all maximum-HP reduction caused by Dáinsleif after a long rest, or by receiving a 1-minute RCT ritual costing 5 CE. The healer must succeed on an Arcana check against the wielder's Relic DC.

Death Before Sheathing

If Dáinsleif is drawn in combat, it cannot be sheathed until it has reduced a creature to 0 HP.

Binding Vow — Certainty for Permanence

The sword spends cursed energy to force a missed stroke into the written history of the fight as a hit; in exchange, every true wound it creates resists being 'rewritten' by RCT.

Designer’s note. Unlike the Soul Split Katana, Dáinsleif is not framed as a soul-cutting weapon. Its technique specifically curses the act of healing.

Rules integration

Cursed-tool attunement

Campaign canon

Cursed-tool attunement: you have PB attunement slots (2–6). This tool occupies one slot and requires a one-hour ritual. Attunement ends voluntarily over a short rest, on death, or when you replace it while over capacity. Distance does not break attunement. Cursed-tool attunement is separate from magic-item attunement.
