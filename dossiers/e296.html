Dáinsleif

Metaphysical Law: what Dáinsleif wounds cannot be restored normally. The wound that refuses to become whole — the blade does not attack the soul, it attacks recovery itself.

Features & procedures

Properties & techniques

Exact player-facing procedures

Unhealing Wound

Damage dealt by Dáinsleif reduces maximum HP by an equal amount until the curse is broken.

RCT fails

RCT cannot restore HP lost to the blade while the wound remains cursed.

Unhealing Injury

A critical hit can inflict an Unhealing Injury on a limb, preventing normal restoration of that limb.

Niche. Dáinsleif occupies a different niche from soul-cutting weapons: it does not attack the soul—it attacks recovery itself.

Rules integration

Cursed-tool attunement

Campaign canon

Cursed-tool attunement: you have PB attunement slots (2–6). This tool occupies one slot and requires a one-hour ritual. Attunement ends voluntarily over a short rest, on death, or when you replace it while over capacity. Distance does not break attunement. Cursed-tool attunement is separate from magic-item attunement.
Balance & counterplay. The attrition ends the moment the curse is broken — prioritize breaking the curse over out-healing it, and keep the blade away from limbs you cannot afford to lose to a critical hit.
