Core law

What the technique actually does.

Core Engine — Style Rank. D→C→B→A→S→SS→SSS. Start D. Once/turn rise one rank for landing a different named attack/style interaction than your previous qualifying action. Taking heavy damage drops one; repeating the same action three turns in a row cannot raise it.

The fiction. The prevailing theory holds that the technique feeds on audacity: the sorcerer's CE rewards novelty because it was born in a moment when the user survived by refusing to repeat themselves. Registry scholars classify the Style Rank as the engine's verdict on the fight — variety raises it, repetition starves it — and Devil Trigger is what the CE does when the verdict comes back magnificent.

Full progression

Level 1 through 20.

L1Style Switch

Bonus action choose Swordmaster (+1 weapon die once/turn), Gunslinger (+30 ranged range and ignore half cover), Trickster (+10 speed, Disengage), or Royalguard (reaction reduce damage 1d10+level).

L3Devil Arm Arsenal

Bind up to PB cursed weapons. Draw/swap freely once/turn; all qualify for your Style features.

L6Royal Release

Each Royalguard reduction stores 1 Guard, max PB. On melee hit spend any Guard for +1d10 force each.

L9Devil Trigger

4 CE, 1 minute: fly 30, heal PB HP/turn, resistance B/P/S, attacks +1d8. At S rank cost becomes 3 CE.

L11Devil Sword Dante

Manifest personal blade. Once/turn 2 CE creates four Mirage Swords: add 2d8 force, defend for +2 AC until next turn, or fire four 1d8 bolts split among targets.

L13Quadruple Style

Switch Style once/turn without bonus action. At SS+, benefit from two Styles simultaneously.

L15Sin Gauge

At SSS, you may convert 6 CE into a full Sin Gauge. Bonus action consume it for Sin Devil Trigger for 3 rounds: fly 80, resistance all but psychic/radiant, attacks +2d10, access to Sin Stinger and Demolition.

L18Quadruple S

At SSS, activate Sin Devil Trigger for one round without consuming Sin Gauge once/short rest.

L20Legendary Dark Knight

Style cannot fall below B while conscious. First Style switch each round is free; at SSS all four Styles function simultaneously.

Pinnacle expression

Maximum, Domain, or equivalent.

Sin Finisher — Judgement: End Sin Devil Trigger; every chosen hostile within 30 ft Dex saves against 12d10 force/fire, half on success. You teleport through the area in a demonic sword barrage, then explode. Cannot Black Flash.

Feats

Build-defining options.

Enemy Step

When you hit a creature your size or larger with a melee attack, you may use your reaction to springboard off them: leap up to 20 ft in any direction without provoking, and the creature makes a Str save or is knocked prone by the push-off.

Royal Guard Savant

You guard others as yourself: as a reaction when an ally within 10 ft takes damage, you may spend 2 Guard to interpose — you take the damage instead, reduced by your Royalguard reduction (1d10 + level).

Weapon Freak

Once per turn, when you hit with a bound Devil Arm, you may draw a second bound Devil Arm and make one attack with it as a bonus action, provided it is a different named attack than your previous qualifying action (it still advances Style).

Jackpot

When you roll a natural 20 on a ranged attack, you may call Jackpot: instead of rolling critical damage, fire a celebratory spread — up to two other creatures within 15 ft of the target take half the attack's damage (Dex save for half).

Rules integration

Edge cases and shared systems.

Shared rulings: Technique DC = 8 + PB + CE ability modifier. Direct rolled attacks may crit and use the campaign Black Flash procedure; autonomous hazards and Domain events cannot. Maximum Output may modify ordinary supported features, never named Maxima/Domains. Two-technique characters use the campaign's agreed character-level progression.
