Core law

What the technique actually does.

Core Engine, Style Rank. The engine in full: D→C→B→A→S→SS→SSS, starting at D, one rank per turn for a different named attack or style interaction than the last qualifying action. A single heavy hit (20+ damage, or 25% of max HP, whichever is lower) drops a rank; repeating the same action three turns running starves it. Novelty is the currency. Spend it. D→C→B→A→S→SS→SSS. Start D. Once/turn rise one rank for landing a different named attack/style interaction than your previous qualifying action. Taking heavy damage (a single damage instance of 20 or more, or 25% of your HP maximum, whichever is lower) drops one rank; repeating the same action three turns in a row cannot raise it.

Field notes. Subject: a technique that feeds on audacity. Three truths govern him. First, the Style Rank is the engine’s verdict on the fight, D to SSS, rising once per turn for landing a different named attack or style interaction than his previous qualifying action; heavy damage drops a rank, and three turns of repetition cannot raise it. Second, his arsenal is variety made material: four switchable Styles, PB bound Devil Arms drawn and swapped freely, Mirage Swords woven into the Devil Sword Dante. Third, Devil Trigger is what the CE does when the verdict comes back magnificent, flight, healing, resistances, and at SSS the Sin Gauge and its finishers. Hence the maxim: he does not win fights. He reviews them, and the review is devastating.

Full progression

Level 1 through 20.

1Style Switch

The foundation: a bonus action chooses Swordmaster (+1 weapon die once per turn), Gunslinger (+30 ranged range, half cover ignored), Trickster (+10 speed, Disengage), or Royalguard (reaction: reduce damage by 1d10 + level). Four doctrines, one fighter. Indecision, weaponized. Bonus action choose Swordmaster (+1 weapon die once/turn), Gunslinger (+30 ranged range and ignore half cover), Trickster (+10 speed, Disengage), or Royalguard (reaction reduce damage 1d10+level).

3Devil Arm Arsenal

He binds up to PB cursed weapons, drawn and swapped freely once per turn, all qualifying for his Style features. The arsenal is not a collection. It is a vocabulary. Bind up to PB cursed weapons. Draw/swap freely once/turn; all qualify for your Style features.

6Royal Release

Every Royalguard reduction stores 1 Guard, max PB, and on a melee hit he may spend any Guard for +1d10 force each. Defense, saved up and spent as offense. The patient guard becomes the impatient blow. Each Royalguard reduction stores 1 Guard, max PB. On melee hit spend any Guard for +1d10 force each.

9Devil Trigger

Action, 4 CE, a minute: 30-ft flight, PB HP healed per turn, resistance to bludgeoning, piercing, slashing, attacks +1d8: and at S rank the cost drops to 3 CE. The trigger is not a transformation. It is the verdict, announced. 4 CE, 1 minute: fly 30, heal PB HP/turn, resistance B/P/S, attacks +1d8. At S rank cost becomes 3 CE.

11Devil Sword Dante

His personal blade: once per turn, 2 CE weaves four Mirage Swords, +2d8 force on an attack, +2 AC until his next turn as a guard, or four 1d8 bolts split among targets. The sword is his. The swords are its entourage. Manifest personal blade. Once/turn 2 CE creates four Mirage Swords: add 2d8 force, defend for +2 AC until next turn, or fire four 1d8 bolts split among targets.

13Quadruple Style

He may switch Style once per turn without spending the bonus action, and at SS+ he benefits from two Styles simultaneously. The fighter stops choosing doctrines and starts conducting them. Switch Style once/turn without bonus action. At SS+, benefit from two Styles simultaneously.

15Sin Gauge

At SSS he may convert 6 CE into a full Sin Gauge, a bonus action consuming it unleashes Sin Devil Trigger for 3 rounds: 80-ft flight, resistance to all but psychic and radiant, attacks +2d10, and access to Sin Stinger and Demolition. Sin Stinger (action, 2 CE): a melee CE attack for 4d10 force, the target saving or launched 20 ft upward. Judgment, with choreography. At SSS, you may convert 6 CE into a full Sin Gauge. Bonus action consume it for Sin Devil Trigger for 3 rounds: fly 80, resistance all but psychic/radiant, attacks +2d10, access to Sin Stinger and Demolition. Sin Stinger (action, 2 CE): make a melee CE attack; on a hit deal 4d10 force and the target makes a Strength save or is launched 20 ft upward, landing prone. Demolition (action, 4 CE): creatures in a 20-ft burst within 60 ft make a Dexterity save, taking 6d10 force on failure, half on success.

18Quadruple S

At SSS, once per short rest: Sin Devil Trigger for one round without consuming the Sin Gauge. The verdict, delivered pro bono. At SSS, activate Sin Devil Trigger for one round without consuming Sin Gauge once/short rest.

20Legendary Dark Knight

Style cannot fall below B while he is conscious; the first Style switch each round is free; at SSS all four Styles function simultaneously. The legend is not that he mastered four styles. It is that he stopped needing to choose. Style cannot fall below B while conscious. First Style switch each round is free; at SSS all four Styles function simultaneously.

Pinnacle expression

Maximum, Domain, or equivalent.

The Sin Finisher: end Sin Devil Trigger, every chosen hostile within 30 ft saves or takes 12d10 force or fire, half on success, as he teleports through the area in a demonic sword barrage, then explodes. It cannot Black Flash. Some endings are too loud for precision. Sin Finisher, Judgement: End Sin Devil Trigger; every chosen hostile within 30 ft Dex saves against 12d10 force/fire, half on success. You teleport through the area in a demonic sword barrage, then explode. Cannot Black Flash.

Feats

Build-defining options.

Enemy Step

When he hits a creature his size or larger with a melee attack, his reaction springboards off them, up to 20 ft in any direction without provoking, the creature saving or knocked prone by the push-off. The enemy is terrain. Rude, load-bearing terrain. When you hit a creature your size or larger with a melee attack, you may use your reaction to springboard off them: leap up to 20 ft in any direction without provoking, and the creature makes a Str save or is knocked prone by the push-off.

Royal Guard Savant

He guards others as himself: reaction when an ally within 10 ft takes damage, spend 2 Guard to interpose, taking the damage instead, reduced by his Royalguard reduction. Chivalry, itemized. You guard others as yourself: as a reaction when an ally within 10 ft takes damage, you may spend 2 Guard to interpose, you take the damage instead, reduced by your Royalguard reduction (1d10 + level).

Weapon Freak

Once per turn, when he hits with a bound Devil Arm, he may draw a second bound Devil Arm and attack with it as a bonus action, provided it is a different named attack than his previous qualifying action, still advancing Style. The arsenal has no bottom. He checked. Once per turn, when you hit with a bound Devil Arm, you may draw a second bound Devil Arm and make one attack with it as a bonus action, provided it is a different named attack than your previous qualifying action (it still advances Style).

Jackpot

On a natural 20 with a ranged attack, he may call Jackpot: instead of critical damage, a celebratory spread, up to two other creatures within 15 ft of the target take half the attack’s damage (Dex save for half). The house always wins. He is the house. When you roll a natural 20 on a ranged attack, you may call Jackpot: instead of rolling critical damage, fire a celebratory spread, up to two other creatures within 15 ft of the target take half the attack's damage (Dex save for half).

Rules integration

Edge cases and shared systems.

Shared rulings: Technique DC = 8 + PB + CE ability modifier. Direct rolled attacks may crit and use the campaign Black Flash procedure; autonomous hazards and Domain events cannot. Maximum Output may modify ordinary supported features, never named Maxima/Domains. Two-technique characters use the campaign's agreed character-level progression.
