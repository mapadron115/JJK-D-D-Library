Explosive Flesh

You can make your body into a deadly bomb.

Power tierTier III · Apex
Combat roleBlaster
Core engineDetachable explosive anatomy
DomainTrue Art

Field notes. Subject: the quartermaster who stocks his arsenal from his own anatomy. Three truths govern this technique, and the first is its honesty: every detonation is paid twice, once in cursed energy and once in blood, for hit points are spent alongside CE on every removal. Second, the logistics are ruthless, the larger the offering, the slower its handling: a tooth leaves by free action, a leg demands the full turn, and the throw's reach shrinks as the payload grows. Third, the technique rewards the accountant's mindset: Reduced Harm discounts the flesh-tax, Regrown Flesh reissues what has not yet burned, and Bites the Dust plants a severed part as a delayed mine. Mark this: the Domain does not stop at your own limbs, at initiative 20 it chooses a limb from someone else and spends it. A sorcerer who must spend himself should make every limb count twice.

Full progression

Feature map

1Self-Destruction.

The engine of the technique, and its price list: every payload on the body, graded from tooth to leg, with the costs written in both energy and flesh. Starting at 1st level, you can make any part of you a deadly bomb. You spend cursed energy to remove and throw your body parts. You remove the part as described below. Throwing takes an action:

  • Tiny (Tooth, Finger, Hair): 1 CE + Con mod HP, free action removal, throw at a point within 120 ft, creatures within 5 ft make a Dex save, failure: Xd6 force damage, success: half.
  • Small (Ears, Nose): 2 CE + Con mod x half your level HP, bonus action removal, throw at a point within 100 ft, creatures within 10 ft make a Dex save, failure: Xd8 force damage and knocked prone, success: half.
  • Medium (Hands, Feet): 4 CE + Con mod x level HP, action removal, throw at a point within 80 ft, creatures within 15 ft make a Dex save, failure: Xd10 force damage and knocked prone, success: half.
  • Large (Arms, Legs): 6 CE + Con mod x level HP, full turn action removal, throw at a point within 60 ft, creatures within 20 ft make a Dex save, failure: Xd12 force damage and knocked prone, success: half.

After using a limb or body part in an explosion, it will be considered removed (Con mod is treated as min of 1). The X is your CE modifier. Damage from this technique deals twice as much damage to objects and structures.

3Faster Throws.

Speed is the second currency of ordnance, this is the user learning to throw what has already been paid to tear off. At 3rd level, you have gained more expertise over your technique, and can now throw your bombs faster. You can now throw a body part as an action or bonus action.

6Guerilla Tactics.

The trap-door doctrine: the bomb no longer needs to leave the body to find its target. At 6th level, your practice with your explosions allowed you to make them in an instant. You can now instantly explode one of your body parts for the normal cost, as a reaction to receiving or hitting a melee attack roll as the body part explodes on your body. All creatures make the Dexterity saving throw with disadvantage unless their passive perception is higher than your cursed energy DC.

11Stronger Explosions.

Mastery turns the blast from wounding to unmaking: senses fail, structures fall, and the burst itself widens. At 11th level, with the extensive use of your technique, your explosions became deadlier. When a creature fails the saving throw of your explosions, they gain the deafened condition for 1 minute. They can make a Constitution saving throw at the beginning of their turns, ending the condition early on a success. Also, your explosions deal three times as much damage to objects and structures and their explosion range is increased by 5 ft. At 18th level, the creature also gains the blinded condition.

18Domain Expansion: True Art.

The air shimmers as though seen through heat-haze, though nothing burns yet. The user's silhouette seems to hold too much light, the way a drawn bow holds too much silence, and every surface in the room reflects a faint, warm glare. Detonation is not an event here. It is a condition of the atmosphere, patiently waiting to be permitted.

The arsenal's final accounting, inside the Domain the user's own flesh detonates for nothing, and the enemy's limbs are spent as his own. At 18th level, you have mastered your technique, allowing you to expand your domain. You can detonate any of your body parts for no CE cost. At initiative 20, you can choose a limb (arm or leg) from a creature inside your domain and detonate it.

Pinnacle expression

Maximum, Domain, or equivalent.

The delayed mine: a severed part planted like a seed, ripening into ruin on the turn after. Maximum: Bites the Dust: prerequisite: must have Stronger Explosions. Your explosive prowess has reached new heights, allowing you to trigger massive blasts from your removed body parts. As an action, you can spend cursed energy equal to the body part to trigger a delayed explosion. Choose a body part you have removed within 30 ft. It detonates at the end of your next turn, forcing all creatures within 20 ft to make a Dexterity saving throw at disadvantage. On a failure, they take Xd12 force damage and are knocked prone. On a success, they take half damage and are not knocked prone. You can spend additional cursed energy up to half your proficiency bonus (rounded up) to increase the damage by an additional Xd12 for every cursed energy spent. The X is your Cursed Energy modifier.

True Art, the quartermaster's ledger, balanced at last by someone else's anatomy. Domain Expansion: True Art: 18: you can detonate any of your body parts for no CE cost. At initiative 20, you can choose a limb (arm or leg) from a creature inside your domain and detonate it.

Innate Technique Feats

Build-defining options.

These are optional character-building feats. You must meet each listed prerequisite before selecting it.

Prerequisite: Must have Faster Throws

Regrown Flesh

The armory restocks itself, what has not yet detonated can be knit back into service, though the blood-tax is never refunded. You have learned to knit your explosive flesh back together. As a bonus action for 2 CE, you can regrow or reattach a body part you removed that has not yet detonated, restoring its use. You do not regain the hit points you spent to remove it.

Prerequisite: Must have Guerilla Tactics

Elemental Bomb

The payload takes on flavors: fire to burn, cold to slow, lightning to share the wound with a second victim. You have learned how to create different types of explosions. When you use a bomb, you can spend additional cursed energy equal to the energy spent to make it an elemental bomb, choosing fire, cold, or lightning. The bomb's damage becomes that type, and it gains an additional effect: fire ignites flammable objects and deals an extra 1d6 fire damage to creatures that failed the save; cold halves the speed of creatures that failed the save until the end of their next turn; lightning arcs to one additional creature within 15 ft of the blast, dealing half the bomb's damage to it.

Prerequisite: Must have Faster Throws

Reduced Harm

The quartermaster learns economy, the flesh-tax shrinks with level, though it is never waived entirely. You have learned to minimize the damage you take when using your technique. You decrease the hit point loss from using any part of your body as an explosive by an amount equal to your Constitution modifier (minimum 1 hit point). At 6th level, this reduction increases to an amount equal to twice your Constitution modifier plus your level (minimum 1 hit point).

Rules integration

Edge cases and shared systems.

Removal vs. throwing: removal and throwing are separate costs and actions, Tiny's free removal does not mean free throwing. A Large full-turn removal leaves no normal action that turn to throw until a later turn or an explicit extra action.
HP costs: pay HP using max(1, CON modifier); round half level up (campaign convention). Reduced HP is a cost, not damage that armor or reaction mitigation can cancel. Removed fingers and limbs count as absent and obey limb rules; repeated hair strands do not count as sacrificing the same whole limb anew. Track each removed part as a finite payload.
Self-preservation: voluntarily paying HP as a technique cost cannot take you below 1 HP, unless a separately declared suicidal sacrifice version permits it. Regrowth needs actual permitted healing or regeneration; receiving healing HP does not recreate an exploded arm. Power at low Constitution remains as printed.
Guerilla Tactics: the reaction is an explicit alternative to the removal + throw actions, it still costs actual HP and the limb. Resolve on the confirmed hit before ordinary damage (campaign timing). The blast is centered on you, and the source grants no immunity, campaign ruling: you save and take the blast too.
Stronger Explosions: "range increased by 5 ft" applies to burst radius, not throw distance. The structure multiplier of 3 replaces the base 2, not stacking to 6. At 18th level blindness shares deafness's duration and recovery.
Domain: free CE does not waive your own HP and anatomy costs. Initiative-20 ruling (campaign fill): choose one eligible arm or leg on one target, remove it, and deal a Large bomb's Xd12 force damage at that limb; other creatures in the burst make the ordinary Dexterity save; the chosen victim is guaranteed hit. No eligible non-existent limb means choosing another target, never inventing an internal organ. Anti-domain protection prevents the forced limb event.
Reduced Harm: the minimum 1 is a final HP cost floor, never a negative cost that heals.
Elemental Bomb: keeps the force damage and adds an equal native dice package of chosen fire, cold, or lightning; pay both the base bomb and the elemental surcharge before other legal cost adjustments.
Bites the Dust: consumes a real removed, not-yet-detonated part within 30 ft, previously exploded parts cannot detonate again. Mark the delay at the end of your next turn; others may move, find, or destroy the actual payload before then. The optional augment adds modifier-number d12s per CE, not a single d12.
