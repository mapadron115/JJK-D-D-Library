Cursewrought Armor

A suit of armor sewn through with cursed talismans that brace themselves before the blade lands.

Field notes. Subject: a fortress you can wear. Two truths govern it, armor needs no more. First, the talismans brace before the blade lands: the armor reinforces itself with cursed energy, +1 to AC while worn, the wall that leans into the blow. No activation, no attunement, fortification as furniture. Second, the greater doctrine: the perfect strike is demoted. Any critical hit against you becomes a normal hit, it still lands, but the luck is refused, and nothing that triggers on a critical may trigger. Hence the maxim: the strongest wall makes the enemy's best day ordinary.

Reinforced Ward

Passive · While worn: +1 to AC.

Sewn through with talismans, the armor reinforces itself, the wall that leans into the blow. This suit of armor is embedded with cursed talismans that automatically reinforce the armor with cursed energy. While you're wearing it, you gain a +1 bonus to AC.

The greater doctrine: the perfect strike is demoted. It still lands, but the luck is refused, and nothing that triggers on a crit may trigger. Any critical hit against you becomes a normal hit while you wear this armor.
