Royal Marionette
Surgical Shikigami Theater

Build the technique around three dolls: Doctor Blythe repairs bodies, Puppeteer turns corpses into remote soldiers, and Terpsichora makes your own body dance beyond its limits. Coordinator note: Puppeteer requires actual corpses, it does not overlap with V.6 Puppet Manipulation's crafted-puppet construction.

Power tierTier IV · Special Grade · Tier V ceiling
Core law

What the technique actually does.

Rules are written to plug into the finalized campaign glossary: normal actions stay normal, direct attacks roll, and only named Domain events bypass the initial roll.

Shared

Shikigami Rule

Shared rule · Only one signature shikigami (Blythe, Puppeteer, or Terpsichora) fully active at a time · Switching costs a bonus action and 1 CE.

The theater’s first law. Command is a resource, not a given; the puppeteer must choose which grief takes the stage.

Action · 3 CE · Focus

Doctor Blythe

Action · 3 CE · Focus · Summon a stationary surgical shikigami tethered within 20 ft · As an action, spend 1–CE modifier CE to heal a willing adjacent creature for 5 HP per CE (8 per CE at L11), or given time reattach a severed exterior limb.

The first doll. While she operates, the other two dolls are silent. This is the refusal of the broken body, answered with a needle.

Sensing

Predatory En

Action · 1 CE per minute · Extend a CE sensory tendril up to your current range: detect living/CE-bearing presences and rough direction.

The stage itself. Not thoughts, not details, the theater’s floor, mapped in bodies.

Field notes. Subject: a surgeon’s grief, with three hands. The prevailing theory holds that this technique is a refusal in triplicate, a body broken, a corpse wasted, a living body limited, and the user’s cursed energy declined to accept any of them, so it built a doll for each refusal. Three truths govern the theater. First, each doll answers a different deathbed: Doctor Blythe repairs what still breathes, the Puppeteer conscripts what does not, and Terpsichora turns the sorcerer’s own flesh into the final instrument. Second, the strings are the doctrine: only one signature shikigami fully active at a time, switching at a bonus action and 1 CE, command is a resource, not a given. Third, En is the stage itself: a sensory tendril that grows from twenty-five feet to two miles, until the whole theater is one body. Hence the maxim: everything the user failed to save, kept.

Full progression

Level 1 through 20.

Milestones use the campaign’s familiar 1 / 3 / 6 / 11 / 18 structure and add an explicit level-20 mastery feature.

1Doctor Blythe

Surgical Doll

Passive · Gain Doctor Blythe (action, 3 CE, Focus) and Predatory En (action, 1 CE per minute) as described above.

The theater opens at first level with its gentlest instrument: the one that repairs.

Emergency Stitch

Reaction · 2 CE (while Blythe is active) · Reduce damage to a creature within Blythe's reach by 4 × PB.

The stitch arrives before the bleeding finishes its argument.

Scaling

Scaling · Doctor Blythe's tether: 20 ft · Predatory En: 25-ft radius at 1st level, growing to 2 miles at level 18.

3Puppeteer

Corpse Puppeteer

Action · 3 CE · Attach a shikigami to a corpse within 10 ft · It rises with HP equal to half its former max (cap 8×level), AC 12+PB, and can take Move + one simple action after your turn · Cannot use its former CT.

The second refusal. The wasted corpse, conscripted, the theater’s cast grows from the graveyard.

Preset Orders

Passive · Give a puppet a simple standing order (guard, patrol, follow, attack designated targets) · It continues within 1 mile without using your action economy.

The strings hold even when the puppeteer’s hands are elsewhere.

Puppet Sight

Bonus action · Perceive through one puppet until the start of your next turn.

The theater has many stages; the puppeteer can watch from any of them.

Scaling

Scaling · Predatory En range extends to 60 ft.

6Terpsichora

Terpsichora

Bonus action · 5 CE · Focus · 1 minute · Ballerina shikigami strings your body: speed +20 ft, jump distance tripled, +PB to Athletics/Acrobatics, one extra unarmed/natural weapon attack per Attack action.

The third doll, and the cruelest refusal: a ballerina shikigami that strings the sorcerer’s own body. The limited living body, answered by making it dance.

Dance Past the Limits

At the start of your turn while Terpsichora is active, spend 2 CE · Advantage on Dex saves and +2 AC until your next turn; take 5 force recoil at the end of the turn.

The strings pull harder than flesh allows; the body pays the difference.

Snap the Strings

Bonus action · 1 CE · Destroy one puppet within 60 ft; choose Snare (hostiles within 10 ft: Dex save or restrained until the end of your next turn), Sling (pull one willing creature within 10 ft up to 20 ft in a straight line of your choice; doesn't provoke), or Suture Surge (one creature within 10 ft heals 10 HP).

A doll’s death, choreographed: every puppet is also a trap waiting for its cue.

Combat Switch

Passive · Switch signature shikigami once per turn without a bonus action (still 1 CE); a new Focus ends the previous one.

The theater changes scenes faster now; the griefs take turns more gracefully.

Scaling

Scaling · Predatory En range extends to 90 ft.

11Royal Guard Control

Puppet Memory

Passive · A corpse repaired by Doctor Blythe retains one mundane proficiency and one non-Domain, non-Maximum combat feature from life · Using that feature costs you its printed CE through the puppet.

The dead keep their skills; the theater re-hires its cast with their talents intact.

Fifty Strings

Passive · Maintain up to 5×PB simple corpse puppets · Groups of five act as one swarm stat block in mass scenes.

The theater scales from chamber piece to company.

Surgical Mastery

Passive · Doctor Blythe's healing becomes 8 HP per CE; tether range 40 ft.

The first doll, perfected: the needle is surer, the reach longer.

Scaling

Scaling · Predatory En range extends to 1 mile.

18Postmortem Terpsichora

Last Dance

Passive · If you die while Terpsichora is active: it persists until the end of your next turn; your body may move and make one final Attack action under its last command · Cannot revive you; ends if the body is destroyed.

Postmortem loyalty: the doll dances on after the dancer is gone.

Royal En

Passive · Predatory En range becomes 2 miles and can branch around obstacles along a continuous path.

The En becomes royal. The stage is now a province; nothing CE-bearing within it goes unmapped.

Maximum: Puppet Master’s Serenity

Action · 10 CE · For 1 minute: Terpsichora no longer uses Focus and grants a second reaction each round (movement or basic attack only) · At the end: gain one level of exhaustion.

The Maximum is orchestration, not destruction. Serenity is the word the dossier chooses, command without strain, priced in advance.

20Royal Theater

Three Dolls at Once

Passive · Maintain Doctor Blythe, one Puppeteer network, and Terpsichora simultaneously · Blythe's 40-ft tether and healing/action restrictions remain.

The twentieth-level law dissolves the theater’s founding restriction. Three griefs, one stage, no intermission.

Perfect Puppets

Passive · Puppet cap becomes 10×PB; five-puppet swarms gain +PB to attack and saves.

The company is now an army, and a drilled one.

No Domain

The dossier states the pinnacle plainly: simultaneous shikigami orchestration and postmortem loyalty, not a barrier. A missing Domain is the norm, never the wound.

Scaling

Scaling · Predatory En range extends to 5 miles.

Pinnacle expression

Domain, Maximum, or equivalent.

Not every technique needs a Domain. When one exists, its sure-hit is a unique autonomous initiative-count-20 event unless the Domain is explicitly rule-based.

No natural Domain The source ability never required a barrier, so this adaptation does not manufacture one. The Royal Theater is the equivalent mastery state: all three shikigami systems can operate together.

Rules integration

Edge cases and shared systems.

These rulings are designed around the revised Player Guide rather than the raw PDF where the two differ.

Doctor Blythe heals HP but does not automatically restore maximum HP, soul damage, or erased techniques.
Corpse puppets are not resurrected people; they are bodies operated by shikigami.
Mass puppet groups should use swarm resolution to avoid action-economy collapse.
Postmortem Terpsichora cannot use RCT or revival on its dead user unless another feature explicitly permits it.
Technique DC: 8 + proficiency bonus + your chosen Cursed Energy ability modifier (Wisdom or Charisma unless your class says otherwise).
Direct criticals: rolled weapon, unarmed, and CE attacks can critical normally. After a qualifying critical, roll the campaign's unmodified Black Flash confirmation d20; a natural 20 triples the completed critical damage. Autonomous Domain events, damage-over-time, hazards, and unrolled pulses cannot Black Flash.
Maximum Output: unless a feature says otherwise, double that feature's base CE cost and enhance one supported axis—damage, range, duration, or accuracy/DC—using the campaign's Maximum Output rules. Named Maximum Techniques and Domains do not receive generic Maximum Output.
Technique Focus: effects marked Focus occupy the shared Technique Focus slot. Opening a Domain ends other Focus effects unless the Domain expressly preserves them.
Two-technique characters: these guides assume each technique progresses from character/class level as agreed by the campaign. Multiclassing itself does not grant another Innate Technique.
