ROYAL MARIONETTE
Core law

What the technique actually does.

Rules are written to plug into the finalized campaign glossary: normal actions stay normal, direct attacks roll, and only named Domain events bypass the initial roll.

Shared

Shikigami Rule

Doctor Blythe, Puppeteer, and Terpsichora are technique manifestations. Unless a feature says otherwise, only one signature shikigami can be fully active at a time; switching is a bonus action and costs 1 CE.

Action · 3 CE · Focus

Doctor Blythe

Summon a stationary surgical shikigami tethered within 20 feet. While active you cannot use Terpsichora or Puppeteer. As an action spend 1–CE modifier CE to heal a willing adjacent creature for 2 Royal Dice per CE, or spend time to reattach/repair a severed exterior limb if the campaign injury rules allow.

Sensing

Predatory En

As an action, 1 CE per minute, extend an irregular CE sensory tendril up to current range. You detect living/CE-bearing presences and rough direction, not exact thoughts or invisible mundane details.

Full progression

Level 1 through 20.

Milestones use the campaign’s familiar 1 / 3 / 6 / 11 / 18 structure and add an explicit level-20 mastery feature.

1Doctor BlytheSurgical Doll and 1 more feature

Surgical Doll

Gain Doctor Blythe and Predatory En.

Emergency Stitch

Reaction, 2 CE while Blythe is active: reduce damage to a creature within Blythe’s reach by PB Royal Dice as the doll physically braces/stitches the wound.

3PuppeteerCorpse Puppeteer and 2 more features

Corpse Puppeteer

Action · 3 CE: attach a clown-like shikigami to a corpse within 10 feet. It rises with HP equal half its former maximum (cap 8×level), AC 12+PB, and can take Move + one simple weapon/unarmed action after your turn. It cannot use its former CT unless level 11 feature says so.

Preset Orders

Give a puppet a simple standing order—guard, patrol, follow, attack marked targets. It continues even beyond your immediate action economy while within 1 mile.

Puppet Sight

Perceive through one puppet as a bonus action until start of next turn.

6TerpsichoraTerpsichora and 2 more features

Terpsichora

Bonus action · 5 CE · Focus · 1 minute: a ballerina shikigami strings your body. Speed +20 feet, jump distance triple, +PB to Athletics/Acrobatics, and once per turn make one additional unarmed/natural weapon attack as part of the Attack action.

Dance Past the Limits

Spend 2 CE at start of turn while Terpsichora active to gain advantage on Dex saves and +2 AC until next turn; take 1 Royal Die force recoil at end of turn.

Combat Switch

Switch between signature shikigami once per turn without a bonus action, still paying 1 CE; activating a new Focus effect ends the previous one.

11Royal Guard ControlPuppet Memory and 2 more features

Puppet Memory

A corpse repaired by Doctor Blythe can retain one mundane proficiency and one non-Domain, non-Maximum combat feature it had in life, chosen when repaired. Using that feature costs you its printed CE through the puppet.

Fifty Strings

Maintain up to 5×PB simple corpse puppets. For mass scenes, groups of five act as one swarm stat block rather than five separate turns.

Surgical Mastery

Doctor Blythe healing becomes 3 Royal Dice per CE and tether range 40 feet.

18Postmortem TerpsichoraLast Dance and 2 more features

Last Dance

If you die while Terpsichora is active, it persists until end of your next turn. Your body may move and make one final Attack action under its last command. This cannot revive you and ends if the body is destroyed.

Royal En

Predatory En range becomes 2 miles and can branch around obstacles as long as a continuous path exists.

Maximum: Puppet Master’s Serenity

Action · 10 CE: for 1 minute, Terpsichora no longer uses Focus and grants a second reaction each round usable only for movement or a basic attack. At end, gain one exhaustion level.

20Royal TheaterThree Dolls at Once and 2 more features

Three Dolls at Once

You may maintain Doctor Blythe, one Puppeteer network, and Terpsichora simultaneously. Doctor Blythe still anchors you within 40 feet while active; its healing/action restrictions remain.

Perfect Puppets

Puppet cap becomes 10×PB; five-puppet swarms gain +PB to attack and saves.

No Domain

Royal Marionette’s pinnacle is simultaneous shikigami orchestration and postmortem loyalty, not a barrier. It gains no innate Domain.

Pinnacle expression

Domain, Maximum, or equivalent.

Not every technique needs a Domain. When one exists, its sure-hit is a unique autonomous initiative-count-20 event unless the Domain is explicitly rule-based.

No natural Domain

The source ability never required a barrier, so this adaptation does not manufacture one. The level-20 Royal Theater is the equivalent mastery state: all three shikigami systems can operate together.

Innate Technique Feats

Four build-defining options.

These broaden the law instead of merely inflating the same number.

Prerequisite: Doctor Blythe

Field Surgeon

Blythe can stabilize a dying creature within tether range as a reaction without CE once per round.

Prerequisite: Puppeteer

Invisible Strings

Your Puppeteer attachments are hidden from passive CE perception; active Perception/CE check vs your DC reveals them.

Prerequisite: Predatory En

Feral En

You can shape En as a narrow tendril at twice normal range or a broad sphere at one-quarter range.

Prerequisite: Terpsichora

Dance Beyond Death

When Terpsichora would end because you are incapacitated, spend 3 CE before becoming unable to act; it persists until end of your next turn and moves you defensively according to a preset command.

Rules integration

Edge cases and shared systems.

These rulings are designed around the revised Player Guide rather than the raw PDF where the two differ.

Doctor Blythe heals HP but does not automatically restore maximum HP, soul damage, or erased techniques.
Corpse puppets are not resurrected people; they are bodies operated by shikigami.
Mass puppet groups should use swarm resolution to avoid action-economy collapse.
Postmortem Terpsichora cannot use RCT or revival on its dead user unless another feature explicitly permits it.
Technique DC: 8 + proficiency bonus + your chosen Cursed Energy ability modifier (Wisdom or Charisma unless your class says otherwise).
Direct criticals: rolled weapon, unarmed, and CE attacks can critical normally. After a qualifying critical, roll the campaign's unmodified Black Flash confirmation d20; a natural 20 triples the completed critical damage. Autonomous Domain events, damage-over-time, hazards, and unrolled pulses cannot Black Flash.
Maximum Output: unless a feature says otherwise, double that feature's base CE cost and enhance one supported axis—damage, range, duration, or accuracy/DC—using the campaign's Maximum Output rules. Named Maximum Techniques and Domains do not receive generic Maximum Output.
Technique Focus: effects marked Focus occupy the shared Technique Focus slot. Opening a Domain ends other Focus effects unless the Domain expressly preserves them.
Two-technique characters: these guides assume each technique progresses from character/class level as agreed by the campaign. Multiclassing itself does not grant another Innate Technique.
