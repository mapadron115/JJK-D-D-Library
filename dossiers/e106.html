Core law

What the technique actually does.

Assembly Points

The workshop’s inventory: AP = proficiency bonus + CE modifier (minimum 2), refreshing on a short or long rest. Advanced reconstruction spends AP in addition to CE; simple destruction does not. The domain of authority is physical matter and biology — never memories, souls, Binding Vows, or concepts. And touch is normally required, unless a feature propagates through contiguous terrain.

  • Assembly Points (AP) = proficiency bonus + CE ability modifier, minimum 2; refresh on short/long rest.
  • Advanced reconstruction spends AP in addition to CE. Simple destruction does not.
  • Overhaul affects physical matter and biology. It cannot rewrite memories, innate souls, Binding Vows, or conceptual effects.
  • Touch is normally required unless a feature explicitly propagates through contiguous terrain.

Field notes. Subject: absolute certainty, given hands. Three truths govern him. First, his CE awakened in a mind that saw bodies and buildings as arrangements that could be improved — and it granted exactly that authority, and nothing more: it cannot touch the soul, because it was never interested in it. Second, his currency is Assembly Points — PB + CE modifier, back on a short rest — spent on advanced reconstruction while simple destruction asks nothing. Third, touch is the law: the technique works through his hands unless a feature explicitly propagates through contiguous terrain. Hence the maxim: he does not heal or harm. He revises.

Overhaul Die progression: d6 at levels 1–4, d8 at levels 5–10, d10 at levels 11–16, d12 at levels 17–20. The original expansion repeatedly references a Overhaul Die without printing its value. It does not rewrite souls or techniques. The city is matter, and matter is his. Rules integration

Binding Rulings

Overhaul is matter/body manipulation; Idle Transfiguration remains superior at soul alteration and transfigured armies.
Reconstruction can heal injuries because it physically rebuilds tissue; it does not restore CE.
Borrowed Fusion techniques are deliberately narrow and GM-reviewed.
Domain's sure-hit is its initiative-20 surgery event, not automatic touch attacks.
