Overhaul

Touch gives complete authority over matter's arrangement, not its soul. This makes Overhaul an execution technique, healer, terrain controller, and horrifying body-fusion tool without duplicating Idle Transfiguration.

Power tierTier IV · Special Grade
Combat roleDisassembly / reconstruction / healing / fusion
Core law

What the technique actually does.

Assembly Points

  • Assembly Points (AP) = proficiency bonus + CE ability modifier, minimum 2; refresh on short/long rest.
  • Advanced reconstruction spends AP in addition to CE. Simple destruction does not.
  • Overhaul affects physical matter and biology. It cannot rewrite memories, innate souls, Binding Vows, or conceptual effects.
  • Touch is normally required unless a feature explicitly propagates through contiguous terrain.

The fiction. Jujutsu scholars classify Overhaul as a technique born of absolute certainty — CE that awakened in a mind which saw bodies and buildings as arrangements that could be improved, and answered by granting exactly that authority and nothing more. It cannot touch the soul because it was never interested in it: the prevailing theory holds the technique expresses its user's conviction that the world is an unfinished assembly, and their energy merely does the finishing. Every fusion and every execution is the same gesture as every healing — the technique makes no moral distinction, because the user who shaped it never learned to.

Full progression

Level 1–20 Progression

L1Disassemble

Action or replace one Attack, touch attack, 1 CE: 2 Overhaul Dice force/necrotic; objects take maximum dice. Reconstruction: action, 1 AP + 1 CE to restore one mundane object or heal touched creature 2 Overhaul Dice.

Overhaul Die progression: d6 at levels 1–4, d8 at levels 5–10, d10 at levels 11–16, d12 at levels 17–20. The original expansion repeatedly references a Overhaul Die without printing its value. This reviewed edition completes that omission using the same four-step progression explicitly established by Vector Authority in the same book. This is a codex clarification, not a claimed quotation from the source.

L3Ground Spikes

Action, 2 CE: reshape a 15-ft line of ground, Dex save, 3 Overhaul Dice piercing and prone; create cover/terrain instead if desired.

L6Biological Repair

Action, 2 AP + 3 CE: heal 4 Overhaul Dice + CE modifier and end one poisoned/blinded/deafened or broken-limb penalty if enough physical tissue remains. Cannot restore soul damage.

L9Instant Reassembly

Reaction, 2 AP + 3 CE when you or adjacent willing creature takes qualifying physical injury: prevent the injury condition after damage resolves. Once/round.

L11Fusion

During 1 minute of contact with willing/incapacitated creature, 4 AP + 5 CE: fuse for up to 10 minutes. Gain temp HP equal half target max HP and one physical trait/proficiency. With explicit GM approval may borrow one non-Domain active technique feature, using your CE. Ending fusion restores separate bodies at their current proportional HP.

L13Terrain Dominion

Action, 5 CE: restructure a 30-ft cube of contiguous nonmagical terrain into walls, spikes, pits, ramps, or open paths. Creatures caught make Dex save or take 6 Overhaul Dice and move to nearest legal space.

L15Complete Rebuild

Action, 4 AP + 6 CE: restore a severed limb/eye or rebuild a creature reduced to 0 HP within the last round to 1 HP if its soul/life has not departed. Once/long rest per creature.

L18Domain Expansion — Operating Theater of Perfect Matter

175-ft barrier. At initiative 20 choose DISASSEMBLE or REASSEMBLE. DISASSEMBLE: hostiles Con save, 8 Overhaul Dice force/necrotic and one piece of carried non-artifact equipment becomes broken on failure. REASSEMBLE: allies of choice heal 5 Overhaul Dice and may end one physical condition. Anti-domain protection affects the event normally; ordinary Overhaul still needs rolls/touch.

L20Perfect Reconstruction

AP maximum doubles. Reconstruction healing uses maximum Overhaul Dice once/turn. Disassemble on a critical applies 2 additional injury-threshold dice for determining Break only, not damage. Once/long rest Complete Rebuild can affect a creature dead for up to 1 minute if its soul is willing and available.

Pinnacle expression

Pinnacle / Maximum

Maximum Technique — City Block Reconstruction (10 CE + 5 AP): reshape a 60-ft cube of contiguous terrain and creatures you are touching. Hostiles in the terrain make Dex save, taking 10 Overhaul Dice force/piercing and becoming Restrained by architecture on failure. Does not rewrite souls or techniques.

Feats

Technique Feats

Prerequisite: Reconstruction

Surgeon's Hand

Your repairs rebuild more than flesh: when you heal a creature with Overhaul, you may spend 1 AP to also end one poison or disease affecting them, disassembling the affliction outright.

Prerequisite: Ground Spikes

Living Architecture

Your structures wake at your command: when you create terrain with Ground Spikes or Terrain Dominion, you may spend 1 CE to animate one 10-ft section for 1 minute — it moves 20 ft per round under your bonus-action command and may make one slam attack (+CE attack bonus, 2 Overhaul Dice bludgeoning).

Prerequisite: Fusion

Clean Fusion

You fuse cleanly under pressure: when you or a willing creature within 5 ft takes damage, you may spend a reaction and 1 AP to begin Fusion immediately; it completes at the start of your next turn, and the triggering damage is divided evenly between you. Normal costs apply when it completes.

Prerequisite: level 9

Plague Mask

You overhaul the very air: as an action, spend 1 AP to disassemble contaminants in a 15-ft cube around you for 1 minute — inhaled poisons and diseases are destroyed on contact, and creatures in the cube have advantage on saves against airborne hazards.

Rules integration

Binding Rulings

Overhaul is matter/body manipulation; Idle Transfiguration remains superior at soul alteration and transfigured armies.
Reconstruction can heal injuries because it physically rebuilds tissue; it does not restore CE.
Borrowed Fusion techniques are deliberately narrow and GM-reviewed.
Domain's sure-hit is its initiative-20 surgery event, not automatic touch attacks.
