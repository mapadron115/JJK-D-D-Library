Shikigami Feed

A Grade 4 cursed potion, a meal made from ground low-grade curses.

FormPotion
GradeGrade 4
AttunementNot required

Field notes. Subject: supply lines. Shredded feed ground from the bodies of low-grade curses, a meal for shikigami, and a shikigami that eats it as an action recovers 2d4+2 hit points on the spot. Two truths govern it. First, the enemy feeds the servant: the low grades that would have killed the shikigami become the shikigami’s rations, the oldest economy in warfare, applied to curse. Second, the meal is a single act, not a stockpile: one use, consumed in the eating, and only shikigami benefit, the master cannot dine on it himself. Mark this: armies march on their stomachs, even the ones made of cursed energy. The general who feeds his officers keeps them in the field.

Feed a Shikigami

Consumable · A shikigami uses an action to eat it · Regains 2d4+2 hit points · Single use, shikigami only.

The quartermaster’s rite: the shikigami spends an action eating, and regains 2d4+2 hit points. One meal, one use, gone in the eating, and no benefit to any mouth but a shikigami’s. A shikigami can eat this as an action to regain 2d4+2 hit points. One meal is a single use and is consumed when eaten. Only shikigami benefit from the feed.
