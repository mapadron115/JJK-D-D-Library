Feature map

Level-by-level features, as the figure refined them.

L1

The Ashen Catalogue

passive

Tier IV · Versatile Blaster · Suggested CE ability: INT · Primary verb: Know. Your technique save DC = 8 + your proficiency bonus + your INT modifier. Your technique attack bonus = your proficiency bonus + your INT modifier.

Hypatia prepares "scrolls" — spells — from the ghost-shelves. She may prepare INT modifier + level spells chosen from the entire wizard and sorcerer spell lists (the burned books hold everything). She casts them as technique activations using CE instead of spell slots: CE cost = 2 × spell level (cantrips 0 CE, cast as the Prestidigitation-of-ash: minor telekinesis of paper and cinder). Spell save DC and attack bonus use her technique DC. Spells retain their normal casting times, ranges, and other rules — only the resource (CE instead of slots) and the DC/attack bonus change. She does not learn spells. She remembers where the ashes are.

L1

Marginalia

bonus action, 1 CE

She annotates a creature she can see within 120 ft: make an INT (Arcana) check vs DC 12 + the target's CR (or 10 + its PB, whichever the GM prefers). On success, she learns its damage resistances, vulnerabilities, immunities, and condition immunities — written in burning script in the air beside it, visible to her allies. She may maintain annotations on up to INT modifier creatures at once.

L3

Peer Review

reaction, 2 CE

When Hypatia observes a creature cast a spell or activate a named technique feature, she may make an INT check vs DC 13 + the spell's level (or 10 + the user's PB for technique features). On success, she names its weakness aloud — choose one, lasting 1 minute: - Refuted resistance: the next attack or spell the party makes against that creature ignores its damage resistances (not immunities). - Unsound premise: the creature has disadvantage on saves against the next spell or technique effect that targets it. - Catalogued: Hypatia (and any ally she tells as a free action) learns the effect's exact mechanics — range, damage, save, duration. No more surprises. She may maintain Peer Review on up to INT modifier creatures. She delivers each one as a lecture. The table should dread the words "I see the flaw."

L3

Burning Scroll

action, 3 CE

She hurls a scroll that combusts mid-flight. Ranged spell attack (+PB + INT to hit), 120 ft: 4d8 fire damage, and the target must succeed on an INT save vs technique DC or have one damage resistance of Hypatia's choice suppressed until the end of her next turn — the fire reads them, and reading is the first step to unmaking.

L6

Theorem of Falling Bodies

action, 4 CE

Philoponus refuted Aristotle; she refutes you. A 20-ft cube within 120 ft: STR save vs technique DC or 5d8 bludgeoning damage and restrained (pinned by the proof) until the save ends at the end of their turns. The cube's floor cracks into geometric fractures — difficult terrain for 1 hour.

L6

The Catalogue Defends

reaction, 2 CE

When she is targeted by a spell or technique effect that allows a save, she may quote the counter-theorem: she gains advantage on the save, and if she succeeds, the originator takes 2d8 psychic damage as their own logic is turned — with a footnote.

L11

Disputation

action, 8 CE

The formal refutation. All enemies within 60 ft: INT save vs technique DC or be refuted for 1 minute — disadvantage on attack rolls, disadvantage on saves against her spells and technique effects, and they cannot benefit from advantage on any roll (their premises are unsound; the universe declines to assist). Re-save at the end of each of their turns.

L11

Scrollstorm

action, 6 CE

A 40-ft radius within 120 ft fills with burning vellum: 8d6 slashing damage (DEX save half — a thousand paper cuts, each one a lost page), and the area becomes difficult terrain of hot ash for 1 minute.

L18

The Burning of the Library

action, 10 CE

The Maximum. For 1 minute, she becomes the burning Library: - Ashen wings: fly speed 60 ft. - Immunity to fire damage; resistance to all other damage except psychic (the body is cinder and conviction). - The shelves are infinite: each turn, as a bonus action, she may cast one prepared spell of 5th level or lower without spending CE. - Unburn (once): during the duration, she may restore one destroyed object or text from its ashes — a burned letter, a destroyed map, a lost page — perfectly, permanently. (The GM decides what this unlocks. It should always unlock too much.)

Domain Expansion

"THE ALEXANDRIA THAT BURNED" (disclosed)

20 CE, full-turn action. 60-ft radius. Clash 800 · burnout 5 rounds. · duration 2 minutes

The reading room as it was, the night of the fire — shelves infinite, smoke sweet with burning vellum, the dome open to a sky of falling stars. The sure-hit is cataloguing: every hostile within is perfectly known — Hypatia automatically learns their full defensive profile (no Marginalia check needed), gains truesight 120 ft (illusions, disguises, and shapechanging fail automatically within), and lies spoken within deal 4d8 psychic damage to the liar as Heaven marks the falsehood.

Level 20 · Capstone

The Unburnable (capstone, passive)

capstone, passive

She cannot be unmade by fire or by forgetting. If destroyed, she reconstitutes from any ashes within 1 mile in 24 hours — unless the ashes are read: someone must read her story aloud, truly and completely, from a true copy, over the ashes. The kill condition is witness. (Sun's network has standing orders: no true copies leave the restricted section. Ever.) And the Catalogue opens fully: her prepared list becomes unlimited — every wizard and sorcerer spell, always prepared. The shelves were never the limit. The librarian's patience was.
