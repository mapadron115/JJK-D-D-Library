SACRED TREASURE • Gowther

Twin Bow Herritt

Grade 1

Paired luminous mental-focus bows

Herritt is intentionally mediocre as a mundane bow and exceptional as a delivery system for mind, soul, illusion, or information techniques.

  1. Twin Shot: ranged cursed-tool attack 120/480 ft, 1d8 radiant/psychic. You may manifest or dismiss Herritt freely.
  2. Lock-On: bonus action, 1 CE; mark one visible creature for 1 minute. Herritt attacks ignore half cover against it and you know its direction while within 1 mile.
  3. Technique Arrow: when using a single-target mind/illusion/soul technique, you may deliver it through Herritt at bow range. It still uses its normal save, cost, and limitations.
  4. Blackout Arrow (2 CE): on hit, Wis save or target cannot take reactions and has disadvantage on its next attack/check before end next turn.
  5. Broadcast Volley (4 CE): choose up to PB creatures within 120 ft; each makes the save against one eligible non-damaging mental technique you know. Pay that technique's cost once, plus 4 CE.
  6. Sacred Treasure Release — Rewrite Light (level 13+, 7 CE): for 1 minute, Lock-On can mark PB targets, Technique Arrow's range is doubled, and once/turn a creature that succeeds against one of your mental effects must reroll the save and use the new result.
