Twin Bow Herritt

Herritt is intentionally mediocre as a mundane bow and exceptional as a delivery system for mind, soul, illusion, or information techniques.

FormPaired luminous mental-focus bows
GradeGrade 1
Features & procedures

Exact player-facing procedures.

Twin Shot:

ranged cursed-tool attack 120/480 ft, 1d8 radiant/psychic. You may manifest or dismiss Herritt freely.

Lock-On:

bonus action, 1 CE; mark one visible creature for 1 minute. Herritt attacks ignore half cover against it and you know its direction while within 1 mile.

Technique Arrow:

when using a single-target mind/illusion/soul technique, you may deliver it through Herritt at bow range. It still uses its normal save, cost, and limitations.

Blackout Arrow (2 CE):

on hit, Wis save or target cannot take reactions and has disadvantage on its next attack/check before end next turn.

Broadcast Volley (4 CE):

choose up to PB creatures within 120 ft; each makes the save against one eligible non-damaging mental technique you know. Pay that technique's cost once, plus 4 CE.

Sacred Treasure Release — Rewrite Light (level 13+, 7 CE):

for 1 minute, Lock-On can mark PB targets, Technique Arrow's range is doubled, and once/turn a creature that succeeds against one of your mental effects must reroll the save and use the new result.

Rules integration

Tool Die & Sacred Treasure attunement

Attunement: spend 1 hour and one cursed-tool attunement slot. A Sacred Treasure supplements your Innate Technique; it does not grant a second one.
Activation: activated properties spend the wielder’s own CE. If a non-sorcerer is explicitly allowed to wield the treasure, its Tool save DC is 8 + proficiency bonus + one chosen mental ability modifier.
Sacred Treasure Release: normally requires level 13 or higher and is usable once per long rest; pay the specific treasure’s listed CE cost. An explicit exception in its individual entry takes precedence.
