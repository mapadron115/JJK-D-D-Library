Field notes. Subject: the bow that is not a bow. The registry's word is the honest one, prosthetic: Herritt was shaped for a wielder whose cursed energy could touch minds but never flesh, so the bow lends thought trajectory, distance, and permission to be aimed. Three truths govern it. First, the string is mediocre and the delivery is not: Twin Shot is a mere 1d8 radiant/psychic at 120/480 ft, the bow never pretended to be a weapon. Second, Technique Arrow lets any single-target mind/illusion/soul technique travel at bow range with its normal save, cost, and limits, thought, posted. Third, the Release, Rewrite Light at level 13+: Lock-On marks PB targets, Technique Arrow's range doubles, and once per turn a creature that saves against your mental effect must reroll. Mark this: Herritt's honesty is its doctrine. A crutch that admits it is a crutch can carry anything.
