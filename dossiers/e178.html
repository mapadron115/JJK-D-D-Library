Core law

Character fantasy

Study people into Masks, borrow appearance and practiced habits, and stack identities until the character becomes useful everywhere—but increasingly unstable about which self is original.

Combat loop

  1. Observe a target long enough to record a Mask.
  2. Assume Masks for infiltration and specific proficiencies.
  3. Use rapid switching to answer changing encounters.
  4. Manage Identity Strain so versatility does not become free permanent skill theft.

Field notes. Subject: a wardrobe of other people. Three truths govern her. First: she does not steal selves, she studies them. Ten minutes of watching buys a face, a voice, and one practiced habit, but never the technique, never the memory, never the thing that made the original worth copying. Second: the masks fit because the original was never quite believed in. The technique wears others easily, and that ease is the tell, and the risk. Third: Identity Strain is the wardrobe's rent. Switch selves too quickly and the energy starts asking which face was real; ten quiet minutes in her own skin is the only answer it accepts. Consider her as an actor, not a thief: her combat value is adaptability, and her tragedy is that she is most herself in someone else's face.

Core law

Masks & Identity Strain

Record Mask:

Ten minutes of observation buys a face, a voice, mannerisms, and one witnessed proficiency, never a technique, never a memory, never expertise. after observing a humanoid or intelligent shapechanging creature for 10 minutes, record its appearance, voice, scent, mannerisms, and one skill/tool proficiency you witnessed it use. Maximum stored Masks = PB. Recording never copies class features, CE reserve, Innate Techniques, memories, passwords, supernatural traits, or expertise.

Assume Mask:

One CE, one action, one hour: the transformation, within a size category, with the stored proficiency but otherwise her own statistics. action, 1 CE, Focus, up to 1 hour. Physically transform to match the recorded outward form within one size category. Gain the stored proficiency (not expertise). Your statistics otherwise remain yours.

Identity Strain:

The rent on the wardrobe. Mask-to-mask switches without a minute of true form accrue Strain, at two, charm finds her; at four, Insight and Focus fray; ten minutes herself clears it all. each time you switch directly from one Mask to another without returning to your true form for at least 1 minute, gain 1 Strain, max 4. At 2+, disadvantage on saves against charm; at 4, also disadvantage on Insight and Concentration/Focus checks. Ten minutes in true form removes all Strain.

Feats

Build-defining options.

Perfect Accent

An hour of listening buys the accent flawless, though not the language beneath it. After listening to a language for 1 hour through a recorded Mask, you can reproduce its accent flawlessly but do not learn the language itself.

Dinner Guest

Where the identity is expected, casual scrutiny slides off, until she behaves out of character. While impersonating someone inside a place where that identity is expected, gain advantage on checks to avoid casual scrutiny until you behave obviously out of character.

Backward Hands

A failed Sleight or Deception reads the room for her: she learns exactly who is suspicious. Once per round, when you fail a Sleight of Hand or Deception check while masked, the Mask reads the room: you immediately learn which creatures that witnessed the check are suspicious of you.

Predator’s Memory

One social note per Mask, a routine, a title, a public preference, carried without secret knowledge. You can store one additional purely social Memory Note per Mask: a public relationship, routine, title, or preference you personally observed. It grants no secret knowledge.

Rules integration

Building the character

Strategist or Scout. This is a high-utility infiltration technique, not a combat copy power. Its combat value is adaptability, not stealing another character sheet.
Rules integration

Counterplay

Use secret information rather than appearances, force the user into true form, exploit Strain, and remember that recorded Masks do not grant memories or techniques.
