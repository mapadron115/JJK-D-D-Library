Core law

Character fantasy

Study people into Masks, borrow appearance and practiced habits, and stack identities until the character becomes useful everywhere—but increasingly unstable about which self is original.

Combat loop

  1. Observe a target long enough to record a Mask.
  2. Assume Masks for infiltration and specific proficiencies.
  3. Use rapid switching to answer changing encounters.
  4. Manage Identity Strain so versatility does not become free permanent skill theft.

The fiction. Jujutsu scholars classify the Masquerade as selfhood treated as a wardrobe — the user's cursed energy learned to wear other people because it never fully believed in the shape of its own wearer. The prevailing theory holds that a Mask is not a copy but a confession: each recorded face is someone the sorcerer studied long enough to want to be, if only for an hour. Identity Strain is the technique's quiet verdict — wear enough selves in a row and the energy starts to wonder which one was ever real.

Core law

Masks & Identity Strain

Record Mask:

after observing a humanoid or intelligent shapechanging creature for 10 minutes, record its appearance, voice, scent, mannerisms, and one skill/tool proficiency you witnessed it use. Maximum stored Masks = PB. Recording never copies class features, CE reserve, Innate Techniques, memories, passwords, supernatural traits, or expertise.

Assume Mask:

action, 1 CE, Focus, up to 1 hour. Physically transform to match the recorded outward form within one size category. Gain the stored proficiency (not expertise). Your statistics otherwise remain yours.

Identity Strain:

each time you switch directly from one Mask to another without returning to your true form for at least 1 minute, gain 1 Strain, max 4. At 2+, disadvantage on saves against charm; at 4, also disadvantage on Insight and Concentration/Focus checks. Ten minutes in true form removes all Strain.

Full progression

Level progression

L1Masquerade

Gain Masks and Identity Strain. You also gain Backward Tell: once per turn while you are masked, when a creature misses you with a melee attack, you may move 5 feet without provoking opportunity attacks from it, using deliberately wrong body language.

L3Borrowed Habit

Each Mask records two proficiencies instead of one; choose one to gain when assuming it. After 1 minute observing a masked identity’s normal routine, advantage on your first Deception check to impersonate it in a scene.

L6Predator Behind the Face

When recording a Mask, also record one mundane movement mode or sense the target naturally has: climb speed up to walking speed, swim speed up to walking speed, darkvision 60 feet, or +5-foot reach if anatomically appropriate. Gain that trait while masked. Supernatural flight, tremorsense, regeneration, and special senses cannot be copied.

L11Many Faces, One Turn

Switch Masks as a bonus action for 1 CE. Mask cap becomes PB + CE modifier. Once per round when you switch, clear 1 Strain if the new Mask is one you have not used since your last short rest. You may maintain one purely cosmetic disguise over armor/equipment without merging their mechanics.

L18Maximum: Palace of a Hundred Borrowed Faces

Action, 8 CE, Focus, 1 minute. You may switch between any stored Mask once per turn without action or CE. Identity Strain is ignored during the Maximum. Each time you switch, choose one benefit until your next switch: +1 AC, +10 speed, proficiency in one skill of your choice, or advantage on your next weapon or unarmed attack roll. Once during the Maximum, combine the movement/sense traits of three stored Masks simultaneously. When it ends, immediately gain 4 Identity Strain and cannot assume a Mask until the end of your next turn.

Feats

Build-defining options.

Perfect Accent

After listening to a language for 1 hour through a recorded Mask, you can reproduce its accent flawlessly but do not learn the language itself.

Dinner Guest

While impersonating someone inside a place where that identity is expected, gain advantage on checks to avoid casual scrutiny until you behave obviously out of character.

Backward Hands

Once per round, when you fail a Sleight of Hand or Deception check while masked, the Mask reads the room: you immediately learn which creatures that witnessed the check are suspicious of you.

Predator’s Memory

You can store one additional purely social Memory Note per Mask: a public relationship, routine, title, or preference you personally observed. It grants no secret knowledge.

Rules integration

Building the character

Strategist or Scout. This is a high-utility infiltration technique, not a combat copy power. Its combat value is adaptability, not stealing another character sheet.
Rules integration

Counterplay

Use secret information rather than appearances, force the user into true form, exploit Strain, and remember that recorded Masks do not grant memories or techniques.
