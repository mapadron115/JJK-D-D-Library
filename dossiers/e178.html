Character fantasy

Study people into Masks, borrow appearance and practiced habits, and stack identities until the character becomes useful everywhere—but increasingly unstable about which self is original.

Combat loop

  1. Observe a target long enough to record a Mask.
  2. Assume Masks for infiltration and specific proficiencies.
  3. Use rapid switching to answer changing encounters.
  4. Manage Identity Strain so versatility does not become free permanent skill theft.

Masks & Identity Strain

  • Record Mask: after observing a humanoid or intelligent shapechanging creature for 10 minutes, record its appearance, voice, scent, mannerisms, and one skill/tool proficiency you witnessed it use. Maximum stored Masks = PB. Recording never copies class features, CE reserve, Innate Techniques, memories, passwords, supernatural traits, or expertise.
  • Assume Mask: action, 1 CE, Focus, up to 1 hour. Physically transform to match the recorded outward form within one size category. Gain the stored proficiency (not expertise). Your statistics otherwise remain yours.
  • Identity Strain: each time you switch directly from one Mask to another without returning to your true form for at least 1 minute, gain 1 Strain, max 4. At 2+, disadvantage on saves against charm; at 4, also disadvantage on Insight and Concentration/Focus checks. Ten minutes in true form removes all Strain.

Innate Technique FeatsOptional character-building feats

Perfect Accent

After listening to a language for 1 hour through a recorded Mask, you can reproduce its accent flawlessly but do not learn the language itself.

Dinner Guest

While impersonating someone inside a place where that identity is expected, gain advantage on checks to avoid casual scrutiny until you behave obviously out of character.

Backward Hands

Once per round when you fail a Sleight of Hand or Deception check while masked, add one Mask Die after seeing the roll but before outcome.

Predator’s Memory

You can store one additional purely social Memory Note per Mask: a public relationship, routine, title, or preference you personally observed. It grants no secret knowledge.

Building the character

Strategist or Scout. This is a high-utility infiltration technique, not a combat copy power. Its combat value is adaptability, not stealing another character sheet.

Technique die: Mask Die follows the shared d6 → d8 → d10 → d12 progression.

Counterplay

Use secret information rather than appearances, force the user into true form, exploit Strain, and remember that recorded Masks do not grant memories or techniques.

Adaptation note. This is original JJK 5e homebrew inspired by Rakshasa. The myth/folklore provides imagery and thematic rules; the combat engine, progression, CE economy, feats, and Domain/Maximum procedures are game design for this codex.
