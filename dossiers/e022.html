Completed rules

  • Great Luck: before making one attack roll, use it to treat the d20 as a natural 20. It is a critical hit if the attack lands but cannot trigger Black Flash. Recharge on a long rest.
  • Idle Death Gamble: once per long rest, use an action to expand it with the source’s chosen-creature rule. It otherwise follows current Domain range and clash rules; the “400 refinement” sentence is replaced by the shared Domain clash procedure. Once the free use is gone, a new expansion costs 25 CE. The Domain lasts 1 minute unless a pending event needs longer; it may remain only until that event resolves.
  • Inside, the three printed Pachinko attacks are bonus actions. On a hit, add their expectation: Green +10%, Red +25%, Gold +50%, to a maximum of 190%. Track one shared expectation total.
  • Place a bet: as a bonus action consume all expectation. Roll d20 + floor(expectation/10), treating a result above 20 as 20, to select the printed event and its jackpot die (d12/d10/d8/d6). The event lasts 1d4 rounds; this replaces the source d10 so it can resolve within a combat Domain. Only one event may run at a time.
  • At its end, roll another d20 with the same bonus for multiplier: 1–12 = 3; 13–15 = 4; 16–17 = 5; 18–19 = 10; 20+ = Rainbow. Roll three jackpot dice. You may reroll any or all of them up to the multiplier, keeping any dice between rerolls. Three matching values win; Rainbow wins automatically. Failure resets expectation to 0 and permits a new cycle while the Domain remains.
  • Jackpot: the Domain ends and benefits last four rounds. At the start of each turn, CE becomes maximum and automatic RCT spends up to CE modifier. Damage cannot reduce you below 1 HP and injuries heal. Destruction of the brain/body or a feature that kills without reducing HP can still kill you. Regain one free Domain use.
  • On a Roll: after completing one Jackpot, you have advantage on event and multiplier d20 rolls until the next long rest. Fever: each time Jackpot prevents you dropping below 1 HP, gain one Fever point, maximum PB, lost when Jackpot ends. Spend one for a critical hit, +10 to one save/check, or one restricted extra action; only one Fever action per turn.
  • Luckiest Sorcerer Alive: this is the level-18 feature. Roll three d20s for the multiplier and choose one. Automatic RCT may spend twice CE modifier; Jackpot lasts CE modifier minutes (minimum one), and you may initiate that RCT with no action when damage is received.

Power identity

Why Tier IV: its average state is intentionally uncertain, but the rules engine itself and repeatable periods of practical immortality demand Special Grade encounter planning.

Contagious Luck

Standard

Prerequisite: Great Luck

When using Great Luck, you may choose one willing creature within 30 feet instead of yourself. The use, duration, die, and any cost are unchanged; you do not also gain the benefit.

Pachinko Manifest

High impact

Prerequisite: Idle Death Gamble

You may use the technique’s Pachinko Attacks outside the Domain at a 30-foot range. Use their normal actions, attack/DC, and CE costs; they gain no sure-hit, expectation modifier, jackpot roll, or Domain-only event unless an entry expressly grants it.

Maximum: Love Train

Pinnacle

Prerequisite: The Fever and Pachinko Manifest

Action, 10 CE: choose an origin within 30 feet and a 200-by-20-foot line. Creatures make Dexterity saves. Failure takes 15d6 bludgeoning, is carried to the line’s far end, and falls prone; success takes half and does not move. A solid obstacle stops movement but does not add damage.

Great Luck
Beginning at 1st level, luck has sided with you, and you may use it
against your foes. Once per long rest you can force one of your rolls
to be critical. You can not activate a black flash on this forced
critical.
Domain Expansion: Idle Death Gamble
At 3rd level, you gain access to your domain expansion. Once per
long rest, as an action, you and all creatures of your choice within
175 feet of you are taken to a demiplane that resembles a subway
station as music starts playing. This domain cannot be reacted to
unless the opposing user has a domain with at least 400 refinement
points and has higher refinement than you.
You gain three types of Pachinko attacks that can be used as a bonus
action, each attack increases the "expectation" by a percentage.
● Green Balls. As a bonus action for 1 cursed energy, make a
ranged cursed energy attack roll against a target within 30ft.
It deals 1d4 bludgeoning damage on a hit and raises
expectations by 10%.
● Red Rod. As a bonus action for 3 cursed energy, make a melee
cursed energy attack roll against a target. It deals 1d8
bludgeoning damage on a hit and raises expectations by 25%.
● Golden Train Door Attack. As a bonus action for 4 cursed energy,
make a ranged cursed energy attack roll against a target within
30ft. of you or a melee cursed energy attack roll. On a hit,
the target takes 1d12 bludgeoning damage and must make a
Strength saving throw or be restrained. This attack increases
expectations by 50%.
You can consume the expectation at any time by using your bonus
action to place your bet and an event takes place.
There are 4 possible types of events, to know which event will
happen you must roll a d20 + your expectation bonus. You get a +1 for
every 10% in expectation, to a maximum of +19 at 190%.

On a Roll
Once you reach 6th level, after activating Idle Death Gamble, after
leaving a Jackpot, you gain advantage on all of your rolls related to
achieving a Jackpot.
The Fever
When you achieve the 11th level, after entering your jackpot, you
start gathering the fever points. Every time you are lowered to one
hit point, you gain a fever point. You can use one of your fever
points to do one of the following effects:
● You can choose to land a critical hit
● You gain an extra action
● Add a +10 to a saving throw or ability check
Luckiest Sorcerer Alive
You now roll 3d20 when deciding your multiplier, and can choose
which roll you want. Your reverse cursed technique makes you regain
hit points as if you had spent a number of cursed energy equal to
your Cursed Energy modifier added twice, and you can use Reverse
Cursed Technique as a free action while you have the jackpot bonus.
In addition, your jackpot duration will now be a number of minutes
equal to your Cursed Energy modifier.
