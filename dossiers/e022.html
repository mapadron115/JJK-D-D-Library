Pachinko Fever

A playable Idle Death Gamble loop with expectation, events, jackpot dice, Fever points, and temporary immortality.

Field notes. Subject: the gambler whose luck is an arena. Three truths govern him. First: his fortune is not a trait but a venue, the Domain is a casino floor, and expectation is the currency; every landed ball raises the odds until the bet is placed and the event runs its rounds. Second: the jackpot is immortality with terms, four rounds where cursed energy refuels fully, wounds knit themselves, and no damage can take him below one hit point; but the terms are absolute, and what destroys the brain or kills without touching hit points is not covered by the house. Third: the house needs its gambler alive, the technique will not let him die mid-game, because a bet is only honest if the player survives to see it land. Assess him correctly: he does not fight to win the exchange. He fights to stay at the table until the machine pays.

Innate Technique Feats

Build-defining options.

These are optional character-building feats. You must meet each listed prerequisite before selecting it.

Prerequisite: Great Luck

Contagious Luck

Luck, it turns out, is transferable: his forced twenty may be dealt to a willing creature within thirty feet. The cost and the recharge are his; the benefit is theirs. The house honors proxies. When using Great Luck, you may choose one willing creature within 30 feet instead of yourself. The use, duration, die, and any cost are unchanged; you do not also gain the benefit.

Prerequisite: Idle Death Gamble

Pachinko Manifest

He carries the parlor's tools outside its doors: the three attacks at thirty feet, normal actions and costs, but no sure-hit, no expectation, no jackpot. The machines are unplugged on the street. You may use the technique’s Pachinko Attacks outside the Domain at a 30-foot range. Use their normal actions, attack/DC, and CE costs; they gain no sure-hit, expectation modifier, jackpot roll, or Domain-only event unless an entry expressly grants it.

Prerequisite: The Fever and Pachinko Manifest

Maximum: Love Train

The Maximum plugs the machines back in. Ten coins, one action: all three attacks as a single pull at thirty feet, hits adding expectation even outside the Domain, and if all three strike the same creature, the bet is placed on the spot. Hits add expectation even outside the Domain. If all three strike the same creature, you may immediately place a bet as part of this action.

Rules integration

Edge cases and shared systems.

Rules priority: the reviewed rulings, explicit codex clarifications, and the level progression always control when original wording conflicts. Legacy Domain auto-hit language, action terminology, event timing, missing saves, and older scaling never override revised player rules.
Why Tier IV: its average state is intentionally uncertain, but the rules engine itself and repeatable periods of practical immortality demand Special Grade encounter planning.

The Fever
When you achieve the 11th level, after entering your jackpot, you
start gathering the fever points. Every time you are lowered to one
hit point, you gain a fever point. You can use one of your fever
points to do one of the following effects:
● You can choose to land a critical hit
● You gain an extra action
● Add a +10 to a saving throw or ability check
Luckiest Sorcerer Alive
You now roll 3d20 when deciding your multiplier, and can choose
which roll you want. Your reverse cursed technique makes you regain
hit points as if you had spent a number of cursed energy equal to
