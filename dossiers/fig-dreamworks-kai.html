Feature map

Level-by-level features, as the figure refined them.

L1

Chi Rip

On melee hit · 2 CE · once per turn.

When you hit a creature with a melee attack, you may spend 2 CE to rip at its chi. The target takes an extra 1d8 necrotic damage and must succeed on a CON save against your CE save DC or lose 2 CE (a creature with no CE pool loses nothing but still takes the damage). You gain Stolen Chi equal to the CE drained (maximum 3 × PB; the pool begins at 0). Stolen Chi may be spent 1-for-1 in place of CE on this technique's features.

L3

Jade Amulet

1-hour ritual · 3 Stolen Chi.

You forge stolen chi into a jade amulet. You may wear up to PB amulets; each holds up to 5 Stolen Chi, and chi stored in amulets does not count against your pool maximum. As a free action on your turn you may move chi between your pool and your amulets. If an amulet is destroyed, its stored chi disperses.

L6

Summon Jombie

Action · 4 Stolen Chi per jombie.

You hang an amulet in the air and a jade warrior steps out of it. Summon a jombie: Medium construct, AC 13 + PB, hit points equal to 5 × your level, speed 30 ft, immune to poison and psychic damage. It makes jade-blade attacks at +PB + your CHA to hit, dealing 1d10 + your CHA slashing damage. It acts immediately after you, obeys your commands, and lasts 1 minute or until destroyed. You may maintain up to PB jombies at once. When a jombie is destroyed, 2 Stolen Chi returns to your pool.

L11

Master's Bane

Passive.

Against those who have mastered themselves, you are inevitable. When you damage a creature that has an innate technique or a CE pool, Chi Rip drains 3 CE instead of 2 (you gain 3 Stolen Chi), and your jombies' attacks against such creatures deal an extra 1d6 force damage. While you wear at least one jade amulet, creatures with a CE pool have disadvantage on saving throws against Chi Rip.

L15

Spirit Realm Chains

Action · 2 CE.

Bladed chains of stolen chi lash from your sleeves. Target up to two creatures within 30 ft: make a CE-ability attack roll against each; on a hit, 2d6 slashing damage and Chi Rip applies without its normal CE cost, and you may pull the creature up to 15 ft toward you.

L18

Jade Tomb

Reaction · when you reduce a creature to 0 hit points.

You seal its fading chi into a new jade amulet instead of letting it disperse. The amulet arrives pre-loaded with Stolen Chi equal to half the creature's remaining CE (minimum 3, maximum 10), and you may immediately summon one jombie from it without paying the summon cost. A creature sealed this way cannot be resurrected while its amulet exists.

Pinnacle expression

Maximum, Domain, or equivalent.

Capstone material.

Maximum Technique

The Oogway Theft

Action · 10 CE · once per long rest · one creature within 30 ft with a CE pool.

The theft that started it all — a master's entire chi, taken whole. The target makes a CHA save against your CE save DC. On a failure, its current CE drains entirely into your pool and amulets (excess disperses), it is stunned for 1 minute (repeating the save at the end of each of its turns), and you gain temporary hit points equal to the CE stolen. On a success, it loses CE equal to your level and is stunned until the end of your next turn. Constructs and creatures without chi are immune — there is nothing to steal.

Level 20 · Capstone

Jade Army

Passive.

You stop collecting warriors and start fielding legions. Your jombie limit becomes 2 × PB, summoning costs 2 Stolen Chi per jombie, and your jombies gain your Master's Bane bonus damage against all creatures. Once per long rest, when you roll initiative with no jombies active, you may summon up to PB jombies as a free action — the amulets were already warm.

Innate Technique Feats

Build-defining options.

These are optional character-building feats. You must meet each listed prerequisite before selecting it.

Prerequisite: Chi Rip

The Dead Keep Their Chi

Chi lingers a minute in the freshly dead, like heat in a stone. When a creature dies within 30 ft of you, you may use your reaction to rip its fading chi: gain Stolen Chi equal to half its remaining CE (minimum 2). You never let a battlefield go to waste.

Prerequisite: Jade Amulet

An Amulet for a Friend

Generosity, Kai-style: the loan, not the gift. You may lend a worn amulet to a willing creature: while they wear it, they may spend its stored chi 1-for-1 in place of CE on their own technique features. You may reclaim it as a bonus action while within 60 ft — the chain snaps home. Lending is not losing.

Prerequisite: Summon Jombie

The Jombie Takes the Blow

The jade was a warrior once; it remembers how to stand in front. When you are hit by an attack, you may use your reaction to have one active jombie within 5 ft take the hit instead — the attack resolves against the zombie's AC. Loyalty, posthumously enforced.

Prerequisite: Spirit Realm Chains

Drag Them Somewhere Worse

The chains do not just pull — they deliver. When Spirit Realm Chains pulls a creature, you may place it in any space of your choice within the 15 ft, including off a ledge, into hazardous terrain, or through an ally's reach (it provokes opportunity attacks as normal for leaving that reach). Position is a weapon you stole along with the chi.
