Living Lightning

The magic word / six patrons / transformation / lightning

Power tierTier V · Special Grade+
Core law

What the technique actually does.

Core Engine, The Word. One word, six gods, ten minutes. Speaking it with cursed energy behind it trades the mortal form for the Champion: Solomon's wisdom, Hercules' strength, Atlas' stamina, Zeus' power, Achilles' courage, Mercury's speed. The transformation is the technique; everything else is the patrons cashing their part of the bargain. Silence the word and there is no Champion.

Field notes. Subject: a child wearing a thunderhead. Three truths govern him. First: the power is borrowed, not owned — the Word is a loan agreement with six signatories, and the interest is paid in cursed energy every time it is spoken. Second: the Champion is one body, not two — damage carries across the transformation, and dropping to zero ends the loan immediately. Third: each patron can be called on individually, but the full panel only sits together in the Champion's chair. Mark the economy: every lightning bolt is Zeus billing you, and the bill always comes due in CE.

Full progression

Level 1 through 20.

1The Word

Action · 2 CE + speak "SHAZAM!" aloud · Transform into the Champion for 10 minutes: advantage on Strength checks and saves; your unarmed strikes deal 1d8 + STR lightning damage; you have resistance to lightning and thunder damage. Revert as a bonus action, or automatically when you drop to 0 HP (damage carries across; the transformation never heals). You cannot speak the Word while silenced.

Six names in one shout: the sky splits, the kid is gone, and something old is wearing his face. The loan begins the moment the word ends.

3Wisdom of Solomon

Passive · You have advantage on Intelligence and Wisdom checks. Once per long rest, reroll a failed Intelligence, Wisdom, or Charisma save.

The oldest patron does not lend muscle; he lends judgment. The kid makes the decisions, Solomon just makes sure they're the right ones.

6Strength of Hercules, Stamina of Atlas

Passive, Champion only · Your unarmed strikes deal +1d8 lightning damage. When you transform, gain temporary HP equal to your level + PB.

Two patrons, one body: the strength to move mountains, the stamina to hold them up. The temporary flesh is Atlas shrugging on your behalf.

11Power of Zeus, Speed of Mercury

Action · 2 CE, Champion only · Call Zeus (once per transformation): creatures in a 90-foot line make a Dexterity save, taking 6d10 lightning damage on a failure (half on a success). Passive, Champion only · You gain a fly speed of 60 feet.

The king of gods sends his regards down a straight line, and the messenger god lends his sandals. Flight was always part of the bargain; it just took a while to read the fine print.

18Courage of Achilles

Passive, Champion only · You are immune to the frightened condition; when you transform, nearby allies (30 ft) may end one frightened condition on themselves. Once per long rest, when damage would drop you to 0 HP, you hold at 1 HP instead and stay transformed.

The last patron's gift is the simplest: he does not flinch, so neither do you. Courage, filed as a loan that never comes due.

Pinnacle expression

Maximum, Domain, or equivalent.

Maximum, The Living Lightning (10 CE): for 1 minute you are the bolt, not the boy. Your movement doesn't provoke opportunity attacks and you may teleport up to 300 feet as part of any move, including vertically; your unarmed strikes deal 4d10 lightning damage. As a bonus action on each of your turns: call down a lightning strike on a point within 300 feet — creatures in a 20-foot radius make a Dexterity save, taking 6d10 lightning damage on a failure (half on a success). Once during the minute, as an action: discharge — every hostile within 60 feet makes a Constitution save, taking 8d10 lightning damage on a failure (half on a success). He stops borrowing the lightning. He becomes it.

Feats

Build-defining options.

Wisdom's Price

When you fail an Intelligence, Wisdom, or Charisma save while transformed, you may choose to succeed instead — but the Word spends itself and you immediately revert to mortal form. Solomon's wisdom was never free; it just lets you choose what it costs.

Seven Thrones

Call on one patron directly: bonus action · 1 CE · for 1 round gain one boon — Solomon: advantage on all saves; Hercules: +1d8 on unarmed strikes; Atlas: resistance to bludgeoning, piercing, and slashing; Zeus: your next bolt deals +1d8; Achilles: immunity to frightened plus temp HP equal to PB; Mercury: your speed doubles. The full panel, available piecemeal.

Lightning Rod

Champion only: when lightning or thunder damage would hit you, use a reaction · 1 CE · to catch the bolt and hurl it back as a ranged CE attack against a creature within 120 feet, dealing 2d10 lightning damage on a hit. Zeus's mail gets returned to sender.

The Family Shares

Action · 3 CE · touch a willing creature and speak the Word over them: they gain a lesser Champion form for 1 minute (unarmed 1d8 lightning, resistance to lightning/thunder, no patron features). The lightning was never a solo act; the family just hadn't been told yet.

Rules integration

Edge cases and shared systems.

Codex rulings: Direct rolled technique/weapon attacks can use the campaign's Black Flash procedure when otherwise eligible. Passive damage, hazards, summons acting independently, and initiative-20 Domain events cannot.
Generic Maximum Output applies only to ordinary features, never named Maxima or Domains.
The Champion form is a transformation of one body: HP, conditions, and limited resources carry across in both directions.
