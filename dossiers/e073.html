Features & procedures

Exact player-facing procedures.

Weapon Equip • Level 5

Puppet Voice Ring.

Weapon Equip (Level 5) · Bonus action · 2 Reserve · 10 minutes · Focus command sound through the voice; project whispers or screams with supernatural precision.

Command is focused through the voice itself, whispers and screams projected with supernatural precision. The vessel becomes a mouthpiece; the ring, a dial. The first instrument of rule was never the sword.

Weapon Equip focuses command sound through the voice; the user can project whispers or screams with supernatural precision.

Action • 2 Reserve

Whisper Seed

Action · 2 Reserve · One creature within 120 ft · Wisdom save vs your CE save DC · Failure: Charmed until the end of your next turn and hears your voice regardless of distance or ambient noise.

The quietest conquest: one creature within one hundred and twenty feet hears his voice no matter the distance or the noise, and is charmed by it until his next turn ends. A seed, planted in the ear. It does not need to grow to be obeyed.

One creature within 120 ft makes a Wisdom save or is Charmed until the end of your next turn and hears your voice regardless of distance or ambient noise.

Action • 3 Reserve

Sensation Lag

Action · 3 Reserve · One creature within 120 ft · Wisdom save · Failure for 1 minute: speed halved, no reactions, and −2 AC; repeat the save at the end of each of its turns.

He unsynchronizes the enemy from their own body, speed halved, reactions gone, armor's ease reduced, for a full minute unless they can re-tune themselves. The cruelest command is not ‘obey’; it is ‘arrive late to your own defense.’

One creature within 120 ft makes a Wisdom save. Failure for 1 minute: speed halved, no reactions, and −2 AC as sensation and thought fall out of sync. Repeat save at end of each turn.

Action • 5 Reserve

The voice, raised: creatures of his choosing in a sixty-foot radius hear one concise order and, failing the save, carry it out, the lesser ones for up to a minute, re-rolling only when harmed. Kingship, distilled: one sentence, and the crowd becomes an instrument.

Creatures of your choice in a 60-ft-radius point within 180 ft make Wisdom saves. On failure, obey one concise non-suicidal command until the end of your next turn; nonboss creatures remain affected up to 1 minute, repeating when harmed.

Full Djinn Equip • Level 9

Full Djinn Equip (Level 9) · Bonus action · 4 Reserve · 1 minute · Fly 100 ft; telepathy 300 ft; immunity to magical silence affecting your Djinn abilities; a creature that fails a Zepar save can be marked for 24 hours, revealing its direction and letting your whispers reach it on the same plane.

He becomes the voice incarnate: flight, telepathy across three hundred feet, immunity to the silence that would gag his art, and any creature that fails his saves may be marked for a full day, its direction known, his whispers finding it on the same plane. The king's reach, extended past the battlefield into the map.

Fly 100 ft; telepathy 300 ft; immunity to magical silence affecting your own Djinn abilities. A creature that fails a Zepar save can be marked for 24 hours, allowing you to know its direction and whisper to it while on the same plane.

14 Reserve • Codex Extreme

Choir of the King

Extreme Magic (Level 13) · Action · 14 Reserve · Requires Full Equip; once per long rest per Djinn · A 300-ft-radius command-wave · Wisdom save vs your CE save DC · Failure: Charmed and Incapacitated until the end of your next turn (nonboss failures instead remain under a single standing command for up to 1 minute, repeating the save at each turn's end); success still prevents reactions until next turn.

The command-wave blankets three hundred feet, the failed charmed and incapacitated, the lesser ones carrying a standing order for up to a minute, the successful still robbed of reactions. This is not an attack. It is an audience, and the audience has been given its instructions. Armies are pacified the way crowds are: by a voice too large to argue with.

A command-wave blankets a 300-ft radius. Wisdom save. Failure: Charmed and Incapacitated until the end of your next turn; nonboss creatures instead remain under a single standing command for up to 1 minute, repeating the save at each turn’s end. Success still prevents reactions until the next turn.

Anime-scale: Strategic scale: pacify or command an army-scale crowd rather than merely tagging a 30-ft encounter cluster.

Field notes. Subject: the voice that learned the shape of obedience. Three truths govern this command. First: the vessel's energy learned language before it learned force, a single spoken word once changed everything, and obedience followed like gravity. Second: domination without touch is the cheapest kind of victory; the whisper-seed costs less than the sword and outlasts it. Third: the slowing of sensation, the charming of the will, the command that rides a whole crowd, these are not three powers but one grammar, spoken louder each time. Mark the maxim: he does not strike his enemies. He edits their intentions and lets the edit do the striking.

Rules integration

Djinn Die, Equip & Extreme Magic

Independent Djinn Reserve: 18 (Rank S). Refreshes on a long rest. Every Djinn cost below, Weapon Equip, Full Equip, abilities, reactions, Extreme Magic, spends this Djinn’s Reserve, never your CE. If this Djinn’s Reserve is empty it simply cannot fuel more until you rest; your other Djinn keep their own separate Reserves.
Weapon Equip · level 5: bonus action, 2 Reserve, lasts 10 minutes.
Full Djinn Equip · level 9: bonus action, 4 Reserve, lasts 1 minute. Only one Equip can be active.
Deep Synchronization · level 11: once per short rest, one Equip activation costs 1 Reserve less.
Extreme Magic · level 13: action; requires that Djinn’s Full Equip; pay the listed Reserve cost from this Djinn’s Reserve; usable once per long rest per Djinn. Full Equip ends afterward, and you cannot Equip that Djinn again until you finish a short rest.
King Vessel · level 17: switch directly from one Full Equip to another as a bonus action, paying the new Equip cost. At level 20, once per long rest, immediately transition from one Djinn’s Extreme into another Djinn’s Full Equip.
Attack / save: use your CE attack modifier and CE save DC unless this Djinn explicitly says otherwise.
