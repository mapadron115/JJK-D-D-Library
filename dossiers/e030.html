Star Rage

Virtual mass translates direct CE investment into larger unarmed dice, flat damage, grappling scale, and critical shockwaves.

Field notes. Subject: the star that learned to fall inward. Three truths govern her. First: mass is purchased, not grown, cursed energy becomes mass points, every two points stepping the unarmed die toward its 2d8 ceiling, every point adding flat damage and a grapple size to Gargantuan; the investment lasts a fixed number of rounds and cannot be dismissed or renewed early. Second: the critical is the detonation, Mass Impact turns an unarmed critical into a shockwave, prone on a failed save, a five-foot cone of copied damage behind the target at two mass, halved speed at three, zeroed at four. Third: the Maximum is the star's death, twenty coins, a hundred-foot radius, and she sacrifices herself irrevocably to become the singularity; what fails its Strength save dies outright, and ordinary RCT or revival cannot restore her. The Domain merely taxes movement four-to-one; the technique's true argument is that mass, once bought, cannot be un-bought.

As a bonus action, spend mass points in CE: up to your CE modifier (minimum 0) at level 1.The purchase: a bonus action spends mass points in cursed energy, up to her modifier at the first rank, and the effect runs its full course in rounds, neither dismissible nor renewable until it ends. The effect lasts your CE modifier in rounds (minimum one). It cannot be dismissed or activated again until that duration ends.

Every two mass points step the unarmed die once along 1The exchange rate, fixed: two points per die step along the ladder to 2d8, one point per flat damage and per grapple size, capped at Gargantuan, while her actual space and reach never change. → d4 → d6 → d8 → d10 → d12 → 2d8; 2d8 is the ceiling. Add +1 to every unarmed damage roll per mass point spent. For grapples, count one size larger per point, maximum Gargantuan; your occupied space and reach do not change.

Ability checks and carrying capacity use your actual statistics; virtual size affects only grapple/shove eligibility and the features above. Extreme mass does not break floors or structures unless the GM determines the fiction calls for it.

Mass Impact. On an unarmed critical, the struck target makes a Constitution save. The detonation: on an unarmed critical, the target saves Constitution. One mass knocks prone on failure; two adds a five-foot cone of copied damage behind the target; three halves speed; four zeroes it. The copied damage strikes once per attack and cannot itself critical. At 1+ mass it falls prone on failure. At 2+, creatures of your choice in a 5-foot cone directly behind it take the same attack damage once, excluding target-only riders. At 3+, failure also halves speed until your next turn; at 4+, speed becomes 0 instead.

The cone is oriented directly away from you through the target. A creature can take this copied damage only once per attack, and the copied damage cannot itself critical, trigger Black Flash, or create another cone.

Build-defining options.

These are optional character-building feats. You must meet each listed prerequisite before selecting it.

Prerequisite: Virtual Mass Increase

Massive Push

The shove of the collapse: once per turn, a critical while mass is active pushes the target fifteen feet directly away, forced movement, no opportunity taken. Once per turn when you score a critical hit while virtual mass is active, push the target 15 feet directly away. This is forced movement and causes no opportunity attack.

Prerequisite: Virtual Mass Improvement

Shooting Star

The kicked star: an action and five coins, while mass is active, to boot one unsecured object within reach down a fifty-by-five line, twice her base unarmed dice plus ability modifier on a failed Dexterity save, half on success. The injury check follows only if the single damage meets the revised threshold. Action, 5 CE while mass is active: kick one unsecured object within reach through a 50-by-5-foot line. Creatures make Dexterity saves, taking twice your base unarmed dice plus attack ability modifier bludgeoning on failure, half on success. A failed save also allows an injury check only if that single damage meets the revised threshold.

Prerequisite: Virtual Mass Mastery

Maximum: Black Hole

The Maximum is the star's death, priced accordingly: twenty coins, a radius to a hundred feet, and she is irrevocably sacrificed, her body the singularity. Strength saves: failure is death, success is survival pulled thirty feet toward the center; the death-immune take 20d10 force instead. No ordinary revival answers. Action, 20 CE: choose a radius up to 100 feet and irrevocably sacrifice yourself; your body becomes the singularity. Other creatures make Strength saves. Failure dies; success survives and is pulled 30 feet toward center. A creature immune to instant death instead takes 20d10 force on failure, half on success. Ordinary RCT or revival cannot restore you.

Rules integration

Edge cases and shared systems.

Rules priority: the reviewed rulings, explicit codex clarifications, and the level progression always control when original wording conflicts. Legacy Domain auto-hit language, action terminology, event timing, missing saves, and older scaling never override revised player rules.
Why Tier III: a normal unarmed build gets major, sustained output from straightforward CE spending. The larger theoretical multipliers are allowed and openly priced rather than used to ban the option.
