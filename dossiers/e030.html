Star Rage

Virtual mass translates direct CE investment into larger unarmed dice, flat damage, grappling scale, and critical shockwaves.

Core law

Completed rules

As a bonus action, spend mass points in CE: up to CE modifier at level 1, twice CE modifier at level 6, and four times CE modifier at level 11, minimum 0. The effect lasts CE modifier rounds (minimum one), or twice that from level 3. It cannot be dismissed or activated again until that duration ends.

Every two mass points step the unarmed die once along 1 → d4 → d6 → d8 → d10 → d12 → 2d8; 2d8 is the ceiling. Add +1 to every unarmed damage roll per mass point spent. For grapples, count one size larger per point, maximum Gargantuan; your occupied space and reach do not change.

Mass Impact:

on an unarmed critical, the struck target makes a Constitution save. At 1+ mass it falls prone on failure. At 2+, creatures of your choice in a 5-foot cone directly behind it take the same attack damage once, excluding target-only riders. At 3+, failure also halves speed until your next turn; at 4+, speed becomes 0 instead.

The cone is oriented directly away from you through the target. A creature can take this copied damage only once per attack, and the copied damage cannot itself critical, trigger Black Flash, or create another cone.

Ability checks and carrying capacity use actual statistics; virtual size affects only grapple/shove eligibility and the features above. Extreme mass does not break floors or structures unless the GM determines the fiction calls for it.

Pinnacle expression

Maximum, Domain, or equivalent.

Event Horizon: 18 — Event Horizon. at initiative 20 every chosen hostile creature automatically takes 10d8 force damage and falls prone. A protected creature makes a Constitution save, taking half damage and remaining standing on a success. This is the Domain’s sole sure-hit event and repeats each round; ordinary mass attacks roll normally.

Innate Technique Feats

Build-defining options.

These are optional character-building feats. You must meet each listed prerequisite before selecting it.

Prerequisite: Virtual Mass Increase

Massive Push

Once per turn when you score a critical hit while virtual mass is active, push the target 15 feet directly away. This is forced movement and causes no opportunity attack.

Prerequisite: Virtual Mass Improvement

Shooting Star

Action, 5 CE while mass is active: kick one unsecured object within reach through a 50-by-5-foot line. Creatures make Dexterity saves, taking twice your base unarmed dice plus attack ability modifier bludgeoning on failure, half on success. A failed save also allows an injury check only if that single damage meets the revised threshold.

Prerequisite: Virtual Mass Mastery

Maximum: Black Hole

Action, 20 CE: choose a radius up to 100 feet and irrevocably sacrifice yourself; your body becomes the singularity. Other creatures make Strength saves. Failure dies; success survives and is pulled 30 feet toward center. A creature immune to instant death instead takes 20d10 force on failure, half on success. Ordinary RCT or revival cannot restore you.

Rules integration

Edge cases and shared systems.

Rules priority: the reviewed rulings, explicit codex clarifications, and character reference above always control when original wording conflicts. Legacy Domain auto-hit language, action terminology, event timing, missing saves, and older scaling never override revised player rules.
Why Tier III: a normal unarmed build gets major, sustained output from straightforward CE spending. The larger theoretical multipliers are allowed and openly priced rather than used to ban the option.

Improved Duration
When you reach 3rd level, you have honed your skills with your
cursed technique, learning how to make it last longer. Virtual mass
increase now lasts for a number of rounds equal to your Cursed Energy
modifier added twice.
Virtual Mass Improvement

refining and optimizing your technique. You can now add up to your
Cursed Energy modifier added twice to your virtual mass.
