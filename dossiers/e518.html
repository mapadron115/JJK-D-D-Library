The Overlord, Dragon of Darkness

It does not hate the light. It is simply unfinished where the light is.

Power tierTier IV · Controller
Combat roleController
Core engineDevour and corrupt
Domain—

Field notes. Subject: hunger, wearing a dragon's shape. Three truths govern the Overlord. First: it does not hate the light; it is simply unfinished where the light is. Darkness is not its element but its appetite — it eats illumination the way a fire eats air, and where it has fed, nothing casts a shadow because there is nothing left to cast one. Second: corruption is digestion begun early. The Blight Seed is the Overlord swallowed in advance, a mouth planted inside the prey, waiting for the command to bite. Third: it is vulnerable the way all hungers are vulnerable — to the one thing it cannot eat. Golden light does not burn it; it refutes it. Consider: the Stone Army does not serve the Overlord out of loyalty. Stone does not hunger, and so the hunger trusts it.

Full progression

Feature map

1Shadow of the Wyrm.

Shadow of the Wyrm

The dragon is the dark, given wings and teeth. You gain darkvision out to 120 feet. Nonmagical lights within 20 feet of you are snuffed out — this requires no action. You gain a flying speed of 40 feet on wings of shadow. Devour the Fallen: as a reaction, when a creature within 30 feet of you drops to 0 hit points, you devour its shadow, regaining cursed energy equal to your proficiency bonus. Bane of Light: radiant damage ignores your resistances, and radiant damage suppresses any regeneration you have until the end of your next turn.

3Breath of the End.

Breath of the End

The exhalation is not wind but ending. As an action for 4 CE, you exhale a 30-foot cone of living darkness. Each creature in the cone must make a Dexterity saving throw against your CE save DC, taking 3d8 necrotic damage on a failure (4d8 at level 6, 5d8 at level 11, 6d8 at level 18). A creature that fails its save also leaves the cone's area filled with magical darkness for 1 minute. On a successful save, a creature takes half damage and the area is not darkened.

6Seed of Corruption.

Seed of Corruption

Corruption is digestion begun early — a mouth planted inside the prey. When you damage a creature, you may spend 1 CE to plant a Blight Seed in it; this requires no extra action. You can have a maximum number of seeds active equal to your proficiency bonus. As a bonus action, you may detonate any number of your seeds within 60 feet of you: each host takes 2d6 necrotic damage, or half as much on a successful Constitution saving throw against your CE save DC. A detonated seed is destroyed.

11Rise, Stone Army.

Rise, Stone Army

Stone does not hunger, and so the hunger trusts it. As an action for 8 CE, you animate up to 2 stone warriors (3 at level 18) from earth or stone within 60 feet of you. Each warrior is a Medium construct with hit points equal to 5 × your level, AC 14, and a slam attack at +6 to hit dealing 1d8+3 bludgeoning damage. The warriors obey your commands; a single bonus action issues one command to all of them. They last for 1 hour or until destroyed.

14Dragon Ascendant.

Dragon Ascendant

The shadow outgrows the sky that held it. You become Huge. Your flying speed becomes 80 feet, and your Breath of the End cone becomes 60 feet. Frightful Gloom: enemies that start their turn within 30 feet of you must succeed on a Wisdom saving throw against your CE save DC or be frightened of you until the end of your next turn.

18Eclipse Eternal.

Eclipse Eternal

For one minute, you are the night. As an action for 12 CE, you wreath yourself in a 120-foot radius of magical darkness centered on you; the darkness moves with you for 1 minute. Enemies that start their turn inside the darkness take 2d8 necrotic damage. While you are in dim light or darkness, you regenerate 10 hit points at the start of each of your turns.

20The Overlord Unbound.

The Overlord Unbound

The hunger stops negotiating with resistance. Your necrotic damage ignores resistance; a creature immune to necrotic damage instead takes half damage. Additionally, when you use Devour the Fallen, you may also end one magical darkness or magical light effect of your choice within 30 feet of you — the devoured shadow pays for the unmaking.

Innate Technique Feats

Build-defining options.

These are optional character-building feats. You must meet each listed prerequisite before selecting it.

Prerequisite: Must have Rise, Stone Army

Stone General

The army learns the shape of its master's hunger. Your stone warriors add your proficiency bonus to their slam damage, and they act on your initiative, obeying one shared command with no bonus action required.

Prerequisite: Must have Seed of Corruption

Possessing Shadow

The seed is a window as well as a mouth. As an action, you may perceive through a seeded creature within 1 mile of you and speak through it. Once per long rest, you may force a seeded creature to fail its saving throw against your seed detonation.

Prerequisite: Must have Breath of the End

Plague Maw

The breath plants as it kills. Creatures that fail their saving throw against your Breath of the End are also seeded with a Blight Seed at no CE cost.

Prerequisite: Must have Devour the Fallen

Devour Magic

Hunger does not distinguish between flesh and spell. As a reaction when you are targeted by a spell or CE technique, make a CE-ability check against a DC of 10 + the effect's level. On a success, the effect is negated and you regain CE equal to its level (minimum 1).

Rules integration

Edge cases and shared systems.

Bane of Light is deliberate: radiant damage is the Overlord's one true weakness. It ignores all of your resistances — including resistances granted by race or other features — and it suppresses Eclipse Eternal's regeneration until the end of your next turn.
Your own darkness blinds you too: the magical darkness from Breath of the End and Eclipse Eternal defeats darkvision, including your own 120-foot darkvision — no devil's sight is granted. Position the darkness, or fight inside it blind, at your own peril.
Seeds: a creature can host only one Blight Seed at a time; planting a second replaces the first. Detonation is a single bonus action covering any number of seeds within 60 feet. A seed is destroyed if its host dies.
Stone warriors: the warriors are constructs under your control. One bonus action commands all of them (no action with the Stone General feat). They occupy the field normally and last 1 hour or until destroyed.
Devour Magic's "effect level": use the spell's level, or for a CE technique the level of the technique feature being used (minimum 1). The GM adjudicates effects with no clear level.
