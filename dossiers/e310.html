Features & procedures

Properties & techniques

Exact player-facing procedures

Reservoir

Maximum capacity: 168 Charges. 1 CE willingly deposited = 1 Charge. The gauntlets can absorb no more than 24 Charges per 24 hours. Stored Charges cannot be withdrawn as CE.

Crystal Bullet / Growing Up

Fire crystalline seeds into creatures or terrain. Seeds can later erupt for piercing damage, restraint, battlefield control, or mobility.

Exceed Charge: Reject

On a melee hit, discharge the reservoir through the target. Damage scales at 1d6 force per stored Charge. A full 168-Charge discharge represents the tool’s true Special Grade output and should carry catastrophic recoil.

Rules integration

Cursed-tool attunement

Campaign canon

Cursed-tool attunement: you have PB attunement slots (2–6). This tool occupies one slot and requires a one-hour ritual. Attunement ends voluntarily over a short rest, on death, or when you replace it while over capacity. Distance does not break attunement. Cursed-tool attunement is separate from magic-item attunement.
Balance & counterplay. It takes a full seven days to fill and the charge rate is capped at 24 per day — catch the bearer with an empty or partial reservoir. Even a full Exceed Charge should carry catastrophic recoil, so a survived discharge leaves the bearer spent.
