Crystal-CE scorpion gauntlets that plant detonable seeds, and charge like a capacitor — up to 168 Charges at 24 per day — to discharge 1d6 force per Charge, or everything at once in a 168d6 Full Reject that drops the wielder to 1 HP with a mangled arm.

Special Grade cursed tool

Gilta Brille

Metaphysical Law: Cursed energy can be stored as matter, planted as matter, and then forced back into violence.

FormPaired Gauntlets
EngineExceed Charge
Attunement1 hour*

Field notes. Subject: a siege train folded into gauntlets. Three truths govern it. First, the crystals mine in three stages: Crystal Bullet — an action, a ranged strike within 120 feet for 4d8 piercing that plants a seed, PB per creature at most; Growing Up — a bonus action detonating one seed within 120 feet, 5d8 piercing, Constitution save for half, and on a failure the victim is restrained by crystal until an Athletics check beats the Relic DC; Terrain Growth — a seed in the ground raised instead as a 10-foot crystal formation for half cover, or a launchpad throwing a willing creature 40 feet unharmed. Second, the capacitor wages the slow war: the gauntlets hold 168 Charges, absorbing 24 per day — a full charge is seven days' labor, and every point is genuinely gone from its contributor until natural recovery. Reject discharges any number of charges for 1d6 force each on a melee hit, at 1 HP recoil per 2 charges; a Full Reject fires all 168 — 168d6 — and drops the wielder to 1 HP with a ruined firing arm, unpreventable by any means. Third, the lattice sets the terms: stored Charges can never be withdrawn as CE. Stored violence is a one-way door.

Features & procedures

Properties & techniques

Exact player-facing procedures

Relic profile

2d8 bludgeoning · +4 to attack and damage rolls · Pair counts as one cursed tool

Jujutsu origin

In JJK terms, the 'crystal scorpion' material is not mundane crystal. It is cursed energy that has physically crystallized inside the exoskeleton of an ancient Special Grade scorpion curse. The gauntlets grind, seed, regrow, and explosively discharge that mineralized CE. Their visible crystals are literally solid cursed energy.

Crystal Bullet — Action

Make a ranged cursed-tool attack against a creature or surface within 120 ft. On a hit, deal 4d8 piercing damage and plant one crystallized-CE seed. A creature can contain a maximum number of your seeds equal to your PB.

Growing Up — Bonus Action

Detonate one seed within 120 ft. A creature carrying it makes a Constitution save against your Relic DC, taking 5d8 piercing damage on a failure or half on a success. On a failure it is also Restrained by CE crystal. It can use its action to make an Athletics check against your Relic DC, ending the restraint on a success.

Terrain Growth

A seed in terrain may instead create a 10-ft crystal formation (AC 18, 40 HP) that provides half cover, or launch a willing adjacent creature up to 40 ft; that creature takes no falling damage from the launch.

Unbroken Light Dance — 2 CE, once per turn

When you take the Attack action, make one additional gauntlet attack as part of that action (no separate action), for 2 CE, once per turn. If that attack hits a creature carrying a Crystal Bullet, you may trigger one of its seeds immediately without using a bonus action.

Cursed Energy Reservoir

The gauntlets store up to 168 Charges. A willing creature touching them may spend CE, adding 1 Charge per 1 CE. The gauntlets can absorb no more than 24 Charges per 24 hours, regardless of how many creatures contribute. Stored Charges remain until used and can never be withdrawn as CE.

Exceed Charge: Reject

When you hit with a melee gauntlet attack, you may discharge any number of stored Charges. The target takes an additional 1d6 force damage per Charge. You then take unavoidable recoil damage equal to 1 HP per 2 Charges expended, rounded up.

Crystal Conductor

If the Reject target contains one of your seeds, Reject ignores force resistance and the target makes a Constitution save. On a failure, it is Restrained by explosive crystal until it escapes as described under Growing Up.

Full Reject — 168 Charges

A full discharge deals 168d6 force damage. After the attack resolves, you are reduced to 1 HP if you have more than 1 HP and automatically suffer a severe injury to the firing arm. This HP loss and injury cannot be prevented, redirected, resisted, or absorbed by temporary HP.

Binding Vow — Seven-Day Capacitor

The 24-Charge daily intake is a physical limit of the crystallized-CE lattice, not an arbitrary cooldown. A complete 168-Charge Full Reject therefore requires at least 7 days of charging, and every point invested is genuinely gone from the contributor's CE pool until they recover normally.

Designer’s note. At maximum charge the gauntlets glow with visible crystalline CE; a competent sorcerer can tell at a glance that the weapon is dangerously saturated.

Rules integration

Cursed-tool attunement

Campaign canon

Cursed-tool attunement: you have PB attunement slots (2–6). This tool occupies one slot and requires a one-hour ritual. Attunement ends voluntarily over a short rest, on death, or when you replace it while over capacity. Distance does not break attunement. Cursed-tool attunement is separate from magic-item attunement.
