Observer’s account

Field notes. Subject: the grudging arrow. The ammunition flies +1 true, and on a miss the archer may spend a reaction to bend the shot mid-flight onto a target within 15 feet of the first. Two truths. First, the curse refuses to be wasted: the redirect is the whole philosophy, a missed shot is merely a shot not yet aimed. Second, the curse burns once: fired, it is spent whether it hits, misses, or redirects, one flight, one chance, then mundane steel. Assess it correctly: not a weapon but a single decision, purchased in advance. Hence the maxim: every shot is two chances wearing one skin, aim the first like the second depends on it.

Redirect scope: the reaction reroll targets a creature within 15 feet of the original target of the missed attack. If no eligible second target exists in range, the miss stands. [INVENTED]
