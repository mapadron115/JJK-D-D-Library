Battle Waltz
Dancing Top Technique

Imbue simple spinning tops with brutally simple orders. Their lack of intelligence is the point: randomness becomes area denial while the user turns into a top too.

BATTLE WALTZ
Core law

What the technique actually does.

Rules are written to plug into the finalized campaign glossary: normal actions stay normal, direct attacks roll, and only named Domain events bypass the initial roll.

Bonus action · 1 CE

Dancing Top

Imbue and launch one prepared top into an open space within range. Top: AC 12+PB, HP 5×PB, speed 30, lasts 1 hour. It obeys one simple command such as “strike anything hostile nearby” or “circle this point.”

Shared attack

Top Strike

Instead of rolling each top separately, once per turn choose up to PB active tops and make one CE attack against a creature within 5 feet of any chosen top. Hit deals one Top Die per chosen top, maximum PB dice. Chosen tops then move up to 10 feet randomly or as their simple command permits.

Object physics

Ricochet

Tops can bounce off solid surfaces and one another. A ricochet changes direction but does not create extra damage unless a feature says so.

Full progression

Level 1 through 20.

Milestones use the campaign’s familiar 1 / 3 / 6 / 11 / 18 structure and add an explicit level-20 mastery feature.

1Dancing TopsBattle Toys and 1 more feature

Battle Toys

Gain Dancing Top, Top Strike, and Ricochet.

Affinity with the Tool

You can prepare PB replacement tops during a short rest from mundane materials; prepared tops count as valid technique focuses and are not consumed unless destroyed.

3Battle WaltzBattle Waltz and 2 more features

Battle Waltz

Action · 3 CE: command up to 2×PB tops to swarm a 20-foot-radius area for 1 minute. Area is difficult terrain for hostiles. First time a hostile enters/starts turn, Dex save or 2 Top Dice + PB bludgeoning and pushed 5 feet.

Unpredictable Orbit

Ranged attacks through your Battle Waltz area have -2 to hit unless attacker can ignore moving cover.

Rebound Order

One top can travel up to 60 feet through up to three visible ricochet points before making its shared Top Strike.

6Tornado TopTornado Top and 2 more features

Tornado Top

Bonus action · 3 CE · Focus · 1 minute: spin your body. +2 AC vs melee attacks, advantage vs shove/prone, and when a creature enters your reach it takes one Top Die bludgeoning once per turn. Your movement speed is reduced by 10.

Spinning Guard

Reaction while Tornado Top active: reduce melee damage by PB Top Dice and push attacker 10 feet if reduced to 0.

Launch from Spin

Once per turn create/launch one Dancing Top as part of Tornado movement without a bonus action; still pay 1 CE.

11Shotgun BluesShotgun Blues and 2 more features

Shotgun Blues

Action · 5 CE: choose up to PB active tops; fire them in a 60-foot line or at one target. Line: Dex save, 5 Top Dice, half success. Single target: one CE attack with advantage for PB Top Dice + CE mod; all selected tops land around target after.

Precision Orders

Your top commands may now include one condition and one action: “if hostile enters this doorway, strike it,” but still cannot plan tactics or recognize complex deceptions.

Long Spin

Tops last until long rest unless destroyed/dismissed.

18Maximum: Fifty-Top FuneralMaximum Technique and 2 more features

Maximum Technique

Action · 10 CE: instantly manifest 50 spectral/physical technique tops for 1 minute, represented as five swarms. Each swarm occupies 15-foot square and at end of your turn you may move each 30 feet. Hostile entering/starting in swarm makes Dex save for 5 Top Dice, half success. Swarms have AC 15, HP 10×level.

Tornado Maestro

Tornado Top no longer reduces speed and its collision damage becomes 2 Top Dice.

No Domain

Battle Waltz does not gain a Domain; the Maximum already fills the board with autonomous hazards.

20Perfect GyroscopeNever Stop Spinning and 2 more features

Never Stop Spinning

Ordinary Dancing Tops do not expire while you remain conscious and on the same plane.

Impossible Ricochet

Once per turn a Top Strike may ignore line of sight if you can describe a continuous ricochet path within 120 feet.

Top Riding

Bonus action: stand on a Large technique top and gain hover speed 40 feet until end of turn; falling rules apply afterward unless another top/support catches you.

Pinnacle expression

Domain, Maximum, or equivalent.

Not every technique needs a Domain. When one exists, its sure-hit is a unique autonomous initiative-count-20 event unless the Domain is explicitly rule-based.

No natural Domain

The technique’s endgame spectacle is Fifty-Top Funeral. Its autonomous hazards are still ordinary saving-throw effects, not sure-hits.

Advantage on Acrobatics checks and saves against prone while at least one top is active within 30 feet.

Rules integration

Edge cases and shared systems.

These rulings are designed around the revised Player Guide rather than the raw PDF where the two differ.

Top Strike is one attack regardless of how many tops contribute.
Battle Waltz hazard damage and Maximum swarm damage cannot critical/Black Flash.
Destroyed tops can be replaced during rests; the technique does not require rare ammunition.
Simple orders remain deliberately limited to preserve the source identity.
Technique DC: 8 + proficiency bonus + your chosen Cursed Energy ability modifier (Wisdom or Charisma unless your class says otherwise).
Direct criticals: rolled weapon, unarmed, and CE attacks can critical normally. After a qualifying critical, roll the campaign's unmodified Black Flash confirmation d20; a natural 20 triples the completed critical damage. Autonomous Domain events, damage-over-time, hazards, and unrolled pulses cannot Black Flash.
Maximum Output: unless a feature says otherwise, double that feature's base CE cost and enhance one supported axis—damage, range, duration, or accuracy/DC—using the campaign's Maximum Output rules. Named Maximum Techniques and Domains do not receive generic Maximum Output.
Technique Focus: effects marked Focus occupy the shared Technique Focus slot. Opening a Domain ends other Focus effects unless the Domain expressly preserves them.
Two-technique characters: these guides assume each technique progresses from character/class level as agreed by the campaign. Multiclassing itself does not grant another Innate Technique.
