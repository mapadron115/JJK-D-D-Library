Character engine

Grow a finite Tail Magazine. Fire specialized quills at range, then close with the lion's maw to recover ammunition, creating a deliberate volley→feed→disengage rhythm.

Technique save DC: 8 + PB + chosen CE ability modifier. Technique attack: PB + chosen CE ability modifier.

Counterplay

Force the finite magazine empty, deny safe recovery with Lion's Maw, and exploit cover that cannot be solved by a simple ricochet.

Adaptation note. This is original JJK 5e homebrew inspired by Manticore and the named tradition. The folklore/myth supplies the motif; the resource engine, CE economy, progression, feats, Maximum and Domain procedures are game mechanics created for this codex.
