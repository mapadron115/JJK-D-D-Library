What the technique actually does.

Character engine

Grow a finite Tail Magazine. Fire specialized quills at range, then close with the lion's maw to recover ammunition, creating a deliberate volley→feed→disengage rhythm.

Technique save DC: 8 + PB + chosen CE ability modifier. Technique attack: PB + chosen CE ability modifier.

Full progression

Level progression

1Tail Volley

Replace one attack; ranged technique 80/160, spend 1 Spine: 1 die + CE mod piercing. Barbed reduces speed 10; Heavy pushes 5.

3Lion's Maw

Melee technique, no Spine: 2 dice piercing. If target was hit by a Spine since your last turn, advantage; on hit recover 1 Spine.

6Three-Quill Load

Unlock 2-Spine payloads: Venom (poison), Screamer (no reactions), Tether (anchors movement to impact point until action to tear free).

11Predator's Geometry

Once/turn ricochet from hard surface to ignore cover if two traced segments remain within range; missed ricochet Spine embeds and can be recovered.

18Maximum: Sky Black With Quills

6 CE, once/long rest: consume 6–10 Spines; 30-ft radius Dex save, one die per Spine, half success; then place PB embedded quill hazards dealing 1 die to first hostile entering nearby.

Innate Technique Feats

Innate Technique Feats

4 optional build choices

Compound Payload

When you spend 2 Spines on a single Tail Volley attack, you may combine two payload effects on that attack, such as a Barbed Venom quill, instead of choosing only one.

Scorpion Tail

You may deliver your Venom payload through your Lion’s Maw instead of a quill. When you hit with Lion’s Maw, you may spend 1 Spine to force the target to make the Venom saving throw.

Quill Catcher

Once per round, when a ranged weapon attack misses you, you may use your reaction to snatch the projectile from the air and add it to your Tail Magazine, recovering 1 Spine. If you do, you may immediately make a Tail Volley attack against a creature within range without spending a Spine, firing the caught missile back. This return shot follows the normal Tail Volley rules but cannot apply payload effects.

Hunter's Mouth

When you hit an object or a dead or incapacitated creature with Lion’s Maw, you recover 2 Spines instead of 1.

Rules integration

Edge cases and shared systems.

Counterplay Force the finite magazine empty, deny safe recovery with Lion's Maw, and exploit cover that cannot be solved by a simple ricochet.
