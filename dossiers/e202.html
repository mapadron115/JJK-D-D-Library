What the technique actually does.

Character engine

Grow a finite Tail Magazine. Fire specialized quills at range, then close with the lion's maw to recover ammunition, creating a deliberate volley→feed→disengage rhythm.

Tail Magazine: holds up to 10 Spines; regain all expended Spines on a long rest.

Technique save DC: 8 + PB + chosen CE ability modifier. Technique attack: PB + chosen CE ability modifier.

The fiction. Scholars classify Man-Eater's Ballistics as predatory thrift — the prevailing theory holds the user's CE learned that ammunition is finite because the body is finite, and built the technique around that honesty. The Tail Magazine is not a weapon but a budget: fire the volley, then close with the lion's maw to feed and be repaid in quills, because a predator that cannot eat after the hunt does not hunt again. Registry notes call it the most disciplined hunger in the codex — every spine spent is a spine the sorcerer must earn back with their own teeth.

Full progression

Level progression

L1Tail Volley

You can replace one of your attacks with a Tail Volley: a ranged technique attack (range 80/160) that spends 1 Spine; on a hit it deals 1 + your CE modifier piercing, the quill embeds (max 3 per creature), and choose a quill type: Barbed (the target’s speed is reduced by 10 while quilled) or Heavy (push the target 5 feet). Embedded quills: the target’s speed is reduced by 5 feet per embedded quill; a creature can use an action to tear free all its embedded quills, or remove them safely with an action and a healer’s kit (DC 15 Medicine). Rattle the Barbs: bonus action, once per turn, no CE — each creature holding your embedded quills makes a Constitution save vs your technique DC; failure: its speed is 0 until the end of its next turn; success: no effect.

L3Lion's Maw

Your Lion's Maw is a melee technique attack that costs no Spine; on a hit it deals 2 + your CE modifier piercing. If the target was hit by one of your Spines since your last turn, you have advantage on the attack; on a hit you recover 1 Spine. Worry: if the target holds embedded quills, you may forgo the Spine recovery — the target makes a Constitution save; failure: it is frightened of you until the end of its next turn as the maw works the barbs deeper.

L6Three-Quill Load

You unlock 2-Spine payloads for your quills: Venom (the target makes a Constitution save; failure: it is poisoned until the end of your next turn, no damage), Screamer (the target cannot take reactions until the start of your next turn), and Tether (the target’s movement is anchored within 15 feet of the impact point until it uses an action to tear free; tearing free also removes its embedded quills).

L11Predator's Geometry

Once per turn, you may ricochet a quill off a hard surface to ignore cover, if two traced segments remain within range. A missed ricochet's Spine embeds in the surface and can be recovered.

L18Maximum: Sky Black With Quills

You spend 6 CE (once per long rest) and consume 6–10 Spines: each creature in a 30-foot radius makes a Dexterity save; failure: 3 quills embed (up to the per-creature maximum) and it cannot take reactions until the end of your next turn; success: 1 quill embeds, no reaction loss. You then place a number of embedded-quill hazards equal to your PB; the first hostile to enter a hazard’s space has a quill embed in it.

Innate Technique Feats

Innate Technique Feats

4 optional build choices

Compound Payload

When you spend 2 Spines on a single Tail Volley attack, you may combine two payload effects on that attack, such as a Barbed Venom quill, instead of choosing only one.

Scorpion Tail

You may deliver your Venom payload through your Lion’s Maw instead of a quill. When you hit with Lion’s Maw, you may spend 1 Spine to force the target to make the Venom saving throw.

Quill Catcher

Once per round, when a ranged weapon attack misses you, you may use your reaction to snatch the projectile from the air and add it to your Tail Magazine, recovering 1 Spine. If you do, you may immediately make a Tail Volley attack against a creature within range without spending a Spine, firing the caught missile back. This return shot follows the normal Tail Volley rules but cannot apply payload effects.

Hunter's Mouth

When you hit an object or a dead or incapacitated creature with Lion’s Maw, you recover 2 Spines instead of 1.

Rules integration

Edge cases and shared systems.

Counterplay Force the finite magazine empty, deny safe recovery with Lion's Maw, and exploit cover that cannot be solved by a simple ricochet.
