Pain Packer

Cruelty, refined into physics. Conjured heat-proof armor drinks every wound dealt to its wearer and banks it as heat — then gives it all back as a rising sun. The more it hurts, the bigger the dawn, and he stands in the middle of it, untouched, watching. Pain is not something he endures. It's something he saves.

Feature map

Level-by-level features

L1

Pain Packer

As an action, spend 1 CE to conjure heat-proof armor around yourself; it lasts until you dismiss it or finish a long rest. While armored: you are immune to fire damage, and you bank Pain — note every point of damage you take (after reductions). The bank holds up to 5 × your character level. As an action, spend 2 CE to vent the bank in a 15-foot-radius burst centered on you: each creature there makes a Dexterity saving throw against your CE save DC, taking 1d8 fire damage per 10 Pain banked (rounded down, minimum 1d8) on a failure, half on a success. The bank empties to 0. Hurt him and he thanks you. It's an investment.

L3

Heat Sense

Pain radiates. As an action (no CE cost), you can sense how much damage any creature within 30 feet has taken since its last rest — a number, not the source. Your armor smolders constantly, shedding dim light in a 10-foot radius. You can smell what the fight cost them.

L6

Backdraft

When a creature hits you with a melee attack, use your reaction and spend 1 CE to vent up to 30 banked Pain outward: the attacker takes 1d6 fire damage per 10 Pain vented (Dexterity save vs. your CE save DC for half). The bank decreases by the vented amount. Touch the stove, get the stove.

L11

Slow Burn

Instead of a burst, release the bank as a sustained aura — 1 minute, no concentration: at the start of each of your turns, each hostile creature within 10 feet of you takes 1d6 fire damage per 20 Pain remaining in the bank (minimum 1d6; no save — the air itself burns), and the bank drains by 20. The aura ends when the bank drops below 20, and the bank cannot go below 0 (invented). Stand still and let the room decide. Area denial, worn like weather.

L15

Deep Bank

The bank's capacity becomes 10 × your character level, and venting the bank (burst or Slow Burn) becomes a bonus action instead of an action. The armor seals fully — you can't be blinded by bright light, including your own dawn. He has stopped bothering to flinch at his own sun.

L20

Solar Core

The pain echoes. The first time each round you take damage while armored, add it to the bank twice. Every cut pays double into the dawn. There is no longer a difference between being hurt and charging up.

Pinnacle expression

Maximum and Domain.

Capstone material

Maximum: Rising Sun

As an action, spend 5 CE: the bank becomes a miniature sun hanging above you, 60-foot radius. Every creature in it makes a Dexterity saving throw against your CE save DC: 1d10 fire damage per 10 Pain banked (rounded down, minimum 3d10) on a failure, half on a success. The sun lingers 1 minute: at the start of each of your turns, creatures still inside take 2d10 fire damage (Dexterity save for half). You are immune — you stand in the middle of the dawn and watch. The bank empties. The bigger the pain, the bigger the sun.

Domain Expansion: High Noon

18th level. Sure-hit domain — the sure-hit is the sun's certainty. Activation: full turn, 20 CE (D008 baseline for a normal Domain). Clash: 800 points; burnout 5 rounds; duration 2 min (Tier III/IV mapping); radius 120 ft.

The sun doesn't miss. At the start of each hostile creature's turn inside the domain, it takes fire damage equal to 1d8 per 10 Pain currently in your bank — no attack roll, no saving throw. Your bank doesn't empty while the domain stands; the dawn is budgeted against a full ledger. You remain immune to your own heat.

Feats

Five feats, banked.

Pain Packer teaches five applications beyond the burn itself. Each is optional, and you must meet its listed prerequisite before taking it.

Prerequisite: Pain Packer

Banked Light

As an action (invented), vent at least 10 Pain as pure light instead of heat (the bank drains by the vented amount; invented): creatures within 30 feet make a Constitution saving throw against your CE save DC or are blinded until the end of your next turn — or raise a beacon of dawn-light visible for miles. No damage. A signal.

Prerequisite: Pain Packer

Cauterize

Press banked heat into a wound: as an action, vent 10 Pain and touch a dying creature — it stabilizes and regains 1 hit point. The same heat that kills, seals.

Prerequisite: Heat Sense

Pain Ledger

Reading radiated pain tells you how it was dealt: you learn the damage types a creature has suffered since its last rest, and roughly what dealt them — blade, flame, fall, teeth. Interrogation without questions.

Prerequisite: Backdraft

Heat Haze

While your bank holds 20 or more Pain, shimmering air bends light around you — you appear 5 feet from where you stand, and attacks against you have disadvantage until the bank drops below 20.

Prerequisite: Rising Sun

Solar Forge

As an action (invented) while the Maximum's sun lingers, focus the sun on a single object: destroy a nonmagical object up to a 10-foot cube, melt through restraints or a locked door, or superheat a weapon — its next hit within 1 minute deals an extra 2d6 fire damage. Only one weapon may be superheated at a time (invented).

Rules integration

Edge cases and shared systems.

Campaign canon notes

Cursed Energy baseline: CE pool is based on class; CE ability is Charisma or Wisdom (player's choice). CE save DC = 8 + CE ability modifier + proficiency bonus. Regain all spent CE on a long rest.
The bank: note damage as you take it (after reductions); capacity 5 × character level (10 × at L15, Deep Bank); unreleased Pain fades when you finish a long rest. Venting (burst) empties the bank to 0.
Immune to your own heat: while armored you are immune to fire damage — yours and everyone else's. Rising Sun, Slow Burn, and Backdraft never harm you. Allies are not exempt; aim accordingly.
Venting costs: the burst costs an action + 2 CE (bonus action at L15); Backdraft costs a reaction + 1 CE; Slow Burn replaces the burst's action with a 1-minute aura. Rising Sun is the Maximum (action + 5 CE) and empties the bank on detonation.
Rounding: "per 10 Pain banked" always rounds down; minimums are stated per feature. Track the bank as a single running number.
Domain activation defaults: rendered as full turn + 20 CE per the D008 normal-Domain baseline, with Tier III/IV clash stats (800 / 5-round burnout / 2 min) and a 120-ft radius.
Maximum vs. Reversal: Pain Packer's pinnacle is written as a Maximum Technique (Rising Sun), not a Reversal; it occupies the technique's pinnacle slot.
