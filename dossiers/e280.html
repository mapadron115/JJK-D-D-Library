Retrograde

The act of regression is the basis of your technique.

Power tierTier III · Apex
Combat roleSupport
Core engineReaction rerolls + positional regression
DomainRequiem of Retrograde (18)

Field notes. Subject: a ledger that refuses to close a bad entry. Three truths govern it. First: the technique does not prevent outcomes, it declines to file them. A failed roll is unwritten, not corrected; Reversion spends cursed energy to reopen a second that was already decided. Second: what travels back is only position. Regression walks the user backward along the exact path they moved, yet hit points, conditions, and resources stay where they fell, the past is revisited, never restored. Third: at its height it does not merely refuse the outcome, it refuses the sequence. Invert Causality answers a blow with a strike that has already landed, and the enemy’s action dies unfired while its spent cursed energy stays spent. This is not mastery of time, it is one sorcerer’s refusal to let three seconds stand, an audit of fate conducted by the very ledger it overrules.

Full progression

Feature map

1Reversion.

Reaction (your failed attack/save vs a visible effect, or a failed attack roll targeting you) · 1 CE · Rewind a few seconds: reattempt the failed roll or force the attack to reroll · At 3rd: +2 to the second attempt (+3/4/5 at 6th/11th/18th); can target a creature within 30 ft for +2 CE (they get no bonus).

The technique’s opening argument is a veto. One coin of cursed energy, spent as a reaction, buys a second look at any failed roll, your own or, by the third level, an ally’s within thirty feet. Beginning at 1st level, when you make an attack roll or saving throw against an effect that you can see or are targeted by an attack roll, you can spend 1 cursed energy as a reaction to reverse time a few seconds, allowing you to reattempt a failed roll or make the attack reroll. At 3rd level, this feature also grants you a +2 bonus to the second attempt this feature grants you. This increases to +3 at 6th level, +4 at 11th level, and +5 at 18th level. Also at 3rd level, you can use this feature on a creature within 30 ft of you that you can see for an additional 2 CE. Other creatures do not gain the bonus to rolls.

3Regression.

Bonus action · 3 CE · Rewind your movement to where you stood at the start of your turn (positional only — rolls, HP, conditions, resources don't change) · Opportunity attacks vs this movement at disadvantage · Blocked by a creature: Dex save or PB d8s bludgeoning + pushed out of your path (Large or smaller) · Blocked by an object: CE check (DC 10 + 3 per size above Medium) or you take 5 and it takes 10× PB bludgeoning · At 6th: +3 CE to regress to the start of your previous turn.

The second truth is given legs: a bonus action and three coins buy the exact path backward, the user physically unmoves their turn. At 3rd level, you can spend 3 cursed energy as a bonus action. You physically regress to the previous point you were standing at the start of your turn, tracing the path you moved. Regression is positional only: it rewinds movement (reversed attacks keep their rolls), but hit-point, condition, and resource changes from the reversed turn are not restored. This completely reverses your movement and actions even defying the laws of physics, such as jumping up backwards or running on nothing if the ground or platform was removed. Any opportunity attacks for this reversed movement are made at disadvantage. At 6th level, you can spend 3 additional cursed energy to continue regression to the point you started your previous turn. If your movement is blocked, you can force your way through. If your path is blocked by a creature, they must make a Dexterity save or take your proficiency bonus in d8s of bludgeoning damage and are pushed to a different space out of your path if their size is Large or smaller (your choice). If your path is blocked by a structure or object that is not fixed in place, you can make a Cursed Energy check to move it (DC 10 + 3 for every size category above Medium). On a success, you move it out of the way. Otherwise you take 5 bludgeoning damage and the structure or object takes 10 times your proficiency bonus in bludgeoning damage. If this destroys the object, you continue moving, otherwise you stop moving.

6Enhanced Reversion.

Upgrade · Reversion without spending your reaction, PB times per round · Spend +3 CE with Reversion to roll the second attempt at advantage.

By the sixth level the veto no longer costs the reaction, it fires on proficiency-bonus instinct, and three more coins turn the second attempt into advantage. At 6th level, you can use Reversion without using your reaction a number of times equal your proficiency bonus each round. Additionally, when you use Reversion you can spend an additional 3 CE to instead roll at advantage.

11Invert Causality.

Reaction (after an attack or damaging effect from a creature within 30 ft damages you) · 8 CE · Time rewinds to before the attack: make one attack vs that creature that ignores range and is guaranteed to hit; the triggering action is wasted (resources not refunded).

Here the technique stops unwriting and starts overruling: struck within thirty feet, the user rewinds to before the blow and lands a guaranteed hit that ignores range, and the enemy’s action dies with its resources unrefunded. At 11th level, rather than reversing events you have learned to use a conceptual curse that inverts causality to rewrite the flow of events so your attack always lands first. After an attack or damaging effect made by a creature within 30 ft damages you, you can spend 8 CE as a reaction. Time is then rewound to before the attack is made or the effect happens. You can immediately make one attack against the creature that ignores range and is guaranteed to hit by reversing causality, causing the triggering action to be wasted. The action is lost and limited resources spent on it (CE, spell slots, limited uses) are not refunded.

18Domain Expansion: Requiem of Retrograde.

Domain (18th level) · Initiative 20: choose one creature — the first thing it does on its turn is reversed and wasted (retarget each initiative 20) · Creatures of your choice are under base Reversion; your Reversion-boosted attack rolls are guaranteed to hit.

The barrier closes like a book being shut in reverse. Inside, the air moves backward in small ways, dust rising instead of settling, breath fogging before it is exhaled, and every surface carries the faint double-image of where it stood seconds ago. At the center hangs a stillness that is not peace, the feeling of a held breath the world forgot to release.

The Domain is the audit made permanent, the first thing a chosen creature does each turn is reversed and wasted, allies fight under Reversion’s protection, and the user’s own rerolled attacks cannot miss. Finally at 18th level, you have learned to expand your domain. At initiative 20, choose one creature within your domain. The first thing that creature does on their turn (Eg. using movement, taking an action, etc) is automatically reversed back to their original point and the movement or action is wasted. If multiple things begin simultaneously, they resolve in the order the creature declares them; the first declared is the one reversed. At initiative 20, you can target a different creature by switching the effects of your domain to that creature. Additionally, all creatures of your choice are under the base effects of Reversion. When you use Reversion on your own attack rolls, they are guaranteed to hit.

Innate Technique Feats

Build-defining options.

These are optional character-building feats. You must meet each listed prerequisite before selecting it.

Prerequisite: Must have Reversion

Retrograde Station

The refusal extends to objects: touch what was destroyed and spend two coins, and the thing un-breaks, fragments vanish, the whole reforms, the timeline of the object stretching back hours, then days, then years. As an action while touching part of a nonliving object that was destroyed an hour ago, you can spend 2 cursed energy to entirely repair it. Any other fragments or separated parts of it disappear and any destroyed parts reform for the object. At 6th level this increases to 1 day, at 8th level this increases to 7 days, at 11th level this increases to 1 month, at 18th level this becomes 1 year and at 20th level this increases to any point in time. Retrograde Station fails on anything that was ever a creature or that currently bears a curse.

Prerequisite: Must have Regression

Flash Regress

The regress learns company, for one more coin a willing creature can be carried along the reversed path. What if we just went backwards, like really fast? When you use Regression, you can bring one willing creature you can touch along with you for an additional 1 cursed energy. The creature traces your reversed path with you and ends in an unoccupied space adjacent to your arrival point. If your path is blocked, the normal Blocked Path rules apply to both of you.

Prerequisite: Must have Invert Causality

Maximum: Sword of Retrograde

The Maximum sharpens the overrule into a blade along the target’s reversed timeline, a Constitution save or be stunned and unmoved, a success or no reactions at all. When you use Invert Causality, your guaranteed attack strikes along the target's reversed timeline. The target must make a Constitution saving throw. On a failed save, it is stunned until the end of your next turn and the movement from its most recent turn is undone, returning it to the position it occupied at the start of that turn (damage and other effects it caused remain). On a successful save, it cannot take reactions until the end of its next turn. You cannot roll a black flash on this attack.

Rules integration

Edge cases and shared systems.

Rules priority: the reviewed rulings, explicit codex clarifications, and the level progression always control when original wording conflicts. Legacy Domain auto-hit language, action terminology, event timing, missing saves, and older scaling never override revised player rules.
Time-theme guidance (one per campaign): Retrograde shares a time theme with e103 and e133. This is guidance, not a merge, the table should run only one time-manipulation technique per campaign. Retrograde's mechanics are unchanged; pick the variant that fits the table.
Regression is a rewind, not a teleport: Regression's position rewind is forced-movement rewinding that traces the path moved, effects that trigger on leaving/entering spaces along that path still resolve, and opportunity attacks are at disadvantage rather than negated (Flash Regress removes them).
Regression HOLD: "reverses your actions" is underspecified, reversed attacks keep their rolls but damage/effects already applied are not unwound by the printed text. Needs a ruling on whether hit-point and condition changes from the reversed turn are restored or merely positional.
Invert Causality vs. resources (HOLD): the triggering action is "wasted" but the text does not say whether spent resources (CE, spell slots, limited uses) are refunded. Proposed default: the action is lost but limited resources are not refunded; needs confirmation.
Invert Causality vs. Black Flash: the guaranteed hit is a printed exception to the normal attack procedure, no Black Flash roll is made on a guaranteed hit unless the table rules otherwise; the Maximum upgrade explicitly bars it.
Domain reversal ordering (HOLD): the Domain's reversal of "the first thing that creature does" needs an ordering ruling for simultaneous triggers.
Retrograde Station scope (HOLD): the feat does not define what happens to creatures or cursed objects fused with the repaired object. Proposed default: it fails on anything that was ever a creature or currently bears a curse; needs confirmation.
Flash Regress CE trade: the CE-for-DC trade replaces only the check bonus, the base 3 CE Regression cost still applies. The +1 to the DC applies to the forced-path Dexterity save DC and the Cursed Energy check; it does not raise the user's CE save DC generally.
Domain conversion: the Domain opens at initiative 20 per the approved Domain rules; clashes resolve on 1–1,000 refinement points with the approved countdown. The "all creatures of your choice under base Reversion" aura means affected creatures' failed rolls may be reattempted under Reversion's normal costs; the user pays the CE. The target switch is declared at initiative 20 and takes effect immediately.
