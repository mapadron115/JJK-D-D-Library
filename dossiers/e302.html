Exact player-facing procedures

Rightful Hand

The weapon’s enhancement scales with how many willing allies recognize the wielder’s authority.

Commands obeyed willingly

Commands made while wielding Excalibur may grant allies temporary bonuses if obeyed willingly.

Betrayal collapses it

If the wielder betrays those who recognize them, the blade’s bonuses collapse until legitimacy is restored.

Rules integration

Cursed-tool attunement

Campaign canon

Cursed-tool attunement: you have PB attunement slots (2–6). This tool occupies one slot and requires a one-hour ritual. Attunement ends voluntarily over a short rest, on death, or when you replace it while over capacity. Distance does not break attunement. Cursed-tool attunement is separate from magic-item attunement.
Balance & counterplay. Its power is lent by followers, not owned — turn the wielder’s allies against them, or force a betrayal of that trust, and the blade’s bonuses collapse until legitimacy is restored.
