Exact player-facing procedures

Relic profile

1d8 slashing (versatile 1d10) · +4 to attack and damage rolls

Jujutsu origin

Rather than treating Excalibur as a generic holy laser sword, this version weaponizes the centuries-old association between the blade and rightful kingship. Its cursed technique is fueled by recognition freely given by other people.

Recognized

During a short or long rest, a willing creature may formally recognize the wielder as its leader. You may have up to PB Recognized creatures at once. Recognition can be withdrawn freely.

Authority Aura

While at least 2 Recognized creatures are within 60 ft, gain +1 AC. While at least 4 are within 60 ft, Excalibur deals an additional 2d8 radiant damage once per turn. If all PB Recognized creatures are present, they gain +1 to saving throws while within 30 ft of you.

King's Command — Action, 5 CE, once per long rest

Each Recognized creature within 60 ft may immediately use its reaction to move up to its speed without provoking opportunity attacks. Up to two of those creatures may also make one weapon or cursed-tool attack.

Promised Protection

When a Recognized creature within 30 ft takes damage, you may use your reaction and spend 2 CE to reduce that damage by 4d10.

Binding Vow — Legitimacy Cannot Be Stolen

If the wielder knowingly betrays a Recognized creature for personal gain, all special abilities except the +4 weapon bonus shut down for 7 days or until meaningful restitution is accepted by the betrayed creature.

Designer’s note. This keeps Excalibur centered on consent and leadership, not bloodline.

Rules integration

Cursed-tool attunement

Campaign canon

Cursed-tool attunement: you have PB attunement slots (2–6). This tool occupies one slot and requires a one-hour ritual. Attunement ends voluntarily over a short rest, on death, or when you replace it while over capacity. Distance does not break attunement. Cursed-tool attunement is separate from magic-item attunement.
