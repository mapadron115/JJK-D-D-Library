Heart Catch

A paired set of projected hands that extend unarmed combat, restraint, cover, and compelled attention.

Field notes. Subject: a guardian’s reflex that refused to stay skin-bound, two hands, still reaching. Three truths govern the protector. First, the hands are not extra limbs but a failure’s answer: the moment two hands were not enough. They are objects, not creatures; strikes, grapples, and shoves may originate from either. Second, the price is posted in the flesh: a hand’s suffering costs him a tenth, unreducible, never below 1 HP, and a destroyed hand stays gone until the pair is summoned anew. Third, restraint becomes devotion: Cutie Honey spends 5 CE after a hand’s hit to charm for a minute, the charmed cannot willingly target another while perceiving him. And the Domain is the gentlest sure-hit in the archive: at every initiative 20, Lover’s Grasp restrains and charms every chosen creature, no save, no damage. It does not wound its guests. It holds them.

Full progression

Feature map

1Hands of Love.

Hands of Love

Two Medium hands, a bonus action and 1 CE, lasting ten minutes, unarmed strikes, grapples, and shoves may originate from either, with advantage on the grapple and shove. They are objects, not creatures; damage to a hand costs the sorcerer a tenth, unreducible, never below 1 HP. The guardian’s reflex, projected. As a bonus action for 1 CE, create two Medium hands in open spaces within 15 feet; they last 10 minutes. Each has speed 30, AC equal to your CE save DC, and HP equal to twice your CE save DC. They are magical objects, not creatures; each must remain within 15 feet of you, and snaps to the nearest legal space if the tether is exceeded.

Hand attacks

At the start of each of your turns, move each hand up to its speed without an action. When you make an unarmed strike, grapple, or shove, you may originate it from either hand use your own statistics. The hands do not grant extra attacks. A hand has advantage on its grapple and shove checks.

Shared damage

When a hand takes damage, you lose one tenth of the damage actually applied to it, rounded down. This loss is not damage and cannot be reduced; it cannot reduce you below 1 HP. A destroyed hand remains gone until you summon the pair again, which dismisses any surviving hand.

3Handy.

Handy

The hands improve: AC rises by PB, hand-originated attacks add the CE modifier, the tether stretches to 30 feet, and a grappled creature is restrained. The reach lengthens; the grip hardens. Your hands improve: AC increases by your PB; attacks originating from a hand add your CE modifier to damage once; the tether range increases to 30 feet; and a creature a hand grapples is restrained.

6Hand Shield.

Hand Shield

The reaction that defines the technique: when the sorcerer or an adjacent ally is targeted, a hand interposes, three-quarters cover against the triggering effect. The hands shield before they ever strike; that is the whole doctrine. As a reaction when you or an ally adjacent to a hand is targeted by an attack, spell, or effect requiring an attack roll or saving throw, place that hand between source and target. The target has three-quarters cover against the triggering effect and until the start of your next turn while it stays adjacent. Area effects that pass through the hand damage it normally.

11Cutie Honey.

Cutie Honey

After a hand’s attack hits, 5 CE buys a Wisdom save, failure charms for a minute, and the charmed cannot willingly target another while perceiving the sorcerer. Restraint, become devotion. After an attack originating from a hand hits, pay 5 CE. The target makes a Wisdom save; on failure it is charmed for up to 1 minute and cannot willingly target another creature while it can perceive and legally target you. It repeats the save at the end of each turn.

Pinnacle expression

Maximum, Domain, or equivalent.

Domain Expansion, Lover’s Grasp: At every initiative count 20, every chosen creature is restrained and charmed until the end of its next turn, no initial save, and no damage. The gentlest sure-hit in the archive: the Domain does not wound its guests. It holds them. 18: full-turn action, 20 CE; 60-foot-radius enclosed Domain centered on you; 1 minute; Burnout 10 rounds. Sure-Hit Event, Lover’s Grasp: at initiative count 20 each round, every chosen creature is restrained and charmed until the end of its next turn, with no initial save. The event deals no damage. A creature protected by an anti-domain defense makes a Wisdom saving throw vs your CE save DC (invented); on a success it is neither restrained nor charmed. While the Domain stands, your Love Hands automatically hit their attack rolls, and creatures automatically fail grapple and shove checks and saving throws forced by the hands (a creature protected by an anti-domain defense rolls those saves normally, invented).

Innate Technique Feats

Build-defining options.

These are optional character-building feats. You must meet each listed prerequisite before selecting it.

Prerequisite: Hands of Love

Thunderous Applause

The two hands clap around a creature within 5 feet of both, 2 CE, a Constitution save, deafness on failure. Applause, weaponized. As an action, spend 2 CE to clap your two active hands together around a creature within 5 feet of both. It must make a Constitution save against your CE DC. On a failure, it is deafened until the end of its next turn and pushed 10 feet directly away from the midpoint between the hands. On a success, it is not deafened and is pushed 5 feet.

Prerequisite: Hand Shield

Handy Dandy

One hand locks into a 10-by-10-foot platform, 2,000 pounds supported, ten minutes, and can no longer attack, grapple, or shield. The guardian becomes architecture. Action, 2 CE: lock one active Hand into a solid 10-by-10-foot platform for up to 10 minutes. It supports up to 2,000 pounds and uses the Hand’s AC/HP. The locked Hand cannot attack, grapple, shove, or shield; bonus action releases it.

Prerequisite: Cutie Honey and CE score 20

Maximum: Love at First Sight

10 CE: one creature in a hand’s reach makes the Cutie Honey save at full strength, no prior hit required. Devotion, declared at first sight. Action, 10 CE: one creature within an active Hand’s reach makes the Cutie Honey Wisdom save. On failure it suffers that feature’s full 1-minute effect and repeating save without requiring a hit or restraint first.

Prerequisites: Must have Hands of Love

Lover's Embrace

Shoves push 10 feet; the grappled and restrained take a d6 at the start of their turns. The embrace leaves marks. You have mastered the basic use of your love hands. Whenever you Shove a creature, they are instead pushed 10 feet. When creatures are grappled or restrained, they take a d6 bludgeoning damage at the start of their turn. Structures also take maximum damage from your Love Hands.

Rules integration

Edge cases and shared systems.

Rules priority: the reviewed rulings, explicit codex clarifications, and the level progression always control when original wording conflicts. Legacy Domain auto-hit language, action terminology, event timing, missing saves, and older scaling never override revised player rules.
Why Tier I: strong reach and protection remain centered on the user’s existing unarmed statistics and action economy; the hands do not become a second full character.

initiative 20 all creatures in the Domain are restrained and charmed.
