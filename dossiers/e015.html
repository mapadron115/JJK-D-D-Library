Completed rules

  • The bonus action creates two Medium hands in open spaces within range for 10 minutes. Each has speed 30, AC equal to CE save DC (plus PB from level 3), and HP equal to twice CE save DC. They are magical objects, not creatures; each must remain within 15 feet of you, or 30 feet from level 3, and snaps to the nearest legal space if the tether is exceeded.
  • At the start of each of your turns, move each hand up to its speed without an action. When you make an unarmed strike, grapple, or shove, you may originate it from either hand and use your own statistics. The hands do not grant extra attacks. A hand has advantage on its grapple and shove checks; from level 3 a creature it grapples is restrained, and attacks originating from a hand add CE modifier to damage once.
  • When a hand takes damage, you lose one tenth of damage actually applied to it, rounded down. This loss is not damage and cannot be reduced; it cannot reduce you below 1 HP. A destroyed hand remains gone until you summon the pair again, which dismisses any surviving hand.
  • Hand Shield: as a reaction when you or an ally adjacent to a hand is targeted, place that hand between source and target. The target has three-quarters cover against the triggering effect and until the start of your next turn while it stays adjacent. Area effects that pass through the hand damage it normally.
  • Cutie Honey: after an attack originating from a hand hits, pay 5 CE. The target makes a Wisdom save; on failure it is charmed for up to 1 minute and cannot willingly target another creature while it can perceive and legally target you. It repeats the save at the end of each turn.
  • Domain: at initiative 20 chosen hostile creatures automatically become restrained and charmed until the next initiative 20. A protected creature makes a Wisdom save, avoiding both on a success. An affected creature repeats the save at the end of each turn, ending both conditions early.

Power identity

Why Tier I: strong reach and protection remain centered on the user’s existing unarmed statistics and action economy; the hands do not become a second full character.

Recovered character-build options

Innate Technique Feats 3

These are optional character-building feats. You must meet each listed prerequisite before selecting it.

Lover’s Embrace

Standard

Prerequisite: Hands of Love

A successful shove originating from a Love Hand pushes 10 feet instead of 5. A creature grappled or restrained by your Hand takes 1d6 necrotic at the start of each of its turns, once regardless of the number of Hands.

Handy Dandy

Standard

Prerequisite: Hand Shield

Action, 2 CE: lock one active Hand into a solid 10-by-10-foot platform for up to 10 minutes. It supports up to 2,000 pounds and uses the Hand’s AC/HP. The locked Hand cannot attack, grapple, shove, or shield; bonus action releases it.

Maximum: Love at First Sight

High impact

Prerequisite: Cutie Honey and CE score 20

Action, 10 CE: one creature within an active Hand’s reach makes the Cutie Honey Wisdom save. On failure it suffers that feature’s full 1-minute effect and repeating save without requiring a hit or restraint first.

Heart Catch
A simple but effective technique,powered by the heart.
Cursed Energy
Beginning at 1st level, you can harness your negative feelings into
a usable source of energy, called cursed energy. These feelings can
be harnessed into attacks or abilities. The number of cursed energy
you possess is based on your class, some classes use a modifier that
being charisma or wisdom (Player’s choice)
Your cursed energy DC is 8 + user cursed energy ability modifier +
proficiency bonus.
Hands of Love
Starting at 1st level, you create a pair of translucent hands as a
bonus action using 1 Cursed Energy. These hands have a 15 ft. range,
and each count as a medium creature. These hands deal your unarmed
damage attack, and have advantage on grappling and pushing. The hands
have an AC equal to your save DC, and you take 1/10th of damage they
take, and have HP equal to twice your save DC.
Handy
At 3rd level, your love hands Improve. Your hands gain the following
benefits:
● Your hands AC increase by your proficiency
● You gain a damage bonus equal to your Stat modifier
● Your range increases to a total of 30 feet
● Grappled creatures are instead restrained
Hand Shield
Beginning at 6th level, you can use your Hands of Love to guard
yourself or an ally. You can give yourself or an ally, ¾ cover.
Cutie Honey
Starting at 11th level, after landing a hit, you can use an
additional 5 Cursed Energy to force a creature you have hit to only
target you for up to 1 minute. The enthralled creature also counts as
charmed, and make a Wisdom the save at the start of their turn.
Domain Expansion: Lover’s Grasp
At 18th level, you have learned the true meaning of love. At
initiative 20 all creatures in the Domain are restrained and charmed.
