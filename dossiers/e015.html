Heart Catch

A paired set of projected hands that extend unarmed combat, restraint, cover, and compelled attention.

The fiction. Registry scholars describe Heart Catch as a guardian's reflex that refused to stay skin-bound: the user's cursed energy learned the shape of the moment their own two hands were not enough — the ally too far away, the blow that landed while they watched. The projected hands are not extra limbs but that failure's answer, still reaching. Which is why they shield and restrain before they ever strike; this is a technique whose first instinct is to get there in time.

Full progression

Feature map

1Hands of Love.

Hands of Love

As a bonus action for 1 CE, create two Medium hands in open spaces within 15 feet; they last 10 minutes. Each has speed 30, AC equal to your CE save DC, and HP equal to twice your CE save DC. They are magical objects, not creatures; each must remain within 15 feet of you, and snaps to the nearest legal space if the tether is exceeded.

Hand attacks

At the start of each of your turns, move each hand up to its speed without an action. When you make an unarmed strike, grapple, or shove, you may originate it from either hand and use your own statistics. The hands do not grant extra attacks. A hand has advantage on its grapple and shove checks.

Shared damage

When a hand takes damage, you lose one tenth of the damage actually applied to it, rounded down. This loss is not damage and cannot be reduced; it cannot reduce you below 1 HP. A destroyed hand remains gone until you summon the pair again, which dismisses any surviving hand.

3Handy.

Handy

Your hands improve: AC increases by your PB; attacks originating from a hand add your CE modifier to damage once; the tether range increases to 30 feet; and a creature a hand grapples is restrained.

6Hand Shield.

Hand Shield

As a reaction when you or an ally adjacent to a hand is targeted by an attack, spell, or effect requiring an attack roll or saving throw, place that hand between source and target. The target has three-quarters cover against the triggering effect and until the start of your next turn while it stays adjacent. Area effects that pass through the hand damage it normally.

11Cutie Honey.

Cutie Honey

After an attack originating from a hand hits, pay 5 CE. The target makes a Wisdom save; on failure it is charmed for up to 1 minute and cannot willingly target another creature while it can perceive and legally target you. It repeats the save at the end of each turn.

Pinnacle expression

Maximum, Domain, or equivalent.

Domain Expansion — Lover’s Grasp: 18 — full-turn action, 20 CE; 60-foot-radius enclosed Domain centered on you; 1 minute; Burnout 10 rounds. Sure-Hit Event — Lover’s Grasp: at initiative count 20 each round, every chosen creature is restrained and charmed until the end of its next turn, with no initial save. The event deals no damage. A creature protected by an anti-domain defense makes a Wisdom saving throw vs your CE save DC (invented); on a success it is neither restrained nor charmed. While the Domain stands, your Love Hands automatically hit their attack rolls, and creatures automatically fail grapple and shove checks and saving throws forced by the hands (a creature protected by an anti-domain defense rolls those saves normally — invented).

Innate Technique Feats

Build-defining options.

These are optional character-building feats. You must meet each listed prerequisite before selecting it.

Prerequisite: Hands of Love

Thunderous Applause

As an action, spend 2 CE to clap your two active hands together around a creature within 5 feet of both. It must make a Constitution save against your CE DC. On a failure, it is deafened until the end of its next turn and pushed 10 feet directly away from the midpoint between the hands. On a success, it is not deafened and is pushed 5 feet.

Prerequisite: Hand Shield

Handy Dandy

Action, 2 CE: lock one active Hand into a solid 10-by-10-foot platform for up to 10 minutes. It supports up to 2,000 pounds and uses the Hand’s AC/HP. The locked Hand cannot attack, grapple, shove, or shield; bonus action releases it.

Prerequisite: Cutie Honey and CE score 20

Maximum: Love at First Sight

Action, 10 CE: one creature within an active Hand’s reach makes the Cutie Honey Wisdom save. On failure it suffers that feature’s full 1-minute effect and repeating save without requiring a hit or restraint first.

Prerequisites: Must have Hands of Love

Lover's Embrace

You have mastered the basic use of your love hands. Whenever you Shove a creature, they are instead pushed 10 feet. When creatures are grappled or restrained, they take a d6 bludgeoning damage at the start of their turn. Structures also take maximum damage from your Love Hands.

Rules integration

Edge cases and shared systems.

Rules priority: the reviewed rulings, explicit codex clarifications, and the level progression always control when original wording conflicts. Legacy Domain auto-hit language, action terminology, event timing, missing saves, and older scaling never override revised player rules.
Why Tier I: strong reach and protection remain centered on the user’s existing unarmed statistics and action economy; the hands do not become a second full character.

Cursed Energy
Beginning at 1st level, you can harness your negative feelings into
a usable source of energy, called cursed energy. These feelings can
be harnessed into attacks or abilities. The number of cursed energy
you possess is based on your class, some classes use a modifier that
being charisma or wisdom (Player’s choice)
Your cursed energy DC is 8 + user cursed energy ability modifier +
proficiency bonus.
Hands of Love
Starting at 1st level, you create a pair of translucent hands as a
bonus action using 1 Cursed Energy. These hands have a 15 ft. range,
and each count as a medium creature. These hands deal your unarmed
damage attack, and have advantage on grappling and pushing. The hands
have an AC equal to your save DC, and you take 1/10th of damage they
take, and have HP equal to twice your save DC.
Handy
At 3rd level, your love hands Improve. Your hands gain the following
benefits:
● Your hands AC increase by your proficiency
● You gain a damage bonus equal to your Stat modifier
● Your range increases to a total of 30 feet
● Grappled creatures are instead restrained
Hand Shield
Beginning at 6th level, you can use your Hands of Love to guard
yourself or an ally. You can give yourself or an ally, ¾ cover.
Cutie Honey
Starting at 11th level, after landing a hit, you can use an
additional 5 Cursed Energy to force a creature you have hit to only
target you for up to 1 minute. The enthralled creature also counts as
charmed, and make a Wisdom the save at the start of their turn.
Domain Expansion: Lover’s Grasp
At 18th level, you have learned the true meaning of love. At
initiative 20 all creatures in the Domain are restrained and charmed.
