Core law

Completed rules

Totality:

The estate law is filed first because it governs everything else. any shikigami reduced to 0 hit points dies and cannot be summoned again. A destroyed shikigami is eligible for Totality, with the exception of Rabbit Escape or Mahoraga. If one of your shikigami is killed, during a short or long rest you can perform a 1-hour ritual to pass the strength of one deceased shikigami into a different non-Totality shikigami (other than Rabbit Escape or Mahoraga), granting it a permanent Totality form: ability scores become the higher of the two (plus the smaller's modifier as a bonus), hit points and speeds are added together, it gains all features, traits, actions, reactions, skills, saves, resistances and immunities of both, size is either, CE cost becomes the higher plus half the lower, and CR becomes the higher plus half the smaller's CR (rounded up). As an optional ruling, your DM can ignore Totality and instead have all shikigami return on a long rest.

Field notes. Subject: a menagerie where every cage is opened by combat. Three truths govern it. First: nothing here is given. The Divine Dogs are the starting pair and the only free ones; every other shadow must be faced alone in ritual combat and beaten to zero, the technique binds only what the summoner has personally subjugated, and being helped voids the claim. Second: this is the only shikigami technique in the archive with an estate law. A shikigami reduced to zero dies permanently, and Totality is the inheritance procedure, the strength of the fallen folded into a living one during a rest, the dead surviving only as improvements. Third: the shadow is not only a door, it is a vault, objects stored for free, enemies trapped by a failed Acrobatics check and left holding their breath. A general who buries his soldiers in his other soldiers.

Full progression

Feature map

1Divine Dogs.

The technique opens with its only free gift, two dogs for a coin each, an hour at a time, commanded by bonus action, and the standing limit is half your level. Starting at 1st level, as an action for 1 cursed energy per dog, you can summon up to 2 Divine Dogs within 10 feet of you. Your Divine Dogs last for 1 hour, until they are reduced to 0 hit points, or until you dismiss any summoned shikigami as a bonus action while within line of sight. As a reaction to seeing your shikigami reduced to 0 hit points, you can unsummon them and cannot summon them again until you finish a long rest.

Your shikigami cannot use actions on their own. You must spend a bonus action to command your shikigami or can spend an action to order them to do something more specific for longer. The number of shikigami you can control at once is equal to half your level (rounded up).

3Trial.

At the third level the rest of the pack must be earned, summon the untamed shadow for free, beat it alone to zero, and it is yours; anything within fifty feet is drafted into the ritual. Once you reach the 3rd level, you have gained access to your other spirits, and you must face them in ritual combat alone to gain access to them. As an action, you can summon an untamed shikigami within 30 feet of you without spending cursed energy. Any creature within 50 feet is considered participating in the ritual. An untamed shikigami will be berserk (attack the nearest creature), but if you have defeated the shikigami alone (not including your shikigami) and reduce it to 0 hit points, you can now summon the shikigami normally.

Summoning costs: 1 CE Gama · 2 CE Nue · 4 CE Great Serpent · 6 CE Rabbit Escape · 7 CE Mourn Tiger · 8 CE Max Elephant · 9 CE Round Deer · 10 CE Piercing Ox · 20 CE Divine General Mahoraga. If reduced to 0 hit points during a trial with multiple participants, you do not make death saving throws until all participants are unconscious.

6Shadow Manipulation.

The sixth level teaches the shadow to keep things, objects stored for free, and enemies trapped by a failed Acrobatics check, holding their breath in the dark until they escape. Starting at 6th level, you've learned to manipulate shadows, forming an extra-dimensional space to hide your things, and the shadows of others. Shadow Storage: store objects in your shadow for free; spend 1 CE to interact with or remove an object. Everything stored counts for your carrying capacity.

You can trap creatures within your shadow: as a bonus action for 2 CE, force a creature to make a Dexterity (Acrobatics) check against your CE save DC, at disadvantage in complete darkness or inside a veil, automatically failed within your domain. On a failure it is trapped in your shadow (counting for carrying capacity), can hold its breath for minutes equal to its Constitution modifier (minimum 1), and can make a Strength or Dexterity saving throw at the end of its turns to escape, appearing prone in an unoccupied space within 5 feet.

11Well's Unknown Abyss.

The eleventh level grafts the pack together, two living shikigami combined into one altered form, the base’s body wearing the other’s gift, at half the summoning cost. When you reach 11th level, you can combine your shikigami into different forms. As an action, you can temporarily combine two of your living shikigami into a single type, granting the base shikigami an alteration feature from the other. You spend half the CE cost of the base shikigami to summon up to your proficiency bonus in altered shikigami at once, spending CE for each. An altered shikigami has half the hit points of the base shikigami, and you can reduce its size category by 1 to reduce the CE cost by 1. When reduced to 0 hit points, it does not affect the shikigami used in its creation and is not eligible for Totality. You cannot summon any unaltered shikigami while altered shikigami are summoned. If the base or alteration shikigami's CR is 4 or higher, you can only create up to two altered shikigami with them at a time.

Alteration grafts: Divine Dogs, Pack Tactics · Gama, Standing Leap · Great Serpent, +15 hit points and Constrict attack · Nue, 80 feet fly speed · Round Deer, Antler attack · Piercing Ox, Relentless Charge · Mourn Tiger, Mourning Roar · Max Elephant, +50 hit points.

18Domain Expansion: Chimera Shadow Garden.

Darkness pours out of the user's feet like spilled ink and does not stop, flooding the domain until the ground is a black mirror. From its surface rise the shapes of the ten, half-formed and circling, and the whole garden breathes with a slow tide, in and out. The shadows here are not absence of light, they are a country.

The Domain is the shadow made geography, a barrier of darkness where enemies sink and are restrained, shadow-clones take the user’s hits, and every shikigami summoned inside costs nothing. Finally, at 18th level you have learned how to expand your domain. Your domain is a huge shadow barrier, filling your surroundings with overwhelming darkness. Any hostile creatures that end their turn within your domain must make a Dexterity (Acrobatics) check or become restrained and sink; they can make a Strength (Athletics) check at the beginning of their turn to escape. The area is difficult terrain for creatures of your choice, and you are under the effects of Freedom of Movement within the domain. Shadow Clones: while inside the domain, as a bonus action you can spend cursed energy up to your Cursed Energy modifier to create that many clones of yourself, they have all your features and stats but not weapons or hit points and cannot use cursed energy; their hit points equal your Cursed Energy modifier and they become a shadow puddle at 0. You can use your reaction to switch places with a clone when you are hit, making the clone take the hit instead. Shikigami Clones: whenever you summon a shikigami, you can instead spend cursed energy equal to the shikigami's cost to summon a perfect copy, up to your Cursed Energy modifier of copies on the same turn; you cannot create copies of Mahoraga. Your normal shikigami cost no cursed energy within your domain.

Innate Technique Feats

Build-defining options.

These are optional character-building feats. You must meet each listed prerequisite before selecting it. The first three are printed in V.6; Shadow Kennel is a codex-authored fill (see appendix).

Prerequisites: Must have Divine Dogs

Shadow User

The user learns the shadow’s older tricks, enter it for full cover and short hops, trip an attacker’s legs out from under them, or borrow two shadow-themed sorcerer spells. You have furthered your use of shadows. Choose one of the following features (this feat can be taken multiple times). Shadow Skulker: as an action for 2 CE you physically enter the shadows, gaining full cover for a turn or until you exit from a shadow within 10 feet. At the start of your turn, you can spend 1 CE to remain in the shadow. You can hear and see through the shadow and can summon tamed shikigami within the shadow. If the shadow is sufficiently illuminated, you exit the shadow. Shadow Ridge: as a reaction for 1 CE against a melee attack, you force a creature within 10 feet to make a Dexterity saving throw; on a failure the target rolls the attack at disadvantage as their legs fall into their shadow briefly and they fall prone. You can instead spend 3 CE to give the target disadvantage on the save. Alternatively, learn any two Sorcerer spells below 5th level of your choice; both must have a shadow/darkness theme approved by the GM. The CE cost equals the spell's level.

Prerequisites: Must have Totality

Chimerafication

The fusion learns monstrosity, sacrifice even a living shikigami, fuse up to half the proficiency bonus plus one, and raise a Chimera that can wear a humanoid form. You have learned how to better fuse the powers of your shadows even at the cost of creating a monstrosity. When creating a Totality, you can sacrifice a tamed shikigami even if it is not destroyed. You can use a number of shikigami up to half your proficiency bonus (rounded up) + 1 that are eligible for Totality to fuse into an eligible non-Totality shikigami, forming a Chimera. Chimeras can have humanoid forms, but cannot be used for Well's Unknown Abyss. You can also give an additional alteration to your altered shikigami created with Well's Unknown Abyss, counting as part of the technique.

Prerequisite: Must have Chimera Shadow Garden

Maximum: Embodiment

The Maximum skips the summoning and wears the shadow, half a shikigami’s cost buys its movement, actions, features, and one ability score for an hour. As an action, you can spend half the cost (minimum 1) of one of your tamed shikigami (no Totality or Chimera). Rather than summoning, you instead gain their movement, actions, legendary actions, features and one ability score of your choice from the shikigami for 1 hour, for example, Divine Dog's movement, Max Elephant's Constitution, or Mahoraga's Adaptation. You can embody one shikigami at a time.

Prerequisite: Must have Shadow Manipulation

Shadow Kennel

The shadow becomes a kennel, store a tamed shikigami as an action, dismissed but kept, and release it as a reaction when an ally is attacked. Your shadow can hold living shadows as well as objects. As an action, you can store one of your tamed shikigami in your shadow; while stored it counts as dismissed. As a reaction when a creature you can see within 30 feet of you is attacked, you can release the stored shikigami into an unoccupied space within 30 feet of you.

Rules integration

Edge cases and shared systems.

Not e035: this dossier is the V.6 author's version of Ten Shadows, the subclass progression plus the visual supplement's shikigami (U07 transcriptions TS101–TS114). It is not e035, "Ten Shadows Technique," zack's consolidated revision dossier, which re-prices the roster, opens trials at level 1, and replaces Totality with inheritance. The two are different implementations of the same canon technique; a character uses one or the other, never both.
U07 transcription calls: Nue's boost is 1d8 lightning damage per CE spent (TS103); Round Deer uses the visual supplement's source model, AC 9+PB, HP 148+4×PB (TS108); Agito's "+2 damage die" is read as 2d8 (TS112); the supplement's pages contain no feat material (TS113).
Domain gaps: V.6 prints no activation CE cost, radius, or duration for Chimera Shadow Garden. Campaign conversion applies: the repeated restraint check stands as the sure-hit event, clash via the point system, and the domain otherwise follows the standard lethal-domain rules.
Totality is optional: as printed, a DM may ignore Totality and have all shikigami return on a long rest instead of dying permanently. The two readings produce very different table feels, permanent-but-productive death versus a long-rest timeout, and the choice should be declared at character creation.
Rules priority: the printed V.6 values, the U07 transcription calls, and the clarifications above always control when interpretations conflict. Trial Veteran is a codex-authored fill, marked as such, and may be removed or replaced by the GM without affecting the printed kit.
