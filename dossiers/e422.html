Exact player-facing procedures

Attunement

Tattoo Attunement

To attune to this item, you hold the needle to your skin where you want the tattoo to appear, pressing the needle there throughout the attunement process. When the attunement is complete, the needle turns into the ink that becomes the tattoo, which appears on the skin. If your attunement to the tattoo ends, the tattoo vanishes, and the needle reappears in your space.

Passive

Protection

While you aren't wearing armor, the tattoo grants you an Armor Class depending on the tattoo's rarity, as shown below. You can use a shield and still gain this benefit.

Grade 3: 13 + your Dexterity modifier
Grade 2: 16 + your Dexterity modifier (maximum of +2)
Grade 1: 20

Edge cases and shared systems.

Campaign canon notes

Cursed-tool attunement: you have PB attunement slots (2–6). This tool occupies one slot and requires a one-hour ritual. Attunement ends voluntarily over a short rest, on death, or when you replace it while over capacity. Distance does not break attunement. Cursed-tool attunement is separate from magic-item attunement.
Table function: the source lists this item's rarity as "varies" and gives AC values for Grade 3, Grade 2, and Grade 1 tattoos. It is catalogued at Grade 3 (the first-listed rarity); rarer tattoos grant the higher AC shown.
