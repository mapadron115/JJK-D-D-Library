Star Dictator

The path of your shikigami constitutes the domain.

Power tierTier III · Apex
Combat roleController
Core engineShikigami orbit geometry
DomainDivine Mandate (L18)

Field notes. Subject: a mandate drawn in orbits. Three truths govern it. First: the pseudo-domain is declared, not constructed, it has no barrier and no sure-hit, because it was never built; it was declared, and the shikigami’s orbit is the line the declaration walks. Where the stars complete their circuit, the Dictator’s law holds, geometry instead of walls. Second: the damage is a census. Every orbiting shikigami is one unit of law; kill one and the radius and the wound shrink together, because a mandate is only as wide as its enforcers. Mark this as the technique’s honest accounting, the enemy can read the orbit and know exactly what it costs. Third: at the apex the law stops asking. Army of the Dictator ignores immunity and resistance to slashing, not cruelty but doctrine: what moves in perfect order cannot be defied, and perfect order does not negotiate with armor.

Full progression

Feature map

1Minor Star Shikigami.

Action · Spend CE up to your PB: summon that many minor shikigami in unoccupied spaces within 5 ft · Bonus action: command any of them (otherwise they Dodge) · Action: share senses with one (you are blinded and deafened while sharing) · Max = PB at a time.

The army begins one coin at a time, each cursed energy buys a minor star in an adjacent space, commanded by bonus action, dodging when uncommanded, capped at the proficiency bonus. Starting at 1st level, you can spend cursed energy up to your proficiency bonus as an action. For every cursed energy, you summon a minor shikigami in an unoccupied space within 5 ft of you. As a bonus action, you can command any shikigami created with your technique, otherwise they take the dodge action. As an action, you can share senses with one of your minor shikigami. While sharing senses with your shikigami, you are blinded and deafened. You can only have up to your proficiency bonus in shikigami at a time.

3Pseudo-Domain Constitution.

Bonus action · Command any number of minor shikigami to orbit a point (they Dash in a circle within 5 ft of the center) · Bonus action each turn: expand radius by 5 ft or add more orbiters.

At the third level the orbit becomes jurisdiction: the shikigami dash in a circuit around a point, and everything inside the circle makes a Dexterity save at the end of the user’s turn or takes a die of slashing per orbiting star. Starting at 3rd level, you have learned to form a pseudo-domain that follows the path of your shikigami. As a bonus action, you command any number of your minor shikigami to create your domain. They spend their action taking the dash action to orbit around a point, forming a circle within 5 ft of the center. On each of your turns, you can use your bonus action to increase the radius of the domain by 5 ft or command more shikigami to join the domain orbit.

Passive · Creatures inside the orbit (not you): Dex save at the end of your turn or 1d6 slashing per orbiting shikigami, half on success · Max radius = 5 ft × orbiting shikigami · Killed orbiters shrink range and damage; ends if all stop orbiting.

The maximum range and damage of the domain depends on the number of shikigami making up the domain. A creature within the domain other than you must make a Dexterity saving throw at the end of your turn. On a failed save, a creature takes 1d6 slashing damage per orbiting shikigami or half as much on a success. The maximum radius of the domain is 5 ft x the number of orbiting shikigami. If one of the orbiting shikigami is killed, the range and damage decreases. If all of your shikigami stop orbiting the domain path, the pseudo-domain ends.

The pseudo-domain is a technique zone, not a Domain Expansion: it has no barrier, no sure-hit, no refinement points, and never participates in Domain clashes.

6Major Star Shikigami.

Action · 6 CE · Summon one major star shikigami; bonus action to command (otherwise Dodges) · One at a time; if killed, can't resummon for 1 hr · Pseudo-domain now deals 1d8 slashing per orbiter.

The sixth level promotes one star to officer, a major shikigami for six coins, one at a time, and a full-turn command that lets the minors move freely inside the orbit without breaking it. Starting at 6th level, you have learned how to keep your pseudo-domain active without sacrificing offensive power. Your pseudo-domain now deals 1d8 slashing damage per shikigami creating the domain. You gain the ability to summon your major star shikigami. You can summon your major star shikigami as an action for 6 CE and can command it as a bonus action, otherwise it takes the dodge action. You can only have one major star shikigami active at a time. Once your major star shikigami is killed, you cannot summon it again for 1 hour.

Full-turn action (Action + Bonus + Movement) · Command the major shikigami to form a special pseudo-domain (completes at the start of your next turn): minors move freely inside without breaking range/damage (still scales with the number of minors inside).

As a Full-Turn Action (Action + Bonus Action + Movement), you can command the major star shikigami to begin forming a special pseudo-domain. The pseudo-domain completes at the start of your next turn after the Full-Turn Action. Once completed, the special domain allows your Minor Star Shikigami to move freely within the domain while still maintaining the range and damage of your pseudo-domain. This special domain still follows the same maximum for range and damage, now scaling with the number of Minor Star Shikigami within the domain.

11Army of the Dictator.

Upgrade · Minor shikigami cap: 3× PB · Two major shikigami at once · Pseudo-domain: 1d10 slashing per orbiter (ignores slashing resistance/immunity); radius = 10 ft × orbiting shikigami.

The eleventh level is mobilization, three times the minors, two majors, the damage up a die and ignoring slashing immunity and resistance, the radius doubled per star. At 11th level, you have learned mass summoning. You can now maintain a number of Minor Star Shikigami equal to three times your proficiency bonus and can have two Major Star Shikigami active at once. Additionally, the damage of your pseudo-domain increases to 1d10 slashing damage per shikigami orbiting and ignores immunity and resistance to slashing damage. The radius of your pseudo-domain increases to 10 ft x the number of orbiting shikigami.

18Domain Expansion: Divine Mandate.

Domain (18th level) · Initiative 20: creatures of your choice take 1d12 slashing per star shikigami you have summoned in the domain.

Night falls all at once, a perfect dome of starfield, and the user's shikigami take their places in it as burning constellations. They orbit in slow, stately procession, trailing light, and the whole domain turns with the patient geometry of a clockwork heaven. To stand inside is to understand, bodily, that the sky has a ruler.

The Domain is the mandate made absolute, at initiative twenty, every chosen creature inside takes a d12 of slashing for every star shikigami the user has summoned. Finally at 18th level, you have learned to expand your domain. At initiative 20, all creatures of your choice within your domain take 1d12 slashing damage for every star shikigami you have summoned within your domain.

Innate Technique Feats

Build-defining options.

These are optional character-building feats. You must meet each listed prerequisite before selecting it.

Prerequisite: Must have Minor Star Shikigami

Constellation Network

The shikigami become a nervous system, two coins attune them for an hour, and the user knows the location of everything within thirty feet of any star they can perceive. You have linked your shikigami into a shared-sense web. As an action for 2 cursed energy, you attune to your minor star shikigami for 1 hour: you know the location of every creature within 30 feet of any of your minor shikigami that the shikigami can perceive, and you can speak brief messages through them. This does not blind or deafen you as normal sense-sharing does.

Prerequisite: Must have Major Star Shikigami

Keystone Star

The major learns to hold the center instead of the orbit, the domain maintains its own radius while the minors fight uncommanded, until the keystone falls. Your major star shikigami can serve as the keystone of your pseudo-domain. When you form your pseudo-domain, the major star shikigami can hold its center instead of orbiting: while it holds the center, you do not need to spend your bonus action to maintain the domain's radius, and you can command your minor shikigami normally. If the major star shikigami is killed or leaves the center, the domain ends.

Prerequisites: Must have Army of the Dictator

Judicial Amputation

The mandate grows teeth: fail the orbit’s save by five or more and the Dictator chooses an arm or a leg, the limb goes, and with it two-handed actions or half the victim’s speed. If a creature fails their saving throw against your pseudo-domain by 5 or more, the damage is treated as having the Limb Removing Effect. You choose which of the target's arms or legs is affected: a removed arm prevents two-handed actions; a removed leg halves the creature's speed.

Edge cases and shared systems.

Rules priority: the reviewed rulings, explicit codex clarifications, and the level progression always control when original wording conflicts. The audit dispositions for AT219 are adopted as a V.6 addition with the exact changes noted above.
Recovered: shikigami stat blocks (V.6 PDF pp. 224–225, transcribed verbatim, resolves the audit HOLD). Minor Star Shikigami (Small shikigami, unaligned): AC 10 + your proficiency. HP 3 + your level. Speed 10 ft., fly 60 ft. STR 6 (−2), DEX 14 (+2), CON 14 (+2), INT 3 (−4), WIS 11 (+0), CHA 10 (+0). Senses: passive Perception 9. Languages: understands common. Challenge 1/8 (25 XP). Orbiter of Authority: while orbiting, the shikigami have advantage on saving throws against area-of-effect effects and attacks made against them are at disadvantage. Actions, Bite: melee weapon attack, + Dexterity modifier + your proficiency to hit, reach 5 ft., one target; hit: (1d4 + Dexterity modifier) piercing damage. Major Star Shikigami (level 6): AC 13 + your proficiency. HP 30 + (5 × your level). Speed 30 ft. STR 16 (+3), DEX 14 (+2), CON 18 (+4), INT 5 (−3), WIS 10 (+0), CHA 12 (+1). Saving Throws: Str +6. Skills: Athletics +6. Senses: blindsight 10 ft., passive Perception 10. Languages: understands common. Challenge 6 (2,300 XP). Controlled Path: while orbiting, the shikigami has advantage on saving throws that would move or teleport it. Siege Monster: the shikigami deals double damage to objects and structures. Actions, Bite: melee weapon attack, + Strength modifier + your proficiency to hit, reach 5 ft., one target; hit: (3d8 + Strength modifier) piercing damage.
Special pseudo-domain timing: the pseudo-domain completes at the start of the user's next turn after the Full-Turn Action (zack's final confirmation pending).
Feat availability: the reviewed Innate Technique Feats list (Constellation Network, Keystone Star, Judicial Amputation) is exhaustive; Domineering Shikigami and Tyrant Shikigami, which appear only in the appendix, are not offered.

FEATS

Domineering Shikigami
Prerequisite: Must have Minor Star Shikigami
You have learned that strengthening cursed energy lets you summon
stronger shikigami. Your Minor Star Shikigami gain the following
benefits.
- The Shikigami's Bite attack increases to 1d6. It becomes 1d8 at
  6th level and 1d10 at 11th level.
- The Shikigami gains a +2 bonus to their Dexterity score and
  again at 6th, and 11th level (max of 20).
- When the shikigami is subjected to an effect that allows it to
  make a Dexterity saving throw to take only half damage, it
  instead takes no damage if it succeeds on the saving throw, and
  only half damage if it fails.

Tyrant Shikigami
Prerequisite: Must have Major Star Shikigami
You have learned that strengthening cursed energy lets you summon
stronger shikigami. You can spend 1 additional cursed energy when you
summon your Major Star Shikigami to increase its size category to
Large. This increases to 2 cursed energy to make it Huge at 11th
level and 4 cursed energy to make it Gargantuan at 18th level. For
every size category larger than medium, it gains the following:
- +4 bonus to Strength score
- +1d8 damage to Bite attacks
- +30 hit points

Judicial Amputation
Prerequisites: Must have Army of the Dictator
If a creature fails their saving throw against your pseudo-domain by
5 or more, the damage is treated as having the Limb Removing Effect
and targets one of their arms or legs.
