Features & procedures

Properties & techniques

Exact player-facing procedures

Soul-wrought frame

Your Constitution score is 20 while you wield these gauntlets. This property has no effect on you if your Constitution is already 20 or higher.

Soul catching

After making a successful unarmed strike while wielding these gauntlets, you can target the soul and deal an extra 2d10 necrotic damage to the target. You can then spend 1 cursed energy to regain hit points equal to the necrotic damage dealt. Alternatively, instead of regaining hit points, you can choose to gain advantage on one attack roll, ability check, or saving throw you make before the end of your next turn.

Rules integration

Edge cases and shared systems.

Campaign canon notes

Cursed-tool attunement: you have PB attunement slots (2–6). This tool occupies one slot and requires a one-hour ritual. Attunement ends voluntarily over a short rest, on death, or when you replace it while over capacity. Distance does not break attunement. Cursed-tool attunement is separate from magic-item attunement.
Note: the hit-point regain or the advantage — choose one per strike, not both. Unarmed strikes benefit from these gauntlets while you wear them; you must be wielding them for Soul-wrought frame.
