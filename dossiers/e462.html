Features & procedures

Properties & techniques

Exact player-facing procedures

Soul-wrought frame

The gauntlets first rebuild the wearer to survive their own trade, Constitution set to 20, if it was less. A fragile man cannot afford this economy. Your Constitution score is 20 while you wield these gauntlets. This property has no effect on you if your Constitution is already 20 or higher.

Soul catching

The blow lands on the body; the wound lands on the soul, 2d10 necrotic past the flesh. Then the choice: spend 1 CE to drink the damage back as health, or take advantage on one coming attack, check, or save. One strike, one harvest, never both. After making a successful unarmed strike while wielding these gauntlets, you can target the soul and deal an extra 2d10 necrotic damage to the target. You can then spend 1 cursed energy to regain hit points equal to the necrotic damage dealt. Alternatively, instead of regaining hit points, you can choose to gain advantage on one attack roll, ability check, or saving throw you make before the end of your next turn.

Field notes. Subject: gauntlets that wound the soul and drink what they wound. Three truths. First: they harden the frame, Constitution set to 20 for any wearer below it, the body rebuilt to carry what the gauntlets demand. Second: they aim past the flesh, after a landed unarmed strike, the soul itself takes 2d10 necrotic, the true target being the thing the body was merely carrying. Third, the exchange: for 1 cursed energy, the wearer may drink that damage back as health, or forgo the drink and take advantage on a coming roll instead, one or the other, never both. Consider the economy: the fist that feeds itself never goes hungry, but it feeds only on the wounded soul. The gauntlets are a well that refills from the enemy, and like all wells, they ask you to draw from something that runs dry.

Rules integration

Edge cases and shared systems.

Campaign canon notes

Cursed-tool attunement: you have PB attunement slots (2–6). This tool occupies one slot and requires a one-hour ritual. Attunement ends voluntarily over a short rest, on death, or when you replace it while over capacity. Distance does not break attunement. Cursed-tool attunement is separate from magic-item attunement.
Note: the hit-point regain or the advantage, choose one per strike, not both. Unarmed strikes benefit from these gauntlets while you wear them; you must be wielding them for Soul-wrought frame.
