Field notes. Subject: the drowned man's answer to the water that killed him — a famous swimmer who feared the water and died in it, whose curse now lets another wear his mastery. Three truths govern the garb. First, the curse is the cure: worn attuned, it grants the ability to breathe underwater and a 60-foot swim speed, the drowned man paying forward what he never owned in life. Second, the bond is formal: it requires attunement, the one-hour ritual and one of your scarce slots — water conquered, but only by agreement. Third, terrain is victory decided early: rivers, harbors, and flooded wards stop being hazards and become highways the enemy cannot use. Mark the irony I keep returning to: the fear that killed the swimmer now serves the wearer, because curses keep faith with their origins even when their owners cannot.

Campaign canon notes

Cursed-tool attunement: you have PB attunement slots (2–6). This tool occupies one slot and requires a one-hour ritual. Attunement ends voluntarily over a short rest, on death, or when you replace it while over capacity. Distance does not break attunement. Cursed-tool attunement is separate from magic-item attunement.
