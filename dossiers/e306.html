Exact player-facing procedures

Gain Order when protecting allies, countering attacks, or punishing broken vows.

Spend Order

Spend Order to resist forced movement, environmental distortion, or unstable transformations.

Strip the altered state

At maximum Order, a successful strike can temporarily strip one altered state: possession, transformation, summoned armor, monstrous form, or similar overlay.

Rules integration

Cursed-tool attunement

Campaign canon

Cursed-tool attunement: you have PB attunement slots (2–6). This tool occupies one slot and requires a one-hour ritual. Attunement ends voluntarily over a short rest, on death, or when you replace it while over capacity. Distance does not break attunement. Cursed-tool attunement is separate from magic-item attunement.
Balance & counterplay. Order must be earned through protection and vow-keeping — deny the wielder righteous targets and the engine starves. The stripping strike only fires at maximum Order, so keep the wielder spending before they peak.
