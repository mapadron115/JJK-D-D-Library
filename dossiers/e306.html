Features & procedures

Properties & techniques

Exact player-facing procedures

Relic profile

1d8 slashing · +4 to attack and damage rolls · Order 0–6

Jujutsu origin

This is a jujutsu-era reconstruction built around Egyptian royal and cosmic-order symbolism rather than a claim that one specific museum khopesh possessed supernatural powers. Centuries of ritual association between kingship, Horus, Ma'at, and the defeat of chaos gave the weapon its technique.

Order

Maximum 6 Order. Once per round, gain 1 Order when you: succeed on a save against a Cursed Technique; cause a Cursed Technique attack to miss you; or hit a creature currently transformed, possessed, fused, or wearing technique-created armor.

Stabilize — Reaction, 1 Order

Gain +2 AC against one attack or +2 to one saving throw after rolling but before the result is resolved.

Cut the Distortion — 2 Order

On a hit, deal an additional 4d8 force damage and end one non-Domain buff or transformation effect with a CE cost of 2 or less.

True Form — 6 Order, Action

Make one weapon attack. On a hit, the target makes a Constitution or Charisma save (its choice) against your Relic DC. On a failure, suppress one possession, body transformation, summoned armor, fusion, alternate form, or similar technique overlay for 1 minute. It repeats the save at the end of each of its turns. Special Grade sources make the initial save with advantage.

Binding Vow — Order Must Be Earned

The khopesh begins every combat at 0 Order. The wielder cannot preload it with CE; stability only accumulates by confronting actual disorder.

Designer’s note. This is deliberately a control/dispel weapon instead of another sun-fire sword.

Rules integration

Cursed-tool attunement

Campaign canon

Cursed-tool attunement: you have PB attunement slots (2–6). This tool occupies one slot and requires a one-hour ritual. Attunement ends voluntarily over a short rest, on death, or when you replace it while over capacity. Distance does not break attunement. Cursed-tool attunement is separate from magic-item attunement.
