Undertaker's Bell

Simple bell, no weapon damage. Ringing it is audible to 300 ft. Gain Toll when a summon, shikigami, clone, or cursed spirit enters within 60 ft, max PB.

FormHand bell
RoleSupport / anti-summon

Features & procedures

Properties & techniques

Exact player-facing procedures

Quiet the Procession

action, 1 CE, spend Toll: one summon within 60 ft subtracts one Tool Die from its next attack.

Recall the Name

2 CE, action to activate (invented): Wis save by summoned creature; on failure it cannot move farther from its creator until end next turn.

Funeral Count

spend 3 Toll when a summon drops to 0 to grant an ally 2 Tool Dice temporary HP; it requires no action and resolves immediately on the trigger (invented).

Feature

No effect treats a player character as a summon merely because their ancestry is supernatural.

The fiction. Registry notes describe the Bell as a tool that attends funerals the living haven't scheduled: to its ear, every summon that crosses within sixty feet is already at its own procession, and the Toll it collects is simply what is owed. The prevailing in-fiction understanding holds that the Bell does not hate the summoned — it merely keeps the ledger, and the ledger always balances.

Rules integration

Cursed-tool attunement

Campaign canon

Cursed-tool attunement: you have PB attunement slots (2–6). This Grade 2 tool functions as a mundane tool while unattuned, or with its full properties for one slot and a one-hour ritual at GM discretion. Attunement ends voluntarily over a short rest, on death, or when you replace it while over capacity. Distance does not break attunement. Cursed-tool attunement is separate from magic-item attunement.
Balance & counterplay. Niche by design; devastating into manifestation-heavy encounters but modest elsewhere.
