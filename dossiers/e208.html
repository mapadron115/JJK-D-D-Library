Undertaker's Bell

Simple bell, no weapon damage. Ringing it is audible to 300 ft. Gain Toll when a summon, shikigami, clone, or cursed spirit enters within 60 ft, max PB.

FormHand bell
RoleSupport / anti-summon
EngineToll
Attunement1 hour*
Properties & techniquesExact player-facing procedures

Quiet the Procession

action, 1 CE, spend Toll: one summon within 60 ft subtracts one Tool Die from its next attack.

Recall the Name

2 CE: Wis save by summoned creature; on failure it cannot move farther from its creator until end next turn.

Funeral Count

spend 3 Toll when a summon drops to 0 to grant an ally 2 Tool Dice temporary HP.

Feature

No effect treats a player character as a summon merely because their ancestry is supernatural.

Balance & counterplay. Niche by design; devastating into manifestation-heavy encounters but modest elsewhere.
