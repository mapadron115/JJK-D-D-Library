Undertaker's Bell

Simple bell, no weapon damage. Ringing it is audible to 300 ft. Gain Toll when a summon, shikigami, clone, or cursed spirit enters within 60 ft, max PB.

FormHand bell A simple bell, no weapon damage, audible three hundred feet; a summons no summoning can ignore.
RoleSupport / anti-summon To its ear every summon is already at its own funeral; the tool merely keeps time for the procession.
EngineToll Collected from every summon that enters its hearing, and spent against them, the ledger in motion.
Attunement1 hour* An hour's ritual, the bell must learn whose ledger you are keeping.
Features & procedures

Properties & techniques

Exact player-facing procedures

Quiet the Procession

Action · 1 CE · Spend 1 Toll · one summon within 60 ft: −1 Tool Die from its next attack.

The bell's first right: spend Toll and one summon within sixty feet strikes weaker, one Tool Die off its next attack. The procession slows; the ledger is already open. action, 1 CE, spend Toll: one summon within 60 ft subtracts one Tool Die from its next attack.

Recall the Name

Action · 2 CE · A summoned creature makes a Wis save; on failure it cannot move farther from its creator until end of its next turn.

Two CE, and the bell speaks to the summon's maker: a Wisdom save, and on failure the creature cannot be ordered farther from its creator. Mark this, it does not break the summoning; it shortens the leash. 2 CE, action to activate (invented): Wis save by summoned creature; on failure it cannot move farther from its creator until end next turn.

Funeral Count

No action · Spend 3 Toll when a summon drops to 0 HP · grant an ally 2 Tool Dice temporary HP.

When a summon falls, no action required, spend three Toll and turn its death into succor: temporary vigor for an ally, counted out of the grave. The ledger always balances. spend 3 Toll when a summon drops to 0 to grant an ally 2 Tool Dice temporary HP; it requires no action and resolves immediately on the trigger (invented).

Feature

Limit · No effect treats a player character as a summon merely because their ancestry is supernatural.

One boundary the bell respects: no player counts as a summon for mere ancestry. The bell keeps a ledger, not a bloodline. No effect treats a player character as a summon merely because their ancestry is supernatural.

Field notes. Subject: an accountant of funerals. Three truths govern it. First, the Bell attends funerals the living haven't scheduled: every summon within sixty feet is already, to its ear, in procession, and the Toll it collects is simply what is owed. Second, it does not hate the summoned; it keeps the ledger. It weakens, it shortens the leash, it turns one death into another's shelter, every entry balances. Third, the tool is niche by design: devastating where manifestations crowd the field, modest where they do not. Mark the restraint, it will not count a player as a summon for ancestry alone. Even the dead must be fairly booked.

Rules integration

Cursed-tool attunement

Campaign canon

Cursed-tool attunement: you have PB attunement slots (2–6). This Grade 2 tool functions as a mundane tool while unattuned, or with its full properties for one slot and a one-hour ritual at GM discretion. Attunement ends voluntarily over a short rest, on death, or when you replace it while over capacity. Distance does not break attunement. Cursed-tool attunement is separate from magic-item attunement.
Balance & counterplay. Niche by design; devastating into manifestation-heavy encounters but modest elsewhere.
