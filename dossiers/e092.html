Attunement: spend 1 hour and one cursed-tool attunement slot. A Sacred Treasure supplements your Innate Technique; it does not grant a second one.

Activation: activated properties spend the wielder’s own CE. If a non-sorcerer is explicitly allowed to wield the treasure, its Tool save DC is 8 + proficiency bonus + one chosen mental ability modifier.

Sacred Treasure Release: normally requires level 13 or higher and is usable once per long rest; pay the specific treasure’s listed CE cost. An explicit exception in its individual entry takes precedence.

SACRED TREASURE • King

Spirit Spear Chastiefol

Special Grade

Living Sacred-Tree spear

The most versatile treasure: a telekinetic, self-restoring weapon whose Forms are separate tactical modes.

  1. Form One — Chastiefol: bonus action command; flying spear makes attacks within 60 ft using your cursed-tool attack. On hit: weapon damage + 1 Tool Die.
  2. Form Two — Guardian: action, 2 CE; create a Large guardian for 1 minute. It occupies space, grants half cover, and can intercept one hit/round, reducing damage by 2 Tool Dice.
  3. Form Three — Fossilization: 2 CE on hit; Con save or target's speed is halved and it cannot take reactions until end of next turn. A second failure before then Restrains until end of following turn.
  4. Form Four — Sunflower: action, 4 CE; 90-ft line or 20-ft burst, Dex save, 5 Tool Dice force/radiant.
  5. Form Five — Increase: action, 3 CE; PB targets within 60 ft make Dex saves, taking 2 Tool Dice piercing each; may concentrate all blades on one target for 4 Tool Dice instead.
  6. Form Six — Yggdra Cloth: reaction, 2 CE; +3 AC and resistance to the triggering weapon/technique damage instance.
  7. Form Seven — Luminosity: 1 CE; bright light 60 ft, dispels mundane darkness and grants advantage to detect hidden/invisible creatures until next turn.
  8. Form Eight — Pollen Garden: action, 4 CE; 15-ft-radius sphere for 1 minute. Allies entering/starting there gain 1 Tool Die temp HP; total healing/temp HP per creature is capped at 3 Tool Dice per use.
  9. Form Nine — Increase: Condense: bonus action, 2 CE; convert swarm into one piercing shot, 90 ft, 4 Tool Dice piercing; ignores half cover.
  10. Form Ten — Guardian Beast: action, 4 CE; for 1 minute summon an autonomous beast-shaped projection that can move 40 ft and make one 3 Tool Die force attack when you command it with a bonus action.
  11. Sacred Treasure Release — True Spirit Spear (level 13+, 6 CE, 1/long rest): for 1 minute Tool Die increases one step (d12→d12+2), range doubles, and you may maintain two Forms at once. Switching either Form is free once/turn.
