Field notes. Subject: the spear that serves. I have read the registry's theory and found it the truest here: Chastiefol is devotion that outlived its master, and it still behaves like a retainer, shifting form to whatever the situation demands. Three truths govern it. First, its versatility is loyalty, not cleverness: ten Forms, spear, guardian, sunflower barrage, pollen garden, beast projection, each the weapon asking what is needed. Second, it is telekinetic and self-restoring: the spear fights at range and repairs itself, because a retainer does not pause to be mended. Third, the Release, True Spirit Spear at level 13+: for one minute the Tool Die climbs, range doubles, and two Forms may be held at once. Mark the prevailing theory as fact: the Forms are not modes but moods, the weapon's answer to a promise it was made for. It never learned how to stop protecting.
