Wyrdscript

Wyrdscript — Elder Futhark

A runecarver technique using all 24 Elder Futhark runes, anomalous Wyrd, Bindrunes, divination, Living Script, and Hall of the Norns.

Cursed TechniqueTier IV · Special Grade
Elder Futhark-inspired original technique
Step One

Build the Runecarver

Wyrdscript fits characters who enjoy tactical decision-making, broad utility, and resource management. You know more answers than you can afford to use, so good play means anticipating the encounter and choosing the cheapest rune that solves the problem.

Primary Role

Controller / Support. You manipulate movement, defenses, CE costs, conditions, healing, and territory while still carrying several damage options.

Best Ability Focus

Prioritize the ability score your class uses for cursed energy. Your Rune save DC, CE attack, healing modifiers, and several defensive effects depend on it.

Combat Rhythm

Open with a persistent inscription, react with defensive runes, then spend expensive Tier III–IV runes only when they materially alter the encounter.

Level 1 choice matters. You begin with only six runes, and they must be Tier I or Tier II. At level 3 the full alphabet awakens, including the Blank Rune.

Core Chassis

Runic Inscription Rules

Rune Save DC

8 + proficiency bonus + cursed-energy ability modifier

Whenever a rune calls for a saving throw, use your normal cursed-energy save DC.

Rune Attack

proficiency bonus + cursed-energy ability modifier

If a rune ever requires a CE attack, use your normal cursed-energy attack modifier.

Inscription

Unless a rune says otherwise, inscribing it is an action and requires a valid target within 5 feet. At level 11, your normal inscription range becomes 30 feet.

Persistent Rune Limit

You can maintain a number of persistent runes equal to your proficiency bonus. Immediate effects do not count. At level 11, the limit becomes PB + CE modifier.

Level 6

Bindrunes

Combine two different runes into one inscription. Pay both runes’ CE costs plus 1 additional CE. Both effects share the same inscription and trigger together when possible. You may maintain one Bindrune at level 6 and two at level 11.

Raidho + Tiwaz

Challenge Road

Move an ally directly toward its marked opponent, then let Tiwaz reward the engagement.

Isa + Hagalaz

Winter Prison

A hail burst becomes much harder to escape when survivors are immediately slowed or restrained.

Gebo + Berkano

Mercy Gift

Use Gebo’s support utility alongside Berkano to make healing easier to reposition through the party.

Algiz + Eihwaz

Yew-Ward

Layer AC protection with damage reduction for a powerful reaction-based defensive inscription.

Nauthiz + Ansuz

Edict of Need

Tax cursed-energy usage while forbidding a specific behavior, forcing the target into an ugly tactical choice.

Othala + Sowilo

Sunlit Sanctuary

Establish defensible territory and punish enemies who try to push through the claimed ground.

Ætt-Binder interaction: if both component runes are in the same ætt, the feat removes the +1 CE surcharge. You still pay both runes’ normal costs.
Level 9 · Noncombat Feature

Rune Casting: Reading of the Three

Spend 10 minutes and 2 CE casting three rune-stones concerning one specific person, location, plan, journey, or objective.

What Was

The GM reveals one truthful and relevant influence from the subject’s past.

What Is

The GM reveals one truthful circumstance that currently matters to the subject.

What May Be

The GM reveals one likely consequence if the present course continues. This is not predestination and can be changed by later choices.

Mechanical reward: after the reading, gain one Foretold Die (d6). Before your next long rest, while acting directly on information revealed by the reading, add or subtract it from one ability check, attack roll, or saving throw.
Technique Feats

Specialization Options

Fast Scribe

Once per turn, you may inscribe a Tier I rune as a bonus action instead of an action.

Blood-Carved Staves

Once per short rest when you invoke a rune, reduce its CE cost by up to your proficiency bonus. You take unavoidable damage equal to twice the CE reduced.

Ætt-Binder

When both runes in a Bindrune belong to the same ætt, remove the normal +1 CE Bindrune surcharge. Wyrd belongs to no ætt and can never benefit from this feat.

Runeseer

Reading of the Three takes 1 minute instead of 10. Its Foretold Die becomes your Rune Die. Once per long rest, you may ask one simple yes/no question concerning the next 24 hours; the GM answers truthfully according to the most likely course of events at the time of the reading.

Level 18

Domain Expansion

Hall of the Norns:
Wyrd Written Before Birth

The Domain manifests as a lightless hall beneath immense roots, every surface carved with burning runes. Your normal rune inscriptions cost 2 less CE while the Domain persists, to a minimum cost of 1 CE.

Sure-Hit — Decree of Fate

At initiative count 20, choose one hostile creature in the Domain and impose one Fate until the next initiative count 20. The creature knows the Fate and its consequence. The Domain does not force obedience; it punishes refusal.

Fate of Stillness

The target may move no more than 10 feet. If it attempts to exceed that limit, its movement immediately ends and it takes 4 Rune Dice force damage.

Fate of Silence

The first cursed technique or CE ability it activates deals 4 Rune Dice psychic damage to it and increases that ability’s CE cost by your proficiency bonus.

Fate of Blood

If the target ends its turn without making an attack or directly attempting to harm another creature, it takes 4 Rune Dice necrotic damage.

Playing Wyrdscript Well

Character-Building Advice

Do not overpay.

If Raidho solves the problem for 2 CE, do not spend 6 CE on a Sovereign Stave simply because it is stronger. Wyrdscript rewards efficient answers.

Use persistent runes before crisis.

Uruz, Tiwaz, Othala, and other persistent effects are strongest when established before the party desperately needs them.

Think in pairs after level 6.

Bindrunes are where the technique becomes truly expressive. Look for combinations where one rune fixes the other rune’s weakness.

You are a toolbox, not every specialist.

Berkano should not out-heal a dedicated healer, Hagalaz should not out-blast a dedicated artillery CT, and Algiz should not out-tank a dedicated defender. Your advantage is having all three.
