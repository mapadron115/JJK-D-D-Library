Features & procedures

Properties & techniques

Exact player-facing procedures

Attack

Shield Bash

Attack action · Make one attack roll using the shield against a target within 5 ft · Proficiency + Strength to hit · On hit: 1d6 piercing + Strength modifier.

When you take the Attack action, you can make one of the attack rolls using the shield against a target within 5 feet of yourself. Apply your proficiency bonus and Strength modifier to the attack roll. On a hit, the shield deals 1d6 piercing damage plus your Strength modifier.

Action

Spike Salvo

Action · 3 CE · Spikes erupt in a 30-ft cone · Each creature: DC 13 Dexterity save · 3d6 piercing on failure, half on success.

As an action for 3 cursed energy, you can cause spikes to erupt from the shield in a 30-foot cone. Each creature in the cone makes a DC 13 Dexterity saving throw, taking 3d6 piercing damage on a failed save or half as much on a successful one.

Observer’s account

Field notes. Subject: a wall with teeth. The shield bashes in close, 1d6 piercing as one attack of the Attack action, and for 3 CE it erupts a 30-foot cone of spikes, DC 13 Dexterity or 3d6 piercing. Two truths. First, defense that attacks twice: the shield holds the line by day and spends its spikes by the action, so the bearer is never choosing between wall and weapon. Second, the spikes are ammunition, not infinity: 3 CE a salvo, and the bash replaces an attack rather than granting one, the economy is honest. Note the discipline: both properties demand the shield be held. Hence the maxim: the best fortress is the one that bites back.
