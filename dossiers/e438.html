Cursed Tool

Spiked Shield

A spike-adorned cursed shield that bashes in melee and erupts spikes in a cone.

FormArmor (Shield)
GradeGrade 3
AttunementNot required
Features & procedures

Properties & techniques

Exact player-facing procedures

Attack

Shield Bash

When you take the Attack action, you can make one of the attack rolls using the shield against a target within 5 feet of yourself. Apply your proficiency bonus and Strength modifier to the attack roll. On a hit, the shield deals 1d6 piercing damage plus your Strength modifier.

Action

Spike Salvo

As an action for 3 cursed energy, you can cause spikes to erupt from the shield in a 30-foot cone. Each creature in the cone makes a DC 13 Dexterity saving throw, taking 3d6 piercing damage on a failed save or half as much on a successful one.

Campaign canon notes

Bash while holding: both properties require you to be holding the shield — Shield Bash replaces one attack of your Attack action, it does not grant an extra attack.
