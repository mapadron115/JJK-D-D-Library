Vairocana

Harmony and Chaos, the infinite universe that makes up all of creation.

TierTier V · Calamity
RoleBlaster / Controller
FranchiseCreeps and Curses

Field notes. Subject: the man who asks the universe to misbehave in his favor. Three truths govern Vairocana. First, it is two techniques wearing one name: Harmony finds the world, locating creatures across miles, speaking to beasts and men, and Chaos unmakes it, through Chaos Bolt and a d100 table of destabilization where X is the measure of the user’s own strength. Second, the chaos is leashed: Balance lets the user choose Order, steering where the storm falls, or Equilibrium, exempting chosen souls from its touch; the storm is never indiscriminate unless he wills it. Third, the pinnacle is authorship: the Reversal writes new laws of physics into a thirty-foot radius, and the Domain makes one chosen table-effect the law of everything inside it. Consider: he does not fight the world. He drafts it, strikes the draft, and lets reality answer.

Pinnacle expression

Reversal and Domain.

Capstone material

Reversal: Cosmic Genesis

Action · 4 CE · Impose a new law of physics in a 30-ft radius for 2× CE modifier rounds (minimum 2); while active you cannot use your other Innate Technique features · Entropy (6th): at the start of your turn, 1 CE for +10 ft range (max 120 ft), an extra minor rule (max 3), or +1 round.

Through reverse cursed technique you create your own logic and laws of physics instead of breaking this world's. As an action for 4 CE, force a new rule of reality into the world in a 30-ft radius centered on you. The rule affects all creatures, objects, and terrain in range for twice your CE modifier rounds (minimum 2 rounds). The rule must alter a fundamental interaction of reality; the DM has final say on validity. While using Cosmic Genesis, you cannot use your other Innate Technique features.

Examples: Rule of Absolute Gravity, gravity cannot be ignored; creatures cannot fly or teleport, jumping distance halved. Rule of Perfect Motion, objects in motion remain in motion forever; creatures ignore difficult terrain and cannot have speed reduced. Rule of Universal Sight, eyes perceive the entire electromagnetic spectrum; invisibility grants no benefit, hidden creatures are automatically revealed. Rule of Damage Inversion, fire always heals: fire damage heals for half the damage roll and deals no damage.

Entropy (6th level): at the start of your turn while Cosmic Genesis is active, spend 1 CE to gain one benefit, Expansion: range +10 ft (max 120 ft); Addition: add a minor rule at half effectiveness (max 3 minor rules); Duration: duration +1 round.

Domain Expansion: Mandala of Mutual Paradox

Full turn · 20 CE · Clash 1000 · 3-round burnout · 3 min · At initiative 20, choose one Chaos Table effect of 90 or below as your guaranteed hit vs. creatures of your choice · Inside, every use of Chaos becomes the guaranteed hit across the whole domain.

A mandala ignites across the floor, rings of gold and impossible color turning slowly against each other, and at its heart sits a stillness that contradicts everything around it. The air tastes of incense and ozone, and the pattern keeps resolving into sense and then refusing it, the way a koan does. It is beautiful the way a trap is beautiful.

18th level. Activation: full turn, 20 CE (D008 baseline for a normal Domain; source is silent). Clash: 1000 points; burnout 3 rounds; duration 3 min (Tier V mapping).

At initiative count 20, choose a Chaos Table effect of 90 or below; it becomes your guaranteed hit and can affect all creatures of your choice within the domain, depending on the effect. While within the domain, every use of Chaos functions as the guaranteed hit, the effect's range encompasses the entirety of the domain and you pick specific targets.

Rules integration

Edge cases and shared systems.

Campaign canon notes

Cursed Energy baseline: CE pool is based on class; CE ability is Charisma or Wisdom (player's choice). CE save DC = 8 + CE ability modifier + proficiency bonus. Regain all spent CE on a long rest.
X = CE modifier everywhere the Chaos Table references X.
Save DC for Chaos effects: the source states no DC for effects that allow saves; use your CE save DC.
Random effects and the user: a random Chaos Table effect can also affect you unless the effect originates from you (source).
Domain activation defaults: the source gives no action or CE cost for the Domain; rendered as full turn + 20 CE per the D008 normal-Domain baseline, with Tier V clash stats (1000 / 3-round burnout / 3 min). The initiative-20 guaranteed-hit selection is from the source.
Cosmic Genesis locks out the rest: while Cosmic Genesis is active you cannot use any other Innate Technique features, Harmony, Chaos, Chaos Amplification, and the Domain's Chaos guaranteed hits are all offline.
Reversal vs. Maximum: Vairocana's pinnacle is written as a Reversal (Cosmic Genesis), not a Maximum Technique; it occupies the technique's pinnacle slot.
