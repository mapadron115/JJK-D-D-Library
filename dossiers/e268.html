Core law

At-the-table character reference

Recovered printed values & campaign clarifications

Thread counterplay:

The only shears in the archive: soul-deep damage cuts the thread, and the truly cursed-energyless cannot be tied at all. an effect that would damage the soul is capable of cutting your thread and removing your technique’s effects. Additionally, you cannot manipulate the fate of a creature that is completely lacking cursed energy (such as via Heavenly Restriction).

Field notes. Subject: the weaver who ties knots the world must obey. Three truths govern her. First, the thread is imposed, never negotiated: the Fates land without an initial save, and only an effect that wounds the soul itself can cut what she has tied. Second, the loom is layered: seven Fates from shared wounds to mirrored rolls, each fiercer than the last, and the cruelest demand her touch, she must lay hands on what she binds. Third, time only tightens the weave: at 11th level the knots become permanent, carried past death itself, and the Maximum is not a knot but a pair of shears, three failed death saves, and the thread is cut with the life. Mark this: the Road of Karma never misses its Destined Foe, because a thread, once tied, does not permit the far end to be elsewhere.

Core law

Completed rules

Simultaneous Fates:

The loom's accounting, the proficiency cap counts the first knot as well as the rest. PB total simultaneous Fates per activation, including the first, rather than PB additional Fates. An empty current CE pool is not Heavenly Restriction; immunity applies only to creatures truly lacking CE. No initial save is added.

Touch timing:

A knot tied to a touch must be touched now; an old handshake is not a current binding. touch both targets during this activation; previously touching someone days ago is insufficient. Where a Fate requires Touch, all linked targets must meet it.

Magnetic escape:

Even the tightest tangle grants the bound a Strength save to tear free at the turn's start. Magnetic gives a Strength save against the CE DC at the start of the affected creature’s turn to escape the tangle; otherwise prone and unable to separate beyond 5 ft until the thread ends or is cut. This escape timing and restraint wording are codex clarifications.

Shared Strengths scope:

The thread shares one chosen strength, not the whole armory. shares one existing trait chosen on activation, not every defense.

Mirrored scope:

The mirror reflects d20s, never damage, and a reflection cannot reflect itself. applies to d20 tests, not damage dice; a replaced roll cannot recursively trigger Mirrored, and a replacement 20 is not a naturally rolled Black Flash confirmation.

Linked Suffering network:

One wound, divided by law: the struck keeps half the floor, and the remainder is portioned to the near. one shared damage packet. Resolve the original hit and its original target’s defenses first. Original target keeps floor(damage/2); divide the remainder evenly among all other linked creatures currently within 30 ft of that target, assigning remainder points in a fixed order chosen on activation. No eligible recipient means the original target takes the whole packet. Transferred damage retains its type but is not reduced a second time, and cannot trigger another Linked Suffering transfer. It still counts as damage for concentration/Stability and other non-transfer reactions.

Information access:

The Cursed Eyes read one category of the thread at a time, the weave shows what it shows, nothing more. one category per Cursed Eyes activation, with AC or one ability modifier as a choice; no opposition save added. Connections outside sight are not automatically identified by Cursed Eyes.

Duration CE:

Knot-work and knot-keeping are separate budgets; record each before the Maximum is counted. duration CE is separate from additional-Fate CE; record each allocation before computing eligible Maximum Output.

Permanent entanglement:

Permanent knots are narrative succession, not an army of borrowed Six Eyes, the proficiency cap holds even here. creates the described narrative succession, not permanent combat Fates, copied current character sheets or an extra Six Eyes owner on demand. Maintain PB total permanently linked creatures including the caster when included. The assimilation procedure remains a campaign ritual requiring a defined subject and consequences before use.

Full progression

Feature map

1Fate Manipulation.

Action · 2 CE (+1 CE per extra Fate, up to PB) · Bind two creatures within 30 ft with an invisible red thread for 1 min · Choose a Fate affecting both while within 30 ft of each other: Bonds (+2 AC); Promised Time (adv/dis on attacks vs each other); Mirrored (a 20/1 on one's turn flips the other's next roll); Magnetic (start turn within 30 ft: pulled 10 ft closer; within 5 ft: tangled and prone, Str save to pull away — requires Touch) · Touch Fates: touch both targets (not necessarily at once) · Effects that damage the soul can cut the thread; creatures with no cursed energy can't be bound.

The first knots: Bonds, Promised Time, Mirrored, Magnetic, four ways to tie two lives together for a minute. As an action for 2 cursed energy, you bind two creatures within 30 ft of you with an invisible red thread for 1 minute. If a Fate requires Touch, you must touch both targets, but not necessarily at the same time. You choose a Fate, +1 Fate per additional CE, up to your proficiency bonus, that affects both while within 30 ft of each other.

Bonds. The targets gain a +2 bonus to AC. (No requirement)

Promised Time. The creatures both have either advantage or disadvantage on attacks against each other. (No requirement)

Mirrored. If one target rolls a 20 or 1 on their turn, the other’s next roll will be the opposite. (No requirement)

Magnetic. If targets start their turn within 30 ft of each other, they are pulled 10 ft closer. If this brings them within 5 ft, they become tangled and prone (Str save to pull away). (Requires Touch)

Shared Strengths and Linked Suffering unlock at 6th level; Destined Death unlocks at 11th level. An effect that would damage the soul is capable of cutting your thread and removing your technique’s effects. Additionally, you cannot manipulate the fate of a creature that is completely lacking cursed energy (such as via Heavenly Restriction).

3Cursed Eyes of Fate.

Bonus action · 1 CE · See the red threads connecting living creatures within sight · OR read one target's thread to learn one: resistances/immunities/vulnerabilities, CR or Level, remaining HP, AC or an ability modifier, current emotions.

The weaver learns to see the threads themselves, and to read a single life's ledger in one glance. bonus action, 1 CE: see the red threads connecting living creatures and who they connect to within sight; or read one target’s personal thread to learn one listed category (resistances/immunities/vulnerabilities, CR or Level, remaining HP, AC or an ability modifier, current emotions).

6Strong Destiny.

Upgrade · Extend a Fate's duration by 1 min per extra CE (1 hr base at 11th, +1 hr per CE) · Shared Strengths: both targets share one of a target's damage resistances/immunities/vulnerabilities or condition immunities · Linked Suffering (requires Touch): linked targets split damage taken half and half.

The knots hold longer, and two crueler Fates join the loom: shared strengths, shared suffering. When you apply a fate to creatures, you can increase the duration by 1 minute per additional cursed energy spent. At 11th level, your Fate Manipulation now lasts for 1 hour and you can increase the duration by 1 hour per additional cursed energy spent.

Shared Strengths. Choose a damage resistance, immunity, vulnerability or condition immunity of a target. Both targets share that strength/weakness.

Linked Suffering. When one of the linked targets takes damage, they take half as much damage and the linked creature takes the other half. (Requires Touch)

11Karmic Entanglement.

Downtime · 8 uninterrupted hours: permanently intertwine the fates of two or more creatures (destined to meet; can carry an Inherited Technique or unique constitution past death) · Max PB creatures at once, including you · You can't die of old age (still age; assimilate every 500 years or evolve) · Destined Death (Touch Fate): both targets have adv or dis on saves against each other.

Eight hours of uninterrupted work, and the knot outlives the knotter, fates entwined past death, and the weaver herself set apart from aging. Over an uninterrupted period of 8 hours, you permanently intertwine the fates of two or more creatures together. The creatures become destined to meet by fate. You can attach this to an incredibly unique part of one of the creatures, such as an Inherited Technique, the Six Eyes, or a unique Constitution like Star Plasma Vessels to cause it to be passed down even after the targets’ deaths. You can only have a number of creatures with permanently intertwined fate equal to your proficiency bonus at once (including yourself). As a side effect of your technique, your fate has become set. You cannot die of old age, but still grow older. Every 500 years you must assimilate with a genetically similar human to reset your body and appearance. Otherwise you begin to evolve beyond humanity, becoming more akin to a Cursed Spirit.

Destined Death. The targets both have either advantage or disadvantage on saves against each other. (Requires Touch)

18Road of Karma.

Domain (18th level) · Initiative 20: designate a Destined Foe inside — all your attacks against them carry the guaranteed hit · The whole domain counts as in range; Touch requirements waived.

The Domain is the weave made inescapable: at initiative 20 the Destined Foe is named, and every strike against them carries the guaranteed hit. Domain Expansion. At initiative 20, designate a Destined Foe within the Domain; all your attacks against them carry the guaranteed hit. The entire Domain counts as in range and Touch requirements are waived.

Pinnacle expression

Maximum, Domain, or equivalent.

The shears come out, fifteen cursed energy to snip a single thread, and death saves decide whether the life follows. Maximum, Shears of Three Fates: 11: as an action for 15 cursed energy, form a scissor with your fingers and snip a thread of fate of a creature you can see within 5 ft. At the start of each of the target’s turns, they must make a Death save: this roll cannot have advantage or disadvantage, and the target gains 1 level of exhaustion for every failed death save. If the target fails 3 death saves, they fall to 0 hit points and die, unable to be revived by cursed techniques. If the target succeeds 3 death saves or you are reduced to 0 hit points or die, the technique ends and the target becomes immune to this technique for 3 days. Death tests are d20 DC 10, one success or failure per test including natural 1/20, with no advantage/disadvantage; each failed test gives one actual exhaustion level; HP healing does not reset the successes/failures.

Road of Karma, the whole Domain counts as in range, Touch is waived, and the Destined Foe is never missed. Domain Expansion, Road of Karma: 18: at initiative 20, designate one foe as your Destined Foe and immediately deliver one legal weapon or unarmed strike as its autonomous sure-hit, including that strike’s normal damage but no free activation of another paid feature. Ordinary turn attacks still roll. All-range and Touch waiver remain Domain laws; anti-domain protection blocks the designation’s sure-hit damage and the hostile Domain-only Touch waiver.

Innate Technique Feats

Build-defining options.

These are optional character-building feats. You must meet each listed prerequisite before selecting it.

Prerequisite: Must have Fate Manipulation

Destined One

The weaver learns to tug the threads she has tied, a bonus-action pull, or a willing swap of places. You have learned to tug the threads you tie. As a bonus action for 1 CE, you can pull the red thread binding a creature affected by one of your Fates: the creature must succeed on a Strength saving throw or be pulled up to 15 ft toward the creature it is bound to. If both bound creatures are willing, no save is required and you can swap their positions instead.

Prerequisite: Must have Cursed Eyes of Fate

Web of Fate

The loom widens: one casting now knots a crowd, though every touch-gated Fate still demands every hand. When you cast a Fate onto targets, you can instead target a number of creatures equal to 1 + your proficiency bonus within range. If the Fate requires Touch, you must touch all targets of the technique.

Prerequisite: Must have Road of Karma

Maximum: Shears of Three Fates

The feat-form of the shears, the same slow death, granted to those who have walked the Road. As an action for 15 cursed energy, you form a scissor with your fingers and snip one of the threads of fate of a creature you can see within 5 ft of you. At the start of each of the target’s turns, they must make a Death save. This roll cannot have advantage or disadvantage and the target gains 1 level of exhaustion for every failed death save. If the target fails 3 death saves, they fall to 0 hit points and die. They are unable to be revived by cursed techniques. If the target succeeds 3 death saves or you are reduced to 0 hit points or die, the technique ends and the target becomes immune to this technique for 3 days.

Rules integration

Edge cases and shared systems.

Rules priority: the reviewed rulings, explicit codex clarifications, and the level progression always control when original wording conflicts. Legacy Domain auto-hit language, action terminology, event timing, missing saves, and older scaling never override revised player rules.
Why Tier IV: seven distinct control Fates, a lethal Maximum with its own death-test convention, and a Domain that never misses its Destined Foe—plus permanent narrative entanglement—place Fate at Special Grade without reaching calamity scale.
Road of Karma sure-hit: designated at initiative 20 and delivered as one autonomous strike (modern system timing); the designation is the sure-hit event, while ordinary turn attacks still roll.
No initial save: Fates apply without an initial saving throw; their counterplay is soul damage to the thread, range, and Touch.

Cursed Eyes of Fate
 At 3rd level, you learn that fate is not simple. It is what makes up
all connections between people and everything else. As a bonus action
for 1 CE, when you look at a creature you can focus until you see the
red threads of fate that connect all living creatures. As part of
this bonus action, you can tell who the threads connect to if they
are within sight, as well as the type of connection. This can range
from familial connections, admiration, or feelings of love.
 Alternatively, you can instead read the target’s personal thread of
fate to learn one of the following:
   - The target’s damage resistances, immunities, or vulnerabilities.
   - The target’s CR or Level.
   - The target’s remaining hit points.
   - The target’s AC or an Ability modifier.
   - The target’s current emotions.

Strong Destiny
 At 6th level, you have learned to strengthen your threads of fate
with cursed energy. When you apply a fate to creatures, you can
increase the duration by 1 minute per additional cursed energy spent.
At 11th level, your Fate Manipulation now lasts for 1 hour and you
can increase the duration by 1 hour per additional cursed energy spent.

Karmic Entanglement
 At 11th level, you have learned to permanently entangle the people
in the web of fate. Over an uninterrupted period of 8 hours, you
permanently intertwine the fates of two or more creatures together.
The creatures become destined to meet by fate. You can attach this to
an incredibly unique part of one of the creatures, such as an
Inherited Technique, the Six Eyes, or a unique Constitution like Star
Plasma Vessels to cause it to be passed down even after the targets’
deaths. You can only have a number of creatures with permanently
intertwined fate equal to your proficiency bonus at once (including
yourself).

 As a side effect of your technique, your fate has become set. You
cannot die of old age, but still grow older. Every 500 years you must
assimilate with a genetically similar human to reset your body and
appearance. Otherwise you begin to evolve beyond humanity, becoming
more akin to a Cursed Spirit.

Domain Expansion: Road of Karma

A road unrolls underfoot, paved not with stone but with woven threads, every color, every life, crossing and knotting and running on. Above, the sky is a loom, and the threads rise from every person present to join the weave, trembling with the weight of everything they have ever done. The road does not fork. It only continues, and it remembers every step.

 Finally at 18th level, you have learned to expand your domain. At
initiative 20, you select a creature within your domain as your
Destined Foe. While your Destined Foe is within your domain, all your
attacks against them are imbued with your guaranteed hit. You can
switch your Destined Foe to another creature within your domain at
initiative 20. Additionally, your innate technique features count the
entirety of your domain as being within range and you can ignore the
Touch Requirement of Fates.

Destined One
Prerequisite: Must have Fate Manipulation
 You or the DM chooses one creature you touch as your Destined One.
You can always tell where your Destined One is as the thread of Fate
always guides your hand. While within 30 ft of your Destined One, you
have advantage on saving throws against being charmed or frightened.
Additionally, once per round you can add your proficiency bonus to
your AC against an attack roll made against you or to a saving throw
you make, potentially changing the outcome. You cannot change the
creature chosen as your Destined One unless they die.
