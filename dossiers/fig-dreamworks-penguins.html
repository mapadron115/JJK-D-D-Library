Feature map

Level-by-level features, as the figure refined them.

L1

One Squad, One Body

Passive · Plans pool (maximum 2 × PB).

You are four penguins and a single stat block. You occupy one space, move once, act once, and track one pool of hit points and CE — the squad never splits up, because the squad never needs to. You carry a pool of Plans (maximum 2 × PB; it begins at 0). At the start of each of your turns, if at least one ally is conscious within 30 ft of you, gain 1 Plan — an audience sharpens the operation. You may spend Plans on this technique's features; unspent Plans fade when combat ends. Operative's Assist: as a bonus action, you may spend 1 Plan to take the Help action — the squad moves as one, and one always has your back.

L3

Skipper's Orders

Bonus action · 1 Plan.

Skipper points a flipper and the field reorganizes. Choose up to PB allies within 60 ft of you: each may use its reaction to move up to half its speed without provoking opportunity attacks. Alternatively, choose one ally within 60 ft: it gains one additional action on its next turn, which it may use only to Dash, Disengage, or Use an Object. Just smile and wave, boys. Smile and wave.

L6

Kowalski's Analysis

Action · 2 CE + 1 Plan.

Kowalski observes, calculates, and announces the weak point. Choose a creature you can see within 60 ft: it makes an INT save against your CE save DC. On a failure it is Analyzed for 1 minute: attacks made against it by you and your allies deal an extra 1d6 damage, and you learn one of its damage resistances, immunities, or vulnerabilities, if it has any. On a success, nothing happens — but Kowalski takes notes.

L11

Rico's Arsenal

Action · 3 CE.

Rico hacks up exactly what the moment needs. Produce one nonmagical, mundane item worth no more than 50 gp — it appears in your space and vanishes after 1 hour. Alternatively, Rico produces an improvised explosive: make a ranged attack (range 20/60) against one creature; on a hit it takes 3d6 bludgeoning damage. 'Kaboom' is a complete sentence.

L15

Private's Heart

Reaction · 2 Plans · once per combat.

The rookie's clutch save. When an ally within 30 ft of you drops to 0 hit points or fails a death saving throw, Private intervenes before the outcome resolves: the ally drops to 1 hit point instead, or the failed save becomes a success, and the ally gains temporary hit points equal to your INT modifier + your PB. You may use this feature once per combat. Everyone underestimates the cute one. That is the plan.

L18

Black-Ops Coordination

Passive.

The squad operates at full synchronization. Your Plans maximum becomes 3 × PB. Skipper's Orders costs no Plan when it targets a single ally. Rico's Arsenal may now produce items worth up to 250 gp, and produced items last 8 hours instead of 1.

Pinnacle expression

Maximum, Domain, or equivalent.

Capstone material.

Maximum Technique

Operation: Smile and Wave

Action · 10 CE · 2 Plans.

The whole squad executes at once. Every ally within 60 ft of you may move up to half its speed without provoking opportunity attacks. Every hostile creature within 60 ft of you that you can see must make an INT save against your CE save DC: on a failure it is Analyzed as though by Kowalski's Analysis. Every hostile creature within 20 ft of you takes 6d6 bludgeoning damage (DEX save against your CE save DC halves) as Rico detonates. Every ally within 30 ft of you gains temporary hit points equal to 2d6 + your INT modifier as Private checks on everyone. Cute and cuddly, boys. Cute and cuddly.

Level 20 · Capstone

The Squad Never Breaks

Passive · 1/long rest nova · bonus action · 1 minute.

The perfect operation. Your Plans no longer fade when combat ends. Once per long rest, as a bonus action, declare the operation live for 1 minute: Skipper's Orders costs no Plans; Kowalski's Analysis targets every hostile creature you can see within 60 ft at once; and Private's Heart costs no Plans and is no longer limited to once per combat. When the minute ends, your Plans empty — even the squad needs a fish break.

Innate Technique Feats

Build-defining options.

These are optional character-building feats. You must meet each listed prerequisite before selecting it.

Prerequisite: Kowalski's Analysis

Kowalski's Contingency

A failed plan is still data. When a creature succeeds on its save against Kowalski's Analysis, you gain 1 Plan and learn one of its damage immunities, resistances, or vulnerabilities anyway — Kowalski was watching the dodge, not the target. Options B through Z are already drafted.

Prerequisite: Rico's Arsenal

Rico's Deep Storage

Rico pre-loads before the mission. During a short rest, you may use Rico's Arsenal up to PB times without spending CE or an action, producing items in advance; items produced this way last until your next long rest. Rico's stomach is bigger on the inside. Do not ask how the squad knows this.

Prerequisite: Private's Heart

Private's Secret Weapon

Weaponized adorableness. As an action, spend 1 Plan: Private performs the cute routine for one creature within 30 ft that can see him. Until the end of your next turn, that creature has disadvantage on attack rolls against creatures other than you — it cannot bring itself to look away. 'Aww' is a tactical condition.

Prerequisite: Skipper's Orders

Need-to-Know Basis

Skipper briefs the team before wheels-up. Skipper's Orders and Private's Heart now reach 120 ft, and Skipper's Orders affects allies you cannot see, provided they can hear you — the squad rehearsed this in the dark.
