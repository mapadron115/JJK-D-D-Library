Core law

Power first. Survival second.

Anime & Manga always contains its entire stockpile. Character progression represents the vessel learning to distribute, reinforce, combine, and survive that power—not the stockpile politely waiting for higher levels.

Weapon users are valid. Anime & Manga does not require punching. A sword, staff, spear, kick, elbow, or similar physical carrier can deliver the stockpile. Mundane weapons used at 80%+ may break at the GM's discretion after the hit; cursed tools are built to survive cursed output.

Always available

Any Percentage

The stockpile’s first law: choose any output from 5% to 100% in 5% increments whenever a feature asks. Safe Output decides only whether it hurts you, the power itself never refuses. You may choose any output from 5% to 100% in 5% increments whenever a Anime & Manga feature asks for output. Your Safe Output only determines whether doing so hurts you.

Physical carrier

Turn a hit into a Smash: pay the Output Band’s CE cost, add the Stockpile Dice, and a direct Smash can critically hit and can trigger Black Flash. This is the stockpile’s native language: the punch the dead spent generations perfecting. Field notes. Subject: a will that outlived every body it inhabited. The prevailing theory holds that each bearer’s resolve, imprinted at death, refused to dissolve back into ordinary cursed energy and instead pooled, generation after generation, into a single stockpile. The vestiges are not ghosts, the registry notes caution; they are what remains when a person’s last thought was too stubborn to die. Three truths govern the embers. First, the power is inherited, not earned: no one inherits the technique, they inherit everyone else’s refusal to let go. Second, the stockpile is dangerous to the bearer: Safe Output decides whether the chosen percentage hurts you, recoil cannot be resisted or redirected, and extreme overdraw breaks the limb that carried the attack. Third, the dead counsel: the past bearers persist as imprints, Blackwhip, Danger Sense, Smokescreen, Float, each a life’s work, loaned to the living. Hence the maxim: the strongest fist is the one nine other people already threw.

Full progression

Level 1–20.

The default awakening order mirrors the technique's narrative escalation, but the GM may swap Blackwhip, Danger Sense, Smokescreen, Float, and Kinetic Surge between their listed awakening milestones.

01Inherited Stockpile · Localized Output · Succession

Gain Stockpiled Strength, Safe Output 5%, Stockpile Die d6, and Smash. You cannot Full Cowl yet: select one arm, leg, or weapon as the output carrier for each Smash. You also gain Yoichi's Succession, allowing voluntary transfer of Anime & Manga during a 10-minute ritual.

100% technically available · 1 Smash/turn · Transferable inheritance

Your technique carries accumulated physical power. You gain a Stockpile Die that scales with level and fuels Smash damage, Air Force, Kinetic Surge, and several combination techniques.

02Air Force · Burst Step

Air Force: replace one attack with a ranged physical-pressure attack, range 60 ft. Make a ranged CE attack; on hit deal 1 Stockpile Die + your Strength or Dexterity modifier bludgeoning damage. You may make it a Smash normally. Burst Step: when you Smash, move 5 feet × the Output Band without provoking from the target you hit.

03Full Cowl · Fifth Vestige: Blackwhip

Gain Full Cowl and awaken Blackwhip. As a bonus action for 1 CE, manifest up to PB tendrils for 1 minute. They have 30-ft reach; you can replace an attack to lash for 1 Stockpile Die force, grapple on hit against a Large-or-smaller target, or anchor to terrain. One creature can be grappled by your Blackwhip at a time.

04Fine Distribution

While Full Cowl is active, you may choose one limb to operate one Output Band higher than the rest of your body for one attack or movement without raising your entire Cowl. Overdraw is calculated for that limb only. This is the foundation of precise percentage control.

05Blackwhip Mobility · Rescue Line

Once on each of your turns, if Blackwhip is anchored to a surface, pull yourself up to 20 feet without an action and without provoking opportunity attacks. As a reaction when a willing creature within Blackwhip range falls or is forcibly moved, spend 1 CE to catch it and move it up to 15 feet toward you or a safe anchor.

06Fourth Vestige: Danger Sense

You cannot be surprised by a creature whose hostile intent you could perceive. When a creature you can perceive intentionally targets you with an attack or forces you to make a Dexterity save, you may spend 1 CE as a reaction to roll your Stockpile Die and add it to AC against that attack or to the save. Accidents and impersonal hazards do not trigger Danger Sense.

07Air Force Mastery · Delaware Pressure

Air Force range becomes 90 ft. Once per turn, when it hits, spend 1 extra CE to create a 15-foot cone behind the target; creatures in the cone make a Dexterity save, taking 2 Stockpile Dice bludgeoning on failure or half on success. This secondary cone cannot crit or Black Flash.

08Sixth Vestige: Smokescreen

Action · 2 CE: exhale a 20-foot-radius cloud centered on yourself for up to 1 minute. The area is heavily obscured to sight. Strong wind disperses it; rain halves its maximum radius. Danger Sense still functions inside your own smoke, but does not grant sight.

09Blackwhip Reinforcement · Tendon Bracing

Wrap Blackwhip around or through one limb as a bonus action. While braced, ignore disadvantage caused by that limb being broken, though it remains injured. When that limb causes Anime & Manga recoil, reduce Recoil Steps by 1 (minimum 1). A severed limb cannot be replaced by this feature.

10Seventh Vestige: Float

Bonus action · 1 CE: for 1 minute, gain a flying speed equal to your walking speed and hover. Full Cowl's speed bonus also increases this flying speed. While carrying a creature with Blackwhip, your flight is not reduced unless normal carrying capacity is exceeded.

11Third Vestige: Kinetic Surge · Continuous Smashes

Former name: this vestige was previously called “Fa Jin”; “Kinetic Surge” is the same feature, and “Kinetic Surge Charges” are the former Fa Jin Charges.

Kinetic Surge Charges: maximum PB. Once per turn after you voluntarily move at least 20 feet, make at least two physical attacks, or spend an action performing repetitive motion, gain 1 Charge. When you move or Smash, spend any Charges; each contributes +15% effective output to that movement or Smash without increasing actual body output or recoil. You may now Smash up to twice per turn. Your Blackwhip reach increases to 60 feet.

12Faux 100%

If actual output + Kinetic Surge contribution reaches 100%, treat one Smash or movement burst as 100% for its benefits while calculating recoil only from the actual output. Example: 40% Full Cowl + four Kinetic Surge Charges = one effective 100% burst with no overdraw if 40% is safe.

13Vestige Council · Full Blast

The Vestiges can communicate coherently during rests and moments of extreme focus. Once per short rest, after failing an Intelligence, Wisdom, Charisma, or technique-related check, add your Stockpile Die to the result. Smokescreen's radius increases to 40 feet; spending +2 CE creates Full Blast, radius 60 feet.

14Second Vestige: Gearshift

Bonus action · 3 CE, up to 3 rounds. Apply Gearshift to yourself, a willing creature/object you touch, or a hostile creature hit by a physical attack (Constitution save negates). Once per turn choose Low, Second, Third, or Top Gear. Gearshift changes velocity without conventional acceleration; see the Vestige section for exact states. Gearshift normally remains gated until this level because it changes the action and velocity game dramatically.

15Transmission · Suspended Smoke

Once per turn when you physically hit a creature while Gearshift is active, change its Gear without an extra action; a hostile target makes the normal Constitution save. You may also apply Low Gear to your own Smokescreen for 1 CE, causing it to remain fixed in place and ignore ordinary wind dispersal until the start of your next turn.

16Deku Overlay · Blackchain

Overlay: bonus action, 2 CE; Blackwhip braces your entire body until the start of your next turn, reducing Full Cowl Recoil Steps by 2 (minimum 1) and allowing a broken limb to function. Blackchain: when Blackwhip pulls or slams a creature, spend Kinetic Surge Charges; each adds one Stockpile Die to the collision and 10 feet to the forced movement.

17Overdrive · Quintuple Sequence

While Gearshift and Full Cowl are both active, spend at least 2 Kinetic Surge Charges when you Smash to enter Overdrive: effective output may reach 120%. Treat 101–120% as 100% for Smash dice but add +2 Stockpile Dice and +20 ft movement. After the sequence ends, Gearshift immediately applies its recoil. Once per turn, after moving at least 30 feet under Gearshift, you may make one additional unarmed/weapon attack as part of your Attack action; this extra attack cannot itself grant another extra attack.

18Anime & Manga Unbound

Stockpile Die becomes 2d8. Full Cowl can support every unlocked Vestige simultaneously. The first time each turn you activate a different Vestige feature, reduce its CE cost by 1 (minimum 0). You may Smash up to PB times per turn, still paying each Smash normally. Kinetic Surge can gain up to 2 Charges per turn if you satisfy two different charging conditions. Your Blackwhip reach increases to 120 feet.

19Forbidden Succession · Vestige Sacrifice

When a physical Smash hits, you may attempt to forcibly transfer one inherited Vestige into the target. Permanently lose access to that Vestige's sub-technique and its combinations; the target makes a Charisma save. On failure, it takes 8 Stockpile Dice psychic/force damage, cannot use reactions until its next turn, and one soul/technique effect of the GM's choice is fractured or suppressed for 1 round. On success, half damage and no suppression. This is a story-altering sacrifice, not a repeatable damage rotation.

20The Ninth's Completion

Safe Output becomes 100% and Stockpile Die becomes 2d10. Full Cowl at 100% costs 1 CE to activate but no maintenance. The first 100% Smash you make on each turn costs 0 CE. Once per turn, one Vestige feature that normally uses a bonus action may be activated as a free action. Your body is finally capable of treating the inherited stockpile as its natural state.

Pinnacle expression

The stockpile becomes a calamity.

Anime & Manga does not receive an intrinsic Domain. Its supreme expressions are physical: one impossible blow, impossible combinations, and the terrifying ability to pass pieces of the inheritance into another soul.

Maximum: United States of Smash Maximum Technique · Level 18+

Action · 12 CE · requires effective output 80%+. Choose one creature or object within your melee reach and make one physical attack. On hit, add 10 Stockpile Dice to the attack and create a 30-foot-radius pressure detonation centered on the target. Other creatures of your choice in the radius make a Dexterity save, taking 5 Stockpile Dice bludgeoning on failure or half on success and being pushed 30 feet. The primary target is pushed 60 feet if Large or smaller.

If your actual output exceeds Safe Output, resolve Anime & Manga recoil normally after the hit. This Maximum does not protect the user's body, cannot receive generic Maximum Output, and cannot be combined with Overdrive 120% unless a campaign breakthrough explicitly permits it.

Overdrive 120% Level 17+

Gearshift + Kinetic Surge + Anime & Manga. Effective output above 100% does not create a seventh Output Band; instead a 101–120% Overdrive Smash uses the 100% package, adds +2 Stockpile Dice and +20 feet of movement, then triggers Gearshift's extended recoil.

Forbidden Succession Level 19+

Sacrifice a Vestige branch to attack a target from inside its own technique/soul structure. This is not merely another damage option: the price permanently rewrites your character sheet. The GM should make the resulting fracture matter narratively as well as mechanically.

The Ninth's Completion Level 20

100% becomes safe rather than special. The user's endgame advantage is therefore not that they finally discover 100%, but that they can use it while simultaneously running Blackwhip, Float, Danger Sense, Kinetic Surge, Smokescreen, and Gearshift without their own body immediately betraying them.

Vestige Realm ≠ Domain Expansion No Domain

The internal landscape can appear in dreams, near-death scenes, soul interactions, or technique clashes that specifically touch consciousness. It does not trap enemies, clash with barriers, negate sure-hits, or grant a Domain's initiative-20 event.

Rules integration

How Anime & Manga behaves in JJK 5e.

These rulings use the finalized Codex rule priority: revised technique text first, then Advanced Sorcery/Core Glossary, then unaltered Project V3, then ordinary 5e.

Black Flash

A rolled unarmed, weapon, Blackwhip lash, or Air Force attack carrying a Smash can critically hit and attempt Black Flash under the campaign's restored procedure. Recoil, Kinetic Surge movement, Smokescreen, and secondary shockwaves cannot.

Maximum Output

Generic Maximum Output may enhance one eligible named feature normally, including Air Force, Blackwhip, Smokescreen, or a Smash. It cannot enhance Overdrive, Forbidden Succession, This Is My Last, or another feature explicitly described as a pinnacle/vow.

Reverse Cursed Technique

RCT can repair Anime & Manga injuries using the revised campaign costs: broken limb/major wound 20 Healing Reserve + 2 CE; severed limb 40 Reserve + 4 CE. Healing HP alone does not repair the injury.

No automatic Reversal

Anime & Manga has no built-in Cursed Technique Reversal. If a player later earns the generic Reversal feat, the GM should approve one exact eligible inversion rather than inventing an entire reversed Vestige roster.

No intrinsic Domain

The Vestige Realm is internal consciousness, not a Domain Expansion and not an anti-domain tool. Anime & Manga's natural pinnacle is embodied stockpile mastery and Vestige combination. A campaign may still award generic barrier techniques separately.

Technique Extermination

Suppression can turn off Full Cowl and active Vestige expressions, but it does not delete accumulated stockpile or kill the Vestiges. When suppression ends, the technique can be reactivated normally.

Mimicry / copying

A copying technique can reproduce one observed Vestige expression at its base form if its own rules allow, but does not gain the historical stockpile, Safe Output progression, Vestige Council, or Succession. Copying Blackwhip does not mean copying Anime & Manga.

Dual-technique characters

Allowed. Anime & Manga does not automatically kill or age a character simply because this campaign permits a second Innate Technique. If the table wants the source-material incompatibility theme, use it as an optional narrative drawback rather than a hidden mechanical punishment.

Gearshift and movement

Gearshift changes velocity but does not grant teleportation. It cannot pass through walls, ignore restraints that prevent movement, or create a legal path where none exists. It can, however, alter speed in midair and ignore ordinary acceleration requirements.

Kinetic Surge charging

You cannot farm unlimited Charges outside meaningful time pressure: Charges expire after 1 minute without combat, chase, strenuous movement, or deliberate training. A long rest always empties them.

Damage stacking

A Smash is one damage amplifier. Kinetic Surge adds its own named dice only where specified, and Overdrive is a named pinnacle combination. Do not multiply the same Stockpile Dice through another wording trick.

Forced Succession

This is intentionally campaign-altering. The player permanently loses the sacrificed Vestige unless a future story explicitly restores it. It should not be used on a disposable minion just to fish for damage.

Reference

Safe Output & Overdraw.

This is the table to keep open during play. Safe Output is level-based by default, but a breakthrough, feat, body modification, or story milestone can permanently raise it without changing level.

Why this exception exists: ordinary injury rules assume an outside attack has to hit hard enough to break you. Anime & Manga recoil is the body tearing itself apart from the inside, so extreme overdraw is allowed to qualify a limb for Break without pretending a small external hit did catastrophic damage.

Source inspiration

What is canon, and what is JJK homebrew.

The ability identities come from My Hero Academia; all CE costs, percentages-as-bands, recoil math, progression levels, feats, and JJK interactions are original adaptation rules for this campaign.

  • My Hero Academia foundation
  • Anime & Manga, successor abilities, stockpile, Vestiges, later combinations
  • Chapter 369 summary, Gearshift, Danger Sense, Smokescreen, Float, Blackwhip, Kinetic Surge combination
  • Chapter 412 summary, Low Gear Smokescreen, Blackwhip reinforcement, Kinetic Surge setup
  • JJK 5e foundation
  • Uses the campaign's revised CE economy, critical-injury thresholds, RCT repair, Maximum Output, Black Flash, Technique Focus, stacking rules, and two-technique rulings from the Comprehensive Player Guide and Advanced Sorcery Systems.
  • Design principle
  • The adaptation deliberately allows terrifying ceilings. It balances Anime & Manga through procedural body risk, action economy, CE use, and Vestige timing rather than pretending 100% does not exist until an arbitrary level.
Appendix

Three very different Anime & Manga characters.

The same technique does not force the same build. These examples are tactical identities, not mandatory subclasses.

Recommended first-campaign lesson: do not open every fight at the highest percentage your CE pool can technically afford. Anime & Manga is at its best when the player is constantly deciding whether a problem deserves safe precision, a complicated Vestige answer, or one spectacular act of self-destruction.

Striker

Close-Quarters Successor

Prioritize Constitution, a physical attack stat, Armored Vessel, Blackwhip reinforcement, and controlled Full Cowl. Use most Vestiges to manufacture one safe opening for a decisive Smash rather than trying to activate everything every round.

Controller

Rescue / Battlefield Successor

Blackwhip, Float, Danger Sense, Smokescreen, and Air Force become the real identity. This version can dominate chases, evacuations, vertical maps, collapsing structures, and enemy positioning without needing to spend every turn gambling a 100% punch.

Technician

Vestige Combo Specialist

Lean into Kinetic Surge, Gearshift, Blackchain, Suspended Screen, and Faux 100%. This character may have lower raw physical stats than the brawler but wins by treating every Vestige as one piece in a planned sequence.
