One For All Inherited Embers

A stockpiled physical inheritance carrying the wills and techniques of its previous bearers. One For All is not seven unrelated abilities: it is a single inherited engine whose vestiges become increasingly capable of acting together.

The important number is not the maximum output you possess. It is the maximum output your body can survive.

Core law

Power first. Survival second.

One For All always contains its entire stockpile. Character progression represents the vessel learning to distribute, reinforce, combine, and survive that power—not the stockpile politely waiting for higher levels.

Level 1

Stockpiled Strength

Your technique carries accumulated physical power. You gain a Stockpile Die that scales with level and fuels Smash damage, Air Force, Fa Jin, and several combination techniques.

Always available

Any Percentage

You may choose any output from 5% to 100% in 5% increments whenever a One For All feature asks for output. Your Safe Output only determines whether doing so hurts you.

Technique-specific risk

Overdraw

Exceed Safe Output and the stockpile rebounds through your body. Recoil cannot be resisted, redirected, or absorbed by temporary HP. Extreme overdraw can break or sever the limb that carried the attack.

Bonus action · 1 CE

Full Cowl

Unlocked at level 3. Technique Focus, up to 1 minute. Distribute your chosen output through your entire body, gaining the movement and defense bonuses of that Output Band. You may change output once at the start of each turn without an action.

One For All exception

Shared Internal Focus

Full Cowl, Blackwhip, Float, Smokescreen, Fa Jin, Danger Sense, and Gearshift are expressions of the same inherited technique. They may coexist inside One For All's single Technique Focus unless a feature specifically says otherwise.

Physical carrier

Smash

When you hit with an unarmed strike, natural weapon, or Strength/Dexterity weapon attack, you may turn that hit into a Smash. Pay the CE cost for the chosen Output Band and add the listed Stockpile Dice. A direct Smash can critically hit and can trigger Black Flash.

Weapon users are valid. One For All does not require punching. A sword, staff, spear, kick, elbow, or similar physical carrier can deliver the stockpile. Mundane weapons used at 80%+ may break at the GM's discretion after the hit; cursed tools are built to survive cursed output.
Signature engine

Safe Output & Overdraw.

This is the table to keep open during play. Safe Output is level-based by default, but a breakthrough, feat, body modification, or story milestone can permanently raise it without changing level.

Overdraw formula

Recoil Steps

Recoil Steps = ceil((chosen output − Safe Output) / 10), minimum 1 whenever chosen output is above Safe Output.

After each over-safe Smash, take 1 Stockpile Die of force damage per Recoil Step. This damage ignores resistance/immunity and temporary HP, and is dealt after the attack resolves.

Full-body danger

Unsafe Full Cowl

If Full Cowl itself is above Safe Output, you take its Recoil damage at the start of each of your turns. Choose one limb carrying the greatest load; that limb is the injury target. A full-body 100% state is therefore drastically more dangerous than sacrificing one arm for a single 100% Smash.

3+ steps

Injury-Tagged Recoil

At 3+ Recoil Steps, the recoil is limb-tagged for the revised injury system. If one recoil instance reaches 25% of your maximum HP, make the normal injury save; failure breaks the loaded limb.

5+ steps

Catastrophic Overdraw

At 5+ Recoil Steps, the stockpile itself counts as meeting the Break qualification for the loaded limb even if the rolled recoil falls below 25% max HP. Make an injury save after recoil. A limb already broken can still only be severed if the recoil reaches the revised 50% Sever threshold.

Why this exception exists: ordinary injury rules assume an outside attack has to hit hard enough to break you. One For All recoil is the body tearing itself apart from the inside, so extreme overdraw is allowed to qualify a limb for Break without pretending a small external hit did catastrophic damage.

Complete progression

Level 1–20.

The default awakening order mirrors the technique's narrative escalation, but the GM may swap Blackwhip, Danger Sense, Smokescreen, Float, and Fa Jin between their listed awakening milestones. Gearshift normally remains gated until level 14 because it changes the action and velocity game dramatically.

01

Inherited Stockpile · Localized Output · Succession

Gain Stockpiled Strength, Safe Output 5%, Stockpile Die d6, and Smash. You cannot Full Cowl yet: select one arm, leg, or weapon as the output carrier for each Smash. You also gain Yoichi's Succession, allowing voluntary transfer of One For All during a 10-minute ritual.

100% technically available1 Smash/turnTransferable inheritance
02

Air Force · Burst Step

Air Force: replace one attack with a ranged physical-pressure attack, range 60 ft. Make a ranged CE attack; on hit deal 1 Stockpile Die + your Strength or Dexterity modifier bludgeoning damage. You may make it a Smash normally. Burst Step: when you Smash, move 5 feet × the Output Band without provoking from the target you hit.

03

Full Cowl · Fifth Vestige: Blackwhip

Gain Full Cowl and awaken Blackwhip. As a bonus action for 1 CE, manifest up to PB tendrils for 1 minute. They have 30-ft reach; you can replace an attack to lash for 1 Stockpile Die force, grapple on hit against a Large-or-smaller target, or anchor to terrain. One creature can be grappled by your Blackwhip at a time.

04

Fine Distribution

While Full Cowl is active, you may choose one limb to operate one Output Band higher than the rest of your body for one attack or movement without raising your entire Cowl. Overdraw is calculated for that limb only. This is the foundation of precise percentage control.

05

Blackwhip Mobility · Rescue Line

Once on each of your turns, if Blackwhip is anchored to a surface, pull yourself up to 20 feet without an action and without provoking opportunity attacks. As a reaction when a willing creature within Blackwhip range falls or is forcibly moved, spend 1 CE to catch it and move it up to 15 feet toward you or a safe anchor.

06

Fourth Vestige: Danger Sense

You cannot be surprised by a creature whose hostile intent you could perceive. When a creature you can perceive intentionally targets you with an attack or forces you to make a Dexterity save, you may spend 1 CE as a reaction to roll your Stockpile Die and add it to AC against that attack or to the save. Accidents and impersonal hazards do not trigger Danger Sense.

07

Air Force Mastery · Delaware Pressure

Air Force range becomes 90 ft. Once per turn, when it hits, spend 1 extra CE to create a 15-foot cone behind the target; creatures in the cone make a Dexterity save, taking 2 Stockpile Dice bludgeoning on failure or half on success. This secondary cone cannot crit or Black Flash.

08

Sixth Vestige: Smokescreen

Action · 2 CE: exhale a 20-foot-radius cloud centered on yourself for up to 1 minute. The area is heavily obscured to sight. Strong wind disperses it; rain halves its maximum radius. Danger Sense still functions inside your own smoke, but does not grant sight.

09

Blackwhip Reinforcement · Tendon Bracing

Wrap Blackwhip around or through one limb as a bonus action. While braced, ignore disadvantage caused by that limb being broken, though it remains injured. When that limb causes One For All recoil, reduce Recoil Steps by 1 (minimum 1). A severed limb cannot be replaced by this feature.

10

Seventh Vestige: Float

Bonus action · 1 CE: for 1 minute, gain a flying speed equal to your walking speed and hover. Full Cowl's speed bonus also increases this flying speed. While carrying a creature with Blackwhip, your flight is not reduced unless normal carrying capacity is exceeded.

11

Third Vestige: Fa Jin · Continuous Smashes

Fa Jin Charges: maximum PB. Once per turn after you voluntarily move at least 20 feet, make at least two physical attacks, or spend an action performing repetitive motion, gain 1 Charge. When you move or Smash, spend any Charges; each contributes +15% effective output to that movement or Smash without increasing actual body output or recoil. You may now Smash up to twice per turn.

12

Faux 100%

If actual output + Fa Jin contribution reaches 100%, treat one Smash or movement burst as 100% for its benefits while calculating recoil only from the actual output. Example: 40% Full Cowl + four Fa Jin Charges = one effective 100% burst with no overdraw if 40% is safe.

13

Vestige Council · Full Blast

The Vestiges can communicate coherently during rests and moments of extreme focus. Once per short rest, after failing an Intelligence, Wisdom, Charisma, or technique-related check, add your Stockpile Die to the result. Smokescreen's radius increases to 40 feet; spending +2 CE creates Full Blast, radius 60 feet.

14

Second Vestige: Gearshift

Bonus action · 3 CE, up to 3 rounds. Apply Gearshift to yourself, a willing creature/object you touch, or a hostile creature hit by a physical attack (Constitution save negates). Once per turn choose Low, Second, Third, or Top Gear. Gearshift changes velocity without conventional acceleration; see the Vestige section for exact states.

15

Transmission · Suspended Smoke

Once per turn when you physically hit a creature while Gearshift is active, change its Gear without an extra action; a hostile target makes the normal Constitution save. You may also apply Low Gear to your own Smokescreen for 1 CE, causing it to remain fixed in place and ignore ordinary wind dispersal until the start of your next turn.

16

Deku Overlay · Blackchain

Overlay: bonus action, 2 CE; Blackwhip braces your entire body until the start of your next turn, reducing Full Cowl Recoil Steps by 2 (minimum 1) and allowing a broken limb to function. Blackchain: when Blackwhip pulls or slams a creature, spend Fa Jin Charges; each adds one Stockpile Die to the collision and 10 feet to the forced movement.

17

Overdrive · Quintuple Sequence

While Gearshift and Full Cowl are both active, spend at least 2 Fa Jin Charges when you Smash to enter Overdrive: effective output may reach 120%. Treat 101–120% as 100% for Smash dice but add +2 Stockpile Dice and +20 ft movement. After the sequence ends, Gearshift immediately applies its recoil. Once per turn, after moving at least 30 feet under Gearshift, you may make one additional unarmed/weapon attack as part of your Attack action; this extra attack cannot itself grant another extra attack.

18

One For All Unbound

Stockpile Die becomes 2d8. Full Cowl can support every unlocked Vestige simultaneously. The first time each turn you activate a different Vestige feature, reduce its CE cost by 1 (minimum 0). You may Smash up to PB times per turn, still paying each Smash normally. Fa Jin can gain up to 2 Charges per turn if you satisfy two different charging conditions.

19

Forbidden Succession · Vestige Sacrifice

When a physical Smash hits, you may attempt to forcibly transfer one inherited Vestige into the target. Permanently lose access to that Vestige's sub-technique and its combinations; the target makes a Charisma save. On failure, it takes 8 Stockpile Dice psychic/force damage, cannot use reactions until its next turn, and one soul/technique effect of the GM's choice is fractured or suppressed for 1 round. On success, half damage and no suppression. This is a story-altering sacrifice, not a repeatable damage rotation.

20

The Ninth's Completion

Safe Output becomes 100% and Stockpile Die becomes 2d10. Full Cowl at 100% costs 1 CE to activate but no maintenance. The first 100% Smash you make on each turn costs 0 CE. Once per turn, one Vestige feature that normally uses a bonus action may be activated as a free action. Your body is finally capable of treating the inherited stockpile as its natural state.

Inherited sub-techniques

The Vestige Suite.

The previous bearers are not summons. They are inherited imprints within the technique. Each sub-technique is strengthened by the accumulated stockpile and uses the same CE ability, save DC, and Technique Focus.

First · Yoichi

Succession

The foundational transfer factor: One For All can be deliberately passed onward.

  • Voluntary Transfer: 10-minute ritual; the intended recipient must knowingly accept.
  • Forced Transfer: only the level-19 Forbidden Succession can weaponize vestige transfer in combat.
  • Embers: after voluntarily transferring the whole technique, you retain an Ember Reserve equal to level + CE ability modifier. At the end of each encounter in which you activate One For All, lose 1 Ember. At 0, the technique leaves you completely.
Second · Kudo

Gearshift

Manipulate velocity directly, bypassing ordinary acceleration and inertia.

  • Low Gear: target's speed is halved; it cannot benefit from Dash. Self-use can deliberately slow timing without falling.
  • Second Gear: +20 ft speed; first physical hit each turn gains +1 Stockpile Die.
  • Third Gear: +40 ft speed; first two physical hits each turn gain +1 Stockpile Die.
  • Top Gear: +60 ft speed; physical movement ignores nonmagical difficult terrain; first physical hit gains +2 Stockpile Dice.
  • Recoil: when Gearshift ends, until end of next turn your speed is halved, you cannot Dash, and you cannot activate Gearshift again. Overdrive extends this recoil to 2 rounds.
Third · Bruce

Fa Jin

Repeated motion stores kinetic energy separately from your dangerous actual One For All output.

  • Maximum Charges = PB.
  • Gain 1 Charge once per turn for 20 ft voluntary movement, 2+ physical attacks, or an action of repetitive motion.
  • At level 18, different charging conditions can each grant one Charge, maximum 2 per turn.
  • Each spent Charge contributes +15% effective output to one Smash or movement burst and can be converted to 10 ft extra movement.
  • Fa Jin never raises actual output for Overdraw calculations.
Fourth · Hikage

Danger Sense

A warning signal keyed to imminent malicious or harmful intent.

  • Prevents surprise from perceivable hostile actors.
  • Reaction · 1 CE: Stockpile Die to AC against one intentional attack or to one Dexterity save caused intentionally by a creature.
  • Does not identify the attacker, reveal exact location, or grant sight.
  • Does not automatically trigger from accidents, environmental collapse, disease, or sincerely non-hostile actions.
Fifth · Daigoro

Blackwhip

Energy tendrils used for restraint, mobility, rescue, reinforcement, and internal body support.

  • 30-ft reach at level 3; 60 ft at level 11; 120 ft at level 18.
  • Lash replaces one attack and deals 1 Stockpile Die force.
  • On hit, choose damage or grapple. Escape uses Athletics/Acrobatics vs CE DC.
  • Terrain anchors enable movement; willing creatures can be carried or rescued.
  • Reinforcement and Overlay can brace broken limbs and reduce One For All recoil.
Sixth · En

Smokescreen

A massive sensory-control tool whose weakness is that it blinds indiscriminately.

  • Action · 2 CE: 20-ft radius, heavily obscured, up to 1 minute.
  • Radius 40 ft at level 13; Full Blast can reach 60 ft for +2 CE.
  • Strong wind disperses it; rain halves its maximum radius.
  • Danger Sense remains useful in smoke, but does not negate blindness.
  • Gearshift's Low Gear can temporarily suspend the cloud against ordinary dispersal.
Seventh · Nana

Float

Levitation magnified by One For All into true aerial mobility.

  • Bonus action · 1 CE, 1 minute.
  • Fly speed equals walking speed and benefits from Full Cowl's speed bonus.
  • You hover; being knocked prone in the air does not cause a fall unless Float ends.
  • Blackwhip can tow willing targets while you fly.
Eighth · All Might

The Symbol's Imprint

The eighth bearer added no separate inherited sub-technique; his contribution is the enormous stockpile and the model for total-body mastery.

  • Mechanically represented by Safe Output growth, Full Cowl, Smash scaling, and level-20 Completion.
  • During Vestige Council, the Symbol's Imprint may offer tactical or moral guidance but does not grant another action or separate power.
Ninth · Current Bearer

Your One For All

At high levels, the technique stops being a list of inherited tricks. Your signature is the combination you build from them. Players are encouraged to name original chained techniques once they repeatedly use the same mechanical sequence.

Smash library

How to build signature attacks.

These are named packages for common uses of the same stockpile. Unless a move says otherwise, it is not a new resource or separate unlock: if you have the component feature, you can perform it.

Basic Smash

Detroit Smash

Any ordinary melee Smash delivered through fist, kick, weapon, or natural attack. Use the chosen Output Band's normal Smash dice and cost. The name is flavor; mechanically this is the technique's default single-target burst.

Air Force

Delaware Smash

Use Air Force as the carrier for a Smash. This lets a physical-power character threaten distant targets without turning One For All into generic energy projection. At level 7, Delaware Pressure can continue behind the first target as a secondary cone.

Movement

St. Louis Smash

Move at least 15 feet immediately before a kick or leg-carried Smash. On hit, you may convert Burst Step into forced movement: push the target 5 feet × Output Band instead of moving yourself. The target may make a Strength save to halve this forced movement.

Aerial

Manchester Smash

While at least 20 feet above a target using Float, falling, or a Blackwhip swing, make a leg-carried Smash. On hit, add 1 Stockpile Die per 20 feet descended, maximum PB dice, and you land in an open space adjacent to the target. These descent dice are one movement amplifier and do not multiply on a miss.

Area control

Groundbreak Smash

Action · normal Smash cost: strike a surface within reach instead of a creature. Creatures in a radius of 5 feet × Output Band make a Dexterity save; failure takes the Smash's Stockpile Dice as bludgeoning damage and falls prone, success takes half and stays standing. Terrain may crack or crater at the GM's discretion.

Rescue

Counterblast

Reaction when a Large-or-smaller unsecured object, cloud, wave, or mundane projectile hazard enters within 15 feet: spend the CE for a Band I or II Smash and release an opposing pressure wave. Reduce the hazard's damage by your Smash dice; if reduced to 0, disperse or redirect it harmlessly.

Blackwhip

Whip-Sling Smash

Anchor Blackwhip, move at least 20 feet through the swing, then release into a melee Smash. Add 1 Stockpile Die and you may continue moving up to half your remaining speed after the hit. This is particularly effective with Float because the anchor can define your arc without requiring ground.

Fa Jin

Faux Smash

Any Smash whose effective output is raised by Fa Jin without raising actual output. If it reaches 100%, it becomes Faux 100%; below that, simply use the effective Output Band for Smash dice and movement while calculating recoil from actual output.

Gearshift

Transmission Smash

Hit while Gearshift is active, then change the target's Gear as part of Transmission. A failed Constitution save can leave the target suddenly slowed after being launched, or accelerated into a Blackwhip/terrain setup. Gearshift's damage bonuses apply only where its state explicitly says.

Team finisher

United Front Smash

Ready a Smash for an ally's forced-movement, restraint, mark, or opening. If the ally's effect causes the target to enter your reach before your next turn, use your reaction to make the readied Smash. This is ordinary Ready action timing; the technique does not grant an extra reaction.

Naming rule

Your city. Your style.

The inherited stockpile does not require the current bearer to repeat another user's move names. A player can build a kick-focused, weapon-focused, grappling, rescue, ranged-pressure, or aerial One For All style and name its techniques accordingly. The rules care about carrier, output, Vestige components, and costs—not the fictional label on the attack.

Combination techniques

One engine, many answers.

These are prebuilt combinations, not additional unlock taxes. If you have the component features and can pay their costs, you can attempt the combination.

Level 12+

Faux 100%

Combine actual Full Cowl output with enough Fa Jin to reach 100% effective output. Use the 100% Smash/movement benefits while Overdraw sees only your actual percentage.

actual output + (15% × Fa Jin Charges) = effective output, max 100%
Blackwhip + Fa Jin

Blackchain

Spend Fa Jin through a Blackwhip pull or slam. Each Charge adds one Stockpile Die to collision damage and 10 ft to the forced movement.

Blackwhip + Full Cowl

Internal Reinforcement

Brace a damaged or overdrawn limb with Blackwhip. At level 9 this reduces its Recoil Steps by 1; at level 16 Overlay can reinforce the whole body by 2.

Danger Sense + Smokescreen

Blind Hunter

Flood the area with smoke, then navigate by warning rather than sight. You still suffer normal blindness penalties when actually trying to identify or target something you cannot see; Danger Sense instead prevents the enemy from treating concealment as complete safety.

Float + Blackwhip

Sky Rescue

Float above danger and use Blackwhip as an aerial rescue net. One anchored line can move you while another catches a willing target, making this combination exceptional for collapsing buildings and civilian extraction.

Gearshift + Smokescreen

Suspended Screen

Apply Low Gear to your own smoke for 1 CE. Until your next turn it remains unnaturally suspended and ignores ordinary wind dispersal, creating a fixed blind zone for a speed-changing ambush.

Gearshift + Fa Jin + OFA

Overdrive 120%

Use Gearshift to violate inertia and Fa Jin to contribute stored motion while One For All provides the actual force. Effective output may reach 120%, adding +2 Stockpile Dice beyond a normal 100% Smash before Gearshift recoil hits.

Gearshift + Blackwhip

Transmission Sling

Change the velocity of a grappled creature or anchored object just before Blackwhip releases it. On a failed Constitution save, add 20 ft to a hostile launch at Second Gear, 40 ft at Third, or 60 ft at Top Gear; this does not independently add damage beyond collision rules.

Level 19+

Vestige Spear

Carry one chosen Vestige imprint through Blackwhip or blood into a physical Smash, then use Forbidden Succession to sacrifice that power into the target's internal/soul structure. Once sacrificed, that Vestige is gone unless the campaign creates a specific way to restore it.

Player-created

Signature Sequence

If you repeatedly chain the same 3+ component features, give the sequence a name. Naming it does not reduce costs or action requirements by itself, but makes it eligible for story boons, vows, Technique Creativity, or a future custom feat.

Maximum & endgame

The stockpile becomes a calamity.

One For All does not receive an intrinsic Domain. Its supreme expressions are physical: one impossible blow, impossible combinations, and the terrifying ability to pass pieces of the inheritance into another soul.

Maximum Technique · Level 18+

Maximum: United States of Smash

Action · 12 CE · requires effective output 80%+. Choose one creature or object within your melee reach and make one physical attack. On hit, add 10 Stockpile Dice to the attack and create a 30-foot-radius pressure detonation centered on the target. Other creatures of your choice in the radius make a Dexterity save, taking 5 Stockpile Dice bludgeoning on failure or half on success and being pushed 30 feet. The primary target is pushed 60 feet if Large or smaller.

If your actual output exceeds Safe Output, resolve One For All recoil normally after the hit. This Maximum does not protect the user's body, cannot receive generic Maximum Output, and cannot be combined with Overdrive 120% unless a campaign breakthrough explicitly permits it.

Level 17+

Overdrive 120%

Gearshift + Fa Jin + One For All. Effective output above 100% does not create a seventh Output Band; instead a 101–120% Overdrive Smash uses the 100% package, adds +2 Stockpile Dice and +20 feet of movement, then triggers Gearshift's extended recoil.

Level 19+

Forbidden Succession

Sacrifice a Vestige branch to attack a target from inside its own technique/soul structure. This is not merely another damage option: the price permanently rewrites your character sheet. The GM should make the resulting fracture matter narratively as well as mechanically.

Level 20

The Ninth's Completion

100% becomes safe rather than special. The user's endgame advantage is therefore not that they finally discover 100%, but that they can use it while simultaneously running Blackwhip, Float, Danger Sense, Fa Jin, Smokescreen, and Gearshift without their own body immediately betraying them.

No Domain

Vestige Realm ≠ Domain Expansion

The internal landscape can appear in dreams, near-death scenes, soul interactions, or technique clashes that specifically touch consciousness. It does not trap enemies, clash with barriers, negate sure-hits, or grant a Domain's initiative-20 event.

Optional binding vows

Power with consequences.

These are examples, not mandatory subclass features. They exist because One For All is unusually suited to characters who knowingly spend their future body for immediate power.

Vow

One Limb, One Blow

Before an over-safe Smash, declare that one specific healthy limb will break after the attack. Ignore all Recoil damage from that Smash and add +2 Stockpile Dice. The limb breaks automatically after resolution and cannot be protected by Blackwhip. If already broken, this vow is unavailable.

Vow

This Is My Last

Once in the character's lifetime, declare the current encounter your final use of One For All. Until the encounter ends, treat Safe Output as 100%, maximize the first Stockpile Die roll of each turn, and ignore Gearshift recoil. When combat ends, immediately transfer the technique if a valid successor is present or lose it and enter Embers.

Vow

Never Above the Line

Choose an output cap no higher than your Safe Output after a long rest. Until the next long rest you cannot exceed it voluntarily. In exchange, the first Full Cowl activation costs 0 CE and the first Smash each encounter costs 0 CE.

Innate Technique feats

Four ways to specialize.

Following the Codex convention, One For All has four dedicated feat options. It is enormous because of its built-in progression, not because every Vestige gets a separate feat tree.

Prerequisite: One For All, level 4+

Armored Vessel

  • Increase Safe Output by 5 percentage points, maximum 100%.
  • Once per turn when calculating Overdraw, reduce Recoil Steps by 1 (minimum 1).
  • Gain proficiency in Constitution saves if you do not already have it; if already proficient, add +1 to One For All injury saves.
Prerequisite: Blackwhip

Blackwhip Savant

  • Blackwhip range increases by 30 ft.
  • You may maintain grapples on up to PB creatures, though each requires its own tendril.
  • Once per turn a Blackwhip lash or collision deals +1 Stockpile Die.
  • Rescue Line may move a willing creature up to 30 ft instead of 15.
Prerequisite: at least two awakened Vestiges

Vestige Harmony

  • Choose one awakened Vestige sub-technique after each long rest.
  • The first activation of that Vestige each turn costs 1 less CE, minimum 0.
  • During Vestige Council, add the Stockpile Die to one additional mental/technique check per short rest.
Prerequisite: Air Force or Fa Jin

Smash Artisan

  • Choose Air Force, Fa Jin, or Close Smash after each long rest.
  • Air Force: range doubles. Fa Jin: maximum Charges increase by 1. Close Smash: once per turn reroll one Stockpile Die showing its minimum value.
  • The choice may change each long rest, representing technique refinement rather than permanent anatomy.
Rules integration

How One For All behaves in JJK 5e.

These rulings use the finalized Codex rule priority: revised technique text first, then Advanced Sorcery/Core Glossary, then unaltered Project V3, then ordinary 5e.

Black Flash

A rolled unarmed, weapon, Blackwhip lash, or Air Force attack carrying a Smash can critically hit and attempt Black Flash under the campaign's restored procedure. Recoil, Fa Jin movement, Smokescreen, and secondary shockwaves cannot.

Maximum Output

Generic Maximum Output may enhance one eligible named feature normally, including Air Force, Blackwhip, Smokescreen, or a Smash. It cannot enhance Overdrive, Forbidden Succession, This Is My Last, or another feature explicitly described as a pinnacle/vow.

Reverse Cursed Technique

RCT can repair One For All injuries using the revised campaign costs: broken limb/major wound 20 Healing Reserve + 2 CE; severed limb 40 Reserve + 4 CE. Healing HP alone does not repair the injury.

No automatic Reversal

One For All has no built-in Cursed Technique Reversal. If a player later earns the generic Reversal feat, the GM should approve one exact eligible inversion rather than inventing an entire reversed Vestige roster.

No intrinsic Domain

The Vestige Realm is internal consciousness, not a Domain Expansion and not an anti-domain tool. One For All's natural pinnacle is embodied stockpile mastery and Vestige combination. A campaign may still award generic barrier techniques separately.

Technique Extermination

Suppression can turn off Full Cowl and active Vestige expressions, but it does not delete accumulated stockpile or kill the Vestiges. When suppression ends, the technique can be reactivated normally.

Mimicry / copying

A copying technique can reproduce one observed Vestige expression at its base form if its own rules allow, but does not gain the historical stockpile, Safe Output progression, Vestige Council, or Succession. Copying Blackwhip does not mean copying One For All.

Dual-technique characters

Allowed. One For All does not automatically kill or age a character simply because this campaign permits a second Innate Technique. If the table wants the source-material incompatibility theme, use it as an optional narrative drawback rather than a hidden mechanical punishment.

Gearshift and movement

Gearshift changes velocity but does not grant teleportation. It cannot pass through walls, ignore restraints that prevent movement, or create a legal path where none exists. It can, however, alter speed in midair and ignore ordinary acceleration requirements.

Fa Jin charging

You cannot farm unlimited Charges outside meaningful time pressure: Charges expire after 1 minute without combat, chase, strenuous movement, or deliberate training. A long rest always empties them.

Damage stacking

A Smash is one damage amplifier. Fa Jin adds its own named dice only where specified, and Overdrive is a named pinnacle combination. Do not multiply the same Stockpile Dice through another wording trick.

Forced Succession

This is intentionally campaign-altering. The player permanently loses the sacrificed Vestige unless a future story explicitly restores it. It should not be used on a disposable minion just to fish for damage.

Table use

Three very different One For All characters.

The same technique does not force the same build. These examples are tactical identities, not mandatory subclasses.

Striker

Close-Quarters Successor

Prioritize Constitution, a physical attack stat, Armored Vessel, Blackwhip reinforcement, and controlled Full Cowl. Use most Vestiges to manufacture one safe opening for a decisive Smash rather than trying to activate everything every round.

Controller

Rescue / Battlefield Successor

Blackwhip, Float, Danger Sense, Smokescreen, and Air Force become the real identity. This version can dominate chases, evacuations, vertical maps, collapsing structures, and enemy positioning without needing to spend every turn gambling a 100% punch.

Technician

Vestige Combo Specialist

Lean into Fa Jin, Gearshift, Blackchain, Suspended Screen, and Faux 100%. This character may have lower raw physical stats than the brawler but wins by treating every Vestige as one piece in a planned sequence.

Recommended first-campaign lesson: do not open every fight at the highest percentage your CE pool can technically afford. One For All is at its best when the player is constantly deciding whether a problem deserves safe precision, a complicated Vestige answer, or one spectacular act of self-destruction.
Source & adaptation notes

What is canon, and what is JJK homebrew.

The ability identities come from My Hero Academia; all CE costs, percentages-as-bands, recoil math, progression levels, feats, and JJK interactions are original adaptation rules for this campaign.

My Hero Academia foundation

One For All — successor abilities, stockpile, Vestiges, later combinations Chapter 369 summary — Gearshift, Danger Sense, Smokescreen, Float, Blackwhip, Fa Jin combination Chapter 412 summary — Low Gear Smokescreen, Blackwhip reinforcement, Fa Jin setup

JJK 5e foundation

Uses the campaign's revised CE economy, critical-injury thresholds, RCT repair, Maximum Output, Black Flash, Technique Focus, stacking rules, and two-technique rulings from the Comprehensive Player Guide and Advanced Sorcery Systems.

Design principle

The adaptation deliberately allows terrifying ceilings. It balances One For All through procedural body risk, action economy, CE use, and Vestige timing rather than pretending 100% does not exist until an arbitrary level.
