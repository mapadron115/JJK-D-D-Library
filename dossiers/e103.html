Chronological Reserve

Power tierTier V · Calamity

Core law

What the technique actually does.

Time Units

The vault’s rules: TU maximum 2 × level + CE modifier, starting each long rest at level’s worth. Theft requires a successful technique effect — the vault fills only from the stolen. One TU is an abstract combat second, never literal lifespan, and extra actions are bounded below.

  • Time Units (TU) maximum = 2 × level + CE ability modifier. Start each long rest with TU equal to level.
  • Stealing time requires a successful technique effect; you do not generate infinite time out of combat.
  • 1 TU is an abstract combat second, not literal lifespan.
  • Temporal effects never give unlimited turns. Extra actions are specifically bounded below.

Field notes. Subject: grief for lost seconds, given a ledger. Three truths govern him. First, his CE hoards time instead of spending it — stolen moments kept in a vault, measured in Time Units, never generated from nothing: out of combat the vault does not fill itself. Second, his power is acceleration and refusal — rewinds, extra actions, a returned position — but extra actions are specifically bounded, never unlimited turns. Third, and heaviest: he was too late once, and resolved never to be too late again; every rewind is an argument with that one failure. Hence the maxim: he does not travel through time. He keeps it.

Full progression

Level 1–20 Progression

1Time Theft

The first crime: on a weapon or CE hit, 1 CE — a failed Con save buys +2 TU and slows the target by 10 ft until his next turn. Once per turn. The technique begins the way all theft begins: by taking what was in someone else’s pocket. On a weapon/CE hit spend 1 CE; target makes Con save. Failure: +2 TU and speed -10 ft until next turn. Once/turn.

Time Die progression: d6 at levels 1–4, d8 at levels 5–10, d10 at levels 11–16, d12 at levels 17–20. The original expansion repeatedly references a Time Die without printing its value. This reviewed edition completes that omission using the same four-step progression explicitly established by Vector Authority in the same book. This is a codex clarification, not a claimed quotation from the source.

3Chrono Step

Bonus action, 2 TU: move half his speed without provoking, or +2 AC until his next turn by shifting his personal timing. He does not dodge the attack. He attends a different moment than the one it was aimed at. Bonus action, spend 2 TU: move up to half speed without provoking, or add +2 AC until start next turn by shifting your personal timing.

6Stasis Sphere

Action, 3 CE + 3 TU: one creature within 60 ft — a failed Con save leaves it Restrained and Incapacitated until the end of his next turn, broken early only by his own damage. A second, set aside. Time is the only prison with no walls. Action, 3 CE + 3 TU: one creature within 60 ft Con save; failure Restrained and Incapacitated until end next turn. Damage from you ends it after resolving.

9Stored Future

Reaction, 4 TU, when he fails a Dex save or is hit: he rewinds to where he began his previous turn and rerolls the triggering save or attack. Once per round. The future he stored turns out to have been the past, wearing a disguise. Reaction, 4 TU when you fail a Dex save or are hit: rewind your position to where you began your previous turn and reroll the triggering save/attack against you. Once/round.

11Accelerate

Bonus action, 4 CE + 5 TU: until end of turn, one additional action — Attack (single), Dash, Disengage, or a technique of 3 CE or less. Once per round. He spends his hoard the way misers never do: decisively, and all at once. Bonus action, 4 CE + 5 TU: until end turn gain one additional action usable only for Attack (one attack), Dash, Disengage, or one technique costing 3 CE or less. Once/round.

13Age to Dust

Action, 6 CE + 8 TU: a creature within 60 ft — Con save, 8 Time Dice necrotic, and failure carves the damage into max HP until a long rest or RCT repair; constructs take maximum dice instead. He does not wound. He collects the years. Action, 6 CE + 8 TU: creature within 60 ft Con save, 8 Time Dice necrotic. Failure also reduces max HP by half damage dealt until long rest/RCT repair. Constructs instead take maximum dice without max-HP loss.

15Chrono Anastasis

Action, all TU (minimum 15), 10 CE, once per long rest: a 30-ft radius rewound to the end of the previous round — terrain restored, HP lost since then returned, positions reset; but not CE, not limited uses, not Domains, not the dead, not erased concepts. Resurrection is beyond the vault’s charter. Action, all TU minimum 15, 10 CE, once/long rest: rewind a 30-ft radius to its state at the end of the previous round. Restore destroyed terrain and HP lost since then, return creatures to prior positions, but do not restore spent CE, limited-use abilities, Domains, dead creatures, or erased concepts.

18Domain Expansion — Archive of the Stolen Hour

The vault becomes a building: 175-ft barrier, and at initiative 20 he declares FREEZE, AGE, or RETURN — reactions and speed stolen, 8 Time Dice of age, or each failure teleported back to its position at the previous initiative 20. Anti-domain protection restores the save; his ordinary attacks still roll. The archive admits everyone. It returns them late. 175-ft barrier. At initiative 20 choose FREEZE, AGE, or RETURN. Hostiles make Con save: FREEZE loses reactions and speed becomes 0 until next initiative 20; AGE deals 8 Time Dice necrotic; RETURN teleports each failure to its position at previous initiative 20. Anti-domain protection uses normal save; ordinary attacks roll normally.

Narrative application: from this level onward, story-scale lifespan theft becomes available as a narrative application of TU (TU remain an abstract combat second, not literal lifespan).

20Time Emperor

The hoard perfected: TU maximum doubles, every combat begins with 10 TU if lower, and a successful Time Theft earns +2 TU once per round. Accelerate may instead grant one full extra action at 8 TU + 6 CE. The emperor of stolen seconds has stopped counting. TU maximum doubles. Start every combat with 10 TU if lower. Once/round a successful Time Theft gains +2 additional TU. Accelerate can instead grant one full extra action at cost 8 TU + 6 CE, still once/round.

Pinnacle expression

Pinnacle / Maximum

The Maximum is a refusal spoken once per long rest: reaction, 12 CE + 12 TU, when a creature within 60 ft would die or a catastrophe resolves — rewind it and its space to the start of the current round, HP and position and status restored, resources not. Some futures are simply not permitted. Maximum Technique — The Future I Refuse (12 CE + 12 TU): reaction when a creature within 60 ft would die or a catastrophic effect resolves. Rewind that creature and its occupied space to start of current round, restoring HP/position/status from then but not resources. Once/long rest.

Feats

Technique Feats

Prerequisite: Time Theft

Second Hand

He lends his hoard: reaction and 2 TU when an ally within 30 ft fails a save — a borrowed moment, a reroll, the better result kept. Once per round. Stolen seconds make excellent gifts. You lend stolen seconds to others: when an ally within 30 ft fails a saving throw, you may spend a reaction and 2 TU to grant them a borrowed moment — they reroll the save and take the better result. Once per round.

Prerequisite: Stasis Sphere

Precise Stasis

His frozen may perceive and speak while still unable to move or act. Even the prison has visiting hours. You may allow a frozen target to perceive/speak while still preventing movement/actions.

Prerequisite: level 9

Borrowed Second

Reaction, 3 TU: an ally within 30 ft gains +10 ft movement and one object interaction, immediately. He loans time the way a banker loans coin — briefly, and at exactly the right moment. Reaction, spend 3 TU to grant an ally within 30 ft +10 ft movement and one object interaction immediately.

Prerequisite: level 15

Grand Clock

His rewind turns surgical: when using Chrono Anastasis, 2 TU per creature excludes up to PB creatures from the rewind — they keep current HP, positions, and conditions. The clockmaker decides who the hands spare. Your mastery of the rewind is surgical: when you use Chrono Anastasis, you may spend 2 TU per creature to exclude up to PB creatures from the rewind — they keep their current HP, positions, and conditions.

Rules integration

Binding Rulings

Temporal rewinds restore state, not expendable resources, or the technique becomes an infinite CE engine.
Age-based max-HP loss is temporal damage and can be repaired by advanced RCT or explicit restoration features.
Extra actions follow the listed restrictions and do not recursively generate more extra actions.
Domain's initiative-20 event is autonomous and does not grant auto-hits.
