Chronological Reserve

Time is a currency. You steal seconds from enemies, store them as Time Units, and spend them to accelerate, freeze, rewind, or restore.

Power tierTier V · Calamity
Combat roleStored time / acceleration / stasis / restoration
Core law

What the technique actually does.

Time Units

  • Time Units (TU) maximum = 2 × level + CE ability modifier. Start each long rest with TU equal to level.
  • Stealing time requires a successful technique effect; you do not generate infinite time out of combat.
  • 1 TU is an abstract combat second, not literal lifespan. Story-scale lifespan theft is a level-18+ narrative application.
  • Temporal effects never give unlimited turns. Extra actions are specifically bounded below.

Time Die

Levels 1–4 d6
Levels 5–10 d8

The original expansion repeatedly references a Time Die without printing its value. This reviewed edition completes that omission using the same four-step progression explicitly established by Vector Authority in the same book. This is a codex clarification, not a claimed quotation from the source.

Full progression

Level 1–20 Progression

1Time Theft

On a weapon/CE hit spend 1 CE; target makes Con save. Failure: +2 TU and speed -10 ft until next turn. Once/turn.

3Chrono Step

Bonus action, spend 2 TU: move up to half speed without provoking, or add +2 AC until start next turn by shifting your personal timing.

6Stasis Sphere

Action, 3 CE + 3 TU: one creature within 60 ft Con save; failure Restrained and Incapacitated until end next turn. Damage from you ends it after resolving.

9Stored Future

Reaction, 4 TU when you fail a Dex save or are hit: rewind your position to where you began your previous turn and reroll the triggering save/attack against you. Once/round.

11Accelerate

Bonus action, 4 CE + 5 TU: until end turn gain one additional action usable only for Attack (one attack), Dash, Disengage, or one technique costing 3 CE or less. Once/round.

13Age to Dust

Action, 6 CE + 8 TU: creature within 60 ft Con save, 8 Time Dice necrotic. Failure also reduces max HP by half damage dealt until long rest/RCT repair. Constructs instead take maximum dice without max-HP loss.

15Chrono Anastasis

Action, all TU minimum 15, 10 CE, once/long rest: rewind a 30-ft radius to its state at the end of the previous round. Restore destroyed terrain and HP lost since then, return creatures to prior positions, but do not restore spent CE, limited-use abilities, Domains, dead creatures, or erased concepts.

18Domain Expansion — Archive of the Stolen Hour

175-ft barrier. At initiative 20 choose FREEZE, AGE, or RETURN. Hostiles make Con save: FREEZE loses reactions and speed becomes 0 until next initiative 20; AGE deals 8 Time Dice necrotic; RETURN teleports each failure to its position at previous initiative 20. Anti-domain protection uses normal save; ordinary attacks roll normally.

20Time Emperor

TU maximum doubles. Start every combat with 10 TU if lower. Once/round a successful Time Theft gains +2 additional TU. Accelerate can instead grant one full extra action at cost 8 TU + 6 CE, still once/round.

Pinnacle expression

Pinnacle / Maximum

Maximum Technique — The Future I Refuse (12 CE + 12 TU): reaction when a creature within 60 ft would die or a catastrophic effect resolves. Rewind that creature and its occupied space to start of current round, restoring HP/position/status from then but not resources. Once/long rest.

Feats

Technique Feats

Prerequisite: Time Theft

Second Hand

You lend stolen seconds to others: when an ally within 30 ft fails a saving throw, you may spend a reaction and 2 TU to grant them a borrowed moment — they reroll the save and take the better result. Once per round.

Prerequisite: Stasis Sphere

Precise Stasis

You may allow a frozen target to perceive/speak while still preventing movement/actions.

Prerequisite: level 9

Borrowed Second

Reaction, spend 3 TU to grant an ally within 30 ft +10 ft movement and one object interaction immediately.

Prerequisite: level 15

Grand Clock

Your mastery of the rewind is surgical: when you use Chrono Anastasis, you may spend 2 TU per creature to exclude up to PB creatures from the rewind — they keep their current HP, positions, and conditions.

Rules integration

Binding Rulings

Temporal rewinds restore state, not expendable resources, or the technique becomes an infinite CE engine.
Age-based max-HP loss is temporal damage and can be repaired by advanced RCT or explicit restoration features.
Extra actions follow the listed restrictions and do not recursively generate more extra actions.
Domain's initiative-20 event is autonomous and does not grant auto-hits.
