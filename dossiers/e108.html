Undead

Tier V · InterpretiveDeath negation / regeneration / blood & bone weapons / interpretation

The technique negates 'approaching death.' Its power grows less through raw numbers than through the user's changing interpretation of what counts as death and what regeneration is allowed to accomplish.

Repair Reserve

  • Repair Reserve = 5 × level. It refreshes on short/long rest. It is separate from RCT/CE.
  • At start of your turn, if conscious or your technique is not explicitly suppressed, spend up to PB Repair to heal equal HP automatically.
  • When damage would inflict a physical injury, you may spend Repair equal to 10 for Break or 20 for Sever to repair it at end of current turn. If insufficient, injury persists until Reserve returns or another feature repairs it.
  • Being at 0 HP does not automatically mean death; normal death saves are replaced by the Undead procedure below.

Pinnacle / Maximum

Maximum Technique — Dead Quartet (10 CE + lose 25 HP): explode skin, blood, bone, and muscle into four controlled weapons for one round. Choose up to four targets within 60 ft; each makes Dex save against 3 Undead Dice slashing/force per body-component assigned to it. After resolution, reconstruct at your original or one targeted space and heal 25 HP from Repair if available.

Level 1–20 Progression

13Dead RoadAction, lose 10 HP, 5 CE: 90-ft line, Dex save, 7 Undead Dice slashing/force. Blood/bone can bend around one corner, ignoring half cover.
15Interpretation ShiftChoose one learned interpretation after long rest: AGE IS DEATH (immune magical aging), STASIS IS DEATH (advantage vs petrify/stasis and may spend 10 Repair to end it), DISMEMBERMENT IS DEATH (sever repair costs halved), or UNCONSCIOUSNESS IS DEATH (cannot be knocked unconscious while Repair remains).
18VictorBonus action, 6 CE, 1 minute, once/long rest: Repair healing doubles, Parts Bullet/Dead Line gain +2 dice, Division swarm becomes two swarms, and voluntary HP costs cannot reduce you below 1.
20Undead CompletionRepair Reserve doubles and refreshes whenever you finish a combat with at least 1 HP. Choose a second Interpretation Shift. Once/long rest, if an effect would kill you despite Undead, make a CE ability/Constitution save against source DC; success reinterpret the death condition and survive at 1 HP, permanently recording that specific method as something Undead can now negate unless conceptual/soul erasure.

Technique Feats

Crimson Bullet

Spend 5 extra Repair after firing to add 2 Undead Dice and increase range to 120 ft.

Prerequisite: Blood Propulsion

Bloody Arts

After propelling at least 20 ft, next melee hit this turn gains +2 Undead Dice.

Prerequisite: Repair Boost

Sword in the Body

Draw/store one one-handed weapon inside your regenerating body; cannot be disarmed of it while conscious.

Prerequisite: level 15

Never Stop

Once/short rest when Restrained, Paralyzed, or Stunned, spend 10 Repair to repeat the relevant save immediately.

Binding Rulings

  • Undead is not ordinary healing and does not require RCT, but technique suppression can shut it off.
  • Concept/soul erasure remains a meaningful counter because the ability negates death of the self, not nonexistence by default.
  • Voluntary HP costs are real HP loss and cannot be reduced, redirected, or paid with temp HP.
  • Undead has no Domain; its capstone is interpretive immortality.

Source Notes
