Undead

The technique negates 'approaching death.' Its power grows less through raw numbers than through the user's changing interpretation of what counts as death and what regeneration is allowed to accomplish.

Power tierTier V · Interpretive
Combat roleDeath negation / regeneration / blood & bone weapons / interpretation
Core law

What the technique actually does.

Repair Reserve

  • Repair Reserve = 5 × level. It refreshes on long rest only. It is separate from RCT/CE.
  • At start of your turn, if conscious or your technique is not explicitly suppressed, spend up to PB Repair to heal equal HP automatically.
  • When damage would inflict a physical injury, you may spend Repair equal to 10 for Break or 20 for Sever to repair it at end of current turn. If insufficient, injury persists until Reserve returns or another feature repairs it.
  • Being at 0 HP does not automatically mean death; normal death saves are replaced by the Undead procedure below.

Undead Die

Levels 1–4 d6
Levels 5–10 d8
Levels 11–16 d10
Levels 17–20 d12

The original expansion repeatedly references a Undead Die without printing its value. This reviewed edition completes that omission using the same four-step progression explicitly established by Vector Authority in the same book. This is a codex clarification, not a claimed quotation from the source.

Full progression

Level 1–20 Progression

1Negate Death

When reduced to 0 HP, do not fall unconscious. Speed halved and attacks/checks at disadvantage until above 0. At start of each turn, if Repair Reserve has any points, heal at least 1 HP before acting. Effects that destroy soul/concept or suppress technique can still kill you.

3Parts Bullet

Action or replace one Attack: voluntarily lose 1d6 HP and fire a severed finger/part 60 ft, ranged attack, 2 Undead Dice piercing. HP loss cannot be prevented but triggers normal regeneration.

6Blood Propulsion

Bonus action, lose up to PB HP: move 10 ft per HP lost, including vertically, without provoking. Dead Line: action, lose 5 HP, 60-ft line, Dex save, 4 Undead Dice slashing.

9Repair Boost

When making a melee weapon/unarmed attack, lose 1–PB HP to add equal number of Undead Dice, once/turn. You can regenerate around implanted weapons for quickdraw attacks.

11Division

Action, lose 10 HP + 3 CE: create up to PB severed-part pseudo-clones for 1 minute. Abstract them as one swarm occupying your space or adjacent space; once/turn the swarm deals 3 Undead Dice bludgeoning/piercing to a creature within 30 ft.

13Dead Road

Action, lose 10 HP, 5 CE: 90-ft line, Dex save, 7 Undead Dice slashing/force. Blood/bone can bend around one corner, ignoring half cover.

15Interpretation Shift

Choose one learned interpretation after long rest: AGE IS DEATH (immune magical aging), STASIS IS DEATH (advantage vs petrify/stasis and may spend 10 Repair to end it), DISMEMBERMENT IS DEATH (sever repair costs halved), or UNCONSCIOUSNESS IS DEATH (cannot be knocked unconscious while Repair remains).

18Victor

Bonus action, 6 CE, 1 minute, once/long rest: Repair healing doubles, Parts Bullet/Dead Line gain +2 dice, Division swarm becomes two swarms, and voluntary HP costs cannot reduce you below 1.

20Undead Completion

Repair Reserve doubles and refreshes whenever you finish a combat with at least 1 HP. Choose a second Interpretation Shift. Once/long rest, if an effect would kill you despite Undead, make a CE ability/Constitution save against source DC; success reinterpret the death condition and survive at 1 HP, permanently recording that specific method as something Undead can now negate unless conceptual/soul erasure.

Pinnacle expression

Pinnacle / Maximum

Maximum Technique — Dead Quartet (10 CE + lose 25 HP): explode skin, blood, bone, and muscle into four controlled weapons for one round. Choose up to four targets within 60 ft; each makes Dex save against 3 Undead Dice slashing/force per body-component assigned to it. After resolution, reconstruct at your original or one targeted space and heal 25 HP from Repair if available.

Rules integration

Binding Rulings

Undead is not ordinary healing and does not require RCT, but technique suppression can shut it off.
Concept/soul erasure remains a meaningful counter because the ability negates death of the self, not nonexistence by default.
Voluntary HP costs are real HP loss and cannot be reduced, redirected, or paid with temp HP.
Undead has no Domain; its capstone is interpretive immortality.
