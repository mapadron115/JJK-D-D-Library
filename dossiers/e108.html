Core law

What the technique actually does.

Repair Reserve

The body’s line of credit: Repair Reserve = 5 × level, refreshing on long rest only, separate from RCT and CE. Each turn begins by spending up to PB Repair to heal the same in HP, if conscious and the technique is not suppressed. Physical injuries may be repaired at turn’s end, 10 Repair for a Break, 20 for a Sever; if the Reserve falls short, the injury waits. And at 0 HP, normal death saves are replaced by the Undead procedure below.

  • Repair Reserve = 5 × level. It refreshes on long rest only. It is separate from RCT/CE.
  • At start of your turn, if conscious or your technique is not explicitly suppressed, spend up to PB Repair to heal equal HP automatically.
  • When damage would inflict a physical injury, you may spend Repair equal to 10 for Break or 20 for Sever to repair it at end of current turn. If insufficient, injury persists until Reserve returns or another feature repairs it.
  • Being at 0 HP does not automatically mean death; normal death saves are replaced by the Undead procedure below.

Field notes. Subject: a man who declined to die, and keeps declining. Three truths govern him. First, Undead is not immortality, it is interpretation: when reduced to 0 HP he does not fall unconscious, merely halved and disadvantaged, because he has decided that is not what death means. Second, his body is a resource, not a boundary: he voluntarily loses HP to fire Parts Bullets, to propel himself, to add Undead Dice to his strikes, and the Repair Reserve (5 × level, long rest only) pays the injuries back. Third, and strangest: at level 15 he chooses what death even is, AGE IS DEATH, STASIS IS DEATH, DISMEMBERMENT IS DEATH, UNCONSCIOUSNESS IS DEATH, each a learned interpretation with its own immunity. Hence the maxim: other men have bodies. He has a position paper.

Full progression

Level 1–20 Progression

1Negate Death

Passive · At 0 HP: no unconsciousness; speed halved, attacks/checks at disadvantage until above 0 · Start of each turn: heal at least 1 HP from Repair Reserve before acting (if points remain) · Soul-destroying or technique-suppressing effects can still kill you.

The founding refusal. He has not beaten death. He has redefined the terms of the meeting.

Undead Die progression: d6 at levels 1–4, d8 at levels 5–10, d10 at levels 11–16, d12 at levels 17–20. The original expansion repeatedly references a Undead Die without printing its value. This reviewed edition completes that omission using the same four-step progression explicitly established by Vector Authority in the same book. This is a codex clarification, not a claimed quotation from the source.

3Parts Bullet

Action (or replace one Attack) · Voluntarily lose 1d6 HP (unpreventable; triggers normal regeneration) · Ranged attack 60 ft: 2 Undead Dice piercing.

Ammunition is himself. He has never once been caught unarmed.

6Blood Propulsion

Bonus action · Lose up to PB HP · Move 10 ft per HP lost, vertically included, without provoking · Dead Line: action, lose 5 HP · 60-ft line: Dex save, 4 Undead Dice slashing.

He pays for his movement in the same coin he pays for everything: himself.

9Repair Boost

On a melee weapon/unarmed attack · Lose 1–PB HP · Add that many Undead Dice · Once/turn · May regenerate around implanted weapons for quickdraw attacks.

Every swing is a small wager against his own Repair Reserve.

11Division

Action · Lose 10 HP + 3 CE · 1 minute · Up to PB severed-part pseudo-clones, abstracted as one swarm in your or an adjacent space · Once/turn, bonus action: CE attack vs one creature within 30 ft, 3 Undead Dice bludgeoning/piercing on hit.

He does not summon allies. He distributes himself. Swarm stat line: AC 12 + PB; HP 3 × your level; the swarm occupies your space or an adjacent space, is immune to charm and fear, and disperses at 0 HP.

13Dead Road

Action · Lose 10 HP + 5 CE · 90-ft line: Dex save, 7 Undead Dice slashing/force · Blood/bone bends around one corner, ignoring half cover.

The road is made of him, so it goes where he says.

15Interpretation Shift

After a long rest, choose one: AGE IS DEATH (immune to magical aging), STASIS IS DEATH (advantage vs petrify/stasis; 10 Repair ends it), DISMEMBERMENT IS DEATH (sever repair costs halved), or UNCONSCIOUSNESS IS DEATH (cannot be knocked unconscious while Repair remains).

Death is a definition. He has started editing the dictionary.

18Victor

Bonus action · 6 CE · 1 minute, once/long rest · Repair healing doubles; Parts Bullet and Dead Line gain +2 dice; Division swarm becomes two swarms; voluntary HP costs cannot reduce you below 1.

The technique, at its most generous: he may spend himself freely.

20Undead Completion

Passive · Repair Reserve doubles and refreshes whenever you finish a combat at 1+ HP · Choose a second Interpretation Shift · Once/long rest: if an effect would kill you despite Undead, CE ability/Constitution save vs the source DC to reinterpret the death condition and survive at 1 HP (records that death method; not vs conceptual/soul erasure).

Death keeps inventing arguments. He keeps ruling them out of order.

Pinnacle expression

Pinnacle / Maximum

The Maximum is self-demolition as art. The quartet plays. The audience is the enemy. Maximum Technique, Dead Quartet (10 CE + lose 25 HP; action): explode skin, blood, bone, and muscle into four controlled weapons for one round. Choose up to four targets within 60 ft; each makes Dex save against 3 Undead Dice slashing/force per body-component assigned to it. After resolution, reconstruct at your original or one targeted space and heal 25 HP from Repair if available.

Feats

Technique Feats

Prerequisite: Parts Bullet

Crimson Bullet

He has learned to plant rather than merely fire: a Parts Bullet may be fired into an unattended object or surface within 60 ft without damaging it, embedded for a minute, detonated as a bonus action: creatures within 10 ft make a Dex save or take 3 Undead Dice slashing. The finger is a mine now.

Prerequisite: Blood Propulsion

Bloody Arts

His propulsion carries more than him: when he Blood Propels, one willing creature he touches comes along (no provoking), or one grappled creature is dragged; a carried willing creature may make one melee attack at any point during the movement. Even his commute is a combat maneuver.

Prerequisite: Repair Boost

Sword in the Body

He draws or stores one one-handed weapon inside his regenerating body, and cannot be disarmed of it while conscious. The sheath is himself. The quickdraw is surgery.

Prerequisite: level 15

Never Stop

Once per short rest, when Restrained, Paralyzed, or Stunned: spend 10 Repair to immediately repeat the relevant save. Conditions are suggestions. He declines most of them.

Rules integration

Binding Rulings

Undead is not ordinary healing and does not require RCT, but technique suppression can shut it off.
Concept/soul erasure remains a meaningful counter because the ability negates death of the self, not nonexistence by default.
Voluntary HP costs are real HP loss and cannot be reduced, redirected, or paid with temp HP.
Undead has no Domain; its capstone is interpretive immortality.
