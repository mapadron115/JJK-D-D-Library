Gamma Rage

Rage / strength / durability / regeneration

Power tierTier V · Calamity
Core law

What the technique actually does.

Core Engine — Rage. Rage 0–10. Gain 1 when damaged, 2 on critical/heavy injury, 1 when ally drops within sight. Lose 1 after a round neither dealing nor taking damage. Rage raises physical output but makes fine control harder.

The fiction. Jujutsu scholars classify Gamma Rage as trauma with a pressure valve — the prevailing theory holds the user's CE learned, wound by wound, that being hurt was the same thing as surviving, so it stopped discarding pain and started keeping it. Rage is the ledger and the body is the debt collected: it accrues when the sorcerer bleeds and spends itself in thunderclaps and leaps, and it drains away the moment there is nothing left to survive, because a body at peace has nothing to be angry at. This is reinforcement that overshot its purpose so far it became a second physiology.

Full progression

Level 1 through 20.

1Hulk Out

As a bonus action, spend 1 CE: for 1 minute you become Large, your unarmed strikes deal 1d10, you have advantage on Strength checks and saves, and you add your Rage to melee damage (maximum +PB).

3Thunder Clap

For 2 CE, create a 15-ft cone; creatures in the area make a Constitution save, taking 3d10 thunder damage on a failure and being pushed 15 ft.

6Hulk Leap

Your jump distance is multiplied by 5; when you land after jumping 30 ft or more, you may spend 2 CE to create a 10-ft burst; creatures in the burst make a Dexterity save, taking 3d10 force on failure, half on success.

9Regeneration

At Rage 5 or higher, you heal hit points equal to your proficiency bonus at the start of each of your turns. Acid damage or explicit anti-healing effects can suppress this.

11Worldbreaker Grip

You count as two sizes larger for grappling and lifting. You may use a grappled target as an improvised weapon, dealing 2d10 + your Strength modifier damage.

13Rage 8

At Rage 8: you gain resistance to all physical damage; your unarmed strikes deal 1d12, plus 1d10 force once per turn. Wisdom and Intelligence checks requiring patience have disadvantage.

15Gamma Quake

For 5 CE, punch the ground: creatures in a 30-ft radius make a Dexterity save, taking 7d10 force damage on a failure and falling prone; structures take maximum damage.

18Worldbreaker

For 8 CE, at Rage 10, for 1 minute: you become Huge, your reach becomes 15 ft, you gain resistance to all damage except psychic, your attacks deal +2d10 damage, and your movement causes hazardous tremors: when you move 20+ ft, creatures within 10 ft of your path make a Dexterity save or fall prone, and the ground along your path becomes difficult terrain until the start of your next turn. Your Rage cannot decrease for the duration.

20Strongest One There Is

Your Rage maximum becomes 12. At Rage 10 or higher, your Strength may exceed the normal cap, reaching 26 for checks, saves, and attacks while transformed. Once per long rest, when you are reduced to 0 HP, you immediately enter Worldbreaker at 1 HP.

Pinnacle expression

Maximum, Domain, or equivalent.

Maximum — Worldbreaker Smash (10 CE, Rage 10; action): 60-ft cone of shattered terrain/shockwave, Str save 12d12 force, half success; failure hurled 60 ft and prone. Rage falls to 5.

Feats

Build-defining options.

Hulk Catch

When a ranged weapon or thrown-object attack hits you or an ally within 10 feet, you may use your reaction to snatch it out of the air: make a Strength (Athletics) check against the attack's total. On a success the attack is negated, and if the projectile is physical you may immediately hurl it back as part of the same reaction — make a ranged attack against a creature within 60 feet, dealing 3d10 + your Strength modifier bludgeoning damage on a hit. You need a free hand, and you cannot catch energy beams, sure-hits, or anything larger than Large.

Collateral Bulwark

at Rage 5+, as a bonus action you can tear up the terrain into an improvised bulwark: until the start of your next turn, ranged attacks against you have disadvantage and you have half cover.

Banner's Mind

You can force a moment of Banner-like lucidity inside the rage. As a bonus action, spend 2 Rage: for the next minute you ignore the disadvantage on Wisdom and Intelligence checks imposed by high Rage, retain full memory of what happens while transformed, and can speak in complete sentences. You may also end Hulk Out early by spending your action — your Rage falls to 0 and you revert to normal size — but your pulse needs a minute to steady, and you cannot transform again until then.

Green Scar

at Rage 5+, as a bonus action you can spend 1 Rage to roar: creatures of your choice within 15 ft make a Wisdom save or become Frightened of you until the end of your next turn.

Rules integration

Edge cases and shared systems.

Codex rulings: Direct rolled technique/weapon attacks can use the campaign's Black Flash procedure when otherwise eligible. Passive damage, hazards, summons acting independently, and initiative-20 Domain events cannot. Generic Maximum Output applies only to ordinary features, never named Maxima or Domains.
