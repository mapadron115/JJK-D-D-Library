Core law

What the technique actually does.

Core Engine, Rage. The ledger of his wounds. Rage rises when he is damaged, two on the heavy blows, and when allies fall in his sight; it cools after a quiet round. Physical output climbs as fine control withers. Rage 0–10. Gain 1 when damaged, 2 on critical/heavy injury, 1 when ally drops within sight. Lose 1 after a round neither dealing nor taking damage. Rage raises physical output but makes fine control harder.

Field notes. Subject: trauma with a ledger. Three truths govern him. First: his cursed energy learned, wound by wound, that being hurt was the same thing as surviving, so it stopped discarding pain and started keeping it; Rage is the account, zero to ten, and every deposit is a blow he did not dodge. Second: the account pays interest in violence, healing at five, resistance at eight, a minute of Worldbreaker at ten, but fines him in patience: fine control withers as the balance grows. Third: the withdrawals escalate, claps, leaps, quakes, until the capstone lets him refuse death itself, entering Worldbreaker at 1 HP when he should have fallen. Mark the maxim: he does not win fights despite his wounds. He banks them.

Full progression

Level 1 through 20.

1Hulk Out

Bonus action · 1 CE · For 1 minute: you become Large, your unarmed strikes deal 1d10, you have advantage on Strength checks and saves, and you add your Rage (max +PB) to melee damage.

The account opens: one CE, one minute Large, 1d10 fists, advantage on Strength, and Rage added to every melee blow. The transformation is the deposit slip. As a bonus action, spend 1 CE: for 1 minute you become Large, your unarmed strikes deal 1d10, you have advantage on Strength checks and saves, and you add your Rage to melee damage (maximum +PB).

3Thunder Clap

Action · 2 CE · 15-ft cone: creatures in the area make a Constitution save, taking 3d10 thunder damage on a failure and being pushed 15 ft.

The first withdrawal: two CE for a fifteen-foot cone of thunder, three dice and fifteen feet of displacement. Pain, spent at range. For 2 CE, create a 15-ft cone; creatures in the area make a Constitution save, taking 3d10 thunder damage on a failure and being pushed 15 ft.

6Hulk Leap

Passive · Your jump distance is multiplied by 5. Special: when you land after jumping 30 ft or more, spend 2 CE for a 10-ft burst — creatures in it make a Dexterity save, taking 3d10 force on a failure, half on a success.

His jump multiplies by five, and landings become artillery, two CE after thirty feet of air buys a ten-foot burst of three dice. The ground pays for his travel. Your jump distance is multiplied by 5; when you land after jumping 30 ft or more, you may spend 2 CE to create a 10-ft burst; creatures in the burst make a Dexterity save, taking 3d10 force on failure, half on success.

9Regeneration

Passive · At Rage 5 or higher: heal hit points equal to your proficiency bonus at the start of each of your turns; suppressed by acid damage or explicit anti-healing effects.

At Rage five the body starts returning the investment: his proficiency in healing at the start of every turn. Acid and anti-healing are the only auditors. At Rage 5 or higher, you heal hit points equal to your proficiency bonus at the start of each of your turns. Acid damage or explicit anti-healing effects can suppress this.

11Worldbreaker Grip

Passive · You count as two sizes larger for grappling and lifting; you may wield a grappled target as an improvised weapon (2d10 + Strength modifier damage).

He counts as two sizes larger for every grapple and lift, and the grappled become improvised weaponry, two dice plus Strength. The enemy is the arsenal now. You count as two sizes larger for grappling and lifting. You may use a grappled target as an improvised weapon, dealing 2d10 + your Strength modifier damage.

13Rage 8

Passive · At Rage 8: resistance to all physical damage; your unarmed strikes deal 1d12 plus 1d10 force once per turn; Wisdom and Intelligence checks requiring patience have disadvantage.

At eight the account matures: resistance to all physical damage, 1d12 fists, a force die once per turn, and the patience fine: Wisdom and Intelligence checks suffer disadvantage. At Rage 8: you gain resistance to all physical damage; your unarmed strikes deal 1d12, plus 1d10 force once per turn. Wisdom and Intelligence checks requiring patience have disadvantage.

15Gamma Quake

Action · 5 CE · Creatures in a 30-ft radius make a Dexterity save, taking 7d10 force damage on a failure and falling prone; structures take maximum damage.

Five CE to punch the ground: thirty feet of seven-dice reckoning, prone on failure, and structures take the maximum. He does not attack the enemy. He attacks the floor's opinion of the enemy. For 5 CE, punch the ground: creatures in a 30-ft radius make a Dexterity save, taking 7d10 force damage on a failure and falling prone; structures take maximum damage.

18Worldbreaker

Action · 8 CE (requires Rage 10) · For 1 minute: you become Huge, reach 15 ft, resistance to all damage except psychic, +2d10 damage on your attacks; moving 20+ ft forces creatures within 10 ft of your path to make a Dexterity save or fall prone, and the ground becomes difficult terrain until your next turn. Your Rage cannot decrease.

Eight CE at Rage ten: a minute Huge, fifteen feet of reach, resistance to all but psychic, two extra dice per attack, and his movement itself becomes hazardous terrain. The flood breaks the dam. For 8 CE, at Rage 10, for 1 minute: you become Huge, your reach becomes 15 ft, you gain resistance to all damage except psychic, your attacks deal +2d10 damage, and your movement causes hazardous tremors: when you move 20+ ft, creatures within 10 ft of your path make a Dexterity save or fall prone, and the ground along your path becomes difficult terrain until the start of your next turn. Your Rage cannot decrease for the duration.

20Strongest One There Is

Passive · Rage maximum becomes 12; at Rage 10+ your Strength may reach 26 while transformed. Special · Once per long rest: when reduced to 0 HP, immediately enter Worldbreaker at 1 HP instead.

Rage caps at twelve, Strength may reach twenty-six past the mortal ceiling, and once per long rest, falling to zero is not death but a Worldbreaker entered at 1 HP. The treasury's final clause. Your Rage maximum becomes 12. At Rage 10 or higher, your Strength may exceed the normal cap, reaching 26 for checks, saves, and attacks while transformed. Once per long rest, when you are reduced to 0 HP, you immediately enter Worldbreaker at 1 HP.

Pinnacle expression

Maximum, Domain, or equivalent.

Maximum, Worldbreaker Smash (10 CE, Rage 10; action): 60-ft cone of shattered terrain/shockwave, Str save 12d12 force, half success; failure hurled 60 ft and prone. Rage falls to 5.

Feats

Build-defining options.

Hulk Catch

He treats incoming fire as a delivery. A reaction and a contested Athletics check, and the ranged attack is caught, the projectile filed as his property. When a ranged weapon or thrown-object attack hits you or an ally within 10 feet, you may use your reaction to snatch it out of the air: make a Strength (Athletics) check against the attack's total. On a success the attack is negated, and if the projectile is physical you may immediately hurl it back as part of the same reaction, make a ranged attack against a creature within 60 feet, dealing 3d10 + your Strength modifier bludgeoning damage on a hit. You need a free hand, and you cannot catch energy beams, sure-hits, or anything larger than Large.

Collateral Bulwark

At Rage five, a bonus action tears the terrain into cover: ranged attacks at disadvantage, half cover until his next turn. The battlefield donates its architecture. at Rage 5+, as a bonus action you can tear up the terrain into an improvised bulwark: until the start of your next turn, ranged attacks against you have disadvantage and you have half cover.

Banner's Mind

Two Rage for a minute of lucidity, the disadvantage lifted, the memory retained. The man inside the account gets a window to count it. You can force a moment of Banner-like lucidity inside the rage. As a bonus action, spend 2 Rage: for the next minute you ignore the disadvantage on Wisdom and Intelligence checks imposed by high Rage, retain full memory of what happens while transformed, and can speak in complete sentences. You may also end Hulk Out early by spending your action, your Rage falls to 0 and you revert to normal size, but your pulse needs a minute to steady, and you cannot transform again until then.

Green Scar

At Rage five, one Rage buys a roar: hostiles within fifteen feet save or fear him until his next turn ends. The account announces its balance. at Rage 5+, as a bonus action you can spend 1 Rage to roar: creatures of your choice within 15 ft make a Wisdom save or become Frightened of you until the end of your next turn.

Rules integration

Edge cases and shared systems.

Codex rulings: Direct rolled technique/weapon attacks can use the campaign's Black Flash procedure when otherwise eligible. Passive damage, hazards, summons acting independently, and initiative-20 Domain events cannot. Generic Maximum Output applies only to ordinary features, never named Maxima or Domains.
