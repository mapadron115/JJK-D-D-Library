Gamma Rage

Rage / strength / durability / regeneration

Power tierTier V · Calamity
Core law

What the technique actually does.

Core Engine — Rage. Rage 0–10. Gain 1 when damaged, 2 on critical/heavy injury, 1 when ally drops within sight. Lose 1 after a round neither dealing nor taking damage. Rage raises physical output but makes fine control harder.

Full progression

Level 1 through 20.

1Hulk Out

Bonus action, 1 CE: Large for 1 minute, unarmed 1d10, advantage Str, +Rage to melee damage (max PB).

3Thunder Clap

2 CE: 15-ft cone, Con save 3d10 thunder and push 15 ft.

6Hulk Leap

Jump distance ×5; landing after 30 ft creates 10-ft 3d10 force burst for 2 CE.

9Regeneration

At Rage 5+, heal PB HP at start turn. Acid/explicit anti-heal can suppress.

11Worldbreaker Grip

Count two sizes larger for grapple/lift. Grappled target may be used as improvised weapon for 2d10 + Str.

13Rage 8

Resistance all physical damage; unarmed 1d12 +1d10 force once/turn. Wis/Int checks requiring patience have disadvantage.

15Gamma Quake

5 CE: punch ground, 30-ft radius Dex save 7d10 force, prone; structures maximum dice.

18Worldbreaker

8 CE, Rage 10, 1 minute: Huge, reach 15, resistance all but psychic, attacks +2d10, movement causes hazardous tremors. Rage cannot decrease.

20Strongest One There Is

Rage max 12. At 10+, Strength may exceed normal cap to 26 for checks/saves/attacks while transformed. Once/long rest when reduced to 0, immediately enter Worldbreaker at 1 HP.

Pinnacle expression

Maximum, Domain, or equivalent.

Maximum — Worldbreaker Smash (10 CE, Rage 10): 60-ft cone of shattered terrain/shockwave, Str save 12d12 force, half success; failure hurled 60 ft and prone. Rage falls to 5.

Rules integration

Edge cases and shared systems.

Codex rulings: Direct rolled technique/weapon attacks can use the campaign's Black Flash procedure when otherwise eligible. Passive damage, hazards, summons acting independently, and initiative-20 Domain events cannot. Generic Maximum Output applies only to ordinary features, never named Maxima or Domains.
