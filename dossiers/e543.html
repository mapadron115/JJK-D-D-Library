Core law

What the technique actually does.

Core law, the unobserved state. Bonus action · 1 CE · Superpose: split into two instances — place an echo of yourself in an unoccupied space within 30 feet. Both instances are you until observed. Observation: when a hostile creature targets you with an attack or single-target effect, it chooses an instance — then you declare which was real. Real: resolve normally, the echo vanishes. Echo: no effect, the echo vanishes, you were at the other instance all along. Area effects: if an area covers one instance, choose whether you were there; if it covers both, you are hit. Superposition lasts 1 minute or until collapse. Your instances do not make opportunity attacks — a possibility cannot commit to a reaction.

Field notes. Subject: a cat, a box, and a sorcerer who read too much physics. Three truths govern the unobserved. First: nobody knows where you are, including you — that is not a weakness, it is the entire technique; certainty is something you sell to other people at a markup. Second: observation is violence — every eye that finds you collapses a universe of maybes into one tired fact, so the skilled practitioner treats attention like weather: to be read, routed around, and occasionally weaponized. Third: the echo is not fake — until the moment of observation both histories are true, both fists were raised, both positions were occupied; the universe simply agrees, afterward, to pretend otherwise. Mark the economy: cursed energy does not buy certainty. It buys the delay before certainty arrives.

Full progression

Level 1 through 20.

1Superpose

Bonus action · 1 CE · Split into two instances as described in the core law. On your turn, choose one instance to act through; enemies cannot tell which is real until they observe you.

The bread and butter: one attack per round simply does not land. The counterplay is honest — area effects, multiattack, and ready actions all still work. You are not invincible; you are merely unobserved, which against most opponents amounts to the same thing.

3Collapse Strike

When you declare an instance was the echo (collapse), you may spend 1 CE to detonate the collapsing possibility: creatures within 10 feet of the echo's position make a Dexterity save, taking 2d8 thunder damage on a failure (half on a success).

The new verb: defense becomes offense. The enemy's observation — the very act of finding you — becomes the trigger for their punishment. Look at me, the technique says, and mean it. It will cost you.

6Many Worlds

Passive · Superpose now creates three instances instead of two, and instance placement range increases to 60 feet. When one instance is declared an echo, it vanishes; the remaining instances stay in superposition until further observation. Three instances require three attacks to land a single hit.

The system arrives and the foundation sharpens with it: more maybes, placed farther apart. The many-worlds interpretation, weaponized — every branch is armed, and the enemy must prune them one by one.

11The Observer

Action · 8 CE · Once per short rest · Observe a creature within 60 feet: collapse their wave function into the state you choose — (a) they are restrained until the end of your next turn (their freedom was the illusion), or (b) they take 4d8 psychic damage (their safety was the illusion).

The turn: you stop dodging observation and start dealing it. One creature, one choice, no appeal — a rehearsal for the Box. The limiter is the short rest, not the CE; at this depth of reserve, only scarcity of permission keeps a power honest.

18The Uncollapsed

Passive · Superpose costs 0 CE (still a bonus action) and lasts until collapse — no 1-minute limit. Once per round, when your superposition collapses, you may re-establish it as a reaction (1 CE).

Enablement, not a nuke: the technique stops charging you for its own existence, and attention itself becomes a revolving door. You are very difficult to handle now — not because you hit harder, but because pinning you down has become a category error.

Pinnacle expression

Maximum, Domain, or equivalent.

The barrier does not rise — it closes. The world becomes a box: seamless, lidless, without corners, and everyone inside it is suddenly two people, the one who lived and the one who didn't, standing in the same shoes. You are the only shape in the room with a single shadow. Somewhere, a lid you cannot see is waiting for your hand.

Domain Expansion, Schrödinger's Box (action, 20 CE, 1 minute, 90-foot radius): every creature inside except you gains Superposed — each is simultaneously alive and dead, hit and missed, present and absent, until observed. You are the Observer: you have PB Observations per round. Spend 1 Observation when a Superposed creature would take damage, fail a saving throw, or gain a condition — declare the event unreal and it does not happen. Spend 1 Observation when a Superposed creature succeeds on a saving throw — declare the success unreal and they fail instead. One Observation per event. You choose what is real, PB times per round, for everyone in the box.

Feats

Build-defining options.

Many Eyes

Passive while in superposition: you perceive through all instances simultaneously. You cannot be surprised, you have advantage on Perception checks, and flanking grants no advantage against you. Superposition, but as perception.

Uncertain Blade

The first attack you make each turn while in superposition has advantage — the defender cannot tell which strike is real until it lands. Superposition, but as accuracy.

The Other Hand

The echo is not a picture of you — it has hands, and they work. While you are in superposition, you may use your object interaction through any of your instances as if you stood there: open a door, pull a lever, take or leave an object. Once per turn, you may interact through two instances at once. Things your echo moves stay moved — if that instance collapses, whatever it carried is left where it stood. The errand was real, even if the errand-runner wasn't. Superposition, but as reach.

Entangled Ally

When you use Superpose, you may grant the split to a willing creature within 30 feet instead of yourself (same cost). They place the echo, and they choose real-or-echo when observed. Superposition, but for another.

Rules integration

Edge cases and shared systems.

Codex rulings: Direct rolled technique attacks (Uncertain Blade) can use the campaign's Black Flash procedure when otherwise eligible. Collapse Strike and The Observer's damage are save effects and cannot critically hit or Black Flash.
Generic Maximum Output applies only to ordinary features, never to Schrödinger's Box.
Observation is triggered by hostile targeting (attacks, single-target effects). Allied targeting — healing, buffs — does not collapse you. Grapples and shoves count as attacks for this purpose.
Instances are indistinguishable to enemies. You choose which instance acts each turn openly; the superposition concerns only enemy targeting, not your own actions.
