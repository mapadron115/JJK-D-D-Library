Features & procedures

Properties & techniques

Exact player-facing procedures

Consult

The first use of learning: spend an Entry from the creature at hand gain a die on one save or check against its technique this round. The Archive does not fight the technique, it reminds you how it works. bonus action spend Entry from that creature: +1 Tool Die to one save/check against its technique this round.

Cross-Reference

The second: spend two Entries and lend the knowledge to an ally, they use their reaction to gain the same die on their save. Scholarship, properly, is shared. 2 Entries: the wielder spends 2 Entries (no action required); the ally uses their own reaction when making the save or check to gain +1 Tool Die against that creature’s technique this round (invented).

Errata

The third: when a recorded creature activates a feature you observed, spend three Entries and three CE to blunt it, less damage, less forced movement. Mark this: it never cancels the feature. The Archive corrects; it does not censor. 3 Entries +3 CE when observed creature activates a feature you've recorded: reduce its damage by 2 Tool Dice or its forced movement by 20 ft; does not cancel feature.

Feature

The honesty of the system: Entries fade after a long rest, or when the creature fundamentally changes. The Archive remembers what a technique did, not who its owner was, and a changed soul is a new edition. Entries vanish after long rest or if the recorded creature fundamentally changes techniques.

Field notes. Subject: a library that fights. Three truths govern it. First, it is permitted only to learn: bound by the same taboo it studies, it cannot copy innate techniques, each Entry is an expression recorded, never a soul stolen. Second, knowledge becomes defense and never offense: bonuses to saves and checks, blunted damage, shortened movement, the Archive arms the reader, never the blade. Third, its memory is honest: Entries fade when the recorded creature changes, because the Archive remembers what a technique did, not who its owner was. Consider the discipline of that. A tool that could so easily become theft, and chose instead to be a footnote.

Rules integration

Cursed-tool attunement

Campaign canon

Cursed-tool attunement: you have PB attunement slots (2–6). This tool occupies one slot and requires a one-hour ritual. Attunement ends voluntarily over a short rest, on death, or when you replace it while over capacity. Distance does not break attunement. Cursed-tool attunement is separate from magic-item attunement.
Balance & counterplay. Knowledge becomes defense, never stolen offense.
