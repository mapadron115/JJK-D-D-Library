Andvaranaut

The ring converts wealth and ownership into cursed reinforcement: the wearer gains bonuses based on the total value of property they personally claim, and stored wealth can be sacrificed for temporary HP, CE recovery, or defensive boosts. As the hoard grows, surrendering possessions demands increasingly difficult Wisdom saves; at extreme thresholds the wearer begins to “dragonize.”

Rules integration

Cursed-tool attunement

Campaign canon

Cursed-tool attunement: you have PB attunement slots (2–6). This tool occupies one slot and requires a one-hour ritual. Attunement ends voluntarily over a short rest, on death, or when you replace it while over capacity. Distance does not break attunement. Cursed-tool attunement is separate from magic-item attunement.
Balance & counterplay. Its power is the hoard — separate the wearer from their claimed wealth and the engine starves. The Wisdom saves against surrendering possessions are the lever: force the choice and the ring tightens its own grip.
