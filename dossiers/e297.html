Andvaranaut

The ring that converts wealth into power: up to 20 Hoard Points, spendable for 2 CE or 20 HP per point, +2 AC for 1 minute (3 points), or advantage on all saves for 1 minute (5 points); at 20 points it can trigger the Fáfnir Threshold. Giving anything away while holding 10+ points requires a Wisdom save.

Relic profile

Hoard Points 0–20 · +4 to checks made to appraise, conceal, bargain for, or defend owned treasure

Jujutsu origin

The cursed hoard of Andvari becomes a perfect jujutsu object because greed itself supplies the negative emotion. Ownership, fear of loss, and murder over wealth continuously feed the ring.

Hoard Points

The ring can hold up to 20 Hoard Points. During a long rest, the wearer may permanently sacrifice valuables worth roughly ¥1,000,000 per point, or a cursed tool of Grade 2 or higher for 3 points per grade step (Grade 2 = 3, Grade 1 = 6, Special Grade = 9). Destroyed offerings cannot be recovered.

Hoarded Vitality

After a long rest, gain 5 temporary HP per Hoard Point, maximum 100.

Spend the Hoard

As a bonus action, spend 1 point to regain 2 CE or 20 HP; spend 3 points to gain +2 AC for 1 minute; spend 5 points to gain advantage on all saving throws for 1 minute.

Fáfnir Threshold

At 20 Hoard Points, the wearer may use an action to transform for 1 minute: fly speed 60 ft, resistance to bludgeoning/piercing/slashing, and unarmed attacks become 3d10 slashing cursed-tool attacks. When the minute ends, the ring loses 10 Hoard Points.

Binding Vow — The Hoard Owns the Hoarder

To willingly give away, destroy, or abandon something personally owned while carrying 10+ Hoard Points, make a Wisdom save, DC 10 + half your current Hoard Points. On a failure, you cannot willingly surrender it for 24 hours.

Designer’s note. At 20 points, removing the ring voluntarily also requires a DC 20 Wisdom save.

Rules integration

Cursed-tool attunement

Campaign canon

Cursed-tool attunement: you have PB attunement slots (2–6). This tool occupies one slot and requires a one-hour ritual. Attunement ends voluntarily over a short rest, on death, or when you replace it while over capacity. Distance does not break attunement. Cursed-tool attunement is separate from magic-item attunement.
