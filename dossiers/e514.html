Crazy Slots

A clown, a lottery, nine weapons. The user spins — a number from 1 to 9 — and whatever the clown deals is what you fight with: scythe, gun, mace, and six surprises. The one rule, and it's iron: once drawn, a weapon can't be dismissed until it's been used. The clown doesn't care what you wanted. It only cares that you play. And there's a number you've never rolled — the one that only comes when you truly don't want to die.

Feature map

Level-by-level features

L1

Spin the Clown

As an action, spend 2 CE: a leering clown manifests over your shoulder and spins — roll a d9 and conjure that weapon. Conjured weapons use your CE ability modifier for attack and damage rolls.

The iron restriction. A drawn weapon cannot be dismissed until it has been used — each weapon's entry names its use-condition. You cannot spin again while a weapon is drawn. The clown is patient; it will wait through a long rest if it must.

The nine numbers: 1 — The Daggers: a pair of returning throwing daggers, range 60 ft, 1d6 + modifier each; used when each dagger has drawn blood. 2 — The Scythe: a huge, utterly silent scythe, 2d10 slashing; your attacks with it make no sound; used when it reaps — reduce a creature to 0 hit points, or land three hits. 3 — The Bow: a longbow that conjures its own arrows, 1d8 piercing, range 150/600; used when three arrows have been loosed. 4 — The Hammer: a sunderer's hammer, 2d6 bludgeoning; used when it sunders — destroy a nonmagical object or shield, or land two hits on a creature. 5 — The Gun: a rifle holding three shots, 3d10 piercing, range 100/300, thunderously loud; used when all three shots are fired. 6 — The Shield: a tower shield, +3 AC while wielded; used when it interposes — block one attack that would hit you or an ally within 5 feet, then it shatters. The clown's idea of a joke. 7 — The Whip: a chain whip with 15-ft reach, 1d8 slashing, can grapple (escape DC = your CE save DC); used when it binds — successfully grapple or disarm a creature. 8 — The Mace: an honest, heavy, unglamorous mace, 2d8 bludgeoning; on a hit you may push the target 10 feet and knock it prone (Strength save vs. your CE save DC negates the prone); used when it lands two solid hits. 9 — the hidden number. You've never rolled it. The clown is saving it.

L3

Second Spin

Once per long rest, when you spin, you may roll twice — but you must take the second result. The clown gives mulligans, not choices. You asked for luck. Luck answered.

L6

Encore

Once per long rest, when a weapon is dismissed after use, you may spin again as a bonus action (spending 2 CE as normal). The show must go on. The clown hates dead air.

L11

Double Feature

Once per long rest, spin once and roll twice — conjure both weapons. Both must be used before you can spin again; if you roll the same number twice, the clown laughs and you spin that one roll a third time. Two props, one stage, no intermission.

L15

The Clown's Contract

Before spinning, you may offer the clown a binding vow — name a price (forfeit your reaction until the start of your next turn, or take 2d6 psychic damage as the clown takes its cut). If you pay it, roll twice and take the result whose weapon better fits a kind you name — melee, ranged, or defensive. You still don't choose. But the clown listens a little, when it's paid. Every deal with the clown is binding. That's the point of a clown.

L20

The Number You Never Rolled

The hidden number — the one that only comes when you truly don't want to die. Once per long rest, when you are reduced to 0 hit points (or would die outright), the clown's grin changes and it deals you the hidden number without a spin: you immediately regain half your maximum hit points, and conjure the Survivor — a plain, perfect blade. The Survivor uses your CE ability modifier; it deals double damage dice to the creature that brought you down, which the clown marks with a laughing skull only you can see. It cannot be dismissed until that creature falls or you finish a short rest. You never rolled a 9. A 9 rolled you.

Pinnacle expression

Maximum and Domain.

Capstone material

Maximum: Full House

As an action, spend 5 CE: for 1 minute, the clown deals all nine — every weapon manifests at once, floating at your command. You may attack with any of them interchangeably as part of your Attack action, and the can't-dismiss restriction is suspended while the Maximum lasts. When it ends, every weapon vanishes and you gain one level of exhaustion — the clown always collects. Nine numbers. One stage. No refunds.

Domain Expansion: The House Always Deals

18th level. Sure-hit domain — the sure-hit is the deal. Activation: full turn, 20 CE (D008 baseline for a normal Domain). Clash: 800 points; burnout 5 rounds; duration 2 min (Tier III mapping); radius 120 ft.

Everyone Plays. Every hostile creature inside is dealt a random weapon by the clown (roll a d9; a 9 deals the Mace — the house never deals the hidden number). The weapon cannot be dismissed until used to make at least one attack, and while a creature holds an unused weapon it has disadvantage on attack rolls not made with the dealt weapon — it's juggling the clown's prop. The clown deals to everyone. No exceptions. No folding. The house doesn't play favorites. The house plays everyone.

Feats

Five feats, dealt.

Crazy Slots teaches five feats — five tricks for playing a hand you didn't choose. Each is optional, and you must meet its listed prerequisite before taking it.

Prerequisite: Spin the Clown

Palmed Card

Hand a drawn weapon to a willing ally: the iron restriction transfers with it — they must satisfy its use-condition before it can be dismissed. They wield it using your CE ability modifier for attack and damage rolls. The clown doesn't mind who plays, as long as someone does.

Prerequisite: Second Spin

Rigged Applause

Flourish a drawn weapon as a bonus action: one creature that can see you must succeed on a Wisdom saving throw against your CE save DC or be taunted — it has disadvantage on attack rolls against anyone but you until the end of its next turn. Showing off doesn't count as using the weapon.

Prerequisite: Double Feature

Prop Master

Leave a used weapon behind as an innocent-looking object for 1 hour: the next creature to touch it triggers one stored attack from it (attack roll using your CE ability modifier against its AC, using the weapon's profile). You can maintain one prop at a time.

Prerequisite: Encore

Curtain Call

Once per long rest, within 1 minute of a weapon being dismissed after use, hurl its echo as a bonus action: make one attack against a creature within 60 feet using the dismissed weapon's profile. Every prop gets a final bow.

Prerequisite: Spin the Clown

The Clown Knows

When you spin during combat, the clown whispers to you the damage vulnerabilities (if any) of one creature you can see. It loves an audience, and it loves a slaughter, in that order.

Rules integration

Edge cases and shared systems.

Campaign canon notes

Cursed Energy baseline: CE pool is based on class; CE ability is Charisma or Wisdom (player's choice). CE save DC = 8 + CE ability modifier + proficiency bonus. Regain all spent CE on a long rest.
The nine numbers: 1 The Daggers, 2 The Scythe, 3 The Bow, 4 The Hammer, 5 The Gun, 6 The Shield, 7 The Whip, 8 The Mace, 9 the hidden number (only via The Number You Never Rolled). Weapons are aura constructs — they cannot be sold, disenchanted, or disarmed of their nature; a disarm attempt merely drops the conjured form, which reforms in your grip.
The iron restriction: a drawn weapon cannot be dismissed until its use-condition is satisfied, and you cannot spin while a weapon is drawn. An unused weapon persists across rests — the clown is patient. "Used" is defined per weapon; the GM adjudicates edge cases in the clown's favor.
Binding vows with the clown: The Clown's Contract is a genuine binding vow — name the price before the spin, pay it whether the roll pleases you or not. The clown remembers debts.
Double Feature duplicates: rolling the same number twice conjures one weapon with a doubled use-condition (the clown laughs; you spin a third time for your second weapon).
Domain activation defaults: rendered as full turn + 20 CE per the D008 normal-Domain baseline, with Tier III clash stats (800 / 5-round burnout / 2 min) and a 120-ft radius.
Maximum vs. Reversal: Crazy Slots' pinnacle is written as a Maximum Technique (Full House), not a Reversal; it occupies the technique's pinnacle slot.
