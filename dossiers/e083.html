Shax — Codex: Fortune Thief

Rank C
Canon master: Nerva Julius CaluadesBasis: Source gap • canonical name/master, original mechanics

Weapon Equip • Level 5

Empty Treasury. Weapon Equip subtly bends chance around the vessel rather than producing raw elemental force.

Misplace Fortune

Reaction • 2 CE

When a creature within 120 ft rolls a d20, subtract one Djinn Die from the result after seeing the roll but before the outcome is declared.

Stolen Opening

Reaction • 2 CE

When a creature within 60 ft misses an attack, move up to 60 ft without provoking and make one weapon attack.

King’s Cheat

Action • 5 CE

For one round, allies of your choice in a 60-ft aura roll attacks/saves with advantage, while enemies make Charisma saves or roll attacks/saves with disadvantage.

Full Djinn Equip • Level 9

Fly 80 ft; once per round reroll one attack, check, or save you make. Once per round when an enemy rolls a natural 20 within 60 ft, force it to reroll (no action, 2 CE).

EXTREME MAGIC • LEVEL 13+

Empty Treasury

12 CE • Codex Extreme

Enemies of your choice in a 180-ft radius make Charisma saves. Failure for 1 minute: no reactions, subtract 1d6 from attacks/saves/checks, and the first successful attack each round must be rerolled. Repeat the save at end of each turn. Success still loses reactions until the next turn.

Anime-scale: Strategic scale: collapse the luck and coordination of an army rather than flatten terrain.
