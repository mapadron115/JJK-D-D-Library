Anime-scale: Strategic scale: collapse the luck and coordination of an army rather than flatten terrain.

Field notes. Subject: the Djinn of stolen luck. I have watched a vessel of Shax play an army the way a sharper plays a mark, nothing flashy, nothing elemental, only the slow arrival of everyone's worst day. Three truths govern him. First, Shax spends nothing of its own: it steals the margin between a miss and a hit from the world and pockets it, every Misplace Fortune is a theft reported as weather. Second, the thief prospers on other people's failure: Stolen Opening moves on the back of a missed attack, because the house always collects when somebody misses. Third, and the whole game: King's Cheat does not make allies lucky; it makes the table unfair, advantage on your side and disadvantage forced on theirs. Mark the lesson, victory here is not power, but the quiet ownership of odds.
