Feature map

Level-by-level features, as the figure refined them.

L1

The Warren Below

Passive · burrow speed 20 ft.

You gain a burrow speed of 20 feet through unworked earth, sand, and loose soil. You can remain buried for up to 10 minutes at a time, and while fully underground you are unseen and cannot be targeted by effects that require sight. Erupt: when you emerge from underground into a space adjacent to a hostile creature, the first melee attack you make against it before the end of your turn has advantage and deals an extra 1d6 bludgeoning damage — the earth itself throws the punch.

L3

Twin Boomerangs

Attack · 2 CE.

Your paired war-boomerangs never leave you for long. As an attack action, throw both: make two ranged weapon attacks (1d8 + your DEX modifier slashing damage each, range 80/160 ft.; you are proficient). The boomerangs return to your hands at the end of the throw whether they hit or miss. If both boomerangs strike the same target, the second deals an additional 1d8 slashing damage as they converge. At 11th level the damage becomes 2d8 + your DEX modifier each.

L6

Egg Constructs

Action · 3 CE · war-egg pool (maximum PB).

You carry painted war-eggs — at most PB armed at once; arming beyond that cracks the oldest. As an action, plant an egg in an unoccupied space within 30 ft and choose its arming. Sentry egg: lasts 1 minute; at the start of each of your turns it fires a yolk-bolt at a hostile creature within 60 ft (+PB + DEX to hit, 1d8 + DEX force damage). Egg bomb: detonates at the start of your next turn, or when a creature enters within 5 ft of it — each creature within 15 ft makes a DEX save vs your CE DC, 3d8 thunder damage on a failure, half on a success. Decoy egg: a hollow painted shell of you; the first attack roll made against you while the decoy stands within 15 ft of you targets the decoy instead, and it cracks. Eggs have AC 12 and 5 hit points.

L11

The Warren Network

Passive.

Your burrow speed becomes 30 ft and you gain tremorsense 60 ft while underground. As a bonus action you may open a warren mouth: one willing creature within 5 ft of you can ride your tunnels — when you burrow, it moves with you and surfaces when you do. Your Erupt strikes now deal an extra 2d6 bludgeoning damage, and a creature hit must succeed on a STR save vs your CE DC or be knocked prone as the earth bucks beneath it.

L15

Spring Militia

Passive.

Your eggs have learned drill. Sentry eggs fire twice per round (two yolk-bolts, same or different targets). Egg bombs deal 5d8 thunder damage and leave the area difficult terrain until cleared — shell caltrops. Decoy eggs sprout little legs and can move up to 20 ft on your turn at your mental command. You may now plant eggs as a bonus action instead of an action.

L18

The Burrow Remembers

Passive · 1/long rest dive.

The earth catches its own. Your burrow speed equals your walking speed. Once per long rest, when you would drop to 0 hit points, you instead crash-dive: you burrow up to your burrow speed without provoking opportunity attacks and regain hit points equal to your level + your DEX modifier. The triggering damage cannot reduce you below 1 hit point before the dive.

Pinnacle expression

Maximum, Domain, or equivalent.

Capstone material.

Maximum Technique

The Great Eruption

Action · 10 CE · up to six eruption points within 120 ft.

You dive, and the whole battlefield becomes your warren. Choose up to six points on the ground within 120 ft of you. You erupt at each in sequence: each creature within 10 ft of an eruption point makes a DEX save vs your CE DC — 4d8 bludgeoning damage and knocked prone on a failure, half damage and no prone on a success. You may plant one egg construct (no action, no CE) at any two of the eruption points, armed as you choose. You end this technique in the space of the final eruption. Spring does not knock. It kicks the door down from underneath.

Level 20 · Capstone

Warrior of Spring

Passive · 1/long rest free Maximum.

The warren is wherever you stand. You can burrow through worked stone as well as earth; your tremorsense while underground extends to 120 ft; your Erupt strikes deal an extra 3d6 bludgeoning damage and score a critical hit on a 19 or 20; and you can maintain twice as many armed eggs (2 × PB). Once per long rest, you may unleash The Great Eruption without spending CE. The Guardians hold the line above. You hold everything underneath it.

Innate Technique Feats

Build-defining options.

These are optional character-building feats. You must meet each listed prerequisite before selecting it.

Prerequisite: The Warren Below

Tremor Ambush

The earth tells you everything, if you listen with your feet. While you are fully underground, you know the exact location of every creature within 60 ft that is in contact with the ground. Additionally, when a hostile creature moves into a space adjacent to your buried position, you may use your reaction to Erupt — surface in that space and make one melee attack with your Erupt benefits, even though it isn't your turn.

Prerequisite: Twin Boomerangs

Bank Shot

Walls are just suggestions. Your boomerangs ricochet off solid surfaces: your Twin Boomerangs attacks ignore half cover and three-quarters cover, and you may curve a throw around a corner or obstacle, treating a target with total cover as having three-quarters cover instead. If a boomerang misses its target, you may redirect it to a different creature within 15 ft of the original target as part of the same throw.

Prerequisite: Egg Constructs

Shell Game

Which one is the rabbit? When you plant a decoy egg, you may swap silhouettes with it: until the start of your next turn, attack rolls against you have disadvantage as enemies strike at the wrong rabbit — the first attack that would hit you instead cracks the decoy, and you may immediately move up to 10 ft without provoking opportunity attacks as the shell bursts in their face.

Prerequisite: The Warren Network

Warren Evac

Nobody gets left in the open. When you open a warren mouth, you may instead reach out to one willing creature within 30 ft: it uses its reaction to dive into your tunnel, and you move it up to your burrow speed through the earth to surface in an unoccupied space of your choice. An unconscious creature can be pulled through — you drag them by the scruff, gently, the way spring does.
