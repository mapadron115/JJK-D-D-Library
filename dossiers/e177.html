Character fantasy

Lay spectral serpent segments across the map, close them into Rings, then decide whether each circle protects, poisons, or constricts what lies within.

Combat loop

  1. Place Coil Segments across useful terrain.
  2. Connect them into complete Rings.
  3. Choose each Ring’s law: Guard, Venom, or Constrict.
  4. Tighten or nest Rings until the battlefield itself becomes the serpent’s body.

Coil Segments & Rings

  • Coil Segment: bonus action, 1 CE, place a 20-foot-long, 5-foot-wide spectral serpent segment on ground within 60 feet. It lasts 10 minutes. Maximum segments = PB.
  • Crossing: a hostile that crosses a segment for the first time on a turn spends +5 feet movement. Segments are not solid walls.
  • Ring: when 3+ segments form a closed boundary of at least one 10-foot space, designate a Ring. A segment may belong to only one Ring unless level 11 says otherwise. Maintain PB/2 rounded up Rings.

Innate Technique FeatsOptional character-building feats

Ocean Coil

Segments may be placed on water and you gain swim speed equal walking speed.

Tail Around the Party

Guard Rings grant allies advantage on saves against forced movement across their boundary.

Venom of the World

Poison immunity treats your Coil poison as resistance; poison resistance still functions normally.

Ouroboros Draft

Once per short rest, when your last placed Segment completes a Ring, refund 1 CE.

Building the character

Excellent for a defensive Strategist or Juggernaut. It rewards players who like drawing geometry on the battlefield but avoids Love Rendezvous-style mandatory sequencing.

Technique die: Coil Die follows the shared d6 → d8 → d10 → d12 progression.

Counterplay

Destroy/suppress segments, fight outside completed Rings, teleport over boundaries, or force the user to spend segments on multiple disconnected fronts.

Adaptation note. This is original JJK 5e homebrew inspired by Jörmungandr. The myth/folklore provides imagery and thematic rules; the combat engine, progression, CE economy, feats, and Domain/Maximum procedures are game design for this codex.
