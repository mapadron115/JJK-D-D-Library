Core law

Character fantasy

Lay spectral serpent segments across the map, close them into Rings, then decide whether each circle protects, poisons, or constricts what lies within.

Combat loop

  1. Place Coil Segments across useful terrain.
  2. Connect them into complete Rings.
  3. Choose each Ring’s law: Guard, Venom, or Constrict.
  4. Tighten or nest Rings until the battlefield itself becomes the serpent’s body.

Field notes. Subject: a surveyor of serpent lines. Three truths govern him. First: he redraws the battlefield before he fights on it. Segments are drawn cheaply and far apart, but closed Rings are law, and he chooses each circle's law once and refuses to unmake it. Second: a circle that protects also strangles. Guard, Venom, Constrict, three verdicts, one geometry. The boundary does not care which side of it you meant to be on. Third: the serpent is patient because the ground is not moving. Tighten a Ring and the walls come inward; nest them and escape becomes a toll road. Mark this: the coil wins long before it closes, victory was decided the moment you stood inside the first line and did not leave.

Core law

Coil Segments & Rings

Coil Segment:

The raw material: a twenty-foot spectral segment, placed within sixty feet for one CE, lasting ten minutes, PB of them at a time. bonus action, 1 CE, place a 20-foot-long, 5-foot-wide spectral serpent segment on ground within 60 feet. It lasts 10 minutes. Maximum segments = PB.

Crossing:

Even an unclosed line taxes the enemy: five extra feet of movement to cross, first time each turn. a hostile that crosses a segment for the first time on a turn spends +5 feet movement. Segments are not solid walls.

Ring:

Three segments closed around ten feet of space become a Ring, and a segment may serve only one. The geometry is the technique. when 3+ segments form a closed boundary of at least one 10-foot space, designate a Ring. A segment may belong to only one Ring. Maintain PB/2 rounded up Rings.

Full progression

Level progression

1Serpent Boundary

The boundary and the fang. Segments gained, plus World Fang, a ten-foot melee strike that poisons what it finds standing on his lines. Gain Coil Segments. You also gain World Fang: a melee CE attack with 10-foot reach that deals 2 + your CE modifier poison damage. If the target is standing on or crossing a Segment, it makes a Constitution save vs your technique DC, failure: it is poisoned until the end of your next turn.

3Three Ring Laws

The closed circle declares its law, once, unreversed: Guard for allies within, Venom for trespassers, Constrict for those who would leave. When completing a Ring choose: Guard allies inside gain +1 AC against attacks from outside; Venom a hostile that enters the Ring or starts its turn inside makes a Constitution save; failure: it is poisoned until the start of its next turn and cannot regain HP until then; success: no effect; Constrict moving from inside to outside costs double movement, and when a hostile attempts to cross the boundary it makes a Strength save, failure: its movement stops at the boundary and it is grappled (escape DC = your technique DC). A creature saves against each Ring at most once per turn.

6World Venom

Venom that lingers a minute, and a reactive fang, spend one CE when a hostile crosses a Segment and the serpent strikes from the line itself. A creature that fails its Venom Ring save is instead poisoned for 1 minute (it may repeat the Constitution save at the end of each of its turns). As a reaction when a hostile crosses a Segment, you may spend 1 CE to make a World Fang attack against it, striking from the Segment’s space as a spectral serpent.

11Nested World

Rings within Rings. The segment cap doubles, one segment may serve two Rings, and Tighten drags the whole circle inward ten feet, walls that walk. Segment cap becomes 2 × PB and a segment may support two overlapping Rings. Bonus action, 2 CE: Tighten one Ring until start of next turn, moving each of its segments inward up to 10 feet while preserving a legal closed shape. Creatures crossed by a moving Segment make Strength save or are pushed with it; forced movement does not trigger Constrict.

18Domain Expansion: Midgard Encircled

The horizon is a serpent, scales the size of houses rising past the sky, circling the world entire. The sea below is black and heaving, and the air tastes of salt and old cold. You are standing on something's back, and it has not noticed you yet.

The serpent becomes the horizon. Sixty feet, six free segments, segments placed without action or CE, and at initiative twenty, one Ring's law falls on every hostile inside it automatically. Full-turn action, 20 CE, 60-foot radius, 1 minute. A colossal serpent forms the Domain boundary and six free Coil Segments appear in any legal positions. You may create/move one Segment each turn without action or CE. At initiative 20 choose one Ring: every hostile inside it automatically suffers that Ring’s law as if it had just triggered, and you may Tighten it 10 feet. A protected creature makes the relevant Ring save; success avoids the initiative-20 effect and forced Tighten movement for that event. Ordinary Ring triggers still resolve normally. Clash bookkeeping: 800 clash points (Tier IV). A 100+ point advantage collapses the weaker Domain; collapse deals no damage. Barrier AC 12 + PB; barrier HP = 10 × max(1, your CE modifier) + 1,600. Technique Burnout: 5 rounds.

Feats

Build-defining options.

Ocean Coil

Jörmungandr's home water: segments on the sea, and a swimmer's speed. Segments may be placed on water and you gain swim speed equal walking speed.

Tail Around the Party

Guard Rings that steady allies against forced movement across their line. Guard Rings grant allies advantage on saves against forced movement across their boundary.

Venom of the World

Venom beyond immunity: the immune treat it as resistance. Poison immunity treats your Coil poison as resistance; poison resistance still functions normally.

Closing Snap

The completed circuit bites, a hostile pulled ten feet toward the Ring's center on a failed Strength save. When a Segment you place completes a Ring, the circuit closes with a snap: one hostile creature of your choice inside the new Ring makes a Strength saving throw against your technique DC; on a failure it is pulled 10 feet toward the Ring’s center.

Rules integration

Building the character

Excellent for a defensive Strategist or Juggernaut. It rewards players who like drawing geometry on the battlefield but avoids Love Rendezvous-style mandatory sequencing.
Rules integration

Counterplay

Destroy/suppress segments, fight outside completed Rings, teleport over boundaries, or force the user to spend segments on multiple disconnected fronts.
