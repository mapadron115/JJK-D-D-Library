Core law

What the technique actually does.

Core Engine — Void. Void 0–10. Gain 1 when you destroy a summon/barrier, drain CE, or a creature fails against spatial distortion. Void represents increasing intrusion from outside ordinary reality.

The fiction. The prevailing theory holds that the Void is not cursed energy at all, but the mark left where cursed energy was taken away — every barrier broken and every summon devoured carving the user a little further out of the world. Registry scholars describe the technique as an absence wearing a sorcerer: the deeper the Void grows, the more the Blind Eternities look through. Nothing here is inherited or awakened in the ordinary sense; it is what answers when a sorcerer's energy learns the shape of being unmade.

Full progression

Level 1 through 20.

L1Void Limb

15-ft reach natural attack 1d10 force. 1 CE on hit drains 1 CE from target on failed Con save, max once/turn; you do not gain it, but gain 1 Void.

L3Warp Space

2 CE: teleport 30 ft or force target within 60 ft Str save to shift 20 ft through folded space.

L6Scion

3 CE: summon two 1-HP Eldrazi Scions. Sacrifice one to reduce a CE cost by 1 or make 2d8 attack.

L9Annihilator

When you hit same target twice in turn, spend 2 Void: it chooses lose reaction, drop held item, or take extra 3d10 force.

L11Reality Hunger

4 CE, 15-ft sphere, Con save 6d10 force/necrotic; summoned creatures/barriers take +2 dice.

L13Titan Anatomy

At Void 6+, Large, reach +5, resistance B/P/S and force, speed +10. At Void 0–5 return normal.

L15Exile

6 CE + 3 Void, Cha save: phase creature out until end next turn; on return it cannot regain CE until end that turn.

L18Domain — Blind Eternities

Initiative 20 choose DISTORT or CONSUME. Distort: Dex save, creatures swap positions according to your chosen legal permutation and take 4d10 force. Consume: Con save 7d10 void damage and lose 1 CE on failure. Clash bookkeeping: 1,000 clash points (Tier X). A 100+ point advantage collapses the weaker Domain; collapse deals no damage. Barrier AC 12 + PB; barrier HP = 10 × max(1, your CE modifier) + 2,000. Technique Burnout: 3 rounds.

L20Titan Manifest

Void max 15. At 10+ become Huge, reach 25, attacks +2d10, resistance all but psychic/radiant. Once/long rest enter at 10 Void for 1 minute regardless of current Void.

Pinnacle expression

Maximum, Domain, or equivalent.

Maximum — Annihilation Process (12 CE + 10 Void): 40-ft radius Con save 12d12 force/necrotic. Failure also destroys one non-legendary summon, construct, or barrier controlled by target; success half and no destruction. It annihilates manifestations, not concepts.

Feats

Technique Feats

Pathrazer

Your Void Limb phases through obstructions: its attacks ignore half and three-quarters cover, and you can strike a creature through a wall or barrier within your reach. The barrier takes the damage first, and any excess carries through to the creature.

Brood Lineage

Your brood feeds on its own dissolution: when one of your Eldrazi Scions is sacrificed or destroyed, you gain 1 Void, or one surviving Scion deals an extra 1d8 damage on its next attack.

Reality Smasher

At Void 6+, your warped mass barrels through the world: you can move through difficult terrain without penalty and smash through nonmagical barriers and walls, leaving a 5-foot breach in your wake.

Ulamog's Hunger

Reality Hunger devours what it unmakes: if its sphere destroys a summon, construct, or barrier, you regain 2 CE and 1 Void.

Kozilek's Distortion

You can twist a hostile illusion back on its caster: as a reaction for 1 Void when you succeed on a save against an illusion, the caster must succeed on an Intelligence save or suffer the illusion's effect as if they were its target.

Emrakul's Whisper

Once per long rest, when a creature fails a save against one of your technique features, you can whisper through the Blind Eternities: it is charmed by you until the end of its next turn, and you can dictate one action it takes on its next turn.

Rules integration

Edge cases and shared systems.

Shared rulings: Technique DC = 8 + PB + CE ability modifier. Direct rolled attacks may crit and use the campaign Black Flash procedure when eligible. Tokens, autonomous summons, passive damage, and initiative-20 Domain effects cannot Black Flash. Maximum Output modifies ordinary supported techniques, never named Maxima or Domains.
