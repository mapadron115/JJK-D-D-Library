Core law

What the technique actually does.

Core Engine — Void. Void is his negative ledger: zero to ten, minted when he destroys a summon or barrier, drains energy, or when a creature fails against his spatial distortion. It measures how far the Blind Eternities have intruded into ordinary reality through him. The account grows by subtraction. Void 0–10. Gain 1 when you destroy a summon/barrier, drain CE, or a creature fails against spatial distortion. Void represents increasing intrusion from outside ordinary reality.

Field notes. Subject: an absence wearing a sorcerer. Three truths govern him. First, the Void is not cursed energy but its mark: Void accrues when he destroys a summon or barrier, drains energy, or when a creature fails against his spatial distortion — every absence he makes, carving him further out of the world. Second, absence is appetite: his Reality Hunger devours spheres of the field, his Annihilator levies its toll of reactions and held items, and in his Domain he chooses to Distort positions or Consume his enemies' very energy. Third, the body is the last thing it takes: past six Void his anatomy turns Titan — Large, resistant, lengthened — and at ten he manifests Huge, and the Blind Eternities look through. Nothing here is inherited or awakened in the ordinary sense. It is what answers when a sorcerer's energy learns the shape of being unmade.

Full progression

Level 1 through 20.

1Void Limb

The reach of nothing. A fifteen-foot natural strike for a die of ten — and one energy on a hit drains one energy from the target on a failed Constitution save, once per turn. He does not gain what he takes; he gains one Void instead. The arithmetic of absence. 15-ft reach natural attack 1d10 force. 1 CE on hit drains 1 CE from target on failed Con save, max once/turn; you do not gain it, but gain 1 Void.

3Warp Space

The folded map. Two energy: he teleports thirty feet, or forces a target within sixty to save its Strength or be shifted twenty feet through folded space. Distance is a suggestion he declines. 2 CE: teleport 30 ft or force target within 60 ft Str save to shift 20 ft through folded space.

6Scion

The brood's small change. Three energy raises two one-hit-point Eldrazi Scions — and one may be sacrificed to reduce an energy cost by one, or commanded as a bonus action to strike for two dice. The void keeps petty cash. 3 CE: summon two 1-HP Eldrazi Scions (AC 12, speed 30 ft). Sacrifice one to reduce a CE cost by 1, or command one to make a 2d8 attack (melee CE attack, 10-ft reach, using your CE attack bonus); commanding the Scions is a bonus action.

9Annihilator

The toll. When he strikes the same target twice in a turn, two Void buys its choice: lose its reaction, drop what it holds, or take three more dice of force. The absence collects at the gate. When you hit same target twice in turn, spend 2 Void: it chooses lose reaction, drop held item, or take extra 3d10 force.

11Reality Hunger

The meal. Four energy hollows a fifteen-foot sphere — a Constitution save against six dice of force and necrotic, with two more dice against summoned things and barriers. It eats manifestations first. 4 CE, 15-ft sphere, Con save 6d10 force/necrotic; summoned creatures/barriers take +2 dice.

13Titan Anatomy

The body rewritten. Past six Void he stands Large — five feet of reach, resistance to the mundane blows and to force, ten feet of speed — and below six he returns to normal. The intrusion wears him the way weather wears a coast. At Void 6+, Large, reach +5, resistance B/P/S and force, speed +10. At Void 0–5 return normal.

15Exile

The removal from the map. Six energy and three Void: a failed Charisma save phases a creature out until his next turn ends — and on its return it cannot regain energy until that turn ends. To be unmade, briefly, has administrative consequences. 6 CE + 3 Void, Cha save: phase creature out until end next turn; on return it cannot regain CE until end that turn.

18Domain — Blind Eternities

At the twentieth count the blind spot widens, and he chooses its manner: Distort — a Dexterity save as creatures swap positions by his chosen permutation, four dice of force; or Consume — a Constitution save, seven dice of void damage and one energy lost on failure. A thousand clash points of nothing, pressing in. Initiative 20 choose DISTORT or CONSUME. Distort: Dex save, creatures swap positions according to your chosen legal permutation and take 4d10 force. Consume: Con save 7d10 void damage and lose 1 CE on failure. Clash bookkeeping: 1,000 clash points (Tier X). A 100+ point advantage collapses the weaker Domain; collapse deals no damage. Barrier AC 12 + PB; barrier HP = 10 × max(1, your CE modifier) + 2,000. Technique Burnout: 3 rounds.

20Titan Manifest

The full intrusion. His Void maximum becomes fifteen — and past ten he stands Huge, twenty-five feet of reach, two more dice on his attacks, resistance to all but psychic and radiant. Once per long rest he may enter at ten Void for a minute regardless of his current count. The Eternities, looking through. Void max 15. At 10+ become Huge, reach 25, attacks +2d10, resistance all but psychic/radiant. Once/long rest enter at 10 Void for 1 minute regardless of current Void.

Pinnacle expression

Maximum, Domain, or equivalent.

Maximum — Annihilation Process (12 CE + 10 Void; action): The unmaking. Twelve energy and ten Void: a forty-foot radius, a Constitution save against twelve dice of force and necrotic — and failure also destroys one non-legendary summon, construct, or barrier the target controls. It annihilates manifestations, the dossier notes, not concepts. The distinction is cold comfort. 40-ft radius Con save 12d12 force/necrotic. Failure also destroys one non-legendary summon, construct, or barrier controlled by target; success half and no destruction. It annihilates manifestations, not concepts.

Feats

Technique Feats

Pathrazer

His Void Limb phases through obstructions — ignoring half and three-quarters cover, striking through walls and barriers within reach, the barrier taking the damage first and the excess carrying through. Absence does not knock. Your Void Limb phases through obstructions: its attacks ignore half and three-quarters cover, and you can strike a creature through a wall or barrier within your reach. The barrier takes the damage first, and any excess carries through to the creature.

Brood Lineage

His brood feeds on its own dissolution: when a Scion is sacrificed or destroyed he gains one Void — or one surviving Scion deals an extra die on its next attack. The void recycles. Your brood feeds on its own dissolution: when one of your Eldrazi Scions is sacrificed or destroyed, you gain 1 Void, or one surviving Scion deals an extra 1d8 damage on its next attack.

Reality Smasher

Past six Void his warped mass barrels through the world — difficult terrain ignored, nonmagical barriers and walls smashed into five-foot breaches in his wake. The map is redrawn by walking. At Void 6+, your warped mass barrels through the world: you can move through difficult terrain without penalty and smash through nonmagical barriers and walls, leaving a 5-foot breach in your wake.

Ulamog's Hunger

His Reality Hunger devours what it unmakes: if its sphere destroys a summon, construct, or barrier, he regains two energy and one Void. The meal pays for itself. Reality Hunger devours what it unmakes: if its sphere destroys a summon, construct, or barrier, you regain 2 CE and 1 Void.

Kozilek's Distortion

He may twist a hostile illusion back on its caster: as a reaction for one Void, when he succeeds against an illusion, its caster saves its Intelligence or suffers the illusion's effect as its target. The nothing returns the lie to sender. You can twist a hostile illusion back on its caster: as a reaction for 1 Void when you succeed on a save against an illusion, the caster must succeed on an Intelligence save or suffer the illusion's effect as if they were its target.

Emrakul's Whisper

Once per long rest, when a creature fails a save against his technique, he may whisper through the Blind Eternities — Charming it until his next turn ends and dictating one action of its next turn. The void speaks, and it gives orders. Once per long rest, when a creature fails a save against one of your technique features, you can whisper through the Blind Eternities: it is charmed by you until the end of its next turn, and you can dictate one action it takes on its next turn.

Rules integration

Edge cases and shared systems.

Shared rulings: Technique DC = 8 + PB + CE ability modifier. Direct rolled attacks may crit and use the campaign Black Flash procedure when eligible. Tokens, autonomous summons, passive damage, and initiative-20 Domain effects cannot Black Flash. Maximum Output modifies ordinary supported techniques, never named Maxima or Domains.
