Core law

What the technique actually does.

Core Engine, Void. Void is his negative ledger: zero to ten, minted when he destroys a summon or barrier, drains energy, or when a creature fails against his spatial distortion. It measures how far the Blind Eternities have intruded into ordinary reality through him. The account grows by subtraction. Void 0–10. Gain 1 when you destroy a summon/barrier, drain CE, or a creature fails against spatial distortion. Void represents increasing intrusion from outside ordinary reality.

Field notes. Subject: the nothing with an appetite. I have catalogued voids. I have catalogued hungers. I have never catalogued a hunger that was also the map. Two truths govern it. First: the absence is the weapon, Void Limb phases through cover and walls, Reality Hunger unmakes manifestations and eats them first, and past ten Void he stands Huge with the Eternities looking through. Second: the map is the casualty, Distort swaps positions by his chosen permutation, Consume takes seven dice and a point of energy, and the Domain is a thousand clash points of nothing pressing in. I have fought on terrain. I have fought for terrain. I have never fought the absence of terrain, and I do not propose to start.

Full progression

Level 1 through 20.

1Void Limb

Attack action · 1d10 force, 15-ft reach · On hit, spend 1 CE: target makes a Con save or loses 1 CE (max once/turn); you gain 1 Void instead of the drained CE.

The reach of nothing. A fifteen-foot natural strike for a die of ten, and one energy on a hit drains one energy from the target on a failed Constitution save, once per turn. He does not gain what he takes; he gains one Void instead. The arithmetic of absence. 15-ft reach natural attack 1d10 force. 1 CE on hit drains 1 CE from target on failed Con save, max once/turn; you do not gain it, but gain 1 Void.

3Warp Space

Action · 2 CE · Teleport 30 ft yourself, or force a target within 60 ft to make a Strength save or be shifted 20 ft through folded space.

The folded map. Two energy: he teleports thirty feet, or forces a target to save or be shifted twenty feet through folded space. Distance is a suggestion he declines. I have declined suggestions. His declinations, I note, are topological. 2 CE: teleport 30 ft or force target within 60 ft Str save to shift 20 ft through folded space.

6Scion

Action · 3 CE · Summon two 1-HP Eldrazi Scions (AC 12, 30 ft) for 1 minute · Sacrifice one to reduce a CE cost by 1, or as a bonus action command one to make a 2d8 melee CE attack (10-ft reach, your CE attack bonus).

The brood's small change. Three energy raises two one-hit-point Eldrazi Scions, and one may be sacrificed to reduce an energy cost by one, or commanded as a bonus action to strike for two dice. The void keeps petty cash. 3 CE: summon two 1-HP Eldrazi Scions (AC 12, speed 30 ft). Sacrifice one to reduce a CE cost by 1, or command one to make a 2d8 attack (melee CE attack, 10-ft reach, using your CE attack bonus); commanding the Scions is a bonus action.

9Annihilator

Passive trigger · Spend 2 Void · When you hit the same target twice in a turn, it chooses: lose its reaction, drop what it holds, or take 3d10 extra force damage.

The toll. When he strikes the same target twice in a turn, two Void buys its choice: lose its reaction, drop what it holds, or take three more dice of force. The absence collects at the gate. When you hit same target twice in turn, spend 2 Void: it chooses lose reaction, drop held item, or take extra 3d10 force.

11Reality Hunger

Action · 4 CE · 15-ft sphere, Con save · 6d10 force or necrotic damage; summoned creatures and barriers take 8d10.

The meal. Four energy hollows a fifteen-foot sphere, six dice, eight against summoned things and barriers. It eats manifestations first. I have seen hunger. This hunger has table manners: the summoned are the appetizer. 4 CE, 15-ft sphere, Con save 6d10 force/necrotic; summoned creatures/barriers take +2 dice.

13Titan Anatomy

Passive · At Void 6+: Large size, reach +5 ft, resistance to bludgeoning/piercing/slashing and force, speed +10 ft · Below Void 6 you return to normal.

The body rewritten. Past six Void he stands Large, reach, resistance, speed, and below six he returns to normal. The intrusion wears him the way weather wears a coast. I have worn armor. He is worn by the absence, and the absence fits him. At Void 6+, Large, reach +5, resistance B/P/S and force, speed +10. At Void 0–5 return normal.

15Exile

Action · 6 CE + 3 Void · Charisma save: phase the creature out until the end of your next turn; on return it cannot regain CE until the end of that turn.

The removal from the map. Six energy and three Void: a failed Charisma save phases a creature out until his next turn ends, and on return it cannot regain energy. To be unmade, briefly, has administrative consequences. I have been removed from maps. The paperwork took longer than the unmaking. 6 CE + 3 Void, Cha save: phase creature out until end next turn; on return it cannot regain CE until end that turn.

18Domain, Blind Eternities

Domain Expansion · Initiative 20: DISTORT (Dex save; creatures swap positions per your chosen legal permutation and take 4d10 force) or CONSUME (Con save; 7d10 void damage, and lose 1 CE on failure) · 1,000 clash points.

At the twentieth count the blind spot widens: Distort or Consume. A thousand clash points of nothing, pressing in. I have pressed with armies. He presses with absence, on schedule. I do not propose to be present for it. Initiative 20 choose DISTORT or CONSUME. Distort: Dex save, creatures swap positions according to your chosen legal permutation and take 4d10 force. Consume: Con save 7d10 void damage and lose 1 CE on failure. Clash bookkeeping: 1,000 clash points (Tier X). A 100+ point advantage collapses the weaker Domain; collapse deals no damage. Barrier AC 12 + PB; barrier HP = 10 × max(1, your CE modifier) + 2,000. Technique Burnout: 3 rounds.

20Titan Manifest

Passive · Void max 15; at Void 10+: Huge size, 25-ft reach, attacks +2d10, resistance to all but psychic/radiant · Once per long rest, enter at 10 Void for 1 minute regardless of current Void.

The full intrusion. Void maximum fifteen, past ten he stands Huge, twenty-five feet of reach, resistance to all but psychic and radiant. The Eternities, looking through. I have looked through windows. I have never been the window, and I do not propose to meet the gaze coming the other way. Void max 15. At 10+ become Huge, reach 25, attacks +2d10, resistance all but psychic/radiant. Once/long rest enter at 10 Void for 1 minute regardless of current Void.

Pinnacle expression

Maximum, Domain, or equivalent.

Maximum, Annihilation Process (12 CE + 10 Void; action): The unmaking. Twelve energy and ten Void: a forty-foot radius, a Constitution save against twelve dice of force and necrotic, and failure also destroys one non-legendary summon, construct, or barrier the target controls. It annihilates manifestations, the dossier notes, not concepts. The distinction is cold comfort. 40-ft radius Con save 12d12 force/necrotic. Failure also destroys one non-legendary summon, construct, or barrier controlled by target; success half and no destruction. It annihilates manifestations, not concepts.

Feats

Technique Feats

Pathrazer

His Void Limb phases through obstructions, ignoring cover, striking through walls, the barrier taking damage first and the excess carrying through. Absence does not knock. I have knocked. I have also been knocked through. Neither prepared me for the limb that does not acknowledge the wall. Your Void Limb phases through obstructions: its attacks ignore half and three-quarters cover, and you can strike a creature through a wall or barrier within your reach. The barrier takes the damage first, and any excess carries through to the creature.

Brood Lineage

His brood feeds on its own dissolution: a sacrificed Scion becomes Void, or a survivor strikes harder. The void recycles. I have recycled supplies. His supply chain, I note, is his own children, and the margins are excellent. Your brood feeds on its own dissolution: when one of your Eldrazi Scions is sacrificed or destroyed, you gain 1 Void, or one surviving Scion deals an extra 1d8 damage on its next attack.

Reality Smasher

Past six Void his warped mass barrels through the world, difficult terrain ignored, walls smashed into five-foot breaches. The map is redrawn by walking. I have redrawn maps. His revisions are made with his body, at speed, and they are not submitted for approval. At Void 6+, your warped mass barrels through the world: you can move through difficult terrain without penalty and smash through nonmagical barriers and walls, leaving a 5-foot breach in your wake.

Ulamog's Hunger

His Reality Hunger devours what it unmakes: destroying a summon refunds two energy and one Void. The meal pays for itself. I have run campaigns on foraging. His foraging, I observe, is ontological, and the yield is excellent. Reality Hunger devours what it unmakes: if its sphere destroys a summon, construct, or barrier, you regain 2 CE and 1 Void.

Kozilek's Distortion

He may twist a hostile illusion back on its caster, the caster saves or suffers its own illusion. The nothing returns the lie to sender. I have returned correspondence. His returns, I note, are addressed to the sender's own mind. You can twist a hostile illusion back on its caster: as a reaction for 1 Void when you succeed on a save against an illusion, the caster must succeed on an Intelligence save or suffer the illusion's effect as if they were its target.

Emrakul's Whisper

Once per long rest, when a creature fails a save, he may whisper through the Blind Eternities, Charming it and dictating one action of its next turn. The void speaks, and it gives orders. I have given orders. His orders arrive from nowhere, in a voice that is not a voice. I have started checking my own orders for provenance. Once per long rest, when a creature fails a save against one of your technique features, you can whisper through the Blind Eternities: it is charmed by you until the end of its next turn, and you can dictate one action it takes on its next turn.

Rules integration

Edge cases and shared systems.

Shared rulings: Technique DC = 8 + PB + CE ability modifier. Direct rolled attacks may crit and use the campaign Black Flash procedure when eligible. Tokens, autonomous summons, passive damage, and initiative-20 Domain effects cannot Black Flash. Maximum Output modifies ordinary supported techniques, never named Maxima or Domains.
