Hoard-Heart Dragon

Fafnir inspiration. Mark valuables, cursed tools, CE-bearing objects, or meaningful trophies as your Hoard. Staying near the Hoard generates Greed; enemies can steal or relocate the objects to weaken you.

Core law

Character engine

The whole arrangement: mark valuables, tools, CE-bearing objects, trophies as the Hoard, stay within thirty feet and Greed accrues to PB, while enemies may steal or relocate the objects to starve her. Mark valuables, cursed tools, CE-bearing objects, or meaningful trophies as your Hoard. Staying near the Hoard generates Greed: gain 1 Greed at the start of your turn while within 30 feet of at least one Hoard Mark, maximum PB (invented); a Hoard Die is a d8 (invented). Enemies can steal or relocate the objects to weaken you.

Technique save DC: 8 + PB + chosen CE ability modifier. Technique attack: PB + chosen CE ability modifier.

Field notes. Subject: avarice made into architecture. Three truths govern her. First: her second body is her property. Marked valuables, cursed tools, trophies, the hoard is where her energy lives, and she stands near it because it is her; steal from it and she pursues like a body chasing a severed limb. Second: greed is her armor and her leash. Greed buys damage shrugged off, dragon's breath, wings, even a refusal to die, but it only accrues within thirty feet of the hoard, and enemies may steal or relocate the objects to starve her. Third: the dragon was never the fantasy. The fantasy was owning something death could not take, and the CE built the dragon to defend it. Consider her as a vault with teeth: peak durability requires risking objects she actually values, and every scale she wears is mortgaged against what she cannot bear to lose.

Full progression

Level progression

1Covetous Scale

The mark itself: one CE and a bonus action claims an eligible object, and Greed may be spent to blunt a hit, one Hoard Die per Greed, up to PB per round. Covetous Scale (bonus action, 1 CE): mark an eligible object as part of your Hoard. When you are hit, you may spend Greed to reduce the damage by one Hoard Die per Greed spent, up to PB per round.

3Dragon's Claim

Theft has a scent. An unpermitted taker reveals its direction for an hour; her reaction carries her half her speed toward it, and her first hit each turn against the thief deals an extra Hoard Die. A creature that takes a Hoard Mark without your permission reveals its direction to you for 1 hour; as a reaction you may move up to half your speed toward it. Your first hit each turn against that thief deals one additional Hoard Die of damage (invented).

6Wyrm Shape

Three CE, one minute: scales, claws, a climb speed, +1 AC, five feet of reach, and two Greed exhaled as a thirty-foot cone of three Hoard Dice of poison. Wyrm Shape (bonus action, 3 CE, 1 minute): grow scales and claws, gain a climb speed, +1 AC, and +5 feet of reach. As an action, spend 2 Greed (invented) to exhale a 30-foot cone (invented); each creature inside makes a Constitution save vs your CE DC, taking 3 Hoard Dice of poison damage on a failure and half on a success (invented).

11Gold-Sick Immortality

Near three or more Marks, death is refused once per long rest: three Greed spent to stand at one HP with five temporary per Greed. While near 3 or more Hoard Marks, when you are reduced to 0 HP you may spend at least 3 Greed to remain at 1 HP and gain temporary HP equal to 5 per Greed spent (invented). Once per long rest.

18Domain Expansion: Hall Beneath the Glittering Heath

The sky goes first: moorland gold under a low aurora, every blade of heath a coin catching light that has no source. Beneath it the hall rises, vaulted and windowless, its walls built from the hoard itself, swords and crowns mortared into the stone like fossils. The air smells of old metal and cold earth, and everything that glitters is watching.

Her vault, enclosed: sixty feet, one minute, and a Domain Cycle at initiative twenty, Claim marks unattended objects, Covet forces the carrying to drop or be Dragon-Marked, Reckon bleeds the marked three Hoard Dice necrotic. The hoard remains stealable inside. Full-turn action, 20 CE, enclosed 60-foot radius centered on you, up to 1 minute, Technique Focus. When the barrier opens, every unattended object inside is catalogued. The Domain Cycle begins at Claim and advances Claim → Covet → Reckon at initiative 20. Claim: choose up to PB unattended objects; they become Hoard Marks until the Domain ends. Covet: each chosen hostile carrying a Hoard Mark must drop it or become Dragon-Marked until the next initiative 20; a creature protected from the sure-hit may make a Wisdom save to avoid the mark while keeping the object. Reckon: each Dragon-Marked hostile automatically takes 3 Hoard Dice necrotic damage; a protected creature makes a Constitution save, taking half on success. Then all Dragon-Marks end and the cycle returns to Claim. Ordinary attacks still roll and Hoard objects remain stealable.

Feats

Build-defining options.

Appraiser's Eye

The dragon knows her gold: a bonus action's appraisal of value, CE, and curse, and a mark placed in the same motion. As a bonus action, you may appraise one object you can see within 30 feet, learning its approximate value, whether it bears cursed energy, and whether it shows signs of a curse, binding, or forgery. If the object is eligible to become a Hoard Mark, you may immediately mark it with Covetous Scale as part of the same bonus action, still paying the 1 CE. Once per long rest, you may appraise a cursed tool worn or carried by a creature this way, reading its nature despite its keeper.

Jealous Wings

Two Greed in Wyrm Shape buy jealous wings, thirty feet of flight until the end of her next turn. While in Wyrm Shape, you may spend 2 Greed as a bonus action to unfurl jealous wings, gaining 30-foot flight until the end of your next turn.

No Thief Escapes

The hunt shared: allies perceive the golden thread to the thief and may spend reactions to pursue. Once per round when Dragon's Claim reveals a thief's direction, you may spend 1 CE as a bonus action to share the hunt: your allies perceive a glinting golden thread pointing at the thief until the start of your next turn, and each may use their reaction to move up to half their speed toward it.

Accursed Gold (11+)

One Greed makes a stolen Mark unbearably heavy, the carrier's speed halved until it drops the prize. As a bonus action, you may spend 1 Greed to make one stolen Hoard Mark within your Claim's 1-hour window unbearably heavy: the carrier's speed is halved while it carries the mark, until it drops it.
