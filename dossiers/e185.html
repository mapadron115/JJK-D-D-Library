Hoard-Heart Dragon

Fafnir inspiration. Mark valuables, cursed tools, CE-bearing objects, or meaningful trophies as your Hoard. Staying near the Hoard generates Greed; enemies can steal or relocate the objects to weaken you.

Combat roleGreed engine / armored dragon bruiser
Power tierTier IV · Special Grade
Core law

Character engine

Mark valuables, cursed tools, CE-bearing objects, or meaningful trophies as your Hoard. Staying near the Hoard generates Greed; enemies can steal or relocate the objects to weaken you.

Technique save DC: 8 + PB + chosen CE ability modifier. Technique attack: PB + chosen CE ability modifier.

Full progression

Level progression

1Covetous Scale

Bonus, 1 CE marks an eligible object. Spend Greed when hit to reduce damage by one Hoard Die each, up to PB/round.

3Dragon's Claim

A creature taking a Hoard Mark without permission reveals its direction for 1 hour; reaction move half speed toward it. First hit/turn against that thief adds one die.

6Wyrm Shape

Bonus, 3 CE, 1 minute: scales, claws, climb speed, +1 AC, +5 reach; spend Greed for a poison breath cone.

11Gold-Sick Immortality

Near 3+ Hoard Marks, when reduced to 0 HP spend at least 3 Greed to remain at 1 HP and gain temporary HP. Once/long rest.

18Domain Expansion: Hall Beneath the Glittering Heath

Full-turn action, 20 CE, enclosed 60-foot radius centered on you, up to 1 minute, Technique Focus. When the barrier opens, every unattended object inside is catalogued. The Domain Cycle begins at Claim and advances Claim → Covet → Reckon at initiative 20. Claim: choose up to PB unattended objects; they become Hoard Marks until the Domain ends. Covet: each chosen hostile carrying a Hoard Mark must drop it or become Dragon-Marked until the next initiative 20; a creature protected from the sure-hit may make a Wisdom save to avoid the mark while keeping the object. Reckon: each Dragon-Marked hostile automatically takes 3 Hoard Dice necrotic damage; a protected creature makes a Constitution save, taking half on success. Then all Dragon-Marks end and the cycle returns to Claim. Ordinary attacks still roll and Hoard objects remain stealable.

Rules integration

Domain procedure

This entry uses the repaired campaign Domain rules. The level-18 feature above is complete: its printed action, CE cost, radius, duration, initiative-20 law, fallback protection, and counterplay replace any legacy blanket auto-hit wording.
Rules integration

Counterplay

Steal, move, destroy, or separate the user from marked treasure. Peak durability requires risking objects the character actually values.
