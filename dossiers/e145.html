Load-Bearing Principle

Build a structural network out of allies and terrain, absorb pressure for the party, then release accumulated Load through decisive counterattacks.

RoleTank / Damage Distributor
Power tierHigh
Main resourceLoad

The fiction. Jujutsu scholars describe the Load-Bearing Principle as a sheltering instinct that outgrew the body: the user's CE learned the shape of a buttress and began building it out of other people. Pressure is borrowed, never destroyed — the supports take it, the technique stores it, and the discharge is the structure settling its accounts. It is the cursed energy of someone who once stood between a disaster and everyone else, and never stopped standing there.

Full progression

Level Progression

1Supports, Brace the Structure, Load, Discharge

Supports

Bonus Action • 1 CE • 30 feet. Designate a willing creature, unattended object, structure, or 5-foot section of terrain as a Support. You can maintain Supports equal to your proficiency bonus. A Support remains for 10 minutes, until beyond 120 feet, or until you dismiss it.

Stress Die

Your Stress Die is d6, increasing to d8 at 6th, d10 at 11th, and d12 at 18th.

Brace the Structure

Reaction • 1 CE. When a creature Support within 30 feet takes damage, roll your Stress Die and reduce the damage by the result + your CE modifier. If damage is reduced, gain 1 Load. You can hold Load up to your proficiency bonus + CE modifier (minimum 3).

Discharge

Once per turn when you hit with a weapon or cursed attack, spend up to 2 Load. Add one Stress Die of bludgeoning or force damage per Load spent. If 2 Load are spent, a Large or smaller target makes a Strength save or is pushed 10 feet.

3Weight Distribution —

when Brace the Structure reduces damage, you may take up to half of the remaining damage yourself after resistances. If you do, gain 1 additional Load. You can also use Brace against forced movement, adding the Stress Die to the Support’s check/save.

6Continuous Truss —

Support range becomes 60 feet and maximum Supports increases by 2. As a bonus action, spend 2 Load to allow one creature Support to move up to half its speed without provoking opportunity attacks.

11Controlled Demolition —

Action, 4 CE. Choose one Support within 60 feet and spend 3–6 Load. Creatures of your choice within 15 feet make a Dexterity save, taking one Stress Die per Load spent on a failure, half on success; failed targets are also knocked prone. The chosen Support remains unless it was an object with insufficient HP to survive the effect.

18Maximum Technique: Cathedral of Supports —

Bonus Action, 10 CE, 1 minute. Every willing creature within 60 feet becomes a Support without counting against your limit. Brace the Structure has 60-foot range and does not cost CE the first time you use it each round. At the start of your turn gain 1 Load if any Support took damage since your last turn.

Feats

Technique Feats

Foundation First

You survey foundations before the first blow: if you spend 10 minutes studying a location, you can designate up to PB willing creatures there as Supports the instant initiative is rolled within the next 24 hours, without using an action.

Shock Absorber

When you take damage redirected through Weight Distribution, you can Discharge the shock as part of the same reaction: one creature within 15 feet makes a Dexterity save or takes that damage as thunder and is pushed 10 feet away from you. Once per round.

Counterweight

When you push a creature with Discharge, you may move the same distance in the opposite direction without provoking opportunity attacks.

Rules integration

Table Rulings

Damage cannot be redirected in a loop. A single damage instance can benefit from Brace the Structure only once.
Load disappears after 1 minute outside initiative or when you finish a short/long rest.
Objects used as Supports do not become indestructible; the technique only allows them to participate in your network.
