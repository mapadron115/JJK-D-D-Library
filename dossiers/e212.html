Mercy Seat

Improvised-looking cursed chair, 1d8 bludgeoning. A creature sitting or being carried in it is Seated.

FormFolded iron chair

Features & procedures

Properties & techniques

Exact player-facing procedures

Feature

A willing Seated creature has advantage on death saves and cannot be forcibly moved without moving the chair.

Evacuation

action, 1 CE: fold chair around adjacent unconscious ally; carry them as one size smaller and they gain half cover.

Stay With Me

reaction, 2 CE when Seated ally takes damage: reduce by 2 Tool Dice.

Feature

You cannot attack with the chair while it contains a creature.

The fiction. Registry notes describe the Mercy Seat as a battlefield prayer the iron still remembers: the tool's CE imprinted on a sorcerer who sat down beside the dying rather than stepping over them, and it has refused to move quickly ever since. Its protection is stubborn, not powerful — it keeps its occupant seated because the one time it mattered, nobody stood up.

Rules integration

Cursed-tool attunement

Campaign canon

Cursed-tool attunement: you have PB attunement slots (2–6). This Grade 2 tool functions as a mundane tool while unattuned, or with its full properties for one slot and a one-hour ritual at GM discretion. Attunement ends voluntarily over a short rest, on death, or when you replace it while over capacity. Distance does not break attunement. Cursed-tool attunement is separate from magic-item attunement.
Balance & counterplay. A rescue tool whose best defense costs the wielder offense and positioning.
