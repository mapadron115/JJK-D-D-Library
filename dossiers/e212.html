Mercy Seat

Improvised-looking cursed chair, 1d8 bludgeoning. A creature sitting or being carried in it is Seated.

FormFolded iron chair Improvised-looking iron, a battlefield prayer the metal still remembers.
RoleProtection / extraction It keeps the dying seated because once, that mattered more than anything else on the field.
EngineMercy Stubborn, not powerful, the refusal to move quickly, made a tool.
Attunement1 hour* An hour's ritual, the chair must accept that you sit beside the dying, not over them.
Features & procedures

Properties & techniques

Exact player-facing procedures

Feature

Passive · A willing Seated creature has advantage on death saves · cannot be forcibly moved without moving the chair.

The seated are steadied: advantage on death saves, and none can be moved without moving the chair. The Mercy Seat keeps its occupant the way a vigil keeps its dead, by sitting down and not leaving. A willing Seated creature has advantage on death saves and cannot be forcibly moved without moving the chair.

Evacuation

Action · 1 CE · Fold the chair around an adjacent unconscious ally · carry them as one size smaller · they gain half cover.

One CE, an action: fold the chair around an adjacent unconscious ally and carry them smaller, half-covered. The rescue is slow and deliberate; the tool has refused to move quickly since the day it mattered. action, 1 CE: fold chair around adjacent unconscious ally; carry them as one size smaller and they gain half cover.

Stay With Me

Reaction · 2 CE · When a Seated ally takes damage · reduce it by 2 Tool Dice.

Two CE as a reaction, when the seated ally is hurt: blunt the blow by two Tool Dice. The chair interposes its iron between the dying and the world, that is its whole technique. reaction, 2 CE when Seated ally takes damage: reduce by 2 Tool Dice.

Feature

Limit · You cannot attack with the chair while it contains a creature.

The cost, stated plainly: you cannot attack with the chair while it carries someone. Its best defense is purchased with your offense, protection and position, paid in stillness. You cannot attack with the chair while it contains a creature.

Field notes. Subject: a vigil. Three truths govern it. First, its cursed energy imprinted on a sorcerer who sat beside the dying rather than stepping over them, and it has refused to move quickly ever since. Second, its protection is stubborn, not powerful: steadied death saves, half cover, a blunted blow, the arithmetic of someone staying put. Third, the price is symmetrical: while the chair carries a body it cannot strike one; wielder and occupant are both removed from the offense to purchase the defense. Mark this: it keeps its occupant seated because the one time it mattered, nobody stood up. A tool that wins nothing but keeps someone alive has kept enough.

Rules integration

Cursed-tool attunement

Campaign canon

Cursed-tool attunement: you have PB attunement slots (2–6). This Grade 2 tool functions as a mundane tool while unattuned, or with its full properties for one slot and a one-hour ritual at GM discretion. Attunement ends voluntarily over a short rest, on death, or when you replace it while over capacity. Distance does not break attunement. Cursed-tool attunement is separate from magic-item attunement.
Balance & counterplay. A rescue tool whose best defense costs the wielder offense and positioning.
