Mercy Seat

Improvised-looking cursed chair, 1d8 bludgeoning. A creature sitting or being carried in it is Seated.

FormFolded iron chair
RoleProtection / extraction
EngineMercy
Attunement1 hour*
Properties & techniquesExact player-facing procedures

Feature

A willing Seated creature has advantage on death saves and cannot be forcibly moved without moving the chair.

Evacuation

action, 1 CE: fold chair around adjacent unconscious ally; carry them as one size smaller and they gain half cover.

Stay With Me

reaction, 2 CE when Seated ally takes damage: reduce by 2 Tool Dice.

Feature

You cannot attack with the chair while it contains a creature.

Balance & counterplay. A rescue tool whose best defense costs the wielder offense and positioning.
