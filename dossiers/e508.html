Feature map

Level-by-level features

L1

Reaper's Sickles

As an action for 1 CE, you manifest a pair of sickles spun from cursed energy into your hands. Each sickle is a melee weapon with the light and finesse properties, dealing 1d6 slashing damage on a hit.

As a bonus action, you can join the pair into a greatsickle — a two-handed weapon with reach 10 ft. that deals 2d6 slashing damage — or split the greatsickle back into the pair. The sickles persist until you dismiss them (no action required) or fall unconscious.

L1

Death's Mark

"I am Death, and I come for you." As a bonus action, you mark one creature you can see within 120 feet as your due. You always know the marked creature's direction and distance from you, and it has disadvantage on Dexterity (Stealth) checks contested by your Wisdom (Perception).

You can maintain only one mark at a time; it lasts until you finish a long rest or mark another creature.

L3

The Whistle

As an action for 2 CE, you whistle — a thin, merry tune that freezes the blood. Each creature of your choice within 60 feet that can hear you must succeed on a Wisdom saving throw against your CE save DC or become frightened of you for 1 minute. A frightened creature repeats the saving throw at the end of each of its turns, ending the effect on a success. You hear him before you see him.

A creature already frightened of you when you whistle takes 2d6 psychic damage (3d6 at 11th level).

L6

"Why Did You Run?"

When your marked target moves, you can use your reaction to move up to your speed toward it; this movement doesn't provoke opportunity attacks.

Additionally, as a bonus action for 1 CE, you can vanish and reappear in an unoccupied space you can see within 15 feet of your marked target — always ahead of it, cutting off its escape. (Teleport range: up to 120 feet.)

L11

Wounds That Do Not Close

The cuts of Death's sickles refuse to mend. When you damage a creature with your sickles, it can't regain hit points until the end of your next turn, and any magical healing — including reverse cursed technique — restores only half its rolled amount unless the healer spends 2 additional CE to close the wound cleanly.

Your marked target has disadvantage on saving throws against your Whistle. "I love the smell of fear."

L18

Domain Expansion: The Last Whistle

Rules domain. Darkness — a forest road under a white moon, and the whistle looping endlessly. Never louder. Never closer. Until it is. While the domain stands, its rules bind every creature inside except you. See Pinnacle below.

No Running. No creature can willingly end its move farther from you than where it began it, and teleportation fails outright — the whistle is already there.

The Whistle Counts. At initiative count 20, the whistle sounds: each creature of your choice inside must succeed on a Wisdom saving throw against your CE save DC or gain one Due counter (maximum 3). One Due: frightened of you while inside. Two Due: speed halved and can't take reactions. Three Due: at the start of each of its turns, Constitution saving throw or drop to 0 hit points.

Face Him. "Pick up your sword!" When a creature hits you with an attack, it removes one Due counter — courage counts. Due counters vanish when the domain ends or when a creature leaves its radius.

L20

The Inevitable

Once per long rest, when you hit your marked target with a sickle attack, you can declare the blow a killing one. The target must succeed on a Constitution saving throw against your CE save DC or drop to 0 hit points. On a success it takes 8d10 necrotic damage instead. Death collects.

Pinnacle expression

Reversal and Domain.

Capstone material

Reversal: Life, Embraced

The only thing that ever turned Death away. As an action for 4 CE, you touch a willing creature (or yourself): it regains 4d8 + your CE modifier hit points, the frightened condition ends on it, and for 1 minute it can't become frightened and has advantage on death saving throws. "Live it well."

Domain Expansion: The Last Whistle

18th level. Rules domain — no sure-hit; the rules are the weapon. Activation: full turn, 20 CE (D008 baseline for a normal Domain). Clash: 1000 points; burnout 3 rounds; duration 3 min (Tier V mapping); radius 120 ft.

No Running: no creature inside except you can willingly end its move farther from you than where it began it; teleportation fails outright. The Whistle Counts: at initiative count 20, each creature of your choice inside makes a Wisdom saving throw against your CE save DC or gains one Due counter (maximum 3) — one: frightened of you while inside; two: speed halved and can't take reactions; three: Constitution saving throw at the start of each of its turns or drop to 0 hit points. Face Him: "Pick up your sword!" — when a creature hits you with an attack, it removes one Due counter. Due counters vanish when the domain ends or when a creature leaves its radius.

Innate Technique Feats

Build-defining options.

These are optional character-building feats. You must meet each listed prerequisite before selecting it.

Prerequisite: The Whistle

Pick Up Your Sword

When a creature frightened of you makes an attack against anyone other than you, you can use your reaction to snarl a challenge: it must succeed on a Wisdom saving throw against your CE save DC or target you with the attack instead. Death despises prey that won't face him.

Prerequisite: Death's Mark

Red in the Dark

Your eyes burn red in the dark. You gain darkvision out to 60 feet (or extend your existing darkvision by 30 feet), and your marked target gains no benefit from invisibility, darkness, or heavy obscurement against you.

Prerequisite: "Why Did You Run?"

Snuffed Light

When you teleport with Why Did You Run?, you can spend 1 additional CE to bring the dark with you: nonmagical light within 20 feet of your destination is extinguished, and magical light there is suppressed for 1 minute.

Prerequisite: Reaper's Sickles

The Parting Cleave

When you hit a creature with the greatsickle, you can split the weapon mid-swing: choose a second creature within 5 feet of the target, which takes 1d6 slashing damage as the separating blades bite.

Prerequisite: Wounds That Do Not Close

Death's Mercy

Once per long rest, when a creature within 30 feet of you drops to 0 hit points, you can use your reaction to stay its end: it stabilizes at 1 hit point instead. Death decides when the due is paid.

Rules integration

Edge cases and shared systems.

Campaign canon notes

Cursed Energy baseline: CE pool is based on class; CE ability is Charisma or Wisdom (player's choice). CE save DC = 8 + CE ability modifier + proficiency bonus. Regain all spent CE on a long rest. Sickle attacks use Dexterity (finesse) for attack and damage rolls.
One mark at a time: marking a new target ends the previous mark.
The Whistle and hearing: only creatures that can hear the whistle are affected; the bonus psychic damage against already-frightened creatures applies once per whistle.
Why Did You Run? teleport: up to 120 ft.; the destination must be unoccupied, visible to you, and within 15 ft. of your marked target.
Domain activation defaults: rendered as full turn + 20 CE per the D008 normal-Domain baseline, with Tier V clash stats (1000 / 3-round burnout / 3 min) and a 120-ft radius.
The Last Whistle is a rules domain: it has no sure-hit — the rules are the weapon. Due counters are tracked per creature (maximum 3); leaving the domain's radius or the domain ending clears them. Only willing movement is bound by No Running — forced movement can still drag a creature out, which is the rescue play.
The Inevitable is a saving-throw effect: legendary resistances, immunities, and other save interactions apply normally.
Reversal vs. Maximum: Death's Due's pinnacle is written as a Reversal (Life, Embraced), not a Maximum Technique; it occupies the technique's pinnacle slot.
