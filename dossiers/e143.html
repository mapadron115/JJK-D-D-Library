Core law

What the technique actually does.

Core Engine — Mana Colors. You have five color tracks, each 0–3. At long rest distribute a number of Mana Mastery points equal to PB+2 among them (max 3 each). A color at 1 unlocks basics, 2 advanced magic, 3 its Signature. You can therefore specialize or become broadly five-color.

Full progression

Level 1 through 20.

1White / Blue

White: 1 CE shield reaction +2 AC or heal 2d8. Blue: 1 CE illusion/30-ft push; reaction 2 CE impose -1d8 on technique attack.

3Black / Red / Green

Black: pay 1d8 HP to add 2d8 necrotic or animate 1-HP token. Red: 60-ft 3d8 fire/lightning. Green: 2 CE reinforce body +10 speed/+1d8 melee or summon vine restraint.

6Two-Color Gold

If two colors are rank 2+, unlock one pair spell: Azorius detention, Dimir mind theft, Rakdos sacrifice-flame, Gruul overrun, Selesnya mass ward, etc. Choose two known pairs at rest.

9Color Signatures

Rank 3: White 40-Integrity barrier; Blue technique contest counter; Black 5d10 drain; Red 20-ft 6d10 blast; Green 60-HP elemental. Each 3–4 CE.

11Three-Color Shard/Wedge

With three colors at 2+, choose one tri-color identity after rest. Its once-per-short-rest Convergence costs an action and 5 CE, deals exactly 7d10 damage in a 20-foot-radius area within 90 feet, and applies up to two explicitly defined color riders; see the complete Three-Color Convergence section below.

13Mana Fixing

Once/turn convert 2 CE into one temporary Mastery rank until end turn, max rank 3. Cannot unlock a Signature more than once/round this way.

15Four-Color Mastery

If four colors at 2+, first spell each turn using a different color than your previous spell costs 1 less CE.

18Domain — The World Tree

At initiative 20 choose a color: White grants allies 4d10 temp HP; Blue hostiles Int save or lose reactions; Black hostiles Con save 5d10 necrotic and you heal half; Red Dex save 7d10 fire; Green summons two 30-HP elementals until next count 20. Clash bookkeeping: 1,000 clash points (Tier X). A 100+ point advantage collapses the weaker Domain; collapse deals no damage. Barrier AC 12 + PB; barrier HP = 10 × max(1, your CE modifier) + 2,000. Technique Burnout: 3 rounds.

20WUBRG

Mana Mastery pool becomes 15, allowing 3 in all five colors. Once/long rest for 1 minute, first feature of each color used during the transformation costs 0 CE and gains +1 die.

Features & procedures

Three-Color Convergence

After a rest, choose one available three-color identity with all three represented colors at rank 2 or higher. Once per short rest, use an action and 5 CE to choose a point within 90 feet and create a 20-foot-radius area.

Choose one represented-color damage type: White = radiant; Blue = cold or force; Black = necrotic; Red = fire or lightning; Green = bludgeoning. Each hostile creature in the area makes a Dexterity save against your CE save DC: 7d10 damage on failure or half on success.

Choose up to two different riders from the represented colors. Enemy riders apply only to creatures that failed the save; White protects chosen allies in the area instead.

  • White: chosen allies gain temporary HP equal to PB + your CE modifier.
  • Blue: push or pull each failed target up to 15 feet.
  • Black: failed targets cannot regain HP until the end of your next turn.
  • Red: failed targets ignite and take 1d10 fire damage at the start of their next turn.
  • Green: failed targets have speed 0 until the end of your next turn.

Repeated applications of the same rider do not stack. Specific feature wording elsewhere may change the save ability, but the default is Dexterity.

Pinnacle expression

Maximum, Domain, or equivalent.

Maximum — Chromatic Convergence (12 CE): requires at least rank 2 in all five colors. Choose five different 15-ft zones within 120 ft: White protects, Blue displaces, Black drains, Red detonates, Green restrains/overgrows. Then a central target affected by at least three zones takes an additional 8d10 radiant/force, Con half.

Feats

Technique Feats

Mana Dork

Once per short rest, you can grow a 1-HP mana dork for 1 minute: it holds one temporary Mastery rank of a color you choose, and any ally within 5 feet of it can spend that rank once as if it were their own rank-1 color feature.

Fetchland

Your mana base is a landscape you can reshape. As an action once per short rest, you may redistribute one Mana Mastery point from one color to another — pulling the right 'land' from the deck, in the old parlance. The point must come from a color with at least 2 ranks, and the receiving color cannot exceed 3 ranks this way. Colors you have already spent from this turn are locked; you cannot fetch what is already cast.

Gold Spell

Your guild repertoire runs deeper than most. You learn one additional two-color pair spell beyond the two you choose at rest, drawn from the same list — Azorius detention, Dimir mind theft, Rakdos sacrifice-flame, Gruul overrun, Selesnya mass ward, and the rest. This third pair is fixed, the guild you apprenticed in, while your other two remain re-choosable at each rest. It costs the same CE and obeys the same rank requirements as your other pair spells.

Command Tower

Once per long rest, you can raise your Command Tower for 1 minute: during that time you can cast any color's rank-1 basics by spending 1 CE, regardless of your current Mastery distribution.

Progenitus Echo

Once per long rest, as a reaction when you or an ally within 10 feet would take damage, you can echo Progenitus's immunity to harm: choose one damage type, and you and allies within 10 feet gain resistance to that type until the start of your next turn.

Door to Nothingness

Once per long rest, while you have at least 1 rank in all five colors, you can open the Door as an action for 10 CE: one creature within 60 feet makes a Charisma save or is exiled to the void between colors until the end of your next turn, returning with 1 level of exhaustion.

Rules integration

Edge cases and shared systems.

Shared rulings: Technique DC = 8 + PB + CE ability modifier. Direct rolled attacks may crit and use the campaign Black Flash procedure when eligible. Tokens, autonomous summons, passive damage, and initiative-20 Domain effects cannot Black Flash. Maximum Output modifies ordinary supported techniques, never named Maxima or Domains.
