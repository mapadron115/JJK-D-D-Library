Core law

What the technique actually does.

Core Engine, Mana Colors. Mana Colors are her five-chambered heart: five tracks, zero to three each, and at every long rest she distributes her proficiency plus two in Mastery among them, three at most per color. Rank one unlocks a color's basics, rank two its advanced magic, rank three its Signature. She may specialize, or become broadly five-color. The council is seated nightly. You have five color tracks, each 0–3. At long rest distribute a number of Mana Mastery points equal to PB+2 among them (max 3 each). A color at 1 unlocks basics, 2 advanced magic, 3 its Signature. You can therefore specialize or become broadly five-color.

Field notes. Subject: the council of five. I have faced coalitions. I have built them. The Ascension is a coalition that never adjourns, five colors, five courts, five signatures, convened in a single will. Two truths govern it. First: the council is the arsenal, White wards, Blue counters, Black drains, Red detonates, Green overruns; pairs unlock at rank two, shards at three colors, and the Convergence strikes for exactly seven dice. Second: the tree is the Domain, at the twentieth count she chooses one color's edict under a thousand clash points, and the full council at Mastery fifteen casts each color's first feature for nothing. I have negotiated with councils. This one does not negotiate. It votes, and the vote is unanimous, and the ayes have it.

The first two courts take their seats. White: shield or healing. Blue: illusion or a thirty-foot push, and a reaction subtracting a die from a hostile technique attack. The other three courts convene. Black pays flesh for necrotic, Red throws fire and lightning, Green reinforces or restrains. Ambition, fury, and growth, the council is full. Five voices. 6Two-Color Gold

Passive · With two colors at rank 2+, unlock one two-color pair spell (choose two known at rest) · Pair spells are actions or bonus actions costing 2–3 CE at 60 ft (see dossier for each spell’s full text).

The alliances. With two colors at rank two she unlocks pair spells, detention, mind theft, sacrifice-flame, overrun, mass ward, choosing two at rest. Diplomacy, with casting costs. I have practiced diplomacy. Hers is binding, and the signatures are in fire. If two colors are rank 2+, unlock one pair spell: Azorius detention, Dimir mind theft, Rakdos sacrifice-flame, Gruul overrun, Selesnya mass ward, etc. Choose two known pairs at rest. Pair spells: Azorius Detention (action, 3 CE, 60 ft): one creature makes a Wisdom save or is restrained by law-magic until the end of your next turn. Dimir Mind Theft (action, 3 CE, 60 ft): the target makes an Intelligence save; on failure you learn its surface thoughts and it takes 3d8 psychic. Rakdos Sacrifice-Flame (action, 3 CE): pay 1d8 HP; creatures in a 15-ft burst within 60 ft make a Dexterity save, taking 5d8 fire on failure, half on success. Gruul Overrun (bonus action, 2 CE): for 1 minute your melee attacks deal +1d8 and you gain +10 ft speed. Selesnya Mass Ward (action, 3 CE): up to PB allies within 30 ft gain 2d8 temporary HP.

Hers, I observe, are written in the battlefield. 11Three-Color Shard/Wedge

Action · 5 CE, once per short rest · 20-ft radius within 90 ft, Dex save · 7d10 damage (type set by represented color; half on success) plus up to two color riders.

The tri-color identity. With three colors at rank two she chooses one shard or wedge, ten names for ten ways the council can agree, and its Convergence strikes for exactly seven dice with two riders. I have named operations. Her names, I note, are not codenames but jurisdictions. With three colors at 2+, choose one tri-color identity after rest. Its once-per-short-rest Convergence costs an action and 5 CE, deals exactly 7d10 damage in a 20-foot-radius area within 90 feet, and applies up to two explicitly defined color riders:

Passive · Convergence damage type set by represented color: White radiant, Blue cold or force, Black necrotic, Red fire or lightning, Green bludgeoning · Hostiles in the area make a Dex save: 7d10 damage, half on success.

Choose one represented-color damage type: White = radiant; Blue = cold or force; Black = necrotic; Red = fire or lightning; Green = bludgeoning. Each hostile creature in the area makes a Dexterity save against your CE save DC: 7d10 damage on failure or half on success.

Passive · Choose up to two different riders from the represented colors; enemy riders apply only to creatures that failed the save, while White protects chosen allies instead.

Choose up to two different riders from the represented colors. Enemy riders apply only to creatures that failed the save; White protects chosen allies in the area instead.

Passive · Riders: White = chosen allies gain PB + CE modifier temp HP · Blue = push or pull each failed target 15 ft · Black = failed targets can’t regain HP until end of your next turn · Red = failed targets ignite, taking 1d10 fire at start of next turn · Green = failed targets’ speed 0 until end of your next turn.

  • White: chosen allies gain temporary HP equal to PB + your CE modifier.
  • Blue: push or pull each failed target up to 15 feet.
  • Black: failed targets cannot regain HP until the end of your next turn.
  • Red: failed targets ignite and take 1d10 fire damage at the start of their next turn.
  • Green: failed targets have speed 0 until the end of your next turn.

Passive · The same rider does not stack on repeated applications; the Convergence’s default save is Dexterity (specific features may change it).

Repeated applications of the same rider do not stack. Specific feature wording elsewhere may change the save ability, but the default is Dexterity.

13Mana Fixing

Free (once/turn) · Convert 2 CE into one temporary Mastery rank until end of turn (max rank 3) · Cannot unlock a Signature more than once per round this way.

The rebalance. Once per turn she may convert two energy into one temporary Mastery rank until her turn ends, three at most, though no Signature may be unlocked this way more than once per round. The council reshuffles its seats mid-debate. Once/turn convert 2 CE into one temporary Mastery rank until end turn, max rank 3. Cannot unlock a Signature more than once/round this way.

15Four-Color Mastery

Passive · With four colors at rank 2+, the first spell each turn using a different color than your previous spell costs 1 less CE.

The rotation. With four colors at rank two, the first spell each turn in a different color than her previous costs one less. The council rewards those who hear every voice in turn. I have rotated troops. She rotates unanimity itself, and the discount is for listening. If four colors at 2+, first spell each turn using a different color than your previous spell costs 1 less CE.

18Domain, The World Tree

Domain Expansion · Initiative 20: choose one color’s edict — White: allies gain 4d10 temp HP · Blue: hostiles Int save or lose reactions · Black: hostiles Con save, 5d10 necrotic, you heal half · Red: hostiles Dex save, 7d10 fire · Green: summon two 30-HP elementals until next initiative 20 · 1,000 clash points.

At the twentieth count the tree shows its branches, and she chooses one color's edict. A thousand clash points hold the trunk. I have held positions. Hers is held by the tree, the council, and the arithmetic of a thousand points. I do not propose to take it. At initiative 20 choose a color: White grants allies 4d10 temp HP; Blue hostiles Int save or lose reactions; Black hostiles Con save 5d10 necrotic and you heal half; Red Dex save 7d10 fire; Green summons two 30-HP elementals until next count 20. Clash bookkeeping: 1,000 clash points (Tier X). A 100+ point advantage collapses the weaker Domain; collapse deals no damage. Barrier AC 12 + PB; barrier HP = 10 × max(1, your CE modifier) + 2,000. Technique Burnout: 3 rounds.

Passive · Mana Mastery pool becomes 15 (rank 3 in all five colors) · Once per long rest for 1 minute: the first feature of each color used costs 0 CE and gains +1 die.

The full council. Mastery fifteen, three in all five colors, and once per long rest, for a minute, each color's first feature costs nothing and gains a die. Five voices, one will, no dissent. I have commanded unity. Hers is not commanded. It is structural. That frightens me more than disobedience ever did. Mana Mastery pool becomes 15, allowing 3 in all five colors. Once/long rest for 1 minute, first feature of each color used during the transformation costs 0 CE and gains +1 die.

Pinnacle expression

Maximum, Domain, or equivalent.

Maximum, Chromatic Convergence (12 CE; action): All five courts speak at once. Twelve energy, rank two in all five colors required: five different fifteen-foot zones within a hundred and twenty feet, White protects, Blue displaces, Black drains, Red detonates, Green restrains, and a central target caught by three or more zones takes eight more dice of radiant force. The council's unanimous verdict. requires at least rank 2 in all five colors. Choose five different 15-ft zones within 120 ft: White protects (allies inside gain resistance to the Convergence damage); Blue displaces (failed-save targets are pushed 15 ft); Black drains (4d10 necrotic, and no healing until your next turn); Red detonates (4d10 fire, Dexterity save for half); Green restrains/overgrows (restrained until your next turn on a failed Strength save). Then a central target affected by at least three zones takes an additional 8d10 radiant/force, Con half.

Mana Dork

Once per short rest she may grow a one-hit-point mana dork, holding one temporary Mastery rank, spendable by any ally within five feet. The council keeps a page. I have kept pages. Hers carries a vote. Once per short rest, you can grow a 1-HP mana dork for 1 minute: it holds one temporary Mastery rank of a color you choose, and any ally within 5 feet of it can spend that rank once as if it were their own rank-1 color feature.

Fetchland

Her mana base is a landscape she can reshape, moving Mastery between colors, never past three, spent colors locked. Pulling the right land from the deck, in the old parlance. I have reshaped terrain. She reshapes the ground her own power stands on, mid-battle, as an action. The parlance, I confess, is inadequate. Your mana base is a landscape you can reshape. As an action once per short rest, you may redistribute one Mana Mastery point from one color to another, pulling the right 'land' from the deck, in the old parlance. The point must come from a color with at least 2 ranks, and the receiving color cannot exceed 3 ranks this way. Colors you have already spent from this turn are locked; you cannot fetch what is already cast.

Gold Spell

Her guild repertoire runs deeper: one additional pair spell, fixed, the guild she apprenticed in, while the other two remain re-choosable. The old allegiance, kept. I have kept allegiances. Hers is not loyalty but load-bearing structure. Your guild repertoire runs deeper than most. You learn one additional two-color pair spell beyond the two you choose at rest, drawn from the same list, Azorius detention, Dimir mind theft, Rakdos sacrifice-flame, Gruul overrun, Selesnya mass ward, and the rest. This third pair is fixed, the guild you apprenticed in, while your other two remain re-choosable at each rest. It costs the same CE and obeys the same rank requirements as your other pair spells.

Command Tower

Once per long rest she may raise her Command Tower, and for a minute, cast any color's rank-one basics for one energy. For sixty seconds, every court is in session. I have called councils into session. Hers convenes at full strength for sixty seconds, on demand. The minutes, I note, are the ones that matter. Once per long rest, you can raise your Command Tower for 1 minute: during that time you can cast any color's rank-1 basics by spending 1 CE, regardless of your current Mastery distribution.

Progenitus Echo

Once per long rest, as a reaction, she echoes Progenitus's immunity: one damage type, she and allies within ten feet resisting it. Protection, from the oldest precedent. I have cited precedent. Hers is not cited but embodied, the oldest immunity, worn for a turn. Once per long rest, as a reaction when you or an ally within 10 feet would take damage, you can echo Progenitus's immunity to harm: choose one damage type, and you and allies within 10 feet gain resistance to that type until the start of your next turn.

Door to Nothingness

Once per long rest, with all five colors ranked, she may open the Door: one creature saves or is exiled to the void between colors, returning with exhaustion. The council's final sanction. I have sanctioned. Her sanctions, I observe, are not punishments but removals, from the map, from the colors, from the proceedings. The ayes have it. Once per long rest, while you have at least 1 rank in all five colors, you can open the Door as an action for 10 CE: one creature within 60 feet makes a Charisma save or is exiled to the void between colors until the end of your next turn, returning with 1 level of exhaustion.

Rules integration

Edge cases and shared systems.

Shared rulings: Technique DC = 8 + PB + CE ability modifier. Direct rolled attacks may crit and use the campaign Black Flash procedure when eligible. Tokens, autonomous summons, passive damage, and initiative-20 Domain effects cannot Black Flash. Maximum Output modifies ordinary supported techniques, never named Maxima or Domains.
