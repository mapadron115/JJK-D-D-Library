Core law

What the technique actually does.

Core Engine — Mana Colors. Mana Colors are her five-chambered heart: five tracks, zero to three each, and at every long rest she distributes her proficiency plus two in Mastery among them — three at most per color. Rank one unlocks a color's basics, rank two its advanced magic, rank three its Signature. She may specialize, or become broadly five-color. The council is seated nightly. You have five color tracks, each 0–3. At long rest distribute a number of Mana Mastery points equal to PB+2 among them (max 3 each). A color at 1 unlocks basics, 2 advanced magic, 3 its Signature. You can therefore specialize or become broadly five-color.

Field notes. Subject: a council of five that shares one throne. Three truths govern them. First, five appetites, one purse: at each long rest they distribute their proficiency plus two in Mana Mastery across five color tracks — White, Blue, Black, Red, Green — three ranks at most per color, and may specialize deep or spread wide. Second, depth buys signatures: rank one unlocks a color's basics, rank two its advanced magic, rank three its Signature — shield and healing, illusion and counter, paid blood, bolt and blast, body and beast. Third, the council convenes: two colors at rank two unlock a gold pair spell, three colors a tri-color Convergence, and the Maximum demands rank two in all five — five zones, five edicts, one central target. Registry scholars call convergence the technique's true expression: not mastery of five colors, but one sorcerer's refusal to be only one kind of person.

Full progression

Level 1 through 20.

1White / Blue

The first two courts take their seats. White: a reactive shield or a healer's two dice. Blue: an illusion or a thirty-foot push — and a reaction that subtracts a die from a hostile technique attack. Order and cunning, at one energy each. White: 1 CE shield (reaction) +2 AC, or heal 2d8 (action). Blue: 1 CE illusion or 30-ft push (action); reaction 2 CE impose -1d8 on a technique attack.

3Black / Red / Green

The other three courts convene. Black pays a die of her own flesh to add two dice of necrotic to a hit, or raises a one-hit-point token. Red throws a sixty-foot bolt of fire and lightning. Green reinforces the body or summons a vine restraint. Ambition, fury, and growth — the council is full. Black (action): pay 1d8 HP to add 2d8 necrotic to a hit, or animate a 1-HP token. Red (action): 60-ft ranged CE attack, 3d8 fire/lightning. Green (action): 2 CE reinforce body (+10 speed, +1d8 melee) or summon a vine restraint.

6Two-Color Gold

The alliances. With two colors at rank two she unlocks pair spells — Azorius detention, Dimir mind theft, Rakdos sacrifice-flame, Gruul overrun, Selesnya mass ward, and the rest — choosing two known pairs at rest. Diplomacy, with casting costs. If two colors are rank 2+, unlock one pair spell: Azorius detention, Dimir mind theft, Rakdos sacrifice-flame, Gruul overrun, Selesnya mass ward, etc. Choose two known pairs at rest. Pair spells: Azorius Detention (action, 3 CE, 60 ft): one creature makes a Wisdom save or is restrained by law-magic until the end of your next turn. Dimir Mind Theft (action, 3 CE, 60 ft): the target makes an Intelligence save; on failure you learn its surface thoughts and it takes 3d8 psychic. Rakdos Sacrifice-Flame (action, 3 CE): pay 1d8 HP; creatures in a 15-ft burst within 60 ft make a Dexterity save, taking 5d8 fire on failure, half on success. Gruul Overrun (bonus action, 2 CE): for 1 minute your melee attacks deal +1d8 and you gain +10 ft speed. Selesnya Mass Ward (action, 3 CE): up to PB allies within 30 ft gain 2d8 temporary HP.

9Color Signatures

The courts' signatures, each an action at three or four energy: White raises a forty-Integrity wall; Blue counters through contest; Black drains five dice of necrotic; Red detonates a twenty-foot blast of six dice; Green calls a sixty-hit-point elemental. Rank three is when a color signs its name. Rank 3 (each an action; 3–4 CE): White — 40-Integrity barrier (10-ft wall, 1 minute); Blue — technique contest counter (see Rules — Shared Resolutions) (reaction); Black — 5d10 necrotic drain on one creature within 60 ft (Constitution save, half); Red — 20-ft blast within 60 ft, 6d10 fire (Dexterity save, half); Green — 60-HP elemental (AC 14, speed 30 ft, slam 3d8 using your CE attack bonus; commanding it is a bonus action).

11Three-Color Shard/Wedge

The tri-color identity. With three colors at rank two she chooses one shard or wedge after rest — Bant, Esper, Grixis, Jund, Naya, Abzan, Jeskai, Sultai, Mardu, or Temur — and its once-per-short-rest Convergence strikes for exactly seven dice in a twenty-foot radius, with two riders drawn from her represented colors. Ten names for ten ways the council can agree. With three colors at 2+, choose one tri-color identity after rest. Its once-per-short-rest Convergence costs an action and 5 CE, deals exactly 7d10 damage in a 20-foot-radius area within 90 feet, and applies up to two explicitly defined color riders:

Choose one represented-color damage type: White = radiant; Blue = cold or force; Black = necrotic; Red = fire or lightning; Green = bludgeoning. Each hostile creature in the area makes a Dexterity save against your CE save DC: 7d10 damage on failure or half on success.

Choose up to two different riders from the represented colors. Enemy riders apply only to creatures that failed the save; White protects chosen allies in the area instead.

  • White: chosen allies gain temporary HP equal to PB + your CE modifier.
  • Blue: push or pull each failed target up to 15 feet.
  • Black: failed targets cannot regain HP until the end of your next turn.
  • Red: failed targets ignite and take 1d10 fire damage at the start of their next turn.
  • Green: failed targets have speed 0 until the end of your next turn.

Repeated applications of the same rider do not stack. Specific feature wording elsewhere may change the save ability, but the default is Dexterity.

13Mana Fixing

The rebalance. Once per turn she may convert two energy into one temporary Mastery rank until her turn ends — three at most — though no Signature may be unlocked this way more than once per round. The council reshuffles its seats mid-debate. Once/turn convert 2 CE into one temporary Mastery rank until end turn, max rank 3. Cannot unlock a Signature more than once/round this way.

15Four-Color Mastery

The rotation. With four colors at rank two, the first spell each turn cast in a different color than her previous spell costs one less energy. The council rewards those who hear every voice in turn. If four colors at 2+, first spell each turn using a different color than your previous spell costs 1 less CE.

18Domain — The World Tree

At the twentieth count the tree shows its branches, and she chooses one color's edict: White grants her allies four dice of temporary flesh; Blue strips hostile reactions; Black drains five dice of necrotic and heals her half; Red answers seven dice of fire; Green summons two thirty-hit-point elementals. A thousand clash points hold the trunk. At initiative 20 choose a color: White grants allies 4d10 temp HP; Blue hostiles Int save or lose reactions; Black hostiles Con save 5d10 necrotic and you heal half; Red Dex save 7d10 fire; Green summons two 30-HP elementals until next count 20. Clash bookkeeping: 1,000 clash points (Tier X). A 100+ point advantage collapses the weaker Domain; collapse deals no damage. Barrier AC 12 + PB; barrier HP = 10 × max(1, your CE modifier) + 2,000. Technique Burnout: 3 rounds.

20WUBRG

The full council. Her Mastery pool becomes fifteen — three in all five colors — and once per long rest, for a minute, the first feature of each color she uses costs nothing and gains a die. Five voices, one will, no dissent. Mana Mastery pool becomes 15, allowing 3 in all five colors. Once/long rest for 1 minute, first feature of each color used during the transformation costs 0 CE and gains +1 die.

Pinnacle expression

Maximum, Domain, or equivalent.

Maximum — Chromatic Convergence (12 CE; action): All five courts speak at once. Twelve energy, rank two in all five colors required: five different fifteen-foot zones within a hundred and twenty feet — White protects, Blue displaces, Black drains, Red detonates, Green restrains — and a central target caught by three or more zones takes eight more dice of radiant force. The council's unanimous verdict. requires at least rank 2 in all five colors. Choose five different 15-ft zones within 120 ft: White protects (allies inside gain resistance to the Convergence damage); Blue displaces (failed-save targets are pushed 15 ft); Black drains (4d10 necrotic, and no healing until your next turn); Red detonates (4d10 fire, Dexterity save for half); Green restrains/overgrows (restrained until your next turn on a failed Strength save). Then a central target affected by at least three zones takes an additional 8d10 radiant/force, Con half.

Feats

Technique Feats

Mana Dork

Once per short rest she may grow a one-hit-point mana dork for a minute — holding one temporary Mastery rank of her chosen color, spendable once by any ally within five feet as their own rank-one feature. The council keeps a page. Once per short rest, you can grow a 1-HP mana dork for 1 minute: it holds one temporary Mastery rank of a color you choose, and any ally within 5 feet of it can spend that rank once as if it were their own rank-1 color feature.

Fetchland

Her mana base is a landscape she can reshape: as an action once per short rest she may move one Mastery point from a color holding at least two to another, never past three — though colors already spent from this turn are locked. Pulling the right land from the deck, in the old parlance. Your mana base is a landscape you can reshape. As an action once per short rest, you may redistribute one Mana Mastery point from one color to another — pulling the right 'land' from the deck, in the old parlance. The point must come from a color with at least 2 ranks, and the receiving color cannot exceed 3 ranks this way. Colors you have already spent from this turn are locked; you cannot fetch what is already cast.

Gold Spell

Her guild repertoire runs deeper: she learns one additional two-color pair spell beyond her two chosen at rest — fixed, the guild she apprenticed in, while the other two remain re-choosable. The old allegiance, kept. Your guild repertoire runs deeper than most. You learn one additional two-color pair spell beyond the two you choose at rest, drawn from the same list — Azorius detention, Dimir mind theft, Rakdos sacrifice-flame, Gruul overrun, Selesnya mass ward, and the rest. This third pair is fixed, the guild you apprenticed in, while your other two remain re-choosable at each rest. It costs the same CE and obeys the same rank requirements as your other pair spells.

Command Tower

Once per long rest she may raise her Command Tower for a minute — and during it, cast any color's rank-one basics for one energy, regardless of her Mastery distribution. For sixty seconds, every court is in session. Once per long rest, you can raise your Command Tower for 1 minute: during that time you can cast any color's rank-1 basics by spending 1 CE, regardless of your current Mastery distribution.

Progenitus Echo

Once per long rest, as a reaction when she or an ally within ten feet would take damage, she echoes Progenitus's immunity: one damage type chosen, she and allies within ten feet resisting it until her next turn begins. Protection, from the oldest precedent. Once per long rest, as a reaction when you or an ally within 10 feet would take damage, you can echo Progenitus's immunity to harm: choose one damage type, and you and allies within 10 feet gain resistance to that type until the start of your next turn.

Door to Nothingness

Once per long rest, with at least one rank in all five colors, she may open the Door as an action for ten energy: one creature within sixty feet saves its Charisma or is exiled to the void between colors until her next turn ends, returning with one level of exhaustion. The council's final sanction. Once per long rest, while you have at least 1 rank in all five colors, you can open the Door as an action for 10 CE: one creature within 60 feet makes a Charisma save or is exiled to the void between colors until the end of your next turn, returning with 1 level of exhaustion.

Rules integration

Edge cases and shared systems.

Shared rulings: Technique DC = 8 + PB + CE ability modifier. Direct rolled attacks may crit and use the campaign Black Flash procedure when eligible. Tokens, autonomous summons, passive damage, and initiative-20 Domain effects cannot Black Flash. Maximum Output modifies ordinary supported techniques, never named Maxima or Domains.
