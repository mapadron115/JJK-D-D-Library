Unclaimed Property

Anything genuinely abandoned can enter your supernatural lost-and-found. In combat, issue temporary Loaners; outside combat, archive and return useful property.

Power tierHigh

Field notes. Subject: the custodian of what no one wanted. Three truths govern her. First: abandonment is transfer, the Archive swallows any unclaimed nonliving object within reach, and possession is a mechanical test, not a philosophy. Second: the archive lends what it hoards, Claim Tickets issue Loaners (weapon, barricade, restraint) that vanish if another creature holds them, because the archive's generosity is conditional. Third: neglect compounds, Return to Sender intercepts missiles in flight, Repossession Notice marks the enemy's gear for the taking, and the Domain misplaces a hostile's wielded item at initiative count 20 as its sure-hit. Assess her correctly: everything you drop is already hers; everything you carry is merely on loan from your own carelessness. Maxim: lost property is never lost; it is merely waiting for its true keeper.

Full progression

Level Progression

1Claim Tickets, Archive, Weapon/Barricade/Restraint Loaners

Claim Tickets

Her measure of the archive's generosity: a ticket per proficiency, renewed with rest. You have Claim Tickets equal to your proficiency bonus, regained after a short or long rest. Your Claim Die scales d6/d8/d10/d12 at levels 1/6/11/18.

Archive

The lost-and-found itself: one action, one coin of CE, and any unclaimed nonliving object within reach is shelved, possession tested, never philosophized. Action • 1 CE. Touch an unattended, nonliving object that no creature within 60 feet currently possesses, wears, wields, or actively claims. Store it in your Archive. At 1st level, maximum size is Medium and the Archive holds items equal to 5 + your sorcerer level. Retrieving a stored object within your reach is a bonus action and 1 CE.

Loaner — Weapon

The archive lends a weapon for a minute, proficient in her hands, cursed in its edge, but it will not serve a second master. Bonus Action • 1 Ticket + 1 CE. Create a temporary cursed weapon for 1 minute. Choose melee (1d8, versatile 1d10) or ranged (1d8, range 60/180). You are proficient and may use your CE ability modifier for attack/damage. The Loaner vanishes if another creature ends its turn holding it.

Loaner — Barricade

The archive lends a wall: five feet square, half cover, one minute of borrowed shelter. Action • 1 Ticket + 1 CE. Create a 5-by-5-foot panel within 10 feet, AC 15, HP equal to 10 + 3×sorcerer level; it grants half cover and lasts 1 minute.

Loaner — Restraint

The archive lends its straps: a CE attack that grapples until escape or the minute's end. Action • 1 Ticket + 2 CE • 30 feet. Make a CE attack. Hit: the target is grappled by cursed straps/chains (escape check vs CE save DC) until it escapes or 1 minute.

3Return to Sender —

The archive intercepts: an unattended missile passing within thirty feet is shelved, and she gains the loan of any tool-kit for ten minutes. Reaction, 2 CE, when an unattended projectile/thrown object passes within 30 feet after resolving its attack. Archive it if it fits your size limit. If it missed you or an ally, this costs no Claim Ticket; retrieving it later follows normal rules. Also gain Loaner Tool: 1 Ticket, conjure one artisan/tool kit for 10 minutes and gain proficiency while using it.

6Evidence Locker —

The archive deepens: Large objects now, cursed tools among them, though their active effects sleep on the shelf. maximum Archive size becomes Large. You may archive unattended cursed tools/objects, but while stored their active effects and recharge cease. A cursed item attuned/bonded to another creature cannot be archived unless that bond is willingly relinquished or broken.

11Repossession Notice —

She serves notice on the enemy's property: marked for a minute, shelved the instant it is dropped, and she gains the Loaner Charge, three Claim Dice thrown thirty feet. Bonus Action, 3 CE, mark one visible non-body item wielded/carried by a creature within 60 feet for 1 minute. The first time it becomes unattended (disarmed, dropped, owner incapacitated, etc.), you may use your reaction to Archive it if eligible. You also gain Loaner Charge: Action, 1 Ticket + 3 CE, throw a temporary charge 30 feet; Dexterity save, 3 Claim Dice force damage, half on success.

18Domain Expansion: Department of Lost Property —

The maximum: at initiative count twenty, every hostile's wielded item is Misplaced, present but unusable until the next count, the sure-hit of bureaucracy itself. 10 CE. At initiative count 20 each hostile creature must make a Wisdom save. On failure, choose one non-body item it is currently wielding or actively using; that item becomes Misplaced until the next count 20: it remains physically present but cannot be activated, attacked with, or grant passive bonuses. If no eligible item exists, the creature instead takes 2 Claim Dice force damage. This is the Domain’s sure-hit.

Innate Technique Feats

Technique Feats

Organized Shelving

Her shelves are cross-indexed by need: once per short rest, the needed mundane item is simply there. Your archive is cross-indexed by need: once per short rest, when you or an ally within 30 feet needs a mundane item (rope, tools, rations, a healer's kit), you can produce it from the archive immediately without spending a Claim Ticket, provided it fits your size limit.

No Receipt Needed

The disarmer's privilege: a successfully disarmed object is shelved without spending the reaction. When you successfully disarm a creature, you may Archive the dropped eligible object immediately without using your reaction.

Premium Loaner

One loaner each rest is presentable enough to lend: an ally may wield it for the hour, even should she fall. Choose Weapon, Barricade, or Restraint after each long rest: your premium loaner is presentable enough to lend. An ally can wield it as if you had created it for them, and it persists for 1 hour even if you fall unconscious.

Rules integration

Table Rulings

“Unclaimed” is a mechanical possession test, not a philosophical loophole. A sword in an enemy’s hand is claimed; a sword they intentionally throw away is not.
The Archive does not duplicate objects and does not pause living creatures, corpses, or ongoing time-sensitive effects unless the GM rules the item itself is nonliving and storable.
A Domain’s Misplaced effect suppresses use but does not transfer ownership or automatically Archive the item.
