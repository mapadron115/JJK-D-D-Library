Unclaimed Property

Anything genuinely abandoned can enter your supernatural lost-and-found. In combat, issue temporary Loaners; outside combat, archive and return useful property.

Power tierHigh
Core law

Core Gameplay Loop

Scaling

Claim Die: d6 → d8 (6th) → d10 (11th) → d12 (18th)

Main resource

Claim Tickets & Archive

Claim Tickets

You have Claim Tickets equal to your proficiency bonus, regained after a short or long rest. Your Claim Die scales d6/d8/d10/d12 at levels 1/6/11/18.

Archive

Action • 1 CE. Touch an unattended, nonliving object that no creature within 60 feet currently possesses, wears, wields, or actively claims. Store it in your Archive. At 1st level, maximum size is Medium and the Archive holds items equal to 5 + your sorcerer level. Retrieving a stored object within your reach is a bonus action and 1 CE.

Loaner — Weapon

Bonus Action • 1 Ticket + 1 CE. Create a temporary cursed weapon for 1 minute. Choose melee (1d8, versatile 1d10) or ranged (1d8, range 60/180). You are proficient and may use your CE ability modifier for attack/damage. The Loaner vanishes if another creature ends its turn holding it.

Loaner — Barricade

Action • 1 Ticket + 1 CE. Create a 5-by-5-foot panel within 10 feet, AC 15, HP equal to 10 + 3×sorcerer level; it grants half cover and lasts 1 minute.

Loaner — Restraint

Action • 1 Ticket + 2 CE • 30 feet. Make a CE attack. Hit: the target is grappled by cursed straps/chains (escape check vs CE save DC) until it escapes or 1 minute.

Full progression

Level Progression

1Claim Tickets, Archive, Weapon/Barricade/Restraint Loaners

3Return to Sender —

Reaction, 2 CE, when an unattended projectile/thrown object passes within 30 feet after resolving its attack. Archive it if it fits your size limit. If it missed you or an ally, this costs no Claim Ticket; retrieving it later follows normal rules. Also gain Loaner Tool: 1 Ticket, conjure one artisan/tool kit for 10 minutes and gain proficiency while using it.

6Evidence Locker —

maximum Archive size becomes Large. You may archive unattended cursed tools/objects, but while stored their active effects and recharge cease. A cursed item attuned/bonded to another creature cannot be archived unless that bond is willingly relinquished or broken.

11Repossession Notice —

Bonus Action, 3 CE, mark one visible non-body item wielded/carried by a creature within 60 feet for 1 minute. The first time it becomes unattended (disarmed, dropped, owner incapacitated, etc.), you may use your reaction to Archive it if eligible. You also gain Loaner Charge: Action, 1 Ticket + 3 CE, throw a temporary charge 30 feet; Dexterity save, 3 Claim Dice force damage, half on success.

18Domain Expansion: Department of Lost Property —

10 CE. At initiative count 20 each hostile creature must make a Wisdom save. On failure, choose one non-body item it is currently wielding or actively using; that item becomes Misplaced until the next count 20: it remains physically present but cannot be activated, attacked with, or grant passive bonuses. If no eligible item exists, the creature instead takes 2 Claim Dice force damage. This is the Domain’s sure-hit.

Innate Technique Feats

Technique Feats

Organized Shelving

Archive capacity increases by 10 objects and retrieving a mundane archived object costs 0 CE once per turn.

No Receipt Needed

When you successfully disarm a creature, you may Archive the dropped eligible object immediately without using your reaction.

Premium Loaner

Choose Weapon, Barricade, or Restraint after each long rest. The first time each short rest you create that Loaner, it costs no Claim Ticket.

Rules integration

Table Rulings

“Unclaimed” is a mechanical possession test, not a philosophical loophole. A sword in an enemy’s hand is claimed; a sword they intentionally throw away is not.
The Archive does not duplicate objects and does not pause living creatures, corpses, or ongoing time-sensitive effects unless the GM rules the item itself is nonliving and storable.
A Domain’s Misplaced effect suppresses use but does not transfer ownership or automatically Archive the item.
