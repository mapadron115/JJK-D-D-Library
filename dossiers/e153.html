Unclaimed Property

Anything genuinely abandoned can enter your supernatural lost-and-found. In combat, issue temporary Loaners; outside combat, archive and return useful property.

Power tierTier III · Apex

Field notes. Subject: the custodian of what no one wanted. Three truths govern her. First: abandonment is transfer, the Archive swallows any unclaimed nonliving object within reach, and possession is a mechanical test, not a philosophy. Second: the archive lends what it hoards, Claim Tickets issue Loaners (weapon, barricade, restraint) that vanish if another creature holds them, because the archive's generosity is conditional. Third: neglect compounds, Return to Sender intercepts missiles in flight, Repossession Notice marks the enemy's gear for the taking, and the Domain misplaces a hostile's wielded item at initiative count 20 as its sure-hit. Assess her correctly: everything you drop is already hers; everything you carry is merely on loan from your own carelessness. Maxim: lost property is never lost; it is merely waiting for its true keeper.

Full progression

Level Progression

1Claim Tickets, Archive, Weapon/Barricade/Restraint Loaners

Claim Tickets

Passive · Claim Tickets equal to PB, regained after a short or long rest · Your Claim Die scales d6/d8/d10/d12 at levels 1/6/11/18.

Her measure of the archive's generosity, renewed with rest.

Archive

The lost-and-found itself: any unclaimed nonliving object within reach is shelved, possession tested, never philosophized.

Loaner — Weapon

Action · 1 Ticket + 1 CE · Manifest one simple or martial melee weapon in your hand (normal base statistics; you are proficient with it; it counts as a cursed tool) · Lasts 1 minute · If another creature holds it, it vanishes.

The archive lends a weapon, proficient in her hands, cursed in its edge, but it will not serve a second master.

Loaner — Barricade

Action · 1 Ticket + 1 CE · Conjure a 5-ft-wide, 8-ft-tall spectral panel in an unoccupied space within 30 ft · It grants half cover and lasts 1 minute.

The archive lends a wall: borrowed shelter for the asking.

Loaner — Restraint

Action · 1 Ticket + 1 CE · 30 ft · One creature makes a Strength save or is grappled by spectral straps (escape against your CE save DC) · The grapple lasts 1 minute.

The archive lends its straps: a cursed grapple that holds until escape.

3Return to Sender —

Reaction · 1 CE · When you or an ally within 30 ft is targeted by a ranged weapon attack: the missile is shelved and the attack misses · You may Archive the projectile immediately if it is eligible.

The archive intercepts: a passing missile is shelved mid-flight.

6Evidence Locker —

Passive · Maximum Archive size becomes Large · May archive unattended cursed tools and objects (their active effects and recharge cease while stored) · Cannot archive an item attuned or bonded to another creature unless the bond is relinquished or broken.

The archive deepens: Large objects now, cursed tools among them, though their active effects sleep on the shelf.

11Repossession Notice —

Repossession Notice: Bonus action · 3 CE · Mark one visible non-body item wielded or carried by a creature within 60 ft for 1 minute; the first time it becomes unattended, use your reaction to Archive it if eligible · Loaner Charge: Action · 1 Ticket + 3 CE · Throw a temporary charge 30 ft: Dexterity save or 3 Claim Dice force damage (half on success).

She serves notice on the enemy's property: marked, shelved the instant it is dropped, and she gains the Loaner Charge.

18Domain Expansion: Department of Lost Property —

Domain Expansion · 10 CE · Sure-hit: at initiative 20, each hostile makes a Wisdom save; on failure, choose one non-body item it is wielding or actively using — it becomes Misplaced until the next count 20 (present but unusable: cannot be activated, attacked with, or grant passive bonuses); if no eligible item exists, it takes 2 Claim Dice force damage instead.

Endless shelves of lost things under flickering fluorescent light, umbrellas and keys and single gloves, each tagged and numbered and forgotten. The floor is linoleum, the air smells of dust and old paper, and somewhere a stamp falls, over and over, marking claims no one will ever collect.

The sure-hit of bureaucracy itself: every hostile's wielded item Misplaced, present but unusable.

Innate Technique Feats

Technique Feats

Organized Shelving

Her shelves are cross-indexed by need: once per short rest, the needed mundane item is simply there. Your archive is cross-indexed by need: once per short rest, when you or an ally within 30 feet needs a mundane item (rope, tools, rations, a healer's kit), you can produce it from the archive immediately without spending a Claim Ticket, provided it fits your size limit.

No Receipt Needed

The disarmer's privilege: a successfully disarmed object is shelved without spending the reaction. When you successfully disarm a creature, you may Archive the dropped eligible object immediately without using your reaction.

Premium Loaner

One loaner each rest is presentable enough to lend: an ally may wield it for the hour, even should she fall. Choose Weapon, Barricade, or Restraint after each long rest: your premium loaner is presentable enough to lend. An ally can wield it as if you had created it for them, and it persists for 1 hour even if you fall unconscious.

Rules integration

Table Rulings

“Unclaimed” is a mechanical possession test, not a philosophical loophole. A sword in an enemy’s hand is claimed; a sword they intentionally throw away is not.
The Archive does not duplicate objects and does not pause living creatures, corpses, or ongoing time-sensitive effects unless the GM rules the item itself is nonliving and storable.
A Domain’s Misplaced effect suppresses use but does not transfer ownership or automatically Archive the item.
