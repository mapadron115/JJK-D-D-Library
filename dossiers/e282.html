Core law

Completed rules

Critical Mark sequencing:

Kept exactly as printed, because the order is the whole ruling. the audit ruling (AT196) retains the printed sequencing exactly: the attack that hits the marked target grants advantage to the NEXT attack, which crits if it hits, the advantage and the crit apply to the follow-up attack, not the triggering one.

Piercing Schrift limits:

The phasing has borders, and the file draws them plainly. object phasing is limited to the listed object counts and 5 feet each; it does not bypass magical barriers unless the barrier is an object.

Field notes. Subject: a contract written in advance. Three truths govern it. First: nothing here is a first shot, every seal is the second signature on a document the first strike already signed. The Critical Mark converts a landed blow into a promise and the promise into a critical; mark the order: the advantage belongs to the next attack, never the triggering one. Second: certainty is bought with stillness. The X-Axis guarantees the hit only for a sorcerer who has not moved and cannot move after; the crosshair is a mortgage on the user’s own position. Third: Intangibility is the same phasing turned inward, the seal that walks shots through walls can teach the body to walk through blows, but a ghost cannot fire a gun, and the Maximum is the long negotiation of that trade. The eye-seal formed last, the technique had run out of anywhere else to look.

Full progression

Feature map

1Piercing Schrift.

Passive · Use your CE modifier for ranged weapon attack or damage rolls · On a ranged attack: spend 1 CE to phase through one solid object (5 ft thick), ignoring cover · Pierces 2 objects at 3rd, 3 at 6th, 4 at 11th.

The seal’s first lesson is that cover is a rumor, one coin of cursed energy phases the shot through five feet of solid object, and the eye learns to spend the cursed-energy modifier on the attack itself. Starting at 1st level, you have a cursed mark somewhere on your body that molds your cursed energy automatically. When you make a ranged weapon attack, you can choose to use your Cursed Energy modifier for the attack or damage rolls. When you make a ranged attack, you can spend 1 CE to ignore cover by phasing through a solid object. You can only pierce through 5 feet of one object. This increases to two objects at 3rd, three at 6th, and four at 11th.

3X-Axis Shot.

Action (only if you haven't moved this turn; speed 0 after) · 3 CE · Automatically hit a creature in range with a ranged weapon attack: +2d6 necrotic (3d6/5d6/7d6 at 6th/11th/18th).

At the third level the seal migrates to the eye and the geometry becomes law: stand still, spend three coins, and the ranged attack simply hits, no roll, plus necrotic, and the user’s speed dies at zero until the turn ends. At 3rd level, your technique has evolved and a cursed seal forms on your eye, providing near perfect accuracy. As an action for 3 cursed energy, you automatically hit a creature within range with a ranged weapon attack without rolling and deal an additional 2d6 necrotic damage. This increases to 3d6 at 6th level, 5d6 at 11th level, and 7d6 at 18th level. You can use this technique only if you haven't moved this turn, and after you use it, your speed is 0 until the end of the current turn.

6Cursed Mark.

Passive · You can have only one mark active at a time — using a mark ends the previous one.

The seal learns to write on other people, but only one mark may live at a time, and the last one signed voids the rest. At 6th level, you have learned to apply your cursed seals onto others, allowing for even more accurate and deadly shots. When you use a mark, any previous marks you have active ends.

Critical Mark:

Bonus action · 3 CE · Mark a target within 30 ft for 1 min · Hit the marked target: your next attack vs it has advantage; that attack is an automatic critical (roll all dice twice + modifiers), and the mark ends.

The seal’s first signature is a promise: the mark does not make the hit, it mortgages the next one. As a bonus action for 3 CE, you can mark a target of your choice within 30 feet for 1 minute. If you hit the marked target with an attack, your next attack against the target is made at advantage. On a hit, the attack deals critical damage, the attack is a critical hit: roll all of the attack's damage dice twice and add them together, then add modifiers as normal, and the mark ends.

Multi-Mark:

Bonus action · 3 CE · Mark up to CE-modifier targets within 30 ft for 1 min · Action: make one ranged attack roll vs each marked target in range (weapon or CE attack), normal damage on a hit; marks end.

The second signature is a broadcast: one mark per enemy, one attack split across all of them. As a bonus action for 3 CE, you can mark a number of targets up to your CE modifier within 30 feet for 1 minute. As an action, you can make a ranged attack and activate the marks, causing the attack to split and attack each marked target within range of your attack. You make a ranged weapon or cursed energy attack roll against each creature, dealing your normal damage on a hit and the mark ends.

11Intangibility.

Bonus action · 6 CE · Phase for 1 min (concentration): immune to bludgeoning/piercing/slashing, resistant to all other damage except psychic · Can't use innate technique features while active (normal attack actions only); end as a free action.

At the eleventh level the phasing turns inward: six coins and a bonus action make the body immune to mundane damage and resistant to the rest, at the price of the technique itself, which falls silent while the ghost walks. At 11th level, you have learned to use your cursed seal to cause yourself to phase instead of your attacks. As a bonus action for 6 cursed energy, you release the cursed seal over your eye to activate your technique. You gain immunity to bludgeoning, piercing and slashing and resistance to all other damage other than psychic for 1 minute and must maintain concentration. While Intangibility is active, you cannot use your innate technique features, only allowing for normal attack actions. You can end the technique at any point as a free action to close your left eye.

Maximum: Sancrosanct.

Upgrade · When activating Intangibility: resistant (not immune) to all damage except psychic, but you CAN use your innate technique features · Piercing Schrift usable free once per turn.

Prerequisite: Intangibility. The Maximum renegotiates the ghost’s contract, resistance instead of immunity, but the technique stays awake, and Piercing Schrift fires free once per turn. When you activate Intangibility, you are instead resistant to all damage other than psychic, but are still able to use your Innate Technique features and for the duration you can use Piercing Schrift for free once per turn.

18Domain Expansion: Divine Sight.

Domain (18th level) · Inside: all your attacks benefit from Piercing Schrift and are guaranteed hits · Initiative 20: every creature inside receives a Cursed Mark of your choice.

The world goes white at the edges, then resolves into impossible clarity. Vast pale wings of script unfurl across the barrier's interior, each feather a seal, each seal an eye, and thin threads of light stitch every creature inside to the user's gaze. Distance becomes a formality, the way it is for an archer who has already decided the shot.

The Domain is the contract enforced at scale, every attack phasing and guaranteed, and at initiative twenty every creature inside receives a mark of the user’s choosing. At 18th level you have achieved the pinnacle of your innate technique. Within your domain, all your attacks benefit from Piercing Schrift and are a guaranteed hit. At initiative 20 every creature within your domain is given a Cursed Mark of your choice.

Innate Technique Feats

Build-defining options.

These are optional character-building feats. You must meet each listed prerequisite before selecting it.

Prerequisite: Must have Piercing Schrift

Piercing Burst

The perfected shot stops asking about lines of fire, one attack roll now threatens everything standing in front of or behind the target along its path. You have perfected your accuracy and combined it with your piercing ability. When you use Piercing Schrift, you can also target additional targets up to your proficiency bonus that are either in front of or behind your target as long as they are within the path of the attack. You use the attack roll against the original target to determine if you would hit the additional targets.

Prerequisite: Must have Cursed Mark

Cursed Seal Expert

Diligent practice breeds new signatures: marks that blind or cripple on a failed Constitution save, the affliction lingering a minute with a save each turn. Through diligent practice and creativity, you have created new types of cursed seals. You gain the following options: Blinding Mark: As a bonus action for 3 CE, you mark a creature within 30 feet for 1 minute. On a hit, the creature must make a Constitution saving throw. On a failed save, they are blinded for 1 minute. A creature can repeat the save at the end of their turn, removing the blinded condition on a success. Crippling Mark: As a bonus action for 3 CE, you mark a creature within 30 feet for 1 minute. On a hit, the target must make a Constitution saving throw or have either their movement speed halved for 1 minute or disadvantage on attacks for 1 minute (your choice). A creature can repeat the save at the end of their turn, ending the effect on a success.

Rules integration

Edge cases and shared systems.

Rules priority: the reviewed rulings, explicit codex clarifications, and the level progression always control when original wording conflicts. The audit dispositions for AT196 are adopted as a new campaign technique with the exact changes noted above.
X-Axis Shot and Black Flash: the automatic hit is a printed exception, it still requires a ranged weapon attack, and ammunition/loaded weapon rules apply normally. The audit holds the proposed default that X-Axis Shot cannot trigger Black Flash, since no d20 is rolled (AT196).
X-Axis Shot range: "within range" means the weapon's full (long) range; no disadvantage applies because no attack roll is made (zack's final call pending).
