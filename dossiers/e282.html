Core law

Completed rules

Critical Mark sequencing:

the audit ruling (AT196) retains the printed sequencing exactly: the attack that hits the marked target grants advantage to the NEXT attack, which crits if it hits — the advantage and the crit apply to the follow-up attack, not the triggering one.

Piercing Schrift limits:

object phasing is limited to the listed object counts and 5 feet each; it does not bypass magical barriers unless the barrier is an object.

Full progression

Feature map

1Piercing Schrift.

Starting at 1st level, you have a cursed mark somewhere on your body that molds your cursed energy automatically. When you make a ranged weapon attack, you can choose to use your Cursed Energy modifier for the attack or damage rolls. When you make a ranged attack, you can spend 1 CE to ignore cover by phasing through a solid object. You can only pierce through 5 feet of one object. This increases to two objects at 3rd, three at 6th, and four at 11th.

3X-Axis Shot.

At 3rd level, your technique has evolved and a cursed seal forms on your eye, providing near perfect accuracy. As an action for 3 cursed energy, you automatically hit a creature within range with a ranged weapon attack without rolling and deal an additional 2d6 necrotic damage. This increases to 3d6 at 6th level, 5d6 at 11th level, and 7d6 at 18th level. You can use this technique only if you haven't moved this turn, and after you use it, your speed is 0 until the end of the current turn.

6Cursed Mark.

At 6th level, you have learned to apply your cursed seals onto others, allowing for even more accurate and deadly shots. When you use a mark, any previous marks you have active ends.

Critical Mark:

As a bonus action for 3 CE, you can mark a target of your choice within 30 feet for 1 minute. If you hit the marked target with an attack, your next attack against the target is made at advantage. On a hit, the attack deals critical damage and the mark ends.

Multi-Mark:

As a bonus action for 3 CE, you can mark a number of targets up to your CE modifier within 30 feet for 1 minute. As an action, you can make a ranged attack and activate the marks, causing the attack to split and attack each marked target within range of your attack. You make a ranged weapon or cursed energy attack roll against each creature, dealing your normal damage on a hit and the mark ends.

11Intangibility.

At 11th level, you have learned to use your cursed seal to cause yourself to phase instead of your attacks. As a bonus action for 6 cursed energy, you release the cursed seal over your eye to activate your technique. You gain immunity to bludgeoning, piercing and slashing and resistance to all other damage other than psychic for 1 minute and must maintain concentration. While Intangibility is active, you cannot use your innate technique features, only allowing for normal attack actions. You can end the technique at any point as a free action to close your left eye.

Maximum: Sancrosanct.

Prerequisite: Intangibility. When you activate Intangibility, you are instead resistant to all damage other than psychic, but are still able to use your Innate Technique features and for the duration you can use Piercing Schrift for free once per turn.

18Domain Expansion: Divine Sight.

At 18th level you have achieved the pinnacle of your innate technique. Within your domain, all your attacks benefit from Piercing Schrift and are a guaranteed hit. At initiative 20 every creature within your domain is given a Cursed Mark of your choice.

Innate Technique Feats

Build-defining options.

These are optional character-building feats. You must meet each listed prerequisite before selecting it.

Prerequisite: Must have Piercing Schrift

Piercing Burst

You have perfected your accuracy and combined it with your piercing ability. When you use Piercing Schrift, you can also target additional targets up to your proficiency bonus that are either in front of or behind your target as long as they are within the path of the attack. You use the attack roll against the original target to determine if you would hit the additional targets.

Prerequisite: Must have Cursed Mark

Cursed Seal Expert

Through diligent practice and creativity, you have created new types of cursed seals. You gain the following options: Blinding Mark: As a bonus action for 3 CE, you mark a creature within 30 feet for 1 minute. On a hit, the creature must make a Constitution saving throw. On a failed save, they are blinded for 1 minute. A creature can repeat the save at the end of their turn, removing the blinded condition on a success. Crippling Mark: As a bonus action for 3 CE, you mark a creature within 30 feet for 1 minute. On a hit, the target must make a Constitution saving throw or have either their movement speed halved for 1 minute or disadvantage on attacks for 1 minute (your choice). A creature can repeat the save at the end of their turn, ending the effect on a success.

Rules integration

Edge cases and shared systems.

Rules priority: the reviewed rulings, explicit codex clarifications, and the level progression always control when original wording conflicts. The audit dispositions for AT196 are adopted as a new campaign technique with the exact changes noted above.
X-Axis Shot and Black Flash: the automatic hit is a printed exception — it still requires a ranged weapon attack, and ammunition/loaded weapon rules apply normally. The audit holds the proposed default that X-Axis Shot cannot trigger Black Flash, since no d20 is rolled (AT196).
X-Axis Shot range: "within range" means the weapon's range. Whether long-range disadvantage still applies is an audit HOLD for zack's call: the text grants an automatic hit but does not waive range penalties.
