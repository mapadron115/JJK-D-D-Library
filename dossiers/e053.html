Twin Moon Armament
Moon-and-Fire Knight

Fight with two blades that should not coexist: one carries Carian moonlight, the other living flame. Alternating them creates a faster, deadlier cadence than repeating either alone.

Power tierTier IV · Special Grade
Combat roleDual-stance spellblade
Core engineConcordant Alternation
Core law

What the technique actually does.

Rules are written to plug into the finalized campaign glossary: normal actions stay normal, direct attacks roll, and only named Domain events bypass the initial roll.

Manifest

Twin Blades

Bonus action · 1 CE · Manifest two light greatswords for 1 minute: one Moon Blade, one Flame Blade (proficient one-handed martial weapon profile).

The foundation is a contradiction sheathed: one hand draws moonlight, the other living flame, and the Armament asks nothing of the wielder except that both be held honestly. The commitment comes first, two light greatswords, one minute, one CE, before a single swing is thrown. As a bonus action for 1 CE, manifest two light greatswords for 1 minute. They use a proficient one-handed martial weapon profile. One is the Moon Blade, one the Flame Blade.

Once per turn

Moon / Flame Imbuement

Once per turn · On a hit, spend 1 CE · Moon: +1 Twin Die force/cold (you choose) and the target Con save or speed reduced by 10 ft until the end of its next turn · Flame: +1 Twin Die fire and the target can't take reactions until the end of the current turn.

Each blade answers differently, and the answer is written on impact: Moon binds, cold that slows the limb; Flame denies, fire that robs the enemy of its reactions. Mark which wound each blade leaves; the whole doctrine depends on it. Spend 1 CE on a hit: Moon adds 1 Twin Die force/cold (the user chooses which applies when the damage is dealt) and can slow the target by 10 feet, the target makes a Constitution save, and on a failure its speed is reduced by 10 feet until the end of its next turn; Flame adds 1 Twin Die fire and prevents reactions until end of current turn.

Sequence rule

Concordant Alternation

Sequence rule · If your last technique-enhanced hit used Moon and the next uses Flame (or vice versa): the second hit adds +1 Twin Die · Repeating the same aspect resets the alternation.

Here the doctrine is named. The second blow of an alternating pair gains +1 Twin Die, but repeat the same aspect and the bonus resets. The Armament rewards only the disciplined rhythm; alternation is the law, not the suggestion. If the last technique-enhanced hit you landed used Moon and the next uses Flame, or vice versa, the second hit adds +1 Twin Die. Repeating the same aspect resets the alternation. No separate meter is tracked beyond “last aspect.”

Field notes. Subject: an awakening that refused to choose. One sorcerer, two impulses, moonlight to grieve by, flame to burn with, and a cursed energy that declined to resolve the contradiction, teaching the divided heart instead to keep time. Three truths govern this Armament. First, alternation is doctrine: the second blow of a Moon–Flame pair strikes with an extra Twin Die, because repetition is predictability and the weapon pays a premium to the unpredictable. Second, each blade wounds differently, Moon slows the limb, Flame steals the enemy’s reactions, so the knight reads her own impacts the way a musician reads a score, and the cadence depends on it. Third, and worth the ink: it has no Domain and needs none. Its ceiling is stance, tempo, and two moons dropped from the sky, refinement expressed as cadence, not barrier. Hence the maxim: never strike the same note twice.

Full progression

Level 1 through 20.

Milestones use the campaign’s familiar 1 / 3 / 6 / 11 / 18 structure and add an explicit level-20 mastery feature.

1Twin-Blade Oath

Twin Blades

Passive · Gain Twin Blades, Moon/Flame Imbuement, and Concordant Alternation as described above.

The oath is taken at first level: twin blades manifested, both imbuements learned, the Alternation law sworn. Everything the Armament ever becomes is already encoded here, the rest is tempo and reach. Gain the Twin Blades (bonus action, 1 CE: manifest two light greatswords for 1 minute, one Moon Blade, one Flame Blade), Moon/Flame Imbuement (once per turn on a hit, 1 CE: Moon adds 1 Twin Die force/cold and can slow 10 feet; Flame adds 1 Twin Die fire and prevents reactions until the end of the current turn), and Concordant Alternation (alternating Moon and Flame technique-enhanced hits adds +1 Twin Die to the second hit).

Knightly Footwork

Passive · While wielding both blades and wearing no heavy armor: +1 AC.

She wears no heavy armor, because this duel is fought on footwork, not plating. While both blades are drawn: +1 AC, the defense of a knight who keeps both edges moving. While wielding both blades and wearing no heavy armor, +1 AC.

3Carian Retinue

Carian Phalanx

Bonus action · 2 CE · Create PB glintblades for 1 minute · Once per turn after you attack: fire one at a target within 60 ft as a CE attack for 1 Twin Die force.

A retinue of glintblades, the moon’s courtiers, summoned for a minute and spent one at a time. Note the economy: the phalanx is a stockpile, not a storm; one blade per turn, fired after the wielder’s own attack. Bonus action · 2 CE: create PB glintblades for 1 minute. Once per turn after you attack, fire one at a target within 60 feet as a CE attack for 1 Twin Die force.

Moonlight Wave

Replace one weapon attack · 2 CE · 60-ft ranged CE attack · 3 Twin Dice force/cold (you choose).

The Moon Blade learns to throw its cold: a sixty-foot wave of force and frost. This is the Armament’s longest early reach, and, per the stance law below, its cheapest when the Moon ascends. Replace one weapon attack · 2 CE: 60-foot ranged CE attack, 3 Twin Dice force/cold; the user chooses which applies when the damage is dealt.

Flame Spiral

Action · 3 CE · Creatures within 10 ft: Dex save or 4 Twin Dice fire (half on success) · Move 10 ft after resolution.

The Flame Blade answers with closeness: a ten-foot whirlwind of fire, and the knight moves ten feet in its wake. Fire here is not thrown, it is worn, and carried through the enemy’s ranks. Action · 3 CE: creatures within 10 feet Dex save for 4 Twin Dice fire, half on success; move 10 feet after resolution.

6Moon-and-Fire Stance

Stance: Sorcerous

Bonus action · 2 CE · Until the start of your next turn: Moon Blade gains 15-ft spectral reach and Moonlight Wave costs 1 less CE.

At sixth level the stance splits. In the Sorcerous stance the Moon Blade gains fifteen feet of spectral reach and its wave cheapens, the knight choosing distance and sorcery over flame. A stance is a declaration of intent for the turn, bought for 2 CE. Bonus action, 2 CE: until start of next turn Moon Blade gains 15-foot spectral reach and Moonlight Wave costs 1 less CE.

Stance: Infernal

Bonus action · 2 CE · Until the start of your next turn: Flame Blade hits deal +1 Twin Die and you may move through hostile reach without provoking from creatures you hit.

In the Infernal stance the bargain reverses: the Flame Blade strikes harder, and the knight moves through hostile reach unpunished by those she has cut. Choose the stance the terrain demands, and change your mind next turn, at the price of another bonus action. Bonus action, 2 CE: until start of next turn Flame Blade hits deal +1 Twin Die and you may move through hostile reach without provoking from creatures you hit.

Perfect Alternation

Passive · Once per turn, when Concordant Alternation triggers: fire one stored Carian Phalanx blade without consuming the once-per-turn phalanx launch.

Reward the rhythm and the rhythm rewards you: when Alternation triggers, one stored glintblade fires without consuming the phalanx’s once-per-turn launch. Alternation is no longer merely doctrine, it is the engine that fires the retinue. Once per turn when Concordant Alternation triggers, fire one stored Carian Phalanx blade without consuming the once-per-turn phalanx launch.

11Carian Sovereignty

Carian Sovereignty

Action · 6 CE · Melee CE attack vs one target within 20 ft: 8 Twin Dice force on hit · Then creatures in a 30-ft cone: Dex save or 4 Twin Dice force (half on success).

The eleventh-level answer to a single stubborn enemy: a colossal magic sword, eight Twin Dice of force on a melee CE attack, then a cone of aftershock. Sovereignty is not subtle, it is the knight ending an argument. Action · 6 CE: form a colossal magic sword. Make a melee CE attack against one target within 20 feet; hit deals 8 Twin Dice force. Then creatures in a 30-foot cone make Dex saves for 4 Twin Dice force, half on success.

Twin Aspect Guard

Reaction · 3 CE · Moon: reduce technique damage by PB Twin Dice · Flame: reduce weapon/unarmed damage by PB Twin Dice.

The blades defend as well as kill: a reaction answered with the correct aspect, Moon against sorcery, Flame against steel. Assess the incoming harm rightly, and pay 3 CE to turn it aside. Reaction · 3 CE: Moon reduces technique damage by PB Twin Dice; Flame reduces weapon/unarmed damage by PB Twin Dice.

18Rellana’s Twin Moons

Maximum: Twin Moons

Action · 10 CE · Rise 20 ft and create two moons · Three sequential 20-ft-radius ground shockwaves centered below you: 4, 4, then 6 Twin Dice force (Dex save for half each) · Failing any: knocked prone; failing two or more: also launched 20 ft upward.

The Maximum is not a barrier but an apocalypse in three strokes: she rises twenty feet, makes two moons, and drops three sequential shockwaves, 4, 4, then 6 Twin Dice of force, each halved on a save, the prone and the launched sorted by how many saves failed. Maximum techniques announce themselves; this one announces itself from the sky. Action · 10 CE: rise 20 feet and create two moons. Each has a Dex save for half; a creature failing any is knocked prone, and failing two+ also launches 20 feet upward.

Moonfire Sovereignty

Passive · Twin Blades remain manifested without duration or CE maintenance until dismissed · Once per turn: apply both imbuements to the same hit for 2 CE (does not count as Alternation by itself).

The blades never demanifest, and once per turn both imbuements can ride the same hit for 2 CE. Note the restraint built into the law: this does not count as Alternation by itself. Even sovereignty pays the rhythm its due. Twin Blades remain manifested without duration or CE maintenance until dismissed. Once per turn use Moon and Flame imbuement on the same hit for 2 CE; this does not count as Alternation by itself.

No Domain

The dossier confesses the fact before it can be called a gap: this technique has no Domain, and the absence is deliberate, refinement expressed as stance and triple moon impact, not barrier. Per the registry’s own rule, a missing Domain is the norm, never the wound. This technique’s refinement is a weapon stance and triple moon impact, not a barrier. It grants no innate Domain.

Pinnacle expression

Domain, Maximum, or equivalent.

Not every technique needs a Domain. When one exists, its sure-hit is a unique autonomous initiative-count-20 event unless the Domain is explicitly rule-based.

The dossier settles the question before it can be asked: No natural Domain Twin Moon Armament is intentionally complete without one. Its late-game ceiling is Rellana’s Twin Moons, permanent twin armaments, and Perfect Concordance.

Innate Technique Feats

Four build-defining options.

These broaden the law instead of merely inflating the same number.

Prerequisite: Twin Moon Armament

Carian Dueling Form

The duelist’s art: mark one creature, strike it with both Moon and Flame while the mark lasts, and it becomes Eclipsed, disadvantage on attacks against anyone but you. Two blades, one opponent; no distractions permitted. As a bonus action for 1 CE, mark one creature within 30 feet as your duelist until the end of your next turn. If you hit it with both a Moon-imbued and a Flame-imbued attack while it is marked, it becomes Eclipsed until the end of its next turn: it has disadvantage on attack rolls against creatures other than you.

Prerequisite: Carian Phalanx

Phalanx Reserve

The retinue can shield as well as strike: order the glintblades into a screening orbit, and ranged attacks against you miss more often, each miss shattering one blade in interception. A shield that spends itself is still a shield; count the cost. As a bonus action, order your active glintblades into a screening orbit until the start of your next turn. Ranged attacks against you have disadvantage; each time a ranged attack misses you, one glintblade of your choice shatters intercepting it.

Prerequisite: Flame Spiral

Messmer Ember

The Spiral leaves a wake of embers, and the knight’s post-resolution movement becomes a second attack, each hostile whose space she enters takes fire. Flame, once worn, keeps burning where she has been. Your spiral leaves an ember wake. Each hostile creature whose space you enter during Flame Spiral’s post-resolution movement takes 1 Twin Die of fire damage; a creature takes this damage only once per Spiral.

Prerequisite: Twin Aspect Guard

Moon Princess’s Guard

The ward extends beyond the self: Twin Aspect Guard can now shelter a willing creature within ten feet against the next matching instance. A knight’s guard was always meant to stand before another. Your ward extends to another. Rules integration

Edge cases and shared systems.

These rulings are designed around the revised Player Guide rather than the raw PDF where the two differ.

Damage-type chooser: when an ability lists two damage types, the user chooses which applies when the damage is dealt.
Concordant Alternation tracks only the last enhanced aspect, not every mundane attack.
Twin Blades may use Strength or Dexterity if the chosen weapon profile permits finesse; otherwise normal weapon rules apply.
Twin Moon shockwaves are saves and cannot critical/Black Flash.
Moonfire Sovereignty’s dual imbuement does not recursively trigger Alternation.
Technique DC: 8 + proficiency bonus + your chosen Cursed Energy ability modifier (Wisdom or Charisma unless your class says otherwise).
Direct criticals: rolled weapon, unarmed, and CE attacks can critical normally. After a qualifying critical, roll the campaign's unmodified Black Flash confirmation d20; a natural 20 triples the completed critical damage. Autonomous Domain events, damage-over-time, hazards, and unrolled pulses cannot Black Flash.
Maximum Output: unless a feature says otherwise, double that feature's base CE cost and enhance one supported axis—damage, range, duration, or accuracy/DC—using the campaign's Maximum Output rules. Named Maximum Techniques and Domains do not receive generic Maximum Output.
Technique Focus: effects marked Focus occupy the shared Technique Focus slot. Opening a Domain ends other Focus effects unless the Domain expressly preserves them.
Two-technique characters: these guides assume each technique progresses from character/class level as agreed by the campaign. Multiclassing itself does not grant another Innate Technique.
