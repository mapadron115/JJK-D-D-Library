Twin Moon Armament
Moon-and-Fire Knight

Fight with two blades that should not coexist: one carries Carian moonlight, the other living flame. Alternating them creates a faster, deadlier cadence than repeating either alone.

Power tierTier IV · Special Grade
Combat roleDual-stance spellblade
Core engineConcordant Alternation
Core law

What the technique actually does.

Rules are written to plug into the finalized campaign glossary: normal actions stay normal, direct attacks roll, and only named Domain events bypass the initial roll.

Manifest

Twin Blades

As a bonus action for 1 CE, manifest two light greatswords for 1 minute. They use a proficient one-handed martial weapon profile. One is the Moon Blade, one the Flame Blade.

Once per turn

Moon / Flame Imbuement

Spend 1 CE on a hit: Moon adds 1 Twin Die force/cold and can slow 10 feet; Flame adds 1 Twin Die fire and prevents reactions until end of current turn.

Sequence rule

Concordant Alternation

If the last technique-enhanced hit you landed used Moon and the next uses Flame, or vice versa, the second hit adds +1 Twin Die. Repeating the same aspect resets the alternation. No separate meter is tracked beyond “last aspect.”

Full progression

Level 1 through 20.

Milestones use the campaign’s familiar 1 / 3 / 6 / 11 / 18 structure and add an explicit level-20 mastery feature.

1Twin-Blade Oath

Twin Blades

Gain the Twin Blades (bonus action, 1 CE: manifest two light greatswords for 1 minute — one Moon Blade, one Flame Blade), Moon/Flame Imbuement (once per turn on a hit, 1 CE: Moon adds 1 Twin Die force/cold and can slow 10 feet; Flame adds 1 Twin Die fire and prevents reactions until the end of the current turn), and Concordant Alternation (alternating Moon and Flame technique-enhanced hits adds +1 Twin Die to the second hit).

Knightly Footwork

While wielding both blades and wearing no heavy armor, +1 AC.

Scaling

Twin Die d6; range melee.

3Carian Retinue

Carian Phalanx

Bonus action · 2 CE: create PB glintblades for 1 minute. Once per turn after you attack, fire one at a target within 60 feet as a CE attack for 1 Twin Die force.

Moonlight Wave

Replace one weapon attack · 2 CE: 60-foot ranged CE attack, 3 Twin Dice force/cold.

Flame Spiral

Action · 3 CE: creatures within 10 feet Dex save for 4 Twin Dice fire, half on success; move 10 feet after resolution.

Scaling

Twin Die remains d6; range extends to 60 feet.

6Moon-and-Fire Stance

Stance: Sorcerous

Bonus action, 2 CE: until start of next turn Moon Blade gains 15-foot spectral reach and Moonlight Wave costs 1 less CE.

Stance: Infernal

Bonus action, 2 CE: until start of next turn Flame Blade hits deal +1 Twin Die and you may move through hostile reach without provoking from creatures you hit.

Perfect Alternation

Once per turn when Concordant Alternation triggers, fire one stored Carian Phalanx blade without consuming the once-per-turn phalanx launch.

Scaling

Twin Die becomes d8; range extends to 90 feet.

11Carian Sovereignty

Carian Sovereignty

Action · 6 CE: form a colossal magic sword. Make a melee CE attack against one target within 20 feet; hit deals 8 Twin Dice force. Then creatures in a 30-foot cone make Dex saves for 4 Twin Dice, half on success.

Twin Aspect Guard

Reaction · 3 CE: Moon reduces technique damage by PB Twin Dice; Flame reduces weapon/unarmed damage by PB Twin Dice.

Dancing Knight

After triggering Concordant Alternation twice in one turn, gain +10 speed and advantage on the next Dex save before your next turn.

Scaling

Twin Die becomes d10; range extends to 120 feet.

18Rellana’s Twin Moons

Maximum: Twin Moons

Action · 10 CE: rise 20 feet and create two moons. Resolve three sequential 20-foot-radius ground shockwaves centered below you: 4 Twin Dice, then 4 Twin Dice, then 6 Twin Dice force. Each has a Dex save for half; a creature failing any is knocked prone, and failing two+ also launches 20 feet upward.

Moonfire Sovereignty

Twin Blades remain manifested without duration or CE maintenance until dismissed. Once per turn use Moon and Flame imbuement on the same hit for 2 CE; this does not count as Alternation by itself.

No Domain

This technique’s refinement is a weapon stance and triple moon impact, not a barrier. It grants no innate Domain.

Scaling

Twin Die becomes d12; range extends to 180 feet.

20Perfect Concordance

Moon and Fire, Always Together

Concordant Alternation can trigger twice per turn. Its bonus die becomes +2 Twin Dice.

Sovereign Knight

When you take the Attack action with Twin Blades, once per turn make one additional Twin Blade attack as part of that action.

Third Moon

When using Maximum: Twin Moons, spend +5 CE to create a third moon before your own slam: add another 4 Twin Die shockwave. Named pinnacle; no Maximum Output.

Scaling

Twin Die remains d12; range extends to 300 feet.

Pinnacle expression

Domain, Maximum, or equivalent.

Not every technique needs a Domain. When one exists, its sure-hit is a unique autonomous initiative-count-20 event unless the Domain is explicitly rule-based.

No natural Domain Twin Moon Armament is intentionally complete without one. Its late-game ceiling is Rellana’s Twin Moons, permanent twin armaments, and Perfect Concordance.

Innate Technique Feats

Four build-defining options.

These broaden the law instead of merely inflating the same number.

Prerequisite: Twin Moon Armament

Carian Dueling Form

As a bonus action for 1 CE, mark one creature within 30 feet as your duelist until the end of your next turn. If you hit it with both a Moon-imbued and a Flame-imbued attack while it is marked, it becomes Eclipsed until the end of its next turn: it has disadvantage on attack rolls against creatures other than you.

Prerequisite: Carian Phalanx

Phalanx Reserve

As a bonus action, order your active glintblades into a screening orbit until the start of your next turn. Ranged attacks against you have disadvantage; each time a ranged attack misses you, one glintblade of your choice shatters intercepting it.

Prerequisite: Flame Spiral

Messmer Ember

Your spiral leaves an ember wake. Each hostile creature whose space you enter during Flame Spiral’s post-resolution movement takes 1 Twin Die of fire damage; a creature takes this damage only once per Spiral.

Prerequisite: Twin Aspect Guard

Moon Princess’s Guard

Your ward extends to another. When you use Twin Aspect Guard, you may also shield one willing creature within 10 feet: it gains the same reduction against the next instance of the matching damage type before the start of your next turn.

Rules integration

Edge cases and shared systems.

These rulings are designed around the revised Player Guide rather than the raw PDF where the two differ.

Concordant Alternation tracks only the last enhanced aspect, not every mundane attack.
Twin Blades may use Strength or Dexterity if the chosen weapon profile permits finesse; otherwise normal weapon rules apply.
Twin Moon shockwaves are saves and cannot critical/Black Flash.
Moonfire Sovereignty’s dual imbuement does not recursively trigger Alternation.
Technique DC: 8 + proficiency bonus + your chosen Cursed Energy ability modifier (Wisdom or Charisma unless your class says otherwise).
Direct criticals: rolled weapon, unarmed, and CE attacks can critical normally. After a qualifying critical, roll the campaign's unmodified Black Flash confirmation d20; a natural 20 triples the completed critical damage. Autonomous Domain events, damage-over-time, hazards, and unrolled pulses cannot Black Flash.
Maximum Output: unless a feature says otherwise, double that feature's base CE cost and enhance one supported axis—damage, range, duration, or accuracy/DC—using the campaign's Maximum Output rules. Named Maximum Techniques and Domains do not receive generic Maximum Output.
Technique Focus: effects marked Focus occupy the shared Technique Focus slot. Opening a Domain ends other Focus effects unless the Domain expressly preserves them.
Two-technique characters: these guides assume each technique progresses from character/class level as agreed by the campaign. Multiclassing itself does not grant another Innate Technique.
