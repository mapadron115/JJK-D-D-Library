Features & procedures

Properties & techniques

Exact player-facing procedures

Thousand-mile chain

Passive · No CE · +4 bonus to attack and damage rolls made with this weapon.

The chain's arithmetic is Special Grade plain, +4 to attack and damage on every swing. Imbued with a technique; the weight is literal and otherwise. You have a +4 bonus to attack and damage rolls made with this cursed tool.

Extended reach

Bonus action · No CE · Extend the chain's range by 60 ft (one end held or anchored); retracts at no action.

And distance is a suggestion: as a bonus action, add sixty feet to the chain's range, one end held or anchored by you, until retracted at no action. The far horizon, on a leash. As a bonus action, you can add 60 ft to the chain's range, as long as one end remains held or anchored by you. The extension lasts until you retract it (no action).

Armed end

Passive · No CE · Attach one light weapon to the chain's end; chain attacks may use its damage die, and the chain's own properties still apply.

The far end may carry one light weapon at a time, and the chain's attacks may borrow that weapon's damage die. The chain's own properties ride along; the borrowed weapon's do not. You can attach a light weapon to the chain's end (one attached weapon at a time). Attacks made with the chain may use the attached weapon's damage die in place of the chain's base damage die.

Field notes. Subject: a weighted chain that declines to accept distance. Three truths. First: it is Special Grade in the plain sense, +4 to strike and wound, a weapon that hits like a verdict. Second: its reach is a decision, not a property, as a bonus action, the chain extends sixty feet, so long as one end stays held or anchored, and stays extended until retracted at no action cost. Third: the far end can be armed, one light weapon attached at a time, and the chain's attacks may borrow the attached weapon's damage die. Mark what travels with the die and what does not: the chain's +4 and properties persist; the attached weapon's own enchantments stay home. Assess it correctly: the chain does not close distance, it abolishes it, the enemy's horizon is the wielder's arm's length.
