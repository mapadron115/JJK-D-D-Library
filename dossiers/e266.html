Fa Jin

You can store and release kinetic energy using your cursed technique.

Power tierTier III · Apex
Combat roleStriker
Core engineJin charge storage and release
DomainHall of Divine Bagua

Field notes. Subject: the miser of momentum, who banks what other fighters discard. Three truths govern him. First, failure is his currency: every missed melee attack mints a Jin charge, and the technique keeps a ledger of every fist that found nothing. Second, the bank has strict rules, the charge pool is capped, each augmentation is capped by proficiency, and what is committed to a miss is forfeit, so the miser must spend like a miser. Third, the Domain is both mint and debt-collector: inside it the user holds infinite charges, yet at initiative 20 every enemy gains negative charges that eat their damage. Consider the whole arc: the miss teaches, the charge remembers, the release repays. Mark this: he does not waste defeat, he compounds it. Nothing is lost in this economy. Least of all losing.

Full progression

Feature map

1Store and Release.

The core ledger: misses become deposits, deposits become strikes, and the bank closes its books on a long rest. Starting at 1st level, you can store and release energy. When you miss with a melee attack, you gain a Jin charge. You can have a number of Jin charges equal to your proficiency bonus + your level. You can spend a Jin charge or 1 Cursed Energy to either gain a +2 to a melee or cursed energy attack roll or deal an additional damage die for each charge spent. Each charge adds one extra damage die of the triggering attack's or technique's base damage die; if the attack or technique uses multiple different dice, use the first damage die listed. You must declare how many Jin charges you are using before making an attack or using a technique and can use a number of Jin charges equal to your proficiency bonus at once. On a miss, you waste the Jin charges. You lose all unused Jin charges on a long rest.

3Instantaneous Movement.

Movement enters the economy, the user may trade stillness itself for a charge, or spend charges to cross the field in an eyeblink. At 3rd level, you gain more flexibility when using your Fa Jin. At the beginning of your turn, you can forgo your movement or spend 1 cursed energy to gain a Jin charge. You can now also use a Jin charge to increase your movement speed by 10 feet and triple your jump distance until the end of your turn or to instantly move 10 feet per charge in any direction as a reaction without provoking opportunity attacks.

6Fist of Explosive Power.

The stored fortune becomes artillery: compressed air, shaped by whatever weapon taught the hand. At 6th level, you have learned to use your cursed energy to amplify your Fa Jin. You can spend 3 cursed energy as an action to launch a blast of compressed air using Jin charges, which have different effects based on the weapon you use.

  • Unarmed or Bludgeoning, 15 feet cone, Con save, 2D6 bludgeoning damage per Jin charge, knocked prone on a failed save.
  • Slashing or Piercing, 30 feet line, Dex save, 2D10 slashing damage per Jin charge.
11Maximize Potential Energy.

The miser becomes the banker, charges are kept through a miss, doubled on purchase, and can even be lent into another fighter's hand. At 11th level, you have learned not only how to avoid wasting your stored energy, but how to use it more effectively, granting you the following benefits:

  • When you spend Jin charges to enhance an attack and miss, you can keep a number of Jin charges up to your Cursed Energy modifier instead of losing the charges.
  • When you spend at least 3 Jin charges for Explosive Power, you can increase the range of your compressed air blast by 30 feet, which now deals thunder damage.
  • When you spend ce to gain a Jin charge, you now gain 2 Jin charges for every ce spent.
  • As an action while touching a creature, you can give them a number of your Jin charges up to your CE modifier.
18Domain Expansion: Hall of Divine Bagua.

The Domain is the ledger made law: unlimited deposits for the user, compounding debt for everyone else. At 18th level, you have learned your domain. Within your domain, you have unlimited Jin charges and at initiative 20, all creatures within your domain gain a permanent negative Jin charge, causing them to deal 1d6 less damage on their next attack or technique for each charge. These charges disappear when your domain ends.

Pinnacle expression

Maximum, Domain, or equivalent.

The vault emptied all at once, three dice per charge, a savaged saving throw, and the target launched like a spent coin. Maximum: Destructive Release: prerequisite: must have Maximize Potential. You have learned the method to magnify your destructive power. As a bonus action, you can spend 2 CE before using Fist of Explosive Power. You deal three damage die per Jin charge spent and all targets gain a -3 to their saving throw, being launched back 10 feet on a failed save, taking 1d6 bludgeoning damage if they hit an object, creature or structure.

Hall of Divine Bagua, where the user's misses are infinite and the enemy's are taxed. Domain Expansion: Hall of Divine Bagua: 18: within your domain, you have unlimited Jin charges and at initiative 20, all creatures within your domain gain a permanent negative Jin charge, causing them to deal 1d6 less damage on their next attack or technique for each charge. These charges disappear when your domain ends.

Innate Technique Feats

Build-defining options.

These are optional character-building feats. You must meet each listed prerequisite before selecting it.

Prerequisite: Must have Store and Release

Kinetic Absorption

He learns to bank the enemy's blows as well as his own, a reaction that turns incoming force into a deposit. As a reaction to bludgeoning, slashing, or piercing damage, you can spend 1 cursed energy to reduce the incoming damage by your Cursed Energy score and gain 1 Jin Charge. You cannot reduce the damage of other damage types.

Prerequisite: Must have Instantaneous Movement

Carried Momentum

The miser shares the road: one willing soul may ride the instantaneous movement beside him. You have learned to share your instantaneous movement. When you use Instantaneous Movement, you can spend 1 additional Jin charge to bring one willing creature you are touching with you, moving them to the same destination without provoking opportunity attacks.

Prerequisite: Must have Instantaneous Movement

Instantaneous Charge

Speed spent as a weapon, three charges of motion behind a single strike, and thunder riding with it. When you use at least 3 Jin charges for Instantaneous Movement, you gain advantage on your next melee attack roll. On a hit, you deal additional thunder damage equal to your Cursed Energy modifier.

Rules integration

Edge cases and shared systems.

Charge bank vs. CE pool: the Jin charge bank and the cursed energy pool are separate. A meaningful hostile missed attack earns one charge; harmless misses against the air or a cooperative target do not.
Augmentation allocation: for attack augmentation, choose an allocation of accuracy and damage-die units totaling at most your proficiency bonus, each unit paid with one charge OR one CE, never both benefits per unit. The PB input cap also applies to mixed CE/charge purchases (campaign clarification), so switching currency cannot bypass the cap. Committed charges are lost on a miss before 11th level.
Movement trade: the start-of-turn trade gives one charge and sets your normal movement to 0 for the turn; it does not forbid separately paid reaction movement. The reaction move needs a legal attack or movement trigger, does not provoke opportunity attacks, can move in any direction, but grants no standing flight after it ends. Jump x3 does not stack exponentially per charge; speed +10 per charge can stack for the turn.
Explosive Power: at most PB charges per activation; the Domain's unlimited storage does not remove the output cap. The table omits success damage, campaign ruling: success takes half and is not prone, for both profiles; the line is 5 ft wide.
Charge transfer: giving charges removes actual charges from you; the recipient uses the Store and Release basic benefits under the PB cap (campaign grant wording), they do not receive your entire Fa Jin technique.
Domain: negative charges accumulate on eligible creatures each initiative 20; campaign ruling: they are consumed together on the next damaging attack or technique event, reducing damage by Nd6 to a minimum of 0, and all remaining charges expire when the Domain ends. "Permanent" means persisting until spent or the Domain ends, not a campaign-permanent debuff. The caster is excluded (campaign choice); anti-domain protection prevents new charges while protected.
Kinetic Absorption: reduction uses your Cursed Energy score (e.g. 20), not your current reserve. The charge is awarded once per reaction even if the damage is fully prevented, but no cooperative pre-combat damage farming.
Instantaneous Charge: advantage applies to one attack, not all, and expires at the end of your next turn (campaign ruling); the thunder modifier applies once.
Destructive Release: replaces 2 dice with 3 per Jin using the chosen profile (d6 or d10); the -3 save applies once, not per charge; the 10 ft push and collision damage apply only on a failed save. Total baseline payment is 5 CE plus the chosen Jin; Maximum Output calculates the complete eligible activation, not just the 2 CE enhancer.
Scope: this author Fa Jin is not an approved replacement for the campaign's OFA/Kinetic Transmission techniques, those comparisons remain deferred.
