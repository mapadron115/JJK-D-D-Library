Features & procedures

Properties & techniques

Exact player-facing procedures

Crossguard

Reaction · 1 CE: +2 AC against one melee attack; if the attack misses, gain 1 Integrity.

One CE and a reaction: two points of AC against a single melee attack, and if the blow misses, the ledger grows. The sword notes every attack it was true against. reaction, 1 CE: +2 AC against one melee attack; if it misses gain Integrity.

Unbroken Edge

On hit · Spend Integrity: +1 Tool Die force damage per Integrity spent, max 2.

The ledger is cashed as force: each banked Integrity becomes a die on a hit, two at most. Defense, converted to offense at the bearer's need, never the reverse. spend Integrity on hit for +1 Tool Die force, max 2.

Hold the Pass

Bonus action · 2 CE · Until your next turn: opportunity hits with Durandal reduce the target's speed to 0 until the end of its next turn.

Two CE for the pass held: until the next turn, any opportunity hit pins the target's speed to zero. The doctrine of the unbroken line, none shall pass, stated as a procedure. 2 CE, bonus: until next turn, opportunity hits with Durandal reduce the target’s speed to 0 until the end of its next turn (invented).

Relic's Refusal

Once per long rest · Reaction when disarmed: summon the blade to your hand if it is within 120 ft.

Once a day, the refusal extends to absence itself: disarmed, the blade answers a reaction and crosses a hundred and twenty feet to the hand. A sword that will not be broken will also not be left. once/long rest when disarmed, summon blade back to hand as reaction if within 120 ft.

Field notes. Subject: an oath that survived its metal. Three truths govern him. First, the blade cannot be damaged by ordinary means, not because the steel is special, but because the refusal burned into it is: the world, reading that conviction in cursed energy, agrees. Second, Integrity is the sword keeping score: every blow blocked or reduced is entered in the ledger, and Unbroken Edge spends that record as force dice the moment the bearer needs it most. Third, the blade defends in all three tenses: Crossguard wards the present with a reaction, Hold the Pass pins the future by stopping the enemy's movement at the line, and Relic's Refusal reclaims the past, disarmed, the sword returns to the hand from a hundred and twenty feet, once a day. Hence the registry's verdict: durability is the fantasy. It does not need Special Grade damage to be valuable.

Rules integration

Cursed-tool attunement

Campaign canon

Cursed-tool attunement: you have PB attunement slots (2–6). This tool occupies one slot and requires a one-hour ritual. Attunement ends voluntarily over a short rest, on death, or when you replace it while over capacity. Distance does not break attunement. Cursed-tool attunement is separate from magic-item attunement.
Balance & counterplay. Durability is the fantasy; it does not need Special Grade damage to be valuable.
