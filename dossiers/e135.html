Core law

What the technique actually does.

Core Engine — Artifacts. Mark PB unattended manufactured objects or willing cursed tools as Artifacts after 1 minute contact. You can animate, reinforce, combine, or temporarily suppress their supernatural functions.

Full progression

Level 1 through 20.

1Silver Body

AC +1 while unarmored, resistance poison, no need to breathe. 1 CE touch deals 2d8 force to construct/object.

3Animate Artifact

2 CE: one marked object becomes 1-minute construct, AC 15, HP 10+level, attack 2d8; max one.

6Liquimetal

2 CE reshape a marked metal object/tool, changing weapon form or creating cover/bridge without changing magical properties.

9Null Rod

Reaction 3 CE against cursed tool within 30 ft: wielder Cha save; on failure its activated properties suppressed until end next turn, mundane weapon function remains.

11Construct Legion

Maintain PB animated Artifacts, but they act as one group and collectively make at most PB attacks.

13Legacy Weapon

Combine three marked objects into one temporary Large construct/tool for 5 CE, 1 minute: AC 18, 60 HP, attack 5d10 or barrier 60 Integrity.

15Creator's Touch

Repair constructs/cursed tools for 4d10 with 3 CE. Once/long rest repair one destroyed non-legendary cursed tool after 10 minutes if pieces remain.

18Domain — Argentum

Initiative 20 choose ANIMATE or SILENCE. Animate: two environmental objects attack for 4d10 each against different targets, Dex save. Silence: one hostile cursed tool makes wielder Cha save or loses activated properties until next count 20.

20Father of Machines

Artifact cap 10. First animation each combat costs 0. Mark an object as action. Legacy Weapon may incorporate one cursed tool activation once/round.

Pinnacle expression

Maximum, Domain, or equivalent.

Maximum — Great Creator (10 CE): animate every marked Artifact for 3 rounds without normal active cap; resolve them as up to three swarms to avoid turn bloat.

Rules integration

Edge cases and shared systems.

Shared rulings: Technique DC = 8 + PB + CE ability modifier. Direct rolled attacks may crit and use the campaign Black Flash procedure when eligible. Tokens, autonomous summons, passive damage, and initiative-20 Domain effects cannot Black Flash. Maximum Output modifies ordinary supported techniques, never named Maxima or Domains.
