Core law

What the technique actually does.

Core Engine — Artifacts. Artifacts are her kinship system: after one minute of contact she marks a number of unattended manufactured objects or willing cursed tools equal to her proficiency. She may animate them, reinforce them, combine them, or suppress their supernatural functions. They are not her servants. They are her relations. Mark PB unattended manufactured objects or willing cursed tools as Artifacts after 1 minute contact. You can animate, reinforce, combine, or temporarily suppress their supernatural functions.

Field notes. Subject: a golem who keeps a household of tools. Three truths govern them. First, manufactured things are kin: after a minute of contact she marks unattended objects or willing cursed tools as Artifacts — her energy spends the minute learning the object's name before it will ever pick it up — and marked things do not obey her; they recognize her. Second, the self is the first artifact: the silver body needs no breath, shrugs off poison, and her Null Rod is the golem's ancient refusal to be ensorcelled, suppressing a cursed tool's properties on a failed save. Third, making is mending: she wakes her kin into constructs, reshapes their metal, combines three into a Legacy Weapon, and repairs what breaks. Classify this correctly: not an animator of objects, but the head of a household that happens to be made.

Full progression

Level 1 through 20.

1Silver Body

The first artifact is herself. Unarmored, her armor class rises by one; poison finds no purchase; breath is a courtesy she no longer extends to herself — and one energy through her touch deals two dice of force to a construct or object. The golem, introduced. AC +1 while unarmored, resistance poison, no need to breathe. 1 CE touch deals 2d8 force to construct/object.

3Animate Artifact

The waking of the household. Two energy rouses one marked object into a minute-long construct — armored, enduring, striking for two dice — one at a time. Her kin, standing up to be counted. 2 CE: one marked object becomes 1-minute construct, AC 15, HP 10+level, attack 2d8; max one.

6Liquimetal

The remolding. Two energy reshapes a marked metal object or tool — a weapon's form changed, cover or a bridge raised — without disturbing its magical properties. The artisan's prerogative: the shape is negotiable; the soul is not. 2 CE reshape a marked metal object/tool, changing weapon form or creating cover/bridge without changing magical properties.

9Null Rod

The refusal. As a reaction for three energy against a cursed tool within thirty feet, its wielder makes a Charisma save — and on failure the tool's activated properties sleep until her next turn ends, the mundane weapon beneath untouched. The golem's ancient answer to being ensorcelled. Reaction 3 CE against cursed tool within 30 ft: wielder Cha save; on failure its activated properties suppressed until end next turn, mundane weapon function remains.

11Construct Legion

The household mobilized. She may maintain her proficiency in animated Artifacts — each armored, each enduring — but they act as one group, making at most her proficiency in attacks on a single bonus-action command. A family that drills together. Maintain PB animated Artifacts (each: AC 14, HP 20, speed 25 ft), but they act as one group and collectively make at most PB attacks (each attack at your CE attack bonus for 2d8 force); commanding the group is a bonus action.

13Legacy Weapon

The heirloom forged. Five energy combines three marked objects into one temporary Large construct or tool for a minute — heavy armor, deep health, a five-dice strike or a sixty-Integrity barrier. Three relations, one ancestor. Combine three marked objects into one temporary Large construct/tool for 5 CE, 1 minute: AC 18, 60 HP, attack 5d10 or barrier 60 Integrity.

15Creator's Touch

The mending hand. Three energy repairs her constructs and cursed tools for four dice — and once per long rest, ten minutes and surviving pieces may restore one destroyed non-legendary cursed tool. In her household, nothing is thrown away. Repair constructs/cursed tools for 4d10 with 3 CE. Once/long rest repair one destroyed non-legendary cursed tool after 10 minutes if pieces remain.

18Domain — Argentum

At the twentieth count the foundry declares itself, and she chooses its shift: Animate — two environmental objects strike for four dice each at different targets; Silence — one hostile cursed tool's wielder saves or its properties sleep until the next count. The workshop, given jurisdiction. Initiative 20 choose ANIMATE or SILENCE. Animate: two environmental objects attack for 4d10 each against different targets, Dex save. Silence: one hostile cursed tool makes wielder Cha save or loses activated properties until next count 20.

20Father of Machines

The patriarch — or matriarch; the golem does not specify. Her Artifact cap becomes ten, her first animation each combat costs nothing, she may mark as an action, and her Legacy Weapon may wield one cursed tool's activation once per round. The household, at full strength. Artifact cap 10. First animation each combat costs 0. Mark an object as action. Legacy Weapon may incorporate one cursed tool activation once/round.

Pinnacle expression

Maximum, Domain, or equivalent.

Maximum — Great Creator (10 CE; action): All things wake. Ten energy animates every marked Artifact for three rounds, past her normal cap — resolved as up to three swarms, so the table does not drown in the family reunion. The creator's whole household, standing. animate every marked Artifact for 3 rounds without normal active cap; resolve them as up to three swarms to avoid turn bloat.

Feats

Technique Feats

Walking Ballista

She animates with purpose. An object with a ranged function wakes as a Walking Ballista — a sixty-foot ranged strike for two dice of force, no melee, five fewer hit points for the delicate clockwork. The household has artillery now. You can animate with purpose. When you use Animate Artifact on an object with a ranged function or shape — bow, crossbow, cannon, sling, or similar — it becomes a Walking Ballista: its attack becomes a 60-foot ranged attack dealing 2d8 force, but it cannot make melee attacks and its HP is reduced by 5 for the delicate clockwork. You choose this configuration when you animate it; changing it requires re-animating.

Voltaic Key

She carries a silver wand of command. Once per round on her turn she may move one animated Artifact ten feet — no action, no command spent — though the key's pull is gentle: nothing lifted, nothing dragged through barriers or out of grapples. A shepherd's crook, not a chain. You carry a voltaic key — a silver wand of command for your machines. Once per round on your turn, you may move one animated Artifact you control up to 10 feet; this costs no action and does not consume your command. The key's pull is gentle: it cannot lift an Artifact off the ground, move it through a barrier, or drag it out of a grapple.

Darksteel

Her plating can lock rigid: as a bonus action one animated Artifact goes immobile until her next turn, granting half cover to those behind it. Sometimes the kindest thing a relation can do is stand still and be a wall. as a bonus action, one animated Artifact becomes immobile until the start of your next turn; while immobile it grants half cover to creatures behind it.

Urza's Legacy

When she uses Creator's Touch she may grant a temporary modification instead of healing — ten feet of range, one damage resistance, or advantage on one save type, lasting until her next long rest. The mending hand also improves. when you use Creator's Touch, you may grant a temporary modification instead of healing: +10 ft range, one damage resistance, or advantage on one save type, lasting until your next long rest.

Rules integration

Edge cases and shared systems.

Shared rulings: Technique DC = 8 + PB + CE ability modifier. Direct rolled attacks may crit and use the campaign Black Flash procedure when eligible. Tokens, autonomous summons, passive damage, and initiative-20 Domain effects cannot Black Flash. Maximum Output modifies ordinary supported techniques, never named Maxima or Domains.
