Feature map

Level-by-level features, as the figure refined them.

L1

The Martyr's Ledger

passive

Tier III · Support / Striker · Suggested CE ability: CHA · Primary verb: Endure. Your technique save DC = 8 + your proficiency bonus + your CHA modifier.

Wallace tracks all damage taken by willing creatures within 60 ft of him since his last turn — the Ledger. At the start of each of his turns, every willing creature in the aura gains temporary HP equal to half the Ledger's total (rounded down), distributed as Wallace chooses (he may pile it onto the dying). The Ledger resets each round. Pain, entered promptly, pays out promptly.

L1

Claymore

action

Melee weapon attack: +PB + STR to hit, reach 5 ft, 2d6 + STR slashing, heavy, two-handed. On a critical hit, the target must succeed on a WIS save vs the technique DC or be frightened of Wallace for 1 minute (the executioner's shadow falls across the field). Against crowned targets (kings, tyrants, those who rule by blood-right), the claymore deals an additional 1d8 force damage — the sword knows.

L3

Stirling Cry

bonus action, 2 CE

All willing creatures within 60 ft gain +2 to attack rolls until the start of his next turn. If any of them took damage since his last turn, the bonus becomes +3 to attacks and +2 to damage — the line steadies because it bled.

L3

Take the Field

reaction, 1 CE

When a willing creature within 30 ft is hit by an attack, Wallace may move up to his speed toward the attacker (this movement provokes no opportunity attacks — the field opens for him) and make one claymore attack against it.

L6

Falkirk Line

action, 4 CE

The shield-wall. For 1 minute, willing creatures within 30 ft of Wallace gain +2 AC, and any enemy that willingly moves through the space of the formation provokes an opportunity attack from every willing creature in it (not just one). The formation moves with him — the wall walks.

L6

Quartered, Not Ended

passive

Once per long rest, when Wallace would be reduced to 0 HP, he is reduced to 1 HP instead and gains temporary HP equal to 5 × his level — the pieces refuse to scatter. Attacks against him have disadvantage until the end of his next turn (the quartering has begun, and it is terrible to watch). Division: as a bonus action, Wallace may split into four aspects — one at each quartering-site (Newcastle, Berwick, Stirling, Perth). Each aspect has hit points equal to one quarter of his maximum, is a quarter of his strength in all other respects (the GM adjudicates the split at the table), and fights on its own initiative. A destroyed aspect does not trigger the death-defiance above; it reforms whole at the next dawn. If all four aspects are destroyed on the same day, Wallace is put down for good — four coordinated strikes across Scotland is the only way to kill him.

L11

The Speech

action, 8 CE, once per day

"They may take our lives, but they'll never take our freedom." All willing creatures within 120 ft that can hear him: regain 4d8 + CHA modifier HP, gain immunity to frightened and charmed for 1 hour, and deal +1d6 damage with weapon attacks for 1 minute. All enemies within 120 ft that can hear him: WIS save vs technique DC or be frightened for 1 minute. The GM is encouraged to deliver the speech in full, in character, before rolling anything. The table will remember.

L18

Freedom Is a Weapon

action, 10 CE

The Maximum. For 1 minute: - Every time a willing creature within 60 ft takes damage, Wallace may make a claymore attack as a reaction against the source (one reaction per triggering creature per turn — the field is full of answers). - The Martyr's Ledger pays out at full value: temp HP equals the total damage taken, not half. He does not dodge. He does not retreat. For one minute, every wound his people take is a sword in his hand.

Pinnacle expression

Maximum, Domain, or equivalent.

Capstone material.

Level 20 · Capstone

FREEDOM (capstone, passive aura)

capstone, passive aura

A permanent 60-ft aura. At the start of each willing creature's turn in the aura, Wallace may use his bonus action to end any number of the following conditions on them: charmed, frightened, paralyzed, restrained, stunned. Chains — mundane or magical — simply fail to hold where he stands (nonmagical restraints break automatically; magical ones grant an immediate save with advantage). And once per long rest, he may ignore any single effect that would kill, banish, possess, or permanently restrain him — no save required, no counter possible. He looks at it, and says the word, and it does not take. You'll never take it.
