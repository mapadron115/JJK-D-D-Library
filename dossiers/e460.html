Cursed bane edge

Passive · No CE · +3 to attack and damage with this weapon; each hit deals an additional 2d6 necrotic damage.

The blade is generous where it counts, +3 to hit and damage on every swing, and each hit carries 2d6 necrotic besides. The steel opens the wound; the rot does the lingering work. When attacking with this weapon you gain a +3 bonus to attack and damage rolls. On a hit, the weapon deals an additional 2d6 necrotic damage.

Severing critical

Passive · No CE · On a critical hit: the target loses a limb; roll a d4 — 1 left arm, 2 right arm, 3 left leg, 4 right leg.

And the critical hit is not a bigger wound but a smaller victim: the target loses a limb outright, chosen by a d4. A man reduced by a quarter is a battle three-quarters won. On a critical hit, the target loses a limb. Roll a d4: 1 left arm, 2 right arm, 3 left leg, 4 right leg.

Field notes. Subject: a greatsword that hunts like an executioner. Two truths. First: every wound it deals is two wounds, the steel's cut, and behind it 2d6 of necrotic rot, because the blade is not content to injure but insists on diminishing. Second: on a critical hit it does not merely wound, it amputates. A d4 decides: left arm, right arm, left leg, right leg. No save, no contest; the crit is the sentence and the limb is the price. Assess the arithmetic coldly: +3 to hit means more crits land, more crits mean more severed limbs, and a foe with three limbs is a problem half-solved. This is not a duelist's sword. It is a butcher's ledger, and every entry is paid in flesh.
