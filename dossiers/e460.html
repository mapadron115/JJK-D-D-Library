Features & procedures

Properties & techniques

Exact player-facing procedures

Cursed bane edge

When attacking with this weapon you gain a +3 bonus to attack and damage rolls. On a hit, the weapon deals an additional 2d6 necrotic damage.

Severing critical

On a critical hit, the target loses a limb. Roll a d4: 1 left arm, 2 right arm, 3 left leg, 4 right leg.

Rules integration

Edge cases and shared systems.

Campaign canon notes

Note: severed limbs follow the campaign's limb-removal rules. Targets without limbs are unaffected by Severing critical.
