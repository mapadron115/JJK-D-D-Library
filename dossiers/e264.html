Esoteric Art

You were born with the ability to create cursed paintings leading to unique subspaces.

Power tierTier IV · Special Grade
Combat roleController
Core enginePainted subspaces with shikigami, structures, traps

Full progression

Feature map

1Cursed Painting.

Starting at 1st level, you can spend 1 cursed energy to create a painting of cursed ink under a Medium or smaller creature within 30 ft, forcing them to make a Dexterity saving throw. On a failed save, they are sucked into the painting. The painting takes the form of an empty 30 ft subspace of greyscale. You can change the color or create simple illustrations within the subspace as an action. You can enter, exit, place, or remove an item from the subspace of your paintings as a free action once per turn without spending cursed energy. Your subspace becomes 120 ft at 3rd level, 600 ft at 6th, and 1200 ft at 11th level.

At the beginning of your turn, you must spend 1 CE to keep any creatures (including yourself) within a painting. Otherwise, they reappear at the point they disappeared.

You can use a binding vow to remove the cost at the beginning of your turn by placing a method of escape. This creates an Exit Painting in a random, but accessible area within the subspace that allows a creature to exit the painting, reappearing in the space they disappeared.

You can have a number of subspaces equal to your CE modifier, but if you have creatures in different paintings, you must spend 1 cursed energy for each subspace at the start of your turn. This increases to twice your CE modifier of paintings at 11th level.

Alternatively, you can paint a physical painting and imbue it with up to 3 cursed energy during its creation, spending 1 hour painting per cursed energy. A creature that gets within 5 ft and can see the painting must make a Dexterity saving throw, getting sucked into the painting on a failed save. A painting imbued this way keeps a creature trapped for 3 rounds. The painting's subspace can only be accessed through the physical painting.

At 3rd level, you can spend additional cursed energy up to your Cursed Energy modifier to imbue the painting with more power and increase the painting time. This increases to twice your CE modifier at 11th level. This allows the painting to keep an unwilling creature trapped for an additional round for every cursed energy spent. You can also use this painting to store items, but it also counts against the number of paintings you can have at once.

At 6th level, you can instead cause the painting to spend the imbued cursed energy to use Ink Wave against a creature that moves within 5 ft of it.

3Impasto Canvas.

Ink Shikigami.

As an action, you can spend up to your Cursed Energy modifier to create one Ink Wolf for each cursed energy spent. Each wolf appears in an unoccupied space you can see anywhere within your painting and acts on your initiative. The wolves obey your verbal commands (no action required).

Exit Shift.

You move the Exit Painting up to 80 ft as an action.

Difficult Terrain.

As an action, you create a 15 ft radius area of difficult terrain anywhere within your painting. The terrain lasts until the painting ends or until you use this feature again.

Structure Generation.

As an action, you create one structure from cursed ink anywhere within your painting. The structure must fit entirely within a 30 ft cube and can be any ordinary structure you choose, such as walls, platforms, towers, bridges, or buildings. The structure's AC equals your CE save DC and each 5 ft by 5 ft section has hit points equal to 5 x your proficiency bonus. If reduced to 0 hit points, it dissolves into ink and disappears. Any creatures standing on the structure when it is destroyed fall or are affected at the DM's discretion. You can create, destroy, or replace a structure while within the painting.

Trap Creation.

As an action, you create one trap of your choice in an unoccupied 5 ft square anywhere within your painting. The trap lasts until triggered or until the painting ends. A creature can detect a trap with an Intelligence (Investigation) or Wisdom (Perception) check against your Cursed Energy save DC. All traps use your CE save DC.

  • Pit Trap. When a creature enters the trapped space, it must succeed on a Dexterity saving throw or fall 20 feet into the pit, taking 2d6 bludgeoning damage and falling prone.
  • Spike Trap. When a creature enters the trapped space, it must succeed on a Dexterity saving throw or fall 10 feet into the pit, taking 2d10 piercing damage and falling prone.
  • Snare Trap. When a creature enters the trapped space, it must succeed on a Dexterity saving throw or become restrained. A creature can use its action to make a Strength (Athletics) or Dexterity (Acrobatics) check, freeing the target on a success.
  • Rolling Stone Trap. When triggered, an ink-formed boulder rolls in a 5 ft wide 30 ft line from the trap's space in a direction you choose. Each creature in the line must make a Dexterity saving throw, taking 4d10 bludgeoning damage and knocked prone on a fail, or half as much damage and not prone on a success.
6Sgraffito.

At 6th level, you have improved your technique. You can now manipulate your painting as a bonus action and gain the following improvements and options:

Ink Shikigami. You can summon new types of shikigami: Greater Ink Wolf (2 CE), Ink Crow (3 CE).

Exit Shift. You cause the location of the Exit Painting to a new random accessible location in the subspace.

Difficult Terrain. You can now create a 30 ft radius area of difficult terrain anywhere within your painting.

Structure Generation. Each 5 ft by 5 ft section of a structure you create now has hit points equal to 10 x your proficiency bonus.

Trap Creation. Your traps now deal an additional 2 damage dice. This increases to an additional 4 damage dice at 11th level.

Ink Wave. As an action for 2 cursed energy, you create a 30 ft cone originating from you or an Ink Shikigami. A creature within the cone must make a Constitution saving throw, taking your proficiency bonus in d6s of necrotic damage and be blinded until the end of their next turn on a failed save. (This can also be used outside your subspace)

Larger Frames. You can now use Cursed Painting on Large or smaller creatures. This increases to Huge at 11th level.

11Panoramic Composition.

At 11th level, as an action, you can spend cursed energy up to twice your CE modifier, forcing that number of creatures within 60 ft to make a Dexterity saving throw. On a failed save, they are sucked into your cursed paintings.

18Domain Expansion: Magnum Opus.

At 18th level you have learned to expand your domain. The inside of your domain counts as your painting's subspace, expanding to the same size as your subspace and following the same rules, except your Ink Shikigami and Traps are imbued with your guaranteed hit. At initiative 20, two different Impasto Canvas effects of your choice happen (if you choose Ink Shikigami, they still cost Cursed Energy).

Innate Technique Feats

Build-defining options.

These are optional character-building feats. You must meet each listed prerequisite before selecting it.

Prerequisite: Must have Cursed Painting

Diptych

You have learned to fold, expand, and even connect the subspaces of your paintings. When you enter one of your painting subspaces, you can spend 4 cursed energy to exit from one of your physical paintings. Additionally, the number of paintings you can have at once increases by your proficiency bonus and the area of your subspace doubles.

Prerequisite: Must have Impasto Canvas

Air Brush

As a reaction to an attack roll, you can spend 1 Cursed Energy to add +4 to your AC against the attack by forming a cursed painting, storing the attack in your subspace on a miss. If you have taken the dodge action, you can use this reaction on allies within 5 feet.

Once per turn when you take the Attack action, you can spend 1 cursed energy to replace one of your attacks on your turn with an attack from a Giant Ink Shikigami. Make a melee cursed energy attack against a creature you can see within 30 ft. You create a painting of cursed energy and one of your Ink Shikigami attacks from the painting, dealing 3d10 + your CE modifier necrotic damage on a hit. This increases to 4d10 at 6th level and 5d10 at 11th level.

Prerequisite: Must have Sgraffito

Gradient

As an action for 5 cursed energy, you create a subspace Gradient that fills a 30 ft cube centered on you for 1 minute (60 ft at 11th level). You can use your innate technique features within this subspace, but they cannot extend beyond it. As a bonus action for 2 CE while the Gradient persists, you can fade one willing creature you can see inside it into the color wash — they become invisible to creatures outside the Gradient until they attack, use a technique, or the Gradient ends.

Rules integration

Edge cases and shared systems.

Instant capture: the source omits an activation action for the instant Cursed Painting — campaign ruling: it is an action.
Subspace geometry: the listed measure (30/120/600/1200 ft) is the cube's side. Capacity is your actual CE modifier, twice at 11th level; residents do not count as separate paintings.
Upkeep: each occupied subspace costs 1 CE at the start of your turn; failure releases residents at the original entry point or the nearest legal safe space. Items can remain stored without occupying-creature upkeep.
Exit Painting vow: the exit must remain physically reachable — shifting it cannot seal it into solid ink or an inaccessible pocket.
Physical painting: the initial 1–3 CE payment gives 3 rounds; at 3rd level, additional input up to your modifier adds one round each (2x modifier at 11th), one work-hour per total CE. A destroyed painting releases occupants safely under campaign ruling, not erases them.
Panoramic Composition: one Dexterity save per chosen target; inherits size eligibility Large at 6th, Huge at 11th — not unlimited Gargantuan capture.
Caps (proposal): at most one active structure and one untriggered trap per subspace unless more paid capacity is expressly granted. A trap triggers once and is consumed. Added trap dice apply only to damaging traps — Snare gains no invented damage.
Domain: keep the source's large interior as a named campaign exception; the normal external barrier size applies. At initiative 20 choose two different Canvas options; a newly created trap may immediately affect one legal creature as the unique sure-hit (damage once), while other traps retain saves. Ordinary shikigami attacks still roll.
Diptych: doubles subspace area, not each linear dimension — a rectangular floor twice as long rather than eightfold cube volume. The 4 CE link connects only your owned physical paintings, not arbitrary portals.
Air Brush: protects you, or an adjacent ally if you took the Dodge action; only an attack missed because of the +4 is stored, not an entire enemy technique. One Attack-action substitution per turn.
Stored attacks: the source gives no release rule for Air Brush's stored attacks — recorded as a hold, not an infinite counterattack arsenal.
Gradient: select the internal-technique mode OR the awareness mode — never both. All temporary contents end on expiry. Ink Wave remains its stated exception usable outside the subspace.
