Decay Marks

The accounting of ruin: Marks of active disintegration, 5 per creature maximum, applied by direct contact, expiring after a minute if the target survives. At 5 Marks the next Decay hit triggers Crumble — extra damage and a severe injury check, never automatic death. Awakened chain spread applies fewer marks, so cities do not become save-or-die maps.

  • A Decay Mark represents active disintegration. A creature can have up to 5.
  • Marks are applied by direct technique contact and normally expire after 1 minute if the target survives.
  • At 5 Marks, the next Decay hit triggers Crumble: extra damage and a severe injury check, not automatic death.
  • Awakened chain spread applies fewer marks than direct touch so cities do not become instant-save-or-die maps.

Field notes. Subject: hatred of the world, given absolute form. Three truths govern him. First, his CE awakened to a single rejection — whatever the hand touches should not exist — and never learned a second answer; the five-finger limit was the last mercy of a human body. Second, the Awakening is what happened when the CE stopped distinguishing the hand from the floor: decay now spreads through connected solid surfaces in widening circles. Third, Marks are the ledger — up to 5 per creature, expiring after a minute, and at 5 the next hit triggers Crumble: extra damage and a severe injury check, not automatic death. Hence the maxim: it is grief that cannot hold anything, so it lets nothing hold together.

Connected solids matter. Fluids, airborne creatures, and disconnected platforms are natural counters.
Maximum Output may improve ordinary Decay damage/radius but not Deika Collapse.
No Domain is necessary.
