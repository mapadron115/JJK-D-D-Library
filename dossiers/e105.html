Core law

What the technique actually does.

Name note:

this dossier was renamed Contact Decay (formerly “Decay”) to distinguish it from V.6 Rot’s unrelated “Decay” sub-feature (infectious blood), a different mechanic.

Decay Marks

  • A Decay Mark represents active disintegration. A creature can have up to 5.
  • Marks are applied by direct technique contact and normally expire after 1 minute if the target survives.
  • At 5 Marks, the next Decay hit triggers Crumble: extra damage and a severe injury check, not automatic death.
  • Awakened chain spread applies fewer marks than direct touch so cities do not become instant-save-or-die maps.

Levels 1–4 d6

The original expansion repeatedly references a Decay Die without printing its value. This reviewed edition completes that omission using the same four-step progression explicitly established by Vector Authority in the same book. This is a codex clarification, not a claimed quotation from the source.

Full progression

Level 1–20 Progression

1Five-Finger Decay

On a successful unarmed/grapple touch, 1 CE: +1 Decay Die necrotic and 1 Mark. Objects take double dice.

3Crumbling Hold

A creature you are grappling gains 1 additional Mark at start of your turn. You may voluntarily suppress Decay on touched targets.

6Crumble

When you hit a 5-Mark target, spend 3 CE: deal +4 Decay Dice necrotic and clear all Marks. If single-hit damage meets injury threshold, it qualifies as a targeted catastrophic hit without disadvantage.

9Four Fingers

Direct Decay no longer requires a full-hand flavor condition; any deliberate hand contact works. Once/turn when a creature with Marks touches another creature or solid structure, you may spend 1 CE to put 1 Mark on the second target.

11Awakening

Action, 5 CE: touch solid ground. Decay spreads through connected solid surfaces in a 30-ft radius. Creatures touching affected solid matter make Dex save, taking 5 Decay Dice necrotic and 2 Marks on failure, half/1 Mark on success.

13Selective Ruin

When using Awakening, choose up to CE modifier creatures/objects that are completely spared. Radius becomes 60 ft.

15Avalanche

Awakening can propagate through debris it creates until end turn; radius 90 ft. Failed save also prone and movement through ruined area is difficult terrain.

18Maximum Technique — Deika Collapse

10 CE, action: 150-ft radius through connected solid matter, Dex save, 10 Decay Dice necrotic and 3 Marks on failure, half/1 on success. Structures take maximum damage. After use you take 4 Decay Dice unpreventable recoil and one arm suffers a Break check.

20Liberated Decay

Awakening radius 120 ft at normal cost. Direct touch applies 2 Marks. Once/turn when a creature reaches 5 Marks, trigger Crumble for 0 CE. You may precisely route chain Decay around chosen creatures/objects.

Pinnacle expression

Pinnacle / Maximum

Maximum is Deika Collapse at level 18. Decay deliberately has no Domain: its awakened ground propagation already creates a pseudo-domain catastrophe without barrier mechanics.

Feats

Technique Feats

Prerequisite: Decay

Dustproof

Your Decay recognizes its maker: you are immune to your own Decay damage, and ruined terrain you create is never difficult terrain for you. When you trigger Awakening or Deika Collapse, you may ride the crumble as part of the same action, moving up to your speed across the affected area — sinking through cracked ground and erupting at its edge. This movement does not provoke opportunity attacks from creatures standing in the decay.

Prerequisite: level 9

Chain Reaction

A creature reduced to 0 by Decay automatically spreads 1 Mark to all creatures touching it.

Prerequisite: level 6

Selective Hand

You can make ordinary hand contact without accidental Decay even under stress; advantage on saves against effects that would force your technique to trigger.

Prerequisite: Awakening

Ruined City

You shape the ruin as you make it: when you use Awakening, you may spend 1 CE to raise one 10-ft section of debris into a 5-ft-high rubble rampart (half cover) or collapse it into a 10-ft-deep pit (climb check to escape).

Rules integration

Binding Rulings

Decay does not instantly kill creatures merely because it is lethal in source fiction; Marks and injury thresholds make it playable.
Connected solids matter. Fluids, airborne creatures, and disconnected platforms are natural counters.
Maximum Output may improve ordinary Decay damage/radius but not Deika Collapse.
No Domain is necessary.
