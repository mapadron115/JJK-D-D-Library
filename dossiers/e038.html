Directive Arrows

Write glowing arrows into the battlefield, ride their instructions at impossible speed, redirect weapons and enemies, and turn a carefully drawn route into one decisive strike.

Combat roleSkirmisher / controller
Core cost1 CE per inscription
Weapon ruleAny weapon attack
Core law

An arrow is an instruction.

The arrows are placed objects of cursed information. They do not roll, accumulate, or become another point currency: their positions and directions are the resource.

Power identity: the map is the engine.

Directive Arrows earns Tier III through repeatable scene control, party movement, reflected ranged attacks, scalable weapon pressure, and a lethal late-game Domain. It has no permanent assets, no nullification, no Evil Eye, and no separate resource meter.

Inscribe

Create a route

As a bonus action, spend 1 CE to create a number of Directive Arrows equal to your PB. Place them one at a time at points you can see within placement range, on a surface or suspended in open space, and choose each arrow’s direction.

Enter

Activate an arrow

While you occupy an arrow’s 5-foot space on your turn, you may expend it, including immediately after creating it in your space. It moves you the full Arrow Distance in its direction without using your speed or provoking opportunity attacks.

Chain

Change direction

If directed movement carries you through another arrow, you may activate that arrow immediately. The new direction replaces any distance remaining from the previous arrow. This is an explicit exception allowing different arrows to chain.

Strike

Cash in the route

Once per turn after you hit with any weapon attack, before rolling damage, add one Arrow Die of force damage for each arrow you activated that turn, maximum PB dice. This is Vector Edge.

Arrow statistics

A visible cursed glyph

  • Each arrow occupies one 5-foot space.
  • Your arrows must be at least 5 feet apart.
  • They are intangible and do not block movement.
  • They can be perceived through normal CE perception.
  • They are technique effects, not creatures or damageable objects.

Active capacity

Replace, never overflow

You may have up to twice your PB in active arrows. At level 18 this becomes three times PB. If an inscription would exceed that number, choose which of your existing arrows disappear before placing the new ones.

Movement limit

PB activations per turn

Before Shared Route, only you can activate your arrows. A permitted creature can activate no more than your PB of your arrows during one turn. Directed movement is not teleportation, ignores difficult terrain, and counts as distance traveled for Directive Arrows features.

Obstructions

Reality still has walls

Directed movement cannot pass through a solid obstruction or total cover. It stops in the last legal space before the obstacle. It causes no collision damage unless a feature expressly supplies it.

Loss of movement

Pin the accelerator

A creature whose speed is 0 cannot voluntarily activate an arrow. Grappling, restraint, tight terrain, readied attacks at visible endpoints, and technique suppression are therefore meaningful counters.

Vector Edge timing:

it is declared after the weapon attack hits but before damage is rolled. Its dice are part of that attack, double on a critical hit, and are included in the completed critical damage if the attack becomes a Black Flash.

Core law

Choose the weapon. Draw the angle.

The technique rewards tactical positioning and accuracy without requiring one prescribed weapon, ancestry, class, or maximum ability score.

Melee weapons

Arrive inside the guard

Heavy and reach weapons turn a route endpoint into immediate pressure. Melee users care most about clear landing spaces and preserving enough arrows to disengage afterward.

Ranged weapons

Fire from impossible sightlines

Bows, firearms, and thrown weapons can attack between route legs without ending adjacent. Projectile Redirection also enables deliberate ricochets prepared before the shot.

Dual wielding

Never lose a handoff

Several weapons protect Acceleration Sphere from ammunition, thrown-weapon, or disarm problems. The technique grants no automatic draw interactions beyond ordinary equipment rules.

Ability priorities

Accuracy and CE both matter

Your weapon ability governs every strike; your CE ability governs Imposed Directive, the Maximum field, and Domain saves. Constitution supports Technique Focus for the Maximum.

Route discipline

Place exits before entrances

A perfect attack route that ends beside the strongest enemy is unfinished. Save an arrow for retreat, elevation, cover, or the ally who needs to escape.

Party play

Draw for other people

From level 6, the party can use your map. Ask where allies want to end their turns and let Acceleraise Boost turn one key route into their own signature strike.

Complete combat loop:

inscribe PB arrows → enter the first arrow → chain visible directions → strike with Vector Edge → draw a free follow-up arrow → preserve or rebuild the escape route.

Full progression

Draw the inevitable route.

The technique gains new uses for the same arrows rather than introducing an unrelated meter or automatic Cursed Technique Reversal.

1Directive Arrows

Inscribe arrows, ride them, chain routes, and empower one weapon hit.

Directive Inscription

Bonus action, 1 CE: create PB arrows within 60 feet. They last 1 minute without Technique Focus. Maintain up to 2 × PB active arrows.

Arrowpath

Each activation moves you 20 feet. Activate up to PB arrows during a turn. An arrow is expended when activated unless a later feature says otherwise.

Vector Edge

Once per turn after a weapon attack hits, add one d6 force per arrow you activated that turn, maximum PBd6. The weapon may be melee, ranged, thrown, improvised, mundane, or cursed; it must use a weapon attack roll.

3Quick Inscription & Acceleration Sphere

Successful weapon attacks draw the next step, and a single target can be encircled.

Quick Inscription

Once on each of your turns immediately after you hit with a weapon attack, create one Directive Arrow without an action or CE. Place it in an unoccupied space within 5 feet of you or the target. It obeys normal duration, spacing, and capacity.

Acceleration Sphere

As an action, spend CE equal to PB and choose one creature within 60 feet. Resolve a sequence of PB temporary arrows and PB weapon attacks using the complete procedure in Advanced Applications.

6Living Directives

Your route can accelerate allies, command enemies, and bend incoming projectiles.

Shared Route

A willing creature you permit may activate your arrows on its turn, up to PB activations. It follows all Arrowpath rules but gains no Vector Edge unless affected by Acceleraise Boost.

Acceleraise Boost

Bonus action and 2 CE: briefly increase a willing ally’s speed and let its weapon strike benefit from your route.

Imposed Directive

Once per turn after a weapon hit, spend 2 CE and force a Strength save to launch the target along an arrow.

Projectile Redirection

Reaction and 1 CE: expend a nearby arrow after seeing a ranged attack roll and turn that attack along the arrow’s displayed direction.

11Perfect Inscription

Longer arrows, heavier impact, reusable routes, and a field that steals speed.

Improved arrows

Placement range becomes 120 feet, Arrow Distance becomes 30 feet, and your Arrow Die becomes a d8.

Persistent Line

Once on each of your turns when you activate one of your arrows, you may leave that arrow active rather than expending it. Movement still resolves normally.

Maximum Technique

Unlock Dominion of Borrowed Speed: a mobile Force Field that transfers movement from hostile creatures to your side.

18Absolute Course

The route stops being preparation and becomes a law imposed in real time.

Perfected arrows

Arrow Distance becomes 40 feet, your Arrow Die becomes a d10, and active capacity becomes 3 × PB.

Continuous Inscription

Quick Inscription may occur up to PB times on each of your turns, but no more than once from the same weapon attack.

Routebreaker

Once on your turn, immediately after you activate at least three arrows as one uninterrupted chain, make one weapon attack without an action. It is not the Attack action but otherwise resolves normally.

Domain Expansion

Unlock Palace of the Predestined Road, where every point is an arrow and every hostile creature is assigned an unavoidable route.

Features & procedures

Movement becomes offense.

These procedures define every action, target, save, duration, and interaction granted at levels 3 and 6.

Level 3 · Action · PB CE

Acceleration Sphere

Choose one creature you can see within 60 feet and a weapon you are wielding. Conjure PB temporary arrows around the target; they do not count against capacity and only you can activate them. Repeat the following sequence PB times:

  1. Move in a straight, unobstructed line to a different legal space within 20 feet of the target. The first leg may move up to 60 feet; every later leg may move up to your Arrow Distance. This is directed movement and does not provoke.
  2. Make one weapon attack against the chosen target from that space. Normal range, reach, ammunition, cover, and weapon requirements apply.

Each leg counts as one arrow activation for Vector Edge. Temporary arrows vanish when the action finishes, and you remain in the final space. If no different legal space or unobstructed path exists for a leg, that leg and all remaining legs are lost. Acceleration Sphere is an action, not the Attack action, so features adding attacks specifically to the Attack action do not add attacks to it.

Level 6 · Bonus action · 2 CE

Acceleraise Boost

Choose one willing creature other than you within 60 feet. Until the end of its next turn, its speed increases by 20 feet. Once during that duration, after it activates at least one of your arrows and hits with a weapon attack, it adds one of your current Arrow Dice per arrow it activated that turn, maximum a number of dice equal to half PB rounded up. These dice are force damage and part of the attack.

Level 6 · On-hit · 2 CE

Imposed Directive

Once per turn after you hit a creature with a weapon attack, choose a direction and spend 2 CE. The target makes a Strength save against your CE save DC. On a failure, it is moved your Arrow Distance in that direction without provoking; on a success, it is not moved.

If a failed target meets a solid obstruction before completing the distance, it stops in the last legal space, falls prone, and takes 1d6 force per 5 feet it could not travel, maximum PBd6. An unattended object takes the same damage. If the obstruction is another creature, that creature makes a Dexterity save, taking the collision damage and falling prone on failure, or taking half damage and remaining standing on success.

Level 6 · Reaction · 1 CE + one arrow

Projectile Redirection

When you see a ranged attack roll made against a creature or object within 10 feet of one of your active arrows, after seeing the roll but before the result is resolved, expend that arrow and spend 1 CE. From the arrow’s space, the attack continues in the arrow’s displayed direction. The first legal creature or object in a 5-foot-wide line within the attack’s normal maximum range becomes its new target; compare the original attack roll to that target’s AC. If the line contains no legal target, the attack misses.

The original attacker remains the source of the attack and all of its damage and riders. An attack can be redirected only once. Projectile Redirection cannot affect an area, cone, line, saving-throw effect, or attack that did not make an attack roll.

Creative permission:

Projectile Redirection may bend an enemy shot away, reflect it toward its source, or turn your own ranged attack into a prepared trick shot. The arrow’s visible direction—not a new choice made during the reaction—determines the redirected line.

Pinnacle expression

Dominion of Borrowed Speed

This is the technique’s Force Field and highest ordinary extension. It is not a Cursed Technique Reversal.

Open the moving field Activation

As an action, spend 10 CE. For up to 1 minute using Technique Focus, a 40-foot-radius aura moves with you. The field is visibly filled with upward arrows around beneficiaries and downward arrows around affected enemies.

Steal and redistribute motion Pulse

When the field appears and at the start of each of your turns, choose up to PB hostile creatures within it. Each makes a Constitution save. On a failure, choose one speed that creature possesses. It loses up to 20 feet from that speed, minimum 0, until the start of your next turn. Record the amount actually lost.

Nothing moves without being taken Transfer

For each creature that fails, choose you or one willing creature in the field and one speed the beneficiary possesses. That speed increases by the amount the hostile creature actually lost until the start of your next turn. You may assign multiple increments to the same creature. An enemy that loses no speed supplies no increase.

Draw without spending Arrow storm

After each pulse, create PB temporary Directive Arrows at points in the field without an action or CE. They ignore active capacity, last until the start of your next turn, and vanish when the field ends. They are still expended normally when activated.

Zero speed:

a creature reduced to 0 speed can still act, attack, and defend itself, but cannot voluntarily activate Directive Arrows. A successful pulse save prevents both the reduction and transfer. Only speed actually removed can be reassigned.

Pinnacle expression

Palace of the Predestined Road

A black mirrored palace unfolds beneath an endless lattice of luminous arrows. Inside, destination precedes motion and the path exists before the traveler chooses to move.

The Certain Path Expansion

Full-turn action, 20 CE, 60-foot-radius enclosed barrier centered on you, lasting 1 minute. Choose creatures to bring inside using the comprehensive Domain rules. Barrier defenses, clashes, anti-domain techniques, and Burnout function normally.

Every point is an arrow Infinite Route

You and willing creatures you permit treat every point in the Domain as containing a virtual Directive Arrow. On its turn, a permitted creature may activate up to PB virtual arrows, choosing each direction as it activates. Each moves it 40 feet, is never expended, and does not count against active capacity.

The route delivers—but does not aim—the strike Ordinary weapon law

Weapon attacks made after activating a virtual arrow remain ordinary rolled attacks and may critically hit or Black Flash normally. The Domain supplies impossible routes and a perfected Vector Edge; it does not guarantee those attacks. Predestined Route is the Domain’s sole sure-hit event.

Full route, every turn Perfect Vector Edge

For your Vector Edge, activating one or more virtual arrows is treated as activating PB arrows. It therefore adds PBd10 force damage to one weapon hit during that turn without requiring six separate movements.

Every enemy follows the line already drawn Initiative 20 · Predestined Route · sure-hit

At initiative count 20, each chosen hostile creature automatically takes 8d10 force damage and you move it along a path of up to 60 feet composed of no more than PB straight segments. This movement causes no opportunity attacks, cannot cross a solid obstruction, and may trigger hazards or other effects from entering a space. A creature protected from the sure-hit instead makes a Strength save, taking the full result on a failure or half damage with no movement on a success.

If the chosen path is stopped by an obstruction before reaching its endpoint, the creature takes an additional 1d10 force per 10 feet of untraveled path, maximum PBd10, and falls prone. An unattended object takes the same collision damage. A creature struck as the obstruction makes a Dexterity save, taking the collision damage and falling prone on failure, or taking half and remaining standing on success.

Certainty belongs to the route Sole sure-hit event

Predestined Route is the only automatic hostile effect. Simple Domain, Hollow Wicker Basket, Falling Blossom Emotion, or a Domain clash allows the protected creature’s Strength save. It does not remove virtual arrows, terrain, or ordinary rolled attacks.

Suppression is not erasure Terrain remains

Anti-domain protection suppresses the guarantee, not the palace or virtual arrows. Protected creatures can still be moved by willing use of an arrow, and you can still make ordinary rolled weapon attacks and use your other technique features.

Domain identity:

this is lethal because Predestined Route guarantees routing and can convert blocked distance into repeated impact damage. Weapon accuracy remains the character’s own responsibility; the Domain does not nullify techniques, erase actions, or fabricate a separate eye ability.

Innate Technique Feats

Every specialist draws a different map.

These optional feats expand preparation, rescue, ricochet, or ally offense. Each occupies a normal feat choice, may be taken only once, and no fifth Directive Arrows feat is part of this guide.

Prerequisite: Directive Inscription

Cartographer’s Reserve

Your active-arrow capacity increases by your PB, and arrows created by Directive Inscription last 10 minutes instead of 1 minute. When an inscription would exceed capacity, you may replace existing arrows after placing the new batch, allowing you to see the completed route before choosing which older glyphs vanish.

Prerequisite: Shared Route

Rescue Vector

When a willing creature is targeted by an attack while within 10 feet of one of your arrows, use your reaction, spend 2 CE, and expend that arrow to move the creature its full Arrow Distance in the displayed direction without provoking. Recalculate the attack’s reach, range, line, and cover from the new space; it misses only if the target is no longer legal.

Prerequisite: Projectile Redirection

Double Bank

When you use Projectile Redirection, you may spend 1 additional CE and expend a second active arrow whose space lies in the redirected line. The same attack changes direction again from that arrow before finding its final target. This is one reaction, uses the original attack roll and source, and is an explicit exception to the ordinary one-redirection limit; it cannot be redirected a third time.

Prerequisite: Acceleraise Boost

Shared Vector Edge

Once per round after a willing creature activates one or more of your arrows and hits with a weapon attack, use your reaction and spend 1 CE. Add one current Arrow Die per arrow it activated that turn, maximum PB dice, to that hit as force damage. This cannot combine with Acceleraise Boost on the same hit, but it can support a different ally or a later turn.

Rules integration

No missing procedures.

Use these rulings whenever Arrowpath intersects critical hits, Maximum Output, conditions, vertical movement, or another creature’s turn.

Technique math Normal CE statistics

CE attack = PB + CE modifier
CE save DC = 8 + PB + CE modifier

Weapon attacks continue using their normal weapon attack modifier. Directive Arrows does not replace Strength, Dexterity, or another weapon statistic with CE.

Vertical vectors Gravity resumes afterward

An arrow may point vertically or diagonally. A creature unsupported when directed movement ends immediately begins falling unless another arrow, feature, surface, or flying speed prevents it.

Conditions Speed 0 prevents voluntary use

Prone creatures may activate arrows. Grappled or restrained creatures normally have speed 0 and cannot voluntarily do so. Imposed Directive can still move them because that movement is forced by you.

Criticals and Black Flash Vector Edge joins the completed attack

Vector Edge and Acceleraise Boost damage are declared after a hit and before damage, so their dice double on that attack’s critical hit and are multiplied with the completed critical if Black Flash succeeds. Every rolled weapon attack in Acceleration Sphere may critically hit and attempt Black Flash independently.

Attack-action stacking Sphere is its own action

Acceleration Sphere makes PB weapon attacks as one technique action. It does not receive attacks granted specifically when taking the Attack action. Routebreaker is one separate weapon attack without an action. Distinct additional actions can still be used normally.

Maximum Output Enhance the chosen application

Maximum Output on Directive Inscription doubles its 1 CE cost. Range mode increases placement range by half; duration makes that batch last 15 rounds. Damage mode marks that batch: the next time you use Vector Edge after activating at least one marked arrow, add Arrow Dice equal to half the marked arrows activated for that hit, rounded up, then end the mark. Accuracy has no effect on a normal inscription.

Maximum Output: Sphere and field One activation, one mode

For Acceleration Sphere, accuracy applies to every attack made by that activation, range increases target range to 90 feet, and damage adds Arrow Dice equal to half PB rounded up to one Sphere hit. For Dominion of Borrowed Speed, range makes the aura 60 feet, duration makes it last 15 rounds, and accuracy increases its save DC by half PB rounded up.

Focus Arrows persist independently

Ordinary arrows and their one-minute duration require no Technique Focus. Dominion of Borrowed Speed does. Opening the Domain follows the comprehensive compatibility rule for an existing Focus effect.

Suppression Glyphs can be erased

Technique Extermination and similar effects may suppress or remove arrows normally. An arrow inside a suppressed area cannot activate; if still present when suppression ends, it resumes until its original duration expires.

No Reversal Not every technique has one

Directive Arrows has no Cursed Technique Reversal. RCT functions through the campaign’s universal rules but does not automatically produce a reversed arrow feature. Dominion of Borrowed Speed is a Maximum Technique, not a Reversal.

Counterplay Read the route and break the approach
  • Arrows are visible to CE perception, including their direction and likely endpoint.
  • A creature can avoid arrow spaces, prepare attacks at endpoints, create total cover, or obstruct straight lines.
  • Speed reduction, grappling, restraint, and effects preventing movement stop voluntary activation.
  • Forced movement can push the user away from a planned starting arrow.
  • Technique suppression can disable the route; destroying ordinary terrain can remove safe landing spaces.
  • Arrow movement still enters hazards and never passes through walls merely because it is fast.
