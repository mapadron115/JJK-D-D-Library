Feature map

Level-by-level features, as the figure refined them.

L1

Harmony

Action, 3 CE (Locate Creature) · Action, 2 CE (Speak with Animals or Tongues)

Connect to all things that exist. Cast Locate Creature with no components (range 1 mile; 1 mile at 6th, 5 miles at 11th, 30 miles at 18th), or cast Speak with Animals or Tongues (at 6th, Tongues extends to CE-mod additional creatures within range).

L3

Chaos

Action, 2 CE (either use)

Cast Chaos Bolt, automatically cast at 3rd level at 6th level, 5th level at 11th level, 8th level at 18th level. Or roll a d100 on the Chaos Table (X = CE modifier) — table reference: Chaos Table; if an effect is random it can also affect you unless it originates from you.

L6

Harmony: extended Tongues

— (passive upgrade)

Tongues cast through Harmony extends to a number of additional creatures equal to your CE modifier within range.

L6

Chaos Amplification

1 CE (amplify) · up to PB CE (upcast Chaos Bolt)

After using Chaos, spend 1 CE to increase the effect's duration or damage dice by your CE modifier (duration of 1 round or 1 minute is doubled instead). When casting Chaos Bolt, spend CE up to your PB to upcast it one spell level per CE spent (max 9th level).

L11

Balance

PB uses per long rest

When you use Chaos, choose Order (control a random element of the rolled effect: area placement, affected creature, teleport destination) or Equilibrium (up to CE-mod creatures are immune to the Chaos effect — no damage, cannot be selected).

L11

— (passive upgrade)

Chaos Bolt cast through Chaos is automatically cast as a 5th-level spell.

L18

Chaos Bolt: automatic 8th level

L18

Reversal: Cosmic Genesis (Entropy)

At start of turn, 1 CE

While Cosmic Genesis is active, at the start of your turn spend 1 CE to gain one benefit — Expansion: range +10 ft (max 120 ft); Addition: add a minor rule at half effectiveness (max 3 minor rules); Duration: +1 round.

Maximum Technique

Reversal: Cosmic Genesis

Action, 4 CE

Through reverse cursed technique you create your own logic and laws of physics. Force a new rule of reality in a 30-ft radius centered on you for twice your CE modifier rounds (min 2). The rule affects all creatures, objects, and terrain in range and must alter a fundamental interaction of reality; the DM has final say on validity. While using Cosmic Genesis, you cannot use your other Innate Technique features. Examples: Absolute Gravity (no flying/teleporting, jumping halved), Perfect Motion (ignore difficult terrain, speed cannot be reduced), Universal Sight (invisibility grants no benefit, hidden creatures revealed), Damage Inversion (fire damage heals for half instead of damaging).

Domain Expansion

Domain Expansion: Mandala of Mutual Paradox

Full turn, 20 CE · 60-ft enclosed radius · Clash 1,000 · burnout 3 rounds · duration 3 minutes (invented: 60-ft enclosed radius)

At initiative count 20, choose a Chaos Table effect of 90 or below as your guaranteed hit, affecting all creatures of your choice within the domain depending on the effect. While within the domain, every use of Chaos functions as the guaranteed hit — the effect's range encompasses the entirety of the domain and you pick specific targets.
