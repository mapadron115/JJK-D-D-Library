The Unopened Letter

An indestructible-looking sealed letter carried in a case. It begins each long rest with 3 intact Seals.

FormSealed envelope / talisman
RoleAnti-technique defense
EngineSeal
Attunement1 hour*
Properties & techniquesExact player-facing procedures

Return to Sender

reaction when a targeted technique would affect only you: break 1 Seal and 3 CE to force the technique attack to reroll or grant yourself advantage on its save; use new result.

Wrong Address

break 1 Seal when a technique would move, mark, curse, or track you; suppress that rider until end of your next turn.

Do Not Open

while at least 1 Seal remains, advantage against divination and remote tracking.

Contents Unknown

if all 3 Seals are broken before a long rest, roll a d6 after the encounter; on 1 the letter opens and releases a GM-scale cursed complication. Repairing seals requires a short ritual and long rest.

Balance & counterplay. Powerful defense carries an escalating narrative risk instead of unlimited negation.
