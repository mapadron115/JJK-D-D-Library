The Unopened Letter

An sealed letter — a Special artifact, destroyable only by artifact-breaking effects carried in a case. It begins each long rest with 3 intact Seals.

FormSealed envelope / talisman
RoleAnti-technique defense
EngineSeal
Attunement1 hour*
Features & procedures

Properties & techniques

Exact player-facing procedures

Return to Sender

reaction when a targeted technique would affect only you: break 1 Seal and 3 CE to force the technique attack to reroll or grant yourself advantage on its save; use new result.

Wrong Address

break 1 Seal when a technique would move, mark, curse, or track you; suppress that rider until end of your next turn. This suppression is not deactivation: the technique itself is not turned off, only the rider is suppressed.

Do Not Open

while at least 1 Seal remains, advantage against divination and remote tracking.

Contents Unknown

if all 3 Seals are broken before a long rest, roll a d6 immediately after the encounter in which the third Seal broke; on 1 the letter opens and releases a GM-scale cursed complication. Repairing seals requires a short ritual and long rest.

Rules integration

Cursed-tool attunement

Campaign canon

Cursed-tool attunement: you have PB attunement slots (2–6). This tool occupies one slot and requires a one-hour ritual. Attunement ends voluntarily over a short rest, on death, or when you replace it while over capacity. Distance does not break attunement. Cursed-tool attunement is separate from magic-item attunement.
Balance & counterplay. Powerful defense carries an escalating narrative risk instead of unlimited negation.
