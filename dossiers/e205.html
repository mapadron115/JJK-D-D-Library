The Unopened Letter

An sealed letter, a Special artifact, destroyable only by artifact-breaking effects carried in a case. It begins each long rest with 3 intact Seals.

FormSealed envelope / talisman A threat in a wrapper, never a weapon, carried in a case, destroyable only by what breaks artifacts.
RoleAnti-technique defense It turns away what would strike, seize, mark, or track its bearer, refusal as a discipline.
EngineSeal Three unopened threats; each broken is one refusal the world must honor.

Trigger · If all 3 Seals break before a long rest: roll d6 after that encounter; on a 1 the letter opens, releasing a GM-scale cursed complication · repairing seals requires a short ritual + long rest.

The price of refusal. Break all three Seals before the day's accounting, and something inside stirs, a complication no archive will define. Mark this: repairing the seals takes a full night's ritual, because the letter must be believed again. if all 3 Seals are broken before a long rest, roll a d6 immediately after the encounter in which the third Seal broke; on 1 the letter opens and releases a GM-scale cursed complication. Repairing seals requires a short ritual and long rest.

Field notes. Subject: a threat, sealed. Three truths govern it. First, its power is the world's certainty: no scholar has ever seen the contents, and that is the entire point, the letter is believed, and belief is armor. Second, its refusals are finite: three Seals, each one spent saying “not this, not now” to a technique that wanted you, a forced reroll here, a suppressed rider there, and every spent Seal brings the envelope nearer to its own terrible honesty. Third, the truth in its name: if all three break before a long rest, the letter may open, and the registry admits the complication is beyond its definition. Assess it correctly: this is not a shield but an escalating bargain, the more it refuses for you, the closer it comes to refusing for itself.

Rules integration

Cursed-tool attunement

Campaign canon

Cursed-tool attunement: you have PB attunement slots (2–6). This tool occupies one slot and requires a one-hour ritual. Attunement ends voluntarily over a short rest, on death, or when you replace it while over capacity. Distance does not break attunement. Cursed-tool attunement is separate from magic-item attunement.
Balance & counterplay. Powerful defense carries an escalating narrative risk instead of unlimited negation.
