Core law

At-the-table character reference

Recovered printed values & campaign clarifications

Free-action limits:

The Domain's free-action transmute covers unheld objects only; dismissing a transmuted object plus the free transmute is once per turn. Transmuted matter must be present — it is not summoned from nothing.

Elemental Manipulation rulings:

Fire Transmutation deals d6s and cannot extinguish magical or supernatural fire. Water Transmutation costs 1 CE per creature and is nonmagical only. Earth Transmutation is limited to 20 feet high, and no fall-damage stacking.

Alchemy of the Soul rulings:

Healing Transmutation costs 2 CE per d8 (the feature map's 1d8 per 2 CE controls) and is nonmagical. Poison Transmutation deals d6s. Flesh Transmutation is self-only, once per long rest, and cannot replicate technique features.

Area Transmutation:

Area Transmutation is once per turn at the same cost as the source, with radius equal to the original's duration value.

Railgun:

Railgun costs 5 CE, fires along a 5-foot-wide line, uses your Strength score + proficiency, deals 8d10 damage, and cannot target creatures inside total cover.

Blast and Sphere:

The blast of raw cursed energy accepts any amount of CE (minimum 2 CE, no cap); the 20-foot-radius sphere is a separate 10 CE activation. Both deal force damage in d8s; objects are not affected.

The fiction. Jujutsu scholars classify Cursed Transmutation as the refusal of fixed form: the user's cursed energy learned, in a moment of absolute necessity, that matter is merely cursed energy that has forgotten it was ever liquid. Registry notes describe the technique's governing law — nothing may be transmuted that is not already present — as the expression of its awakening: the sorcerer did not ask for something from nothing, only for what was already there to be something else. The prevailing theory holds that Alchemy of the Soul is the technique's true thesis: if stone can be rewritten, then so can flesh, and flesh can remember being rewritten.

Full progression

Feature map

1Cursed Transmutation.

Bonus action for 2 CE: transmute a 10-foot cube of inanimate material or a nonmagical object within 60 feet (grows to 20/30/40-foot cubes at 6th/11th/18th). Create weapons and tools but not complex machinery; AC 15, 20 HP per 10-foot cube (minimum of 20). Unheld objects last 24 hours; weapons/tools 1 hour; held objects last with concentration up to 1 hour. If a transmutation affects an area that is already occupied by a creature, the transmutation fails. You can dismiss a transmuted object as a free action.

3Elemental Manipulation.

Fire Transmutation. As an action, you can spend 2 CE to transmute a 10 ft cube of flammable material into fire. All creatures within the area must make a Dexterity saving throw. On a failed save, they take 3d6 fire damage, or half as much on a successful save.

Water Transmutation. As an action, you can spend 2 CE to transmute a 10 ft cube of material into water. You can choose to have the water be fresh or salt water.

Earth Transmutation. As an action, you can spend 3 CE to transmute a 10 ft cube of earth or stone into a different shape. You can create walls, pillars, or other structures. These structures have an AC of 15 and 30 hit points per 10 ft cube.

6Alchemy of the Soul.

At 6th level you have learned to transmute living matter. You gain the following benefits:

Healing Transmutation. As an action, you can spend any amount of CE to heal a creature you touch. The creature regains 1d8 hit points per 2 CE spent.

Poison Transmutation. As an action, you can spend 3 CE to transmute the air around a creature into poison. Choose a creature within 30 ft. They must make a Constitution saving throw. On a failed save, they take 4d6 poison damage and are poisoned for 1 minute. On a successful save, they take half as much damage and are not poisoned.

Flesh Transmutation. As an action, you can spend 5 CE to transmute your own flesh. You can heal yourself for 3d8 hit points, or you can grow an extra limb for 1 hour. The extra limb can hold and manipulate one additional object but grants no additional attacks or actions.

11Cursed Energy Transmutation.

At 11th level you have learned to transmute cursed energy itself. As an action, you can spend any amount of cursed energy to create a blast of raw cursed energy. All creatures in a 60 ft line must make a Dexterity saving throw. On a failed save, they take 1d8 force damage per 2 CE spent, or half as much on a successful save. Alternatively, you can spend 10 CE as an action to create a 20 ft radius sphere of cursed energy within 60 ft. All creatures within the sphere must make a Constitution saving throw. On a failed save, they take 6d8 force damage and are stunned until the end of their next turn. On a successful save, they take half as much damage and are not stunned.

Pinnacle expression

Maximum, Domain, or equivalent.

Domain Expansion — Mandala of Dependant Origination: 18 — full-turn action, 20 CE; 60-foot-radius enclosed Domain centered on you; 1 minute; Burnout 10 rounds. Sure-Hit Event — Mandala of Dependant Origination: at initiative count 20 each round, your entire Domain is affected by one Areal Transmute of your choice, which takes effect immediately; you may also use Transmute Self on willing creatures inside. An unprotected creature suffers the transmute’s full effect with no initial save. A creature protected by an anti-domain defense uses the Areal Transmute’s normal saving throw, or an Intelligence saving throw vs your CE save DC if it has none (invented); on a success it halves or negates the effect as the transmute describes.

Innate Technique Feats

Build-defining options.

These are optional character-building feats. You must meet each listed prerequisite before selecting it.

Prerequisite: Must have Cursed Transmutation

Master Alchemist

You can transmute magical objects, and you may perform a transmutation on an object you can see within 30 feet without touching it.

Prerequisite: Must have Elemental Manipulation

Philosopher's Stone

You have created a Philosopher’s Stone, a powerful alchemical artifact. It grants you two masterworks. Chrysopoeia: once per long rest, you may transmute up to 1 cubic foot of base metal into gold worth up to 100 gp. Panacea: you may crush a fragment of the Stone, which reforms at dawn, to end one poison, disease, or curse afflicting a creature you touch.

Prerequisite: Must have Alchemy of the Soul

Soul Alchemy

You have learned to transmute souls. As an action for 8 CE, you can forge a soul bond with a willing creature within 30 ft. While bonded, you can sense each other's emotions and location, and you can communicate telepathically. If one of you takes damage, the other can choose to take half of that damage instead. This bond lasts until dispelled or until one of you dies.

Rules integration

Edge cases and shared systems.

Rules priority: the reviewed rulings, explicit codex clarifications, and the level progression always control when original wording conflicts. Legacy Domain auto-hit language, action terminology, event timing, missing saves, and older scaling never override revised player rules.
Why Tier II: battlefield-shaping transmutation with elemental blasts, soul-bond teamwork, and raw-CE force blasts — broad utility with strong control, short of calamity output.
Healing Transmutation: 1d8 per 2 CE is kept as printed in the feature map; it is nonmagical healing and does not interact with spell-based healing modifiers.
Soul Alchemy: one soul bond at a time; forging a new bond ends the old one.
Philosopher's Stone: temporary hit points granted by Healing Transmutation never stack with other temporary hit points.

Cursed Transmutation
Starting at 1st level, your Cursed Energy Technique allows you to
transmute matter. As a bonus action for 2 CE, you can perform a
transmutation on a 10 ft cube of inanimate material or non magical
object within 60 ft of you. At 6th level, the size increases to a 20
ft cube. At 11th level, it increases to a 30 ft cube. At 18th level,
it increases to a 40 ft cube. This includes the creation of weapons
and tools from raw materials, but not the creation of complex
machinery. Objects created in this way have an AC of 15, 20 hit
points per 10 ft cube (minimum of 20), and are considered to be non
magical. Transmuted objects can also be used as cover, with an AC of
15 and 20 hit points per 10 ft cube. A transmuted object that is not
being held or carried will last for 24 hours, but a transmuted weapon
or tool will only last for 1 hour. A transmuted object that is held or
carried will last for as long as you maintain concentration on it, up
to 1 hour. If a transmutation affects an area that is already occupied
by a creature, the transmutation fails. You can dismiss a transmuted
object as a free action.

Domain Expansion: Grand Transmutation
At 18th level, you have mastered your technique, allowing you to
expand your domain. Within your domain, you can transmute matter as a
free action without spending cursed energy. At initiative 20, all
creatures within your domain must make a Constitution saving throw.
On a failed save, they take 10d10 force damage as their bodies are
transmuted. On a successful save, they take half as much damage.
