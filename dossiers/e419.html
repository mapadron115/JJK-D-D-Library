Features & procedures

Properties & techniques

Exact player-facing procedures

1 CE

Wailing Ring

As an action, you can spend 1 cursed energy to ring the bell. All cursed spirits within 15 feet must succeed on a DC 14 Constitution saving throw or take 2d10 thunder damage. A creature that succeeds on the save takes no damage.

Rules integration

Edge cases and shared systems.

Campaign canon notes

Table function: the source does not state the effect on a successful save. At the table, the save negates the damage entirely (save-or-nothing).
