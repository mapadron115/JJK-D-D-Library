Kinetic Imbuement

Core law

CE becomes stored motion.

Charge is not a second character resource. It is CE already paid and physically deposited in a specific object. Write the amount beside that object; when the object releases, the Charge is spent.

Level 1 · charge step

Charge Object

Touch an inorganic object and spend a positive amount of CE up to the deposit limit for its scale. The object gains that much Charge, up to its capacity. Further charge steps add Charge and reset its duration. Charge lasts 1 minute, glows or crackles under CE perception, and dissipates harmlessly when it expires. Expired or dispersed Charge never refunds CE.

You may maintain any number of charged objects. Available CE, object capacity, action economy, physical access, and duration are the limits; there is no separate active-object cap.

Impact release

Detonate

When a charged object strikes a creature or solid surface, it may detonate. Let C be the Charge released. Roll 2C Kinetic Dice as force damage. Maximum blast radius is 5 × C feet; choose any smaller radius in 5-foot increments, including 0 feet for a focused discharge.

You are unaffected by your own detonation. Every other creature in the radius makes a Dexterity save for half unless it was directly hit by the delivering attack.

Direct delivery

The attack carries the blast

If a charged object or Bound Conduit hits with an attack roll, its direct target takes the released kinetic damage without a save as part of that attack. It is not damaged again by the surrounding blast. Every other creature in the selected radius saves against the same unmodified, noncritical kinetic damage roll.

The direct kinetic dice double on a critical hit and belong to the completed critical damage for Black Flash. Secondary blast damage never copies the critical or Black Flash multiplier.

Destruction

The ammunition pays too

An ordinary charged object is destroyed when it detonates. The centered object takes maximum kinetic damage; other unattended objects and structures in the radius automatically fail their save and also take maximum kinetic damage. A cursed or magic item uses its campaign durability instead of being automatically erased. A Bound Conduit survives by definition.

Level 1 · replaces one attack

Loaded Toss

Throw a charged Token or throwable Handheld object at a creature within 60/180 feet. Make a ranged CE attack. On a hit, the projectile deals 1 Kinetic Die + CE modifier force damage and then detonates as a direct delivery. On a miss, choose an unoccupied impact point within 10 feet of the target that the projectile could physically reach; resolve an ordinary area detonation there.

If the object is a thrown weapon or ammunition, you may instead use its normal attack, range, and weapon damage; its charge still joins a hit.

One-hour bond

Bound Conduit

Bond one weapon or sturdy held implement during a short or long rest. It is a Handheld object with capacity 2 × PB. Charge it normally. After an attack made with it hits—including ammunition launched by a ranged conduit—you may release any positive amount of its stored Charge. The released kinetic damage joins the attack; unreleased Charge remains and its current duration continues.

The Conduit survives every discharge. Bonding another ends the first bond and harmlessly disperses its remaining Charge. The technique grants no weapon proficiency.

Level 1 · action · 1 CE

Fine Charge

Touch one inorganic lock, latch, simple machine, power input, or mechanism no larger than a 5-foot cube. Make a CE ability check against a DC chosen from the object’s construction and security. On success, quietly open, close, power, depower, or move one mechanism for up to 1 minute without an explosion. On failure, nothing changes and the CE is spent.

Fine Charge supplies energy and precise motion, not knowledge: it cannot invent a password, rewrite software, understand an unknown machine, open a metaphysical seal, or reproduce a magic item’s power.

Core damage example: a card carrying 3 Charge creates 6 Kinetic Dice and a blast radius from 0 to 15 feet. A direct Loaded Toss hit deals its 1 base Kinetic Die + CE modifier plus those 6 dice; creatures around the target save against only the 6-die blast.

Object procedure

Small loads quickly. Large remembers longer.

The player states the intended object; the GM selects the closest scale. A coherent vehicle or manufactured object may be one target. For sprawling terrain or a structure with unclear boundaries, each selected 20-foot cube is one Set Piece.

One object

No semantic planet charge

A charge step targets one discrete object, coherent vehicle, or defined structure section. Connected matter does not make the entire street, city, continent, or planet one object. Chain Reaction and the Domain are the tools for spreading across distinct targets.

Adding Charge

Capacity is not deposit rate

A Handheld object can hold 2 × PB but a normal bonus action deposits only PB. It therefore takes at least two charge steps to fill. A Landmark has no capacity ceiling, but the user must remain in contact and spend full turns to build it.

Level upgrades

Mass becomes easier

At level 11, a Set Piece or Landmark step may deposit up to 2 × PB. At level 18 it may deposit up to 3 × PB. Token and Handheld deposit limits remain PB because their rapid action already supplies their advantage.

Choosing a scale is not a balance negotiation: use how the object functions in the scene. A deck held as one pack is Handheld; individual cards are Tokens. A train is a Landmark even if a character has supernatural carrying capacity.

Advanced applications

Every object is a choice of timing.

These procedures make mines, ricochets, planted charges, movement, and chain reactions playable without hidden GM rulings.

Level 3

Trigger Discipline

Whenever you add Charge, choose or replace its trigger:

  • Impact: it arms when it leaves your possession or when you choose to release it through a successful Conduit strike; routine handling does not set it off.
  • Timer: choose from 1 to 10 rounds. It detonates at the end of your turn that many rounds later.
  • Command: during your turn, detonate one perceived charged object within 300 feet without an action. Outside your turn, use your reaction when a perceivable condition you name occurs.

At level 11, you sense the direction of your charges and command them within 1 mile on the same plane. At level 18, command range is anywhere on the same plane if you know the object’s location.

Level 3 · alternate release

Vector Discharge

Instead of damage and an area, expend all Charge from one object to direct motion. Choose that object or one creature in contact with it. A willing target moves along a path you choose; an unwilling target makes a Strength save and moves on failure. Distance is 10 feet per Charge, becoming 20 feet at level 11 and 30 feet at level 18. This movement causes no opportunity attacks.

If a solid obstruction stops the movement, the target falls prone and takes 1d6 bludgeoning per 10 feet it could not travel, maximum PBd6. An unattended object needs no save and is not destroyed by a purely vector release.

Level 3 · passive

Kinetic Footwork

Your walking speed increases by 10 feet. Once on each of your turns immediately after one of your charged objects releases, move up to 10 feet without spending speed or provoking opportunity attacks. This movement may occur before a Chain Reaction continues.

Level 3 · action · N × X CE

Cardsharp Barrage

Choose N Tokens you hold, from 1 to PB, and one Charge value X, from 1 to PB. Spend N × X CE. Each Token gains X Charge and is immediately thrown with Loaded Toss; make N separate ranged CE attacks and distribute them among targets within 60/180 feet. Each hit deals its normal Loaded Toss damage plus the direct detonation. Each miss creates its own impact-point blast.

Every attack may critically hit and attempt Black Flash independently. The action is not the Attack action, requires actual Tokens, and cannot draw ammunition that the character does not possess.

Level 6

Chain Reaction

After one detonation fully resolves, you may detonate any charged objects in its area without an action, even if their chosen trigger has not occurred. Resolve each object separately, then continue outward. A creature can take damage once from each distinct detonation; overlapping paid charges are not collapsed into one same-name damage instance.

Counterplay

Disperse or throw it away

A creature touching a charged object can use an action to make an Intelligence (Arcana) or CE ability check against your CE save DC. On success, its Charge dissipates; on failure it remains. A carried object can be dropped normally. Technique Extermination and similar effects suppress or remove Charge as appropriate.

Level 6 · melee CE attack

Saboteur’s Touch

Touch an inorganic object worn or carried by an unwilling creature and make a melee CE attack. On a hit, spend up to PB CE and give the object equal Charge without exceeding its normal capacity. It cannot detonate before the start of your next turn unless the carrier deliberately creates its Impact trigger.

The carrier therefore has a turn to drop, remove, suppress, or disperse the object. Armor may be difficult to remove, but this does not waive the action and check options above.

Level 6 · charged object required

Static Mind

While carrying at least one charged object, you have advantage on saving throws against being charmed, possessed, or subjected to telepathic reading or intrusion. When you fail one of those saves, use your reaction and expend 1 Charge from an object you hold to reroll it, using the new result.

Level 11

Remote Charge

You may perform a charge step on an inorganic object you perceive within 60 feet without touching it. Use the object’s normal action and capacity. A worn or carried object still requires the Saboteur’s Touch attack, now made as a ranged CE attack; on a hit, it follows the same one-turn arming delay. Range becomes 300 feet at level 18.

Level 11

Advanced Agility

Kinetic Footwork’s speed increase becomes 20 feet and its post-release movement becomes 20 feet. When subjected to an effect that allows a Dexterity save for half damage, take no damage on success and half on failure. During your turn, you may move across vertical surfaces and liquids without falling during the movement; you fall or sink if unsupported when the turn ends.

Level 11

Perfect Conversion

Your Kinetic Die becomes d8 and Charge lasts 10 minutes. Kinetic Imbuement force damage ignores resistance; immunity becomes resistance instead. Set Piece and Landmark charge steps may deposit up to 2 × PB Charge if you pay that much CE.

Level 18 · no action once per turn

Ambient Mastery

Choose up to PB unattended Token or Handheld objects you perceive within 300 feet. Divide up to PB Charge among them, minimum 1 each, and spend matching CE. This obeys individual capacities and does not consume their normal charge-step action. Remote Charge range becomes 300 feet; Set Piece and Landmark steps may deposit 3 × PB.

Level 18

Perfect Trigger

During your turn, command any number of your charged objects without an action. As a reaction to one perceived condition, command any number assigned to that same condition. Resolve simultaneous choices in the order you choose. Charge lasts 1 hour.

Level 18

Kinetic Flight

You gain a 90-foot flying speed and can hover by continuously redirecting ambient motion through your body. Kinetic Footwork’s post-release movement may use any speed you possess. Your ordinary Kinetic Imbuement force damage ignores both resistance and immunity.

Level 11 · named Maximum

Maximum: Fifty-Two Pickup.

A full deck becomes a rotating execution field. The visual can use cards, coins, glass, nails, or another collection of Tokens; the user supplies the objects.

Action · 10 CE · 300 feet

The deck falls all at once

Choose a point you perceive within 300 feet. Charged Tokens fill a 60-foot-radius, 120-foot-high cylinder centered there. Choose any number of creatures in the area. Each chosen creature makes a Dexterity save, taking (12 + PB) Kinetic Dice + CE modifier force damage on failure or half on success. A failed creature is also launched up to 30 feet in a direction you choose and falls prone. On success it is not moved.

Unattended objects, structures, and barriers take maximum damage. The Maximum is instantaneous and consumes fifty-two suitable Tokens if that many are available; if fewer are carried, CE forms visible kinetic afterimages that complete the pattern without creating recoverable matter.

Named Maximum: Fifty-Two Pickup cannot critically hit, trigger Black Flash, or receive generic Maximum Output or Chanting. It is one activation and one damage instance, not fifty-two separate attacks or detonations.

Level 18 · Domain Expansion

House of the Loaded World.

An endless thieves’ casino unfolds across a suspended city. Every card, chandelier, chip, weapon, window, and piece of masonry is already part of the hand.

Expansion

The house closes

As a full-turn action, spend 20 CE to form an enclosed 60-foot-radius Domain centered on you for 1 minute. Choose creatures to bring inside using the campaign’s complete Domain rules. Barrier defenses, clashes, refinement, anti-domain techniques, and Technique Burnout function normally.

Loaded environment

Everything inorganic is in reach

Every inorganic object and terrain section inside is a valid Remote Charge target, even through cover. Once on each of your turns, allocate up to twice PB Charge among objects inside without an action or CE. Individual capacity remains, but the Domain supplies this Charge until it ends.

Sole sure-hit event

The House—not every card—collects

House Collection at initiative count 20 is the Domain’s only guaranteed hostile effect. Loaded Toss, charged weapons, Conduit attacks, Cardsharp Barrage, detonations, Vector Discharge, and every separately activated object use their normal attacks and saves.

Trigger freedom

The dealer resolves the order

You may command any charged objects in the Domain without an action or range limit and choose the order of every Chain Reaction. Those detonations remain ordinary technique activations rather than becoming part of the House’s guarantee.

Initiative 20 · House Collection · sure-hit

The environment cashes every debt

Each chosen hostile creature automatically takes 2 × PB Kinetic Dice + CE modifier force damage and receives one House Debt of your choice until the next initiative count 20:

Loaded Hand. The first time the creature attacks with or activates an inorganic object, it takes PB Kinetic Dice force damage after that activity resolves and drops one nonmagical object it is holding, chosen by you. The Debt then ends.

Loaded Floor. The first time the creature voluntarily moves at least 10 feet, it takes PB Kinetic Dice force damage after that movement, is launched up to 30 feet along a path you choose, and falls prone. The Debt then ends.

A creature protected from the sure-hit makes a Dexterity save. On a failure it suffers the complete result; on a success it takes half initial damage and receives no House Debt. The event costs no CE and consumes no stored Charge.

One collection, many real explosions: House Collection affects each chosen hostile once per initiative count 20. Separately paid or Domain-loaded objects may still be detonated and chained normally, using their ordinary rolls; those remain distinct activations.

Optional level-18 module

Unshackled Potential: New Sun.

This module is a standard selectable Calamity option when the campaign includes it. It is separated because ordinary Gambit does not possess these powers continuously; adding it changes the technique’s descriptor from Tier IV to Tier V.

Tier V · Calamity · no ability-score prerequisite

Remove the safety imposed on the technique

A level-18 Kinetic Imbuement user may take New Sun as their final breakthrough. It does not replace Ambient Mastery or the Domain. The full-potential state expands the core law from touched inorganic objects to ambient matter, living bodies, self-propulsion, and catastrophic regional conversion.

Bonus action · 15 CE · Technique Focus

New Sun State

For up to 1 minute, your Kinetic Die becomes d12, flying speed becomes 120 feet, Remote Charge range becomes 600 feet, and you may charge organic or inorganic matter. Token, Handheld, and Set Piece capacities double. All New Sun damage ignores resistance and immunity.

The state has no per-rest restriction. If you possess the CE and can maintain Technique Focus, you may invoke it again.

New Sun State · replaces one attack · X CE

Living Charge

While New Sun State is active, choose a creature within 600 feet, spend any positive X CE, and make a ranged CE attack. On a hit, immediately convert and release its organic potential energy for 2X d12 + CE modifier force damage. This is one direct technique attack, may critically hit, and may become a Black Flash. A surviving target makes a Constitution save or is stunned until the end of its next turn.

Alternatively, leave X Charge inside the target for up to 1 minute and assign a normal trigger. A delayed release is a Constitution saving-throw effect for full or half damage and cannot critically hit.

New Sun State · full-turn action · all current CE, minimum 20

New Sun Release

While New Sun State is active, choose a point you perceive within 1 mile and spend all current CE, with a minimum expenditure of 20 CE. The radius is 50 feet per CE spent. Choose any creatures in that sphere. Each makes a Constitution save, taking (3 × CE spent)d12 + CE modifier force damage on failure or half on success. Inorganic objects, structures, and terrain take maximum damage; living creatures reduced to 0 HP are disintegrated.

After resolution, New Sun State ends and the Unshackled Potential module burns out completely. Base Kinetic Imbuement remains, but New Sun cannot be used again until a major story breakthrough restores it. This is not a short- or long-rest recharge and cannot be repaired by ordinary RCT.

Why this changes the tier: organic charging, uncapped d12 direct attacks, a mile-scale release, and the ability to threaten every kind of matter invalidate ordinary encounter assumptions. The module is playable precisely because those consequences are written, not because the potential is denied.

Rules reference

A clean ledger for every loaded object.

Use these rulings for attack delivery, overlapping explosions, critical hits, Maximum Output, equipment, organic targets, and multiclass action stacking.

Technique math

Normal CE statistics

CE attack = PB + CE modifier
CE save DC = 8 + PB + CE modifier

A normal weapon attack keeps its own attack statistic. Loaded Toss uses the CE attack unless you choose the object’s normal weapon attack.

Kinetic Dice

Level, not Charge, changes size

The Kinetic Die is d6 at levels 1–10, d8 at 11–17, and d10 at 18–20. New Sun temporarily replaces it with d12. Charge determines how many dice are rolled.

Organic boundary

Equipment is not the wearer

Base Kinetic Imbuement cannot charge flesh, plants, blood, bone, a living creature, or an organic corpse. It may charge inorganic armor, weapons, prostheses, ammunition, and carried objects using Saboteur’s Touch. New Sun expressly removes this boundary.

Criticals and Black Flash

Only the direct target receives the miracle

Loaded Toss, charged weapon hits, Conduit hits, and Cardsharp Barrage attacks may critically hit. Their released kinetic dice are declared after the hit and before damage, so they double with the attack. After a qualifying critical, roll the second unmodified d20; a natural 20 makes Black Flash and triples the entire completed critical damage. There is no frequency cap.

Secondary blast

Copy the ordinary pool once

Roll the detonation’s ordinary 2C Kinetic Dice once for creatures other than the direct target. A critical or Black Flash changes only the complete direct attack. Secondary creatures never receive weapon dice, Loaded Toss’s base die, CE modifier, critical doubling, Black Flash multiplication, or target-only riders.

Maximum Output: prepared charge

Tag the CE when it is deposited

Declare Maximum Output before one charge step and double the CE deposited as its cost; stored Charge remains the original amount. Damage adds one Kinetic Die per original Charge when that object next detonates. Range increases its maximum blast radius by half. Duration makes a 1-minute charge last 15 rounds, a 10-minute charge 15 minutes, or a 1-hour charge 90 minutes. Accuracy applies to the next attack delivering that object.

Maximum Output: barrage

One declaration, all attacks

For Cardsharp Barrage, double its total N × X CE cost. Accuracy enhances every attack. Damage adds X Kinetic Dice to each successful direct detonation, because each Token’s original charge damage is 2X dice. Range increases both Loaded Toss ranges by half. Maximum Output cannot enhance Fifty-Two Pickup, the Domain, New Sun State, or New Sun Release.

Overlaps

Each paid object resolves

A creature can be damaged once by each separate detonation. One object never damages the same creature twice, even if the creature occupies several squares. Chain Reaction defines the resolution order.

Partial release

Only the Conduit keeps the rest

An ordinary object releases all stored Charge and is destroyed. A Bound Conduit may release any positive amount after a hit and retain the rest. New Sun Living Charge follows its own immediate or delayed procedure.

Conditions

Charge is not a creature condition

Condition immunity does not remove Charge from worn equipment. Dropping, removing, dispersing, suppressing, destroying, or leaving the object behind does. A delayed organic New Sun charge is part of the technique and ends if the technique is removed.

Focus

Only active loading needs concentration

Ordinary Charge requires no Technique Focus after deposit. A Landmark uses Focus only across consecutive full-turn deposits; once the user stops adding Charge, it persists for its normal duration without Focus. New Sun State uses Focus.

No Reversal

Stasis is not granted for symmetry

Kinetic Imbuement has no Cursed Technique Reversal. RCT does not automatically create motion cancellation, time freezing, or an absorption technique. New Sun is an expanded forward expression, not a Reversal.

Actions and multiclassing

Specific sources coexist

Charge steps use their printed action. Cardsharp Barrage is an action, not the Attack action. Loaded Toss replaces one attack only within the Attack action. Distinct additional actions, attacks, reactions, and damage riders from other features can stack unless one expressly forbids it.

Ammunition and conduit

Choose where Charge resides

A ranged Conduit may deliver its stored Charge through ammunition on a hit; the weapon retains unreleased Charge. Separately charged ammunition carries its own Charge and is destroyed. If both are released on one hit, they are distinct paid activations and both join that attack.

Counterplay

The threat is visible and movable

  • CE perception identifies a charged object, its approximate Charge, and whether it has an impact, timer, or command trigger after an action of inspection.
  • Objects can be dropped, thrown away, moved behind cover, destroyed, suppressed, or dispersed with the defined action and check.
  • Breaking the user’s line of effect prevents Remote Charge but does not erase existing Charge.
  • A Landmark charge requires the user to remain touching it through conspicuous full-turn actions.
  • Forced movement can separate the user from an intended object; disarming can remove the Bound Conduit without erasing its stored danger.
  • A blast uses ordinary line of effect from its center. Total cover blocks it unless the object or effect expressly destroys that cover first.
Four Innate Technique feats

Choose what stays loaded.

These optional feats specialize preparation, conduit fighting, interception, or full-hand barrages. Each occupies a normal feat choice, may be taken only once, and this is the complete Kinetic Imbuement feat list.

Prerequisite: Charge Object

Ace in the Hole

During a short or long rest, you may charge up to PB Token or Handheld objects you touch. Pay every point of CE normally; those specific charges last 24 hours instead of their ordinary duration. Preparing another object beyond this limit causes one prepared charge of your choice to dissipate. A release, dispel, or destroyed object still ends its charge normally.

Prerequisite: Bound Conduit

Twin Conduits

You may maintain two Bound Conduits at once. Once on each of your turns while touching both, transfer up to PB Charge between them without an action; neither may exceed its capacity, and transferred Charge keeps the earlier of its two remaining durations. Bonding a third conduit ends one bond of your choice.

Prerequisite: Loaded Toss, level 6

Kinetic Catch

When you see a physical ranged attack made against a creature within 30 feet, after seeing its roll but before resolution, use your reaction and spend 2 CE. Make a CE attack roll. If your total equals or exceeds the triggering attack roll, that attack misses and its projectile lands in an empty hand or at your feet with 1 Charge. This cannot catch an area, energy beam, siege projectile larger than you, artifact, or attack without a physical projectile.

Prerequisite: Cardsharp Barrage, level 9

Royal Flush

After one Cardsharp Barrage resolves, if at least four of its separate attacks hit the same creature, that creature makes a Constitution save. On failure it is stunned until the start of your next turn as overlapping kinetic vectors arrest its body. On success it cannot take reactions until the start of your next turn. The condition is one result of the barrage, not an additional hit or detonation.

Character guide

Carry a hand. Steal the table.

Kinetic Imbuement works as a weapon technique, ranged technique, infiltrator, controller, or demolition system without demanding one optimal build.

Token fighter

Cards are replaceable geometry

Tokens make charge and delivery fast. Carry several kinds: cards hide easily, coins ricochet, nails plant into walls, and knives remain weapons before they explode.

Conduit fighter

Every weapon strike can be loaded

A staff is iconic, not mandatory. A sword, firearm, bow, spear, chain, shield, gauntlet, or improvised implement can store Charge and deliver it through successful attacks.

Saboteur

Prepare exits and objectives

Fine Charge solves quiet mechanisms; timed charges erase pursuit routes; command charges transform cover into a hostage; Vector Discharge moves allies without damaging them.

Ability priorities

CE controls the loaded field

Wisdom or Charisma governs Loaded Toss, sabotage attacks, saves, and Fine Charge. Dexterity supports stealth and ordinary weapons; Constitution protects Landmark Focus. None must begin at its maximum.

CE economy

Every mine is prepaid

A 6-Charge object already removed 6 CE from the user. Layering a room is powerful because it spends real reserve and preparation time, not because the guide pretends several explosions are one.

Party play

Tell allies what is armed

The user is immune to personal blasts; allies are not. Declare blast radii, place focused charges near the party, and use Vector Discharge when damage would ruin the plan.

Complete combat loop: load Tokens or Conduit → deliver a direct strike → select radius → move through the release → plant delayed objects → trigger the chain when the battlefield finally contains the right geometry.
