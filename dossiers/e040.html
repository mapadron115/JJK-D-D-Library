Core law

CE becomes stored motion.

Charge is not a second character resource. It is CE already paid and physically deposited in a specific object. Write the amount beside that object; when the object releases, the Charge is spent.

Cards are ammunition, not a restriction.

Direct delivery

Core damage example:

Field notes. Subject: the sorcerer who taught his cursed energy to wait. Three truths govern him. First, Charge is not a resource, it is cursed energy already paid and physically parked in dead matter: write the amount beside the object, because when the object releases, the debt is collected. Second, this is not an explosion technique so much as delayed violence: triggers of impact, timer, or command let him decide when the past detonates, and everything he survives gets paid forward. Third, the deck is any room he has touched, cards, conduit weapons, charged equipment on hostile bodies, and at the last, the House itself, where every inorganic thing is in reach. Mark the gambler’s patience: he never throws a punch when he can plant a mine.

There is no upfront damage, the House is patient, and the debtors detonate themselves. A creature protected from the sure-hit makes a Dexterity save, receiving no House Debt on a success. The event costs no CE and consumes no stored Charge.

House Collection affects each chosen hostile once per initiative count 20. Separately paid or Domain-loaded objects may still be detonated and chained normally, using their ordinary rolls; those remain distinct activations.

Rules integration

A clean ledger for every loaded object.

Use these rulings for attack delivery, overlapping explosions, critical hits, Maximum Output, equipment, organic targets, and multiclass action stacking.

Technique math Normal CE statistics

CE attack = PB + CE modifier
CE save DC = 8 + PB + CE modifier

A normal weapon attack keeps its own attack statistic. Loaded Toss uses the CE attack unless you choose the object’s normal weapon attack.

Kinetic Dice Level, not Charge, changes size

New Sun temporarily replaces the Kinetic Die with d12. Charge determines how many dice are rolled.

Organic boundary Equipment is not the wearer

Base Kinetic Imbuement cannot charge flesh, plants, blood, bone, a living creature, or an organic corpse. It may charge inorganic armor, weapons, prostheses, ammunition, and carried objects using Saboteur’s Touch. New Sun expressly removes this boundary.

Criticals and Black Flash Only the direct target receives the miracle

Loaded Toss, charged weapon hits, Conduit hits, and Cardsharp Barrage attacks may critically hit. Their released kinetic dice are declared after the hit and before damage, so they double with the attack. After a qualifying critical, roll the second unmodified d20; a natural 20 makes Black Flash and triples the entire completed critical damage. There is no frequency cap.

Secondary blast Copy the ordinary pool once

Roll the detonation’s ordinary 2C Kinetic Dice once for creatures other than the direct target. A critical or Black Flash changes only the complete direct attack. Secondary creatures never receive weapon dice, Loaded Toss’s base die, CE modifier, critical doubling, Black Flash multiplication, or target-only riders.

Maximum Output: prepared charge Tag the CE when it is deposited

Declare Maximum Output before one charge step and double the CE deposited as its cost; stored Charge remains the original amount. Damage adds one Kinetic Die per original Charge when that object next detonates. Range increases its maximum blast radius by half. Duration makes a 1-minute charge last 15 rounds, a 10-minute charge 15 minutes, or a 1-hour charge 90 minutes. Accuracy applies to the next attack delivering that object.

Maximum Output: barrage One declaration, all attacks

For Cardsharp Barrage, double its total N × X CE cost. Accuracy enhances every attack. Damage adds X Kinetic Dice to each successful direct detonation, because each Token’s original charge damage is 2X dice. Range increases both Loaded Toss ranges by half. Maximum Output cannot enhance Fifty-Two Pickup, the Domain, New Sun State, or New Sun Release.

Overlaps Each paid object resolves

A creature can be damaged once by each separate detonation. One object never damages the same creature twice, even if the creature occupies several squares. Chain Reaction defines the resolution order.

Partial release Only the Conduit keeps the rest

An ordinary object releases all stored Charge and is destroyed. A Bound Conduit may release any positive amount after a hit and retain the rest. New Sun Living Charge follows its own immediate or delayed procedure.

Conditions Charge is not a creature condition

Condition immunity does not remove Charge from worn equipment. Dropping, removing, dispersing, suppressing, destroying, or leaving the object behind does. A delayed organic New Sun charge is part of the technique and ends if the technique is removed.

Focus Only active loading needs concentration

Ordinary Charge requires no Technique Focus after deposit. A Landmark uses Focus only across consecutive full-turn deposits; once the user stops adding Charge, it persists for its normal duration without Focus. New Sun State uses Focus.

No Reversal Stasis is not granted for symmetry

Kinetic Imbuement has no Cursed Technique Reversal. RCT does not automatically create motion cancellation, time freezing, or an absorption technique. New Sun is an expanded forward expression, not a Reversal.

Actions and multiclassing Specific sources coexist

Charge steps use their printed action. Cardsharp Barrage is an action, not the Attack action. Loaded Toss replaces one attack only within the Attack action. Distinct additional actions, attacks, reactions, and damage riders from other features can stack unless one expressly forbids it.

Ammunition and conduit Choose where Charge resides

A ranged Conduit may deliver its stored Charge through ammunition on a hit; the weapon retains unreleased Charge. Separately charged ammunition carries its own Charge and is destroyed. If both are released on one hit, they are distinct paid activations and both join that attack.

Counterplay The threat is visible and movable
  • CE perception identifies a charged object, its approximate Charge, and whether it has an impact, timer, or command trigger after an action of inspection.
  • Objects can be dropped, thrown away, moved behind cover, destroyed, suppressed, or dispersed with the defined action and check.
  • Breaking the user’s line of effect prevents Remote Charge but does not erase existing Charge.
  • A Landmark charge requires the user to remain touching it through conspicuous full-turn actions.
  • Forced movement can separate the user from an intended object; disarming can remove the Bound Conduit without erasing its stored danger.
  • A blast uses ordinary line of effect from its center. Total cover blocks it unless the object or effect expressly destroys that cover first.

Carry a hand. Steal the table.

Kinetic Imbuement works as a weapon technique, ranged technique, infiltrator, controller, or demolition system without demanding one optimal build.

Token fighter

Cards are replaceable geometry

Tokens make charge and delivery fast. Carry several kinds: cards hide easily, coins ricochet, nails plant into walls, and knives remain weapons before they explode.

Conduit fighter

Every weapon strike can be loaded

A staff is iconic, not mandatory. A sword, firearm, bow, spear, chain, shield, gauntlet, or improvised implement can store Charge and deliver it through successful attacks.

Saboteur

Prepare exits and objectives

Fine Charge solves quiet mechanisms; timed charges erase pursuit routes; command charges transform cover into a hostage; Vector Discharge moves allies without damaging them.

Ability priorities

CE controls the loaded field

Wisdom or Charisma governs Loaded Toss, sabotage attacks, saves, and Fine Charge. Dexterity supports stealth and ordinary weapons; Constitution protects Landmark Focus. None must begin at its maximum.

CE economy

Every mine is prepaid

A 6-Charge object already removed 6 CE from the user. Layering a room is powerful because it spends real reserve and preparation time, not because the guide pretends several explosions are one.

Party play

Tell allies what is armed

The user is immune to personal blasts; allies are not. Declare blast radii, place focused charges near the party, and use Vector Discharge when damage would ruin the plan.
