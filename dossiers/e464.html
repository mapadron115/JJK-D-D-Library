Features & procedures

Properties & techniques

Exact player-facing procedures

Storm quarterstaff

Passive · No CE · Reach property; +3 to attack and damage; each hit deals an additional 1d12 lightning damage.

The staff's baseline manners need no charging, a quarterstaff with reach, +3 to hit and damage, and 1d12 lightning on every landed blow. This acts as a quarterstaff with the Reach property. You have a +3 bonus to attack and damage rolls, and on a hit you deal an additional 1d12 lightning damage.

Positive charge

Free (once per turn) · Spend 1 CE · Mythical Beast Amber wielder only: store one positive charge in the staff; charges persist until released.

But in Mythical Beast Amber hands it keeps accounts: once per turn, 1 CE as a free action buys a positive charge that persists until released. The staff becomes a battery with a memory. If wielded by a creature with Mythical Beast Amber, once per turn they can spend 1 cursed energy as a free action to give the staff a positive charge. Charges persist until released.

Released lightning bolt

Action · No CE · Staff within 30 ft: lightning bolt along the line between you and the staff — 1d12 lightning per charge, Dexterity save vs your CE DC (half on success); consumes all charges.

The withdrawal is an action, and it is all or nothing, the staff, within thirty feet, fires along the line between you and it: 1d12 lightning per charge, Dexterity against your CE DC, half on success, every charge spent. Plant it between yourself and the enemy; the sky aims itself. As an action, if the staff is within 30 ft of you, you can release a lightning bolt along the line between you and the staff. All creatures on that line must make a Dexterity saving throw against your Cursed Energy save DC, taking 1d12 lightning damage per charge on a failed save or half as much on a successful save. Releasing consumes all charges.

Field notes. Subject: a quarterstaff that saves its thunder for later. Three truths. First: the staff is a storm in its own right, reach, +3 to strike and wound, and 1d12 lightning riding every hit. Second: in the hands of a Mythical Beast Amber bearer it becomes a ledger, once per turn, 1 CE as a free action buys one positive charge, and charges persist until spent. The thunder does not spoil. Third: the release is positional, as an action, with the staff within thirty feet, it hurls a bolt along the line between you and it, 1d12 per charge, Dexterity against your CE save, consuming all charges at once. Consider the geometry: the wise bearer plants the staff between himself and his enemies, and lets the sky do the aiming. It is patience weaponized, every charge a promise the storm will keep.
