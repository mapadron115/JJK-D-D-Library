The truth. The tool does not make the wearer unlucky. It makes everyone who loves them unlucky.

Rules integration

Cursed-tool attunement

Campaign canon

Cursed-tool attunement: you have PB attunement slots (2–6). This tool occupies one slot and requires a one-hour ritual. Attunement ends voluntarily over a short rest, on death, or when you replace it while over capacity. Distance does not break attunement. Cursed-tool attunement is separate from magic-item attunement.
Balance & counterplay. It never harms the wearer directly — the price falls on their attachments. Isolate the wearer from bonded allies to starve the transfer, and remember every invoked benefit deepens the eventual misfortune.
