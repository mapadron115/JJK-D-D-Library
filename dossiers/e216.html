Feature

Mask shatters for 24 hours if a creature beats the disguise check by 5+ and publicly exposes you.

The fiction. Registry scholars classify Borrowed Face as the curse of someone who survived by being no one: the porcelain mask holds the cursed energy of a sorcerer whose own face was their weakness, and whose CE learned to wear other people's instead. It reproduces face, voice, and gait but never memory or technique, because it was never built to understand anyone — only to hide the one wearing it. The prevailing theory holds that the mask's crack under scrutiny is the original sorcerer's old wound showing: the disguise fails the moment someone looks at it the way they were once looked at.

Rules integration

Cursed-tool attunement

Campaign canon

Cursed-tool attunement: you have PB attunement slots (2–6). This tool occupies one slot and requires a one-hour ritual. Attunement ends voluntarily over a short rest, on death, or when you replace it while over capacity. Distance does not break attunement. Cursed-tool attunement is separate from magic-item attunement.
Balance & counterplay. Strong infiltration with concrete identity-check counterplay.
