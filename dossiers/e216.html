Borrowed Face

After 10 minutes observing a humanoid, store one Impression, max PB. Wearing the mask can reproduce face and voice but not memories, CE signature, technique, or statistics.

FormPorcelain mask The curse of someone who survived by being no one, it hides the wearer; it understands no one.
RoleInfiltration / social utility Face, voice, and gait reproduced, but never memory, never technique.
EngineImpression Faces and voices observed and stored, reproduced without their souls.

Feature

Passive · If beaten by 5+ and publicly exposed, the mask shatters for 24 hours.

The price of exposure: beaten by five and publicly unmasked, the mask shatters for twenty-four hours. The disguise fails the moment someone looks at it the way its maker was once looked at. Mask shatters for 24 hours if a creature beats the disguise check by 5+ and publicly exposes you.

Field notes. Subject: a survivor's habit. Three truths govern it. First, the mask holds the cursed energy of a sorcerer whose own face was their weakness, it learned to wear other people's instead, reproducing face, voice, and gait but never memory or technique. Second, the counterplay is human: strangers are fooled, but the familiar may see through after real interaction, the crack in the glaze is the old wound showing, the disguise failing the moment someone looks the way they were once looked at. Third, exposure is catastrophic: publicly unmasked, it shatters for a day. Assess it correctly: not a thief's tool but a refugee's, built by someone who survived by being no one, for someone who may need to do the same.

Rules integration

Cursed-tool attunement

Campaign canon

Cursed-tool attunement: you have PB attunement slots (2–6). This tool occupies one slot and requires a one-hour ritual. Attunement ends voluntarily over a short rest, on death, or when you replace it while over capacity. Distance does not break attunement. Cursed-tool attunement is separate from magic-item attunement.
Balance & counterplay. Strong infiltration with concrete identity-check counterplay.
