Earth Manipulation

A simple yet powerful technique to mold rock as you please.

Power tierTier III · Apex
Combat roleController
Core engineStone cubes, armor, tremors, walls, grasping arms
DomainTrichiliocosm

Field notes. Subject: the mason of battlefields. Three truths govern him. First, the material answers: Excavation, an action, one CE, shapes five-foot cubes of stone within thirty feet. The sorcerer once needed the earth to hold him up; cursed energy that has been held up never forgets. Second, the craft scales: Sculpted Earth at the third, Rock Blast for d12s at thirty feet, Earthen Armor for AC, Tremor for 3d10 in a twenty-foot radius; Wall of Stone at the sixth, four CE, ten ten-foot panels, an hour; Stone Arms at the eleventh, five CE, great hands that catch a target at sixty feet. Third, the cathedral: the Domain, Trichiliocosm, at initiative 20, all creatures of his choice take 6d12 and are restrained by shifting stone, and an action reshapes the environment itself: a chasm, a hundred-foot tower, a hundred feet of piercing difficult terrain. Maxim: terrain is not where the battle happens. Terrain is the first soldier.

Full progression

Feature map

1Excavation.

The foundation: an action, one CE, five-foot cubes of stone within thirty feet, and multiples if he likes. The mason’s first lesson is that the battlefield arrives unfinished. Starting at 1st level, your technique allows you to mold stone using your cursed energy. As an action for 1 cursed energy, you can shape the stone around yourself to make a 5-foot cubes of stone within 30 feet. You can choose to create multiple stone cubes up to your CE modifier (the cubes must be connected) as part of the same action, spending 1 CE for each cube. Each cube has an AC equal to your cursed energy DC and hit points equal to 5 times your CE modifier.

You can move a creature using these cubes. When creating the cubes in a creature's area, you can attempt to move the target to a space within 5 feet. The creature must make a Dexterity saving throw, being pushed on a failure. If you create multiple stone cubes as part of the same action, a creature that fails the save is pushed to the end of the connected cubes.

3Sculpted Earth.

Rock Blast.

Sculpted Earth, first tool: one CE, a rock imbued and launched at thirty feet, d12s of bludgeoning, necrotic for one more. The mason’s thrown chisel. As an action, you can spend 1 CE to imbue your cursed energy into a rock and launch it at a target within 30 feet. You can use your CE modifier or Strength modifier for the attack and damage roll. On a hit, you deal 1d12 bludgeoning damage (you can spend 1 additional CE to instead deal necrotic damage). If you have the extra attack feature, you can replace one of your attacks this way.

Earthen Armor.

Second tool: a bonus action, CE up to his proficiency bonus, earth worn, AC equal to the coin spent, one minute. The mason dresses for the quarry. As a bonus action, you can spend CE up to your proficiency bonus to protect yourself with earth. For 1 minute, you gain a bonus to your AC equal to the cursed energy spent.

Tremor.

Third tool: three CE, an action, a twenty-foot radius, Dexterity or 3d10 and prone, climbing to 6d10 by the eighteenth; structures fail outright and take maximum. The mason’s hammer, sized for the whole floor. As an action, you can spend 3 cursed energy to strike the ground. Creatures in a 20-foot radius around yourself must make a Dexterity saving throw. On a failure, they take 3d10 bludgeoning damage and are knocked prone. On a success, they take half as much damage and are not prone. Structures automatically fail and take maximum damage. This increases to 4d10 at 6th level, 5d10 at 11th level, and 6d10 at 18th level.

6Wall of Stone.

At the sixth, Wall of Stone: four CE, an action, ten ten-foot panels within sixty feet, grounded, an hour. Architecture, at combat speed. At 6th level, you have learned to create large scale constructs. As an action for 4 cursed energy, you make a wall of stone. The wall must be placed within 60 ft and connected to the ground, and it lasts 1 hour. This wall is made of ten 10 foot by 10 foot panels that are 1 inch thick. Each panel must be touching at least one other panel. The wall has an AC equal to your cursed energy DC and a number of hit points equal to 10 times your CE modifier.

You can spend 1 CE as a bonus action to move connected panels within 60 feet of you up to 30 feet in a direction of your choice that is in contact with the ground. If this movement would move a creature, they must make a Dexterity saving throw or be pushed by the wall.

11Stone Arms.

At the eleventh, Stone Arms: five CE, an action, two great arms that catch a target within sixty feet on a failed Dexterity save. The quarry grows hands. At 11th level, you have learned a new way to use the stone you manipulate. You are now able to create two big arms that can catch a target. As an action for 5 cursed energy, you can force a target within 60 ft to make a Dexterity saving throw. On a failure, they are wedged between the arms taking 6d12 bludgeoning damage, and are restrained, becoming stuck between the hands until the start of your next turn. On a success, they are not restrained and take half damage.

You can also choose to keep the hands active by spending 1 CE at the start of your turns. Every turn you have it active you can use an action to crush a creature restrained by your hands, forcing them to make a Strength saving throw. If they fail they take another 4d12 bludgeoning damage or half as much on a success. A creature can use their action to make an Athletics or Acrobatics check against your Cursed Energy DC, escaping on a success. While maintaining your stone arms, they have AC 18 and HP equal to your maximum hit points and you must maintain concentration.

18Domain Expansion: Trichiliocosm.

Stone rises in terraces and stupas, a thousand small worlds stacked within the one, each turning slowly on its own axis. Dust hangs in the air like incense, and the ground rearranges itself with the deliberation of a monk raking sand, patient and inexorable. It is a cosmology you can stand inside, and it does not notice you.

The Domain, first telling: Trichiliocosm. At initiative 20, all creatures of his choice take 6d12 bludgeoning and are restrained by shifting stone until their next turn’s start. At 18th level you have learned to expand your domain. At initiative 20, all creatures of your choice within your domain take 6d12 bludgeoning damage and are restrained by shifting stone until the start of their next turn.

You can spend an action to change the environment of your domain.

  • You can create a large 20 ft radius, 1d10 x 10 feet deep chasm causing creatures to fall and take falling damage. Creatures in the chasm area make a Dexterity saving throw to reach an adjacent safe edge where one is available; on a failure they fall and take falling damage.
  • You can create a 100 ft tall tower of stone (as if casting Galdur's Tower with 10 stories).
  • You can create 100 ft of difficult terrain in an area you can see, as a 100 × 5-ft path within the Domain. A creature that moves through this area takes 2d4 piercing damage for every 5 feet moved.
Pinnacle expression

Maximum, Domain, or equivalent.

Domain Expansion: Trichiliocosm: The pinnacle, second telling: Trichiliocosm, 18, at initiative 20, 6d12 and restraint, and an action reshapes the Domain’s environment: a chasm d10s of tens of feet deep, a hundred-foot tower, a hundred feet of piercing difficult terrain. The cathedral, remodeled mid-service. 18: at initiative 20, all creatures of your choice within your domain take 6d12 bludgeoning damage and are restrained by shifting stone until the start of their next turn. You can spend an action to change the environment of your domain: a large 20 ft radius, 1d10 x 10 feet deep chasm causing creatures to fall and take falling damage; a 100 ft tall tower of stone (as if casting Galdur's Tower with 10 stories); 100 ft of difficult terrain in an area you can see, dealing 2d4 piercing damage for every 5 feet a creature moves through it.

Innate Technique Feats

Build-defining options.

These are optional character-building feats. You must meet each listed prerequisite before selecting it.

Prerequisites: Must have Excavation

Density Manipulation

One CE, a bonus action, a touched construct: light as pumice, carried, moved, or dense as lead. The mason adjusts the stone’s honesty. You have learned to change the density of the stone you shape. As a bonus action for 1 CE, you can touch one of your stone constructs and either make it light as pumice, it can be carried or moved freely and no longer blocks movement, or hyper-dense: a creature underneath or adjacent to it must succeed on a Strength saving throw or be pinned beneath it, restrained until it uses its action to make a Strength (Athletics) check against your CE save DC to shove free.

Prerequisites: Must have Sculpted Earth

Rock Burst

A reaction: one construct within sixty feet explodes, five feet, Dexterity or d12s of bludgeoning. What was built can be unbuilt, violently. As a reaction, you can cause one of your stone constructs within 60 feet of you to explode. Any creature within 5 feet must make a Dexterity saving throw. On a failed save, they take 1d12 bludgeoning damage and are knocked prone. You can spend CE up to half your proficiency bonus (rounded down) as part of this reaction to heighten the explosion and deal an additional 1d12 bludgeoning damage per CE and cause creatures in range to take half as much damage on a success.

Prerequisite: Must have Stone Arms

Arms of the Giant

The Stone Arms learn new grips: Interposing Fist, the arms close around him as a shield, and more. The quarry’s hands learn gentleness, selectively. You have taught your Stone Arms new grips. While maintaining your Stone Arms, you can use your action for one of the following instead of crushing: Interposing Fist, the arms close around you, granting you three-quarters cover until the start of your next turn. Sundering Grip, crush a restrained creature as normal, and the shock of the impact gives it disadvantage on its next attack roll. Hurl, throw a restrained creature up to 30 ft to an unoccupied space you can see; if it strikes a solid surface, it takes 3d6 bludgeoning damage.

Rules integration

Edge cases and shared systems.

Excavation material: cubes need available stone or earth. Creating cubes in an occupied space allows a Dexterity save to avoid displacement, on failure the creature is pushed to the end of the connected cubes, never crushed inside an object.
Rock Blast: with Extra Attack it may replace one attack per Attack action (the source's "one" read explicitly).
Wall of Stone: the 10-times-CE-modifier hit points apply per panel. Proposed placement: within 60 ft, ground-connected, 1-hour duration; a bonus action and 1 CE moves connected panels 30 ft within 60 ft, a Dexterity save prevents forced pushing, and a blocked panel stops rather than phasing through creatures.
Stone Arms: the whole manifestation is one HP pool, not maximum HP per finger. Upkeep is declared before the old restraint ends; failing to pay or breaking Focus releases the victim immediately. Escape is available every turn. Proposed: 1-minute maximum hold unless a specific extension maintains it.
Domain restraint: the initiative-20 restraint ends at the start of the target's turn as printed, it does not steal their turn, though it constrains reactions between. Chasm victims get a Dexterity save to reach an adjacent safe edge where available; the spike field is a 100 x 5-ft path within the Domain.
Density Manipulation: the 10-times replacement supersedes the 5-times value, do not add both. The +10 hit points applies once per actual object, not per new panel segment.
Rock Burst: the chosen construct is consumed (explicit destroy clause). Trigger: when the construct is attacked or a creature enters adjacent, one event. Unaugmented success deals zero; augmented success deals half.
Arms of the Giant: removes Focus but still pays 1 CE upkeep and uses the Arms' action budget; Bigby's Hand options grant no additional independent hand turn. The slow from restraint continues after escape until 1 minor actual healing; while restrained speed is already 0, not negative.

Prerequisite: Must have Stone Arms
