Limitless

Infinity, Blue, Red, Hollow Purple, and Unlimited Void—the campaign’s clearest calamity-scale spatial package.

Field notes. Subject: control rather than power, a sorcerer who declares where the world is permitted to end. Three truths govern the surveyor of a private geometry. First, Infinity is arithmetic, not armor: a bonus action and 1 CE encase him, 1 CE upkeep each turn, and the Infinity d6 decides every attack, on 4+, it never arrived. Mark the posted limits: poison already inside, suffocation, starvation, older conditions, soulless effects, what already touches him. Second, two moods, one truth inverted: Lapse Blue draws the world toward an impossible center, (X+2)d8 force; Reversal Red rescinds the invitation, (X+4)d8, pushed 20 × X feet. Their combination is the registry’s cautionary example: Hollow Purple. Third, the declaration turned outward: Unlimited Void floods rather than refuses, an Intelligence save or 12d8 psychic, mental scores to 1, a full minute of unconsciousness. His certainty, given directly to everyone inside.

Pinnacle expression

Maximum, Domain, or equivalent.

Hollow Purple: 11: Maximum: Hollow Purple. the first action charges; the second action must occur by the end of your next turn, costs 10 CE, and releases a 100-foot-long, 10-foot-wide line. Unlimited Void:

The barrier opens and the concept of distance politely excuses itself. Stars are born and die in the space between heartbeats, information floods every sense at once, the color of infinity, the sound of everything happening simultaneously, the texture of knowing too much. It is the universe without the courtesy of a filter, and the mind was not built to drink from this river.

The declaration turned outward: no longer refusing information but flooding with it. A creature whose turn begins touching the sorcerer is spared that turn; all others make the Intelligence save, failure is 12d8 psychic, mental scores to 1, incapacitation and unconsciousness for a full minute. The sorcerer’s certainty, given directly to everyone inside. 18: Unlimited Void. a creature touching you when its turn begins is immune for that turn. Otherwise it makes the printed Intelligence save. Failure applies 12d8 psychic, sets mental scores to 1, incapacitates and renders unconscious for 1 minute. This is an explicit exception to repeat-each-turn control: it repeats the save at the end of each full minute, and Greater Restoration or Wish ends it.

Rules integration

Edge cases and shared systems.

Rules priority: the reviewed rulings, explicit codex clarifications, and the level progression always control when original wording conflicts. Legacy Domain auto-hit language, action terminology, event timing, missing saves, and older scaling never override revised player rules.
Why Tier V: ordinary Infinity changes what can threaten the user; the full kit supplies mass displacement, enormous erasure damage, and a lore-faithful neurological shutdown. Limitless remains selectable without Six Eyes; any separate Six Eyes option modifies it only as that option states.
