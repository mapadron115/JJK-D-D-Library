At-the-table character reference

Maximum Technique: Hollow Purple · level 11: use one action to charge; on a following turn, use an action and spend 10 CE to release a 100-foot-long, 10-foot-wide line. Each creature makes a Dexterity save: 20d12 force damage, relocation to the end of the line, and one exhaustion level on failure; half damage with neither rider on success. Being incapacitated or initiating another Maximum ends the charge.

Completed rules

  • Infinity: activation is a bonus action for 1 CE; pay 1 CE at the start of each later turn or it ends. Before resolving each distinct external attack or harmful saving-throw effect against a protected creature, roll the Infinity d6. On a success, that creature is not hit or affected and makes no save. On failure, resolve normally. One area effect calls for one Infinity roll per protected creature, not per damage die.
  • Infinity cannot stop poison already inside the body, suffocation, starvation, a condition applied before activation, soul effects with no spatial path, or a creature/effect already touching you when it activates. Before level 6 it also cannot stop a source you cannot perceive. From level 6, unseen sources are eligible; poison remains ineligible.
  • At level 14, reaction activation occurs after you are targeted but before the attack or save. The 6-CE automatic mode makes every Infinity die succeed and costs 2 CE at the start of each turn. Dodge gives advantage on each Infinity die and extends protection to creatures within your reach; each ally rolls separately. Infinity is an explicit non-Focus sustained technique.
  • Blue: X is CE spent, capped at PB unless Maximum Output raises it under the core rules. Radius is 5 × X feet and damage is (X + 2)d8 force. Failure pulls to nearest legal spaces adjacent to the 5-foot sphere; success takes half and is not pulled. Maximum Output lasts up to 1 minute with Technique Focus. Its center must stay within 30 feet of its original point; a creature saves at most once per turn from its movement.
  • Red: spend 2X CE, with X capped at PB. One creature within 60 feet makes a Strength save, taking (X + 4)d8 force and being pushed 20 × X feet on failure, or half with no push on success. A steel-strength impact converts each 10 feet of prevented movement to 1d6 collision damage, maximum 20d6. Maximum Output doubles push and knocks prone on failure.
  • Hollow Purple: the first action charges; the second action must occur by the end of your next turn, costs 10 CE, and releases a 100-foot-long, 10-foot-wide line. Losing consciousness or starting another Maximum ends the charge. A failed Dexterity save deals 20d12 force damage, moves the creature to the end of the line, and adds one exhaustion level; success deals half damage with neither rider.
  • Unlimited Void: a creature touching you when its turn begins is immune for that turn. Otherwise it makes the printed Intelligence save. Failure applies 12d8 psychic, sets mental scores to 1, incapacitates and renders unconscious for 1 minute. This is an explicit exception to repeat-each-turn control: it repeats the save at the end of each full minute, and Greater Restoration or Wish ends it.

Power identity

Why Tier V: ordinary Infinity changes what can threaten the user; the full kit supplies mass displacement, enormous erasure damage, and a lore-faithful neurological shutdown. Limitless remains selectable without Six Eyes; any separate Six Eyes option modifies it only as that option states.

Recovered character-build options

Innate Technique Feats 4

These are optional character-building feats. You must meet each listed prerequisite before selecting it.

Offensive Infinity

High impact

Prerequisite: Infinity

Action, spend 1–PB CE: choose a creature within 15 feet and within 10 feet of a solid surface. Constitution save; failure takes Xd6 force and is restrained against that surface until start of your next turn, success takes half and is not restrained. X equals CE spent.

Spatial Skirmishing

High impact

Prerequisite: Blue

Once per turn when making an unarmed strike, pay Blue’s normal cost and use CE ability for the attack; on hit apply Blue and gain advantage on that attack. After learning Red, choose Red instead: on hit the target makes the normal Strength save and is launched as Red states. One strike cannot carry both.

Teleportation

High impact

Prerequisite: Blue and CE score 20

Action, spend 1–PB CE: teleport yourself to an open space you can see, 10 feet per CE. You may carry worn/held equipment and one willing creature no larger than you if you spend 1 additional CE within the same PB cap.

Delayed Purple

Pinnacle

Prerequisite: Hollow Purple

While one of your Blues persists within 120 feet, activate Red into it as an action, or as a reaction when a creature enters or ends a turn in the Blue. Pay both Red and Hollow Purple costs. Every creature—including you—in a 100-foot radius makes a Dexterity save; failure takes the maximum value of Hollow Purple’s 20d12 base dice (240 force damage), success half. Riders and amplifier dice are rolled normally.
