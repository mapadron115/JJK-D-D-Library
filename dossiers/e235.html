Field notes. Subject: a charge that refuses to end. Four truths govern this guandao. First, motion is the fuel: move 15 ft before a hit and bank Momentum, to a maximum of your PB, held until combat ends, standing still is the only true disarm. Second, Momentum is spent, never saved: one or two Tool Dice added on a hit turn gathered ground into delivered steel. Third, the sweep and the ride: Crescent Sweep cuts up to PB foes in one reach, and a takedown opens a free 15-ft Ride Through past the fallen, the line never halts for the dead. Fourth, mass becomes authority: at 3+ Momentum, advantage against prone and forced movement, the general who keeps moving cannot be dragged down. Mark this: a weapon that has only ever known one direction.

Rules integration

Cursed-tool attunement

Campaign canon

Cursed-tool attunement: you have PB attunement slots (2–6). This tool occupies one slot and requires a one-hour ritual. Attunement ends voluntarily over a short rest, on death, or when you replace it while over capacity. Distance does not break attunement. Cursed-tool attunement is separate from magic-item attunement.
Balance & counterplay. Strong martial tool with no pseudo-technique package; ideal for Heavenly Restriction characters.
