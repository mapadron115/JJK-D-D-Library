Refining Clockworks

Feed gear into a portable cursed clockwork, choose how aggressively the machine interprets “refinement,” and decide whether one more Very Fine cycle is worth the Risk.

RoleArtificer / equipment support / gamblerSourceSCP Wiki

Core Engine — Portable Clockworks

  • Refinement Die: d6 → d8 → d10 → d12.
  • Portable Clockworks. Action, 2 CE: manifest adjacent Intake and Output frames for 10 minutes. Only one set may exist. At level 1 they accept Tiny or Small nonliving objects that are not being worn or carried by an unwilling creature.
  • Scrap. Rough and Coarse processes generate Scrap, a technique resource with a cap of PB + CE modifier. Unspent Scrap vanishes after a long rest.
  • Rough. Action: destroy a mundane input and gain Scrap by size: Tiny 1, Small 2, Medium 3, Large 4. Cursed or magical objects do not automatically yield Scrap and may resist destruction at the GM’s discretion.
  • Coarse. Action: disassemble an input into usable components and gain 1 less Scrap than Rough, minimum 1, or remove one mundane mechanical property such as armor plating, a lock, a blade, or a wheel while preserving the remainder.
  • 1:1. Action, 1 CE: turn a mundane input into another mundane object of similar size, material value, and complexity. It cannot create currency, ammunition in bulk, explosives, poisons, rare components, or supernatural items from nothing.

Fine / Anomalous Output Menus

Anomalous Property — Phase Edge

Weapon or tool: once per turn ignore half and three-quarters cover and treat resistance to its physical damage as normal damage for that hit.

Anomalous Property — Zero-Loss Cell

Item stores up to PB CE you willingly place into it. Stored CE may pay only for that item’s own technique features and vanishes when the output ends.

Anomalous Property — Spatial Extension

Double the item’s normal reach or range; once per turn it may affect around one ordinary corner or obstacle as though the origin were 5 feet to either side.

Extraordinary Output — Impossible Conductor

For 1 minute, one CE ability per turn channeled through the item gains +2 to its attack roll or +1 to its save DC, chosen when used.

Extraordinary Output — Perfect Tool

For 1 minute, the item grants advantage on every appropriate ability check and adds PB Refinement Dice to one such check per round.

Extraordinary Output — Impossible Battery

Stores CE up to twice PB and may release all of it at once to pay a single technique activation. Any unused charge disappears with the object.

Maximum Technique — The Interpretation Engine

Activation

Level 18, full-turn action, 10 CE, 3 Scrap. Process one valid nonliving item as though Very Fine rolled 10+, ignoring current Risk.

Duration

The chosen Extraordinary Output lasts 10 minutes instead of 1. It still disappears at the end and cannot become a permanent Special Grade item.

Backlash

After processing, Refinement Risk becomes 5 and you suffer Technique Burnout until the end of your next turn in addition to any normal cost.

Preparation ceiling

Special Grade cursed tools, artifacts, living creatures, and campaign-critical objects are never automatically destroyed, duplicated, or permanently upgraded. Those require explicit GM adjudication or a dedicated feat/ritual.

Table Rulings

  • 914-style “refinement” is not synonymous with improvement. Very Fine is deliberately unpredictable and increasingly dangerous when spammed.
  • 1:1 cannot bypass wealth, ammunition, rare-material, or cursed-tool progression. It changes form, not campaign economy.
  • Fine and Very Fine changes are temporary. Permanent item crafting still uses the campaign’s normal crafting or narrative systems.
  • Living biological processing is not part of the base technique because the source article treats biological tests as exceptionally hazardous. A future dedicated feat can add it with its own failure table.
