Refining Clockworks

Feed gear into a portable cursed clockwork, choose how aggressively the machine interprets “refinement,” and decide whether one more Very Fine cycle is worth the Risk.

Combat roleArtificer / equipment support / gambler
Core law

Fine / Anomalous Output Menus

Anomalous Property — Phase Edge

Weapon or tool: once per turn ignore half and three-quarters cover and treat resistance to its physical damage as normal damage for that hit.

Anomalous Property — Zero-Loss Cell

Item stores up to PB CE you willingly place into it. Stored CE may pay only for that item’s own technique features and vanishes when the output ends.

Anomalous Property — Spatial Extension

Double the item’s normal reach or range; once per turn it may affect around one ordinary corner or obstacle as though the origin were 5 feet to either side.

Extraordinary Output — Impossible Conductor

For 1 minute, one CE ability per turn channeled through the item gains +2 to its attack roll or +1 to its save DC, chosen when used.

Extraordinary Output — Perfect Tool

For 1 minute, the item grants advantage on every appropriate ability check and adds twice your proficiency bonus to one such check per round.

Extraordinary Output — Impossible Battery

Stores CE up to twice PB and may release all of it at once to pay a single technique activation. Any unused charge disappears with the object.

The fiction. Registry scholars classify the Clockwork as a shikigami with opinions — a bound machine-spirit whose understanding of “refinement” was set by whoever first wound it, and which the user has never fully renegotiated. The prevailing theory holds that the user's CE is not doing the refining at all; it is feeding a hunger it inherited, one whose appetite for improvement outran any sense of sufficiency. Every cycle is a gamble because the machine does not gamble: it simply believes nothing is ever finished.

Full progression

Progression

1Rough / Coarse / 1:1

Portable Clockworks.

Action, 2 CE: manifest adjacent Intake and Output frames for 10 minutes. Only one set may exist. At level 1 they accept Tiny or Small nonliving objects that are not being worn or carried by an unwilling creature.

Scrap.

Rough and Coarse processes generate Scrap, a technique resource with a cap of PB + CE modifier. Unspent Scrap vanishes after a long rest.

Rough.

Action: destroy a mundane input and gain Scrap by size: Tiny 1, Small 2, Medium 3, Large 4. Cursed or magical objects do not automatically yield Scrap and may resist destruction at the GM’s discretion.

Coarse.

Action: disassemble an input into usable components and gain 1 less Scrap than Rough, minimum 1, or remove one mundane mechanical property such as armor plating, a lock, a blade, or a wheel while preserving the remainder.

1:1.

Action, 1 CE: turn a mundane input into another mundane object of similar size, material value, and complexity. It cannot create currency, ammunition in bulk, explosives, poisons, rare components, or supernatural items from nothing.

3Fine

Action, 3 CE + 1 Scrap: enhance one item for 1 minute. Choose Keen (+1 attack and damage), Reinforced (+1 AC if armor/shield, otherwise +5 × PB object HP), Specialized (once per round, add +2 to one ability check made with the item), or Efficient (first CE cost paid through a cursed tool each round is reduced by 1, minimum 1). One Fine upgrade per item.

6Rapid / Compound Cycle

Clockworks may accept Medium objects and up to PB inputs in one cycle. Once per turn you may process one valid item as a bonus action. Fine may affect a non-Special-Grade cursed tool you willingly place in the Intake; its innate abilities are not copied or permanently altered.

11Very Fine

Unlock Very Fine. Action, 5 CE + 3 Scrap; roll d10 − current Refinement Risk, minimum result 1, then gain 1 Risk after resolving. Risk resets on a long rest. Spend 2 Scrap before rolling to reduce Risk by 1. Large objects become valid inputs.

18Master Dial

Very Fine uses d12 instead of d10. Once per long rest you may declare a controlled result of 10 before rolling; resolve Impossible and immediately set Risk to 5. Fine upgrades may last 10 minutes instead of 1 minute.

Pinnacle expression

Maximum Technique — The Interpretation Engine

Activation Level 18, full-turn action, 10 CE, 3 Scrap. Process one valid nonliving item as though Very Fine rolled 10+, ignoring current Risk.

Duration The chosen Extraordinary Output lasts 10 minutes instead of 1. It still disappears at the end and cannot become a permanent Special Grade item.

Backlash After processing, Refinement Risk becomes 5 and you suffer Technique Burnout until the end of your next turn in addition to any normal cost.

Preparation ceiling Special Grade cursed tools, artifacts, living creatures, and campaign-critical objects are never automatically destroyed, duplicated, or permanently upgraded. Those require explicit GM adjudication or a dedicated feat/ritual.

Innate Technique Feats

Technique Feats

Refining Clockworks

Salvage Intake

When a construct, vehicle, or unattended object is destroyed within 30 feet of your manifested Clockworks, you may use your reaction to feed the wreckage straight into the Intake, gaining Scrap as though you had used Rough on it for its size, without spending an action.

Very Fine

Restorative Refinement

When you activate Very Fine, you may choose restoration instead of rolling: rebuild one damaged or broken mundane object, or one non-Special-Grade cursed tool, placed in the Intake, restoring it to full function and clearing the broken condition. Pay the normal activation cost; no roll is made and no Risk is gained.

Portable Clockworks

Decoupled Line

When you manifest the Clockworks, you may place the Intake and Output frames up to 30 feet apart — both within 60 feet of you — instead of adjacent. Items fed into the Intake emerge refined from the Output normally.

Fine, level 6

Hot-Swap Tuning

As a bonus action, move one active Fine upgrade from an item you touch to another valid item you touch; the upgrade’s remaining duration continues on the new item. An item may still hold only one Fine upgrade.

Rules integration

Table Rulings

914-style “refinement” is not synonymous with improvement. Very Fine is deliberately unpredictable and increasingly dangerous when spammed.
1:1 cannot bypass wealth, ammunition, rare-material, or cursed-tool progression. It changes form, not campaign economy.
Fine and Very Fine changes are temporary. Permanent item crafting still uses the campaign’s normal crafting or narrative systems.
Living biological processing is not part of the base technique because the source article treats biological tests as exceptionally hazardous. A future dedicated feat can add it with its own failure table.
