Refining Clockworks

Feed the machine, choose your risk, and never ask it whether ‘done’ is a real word. Feed gear into a portable cursed clockwork, choose how aggressively the machine interprets “refinement,” and decide whether one more Very Fine cycle is worth the Risk.

Combat roleArtificer / equipment support / gambler
Core law

Fine / Anomalous Output Menus

Anomalous Property — Phase Edge

The mill's first mercy: cover and resistance, reinterpreted away. Weapon or tool: once per turn ignore half and three-quarters cover and treat resistance to its physical damage as normal damage for that hit.

Anomalous Property — Zero-Loss Cell

The machine learns to keep a packed lunch, PB cursed energy, for the item's own use only. Item stores up to PB CE you willingly place into it. Stored CE may pay only for that item’s own technique features and vanishes when the output ends.

Anomalous Property — Spatial Extension

Calibration finding: reach doubled, corners cheated, geometry, refined. The subject's workspace is larger than the room it is in. We have stopped measuring. Double the item’s normal reach or range; once per turn it may affect around one ordinary corner or obstacle as though the origin were 5 feet to either side.

Extraordinary Output — Impossible Conductor

One CE ability per turn, conducted through the impossible. For 1 minute, one CE ability per turn channeled through the item gains +2 to its attack roll or +1 to its save DC, chosen when used.

Extraordinary Output — Perfect Tool

Tool finding: advantage on every appropriate check, the tool, perfected, remembers its purpose. Items refined by the subject perform as though they remember being better than they were. Materials science has no comment. The items do. For 1 minute, the item grants advantage on every appropriate ability check and adds twice your proficiency bonus to one such check per round.

Extraordinary Output — Impossible Battery

A battery that holds twice PB and spends it all at once, the extraordinary, bottled. Stores CE up to twice PB and may release all of it at once to pay a single technique activation. Any unused charge disappears with the object.

Research log. Subject: a shikigami with opinions about 'finished.' The subject was manifested in the fabrication bay and immediately began disassembling a wrench, improving it, and presenting the result with what witnesses described as pride. Two truths govern this file. First: the machine is a gambler that never believes it is gambling, every Very Fine cycle rolls against Refinement Risk, and the Risk climbs because the machine's hunger for better outruns its sense of sufficient. Second: waste is the fuel, Scrap is thrift made tangible, every Rough destruction a small sermon on using the whole animal; the menus of Fine, Anomalous, and Extraordinary outputs let the user choose exactly how far to push, which is to say exactly how much to risk. The subject has begun refining our equipment without authorization. The equipment is, we admit, better now. We have stopped filing the complaints.

Full progression

Progression

1Rough / Coarse / 1:1

Portable Clockworks.

Manifestation spec: two frames, ten minutes, one machine-spirit with opinions. The opinions are the load-bearing component. We have tried manifesting it without them; it refines nothing and sulks, mechanically. Action, 2 CE: manifest adjacent Intake and Output frames for 10 minutes. Only one set may exist. At level 1 they accept Tiny or Small nonliving objects that are not being worn or carried by an unwilling creature.

Scrap.

Currency analysis: Scrap, the machine's coin, minted from ruin. Every destruction is a deposit. The subject is the only entity in the facility with a positive savings rate. Rough and Coarse processes generate Scrap, a technique resource with a cap of PB + CE modifier. Unspent Scrap vanishes after a long rest.

Rough.

Destruction, itemized by size. Action: destroy a mundane input and gain Scrap by size: Tiny 1, Small 2, Medium 3, Large 4. Cursed or magical objects do not automatically yield Scrap and may resist destruction at the GM’s discretion.

Coarse.

Rough process: disassembly with an eye for parts worth keeping. The subject wastes nothing. Our disposal budget has fallen 40%. Our unease has risen proportionally. Action: disassemble an input into usable components and gain 1 less Scrap than Rough, minimum 1, or remove one mundane mechanical property such as armor plating, a lock, a blade, or a wheel while preserving the remainder.

1:1.

Fine process: transmutation's honest middle, nothing from nothing. Mass is conserved; quality is not. The subject considers this a fair trade. Physics has filed no objection, which troubles physics. Action, 1 CE: turn a mundane input into another mundane object of similar size, material value, and complexity. It cannot create currency, ammunition in bulk, explosives, poisons, rare components, or supernatural items from nothing.

3Fine

Action, 3 CE + 1 Scrap: enhance one item Four small mercies, one per item, one minute at a time. for 1 minute. Choose Keen (+1 attack and damage), Reinforced (+1 AC if armor/shield, otherwise +5 × PB object HP), Specialized (once per round, add +2 to one ability check made with the item), or Efficient (first CE cost paid through a cursed tool each round is reduced by 1, minimum 1). One Fine upgrade per item.

6Rapid / Compound Cycle

Passive · Clockworks may accept Medium objects and up to PB inputs in one cycle · Once per turn, process one valid item as a bonus action · Fine may affect a non-Special-Grade cursed tool you willingly place in the Intake (its innate abilities are not copied or permanently altered).

Clockworks may accept Medium objects The line speeds up, more inputs, bonus-action cycles, cursed tools welcome. and up to PB inputs in one cycle. Once per turn you may process one valid item as a bonus action. Fine may affect a non-Special-Grade cursed tool you willingly place in the Intake; its innate abilities are not copied or permanently altered.

11Very Fine

Action · 5 CE + 3 Scrap · Roll d10 minus current Refinement Risk (minimum result 1), then gain 1 Risk after resolving · Risk resets on a long rest; spend 2 Scrap before rolling to reduce Risk by 1 · Large objects become valid inputs.

Threshold event: Very Fine unlocked. Here the gambling starts, roll against the Risk you have been feeding. Every prior cycle has been fattening the odds against you. The subject calls this 'seasoning.' We call it compound interest on hubris. Action, 5 CE + 3 Scrap; roll d10 − current Refinement Risk, minimum result 1, then gain 1 Risk after resolving. Risk resets on a long rest. Spend 2 Scrap before rolling to reduce Risk by 1. Large objects become valid inputs.

18Master Dial

Passive · Very Fine uses d12 instead of d10 · Once per long rest, declare a controlled result of 10 before rolling (resolve Impossible, then set Risk to 5) · Fine upgrades may last 10 minutes instead of 1 minute.

Very Fine uses d12 instead of d10. The miller learns the dial at last, and once per day, tells it the answer. Once per long rest you may declare a controlled result of 10 before rolling; resolve Impossible and immediately set Risk to 5. Fine upgrades may last 10 minutes instead of 1 minute.

Pinnacle expression

Maximum Technique — The Interpretation Engine

Activation One perfect cycle, ordered by name, the machine refines as though the dice had said ten. Level 18, full-turn action, 10 CE, 3 Scrap. Process one valid nonliving item as though Very Fine rolled 10+, ignoring current Risk.

Duration Ten minutes of the extraordinary; then it vanishes, as all borrowed perfection does. The chosen Extraordinary Output lasts 10 minutes instead of 1. It still disappears at the end and cannot become a permanent Special Grade item.

Backlash Backlash finding: Risk set to five, technique burned out, the machine collects its fee. The subject does not punish failure. It invoices it. The invoice is always paid. After processing, Refinement Risk becomes 5 and you suffer Technique Burnout until the end of your next turn in addition to any normal cost.

Preparation ceiling Containment fence, registry's own: Special Grade stays out of the machine's reach, never automatically destroyed, duplicated, or permanently upgraded. The subject has read this rule. It has not tested it. We choose to believe this is respect. Special Grade cursed tools, artifacts, living creatures, and campaign-critical objects are never automatically destroyed, duplicated, or permanently upgraded. Those require explicit GM adjudication or a dedicated feat/ritual.

Innate Technique Feats

Technique Feats

Refining Clockworks

Salvage Intake

Scavenging behavior: the machine learns to catch what the battlefield throws away, wreckage within thirty feet fed straight to the Intake as a reaction. Nothing is wasted. Including, we note, our battlefields. When a construct, vehicle, or unattended object is destroyed within 30 feet of your manifested Clockworks, you may use your reaction to feed the wreckage straight into the Intake, gaining Scrap as though you had used Rough on it for its size, without spending an action.

Very Fine

Restorative Refinement

Restoration mode: refinement run backward, the machine remembers what the object used to be. It rebuilds the broken to full function, no roll, no Risk gained. The subject's memory of objects is better than the objects' memory of themselves. When you activate Very Fine, you may choose restoration instead of rolling: rebuild one damaged or broken mundane object, or one non-Special-Grade cursed tool, placed in the Intake, restoring it to full function and clearing the broken condition. Pay the normal activation cost; no roll is made and no Risk is gained.

Portable Clockworks

Decoupled Line

Spatial finding: Intake here, Output thirty feet there, the mill stretches. The subject's workflow now spans the room. Our floor plan has been updated. The subject updated it first. When you manifest the Clockworks, you may place the Intake and Output frames up to 30 feet apart, both within 60 feet of you, instead of adjacent. Items fed into the Intake emerge refined from the Output normally.

Fine, level 6

Hot-Swap Tuning

Upgrade mobility: upgrades become tenants; they may change rooms. Nothing the subject improves is fixed in place. Including, we have begun to suspect, the subject's definition of 'improved.' As a bonus action, move one active Fine upgrade from an item you touch to another valid item you touch; the upgrade’s remaining duration continues on the new item. An item may still hold only one Fine upgrade.

Rules integration

Table Rulings

914-style “refinement” is not synonymous with improvement. Very Fine is deliberately unpredictable and increasingly dangerous when spammed.
1:1 cannot bypass wealth, ammunition, rare-material, or cursed-tool progression. It changes form, not campaign economy.
Fine and Very Fine changes are temporary. Permanent item crafting still uses the campaign’s normal crafting or narrative systems.
Living biological processing is not part of the base technique because the source article treats biological tests as exceptionally hazardous. A future dedicated feat can add it with its own failure table.
