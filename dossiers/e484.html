Minor Cursed Technique

Flow Technique

Every hit against you leaves something behind. Spend it back.

ClassificationMinor Cursed Technique
TierTier I · Focused
CE abilityWIS
RoleTank

Field notes. Subject: the river’s answer to the blow. Three truths govern the Flow. First, every strike leaves something behind: when cursed force lands on him, his reaction catches the residue and blunts the blow. Second, the river answers: the caught energy is not kept but spent back into his next strike. Third, the discipline has banks: ordinary blows, weapon attacks, falls — the un-cursed world — pass through diminished, but pass through. Mark the maxim: he does not block the river. He drinks from it, and pours it back.

Feature map

Every feature the source provides

Base

Residual Catch

Reaction (when hit by a cursed technique, innate technique, or cursed strike) · No CE · Reduce the damage by 2× PB (3× PB at 6th); add your PB to your next damage roll before the end of your next turn.

Every blow leaves residue, and the river-drinker wastes nothing. Catch the cursed energy as it lands; spend it back before it cools.

Build-defining options.

Feats for this technique

Catch and Release — Prerequisite: Residual Catch

When Residual Catch reduces the triggering damage to 0 · No CE · Immediately make one weapon or technique attack against the attacker as part of the same reaction (your PB damage bonus applies).

The perfect catch returns the blow in the same motion. The river does not pause between drinking and pouring.

Still Water — Prerequisite: Residual Catch

Passive · No CE · Residual Catch also works against non-cursed damage (weapon attacks, falls, and the like), but the reduction is only PB instead of 2× PB.

Still water reflects everything, cursed or not. The discipline widens: even an ordinary fall leaves something to drink.

Ripples — Prerequisite: level 6

Passive · No CE · Split the PB damage bonus from Residual Catch across any number of damage rolls you make before the end of your next turn.

One stone, many ripples. The caught energy spreads across every strike instead of spending itself on one.

Campaign canon notes

Note: The source says "this later increases to three times your proficiency bonus" without naming a level or which half scales; this dossier reads "this" as the damage reduction and sets the bump at 6th level. The carried +PB to your next damage roll does not scale.
Note: The trigger is literal: cursed technique, innate technique, or cursed strike hits only. Plain weapon attacks and spells without a cursed source do not qualify.
Note: The carried bonus applies to your next damage roll of any kind, weapon, unarmed, or technique, made before the end of your next turn, and is lost if unused.
Note: The reduction applies only to the triggering damage.
