Flow Technique

Every hit against you leaves something behind. Spend it back.

ClassificationMinor Cursed Technique
TierTier I · Focused
CE abilityWIS
RoleTank

Field notes. Subject: the river’s answer to the blow. Three truths govern the Flow. First, every strike leaves something behind: when a cursed technique, innate technique, or cursed strike lands on him, his reaction catches the residue, damage reduced by twice his proficiency bonus, three times at sixth level. Second, the river answers: the caught energy is not kept but spent back, his proficiency bonus added to his next damage roll before the end of his next turn. Third, the discipline has banks: ordinary blows, weapon attacks, falls, the un-cursed world, pass through with only a single proficiency bonus of reduction. Mark the maxim: he does not block the river. He drinks from it, and pours it back.

Base

Residual Catch

Reaction (when hit by a cursed technique, innate technique, or cursed strike) · No CE · Reduce the damage by 2× PB (3× PB at 6th); add your PB to your next damage roll before the end of your next turn.

A technique based on using CE residuals. When a creature hits you with a cursed technique, innate technique, or cursed strike, you can spend your reaction to reduce the damage by twice your proficiency bonus. You can then add your proficiency bonus to the next damage roll you make before the end of your next turn. At 6th level the reduction increases to three times your proficiency bonus.

Catch and Release — Prerequisite: Residual Catch

When Residual Catch reduces the triggering damage to 0 · No CE · Immediately make one weapon or technique attack against the attacker as part of the same reaction (your PB damage bonus applies).

[Licensed fill] When Residual Catch reduces the triggering damage to 0, you may immediately make one weapon attack or technique attack against the attacker as part of the same reaction; your proficiency bonus damage bonus from Residual Catch applies to this attack.

Still Water — Prerequisite: Residual Catch

Passive · No CE · Residual Catch also works against non-cursed damage (weapon attacks, falls, and the like), but the reduction is only PB instead of 2× PB.

[Licensed fill] You can use Residual Catch against damage that is not from a cursed technique, innate technique, or cursed strike (weapon attacks, falls, and the like), but the reduction is only your proficiency bonus instead of twice it.

Ripples — Prerequisite: level 6

Passive · No CE · Split the PB damage bonus from Residual Catch across any number of damage rolls you make before the end of your next turn.

[Licensed fill] When Residual Catch lets you add your proficiency bonus to a damage roll, you may instead split that bonus across any number of damage rolls you make before the end of your next turn.

Campaign canon notes

Note: The source says "this later increases to three times your proficiency bonus" without naming a level or which half scales; this dossier reads "this" as the damage reduction and sets the bump at 6th level. The carried +PB to your next damage roll does not scale.
Note: The trigger is literal: cursed technique, innate technique, or cursed strike hits only. Plain weapon attacks and spells without a cursed source do not qualify.
Note: The carried bonus applies to your next damage roll of any kind, weapon, unarmed, or technique, made before the end of your next turn, and is lost if unused.
Note: The reduction applies only to the triggering damage.
