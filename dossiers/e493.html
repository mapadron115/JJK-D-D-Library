Field entry

Journal Entry field report, converted to a table-ready stat block

Field notes. Subject: a siege engine shaped like a locker. It cannot leave its container, speed zero, bound permanently, and this is not a weakness to exploit but a perimeter to respect: it has solved immobility by making the prey come to it. Two snaring tendrils reach twenty feet (+5 to hit, 1d6+3, and grappled on the hit), a bonus-action Drag In hauls the grappled ten feet closer, and anything pulled fully inside begins drowning on a schedule, a DC 12 Constitution save at the start of each turn or 1d8 necrotic as the curse drinks its life. Mark the terrain: deadly in tight rooms, helpless beyond its reach. Determine the reach first; exorcise it from a distance second. Never reverse the order.

Classification & Risk

A Vengeful Cursed Spirit, Grade 4 (CR 1/8–1/2 band). Bound permanently to a container; forms where claustrophobic anxiety accumulates. Its reach makes it dangerous in tight environments.

Passive · No CE · Creature pulled fully inside: DC 12 Constitution save at the start of each of its turns or 1d8 necrotic damage as the curse drains its life.

Life Drain. A creature pulled fully inside the container must succeed on a DC 12 Constitution saving throw at the start of each of its turns or take 1d8 necrotic damage as the curse drains its life force. Once its reach is determined, deal with it from a distance — at range it is simple to exorcise.
