████ and Light

An Innate Technique that forms killing intent into bars of light, a conjured radiant blade, burst barrages, dawn-fire, and hyperspeed movement, culminating in the Maximum Technique Light Speed, the Domain Expansion Reverse Transcendence, and a Reverse Cursed Energy inversion: ████ and Dark.

Innate Technique

████ and Light

"Your technique allows you to form killing intent into bars of light." Cursed Energy DC: 8 + CE ability modifier (Wisdom or Charisma, your choice) + proficiency bonus.

CE abilityWisdom or Charisma
Combat roleBlaster / Striker
Damage typeRadiant (Necrotic in Dark)
DomainReverse Transcendence (L18)

Field notes. Subject: murder, made luminous. Three truths govern the redacted art. First, it weaponizes intent itself: killing intent hardened into bars of light, a conjured radiant blade, burst barrages, dawn-fire that disperses darkness and blinds the hostile. Second, speed is the weapon and the wound: Hyperspeed, and the Maximum, instantaneous motion along a line — ruin for everything it crosses, and ruin risked by the body that dares the crossing, torn apart by its own passage’s own ruin. Third, the Reversal is the confession: the same killing intent, inverted into darkness and necrotic hunger, the vortex pulling all things closer. Mark this: the light was never the point. The killing was. Light merely delivers him.

Feature map

Level-by-level features

L1

Light Constructs

Bonus action · 2 CE · Conjure a bar of light in a free hand for 1 minute: simple melee weapon with finesse/light/thrown (20/60), 2d8 radiant damage, CE modifier to attack and damage rolls.

Murder, given a handle. The technique takes the oldest impulse — the wish that someone stop existing — and forges it into something that can be gripped, thrown, and called back. It glows first and cuts second; the cantrip is only the art warming up.

L3

Rain of Light

Action · 3 CE · 15-ft cone or 10-ft radius around you: Dex save vs. 3d10 radiant (5d10 at 6th, 8d10 at 11th, 10d10 at 18th), restrained on a failed save (repeat save at end of turn ends).

The sky remembers it is full of knives. Bars of light fall like a verdict and pin what they touch — the restrained do not escape the light, only negotiate with it, one save at a time.

L3

████ of Dawn

Action · 2 CE · Dispel magical darkness within 30 ft; hostiles within 30 ft make a Con save vs. 2d10 + CE modifier radiant (3d10 at 6th, 5d10 at 11th, 8d10 at 18th), blinded until end of turn on fail (half, not blinded on success).

Dawn, weaponized. Whatever darkness the enemy was counting on — conjured, cursed, or merely the absence of witnesses — comes apart, and what remains is too bright to look at.

L6

Hyperspeed

Passive · No CE · Resistance to radiant damage.

The body learns the speed of what it throws. Light stops burning the hand that holds it.

L6

Light Beam

When you attack with your Light Construct · 1 CE: make the attack at advantage and launch the bar as a ranged spell attack vs. a creature within 60 ft · On a melee hit with the construct, teleport 10 ft to an unoccupied space you can see.

The throw becomes the travel. The bar leaves the hand as a spear of light, and the hand follows it — ten feet of becoming the beam, then being a body again.

L6

Laser Light Techniques

When you use a Light Technique · Spend up to your CE modifier in CE · +1 additional damage die per CE spent (2 dice per CE at 11th, 3 dice per CE at 18th).

Intent, compounded. The same killing thought, pushed harder through the same light — the beam does not widen, it deepens.

L11

Maximum: Light Speed

Action · 8 CE · Instantly travel a 5-ft-wide, 120-ft line (200 ft at 18th, 300 ft at 20th): creatures in the line make a Con save vs. 18d10 radiant and are knocked prone (half, not prone on success) · For each creature, object, or structure passed through, Con save vs. your CE save DC or take 20 bludgeoning.

The body stops pretending it is solid. The line is crossed all at once, and everything on it learns what it is to be struck by light — including, if he is careless, himself.

L18

Domain Expansion: Reverse Transcendence

Full turn · 20 CE · 150-ft radius · At initiative 20, all creatures in the domain take 6d12 radiant · Inside: movement speed doubles and all movement is unreactable teleportation; as an action, teleport anywhere and deal 4d12 radiant to one creature within 5 ft of your start or end point.

There is a sound like the world inhaling, and then everything is white-gold. The domain is a corridor of pure radiance with no visible end, and the user's body comes apart at the edges into drifting particles of light, reforming with every step. To look directly at them is to look at the sun through a keyhole.

Reversal: ████ and Dark

Darkness spell: Action · 2 CE · Dark Constructs: Bonus action · 4 CE, 1-minute bar of darkness, simple melee weapon, 4d8 necrotic, finesse/light/thrown (20/60), CE modifier to attack and damage, hit halves target speed till end of its next turn · Dark Vortex: Action · 12 CE, concentration up to 1 minute: creatures within 60 ft Str save vs. 6d10 necrotic (10d10 at 6th, 14d10 at 11th, 18d10 at 18th), pulled 10 ft closer on fail (half, not pulled on success); maintain as an action; reaction: roll vortex damage to reduce incoming damage, nullified at 0.

The confession of the art: the killing intent was never about light. Inverted, it drinks the dark instead of burning it — constructs of hunger, and a vortex that pulls the world closer to be unmade.

Campaign canon notes

Name redaction: the source redacts the technique's first word ("████ and Light", "████ of Dawn", "████ and Dark"). The redaction is preserved verbatim throughout; no name has been invented. If the source document's redaction is ever lifted, this dossier's title should be corrected.
Domain defaults (filled): the source gives no activation action, CE cost, radius, or clash stats for Reverse Transcendence. Per D008 campaign canon: full-turn activation, 20 CE, 150-ft radius, Tier IV, 800 clash points, 5-round burnout, 2-minute duration. The initiative-20 sure-hit is read as recurring each round at initiative count 20 (lair-action timing); the caster is exempt per standard Domain canon, but allies are not exempt, the source says "all creatures".
"Light Techniques" (Laser): Laser Light Techniques' extra dice apply to Rain of Light and ████ of Dawn, the two features introduced under the source's "Light Techniques" heading. Each die is the feature's own damage die (d10).
Defensive Constructs shield: the source gives no statistics for the shield; treat it as an ordinary shield (+2 AC while held). The 5 × 5 ft panel's AC, hit points, and immunities are per the source and are destroyable objects, not creatures.
████ of Dawn vs. your own Darkness: ████ of Dawn dispels any magical darkness within 30 ft, including the Darkness you create via ████ and Dark. Positioning matters.
Dark Vortex maintenance: maintaining the vortex consumes your action each turn. The reaction absorption rolls the vortex's current-level damage (6d10, or 10d10/14d10/18d10 at 6th/11th/18th). "Limb Removing Effect" at Maximum Output is the source's wording, resolve limb loss with the table's injury rules; a 5-ft creature that fails its save is also subject to it.
Light Beam teleport: the 10-ft teleport triggers when you hit a creature with a melee attack using your Light Construct, it is not limited to once per turn by the source text, but each teleport requires a separate melee hit.
