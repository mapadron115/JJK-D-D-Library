████ and Light

An Innate Technique that forms killing intent into bars of light — a conjured radiant blade, burst barrages, dawn-fire, and hyperspeed movement — culminating in the Maximum Technique Light Speed, the Domain Expansion Reverse Transcendence, and a Reverse Cursed Energy inversion: ████ and Dark.

Innate Technique

████ and Light

"Your technique allows you to form killing intent into bars of light." Cursed Energy DC: 8 + CE ability modifier (Wisdom or Charisma, your choice) + proficiency bonus.

CE abilityWisdom or Charisma
Combat roleBlaster / Striker
Damage typeRadiant (Necrotic in Dark)
DomainReverse Transcendence (L18)

Field notes. Subject: murder, made luminous. Three truths govern the redacted art. First, it weaponizes intent itself: killing intent hardened into bars of light — a conjured radiant blade, burst barrages, dawn-fire that disperses darkness and blinds the hostile. Second, speed is the weapon and the wound: Hyperspeed, and the Maximum — instantaneous motion along a line, 18d10 radiant ruin on all it crosses — but the body risks tearing apart for every thing passed through, a Constitution save against one’s own ruin. Third, the Reversal is the confession: the same killing intent, inverted into darkness and necrotic hunger, the vortex pulling all things closer. Mark this: the light was never the point. The killing was. Light merely delivers him.

Full progression

Feature map

Level-by-level features

L1

Light Constructs

You learn the Light cantrip. As a bonus action for 2 cursed energy, you mold killing intent into a bar of light in a free hand. The weapon lasts 1 minute and counts as a simple melee weapon with which you are proficient. It deals 2d8 radiant damage on a hit and has the finesse, light, and thrown properties (range 20/60). You can use your Cursed Energy modifier for attack and damage rolls with this weapon. If you drop the weapon or throw it, it dissipates and reappears in your hand.

L3

Rain of Light

As an action for 3 cursed energy, you manifest bars of light in either a 15 ft cone or a 10 ft radius around yourself. All creatures in the area make a Dexterity saving throw; on a failed save they take 3d10 radiant damage and are restrained by bars of light piercing their body. A restrained creature can repeat the save at the end of their turn, escaping on a success. Damage increases to 5d10 at 6th level, 8d10 at 11th level, and 10d10 at 18th level. The cone's range increases by 15 ft and the radius increases by 5 ft at 6th, 11th, and 18th level.

L3

████ of Dawn

As an action for 2 cursed energy, any magical darkness within 30 feet of you is dispelled. Each hostile creature within 30 feet of you must make a Constitution saving throw: on a failure they take 2d10 + your CE modifier radiant damage and are blinded until the end of their turn; on a success they take half as much damage and are not blinded. A creature behind total cover is unaffected. Damage increases to 3d10 at 6th level, 5d10 at 11th level, and 8d10 at 18th level.

L6

Hyperspeed

You learn to imbue your body and constructs with high speed. You gain resistance to radiant damage.

L6

Light Beam

When you make an attack with your Light Construct, you can spend 1 CE to make the attack at advantage and launch the bar, making a ranged spell attack against a creature within 60 ft. When you hit a creature with a melee attack using your Light Construct, you can teleport 10 ft to an unoccupied space you can see, your body becoming a beam of light for a moment.

L6

Laser Light Techniques

When you use a Light Technique, you can spend additional CE up to your CE modifier, dealing an additional damage dice per CE spent. This increases to 2 additional dice per CE at 11th level and 3 additional dice per CE at 18th level.

L11

Maximum: Light Speed

As an action for 8 CE, you instantaneously move in a 5 ft wide, 120 feet long line. All creatures in that line must make a Constitution saving throw: on a failure they take 18d10 radiant damage and are knocked prone; on a success they take half as much damage and are not knocked prone. The technique risks tearing your body apart: for every creature, object, or structure you move through, you must make a Constitution saving throw against your Cursed Energy save DC; on a failed save you take 20 bludgeoning damage. At Maximum Output, objects and structures take maximum damage, and creatures with a Constitution score lower than your CE score automatically fail the saving throw. At 18th level the line increases to 200 ft, and at 20th level to 300 ft.

L18

Domain Expansion: Reverse Transcendence

At initiative 20, all creatures in your domain take 6d12 radiant damage. While within your domain, your movement speed doubles, and all movement on your turn is considered an instantaneous teleport and cannot be reacted to, as your every move is at light speed and your body breaks down into light particles whenever you move. As an action on your turn, you can teleport anywhere within your domain; choose one creature within 5 ft of the point you start from or end up at, and that creature takes 4d12 radiant damage.

Pinnacle expression

Maximum, Domain, or equivalent.

Capstone material

Maximum Technique: Light Speed

Action, 8 CE. You move instantaneously along a 5-ft-wide line — 120 ft (11th), 200 ft (18th), 300 ft (20th) — and every creature in the line makes a Constitution save or takes 18d10 radiant damage and falls prone (half damage, not prone on success). Each creature, object, or structure you pass through forces a Constitution save against your Cursed Energy save DC; on failure you take 20 bludgeoning damage as your body risks tearing apart. At Maximum Output, objects and structures take maximum damage and creatures with a Constitution score lower than your CE score automatically fail their save.

Domain Expansion: Reverse Transcendence — Tier IV

Full-turn, 20 CE, 150-ft radius. 800 clash points, 5-round burnout, 2-minute duration. Sure-hit: at initiative 20, all creatures in the domain take 6d12 radiant damage. While inside, your movement speed doubles and all your movement is instantaneous, unreactable teleportation. As an action, teleport anywhere within the domain and deal 4d12 radiant damage to one creature within 5 ft of your departure or arrival point. (Clash tier, activation, CE cost, and radius filled per D008 campaign canon; the source states none. See Rules integration.)

Reversal: ████ and Dark

Through studying reverse cursed energy you learn your reversal, creating constructs of darkness from your killing intent. You learn the Darkness spell and cast it as an action for 2 CE. Dark Constructs: as a bonus action for 4 CE, mold a bar of darkness in a free hand — lasts 1 minute, simple melee weapon you are proficient with, 4d8 necrotic damage, finesse / light / thrown (20/60), CE modifier to attack and damage; dropped or thrown, it dissipates and reappears in your hand. On a hit, the target's speed is halved until the end of their next turn (does not stack). Dark Vortex: as an action for 12 CE, create a dark vortex in your hands for up to 1 minute while you maintain concentration. All creatures within 60 ft make a Strength saving throw: failure — 6d10 necrotic damage and pulled 10 ft closer; success — half damage, not pulled. You maintain the vortex as an action on your turn, forcing all creatures in range to repeat the save. As a reaction to taking damage while maintaining the vortex, you can absorb the attack into it: roll your vortex damage and reduce the attack by that much; if reduced to 0, the attack or effect is nullified. If the damage is from a melee attack, the weapon is destroyed unless it is a Grade 1 cursed tool or higher. At Maximum Output, a creature pulled within 5 ft, or within 5 ft and failing its save, treats this damage as a Limb Removing Effect. Vortex damage increases to 10d10 at 6th level, 14d10 at 11th level, and 18d10 at 18th level.

Innate Technique Feats

Build-defining options.

Feats for this technique

Dazzling Glare — Prerequisite: must have Light Constructs

As a reaction for 2 cursed energy when you are attacked by a creature within 30 feet of you that you can see, you impose disadvantage on the attack roll with a flare of light. An attacker that can't be blinded is immune to this feature. At 6th level the target also takes 2d8 radiant damage; at 11th level, 4d8 radiant damage.

Irradiated Light — Prerequisite: must have Light Techniques

You weaponize the radiation of your light constructs. When a creature fails a save against one of your innate technique features, you can spend 1 cursed energy to give it the poisoned condition and make it take radiant damage equal to your CE modifier at the beginning of each of its turns. This lasts a number of rounds equal to your CE modifier. The creature can make a Constitution saving throw at the end of each of its turns, ending the effect on a success.

Defensive Constructs — Prerequisite: must have Hyperspeed

When you use Light Constructs, you can instead create a shield in a free hand or a flat 5 ft × 5 ft panel of light within 20 ft of you for 1 minute. The panel has an AC equal to your CE save DC, hit points equal to your level × your CE modifier, and immunity to radiant, poison, and psychic damage. As a bonus action, you can choose to make the panel solid or not, allowing targets to move through it when non-solid. (The shield follows standard shield rules — see Rules integration.)

Light Speed Versatility — Prerequisite: must have Maximum: Light Speed

You have adapted to the process of gaining the properties of light. If you fail your saving throw when using Light Speed, you take only 10 bludgeoning damage. Additionally, as a bonus action for 3 cursed energy, you can activate Light Speed as a bonus action, sacrificing speed and damage for quicker activation: the length you travel and the technique's damage dice are halved. This cannot be used with Maximum Output.

████ and Dark — Prerequisite: must know reverse cursed energy

The technique's reversal. You learn the Darkness spell, castable as an action for 2 CE. Dark Constructs (bonus action, 4 CE): bar of darkness, 1 minute, simple melee weapon, 4d8 necrotic, finesse / light / thrown (20/60), CE modifier to attack and damage; a hit halves the target's speed until the end of their next turn (does not stack); dropped or thrown, it dissipates and reappears in your hand. Dark Vortex (action, 12 CE, concentration, up to 1 minute): creatures within 60 ft make a Strength save — failure: 6d10 necrotic damage and pulled 10 ft closer (scaling to 10d10 / 14d10 / 18d10 at 6th / 11th / 18th); maintain it as an action to force repeated saves; as a reaction while maintaining, absorb damage into the vortex by rolling its damage and reducing the attack (nullified at 0; a melee weapon is destroyed unless Grade 1 cursed tool or higher). At Maximum Output, a creature pulled within 5 ft (or within 5 ft and failing its save) treats the damage as a Limb Removing Effect.

Rules integration

Edge cases and shared systems.

Campaign canon notes

Name redaction: the source redacts the technique's first word ("████ and Light", "████ of Dawn", "████ and Dark"). The redaction is preserved verbatim throughout; no name has been invented. If the source document's redaction is ever lifted, this dossier's title should be corrected.
Domain defaults (filled): the source gives no activation action, CE cost, radius, or clash stats for Reverse Transcendence. Per D008 campaign canon: full-turn activation, 20 CE, 150-ft radius, Tier IV — 800 clash points, 5-round burnout, 2-minute duration. The initiative-20 sure-hit is read as recurring each round at initiative count 20 (lair-action timing); the caster is exempt per standard Domain canon, but allies are not exempt — the source says "all creatures".
"Light Techniques" (Laser): Laser Light Techniques' extra dice apply to Rain of Light and ████ of Dawn — the two features introduced under the source's "Light Techniques" heading. Each die is the feature's own damage die (d10).
Defensive Constructs shield: the source gives no statistics for the shield; treat it as an ordinary shield (+2 AC while held). The 5 × 5 ft panel's AC, hit points, and immunities are per the source and are destroyable objects, not creatures.
████ of Dawn vs. your own Darkness: ████ of Dawn dispels any magical darkness within 30 ft — including the Darkness you create via ████ and Dark. Positioning matters.
Dark Vortex maintenance: maintaining the vortex consumes your action each turn. The reaction absorption rolls the vortex's current-level damage (6d10, or 10d10/14d10/18d10 at 6th/11th/18th). "Limb Removing Effect" at Maximum Output is the source's wording — resolve limb loss with the table's injury rules; a 5-ft creature that fails its save is also subject to it.
Light Beam teleport: the 10-ft teleport triggers when you hit a creature with a melee attack using your Light Construct — it is not limited to once per turn by the source text, but each teleport requires a separate melee hit.
