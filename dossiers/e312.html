Features & procedures

Properties & techniques

Exact player-facing procedures

Empowered edge

Passive · +4 to attack and damage rolls made with this cursed tool.

Beneath the miracle, a master’s edge: plus four to strike and wound. The spear needs no technique to kill, its negation is for the moments when killing alone is not enough. You have a +4 bonus to attack and damage rolls made with this cursed tool.

Heaven's inversion

Passive · All magic and cursed-energy features are deactivated against this weapon; a technique, barrier, or CE-fueled effect the spear touches stops functioning against it while in contact.

The whole of the weapon in one sentence: what it touches, ceases, against it, for as long as contact holds. It does not break techniques. It simply stops attending to them. All magic and cursed energy-related features are deactivated against this weapon. A technique, barrier, or CE-fueled effect the spear touches stops functioning against it while in contact.

Field notes. Subject: the argument ended. Registry theory holds that technique supremacy was always conditional, that any sorcerer, however refined, stands upon rules his enemy never agreed to keep. This dagger is that theory, weaponized. Two truths govern it. First, it does not overpower a technique; it declines to participate in it, whatever the spear touches, the world’s cursed-energy rules briefly stop applying to it, and barriers, wards, and fueled effects simply cease against it while in contact. Second, the negation is local, not imperial: it deactivates what it touches, and only while it touches it, sheathed, it is merely a knife. Mark this: the strongest techniques in the world were built to win arguments. This one leaves the room.

Rules integration

Cursed-tool attunement

Campaign canon

Cursed-tool attunement: you have PB attunement slots (2–6). This tool occupies one slot and requires a one-hour ritual. Attunement ends voluntarily over a short rest, on death, or when you replace it while over capacity. Distance does not break attunement. Cursed-tool attunement is separate from magic-item attunement.
Balance & counterplay. Deactivation applies against the weapon itself, it does not strip a target's techniques globally, and it offers no defense while sheathed or out of contact.
