Blood Techniques

The Kamo clan’s blood art: blood shaped into constructs and weapons, pushed to its absolute limit — a striker’s package of red scale, piercing beams, and the Bloodbath domain.

Power tierTier IV · Special Grade
Combat roleStriker / blaster
Core engineBlood constructs + Convergence
Domain · pinnacleBloodbath (L18)

The fiction. The Kamo inherited this technique the way other families inherit debts: the clan's CE treats blood as the body's own cursed energy made liquid, and every construct is a withdrawal against an account the flesh keeps. Jujutsu scholars classify Convergence as the blood remembering it was always one substance — separate streams refusing to stay divided. The price is literal by design: a technique that spends the body cannot be borrowed or stolen, only paid for in the currency only the living carry.

Full progression

Feature map

1Blood Constructs · Blood Techniques.

Starting at 1st level, you have been blessed with the Innate Technique of the Kamo clan, making you begin with the most basic technique, the blood constructs. You can pull blood from an available source to create a semi-solid construct. As an action for 1 CE and 1 HP, you can create a small construct smaller than a 5ft by 5ft space. For every 5 ft the price increases by 1 CE and HP. You can ignore the HP cost if you have an alternate source of blood. These constructs have HP equal to your level times CE spent. Once as part of a long rest, you can spend $100 and hit points up to your proficiency bonus to fill a Blood Bag with your own blood. This Blood Bag counts as a source of hit points for your Blood Manipulation features. A Blood Bag lasts 5 days outside of refrigeration before it goes bad. You can have a number of Blood Bags equal to your Constitution modifier at once. Starting at 1st level, you can harness your blood in a manner fit for battle. When you use a Blood Manipulation feature, you can ignore the HP cost if you have an alternate source of blood. You gain three of following techniques and gain an additional two at 6th and 11th level.

Crimson Binding. As an action for 1 CE and 1 HP, you unleash a red blood net, forcing a target Large or smaller within 30ft to make a Strength saving throw or be restrained. They can repeat the save at the start of their turn.

Slicing Exorcism. As an action for 1 CE and 1 HP you spin a ring of blood and unleash it as a projectile. Make a ranged CE attack against a target within 60 ft, on a hit you deal 1d10 + CE modifier necrotic Damage. If used with Convergence, the attack still deals your CE modifier on a miss of 5 or less.

Blood Edge. You can spend CE up to your Cursed Energy modifier to create a melee weapon as a free action which counts as magical for overcoming resistances. For every two Cursed Energy used the weapon gains an additional damage tier(d10->d12->2d8) and lasts 1 minute. If used with Convergence, it only gains the benefit of the damage tier increase.

Crimson Rain. As an action for 3 CE and 2 HP, you pick an area within 60 ft, that spot becomes the center of a 15 ft radius of a rain of blood spikes. All creatures in that radius must make a Dexterity Saving throw taking 3d10 piercing damage on failure or half as much on a success. If used with Convergence, a target is also knocked prone on a failed save.

Blood Burst. For 1 CE and 1 HP upon hitting with a weapon attack once per turn, you cover your strike with blood, causing it to explode on impact. The creature must make a Dexterity saving throw. On a failure they take 4d6 necrotic damage.

Blood Meteorite. As an action for 1 CE and 3 HP you create a solid crystal of blood launched like an asteroid. You can make a ranged CE attack against a target in 30 ft. On a hit, this attack deals 4d4 + CE modifier in bludgeoning damage.

Blood Geyser. As an action for 3 CE and 3 HP, you can choose a 5 feet space within 10 ft of yourself and saturate it with blood. When a creature starts their turn there or enters that area, all creatures within 5 feet must make a Dexterity saving throw or take 5d10 bludgeoning damage and get launched 30ft in the air or half as much on a success.

3Flowing Red Scale.

At 3rd level, this technique can increase the user's body temperature, pulse rate, and the number of red blood cells to give themselves greatly increased energy and physical capabilities. You can spend 3 Cursed Energy as a bonus action to gain the following benefits for 1 minute: Your speed is doubled. You will gain a +2 bonus to your AC. You gain advantage on Dexterity saving throws and Strength checks. You will gain an additional action on each of your turns. That action can be used only to take the Attack (one weapon attack only), Dash, Disengage, Hide, or Use an Object action.

6Convergence.

At 6th level, you compress and condense blood to its absolute limit. Before you use a blood manipulation technique with a casting time of one action, you can spend your bonus action, reaction and movement to enhance the technique's potency, gaining the following benefits: You deal additional damage die equal to your proficiency bonus. Your damage die increases by 1 level(d10->d12->2d8). The range increases by 30 feet. If you spend an additional turn using your action, bonus action, reaction and movement to charge up Convergence before using your technique, you gain these benefits an additional time and gain advantage on the first technique attack roll or can force creatures to make their first saving throw against your technique at disadvantage. Certain techniques may have additional or different bonuses if used with Convergence that are listed with each technique.

11Piercing Blood.

Beginning at 11th level, you can compress your blood into a deadly supersonic beam. As an action for 8 CE and 5 HP, you unleash a 100ft line of blood. Creatures in range must make a Dexterity save taking 10d8 piercing on a failure. If used with Convergence, you can continue shooting the beam for an additional round. Any creature that enters the lingering Piercing Blood must make the saving throw. As an action you can swing the line, making it into a 130 feet cone forcing an additional Dexterity save and the technique ends.

18Domain Expansion: Bloodbath.

At Initiative 20, all creatures take 4d12 necrotic damage as their blood attacks them from the inside. While in the domain your blood manipulation techniques can use hit points from other creatures within the domain.

Innate Technique Feats

Build-defining options.

These are optional character-building feats. You must meet each listed prerequisite before selecting it.

Prerequisites: Must have Blood Manipulation

Blood Production

You have found a way to link your blood to Cursed Energy. For 1 CE, you can create a sufficient source of blood worth 10 HP that can only be used for your Blood Manipulation features. This does not regain HP.

Prerequisites: Must have Blood Manipulation

Poisonous Blood

As an action, you may draw your blood into a sealed vessel, spending up to 10 HP; the vessel holds that blood for 24 hours. A creature can drink it as a bonus action to regain hit points equal to the HP stored. You can maintain only one such vessel at a time.

Prerequisites: Must have Blood Constructs

Blood Crystallization

As an action for 3 CE, you crystallize your blood into a solid object: a simple weapon, a tool, or a 10-foot plank or pole. The object lasts 1 hour, has AC 15 and hit points equal to twice your level, and shatters into harmless shards when destroyed.

Prerequisites: Must have Flowing Red Scale

Crimson Afterimage

While Flowing Red Scale is active, your accelerated blood lets you leave a bloody afterimage. As a bonus action for 3 CE, you create an illusory duplicate in your space until the start of your next turn; the first attack roll made against you before then targets the duplicate, AC 10 + your proficiency bonus, instead of you, and the duplicate vanishes whether the attack hits or misses.

Prerequisites: Must have Piercing Blood

Supernova

You can create small orbs of condensed blood that explode like a buckshot. As an action, you condense your Piercing Blood into a tiny orb of blood in an unoccupied space that you can see within 30 feet, which stays there for a maximum of 1 minute before exploding. You can create as many orbs as your proficiency bonus in an action with each having its own cost, spending 5 CE and 5 HP for each, and can choose for them to explode immediately upon creation or manually as a reaction to a creature entering the range of explosion. The orb then explodes, forcing every creature within 10 feet of it to make a Dexterity saving throw for each orb. On a failure, the target takes 8d8 piercing damage and is knocked prone. On a success, they take half as much damage and aren’t knocked prone.

Prerequisites: Must have Piercing Blood

Wing King Imitation

You condense your blood into wings resembling veins as a bonus action for 10 CE, gathering the wings behind you and gaining the following benefits for 1 minute: your walking speed is quadrupled; you gain a flying speed equal to half your walking speed; as a free action on your turn, you can unleash lines of blood. This takes shape as three ranged Cursed Energy attacks against a creature within 60 feet. Each attack deals 3d10 + your CE modifier in necrotic damage. While using Wing King, you cannot use Piercing Blood.

Rules integration

Edge cases and shared systems.

Blood Bags: ADOPT finite bags. Pay actual HP when filling and record remaining blood-units, age and storage. One blood-unit replaces one HP cost and is consumed; "alternate source" is not an inexhaustible exemption. Propose external blood must be own blood or an explicitly technique-compatible source. Constructs retain HP = technique level × CE spent. Proposed construct AC = CE DC, duration 1 minute; unoccupied space only, no creation inside a creature. Source does not provide these AC/duration details, so they remain new review choices.
Boundary: Filling is once per long rest, not once per each recovered hit point. RCT healing the donor does not refill an already-spent blood bag.
Seven options: ADOPT all seven with exact costs. Binding Large-or-smaller 30 ft STR repeat start turn. Exorcism 60 ft CE attack 1d10 + modifier; Convergence near-miss within 5 AC deals modifier only. Edge free action 1 minute pays 1..actual CE mod CE; choose a named melee weapon, each complete 2 CE raises native die one tier; ordinary proficiency/attack rules. Rain 15-ft radius within 60 DEX 3d10 piercing half; Convergence failure prone. Burst once/turn after weapon hit DEX 4d6 necrotic failure/zero success. Meteorite 30 ft CE attack 4d4 + modifier bludgeoning. Geyser 5-ft space within 10, entry/start trigger 5-ft burst DEX 5d10 bludgeoning half; failure launches 30 ft. Proposed Geyser lasts 1 minute and triggers once then ends; both are missing source limits, not verified facts.
Boundary: Do not add a nonexistent HP price to Blood Edge. Source has no pre-PB cap on Geyser persistence; new explicit one-trigger profile requires review. Reference a complete damage-tier table before deploying unusual base weapon dice.
Convergence: ADOPT V6 action-economy model; delete old CE/HP augmentation as the base Convergence feature. Declare before an eligible action-time Blood feature on your turn; cannot commit a reaction already spent or movement already used this turn. No additional CE cost invented. Maximum one additional charge turn, not unbounded repeated charge turns. Propose transform each native die by one tier, then add PB dice of the resulting single-die type; for compound-tier results such as 2d8, PB adds PB d8, not PB×2d8. Second stage raises tier a second time and adds a total 2 PB final dice. Damage-tier endpoints outside the published ladder remain held.
Boundary: V6 grants Blood Edge a Convergence exception despite free-action activation. Proposed exception: Converged Edge uses your action to create it and gains tier increases only. This explicit resolution is needed; generic action compression does not qualify an otherwise ineligible feature retroactively.
Flowing Red Scale: KEEP Red Scale: double speed, +2 AC, DEX save/STR check advantage, one restricted extra action (one weapon attack or listed utility). No borrowed Haste lethargy/Focus clause.
Piercing Blood: ADOPT Piercing Blood 8 CE/5 HP; HYBRID retain V3 half damage on successful DEX save, explicitly a campaign choice because V6 does not print it. Beam 100 ft, 10d8 piercing; propose 5-ft width. Converged beam persists through end of your next turn; each creature saves at most once per turn for entering it. Action sweep ends beam and makes 1 30-ft cone with same damage/save. Separate sweep is a new damage event, not a refund/new paid activation.
Boundary: Convergence and Maximum Output can combine if each remains legal; Chanting expressly excludes Maximum Output. MO 8 CE → 12 CE does not increase or waive 5 HP. Lingering-beam trigger limits and width are new fills.
Bloodbath: HYBRID preserve V3 chosen-enemy targeting for 4d12 init 20, rather than quietly importing friendly fire. Use adopted Domain chassis and one D009 potency addition. Proposed each Blood feature can pay its HP cost from one chosen creature actually affected by that feature in the Domain, limited to actual HP lost to the substitution. This loss is part of that feature's damage and obeys the feature's protection/counterplay; it is not healing and cannot draw from an untouched bystander through suppressed sure-hit protection. Own/bag blood pays any shortfall.
Boundary: This is a substantive hybrid choice requiring review. A stat block lacking literal biological blood is not made immune merely by narrative inference; exact spirit/construct interaction should be stated in the final dossier.
Production, Poison, Crystallization: ADOPT V6. Propose Blood Production paid as part of the Blood activation needing it; each 1 CE gives 10 consumable blood units, excess lasts until current encounter ends (new anti-stockpile duration). No Healing Reserve recovery. Poison trigger hit by 5 or fail by 5, CON repeat end turn, 1 minute; normal poison immunity. Crystallization BA 3 CE, 1 minute, weapon/unarmed rules unchanged except two unarmed die tiers; reaction +5 AC, physical damage resistance; roll exhaustion at end of each active turn, 2 or less. End voluntarily before future turns as a proposed BA; exhaustion already incurred remains.
Boundary: New timing/storage/dismissal fills require review. Do not restore the superseded fixed DC 10 Poisonous Blood noted elsewhere in historical campaign records.
Stack, Supernova, Wing King: ADOPT Stack as replacement not addition to Red Scale: one extra unrestricted action and one extra reaction total, no stacked base extra action/+2 AC. Retain 6 or 3 cost and 1 minute. Supernova action creates up to PB orbs each 5 CE/5 HP, within 30, 10-ft burst 8d8 piercing DEX half/prone failure; immediate/manual reaction/1-minute expiry. Proposed one manual reaction detonates any selected created orbs in range, each separate save. Wing King BA 10 CE 1 minute, quadruple walk, fly half walk, one free volley of three CE attacks per YOUR turn at 60 ft, each 3d10 + modifier; no Piercing Blood while active. Remove V3 once/LR limit, retain cost. Movement multipliers replace the weaker simultaneous multiplier rather than multiply 3×4.
Boundary: Wing King's once-per-turn limit is a necessary campaign fill; otherwise free action would be unbounded. One MO activation of a multi-orb action must pay its entire selected orb CE input plus ceil half; it cannot surcharge one orb and boost every orb's damage.
Blood Edge migration: MIGRATE existing source-based character: if paid a feat for Blood Edge, refund that feat for another qualified feat, and choose Blood Edge as one of the proper technique selections. Do not retain both a free extra selection and refunded feat unless the GM explicitly grants that boon. Supernova/Wing King former Maximum labels are not additional feat copies; update names/prerequisites without charging another feat.
Boundary: This migration rule concerns author-technique characters. Our original library's unrelated or heavily rewritten Blood-inspired dossiers remain deferred.

Poisonous Blood

 Your blood has grown to be harmful to others. If you hit a creature

    You gain resistance to bludgeoning, piercing, and slashing
       damage
