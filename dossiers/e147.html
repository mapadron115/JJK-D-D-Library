Faultline Ledger

Mark faults in bodies and terrain, let ordinary movement and impacts accumulate Strain, then cash that stored stress out in controlled ruptures.

RoleStriker / Controller
Power tierHigh
Main resourceStrain

The fiction. Registry scholars describe it as a surveyor's eye weaponized — the user's cursed energy learned to read bodies and terrain the way an engineer reads fault lines, tallying every tremor, fall, and impact in a private ledger. Nothing here strikes the killing blow itself; it merely keeps the accounts until the ground demands payment. The prevailing theory holds that the technique's patience is its horror: ordinary movement becomes the evidence that convicts.

Full progression

Level Progression

1Inscribe Fault, Accumulate Strain, Survey, Rupture

Inscribe Fault

Once per turn when you damage a creature, object, or surface, you may place a Fault on it. You can maintain Faults equal to your proficiency bonus. Faults last 1 minute.

Accumulate Strain

A Fault gains 1 Strain (maximum 6) the first time each round its subject: moves at least 10 feet, is forcibly moved, falls, or takes bludgeoning/force/thunder damage. Your own attacks can trigger this. A Fault cannot gain more than 2 Strain per round before 11th level.

Survey

Bonus Action • 1 CE • 60 feet. Choose one Fault you can see and add 1 Strain to it.

Rupture

Once per turn on hit, spend 2–6 Strain from the target’s Fault — no damage. 2 Strain (Tremor): Dex save or prone; its space becomes difficult terrain until the start of your next turn. 4 Strain (Fissure): Strength save or prone + loses reactions until the start of its next turn; tear a 15×5-ft fissure (crossing requires Dex save or prone). The fissure lasts 1 minute (invented). 6 Strain (Upheaval): Strength save or prone + pushed 15 ft; 20-ft-radius difficult terrain until the start of your next turn; success: pushed 5 ft, stays up. 8 Strain (Cataclysm, requires Deep Fault): Constitution save or −2 AC until the end of your next turn; knocked prone; 20-ft difficult terrain.

3Mapped Ground —

you can place a Fault on a 10-foot section of terrain as an action for 1 CE. When a creature crosses it, you may use your reaction to add 1 Strain and make that area difficult terrain until the start of your next turn.

6Aftershock —

when you Rupture a Fault using 4+ Strain, choose another Fault within 30 feet; it gains 1 Strain.

11Deep Fault —

maximum Strain becomes 8 and a Fault can gain up to 3 Strain per round, unlocking the 8-Strain Cataclysm Rupture tier.

18Domain Expansion: World Beneath the Cracks —

10 CE. At initiative count 20, every hostile creature gains a Fault if it lacks one; every hostile Fault gains 2 Strain. Choose one Fault — it erupts automatically at the Cataclysm tier without consuming Strain (the sure-hit): affected creatures are knocked prone and the ground in 20 ft becomes difficult terrain until the next initiative 20 (no save); each makes a Constitution save or suffers −2 AC until the end of your next turn.

Feats

Technique Feats

Seismograph

You can read the earth's complaints: as an action, take a seismic reading. You learn how many creatures within 60 feet are touching the ground, and whether any Huge or larger creature is moving within 300 feet.

Hairline Crack

When you roll initiative, place one Fault on a visible surface or creature that has already taken damage, no action required.

Cascade Failure

After a creature bearing your Fault drops to 0 HP, one Fault within 30 feet immediately gains 2 Strain.

Rules integration

Table Rulings

A target can bear only one Fault from you at a time.
Strain is not damage and cannot be healed away. Ending the Fault removes all its Strain.
Normal walking can feed a Fault, but movement below 10 feet does not trigger Accumulate Strain.
Rupture deals no damage — Strain tiers are control effects.
