Core law

What the technique actually does.

Core law, the captured moment. Cursed energy remembers. When you witness a moment — an attack roll, a position occupied, an effect manifesting — you can photograph it with cursed energy and keep it. A captured moment is not a resource pool or a currency; it is a specific banked event, like a hand of cards. You hold a small number of them (two at first). Developing a moment replays it exactly: the attack fires again, you return to the position, the effect manifests anew. Moments fade if undeveloped after 1 minute.

Field notes. Subject: a photographer, not a fighter. Three truths govern the shutter. First: the camera does not create — it keeps. Every technique on this page is something the world already did; the user merely refused to let it end. Second: timing is the whole technique. A captured sword-swing is worthless in a scabbard and priceless mid-duel — the eye that knows when to press the shutter wins before the developing. Third: the negative is honest. The camera shows what happened, not what anyone wished had happened, and that honesty is a weapon.

Progression

Levels 1–18.

1First Exposure

Reaction · 1 CE · When a creature within 60 feet makes an attack roll, capture the attack — the roll and its damage are stored as a moment. Bonus action · 1 CE · Capture your current position as a moment. You can hold up to 2 captured moments at a time; undeveloped moments fade after 1 minute.

The shutter clicks and the world holds still. Not metaphorically — the cursed energy of that instant is bottled, labeled, and shelved.

Action · Develop: replay one captured moment. A captured attack fires again using the original attack roll, against a target within 60 feet of you. A captured position returns you to it instantly (no opportunity attacks). The moment is consumed.

Developing is the payoff. Their sword-swing, fired back at them. Your position three seconds ago, reclaimed in an instant. The past, weaponized.

3Exposure

Bonus action · 1 CE · Your lens sees truth for 1 minute: you perceive through illusions, invisibility, and shapechanging within 60 feet, and you automatically notice hidden doors, traps, and disguised creatures. The camera does not lie, and for one minute neither does the world.

The technique's second sense has nothing to do with capturing. It is the photographer's eye — trained on what is actually there, not what something wishes you would see.

3Flash

Action · 2 CE · Overexpose a moment: creatures within 30 feet make a Constitution save or are blinded until the end of your next turn. If you develop a captured attack on the same turn, the developed attack has advantage. Light, weaponized — the flash blinds them and the developed strike lands in the dark.

Every photographer knows the flash. Yours just happens to be made of cursed energy and bad intentions.

6Contact Sheet

Action · 2 CE · Develop two captured moments with a single action instead of one. The darkroom works faster now — two prints from one session, both fully developed.

This is where the technique becomes a system. One developed moment is a trick. Two, in the same breath, is a sequence — and sequences are where fights are won.

Passive · Your captured moments increase to 4, and your capture range extends to 90 feet. You can now capture a creature's spell or technique as it is cast (reaction · 1 CE · capture the effect; developing it replays the effect with the original roll or save DC).

The foundation levels up with you. More shelf space, longer lens, and now the camera eats techniques — not just sword-swings. Their fireball, filed away for later.

11Decisive Moment

Once per short rest · When a creature within 60 feet drops to 0 hit points, or when a Domain Expansion or equivalent major effect resolves within 60 feet, you may capture THAT moment as a bonus action (no CE cost). Developing a captured death replays the killing blow (original roll) against any target in range. Developing a captured pre-death moment instead returns the fallen creature to 1 hit point — you develop the instant before the fall, not the fall itself.

The decisive moment is the photographer's holy grail — the one frame that changes everything. Once per short rest the universe holds still at the worst possible instant, and you get to decide what that instant means.

18The Darkroom

You have reached the peak expression of the technique: the Domain below. While your Domain is active, developing a captured moment is a bonus action instead of an action, and your captured moments do not fade.

The photographer stops worrying about shelf space. Inside the darkroom, time is just another chemical — and every print comes out perfect.

Pinnacle expression

Maximum, Domain, or equivalent.

The red safelight clicks on. The world goes dark around the edges, and the air smells of developer fluid and stopped time. Negatives hang from every surface — every attack thrown, every step taken, every spell cast inside, dangling in long wet strips. This is where moments go to wait.

Domain Expansion, The Darkroom (action, 20 CE, 1 minute): every moment inside the Domain is captured automatically — every attack roll, every position change, every technique or spell cast. You hold an unlimited number of captured moments while the Domain lasts (they fade when it ends). Developing is a bonus action. Additionally, once per round you may develop a moment you did not personally capture — anything that happened inside the Darkroom is on your contact sheet, whether you pressed the shutter or not.

Feats

Build-defining options.

Double Exposure

Action · 2 CE · Overlay two captured moments and develop them as one: replay a captured attack originating from a captured position, or combine two captured attacks into a single strike using the higher of the two rolls. Two negatives, one print — the images bleed together into something neither was alone.

Spirit Photograph

Action · 2 CE · Capture a willing creature's memory — or the last sight of a corpse dead less than 1 hour — and project it as a visible image for 1 minute. The camera as witness: what they saw, shown. Courts, interrogations, and crime scenes all answer to the shutter eventually.

Tripwire Shutter

Bonus action · 1 CE · Set a captured moment to develop automatically when a trigger condition you name occurs ("when a hostile enters this doorway," "when I drop below half health"). The trap waits patiently — photography as an ambush, the shutter pressed by someone else's footstep.

Negative

Reaction · 2 CE · When you take damage, capture the wound as a moment — then develop its negative: instead of replaying the damage, you heal it. The inverse print. The camera that took the picture of the injury develops the picture of it never happening.
