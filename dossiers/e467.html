Exact player-facing procedures

Drinking edge

Passive · No CE · +2 to attack and damage; on a critical hit the target loses 1d4 cursed energy (absorbed by the sword; stores up to 6).

The blade's manners are steady, +2 to hit and damage on every swing, and its appetite wakes on the critical hit: the target loses 1d4 cursed energy, absorbed into a store that holds at most six. You gain a +2 bonus to attack and damage rolls with this weapon. On a critical hit, the target loses 1d4 cursed energy, which is absorbed by the sword. The sword can store up to 6 cursed energy.

Spent focus

Free (once per turn) · No CE · Spend up to 3 stored energy: gain an equal bonus to one attack roll with this weapon.

The purse opens once per turn: spend up to 3 stored energy for an equal bonus to an attack roll with the sword. Stolen force, returned as certainty, but never as fuel for your own techniques. Once per turn, you can spend up to 3 cursed energy stored in the sword to gain a bonus to an attack roll you make with this weapon equal to the cursed energy spent.

Field notes. Subject: a longsword that drinks what it spills and spends what it drinks. Three truths. First: the blade is a toll-collector, on a critical hit, the target loses 1d4 cursed energy, and the sword keeps it, up to a purse of six. The wound is taxed, not just dealt. Second: the purse is the sword's own, the stored energy cannot be drawn into your reserve or spent on techniques; it exists only to sharpen the blade that gathered it. Third: the spending is measured, once per turn, up to 3 stored energy may be poured into an attack roll as an equal bonus. Consider the loop: the harder the crits, the fuller the purse, the surer the next strikes. Seki is a miser with one virtue, it pays its master back, in accuracy, for every drop it stole.
