Seki

A Grade 1 cursed longsword that feeds on the energy it spills.

Features & procedures

Properties & techniques

Exact player-facing procedures

Drinking edge

You gain a +2 bonus to attack and damage rolls with this weapon. On a critical hit, the target loses 1d4 cursed energy, which is absorbed by the sword. The sword can store up to 6 cursed energy.

Spent focus

Once per turn, you can spend up to 3 cursed energy stored in the sword to gain a bonus to an attack roll you make with this weapon equal to the cursed energy spent.

Rules integration

Edge cases and shared systems.

Campaign canon notes

Note: absorbed CE is the sword's own fuel — it cannot be drawn into your reserve or spent on techniques. Stored energy persists until spent; the sword cannot absorb past 6.
