How this shelf works

Three kings, three arts

Each manuscript was authored by one of the four kings — and each teaches a way of using cursed energy that no technique contains. They are studied, not chosen: thirty days with the text, an examination of understanding, and the application becomes part of your jujutsu permanently.

Not techniques: manuscripts do not count against the two-technique limit and have no level-by-level progression. Each teaches one core application plus advanced uses.
Study: each manuscript lists its requirements — a CE Manipulation tier, a character level, thirty days of downtime, and an INT check vs DC 15. The builder tracks what you have studied; the downtime is an honor rule at the table.
The marginalia: Sun Tzu authored none of these texts. His notes appear in the margins of every copy — corrections, cold assessments, the occasional grudging respect. Every student of these arts studies inside Sun's annotations. He does not need to write the books to own the classroom.
