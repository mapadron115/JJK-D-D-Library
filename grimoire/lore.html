Office of the Principal · Tokyo Prefecture

To whom it may concern,

You may be confused as to how this letter may have found you with no address, name, or other method of identification. You may also be confused as to why you now possess supernatural, unexplained, or otherwise unwanted abilities. The answers to both those questions are a lot more complicated and will require more than this letter. It is precisely for this reason that you are being recruited and enrolled into the Tokyo Metropolitan Curse Technical College. You did not make a mistake. That word really says curse. In this world curses are very real and are manifestations of human negative energy given form. The abilities you may have noticed you now possess come from that Cursed Energy and are manifestations of innate abilities you possess. Here at the Tokyo Jujutsu High, we will teach you to control this Cursed Energy and master it. You will work alongside brilliant staff and an array of new students until your abilities are safe for yourself and those around you. During your time here you would normally also be expected to learn how to exorcise curses, but due to a sudden increase in awakening events for sorcerers, we now offer a curriculum based purely around control and not combat. With that we would like to request the following:

  1. Expect a driver showing the same emblem on this letter to pick you up in exactly ten days. Take this time to say goodbye to your loved ones and seek closure on any pending arrangements. We would like to make it known that while you can choose to not join our school, you will become an active danger to those around you unless you manage to master your newfound abilities. We have already sent notice to your parents and informed them of our prestigious facility, should you need to reject our invitation, please let them know so that they can contact our principal directly.
  2. Pack heavy, you will be studying at Jujutsu High year round, you will be given a calendar with holidays during your first week, but for now assume the campus will be your new home.
  3. Please be sure to read up on the Memorandum on the duty of jujutsu sorcerers attached below, do not worry if you cannot understand it yet, just commit it to memory.

We wish you the best,

— Masamichi Yaga

Principal of Tokyo Metropolitan Curse Technical College

Memorandum on the duty of jujutsu sorcerers

The Zenin family, the Gojo family and the Kamo family agreed with the Japanese Kokufu as follows on behalf of all the sorcerers, and established this memorandum.

1  (Mission)

1. The sorcerer's mission is to prevent calamity brought by jujutsu, curse spirits and cursed objects to mankind and to maintain the peace and security of society.

2. All sorcerers must follow this memorandum. The rules stipulated in this Memorandum shall be applied in addition to laws and regulations.

2  (Jujutsu Headquarters)

1. As the highest institution that controls sorcerers, the head is the Director of Jujutsu.

2. The Director of Jujutsu is appointed by the Prime Minister based on the nomination of the Zenin family and the Gojo family and the Kamo family.

3  (Jujutsu Technical College)

1. As institutions for the education, guidance and supervision of jujutsu sorcerers, Jujutsu Technical Colleges will be established in the Tokyo and Kyoto Prefectures.

2. As the head of the Jujutsu Technical College, the principal is in charge of the affairs related to the training of sorcerers, responding to threats, and oversees all sorcerers in command and supervision.

3. The principal is appointed by the Director of Jujutsu based on the recommendation of the Zenin family, the Gojo family and the Kamo family.

4. The Director of Jujutsu can command and supervise the principal.

4  (Grade of Jujutsu Sorcerers)

1. Sorcerers are assigned grades of first grade, semi-first grade, second grade, semi-second grade, third grade and fourth grade. On a mission, a higher level sorcerer leads a lower level sorcerer.

2. The Director of Jujutsu recognises or changes the grade of each sorcerer according to appropriate criteria.

3. The sorcerer can receive salary and allowance conforming to their grade in accordance with the rules stipulated by the Director of Jujutsu.

5  (Punishment)

The Director of Jujutsu may impose one or more of the following punishments on any sorcerer who does not obey a command or violates the obligations of this memorandum.

  • Public execution
  • Secret execution
  • Recognition as a curse user
  • Sealing
  • Imprisonment
  • Disciplinary confinement
  • Confiscation of property

6  (Retirement)

1. A sorcerer can retire with the approval of the Director of Jujutsu. A retired sorcerer is gradeless.

2. A retired sorcerer may not use any jujutsu. However, this does not apply to cases where it is necessary to fulfill the obligations of the next article.

7  (Responding to threats)

1. Cursed spirits, cursed objects and curse users are all threats.

2. If a sorcerer discovers a threat, they must immediately report it to the principal. The principal will assess the threat grade based on the report, notify the Director of Jujutsu, and organize and dispatch the necessary personnel and equipment to suppress it.

3. Regardless of the preceding paragraph, if the threat is imminent the sorcerer can take considerable measures to suppress it.

8  (Secret)

A sorcerer must not reveal the existence of jujutsu, cursed spirits or cursed objects to a non-sorcerer. However, this does not apply when it is necessary to fulfill the obligations of the preceding article.

9  (On the safety of non-sorcerers)

Sorcerers are not to harm non-sorcerers via Jujutsu, cursed spirits, or cursed objects. They are also not to overlook any potential threat to the non-sorcerer. However, this does not apply in the case of one's life or others in danger.

What You Know — The School — what every new student is told

❖ Tokyo Jujutsu High

Formally the Tokyo Metropolitan Curse Technical College — the Tokyo Prefecture jujutsu technical college, where you now study. One of only two such schools in Japan, the other standing in Kyoto.

The campus is your new home: you study here year round, and your first week's calendar will lay out the holidays.

❖ Principal Masamichi Yaga

The man who signed your letter. The principal is in charge of the training of sorcerers, of responding to threats, and of the command and supervision of every sorcerer at the college.

❖ A Control Curriculum

You were not admitted to learn how to fight curses — at least, not yet.

"due to a sudden increase in awakening events for sorcerers, we now offer a curriculum based purely around control and not combat."

Normally new sorcerers also learn to exorcise curses. For your intake, the work is control: mastering your Cursed Energy until your abilities are safe for yourself and those around you.

❖ What Cursed Energy Is

As the letter puts it: in this world curses are very real, and they are manifestations of human negative energy given form. The abilities you noticed awakening in you come from that Cursed Energy — they are manifestations of innate abilities you possess.

❖ Fellow New Students

You are not the only one who received a letter. You will train alongside an array of new students — other newly awakened sorcerers, all learning to control what they cannot yet master.

❖ The Driver & The Trunk

A driver bearing the same 呪 emblem on your letter came for you ten days after it arrived. Before that, you were asked to say goodbye to your loved ones, settle your pending arrangements, and pack heavy — the campus is your home now.

The Memorandum, Plainly — rules every new student must live by

The memorandum attached to your letter is dense. This is what it asks of you, in plain words — drawn only from the text you received.

❖ Plain-language summary ❖
  1. Your mission: prevent the calamity that jujutsu, cursed spirits, and cursed objects bring to mankind, and maintain the peace and security of society. The memorandum's rules apply in addition to ordinary laws.
  2. Your grades: sorcerers are graded from fourth grade up through third, semi-second, second, semi-first, and first grade. On a mission, the higher-grade sorcerer leads. Grades are granted or changed by the Director of Jujutsu, and salary follows grade.
  3. Your chain of command: the highest authority over sorcerers is the Director of Jujutsu — appointed by the Prime Minister on the nomination of the Zenin, Gojo, and Kamo families. The Director can command and supervise even your principal.
  4. If you see a threat: cursed spirits, cursed objects, and curse users are all threats. If you discover one, report it to the principal immediately — the threat is graded, the Director is notified, and personnel are dispatched to suppress it. If the threat is imminent, you may take considerable measures yourself.
  5. Keep the secret: never reveal the existence of jujutsu, cursed spirits, or cursed objects to a non-sorcerer — unless secrecy would stand in the way of responding to a threat.
  6. Never harm the innocent: sorcerers are not to harm non-sorcerers by jujutsu, cursed spirits, or cursed objects — and they are not to overlook any potential threat to a non-sorcerer. The only exception is when your life, or the lives of others, are in danger.
