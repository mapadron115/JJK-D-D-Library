Shared campaign language

Technique Die. Each technique has a named Technique Die: d6 at level 1, d8 at level 6, d10 at level 11, and d12 at level 18 unless a feature says otherwise.
Attack & save. Technique save DC = 8 + proficiency bonus + your Cursed Energy ability modifier. Technique attack bonus = proficiency bonus + your Cursed Energy ability modifier.
Technique Focus. Effects marked Focus use the campaign’s shared Technique Focus. Opening a Domain ends another Focus effect unless the Domain explicitly preserves it.
Domains. Unless an entry is explicitly a rules-based Domain, its guaranteed hostile effect occurs at initiative count 20. Ordinary attacks and separately activated abilities still roll or allow saves normally.
Maximum Output. Generic Maximum Output may enhance eligible ordinary technique features under the campaign rules. Named Maximum Techniques and Domains do not receive generic Maximum Output unless they explicitly say otherwise.
Character creation. These frameworks assume the campaign’s normal class CE reserve, actions, Black Flash procedure, injury rules, RCT, and Domain clash system. A technique feature never grants a second class turn or duplicated concentration unless it explicitly says so.

Bestiary Technique #01

Red-Eyed Omen

See disasters before they fully exist, stockpile Omens, and spend those warnings to move allies, spoil enemy timing, or make a foretold opening unavoidable.

InspirationMothman
RoleDivination / Reaction Support
TierTier III · Apex
Core statWIS or CHA
Scaling dieOmen Die

Character fantasy

See disasters before they fully exist, stockpile Omens, and spend those warnings to move allies, spoil enemy timing, or make a foretold opening unavoidable.

Combat loop

  1. Mark a dangerous creature or situation with Omen Sight.
  2. Gain Omen when the marked threat meaningfully advances a danger you predicted.
  3. Spend Omen on warnings, pre-emptive movement, defensive corrections, and predicted openings.
  4. Escalate major threats through the Disaster Clock until you understand exactly what is about to go wrong.

Omens & the Disaster Clock

  • Omen cap: proficiency bonus. You begin combat with 1 Omen if you are not surprised. Omens vanish on a short or long rest.
  • Omen Sight: bonus action, 1 CE, 60 feet. Mark one creature, unstable object, barrier, or obvious environmental danger for 1 minute. You may maintain one mark; level 11 raises this to three.
  • Gain Omen: once per round when a marked threat hits with an attack, forces a saving throw, spends 3+ CE on one activation, causes a creature to fall prone/restrained/unconscious, or materially worsens a marked environmental hazard, gain 1 Omen.
  • Disaster Clock: one marked creature may carry a 0–3 Clock. The first time each round it uses an action, bonus action, reaction, or movement to directly advance the same dangerous objective, add 1 tick. At 3 ticks you may ask the GM which one of the following it is currently best positioned to do before the end of its next turn: attack, move/reposition, defend, or activate a technique. The answer is truthful but does not force its behavior.

Building the character

Best on Support Specialist, Strategist, or a mobile Scout. Prioritize your CE stat and reaction economy. The technique is strongest in parties that can exploit movement and information rather than simply asking you to deal damage.

Technique die: Omen Die follows the shared d6 → d8 → d10 → d12 progression.

Counterplay

Blind or isolate the user, force multiple unrelated threats at once, or stop marked creatures from telegraphing the same objective. Omen generation is capped per round and prediction does not mind-control anyone.

Bestiary Technique #02

Braxton Veil

Flood the battlefield with a nauseating, electrically wrong atmosphere whose modes reshape sight, weight, cursed sensing, and stamina.

InspirationFlatwoods Monster
RoleArea Controller / Debuffer
TierTier III · Apex
Core statWIS or CHA
Scaling dieVeil Die

Character fantasy

Flood the battlefield with a nauseating, electrically wrong atmosphere whose modes reshape sight, weight, cursed sensing, and stamina.

Combat loop

  1. Create your Veil and choose its environmental mode.
  2. Keep enemies inside long enough to build Exposure.
  3. Switch modes to attack the weakness their current Exposure creates.
  4. Float through your own atmosphere while enemies struggle to perceive or approach you.

Veil Modes & Exposure

  • Veil: bonus action, 2 CE, Focus, 1 minute. A 15-foot aura centered on you moves with you. At level 6 it becomes 20 feet; level 11, 30 feet.
  • Exposure: the first time each turn a hostile creature enters the Veil or starts its turn there, it makes a Constitution save. Failure adds 1 Exposure, maximum 4. Leaving the Veil for a full round removes 1 Exposure; a short rest removes all.
  • Exposure thresholds: 1 = speed −10 feet inside the Veil; 2 = subtract 1d4 from Perception and Technique Focus/Concentration checks; 3 = cannot take reactions while inside; 4 = poisoned while inside and cannot benefit from advantage on attacks against you.
  • Mode: choose Sulfur, Crimson, Gravitic, or Static when activated and switch as a bonus action for 1 CE.

Building the character

Excellent for Strategists and Support Specialists who want a moving hazard rather than spell-like burst damage. Constitution is useful because you want to remain near enemies, but your CE stat drives the important saves.

Technique die: Veil Die follows the shared d6 → d8 → d10 → d12 progression.

Counterplay

Leave the aura, force the user away from the fight, or use long-range attacks. Exposure is dangerous only if the user can keep pressure on the same targets.

Bestiary Technique #03

Leeds Infernal

Build Hunt Momentum through aggressive movement, then cash it into dive-bombs, horn launches, pursuit reactions, and impossible aerial combinations.

InspirationJersey Devil
RoleAerial Striker / Pursuer
TierTier III · Apex
Core statSTR, DEX, or CE stat
Scaling dieHunt Die

Character fantasy

Build Hunt Momentum through aggressive movement, then cash it into dive-bombs, horn launches, pursuit reactions, and impossible aerial combinations.

Combat loop

  1. Move in a meaningful new way to gain Momentum.
  2. Mark prey so fleeing enemies become fuel instead of escaping.
  3. Spend Momentum on attacks that launch, frighten, or reposition.
  4. Keep moving—ending turns planted in one place lets the hunt cool down.

Hunt Momentum

  • Momentum cap: PB. Gain 1 Momentum once per turn when you move at least 20 feet before hitting a creature, descend at least 10 feet, leap a gap, Dash, or willingly pass within 5 feet of a hostile without stopping. The same movement event grants only 1.
  • Cooling: if you end your turn having moved less than 10 feet, lose all Momentum.
  • Cryptid Body: your unarmed strikes may deal bludgeoning, piercing, or slashing and can use Strength or Dexterity. Wings, hooves, horns, and elongated limbs are visible manifestations, not separate creatures.

Building the character

Warrior and Scout shells both work. A Strength build plays like a flying bruiser; Dexterity plays like a skirmisher. The technique deliberately rewards terrain, height, and aggressive movement over standing still and taking the Attack action every round.

Technique die: Hunt Die follows the shared d6 → d8 → d10 → d12 progression.

Counterplay

Difficult terrain, immobilization, cramped rooms, readied attacks, and forced stationary objectives all attack the Momentum engine directly.

Bestiary Technique #04

May We Come In?

Power grows only through genuine permission. Offer useful bargains, collect Invitations, then turn hospitality and personal thresholds into supernatural access.

InspirationBlack-Eyed Children
RoleSocial Controller / Curse Specialist
TierTier IV · Special Grade
Core statCHA or WIS
Scaling dieInvitation Die

Character fantasy

Power grows only through genuine permission. Offer useful bargains, collect Invitations, then turn hospitality and personal thresholds into supernatural access.

Combat loop

  1. Offer a target something genuinely useful.
  2. If they voluntarily accept, record an Invitation.
  3. Use Invitations for tracking, access, curses, and threshold-breaking.
  4. Engineer situations where accepting your help is tempting without ever forcing consent.

Invitations & Thresholds

  • Offer: once per turn as a bonus action, spend 1 CE and make a concrete offer to one creature within 30 feet: 10 feet of movement without provoking, Help on one check/attack before your next turn, PB temporary HP, or allowing it to end prone for free. If it knowingly and voluntarily accepts the benefit, it gains 1 Invitation, maximum 3. Charm, domination, threats of immediate technique harm, or mind control cannot create Invitations.
  • Duration: Invitations last until the next long rest. At level 11 they last 7 days.
  • Threshold: a home, room, vehicle, barrier, camp, ward, or space that a creature meaningfully controls can count as its Threshold. Domains are not automatically bypassed by this feature.

Building the character

Charisma-heavy Support Specialist or Strategist is natural, but a martial character can weaponize the “help first, curse later” loop. This technique is intentionally strongest when the player is socially clever rather than when the GM makes NPCs foolish.

Technique die: Invitation Die follows the shared d6 → d8 → d10 → d12 progression.

Counterplay

Refuse offers, communicate the danger, isolate the user from controlled spaces, and avoid granting explicit access. Forced or manipulated consent never counts.

Long Step Procession

Turn distance itself into a resource: long, uncanny strides create Steps that can evade attacks, bridge terrain, or leave walking echoes behind.

InspirationFresno Nightcrawlers
RoleEvasion / Positioning Controller
TierTier II · Versatile
Core statDEX or CE stat
Scaling dieStep Die

Character fantasy

Turn distance itself into a resource: long, uncanny strides create Steps that can evade attacks, bridge terrain, or leave walking echoes behind.

Combat loop

  1. Keep moving to accumulate Steps.
  2. Spend Steps to ignore terrain, dodge through attack windows, or reposition allies.
  3. Leave Procession paths and movement Echoes behind.
  4. Use prior positions as a second battlefield only you fully understand.

Long Steps

  • Step cap: PB. The first time each turn you move 10 feet without being forcibly moved, gain 1 Step for every full 10 feet moved, up to 2 Steps per turn. Steps vanish if you end two consecutive turns without moving at least 10 feet.
  • Long-Legged Body: your reach for unarmed strikes is 10 feet while you have at least 1 Step. Your appearance may become a pale torso balanced on absurdly long legs.

Scout is the obvious shell, but any character who wants mobility support can use it. Dexterity matters if you fight through Stilt Kick; a pure controller can invest almost entirely in CE stat.

Restrict movement, flood the area with persistent hazards, destroy lines of sight to Echoes, or force the user to hold a fixed objective.

Bestiary Technique #06

One Too Many

Make a group lose certainty about its own membership. Headcounts become cursed facts, and every contradiction gives you Uncertainty to sabotage teamwork.

InspirationGoatman / campfire impostor folklore
RoleInfiltration / Coordination Disruption
TierTier III · Apex
Core statCHA or WIS
Scaling dieDoubt Die

Character fantasy

Make a group lose certainty about its own membership. Headcounts become cursed facts, and every contradiction gives you Uncertainty to sabotage teamwork.

Combat loop

  1. Establish a Headcount on a group.
  2. Create contradictions while appearing to belong.
  3. Gain Uncertainty when members waste effort verifying or coordinating around false assumptions.
  4. Spend Uncertainty to break Help, reactions, formation bonuses, and confident targeting.

Headcount & Uncertainty

  • Headcount: action, 2 CE, choose up to PB creatures you can see within 60 feet that regard themselves as one group. For 10 minutes you know whenever one enters/leaves 60 feet and the technique records the original count.
  • False Member: while Headcount is active you can make your appearance, voice, scent, and superficial CE signature read as “one member of the group” without copying a specific individual. A creature inspecting you may make Insight against your CE save DC.
  • Uncertainty cap: PB + 2. Once per creature per round, gain 1 Uncertainty when a Headcount member spends an action/bonus action to verify identity, uses Help on the wrong creature/decoy, misses an opportunity because it mistook you for an ally, or publicly contradicts another member about the group’s count or position.

Split the party deliberately, use predetermined passwords, rely on area effects instead of coordination, and spend actions to identify the impostor early.

Empty Herd

Character fantasy

Open Feeding Wounds, drain cursed energy or vitality through them, and turn Satiety into healing, speed, and increasingly predatory attacks.

Combat loop
