Bestiary Codex · Set One

Ten cryptid-inspired and ten mythology-inspired JJK 5e techniques. Each uses the shared Technique DC, 1 / 3 / 6 / 11 / 18 cadence, four dedicated feats, and initiative-count-20 Domain convention.

Mothman · Tier III

Red-Eyed Omen

Read structural disaster, generate Omens and a Disaster Clock, and turn forewarning into reaction support.

Flatwoods Monster · Tier III

Braxton Veil

Move an alien Veil through the map, switch its modes, and accumulate Exposure while seeing through your own haze.

Jersey Devil · Tier III

Leeds Infernal

Build Hunt Momentum through aerial pursuit, vertical movement, raking passes, and hellish charge pressure.

Black-Eyed Children · Tier IV

May We Come In?

Offer genuinely voluntary Invitations through thresholds or live communication; accepting the bargain gives the technique its teeth.

Fresno Nightcrawlers · Tier III

Long Step Procession

Accumulate Steps through elongated movement and spend them on evasion, strange positioning, and position-only rewind.

Goatman · Tier III

One Too Many

Corrupt group Headcount and accumulate Uncertainty whenever people fail to identify who actually belongs.

Chupacabra · Tier III

Empty Herd

Track wounded prey, create Feeding Wounds, and build Satiety by successfully draining rather than regenerating for free.

Mongolian Death Worm · Tier III

Red Sand Below

Build a Tremor Path network through natural ground and erupt from prepared routes with venomous ambushes.

Bigfoot · Tier III

Proof Without Capture

Evidence of your existence becomes a resource: investigators get closer to proof while you become harder to conclusively document.

Hopkinsville Goblins · Tier III

Visitors at the Window

One coordinated Visitor Swarm turns buildings into siege maps of windows, roofs, thrown objects, and relentless harassment.

Medusa / Gorgon · Tier IV

Gorgon’s Regard

Enemies choose to Avert or Look; meeting your gaze stacks Calcification toward petrification.

Lernaean Hydra · Tier IV

Death Makes Two

Destroyed heads multiply unless neck wounds are properly cauterized, turning enemy damage into the technique's growth engine.

Ammit · Tier IV

Weight of the Heart

Voluntary Temptations create Heart Weight; the technique tracks bargains and consequences, not moral alignment.

Fenrir · Tier V

Gleipnir Breaker

Accept restrictive Bonds that hold back your power, then break them deliberately to climb through Ragnarök states.

Jörmungandr · Tier V

World Coil

Lay Coil Segments around the battlefield, close them into Rings, and impose distinct laws inside completed circles.

Rakshasa · Tier III

Backward-Handed Masquerade

Record mundane Masks—faces, voices, habits and skills—without ever copying supernatural powers.

Nāga · Tier IV

Seven Sacred Coils

Allocate Coils between Ward and Constrict functions, deciding whether serpent power protects or binds.

Kitsune · Tier IV

Nine Tails, Nine Lies

Earn Tails only when deception makes an enemy waste a concrete resource, then spend them on stronger fox tricks.

Gashadokuro · Tier V

Starvation Colossus

Build Hunger from nearby injury/death and spend it to assemble gigantic skeletal body parts.

Simurgh · Tier IV

Feathers of Remembered Wisdom

Record ally successes as Memory Feathers and replay those lessons as foresight, rescue, and support.
