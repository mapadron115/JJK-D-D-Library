Chimera Shadow: Wildebeast of Hell’s Savannah — Zagroth

Predator Core. A high-pressure hunting chassis intended to stay recognizably one base shikigami rather than becoming an indiscriminate pile of every roster ability.
Oxbreaker Charge. Borrows Piercing Ox's commitment to a straight-line approach and converts distance into impact.
Tiger Phase. Adds Mourn Tiger's close-range predatory identity.
Serpent Lash. Adds Great Serpent reach/control to keep prey from simply disengaging.
Hell Savannah Pounce. The preset's combined finisher, tying charge, predation, and reach into one decisive sequence.

Chimera Shadow: The Last Standing Guardian of the Forest — Hageshi

Guardian Frame. Defensive base identity built to hold space and protect rather than race Zagroth for raw damage.
Round Deer Core. Positive-energy/restorative identity anchors the chimera.
Rabbit Redistribution. Adds diffusion and protection utility rather than independent extra turns.
Elephant Bulwark. Adds Max Elephant's mass and refusal to be displaced.
Horn Slam / Last Standing Wall. Gives Hageshi enough physical presence to punish enemies that try to simply walk through its protection.

Both presets obey the same custom Chimerafication rule: one base + up to three additions, one trait/ability and one-quarter HP from each addition.
