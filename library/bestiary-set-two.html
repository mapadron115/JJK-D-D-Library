Bestiary Codex · Set Two

Twenty mythology-focused techniques. The canon edition expands their Domains into complete player-facing procedures with action, CE cost, radius, duration, Focus, initiative-20 behavior, anti-domain fallback, and counterplay.

Typhon · Tier V Calamity

Stormfather Catastrophe

Build Cataclysm by alternating destructive phenomena; spend it to layer fire, lightning, thunder, poison, and forced movement. Maximum: Siege of Heaven.

Hecatoncheires · Tier IV

Hundred-Handed Arsenal

Allocate a Hand Pool equal to PB + CE modifier between offense, defense, grappling, and utility. Maximum: Three Hundred Stones.

Fafnir · Tier IV

Hoard-Heart Dragon

Mark valuables as your Hoard; proximity generates Greed and enemies can steal or relocate the objects to weaken you. Domain: Hall Beneath the Glittering Heath.

Draugr · Tier III

Grave-Mound Tenacity

Choose a Grave Anchor. Intrusion, escape, and attacks on the anchor generate Restlessness for tanking and retaliation.

Valkyrie · Tier IV

Chooser of the Slain

Record ally Deeds from crits, survival, rescues, and takedowns; spend their remembered heroism to protect or empower them.

Yuki-onna · Tier IV

White Silence

Stack Frost: slow → no reactions → Frostbound. Heat, shelter, and proximity remain real counterplay.

Oni · Tier IV

Hundred-Demon Revel

Switch visible Red, Blue, and Black Masks for force, endurance, or terror. Maximum Ō-Oni overlaps the identities without erasing stance choices.

Tsuchigumo · Tier III

Earth-Spider Loom

Place visible Anchor Threads between surfaces; triangles become traps, pulls, restraints, and geometry-based control.

Nue · Tier IV

Chimeric Night Cry

Choose Monkey, Tiger, Serpent, or Tanuki each turn and avoid repeating the same Aspect three turns consecutively.

Garuda · Tier IV

Sun-Eater Wings

Generate Pinions through flight and liberation from poison/restraint, then spend them on rescue movement and radiant strikes.

Apep / Apophis · Tier V

Serpent Against the Dawn

Track Night Depth 0–3. Extinguishing light and necrotic pressure deepen Night; radiant effects reduce it. Domain: Twelve Gates Before Sunrise.

Sekhmet · Tier IV

Red Lioness Fever

Consecutive damaging turns build Heat 0–5. Heat grants speed and damage but high Heat burns the user too.

Dullahan · Tier IV

Headless Road

Manifest a black steed and spectral Head, mark a Road's End, and build Gallop by closing distance.

Banshee · Tier IV

Keening Before Death

Death Notes create Laments tied to endangered creatures; spend those omens on warning, survival, and stronger wails.

Nuckelavee · Tier IV

Skinless Blight

Create Blight patches that add Wither and suppress healing. Fresh running water is a hard Binding-Vow weakness.

Camazotz · Tier IV

House of Bats

Gain Echoes from darkness, hiding, and ceiling/floor transitions; spend them on echolocation, swoops, and bounded injury pressure.

Feathered Serpent · Tier IV

Wind Between Heaven and Earth

Draw directional Wind Roads; allies ride flow, enemies pay extra movement against it, and intersections become Crossroads.

Lamassu · Tier IV

Gate of the Protective Colossus

Designate Thresholds. Hostile crossings generate Vigilance for interception, ally relocation, and guardian pressure.

Humbaba · Tier IV

Seven Terrors of the Cedar Forest

Manifest one of seven Terror Faces at a time; Cedar zones carry the selected fear-law through the battlefield.

Manticore · Tier III

Man-Eater's Ballistics

Manage a finite Tail Magazine: volley specialized quills, close with Lion's Maw to recover ammunition, then disengage.
