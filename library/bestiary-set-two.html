Shared Bestiary Rules

Technique save DC: 8 + PB + chosen CE ability modifier (usually Wisdom or Charisma). Technique attack: PB + that modifier. Scaling dice are d6 at levels 1–5, d8 at 6–10, d10 at 11–17, and d12 at 18+. Domains use autonomous initiative-count-20 laws; they do not turn ordinary attacks into automatic hits. Maximums and named pinnacle effects do not receive generic Maximum Output stacking unless a feature explicitly says otherwise. Complete Domain baseline: unless an individual entry explicitly changes it, activation is a full-turn action, cost 20 CE, enclosed 60-foot radius centered on the user, up to 1 minute, and it occupies Technique Focus. A Domain-specific larger radius is intentional and remains printed. When a lethal sure-hit would normally call for a save, unprotected targets receive its failed-save result automatically and anti-domain protection restores the named fallback save. Rule-based or beneficial Domains disclose their own procedure and need not deal damage.
