Authority order

Newest explicit technique revision → Canon Comprehensive / Canon Advanced Sorcery → unaltered Project V3 → ordinary 5e. A technique may break a shared rule when it clearly identifies the exception. Vague legacy boilerplate does not.

Fix 01 · Domains

One guaranteed event, not an auto-hit character sheet.

Standard completed Domain: full-turn action, 20 CE, enclosed 60-foot radius, up to 1 minute, and Technique Focus. Lethal Domains resolve one named autonomous sure-hit event at initiative 20. Ordinary attacks and saves remain ordinary. Rule-Domains enforce their disclosed procedure instead. Explicitly larger, smaller, early, open, or Binding-Vow Domains keep their written exception.

Fix 02 · Burnout

Technique lock, not exhaustion.

When a Domain ends, current Technique Burnout applies for the character's refinement-tier duration. It does not automatically create exhaustion and Black Flash does not automatically clear it. RCT Burnout Recovery follows the Canon Advanced Sorcery procedure.

Fix 03 · RCT

A finite tissue reserve.

Healing Reserve = 10 × total character level. Long-rest recovery only; no passive two-round pulse. Once per round while conscious, self-heal by bonus action or qualifying reaction at 10 HP per CE, limited by missing HP and remaining Reserve. Old Charisma-multiplied pools and short-rest full refills are superseded.

Fix 04 · Black Flash

The original miracle remains.

Preserve Project V3: land a critical hit, roll a second unmodified d20, natural 20 confirms, then deal 3 × the completed critical-hit damage. It bypasses resistance, not immunity. Every qualifying critical may attempt it. The Zone is bounded to two stages and never refills Healing Reserve or automatically clears Burnout.

Fix 05 · Progression

Major slots versus internal unlocks.

Standard Innate Technique feature slots occur at class levels 1, 3, 6, 11, and 18. A completed framework can print automatic internal evolutions at other levels without consuming another class feature slot. Multiclass standard characters use the highest individual level in a class that grants the technique.

Fix 06 · Dual Technique Variant

Two techniques, one character economy.

A GM-approved Dual Technique character advances both techniques by total character level. Both share one CE Reserve, one CE ability, one action/bonus/reaction economy, one Technique Focus slot, and the normal Domain/Burnout limits. The variant grants breadth, not a duplicate resource chassis.

Fix 07 · Summons

No accidental army multiplication.

A combat manifestation requires a complete stat block or identified published stat block. Unless autonomous, it follows its technique's command procedure and acts immediately after its creator. Pairs, swarms, and grouped manifestations use their shared action/reaction procedure. Technique-specific summon rules always override the generic defaults.

Fix 08 · Ten Shadows

The ritual is the gate.

Divine Dogs begin tamed. Every other shadow—including Mahoraga—is challengeable from level 1 if the user can pay the listed ritual cost. Level 3 increases normal manifestation capacity rather than unlocking the roster. Outside aid invalidates taming; Mahoraga remains the full CR 25 creature.

Fix 09 · Bestiary Set Two

All thirteen Domains now have complete procedures.

Every Domain in Mythology Set Two now states action, CE cost, intentional radius, duration, Focus, initiative-20 law, protection fallback, and its counterplay. Beneficial and rule-Domains explicitly identify themselves instead of pretending every Domain must be a damage pulse.

Removed

Witch Hat material is not part of this project.

The supplemental Witch Hat dossier and its collection entry are removed from the active Reviewed Player Edition. It will not be imported into the GitHub master library.

Preserved exceptions

Powerful weird rules stay powerful and weird.

This repair pass is not a universal nerf. Explicit exceptions such as Infinite Retail's early 6-CE non-damaging rule-Domain, unusually large X-tier or Binding-Vow Domains, Mahoraga, extreme Djinn magic, and other intentionally high-ceiling features remain legal. The goal is to eliminate ambiguity and accidental legacy drift, not flatten the library.
