2026 Canon Repair Ledger

The consolidated library uses these rulings whenever older source wording conflicts with the repaired canon.

1 · Domains

Standard complete Domain: full-turn action, 20 CE, 60-foot enclosed radius, up to 1 minute, Focus. Explicit dossier exceptions win. Ordinary attacks roll; the autonomous sure-hit resolves at initiative count 20 unless the Domain is explicitly procedural.

2 · Reverse Cursed Technique

Healing Reserve = 10 × total character level, restored on long rest. The obsolete passive healing pulse and older pool formula are superseded.

3 · Summons

Combat summons need usable statistics. Ordinary manifestations act immediately after their creator; grouped manifestations use shared actions unless their dossier explicitly says otherwise.

4 · Progression

1 / 3 / 6 / 11 / 18 are normal major Innate Technique milestones. Explicit internal unlocks printed at other levels remain valid and do not automatically consume another technique feature.

5 · Dual Technique

Multiclassing does not grant another Innate Technique. An approved Dual Technique character advances both techniques by character level but shares CE, actions, bonus actions, reactions, Focus, Domain limitations, and every other character resource.

6 · Ten Shadows

Every subjugation ritual is challengeable from level 1. The ritual itself is the gate. Mahoraga remains a full pinnacle threat. Shadow Wearing, Wells Unknown Abyss, Totality, and Chimerafication use the standalone Ten Shadows archive.

7 · Bestiary Set Two

The mythology set uses complete player-facing Domain procedures: action, CE cost, area, duration, Focus, initiative-20 law/event, counterplay, and normal anti-domain interactions.

8 · Witch Hat

Removed from the active canon library entirely.
