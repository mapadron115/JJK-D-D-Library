Jujutsu Kaisen 5e · Tier V Transformation Technique

Omnitrix: Codon Archive

Nineteen alien bodies contained in one cursed formula. The first ten are inherited as obedient transformations; every later genome manifests as a hostile biological echo that must be subjugated alone before the wielder can transform into it.

Shared body. Transformations use the same HP, CE, actions, bonus action, reaction, Extra Attack allotment, conditions and Focus as the wielder. The technique changes the body; it does not create a second character.
Omni Die. d6 early → d8 → d10 → d12 at the technique's printed milestones. Alien attacks and abilities that say OD use the current Omni Die.
Azmuth Protocol — Subjugation Ritual. Candidate genomes after the inherited ten must be defeated alone. Enter with current HP/resources; outside assistance invalidates the ritual. Victory permanently unlocks that genome. Master Control cannot bypass an undefeated Candidate.

Inherited Ten · level 1

Heatblast · Pyronite
Fire artillery, heat control and propulsion. Ranged flame, ignition, heat absorption and jet-assisted movement define the form.
Wildmutt · Vulpimancer
Sightless tracking, pursuit and pouncing melee. Exceptional scent/hearing replaces ordinary sight.
Diamondhead · Petrosapien
Crystal armor, weapons, structures and refraction. A durable constructor/striker body.
XLR8 · Kineceleran
Extreme speed, reflex defense and vortex control without borrowing Projection Sorcery's frame law.
Grey Matter · Galvan
Superintelligence, technical analysis, sabotage and Tiny-body access at the cost of physical vulnerability.
Four Arms · Tetramand
Large-frame strength, multiple grapples, shock waves and overwhelming close-range force.
Stinkfly · Lepidopterran
Flight, aerial control, adhesive slime and sensory utility.
Ripjaws · Piscciss Volann
Aquatic supremacy, brutal jaws and underwater pursuit with a genuine dependence on water.
Upgrade · Galvanic Mechamorph
Merge with machines, improve their function and reshape technological bodies without permanently creating cursed tools.
Ghostfreak · Ectonurite
Intangibility, stealth, possession pressure and an intentionally dangerous relationship between user and genome.

Candidate Genomes · subjugation required

Cannonbolt
Armored rolling impact and ricochet momentum.
Wildvine
Plant-body reach, regeneration, vines and environmental control.
Upchuck
Consume matter/energy and spit the stored force back as artillery.
Ditto
Multiple bodies with one shared HP pool, CE pool, action, bonus action, reaction and Extra Attack allotment.
Eye Guy
Distributed vision and multi-angle energy projection.
Blitzwolfer
Predatory physicality and destructive sonic howls.
Snare-oh
Bandage-body extension, binding and unusual physical resilience.
Frankenstrike
Electromagnetic strength, conductivity and heavy electrical offense.
Way Big
Special level-18 Maximum genome with its own subjugation trial; colossal body and cosmic-scale beam output. It is never a free Master Control unlock.

Optional future-only candidates

Spitter, Buzzshock and Arctiguana can be added as optional future-Ben Candidates if the campaign wants them; they are not part of the nineteen-form young-Ben roster.

Master Control · level 20

Master Control removes much of the transformation friction and allows rapid form selection/switching under the guide's level-20 rules, but it never unlocks a genome whose subjugation ritual has not been won.
