Cursed Tool Arsenal

Forty additional cursed tools: fifteen original concepts, mythic/legendary adaptations, and a smaller media-inspired armory. Grade 1/Special Grade tools normally require one hour and one cursed-tool attunement slot; activated properties spend the wielder's CE. Tool Die: d6 levels 1–4, d8 5–10, d10 11–16, d12 17–20.

Original · Grade 1

Last Word

1d10 revolver. Misses build Certainty against the target; spend it on cover penetration, accuracy, or a Dead Certain burst.

Original · Grade 1

Pallbearer

2d6 executioner maul. Takedowns build Burden for momentum, grave-weight control, and Interment.

Original · Special Grade

The Unopened Letter

Three Seals interfere with targeted techniques and tracking. Break them all and the letter may finally open.

Original · Grade 1

Second Opinion

Paired surgical knives: Diagnosis marks the first wound; Revision changes the second wound's battlefield consequences.

Original · Special Grade

Dead Man's Switch

Stores Failsafe charges as its bearer approaches death; strongest while losing, with almost no offensive value while healthy.

Original · Grade 2

Undertaker's Bell

Anti-summon bell that gains Toll around shikigami, clones, spirits, and manifestations.

Original · Grade 2

Red Thread of the Unmet

Pairs two endpoints for direction sense, rescue movement, and emergency position swapping.

Original · Grade 1

Hour Hand

Tempo rapier whose hits cycle Beat 1 speed → Beat 2 force → Beat 3 reaction denial.

Original · Grade 1

Widow's Key

Creates temporary keyholes in cursed barriers and wards without casually deleting Domains.

Original · Grade 2

Mercy Seat

Folded iron rescue chair that protects, carries, and stabilizes wounded allies at the cost of offense.

Original · Grade 1

Gallows Coin

Flip for Credit or Debt; offensive gambling creates a visible liability enemies can exploit.

Original · Special Grade

Black Archive

Records observed technique features as defensive Entries. It studies techniques but never copies them.

Original · Grade 1

Graveyard Shift

Workman's shovel that marks Grave Plots for terrain control and anti-spirit pressure.

Original · Grade 1

Borrowed Face

Porcelain mask storing observed faces and voices, never memories, CE signatures, techniques, or statistics.

Original · Grade 2

White Flag

Spear-banner creating a symmetric Truce Zone where violence remains possible but betrayal is costly.

Mythic · Special Grade

Gáe Bolg

Barbed spear whose embedded spectral Barbs reduce healing and build toward a fatal-course Release.

Mythic · Special Grade

Mjölnir

Returning 1d10 war hammer; movement, catches, throws, and lightning build Charge for storm pressure.

Legendary · Special Grade

Excalibur

Declare Protect, Lead, or Endure after a long rest. Breaking your own Oath suppresses its special properties.

Mythic · Special Grade

Gungnir

Returning marked-target spear; legendary accuracy becomes relentless targeting assistance, not automatic hits.

Mythic/Literary · Special Grade

Ruyi Jingu Bang

Size-changing staff from hairpin concealment to Heaven-Measuring reach and pillar attacks.

Mythic · Special Grade

Vajra

Thunderbolt implement with lightning pressure and barrier-damaging techniques rather than casual Domain deletion.

Mythic · Special Grade

Kusanagi

Grass-cutting tachi that stores Gale by cutting through terrain, vegetation, smoke, and wind effects.

Mythic · Grade 1

Harpe

Monster-hunter sickle-sword gaining Sever against transformed, regenerating, and summoned creatures.

Mythic · Special Grade

Ame-no-Habakiri

Colossal/serpent boss-hunter blade that builds Scale through attacks on the prey it was made to kill.

Legendary · Special Grade

Gram / Balmung

Choose a Bane after a long rest; the greatsword adapts its pressure toward that declared prey category.

Legendary · Special Grade

Caladbolg

Terrain-cleaving greatsword that builds Arc through full swings and structural destruction.

Legendary · Grade 1

Durandal

Indestructible defender's blade; successful blocks build Integrity for counterpressure.

Legendary · Special Grade

Gáe Buidhe & Gáe Dearg

Paired spears: Buidhe wounds healing; Dearg briefly suppresses active cursed effects without deleting techniques.

Mythic · Special Grade

Aegis

Shield/mantle storing Gaze from failed attacks and fear effects for defense, terror, and bounded reflection.

Legendary · Grade 1

Clarent

Betrayal sword that accumulates Grievance against former allies or marked leaders who harm your side.

Legendary · Special Grade

Fragarach

Counter-sword earning Answer through readied triggers and retaliation; let the opponent commit first.

Mythic · Special Grade

Sharur

Talking returning mace that scouts and offers counsel but never becomes a free autonomous extra turn.

Legendary/Literary · Grade 1

Green Dragon Crescent Blade

Guandao that builds Momentum by moving into attacks; excellent for martial/Heavenly Restriction builds.

Media/Folkloric · Special Grade

Blood-Red Muramasa

Hungry katana whose rising Hunger increases damage while eventually hurting the wielder too.

Media-inspired · Special Grade

Key of the Closed Sky

Originalized key-sword for cursed locks, anti-control support, and tactical doors.

Media-inspired · Grade 1

Returning Frost Axe

Throw/recall battleaxe that places Frostmarks and shatters them in melee.

Media-inspired · Special Grade

Living Cursed Carapace

Symbiotic gauntlet morphing between Blade, Shield, and Tendril; damage taken builds Instinct.

Media-inspired · Grade 1

Mass Driver

Gravity hammer gaining Mass from forced movement, then spending it on impact waves and collisions.

Media-inspired · Special Grade

Threshold Knife

Cuts short tactical portals into solid surfaces without unrestricted teleportation or Domain escape.

Original · Special Grade

Black Box

Extradimensional cursed-tool case enabling rapid loadout switching without extra attunement slots.
