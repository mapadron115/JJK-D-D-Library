Shared cursed-tool framework

Equipment should add a gimmick, not quietly become a second character.

These tools follow the revised library philosophy: preserve spectacular exceptions, but define their trigger, action, CE cost, target, duration, limits and counterplay. Tool abilities are not Innate Techniques and do not consume a technique slot; they still share the wielder's actions, CE, Focus/concentration, reactions and other normal resources.

Grade

Grade 2 is strong specialized equipment. Grade 1 can define a build. Special Grade has an exceptional law or artifact-scale ceiling. Grade is a campaign signal, not a level gate.

Tool Die

d6 at levels 1–4, d8 at 5–10, d10 at 11–16, d12 at 17–20. When a feature says Tool Die, use the wielder's character level.

Attunement

Unless an entry says otherwise, Grade 1 and Special Grade tools require 1 hour and one cursed-tool attunement slot. Grade 2 tools may be run unattuned at the GM's discretion. Activated properties spend the wielder's CE.

Tool DC

A sorcerer uses their normal CE save DC. A wielder without CE who is explicitly able to activate the tool uses 8 + PB + one chosen mental modifier. A tool never creates CE for a zero-CE character unless it explicitly says so.

Release

Entries explicitly labeled Release normally require level 13+, the printed CE cost, and are once per long rest. Release is Maximum-style equipment mastery, not a Domain and not an Innate Technique.

Black Flash & Maximum Output

Ordinary cursed-tool weapon hits can interact with Black Flash when the campaign's normal requirements are met. Save-only tool effects cannot. Activated tool abilities do not receive generic Maximum Output unless they explicitly channel the wielder's Innate Technique.

Destruction

Grade 2 tools can be damaged as extraordinary magic items. Grade 1 tools should require deliberate cursed destruction. Special Grade artifacts are not casually deleted by ordinary damage; artifact-breaking effects or major narrative events can damage them.
