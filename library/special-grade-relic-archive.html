Special Grade Relic Archive

A curated collection of Special Grade cursed tools built around one rule: the strongest relics do not merely deal more damage—they impose a metaphysical law. Some are historical objects, some are mythic, some are folkloric, and some are modern legends whose stories became curses.

Historical and mythic provenance is intentionally treated as in-world JJK lore. Where an artifact is legendary rather than archaeologically certain, the curse is born from the belief surrounding it.

Special GradeRelic SpearReligious Legend

The Holy Lance

Spear of Longinus · “Even the divine can be wounded.”

Metaphysical Law: Nothing may declare itself absolutely beyond harm.

Core Technique — Absolute Piercing

The lance does not simply bypass armor. It attacks the claim that something cannot be pierced. Barriers, invulnerability, immortality, reincarnation anchors, soul-body separation, and “cannot be harmed” clauses are all treated as valid targets.

  • Attacks ignore resistance granted specifically by barriers, reinforcement, or invulnerability effects.
  • When striking a barrier, Domain wall, or metaphysical seal, the Lance treats the target as one grade lower.
  • Once per long rest, the wielder may declare a named defense as the target of the strike. On a hit, that defense is suppressed until the end of the wielder’s next turn.
Archive Warning: Do not describe a defense as “absolute” in the presence of the Lance.
Special GradeGreatswordNorse Legend

Tyrfing

The blade that demands a death whenever it is drawn.

Metaphysical Law: A drawn Tyrfing must be paid for with a life.

Core Technique — Death Debt

Once Tyrfing leaves its sheath, it cannot be willingly sheathed until a creature dies within the encounter.

  • While the Debt is unpaid, Tyrfing gains escalating bonuses to damage each round.
  • Its strikes ignore mundane hardness and heavily punish defensive stalling.
  • If combat ends without a death, the curse turns inward and selects the nearest valid bearer of the debt.
Binding Vow: The sword is monstrously powerful precisely because it cannot be drawn safely.
Special GradeSwordNorse Legend

Dáinsleif

The wound that refuses to become whole.

Metaphysical Law: What Dáinsleif wounds cannot be restored normally.

Core Technique — Unhealing Wound

  • Damage dealt by Dáinsleif reduces maximum HP by an equal amount until the curse is broken.
  • RCT cannot restore HP lost to the blade while the wound remains cursed.
  • A critical hit can inflict an Unhealing Injury on a limb, preventing normal restoration of that limb.
Dáinsleif occupies a different niche from soul-cutting weapons: it does not attack the soul—it attacks recovery itself.
Special GradeRingNorse Legend

Andvaranaut

The treasure that teaches its owner to become its dragon.

Metaphysical Law: Possession becomes power, and power becomes possession.

Core Technique — Hoard

The ring converts wealth and ownership into cursed reinforcement.

  • The wearer gains bonuses based on the total value of property they personally claim.
  • Stored wealth can be sacrificed for temporary HP, CE recovery, or defensive boosts.
  • As the hoard grows, voluntarily surrendering possessions requires increasingly difficult Wisdom saves.
  • At extreme thresholds, the wearer begins to physically and spiritually “dragonize.”
Special GradeNecklaceGreek Myth

Necklace of Harmonia

Perfect fortune for the wearer, ruin for everyone around them.

Metaphysical Law: The wearer’s blessing is paid for by their attachments.

Core Technique — Transferred Calamity

  • The wearer gains supernatural beauty, presence, and resistance to aging.
  • Failed saving throws or catastrophic consequences may be redirected to a bonded ally, lover, family member, or sworn companion.
  • The more benefits invoked, the more severe the transferred misfortune becomes.
The tool does not make the wearer unlucky. It makes everyone who loves them unlucky.
Special GradeGemModern Legend

The Hope Diamond

A curse made true by people repeating the story.

Metaphysical Law: Fortune can be bought only by moving misfortune elsewhere.

Core Technique — Borrowed Luck

  • The wearer may turn a failed d20 roll into a success.
  • Each correction stores an equal “Misfortune Debt.”
  • The GM may later spend that debt to worsen unrelated outcomes around the wearer.
  • At high debt, improbable accidents become increasingly common.
Special GradeGrimoireReligious Folklore

Codex Gigas

The Devil’s Bible · Knowledge that studies the reader back.

Metaphysical Law: Every truth learned from the Codex grants the Codex an equal truth about you.

Core Technique — Reciprocal Knowledge

  • The reader may ask the book a question concerning curses, barriers, rituals, souls, or history.
  • For each answer, the Codex learns one truth of equal significance about the reader.
  • The book may later exploit learned truths to impose disadvantage, reveal secrets, or bypass protections.
Special Grade Set30 CoinsReligious Legend

The Thirty Pieces of Silver

Payment with enough symbolic weight to purchase betrayal itself.

Metaphysical Law: Once a price is accepted, the betrayal has already begun.

Core Technique — Price of Treachery

  • A coin may be offered as payment for a declared betrayal or broken allegiance.
  • If the recipient willingly accepts, resisting the agreed betrayal becomes increasingly difficult.
  • More coins strengthen the compulsion and broaden what may be “purchased.”
  • All thirty together can form a single catastrophic Binding Vow transaction.
Special GradeSwordArthurian Legend

Excalibur

A blade whose strength comes from legitimacy, not raw force.

Metaphysical Law: Authority accepted is stronger than authority seized.

Core Technique — Rightful Hand

  • The weapon’s enhancement scales with how many willing allies recognize the wielder’s authority.
  • Commands made while wielding Excalibur may grant allies temporary bonuses if obeyed willingly.
  • If the wielder betrays those who recognize them, the blade’s bonuses collapse until legitimacy is restored.
Special GradeVesselReligious Legend

The Holy Grail

A vessel capable of holding positive energy without destroying itself.

Metaphysical Law: What is poured into the Grail may return purified.

Core Technique — Consecrated Reversal

  • CE poured into the Grail can be converted into RCE at a steep efficiency loss.
  • The Grail can store positive energy for later use.
  • Once per long rest, it can remove one severe curse, poison, disease, or cursed-energy contamination effect.
  • Overuse risks “purifying” beneficial cursed techniques as well.
Special GradeSwordNorse Myth

Sword of Summer

Freyr’s self-fighting blade · Worthiness through conduct.

Metaphysical Law: The sword fights for the worthy, not merely the strong.

Core Technique — Worthy Hand

  • The sword can hover, move, and attack independently beside its attuned wielder.
  • At full synchronization, it gains its own turn immediately after the wielder.
  • Acts of cowardice, betrayal, or abandonment of a declared duty suppress its autonomous abilities.
  • If the wielder willingly gives the sword away, they can never attune to it again.
Special GradeDaggerAncient Egypt

Tutankhamun’s Meteoric Iron Dagger

Star-fallen metal forged before ordinary iron belonged to Egypt.

Metaphysical Law: Matter born outside the world is not fully recognized by the world’s cursed-energy rules.

Core Technique — Star-Fallen Iron

  • Barrier reinforcement and CE-hardening are treated as one grade weaker against the blade.
  • The dagger gains advantage on checks made to damage or sever barrier anchors.
  • It does not nullify techniques; it simply interacts with them as if it were partly foreign to the system enforcing them.
Special GradeKhopeshAncient Egypt

Khopesh of the Two Horizons

Ma’at over Isfet · Order cuts disorder away.

Metaphysical Law: Chaos may be forced back toward a stable, truthful state.

Core Technique — Ma’at Over Isfet

  • Gain Order when protecting allies, countering attacks, or punishing broken vows.
  • Spend Order to resist forced movement, environmental distortion, or unstable transformations.
  • At maximum Order, a successful strike can temporarily strip one altered state: possession, transformation, summoned armor, monstrous form, or similar overlay.
Special GradeAmuletAncient Egypt

Wedjat Eye of Horus

The restored eye · Protection, wholeness, and return from loss.

Metaphysical Law: That which was broken may be restored to its intended whole.

Core Technique — Restored Measure

  • The bearer gains improved efficiency when using RCT on others.
  • Once per long rest, the Eye can restore a damaged limb or major bodily injury without the usual penalty.
  • When the wearer would be reduced to 0 HP, the Eye may break one stored “segment” to leave them at 1 HP instead.
  • Its strongest restoration cannot revive the truly dead; it restores what still possesses a valid living pattern.
Special GradeCursed VehicleModern Legend

Little Bastard

Porsche 550 Spyder · A route that wants to become a fatality report.

Metaphysical Law: Once momentum becomes fate, stopping is the dangerous choice.

Core Technique — Dead Man’s Route

  • The vehicle accumulates Fatal Momentum while traveling at high speed without stopping.
  • Fatal Momentum boosts durability, impact damage, and pursuit speed.
  • Once a destination is declared, probability bends toward keeping the route open.
  • Voluntarily stopping becomes harder as Fatal Momentum rises; a crash releases all accumulated force at once.
Special GradeCursed ObjectModern History

The Demon Core

Criticality as a cursed-energy law.

Metaphysical Law: The closer the system comes to disaster, the more power it produces.

Core Technique — Criticality

  • The Core can amplify CE invested into a technique, barrier, or Domain.
  • Each doubling raises a Criticality score.
  • At high Criticality, checks are required to prevent runaway discharge.
  • Failure releases a Special Grade CE pulse that ignores allegiance and strikes everyone in range.
Archive Protocol: No activation without multiple barrier-capable sorcerers present.
Special GradeGauntletsSLF-Inspired

Gilta Brille

Brilliant Scorpion Gauntlets · A seven-day cursed-energy capacitor.

Metaphysical Law: Stored cursed energy becomes crystalline violence.

Reservoir

  • Maximum capacity: 168 Charges.
  • 1 CE willingly deposited = 1 Charge.
  • The gauntlets can absorb no more than 24 Charges per 24 hours.
  • Stored Charges cannot be withdrawn as CE.

Crystal Bullet / Growing Up

Fire crystalline seeds into creatures or terrain. Seeds can later erupt for piercing damage, restraint, battlefield control, or mobility.

Exceed Charge: Reject

On a melee hit, discharge the reservoir through the target. Damage scales at 1d6 force per stored Charge. A full 168-Charge discharge represents the tool’s true Special Grade output and should carry catastrophic recoil.

Special GradeGreatswordSLF-Inspired

Antinomy

Greatsword of Gods and Demons · CE and RCE in one impossible circuit.

Metaphysical Law: Two contradictory energy states may coexist only long enough to destroy something.

Satanel — Negative Circuit

The black form channels ordinary cursed energy and applies Apocalypse Marks.

Satan — Reversed Circuit

The white form internally reverses CE into positive energy and applies Catastrophe Marks.

Armageddon

  • Requires 5 Apocalypse Marks and 5 Catastrophe Marks on the same target.
  • The wielder must complete the activation chant.
  • The marks collapse into one another for a catastrophic finishing strike.
  • Afterward, the wielder suffers severe stat and technique suppression, while Antinomy itself remains weakened for days.
