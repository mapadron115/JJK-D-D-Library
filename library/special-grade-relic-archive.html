Special Grade Relic Archive
Full Mechanical Draft · v2
JJK 5e · Cursed Tools · Binding Vows

Relics that impose rules on reality.

These are not ordinary magic items with historical names painted over them. Each relic is treated as a Jujutsu Kaisen cursed tool: belief, fear, ritual use, death, vows, or concentrated cursed energy created a technique inside the object. Special Grade power comes with a Special Grade condition.

Special Grade BaselineWeapon-type relics use the system's +4 Special Grade enhancement unless a feature says otherwise.
Relic DC8 + proficiency bonus + the ability modifier used to wield the relic. CE-powered effects use the wielder's CE modifier; a no-CE wielder may use the relevant physical ability when appropriate.
CE Is Real CostCE spent into a relic is actually spent. Unless an item explicitly stores withdrawable energy, it cannot be reclaimed as CE.

Historical and mythic material is intentionally separated from the in-world jujutsu explanation. Legendary claims are treated as belief-vectors capable of creating cursed effects, not as assertions that the legends were historically literal.

Special GradeWeapon (Spear)

The Holy Lance

Full dossier →
Spear of Longinus · Spear of Destiny
Mechanical Profile1d8 piercing (versatile 1d10) · +4 to attack and damage rolls · Relic DC

Jujutsu Origin

The Gospel tradition gave the weapon a wound; later centuries gave it a name, a relic cult, and the reputation of a spear that touched the divine. In jujutsu terms, the accumulated belief did not make the spear holy so much as it taught the cursed energy inside the relic one impossible proposition: even the untouchable can be pierced.

Metaphysical Law: Nothing may declare itself absolutely beyond harm.

Special Grade Technique

  • Absolute Piercing. Attacks with the Lance ignore resistance or immunity granted specifically by a barrier, Cursed Technique, cursed-energy reinforcement, or an effect that states the target cannot be damaged. This does not deactivate the feature; it only allows the Lance to pass through it.
  • Pierce the Absolute — 3 CE. Before making a melee attack, name one active defensive feature on the target. On a hit, the attack deals an additional 6d10 force damage and that named feature is suppressed against the Lance until the end of your next turn. Against a barrier or Domain wall, this instead deals 12d10 force damage to the barrier.
  • Godwound — 8 CE, once per 7 days. When the Lance reduces a creature to 0 HP, you may pin its body and soul together. For 24 hours, it cannot regain HP, regenerate, reincarnate, revive, or transfer its soul to another vessel. A Special Grade effect may contest this with an Arcana check against your Relic DC.
Binding Vow / Limitation
Declared Target. Pierce the Absolute only works if the wielder names the protection being challenged before the attack roll. If the attack misses, the 3 CE is still lost. The Lance receives its absurd authority because it never pierces an unnamed abstraction.
Designed to sit beside, not replace, the Inverted Spear of Heaven: Longinus pierces a defense; it does not globally deactivate cursed techniques.
Special GradeWeapon (Greatsword)

Tyrfing

Full dossier →
The Sword That Must Be Paid
Mechanical Profile2d6 slashing · Heavy, Two-Handed · +4 to attack and damage rolls

Jujutsu Origin

Tyrfing's legend already behaves like a binding vow: extraordinary killing power purchased with a compulsory death whenever the sword is drawn. In this setting, generations of retelling hardened that condition into the weapon itself.

Metaphysical Law: If Tyrfing leaves its sheath, a meaningful life must pay for it.

Special Grade Technique

  • Blood-Hungry Edge. Once per turn on a hit, Tyrfing deals an additional 3d10 necrotic damage. While its Blood Debt is unpaid, attacks with Tyrfing score a critical hit on a 19–20.
  • Blood Debt. Drawing Tyrfing begins the debt. The blade cannot be willingly sheathed, dismissed, or abandoned until a non-trivial creature dies within 60 ft of the wielder after the draw. Summoned fodder, helpless livestock, and deliberately manufactured sacrifices do not satisfy the curse.
  • Unpaid Price. If 1 minute passes after drawing Tyrfing without satisfying the Blood Debt, the wielder takes 12d10 necrotic damage and their maximum HP is reduced by the same amount. This damage ignores resistance and immunity. The effect repeats every minute until a valid death pays the debt.
Binding Vow / Limitation
A Sword Drawn for Death. Tyrfing receives its critical range and additional damage from the certainty that a death will occur. The curse does not care whose.
Tyrfing is the more explosive of the two Norse death-swords: enormous pressure now, horrific payment if the wielder tries to bluff the vow.
Special GradeWeapon (Longsword)

Dáinsleif

Full dossier →
Högni’s Unhealing Sword
Mechanical Profile1d8 slashing (versatile 1d10) · +4 to attack and damage rolls

Jujutsu Origin

The myth of Dáinsleif gives it three brutal qualities: a stroke that does not fail, wounds that do not heal, and a blade that demands death once drawn. The cursed tool expresses those ideas literally rather than as generic bonus damage.

Metaphysical Law: A wound acknowledged by Dáinsleif does not accept restoration.

Special Grade Technique

  • Fated Stroke — 2 CE, once per turn. After you miss with Dáinsleif, you may spend 2 CE to turn the miss into a hit. A natural 1 cannot be changed this way.
  • Unhealing Wound. Once per turn when Dáinsleif hits, it deals an additional 3d8 necrotic damage. The target's maximum HP is reduced by the necrotic damage dealt. That reduction cannot be repaired by ordinary healing or Reverse Cursed Technique.
  • Breaking the Wound. A creature can remove all maximum-HP reduction caused by Dáinsleif after a long rest, or by receiving a 1-minute RCT ritual costing 5 CE. The healer must succeed on an Arcana check against the wielder's Relic DC.
  • Death Before Sheathing. If Dáinsleif is drawn in combat, it cannot be sheathed until it has reduced a creature to 0 HP.
Binding Vow / Limitation
Certainty for Permanence. The sword spends cursed energy to force a missed stroke into the written history of the fight as a hit; in exchange, every true wound it creates resists being 'rewritten' by RCT.
Unlike the Soul Split Katana, Dáinsleif is not framed as a soul-cutting weapon. Its technique specifically curses the act of healing.
Special GradeWondrous Cursed Tool (Ring)

Andvaranaut

Full dossier →
The Ring of Andvari
Mechanical ProfileHoard Points 0–20 · +4 to checks made to appraise, conceal, bargain for, or defend owned treasure

Jujutsu Origin

The cursed hoard of Andvari becomes a perfect jujutsu object because greed itself supplies the negative emotion. Ownership, fear of loss, and murder over wealth continuously feed the ring.

Metaphysical Law: Possession becomes power, and power becomes possession.

Special Grade Technique

  • Hoard Points. The ring can hold up to 20 Hoard Points. During a long rest, the wearer may permanently sacrifice valuables worth roughly ¥1,000,000 per point, or a cursed tool of Grade 2 or higher for 3 points per grade step (Grade 2 = 3, Grade 1 = 6, Special Grade = 9). Destroyed offerings cannot be recovered.
  • Hoarded Vitality. After a long rest, gain 5 temporary HP per Hoard Point, maximum 100.
  • Spend the Hoard. As a bonus action, spend 1 point to regain 2 CE or 20 HP; spend 3 points to gain +2 AC for 1 minute; spend 5 points to gain advantage on all saving throws for 1 minute.
  • Fáfnir Threshold. At 20 Hoard Points, the wearer may use an action to transform for 1 minute: fly speed 60 ft, resistance to bludgeoning/piercing/slashing, and unarmed attacks become 3d10 slashing cursed-tool attacks. When the minute ends, the ring loses 10 Hoard Points.
Binding Vow / Limitation
The Hoard Owns the Hoarder. To willingly give away, destroy, or abandon something personally owned while carrying 10+ Hoard Points, make a Wisdom save, DC 10 + half your current Hoard Points. On a failure, you cannot willingly surrender it for 24 hours.
At 20 points, removing the ring voluntarily also requires a DC 20 Wisdom save.
Special GradeWondrous Cursed Tool (Necklace)

Necklace of Harmonia

Full dossier →
The Beautiful Calamity
Mechanical Profile+4 to Charisma (Deception, Performance, Persuasion) checks · Advantage on saves against being charmed

Jujutsu Origin

The necklace is remembered as an heirloom of impossible beauty that carries disaster from one generation to the next. Jujutsu society classifies it as a transfer-type cursed tool: the wearer is not spared consequence; the consequence is reassigned.

Metaphysical Law: The wearer's blessing is paid for by the people attached to them.

Special Grade Technique

  • Perfected Appearance. While worn, the bearer does not visibly age and has advantage on Charisma checks made against a creature that can see them, in addition to the tool's +4 where applicable.
  • Transferred Calamity — Reaction, 2 CE. When you take damage or fail a saving throw, choose a Bonded creature within 120 ft. A willing Bonded creature takes the damage or suffers the failed-save effect instead. An unwilling Bonded creature makes a Charisma save against your Relic DC; on a success, the transfer fails and the CE is still spent.
  • Bonded. The necklace recognizes at most PB creatures with whom the wearer has a genuine ongoing personal bond. A bond cannot be created purely to feed the necklace.
  • Calamity Marks. Each time a creature receives Transferred Calamity, it gains 1 Calamity Mark, maximum 3. Each mark imposes a cumulative -1 to d20 rolls. All marks disappear after that creature finishes a long rest.
Binding Vow / Limitation
Beauty Cannot Pay Its Own Price. The necklace's defensive power only functions by moving the cost to a relationship the wearer actually values. It refuses disposable substitutes.
At 3 Calamity Marks, a creature cannot be selected again until it finishes a long rest.

The Hope Diamond

Full dossier →
The Curse That Was Told Into Existence
Mechanical Profile3 Fortune uses per long rest · Doom 0–3

Jujutsu Origin

Its 'curse' is useful here precisely because much of the popular curse narrative was amplified by sensational retelling. Jujutsu theory treats that as the point: enough people expected catastrophe around the stone that expectation eventually acquired cursed energy and became self-enforcing.

Metaphysical Law: Good fortune is not created; its opposite is displaced into the future.

Special Grade Technique

  • Borrowed Fortune — Reaction. After you or a creature within 30 ft rolls a d20 but before the result is resolved, add +10 to the roll. You can do this 3 times per long rest. Each use adds 1 Doom to the diamond, maximum 3.
  • Collected Doom. The GM may spend 1 Doom after the wearer or a creature within 30 ft rolls a d20 to impose -10 on that roll. Doom can only be removed by being spent this way; a long rest does not erase it.
  • Blue Catastrophe. If the diamond is at 3 Doom and would gain another, it instead releases the overflow: the wearer takes 8d10 psychic damage, and every creature within 15 ft takes half that amount. Doom then becomes 0.
Binding Vow / Limitation
Luck Must Balance. Every +10 the diamond gives creates a future -10. Its technique cannot manufacture net probability.
Borrowed Fortune cannot modify Black Flash confirmation rolls, Domain clashes, or other rolls the GM rules are metaphysical rather than probabilistic.
Special GradeWondrous Cursed Tool (Grimoire)

Codex Gigas

Full dossier →
The Devil’s Bible
Mechanical Profile+4 to Arcana, History, and Religion checks concerning curses, barriers, vows, or cursed objects

Jujutsu Origin

The manuscript's immense physical presence, demonic imagery, and centuries of legend accumulated into a knowledge-type cursed object. The dangerous part is not that a devil lives in the book; it is that the book has been treated as though something on the other side of the page is always reading back.

Metaphysical Law: Every truth taken from the Codex purchases an equal truth about the reader.

Special Grade Technique

  • Devil's Index — Action, 2 CE. Study one creature, barrier, cursed tool, or visible technique within 60 ft. Make an Arcana check against the source's CE save DC. On a success, learn two of the following: current CE, CE maximum, highest saving throw, lowest saving throw, damage resistances/immunities, current active technique effects, or barrier HP/AC.
  • Answer in the Margin — 5 CE, once per long rest. Spend 10 minutes asking one concrete question concerning a curse, ritual, historical sorcerer, barrier, or cursed object. The book returns a truthful answer to the extent that the information exists in the campaign. You gain 1 Notice.
  • Notice. Maximum 3. For each Notice, you suffer a cumulative -1 on saving throws against curses, possession, and fear. As an action, you may willingly reveal a personally meaningful secret aloud to erase 1 Notice.
  • Page of Repetition — 4 CE. Once per long rest after successfully identifying a technique with Devil's Index, gain advantage on the next saving throw you make against that technique before your next long rest.
Binding Vow / Limitation
Reciprocal Knowledge. The Codex cannot answer without learning. A reader who refuses to become legible cannot use Answer in the Margin.
The book is an intelligence relic first and a combat tool second.
Special GradeWondrous Cursed Tool Set (30 Coins)

The Thirty Pieces of Silver

Full dossier →
Judas Silver · Set of Thirty
Mechanical Profile30 physical coins · Functions through willing acceptance

Jujutsu Origin

The individual coins are ordinary enough; the story attached to 'thirty pieces of silver' is not. Betrayal, price, guilt, and the idea that loyalty can be purchased condensed into the set as a contractual cursed technique.

Metaphysical Law: A betrayal that accepts a price becomes a binding transaction.

Special Grade Technique

  • Price of Betrayal. Offer 1–10 coins to a creature while stating a specific act of betrayal, broken allegiance, or breach of duty. The technique only activates if the creature knowingly and willingly accepts the coins.
  • Purchased Treachery. For 24 hours, if the recipient knowingly acts against the purchased terms, it takes 2d10 psychic damage per coin accepted (maximum 20d10 per breach) and loses 1 CE per 2 coins accepted, rounded up. This damage cannot be reduced.
  • Fulfilled Payment. If the recipient completes the purchased betrayal, the coins become inert until the next dawn and then return to the set.
  • Thirtyfold Purchase — all 30 coins, once per 30 days. If a creature knowingly accepts the complete set for one stated betrayal, breaking that bargain deals 30d10 psychic damage, reduces its maximum HP by the damage taken until a long rest, and prevents it from using a Domain Expansion for 24 hours.
Binding Vow / Limitation
Consent Is the Trigger. The coins do not dominate minds. Their terrifying strength comes from the recipient voluntarily agreeing that loyalty has a price.
The set is strongest in political, clan, and underworld play because it turns corrupt bargains into literal Binding Vows.

Excalibur

Full dossier →
The Recognized Blade
Mechanical Profile1d8 slashing (versatile 1d10) · +4 to attack and damage rolls

Jujutsu Origin

Rather than treating Excalibur as a generic holy laser sword, this version weaponizes the centuries-old association between the blade and rightful kingship. Its cursed technique is fueled by recognition freely given by other people.

Metaphysical Law: Authority accepted is stronger than authority seized.

Special Grade Technique

  • Recognized. During a short or long rest, a willing creature may formally recognize the wielder as its leader. You may have up to PB Recognized creatures at once. Recognition can be withdrawn freely.
  • Authority Aura. While at least 2 Recognized creatures are within 60 ft, gain +1 AC. While at least 4 are within 60 ft, Excalibur deals an additional 2d8 radiant damage once per turn. If all PB Recognized creatures are present, they gain +1 to saving throws while within 30 ft of you.
  • King's Command — Action, 5 CE, once per long rest. Each Recognized creature within 60 ft may immediately use its reaction to move up to its speed without provoking opportunity attacks. Up to two of those creatures may also make one weapon or cursed-tool attack.
  • Promised Protection. When a Recognized creature within 30 ft takes damage, you may use your reaction and spend 2 CE to reduce that damage by 4d10.
Binding Vow / Limitation
Legitimacy Cannot Be Stolen. If the wielder knowingly betrays a Recognized creature for personal gain, all special abilities except the +4 weapon bonus shut down for 7 days or until meaningful restitution is accepted by the betrayed creature.
This keeps Excalibur centered on consent and leadership, not bloodline.

The Holy Grail

Full dossier →
Vessel of Consecrated Reversal
Mechanical Profile10 Sanctified Charges maximum

Jujutsu Origin

The Grail is a vessel whose legend is overwhelmingly about sacred contact, restoration, and impossible grace. In jujutsu terms, its structure has become uniquely tolerant of positive energy: it can hold RCE without being destroyed by it.

Metaphysical Law: What enters as cursed energy may leave purified.

Special Grade Technique

  • Consecrated Reservoir. During a short or long rest, a creature may spend CE into the Grail, converting 1 CE into 1 Sanctified Charge. The Grail can accept no more than PB charges per long rest and holds 10.
  • Healing Draught — Action, 1 charge. A living creature touching the Grail regains 20 HP. A Cursed Spirit or other creature harmed by positive energy instead takes 4d10 radiant damage.
  • Restoration — Action, 3 charges. End one of the following on a touched creature: blinded, deafened, paralyzed, poisoned, stunned, or one ongoing non-Special-Grade curse. You may instead repair one damaged limb that has not been removed.
  • Make Whole — 5 charges, 1 minute. Restore a severed limb, remove one level of exhaustion, or attempt to end a Special Grade curse. For a Special Grade curse, make an Arcana check against the source's CE save DC.
  • Second Breath — Reaction, 10 charges. When a living creature within 5 ft would fall to 0 HP, it instead falls to 1 HP, then regains 50 HP and removes all non-permanent limb injuries.
Binding Vow / Limitation
Vessel of Mercy. The Grail cannot use its positive energy to directly damage living humans. It may harm Cursed Spirits and other beings naturally destroyed by RCE.
It does not resurrect the truly dead; it restores a living pattern that still exists.

Sword of Summer

Full dossier →
Freyr’s Self-Fighting Sword
Mechanical Profile1d10 slashing · +4 to attack and damage rolls · Autonomous movement 60 ft

Jujutsu Origin

Freyr's sword is remembered as capable of fighting on its own. The jujutsu interpretation turns 'worthiness' into a Binding Vow: the sword will spend its own stored cursed technique only for a wielder who continues to act in accordance with a declared duty.

Metaphysical Law: The sword fights for the worthy, not merely the strong.

Special Grade Technique

  • Self-Fighting Blade. As a bonus action, command the sword to fly up to 60 ft and make one attack against a creature within 5 ft of it. Use your CE modifier for the attack and damage rolls if it is better than Strength or Dexterity. The autonomous attack deals 2d10 slashing damage before modifiers.
  • Hero's Oath. At the end of a long rest, declare one concrete duty for the coming day. While you are actively honoring it, the sword's autonomous attack requires no bonus action once per turn, but you must spend 2 CE each time it attacks this way.
  • Interposing Steel — Reaction, 2 CE. If the sword is within 30 ft of you or a creature named in your Hero's Oath, move it up to 30 ft and grant the target +4 AC against one attack.
  • Summer's Edge — 4 CE, once per turn. On a hit, deal an additional 5d8 fire or slashing damage.
Binding Vow / Limitation
The Gift Cannot Be Reclaimed. If you willingly give permanent ownership of the sword to another creature, you can never attune to or command it again. If you knowingly betray your Hero's Oath, autonomous features shut down until your next long rest.
The sword's strongest 'extra action' is paid with both CE and conduct, keeping the self-fighting fantasy without granting a free second character.

Star-Fallen Iron
Mechanical Profile1d6 piercing · Finesse, Light · +4 to attack and damage rolls

Jujutsu Origin

The historical blade's meteoritic iron becomes the key jujutsu fact. Its material entered Earth's cursed-energy environment from outside it, so barriers and reinforcement do not fully 'recognize' the metal when deciding what should be stopped.

Metaphysical Law: Matter born outside the local cursed-energy ecology is only partially subject to its rules.

Binding Vow / Limitation
No Native Circuit. The dagger cannot store CE and cannot be used as a battery or focus for a Domain. Its anti-barrier qualities come from remaining foreign to the system.

Khopesh of the Two Horizons

Full dossier →
Ma’at Over Isfet
Mechanical Profile1d8 slashing · +4 to attack and damage rolls · Order 0–6

Jujutsu Origin

This is a jujutsu-era reconstruction built around Egyptian royal and cosmic-order symbolism rather than a claim that one specific museum khopesh possessed supernatural powers. Centuries of ritual association between kingship, Horus, Ma'at, and the defeat of chaos gave the weapon its technique.

Metaphysical Law: Disorder can be cut away from the state reality recognizes as true.

Special Grade Technique

On a failure, suppress one possession, body transformation, summoned armor, fusion, alternate form, or similar technique overlay for 1 minute. It repeats the save at the end of each of its turns. Special Grade sources make the initial save with advantage.
Binding Vow / Limitation
Order Must Be Earned. The khopesh begins every combat at 0 Order. The wielder cannot preload it with CE; stability only accumulates by confronting actual disorder.
This is deliberately a control/dispel weapon instead of another sun-fire sword.

The Restored Eye

Little Bastard

Full dossier →
Porsche 550 Spyder · Cursed Vehicle
Mechanical ProfileAC 18 · HP 120 · Damage Threshold 10 · Speed 180 ft · 2 seats · Fatal Momentum 0–6

Jujutsu Origin

The modern curse is not treated as proof that every story surrounding the car was historical fact. In-world, the repeated association of the car with fatality, wreckage, and inherited bad luck is exactly what created the cursed vehicle after the fact.

Metaphysical Law: Once momentum becomes fate, stopping becomes more dangerous than continuing.

Special Grade Technique

  • Cursed Chassis. Add +4 to driving checks made with the vehicle. A creature proficient with land vehicles may use Dexterity + PB + 4.
  • Fatal Momentum. At the end of a round in which the car moves at least 120 ft, gain 1 Fatal Momentum, maximum 6. If it ends a round at 0 speed, all Momentum is lost.
  • Ram. If the car moves at least 60 ft directly toward a creature or object, the driver may make a vehicle attack. On a hit, deal 4d10 + 2d10 per Fatal Momentum bludgeoning damage and knock a Huge-or-smaller creature prone. The car takes half the damage dealt after Damage Threshold.
  • Dead Man's Route — Action, 4 CE, once per long rest. Declare a destination within 5 miles. For 10 minutes, the car ignores nonmagical difficult terrain, has advantage on driving checks, and cannot have its speed reduced below half by an external effect unless the source wins a contested check against the driver's result.
  • Don't Stop. During Dead Man's Route, voluntarily stopping before reaching the destination requires a Wisdom save with DC 12 + current Fatal Momentum. On a failure, the driver must continue moving by the safest available route.
  • Terminal Wreck. If the car is destroyed while at 4+ Momentum, creatures within 20 ft make a Dexterity save against the driver's Relic DC, taking 10d10 force damage on a failure or half on a success.
Binding Vow / Limitation
The Route Wants Completion. Dead Man's Route gains its reality-bending pathfinding because the driver surrenders the uncomplicated right to stop.
The car can be repaired by normal means; a creature may also spend 1 CE and 10 minutes to restore 10 HP to its cursed chassis.
Special GradeCursed Object

The Demon Core

Full dossier →
Criticality Engine
Mechanical ProfileCriticality Stage 0–4 · 5-ft operating radius

Jujutsu Origin

Unlike many ancient relics, the Demon Core did not need millennia of folklore. Two fatal criticality accidents, the terror of invisible radiation, and the symbolic weight of early nuclear weapons loaded a very modern object with concentrated fear. The cursed technique mirrors criticality: power rises fastest at the exact point safety disappears.

Metaphysical Law: The closer the system is pushed toward catastrophe, the more violently cursed energy multiplies.

Special Grade Technique

  • Reflector Assembly. A creature within 5 ft that activates a CE-powered feature may choose Criticality Stage 1–4 before resolving it. The feature receives one benefit based on stage: +2/+4/+8/+12 damage dice of one existing damage type, or +1/+2/+3/+4 to its save DC. A feature with no damage roll or save DC cannot be amplified.
  • Criticality Check. After the amplified feature resolves, the operator makes a Cursed Energy (Arcana) check, or Intelligence (Arcana) if they have no CE. DC is 14 / 18 / 22 / 26 for stages 1–4.
  • Runaway Reaction. On a failed Criticality Check, the Core releases a pulse in a radius of 15 ft per stage. Every creature in the area, including the operator, takes 4d10 force damage per stage; a Constitution save against the same DC halves the damage for everyone except the operator. The operator automatically takes full damage.
  • Prompt Criticality. On a natural 1 on the Criticality Check, double the Runaway Reaction damage dice and the Core becomes inert for 7 days.
  • Scram — Reaction. A second creature adjacent to the Core may spend 3 CE to give the operator advantage on the Criticality Check. Only one creature can Scram per activation.
Binding Vow / Limitation
Power Requires Proximity to Failure. The Core cannot provide safe amplification. Any attempt to eliminate the Criticality Check also eliminates the bonus.
Jujutsu Headquarters would classify transport of the Core itself as a Special Grade incident.

Gilta Brille

Full dossier →
Brilliant Scorpion Gauntlets · Crystal-CE Armament
Mechanical Profile2d8 bludgeoning · +4 to attack and damage rolls · Pair counts as one cursed tool

Jujutsu Origin

In JJK terms, the 'crystal scorpion' material is not mundane crystal. It is cursed energy that has physically crystallized inside the exoskeleton of an ancient Special Grade scorpion curse. The gauntlets grind, seed, regrow, and explosively discharge that mineralized CE. Their visible crystals are literally solid cursed energy.

Metaphysical Law: Cursed energy can be stored as matter, planted as matter, and then forced back into violence.

Special Grade Technique

  • Crystal Bullet — Action. Make a ranged cursed-tool attack against a creature or surface within 120 ft. On a hit, deal 4d8 piercing damage and plant one crystallized-CE seed. A creature can contain a maximum number of your seeds equal to your PB.
  • Growing Up — Bonus Action. Detonate one seed within 120 ft. A creature carrying it makes a Constitution save against your Relic DC, taking 5d8 piercing damage on a failure or half on a success. On a failure it is also Restrained by CE crystal. It can use its action to make an Athletics check against your Relic DC, ending the restraint on a success.
  • Terrain Growth. A seed in terrain may instead create a 10-ft crystal formation (AC 18, 40 HP) that provides half cover, or launch a willing adjacent creature up to 40 ft; that creature takes no falling damage from the launch.
  • Unbroken Light Dance — 2 CE, once per turn. When you take the Attack action, make one additional gauntlet attack. If that attack hits a creature carrying a Crystal Bullet, you may trigger one of its seeds immediately without using a bonus action.
  • Cursed Energy Reservoir. The gauntlets store up to 168 Charges. A willing creature touching them may spend CE, adding 1 Charge per 1 CE. The gauntlets can absorb no more than 24 Charges per 24 hours, regardless of how many creatures contribute. Stored Charges remain until used and can never be withdrawn as CE.
  • Exceed Charge: Reject. When you hit with a melee gauntlet attack, you may discharge any number of stored Charges. The target takes an additional 1d6 force damage per Charge. You then take unavoidable recoil damage equal to 1 HP per 2 Charges expended, rounded up.
  • Crystal Conductor. If the Reject target contains one of your seeds, Reject ignores force resistance and the target makes a Constitution save. On a failure, it is Restrained by explosive crystal until it escapes as described under Growing Up.
  • Full Reject — 168 Charges. A full discharge deals 168d6 force damage. After the attack resolves, you are reduced to 1 HP if you have more than 1 HP and automatically suffer a severe injury to the firing arm. This HP loss and injury cannot be prevented, redirected, resisted, or absorbed by temporary HP.

Seven-Day Capacitor. The 24-Charge daily intake is a physical limit of the crystallized-CE lattice, not an arbitrary cooldown. Greatsword of Gods and Demons · Satanel / Satan
Mechanical Profile3d6 slashing · Heavy, Two-Handed · +4 to attack and damage rolls

Jujutsu Origin

Antinomy is built around a contradiction that should be impossible in ordinary jujutsu engineering: one cursed tool houses a negative cursed-energy circuit and a reversed positive-energy circuit. A stabilizing Binding Vow forbids the two currents from occupying the blade simultaneously. The only place they are allowed to coexist is inside a marked target.

Metaphysical Law: Negative CE and positive energy may coexist only long enough to annihilate the thing containing both.

Special Grade Technique

  • Satanel — Black Circuit. Antinomy begins in Satanel. Once per turn on a hit, spend 2 CE to deal an additional 4d10 necrotic damage and place 1 Apocalypse Seal on the target, maximum 5.
  • Principle of Antinomy — Bonus Action. Invert the weapon's internal circuit, switching between Satanel and Satan. The sword never holds both states at once.
  • Satan — White Circuit. Once per turn on a hit, spend 2 CE to deal an additional 4d10 radiant damage and place 1 Catastrophe Seal on the target, maximum 5. Creatures of your choice within 10 ft of the struck target make a Strength save against your Relic DC; on a failure they are pushed 15 ft and knocked prone. While Satan is active, you have resistance to bludgeoning, piercing, and slashing damage.
  • Armageddon Requirement. One target must carry 5 Apocalypse Seals and 5 Catastrophe Seals. The seals last until the end of combat or until Armageddon is attempted.
  • Armageddon Chant. Spend your action and bonus action chanting. Your speed becomes 0 until the start of your next turn and you cannot activate another Cursed Technique, Maximum, Reversal, or Domain during the chant. If incapacitated before your next turn, the chant fails but the seals remain.
  • Armageddon. On your next turn, make one Antinomy attack against the sealed target with advantage. On a hit, in addition to the weapon's normal damage, deal 20d12 radiant + 20d12 necrotic + 10d12 force damage. This damage ignores resistance and immunity to radiant and necrotic damage. An effect that would reduce the Armageddon damage to 0 instead reduces it by half.
  • Aftermath — 24 Hours. For 24 hours after Armageddon, treat all six of your ability scores as half their normal values for attacks, checks, saves, DCs, and derived statistics. You cannot activate Cursed Techniques, Maximum Techniques, Reversals, Domains, or cursed-tool techniques during this period.
  • Aftermath — 7 Days. For 7 days, Antinomy loses its +4 bonus and all special abilities and functions as an ordinary greatsword.
Binding Vow / Limitation
Mutual Exclusion. Satanel and Satan may never occupy the blade together. The ten seals move the contradiction into the enemy instead. Armageddon is the one permitted instant where all ten collapse into one point, and the week-long ruin of the weapon is the price that allows it.
The sword itself performs the reversal needed for Satan; the wielder does not need to know Reverse Cursed Technique.
