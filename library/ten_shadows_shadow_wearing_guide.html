Shadow Wearing

Level 8 Ten Shadows feature. Bonus action: choose one tamed, available shikigami that is not manifested or contributing to a fusion. Spend half its normal manifestation cost, rounded up, minimum 1 CE. For 1 minute, gain its Shadow Wearing feature. The source shadow is locked from manifestation/fusion. Only one form at a time.

Divine Dog · Keen Hunt
Enhanced hearing/smell and team-hunting advantage.
Divine Dog Totality · Black Fang
Once-per-turn extra melee damage and knockdown pressure.
Gama · Shadow Tongue
Long-reach cursed-energy tongue attack and grapple utility.
Nue · Storm Wing
Aerial mobility and electric offense.
Rabbit Escape · Diffusion
Evasive duplication/screening utility rather than extra independent turns.
Great Serpent · Coiling Reach
Extended constriction and control.
Max Elephant · Elephant Bulwark
Mass, durability, water pressure, and resistance to displacement.
Round Deer · Positive Current
Healing/cleansing support through the deer's positive-energy identity.
Piercing Ox · Linear Momentum
Charge power that rewards long uninterrupted approaches.
Mourn Tiger · Predator Frame
High-end melee predation and finishing pressure.

Wheel Wearing · level 17

Mahoraga Shadow Wearing. Requires Mahoraga to be tamed and currently available. The adaptive wheel is placed on the user's own body under the feature's high-level adaptation rules; Mahoraga cannot simultaneously be manifested or used in a fusion.

Attack & save rules

All Shadow Wearing attacks and saves use the user's normal CE attack bonus and Technique save DC. Wearing is strong because it converts a summon function into personal action economy, but it costs CE, lasts briefly, and locks the source shikigami.
