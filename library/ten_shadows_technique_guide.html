Revised Jujutsu Kaisen 5e Innate Technique

Ten Shadows Technique

A complete campaign-ready chassis for taming, manifesting, commanding, wearing, combining, and ultimately mastering the ten sacred shadows—without diminishing the danger of earning them.

Core manifestation rules

Call from shadow.
Manifest a tamed, available shikigami by forming its sign and paying its printed CE cost.
Act as one.
Your manifestations use the technique's shared command/action procedure rather than multiplying full player turns.
One manifestation.
A fusion or swarm that says it counts as one manifestation occupies one slot regardless of how many visible bodies it creates.
Your cursed energy.
Shikigami attacks and saves use the user's CE attack modifier and Technique save DC unless their stat block explicitly says otherwise.

Technique progression

Level 1 · Divine Dogs & Open Ritual Roster.
Divine Dogs begin tamed. Every other Ten Shadows subjugation ritual is immediately challengeable. There is no level gate on the ritual roster.
Level 3 · Expanded Manifestation.
Your ability to keep shadows active improves; this milestone increases manifestation capacity rather than unlocking the roster.
Level 6 · Shadow Storage.
Use your shadow as supernatural storage and improve battlefield utility.
Level 8 · Shadow Wearing.
Wear one available shikigami's function on your own body instead of manifesting it.
Level 11 · Wells Unknown Abyss.
Access the stable two-shadow fusion framework and its fifteen ready-to-use recipes.
Level 17 · Wheel Wearing.
If Mahoraga has actually been tamed and is available, wear the adaptive wheel under its dedicated high-level rules.
Level 18 · Chimera Shadow Garden.
Open the Ten Shadows Domain and exploit the technique's complete shadow environment.

The taming ritual

Invoke the untamed. Choose any untamed roster member and begin its subjugation ritual. The shikigami appears at full intended threat. If outside help participates, the ritual can be survived but the shadow is not tamed. Victory must be yours.
Mahoraga. The rules allow the attempt from level 1. They do not make that remotely wise. Mahoraga remains the pinnacle ritual rather than receiving an artificial low-level version.

Fusion rules

Chimerafication: choose one base shadow, then add up to three other shadows. Each addition contributes one trait/ability plus one-quarter of its HP; the base identity remains intact. Wells Unknown Abyss provides fifteen stable premade two-shadow recipes.
