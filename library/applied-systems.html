Original JJK 5e Technique Codex

Applied Systems

Ten mechanically distinct techniques inspired by systems, infrastructure, craft, logistics, and rules people normally overlook. Each follows the library's 1 / 3 / 6 / 11 / 18 major progression cadence and carries four dedicated feats in the full codex design.

Defender / Support Bruiser

Golden Fault

Once per round when something within 60 feet takes damage, designate it as a Fracture, max 3 per target. Mend breaks into stronger seams or Exploit them before they heal. Lacquer Dice power both sides. Higher levels add Reinforced Seam, Living Kintsugi, Catastrophic Break, and the technique's pinnacle repair/break authority.

Tank / Damage Distributor

Load-Bearing Principle

Mark willing creatures, objects, structures, or terrain as Supports. Brace them, take pressure into yourself as Load, then Discharge accumulated structural stress. Higher levels add Weight Distribution, Continuous Truss, Controlled Demolition, and a true load-bearing battlefield network.

Controller / Tactical Support

Nine Knots

Manifest cursed cord and place different Knot types on creatures and terrain. Anchor, Hitch, and Stitch become Snare & Slip, Compound Knots, and eventually Gordian Binding as overlapping knotwork grows more dangerous.

Striker / Controller

Faultline Ledger

Place Faults on damaged creatures, objects, and surfaces. Movement and impacts accumulate Strain; Rupture cashes the ledger out. Mapped Ground, Aftershock, and Deep Fault make the environment itself part of the combo.

Battlefield Controller / Tactician

Territorial Influence

Deploy cursed Go stones that create Influence zones. Atari, Capture, Sente, Cut and Connect, and Living Board reward enclosure and positional reading rather than raw spell spam.

Support / Attrition Controller

Mycelial Testament

Plant Nodes and connect them into a mycelial network. Remote Fruiting, Symbiotic Pulse, Nutrients, Grasping Mycelium, and specialized Fruiting Bodies let the same colony heal, restrain, reveal, protect, or decompose.

Utility Controller / Rule Editor

Palimpsest

Spend Revision Dice to Record states and write new layers over objects, terrain, and eventually living creatures. Position Note, Restore Object, Persistent Margins, Living Layer, and Redaction make restoration and contradiction the technique's language.

Mobility Controller / Duelist

Right of Way

Create directional traffic Lanes with Green, Yellow, or Red Signals. Violations create Citations. Signal Controller, Intersections, and Priority Vehicle turn supernatural traffic law into a movement-control technique.

Investigator / Support Controller

Osmothèque

Prepare Scent Notes and spend them on formulas such as Courage Accord, Funeral Myrrh, Hunter's Trace, Veil of Familiarity, Anesthetic Fog, Bottled Memory, and Counter-Scent. Recognition and atmosphere become resources.

Utility / Improvised Arsenal

Unclaimed Property

Spend Claim Tickets to Archive abandoned objects and issue temporary Loaners. Return to Sender, Loaner Tool, and Evidence Locker expand what counts as usable unclaimed property without stealing bonded equipment for free.
