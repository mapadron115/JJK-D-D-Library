Shared table language

How to read these techniques

CE attack

Use your normal cursed-energy attack bonus from your class.

CE save DC

8 + proficiency bonus + CE ability modifier.

Progression

Each technique’s major chassis advances at levels 1, 3, 6, 11, and 18, matching the base JJK 5e innate-technique feature cadence.

Technique Focus

If your class uses Technique Focus/concentration-style limitations, these abilities use the normal rules. This codex never grants a second Focus.

Domain initiative

Where a Domain appears, its unique sure-hit resolves at initiative count 20. Normal attacks and ordinary technique abilities still roll attacks/saves unless explicitly stated otherwise.

Feat access

Technique feats are optional character-building choices, not automatic level features, unless your table grants them separately.

Defender / Support Bruiser

Golden Fault

Damage creates Fractures. You decide whether to mend those breaks into stronger seams or exploit them before they can heal.

High TierLacquer Dice
RoleDefender / Support Bruiser
Power tierHigh
Scalingd6 → d8 (6th) → d10 (11th) → d12 (18th)
Main resourceLacquer Dice

Core Gameplay Loop

Fracture Sense

Once per round when a creature, object, construct, or barrier you can see within 60 feet takes damage, you may designate that damage as a Fracture without using an action. A target can hold up to 3 Fractures from you. Fractures last for 1 minute. A creature can hold Fractures whether it is willing or hostile.

Lacquer Dice

You have a number of Lacquer Dice equal to your proficiency bonus + your CE ability modifier (minimum 2). You regain all expended dice after a long rest. Your Lacquer Die is d6, becoming d8 at 6th level, d10 at 11th, and d12 at 18th.

Mend the Break

Bonus Action • 2 CE • 30 feet. Choose a willing creature with one of your Fractures, consume one Fracture, and expend one Lacquer Die. The target regains HP equal to the die + your CE modifier. An object, construct, or barrier instead regains HP equal to twice the result. This healing cannot restore a destroyed limb by itself.

Exploit the Break

Once per turn when you hit a hostile creature that bears one of your Fractures, you may consume one Fracture and expend one Lacquer Die. Add the die to the attack damage and the target cannot regain HP until the start of your next turn.

Level Progression

LevelFeature
1Fracture Sense, Lacquer Dice, Mend the Break, Exploit the Break
3Reinforced Seam — after you Mend a creature, choose +1 AC or advantage against forced movement until the start of your next turn. If you Mend the same creature twice before the end of your next turn, it also gains temporary HP equal to one Lacquer Die.
6Living Kintsugi — your Mend may end bleeding and stabilize automatically. By spending 4 CE and two Lacquer Dice, you may repair a non-severed limb injury by one step if the target has received appropriate mundane treatment. Once a creature benefits from this limb repair, it cannot do so again until a long rest.
11Catastrophic Break — your targets may hold 5 Fractures. When you hit, you may consume up to 3 Fractures at once, expending one Lacquer Die per Fracture. If at least 3 are consumed, the target also makes a Constitution save; on a failure its AC is reduced by 2 until the start of your next turn.
18Maximum Technique: Golden Reassembly — Action, 12 CE, once per long rest. Choose up to your proficiency bonus creatures/objects within 30 feet. Willing targets heal 4 Lacquer Dice + twice your CE modifier and may repair one non-severed limb injury; hostile targets instead detonate every Fracture they bear, taking one Lacquer Die of force damage per Fracture and falling prone on a failed Constitution save.

Technique Feats

Museum-Grade Repair

When you repair an object, construct, cursed tool, or barrier, add your proficiency bonus to each Lacquer Die rolled. Mundane objects you fully repair become resistant to the next instance of damage they take.

Beautiful Scar

A creature you Mend gains advantage on its next saving throw before the end of your next turn. Once per creature per short rest.

Shatterpoint

When a hostile creature reaches its maximum number of Fractures, you may immediately move 10 feet without provoking opportunity attacks. Your next attack against it before the end of the turn has advantage.

Table Rulings

  • Fractures are technique marks, not physical objects and cannot be scraped off; ending your technique removes them.
  • Healing from this technique still obeys any anti-healing effects already on the target.
  • A repaired cursed tool regains HP/integrity, not expended charges or once-per-rest abilities.
Tank / Damage Distributor

Load-Bearing Principle

Build a structural network out of allies and terrain, absorb pressure for the party, then release accumulated Load through decisive counterattacks.

High TierLoad
RoleTank / Damage Distributor
Power tierHigh
ScalingStress Die: d6 → d8 (6th) → d10 (11th) → d12 (18th)
Main resourceLoad

Core Gameplay Loop

Supports

Bonus Action • 1 CE • 30 feet. Designate a willing creature, unattended object, structure, or 5-foot section of terrain as a Support. You can maintain Supports equal to your proficiency bonus. A Support remains for 10 minutes, until beyond 120 feet, or until you dismiss it.

Stress Die

Your Stress Die is d6, increasing to d8 at 6th, d10 at 11th, and d12 at 18th.

Brace the Structure

Reaction • 1 CE. When a creature Support within 30 feet takes damage, roll your Stress Die and reduce the damage by the result + your CE modifier. If damage is reduced, gain 1 Load. You can hold Load up to your proficiency bonus + CE modifier (minimum 3).

Discharge

Once per turn when you hit with a weapon or cursed attack, spend up to 2 Load. Add one Stress Die of bludgeoning or force damage per Load spent. If 2 Load are spent, a Large or smaller target makes a Strength save or is pushed 10 feet.

Level Progression

LevelFeature
1Supports, Brace the Structure, Load, Discharge
3Weight Distribution — when Brace the Structure reduces damage, you may take up to half of the remaining damage yourself after resistances. If you do, gain 1 additional Load. You can also use Brace against forced movement, adding the Stress Die to the Support’s check/save.
6Continuous Truss — Support range becomes 60 feet and maximum Supports increases by 2. As a bonus action, spend 2 Load to allow one creature Support to move up to half its speed without provoking opportunity attacks.
11Controlled Demolition — Action, 4 CE. Choose one Support within 60 feet and spend 3–6 Load. Creatures of your choice within 15 feet make a Dexterity save, taking one Stress Die per Load spent on a failure, half on success; failed targets are also knocked prone. The chosen Support remains unless it was an object with insufficient HP to survive the effect.
18Maximum Technique: Cathedral of Supports — Bonus Action, 10 CE, 1 minute. Every willing creature within 60 feet becomes a Support without counting against your limit. Brace the Structure has 60-foot range and does not cost CE the first time you use it each round. At the start of your turn gain 1 Load if any Support took damage since your last turn.

Technique Feats

Foundation First

When initiative is rolled, designate one willing creature within 30 feet as a Support for free and gain 1 Load.

Shock Absorber

When you personally take damage redirected through Weight Distribution, reduce that damage by your proficiency bonus.

Counterweight

When you push a creature with Discharge, you may move the same distance in the opposite direction without provoking opportunity attacks.

Table Rulings

  • Damage cannot be redirected in a loop. A single damage instance can benefit from Brace the Structure only once.
  • Load disappears after 1 minute outside initiative or when you finish a short/long rest.
  • Objects used as Supports do not become indestructible; the technique only allows them to participate in your network.
Controller / Tactical Support

Nine Knots

Bind motion, distance, wounds, and relationships with cursed cord. The technique is simple to learn and frighteningly flexible once several knots overlap.

Exceptional TierActive Knots
RoleController / Tactical Support
Power tierExceptional
ScalingKnot Die: d6 → d8 (6th) → d10 (11th) → d12 (18th)
Main resourceActive Knots

Core Gameplay Loop

Cursed Cord

You manifest cursed cord from your hands. It has 30-foot reach for technique purposes, cannot be used as mundane rope by another creature, and vanishes when detached from all your Knots.

Knot Capacity

You can maintain active Knots equal to your proficiency bonus + CE modifier. A creature may bear no more than 3 of your Knots before 11th level. Each Knot normally costs 1 CE to tie as a bonus action after you touch the target or hit it with a weapon/cursed attack this turn.

Anchor Knot

A creature’s speed is reduced by 10 feet. It adds your Knot Die to checks/saves made to resist forced movement, but it cannot willingly teleport more than 30 feet at once without first succeeding on a CE save.

Hitch Knot

Tie two creatures/objects within 30 feet of one another. Until the knot ends, they cannot willingly move more than 30 feet apart. A creature attempting to exceed the tether makes a Strength save; on a failure its movement ends at the boundary.

Stitch Knot

Tie two willing creatures. Reaction: when one takes damage, you may transfer damage up to your Knot Die + CE modifier to the other creature. This does not change damage type and cannot reduce the original damage below 1.

Level Progression

LevelFeature
1Cursed Cord, Anchor Knot, Hitch Knot, Stitch Knot
3Snare & Slip — Snare: if a hostile creature bears 2 of your Knots, spend 2 CE as a bonus action; it makes a Strength save or its speed becomes 0 until the end of its next turn. Slip: reaction when a knotted creature moves; spend 1 CE to shift it 5 feet to an unoccupied space it could normally occupy (Dexterity save negates if hostile).
6Compound Knot — a creature can bear 4 Knots. When two different Knot types affect the same creature, your first damaging hit against it each turn deals one Knot Die extra force damage. You may also tie or remove one Knot as part of the Attack action once per turn.
11Gordian Binding — a creature may bear 5 Knots. As an action, spend 4 CE and consume 3 Knots on one creature. It makes a Strength save; failure restrains it for 1 minute. It repeats the save at the end of each turn, ending the effect on success. On a success against the initial save, its speed is merely halved until the end of its next turn.
18Domain Expansion: Temple of the Nine Ties — 10 CE. At initiative count 20, choose one ephemeral Knot effect for every hostile creature in the Domain: Anchor, Hitch (to a fixed point), or Snare. This is the Domain’s sure-hit and lasts until the next initiative count 20. Your ordinary cord, attacks, and Gordian Binding still resolve normally.

Technique Feats

Sailor’s Hands

Your Knot capacity increases by 2 and you can tie knots to unattended objects within 60 feet instead of 30.

Quick Release

When one of your Knots ends, move up to 5 feet without provoking opportunity attacks. Once per turn.

Executioner’s Knot

Once per turn when you damage a creature bearing 3+ Knots, add one Knot Die to the damage without consuming a Knot.

Table Rulings

  • Knots are technique effects; cutting visible cord does not automatically end them unless an ability specifically suppresses cursed techniques.
  • A creature can be affected by only one instance of each named Knot from you at a time.
  • Forced movement created by Slip cannot place a creature inside solid matter or into a space it cannot physically occupy.
Striker / Controller

Faultline Ledger

Mark faults in bodies and terrain, let ordinary movement and impacts accumulate Strain, then cash that stored stress out in controlled ruptures.

High TierStrain
RoleStriker / Controller
Power tierHigh
ScalingRupture Die: d6 → d8 (6th) → d10 (11th) → d12 (18th)
Main resourceStrain

Core Gameplay Loop

Inscribe Fault

Once per turn when you damage a creature, object, or surface, you may place a Fault on it. You can maintain Faults equal to your proficiency bonus. Faults last 1 minute.

Accumulate Strain

A Fault gains 1 Strain (maximum 6) the first time each round its subject: moves at least 10 feet, is forcibly moved, falls, or takes bludgeoning/force/thunder damage. Your own attacks can trigger this. A Fault cannot gain more than 2 Strain per round before 11th level.

Survey

Bonus Action • 1 CE • 60 feet. Choose one Fault you can see and add 1 Strain to it.

Rupture

Once per turn on hit. Spend 2–6 Strain from the target’s Fault. Deal one Rupture Die of extra force damage per 2 Strain spent. At 4 Strain the target also loses reactions until the start of its next turn; at 6 Strain it makes a Strength save or falls prone and is pushed 10 feet.

Level Progression

LevelFeature
1Inscribe Fault, Accumulate Strain, Survey, Rupture
3Mapped Ground — you can place a Fault on a 10-foot section of terrain as an action for 1 CE. When a creature crosses it, you may use your reaction to add 1 Strain and make that area difficult terrain until the start of your next turn.
6Aftershock — when you Rupture a Fault using 4+ Strain, choose another Fault within 30 feet; it gains 1 Strain. Your Rupture damage ignores resistance to nonmagical bludgeoning, piercing, and slashing damage on structures.
11Deep Fault — maximum Strain becomes 8 and a Fault can gain up to 3 Strain per round. Spending 8 Strain deals 4 Rupture Dice and, on a failed Constitution save, reduces the creature’s AC by 2 until the end of your next turn.
18Domain Expansion: World Beneath the Cracks — 10 CE. At initiative count 20 every hostile creature gains a Fault if it lacks one, then every hostile Fault gains 2 Strain. Choose one Fault: it ruptures automatically for 2 Rupture Dice without consuming Strain. This automatic rupture is the sure-hit.

Technique Feats

Seismograph

You know the exact location of every creature bearing your Fault within 120 feet unless separated by a Domain/barrier that blocks cursed sensing.

Hairline Crack

When you roll initiative, place one Fault on a visible surface or creature that has already taken damage, no action required.

Cascade Failure

After you reduce a creature to 0 HP with Rupture, one Fault within 30 feet immediately gains 2 Strain.

Table Rulings

  • A target can bear only one Fault from you at a time.
  • Strain is not damage and cannot be healed away. Ending the Fault removes all its Strain.
  • Normal walking can feed a Fault, but movement below 10 feet does not trigger Accumulate Strain.
Battlefield Controller / Tactician

Territorial Influence

Turn the battlefield into a Go board. Stones create overlapping zones of influence, and opponents who allow themselves to be surrounded lose movement and tactical options.

Exceptional TierCursed Stones
RoleBattlefield Controller / Tactician
Power tierExceptional
ScalingInfluence Die: d4 → d6 (6th) → d8 (11th) → d10 (18th)
Main resourceCursed Stones

Core Gameplay Loop

Cursed Stones

You carry or manifest a set of cursed black/white stones. You have 6 Stones at 1st level. A deployed Stone is Tiny, AC 15, HP equal to 5 + your sorcerer level, and immune to poison/psychic damage. Destroyed Stones return after a short or long rest.

Place Stone

Bonus Action • 1 CE • 30 feet. Place one Stone on an unoccupied surface. You may retrieve one within reach for free on your turn. Each Stone projects 10 feet of Influence.

Atari

A hostile creature inside the overlapping Influence of at least 2 Stones is in Atari: its speed is reduced by 10 feet and it cannot take the Dash action.

Capture

When a hostile creature starts its turn inside the overlapping Influence of at least 3 Stones, it makes a Strength or Dexterity save (its choice). On failure it is Captured until the start of its next turn: speed 0 and no reactions. A creature can suffer Capture only once per round.

Level Progression

LevelFeature
1Cursed Stones, Influence, Atari, Capture
3Sente — reaction when a hostile creature enters one of your Influence zones: move one deployed Stone up to 10 feet across a surface. This movement cannot itself trigger another Sente this round.
6Cut and Connect — you have 8 Stones. As an action for 2 CE, draw a cursed line between two Stones within 40 feet until the start of your next turn. The first hostile creature each turn to cross the line makes a Dexterity save, taking 2 Influence Dice force damage and stopping at the line on failure; half damage on success.
11Living Board — you have 10 Stones and their Influence radius becomes 15 feet. Once per turn when you hit a creature, you may place one Stone in an unoccupied space within 5 feet of it without spending an action (still costs 1 CE).
18Domain Expansion: Nineteen-by-Nineteen Funeral Board — 10 CE. Nine spectral Stones appear throughout the Domain and do not count against your set. At initiative count 20, relocate up to 3 spectral Stones anywhere on the ground. Every hostile creature influenced by 3+ Stones becomes Captured until the next initiative count 20 and takes 3 Influence Dice force damage. This Capture/damage is the sure-hit.

Technique Feats

Joseki Study

After a short rest, recover one destroyed Stone. Once per round when you place a Stone adjacent to another Stone, gain temporary HP equal to your proficiency bonus.

Sacrifice Play

Reaction when one of your Stones would be destroyed: detonate it. Creatures within 5 feet make a Dexterity save or take 2 Influence Dice force damage.

Reading Ahead

At the start of combat, after initiative is rolled, place one Stone within 10 feet of yourself for free.

Table Rulings

  • Influence ignores creatures and low cover but cannot pass through a solid wall or sealed barrier.
  • A creature can be in Atari/Captured regardless of which side of the Stones it visually stands on; overlapping Influence is the mechanical test.
  • Moving a Stone can immediately end Atari/Capture if the required overlap no longer exists.
Support / Zone Control

Mycelial Testament

Grow a network through the battlefield, cast support effects from remote Nodes, and convert deaths or spare cursed energy into Nutrients for stronger fungal blooms.

High TierNodes & Nutrients
RoleSupport / Zone Control
Power tierHigh
ScalingSpore Die: d6 → d8 (6th) → d10 (11th) → d12 (18th)
Main resourceNodes & Nutrients

Core Gameplay Loop

Mycelial Nodes

Bonus Action • 1 CE • 30 feet. Plant a Node on a surface. You can maintain Nodes equal to your proficiency bonus. Nodes are Tiny, AC 13, HP equal to 5 + twice your sorcerer level. Nodes within 30 feet of another Node or you are Connected.

Remote Fruiting

Your Mycelial Testament abilities may originate from you or any Connected Node. If an ability measures an aura or range, measure from the chosen origin.

Symbiotic Pulse

Bonus Action • 1 CE. One willing creature within 10 feet of a Connected Node regains HP equal to one Spore Die + your CE modifier. A creature can benefit from this healing only once per round.

Nutrients

You can hold Nutrients equal to your proficiency bonus. When a Small or larger creature drops to 0 HP within 10 feet of a Node, gain 1 Nutrient (once per creature). Once per turn you may also spend 1 CE to gain 1 Nutrient. Nutrients vanish after a long rest.

Level Progression

LevelFeature
1Nodes, Remote Fruiting, Symbiotic Pulse, Nutrients
3Grasping Mycelium — Action, 2 CE or 1 Nutrient. A 10-foot radius around one Node becomes difficult terrain for 1 minute. A hostile entering/starting there makes a Strength save or is restrained until the start of its next turn.
6Fruiting Bodies — spend 2 Nutrients as a bonus action to convert one Node for 1 minute into one Bloom: Bulwark grants half cover to adjacent allies; Lantern reveals invisible creatures within 15 feet that fail a Dexterity save; Puffball uses your reaction when a hostile enters 10 feet to deal 2 Spore Dice poison/necrotic damage (Constitution save half). Only one Bloom per Node.
11Distributed Organism — once per round when a willing creature within 10 feet of a Node takes damage, reaction and 2 CE: teleport it to an unoccupied space within 10 feet of another Connected Node within 60 feet, reducing the triggering damage by one Spore Die + CE modifier.
18Maximum Technique: Worldroot Fruiting — Action, 10 CE, 1 minute. Ground within 60 feet counts as Connected network terrain. At the start of each turn gain 1 Nutrient. Symbiotic Pulse may target two creatures, and hostile creatures that start within your network make a Constitution save or take 2 Spore Dice necrotic damage and have speed reduced by 10 feet until their next turn.

Technique Feats

Old Growth

Your Node maximum increases by 2 and Node HP gains your proficiency bonus.

Medicinal Culture

When Symbiotic Pulse rolls its maximum value, the target also gains temporary HP equal to your CE modifier.

Cordyceps Reflex

When a hostile creature within 10 feet of a Node falls prone, grappled, or restrained, gain 1 Nutrient. Once per round.

Table Rulings

  • Nodes are manifestations, not independent creatures; they do not take turns.
  • A corpse can grant Nutrient only once, regardless of being revived and reduced again.
  • Worldroot Fruiting does not pass through floors/walls into disconnected rooms unless there is a physical route for the network.
Utility Controller / Repositioner

Palimpsest

Record a prior layer of an object, location, or person and selectively restore pieces of that earlier state without becoming unrestricted time travel.

Exceptional TierRecords & Revision Dice
RoleUtility Controller / Repositioner
Power tierExceptional
ScalingRevision Die: d6 → d8 (6th) → d10 (11th) → d12 (18th)
Main resourceRecords & Revision Dice

Core Gameplay Loop

Revision Dice

You have Revision Dice equal to your proficiency bonus + CE modifier (minimum 2), regained on a long rest. Die size scales d6/d8/d10/d12 at levels 1/6/11/18.

Records

You can maintain Records equal to your proficiency bonus. A Record captures a subject’s state at the instant of inscription. At 1st level, Records last 10 minutes and may capture an unattended object, a 5-foot cube of terrain, or a creature’s position only.

Position Note

Bonus Action • 1 CE • 60 feet. Record a creature’s current space until the start of your next turn. As a reaction after it moves, spend one Revision Die: a hostile target makes a Dexterity save. On failure it returns to the recorded legal space; on success its remaining speed becomes 0. Willing targets return automatically.

Restore Object

Action • 2 CE + one Revision Die. Restore a recorded unattended object or 5-foot terrain section toward its recorded state. Restore HP/integrity equal to twice the die + twice your CE modifier. This cannot duplicate an item: if the recorded original still exists elsewhere, the restoration fails.

Level Progression

LevelFeature
1Revision Dice, Records, Position Note, Restore Object
3Persistent Margins — object/terrain Records last 1 hour. You may record an object currently held by a willing creature. When restoring a broken weapon/tool, you may also remove one mundane jam, bend, tear, or similar impairment.
6Living Layer — Action, 4 CE + 2 Revision Dice. Record a willing creature for 1 round. Before that Record ends, you may restore it as a reaction: it heals both dice + CE modifier and may end one frightened, poisoned, grappled, or restrained condition gained after the Record. Each creature may benefit once per short rest.
11Redaction — Action, 5 CE. Choose a creature or manifested technique effect within 60 feet. Make a CE ability check contested by the creator’s CE ability check (or target makes a CE save). On success, suppress one temporary buff, summoned construct, aura, or movement-altering technique effect until the end of your next turn. You do not erase innate techniques, Domains, or permanent effects.
18Maximum Technique: First Draft — Action, 12 CE, once per long rest. Choose a 30-foot-radius area you personally observed within the last minute. Terrain and unattended objects return toward that recorded layout. Willing creatures may return to their recorded positions and heal 4 Revision Dice. Hostile creatures make a Dexterity save; on failure they return to their recorded position and lose one temporary buff gained since then. This never restores expended CE, class resources, Domain uses, or dead creatures.

Technique Feats

Archivist’s Eye

Record capacity increases by 2 and you can identify whether an object has been magically altered since you last touched it.

Margin Walker

When you use Position Note on yourself, returning to the Record does not use your reaction once per short rest.

Errata

When a creature succeeds on a save against Position Note or Redaction, it still subtracts one Revision Die from its speed until the end of the turn.

Table Rulings

  • Palimpsest restores recorded state; it does not reverse the entire timeline.
  • Restoration cannot duplicate consumables, ammunition, money, cursed tools, or creatures.
  • If a recorded destination is occupied or hazardous in a way the subject could not legally occupy, use the nearest legal unoccupied space.
Mobility Controller / Skirmisher

Right of Way

Paint supernatural traffic lanes onto the battlefield. Allies exploit legal movement while enemies accumulate Citations for crossing, rushing, or ignoring your signals.

High TierLanes & Citations
RoleMobility Controller / Skirmisher
Power tierHigh
ScalingCitation Die: d6 → d8 (6th) → d10 (11th) → d12 (18th)
Main resourceLanes & Citations

Core Gameplay Loop

Traffic Lane

Bonus Action • 1 CE. Create a 5-foot-wide, 30-foot-long Lane on a surface within 30 feet. It lasts 1 minute. You can maintain one Lane at 1st level. Choose its direction and current Signal: Green, Yellow, or Red.

Green Signal

Willing creatures moving in the Lane’s direction gain +10 feet speed for that movement. The first 10 feet they move inside the Lane each turn does not provoke opportunity attacks.

Yellow Signal

A hostile creature that voluntarily moves 10+ feet while inside the Lane gains 1 Citation at the end of that movement. A creature can gain only one Citation from Yellow each round.

Red Signal

The first time each round a hostile creature enters or crosses a Red Lane, it makes a Dexterity save. Failure ends its current movement and gives it 1 Citation; success allows movement normally.

Cash Citation

Bonus Action • 60 feet. Spend Citations on one creature: 1 Citation deals one Citation Die force damage and reduces speed 10 feet until next turn; 2 Citations makes speed 0 until next turn; 3 Citations deals 3 Citation Dice and forces a Strength save or knocks prone. A creature can hold at most 3 Citations.

Level Progression

LevelFeature
1Traffic Lane, Green/Yellow/Red Signals, Citations
3Signal Controller — once per turn change one Lane’s Signal without using an action. Reaction when a hostile gains a Citation: one willing creature in that Lane may move 5 feet.
6Intersection — maintain 2 Lanes, each up to 40 feet. When a hostile crosses the point where two of your Lanes intersect, it makes a Dexterity save; failure grants 1 Citation and deals one Citation Die force damage.
11Priority Vehicle — maintain 3 Lanes. You and one ally chosen at the start of your turn may move through hostile creature spaces while in a Green Lane (spaces are difficult terrain). The first weapon/cursed attack after moving at least 20 feet in a Lane deals one Citation Die extra damage.
18Domain Expansion: Metropolitan Gridlock — 10 CE. The Domain becomes a luminous road network. At initiative count 20 choose Green, Yellow, or Red as the global Signal until the next count 20. Every hostile creature that moved 10+ feet since the prior count gains 1 Citation and takes one Citation Die force damage; this automatic Citation/damage is the sure-hit. Your own Lanes remain available for their normal effects.

Technique Feats

Emergency Lane

Reaction when an ally within 30 feet is targeted by an attack: spend 2 CE to create a temporary Green Lane from them 15 feet in any direction and let them move 5 feet. This movement can cause the attack to lose range.

Traffic Camera

You know the current speed and number of Citations of every creature standing in one of your Lanes.

Repeat Offender

When a creature reaches 3 Citations, your attacks against it ignore half cover until its Citations are spent.

Table Rulings

  • A Lane is a technique zone, not physical paint. It can follow flat/sloped surfaces but cannot float unsupported before 18th level.
  • Forced movement never grants Yellow Citations because it is not voluntary. It may still cross Red and trigger the save.
  • A creature cannot be given more than 3 Citations; excess Citations are lost.
Support / Debuffer / Investigator

Osmothèque

Distill emotions, memories, and biological traces into supernatural perfumes. Prepare formulas before battle, then deploy them as buffs, debuffs, tracking tools, and sensory tricks.

High TierScent Notes
RoleSupport / Debuffer / Investigator
Power tierHigh
ScalingScent Die: d6 → d8 (6th) → d10 (11th) → d12 (18th)
Main resourceScent Notes

Core Gameplay Loop

Scent Notes

After each long rest, prepare Scent Notes equal to your proficiency bonus + CE modifier (minimum 3). A Note is spent when you use a Formula. You may recover one Note during a short rest by spending 2 CE and ten minutes distilling nearby scents.

Scent Die

Your Scent Die scales d6 at 1st, d8 at 6th, d10 at 11th, d12 at 18th.

Formula — Courage Accord

Bonus Action • 1 Note + 1 CE • 30 feet. One willing creature gains temporary HP equal to Scent Die + CE modifier and may add your Scent Die to its next saving throw against frightened/charmed before the end of your next turn.

Formula — Funeral Myrrh

Action • 1 Note + 2 CE • 30 feet. One creature makes a Wisdom save. On failure subtract your Scent Die from its next attack roll or saving throw before the end of your next turn (minimum total result 1).

Formula — Hunter’s Trace

Bonus Action • 1 Note + 1 CE. Mark the scent of one creature/object you can perceive within 30 feet. For 1 hour you have advantage on checks to track it and know its direction while within 120 feet unless the trail is completely sealed.

Level Progression

LevelFeature
1Scent Notes, Courage Accord, Funeral Myrrh, Hunter’s Trace
3New Formulas — Veil of Familiarity: 1 Note + 2 CE, one creature gains advantage on Deception/Stealth checks to pass as belonging in the immediate environment for 10 minutes (does not alter appearance). Anesthetic Fog: Action, 1 Note + 2 CE, 10-foot-radius cloud for 1 round; Constitution save or speed -10 and no reactions until next turn.
6Bottled Memory — 2 Notes + 3 CE, touch a willing creature/object and capture up to 1 minute of recent sensory impression. Releasing it recreates an illusory scent/sound/feeling replay in a 10-foot area for up to 1 minute. Also gain Counter-Scent: reaction, 1 Note + 2 CE, let an ally within 30 feet reroll a save against charmed/frightened/poisoned.
11Synesthetic Poison — Action, 2 Notes + 4 CE, 30 feet. Constitution save; failure takes 2 Scent Dice poison/psychic damage and choose blinded, deafened, or speed halved until the end of its next turn. Success halves damage and avoids condition. Group Accord: Courage Accord may affect up to PB creatures if you spend one Note per target.
18Maximum Technique: Perfect Accord — Action, 10 CE + 3 Notes, 1 minute, 30-foot aura. When activated choose Courage, Dread, and Clarity. Allies entering/start in aura gain one Scent Die temp HP once per round and advantage against frightened/charmed. Hostiles entering/start make Wisdom save or subtract one Scent Die from their first attack/save that round. You and allies also have advantage on Perception/Investigation relying on senses.

Technique Feats

Perfumer’s Case

Gain 2 additional Scent Notes after a long rest and one additional prepared bottle can survive indefinitely until used.

Cold Extraction

Core Gameplay Loop

Claim Tickets

You have Claim Tickets equal to your proficiency bonus, regained after a short or long rest. Your Claim Die scales d6/d8/d10/d12 at levels 1/6/11/18.

Archive

Action • 1 CE. Touch an unattended, nonliving object that no creature within 60 feet currently possesses, wears, wields, or actively claims. Store it in your Archive. At 1st level, maximum size is Medium and the Archive holds items equal to 5 + your sorcerer level. Retrieving a stored object within your reach is a bonus action and 1 CE.

Loaner — Weapon

Bonus Action • 1 Ticket + 1 CE. Create a temporary cursed weapon for 1 minute. Choose melee (1d8, versatile 1d10) or ranged (1d8, range 60/180). You are proficient and may use your CE ability modifier for attack/damage. The Loaner vanishes if another creature ends its turn holding it.

Loaner — Barricade

Action • 1 Ticket + 1 CE. Create a 5-by-5-foot panel within 10 feet, AC 15, HP equal to 10 + 3×sorcerer level; it grants half cover and lasts 1 minute.

Loaner — Restraint

Action • 1 Ticket + 2 CE • 30 feet. Make a CE attack. Hit: the target is grappled by cursed straps/chains (escape check vs CE save DC) until it escapes or 1 minute.

Technique Feats

Organized Shelving

Archive capacity increases by 10 objects and retrieving a mundane archived object costs 0 CE once per turn.

No Receipt Needed

When you successfully disarm a creature, you may Archive the dropped eligible object immediately without using your reaction.

Premium Loaner

Choose Weapon, Barricade, or Restraint after each long rest. The first time each short rest you create that Loaner, it costs no Claim Ticket.

Table Rulings

  • “Unclaimed” is a mechanical possession test, not a philosophical loophole. A sword in an enemy’s hand is claimed; a sword they intentionally throw away is not.
  • The Archive does not duplicate objects and does not pause living creatures, corpses, or ongoing time-sensitive effects unless the GM rules the item itself is nonliving and storable.
  • A Domain’s Misplaced effect suppresses use but does not transfer ownership or automatically Archive the item.
