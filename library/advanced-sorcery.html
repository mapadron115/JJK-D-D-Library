Advanced Sorcery Systems

This compact web reference exposes the repaired canon used by every dossier in the library. The complete source guide remains part of the project archive.

Domain Expansion

Complete Domain baseline. Full-turn action, 20 CE, 60-foot enclosed radius, up to 1 minute, Technique Focus. A technique may explicitly override the radius, cost, shape, or timing.
Sure-hit procedure. Ordinary attacks inside a Domain still roll. The Domain's unique autonomous sure-hit resolves at initiative count 20. Rule-based Domains instead enforce the procedural law printed in their own dossier.
Counterplay. Domain clashes, Simple Domain, Hollow Wicker Basket, barrier refinement, explicit anti-domain abilities, and technique-specific procedures remain valid. Early Domains are legal when their own Binding Vows justify them.

Reverse Cursed Technique

Healing Reserve: 10 × total character level. Reserve represents tissue that can be rebuilt and returns on a long rest.
No passive healing pulse. RCT is actively invoked under the revised procedure; CE controls burst speed while Healing Reserve controls total reconstructable damage.
Injuries: severe limb and organ damage follows the campaign injury thresholds. RCT can repair them only when the character has the required reserve, CE, access, and time specified by the injury rules.

Black Flash

Preserved Project V3 procedure: score a qualifying critical hit, then roll the campaign's unmodified Black Flash confirmation d20. A natural 20 triples the completed critical damage.
Rolled weapon, unarmed, and eligible direct CE attacks can qualify. Autonomous Domain events, damage-over-time, passive hazards, and unrolled pulses cannot Black Flash.

Maximum Output

Generic Maximum Output applies only to eligible ordinary technique features under the campaign procedure. Named Maximum Techniques, Domains, Extreme Magic, Sacred Treasure Releases, and other printed pinnacle effects do not stack generic Maximum Output unless they explicitly say otherwise.

Technique Focus

Effects marked Focus occupy the shared Technique Focus slot. Opening a Domain normally ends another Focus effect unless the Domain or feature explicitly preserves it.
