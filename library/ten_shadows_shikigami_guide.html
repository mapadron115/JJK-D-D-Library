The Ten Shadows

Quick roster reference. The full campaign stat blocks use the user's CE attack bonus and save DC where specified; the subjugation ritual, not level, gates ownership.

Divine Dogs

Keen Hearing and Smell, Pack Tactics, Bite, and paired manifestation. They are the inherited starting shadows and form the basis of Totality.

Gama

Amphibious leaping toad with a long Tongue for grappling, Swallow utility, and collective-control applications.

Nue

Flying electric shikigami with Flyby, Shock attacks, water conduction, and rapid aerial extraction.

Great Serpent

Large constrictor built for sudden reach, binding, dragging, and controlling a single dangerous target.

Rabbit Escape

A swarm manifestation whose strength is diffusion, screening, concealment, distraction, and creating too many bodies to track cleanly.

Max Elephant

Heavy manifestation with enormous mass, water output, trampling presence, and bulwark utility.

Round Deer

Support shikigami centered on positive energy, healing, cleansing, and interference with hostile cursed-energy effects.

Piercing Ox

A linear-charge specialist that becomes more dangerous the farther it can run uninterrupted toward the target.

Mourn Tiger

A high-end predatory melee shadow designed around raw close-range killing pressure.

Eight-Handled Sword Divergent Sila Divine General Mahoraga

The pinnacle ritual. Mahoraga's adaptive wheel learns from phenomena it survives and forces the table to solve it before adaptation overtakes the fight. No low-level safety version exists.

Totality & inheritance

When a shadow is permanently destroyed under the technique's rules, eligible power can pass into a surviving shadow as Totality. Destruction is therefore consequential rather than a free resummon timer.
