Wyrdscript — Elder Futhark

A controller/support/utility Innate Technique that converts cursed energy into runic staves. Its strength is not raw specialization: it is possessing a dangerous magical vocabulary and knowing which word is worth writing.

Rune Die: d6 levels 1–5 · d8 levels 6–10 · d10 levels 11–17 · d12 levels 18–20.
Progression: level 1 choose six Tier I/II runes. At level 3 all 24 Elder Futhark runes and anomalous Wyrd become available. Level 6 unlocks Bindrunes; level 9 Reading of the Three; level 11 Living Script; level 18 Domain Expansion.

  Wyrd · Blank Rune

Wyrd belongs to no ætt and is explicitly a modern/anomalous addition rather than a historical Elder Futhark rune. It costs 3 CE and invokes the technique's mystery table rather than a fixed historical meaning.

Bindrunes · level 6

Combine two runes into one inscription. Pay both rune costs +1 CE. The result must express a coherent interaction between the two printed rune functions; Bindrunes combine vocabulary rather than granting arbitrary spell invention.

Reading of the Three · level 9

A noncombat divination procedure: What Was · What Is · What May Be. The reading supplies bounded information and a Foretold Die rather than perfect prophecy.

Living Script · level 11

Your inscriptions become faster, more mobile, and capable of persisting through combat under the full technique procedure.

Domain Expansion · Hall of the Norns: Wyrd Written Before Birth

The level-18 rule Domain turns runic fate into declared procedural law. It follows the campaign's initiative-count-20 Domain convention rather than making ordinary rune attacks automatically hit.
