Shared JJK 5e Rules

Technique DC

8 + proficiency bonus + your chosen Cursed Energy ability modifier unless a feature states otherwise. Technique attacks use proficiency bonus + that modifier.

Scaling Die

Every dossier has a named Technique Die: d6 at levels 1–5, d8 at 6–10, d10 at 11–17, d12 at 18–20. When an ability says “X Technique Dice,” use the current die.

Domains

Domains use the campaign barrier, clash, anti-domain, and burnout rules. Ordinary attacks inside a Domain still roll normally. A listed initiative-count-20 event is the Domain’s sole sure-hit unless the dossier expressly identifies a rule-Domain exception.

Maximum Output

Generic Maximum Output may improve ordinary technique features under the campaign rules. It does not stack onto a named Maximum Technique or Domain unless the feature explicitly says it can.

SCP-173 inspired • Tier III · Apex

Blind Spot

Tax enemy reactions to maintain observation, then explode through any lapse in line of sight with impossible movement and a punishing follow-up strike.

RoleAssassin / movement controllerSourceSCP Wiki

Core Engine — Observation Protocol

  • Blink Die: d6 → d8 → d10 → d12 using the shared scaling table.
  • Observe. At the start of its turn, a hostile creature within 60 feet that can see you may spend its reaction to Observe you until the start of its next turn. Observation ends early if it loses line of sight, becomes blinded or incapacitated, or an effect suppresses its senses. A creature cannot Observe if it has no reaction available.
  • Source Rule. While at least one Observer has direct line of sight to you, you cannot use Blind Spot Step, Blink Reflex, or another feature in this technique that says it requires an unobserved interval. Your ordinary movement and class features still work; this is the playable translation of SCP-173 being immobile under direct observation.
  • Blind Spot Step. Bonus action, 1 CE. If no hostile Observer can currently see you, teleport up to 20 feet to an unoccupied space you can perceive. This movement provokes no opportunity attacks.
  • Snap Strike. Once per turn, the first melee weapon or unarmed hit you land after a Blind Spot Step deals +1 Blink Die force damage. The target makes a Constitution save; on a failure it cannot take reactions until the end of its next turn.

Progression

1Observation ProtocolObserve, Blind Spot Step, and Snap Strike establish the reaction-tax loop.
3Blink ReflexOnce per round when the final Observer currently watching you stops Observing, you may use your reaction to teleport up to half your Blind Spot Step range without spending CE.
6Peripheral FailureAction, 2 CE: up to PB Observers within 60 feet make Constitution saves. On a failure their Observation ends and they cannot Observe you again until the end of their next turn. Snap Strike becomes +2 Blink Dice.
11Impossible IntervalBlind Spot Step range becomes 60 feet. Once on your turn you may pay its CE cost and use it immediately before one attack in your Attack action instead of using a bonus action. Snap Strike becomes +3 Blink Dice.

Domain Expansion — Containment Chamber: DON’T BLINK

Expansion

Full-turn action, 20 CE, enclosed 175-foot-radius Domain for 1 minute. Every hostile creature knows exactly what Observation means when the barrier forms.

Biological Blink — sole sure-hit

At initiative count 20, every chosen hostile creature’s Observation immediately ends and it cannot Observe you until the start of its next turn. A protected creature instead makes a Constitution save; on a success it may continue Observing if it already was.

Blink Windows

Gain one Blink Window for each hostile affected by Biological Blink, maximum PB. Before the next initiative count 20, spend one Window instead of CE when using Blind Spot Step. You may spend no more than one Window per turn or reaction.

Accuracy remains yours

Neck attacks, unarmed strikes, weapon attacks, and Snap Strike still require their normal attack rolls. The Domain guarantees the lapse in observation, not the kill.

Technique Feats

Blind Spot

Blink Hunter

When a creature that is currently Observing you spends or loses its reaction for any other reason, its Observation immediately ends. Once per round this may trigger Blink Reflex.

Blind Spot, level 3

Concrete Idol

If you willingly move 0 feet on your turn, you may use a reaction before your next turn to reduce one damage instance by PB Blink Dice + CE modifier.

Blind Spot, level 6

No Peripheral Vision

If exactly one Observer can see you, you may attempt Blind Spot Step by paying +2 CE. That Observer makes a Wisdom save; on failure it experiences a micro-blink and ceases Observing before the Step resolves.

Blind Spot, level 11

Neck-Line Anatomy

Once per short rest when Snap Strike hits and the target fails its Constitution save by 5 or more, it is stunned until the end of your next turn instead of merely losing reactions.

Table Rulings

  • Observation is a reaction commitment, not passive 360-degree D&D vision. This is what makes the SCP rule tactical instead of shutting the user off whenever an enemy exists.
  • Invisible creatures can Observe only if they can actually perceive you. Blindsight, truesight, cameras, familiars, and similar senses count if they provide direct perception.
  • Blind Spot Step is teleportation for rules purposes. It does not pass through anti-teleportation effects.
  • The technique never grants a literal instant neck snap against a healthy combatant; high-level lockdown is represented by Snap Strike riders and the Domain.
SCP-096 inspired • Tier IV · Special Grade

Shamebound Pursuit

Make seeing your face a terrible commitment: Witnesses become valid quarry, distance builds Fury, and every obstacle between you and the quarry becomes part of the attack.

RoleSingle-target berserker / pursuit strikerSourceSCP Wiki

Core Engine — Witness and Pursuit

  • Pursuit Die: d6 → d8 → d10 → d12.
  • Covered Face. You normally keep your face covered. A creature may declare that it averts its gaze from you; while doing so it cannot become a Witness, but has disadvantage on sight-based attacks and Perception checks involving you and cannot use a sight-dependent reaction against you.
  • Witness. As a bonus action you may reveal your face. Any creature within 60 feet with direct sight that is not averting its gaze becomes a Witness for 24 hours. There is no save; seeing the face is the trigger. A Witness remains marked even if it later closes its eyes or leaves your sight.
  • Pursuit. Bonus action, 1 CE: choose one Witness as your Quarry. Until Pursuit ends, you know the Quarry’s direction and approximate distance while on the same plane, gain +10 feet × PB speed, ignore nonmagical difficult terrain while moving toward it, and have advantage on Strength checks to break obstacles between you and it.
  • Fury. At the end of your turn, if the Quarry remains more than 10 feet away and you spent at least half your movement moving toward it, gain 1 Fury, maximum PB. If you willingly move away from the Quarry without being forced, lose all Fury. On a melee hit against the Quarry, spend any amount of Fury to add that many Pursuit Dice to the damage.

Domain Expansion — Face in Every Frame

Expansion

Full-turn action, 20 CE, enclosed 175-foot-radius Domain for 1 minute. Every reflective surface, sign, screen, and wall carries an impossible fragment of your face.

Compulsory Recognition — sole sure-hit

At initiative count 20, every chosen hostile creature becomes a Witness whether or not it is averting its gaze. A protected creature makes a Wisdom save and avoids becoming a Witness on success.

Unbroken Hunt

At initiative count 20 you may also choose one Witness as Quarry without an action and set your Fury to at least half PB, rounded up. If that Quarry drops to 0 HP, you may immediately choose another Witness without ending Pursuit.

No automatic execution

The Domain guarantees recognition and pursuit state. Your attacks still roll normally; the source’s inevitability is represented by tracking, speed, Fury, and obstacle destruction.

Technique Feats

Shamebound Pursuit

Grief-Fueled Momentum

Once per round when your Quarry damages you, gain 1 Fury after that damage resolves.

Four Pixels

Unseen Photograph

Your cursed face imprints may be as small as a four-pixel detail. A creature must actually perceive the detail to become a Witness; merely possessing the image is not enough.

Shamebound Pursuit, level 6

No Rest Until Silence

When your Quarry drops to 0 HP, retain up to half your current Fury until the end of your next turn. If you choose another Witness as Quarry before then, transfer that Fury.

Shamebound Pursuit, level 11

Inhuman Acceleration

Once per short rest, when you start your turn at least 60 feet from the Quarry, you may Dash without an action and your movement that turn may cross liquid surfaces and vertical walls.

Table Rulings

  • Witness is informationally persistent but not a death sentence. The user still spends actions, movement, CE, and attack rolls to reach the target.
  • Averting gaze is real counterplay. A creature that averts cannot use effects requiring a clear look at the user.
  • Artistic drawings do not trigger Witness unless produced by Four Pixels; ordinary photos, recordings, mirrors, and live video do if the face is actually visible.
  • Pursuit bonuses apply only against the chosen Quarry. This prevents the technique from becoming generic permanent super-speed.
SCP-049 inspired • Tier IV · Special Grade

Pestilence Doctrine

Diagnose a target, accumulate Symptoms, cash those Symptoms into a brutal “Cure,” then turn the dead into crude Patients that extend your action economy without multiplying attacks.

RoleDebuffer / executioner / corpse-summonerSourceSCP Wiki

Core Engine — Symptoms

  • Diagnosis Die: d6 → d8 → d10 → d12.
  • Clinical Examination. Bonus action, no CE: choose one creature you can perceive within 30 feet. Learn whether it is above or below half HP, whether it is poisoned or diseased, and whether its current CE is above or below half its maximum. You have advantage on Medicine checks concerning that creature for 10 minutes.
  • Clinical Touch. Once per turn after you hit a creature with a melee weapon, unarmed strike, or CE attack, spend 1 CE to give it 1 Symptom. A creature may hold up to your PB Symptoms; they last 1 minute after the most recent application.
  • Symptomatic Decline. For every 2 Symptoms a creature has, reduce healing it receives by one Diagnosis Die and subtract 1 from Constitution saves against this technique. Healing reduction cannot reduce healing below 0.
  • The Cure. Starting at level 3, action, 2 CE, melee CE attack. On hit, consume every Symptom on the target and deal (2 + Symptoms consumed) Diagnosis Dice + CE modifier necrotic damage. At 3+ consumed Symptoms the target cannot regain HP until the start of your next turn. If you consume the target’s maximum possible Symptoms while it is at or below one-quarter of its maximum HP, it makes a Constitution save after the damage; on failure it drops to 0 HP.

Domain Expansion — Ward of the Final Physician

Expansion

Full-turn action, 20 CE, enclosed 175-foot-radius hospital/operating-theater Domain for 1 minute.

Perfect Diagnosis — sole sure-hit

At initiative count 20, each chosen hostile creature automatically gains 2 Symptoms, up to its cap. A protected creature makes a Constitution save, gaining no Symptoms on success.

Sterile Field

Clinical Examination works at any range inside the barrier. Once on each of your turns, you may apply one Symptom to a creature you can see as a bonus action for 1 CE; this is a normal technique activation, not a sure-hit.

Operating Staff

Patients in the Domain gain +10 feet speed and may Help without being commanded. Their attacks still obey the one-Patient-attack-per-round limit.

Technique Feats

Pestilence Doctrine

House Call

Clinical Touch and The Cure gain 15-foot reach through a spectral gloved hand. This does not let you pass through total cover.

Clinical Examination

Second Opinion

After examining a creature, your next successful Clinical Touch against it before the end of your next turn applies 2 Symptoms instead of 1. Once per creature per short rest.

Crude Surgery

Field Surgeon

Outside combat, Crude Surgery takes 1 minute instead of 10. In combat, a corpse you personally reduced to 0 HP may be made a Patient as a full-turn action for 4 CE even before level 11.

Pestilence Doctrine, level 11

Humoral Imbalance

A creature with 4+ Symptoms also has its speed reduced by 10 feet and cannot take reactions after it fails a Constitution save against your technique until the start of its next turn.

Table Rulings

  • Symptoms are a technique mark, not a mundane disease. Poison immunity does not prevent them unless an effect specifically blocks cursed-technique marks.
  • The Cure dropping a creature to 0 HP is not instant death. Normal death saves, stabilization, and execution rules still apply unless the creature is an NPC that the GM resolves immediately.
  • Patients retain no class features, techniques, spellcasting, memories, or equipment attunement from the corpse.
  • Only one Patient can make an attack per round unless a feature explicitly overrides the shared command rule.
SCP-106 inspired • Tier IV · Special Grade

Corrosive Threshold

Infect surfaces and prey with Corrosion, turn those marks into doors, then drag sufficiently degraded targets into a private hunting space where the geometry belongs to you.

RoleTerrain controller / abducting duelistSourceSCP Wiki

Core Engine — Corrosion

  • Corrosion Die: d6 → d8 → d10 → d12.
  • Corrosive Touch. Action or replacement for one attack, 1 CE, melee CE attack. Hit: 1 Corrosion Die + CE modifier necrotic damage and 1 Corrosion Mark. Damage increases to 2 dice at level 6, 3 at 11, and 4 at 18. A creature may hold up to 3 Marks for 1 minute.
  • Predigestion. Each Corrosion Mark reduces the creature’s speed by 5 feet and grants you +1 on Athletics checks to grapple or shove it, maximum +3. This does not reduce AC.
  • Surface Corrosion. As part of touching a solid unattended surface, spend 1 CE to place a Surface Mark on a 5-foot section. Maintain up to PB + CE modifier Surface Marks for 10 minutes. Corrosive Touch deals maximum damage to mundane unattended objects.
  • Threshold Walk. Bonus action, 1 CE: enter a Surface Mark within 5 feet and emerge from another Surface Mark within 30 feet. This is teleportation; you leave black residue but do not damage creatures merely by passing through.

Domain Expansion — House Beneath Every Surface

Expansion

Full-turn action, 20 CE, enclosed 175-foot-radius Domain for 1 minute. Its halls and rooms overlap the battlefield; every solid surface counts as one of your Surface Marks.

Predigestion Tide — sole sure-hit

At initiative count 20, every chosen hostile creature gains 1 Corrosion Mark and is pulled up to 10 feet toward the nearest solid surface. If it already had 3 Marks, it instead becomes restrained by sinking residue until the next initiative count 20. A protected creature makes a Strength save and avoids both effects on success.

Every wall is a mouth

Threshold Walk may begin and end at any solid surface in the Domain and costs no CE once on each of your turns. Carrying a willing creature costs 1 CE.

Pocket-space supremacy

Rotting World costs 3 CE inside the Domain. A target still receives its normal save; the Domain does not automatically abduct it.

Technique Feats

Corrosive Threshold

Black Mucus

A Surface Mark creates a 5-foot patch of difficult terrain for enemies. A creature that falls prone in it cannot stand without spending an additional 5 feet of movement.

Predatory Emergence

Predator Beneath

After Threshold Walk, you may remain partially submerged until the start of your next turn. You have half cover but your speed is 0 and you cannot make opportunity attacks while submerged.

Corrosive Touch, level 6

Liquefying Grip

When you apply the third Corrosion Mark to a creature, it cannot take reactions until the end of its next turn and you may attempt to grapple it as part of the same attack.

Corrosive Threshold, level 11

Recall Protocol

Once per short rest when damage would reduce you below one-quarter HP, use your reaction and 2 CE to teleport to any Surface Mark within 120 feet after the damage resolves.

Containment Counterplay

  • Sudden intense light: when an effect comparable to daylight or a blinding flash catches you while emerging from a surface, you must succeed on a Constitution save against the source’s DC or be forced to the nearest legal space and unable to Threshold Walk until the start of your next turn.
  • Lead or liquid layers: a Surface Mark separated from you by a substantial lead-lined layer or continuous body of liquid costs +2 CE to use as a Threshold Walk destination.
  • Complex layered structures: the GM may rule that several disconnected layered materials count as multiple thresholds rather than one. This is counterplay, not permanent immunity to the technique.
SCP-939 inspired • Tier III · Apex

With Many Voices

Archive perfect voices, breathe away short-term memory, lure confused enemies toward false cries, and convert Lost Moments into reliable ambush damage.

RoleAmbusher / memory debuffer / scoutSourceSCP Wiki

Core Engine — Voiceprints and Lost Moments

  • Echo Die: d6 → d8 → d10 → d12.
  • Voiceprint Archive. After hearing a creature speak a complete sentence within 60 feet, you may record its Voiceprint without an action. Maintain PB Voiceprints; recording another replaces one. You can reproduce an archived voice perfectly.
  • Thrown Voice. Bonus action, 1 CE: cause one Voiceprint to originate from any point within 60 feet for up to 1 minute. A suspicious listener may use an action for Insight against your CE DC; success reveals that the sound is anomalous, not who created it.
  • Amnestic Breath. Action, 2 CE, 15-foot cone. Creatures make Constitution saves; on failure gain 1 Lost Moment, maximum 3. A creature outside your amnestic effects may repeat the save at the end of each turn, removing 1 Lost Moment on success.
  • Lost Moment 1: the creature cannot take reactions against you or your auditory decoys. 2: it has disadvantage on Perception and Insight checks involving you and cannot benefit from Help on attacks against you. 3: whenever you move at least 10 feet, you become hidden from that creature until you attack, speak in your natural voice, or it succeeds on a Search action against your CE DC.
  • Predatory Strike. Once per turn when you hit a creature with at least 1 Lost Moment while you are hidden from it or it cannot react to you, add 1 Echo Die damage of the attack’s type. This becomes 2 dice at level 6, 3 at 11, and 4 at 18.

3Troglobitic SensesGain blindsight 30 feet, a climb speed equal to walking speed, and advantage on Stealth in dim light or darkness. Bright light does not damage you but prevents advantage from this feature.

On failure it must use its reaction, if available, to move up to half its speed toward that sound by the safest route. Predatory Strike becomes +2 Echo Dice.

Domain Expansion — Corridor Without Memory

Expansion

Full-turn action, 20 CE, enclosed 175-foot-radius maze of dark bio-containment corridors for 1 minute.

Memory Break — sole sure-hit

At initiative count 20, each chosen hostile creature gains 1 Lost Moment, up to 3. A protected creature makes a Constitution save and gains none on success.

Voices everywhere

Thrown Voice may originate from any point in the barrier, requires no CE once per turn, and may use any Voiceprint you have ever archived during the current day.

Predator remains rolled

Bites, weapon attacks, ambushes, and Predatory Strike still use normal attack rolls. The Domain guarantees memory disruption, not damage.

Technique Feats

With Many Voices

Perfect Voiceprint

Voiceprint capacity becomes PB + CE modifier. You may also reproduce nonverbal sounds made by archived creatures, such as breathing, crying, or footsteps.

Troglobitic Senses

Dark Hunter

Your blindsight becomes 60 feet in darkness. Once per turn after moving at least 20 feet while hidden, add one Echo Die to your next Athletics or Acrobatics check before the end of the turn.

Amnestic Breath, level 6

No New Memories

A creature that fails Amnestic Breath by 5 or more gains 2 Lost Moments instead of 1. Once a creature suffers this effect, it cannot do so again until after a short or long rest.

With Many Voices, level 11

Pack Echo

When an ally within 30 feet attacks a creature with 2+ Lost Moments, use your reaction and 1 CE to make the target hear the ally from the wrong direction. That attack gains advantage; this does not make you hidden.

Table Rulings

  • Lost Moments do not erase campaign facts or force players to forget roleplay information. They model temporary inability to form reliable new combat memories.
  • Blindsight represents heat, airflow, vibration, and other nonvisual hunting senses; it does not see through solid walls.
  • Voiceprints cannot duplicate a verbal component, cursed chant, command word, or identity lock well enough to activate another creature’s supernatural feature unless the feature specifically accepts recordings.
  • Wind, sealed breathing gear, or effects that block inhaled agents may provide advantage or immunity against Amnestic Breath at the GM’s discretion.
SCP-682 inspired • Tier V · Calamity

Adaptive Hatred

Survive something once, record what hurt you, and spend limited Adaptation Slots deciding which threats your body is allowed to stop caring about.

RoleAdaptive tank / bruiserSourceSCP Wiki

Core Engine — Adaptation Data

  • Adaptation Die: d6 → d8 → d10 → d12.
  • Threat Tag. A Tag is one exact damage type, one named condition, or one recurring environmental hazard. “Fire damage,” “restrained,” and “vacuum” are valid; “this enemy” and “all cursed techniques” are not.
  • Exposure Record. When you take damage or fail a save that imposes a condition, use your reaction and 1 CE to record one relevant Threat Tag and gain 1 Data in that Tag, maximum 3. You can gain at most 1 Data in the same Tag per round. Data lasts until your next long rest.
  • Install Adaptation. Bonus action, 1 CE, spend 1 Data and fill an Adaptation Slot until you replace it or finish a long rest. At level 1 you have 1 Slot. For a damage Tag, gain resistance to that damage type. For a condition Tag, gain advantage on saves against that condition and reduce any duration measured in rounds by 1, minimum 1. Environmental Tags grant an appropriate survival trait such as a climb/swim speed, water breathing, or tolerance to extreme temperature, subject to GM approval.
  • Biological Rule. Installed Adaptations are internal biology and do not use Technique Focus. You may replace one as a bonus action by paying the normal install cost and Data.

Progression

LevelFeatureRules
1Exposure / AdaptationRecord threats and maintain one active defensive adaptation.
3Regenerative HateOnce per round when you take damage that matches an installed damage Adaptation and you are below half HP, regain 1 Adaptation Die + CE modifier HP at the start of your next turn. This healing cannot trigger from self-inflicted damage.
6Reactive MorphologyAdaptation Slots become 2. Each installed Adaptation also grants one morphology: +10 feet speed, climb speed, swim speed, +5-foot melee reach, +1 AC, or +1 Adaptation Die damage once per turn with a natural/unarmed attack. The same morphology cannot be chosen twice.

Force, psychic, radiant, necrotic, soul, spatial, conceptual, and Domain-derived damage instead remains resistance and lets you reduce one matching damage instance per round by PB Adaptation Dice. A standard condition Tag may become immunity, but never unconscious/incapacitated from 0 HP or a Domain’s unique rule.

Maximum Technique — Abhorrent Evolution

Activation

Level 18, bonus action, 10 CE. Consume all stored Data in at least two different Tags. For 1 minute, gain two temporary Adaptation Slots and may immediately install one Basic Adaptation for each consumed Tag without further CE.

Predatory body

Become Large if space permits, gain +10 feet speed, and your unarmed strikes deal an additional Adaptation Die.

Cost of overgrowth

When the Maximum ends, lose every temporary Slot and all Data consumed to activate it. You gain one level of exhaustion and cannot record Data until the end of your next turn.

No Domain required

Adaptive Hatred intentionally uses its level-18 power budget on biological survival and a named Maximum rather than inventing a barrier that the source concept does not need.

Technique Feats

Adaptive Hatred

Retained Mutation

Choose one Threat Tag when you finish a long rest. Preserve 1 Data in that Tag instead of losing all of it.

Reactive Morphology

Hostile Evolution

Once per turn after a creature damages you with a Tag for which you have an installed Adaptation, your next hit against that creature before the end of your next turn deals +1 Adaptation Die damage.

Adaptive Hatred, level 6

Keter Genome

Gain +1 Adaptation Slot. Installing or replacing an Adaptation in this bonus Slot costs +1 CE.

Adaptive Hatred, level 11

Consume and Rebuild

During a short rest you may consume or incorporate a meaningful quantity of organic or inorganic matter. Regain PB Adaptation Dice HP and gain 1 Data for one environmental or material Threat Tag that the consumed matter reasonably demonstrates; GM adjudicates the Tag.

Table Rulings

  • You must actually experience a threat to record it. Seeing fire, reading about a Domain, or touching a harmless sample does not generate Data.
  • One enormous attack still provides only 1 Data because Exposure Record is a reaction. Multiattack can generate additional Data only across later rounds.
  • Hardened immunity is intentionally powerful but expensive in Data and Slots. Exotic categories remain resistance rather than immunity so soul, spatial, conceptual, and Domain threats still matter.
  • Adaptations disappear on long rest unless a feat says otherwise. This prevents the campaign from turning into a permanent list of every immunity ever encountered.
SCP-035 inspired • Tier IV · Special Grade

The Possessive Mask

Wear one body until it rots, tempt another into accepting the mask, then move your consciousness across humanoid hosts while keeping one shared HP, CE, and action economy.

RoleSocial controller / body-hopper / infiltratorSourceSCP Wiki

Core Engine — Mask, Temptation, and Hosts

  • Mask Die: d6 → d8 → d10 → d12.
  • Prime Host. Your original body is your Prime Host. Your consciousness, CE, HP, class features, and technique belong to the mask-bound soul, not to whichever body currently wears it.
  • Manifest Mask. Bonus action, 1 CE. A porcelain mask forms on your face. While worn by your Prime Host, gain telepathy 30 feet and proficiency in Deception or Persuasion; if already proficient, add PB again to checks with that skill.
  • Corrosive Secretion. Once per turn after a melee hit, grapple, or touch against an object, spend 1 CE to deal +1 Mask Die acid or necrotic damage. Unattended mundane objects take maximum damage from this die.
  • Temptation. Action, 1 CE: a creature within 30 feet that can see or hear the mask makes a Wisdom save. On failure it gains 1 Temptation, maximum 3, for 1 minute. At 1 it has disadvantage on Insight against you; at 2 its speed is halved when willingly moving farther from the mask; at 3 it becomes a valid conscious target for Host Transfer. At the end of each turn it may repeat the Wisdom save, removing 1 Temptation on success.
  • Shared Vitality. Possessing another body never creates a second HP or CE pool. Damage to your current Host damages your normal HP.

Host Integrity

Living Host

Host Integrity = PB + CE modifier + the host’s Constitution modifier, minimum 3. Lose 1 Integrity at the end of each of your turns while in a non-Prime living Host.

Using a Host

Use the Host’s size, STR, DEX, CON, mundane senses, movement modes, and natural weapons. Keep your own INT, WIS, CHA, HP, CE, PB, proficiencies, class features, and Innate Technique. You do not gain the Host’s class features, spellcasting, Innate Technique, attunements, or legendary actions.

Structural collapse

At 0 Integrity the Host can no longer move. Your consciousness immediately returns to the living Prime Host if it exists and is on the same plane; otherwise the mask falls free and you are incapacitated until a valid Host wears it.

Prime Host death

If the Prime Host dies while you are elsewhere, you do not immediately die. You become dependent on the mask and must occupy a valid Host to act. Restoring or replacing a true Prime Host becomes a campaign-level objective rather than free immortality.

When you voluntarily leave a Host, you may choose another valid Host within 60 feet instead of returning to the Prime Host. Each transfer still pays its normal CE cost unless a feature says otherwise.

Refining Clockworks

Feed gear into a portable cursed clockwork, choose how aggressively the machine interprets “refinement,” and decide whether one more Very Fine cycle is worth the Risk.

RoleArtificer / equipment support / gamblerSourceSCP Wiki

Core Engine — Portable Clockworks

  • Refinement Die: d6 → d8 → d10 → d12.
  • Portable Clockworks. Action, 2 CE: manifest adjacent Intake and Output frames for 10 minutes. Only one set may exist. At level 1 they accept Tiny or Small nonliving objects that are not being worn or carried by an unwilling creature.
  • Scrap. Rough and Coarse processes generate Scrap, a technique resource with a cap of PB + CE modifier. Unspent Scrap vanishes after a long rest.
  • Rough. Action: destroy a mundane input and gain Scrap by size: Tiny 1, Small 2, Medium 3, Large 4. Cursed or magical objects do not automatically yield Scrap and may resist destruction at the GM’s discretion.
  • Coarse. Action: disassemble an input into usable components and gain 1 less Scrap than Rough, minimum 1, or remove one mundane mechanical property such as armor plating, a lock, a blade, or a wheel while preserving the remainder.
  • 1:1. Action, 1 CE: turn a mundane input into another mundane object of similar size, material value, and complexity. It cannot create currency, ammunition in bulk, explosives, poisons, rare components, or supernatural items from nothing.

SCP-3008 inspired • Tier IV · Special Grade Rule Domain

Infinite Retail

Manifest useful store fixtures in ordinary play, then drag the battlefield into an impossible retail interior whose real weapon is its ecology: scavenge while the lights are on, build somewhere defensible before closing, and survive the Staff when night begins.

RoleRule-Domain controller / fortification supportSourceSCP Wiki

Level 1 — Flat-Pack Catalog

  • Store Die: d6 at levels 1–5, d8 at 6–10, d10 at 11–17, and d12 at 18+.
  • Flat-Pack Manifestation. Action, 1 CE: create one Merchandise object in an unoccupied space within 30 feet. Maintain up to PB Merchandise at once for 10 minutes; creating beyond the cap dismisses one of your choice. Dismiss your own Merchandise as a bonus action.
  • Shelf Wall. Large 10-by-5-foot object, AC 13, HP = 5 × level. It provides cover normally.
  • Shopping Cart. Medium object, AC 13, HP = 3 × level. A creature may push it while moving. After pushing it at least 15 feet in a straight line, replace one attack with a CE attack against a creature ahead of it: 1 Store Die + CE modifier bludgeoning and a Strength save or prone.
  • Display Fixture. Occupies a 10-foot square. Enemies treat the square as difficult terrain; allies you designate can climb or move across it normally.
  • Floor Lamp / Sign. Medium object, AC 12, HP = 2 × level, bright light 30 feet and dim light 30 more. Creatures within 10 feet have advantage on Perception checks to locate hidden creatures that are not invisible.

Level 3 — Domain Expansion: Infinite Retail — Closing Shift

Early Rule-Domain Binding Vow

Full-turn action, 6 CE, enclosed 60-foot-radius Domain for up to 1 minute. The Domain's guaranteed laws never deal damage themselves. That non-lethal limitation is the Binding Vow that permits level-3 access and the reduced CE cost. Ordinary attacks, techniques, hazards, and Staff can still cause damage normally.

Everyone Is Stranded

There is no Employee/Customer hierarchy. You and every other creature inside are Stranded. You are subject to the same store cycle and can be attacked by Staff at night. Your advantage is that your technique understands the building: you always know the current Store Phase, the locations of Store Nodes and Settlements, and which objects are your Merchandise.

Impossible Interior — continuous Domain law

Ordinary doors, emergency exits, and attempts to simply walk to the barrier's edge turn into additional aisles and showrooms. They do not leave the Domain. Barrier clashes, anti-domain techniques, teleportation that explicitly crosses Domains, and destruction of the barrier still use the shared Domain rules; Infinite Retail does not make itself unbreakable.

Store Nodes

When the Domain forms, place three indestructible 10-foot Store Nodes at least 15 feet apart: Restaurant, Warehouse, and Showroom. They are resources, not checkpoints. No creature is required to visit them in any order.

Open-Hours Scavenge

Once during each Open Hours, a Stranded creature adjacent to any Store Node may use an action to Scavenge. At the Restaurant, heal 1 Store Die + PB HP. At the Warehouse, create one 10-by-5-foot Barricade within 5 feet (AC 13, HP = 3 × caster level). At the Showroom, repair one unattended object or Merchandise within 10 feet for 2 Store Dice + CE modifier HP. A creature can benefit from only one Scavenge action per Open Hours.

Found a Settlement

A creature adjacent to at least two Barricades, Merchandise objects, or substantial pieces of cover may use an action to establish a 15-foot-radius Settlement centered on itself. A Settlement remains while at least one founding structure survives. Stranded creatures inside gain half cover against Staff and advantage on checks or saves against Staff grapples. Staff outside a Settlement must attack an obstructing founding structure before targeting a creature fully sheltered behind it.

  • The phase transition itself is the Domain's guaranteed rule effect. It does not make an attack roll, deal automatic damage, or force a creature through a prescribed route.

Shifting Exit

Searching Instead of Following a Route

During Open Hours, a Stranded creature may use an action to search for the anomalous exit with Intelligence (Investigation) or Wisdom (Survival) against your Technique DC. It must be able to move and perceive its surroundings.

The Store Learns Your Search

On a failure, that creature gains a cumulative +2 bonus to its next Search for an Exit check during this Domain, maximum +6. On a success, an Exit manifests in an unoccupied space within 10 feet of it until the end of its turn. Moving through it costs 5 feet of movement and removes that creature from the Domain.

No Free Caster Escape

You cannot use Search for an Exit to abandon your own Domain; you may always dismiss the Domain normally. A creature that leaves through an Exit does not end the Domain for anyone still inside.

Staff Remains — The Bad Decision That Snowballs

Remains

When a Staff is reduced to 0 HP, leave a Staff Remains marker in its space. Remains are unattended objects, AC 10, 10 HP, but destroying them only scatters them and does not remove the marker.

Attraction

At the start of each Closed Hours, for every two Staff Remains currently in the Domain, one additional Staff manifests, to a maximum of PB additional Staff. If Remains are inside or within 20 feet of a Settlement, those new Staff appear as close to that Settlement as the map legally allows.

Disposal

During Open Hours, a creature adjacent to Remains can use an action to haul one marker up to half its speed. A creature adjacent to the Warehouse may use its once-per-Open-Hours Scavenge action to dispose of one Remains marker instead of taking the normal Warehouse benefit.

Why This Matters

Killing every Staff is sometimes the worst possible strategy. A party that wins one night carelessly can create a much harder next night, while clever enemies can drag Remains toward someone else's Settlement before closing.

Rule-Domain Rulings

  • This is not a movement-sequence Domain. Store Nodes may be visited in any order or ignored entirely. They are resources, not mandatory checkpoints.
  • This is not a jackpot Domain. Store Phases are deterministic and public. The strategy comes from resource use, fortification, night survival, and manipulating Staff behavior.
  • The Domain's guaranteed laws are Impossible Interior and the Store Cycle. They change topology, availability of resources, and Staff state; they never automatically damage a creature.
  • Staff are autonomous Domain entities. Their attacks and grapples roll normally, can miss, and are not the Domain's sure-hit.
  • The caster can be attacked by Staff during Closed Hours and can worsen their own situation by leaving Staff Remains near their Settlement.
  • Attacking a passive Staff during Open Hours or Closing Warning makes only that Staff hostile to the attacker until the next phase transition.
  • Settlements do not make occupants invulnerable. Staff can destroy the structures, enter through gaps, be teleported inside, or attack anyone who is no longer sheltered behind a structure.
  • Barrier clashes, Simple Domain, Hollow Wicker Basket, Domain Amplification, barrier destruction, and Technique Burnout follow the campaign's shared rules. Search for an Exit is an additional SCP-3008-flavored escape option, not a replacement for those systems.
