JJK 5e Anomaly Codex

SCP-Inspired Techniques

Nine complete techniques translating anomaly motifs into playable JJK mechanics rather than reproducing SCP objects literally.

SCP-173 inspired · Tier III Apex

Blind Spot

Observation Protocol taxes reactions to keep you watched. Any lapse creates impossible movement and a punishing follow-up. Domain — Containment Chamber: DON'T BLINK: Biological Blink is the sole initiative-20 sure-hit.

SCP-096 inspired · Tier IV

Shamebound Pursuit

Creatures that witness your face become Quarry; distance builds Fury and obstacles become part of the pursuit. Domain — Face in Every Frame imposes compulsory recognition as its sole sure-hit.

SCP-049 inspired · Tier IV

Pestilence Doctrine

Diagnose targets, stack Symptoms, cash them into The Cure, and raise crude Patients under bounded shared-action rules. Domain — Ward of the Final Physician perfects diagnosis at initiative 20.

SCP-106 inspired · Tier IV

Corrosive Threshold

Spread Corrosion Marks, turn surfaces into entry points, and drag prey into a private pocket dimension. Domain — House Beneath Every Surface makes architecture itself hostile.

SCP-939 inspired · Tier III

With Many Voices

Store Voiceprints, create Lost Moments in enemy memory, and spend Echo Dice to exploit the confusion. Domain — Corridor Without Memory turns communication into unreliable evidence.

SCP-682 inspired · Tier V Calamity

Adaptive Hatred

Record Data/Threat Tags from what hurts you, fill adaptive slots, regenerate, and evolve targeted counters. Maximum — Abhorrent Evolution. Intentionally has no Domain.

SCP-035 inspired · Tier IV

The Possessive Mask

Tempt hosts, transfer bodies, and manage Host Integrity. Domain — Theater of the Anguished Mask makes the social bargain into a battlefield law.

SCP-914 inspired · Tier IV

Refining Clockworks

Feed objects into Portable Clockworks and choose Rough, Coarse, 1:1, Fine, or Very Fine. Scrap and output uncertainty are the balancing engine. Maximum — The Interpretation Engine.

SCP-3008 inspired · Tier IV Rule Domain

Infinite Retail

At level 3, open Infinite Retail — Closing Shift: full-turn action, 6 CE, enclosed 60-ft radius, up to 1 minute. Its Binding Vow guarantees that Domain laws themselves deal no damage. Everyone becomes Stranded; Open Hours permit scavenging and exit searches, Closed Hours make autonomous Staff hostile. Initiative 20 cycles Open Hours → Closing Warning → Closed Hours → Open Hours. Settlements, Store Nodes, Staff Remains, and Search for Exit are all real procedures rather than flavor.
