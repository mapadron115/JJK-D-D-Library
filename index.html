Rules canon

The repaired spine.

These are the decisions that keep hundreds of options speaking the same language without sanding off their extremes.

Domain Expansion

60-foot complete baseline; one true sure-hit

A normal complete Domain is a full-turn action, 20 CE, 60-foot enclosed radius and one minute unless its own text says otherwise. Ordinary attacks still roll. The autonomous sure-hit resolves at initiative count 20; procedural Domains enforce their stated law.

Domain rules →
Reverse Cursed Technique

Finite tissue, explosive emergency healing

Healing Reserve is 10 × total character level and returns on a long rest. CE governs burst speed; Reserve governs how much body can actually be rebuilt.

RCT rules →
Black Flash

Preserved Project confirmation

Qualifying critical → confirmation d20 → natural 20 triples the completed critical damage. Autonomous hazards and unrolled Domain pulses do not Black Flash.

Black Flash →
Dual Technique variant

Two techniques, one character economy

Multiclassing alone never grants another CT. Approved Dual Technique characters advance by character level but still share CE, actions, reactions, Focus and Domain constraints.
