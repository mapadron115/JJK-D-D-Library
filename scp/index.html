Document 01 · Recovered briefing

What the Office is

Research log. The following was recovered from a ████████ server during ████████████. Transcribed as found; redactions as received.

The Special Measures Office for Cursed Phenomena is an American program operating in Japan, funded through ██████████████ appropriations and answering to ██████████. Its charter fragment states the mandate plainly: "the systematic recreation of jujutsu phenomena under controlled conditions." Japan was chosen for exactly one reason — the density of cursed energy. Nowhere else on earth produces so many workable test conditions per square mile.

Nine manufactured subjects. Not exorcised curses, not inherited techniques — built. Each file below is the Office's own record: observation, hypothesis, containment procedure. Read them the way the Office wrote them, and remember that the Office writes to reassure itself.

One subject is no longer in its cell. That is why you are reading this.

Document 11 · Active thread

'The Passenger'

Subject e160, The Possessive Mask, is uncontained. The active operation carries the working designation 'The Passenger' — a two-to-three-session chapter built around civilians behaving subtly wrong, a facility that should not exist, and a mask that is always looking for its next face. Nothing in this file beyond the e160 dossier is required to run it.

The remaining eight subjects are in their cells. They are not idle. They are waiting. That is the long game — this page will grow as the table does.

❖ GM only — players stop here

GM counsel

  • One subject at a time. The subplot opens with e160 and only e160, given a full 2–3 session chapter. The other eight are background dread — mentioned in passing, glimpsed in redacted files, never deployed until the table is ready for them.
  • The cells are the point. Eight contained subjects the party knows about but cannot reach is stronger than eight fights. Let the stamps do the work: every session the party sees CONTAINED next to a name they haven't met is a session the dread compounds.
  • The party gains nothing from the lab. They encounter the Office from outside, as Jujutsu High students and employees. No gear, no techniques, no alliances come out of the facility — it is something that happens to the story, not a resource within it.
  • Keep the Office deniable and thin. No named staff, no mapped org chart, no larger conspiracy on the page. The Office exists to be glimpsed through redactions, not understood. If the party starts mapping it, you are showing too much.
  • Escalation ladder (suggested): e160 uncontained now → one contained subject shows signs of waking (your pick, when the table needs a jolt) → the Office notices the party noticing. Never more than one active subject at a time.
