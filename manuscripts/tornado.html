The Imperial Workshop

Creation as imperial prerogative: three or more spinners pool their cursed energy to make — objects, structures, repairs. It cannot destroy; it can only authorize. Authored by Qin Shi Huang, All Under Heaven.

CE Application Manuscript · Qin Shi Huang · The Imperial Workshop
The treatise

In the Emperor's hand

All under heaven is material. The mountain disagrees; the mountain has not been asked properly.

One spinner is weather. Three spinners are a workshop. When the whirlwinds join, the spin no longer deflects or carries — it decides. What the joined spin declares, the world provides: timber, stone, iron, whole walls raised from dust. My tomb guard was not carved. It was authorized.

Bring your spinners. Bring your cursed energy. The Empire provides the rest, as it always has.

The application

Tornado of Creation

One simple engine. The applications multiply.

Tornado of Creation

Ritual · 10 CE · 3 or more participants.

Three or more spinners whirl in concert, pooling cursed energy to CREATE. The ritual takes 10 minutes. The spinners may create objects, structures, or repairs with total value and scale set by the GM, guided by total CE spent (roughly one wagon-load of simple material per 10 CE). What is made is real and permanent. The Tornado cannot destroy, damage, or unmake — attempts fizzle, the CE still spent.

The Workshop Remembers Advanced

The terracotta lesson. The spinners may rebuild a destroyed nonmagical object or structure from its remnants — cracks close, shards rejoin, walls stand again. The thing rebuilt is whole, not new.

Many Hands Advanced

With six or more spinners, the Workshop produces fine work: complex mechanisms with moving parts — locks, traps, winches, waterwheels — not merely static objects. The Emperor's scale was never the point; his precision was.

Rules integration

Study

How the application is learned

Study requirement: CE Manipulation (Mastered) or better, character level 11+. Thirty days of study and an INT check vs DC 15 (downtime is an honor rule at the table — the builder tracks only that you have studied it).
Group rite: the Tornado requires three or more spinners — every participant must have studied this manuscript. A lone student knows the theory; the Workshop demands the workforce.
Not a technique: manuscripts teach CE Applications — they do not count against the two-technique limit, have no level progression, and are learned, not awakened.
Marginalia

In another hand

Sun Tzu's notes appear in every copy of the treatise.

Margin note — S.

The Emperor creates because he cannot tolerate anything he did not authorize, including the landscape. Understand this and you understand the entire art.

Margin note — S.

He is correct that creation is the greater power. He is wrong about why. Destruction is a single decision; creation is a thousand. The Workshop does not make builders. It makes accountants of possibility.

Margin note — S.

Three spinners minimum, he writes, as if the number were the teaching. The teaching is that no emperor creates alone, and every emperor pretends otherwise.
