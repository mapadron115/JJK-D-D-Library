The treatise

In the Chancellor's hand

To fly, first understand that you are already falling, and have been since birth. Every step is a negotiation with the ground. I merely teach you to decline the negotiation.

The whirlwind spins and remains. This is the lesser art, and I honor it. But the whirlwind that spins faster — that refuses the earth entirely — that is the greater teaching. The sky is empty. Be empty with it, and it will hold you the way it holds nothing: perfectly, without effort, without end.

Do not flap. Do not strive. The bird works; the cloud does not. Be the cloud.

The application

Airjitzu

One simple engine. The applications multiply.

Airjitzu

Action · 3 CE · concentration, 10 minutes.

You sustain the spin and rise. You gain a fly speed of 60 ft for up to 10 minutes (concentration). While airborne this way you are lightly obscured by the vortex — ranged attacks against you have disadvantage. If your concentration breaks, you descend safely at 60 ft per round; you take no falling damage from this descent.

Thunderhead Descent Advanced

The sky, falling on schedule. While airborne via Airjitzu, if you descend at least 30 ft in a straight line toward a creature and hit it with a melee attack on the same turn, the attack deals an additional 2d6 force damage and the target must succeed on a Strength save against your CE save DC or be knocked prone.

Wind Reader Advanced

Emptiness perceives. While concentrating on Airjitzu, you have advantage on Wisdom (Perception) checks to detect hidden creatures within 60 ft — disturbed air betrays them.

Rules integration

Study

How the application is learned

Study requirement: CE Manipulation (Advanced) or better, character level 7+. Thirty days of study and an INT check vs DC 15 (downtime is an honor rule at the table — the builder tracks only that you have studied it).
Not a technique: manuscripts teach CE Applications — they do not count against the two-technique limit, have no level progression, and are learned, not awakened.
Marginalia

In another hand

Sun Tzu's notes appear in every copy of the manual.

Margin note — S.

My old opponent mistakes emptiness for escape. The sky is not empty because you left it. It is empty because nothing in it requires you. He nearly understands his own art.

Margin note — S.

A man who can leave the battlefield at will has already won every battle he declines. This is the only honest sentence in the manual. Read it twice.

Margin note — S.

He warns against the storm and does not say why. I will: the wind does not distinguish. Fly too long and you forget which army was yours.
