The treatise

In the Khagan's hand

Do not stand. The standing man is a target. The marching man is a column. But the spinning man is weather, and no army in the history of the world has ever defeated weather.

I did not invent the whirlwind. I watched the steppe invent it, every spring, and I had the sense to write down what the grass already knew: that motion is armor, that the circle is a wall with no gates, that a rider who never stops is a rider who never dies.

Spin until the enemy's eyes water. Spin until their spears find only wind. Then — and only then — let the spin carry your blade. The whirlwind does not strike. It arrives.

The application

Spinjitzu

One simple engine. The applications multiply.

Spinjitzu

Bonus action · 2 CE · 1 minute.

You enter the whirlwind. For 1 minute: attacks against you have disadvantage (the deflecting vortex); your movement does not provoke opportunity attacks; your melee attacks deal an additional 1d6 force damage (momentum). You cannot spin while wearing heavy armor or while your speed is 0.

Dust Devil Advanced

Your spin kicks up a 15-ft radius storm of dust and debris centered on you, moving with you. The area is heavily obscured to everyone except you — you see through your own storm. The storm ends when the whirlwind ends.

The Noose Tightens Advanced

The horde's encirclement. While spinning, when you move within 5 ft of a creature, it cannot take reactions until the start of your next turn — no one slips the noose clean.

Rules integration

Study

How the application is learned

Study requirement: CE Manipulation (Skilled) or better, character level 3+. Thirty days of study and an INT check vs DC 15 (downtime is an honor rule at the table — the builder tracks only that you have studied it).
Not a technique: manuscripts teach CE Applications — they do not count against the two-technique limit, have no level progression, and are learned, not awakened.
Marginalia

In another hand

Sun Tzu's notes appear in every copy of the treatise.

Margin note — S.

The Khagan writes of motion and believes he has written of war. He is half right, which is the most dangerous way to be right. The whirlwind does not win because it spins. It wins because the enemy prepares for a charge and meets weather instead.

Margin note — S.

Note the honesty here, rare in conquerors: he admits the spin ends. Every art that spends the body must budget the body. The ones who forget this die tired.

Margin note — S.

He teaches encirclement as geometry. It is not. It is appetite. The circle closes because the riders are hungry, not because the map demands it.
